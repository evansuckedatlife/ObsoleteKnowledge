## summary

**Esther**, originally named **Hadassah**, was a Jewish orphan raised in the Persian citadel of Susa who rose to become queen consort to the Persian emperor *Ahasuerus* (widely identified with *Xerxes I*). Narrated in the **Book of Esther** within the Ketuvim section of the [[hebrew-bible]], her story recounts how she risked royal execution to thwart an empire-wide plot to annihilate the Jewish people orchestrated by the prime minister *Haman*. Her diplomatic wisdom and courageous intervention secured imperial permission for Jewish communities to defend themselves, an event commemorated in [[judaism]] through the festive holiday of *Purim*.

## you gotta know

- Raised by her older cousin and legal guardian *Mordecai*, who instructed her to keep her Jewish heritage secret when recruited into the imperial harem.
- Chosen as queen consort after King *Ahasuerus* banished his former wife, Queen *Vashti*, for refusing to display her beauty before the royal court.
- Risked immediate death by entering the king's inner court without being summoned, gaining royal clemency only when *Ahasuerus* extended his golden scepter to touch her.
- Foiled the extermination decree orchestrated by the Agagite grand vizier *Haman*, who cast lots (*purim*) to determine the calendar date for the slaughter of all Jews.
- Hosted two successive private banquets for the king and *Haman*, prudently waiting until the second gathering to reveal her ethnic identity and denounce *Haman*'s murderous scheme.
- Brought about the poetic execution of *Haman*, who was hanged upon the towering seventy-five-foot gallows he had constructed specifically for *Mordecai*.
- Notable within biblical literature for containing no direct reference to the divine name of God, emphasizing providential guidance operating discreetly through human initiative.
- Celebrated during the holiday of *Purim* with the public chanting of the scroll (*Megillah*), alongside reciprocal food gifts, almsgiving, and joyous carnival celebrations.

## connections

- [[hebrew-bible]] — the canonical collection that includes her story as one of the Five Megillot (*Five Scrolls*) in Ketuvim.
- [[judaism]] — the religious tradition that observes *Purim* every spring to celebrate her defense and preservation of the community.
- [[middle-east]] — the imperial territory of the ancient Achaemenid Empire centered at Susa where her story takes place.
- [[temple-in-jerusalem]] — the sacred site rebuilt during the broader Persian period when her narrative is historically situated.
- [[elisha]] — a prominent northern prophet whose miraculous deeds also safeguarded the survival of the covenant people against hostile foreign powers.
- [[david]] — the royal exemplar whose lineage ruled Israel and whose messianic hope sustained Jewish identity during post-exilic Persian rule.
- [[moses]] — the founding prophet whose legal intercessions before Pharaoh prefigured her advocacy on behalf of her people before the Persian monarch.

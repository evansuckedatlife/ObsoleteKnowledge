## summary

**Adam** and **Eve** are the first human beings in biblical tradition, created by God and placed in the Garden of Eden. According to Genesis, they lived in paradise until they disobeyed God by eating fruit from the forbidden Tree of Knowledge at the serpent's urging, resulting in humanity's expulsion from Eden and the introduction of suffering and death. Their story establishes the foundation of Judeo-Christian theology regarding human nature, free will, and the origin of sin.

## you gotta know

- Adam was created first, directly from the earth, as the image of God; Eve was created from one of his ribs as a companion.
- Placed in the Garden of Eden with abundant food and one restriction: not to eat from the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil.
- The serpent tempted Eve to eat the forbidden fruit, promising she would become like God; she then gave the fruit to Adam.
- Their disobedience triggered the Fall of Man—expulsion from paradise, loss of immortality, and the curse that humans must labor and suffer.
- Eve is often blamed in traditional theology for humanity's downfall, making her a complex symbol of both creation and transgression.
- Their sons *Cain* and *Abel* represent the first human conflict, with Cain murdering Abel in jealousy, introducing murder into the world.
- In Christian theology, Adam is called the *First Adam* and Jesus is the *Second Adam* who reverses the damage of the fall through redemption.

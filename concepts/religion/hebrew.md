## summary

**Hebrew** is an ancient Semitic language, the sacred and spoken language of Jewish tradition and the original language of most of the Hebrew Bible (*Tanakh*). Though it fell largely into disuse for centuries, it was revived as a living language in the nineteenth century and became the official language of Israel in the twentieth. Hebrew carries immense religious and cultural significance in Judaism, with Jewish law, liturgy, and religious study traditionally conducted in the language.

## you gotta know

- Hebrew emerged as the principal language of ancient Judea and was the primary medium for biblical prophecy, law, and wisdom literature.
- The Hebrew Bible contains *Torah* (the five books of Moses), *Nevi'im* (the prophets), and *Ketuvim* (the writings), all composed originally in Hebrew.
- Over centuries of diaspora, Hebrew became primarily a language of prayer and study among Jews while Aramaic and later local languages dominated daily speech.
- The revival of Hebrew as a spoken language in the late nineteenth century was central to the Zionist movement and the establishment of the modern State of Israel.
- Modern Hebrew incorporates biblical roots and grammar while adopting new vocabulary and syntax to suit contemporary secular and religious life in the modern world.
- Biblical and Modern Hebrew maintain remarkable linguistic continuity, allowing Jews to read ancient sacred texts with substantial comprehension of the original language.

## summary

**Bethlehem** is an ancient city in the Judean hill country of the [[middle-east]], situated approximately ten kilometres south of the [[temple-in-jerusalem]]. Revering an ancient heritage as the home of *Jesse* and the birthplace of King [[david]], it is celebrated throughout [[christianity]] as the birthplace of [[jesus]] of Nazareth in fulfilment of biblical prophecy. The city's storied history spans the Hebrew patriarchs, royal monarchy, imperial administration under the [[roman-empire]], and millennia of pilgrimage centered on the Church of the Nativity.

## you gotta know

- Identified in the [[gospels]] of *Matthew* and *Luke* as the historic birthplace of [[jesus]], where Mary gave birth in a manger or cave.
- Site of the biblical narrative known as the Massacre of the Innocents, wherein King *Herod the Great* ordered the execution of young boys to eliminate the prophesied King of the Jews.
- Famous in the [[hebrew-bible]] as the childhood home and town of King [[david]], earning it the lasting scriptural epithet the "City of David".
- Associated with the prophet *Micah*, whose fifth chapter prophesies that a future ruler of Israel would emerge from *Ephrathah*, an archaic name for the settlement.
- Features prominently in the Book of Ruth as the agricultural community where *Ruth* gleans the barley fields of *Boaz* and establishes the Davidic royal lineage.
- Revered as the traditional burial location of the matriarch *Rachel*, wife of [[jacob]], marked by the centuries-old pilgrimage shrine known as Rachel's Tomb.
- Houses the ancient Church of the Nativity, originally commissioned by Roman Emperor *Constantine the Great* and his mother *Helena* in the fourth century.

## connections

- [[jesus]] — the Christian messiah whose nativity took place in the town.
- [[david]] — the celebrated Israelite king who was born, anointed, and raised there.
- [[gospels]] — the scriptural texts of Matthew and Luke detailing the Nativity and Herod's decree.
- [[jacob]] — the Hebrew patriarch whose beloved wife Rachel was buried along its borders.
- [[temple-in-jerusalem]] — the sacred sanctuary situated just north of the town in the Judean hills.
- [[roman-empire]] — the imperial power governing Judea whose census prompted the journey of Mary and Joseph.
- [[christianity]] — the worldwide religious tradition for which the city remains a foremost holy site.

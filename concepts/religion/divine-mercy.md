## summary

**Divine Mercy** is a foundational theological doctrine across Abrahamic monotheism, expressing God's limitless compassion, unmerited grace, and active loving-kindness toward fallible humanity. In the traditions of [[judaism]], it is anchored in the Hebrew concept of *chesed* and the solemn revelation of the divine nature given to [[moses]]. Within [[christianity]], particularly through the teachings of [[jesus]] and the devotions of the [[catholic-church]], it constitutes the ultimate spiritual mechanism that permits sincere repentance to overcome guilt, enabling moral restoration, sanctification, and eternal salvation rather than severe retributive destruction.

## you gotta know

- Proclaimed directly to [[moses]] atop *Mount Sinai* through the *Thirteen Attributes of Mercy*, which form the liturgical backbone of Jewish penitential prayers during *Yom Kippur*.
- Contrasted in classical Jewish rabbinic commentary with strict justice, conceptualizing God as constantly restraining the divine measure of judgment through the measure of compassion.
- Taught extensively by [[jesus]] throughout the [[gospels]], most famously encapsulated in the parables of the *Prodigal Son*, the *Lost Sheep*, and the *Unmerciful Servant*.
- Emphasized as a mandatory human ethical duty in the *Sermon on the Mount*, where Christ proclaims that the merciful are blessed because they shall obtain mercy.
- Popularized in modern Catholic devotion by Saint *Faustina Kowalska*, a Polish nun whose mystical diary sparked the worldwide movement honoring Christ's merciful heart.
- Symbolized in the widely venerated *Divine Mercy Image*, depicting Christ raising his hand in blessing while red and pale rays stream from his chest, signifying blood and cleansing water.
- Instituted as a universal feast day across the [[catholic-church]] by Pope *John Paul II* in 2000, designated to be observed annually on the Sunday following Easter.
- Invoked continually in [[islam]] through the sacred formula known as the *Basmala*, honoring God as *Ar-Rahman* and *Ar-Rahim*, the most merciful and beneficent.

## connections

- [[divine-judgment]] — twin theological reality balancing righteous punishment with compassionate clemency in sacred scripture.
- [[jesus]] — central prophetic figure whose earthly ministry, healing miracles, and sacrificial atonement incarnate God's loving-kindness.
- [[catholic-church]] — global Christian communion that formally canonized Saint *Faustina Kowalska* and established the liturgical feast day.
- [[judaism]] — original covenantal faith celebrating God's enduring loving-kindness (*chesed*) across generations of prophetic history.
- [[disciples]] — initial circle of followers commanded by Christ to forgive offenders repeatedly and embody unconditional charity.
- [[moses]] — Hebrew prophet who petitioned God on behalf of Israel and witnessed the divine proclamation of mercy on *Mount Sinai*.
- [[gospels]] — New Testament books documenting Christ's theological parables and interactions demonstrating unmerited grace to outcasts.
- [[islam]] — monotheistic faith whose every devotional prayer begins with the invocation of God's supreme mercy.

## summary

The **Binding of Isaac**, known in Hebrew tradition as the *Akedah*, is the climactic trial of faith recorded in the twenty-second chapter of the Book of Genesis. In the narrative, God commands the patriarch [[abraham]] to journey to Mount Moriah and offer his cherished son *Isaac* as a burnt sacrifice. Serving as a foundational theological episode across [[judaism]], [[christianity]], and [[islam]], the event dramatically explores divine obedience, the rejection of child sacrifice, and the eternal renewal of the patriarchal covenant.

## you gotta know

- Initiated when God commands [[abraham]] to sacrifice his "only son" *Isaac*, whom he loves, on a mountain in the land of Moriah.
- Halted at the critical moment by the Angel of the Lord, who calls out from heaven to stay Abraham's hand just as the blade is raised.
- Resolved by the miraculous substitution of a ram caught in a nearby thicket by its horns, which Abraham offers in place of his son.
- Commemorated within Jewish liturgy during Rosh Hashanah, where the sounding of the shofar or ram's horn recalls Abraham's unwavering obedience.
- Linked in Jewish tradition and the Chronicles narrative to Mount Moriah, identified as the future site of the [[temple-in-jerusalem]].
- Interpreted in early Christian theology as an essential typological foreshadowing of God the Father sacrificing his son [[jesus]] on the cross.
- Paralleled in [[islam]] by the account of Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, an act commemorated globally during the festival of *Eid al-Adha*.
- Analyzed in 19th-century philosophy by *Søren Kierkegaard* in *Fear and Trembling* as an emblem of the teleological suspension of the ethical and the knight of faith.

## connections

- [[abraham]] — the faithful patriarch who is tested by the divine command to surrender his beloved son.
- [[birthright]] — the covenantal inheritance passed downward from Abraham and Isaac to [[jacob]].
- [[jacob]] — the son of Isaac who inherits the patriarchal blessing preserved through this event.
- [[temple-in-jerusalem]] — the holy sanctuary traditionally believed to occupy the crest of Mount Moriah.
- [[jesus]] — viewed in Christian typology as the archetype prefigured by Isaac carrying the wood for his own sacrifice.
- [[torah]] — the sacred scroll of the law containing the foundational Genesis narrative.
- [[judaism]] — the religious heritage wherein the event is recalled for divine mercy and ancestral merit.
- [[islam]] — the sister Abrahamic tradition honoring the supreme submission of Ibrahim.

## summary

**Joshua** is the military leader and judge who succeeded *Moses* and led the Israelites into the Promised Land of *Canaan* after forty years of wilderness wandering. Where *Moses* was the lawgiver and liberator from Egypt, *Joshua* became the conqueror and consolidator—his campaigns against the Canaanite kingdoms are narrated in the book bearing his name, and he represents the transition from nomadic desert life to settled agricultural existence. His strategic brilliance and faith in God's guidance made him the paradigmatic warrior-leader of the Hebrew Bible.

## you gotta know

- *Joshua* was originally named *Hosea* but *Moses* renamed him *Joshua*, meaning "the Lord saves," signaling his destined role in leading Israel to salvation in the promised land.
- He was one of twelve spies *Moses* sent to scout *Canaan*; only he and *Caleb* returned with faith that Israel could conquer the land, though he was forced to wait forty years as punishment for the people's doubt.
- After *Moses's* death, *God* appointed *Joshua* as the new leader of Israel, and the book of *Joshua* narrates his military campaigns to conquer the land west of the *Jordan* River.
- The miraculous crossing of the *Jordan* River on dry ground paralleled the crossing of the Red Sea under *Moses*, establishing *Joshua's* legitimacy as *Moses's* successor.
- The most famous episode is the siege of *Jericho*, where the Israelite army marched around the city walls for seven days; on the seventh day, they trumpeted and the walls collapsed, allowing entry.
- *Joshua* divided the conquered territory among the twelve tribes of Israel, establishing tribal borders and inheritance portions that became foundational to Israelite settlement patterns.
- At the end of his life, *Joshua* gathered the Israelites and led a covenant renewal ceremony, reaffirming their commitment to the God of *Abraham*, *Isaac*, and *Jacob*.
- The biblical account portrays him as a military genius and a man of unwavering faith, standing in clear contrast to the murmuring and doubt of the wilderness generation.

## connections

- [[moses]] — his predecessor and the one who designated him as his successor.
- [[hebrew-bible]] — the scriptural tradition documenting his life and conquests.
- [[israel]] — the nation he united and settled in the promised land.
- [[canaan]] — the territory he conquered and apportioned to the tribes.
- [[judges]] — the period immediately following his era, when local judges governed Israel.
- [[david]] — the later warrior-king who built upon the territorial foundation *Joshua* established.
- [[aaron]] — the high priest during *Joshua's* era, continuing priestly legitimacy.

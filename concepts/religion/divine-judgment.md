## summary

**Divine Judgment** is the theological doctrine asserting that God exercises supreme judicial authority over creation, holding moral agents accountable and executing righteous verdicts against sin. Across the scriptures of the [[hebrew-bible]], [[christianity]], and [[islam]], it encompasses both historical interventions within human affairs and an ultimate eschatological reckoning at the culmination of history. Whether manifested through dramatic biblical chastisements or the cosmic courtroom of the *Last Judgment*, the principle affirms that cosmic justice is real, evil will be vanquished, and moral choices possess eternal significance.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the preeminent theological theme underlying the primeval flood narrative in *Genesis*, where rampant human wickedness prompted a global watery purge.
- Characterized in the prophetic literature of the [[hebrew-bible]] as the *Day of the Lord*, an apocalyptic reckoning bringing terror to the arrogant and vindication to the righteous.
- Illustrated in historic biblical accounts through severe acts of divine wrath, including the rain of fire upon *Sodom and Gomorrah* and the plagues cast upon Pharaoh's Egypt.
- Preached vigorously by [[jesus]] in the [[gospels]], who warned of outer darkness and articulated the separation of the righteous sheep from the cursed goats.
- Directly resolved in Christian soteriology through the [[crucifixion]], wherein Christ voluntarily bears vicarious judgment to satisfy divine holiness and reconcile believers.
- Conceptualized in Christian eschatology as the *General Judgment* or *Great White Throne Judgment*, where the dead are resurrected to face examination of their earthly deeds.
- Visualized across European art in monumental depictions of the *Last Judgment*, including *Michelangelo*'s Sistine Chapel altar fresco and *Giotto*'s Arena Chapel murals.
- Articulated in [[islam]] as *Yawm al-Din* (the Day of Judgment), where individual deeds recorded in heavenly ledgers are weighed upon an infallible cosmic scale.

## connections

- [[divine-mercy]] — indispensable complementary doctrine that offers pardon, redemption, and refuge from the severity of retributive wrath.
- [[hebrew-bible]] — primary scriptural collection recording covenantal stipulations, divine warnings, and historical catastrophes befalling disobedience.
- [[crucifixion]] — defining New Testament event where Christian theology locates the supreme collision and satisfaction of divine wrath and grace.
- [[jesus]] — messianic figure described in Christian scripture as the designated sovereign judge who will return to judge the living and dead.
- [[disciples]] — apostles instructed by Christ on apocalyptic readiness, spiritual vigilance, and their future role judging the tribes of Israel.
- [[temple-in-jerusalem]] — sacred sanctuary whose destruction by Babylon and Rome was interpreted by prophets as catastrophic divine discipline.
- [[delilah]] — instrumental figure whose treacherous betrayal triggered *Samson*'s ultimate plea for God to execute vengeance on his Philistine tormentors.
- [[judaism]] — religious tradition focusing on ethical monotheism, covenantal accountability, and divine justice throughout human history.

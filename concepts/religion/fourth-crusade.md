## summary

The **Fourth Crusade** was a Latin Christian military campaign initiated in 1202 with the intended goal of recapturing Ayyubid-controlled Jerusalem by invading Egypt. Plagued by catastrophic financial debts owed to Venice, the crusader army became entangled in Byzantine dynastic politics and infamously diverted to Constantinople. In 1204, the crusaders breached and brutally pillaged the Christian capital, partitioning the Byzantine realm into the short-lived Latin Empire and deepening the East-West Schism beyond repair.

## you gotta know

- Proclaimed by *Pope Innocent III* in 1198 to reclaim the Holy Sepulchre following the failures of the Third Crusade.
- Contracted the Republic of Venice under the blind, octogenarian doge *Enrico Dandolo* to construct an immense transport fleet, but the crusaders arrived with far fewer men than expected and were unable to pay.
- Diverted first to siege and capture the Catholic city of *Zara* in Dalmatia to pay off maritime transport debts, earning an immediate papal excommunication from *Pope Innocent III*.
- Enticed to redirect toward Constantinople by the exiled prince *Alexios IV Angelos*, who promised massive cash payouts, military levies, and reunion of the Eastern church with Rome.
- Sacked Constantinople over three devastating days in April 1204 after *Alexios IV* was murdered in an anti-Latin palace coup led by *Alexios V Doukas* ("Mourtzouphlos").
- Dispersed centuries of imperial wealth, ancient relics, and classical statuary across Western Europe, including the bronze *Horses of Saint Mark* carried off to Venice.
- Established the *Latin Empire of Constantinople* under *Baldwin IX of Flanders*, forcing the Byzantine elite into splinter states such as the *Empire of Nicaea* until 1261.

## connections

- [[first-crusade]] — the original armed expedition that established the Latin states the later campaigns sought to rescue.
- [[catholic-church]] — the Western ecclesiastical institution whose pontiff launched the crusade but lost control of the army.
- [[roman-empire]] — the medieval Byzantine polity that suffered catastrophic collapse when its capital was conquered.
- [[christianity]] — the shared religion whose eastern and western branches fractured irrevocably after the 1204 sack.
- [[italy]] — the home peninsula of Venice and *Enrico Dandolo*, who orchestrated the tactical diversion.
- [[france]] — the origin of most baronial leadership, including *Geoffroi de Villehardouin* and *Boniface of Montferrat*.
- [[middle-east]] — the intended theater of operations that the expedition ultimately abandoned entirely.

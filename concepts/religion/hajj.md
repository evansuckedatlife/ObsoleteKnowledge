## summary

The **Hajj** (also transliterated as **Hadj**) is the sacred annual pilgrimage to *Mecca* in modern-day *Saudi Arabia* that constitutes the fifth of the *Five Pillars of Islam*. Undertaken during the Islamic lunar month of *Dhu al-Hijjah*, the journey is an obligatory religious duty that every adult Muslim who possesses the physical and financial capability (*istita'ah*) must perform at least once in their lifetime. As one of the largest peaceful human gatherings on Earth, it unites millions of worshippers across national, ethnic, and linguistic divides in a shared sequence of rites tracing back to the patriarch *[[abraham]]* and the final prophet *[[muhammad]]*.

## you gotta know

- Pilgrims enter a consecrated spiritual state known as *ihram* before crossing geographical boundaries called *miqat*, during which men don two unstitched white garments and all participants abstain from perfumed cosmetics, cutting hair, dispute, and marital relations to manifest absolute equality before God.
- The primary ritual sequence centers in *Mecca* on *tawaf*, the seven counter-clockwise circumambulations of the cube-shaped *[[kaaba]]*, followed by *sa'i*, a brisk walk repeated seven times between the hills of *Safa* and *Marwa* reenacting *Hagar*'s desperate search for water for her infant son *Ishmael*.
- The spiritual pinnacle and indispensable prerequisite of the pilgrimage is *Wuquf* on the plains of Mount *Arafat* on the ninth of *Dhu al-Hijjah*, where worshippers spend from midday to dusk in supplication and repentance upon the site where *[[muhammad]]* delivered his Farewell Sermon.
- After gathering pebbles overnight under the open sky at *Muzdalifah*, pilgrims travel to *Mina* to perform *Ramy al-Jamarat*, the symbolic stoning of three stone pillars that represent the devil's attempts to persuade *[[abraham]]* to disobey God's command.
- The culmination of the rites coincides with the worldwide celebration of *Eid al-Adha*, marked by the sacrificial slaughter of livestock (*qurbani*), the ritual shaving or trimming of hair (*halq* or *taqsir*), and a final departure circumambulation (*tawaf al-wada*).
- The pilgrimage famously revolutionized the thought of *[[civil-rights-movement]]* leader *Malcolm X* in 1964, who wrote that witnessing sincere brotherhood among worshippers of every race compelled him to abandon racial separatism and adopt mainstream Sunni *[[islam]]* under the name *el-Hajj Malik el-Shabazz*.
- Historically, pilgrim caravans reshaped Afro-Eurasian trade networks, most dramatically in 1324 when *Mansa Musa* of the Mali Empire distributed so much West African gold during his transit through Cairo that he depressed bullion values across the Mediterranean basin for over a decade.

## connections

- [[islam]] — the global religion in which this pilgrimage stands as the fifth essential pillar of practice.
- [[muhammad]] — the prophet whose 632 CE Farewell Pilgrimage established the enduring liturgical model for all believers.
- [[abraham]] — the revered patriarch whose devotion, trials, and purification of the sanctuary are reenacted through the rites.
- [[quran]] — the foundational text that mandates the pilgrimage and outlines its seasonal prescriptions in Surah *Al-Baqarah* and Surah *Al-Hajj*.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — the twentieth-century American struggle deeply influenced when *Malcolm X* returned from *Mecca* with an altered philosophy of racial equality.
- [[ottoman-empire]] — the imperial dynasty whose rulers drew immense prestige and legitimacy from their title as protectors and sponsors of the annual Damascus and Cairo caravans.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the transformative era in which European maritime expansion across the Indian Ocean brought traditional sea routes carrying pilgrims under Portuguese, Dutch, and British naval oversight.

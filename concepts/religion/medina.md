## summary

**Medina** is the second holiest city in Islam and the sanctuary that received *Muhammad* and his followers during the *Hijra* (migration) from *Mecca*. Formerly known as *Yathrib*, the oasis city became the center of early Islamic community and governance under *Muhammad*, and it was in *Medina* that Islam's legal and social framework crystallized. The Prophet's presence and the establishment of the first Muslim state in *Medina* transformed it from a prosperous trade town into the foundational center of Islamic civilization.

## you gotta know

- *Medina* is located approximately two hundred ninety miles north of *Mecca* in the Hejaz region of western Arabia, an oasis settlement that had previously been important for trade along caravan routes.
- The pre-Islamic city of *Yathrib* was divided among rival Arab tribes and Jewish communities; *Muhammad* was invited to arbitrate their conflicts, which set the stage for his move there.
- *Muhammad's* migration to *Medina* in 622 CE marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar—year 1 AH (Anno Hegirae, "Year of the Hijra")—making the *Hijra* the foundational event of Islamic civilization.
- In *Medina*, *Muhammad* established the first Muslim community (*umma*), combining spiritual leadership with political and military authority, and developed much of Islamic law and practice.
- The *Constitution of Medina*, a document traditionally attributed to *Muhammad's* first year there, established agreements between the Muslim community and the various tribes and religious groups in the city.
- *Medina* was the site of several crucial early Islamic battles, including the Battle of Uhud and the Battle of the Trench, which secured the Muslim community's position and demonstrated Islamic military organization.
- *Muhammad* lived and preached in *Medina* for approximately ten years before his death in 632 CE; his house there became the foundation for the first mosque, the Masjid an-Nabawi (Prophet's Mosque).
- The Masjid an-Nabawi contains *Muhammad's* tomb and remains one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in Islam, second only to the Kaaba in *Mecca*; pilgrimage to *Medina* is not obligatory (*hadj*) but is highly encouraged.

## connections

- [[islam]] — the religion founded and established in *Medina* under *Muhammad's* leadership.
- [[muhammad]] — the Prophet whose presence and governance transformed *Medina* into the center of Islam.
- [[hijra]] — the migration to *Medina* that marked the beginning of Islamic civilization and the Islamic calendar.
- [[mecca]] — the holiest city in Islam, where *Muhammad* was born and from which the *Hijra* departed.
- [[saudi-arabia]] — the modern nation in which *Medina* is located, making it a key geographic center of Islamic faith.
- [[umma]] — the Muslim community (*ummah*) that *Muhammad* established as a political and spiritual entity in *Medina*.
- [[hadj]] — the pilgrimage that brings millions to Arabia annually, with *Medina* a major destination alongside *Mecca*.

## summary

The **Baháʼí Faith** (also written as the **Baha'i Faith**) is an independent global monotheistic religion established in nineteenth-century *Persia* by *Bahá'u'lláh*. Originating from the messianic *Bábí* revival, the faith proclaims the essential unity of God, the fundamental kinship of all world religions, and the inevitable unification of humanity. Headquartered at the *Universal House of Justice* in *Haifa*, it is known for its egalitarian social doctrines, administrative governance without clergy, and distinctive nine-sided houses of worship.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1863 in *Baghdad* when *Mírzá Husayn-ʻAlí Núrí*, known as *Bahá'u'lláh* ("Glory of God"), announced his prophetic station during the twelve-day festival in the *Garden of Ridván*.
- Preceded by the millenarian *Bábí* movement founded in 1844 by *Siyyid ʻAlí Muhammad Shírází*, known as the *Báb* ("the Gate"), who was executed in 1850 in *Tabriz*.
- Its central book of laws and community ordinances is the *Kitáb-i-Aqdas* ("The Most Holy Book"), composed while *Bahá'u'lláh* was imprisoned in the Ottoman penal colony of *Acre*.
- Key theological works include the *Kitáb-i-Íqán* ("The Book of Certitude"), which expounds the doctrine of progressive revelation, and the mystical meditations *The Hidden Words* and *The Seven Valleys*.
- Central theology holds that religious truth is progressive and unrolled cyclically through divine messengers or "Manifestations of God," a lineage that includes *Abraham*, *Moses*, *Buddha*, *Jesus*, and *Muhammad*.
- The worldwide faith is governed by an elected nine-member council known as the *Universal House of Justice*, seated on the slopes of *Mount Carmel* in *Haifa*, *Israel*, overlooking the monumental terraces and the *Shrine of the Báb*.
- Successive leadership was conferred upon *Bahá'u'lláh*'s eldest son *ʻAbdu'l-Bahá* as the appointed interpreter and "Center of the Covenant", followed by his great-grandson *Shoghi Effendi*, the "Guardian" of the faith.
- Continental temples, or Houses of Worship (*Mashriq al-Adhkár*), are defined by a universal nine-sided dome architecture with nine entrances welcoming all peoples, most famously exemplified by the Lotus Temple in *New Delhi*.

## connections

- [[islam]] — the faith's historical origin and theological matrix in nineteenth-century Twelver Shia *Persia*.
- [[middle-east]] — the geographic theater of the faith's formative persecutions, exiles, and administrative establishment.
- [[muhammad]] — revered as a divine Manifestation of God whose prophetic legacy directly preceded the Bábí and Bahá'í revelations.
- [[jesus]] — honored as a Manifestation of God whose gospel of love foreshadowed the unification of humanity.
- [[abraham]] — venerated as a foundational prophet whose ancient covenant links the monotheistic traditions.
- [[buddhism]] — integrated into the Bahá'í theology of universal, cyclic revelation across Eastern and Western traditions.
- [[hinduism]] — recognized as a divinely inspired dispensation whose historic teachers revealed divine truth suited to their era.

## summary

**Canaan** was the ancient Semitic-speaking region encompassing the Levant, roughly corresponding to modern Levant territories including Israel, the Palestinian territories, Lebanon, and western Jordan. In the biblical tradition of the *[[hebrew-bible]]*, it is venerated as the Promised Land pledged by God to *[[abraham]]* and his descendants. The narrative of its conquest under Joshua and apportionment among the Twelve Tribes serves as a foundational geopolitical and theological turning point in ancient Jewish history.

## you gotta know

- Pledged to the patriarch *[[abraham]]* in the covenant recorded in the Book of Genesis, where it is famously described as a land flowing with milk and honey.
- Inhabited prior to the Israelite settlement by various indigenous nations, including the Jebusites, Amorites, and Phoenicians, who practiced polytheistic religions centered on deities like Baal and Asherah.
- Spied out on the instructions of *[[moses]]* by twelve scouts sent from Kadesh Barnea, though only Joshua and Caleb returned with an encouraging assessment of conquering the fortified territory.
- Conquered through the campaigns led by Joshua, beginning with the dramatic miraculous fall of the fortified walls of Jericho.
- Apportioned among the Twelve Tribes of Israel, establishing the tribal boundaries that preceded the establishment of the united monarchy under Saul and *[[david]]*.
- Governed during its transitional settlement phase by charismatic military chieftains known as judges, who repelled regional oppressors such as the Philistines and Midianites.
- Known in archaeological and ancient Egyptian records through the Amarna letters, which describe fourteenth-century BCE diplomatic turbulence and vassal city-states in the territory.

## connections

- [[abraham]] — the patriarch who received the divine covenant promising the territory to his seed.
- [[moses]] — the prophet who led the Israelites out of Egypt to the eastern border of the promised territory.
- [[hebrew-bible]] — the canonical collection that chronicles the conquest and settlement of the region.
- [[torah]] — the foundational text detailing the patriarchal wanderings and territorial promises.
- [[judaism]] — the religion rooted in the covenantal inheritance of the holy territory.
- [[david]] — the monarch who established Jerusalem within the territory as the national and religious capital.
- [[temple-in-jerusalem]] — the central sanctuary constructed in the territory by Solomon.

## summary

The **Disciples** were the dedicated students and followers of [[jesus]] during his first-century ministry across Judea and Galilee, called to learn his teachings and proclaim the kingdom of God. While the term applied broadly to the wider multitude adhering to his doctrine, it specifically designated the inner fraternity known as the *Twelve Apostles*, hand-picked by Christ to form the foundational core of the early church. Their transition from everyday laborers into empowered apostolic leaders after the resurrection represents the historical genesis and missionary engine of [[christianity]].

## you gotta know

- Selected by [[jesus]] from diverse backgrounds in Galilee, encompassing humble fishermen such as [[peter]], *Andrew*, *James*, and [[john-the-apostle]], alongside *Matthew*, a despised tax collector.
- Endowed with the title of *Apostles*, derived from the Greek verb *apostellein* meaning to send out, designating their commission to preach, heal illnesses, and cast out demons.
- Accompanied Christ through all major events of his ministry, including his public miracles, the *Transfiguration* upon the mount, and the intimate gathering of the *Last Supper*.
- Portrayed with candid human vulnerability in the [[gospels]], regularly misunderstanding Christ's spiritual mission, quarreling over personal rank, and fleeing upon his arrest in *Gethsemane*.
- Entrusted with the *Great Commission* following the resurrection, which charged them to make disciples of all nations and baptize believers throughout the world.
- Transformed on the Jewish festival of *Pentecost* when the Holy Spirit descended upon them as tongues of fire, granting them miraculous speech and fearless evangelical boldness.
- Endured the betrayal of *Judas Iscariot*, who surrendered Christ for thirty silver coins and whose subsequent suicide led to *Matthias* being elected to restore the twelve.
- Suffered widespread martyrdom according to apostolic tradition throughout the [[roman-empire]] and beyond, establishing the lineage of apostolic authority claimed by the [[catholic-church]].

## connections

- [[jesus]] — central messianic master whose life, teachings, ethical parables, and miraculous signs served as the sole focus of their discipleship.
- [[peter]] — outspoken Galilean fisherman designated by Christ as the rock upon which the community of the faithful would be established.
- [[john-the-apostle]] — the beloved disciple who reclined beside Jesus at the Last Supper and stood beside the cross during the [[crucifixion]].
- [[paul]] — missionary visionary and theologian who claimed apostolic status through a post-resurrection apparition on the road to Damascus.
- [[gospels]] — canonical biographical texts detailing their calling, instruction, moral shortcomings, and commission to spread the faith.
- [[christianity]] — world religion whose early theology, worship practices, and institutional expansion were forged by their global witness.
- [[divine-mercy]] — transformative grace experienced personally by the twelve when the risen Christ pardoned their cowardice and abandonment.
- [[divine-judgment]] — solemn theological warning communicated to them regarding faithful stewardship, apocalyptic trials, and final accountability.

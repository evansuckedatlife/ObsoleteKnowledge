## summary

**Ahab** was the seventh king of the northern kingdom of Israel and the prominent son of King *Omri*, ruling during the ninth century BCE from his royal capital at Samaria. His reign is depicted in the *[[hebrew-bible]]* as a nadir of apostasy and moral decay due to his diplomatic alliance with Phoenicia and state promotion of pagan worship. Despite achieving considerable military power and diplomatic stature across the ancient *[[middle-east]]*, he is best remembered in religious tradition for his bitter clashes with the prophet *[[elijah]]*.

## you gotta know

- Condemned by the biblical text of *[[1-kings]]* as having done more evil in the sight of the Lord than all who were before him.
- Married the Tyrian princess *[[jezebel]]*, establishing diplomatic and trade ties with Phoenicia while sponsoring the cults of *Baal* and *Asherah* in Samaria.
- Clashed with the prophet *[[elijah]]*, who confronted the royal court and announced a multi-year drought across the land as divine judgment.
- Summoned the assembly at Mount Carmel where *[[elijah]]* confronted the prophets of *Baal* in a contest of fire from heaven.
- Coveted the ancestral property of *Naboth* the Jezreelite, prompting *[[jezebel]]* to arrange *Naboth*'s judicial murder by false witnesses.
- Repented with sackcloth and fasting when warned of dynastic destruction by *[[elijah]]*, causing the divine judgment to be postponed to the days of his sons.
- Participated in historical regional alliances against the Neo-Assyrian Empire, contributing two thousand chariots at the Battle of Qarqar as recorded on the Kurkh Monolith.
- Died in battle at Ramoth-Gilead after entering combat in disguise, struck by a randomly shot arrow, fulfilling prophecies that dogs would lick his blood.

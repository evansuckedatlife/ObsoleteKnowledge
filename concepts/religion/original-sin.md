## summary

**Original sin** is the Christian theological doctrine that humanity inherits a fallen, sinful nature from the transgression of *Adam* and *Eve* in the Garden of *Eden*. This inherited corruption is understood to affect all humans universally, separating them from God and requiring divine intervention for salvation. The concept profoundly shaped Western Christianity's understanding of human nature, divine grace, and the necessity of *Jesus* Christ's redemptive sacrifice.

## you gotta know

- Original sin stems from *Adam* and *Eve's* disobedience in eating the forbidden fruit from the Tree of Knowledge, an act that violated God's command and brought death and exile into the human condition.
- The doctrine teaches that this transgression is not confined to *Adam* and *Eve* but has been transmitted to all their descendants—humanity is born into a state of sinfulness inherited from the first humans.
- *Augustine* of Hippo systematized the doctrine of original sin in Christian theology, arguing that all humans are corrupted by the sin of *Adam* and require baptism and God's grace to be saved.
- The doctrine differs markedly from Jewish theology, which emphasizes the individual's capacity for repentance and righteousness rather than inherited sinfulness as the human condition.
- In Christian understanding, original sin is the reason *Jesus* Christ was necessary—his sacrificial death and resurrection provide the grace that cleanses humanity of inherited sin.
- The concept is tied to anthropological pessimism: humans are viewed as incapable of achieving righteousness through their own efforts; salvation depends entirely on divine mercy.
- Different Christian traditions interpret original sin in varying degrees—some emphasizing total depravity, others holding that humans retain some capacity for good despite the Fall.
- The protoevangelium (the "proto-gospel" in Genesis 3:15) is interpreted in Christian tradition as God's promise that a redeemer would eventually crush the serpent, referring to *Jesus*.

- [[christianity]] — the faith that developed and centralized the doctrine of original sin.

- [[divine-punishment]] — the consequence of transgression that brought death and suffering into the world.

- [[grace]] — the unmerited divine favor necessary to overcome inherited sinfulness.

## summary

The **First Crusade** was a major Western Christian military expedition launched in 1095 to capture the Holy Land from Muslim control and aid the Byzantine Empire. Spurred by papal preaching, tens of thousands of knights, commoners, and clerics marched across Europe to Anatolia and the Levant, culminating in the bloody capture of Jerusalem in 1099. It established four Latin crusader states in the Near East and transformed medieval religious warfare, Mediterranean geopolitics, and Christian-Muslim relations for centuries.

## you gotta know

- Preached by *Pope Urban II* at the *Council of Clermont* in 1095 with the famous rallying cry *Deus vult* ("God wills it"), offering plenary indulgences to participants.
- Triggered in part by an appeal from the Byzantine emperor *Alexios I Komnenos*, who sought Western mercenary aid against the expanding Seljuk Turks.
- Preceded by the ill-fated *People's Crusade* led by *Peter the Hermit* and *Walter Sans Avoir*, whose disorganized mobs carried out brutal anti-Jewish pogroms across the Rhineland before being annihilated in Anatolia.
- Led by prominent feudal princes rather than reigning monarchs, including *Godfrey of Bouillon*, *Raymond IV of Toulouse*, *Bohemond of Taranto*, and *Robert Curthose* of Normandy.
- Survived a grueling, disease-ridden siege at Antioch in 1097–1098, where morale was revived by the controversial discovery of the purported *Holy Lance* by *Peter Bartholomew*.
- Stormed Jerusalem in July 1099, resulting in an infamous, indiscriminate massacre of Muslim and Jewish inhabitants, after which *Godfrey of Bouillon* took the title *Advocatus Sancti Sepulchri* ("Defender of the Holy Sepulchre").
- Established the *Outremer* crusader polities: the *Kingdom of Jerusalem*, the *County of Edessa*, the *Principality of Antioch*, and later the *County of Tripoli*.

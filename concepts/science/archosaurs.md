## summary

**Archosaurs** are a premier clade of diapsid reptiles that includes living crocodilians and birds, as well as extinct non-avian dinosaurs, pterosaurs, and diverse pseudosuchian relatives. Originating in the Early Triassic following the catastrophic Permian-Triassic extinction, they rapidly radiated to establish ecological dominance over terrestrial and aerial environments throughout the Mesozoic Era. Their anatomical innovations, particularly specialized skull fenestrae, erect limb postures, and highly efficient flow-through pulmonary systems, cemented their standing as one of the most successful vertebrate lineages in evolutionary history.

## you gotta know

- Crown group defined phylogenetically as the most recent common ancestor of birds and crocodilians along with all of its descendants.
- Split basal evolutionary lines into two primary lineages: *Pseudosuchia* (crocodile-line archosaurs) and *Avemetatarsalia* or *Pan-Aves* (bird-line archosaurs).
- Characterized by distinct osteological markers, notably the presence of *antorbital fenestrae* in the skull and *mandibular fenestrae* along the lower jaw.
- Evolved serrated teeth anchored within deep sockets, an anatomical condition termed *thecodonty*, minimizing tooth loss during active predation.
- Transitioned from sprawling reptilian locomotion toward semi-erect and fully erect pillar-erect or parasagittal limb postures, improving running stamina and gait biomechanics.
- Developed continuous unidirectional airflow within the lungs facilitated by air sacs, an evolutionary trait preserved in modern avian and crocodilian lineages.
- Produced the first vertebrates capable of powered flight through *pterosaurs* in the Triassic and later through paravian theropod dinosaurs in the Jurassic.
- Survived catastrophic extinction events, with avian neornithines and semi-aquatic crocodyliforms persevering beyond the end-Cretaceous asteroid impact.

## connections

- [[evolution]] — diverse radiations show extensive evolutionary transitions including bipedal running, gigantism, homeothermy, and powered avian flight.
- [[armored-fish]] — earlier aquatic vertebrates whose defensive dermal structures foreshadowed the osteoderms that armor many crocodylian archosaurs.
- [[cell]] — specialized osteocytes and vascular channels within fibrolamellar bone enabled rapid juvenile growth rates across Mesozoic lineages.
- [[dna]] — phylogenetic studies utilizing modern avian and crocodilian genomics calibrate molecular clock estimates for deep reptile divergence times.
- [[oxygen]] — low atmospheric levels across the Triassic period drove selection toward highly efficient respiratory architectures with unidirectional airflow.
- [[carbon]] — massive perturbations in atmospheric isotopic ratios triggered the extinction filters through which archosaur ancestors radiated.

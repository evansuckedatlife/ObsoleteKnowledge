## summary

**Vitamin A** refers to a group of fat-soluble, unsaturated organic compounds that includes *retinol*, *retinal*, and *retinoic acid*, along with provitamin A carotenoids such as *beta-carotene*. It plays an indispensable role in human physiology by enabling vision through the light-absorbing pigment *rhodopsin*, directing cellular differentiation and embryonic development, and maintaining epithelial tissue integrity. Deficiency in vitamin A remains a leading cause of preventable childhood blindness worldwide, while excessive intake results in toxic hypervitaminosis.

## you gotta know

- Comprises several biologically active forms, primarily the storage and transport form *retinol*, the visual chromophore *retinal*, and the transcription-regulating hormone *retinoic acid*.
- Forms the visual chromophore *11-cis-retinal*, which binds covalently to the opsin protein to constitute the visual pigment *rhodopsin* within retinal rod cells.
- Undergoes photoisomerization from *11-cis-retinal* to *all-trans-retinal* upon absorbing a photon, triggering a conformational change that activates the G-protein transducin and initiates the visual signal cascade.
- Functions through its metabolite *retinoic acid* as a high-affinity nuclear receptor ligand, binding retinoic acid receptors (RAR) and retinoid X receptors (RXR) to regulate gene expression during embryogenesis and organogenesis.
- Sourced dietary-wise as preformed retinyl esters from animal tissues such as liver, fish oils, and dairy, or as provitamin A carotenoids like *beta-carotene* found in orange and dark green leafy vegetables.
- Severe deficiency causes *nyctalopia* (night blindness), progression to dry eyes (*xerophthalmia*), the appearance of keratinized *Bitot's spots* on the conjunctiva, and eventual irreversible corneal ulceration.
- Essential for immune competence and mucosal barrier maintenance, making deficient children significantly more vulnerable to fatal complications from measles and diarrheal illnesses.
- Displays severe teratogenicity when consumed in excess during pregnancy, as high systemic retinoid concentrations disrupt normal craniofacial, cardiac, and central nervous system development.

## connections

- [[cell]] — requires retinoic acid signaling to regulate epithelial differentiation and prevent pathological squamous metaplasia.
- [[protein]] — provides the opsin apoproteins that couple with retinal to form light-sensing pigments across photoreceptors.
- [[enzyme]] — includes alcohol dehydrogenases and retinaldehyde dehydrogenases that catalyze the multistep oxidation of retinol to retinoic acid.
- [[photosynthesis]] — shares evolutionary links and metabolic pathways in plants and algae that biosynthesize carotenoid accessory pigments like beta-carotene.
- [[dna]] — contains retinoic acid response elements (RAREs) targeted by ligand-bound retinoid receptors to activate transcription.
- [[cell-cycle]] — regulated in specialized tissues by retinoic acid, which arrests proliferation and promotes terminal differentiation.

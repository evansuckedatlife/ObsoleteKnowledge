## summary

**Carbon dioxide** is a linear, colorless chemical compound composed of a single carbon atom covalently double-bonded to two oxygen atoms. Naturally occurring in Earth's atmosphere, it plays an indispensable role in the terrestrial carbon cycle as the substrate for organic synthesis and the primary long-lived anthropogenic greenhouse gas. Its atmospheric concentration strongly dictates planetary surface temperatures and global climate dynamics.

## you gotta know

- Discovered as an individual gas distinct from air by Scottish physician *Joseph Black*, who called it *fixed air* in the 1750s.
- Features a linear molecular geometry with a central carbon atom flanked by two oxygen atoms, resulting in no permanent net dipole moment.
- Absorbs and re-emits terrestrial infrared radiation through fundamental vibrational modes, principally its degenerate bending vibration around 15 micrometers.
- Fixed into organic sugar molecules during the light-independent reactions of biological carbon fixation by the plant enzyme *RuBisCO*.
- Serves as the primary chemical byproduct of aerobic cellular respiration across eukaryotic life and industrial fossil fuel combustion.
- Atmospheric concentrations have risen significantly since pre-industrial times, climbing from approximately 280 parts per million to over 420 parts per million.
- Dissolves in planetary oceans to yield aqueous *carbonic acid*, which dissociates into bicarbonate and hydronium ions, driving oceanic acidification.

## summary

**Christa McAuliffe** was an American educator from Concord, New Hampshire, chosen from more than 11,000 applicants to become the first private citizen and teacher in space. Selected for the Teacher in Space Project established by the Reagan administration, she trained as a payload specialist aboard the Space Shuttle *Challenger* on mission STS-51-L. Her tragic death alongside six crewmates when the shuttle broke apart 73 seconds into flight on January 28, 1986, profoundly altered the American space program.

## you gotta know

- Taught American history, law, and economics as a high school social studies teacher at Concord High School in New Hampshire.
- Selected in July 1985 out of over 11,000 educators who applied for NASA's newly created *Teacher in Space Project*.
- Planned to teach several live educational television lessons to schoolchildren across the United States while in orbit aboard the orbiter.
- Assigned as a payload specialist on mission *STS-51-L*, flying aboard the Space Shuttle *Challenger* alongside commander *Dick Scobee*.
- Perished on January 28, 1986, when the vehicle disintegrated 73 seconds after liftoff due to the catastrophic failure of rubber *O-rings* in cold temperatures.
- Her backup in the Teacher in Space Program, educator *Barbara Morgan*, eventually flew to space in 2007 aboard mission STS-118.
- Posthumously awarded the *Congressional Space Medal of Honor* in 2004 alongside the rest of the fallen STS-51-L crew.
- Commemorated by dozens of elementary and secondary schools, planetariums, and asteroid *3352 McAuliffe*.

## summary

**Glaciation** refers to the extended geological interval and planetary climate process wherein sustained cooling drives the massive advance of ice sheets, alpine glaciers, and polar ice caps across continental landmasses. Operating over cycles lasting thousands to millions of years, these glacial periods aggressively sculpt terrestrial topography by eroding bedrock, carving deep valleys, and gouging vast tectonic depressions that later become freshwater lake systems. On a global scale, runaway glacial episodes during deep time precipitated *Snowball Earth* conditions, drastically altering atmospheric chemistry, dropping eustatic sea levels, and fundamentally redirecting the trajectory of biological [[evolution]].

## you gotta know

- Massive continental ice sheets act as immense geological plows that scour bedrock, gouge glacial basins such as the Great Lakes in the [[united-states]], and leave behind diagnostic landforms including moraines, drumlins, and U-shaped valleys.
- Cyclical pacing of Quaternary glacial and interglacial intervals is governed primarily by *Milankovitch cycles*, periodic variations in Earth's orbital eccentricity, axial tilt, and precession that alter seasonal solar insolation in the northern hemisphere.
- Vast accumulation of terrestrial ice during glacial maxima sequesters tens of millions of cubic kilometers of water, lowering global sea levels by over one hundred meters and exposing vital land bridges such as *Beringia* for species migration.
- The onset of glacial advance is heavily accelerated by positive ice-albedo feedback, an instability wherein reflective snow and ice bounce solar radiation away from Earth, cooling the surface further and promoting rapid, runaway freezing.
- Neoproterozoic *Snowball Earth* episodes during the *Sturtian* and *Marinoan* glaciations encased the planet in ice down to tropical latitudes, terminating only when unweathered volcanic emissions of [[carbon]] dioxide accumulated to trigger super-greenhouse thaws.
- The catastrophic Huronian glaciation of the [[proterozoic-eon]] was initiated after the [[great-oxidation-event]] introduced biogenic [[oxygen]], which rapidly destroyed atmospheric methane and triggered a severe, long-lasting planetary deep freeze.
- Diagnostic geological evidence confirming ancient glacial action includes unsorted sedimentary tillites, striated bedrock pavements, and anomalous dropstones rafted by calved icebergs into finely laminated deep-sea sediments.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — continental advance of the Laurentide ice sheet carved out the Great Lakes, deposited fertile midwestern till, and sculpted northern geography.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — anthropogenic combustion of fossil fuels released subterranean carbon, terminating natural Milankovitch cooling cycles and triggering worldwide retreat of modern glaciers.
- [[europa]] — the frozen exterior of this Jovian moon presents extreme astrobiological and glaciological parallels to Earth's ancient, fully ice-covered planetary conditions.
- [[evolution]] — intense climatic pressures and ecological fragmentation imposed by glacial cycles drove hominid dispersal and stimulated the radiation of [[multicellular-life]].
- [[carbon]] — the geochemical silicate weathering thermostat and volcanic outgassing govern the greenhouse gas inventory necessary to initiate or reverse global freeze events.
- [[great-oxidation-event]] — early photosynthetic production of reactive [[oxygen]] scrubbed potent methane from the atmosphere, plunging the ancient biosphere into prolonged icehouse conditions.
- [[proterozoic-eon]] — the vast Precambrian geological division that witnessed both the earliest planetary glaciation and the extreme Cryogenian global glaciations.

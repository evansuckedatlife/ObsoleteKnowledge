## summary

**Conduction**, specifically **thermal conduction**, is the transfer of internal thermal energy through microscopic collisions of particles and the movement of free electrons within a stationary body. Formalized mathematically in 1822 by *Joseph Fourier* in *France*, this mechanism governs how heat flows down a temperature gradient without macroscopic bulk motion of matter. Conduction plays a pivotal role in materials science, mechanical engineering, and the thermal management of modern electronic systems.

## you gotta know

- Governed macroscopic heat transfer by *Fourier's law*, which states that local heat flux is directly proportional to the negative gradient of temperature.
- In solid non-metals, thermal energy is conducted primarily through quantized vibrational lattice waves termed *phonons*.
- In electrical conductors such as metals, conduction is dominated by the flow of delocalized *electron* carriers, explaining the correlation between thermal and electrical conductivity.
- The *Wiedemann-Franz law* states that the ratio of thermal conductivity to electrical conductivity in metals is proportional to absolute temperature via the *Lorenz number*.
- Diamonds and graphene exhibit exceptionally high thermal conductivities due to strong covalent carbon bonding and efficient phonon transport.
- Thermal interface material, or *TIM*, is applied between hot silicon components and metallic heatsinks to eliminate insulating air gaps and ensure efficient contact conduction.
- Transient conduction in solids is described by the heat diffusion equation, coupling thermal conductivity, specific heat capacity, and mass density into thermal diffusivity.

## connections

- [[atom]] — constituent particles whose microscopic thermal vibrations and lattice dynamics propagate conductive energy.
- [[electron]] — free charge carriers responsible for dominant heat conduction across metallic conductors.
- [[resistor]] — circuit element where electrical resistance converts electrical current into dissipated thermal energy.
- [[central-processing-unit]] — microchip generating localized thermal flux that must be dissipated via conduction into heatsinks.
- [[motherboard]] — printed circuit board structured with copper planes providing structural heat conduction and grounding.
- [[computer-architecture]] — overall structural system whose power distribution and component packaging depend on thermal conduction limits.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical era propelled by thermodynamic heat engines and metallurgical developments governed by conductive heat flow.

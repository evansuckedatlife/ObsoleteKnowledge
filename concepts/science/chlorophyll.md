## summary

**Chlorophyll** is the primary lipid-soluble pigment responsible for the green coloration of plants, algae, and cyanobacteria. Located within the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts, it absorbs light energy from the blue and red portions of the electromagnetic spectrum while reflecting green light. Its central role in harvesting light drives the conversion of water and carbon dioxide into sugars and oxygen, making it fundamental to terrestrial and aquatic primary productivity.

## you gotta know

- Features a central magnesium ion coordinated inside a substituted *porphyrin ring* system, closely related structurally to the heme group found in animal hemoglobin.
- Anchored firmly within the lipid bilayer of thylakoid membranes by a long, hydrophobic isoprenoid hydrocarbon tail known as *phytol*.
- Chlorophyll *a* serves as the essential reaction-center pigment in both *photosystem I* and *photosystem II*, directly driving photochemical charge separation.
- Chlorophyll *b* acts as an accessory antenna pigment in land plants and green algae, expanding the spectral absorption band by transferring absorbed energy via resonance to chlorophyll *a*.
- Exhibits two pronounced absorption peaks: a prominent *Soret band* in the blue region around 430–450 nm and a *Q band* in the red region around 640–660 nm.
- The special pairs of reaction-center chlorophyll *a* molecules are designated *P680* in photosystem II and *P700* in photosystem I according to their maximum absorption wavelengths.
- Discovered and named in 1817 by French chemists *Joseph Bienaimé Caventou* and *Pierre Joseph Pelletier*, who isolated the substance from plant leaves.
- During autumn senescence, the breakdown of green chlorophyll reveals underlying accessory pigments such as yellow and orange *carotenoids*.

## connections

- [[photosynthesis]] — the light-driven metabolic pathway powered by light absorption in chlorophyll complexes.
- [[photon]] — the fundamental quantum of light captured by antenna complexes containing chlorophyll.
- [[electron]] — transferred down the electron transport chain following the photo-oxidation of reaction-center chlorophyll.
- [[oxygen]] — liberated during the photolysis of water that resupplies electrons lost by chlorophyll *P680*.
- [[carbon]] — forms the organic porphyrin skeleton and phytol anchor of the chlorophyll molecule.
- [[cell]] — contains the specialized chloroplast organelles within which thylakoid-bound chlorophyll resides.
- [[enzyme]] — catalyzes the multi-step biosynthetic pathway converting glutamyl-tRNA into protoporphyrin and chlorophyll.

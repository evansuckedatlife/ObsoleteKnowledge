## summary

**Radon** is a radioactive, colorless, odorless noble gas with atomic number 86 and chemical symbol *Rn*, historically designated **niton** from the Latin word for shining. Discovered in 1899 by *Ernest Rutherford* and *Robert Owens* as a radioactive emission emanating from thorium and isolated in 1900 by *Friedrich Ernst Dorn*, it represents the densest gas found under standard ambient conditions. Because it arises as an unreactive gaseous intermediate in the natural decay series of primordial uranium and thorium, it seeps out of bedrock and soil into confined spaces, where its short-lived decay products constitute the leading environmental cause of pulmonary carcinoma worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Formed primarily as radon-222 through the alpha decay of radium-226 in the primordial uranium-238 decay chain, possessing a radioactive half-life of 3.82 days before disintegrating into short-lived particulate isotopes.
- Historical names for its various isotopes reflected their specific radioactive origins: *radon* referred to radon-222 from radium, *thoron* referred to radon-220 from thorium, and *actinon* denoted radon-219 from actinium.
- Releases high-energy alpha particles during decay, but its principal biological danger stems from solid daughter radionuclides like polonium-218 and polonium-214 that adhere to airborne dust, deposit within bronchial lining cells, and irradiate respiratory DNA.
- Identified as the leading cause of lung cancer among non-smokers and the second leading cause overall behind cigarette smoking, leading health agencies across the [[united-states]] to recommend sub-slab depressurization and mitigation when residential concentrations exceed 4.0 picocuries per liter.
- Condenses below 211 Kelvin into a luminous liquid that displays brilliant yellow-to-orange radioluminescence caused by intense alpha self-irradiation exciting the surrounding atoms.
- Occupies group 18 of the periodic table, yet relativistic electron contraction around its heavy core makes it more chemically reactive than lighter noble gases, allowing it to synthesize binary fluorides like radon difluoride (*RnF2*).
- Deployed in historical radiotherapy via *radon seeds*—sealed gold or glass capillary needles inserted directly into solid neoplasms to deliver targeted local interstitial brachytherapy.

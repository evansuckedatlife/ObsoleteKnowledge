## summary

**Wind** is the movement of air masses caused by differences in atmospheric pressure and temperature across Earth's surface. Driven by solar heating and planetary rotation (the Coriolis effect), wind is a fundamental driver of weather systems, ocean currents, and regional climates. From gentle breezes to catastrophic hurricanes, wind shapes landscapes, influences human settlement, and powers renewable energy.

## you gotta know

- Created when air at lower pressure expands and flows toward regions of higher pressure, a process intensified by uneven solar heating across different latitudes and between land and ocean.
- Deflected by the Coriolis effect—Earth's rotation causes moving air to curve rightward in the Northern Hemisphere and leftward in the Southern Hemisphere, creating the trade winds and westerlies.
- Measured quantitatively by speed (meters per second, knots, or miles per hour) and characterized qualitatively by the [[beaufort-wind-force-scale]] (from Beaufort 0 "calm" to 12 "hurricane" force).
- Trade winds and westerlies are persistent global wind patterns that have shaped human history—enabling age-of-exploration voyages and determining historical settlement patterns across continents.
- Jet streams are fast-moving rivers of air in the upper atmosphere (8–12 km altitude) that steer weather systems, influence climate on continental scales, and can reach speeds exceeding 200 mph.
- Wind shear—the change in wind speed and direction with altitude—drives the formation of severe thunderstorms and tornadoes by creating vertical rotation in the atmosphere called vorticity.
- Driven primarily by solar energy; without the Sun's uneven heating of Earth's surface and atmosphere, there would be no wind or weather systems.
- Local wind patterns (sea breezes, mountain winds, föhn winds) form due to pressure and temperature gradients over shorter distances, creating distinct microclimates and regional weather patterns.
- Wind has been harnessed by civilizations for millennia—sailboats, windmills, and modern wind turbines extract energy from air currents for transportation, grain milling, and renewable electricity generation.

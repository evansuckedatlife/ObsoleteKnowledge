## summary

**Electric charge** is a fundamental property of matter—the source of electromagnetic force—existing in two types: positive (associated with protons) and negative (associated with electrons). Charge is quantized: all charges are integer multiples of the elementary charge *e* ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ coulombs. The interaction between charges governs atomic structure, chemical bonding, electromagnetism, and the vast majority of everyday phenomena.

## you gotta know

- Opposite charges attract (Coulomb's law: *F* ∝ *q*₁*q*₂/*r*²), while like charges repel; this imbalance is the basis of ionic bonding and electrostatic forces.
- The elementary charge (*e* ≈ 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) is the smallest charge unit; electrons carry charge −*e*, protons carry charge +*e*.
- Charge quantization means that free charges always appear as multiples of the elementary charge; the electron's charge has never been measured fractionally (despite quarks being fractionally charged inside hadrons).
- The coulomb (C) is the SI unit of charge; one coulomb is defined as the charge flowing past a point in one second when current is one ampere.
- Electric field *E* is the force per unit charge exerted by charged objects; a positive test charge placed at field point experiences force *F* = *qE*.
- Gauss's law relates electric field to enclosed charge: ∮ *E* · d*A* = *Q*_enclosed / ε₀, showing charge as the source of electric field.
- Moving charges create magnetic fields (Ampère's law), and changing magnetic fields induce electric fields (Faraday's law), unifying electricity and magnetism as electromagnetism.
- The Millikan oil-drop experiment (1909) directly measured the elementary charge by observing the motion of charged oil droplets in electric fields.

## connections

- [[electron]] — the negatively charged fundamental particle; electron count determines ionic charge.
- [[proton]] — the positively charged nucleon; proton count is element identity and determines positive nuclear charge.
- [[electromagnetism]] — the unified theory of electric and magnetic phenomena; charge is the source.
- [[electric-field]] — the entity mediating force between charges.
- [[quantisation]] — charge is quantized as multiples of the elementary charge.
- [[millikan-oil-drop-experiment]] — the experiment confirming charge quantization and measuring *e*.

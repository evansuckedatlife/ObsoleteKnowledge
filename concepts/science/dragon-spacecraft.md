## summary

The **Dragon** is a cargo and crewed spacecraft designed and operated by *SpaceX* to resupply the [[international-space-station]] and transport astronauts to orbit. Launched atop the *Falcon 9* rocket, it was the first commercial spacecraft to dock with the ISS, revolutionizing how humanity accesses space.

## you gotta know

- First cargo vehicle built entirely by a private company to dock with the ISS, proving commercial spaceflight viable.
- Dragon capsule is cone-shaped with a PICA-X heat shield for re-entry protection; the unpressurized trunk section carries solar panels, radiators, and external cargo; this modular design allows rapid turnaround between flights.
- Powered by Merlin rocket engines during rendezvous; autonomous docking system eliminates need for manual control by astronauts.
- Cargo Dragon has resupplied the ISS with food, equipment, and experiments; later evolved into Crew Dragon (with life support, seats, and abort systems) to transport astronauts directly, reducing reliance on expensive Russian Soyuz vehicles for crew transport.
- Reusable design—the same capsules can fly multiple missions after inspection and refurbishment, dramatically reducing costs per launch compared to expendable spacecraft; this economics drives the commercial spaceflight industry.
- Splashes down in the Pacific Ocean under parachutes, allowing recovery and refurbishment for future flights.
- Crew Dragon features an emergency escape abort system (powered by SuperDraco thrusters) for crew safety during launch; seats up to seven passengers with redundant avionics, life-support, and environmental control systems.

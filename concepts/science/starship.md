## summary

**Starship** (also known as the **SpaceX Starship**) is a fully reusable, two-stage super heavy-lift launch vehicle developed by [[spacex]] in the [[united-states]]. Composed of the *Super Heavy* booster and a dedicated spacecraft upper stage, the system is engineered to radically reduce orbital launch costs through rapid, total reusability. Standing as the tallest and most powerful rocket ever flown, it anchors the modern era of [[commercial-spaceflight]] and serves as the designated lunar lander for the [[nasa]] *Artemis* program, carrying crew to the surface to succeed the historic achievements of the [[apollo-program]].

## you gotta know

- Represents the tallest and most powerful rocket system ever flown in human history, standing approximately 121 metres tall and generating over sixteen million pounds of liftoff thrust from its clustered *Raptor* engines.
- Engineered for rapid and total reusability of both stages, returning the *Super Heavy* booster directly to the launch mount to be caught mid-air by the mechanical tower arms nicknamed *Mechazilla*.
- Selected by [[nasa]] as the *Human Landing System* for the *Artemis III* and *Artemis IV* missions, tasked with landing the first astronauts on the lunar South Pole since the [[apollo-program]].
- Fueled entirely by sub-cooled liquid methane and liquid [[oxygen]] (*methalox*), selected largely because methane can be synthesized on *Mars* through the *Sabatier reaction* using local atmospheric [[carbon]] dioxide and subsurface water ice.
- Executes an unprecedented aerodynamic descent profile for the upper stage called the "belly flop", utilizing four independently actuated body flaps to bleed speed through atmosphere before performing a rapid flip maneuver to land vertically.
- Constructed from cold-rolled *304L stainless steel* rather than [[carbon]]-fiber composites, trading minor mass penalties for extreme thermal resistance during atmospheric reentry, rapid manufacturing speed, and drastically reduced material costs.
- Requires in-orbit cryogenic propellant transfer to reach deep-space destinations, deploying specialized tanker variants to refill an orbital propellant depot before departing for the Moon or planetary targets.
- Features the full-flow staged combustion cycle on its *Raptor* engines, an exceptionally efficient but historically elusive rocket engine architecture that powers fuel-rich and oxygen-rich preburners simultaneously to maximize chamber pressure.

## connections

- [[spacex]] — aerospace company founded by [[elon-musk]] that conceived, manufactured, and operates the launch vehicle.
- [[nasa]] — American space agency that contracted the spacecraft as the primary crew lander for the *Artemis* lunar campaign.
- [[falcon-rocket]] — workhorse two-stage launcher whose pioneering booster recovery established the operational foundation for full reusability.
- [[dragon-spacecraft]] — orbital crew and cargo capsule that proved commercial human spaceflight prior to the super heavy vehicle's introduction.
- [[space-launch-system]] — expendable heavy launcher built by [[nasa]] that serves as the contemporary government counterpart for deep-space transport.
- [[apollo-program]] — benchmark lunar exploration effort whose *Saturn V* stood as the previous standard for super heavy lift.
- [[commercial-spaceflight]] — private aerospace industry sector driven by reusable architectures that aim to expand orbital access and space infrastructure.
- [[elon-musk]] — chief designer and executive whose multi-planetary vision for human civilization drove the system's creation.

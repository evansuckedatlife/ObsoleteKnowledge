## summary

**Transfer RNA** (tRNA) is a small RNA molecule that delivers *amino acids* to the ribosome during [[protein-synthesis]]. Each tRNA carries a specific amino acid and possesses an **anticodon**—a three-base sequence that pairs with complementary mRNA codons. This anticodon-codon matching is the molecular mechanism that translates the genetic code into protein sequences, making tRNA the interpreter between nucleic acids and proteins.

## you gotta know

- tRNA is a cloverleaf-shaped RNA that folds into an L-shaped 3D structure in the ribosome.
- Each tRNA is "charged" (loaded) with a specific [[amino-acids|amino acid]] by an aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase enzyme.
- The **anticodon** (typically located in the anticodon loop) base-pairs with an mRNA codon, ensuring correct amino acid insertion.
- tRNA occupies three sites on the ribosome during translation: A (aminoacyl, incoming), P (peptidyl, current), and E (exit, leaving).
- Multiple tRNAs may recognize the same amino acid due to "wobble base pairing" at the third codon position.
- In the ribosome, peptide bonds form between amino acids carried by consecutive tRNAs, extending the growing chain.
- Human cells employ roughly 40-60 different tRNA species to cover the 61 sense codons and their variations.
- The 3' CCA tail of tRNA is where the amino acid attaches; this end is added post-transcriptionally and is highly conserved.
- Mutations in tRNA genes or recognition sites can cause genetic diseases; some cancers exploit tRNA mischarging for cell survival.
- tRNA structure and interactions are among the most highly conserved features across all domains of life, underscoring their evolutionary importance.

## connections

- [[rna]] — the nucleic-acid family to which tRNA belongs.

- [[protein-synthesis]] — the cellular process tRNA enables by delivering amino acids.
- [[ribosome]] — the molecular machine where tRNA docks and transfers amino acids.
- [[amino-acids]] — the molecules tRNA carries to the growing protein chain.
- [[codon]] — the three-base mRNA unit that tRNA anticodons recognize.

## summary

**Cholesterol** is an essential polycyclic amphipathic lipid belonging to the steroid family, synthesized predominantly by animal cells. It inserts intercalated within the phospholipid bilayers of animal plasma membranes, functioning as a vital bidirectional regulator of membrane fluidity and permeability. Beyond its critical structural duties in cellular membranes, it serves as the obligatory biochemical precursor for all steroid hormones, vitamin D, and bile salts.

## you gotta know

- Chemical structure features a characteristic cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene core consisting of four fused hydrocarbon rings labeled A, B, C, and D.
- Phenyl rings and an attached eight-carbon hydrocarbon tail make the molecule predominantly hydrophobic, save for a single hydrophilic polar hydroxyl group at carbon-3.
- Acts as a *fluidity buffer* in animal cell membranes: decreases fluidity at elevated temperatures by immobilizing lipid tails, and prevents crystallization at low temperatures.
- Concentrated along with sphingolipids in distinct, liquid-ordered microdomains within cell membranes known as *lipid rafts*.
- Biosynthesized endogenously through the *mevalonate pathway*, whose committed rate-limiting step is catalyzed by the enzyme *HMG-CoA reductase*.
- Circulates in human blood plasma enclosed inside macromolecular complexes termed lipoproteins, notably *low-density lipoprotein* (LDL) and *high-density lipoprotein* (HDL).
- Elevated circulating LDL cholesterol levels promote the formation of arterial plaques, leading directly to *atherosclerosis* and coronary heart disease.
- Serves as the biochemical parent molecule for steroid hormones including *cortisol*, *aldosterone*, *testosterone*, *estradiol*, and *progesterone*.

## connections

- [[cell]] — uses cholesterol to maintain structural integrity and modulate plasma membrane fluidity.
- [[carbon]] — provides the core twenty-seven-atom carbon skeleton composing the tetracyclic steroid ring system.
- [[oxygen]] — found in the defining single polar C-3 hydroxyl group that interfaces with hydrophilic phospholipid heads.
- [[enzyme]] — controls the metabolic synthesis of cholesterol, notably the drug target HMG-CoA reductase.
- [[protein]] — forms the apolipoprotein coats that package hydrophobic cholesterol esters for vascular transport.
- [[sodium]] — transported across cellular membranes via ion pumps and channels anchored in cholesterol-regulated lipid bilayers.

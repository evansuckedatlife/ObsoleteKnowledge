## summary

In subatomic physics, **annihilation** is the fundamental reaction that occurs when a subatomic particle collides with its corresponding antiparticle, resulting in their mutual conversion into neutral force-carrier gauge bosons. Governed by the mass-energy equivalence formulated by *Albert Einstein*, the total invariant rest mass of the interacting pair is transformed entirely into energetic radiation such as gamma rays or gluons. The process is a central prediction of relativistic quantum field theory and serves as a vital diagnostic tool in medicine as well as experimental particle physics.

## you gotta know

- Converts the entire invariant rest mass of a particle and its antiparticle directly into radiant kinetic energy in exact accordance with *E = mc²*.
- Most famously exemplified by low-energy **positron-electron annihilation**, where an *electron* and a *positron* collide at near-zero relative momentum to produce two collinear *photons* of roughly 511 kiloelectronvolts each, emitted at 180 degrees to conserve linear momentum.
- Forms the physical mechanism underlying *Positron Emission Tomography* (*PET*), a non-invasive medical imaging technique that maps metabolic processes by detecting coincident pairs of 511-keV gamma photons.
- At extremely low relative energies, an electron and a positron can briefly form an exotic hydrogen-like bound state called *positronium* prior to decay, annihilating into two photons from the singlet state (*para-positronium*) or three photons from the triplet state (*ortho-positronium*) to conserve charge conjugation parity.
- Hadronic collisions, such as proton-antiproton interactions, proceed via constituent *quark* and antiquark reactions, yielding showers of relativistic pions that subsequently decay into photons, muons, and neutrinos.
- Represents a primary benchmark process in high-energy storage rings such as the *Large Electron-Positron Collider* (*LEP*) at *CERN*, which verified electroweak parameters through resonant production of *Z bosons*.
- Raises one of the great unresolved problems in physical cosmology: why the early universe developed a slight matter-antimatter asymmetry (*baryogenesis*) rather than total mutual destruction leaving behind only a radiation bath.

## connections

- [[electron]] — the negatively charged lepton that annihilates with its antimatter partner, the positron.
- [[photon]] — the massless electromagnetic gauge boson generated as the primary decay product of leptonic collisions.
- [[albert-einstein]] — established the mass-energy equivalence principle that dictates the energy output of the reaction.
- [[quantum-mechanics]] — provides the theoretical framework, through *Paul Dirac's* relativistic wave equation, that predicted the existence of antimatter.
- [[standard-model]] — formalizes the conservation laws of charge, lepton number, and color charge that govern all particle interactions.
- [[quark]] — fundamental constituents of hadrons that undergo annihilation during high-energy proton-antiproton collisions.

## summary

**Genetics** is the science of inheritance — how traits pass from parents to offspring and how variation arises within populations. It bridges [[heredity]] and molecular biology, explaining the mechanisms by which genes are transmitted, expressed, and altered. From *Gregor Mendel's* pea experiments to modern genomics, genetics has revealed how DNA encodes the information for life.

## you gotta know

- Gregor Mendel's laws of inheritance (segregation, independent assortment) established the particulate nature of heredity — traits are inherited through discrete units, not blended.
- Genotype (the genetic makeup) and phenotype (observable traits) are distinct; the same genotype can produce different phenotypes depending on environment.
- Alleles are alternate versions of a gene; dominant alleles mask recessive ones in heterozygotes (one dominant, one recessive allele).
- *Thomas Hunt Morgan* used fruit flies to map genes to chromosomes, proving that genes reside on chromosomes and can be linked.
- Pedigree analysis tracks trait inheritance in families, revealing patterns of Mendelian, X-linked, and multi-gene inheritance.
- Population genetics applies statistical methods to understand allele frequencies and how evolution changes them via natural selection, mutation, and genetic drift.

## summary

**SpaceX** is a private aerospace manufacturer founded by Elon Musk in 2002, dedicated to reducing the cost of spaceflight and enabling human colonization of Mars. The company developed the Falcon rocket family and the Dragon spacecraft, broke NASA's launch monopoly through commercial cargo contracts, and pioneered rocket landing and reuse. SpaceX transformed spaceflight from a government-only domain into a competitive commercial industry.

## you gotta know

- Founded 2002 after Musk's frustration with the cost of space access; initial *Falcon 1* rocket failed three times before succeeding in 2008.
- Developed the *Falcon 9*, the first orbital-class rocket to successfully land its first stage for reuse; Falcon 9 has become the world's most-launched orbital rocket.
- *Dragon* spacecraft: cargo variant (*Dragon CRS*) delivers supplies to the International Space Station; crewed variant (*Dragon Crew*) transports astronauts.
- First private company to dock with the International Space Station (2012) and to carry NASA astronauts to orbit (2020).
- *Starship* program aims to build a fully reusable super-heavy launch vehicle for Mars missions, deep-space exploration, and high-capacity Earth-to-orbit transport.
- Reusability is the key innovation: recovering and reflying expensive hardware reduces per-launch costs from thousands per kilogram to hundreds, fundamentally altering spaceflight economics.
- Vertical integration: SpaceX manufactures engines, avionics, and structures in-house rather than relying on contractors.

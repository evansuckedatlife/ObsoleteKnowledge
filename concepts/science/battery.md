## summary

A **battery** is an electrochemical hardware device containing one or more galvanic cells that directly converts stored chemical potential energy into electrical energy through spontaneous reduction-oxidation reactions. First demonstrated in 1800 by Alessandro Volta with his alternating zinc-copper pile, it represents the most common physical realization of a direct-current voltage source in electric circuits. Across contemporary science and engineering, batteries provide portable and stationary energy storage ranging from microscale electronic components to grid-level reserves and electrified transportation.

## you gotta know

- Core operating structure consists of an anode where oxidation releases electrons, a cathode where reduction accepts electrons, an ion-conducting electrolyte, and a physical separator that prevents internal short circuits.
- Primary cells are non-rechargeable units intended for a single continuous discharge cycle, epitomized by conventional alkaline cells that utilize an aqueous potassium hydroxide [[base]] as their conductive electrolyte.
- Secondary cells are rechargeable energy storage systems whose internal electrode chemistry can be repeatedly restored by passing an external electric current in the reverse direction, including lead-acid and lithium-ion cells.
- Internal resistance within realistic electrochemical cells causes output terminal voltage to fall below the open-circuit electromotive force whenever an external [[resistor]] or dynamic circuit draws heavy current.
- Alessandro Volta invented the voltaic pile in 1800 using alternating zinc and copper plates separated by brine-saturated cloth, effectively refuting Luigi Galvani's hypothesis of organic "animal electricity."
- Gaston Planté developed the lead-acid battery in 1859, creating the first commercially viable secondary storage cell that remains indispensable for automotive engine starting and standby power.
- Lithium-ion battery technology, commercialized in 1991, revolutionized modern portable electronics by intercalating lithium ions between graphite and cobalt oxide lattices to achieve superior gravimetric energy density.
- Non-chemical betavoltaic devices harvest energetic high-speed electrons emitted through radioactive [[beta-decay]] to maintain long-lasting, milliwatt-scale power for deep-space equipment and medical implants.

## connections

- [[resistor]] — fundamental circuit element that impedes current flow and dissipates battery power as heat.
- [[electron]] — subatomic charge carrier whose movement through an external circuit constitutes electric current.
- [[base]] — alkaline compound, specifically potassium hydroxide, used as the primary electrolyte in alkaline cells.
- [[beta-decay]] — nuclear transmutation process exploited in betavoltaic batteries to generate continuous current over decades.
- [[power-supply]] — broad technological class of equipment designed to deliver controlled electrical energy to circuits.
- [[transformer]] — inductive device designed for alternating currents that cannot alter direct-current battery voltages without an inverter.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — era of industrial transformation accelerated by early electrochemical power in telegraphy and metallurgy.

## summary

**Salt**, most famously sodium chloride (NaCl), is an ionic compound formed by the reaction of an acid with a base. In the narrower chemical sense, any ionic compound composed of cations and anions is a salt; in everyday usage, "salt" refers to table salt, an essential seasoning and electrolyte in food and biology. Salt represents one of the most abundant and economically significant compounds on Earth.

## you gotta know

- Sodium chloride forms through the reaction of sodium (a highly reactive metal) with chlorine gas (a toxic nonmetal), producing a stable ionic solid held together by electrostatic attractions.
- Table salt crystallizes in the cubic rock salt crystal structure, where each sodium ion is surrounded by six chloride ions and vice versa, forming an infinitely extended ionic lattice.
- Dissolves readily in polar solvents like water, dissociating into free sodium and chloride ions that conduct electricity; this ionic dissociation is essential to its role as an electrolyte.
- Comprises approximately 3.5% of seawater by mass, making the ocean a vast reservoir of salt; salt has been harvested from seawater for millennia through evaporation.
- Essential to human and animal physiology: sodium and chloride ions regulate nerve impulses, muscle contraction, and fluid balance across cell membranes.
- Mined from underground salt deposits (rock salt or halite) formed by the evaporation of ancient seas; these deposits represent geological records of past marine environments.
- Critical industrial chemical: used in food preservation, de-icing, chlor-alkali processes to produce chlorine and sodium hydroxide, and countless other applications.

## connections

- [[sodium]] — the metal element that comprises the cation in sodium chloride.
- [[chlorine]] — the nonmetallic element that comprises the anion in sodium chloride.
- [[ion]] — the charged particles (Na⁺ and Cl⁻) that comprise salt through ionic bonding.
- [[chemistry]] — the discipline that explains salt formation and reactivity through bonding theory.
- [[ocean]] — the natural reservoir where dissolved salt exists in equilibrium with marine life.

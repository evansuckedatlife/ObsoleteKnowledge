## summary

**Electromagnetism** is the unified theory of electricity and magnetism, formalized by *James Clerk Maxwell* in the 1860s. It reveals that electric and magnetic fields are two aspects of a single phenomenon, and that accelerating charges produce waves that propagate at the speed of light. This framework underpins all of classical physics and revealed that light itself is an electromagnetic wave.

## you gotta know

- Gauss's law: charges create electric fields; the total flux through a surface equals the enclosed charge divided by permittivity.
- Faraday's law: changing magnetic flux induces an electric field (basis of electric generators and transformers).
- Ampère-Maxwell law: currents and changing electric fields produce magnetic fields.
- Maxwell unified these four equations into a coherent framework predicting electromagnetic waves traveling at speed c.
- The electric field from a positive charge points away; the field from a negative charge points inward.
- Perpendicular electric and magnetic fields oscillate in phase in an electromagnetic wave, with both perpendicular to the direction of propagation.

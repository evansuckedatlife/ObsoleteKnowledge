## summary

The **Large Hadron Collider** (**LHC**) is the world's most powerful and highest-energy particle accelerator, housed within a twenty-seven-kilometer circular tunnel beneath the border of *Switzerland* and [[france]] near *Geneva*. Constructed by the *European Organization for Nuclear Research* (*CERN*) between 1998 and 2008, it propels opposing beams of protons or heavy ions close to the speed of light to collide at record-breaking center-of-mass energies. It is most celebrated for the landmark 2012 discovery of the *Higgs boson*, an elusive elementary particle that confirmed the mechanism by which gauge bosons acquire mass within the [[standard-model]].

## you gotta know

- Discovered the *Higgs boson* in 2012 at a mass of approximately 125 gigaelectronvolts, announced jointly by the independent *ATLAS* and *CMS* collaborations.
- Occupies a circular underground tunnel originally excavated for the *Large Electron-Positron Collider* (*LEP*), situated between fifty and one hundred seventy-five meters below ground.
- Steers and focuses particle bunches using thousands of superconducting niobium-titanium electromagnets cooled by superfluid helium to 1.9 kelvins, a temperature colder than outer space named after [[lord-kelvin]].
- Accelerates two counter-rotating hadron beams through dual vacuum pipes before crossing them at four primary interaction points monitored by huge detectors: *ATLAS*, *CMS*, *ALICE*, and *LHCb*.
- Recreates primordial quark-gluon plasma, the ultra-dense state of matter existing microseconds after the *Big Bang*, by colliding lead ions instead of solitary protons.
- Serves as a primary testing ground for physics beyond the [[standard-model]], placing stringent empirical limits on supersymmetry, extra spatial dimensions, micro black holes, and candidates for dark matter.
- Generates dozens of petabytes of collision data annually, prompting the development and worldwide deployment of the *Worldwide LHC Computing Grid*.

## connections

- [[standard-model]] — the foundational framework of particle physics verified by the collider's discovery of the *Higgs boson*.
- [[quark]] — the fundamental constituent of hadrons colliding at relativistic speeds to form quark-gluon plasma.
- [[lord-kelvin]] — the pioneer of absolute thermodynamics whose temperature unit measures the 1.9 kelvin operating chill of the machine's magnets.
- [[france]] — the sovereign nation containing the majority of the underground tunnel circumference alongside *Switzerland*.
- [[albert-einstein]] — whose special relativity dictates the energy-mass conversions and momentum equations governing high-energy beam dynamics.
- [[photon]] — the massless gauge boson produced in hallmark decay channels alongside Z boson pairs during Higgs detection events.
- [[electron]] — the lepton previously collided against positrons in the exact same subterranean tunnel during the predecessor *LEP* era.

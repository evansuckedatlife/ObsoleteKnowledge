## summary

The **conservation of energy** (often formulated as the **first law of thermodynamics** or **energy conservation**) is an invariant principle of physics stating that the total energy of an isolated system remains constant over time. Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, but only converted from one physical modality to another, including kinetic, potential, thermal, chemical, nuclear, and electromagnetic states. In modern theoretical physics, this inviolable balance is understood as the exact mathematical consequence of the universe possessing continuous time-translation symmetry.

## you gotta know

- The first law of thermodynamics expresses the principle for closed thermodynamic systems as $\Delta U = Q - W$, balancing changes in internal energy against added heat and mechanical work.
- Mathematician *Emmy Noether* proved in her celebrated 1915 theorem that continuous time-translation invariance in a physical system's Lagrangian directly produces the conservation of energy.
- *James Prescott Joule* established the mechanical equivalent of heat during the 1840s using a paddle-wheel calorimeter, demonstrating that mechanical work converts reliably into equivalent thermal energy.
- *Albert Einstein* revolutionized energy conservation in 1905 by formulating mass-energy equivalence ($E = mc^2$), demonstrating that rest mass represents a concentrated form of invariant relativistic energy.
- In classical and analytical mechanics, dynamical systems characterized by a time-independent *Hamiltonian* function unconditionally preserve their total mechanical energy throughout phase space evolution.
- *Wolfgang Pauli* famously hypothesized the existence of the elusive neutral neutrino in 1930 specifically to preserve conservation of energy and angular momentum during nuclear beta decay.
- In general relativity, global conservation of energy becomes mathematically subtle in expanding cosmological spacetimes described by the *Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker* metric, because cosmic expansion lacks a timelike *Killing vector field*.

## connections

- [[quantum-mechanics]] — incorporates energy conservation through stationary eigenstates of the time-independent Schrödinger equation.

- [[resistor]] — circuit element that irreversibly converts electrical work into thermal dissipation while strictly maintaining total energy balance.
- [[constructive-interference]] — wave interaction that redistributes local energy density into bright fringes without creating new energy.

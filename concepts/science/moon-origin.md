## summary

The **origin of the Moon** is best explained by the *giant-impact hypothesis*, which posits that a *Mars*-sized protoplanet collided with the proto-*Earth* approximately 4.5 billion years ago during the early *Hadean* eon. This cataclysmic grazing collision ejected vast amounts of vaporized and molten silicate debris into orbit around *Earth*, which subsequently cooled and rapidly accreted into a distinct satellite within a remarkably brief geological timeframe. Scientific inquiry into this catastrophic planetary collision accounts for the compositional peculiarities of lunar rocks, the dynamical orientation of the Earth-Moon system, and the physical conditions that shaped early terrestrial evolution.

## you gotta know

- Explains why the *Moon* possesses a diminutive metallic core and an anomalously low bulk density compared to *Earth*, as the impactor stripped away outer silicate mantle materials while its heavy iron core sank rapidly into the terrestrial center.
- Supported directly by lunar samples gathered during the [[apollo-program]], which proved that terrestrial and lunar silicate rocks share nearly identical isotopic compositions of [[oxygen]], silicon, and titanium, pointing to an intimate genetic relationship.
- Proposes that the impacting protoplanet, traditionally termed *Theia* after the mythical mother of the moon goddess *Selene*, collided obliquely at moderate velocity during the final accretionary epoch of inner *planetary formation*.
- Replaced earlier nineteenth- and twentieth-century mechanisms, including George Darwin's centrifugal *fission hypothesis*, Thomas Jefferson Jackson See's gravitational *capture theory*, and the co-accretion model of simultaneous planetary formation from a circumterrestrial nebula.
- Accounts for the uniquely high angular momentum of the Earth-Moon system and establishes the approximately 23.5-degree axial obliquity of *Earth*, which stabilizes planetary precession and sustains long-term climatic stability necessary for life.
- Predicts the formation of an extensive global *lunar magma ocean*, which fractionally crystallized as dense mafic minerals settled downward while buoyant, plagioclase-rich anorthosite floated to the surface to generate the bright lunar highlands.
- Confronts the prominent *isotopic crisis* in modern astrophysics—namely, why lunar material matches *Earth* rather than the foreign impactor—leading to newer computational models such as high-energy impacts that create a vaporized donut-shaped structure known as a *synestia*.

## connections

- [[apollo-program]] — returned pristine lunar highland anorthosite and basalt samples whose geochemical signatures provided decisive empirical testing for collision theories.
- [[oxygen]] — stable isotope ratios $^{17}\text{O}$ and $^{18}\text{O}$ in lunar rock samples match terrestrial ratios precisely, presenting the central compositional constraint on formation models.
- [[nasa]] — space agency that directed lunar exploration missions, curated returned extraterrestrial materials, and funded hydrodynamic supercomputer simulations of planetary collisions.
- [[jupiter]] — massive gas giant whose orbital migrations during early Solar System development perturbed the trajectories of protoplanets and inner rocky bodies.
- [[space-race]] — historical scientific and technological rivalry between superpowers that enabled the acquisition of lunar surface material through crewed exploration.
- [[carbon]] — volatile light element whose severe depletion in lunar material reflects the extreme thermal vaporization that accompanied the giant collision.
- [[albert-einstein]] — developed the general theory of relativity, providing the fundamental framework for modern celestial mechanics, orbital dynamics, and planetary tidal interactions.

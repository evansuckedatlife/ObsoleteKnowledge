## summary

**Convection** is the transfer of thermal energy between a solid boundary and a moving fluid, combining microscopic molecular conduction with macroscopic fluid advection. Identified as one of the three primary modes of heat transfer alongside conduction and radiation, it operates across natural and engineered scales ranging from planetary atmospheres to personal computer cooling. Its dynamics depend upon fluid velocity, buoyancy differentials, surface morphology, and the thermodynamic properties of the circulating medium.

## you gotta know

- Categorized into natural convection, driven entirely by buoyancy forces arising from density gradients, and forced convection, propelled by external fans or pumps.
- Modeled mathematically by Newton's law of cooling, which states heat flux is proportional to the difference between surface temperature and bulk fluid temperature.
- Characterized in fluid dynamics by dimensionless parameters including the Nusselt, Reynolds, Prandtl, Rayleigh, and Grashof numbers.
- Generates cellular convective patterns known as Rayleigh-Bénard convection cells in thin fluid layers heated uniformly from below.
- Drives atmospheric weather patterns, oceanic circulation belts, and mantle convection currents responsible for planetary plate tectonics.
- Shapes banded zonal jet streams and massive cyclonic storms, such as the Great Red Spot observed across the gas giant *[[jupiter]]*.
- Cools modern computing hardware by drawing air across radiator fins to accelerate the *[[cooling-rate]]* of a high-load *[[central-processing-unit]]*.

## connections

- [[cooling-rate]] — rate of temperature reduction achieved in a system, which scales proportionally with the convective heat transfer coefficient.
- [[central-processing-unit]] — silicon integrated circuit that dissipates substantial thermal energy into finned heat exchangers via air or liquid convection loops.
- [[control-circuit]] — electronic regulatory circuit that dynamically adjusts cooling fan speeds via pulse-width modulation to match convective load requirements.
- [[jupiter]] — jovian gas giant whose atmospheric dynamics and outer weather bands are propelled by intense internal convective heat transport.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical epoch reliant on boilers, steam engines, and blast furnaces engineered around convective thermodynamics.
- [[oxygen]] — atmospheric diatomic gas component that serves as part of the air mixture circulating in forced-convection cooling fans.

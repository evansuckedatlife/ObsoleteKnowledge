## summary

**Chromosomes** are compact, thread-like structures made of tightly coiled DNA and proteins that package and organize a cell's genetic material. In eukaryotes, chromosomes exist in the nucleus and are visible only during cell division when the DNA is maximally condensed. They are the physical basis of heredity and the stage upon which genes are distributed to daughter cells.

## you gotta know

- Composed of DNA wrapped around histone proteins in a repeating structure called nucleosomes.
- Eukaryotic cells are diploid — they contain pairs of homologous chromosomes (one from each parent).
- Humans have 46 chromosomes: 22 pairs of autosomes plus sex chromosomes (XX or XY).
- During mitosis, replicated chromosomes are pulled apart to opposite poles of the cell and segregated.
- During meiosis, homologous chromosomes pair and recombine, creating genetic variation.
- Visible with a light microscope only when highly condensed during metaphase; otherwise diffuse in the nucleus as chromatin.

## connections

- [[dna]] — the molecular content that chromosomes package.
- [[cell-division]] — the process where chromosomes are segregated.
- [[mitosis]] — division that produces identical daughter cells with the same chromosome set.
- [[meiosis]] — division that halves the chromosome number for sexual reproduction.
- [[gene]] — functional segments of DNA on chromosomes.
- [[nucleosome]] — the repeating unit of DNA and proteins that form chromosomes.

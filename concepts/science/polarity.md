## summary

**Polarity** is the asymmetric distribution of electric charge within a molecule or chemical bond, arising from differences in electronegativity between atoms. Polar molecules possess a dipole moment and exhibit distinct chemical and physical behavior compared to nonpolar molecules. Molecular polarity is fundamental to chemistry, determining solubility, reactivity, and intermolecular forces.

## you gotta know

- Arises when two atoms with different electronegativities form a bond; the more electronegative atom pulls electron density toward itself, creating partial positive and negative charges denoted δ+ and δ−.
- A molecule's overall polarity depends on both the individual bond polarities and the three-dimensional geometry of the molecule; symmetric shapes may cancel out bond dipoles, making the molecule nonpolar overall despite polar bonds.
- Water is the canonical example of a polar molecule: the highly electronegative oxygen pulls electrons from the hydrogen atoms, creating a dipole with negative charge on oxygen and positive charge on hydrogen.
- Polar molecules follow the rule "like dissolves like"—they dissolve readily in other polar solvents (especially water) but poorly in nonpolar solvents like hydrocarbons; this principle explains why oil and water don't mix.
- Polar molecules exhibit stronger intermolecular forces, including hydrogen bonding, which results in higher melting and boiling points compared to similarly-sized nonpolar molecules; this is why water boils at 100°C while methane boils at −161°C.
- The polarity of a bond is quantified by electronegativity difference; large differences (greater than ~1.7 on the Pauling scale) typically indicate ionic bonding rather than covalent polarity.
- Polarity is critical to molecular recognition and enzyme catalysis; many biological processes depend on specific interactions between polar groups on different molecules; the cell membrane's selective permeability relies on polarity differences.

## connections

- [[electronegativity]] — the property that gives rise to polarity through unequal electron sharing.
- [[chemical-bond]] — the localized region where polarity develops between two atoms.
- [[water]] — the quintessential polar molecule that dominates chemical and biological systems.
- [[dipole]] — the separation of charge that characterizes a polar molecule.

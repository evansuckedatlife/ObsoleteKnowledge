## summary

**Saturn** is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the solar system, a gas giant famous for its spectacular system of rings. Composed primarily of hydrogen and helium, Saturn orbits the Sun every 29 Earth years and hosts at least 146 moons, including the giant Titan with its thick atmosphere. Its rings make it the most visually distinctive planet and a cornerstone of planetary science.

## you gotta know

- Enormous ring system composed of billions of particles of ice and rock, ranging from grains to house-sized chunks; rings extend hundreds of thousands of kilometers but are remarkably thin (typically 10 meters).
- Second-largest planet by diameter; could fit 764 Earths inside it, yet has lower density than water and would float in an ocean.
- Gas giant with no solid surface; composed of layers of hydrogen, helium, ammonia, and water ice.
- *Titan* is its largest moon and the only moon in the solar system with a dense atmosphere (mostly nitrogen); hosts methane lakes and possible subsurface water.
- *Enceladus*, a smaller moon, ejects water vapor geysers from its south polar region, indicating a subsurface ocean beneath its icy crust.
- Visible to the naked eye and known since antiquity; Christiaan Huygens discovered Titan in 1655.
- Cassini-Huygens probe orbited Saturn for 13 years (2004–2017), providing unprecedented detail about the planet, rings, and moons.

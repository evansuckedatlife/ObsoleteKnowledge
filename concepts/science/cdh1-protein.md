## summary

**CDH1 protein**, also designated as *Fzr1* or *Fizzy-related protein 1*, is a vital WD40-repeat regulatory co-activator of the anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) that governs eukaryotic cell division. Operating as a specialized substrate-recognition subunit, it directs the multi-protein APC/C ubiquitin ligase to destroy mitotic cyclins and spindle assembly factors from late anaphase throughout the G1 phase. By enforcing the targeted proteolysis of cell division machinery, **Cdh1** prevents premature entry into S phase, preserves genomic integrity, and maintains the cell cycle checkpoint controls essential for cellular differentiation and tumor suppression. Its cyclical activation and inactivation represent one of the primary biochemical switches ensuring that DNA replication occurs only once per mitotic division.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the primary stoichiometric co-activator of the anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) during late mitosis and G1 phase, distinguishing its role from the early mitotic co-activator *Cdc20*.
- Recognizes destruction motifs on target substrates, binding specifically to conserved amino acid sequences such as the destruction box (D-box), KEN box, and CRY box to facilitate polyubiquitination and 26S proteasome-mediated degradation.
- Regulated directly by phosphorylation, wherein high cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) activity in early mitosis phosphorylates its N-terminal serine and threonine residues, preventing it from binding to the APC/C core.
- Activated during mitotic exit when the dual-specificity phosphatase *Cdc14* (or PP2A/PP1 in mammals) dephosphorylates its inhibitory residues, allowing it to assemble the active APC/C-Cdh1 complex.
- Drives the proteolysis of *Cdc20*, *cyclin B*, *Plk1*, and *Aurora A* kinases, effectively extinguishing mitotic kinase activity and establishing the low-CDK environment necessary for the assembly of pre-replication complexes on [[dna]].
- Inactivated at the G1/S transition through binding by the specialized stoichiometric pseudo-substrate inhibitor *Emi1* (early mitotic inhibitor 1) and renewed phosphorylation by *Cyclin E-Cdk2* and *Cyclin A-Cdk2*.
- Frequently modeled in budding yeast (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*) genetics using temperature-sensitive alleles calibrated and arrested at restrictive growth temperatures measured in [[celsius]].
- Functions as a haploinsufficient tumor suppressor whose functional loss or deregulation permits genomic instability, centrosome amplification, and unregulated cell proliferation, distinct from the epithelial cell-adhesion molecule E-cadherin that shares the identical genetic symbol *CDH1*.

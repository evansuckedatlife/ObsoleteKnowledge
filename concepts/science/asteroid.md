## summary

**Asteroid**, historically designated as a **minor planet** or **planetoid**, refers to a rocky, metallic, or carbonaceous celestial body orbiting the Sun that represents a primordial remnant from the formation of the [[solar-system]]. Concentrated predominantly within the circumstellar main belt situated between Mars and [[jupiter]], these objects span dimensions from meter-scale fragments to massive dwarf bodies hundreds of kilometers across. Because they escaped coalescence into larger planetary bodies during the accretion epoch, asteroids serve as invaluable chemical time capsules that preserve the pristine materials and physical conditions of the early solar nebula.

## you gotta know

- Discovered on New Year's Day in 1801 by Italian astronomer *Giuseppe Piazzi*, *Ceres* was the first asteroid observed; initially hailed as a primary planet, it was later classified as an asteroid and ultimately designated a dwarf planet.
- A catastrophic impact by a ten-kilometer asteroid produced the *Chicxulub crater* roughly sixty-six million years ago, precipitating the Cretaceous-Paleogene extinction event that extinguished non-avian dinosaurs and opened ecological niches for mammals.
- The vast majority of cataloged asteroids orbit within the main asteroid belt between Mars and [[jupiter]], where the intense gravitational influence of [[jupiter]] prevented these planetesimals from coalescing into a single planetary body.
- Asteroids are partitioned spectroscopically into three dominant taxonomic classes: C-type objects rich in volatile [[carbon]] compounds, stony S-type bodies composed of silicates and [[oxygen]] matrices, and metallic M-type remnants consisting primarily of nickel and iron.
- Orbital resonances with [[jupiter]] carve out sparsely populated gaps in the main belt known as *Kirkwood gaps*, where recurring gravitational perturbations systematically clear away asteroidal material over geological timescales.
- Co-orbital asteroidal populations occupy the stable fourth and fifth *Lagrangian points* along planetary orbits, most prominently the *Trojan asteroids* that cluster sixty degrees ahead of and behind [[jupiter]].
- Exploration led by [[nasa]] and international space agencies has achieved orbital rendezvous and sample returns via spacecraft such as *NEAR Shoemaker*, *DAWN*, and *OSIRIS-REx*, alongside planetary defense testing by *DART*, which altered an asteroid's orbital period via kinetic impact.

## connections

- [[jupiter]] — exerts gravitational perturbations that cleared the *Kirkwood gaps*, shepherds the *Trojan asteroids*, and disrupted planetary accretion in the main asteroid belt.
- [[nasa]] — the space agency that directed milestone robotic exploration and sample-return missions to asteroids, including *DAWN*, *OSIRIS-REx*, and the *DART* kinetic deflector.
- [[carbon]] — constitutes the abundant organic polymers, graphite, and volatile compounds characteristic of primitive C-type chondritic bodies.
- [[oxygen]] — binds with metallic elements to form the olivine and pyroxene silicates that comprise the crusts of stony S-type asteroids.
- [[europa]] — Jovian moon whose differentiated interior and hydrated chemistry offer comparative models for volatile-rich asteroids and dwarf planets.
- [[space-race]] — geopolitical competition that drove the development of rocketry, trajectory computation, and deep-space telemetry necessary to navigate and land on small celestial bodies.
- [[solar-system]] — the planetary system whose early accretion dynamics left behind asteroids as unaccreted, primordial planetesimal remnants.

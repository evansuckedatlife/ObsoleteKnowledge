## summary

The **ampere** (symbol: *A*), frequently abbreviated as the **amp**, is the base unit of electric current within the *International System of Units* (*SI*). Named in honour of the French physicist and mathematician *André-Marie Ampère*, who formulated the fundamental laws of electrodynamics, the unit measures the net rate of electric charge flowing past a given point per unit time. Following the comprehensive 2019 revision of the SI system, the **ampere** is explicitly defined by fixing the exact numerical value of the elementary charge of an [[electron]].

## you gotta know

- *André-Marie Ampère* laid the foundations of electromagnetism in the 1820s by discovering that parallel current-carrying conductors exert attractive magnetic forces when current flows in identical directions and repulsive forces when currents oppose.
- In the 2019 revision of SI base units, the **ampere** was redefined by setting the elementary charge *e* to precisely $1.602176634 \times 10^{-19}$ coulombs, making one unit equivalent to a flow of approximately $6.241509074 \times 10^{18}$ elementary charges per second.
- The historical definition of the **ampere** relied upon the magnetic force produced between two infinite, parallel conductors of negligible circular cross-section positioned one meter apart in a vacuum, generating a force of $2 \times 10^{-7}$ newtons per meter.
- According to *Ohm's law*, the current in **amperes** through an electrical pathway equals the electromotive potential difference in volts divided by the resistance in ohms across a [[resistor]], expressed concisely as $I = V / R$.
- *Ampère's circuital law*, one of *Maxwell's equations*, asserts that the line integral of a magnetic field around a closed loop is directly proportional to the total electric current passing through the enclosed surface.
- Current flow is characterized as *direct current* (*DC*), in which charge carriers travel unidirectionally, or *alternating current* (*AC*), in which the magnitude and vector direction of the current fluctuate cyclically.
- Experimental measurement of current is conducted using an ammeter, which must be connected in series with the load and possess minimal internal resistance to prevent voltage drops across the measurement apparatus.

## connections

- [[electron]] — the elementary subatomic lepton whose collective drift through conductive metallic lattices constitutes conventional electric current.
- [[resistor]] — the fundamental circuit element that limits the flow of current in direct accordance with *Ohm's law*.
- [[atom]] — the atomic structure whose weakly bound valence electrons break free into a shared conduction band to carry charge.
- [[standard-model]] — the foundational theoretical framework that classifies fundamental charged particles and the electromagnetic force mediated by the [[photon]].
- [[photon]] — the massless gauge boson that mediates electromagnetic interactions between propagating electric currents and magnetic fields.
- [[quantum-mechanics]] — dictates the quantum conductance channels and ballistic electron transport observed within nanoscale conductors.
- [[alpha-decay]] — a radioactive decay mode that ejects charged helium nuclei, producing micro-currents that can be quantified in this unit.

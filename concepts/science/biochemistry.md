## summary

**Biochemistry** is the scientific discipline that explores the chemical substances, molecular structures, and metabolic processes operating within living organisms. Emerging at the historical intersection of organic chemistry and cellular physiology, the field elucidates how inanimate chemical constituents assemble to generate living structures, catalyze vital transformations, and transmit hereditary information. From the enzymatic cascades of cellular respiration to nucleic acid replication, **biological chemistry** provides the foundational molecular framework for modern genetics, pharmacology, biotechnology, and medicine.

## you gotta know

- Four major classes of biological macromolecules form the structural, functional, and informational basis of all cellular biochemistry: proteins, nucleic acids, carbohydrates, and lipids.
- Enzymes function as macromolecular biological catalysts that accelerate reaction rates by many orders of magnitude by stabilizing transition states and lowering activation energy barriers.
- The *central dogma of molecular biology*, formulated by *Francis Crick*, delineates the directional transmission of genetic information from [[dna]] through RNA to [[protein]].
- *Adenosine triphosphate* (ATP) acts as the universal chemical energy currency of the [[cell]], releasing Gibbs free energy upon hydrolysis of its phosphoanhydride bonds to drive endergonic cellular processes.
- The *Michaelis-Menten model* mathematically describes initial reaction velocity in enzyme kinetics as a function of substrate concentration, defining the parameters $V_{\max}$ and the Michaelis constant $K_m$.
- Cellular respiration couples glycolysis, the citric acid cycle (*Krebs cycle*), and oxidative phosphorylation across mitochondrial membranes to generate ATP via chemiosmosis using molecular [[oxygen]].
- Allosteric regulation and feedback inhibition permit multi-enzyme metabolic pathways to dynamically modulate catalytic flux based on cellular energy charge and metabolite concentrations.
- Photosynthetic light reactions convert solar photons into chemical reducing power (NADPH) and ATP, which subsequently power the enzymatic Calvin cycle to fix atmospheric carbon.

## connections

- [[protein]] — linear polypeptide polymers of amino acids whose complex three-dimensional conformations dictate enzymatic activity, structural scaffolding, and molecular transport.
- [[enzyme]] — specialized protein catalysts possessing high-affinity active sites that bind specific substrates to accelerate biological reactions without being consumed.
- [[carbon]] — tetravalent atomic foundation whose capacity to form stable, diverse covalent bonds enables the vast structural complexity of biochemical metabolites and macromolecules.
- [[photosynthesis]] — light-dependent anabolic pathway in autotrophs that converts radiant solar energy into stored biochemical bond energy in hexose sugars.
- [[oxygen]] — terminal electron acceptor in aerobic oxidative phosphorylation, enabling efficient thermodynamic energy yield during biochemical carbohydrate catabolism.
- [[dna]] — double-stranded helical polymer of deoxyribonucleotides storing the complete genetic blueprint translated by biochemical machinery into functional proteins.
- [[cell]] — membrane-delimited structural unit maintaining the specialized ionic, enzymatic, and pH microenvironments essential for biochemical reactions.
- [[biome]] — macroscopic ecological manifestation of aggregated primary productivity driven by photosynthetic and metabolic biochemical processes across climates.
- [[bipedalism]] — physical locomotor strategy relying on oxidative skeletal muscle biochemistry and finely tuned aerobic cellular respiration.

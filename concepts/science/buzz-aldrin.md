## summary

**Buzz Aldrin** (born 1930) is an American former astronaut and engineer who was the second person to walk on the Moon during Apollo 11 on July 21, 1969, 19 minutes after *Neil Armstrong*. Serving as the Lunar Module pilot, Aldrin performed experiments, collected samples, and spent 2 hours 31 minutes on the lunar surface. He represents the culmination of the Cold War space race and remains a prominent advocate for continued lunar and Mars exploration.

## you gotta know

- Born *Edwin Eugene Aldrin Jr.* in Glen Ridge, New Jersey; adopted the nickname "Buzz" as a child.
- Earned a doctorate in astronautics from MIT with a thesis on orbital rendezvous techniques crucial to Apollo missions.
- Conducted seven spacewalks on Gemini 12 (1966), advancing techniques for extravehicular activity and rendezvous maneuvering.
- Deployed the Solar Wind Collector on the Moon, collected 22 kilograms of lunar samples, and took the famous photograph of Armstrong on the lunar surface.
- Famously described the lunar landscape as "magnificent desolation," capturing both the stark beauty and scientific import of the Moon.
- Experienced psychological depression after returning from Apollo 11, becoming a vocal advocate for mental health awareness among astronauts.
- Continued advocating for space exploration into his 90s, promoting a vision of sustainable lunar bases and Mars missions for future generations.
- Developed cycling trajectories for efficient Mars transit, contributing innovative orbital mechanics concepts to spaceflight planning.

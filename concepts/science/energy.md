## summary

**Energy** is the capacity of a system to do work or undergo change. It cannot be created or destroyed (conservation of energy), only transformed between forms: kinetic (motion), potential (position), thermal (heat), chemical (bonds), and others. Energy is the central quantity in both classical mechanics and quantum mechanics, making it fundamental to understanding how the universe operates.

## you gotta know

- Kinetic energy is proportional to mass times velocity squared (KE = ½mv²); faster objects carry more energy.
- Potential energy depends on position in a force field; gravitational potential energy is mgh, electrostatic potential is k*q₁*q₂/r.
- In thermodynamics, energy is quantified as the capacity to raise temperature or perform work; the first law states that internal energy equals heat added minus work done.
- The Hamiltonian in quantum mechanics is the operator representing total energy (kinetic plus potential); its eigenvalues are the possible measured energies.
- Energy conservation constrains all physical processes: total energy before an interaction equals total energy after.
- In relativity, E = mc² shows energy and mass are equivalent; a tiny amount of mass yields enormous energy.

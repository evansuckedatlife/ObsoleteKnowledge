## summary

The **Tennessee Titans** are a professional American football franchise based in Nashville, Tennessee, competing in the South Division of the American Football Conference in the *[[national-football-league]]*. Founded in 1959 by *Bud Adams* as the **Houston Oilers**, the team was a charter member of the American Football League and won the league's first two championships in 1960 and 1961. After relocating to Tennessee in 1997 as the **Tennessee Oilers**, the franchise adopted the Titans moniker in 1999 and promptly engineered an unforgettable postseason run to the *[[super-bowl]]*.

## you gotta know

- Played in *[[super-bowl]]* XXXIV against the St. Louis Rams in January 2000, which ended in iconic fashion on the final play known as *The Tackle*, when Rams linebacker *Mike Jones* tackled Titans receiver *Kevin Dyson* one yard short of the goal line.
- Reached that title game thanks to the *Music City Miracle*, a legendary lateral pass from *Frank Wycheck* to *Kevin Dyson* on a kickoff return with sixteen seconds remaining to defeat the Buffalo Bills in the 1999 AFC Wild Card game.
- Led during their late-1990s and early-2000s golden era by charismatic quarterback *Steve McNair*, who shared the 2003 NFL Most Valuable Player Award with *[[peyton-manning]]*, and bruising running back *Eddie George*, winner of the 1995 Heisman Trophy.
- Began franchise history as the **Houston Oilers**, boasting explosive offenses guided by Hall of Fame passer *Warren Moon* in the famous *Run and Shoot* scheme of the late 1980s and early 1990s.
- Suffered the greatest playoff collapse in NFL history at the time during the 1992–93 postseason, surrendering a thirty-two-point lead to *Frank Reich* and the Buffalo Bills in a game dubbed *The Comeback*.
- Reached AFC Championship contention in the modern era behind running back *Derrick Henry*, who rushed for over two thousand yards in 2020 and established himself as one of the most dominant physical rushers of his generation.
- Sustained intense divisional rivalries within the AFC Central and AFC South, notably battling AFC powerhouses led by legendary passers such as *[[peyton-manning]]*, *[[tom-brady]]*, and rival AFC franchises like the *[[san-diego-chargers]]*.

## connections

- [[peyton-manning]] — the longtime division rival quarterback of the Indianapolis Colts who co-shared the 2003 NFL MVP award with *Steve McNair*.
- [[san-diego-chargers]] — fellow AFL charter franchise with whom the Oilers and Titans have shared historic playoff and regular season clashes.
- [[tom-brady]] — the legendary quarterback whose final pass as a New England Patriot was intercepted for a pick-six by Titans cornerback *Logan Ryan* in the 2019 playoffs.
- [[drew-brees]] — contemporary elite NFL passer whose high-powered passing attacks challenged AFC and NFL defenses during the Titans' twenty-first-century campaigns.
- [[united-states]] — the country whose sporting culture and Southern football traditions anchor the franchise in Texas and Tennessee.

## summary

The **Boston Celtics** are an American professional basketball team based in Boston, Massachusetts, competing in the National Basketball Association's Eastern Conference. Founded in 1946 as a charter member of the Basketball Association of America, the franchise stands as one of the most successful and storied athletic institutions in North American sports history. Celebrated for iconic championship runs, defensive innovation, and team-first play, the team has captured eighteen NBA championships and established indelible rivalries that shaped the trajectory of modern basketball.

## you gotta know

- Captured eleven NBA championships in thirteen seasons between 1957 and 1969 under the leadership of center *Bill Russell* and coach *Red Auerbach*.
- Won eight consecutive NBA titles from 1959 through 1966, marking the longest continuous championship streak in North American professional sports history.
- Plays home games on a distinctive parquet floor, an iconic surface originally assembled from scrap oak at the old Boston Garden.
- Contested legendary NBA Finals matchups against the Los Angeles Lakers across several eras, spotlighted by the famous 1980s rivalry between *Larry Bird* and *Magic Johnson*.
- Drafted *Chuck Cooper* in 1950, breaking the NBA's color barrier by selecting the league's first African-American player.
- Hired *Bill Russell* in 1966 as the first African-American head coach in major North American professional sports history.
- Captured their historic eighteenth NBA championship in 2024, breaking a tie with the Los Angeles Lakers for the most titles in league history.

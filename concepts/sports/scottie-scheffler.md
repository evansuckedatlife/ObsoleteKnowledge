## summary

**Scottie Scheffler** is an American professional golfer who rose to become the dominant World No. 1 player of the 2020s. Renowned for his extraordinary ball-striking consistency and unconventional footwork through impact, he has claimed multiple major titles at the Masters Tournament alongside victories at the Players Championship. Scheffler's clinical efficiency and record-shattering scoring averages have cemented his place among golf's modern elite.

## you gotta know

- Captured his first major championship title at the 2022 Masters Tournament at Augusta National, returning to win a second green jacket in 2024.
- Became the first player in PGA Tour history to successfully defend the title at The Players Championship with his historic 2024 repeat.
- Won the men's individual gold medal for the [[united-states]] at the 2024 Paris Summer [[olympic-games]] with a sensational final-round 62 at Le Golf National.
- Possesses a distinctive, unorthodox swing characterized by sliding footwork and a dancing right heel through downswing impact.
- Led the PGA Tour in greens in regulation and strokes gained tee-to-green across multiple consecutive seasons, drawing comparisons to peak ball-strikers.
- Dominated the 2024 PGA Tour season by capturing the FedEx Cup alongside seven tournament victories, the most in a single season since the mid-2000s.

## summary

**Brett Favre** is a Hall of Fame American football quarterback who became an iconic figure of the modern *National Football League* through his relentless endurance, gunslinger passing style, and emotional charisma. Spending the bulk of his storied career with the *Green Bay Packers*, he revitalized one of the sport's most storied franchises during the 1990s and led them to victory in *Super Bowl XXXI*. His legendary durability produced an NFL record 297 consecutive regular-season starts, cementing his legacy as one of the toughest competitors in the history of the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Won three consecutive AP NFL Most Valuable Player awards from 1995 to 1997, remaining the only player in league history to achieve a three-peat as MVP.
- Started 297 consecutive regular-season games (321 including playoffs) from September 1992 to December 2010, an iron-man endurance streak long considered unbreakable.
- Led the *Green Bay Packers* to victory over the *New England Patriots* in *Super Bowl XXXI*, opening the contest with a signature 54-yard touchdown pass to *Andre Rison*.
- Retired holding all major career passing milestones, including all-time passing yards (71,838), completions, and touchdown passes (508), though he also retired with the record for most career interceptions (336).
- Succeeded in Green Bay by *Aaron Rodgers*, whom the Packers selected in the first round of the 2005 NFL Draft to eventually replace him following several contentious retirement sagas.
- Traded to the *New York Jets* in 2008 before joining the division rival *Minnesota Vikings* in 2009, leading Minnesota to the NFC Championship Game at age forty.
- Played college football at the *University of Southern Mississippi*, famously returning to lead a comeback victory against *Alabama* just months after surviving a near-fatal car crash.
- Inducted into the *Pro Football Hall of Fame* in 2016, celebrated for his infectious passion, backyard style, and cannon arm.

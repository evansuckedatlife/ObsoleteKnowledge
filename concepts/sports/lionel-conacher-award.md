## summary

The **Lionel Conacher Award** is an annual sports honor presented by *The Canadian Press* to Canada's outstanding male athlete of the year. Established in 1932, the accolade was named in honor of *Lionel Conacher*, a multitalented sportsman nicknamed *The Big Train* who dominated Canadian football, lacrosse, baseball, and [[ice-hockey]]. Selected through a ballot of national sports journalists, the roster of recipients constitutes a chronicle of Canadian sporting excellence across nearly a century.

## you gotta know

- Named for *Lionel Conacher*, voted Canada's top male athlete of the first half of the twentieth century after winning the *Grey Cup* and two [[stanley-cup]] championships.
- First presented in 1932 to baseball outfielder *Ross Somerville* following his triumph at the United States Amateur Championship.
- Held in record fashion by [[ice-hockey]] legend [[wayne-gretzky]], who captured the honor six times during his high-scoring prime in the 1980s.
- Awarded across a wide variety of winter and summer disciplines, spanning alpine skiing, track and field, auto racing, and basketball.
- Paired alongside the *Bobbie Rosenfeld Award*, its companion prize given annually to Canada's top female athlete.
- Presented to Formula One driver *Jacques Villeneuve* in 1997 after he clinched the World Drivers' Championship.
- Bestowed upon modern icons including basketball star *Steve Nash*, baseball pitcher *Ferguson Jenkins*, and hockey captain *Sidney Crosby*.

## connections

- [[ice-hockey]] — the primary sport associated with many of the award's most frequent and famous winners.
- [[stanley-cup]] — hockey prize lifted by *Lionel Conacher* with the *Chicago Black Hawks* and *Montreal Maroons*, and by many recipients.
- [[wayne-gretzky]] — won the award a record six times between 1980 and 1989 while setting scoring benchmarks.
- [[bobby-orr]] — legendary defenseman and recipient of the award during his defensive dominance with the Boston Bruins.
- [[gordie-howe]] — renowned hockey icon recognized with the honor during his illustrious professional career.
- [[olympic-games]] — major multi-sport competition where numerous award winners produced their signature performances.
- [[lionel-messi]] — shares the given name of the multi-sport athlete for whom this trophy was founded.

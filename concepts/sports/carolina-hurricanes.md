## summary

The **Carolina Hurricanes** are a professional *ice hockey* franchise competing in the National Hockey League's Eastern Conference and based in Raleigh, North Carolina. Established in 1971 as the New England Whalers in the World Hockey Association before becoming the *Hartford Whalers* upon joining the NHL, the club relocated south in 1997. The franchise achieved its greatest pinnacle by capturing the coveted *Stanley Cup* championship in 2006 under head coach Peter Laviolette.

## you gotta know

- Relocated to Raleigh in 1997 from Connecticut, where the franchise had enjoyed a devoted regional following as the beloved *Hartford Whalers*, famous for playing the jaunty Brass Bonanza goal song.
- Won the franchise's first and only championship in 2006 by defeating the Edmonton Oilers in a thrilling seven-game final series to hoist the *Stanley Cup*.
- Captained during that memorable 2006 title run by center *Rod Brind'Amour*, who later returned to the franchise behind the bench to earn the Jack Adams Award as NHL Coach of the Year.
- Goaltender *Cam Ward* became the first rookie netminder since Patrick Roy to capture the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff Most Valuable Player during their championship postseason.
- Gained widespread cultural attention and modern media notoriety in 2018–19 with exuberant post-game victory celebrations known as the *Storm Surge*, dubbed a "bunch of jerks" by Canadian commentator Don Cherry.
- Played their initial two seasons in North Carolina at the Greensboro Coliseum while waiting for their permanent home arena, now known as the Lenovo Center, to finish construction in Raleigh.
- Hockey Hall of Fame legend *Gordie Howe* famously skated for the original franchise alongside his sons Mark and Marty during its final WHA season and inaugural NHL campaign.

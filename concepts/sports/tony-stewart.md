## summary

**Tony Stewart** (born **Anthony Wayne Stewart**), universally known in motorsport by his nickname **Smoke**, is an American racing driver, team owner, and track proprietor renowned as one of the most versatile and fiercely competitive wheelmen in automotive history. Raised on the grassroots short tracks of *Indiana*, he made racing history as the only driver to win championships in both *IndyCar* open-wheel racing and *NASCAR's* premier stock car series. Combining extraordinary car control with an uncompromising, fiery temperament, Stewart won three Cup Series titles across three different decades before establishing *Stewart-Haas Racing* as an elite championship-winning enterprise.

## you gotta know

- Captured three *NASCAR* Cup Series championships across distinct eras (2002, 2005, and 2011), winning his first two titles driving for legendary coach *Joe Gibbs* before claiming his third as a driver-owner with *Stewart-Haas Racing*.
- Won the 1997 *Indy Racing League* (*IRL*) championship, cementing his unique legacy as the only driver in motorsports history to capture both a premier American open-wheel championship and a *NASCAR* Cup crown.
- Achieved the historic *USAC Triple Crown* in 1995 by winning the National Midget, Sprint, and Silver Crown championships in a single calendar year, becoming the first competitor in history to sweep all three divisions.
- Completed the legendary Memorial Day weekend "Double Duty" in 1999 and 2001, competing in both the *Indianapolis 500* and the *Coca-Cola 600* in *Charlotte* on the same day, becoming the only driver to complete all 1,100 miles in 2001 while finishing sixth and third.
- Won the 2011 Cup Series title in one of the most dramatic championship duels in racing history, winning five of the ten postseason races to tie *Carl Edwards* on total points and claiming the title on a victory tiebreaker.
- Inducted into the *NASCAR Hall of Fame* as part of the class of 2020, honoring a stellar stock-car career that included forty-nine premier series victories, including two beloved home-state wins at the *Brickyard 400*.
- Maintained a lifelong devotion to grassroots dirt racing, purchasing and operating the iconic *Eldora Speedway* in *Ohio* while later transitioning as a driver into professional *NHRA* drag racing competition.

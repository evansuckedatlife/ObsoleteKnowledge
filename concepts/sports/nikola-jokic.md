## summary

**Nikola Jokic**, widely nicknamed **The Joker**, is a Serbian professional basketball player who revolutionized the center position in the modern era with the Denver Nuggets. Renowned as one of the greatest passing big men in basketball history, he combines unprecedented court vision, elite rebounding, and pinpoint scoring efficiency to operate as a primary offensive hub. His unconventional, ground-bound style confounded traditional athletic expectations and carried his franchise to its first league championship in 2023.

## you gotta know

- Selected by the Denver Nuggets with the 41st overall pick in the second round of the 2014 draft, famously during a televised commercial for Taco Bell.
- Won three Most Valuable Player awards across four seasons (2021, 2022, and 2024), joining an elite fraternity of multi-time winners in modern professional basketball history.
- Led the Denver Nuggets to their first championship title in franchise history in 2023, earning the Finals Most Valuable Player award after leading all playoff participants in total points, rebounds, and assists.
- Redefined the tactical possibilities of the center position through elite playmaking, regularly recording triple-doubles with pinpoint cross-court passes and touch-passes out of the high post.
- Represented Serbia internationally, capturing a silver medal at the 2016 Rio Summer Games and a bronze medal at the 2024 Paris Summer Games in men's basketball.
- Developed his early fundamentals in the Adriatic League with Mega Basket, winning the league MVP award in 2015 prior to making his journey across the Atlantic to North America.
- Celebrated for his laconic, deadpan public persona and his passionate personal devotion to harness racing and racehorses in his hometown of Sombor, Serbia.

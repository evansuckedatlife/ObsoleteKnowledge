## summary

**Andrew Wiggins** is a Canadian professional basketball player who rose from a heavily hyped teenage prodigy to an NBA champion and All-Star forward. Selected first overall in the 2014 NBA Draft, he initially played for the Minnesota Timberwolves before reviving his career with the Golden State Warriors, where his elite two-way play and perimeter defense proved vital to their 2022 championship run. Born in Toronto to an Olympic track medalist mother and an NBA father, he remains a central figure in the rapid global emergence of Canadian basketball.

## you gotta know

- Selected first overall in the 2014 NBA Draft by the Cleveland Cavaliers, becoming only the second Canadian player ever drafted number one after *Anthony Bennett*.
- Traded to the Minnesota Timberwolves prior to playing a game for Cleveland as part of a blockbuster deal that sent forward *Kevin Love* to join *LeBron James*.
- Won the 2014–15 NBA Rookie of the Year Award after averaging 16.9 points per game for the Timberwolves.
- Played a pivotal role as the secondary star alongside *Stephen Curry* on the Golden State Warriors team that won the 2022 NBA Championship over the Boston Celtics.
- Earned his first career NBA All-Star selection as a starter in 2022, fueled by strong perimeter defensive play and efficient outside shooting.
- Played one season of collegiate basketball in the *United States* for the Kansas Jayhawks under head coach *Bill Self* before entering the professional ranks.
- Comes from an elite athletic lineage: his father *Mitchell Wiggins* played in the NBA, and his mother *Marita Payne-Wiggins* won two Olympic silver medals for Canada in track and field.

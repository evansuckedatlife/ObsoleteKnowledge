## summary

**Barry Bonds** is an American former professional baseball left fielder who played twenty-two seasons in Major League Baseball for the Pittsburgh Pirates and San Francisco Giants. Widely regarded as one of the most formidable and dominant hitters in baseball history, he holds MLB all-time career records for home runs and bases on balls. His exceptional achievements earned him seven National League Most Valuable Player awards, though his historic accomplishments became central to public debates surrounding performance-enhancing substances in modern athletics.

## you gotta know

- Established Major League Baseball's all-time career home run record with 762 home runs, surpassing the longstanding mark of 755 previously held by *Hank Aaron*.
- Set the single-season home run record in 2001 by hitting 73 home runs, eclipsing the record set by *Mark McGwire* three years earlier.
- Won seven National League Most Valuable Player Awards, an unprecedented feat that exceeds any other player in baseball history.
- Remains the only player in Major League Baseball history to hit at least 500 home runs and steal at least 500 bases throughout a career.
- Garnered 2,558 career walks and 688 intentional walks, including an intentional walk with the bases loaded against the Arizona Diamondbacks in 1998.
- Won eight Gold Glove Awards in left field during his early career with the Pittsburgh Pirates and San Francisco Giants before transitioning toward pure plate dominance.
- Formed the central subject of the investigative book *Game of Shadows* and was later indicted on perjury charges stemming from the federal investigation of the Bay Area Laboratory Co-operative.

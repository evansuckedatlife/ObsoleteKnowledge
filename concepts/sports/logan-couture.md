## summary

**Logan Couture** is a Canadian professional *ice hockey* centre and longtime captain of the San Jose Sharks in the National Hockey League. Drafted ninth overall in 2007, he developed into one of the league's most reliable two-way forwards and clutch postseason scorers of his generation. His standout play led the Sharks to their first [[stanley-cup]] Finals appearance in franchise history in 2016.

## you gotta know

- Selected ninth overall by the San Jose Sharks in the 2007 NHL Entry Draft after a standout junior career with the Ottawa 67's of the Ontario Hockey League.
- Finished as runner-up for the Calder Memorial Trophy as NHL rookie of the year in 2011, trailing only Carolina Hurricanes forward *Jeff Skinner*.
- Led the entire 2016 [[stanley-cup]] playoffs in scoring with 30 points in 24 games, propelling San Jose to the Western Conference championship and the finals against the Pittsburgh Penguins.
- Led the NHL postseason in goal scoring in 2019 with 14 goals in 20 games during another deep playoff run to the Western Conference Finals.
- Named the tenth captain in San Jose Sharks history in September 2019, succeeding veteran forward *Joe Pavelski*.
- Sustained multiple severe facial and dental injuries during play, notably blocking a shot in 2017 that shattered his mouth and required extensive reconstructive surgery.
- Played alongside franchise icons *Joe Thornton* and *Patrick Marleau* throughout the most competitive era in San Jose franchise history.

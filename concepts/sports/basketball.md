## summary

**Basketball** is a global team sport contested by two squads of five players on a rectangular court, who score points by shooting an inflated ball through an elevated hoop. Invented in December 1891 by Canadian physical education instructor *James Naismith* in Springfield, Massachusetts, the game was devised as an energetic indoor activity during harsh New England winters. Today, it stands as one of the most widely played and culturally influential sports in the world, headlined internationally by the *National Basketball Association* and global tournaments.

## you gotta know

- Invented in 1891 by *James Naismith*, a Canadian educator at the *International YMCA Training School* in Springfield, Massachusetts, using two peach baskets and a soccer ball.
- Established thirteen original rules that forbade running while holding the ball, introduced the dribble over time, and positioned the peach baskets ten feet above the gymnasium floor.
- Played globally on courts measuring 94 by 50 feet in professional leagues, featuring a three-point arc introduced to professional American play by the *American Basketball League* and later popularized by the *ABA* and *NBA*.
- Became an official medal event for men at the 1936 *Berlin Summer Games* and for women at the 1976 *Montreal Summer Games*, gaining massive global visibility through the 1992 *Dream Team* at the [[olympic-games]].
- Featured iconic dynastic rivalries throughout the twentieth century, including the *Boston Celtics* under *Bill Russell*, the *Los Angeles Lakers* of *Magic Johnson*, and the six-time champion *Chicago Bulls* led by *Michael Jordan*.
- Served as a critical cultural platform during the American [[civil-rights-movement]], breaking collegiate and professional color barriers through pioneer players such as *Earl Lloyd*, *Chuck Cooper*, and *Nat Clifton*.
- Hosted landmark professional milestones across historic arenas, including the very first *Basketball Association of America* game played at [[maple-leaf-gardens]] in 1946 between the *Toronto Huskies* and the *New York Knickerbockers*.

## connections

- [[olympic-games]] — stage for international competition and showcase of the historic 1992 *Dream Team*.
- [[united-states]] — country where the sport was devised, organized, and elevated to worldwide professional prominence.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — historical era whose integration efforts paralleled the desegregation of professional basketball leagues.
- [[maple-leaf-gardens]] — historic Toronto arena that staged the opening game of the *Basketball Association of America* in 1946.
- [[ice-hockey]] — fellow winter sport sharing many multi-purpose municipal arenas and collegiate athletic departments.
- [[super-bowl]] — premier American sporting championship that rivals major professional basketball finals in media reach.

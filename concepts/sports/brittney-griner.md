## summary

**Brittney Griner** is an American professional basketball center for the *Phoenix Mercury* of the Women's National Basketball Association and a three-time Olympic gold medalist. Standing six feet nine inches tall, she emerged as one of the most dominant two-way forces in women's basketball history, revolutionizing interior defense, shot-blocking, and dunking at Baylor University before anchoring Phoenix to the 2014 WNBA championship. She gained widespread international prominence in 2022 when she was detained in Russia on drug charges and subsequently freed in a high-profile prisoner exchange.

## you gotta know

- Led the *Baylor Lady Bears* to an undefeated 40-0 season and the NCAA Division I National Championship in 2012, capturing the *AP Player of the Year* award and the *Wade Trophy*.
- Finished her NCAA career with 748 blocked shots, establishing an all-time Division I record for both women's and men's collegiate basketball, alongside eighteen collegiate in-game dunks.
- Selected first overall in the 2013 WNBA Draft by the *Phoenix Mercury*, where she teamed with legendary guard *Diana Taurasi* to win the 2014 WNBA championship.
- Two-time WNBA Defensive Player of the Year, eight-time WNBA block leader, and two-time WNBA peak scoring leader, regularly leading the league in blocked shots, rebounds, and field goal percentage.
- Won three consecutive Olympic gold medals representing the [[united-states]] at the [[olympic-games]] in Rio de Janeiro in 2016, Tokyo in 2021, and Paris in 2024, highlighted by scoring thirty points in the Tokyo gold medal final against Japan.
- Detained at *Sheremetyevo International Airport* near Moscow in February 2022 after customs officials found vape cartridges containing hashish oil in her luggage while she was traveling to play for *UMMC Ekaterinburg* during the WNBA offseason.
- Sentenced to nine years in a Russian penal colony amid intense geopolitical friction surrounding the Russo-Ukrainian War before being released in December 2022 in exchange for convicted Russian arms dealer *Viktor Bout*.
- Authored the moving 2024 memoir *Coming Home*, chronicling her grueling ten-month imprisonment, survival in Russian penal labor camps, and emotional return to professional basketball in the WNBA and the Olympic Games.

## summary

The **Calgary Flames** are a professional [[ice-hockey]] franchise based in Calgary, Alberta, competing in the Pacific Division of the Western Conference of the *National Hockey League*. Founded in 1972 as the *Atlanta Flames*, the franchise relocated to western Canada in 1980, adopting the *Scotiabank Saddledome* as their long-term arena. The team reached the pinnacle of the sport by winning the [[stanley-cup]] in 1989, fueled by an intense, enduring provincial rivalry against the *Edmonton Oilers*.

## you gotta know

- Established initially as an expansion team in Georgia named the *Atlanta Flames* in 1972, referencing the burning of Atlanta during the [[american-civil-war]].
- Relocated north to Alberta in 1980, keeping their flaming logo and moniker to represent the boomtown energy and petroleum heritage of western Canada.
- Captured their first and only [[stanley-cup]] championship in 1989, defeating the *Montreal Canadiens* in six games under captain *Lanny McDonald* and head coach *Terry Crisp*.
- Maintained one of hockey's fiercest regional rivalries, the *Battle of Alberta*, trading brutal regular-season bouts and playoff series against the *Edmonton Oilers* led by [[wayne-gretzky]].
- Reached the Stanley Cup Finals again in 2004 under captain *Jarome Iginla* and netminder *Miikka Kiprusoff*, ultimately falling to the *Tampa Bay Lightning* in a dramatic seven-game series.
- Hosted the *Heritage Classic* outdoor game in February 2011 at *McMahon Stadium*, shutting out the *Montreal Canadiens* before over 41,000 cold-weather fans.
- Anchored major professional sports culture in Calgary, sharing facilities and developmental visibility with elite athletes such as [[hayley-wickenheiser]] during her collegiate career with the *Calgary Dinos*.

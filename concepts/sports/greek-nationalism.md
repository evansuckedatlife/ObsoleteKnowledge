## summary

**Greek nationalism** (also known as **Hellenic nationalism**) is the political, cultural, and ideological movement asserting the historical continuity and collective self-determination of the Greek people from classical antiquity through the modern era. Originating in the late eighteenth century under the influence of the European [[enlightenment]] and culminating in the Greek War of Independence against the *Ottoman Empire*, it framed modern Hellas as the direct heir to [[ancient-greece]]. In the late nineteenth century, this national consciousness found profound symbolic expression when *Athens* hosted the 1896 [[olympic-games]], serving as an immense source of national pride and civilizational legitimacy.

## you gotta know

- Fostered during the eighteenth-century *Diafotismos* (Modern Greek Enlightenment) by intellectuals such as *Adamantios Korais* and *Rigas Feraios*, who sought to revive ancient classical literacy and secular democratic ideals to spark national liberation.
- Catalyzed the 1821 Greek Revolution with enthusiastic military and financial backing from Philhellenes across [[france]] and [[england]], who viewed the revolt romantically as classical civilization rising against eastern imperial subjugation.
- Driven throughout the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries by the *Megali Idea* (Great Idea), an irredentist program aimed at uniting all ethnic Greek populations of the eastern Mediterranean and restoring *Constantinople* as the national capital.
- Synthesized the pagan heritage of [[ancient-greece]] with Byzantine Eastern Orthodox [[christianity]], creating an enduring national identity grounded in both classical intellectual glory and medieval religious tradition.
- Culminated in a triumph of statecraft when *Athens* hosted the inaugural modern [[olympic-games]] in 1896, an international festival orchestrated by the royal court to validate Greece's modern European standing.
- Galvanized national euphoria during the 1896 Olympic Games when peasant water-carrier *Spyridon Louis* won the inaugural marathon race, creating an iconic folk hero bridging ancient military legend with modern athletic accomplishment.
- Suffered a catastrophic collapse following [[world-war-i]] during the Greco-Turkish War of 1919–1922, ending the *Megali Idea* through the Asia Minor Catastrophe and massive refugee exchanges across the [[middle-east]].

## connections

- [[ancient-greece]] — the ancient civilization whose philosophy, democratic ideals, and athletic traditions provided the core narrative of modern Greek revival.
- [[olympic-games]] — international sporting competition revived at *Athens* in 1896, serving as a global platform for modern Hellenic pride.
- [[enlightenment]] — western European intellectual epoch whose tenets of popular sovereignty and philhellenic scholarship inspired the Greek independence movement.
- [[world-war-i]] — global conflict that created the geopolitical opening and ultimate demise for the *Megali Idea* irredentist campaign.
- [[roman-empire]] — historic realm whose eastern Byzantine continuation preserved the Greek language and Orthodox religious heritage through the Middle Ages.
- [[middle-east]] — neighbouring geographic region that was home to ancient Greek diaspora communities prior to the compulsory population exchanges of 1923.
- [[france]] — western European nation whose republican revolutions and philhellenic societies offered ideological and diplomatic assistance to Greek rebels.

## summary

**Matea Roach** is a celebrated Canadian quiz competitor, writer, and media commentator who rose to international prominence during a historic run on the American television game show *Jeopardy!* in the spring of 2022. Originating from Halifax, Nova Scotia, and based in Toronto, Ontario, they captured the public imagination with a 23-game winning streak that established them as the most successful Canadian contestant in the history of the franchise. Their remarkable mastery of rapid trivia recall, combined with energetic physical mannerisms and strategic buzzer speed, cemented their reputation as an elite intellectual competitor in modern televised quiz competition.

## you gotta know

- Amassed a 23-game winning streak on *Jeopardy!* between April 5 and May 6, 2022, which placed them fifth on the all-time regular-season consecutive wins list at the conclusion of their run.
- Earned a total of $560,983 during their regular-season appearances, ranking as the highest-earning Canadian player and one of the most lucrative regular-season competitors in show history.
- Became an international fan favorite due to distinctive on-stage mannerisms, including talking through clues aloud, expressive hand gestures, and self-effacing humor while calculating wagers.
- Advanced to the championship final of the inaugural *Jeopardy! Masters* tournament in May 2023, competing in a high-stakes three-day final against champions *James Holzhauer* and *Matt Amodio* and finishing as the tournament runner-up by a razor-thin margin.
- Displayed exceptional command of humanities categories, frequently dominating boards involving [[linguistics]], world history, pop music, art history, philosophy, and [[american-literature]].
- Competed in the prestigious 2022 *Tournament of Champions*, entering as the third seed behind fellow super-champions *Amy Schneider* and *Matt Amodio* before being narrowly defeated in the semifinals.
- Served as the host of the Canadian political podcast *The Backbench* for the *Canadaland* network, transitioning from competitive trivia into prominent political commentary, debate moderation, and cultural journalism.
- Graduated from the University of Toronto in 2020 with a bachelor's degree in sexual diversity studies and political science, honing analytical and research skills that underpinned their trivia expertise.

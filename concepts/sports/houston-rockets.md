## summary

The **Houston Rockets** are an American professional basketball franchise based in Houston, Texas, competing in the Western Conference of the National Basketball Association. Founded in 1967 as the **San Diego Rockets**, the team relocated to Texas in 1971 and reached the pinnacle of the sport by capturing back-to-back NBA championships in 1994 and 1995 behind superstar centre Hakeem Olajuwon. Renowned for fielding dominant big men and pioneering modern analytic strategies, the team remains a marquee institution in professional American athletics.

## you gotta know

- Won back-to-back NBA championships in 1994 and 1995 under head coach Rudy Tomjanovich, inspiring the famous quote: "Don't ever underestimate the heart of a champion!"
- Led to their title runs by Nigerian-born Hall of Fame centre Hakeem Olajuwon, who earned Finals MVP honors both seasons and was joined in 1995 by Clyde Drexler.
- Originally established in California in 1967 before moving to Texas in 1971, retaining their cosmic moniker due to the presence of NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston.
- Drafted University of Houston star Elvin Hayes first overall in 1968 and later acquired perennial MVP centre Moses Malone, who carried the team to the 1981 Finals.
- Drafted seven-foot-six Chinese centre Yao Ming first overall in 2002, catalyzing a massive global expansion of basketball fandom and commerce throughout Asia.
- Defined the late 2010s "Moreyball" analytics era under general manager Daryl Morey and coach Mike D'Antoni, relying heavily on three-pointers, layups, and isolation scoring from James Harden.
- Plays home games at the Toyota Center in downtown Houston, wearing primary colors of red, black, and silver.

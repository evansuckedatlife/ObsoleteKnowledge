## summary

The **Chicago Blackhawks** are a professional ice hockey franchise based in Chicago, Illinois, competing as a member of the Central Division in the National Hockey League. Founded in 1926 by coffee magnate *Frederic McLaughlin*, the team is celebrated as one of the league's famed "Original Six" members that sustained professional hockey through the mid-twentieth century. The team holds a prominent place in sports history through multiple championship eras, iconic scoring legends, and heated cross-border rivalries.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1926 by *Frederic McLaughlin*, who named the team in honour of the 86th Infantry Division, nicknamed the "Blackhawk Division," in which he had served during World War I.
- Stands as one of the NHL's venerable "Original Six" franchises alongside the Boston Bruins, Detroit Red Wings, Montreal Canadiens, New York Rangers, and Toronto Maple Leafs.
- Captured their first two championships in 1934 and 1938, the latter led by star goaltender *Alfie Moore* during a remarkable Cinderella postseason run.
- Hall of Fame forward *Bobby Hull*, known as "The Golden Jet," and center *Stan Mikita* carried the franchise during an iconic era in the 1960s, winning the 1961 championship.
- Renowned for home-game traditions at the old Chicago Stadium and United Center, including thunderous cheering throughout the entire performance of the national anthem.
- Ended a 49-year championship drought by winning three titles in six seasons (2010, 2013, 2015) anchored by *Jonathan Toews*, *Patrick Kane*, and *Duncan Keith*.
- Longtime rivals include fellow Midwestern powerhouse the Detroit Red Wings, with whom they contested hundreds of regular season and playoff encounters.

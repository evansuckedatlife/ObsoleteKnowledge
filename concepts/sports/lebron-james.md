## summary

**LeBron James**, often hailed as **King James**, is an American professional basketball player celebrated as one of the greatest athletes in sports history. Emerging into national prominence as an Ohio high school prodigy, he went on to capture four National Basketball Association championships across three distinct franchises. His longevity, playmaking vision, and scoring prowess culminated in his surpassing *Kareem Abdul-Jabbar* as the league's all-time leading scorer.

## you gotta know

- Selected first overall by the *Cleveland Cavaliers* in the 2003 draft directly out of *St. Vincent–St. Mary High School* in *Akron*, *Ohio*.
- Formed the celebrated *Big Three* alongside *Dwyane Wade* and *Chris Bosh* on the *Miami Heat* following his controversial 2010 television special *The Decision*.
- Delivered the city of *Cleveland* its first major professional sports title in fifty-two years by overcoming a 3–1 deficit against the *Golden State Warriors* in the 2016 Finals.
- Captured four championship rings, earning Finals MVP honors each time with the *Miami Heat*, *Cleveland Cavaliers*, and *Los Angeles Lakers*.
- Surpassed *Kareem Abdul-Jabbar* in February 2023 to become the National Basketball Association's career regular-season scoring record holder.
- Won three Olympic gold medals representing the [[united-states]] men's basketball team at Beijing 2008, London 2012, and Paris 2024.
- Founded the *I Promise School* in his hometown of *Akron*, providing comprehensive educational and social support for at-risk youth.
- Shared an NBA court with his son *Bronny James* as teammates on the *Los Angeles Lakers*, marking the first active father-son duo in league annals.

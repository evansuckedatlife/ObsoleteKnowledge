## summary

The **Masters Tournament**, commonly referred to simply as **The Masters**, is one of the four major championships in professional golf, held annually during the first full week of April. Established in 1934 by amateur golf legend *Bobby Jones* and investment banker *Clifford Roberts*, the invitational event is staged permanently at the *Augusta National Golf Club* in Augusta, Georgia. It is widely revered for its rich traditions, including the awarding of an iconic *Green Jacket* to the victor, the ceremonial opening tee shots by legendary champions, and the unforgiving challenge posed by its famous three-hole stretch known as *Amen Corner*.

## you gotta know

- The winner receives the coveted *Green Jacket*, a distinctive coat originally introduced in 1937 for club members to wear during the tournament so patrons could easily identify them, with the tradition of awarding one to the champion beginning with *Sam Snead* in 1949.
- [[jack-nicklaus]] holds the record for the most victories in tournament history with six titles, securing his final historic victory in 1986 at the age of forty-six to become the oldest champion in tournament history.
- [[tiger-woods]] redefined modern golf at the tournament in 1997 by winning by a record twelve strokes at age twenty-one, becoming the youngest champion ever and later claiming four more titles, culminating in a dramatic fifth victory in 2019.
- [[arnold-palmer]] helped transform the tournament into a global television spectacle during the late 1950s and 1960s, capturing four titles while commanding his fiercely loyal gallery of supporters known as *Arnie's Army*.
- The tournament features *Amen Corner*, a moniker coined in 1958 by sportswriter *Herbert Warren Wind* in *Sports Illustrated* to describe the dramatic, water-guarded eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth holes where tournaments are frequently won or lost.
- Unlike the other three men's major championships—the [[us-open]], the *PGA Championship*, and *The Open Championship*—the tournament is the only major staged at the exact same venue every single year.
- Competitors who claim victory are granted honorary lifetime membership at *Augusta National Golf Club* and an invitation to participate in the exclusive annual *Champions Dinner*, hosted on the Tuesday of tournament week by the defending winner, who selects the menu.

## connections

- [[arnold-palmer]] — legendary four-time champion whose charisma and dedicated fan base popularized the tournament on national television.
- [[jack-nicklaus]] — all-time record holder with six tournament victories spanning from 1963 to his dramatic final win in 1986.
- [[tiger-woods]] — five-time champion who shattered scoring records in 1997 and captured five *Green Jackets*.
- [[us-open]] — fellow American major championship that joins the tournament in the traditional men's Grand Slam calendar.
- [[united-states]] — home country of *Augusta National Golf Club* and host nation of the annual April classic.
- [[wimbledon]] — prestigious tennis major that shares a comparable aura of strict tradition, private club heritage, and immaculate landscaping.

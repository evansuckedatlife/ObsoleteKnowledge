## summary

**Spring training** is the annual preseason training and exhibition period preceding the Major League Baseball regular season in the *[[united-states]]*. Held primarily throughout February and March in warm-weather complexes across Florida and Arizona, it serves to condition players, evaluate prospects, and finalize major-league rosters. The tradition dates back to the late nineteenth century and has grown into a major cultural and economic fixture of modern American baseball.

## you gotta know

- Divided geographically into two distinct exhibition circuits: the *Grapefruit League* in Florida and the *Cactus League* in Arizona.
- Pitchers and catchers historically report approximately a week before position players due to the extended arm conditioning required for throwing.
- Originating in the 1870s and 1880s, early barnstorming tours to warm southern states were pioneered by teams like the *Chicago White Stockings* under *Cap Anson*.
- In Florida, early training venues became deeply entangled with racial segregation and the *[[civil-rights-movement]]*, as Black ballplayers like *Jackie Robinson* were barred from team hotels until desegregation battles succeeded in the early 1960s.
- During *[[world-war-ii]]*, federal travel restrictions forced teams to hold preseason camps in northern or midwestern sites, often called the "jambalaya" or "pot-roast" circuits.
- The *Cactus League* was formally established in 1947 after the *Cleveland Indians* and *New York Giants* moved their spring headquarters to Arizona, partly to avoid southern Jim Crow laws.
- The period culminates in late March with traditional exhibitions in home cities or international venues before Opening Day of the regular season.

## connections

- [[the-new-york-yankees]] — legendary franchise that trained famously in St. Petersburg and later Tampa, Florida.
- [[babe-ruth]] — iconic slugger whose towering home runs during Florida exhibitions dramatically popularized spring exhibition games.
- [[derek-jeter]] — longtime captain who prepared for twenty major-league seasons at the Yankees' Florida facilities.
- [[mickey-mantle]] — star outfielder whose frequent spring camp battles and physical recoveries were closely followed by the national press.
- [[mariano-rivera]] — premier relief pitcher who honed his signature cut fastball during February conditioning drills.
- [[united-states]] — host nation whose warm southern regions developed specialized infrastructure for professional baseball tourism.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — crucial social struggle that forced the desegregation of spring housing and training facilities in the American South.

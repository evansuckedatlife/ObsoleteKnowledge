## summary

**Julia Grosso** is a Canadian professional soccer midfielder who gained worldwide prominence for her heroics on the international stage. Representing the Canada women's national soccer team, she scored the historic, gold-medal-clinching penalty kick in the shootout against Sweden at the Tokyo 2020 [[olympic-games]]. Grosso has competed at the highest levels of collegiate and European club soccer, winning domestic titles with Juventus in [[italy]] and playing in major international tournaments.

## you gotta know

- Converted the decisive sixth-round penalty against Swedish goalkeeper *Hedvig Lindahl* in the gold-medal shootout at the Tokyo 2020 [[olympic-games]], securing Canada's first Olympic gold in women's soccer.
- Starred collegiately for the Texas Longhorns in the [[united-states]], earning Big 12 Conference honors and developing into an exceptionally composed, two-way central midfielder.
- Signed her first major professional contract with Italian powerhouse Juventus Women in December 2021, helping the club capture Serie A, Coppa Italia, and Supercoppa Italiana silverware.
- Earned the Golden Boot as the top goalscorer at the 2022 CONCACAF W Championship in Mexico, scoring three goals for Canada.
- Named Serie A Midfielder of the Season for the 2022–2023 campaign in [[italy]], cementing her status among the elite central midfielders across European club soccer.
- Transitioned to the National Women's Soccer League by signing with the Chicago Red Stars in 2024 following her successful multi-year European spell with Juventus.
- Developed through the Vancouver Whitecaps FC Girls Elite academy system before making her senior international debut for Canada at just seventeen years of age.

## summary

The **Edmonton Oilers** are a professional ice hockey franchise based in Edmonton, Alberta, competing in the Pacific Division of the Western Conference in the National Hockey League. Founded in 1972 as a charter member of the World Hockey Association, the team joined the NHL in 1979 and rapidly established one of the greatest dynasties in professional sports history during the 1980s. Powered by transcendent center *[[wayne-gretzky]]* and a constellation of future Hall of Fame inductees, the franchise captured five *[[stanley-cup]]* championships between 1984 and 1990.

## you gotta know

- Began play in the World Hockey Association in 1972 as the *Alberta Oilers* before becoming one of four franchises—alongside the *Hartford Whalers*, *Quebec Nordiques*, and *Winnipeg Jets*—admitted to the NHL during the 1979 league merger.
- Built a legendary offensive juggernaut in the 1980s under head coach and general manager *Glen Sather*, utilizing an innovative, up-tempo, transition-based skating system that fundamentally redefined modern *[[ice-hockey]]*.
- Featured an unprecedented core of Hall of Fame talent centered on *[[wayne-gretzky]]*, *Mark Messier*, *Jari Kurri*, *Glenn Anderson*, *Paul Coffey*, and goaltender *Grant Fuhr*.
- Captured their first *[[stanley-cup]]* championship in 1984 by dethroning the four-time defending champion *New York Islanders*, followed by additional titles in 1985, 1987, 1988, and 1990 (the last won after Gretzky's departure).
- Stunned the sports world on August 9, 1988, when team owner *Peter Pocklington* traded *[[wayne-gretzky]]* to the *Los Angeles Kings* in a monumental transaction that transformed the geography and economics of North American hockey.
- Reached the Stanley Cup Finals as an eighth seed in 2006 behind defenseman *Chris Pronger* and goaltender *Dwayne Roloson*, falling in seven games to the *Carolina Hurricanes*.
- Underwent a prolonged postseason drought known as the "Decade of Darkness" before selecting generational superstar *Connor McDavid* first overall in the 2015 NHL Entry Draft.
- Reestablished themselves as a perennial contender led by *Connor McDavid* and *Leon Draisaitl*, capturing the Clarence S. Campbell Bowl and returning to the Stanley Cup Finals in 2024.

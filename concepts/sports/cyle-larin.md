## summary

**Cyle Larin**, in full **Cyle Christopher Larin**, is a Canadian professional soccer player who plays as a striker and is the all-time leading goalscorer for the Canada men's national soccer team. After launching his senior career in Major League Soccer in the [[united-states]], he migrated to top European leagues, starring in Turkey, Belgium, and [[spain]]. His goalscoring exploits propelled Canada to qualify for the 2022 FIFA World Cup, ending a thirty-six-year drought and spearheading a golden era for Canadian soccer.

## you gotta know

- Stands as the all-time leading goalscorer for the *Canada* men's national soccer team, eclipsing the previous mark established by *Dwayne De Rosario*.
- Finished as the overall top goalscorer across the entire *CONCACAF* qualification tournament for the 2022 FIFA World Cup, netting thirteen goals to lead Canada to Qatar.
- Selected first overall in the 2015 *MLS SuperDraft* by *Orlando City SC* in the [[united-states]], winning MLS Rookie of the Year after scoring seventeen goals in his debut season.
- Forged a potent international attacking partnership with forward *Jonathan David*, leading a renaissance in Canadian international football alongside winger *Alphonso Davies*.
- Captured a domestic double with Turkish powerhouse *Besiktas* during the 2020–21 campaign, scoring over thirty career goals for the Istanbul club.
- Established himself in *La Liga* in [[spain]], scoring crucial goals during stints with *Real Valladolid* and *RCD Mallorca*.
- Developed his collegiate game with the *UConn Huskies*, earning back-to-back American Athletic Conference honors before entering professional soccer.
- Provided a soccer scoring benchmark for his home country comparable to international icons like [[cristiano-ronaldo]], helping elevate the sport in a nation historically synonymous with [[ice-hockey]].

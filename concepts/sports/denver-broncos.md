## summary

The *Denver Broncos* are a professional American football franchise based in Denver, Colorado, competing in the *National Football League*. Founded in 1960 as part of the American Football League before joining the NFL in 1970, they emerged as a major force in the 1980s and achieved their greatest success in the late 1990s and early 2010s. The Broncos won their first *Super Bowl* in 1998 and their second in the 2015 season with a legendary defense.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1960 as an AFL team, joining the NFL in 1970 when the leagues merged; one of the oldest existing NFL franchises.
- Won consecutive Super Bowls (XXXII and XXXIII) in the 1998 and 1999 seasons with quarterback *John Elway*, establishing the franchise's first dynasty.
- In 2015, with quarterback *Peyton Manning*, the Broncos won Super Bowl 50 with what many consider one of the greatest defenses in NFL history.
- Quarterback *Peyton Manning* joined the Broncos after his neck injury and early retirement from the *Indianapolis Colts*, leading a remarkable resurgence at age 39.
- Russell Wilson became the Broncos' quarterback in 2022, attempting to build a new era of success following Peyton Manning's retirement.
- Play their home games at Empower Field at Mile High, formerly known as Mile High Stadium, at high altitude in Denver (the "Mile High City").
- The franchise has consistently fielded competitive teams throughout its history, earning one of the NFL's higher win percentages overall.
- *John Elway*, after his playing career, became the Broncos' executive vice president of football operations, shaping the franchise's strategy and talent acquisition.
- The 2015 Super Bowl 50 defense featured dominant pass-rushers and was led by *Von Miller*, who won the Super Bowl MVP award with a legendary performance.
- The Broncos' altitude advantage at Mile High Stadium has been a traditional home-field benefit, with visiting teams struggling to adapt to Denver's thin air.
- The franchise developed a strong rivalry with the Kansas City Chiefs and Pittsburgh Steelers, creating heated AFC West and divisional playoff matchups.
- Denver's sports culture embraced the Broncos as central to civic identity, with parade celebrations for championship runs drawing hundreds of thousands of fans.

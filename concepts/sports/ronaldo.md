## summary

**Ronaldo**, born *Ronaldo Luís Nazário de Lima* and widely celebrated as **Ronaldo Nazário** or **O Fenômeno**, is a Brazilian former professional footballer universally acclaimed as one of the greatest strikers in the history of the sport. During the 1990s and 2000s, he revolutionized the traditional center-forward role by marrying blistering sprint speed, dazzling dribbling, and clinical two-footed finishing. A two-time *FIFA World Cup* champion and two-time recipient of the *Ballon d'Or*, he achieved enduring global prominence while starring for premier European clubs in *Spain* and *Italy*.

## you gotta know

- Led the Brazil national football team to victory at the 2002 *FIFA World Cup* in South Korea and Japan, claiming the Golden Boot with eight goals, including both goals in the final against Germany.
- Dubbed *O Fenômeno* by the Italian press while playing for *Inter Milan*, where his lightning step-overs, explosive acceleration, and technical wizardry confounded defenders throughout Serie A.
- Became the youngest recipient of the *FIFA World Player of the Year* award at age twenty in 1996, eventually winning the trophy three times alongside two *Ballon d'Or* awards in 1997 and 2002.
- Overcame catastrophic career-threatening patellar tendon ruptures in his right knee between 1999 and 2001, completing an arduous rehabilitation to return at the highest international level.
- Held the all-time individual scoring record in World Cup finals history with fifteen goals across four tournaments from 1994 to 2006, a benchmark later surpassed by Germany's *Miroslav Klose*.
- Starred across European club football for *Cruzeiro*, *PSV Eindhoven*, *Barcelona*, *Inter Milan*, *Real Madrid* as a marquee member of the *Galácticos*, and *AC Milan*, scoring over four hundred career goals.
- Suffered an unexplained convulsive seizure hours before the 1998 World Cup final against host nation *France*, starting the match while visibly unwell in an episode that sparked decades of debate.
- Popularized iconic attacking feints such as the *elastico* and the scissors step-over, before later transitioning to sports executive leadership as majority club owner of *Real Valladolid* and *Cruzeiro*.

## summary

The **Philadelphia Eagles**, colloquially known as **The Birds**, are a professional American football franchise based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, competing in the National Football League. Established in 1933 to replace the bankrupt Frankford Yellow Jackets, the club took its moniker in tribute to the Blue Eagle emblem of Franklin D. Roosevelt's National Recovery Administration. Celebrated for an intensely passionate fan base and a rich legacy of fierce defensive play and dynamic quarterbacks, the franchise claimed three pre-merger league crowns before capturing its first modern championship in Super Bowl LII.

## you gotta know

- Secured their first modern championship title by defeating the New England Patriots in Super Bowl LII, propelled by backup quarterback Nick Foles and the iconic trick play known as the *Philly Special*.
- Captured three pre-merger league championships in 1948, 1949, and 1960, with the 1960 victory representing the only playoff defeat ever handed to Green Bay Packers coach Vince Lombardi.
- Temporarily merged rosters with their intrastate rivals, the Pittsburgh Steelers, during the 1943 campaign due to wartime manpower shortages caused by *World War II*, competing under the unofficial portmanteau *Steagles*.
- Revitalized the career of mobile quarterback Michael Vick following his prison sentence, naming him the starter in 2010 where he produced a Comeback Player of the Year campaign.
- Maintained a storied and hostile rivalry within the NFC East division against the Dallas Cowboys, the New York Giants, and the Washington Commanders.
- Defined defensively by aggressive tacticians, notably Buddy Ryan and his ferocious *46 defense* in the late 1980s featuring Hall of Fame defensive end Reggie White.
- Adopted the fight song *Fly, Eagles Fly* and popularized the nearly unstoppable short-yardage quarterback sneak known nationally as the *Brotherly Shove* or *Tush Push*.

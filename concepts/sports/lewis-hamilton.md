## summary

**Lewis Hamilton**, formally **Sir Lewis Hamilton**, is a British racing driver who stands as the most successful competitor in Formula One history. Hailing from *Stevenage*, *Hertfordshire*, he broke racial barriers as the championship's first Black driver before capturing a record-tying seven World Drivers' Championships. His career with *McLaren* and *Mercedes-AMG* established all-time records for pole positions, podium finishes, and race victories.

## you gotta know

- Entered Formula One in 2007 with *McLaren*, finishing runner-up in his debut championship before winning his maiden title dramatically on the final lap in 2008.
- Secured six World Drivers' Championships in seven seasons between 2014 and 2020 during a period of engine supremacy with *Mercedes-AMG*.
- Holds the all-time Formula One records for total race victories and pole positions, having surpassed the century mark in both categories.
- Tied the legendary German driver *Michael Schumacher* for the most World Drivers' Championships in series history with seven titles.
- Knighted in the 2021 New Year Honours in [[england]] for his services to motorsport and societal activism.
- Established *Mission 44* and the *Hamilton Commission* to expand diversity, STEM engagement, and opportunities for underrepresented youth in motorsport.
- Announced a landmark career transition to join *Scuderia Ferrari* for the 2025 racing season after over a decade with Mercedes.

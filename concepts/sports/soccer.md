## summary

**Soccer**, officially designated as **association football** and known across the majority of the globe simply as **football**, is a team sport played between two teams of eleven players on a rectangular grass or turf pitch. First codified in nineteenth-century [[england]], the objective of the game is to maneuver a spherical ball across the opposing goal line using primarily the feet, head, and torso, with hand contact strictly prohibited for all outfield players. Governed globally by FIFA and featured at the Summer [[olympic-games]], it stands as the most popular, widely played, and commercially prominent athletic competition in human history.

## you gotta know

- Standardized into modern athletic form in [[england]] in 1863 when representatives formed The Football Association, drafting the initial unified rules and banning handling and shin-hacking.
- Acquired the colloquial name "soccer" in late nineteenth-century Britain as an Oxford-style abbreviation of "Association," used to distinguish the sport from rugby football before spreading to the [[united-states]] and other territories.
- Features the quadrennial FIFA World Cup as its pinnacle international tournament, drawing billions of television viewers and standing as the most watched sporting event in the world.
- Inspired the historic Christmas Truce of 1914 during [[world-war-i]], in which British and German soldiers climbed out of their trenches into No Man's Land to share food and play informal matches.
- Hosted at prominent American sports venues, notably Chicago's [[soldier-field]], which served as the backdrop for the opening match of the 1994 FIFA World Cup.
- Serves as one of the largest and most widely participated international team sports in the humanitarian [[special-olympics]] movement.
- Regulated by the International Football Association Board through the seventeen fundamental Laws of the Game, governing pitch dimensions, fouls, misconduct, and the tactical offside law.
- Shaped by iconic global figures such as *Pelé*, *Diego Maradona*, *Lionel Messi*, and *Cristiano Ronaldo*, whose individual brilliance transformed the game into an international cultural phenomenon.

## connections

- [[england]] — the birthplace where modern association rules and the original Football Association were instituted in 1863.
- [[olympic-games]] — the international multi-sport competition where association football has been contested as an official medal event since 1900.
- [[soldier-field]] — the historic stadium in Chicago that hosted the opening ceremonies and competitive fixtures during the 1994 World Cup.
- [[special-olympics]] — the global athletic movement in which football represents one of the most prominent and widely played team sports.
- [[world-war-i]] — the global war during which soldiers paused combat to play spontaneous football matches during the 1914 Christmas Truce.
- [[british-empire]] — the global commercial and diplomatic network that facilitated the rapid spread of the sport across South America, continental Europe, and Asia.
- [[france]] — the country where global governing body FIFA was established in Paris in 1904 and where *Jules Rimet* spearheaded the World Cup.
- [[spain]] — a major European soccer center renowned for dominant domestic clubs and winning the 2010 FIFA World Cup.

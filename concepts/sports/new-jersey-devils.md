## summary

**New Jersey Devils** is an American professional *ice hockey* franchise based in Newark, New Jersey, competing in the Metropolitan Division of the *National Hockey League*. Relocated to the Garden State in 1982 after earlier stints as the *Kansas City Scouts* and *Colorado Rockies*, the club evolved from a struggling expansion outfit into one of the league's preeminent defensive powerhouses. Under the meticulous stewardship of general manager *Lou Lamoriello*, the team captured three *Stanley Cup* titles between 1995 and 2003. Known for perfecting the suffocating neutral zone trap and backstopped by Hall of Fame goaltender *Martin Brodeur*, the franchise defined an era of tactical discipline in modern hockey.

## you gotta know

- Captured three [[stanley-cup]] titles in 1995, 2000, and 2003, establishing a decade-long dynasty of disciplined team defense and tactical structure.
- Backstopped for over two decades by legendary goaltender *Martin Brodeur*, who retired with *NHL* records for most career wins, shutouts, and playoff shutouts.
- Relocated to East Rutherford, New Jersey, in 1982 after operating as the *Kansas City Scouts* from 1974 to 1976 and the *Colorado Rockies* from 1976 to 1982.
- Infamously dubbed a "Mickey Mouse organization" by superstar *Wayne Gretzky* in November 1983 after his *Edmonton Oilers* routed the fledgling team 13–4.
- Revolutionized defensive play across [[ice-hockey]] under head coach *Jacques Lemaire* by mastering the "neutral zone trap," a system designed to choke off opposing rushes.
- Anchored defensively by Hall of Fame defenseman and longtime captain *Scott Stevens*, famed for his bone-crushing open-ice checks, alongside smooth-skating blueliner *Scott Niedermayer*.
- Contested the fierce "Battle of the Hudson River" against the neighboring *New York Rangers*, punctuated by the legendary double-overtime Game 7 in the 1994 conference finals.
- Relocated from the Meadowlands Arena in East Rutherford to their current home at the Prudential Center in Newark in 2007.

## summary

**Brooks Koepka** is an American professional golfer known for his extraordinary composure, power, and clutch play in men's major championships. Rising to global prominence in the late 2010s, he achieved the rare feat of winning four major titles in an eight-major stretch between 2017 and 2019, including consecutive victories at both the [[us-open]] and the PGA Championship. A former world number one in the Official World Golf Ranking, he became the first active LIV Golf player to win a major championship by capturing his third PGA Championship in 2023.

## you gotta know

- Captured back-to-back [[us-open]] championships in 2017 at *Erin Hills* and 2018 at *Shinnecock Hills*, becoming the first male golfer to successfully defend the national championship since *Curtis Strange* in 1989.
- Won the PGA Championship in consecutive years in 2018 at *Bellerive Country Club* and 2019 at *Bethpage Black*, joining *Tiger Woods* as the only players in the stroke-play era to win back-to-back PGA Championships.
- Claimed his fifth career major championship at the 2023 PGA Championship at *Oak Hill Country Club*, making him the third golfer in modern history alongside *Jack Nicklaus* and *Walter Hagen* to win the tournament three or more times.
- Known for an unusual developmental route to golf stardom, bypassing early PGA Tour status to compete on the *European Challenge Tour* and *European Tour*, winning events across Kenya, Scotland, Spain, and Turkey to forge his mental toughness.
- Reached world number one in the Official World Golf Ranking in October 2018 after winning the *CJ Cup*, maintaining the top ranking across multiple stretches totaling forty-seven weeks.
- Fostered a widely publicized rivalry with fellow major champion *Bryson DeChambeau*, marked by social media exchanges, slow-play debates, and an eventual head-to-head exhibition match in *The Match* series.
- Signed with the breakaway *LIV Golf* league in June 2022, captaining *Smash GC* and demonstrating the league's competitive relevance by winning the 2023 PGA Championship and finishing runner-up at the 2023 Masters Tournament.
- Competed for the [[united-states]] across multiple *Ryder Cup* and *Presidents Cup* teams, establishing a reputation as an intimidating match-play competitor with immense driving distance and unflappable pressure putting.

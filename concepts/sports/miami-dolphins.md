## summary

The **Miami Dolphins** are a professional American football franchise based in the Miami metropolitan area, competing in the American Football Conference East division of the [[national-football-league]]. Founded by lawyer-politician *Joe Robbie* and actor *Danny Thomas*, the team began play in 1966 as an expansion team in the American Football League before joining the merged league in 1970. The franchise is most celebrated for achieving the only undefeated and untied season in modern professional football history, capturing back-to-back [[super-bowl]] titles in the early 1970s and later showcasing one of the most prolific passing offenses in history.

## you gotta know

- The 1972 squad achieved perfection by finishing with a 14–0 regular-season record and winning three postseason games to culminate in victory at [[super-bowl]] VII, standing as the only team in [[national-football-league]] history to complete an entire season and postseason undefeated.
- Hall of Fame head coach *Don Shula* led the franchise from 1970 to 1995, orchestrating back-to-back championship victories in [[super-bowl]] VII and [[super-bowl]] VIII and retiring with 347 total career victories, the most in league history.
- Legendary quarterback *Dan Marino* was drafted twenty-seventh overall in 1983 and revolutionized offensive football by throwing for a then-unfathomable 5,084 passing yards and 48 touchdowns during his 1984 MVP campaign.
- The 1970s championship defense was dubbed the *No-Name Defense* by head coach *Don Shula* and the media because it lacked flamboyant superstars, relying instead on cohesive team execution led by linebacker *Nick Buoniconti* and defensive tackle *Manny Fernandez*.
- The team played its home games at the historic *Orange Bowl* in Miami until moving north in 1987 to *Joe Robbie Stadium* (now *Hard Rock Stadium*) in Miami Gardens, which has hosted numerous title games.
- The franchise maintained one of the most storied rivalries in sports with the *Buffalo Bills*, particularly during the 1990s, and battled rivals like the [[san-diego-chargers]] in classic postseason shootouts like the 1982 *Epic in Miami*.
- The running attack of the early 1970s was anchored by fullback *Larry Csonka* and halfback *Mercury Morris*, who became the first pair of teammates in league history to each rush for over 1,000 yards in the same season during 1972.

## connections

- [[national-football-league]] — premier American professional gridiron organization in which the franchise has competed since the 1970 AFL-NFL merger.
- [[super-bowl]] — annual championship showcase won by the franchise in consecutive appearances at VII and VIII.
- [[san-diego-chargers]] — historic playoff adversary who participated in the famed overtime postseason contest known as the *Epic in Miami* in January 1982.
- [[tom-brady]] — longtime divisional rival quarterback for the *New England Patriots* whom the franchise frequently upset in South Florida.
- [[peyton-manning]] — AFC quarterback adversary against whom the franchise frequently competed during his tenure with the *Indianapolis Colts*.
- [[drew-brees]] — standout quarterback who famously almost signed with the franchise in free agency in 2006 before joining the *New Orleans Saints*.
- [[united-states]] — North American nation where the franchise operates as Florida's oldest continuous major professional sports team.

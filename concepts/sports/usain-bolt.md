## summary

**Usain Bolt** is a retired Jamaican sprinter widely celebrated as the greatest sprinter in the history of *track and field*. An eight-time Olympic gold medalist, he dominated international sprinting across three consecutive editions of the summer *Olympic Games* from 2008 to 2016. He holds the world records in the 100 meters, 200 meters, and 4x100 meters relay, captivating global sports audiences with his unprecedented speed, towering stature, and iconic celebratory pose.

## you gotta know

- Achieved historic "triple-double" triumphs by sweeping gold medals in the 100-meter and 200-meter sprints across three consecutive *Olympic Games* (Beijing 2008, London 2012, and Rio 2016).
- Set enduring world records of 9.58 seconds in the 100 meters and 19.19 seconds in the 200 meters at the 2009 *World Athletics Championships* in Berlin, *Germany*.
- Famous for his signature celebratory gesture, nicknamed the "To Di World" or "Lightning Bolt" pose, extending his arms dramatically toward the sky.
- Shattered long-held sprinting dogmas regarding biomechanics, demonstrating that his 6-foot-5-inch frame could combine exceptional stride frequency with immense stride length.
- Won eleven World Championship gold medals between 2009 and 2015, solidifying his status as the most successful male athlete in the history of the championships.
- Captured global attention at the 2008 Beijing Olympics when he set a 100-meter world record of 9.69 seconds while famously slowing down and celebrating before crossing the finish line.
- Competed alongside Jamaican sprint teammates including *Yohan Blake*, *Asafa Powell*, and *Nesta Carter* to dominate global relay competition for nearly a decade.

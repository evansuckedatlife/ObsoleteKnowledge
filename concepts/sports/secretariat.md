## summary

**Secretariat**, affectionately known as *Big Red*, was an American Thoroughbred racehorse who became the ninth winner of the American Triple Crown in 1973. Trained by *Lucien Laurin* and ridden by jockey *Ron Turcotte*, he shattered track records in all three legs of the series that remain unbroken after half a century. His iconic thirty-one-length victory in the Belmont Stakes is widely celebrated as one of the most sublime individual athletic performances ever witnessed.

## you gotta know

- Swept the Kentucky Derby, the Preakness Stakes, and the Belmont Stakes in 1973 to break a twenty-five-year Triple Crown drought.
- Won the 1973 Belmont Stakes by a staggering thirty-one lengths while stopping the clock at 2:24 flat, a world record on dirt for 1½ miles.
- Accelerated throughout the Kentucky Derby by running every subsequent quarter-mile fraction faster than the previous one, finishing in under two minutes.
- Named American Horse of the Year as a two-year-old in 1972, a rare feat for a juvenile racehorse in modern turf history.
- Sired by *Bold Ruler* out of *Somethingroyal* at Meadow Stable in Doswell, Virginia, owned by *Penny Chenery*.
- Post-mortem examination by necropsy revealed an extraordinarily enlarged heart weighing approximately twenty-two pounds, nearly three times normal equine size.

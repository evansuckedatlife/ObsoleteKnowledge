## summary

**Alphonso Davies**, widely known by the nickname **Phonzie**, is a Canadian professional soccer player celebrated as one of the most explosive left-backs and wingers of his generation. Rising from humble origins as a child refugee, he achieved global stardom with German powerhouse Bayern Munich, where his blistering pace and tactical versatility helped secure a continental treble in 2020. As the talisman of the Canada men's national team, he propelled his country back to the FIFA World Cup stage for the first time in thirty-six years.

## you gotta know

- Born on 2 November 2000 in the Buduburam refugee camp in Ghana to Liberian parents who had fled the Second Liberian Civil War, before immigrating to Windsor, Ontario and settling in Edmonton, Alberta when he was five years old.
- Began his professional club career with the Vancouver Whitecaps FC in Major League Soccer, making his senior debut at age fifteen and becoming one of the most coveted young talents in North American soccer history.
- Completed a then-record transfer from Major League Soccer to Bayern Munich in July 2018 for a base fee of over thirteen million dollars, subsequently developing under Hansi Flick from an attacking winger into a world-class attacking fullback.
- Played a pivotal role in Bayern Munich's historic 2019–20 sextuple season, memorably dismantling Barcelona in an 8-2 UEFA Champions League quarter-final victory that featured a viral, baseline-dribbling assist to Joshua Kimmich.
- Became the first Canadian international to win the men's UEFA Champions League, earning inclusion in the FIFA FIFPRO Men's World 11 in 2020.
- Scored the first goal in Canada men's national soccer team history at a FIFA World Cup, heading home sixty-eight seconds into their group stage encounter against Croatia at Qatar in 2022.
- Served as a Global Goodwill Ambassador for the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), becoming the first soccer player to hold the prestigious humanitarian post.

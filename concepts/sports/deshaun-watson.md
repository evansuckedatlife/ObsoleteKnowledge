## summary

**Deshaun Watson** is an American football quarterback who attained national stardom at *Clemson* before being drafted into the *[[national-football-league]]*. Celebrated for his exceptional athletic mobility, arm strength, and improvisational pocket playmaking, he secured multiple Pro Bowl selections with the *Houston Texans* and led the league in passing yards during the 2020 campaign. His professional career subsequently became overshadowed by unprecedented legal controversies, extensive league suspensions stemming from numerous sexual misconduct allegations, and high-profile contract scrutiny.

## you gotta know

- Led the *Clemson Tigers* to back-to-back national title appearances under head coach *Dabo Swinney*, culminating in a victory over the *Alabama Crimson Tide* in the 2017 College Football Playoff National Championship game where he threw the game-winning two-yard touchdown pass to *Hunter Renfrow* with one second remaining.
- Finalist for the *Heisman Trophy* in both 2015 and 2016, claiming the *Davey O'Brien Award*, the *Manning Award*, and the *Johnny Unitas Golden Arm Award* as the premier collegiate quarterback in the *[[united-states]]*.
- Selected twelfth overall in the 2017 NFL Draft by the *Houston Texans* after the team traded up to secure him, quickly posting nineteen passing touchdowns in seven games as a rookie before a torn anterior cruciate ligament prematurely ended his debut campaign.
- Earned three consecutive Pro Bowl selections from 2018 to 2020 while guiding Houston to consecutive AFC South division titles, engineering memorable fourth-quarter comebacks including an overtime playoff victory against the *Buffalo Bills*.
- Led the *[[national-football-league]]* in total passing yardage in 2020 with 4,823 yards, thirty-three touchdowns, and a 70.2 percent completion rate, becoming one of the few quarterbacks in league history to lead the NFL in passing on a team with fewer than five victories.
- Sat out the entire 2021 season after requesting a trade from Houston and subsequently facing more than two dozen civil lawsuits alleging coercive sexual behavior and sexual assault during personal massage sessions.
- Acquired by the *Cleveland Browns* in March 2022 in a blockbuster trade involving three first-round draft selections, immediately receiving an unprecedented fully guaranteed five-year contract worth $230 million.
- Received an eleven-game suspension, a record $5 million fine, and mandatory professional evaluation from the *[[national-football-league]]* in 2022 following a settlement over violations of the personal conduct policy, returning to face severe struggles, recurring shoulder injuries, and an Achilles tear.

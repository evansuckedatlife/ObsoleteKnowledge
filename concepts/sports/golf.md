## summary

**Golf** is an individual sport played over a course of 9 or 18 holes, where players use clubs to strike a ball into a hole in the fewest strokes possible. Originating in Scotland in the 15th century, golf evolved into an international pastime and professional sport of immense commercial and cultural significance. The sport combines athleticism, strategy, and mental fortitude, with major championships attracting global audiences and producing legendary competitors.

## you gotta know

- A regulation golf course has 18 holes, each with a designated par (standard number of strokes for a skilled golfer to complete the hole).
- Players compete individually to shoot the lowest score over the course; in professional tournaments, the lowest cumulative score wins.
- The major championships in professional golf are the *Masters*, the *U.S. Open*, *The Open Championship* (British Open), and the *PGA Championship*.
- *Tiger Woods* revolutionized golf in the late 1990s and 2000s, becoming the first truly global celebrity in the sport and achieving 15 major victories.
- Golf professionalized dramatically in the 20th century through television broadcasts, sponsorships, and the establishment of a global professional tour circuit.
- The sport has traditionally emphasized etiquette, fairness, and integrity; golfers are largely responsible for enforcing rules and calling penalties on themselves.
- Unlike team sports, golf relies entirely on individual skill, mental discipline, and the ability to perform under pressure in front of large galleries.
- A golf score of one under par for a hole is called a "birdie," two under a "eagle," three under an "albatross" or "double eagle"—terminology reflecting the sport's whimsical traditions.
- Professional golf players compete on organized tours like the PGA Tour, European Tour, and others, with earnings from tournament purses and sponsorships creating a multi-billion dollar industry.
- Handicap systems allow golfers of different skill levels to compete fairly by adjusting scores based on course difficulty and player ability.
- Women's professional golf has produced legendary players like *Patty Berg*, *Mickey Wright*, and *Annika Sorenstam*, establishing parallel competitive traditions.
- Golf courses vary dramatically in design and difficulty, with championship courses requiring precision and strategic shot-making distinct from recreational play.

## summary

**Bianca Andreescu** is a Canadian professional tennis player who achieved international fame by winning the women's singles title at the 2019 US Open. Defeating twenty-three-time Grand Slam champion *Serena Williams* in the final, she became the first Canadian singles player to win a Grand Slam tournament and the first teenager to win a major singles title since 2006. Renowned for her versatile shot-making, tactical variety, and aggressive baseline play, she reached a career-high ranking of world number four.

## you gotta know

- Captured the 2019 US Open women's singles championship as a nineteen-year-old by defeating *Serena Williams* in straight sets at Arthur Ashe Stadium.
- Became the first Canadian player in tennis history to claim a Grand Slam singles championship across both the men's and women's circuits.
- Won the 2019 Indian Wells Open as an unseeded wild-card entrant, defeating *Angelique Kerber* in an acclaimed three-set final.
- Claimed the 2019 Canadian Open title in Toronto after *Serena Williams* was forced to retire in the opening set due to back spasms.
- Gained widespread renown for her aggressive, multi-faceted style characterized by heavy topspin, slice variations, drop shots, and sudden pace changes.
- Honored with the 2019 Lou Marsh Trophy as Canada's top athlete of the year across all sports following her historic major triumph.
- Overcame multiple injury setbacks, including serious shoulder and knee ailments, to reach the mixed doubles final at the 2023 French Open.

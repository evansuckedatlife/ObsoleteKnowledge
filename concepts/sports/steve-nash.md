## summary

**Steve Nash** is a Canadian former professional basketball player and coach who revolutionized offensive play in the National Basketball Association as a visionary point guard. Celebrated for his elite playmaking, precision three-point shooting, and leadership of the *Phoenix Suns*' uptempo "Seven Seconds or Less" offense, he claimed consecutive NBA Most Valuable Player awards in 2005 and 2006. A basketball hall of famer and international icon, he remains one of the most influential floor generals and shooters in modern sports history.

## you gotta know

- Won back-to-back NBA Most Valuable Player awards in 2005 and 2006 with the *Phoenix Suns*, becoming only the second point guard after *Magic Johnson* to achieve consecutive MVPs.
- Orchestrated head coach *Mike D'Antoni*'s revolutionary "Seven Seconds or Less" offense, an innovative spaced-floor attack that anticipated the modern NBA's pace-and-space era.
- Member of the exclusive 50–40–90 club for shooting efficiency (50% field goals, 40% three-pointers, 90% free throws) an NBA-record four separate seasons.
- Led the Canadian national men's basketball team to a surprising quarterfinal appearance at the 2000 Sydney *[[olympic-games]]*, earning tournament MVP honors.
- Starred collegiately at *Santa Clara University*, leading the fifteenth-seeded Broncos to a famous upset over second-seeded *Arizona* in the 1993 NCAA tournament.
- Formed a prolific pick-and-roll partnership with *Dirk Nowitzki* on the *Dallas Mavericks* before returning to Phoenix as a free agent in 2004.
- Served as head coach of the *Brooklyn Nets* in *[[new-york-city]]* from 2020 to 2022, guiding superstar rosters during turbulent postseason campaigns.

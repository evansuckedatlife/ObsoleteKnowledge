## summary

The **Jumpman** is a globally recognized silhouette logo owned by Nike that represents the Air Jordan brand and the athletic legacy of basketball superstar *Michael Jordan*. First appearing on basketball footwear in 1988, the design depicts an airborne figure performing a balletic grand jeté toward the basket while clutching a basketball in his extended left hand. The mark transformed modern sports apparel into high fashion and established footwear marketing as a dominant force within contemporary global pop culture.

## you gotta know

- Designed by iconic sneaker architect *Tinker Hatfield* for the release of the Air Jordan III in 1988, replacing the earlier winged-basketball emblem of the Air Jordan line.
- Derived from a 1984 promotional photoshoot for *Life* magazine prior to the 1984 Summer [[olympic-games]], in which *Michael Jordan* performed a staged, soaring ballet movement rather than an actual basketball dunk.
- Successfully persuaded *Michael Jordan* to remain with Nike during pivotal contract negotiations in 1987, largely because *Tinker Hatfield* incorporated the logo alongside elephant print and visible air technology.
- Became the subject of an unsuccessful copyright infringement lawsuit filed by photographer *Jacobus Rentmeester*, who claimed Nike's stylized silhouette infringed upon his original 1984 photograph.
- Expanded beyond basketball sneakers to sponsor collegiate athletic programs across the [[united-states]] as well as elite European football clubs such as Paris Saint-Germain.
- Transitioned into hip-hop vernacular and popular music, serving as the title for a multi-platinum 2015 collaboration by *Drake* and *Future*.
- Generated billions of dollars in annual revenue, fueling Nike's Jordan Brand as a largely autonomous division that fundamentally redefined global urban streetwear.

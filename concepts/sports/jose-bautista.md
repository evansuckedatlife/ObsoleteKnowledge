## summary

**Jose Bautista** is a Dominican former professional baseball outfielder and third baseman who became one of the most feared power hitters of his generation in Major League Baseball. Best known for his tenure with the Toronto Blue Jays, he led the American League in home runs twice and earned six consecutive All-Star selections. Bautista is immortalized in baseball lore for his iconic, emphatic bat flip following a go-ahead home run in the seventh inning of Game 5 of the 2015 American League Division Series.

## you gotta know

- Hit an astonishing, franchise-record 54 home runs in 2010 after transforming his swing timing and leg kick under hitting coach Dwayne Murphy, leading all of Major League Baseball and winning the Hank Aaron Award.
- Famously executed the legendary bat flip after crushing a tie-breaking three-run home run off *Sam Dyson* in Game 5 of the 2015 ALDS against the Texas Rangers, capping a chaotic 53-minute seventh inning.
- Led the American League in home runs for a second consecutive season in 2011 with 43 home runs while posting an extraordinary 1.056 on-base plus slugging percentage.
- Was punched squarely in the jaw by second baseman *Rougned Odor* during a notorious bench-clearing brawl between the Blue Jays and Texas Rangers in May 2016.
- Began his professional career as a journeyman utility player, famously playing for four different franchises in the 2004 season alone: Baltimore Orioles, Tampa Bay Devil Rays, Kansas City Royals, and Pittsburgh Pirates.
- Represented the Dominican Republic national baseball team as an outfielder and first baseman at the Tokyo 2020 [[olympic-games]], earning a bronze medal late in his career.
- Inducted into the Toronto Blue Jays Level of Excellence in August 2023, cementing his enduring legacy alongside the franchise's all-time greats.

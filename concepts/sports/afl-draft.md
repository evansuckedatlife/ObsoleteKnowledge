## summary

The **AFL Draft**, officially the **American Football League Draft**, was the annual selection process conducted by the American Football League from late 1959 through 1966 to distribute incoming collegiate talent among its member franchises. Serving as the primary battleground in the bitter competition against the older [[national-football-league]], the draft produced relentless financial bidding wars and aggressive recruiting tactics across the [[united-states]]. The spiraling costs of signing top collegiate stars directly pressured team owners into negotiations that culminated in the landmark [[afl-nfl-merger]] and the establishment of a unified common draft in 1967.

## you gotta know

- Inaugural player selection convened via telephone conference in November 1959, lasting thirty-three rounds without formal scouting systems and picking players before collegiate bowl games had concluded.
- Sparked elaborate "babysitting" operations, where team representatives sequestered top collegiate athletes in undisclosed hotel rooms to prevent rival NFL scouts from signing them to contracts.
- First overall selection in the inaugural 1960 draft was Heisman Trophy winner *Billy Cannon*, who signed with the *Houston Oilers* despite having signed with the NFL *Los Angeles Rams*, a contract dispute resolved in federal court in the AFL's favor.
- The *Dallas Texans* made history in 1963 by selecting defensive tackle *Buck Buchanan* with the first overall pick, making him the first player from a historically Black college and university to be selected first overall in professional football.
- Shifted the balance of sporting power in 1965 when the *New York Jets* signed University of Alabama quarterback *Joe Namath* to a record-breaking $427,000 contract after selecting him first overall.
- Provided cornerstone rosters for the [[los-angeles-chargers]] and [[san-diego-chargers]], securing future hall-of-fame talents such as wide receiver *Lance Alworth* who opted for the AFL over rival NFL suitors.
- Ceased independent operations following the November 1966 draft, after which the [[afl-nfl-merger]] agreement merged talent procurement into a single common draft beginning in March 1967.

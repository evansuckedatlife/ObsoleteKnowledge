## summary

**O. J. Simpson**, born **Orenthal James Simpson** and famously nicknamed **The Juice**, was an American football running back, actor, and broadcaster who became the center of the most publicized criminal trial in modern American history. A collegiate national champion at USC and a record-shattering professional running back for the Buffalo Bills, he smoothly transitioned into commercial endorsements and Hollywood acting. His cultural image was permanently altered in 1994 following the murders of Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman, resulting in an internationally televised trial that deeply divided the nation along racial lines.

## you gotta know

- Became the first player in professional football history to rush for over 2,000 yards in a single regular season, achieving 2,003 yards in 1973 over just 14 games with the Buffalo Bills.
- Awarded the 1968 Heisman Trophy while playing for the USC Trojans, highlighted by his game-winning 64-yard touchdown run against archrival UCLA in 1967.
- Inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1985 after a celebrated career that included five first-team All-Pro selections and four league rushing titles.
- Transitioned into mainstream commercial stardom as a spokesman for Hertz car rentals and pursued a film acting career, notably co-starring as Detective Nordberg in *The Naked Gun* comedy franchise.
- Broadcast live to an audience of ninety-five million Americans during a slow-speed highway pursuit across Los Angeles in a white Ford Bronco driven by friend Al Cowlings in June 1994.
- Acquitted of the double murder of his former wife Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman in October 1995 following an eleven-month trial dubbed the *Trial of the Century*.
- Found liable for their deaths in a 1997 civil lawsuit that ordered him to pay $33.5 million to the victims' families, and was later imprisoned in Nevada from 2008 to 2017 for armed robbery and kidnapping.

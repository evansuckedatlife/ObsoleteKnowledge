## summary

**Tonya Harding** (born **Tonya Maxene Harding**) is an American former competitive figure skater who captured the 1991 *United States* national championship and won a world championship silver medal before becoming the central figure in one of the most explosive scandals in athletic history. Hailing from a working-class background in *Portland*, *Oregon*, she gained widespread acclaim as an exceptionally powerful jumper who broke technical barriers in women's figure skating. Her competitive career ended in disgrace in 1994 following a violent assault on rival skater *Nancy Kerrigan* orchestrated by Harding's associates, leading to a media sensation and a lifetime ban from the sport.

## you gotta know

- Landed a historic triple Axel jump at the 1991 U.S. Figure Skating Championships in *Minneapolis*, becoming the first American woman and only the second woman in history, following Japan's *Midori Ito*, to complete the grueling three-and-a-half-revolution jump in international competition.
- Became indelibly linked to the January 6, 1994 attack on chief American rival *Nancy Kerrigan* at Cobo Arena in *Detroit*, where assailant *Shane Stant* struck Kerrigan's right leg with a collapsible metal baton in a plot conceived by Harding's ex-husband *Jeff Gillooly* and bodyguard *Shawn Eckardt*.
- Competed under extraordinary media scrutiny at [[olympic-games]] in 1994 in *Lillehammer*, where she famously broke down in tears before the judging panel due to a broken skate lace, was granted a re-skate, and finished in eighth place while Kerrigan captured the silver medal.
- Pleaded guilty in March 1994 to a felony count of conspiracy to hinder prosecution, agreeing to three years of probation, five hundred hours of community service, and a heavy fine to avoid prison time for her role in concealing details of the attack.
- Stripped of her 1994 national title and permanently banned for life from the *United States Figure Skating Association* (*USFSA*) in June 1994 following a disciplinary panel's determination that she had prior knowledge of the assault plot.
- Re-entered popular culture through the acclaimed 2017 biographical feature film *I, Tonya*, starring *Margot Robbie*, which critically re-examined Harding's impoverished upbringing, domestic abuse, and the intense class snobbery pervasive in elite American figure skating.
- Pivoted to novelty entertainment and professional celebrity boxing during the early 2000s, fighting televised exhibition matches against opponents including *Paula Jones* after being legally excluded from coaching or competing in sanctioned ice sports.

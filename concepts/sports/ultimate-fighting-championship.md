## summary

The **Ultimate Fighting Championship**, widely known as the **UFC**, is the world's premier mixed martial arts (MMA) promotion company, headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada. Founded in 1993, it originally sought to determine the single most effective martial art in an open-weight, minimal-rules tournament format. Under modern corporate ownership and the adoption of the *Unified Rules of Mixed Martial Arts*, the UFC evolved into a multi-billion-dollar global sports powerhouse that transformed combat athletics into a mainstream discipline.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in Denver, Colorado in November 1993 with *UFC 1*, co-founded by *Art Davie*, *Bob Meyrowitz*, and Brazilian jiu-jitsu master *Rorion Gracie*.
- Brazilian fighter *Royce Gracie* won three of the first four single-elimination tournaments, demonstrating the overwhelming efficacy of Brazilian jiu-jitsu and submission grappling over larger pure striking stylists.
- Fought inside a signature eight-sided fenced enclosure known as the *Octagon*, which became a globally recognized trademark of the promotion.
- Faced intense political backlash in the mid-1990s, famously criticized by US Senator *John McCain* as "human cockfighting," leading to widespread bans and near financial collapse.
- Purchased in 2001 by *Dana White* and casino executives *Frank Fertitta III* and *Lorenzo Fertitta* (Zuffa LLC), who stabilized the organization through strict regulatory sanctioning and the *Unified Rules of Mixed Martial Arts*.
- Exploded into American mainstream popularity in 2005 following the success of the reality television series *The Ultimate Fighter*, punctuated by the iconic bout between *Forrest Griffin* and *Stephan Bonnar*.
- Produced iconic crossover global combat sports superstars including *Conor McGregor*, *Ronda Rousey*, *Anderson Silva*, *Jon Jones*, and *Georges St-Pierre*.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — home base of the promotion, where it originated in Colorado and established its headquarters in Las Vegas.
- [[japan]] — prominent historical rival combat sports market that hosted major promotions such as *Pride Fighting Championships*, later acquired by Zuffa.
- [[ancient-greece]] — origin of pankration, the ancient Greek Olympic combat sport that served as an ideological predecessor to modern mixed martial arts.
- [[olympic-games]] — origin of many constituent Olympic combat sports practiced by UFC athletes, including freestyle wrestling, judo, and boxing.
- [[new-york-city]] — major legal battleground where MMA was banned until 2016, leading to the historic *UFC 205* event at Madison Square Garden.

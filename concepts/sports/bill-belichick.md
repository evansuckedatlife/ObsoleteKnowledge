## summary

**Bill Belichick** is an American football coach widely regarded as one of the greatest tacticians and defensive minds in the history of the *National Football League*. Serving as the head coach of the *New England Patriots* from 2000 to 2023, he orchestrated an unprecedented two-decade dynasty characterized by situational mastery and defensive adaptability. Paired with quarterback *Tom Brady*, he captured six *Super Bowl* titles as a head coach, establishing enduring standards for organizational efficiency and postseason execution across the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Won six *Super Bowl* championships (XXXVI, XXXVIII, XXXIX, XLIX, LI, LIII) as head coach of the *New England Patriots*, the most by any head coach in NFL history.
- Won two additional *Super Bowl* rings (XXI, XXV) serving as defensive coordinator for the *New York Giants* under head coach *Bill Parcells*, famously devising the defensive game plan against the high-powered *Buffalo Bills* in 1991 that was inducted into the *Pro Football Hall of Fame*.
- Formed the central pillar of the Patriots dynasty alongside quarterback [[tom-brady]], maintaining competitive dominance across two decades and recording an undefeated 16-0 regular season in 2007.
- Ranks second in all-time NFL head coaching victories behind only *Don Shula*, surpassing figures like *George Halas* in total regular-season and postseason wins.
- Garnered three AP NFL Coach of the Year awards (2003, 2007, 2010) while establishing a distinct team culture commonly dubbed the *Patriot Way*, centered on strict situational discipline and the motto "Do Your Job."
- Entangled in league controversies over competitive rules and filming protocols, notably the 2007 *Spygate* disciplinary investigation and the 2014–15 *Deflategate* AFC Championship dispute.
- Replaced the departing [[tom-brady]] in 2020 by signing former league MVP [[cam-newton]] as his starting quarterback during a transitional rebuilding season.
- Known for his deadpan press conferences, cutoff grey hooded sweatshirts, and extensive scholarship on football history, including deep ties to the *United States Naval Academy* where his father *Steve Belichick* coached.

## connections

- [[tom-brady]] — quarterback drafted in the sixth round in 2000 who became his primary on-field partner across six championship runs.
- [[super-bowl]] — premier championship stage where he made a record twelve total appearances as an assistant and head coach.
- [[national-football-league]] — professional league where he served as a coach for nearly five decades.
- [[peyton-manning]] — longtime rival quarterback whose legendary playoff battles against his defenses defined AFC competition.
- [[cam-newton]] — dual-threat quarterback who succeeded Brady as his starter in New England for the 2020 campaign.
- [[brett-favre]] — Hall of Fame quarterback who defeated his Patriots defense in Super Bowl XXXI while Belichick served as an assistant under Parcells.

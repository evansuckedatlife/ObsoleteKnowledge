## summary

**Henri Richard**, famously known as **The Pocket Rocket**, was a French-Canadian professional ice hockey centre who spent his entire twenty-season National Hockey League career with the Montreal Canadiens. Playing from 1955 to 1975, he won eleven Stanley Cup championships, the most by any player in NHL history. A tenacious playmaker and revered leader, Richard was elected to the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1979.

## you gotta know

- Holds the unmatched all-time NHL record of winning eleven *Stanley Cup* championships as an active player, all with the Montreal Canadiens.
- Earned the iconic nickname **The Pocket Rocket** because of his smaller five-foot-seven stature compared to his legendary older brother, Maurice *Rocket* Richard.
- Centered Montreal's dynastic teams, winning five consecutive Stanley Cups between 1956 and 1960 alongside his brother and stars like Jean Béliveau and Jacques Plante.
- Scored two famous Cup-clinching goals: the overtime winner against the Detroit Red Wings in Game 6 of the 1966 finals and the deciding third-period tally in Game 7 of the 1971 finals against the Chicago Black Hawks.
- Succeeded Jean Béliveau as team captain of the Montreal Canadiens in 1971, wearing the captain's "C" until his retirement in 1975.
- Amassed 1,046 total points across 1,258 regular season games, winning the Bill Masterton Memorial Trophy in 1974 for perseverance and sportsmanship.
- Had his uniform number 16 retired to the rafters of the Montreal Forum in 1975 before his subsequent Hall of Fame induction in 1979.

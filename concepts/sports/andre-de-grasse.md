## summary

**Andre De Grasse** is a Canadian Olympic champion sprinter who became one of the most consistent global championship medalists of his generation. Emerging onto the international stage in the mid-2010s, he achieved worldwide fame by dueling with *Usain Bolt* at the Rio Summer Games before capturing gold in the 200 metres at Tokyo in 2021. Known for his finishing kick in the short sprints and clutch championship performances, he stands alongside historical figures like *Donovan Bailey* as a cornerstone of Canadian track and field.

## you gotta know

- Won the gold medal in the men's 200 metres at the Tokyo Summer Olympics held in *Japan*, establishing a Canadian national record of 19.62 seconds.
- Achieved international celebrity during the 2016 Rio Games for his playful mid-race smile with Jamaican superstar *Usain Bolt* in their 200-metre semifinal heat.
- Captured six Olympic medals across his first two Olympic appearances, medaling in the 100 metres, 200 metres, and 4x100-metre relay at both the 2016 Rio and 2020 Tokyo Games.
- Anchored Canada to a gold medal in the men's 4x100-metre relay at the 2024 Paris Games alongside teammates *Aaron Brown*, *Jerome Blake*, and *Brendon Rodney*.
- Competed in American collegiate track at the University of Southern California in the *United States*, where he won both the 100-metre and 200-metre individual NCAA titles in 2015.
- Discovered late as a sprinter at Milliken Mills High School in Markham, Ontario, famously running his first competitive race in basketball shorts and borrowed spikes without starting blocks.
- Overcame persistent hamstring injuries between 2017 and 2019 to win the 200-metre silver medal and 100-metre bronze medal at the 2019 World Athletics Championships in Doha.

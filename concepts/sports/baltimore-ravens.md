## summary

The **Baltimore Ravens** are a professional American football franchise in the *National Football League* based in Baltimore, Maryland. Established in 1996 following the controversial relocation of the original Cleveland Browns personnel by owner *Art Modell*, the team adopted its moniker in honor of poet *Edgar Allan Poe*, who lived and died in Baltimore. The franchise established an enduring legacy centered on historically suffocating defensive units, capturing two championship titles and developing perennial contenders in the American Football Conference.

## you gotta know

- Captured their first league title at *Super Bowl* XXXV in January 2001, led by a historic defense featuring middle linebacker *Ray Lewis* and safety *Rod Woodson* that allowed only 165 total points in the regular season.
- Named after *Edgar Allan Poe*'s celebrated narrative poem *The Raven*, reflecting Baltimore's rich heritage in *American literature* through a fan-selected contest.
- Won their second championship at *Super Bowl* XLVII in February 2013, defeating the San Francisco 49ers in a game famous for an unexpected stadium power outage.
- Transitioned into a dynamic offensive powerhouse under head coach *John Harbaugh* following the 2018 draft selection of quarterback *Lamar Jackson*, who won multiple league Most Valuable Player awards.
- Conducted one of the most iconic rivalries in modern professional sports against the Pittsburgh Steelers, defining AFC North division football through brutal physical defensive play.
- Formed the modern foundation of their organization through general manager *Ozzie Newsome*, who drafted Hall of Fame inductees *Jonathan Ogden*, *Ray Lewis*, and *Ed Reed*.
- Set the all-time league regular season team rushing record in 2019 by amassing 3,296 yards on the ground behind an innovative read-option scheme.

## summary

**Major League Soccer** is the highest-level men's professional soccer league in the United States and Canada. Founded in 1993 as part of the successful American bid to host the 1994 FIFA World Cup, the league began competitive play in 1996 with ten teams. Structured under a unique single-entity business model, it has grown through stadium construction, global superstar signings, and steady expansion into a prominent fixture of North American sports.

## you gotta know

- Established in December 1993 by the United States Soccer Federation to fulfil a condition set by FIFA when awarding the 1994 World Cup to the [[united-states]].
- Operates under a single-entity structure in which club operators invest in the league itself, and player contracts are held centrally by the league rather than by individual franchises.
- Kickstarted a modern era of international prominence in 2007 by introducing the Designated Player Rule, colloquially known as the *David Beckham* Rule, allowing clubs to sign marquee stars above the salary cap.
- Teams compete annually for two primary league honours: the Supporters' Shield for the best regular-season record and the MLS Cup awarded to the postseason playoff champion.
- Transformed its financial stability by pioneering soccer-specific stadiums across North America, beginning with Columbus Crew Stadium in 1999.
- Experienced massive global media expansion in 2023 with the signing of Argentine legend *Lionel Messi* to Inter Miami CF alongside a worldwide broadcast agreement with Apple TV.
- Includes clubs operating in major metropolitan markets across both the [[united-states]] and Canada, fostering fierce regional rivalries like the Cascadia Cup.

## summary

**Oscar Pistorius**, widely known by the moniker **Blade Runner**, is a South African sprint runner who became the first amputee to compete in track events at the able-bodied Olympic Games. Running on carbon-fiber prosthetic blades, he dominated Paralympic sprinting before waging a landmark legal and athletic campaign to enter mainstream international competition. His global standing as an inspirational pioneer was abruptly shattered in 2013 when he shot and killed his girlfriend, Reeva Steenkamp, leading to a sensational murder trial and his eventual imprisonment.

## you gotta know

- Made history at the 2012 London Summer Games by becoming the first double-amputee athlete to compete in track events at the Olympic Games, reaching the semifinals of the men's 400 meters.
- Ran using custom carbon-fiber prostheses known as the *Flex-Foot Cheetah*, which earned him international celebrity and the popular moniker *Blade Runner*.
- Successfully challenged an International Association of Athletics Federations ban before the Court of Arbitration for Sport in 2008, which had claimed his blades conferred an unfair metabolic advantage.
- Amassed six Paralympic gold medals across the 2004 Athens, 2008 Beijing, and 2012 London Games, setting multiple world records in the T44 classification.
- Shot and killed his girlfriend, model Reeva Steenkamp, in his Pretoria home on Valentine's Day in 2013, claiming he mistook her for an armed intruder hidden behind a locked bathroom door.
- Convicted initially of culpable homicide in 2014, a verdict overturned on appeal by the Supreme Court of Appeal of South Africa to murder, resulting in a thirteen-year prison sentence before his release on parole in 2024.
- Drew immense worldwide media attention, turning his high-profile courtroom trial into one of the most widely broadcast legal spectacles of the twenty-first century.

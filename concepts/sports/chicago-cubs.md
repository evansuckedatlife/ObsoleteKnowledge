## summary

The **Chicago Cubs** are a charter Major League Baseball club based in Chicago, Illinois, competing in the National League Central division. Originating in 1876 as the Chicago White Stockings, the team has played home games at the historic, ivy-covered *Wrigley Field* on the city's North Side since 1916. The franchise became legendary for enduring a monumental 108-year championship drought before dramatically triumphing in the 2016 World Series against the Cleveland Indians.

## you gotta know

- Ended a celebrated 108-year championship drought by winning the 2016 World Series in a dramatic extra-inning Game 7 delayed by rain in Cleveland.
- Play in iconic *Wrigley Field*, famed for its hand-turned scoreboard, brick outfield walls covered in Boston ivy, and rooftop bleachers along Waveland Avenue.
- The subject of the infamous *Curse of the Billy Goat*, allegedly cast in 1945 by tavern owner Billy Sianis when his pet goat Murphy was denied entrance to a World Series game.
- Popularized the immortal double-play combination of "Tinker to Evers to Chance," immortalized in Franklin Pierce Adams's classic 1910 baseball poem "Baseball's Sad Lexicon."
- Site of *Babe Ruth*'s legendary "called shot" during the 1932 World Series at Wrigley Field against pitcher Charlie Root.
- Celebrated "Mr. Cub" *Ernie Banks*, the exuberant Hall of Fame shortstop and two-time MVP renowned for his sunny catchphrase, "It's a beautiful day for baseball, let's play two!"
- Suffered a heartbreaking postseason collapse in Game 6 of the 2003 National League Championship Series against the Florida Marlins following the foul ball disruption involving spectator Steve Bartman.

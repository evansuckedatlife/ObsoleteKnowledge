## summary

**Sidney Crosby**, known widely by the popular moniker **Sid the Kid**, is a Canadian professional ice hockey center and long-tenured captain of the Pittsburgh Penguins in the [[ice-hockey|NHL]]. Widely acclaimed as one of the greatest and most accomplished players in the history of the sport, he led Pittsburgh to three [[stanley-cup]] championships and scored the famous golden goal for Canada at the 2010 Winter [[olympic-games]]. His combination of vision, edge work, backhand passing, and leadership established him as the defining face of twenty-first-century hockey.

## you gotta know

- Selected first overall by the Pittsburgh Penguins in the 2005 NHL Entry Draft following an extraordinary junior career with the Rimouski Océanic of the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League.
- Scored the iconic sudden-death overtime "Golden Goal" against the [[united-states]] at the 2010 Vancouver Winter [[olympic-games]], securing gold for Canada in one of the most celebrated moments in Canadian sports history.
- Captained the Pittsburgh Penguins to three [[stanley-cup]] championships in 2009, 2016, and 2017, capturing the Conn Smythe Trophy as postseason most valuable player in back-to-back title runs.
- Won the Art Ross Trophy as scoring champion and the Hart Memorial Trophy as league MVP in his second professional season, becoming the youngest scoring champion in the history of major North American sports.
- Gained induction into the prestigious Triple Gold Club by capturing an IIHF World Championship gold medal to accompany his Olympic championships and multiple [[stanley-cup]] rings.
- Overcame debilitating post-concussion symptoms and associated soft-tissue neck injuries between 2011 and 2012 following the NHL Winter Classic, missing substantial portions of his athletic prime before staging a triumphant return.
- Renowned for deep personal superstitions centered on the number 87, matching his birthdate of August 7, 1987, including his jersey number, superstitious equipment routines, and a long-standing contract carrying an 8.7 million dollar cap hit.
- Widely compared to legendary figures such as [[wayne-gretzky]], [[gordie-howe]], and [[bobby-orr]] for his peerless ice vision, lower-body balance, and elite playmaking below the goal line.

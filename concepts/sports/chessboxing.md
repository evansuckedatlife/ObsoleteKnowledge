## summary

**Chessboxing** is a hybrid combat sport that alternates between rounds of classical competitive boxing and blitz chess. Originally conceived in a 1992 French graphic novel by Enki Bilal and brought to life in Berlin by Dutch performance artist *Iepe Rubingh*, the discipline demands extreme physical conditioning paired with high-level cognitive composure. Competitors must rapidly calm their heart rates to calculate deep chess variations immediately after absorbing heavy physical blows inside the ring.

## you gotta know

- Created as a performance art concept in 1992 within the science fiction graphic novel *Froid Équateur* by artist and filmmaker Enki Bilal.
- Realized as a legitimate global sport in 2003 by Dutch artist *Iepe Rubingh*, who founded the World Chess Boxing Organisation (WCBO) in Berlin, *Germany*.
- A standard regulation bout consists of 11 alternating rounds: six rounds of blitz chess and five rounds of pugilism in a standard boxing ring.
- The contest begins with a four-minute round of speed chess, followed by a three-minute round of boxing, separated by one-minute intervals.
- Victory can be attained through a knockout, technical knockout, checkmate, resignation, or penalty when an opponent exhausts their allotted chess clock time.
- Competitors wear specialized sound-dampening headphones while seated at the chessboard in the center of the ring to block crowd noise and tactical commentary.
- Reached modern mainstream online audiences through influencer events, most prominently streamer Ludwig Ahgren's 2022 Mogul Chessboxing Championship in Los Angeles.

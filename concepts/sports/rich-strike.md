## summary

**Rich Strike** is an American Thoroughbred racehorse famous for staging one of the most astonishing upsets in racing history at the 148th Kentucky Derby in 2022. Entering the race as an unheralded 80–1 longshot after drawing into the twenty-horse field only thirty seconds before the scratch deadline, he surged through traffic along the rail under jockey *Sonny Leon*. Trained by *Eric Reed* and owned by *Rick Dawson*, his improbable triumph stands alongside *Donerail* in 1913 as the second-longest odds victory in the history of the Run for the Roses.

## you gotta know

- Captured the 148th running of the Kentucky Derby on May 7, 2022, at Churchill Downs in Louisville, Kentucky, posting an extraordinary 80–1 upset victory.
- Drew into the race on the morning before the event only after *Ethereal Road*, trained by *D. Wayne Lukas*, was scratched thirty seconds prior to the official deadline.
- Became the second-longest shot ever to win the Kentucky Derby, trailing only *Donerail*, who captured the 1913 classic at 91–1 odds.
- Piloted by Venezuelan-born jockey *Sonny Leon*, who executed an audacious rail-skimming ride, weaving past tiring frontrunners *Epicenter* and *Zandon* in the final furlong to finish in 2:02.61.
- Trained by veteran Kentucky horseman *Eric Reed*, who had rebuilt his career and stables at the *Mercury Equine Center* near Lexington after losing twenty-three horses in a catastrophic barn fire in December 2016.
- Owned by Oklahoma businessman *Rick Dawson* under the stable name *RED TR-Racing*, who originally claimed the chestnut colt for thirty thousand dollars at Churchill Downs in September 2021.
- Sired by *Keen Ice*—a stallion renowned for defeating Triple Crown winner *American Pharoah* in the 2015 Travers Stakes—out of the Canadian champion broodmare *Gold Strike*, bred by *Calumet Farm*.
- Forfeited an opportunity to pursue the Triple Crown after his connections elected to skip the Preakness Stakes at Pimlico, later finishing sixth in the Belmont Stakes and fourth in the Travers Stakes.

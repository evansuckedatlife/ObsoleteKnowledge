## summary

**Hockey scoring records** encompass the statistical pinnacles of goals, assists, and total offensive output achieved throughout the history of professional [[ice-hockey]]. Dominated by the unprecedented offensive explosion of the 1980s, these benchmarks define individual dominance and distinguish the most elite offensive innovators to ever play the game. Because modern league parity, improved goaltending technique, and tactical defensive structures have significantly reduced league-wide goal scoring, many established individual records are considered virtually unassailable.

## you gotta know

- [[wayne-gretzky]] holds sixty-one official NHL records, including most career regular-season goals (894), career assists (1,963), and total points (2,857), accumulating more career assists than any other player has total points.
- The single-season scoring record belongs to [[wayne-gretzky]], who recorded 215 points in 1985-86, as well as the single-season goal record with 92 goals in 1981-82.
- The fastest 50 goals in 50 games record was shattered when [[wayne-gretzky]] scored five goals in his 39th game of the 1981-82 campaign against the *Philadelphia Flyers*.
- *Darryl Sittler* recorded the highest single-game offensive output in league history on February 6, 1976, tallying six goals and four assists for ten points with the *Toronto Maple Leafs*.
- *Teemu Selänne* established the rookie scoring standard in 1992-93 by scoring 76 goals and 132 points for the *Winnipeg Jets*, marks that remain untouched by modern first-year skaters.
- Defenseman [[bobby-orr]] is the only blueliner in league history to capture the *Art Ross Trophy* as the league's leading scorer, achieving the feat twice, including a 139-point season in 1970-71.
- *Joe Malone* holds the oldest individual game standard, scoring seven goals in a single contest for the *Quebec Bulldogs* on January 31, 1920.

## connections

- [[wayne-gretzky]] — the singular central figure of hockey statistics who holds virtually every major regular season and playoff scoring record.
- [[ice-hockey]] — the sport whose tactical evolution from low-scoring early eras to 1980s offensive explosions shaped statistical history.
- [[gordie-howe]] — early milestone setter nicknamed *Mr. Hockey*, who held all major career scoring marks prior to the modern expansion era.
- [[bobby-orr]] — revolutionary defenseman whose unprecedented rushing style shattered expectations for defensive point production.
- [[hockey-injury-comeback]] — historical thread where superstars like *Mario Lemieux* mounted spectacular statistical chases while defying severe ailments.
- [[hockey-racism]] — historical exclusionary practices that systematically prevented talented minority stars like *Herb Carnegie* from challenging elite scoring records.
- [[stanley-cup]] — championship competition where postseason scoring records by *Wayne Gretzky* and *Mark Messier* established dynasty legacies.

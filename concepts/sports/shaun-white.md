## summary

**Shaun White** is an American snowboarder and skateboarder who stands as the most decorated halfpipe rider in winter sports history. Widely recognized by his fiery red hair and moniker *The Flying Tomato*, he captured three Olympic gold medals across four quadrennials in the men's halfpipe. White's technical daring, aerial amplitude, and cultural appeal drove snowboarding from an anti-establishment counterculture into a premier Olympic television spectacle.

## you gotta know

- Captured three Olympic gold medals in the halfpipe at the 2006 Turin, 2010 Vancouver, and 2018 Pyeongchang Winter [[olympic-games]].
- Earned an unprecedented fifteen gold medals at the Winter X Games, dominating both slopestyle and superpipe disciplines for over a decade.
- Perfected and debuted the *Double McTwist 1260* (dubbed *The Tomahawk*) during his triumphant victory run at the 2010 Vancouver Games.
- Recovered from a severe training crash requiring sixty-two facial stitches in 2017 to secure his third Olympic gold medal on his final run in Pyeongchang.
- Competed at an elite professional level in vert skateboarding under the mentorship of *Tony Hawk*, claiming multiple summer X Games medals.
- Retired from competitive snowboarding following a fourth-place finish at the 2022 Beijing Winter Games, concluding a two-decade international career.

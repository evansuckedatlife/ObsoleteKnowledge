## summary

**Lionel Messi**, widely known as **Leo Messi** or **La Pulga**, is an Argentine professional footballer regarded by many as the greatest player in the history of association football. Developing through *FC Barcelona*'s storied *La Masia* academy after leaving *Rosario*, he orchestrated a golden era of European dominance marked by four UEFA Champions League crowns and ten La Liga titles. His international journey achieved ultimate glory when he captained *Argentina* to victory at the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

## you gotta know

- Relocated from *Rosario*, Argentina, to Catalonia as a youth after *FC Barcelona* agreed to finance treatments for his growth hormone deficiency.
- Won a record eight *Ballon d'Or* awards as the world's preeminent footballer, spanning three distinct decades.
- Formed the attacking core of *Pep Guardiola*'s historic *Barcelona* sextuple-winning squad in 2009 alongside *Xavi* and *Andrés Iniesta*.
- Set the recognized world record for most official goals scored in a single calendar year by tallying ninety-one goals in 2012.
- Guided *Argentina* to the 2022 FIFA World Cup title in Qatar, scoring twice in the final against [[france]] and collecting the Golden Ball.
- Captured an Olympic gold medal alongside *Sergio Agüero* and *Ángel Di María* at Beijing 2008 in the [[olympic-games]].
- Ended his lengthy tenure at *Barcelona* in 2021 due to financial constraints, moving to *Paris Saint-Germain* before joining *Inter Miami CF* in the [[united-states]].
- Anchored Argentina's continental resurgence by winning back-to-back Copa América crowns in 2021 and 2024.

## connections

- [[spain]] — nation where he spent over two decades anchoring the domestic and continental supremacy of *FC Barcelona*.
- [[france]] — country where he played for *Paris Saint-Germain* and against whom he scored twice in the 2022 World Cup final.
- [[olympic-games]] — won international gold for Argentina in the men's football tournament at the 2008 Beijing Games.
- [[lebron-james]] — fellow generational icon who relocated to South Florida to reshape an American sports market.
- [[lewis-hamilton]] — shared the 2020 Laureus World Sportsman of the Year award with him in an unprecedented tie.
- [[lionel-conacher-award]] — shares a namesake with the historic award honoring versatile male athletic supremacy.
- [[united-states]] — nation where he spearheaded Major League Soccer's global popularity with *Inter Miami CF*.

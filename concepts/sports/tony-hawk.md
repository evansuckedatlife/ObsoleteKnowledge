## summary

**Tony Hawk** (born **Anthony Frank Hawk**), celebrated worldwide by his moniker **The Birdman**, is an American professional skateboarder, entrepreneur, and global pop-culture figure who revolutionized vertical skateboarding. Emerging from the Southern California skate culture of the late 1970s, he achieved unparalleled dominance on the contest circuit throughout the 1980s as the leading technical innovator of the renowned *Bones Brigade*. Through his historic completion of the 900-degree aerial spin in 1999 and the explosive global success of his eponymous video game series, Hawk single-handedly transformed skateboarding from a stigmatized countercultural pastime into a respected international sporting discipline.

## you gotta know

- Landed the first officially documented 900—a perilous two-and-a-half-revolution aerial maneuver on a vertical halfpipe—at the 1999 *X Games* in *San Francisco*, successfully sticking the landing on his twelfth attempt after competition regulation time had expired.
- Headlined the blockbuster video game franchise *Tony Hawk's Pro Skater*, launched by *Activision* and *Neversoft* in 1999, which sold tens of millions of units worldwide, popularized punk and alternative music, and shaped millennial youth culture.
- Reigned supreme over competitive vertical skateboarding during the 1980s, winning twelve consecutive *National Skateboard Association* (*NSA*) world championship titles and capturing victories in over seventy individual competitions.
- Invented roughly eighty innovative vertical halfpipe tricks, establishing foundational maneuvers of modern skating such as the *stalefish*, the *Madonna*, the *gymnast plant*, the *airwalk*, and the *ollie 540*.
- Rose to prominence as a teenaged standout for *Stacy Peralta's* legendary *Bones Brigade* team under *Powell Peralta*, starring in seminal skate films including 1987's *The Search for Animal Chin*.
- Founded the influential skate company *Birdhouse Projects* (later *Birdhouse Skateboards*) with *Per Welinder* in 1992, weathering an economic downturn in ramp skating before revitalizing the industry.
- Founded *The Skatepark Project* (originally the *Tony Hawk Foundation*), a non-profit charity that has distributed millions of dollars in matching grants to build hundreds of free public skateparks in low-income communities across the [[united-states]].
- Served as a guest television commentator and international ambassador when skateboarding made its historic official debut at [[olympic-games]] during the Tokyo 2020 Summer Games.

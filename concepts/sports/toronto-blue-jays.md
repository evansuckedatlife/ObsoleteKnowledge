## summary

The **Toronto Blue Jays** are a Canadian professional baseball franchise based in Toronto, Ontario, competing in Major League Baseball's American League East division. Founded in 1977 as an expansion franchise alongside the Seattle Mariners, the club made history by capturing consecutive World Series championships in 1992 and 1993. Following the relocation of the [[montreal-expos]] after 2004, the **Blue Jays** stand as the only active Major League Baseball team located outside the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Became the first franchise based outside the [[united-states]] to win the World Series, defeating the Atlanta Braves in 1992 under legendary manager *Cito Gaston*.
- Clinched their second straight title in 1993 on *Joe Carter*'s dramatic walk-off three-run home run against *Mitch Williams* of the Philadelphia Phillies, famously punctuated by broadcaster *Tom Cheek*'s call: "Touch 'em all, Joe!"
- Moved into the revolutionary *SkyDome* (later renamed *Rogers Centre*) in 1989, which introduced the world's first fully functional motorized retractable stadium roof and featured a hotel overlooking the outfield.
- Produced iconic franchise ace *Dave Stieb*, who dominated the 1980s and threw the organization's first official no-hitter against the Cleveland Indians on September 2, 1990.
- Boasted Hall of Fame second baseman *Roberto Alomar*, whose legendary game-tying ninth-inning home run off *Dennis Eckersley* in Game 4 of the 1992 ALCS turned franchise history.
- Feature a storied legacy of elite sluggers and award winners, including Cy Young winners *Pat Hentgen*, *Roger Clemens*, and *Roy Halladay*, as well as home run king *Carlos Delgado*.
- Re-energized Canadian baseball culture in the 2010s led by *Jose Bautista*, whose dramatic seventh-inning bat flip home run in Game 5 of the 2015 ALDS against the Texas Rangers became an enduring modern sports moment.
- Competed against their provincial and national counterpart, the [[montreal-expos]], in the regular exhibition and interleague showcase known as the *Pearson Cup*.

## connections

- [[montreal-expos]] — provincial rivals and fellow Canadian MLB franchise with whom they contested the *Pearson Cup*.
- [[the-new-york-yankees]] — primary American League East division rival against whom they battle for postseason contention.
- [[derek-jeter]] — longtime rival captain and shortstop of the division-rival Yankees throughout the late 1990s and 2000s.
- [[mariano-rivera]] — Hall of Fame closer whose American League East dominance frequently challenged Toronto's pennant hopes.
- [[babe-ruth]] — iconic baseball figure who famously hit his first professional home run at Toronto's Hanlan's Point Stadium in 1914.
- [[mickey-mantle]] — legendary Yankees center fielder who shaped the tradition of Toronto's primary division opponent.
- [[united-states]] — home country of Major League Baseball and every rival organization in the league.

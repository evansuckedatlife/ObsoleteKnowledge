## summary

**Danica Patrick**, born **Danica Sue Patrick**, is an American former professional auto racing driver who stands as the most accomplished and pioneering woman in the history of American open-wheel racing. Competing across both the *IndyCar Series* and the *NASCAR Cup Series*, she shattered gender boundaries in elite motorsports across the [[united-states]]. Her historic triumph at the 2008 Indy Japan 300 established her as the only female victor in IndyCar history, transforming her into an enduring cultural and athletic icon.

## you gotta know

- Captured victory at the 2008 *Indy Japan 300* at *Twin Ring Motegi*, becoming the first and only woman to win a race in the *IndyCar Series*.
- Won the pole position for the 2013 *Daytona 500*, making her the first woman to earn a pole position in the top-tier *NASCAR Cup Series*.
- Finished fourth in the 2005 *Indianapolis 500* as a rookie after leading nineteen laps, securing the race's Rookie of the Year honors and sparking widespread excitement dubbed *Danica-Mania*.
- Recorded a third-place finish at the 2009 *Indianapolis 500*, which stands as the highest finish ever achieved by a woman in the legendary race's history.
- Transitioned to full-time stock car competition in 2012, driving the number ten Chevrolet for *Stewart-Haas Racing* across several grueling seasons in *NASCAR*.
- Concluded her competitive career in 2018 by completing the *Danica Double*, entering both the *Daytona 500* and *Indianapolis 500* as her final farewell events.
- Honed her driving technique as a teenager in [[england]], where she placed second at the prestigious 2000 *Formula Ford Festival* at Brands Hatch.
- Cultivated an expansive business brand outside racing through Super Bowl commercials, lifestyle authorship, wine ventures, and television broadcasting for Formula One.

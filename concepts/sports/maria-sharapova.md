## summary

**Maria Sharapova** is a Russian former world No. 1 tennis player who achieved a career Grand Slam across four major tournaments. Bursting onto the international stage with a stunning upset victory at *Wimbledon* in 2004 at age 17, she captured five Grand Slam singles titles during her decorated career. Known for her powerful baseline play, fiery competitiveness, and high-profile endorsements, she remains one of the most commercially successful female athletes in global sports history.

## you gotta know

- Captured her breakthrough first Grand Slam singles title at [[wimbledon]] in 2004 by defeating defending two-time champion *Serena Williams* in straight sets at just 17 years old.
- Completed the prestigious career Grand Slam in women's singles by winning the [[french-open]] at Roland Garros in 2012, later adding a second title there in 2014.
- Captured the [[us-open]] title in 2006 by defeating *Justine Henin* in the final and won the [[australian-open]] in 2008 without dropping a single set throughout the tournament.
- Became the first Russian woman to achieve the world No. 1 singles ranking by the Women's Tennis Association (WTA) in August 2005.
- Won the silver medal in women's singles at the 2012 London [[olympic-games]], representing Russia after serving as her nation's flag bearer in the opening ceremony.
- Served a 15-month suspension by the International Tennis Federation after testing positive for the banned substance meldonium at the 2016 [[australian-open]].
- Founded the premium confectionery and candy brand Sugarpova in 2012, showcasing her business acumen alongside her tennis career.

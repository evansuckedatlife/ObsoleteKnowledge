## summary

**John Tavares** is a Canadian professional *[[ice-hockey]]* centre and longtime National Hockey League star who has served as captain of both the New York Islanders and the Toronto Maple Leafs. Granted exceptional player status to enter major junior hockey a year early as a teenager, he was selected first overall in the 2009 NHL Entry Draft and developed into an elite playmaker and goalscorer. He achieved international triumph by capturing an Olympic gold medal with Canada at the 2014 Winter *[[olympic-games]]* in Sochi.

## you gotta know

- Granted the historic *Exceptional Player Status* by the Canadian Hockey League in 2005, allowing him to be drafted into the Ontario Hockey League at just fourteen years old.
- Drafted first overall in the 2009 NHL Entry Draft by the New York Islanders, immediately becoming the franchise cornerstone in suburban *[[new-york-city]]* and serving as team captain from 2013 to 2018.
- Shattered junior records previously held by *[[wayne-gretzky]]*, passing Gretzky's mark for the most goals by a sixteen-year-old in a single Ontario Hockey League season.
- Signed a blockbuster seven-year contract with his hometown Toronto Maple Leafs in free agency in 2018, famously posting a childhood photograph of himself sleeping beneath Maple Leafs bedsheets.
- Named the twenty-fifth captain in Toronto Maple Leafs history in October 2019, anchoring the team alongside young stars like *Auston Matthews* and *Mitch Marner*.
- Scored the series-clinching overtime goal in Game Six of the 2023 first round against the Tampa Bay Lightning, handing Toronto its first playoff series win in nineteen years in its pursuit of the *[[stanley-cup]]*.
- Represented Canada at the 2014 Winter *[[olympic-games]]* in Sochi, winning a gold medal alongside national teammates despite suffering a tournament-ending knee injury in the quarterfinals.
- Surpassed the milestone of one thousand career NHL points in December 2023, cementing his legacy among the most consistent offensive centres of his generation.

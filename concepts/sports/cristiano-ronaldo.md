## summary

**Cristiano Ronaldo**, in full **Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro** and popularly celebrated as **CR7**, is a Portuguese professional footballer widely acclaimed as one of the greatest goalscorers in the history of world sports. Rising from humble origins in Madeira through the academy of Sporting CP, he attained historic club dominance across Europe with Manchester United in [[england]], Real Madrid in [[spain]], and Juventus in [[italy]]. He captained Portugal to continental glory at UEFA Euro 2016 and established the all-time records for most goals in men's international football and the UEFA Champions League.

## you gotta know

- Won five *Ballon d'Or* awards and five *UEFA Champions League* titles, becoming the first player in modern football history to win the Champions League five times.
- Became the all-time leading goalscorer in men's international football history, surpassing Iran's *Ali Daei* and setting the international standard for goalscorers such as [[cyle-larin]].
- Spent nine prolific seasons with *Real Madrid* in [[spain]], scoring an astounding 450 goals in 438 competitive matches to stand as the club's all-time leading scorer.
- Captained *Portugal* to their first major international tournament trophy at *UEFA Euro 2016*, passionately coaching his teammates from the technical area after suffering an early injury against [[france]].
- Developed into a complete forward under *Sir Alex Ferguson* at *Manchester United* in [[england]], capturing three Premier League titles and his first Champions League crown in 2008.
- Engaged in a decade-long individual rivalry with Argentina's *Lionel Messi*, elevating Spanish football's *El Clásico* into the most watched sporting spectacle on earth.
- Represented Portugal at the 2004 Summer [[olympic-games]] in Athens and went on to score in five distinct FIFA World Cup tournaments across his international career.
- Overcame a diagnosed racing heart condition as a teenager in Lisbon before making his senior debut and securing a high-profile transfer to English football.

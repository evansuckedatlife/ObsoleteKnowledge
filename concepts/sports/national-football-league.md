## summary

The *National Football League* (*NFL*) is the premier professional American football league in the United States and one of the world's most profitable sports organizations. Founded in 1920 and professionalized through the 20th century, the NFL evolved from a regional sport into America's dominant professional league, with the *Super Bowl* championship game attracting billions of viewers globally. The league's cultural, economic, and media dominance shapes American sports and entertainment landscape.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1920, making it the oldest continuously operating professional American football league, predating even Major League Baseball's modern era.
- Each season features 32 teams divided into two conferences (AFC and NFC), playing a regular season followed by a playoff structure culminating in the Super Bowl.
- Games are played on a 100-yard field with 11 players per side, divided into offensive, defensive, and special-teams units with regular substitutions.
- The NFL's labor negotiations, team movements, and franchise values make it a major business; franchise valuations exceed billions of dollars.
- Super Bowl Sunday has become a de facto national holiday in the United States, with viewership regularly exceeding 100 million, making it a cultural phenomenon.
- The *NFL Draft*, held annually, is where teams select college players; it has become a major media event watched by millions of fans and sporting commentators.
- The league has evolved from a regional, brutal sport to a high-passing-game dominated league emphasizing skill, speed, and athletic prowess.
- NFL seasons consist of 17 regular-season games (increased from 16 in 2022), followed by 14 teams advancing to a expanded playoff structure with multiple wild-card rounds.
- The league's television contracts are among the most valuable in sports, generating billions in annual revenue from networks competing for broadcasting rights.
- Iconic rivalries like Cowboys-Eagles, Steelers-Ravens, and Packers-Bears create longstanding cultural divisions and intense annual competition across geographic lines.
- Rule changes over decades have evolved the game—from favoring running attacks to emphasizing passing, with regulations protecting quarterbacks and receivers reshaping offensive strategy.
- The NFL Player's Association and league ownership have negotiated complex collective bargaining agreements affecting salary caps, player benefits, and revenue sharing structures.

## summary

The **Green Bay Packers** are a professional American football franchise based in Green Bay, Wisconsin, competing in the *National Football League* as a member of the NFC North division. Founded in 1919 by *Curly Lambeau* and *George Whitney Calhoun*, the **Packers** stand as the third-oldest franchise in the league and the only non-profit, community-owned major professional sports team in the [[united-states]]. Renowned for their unmatched total of thirteen league championships, the club represents one of the most storied traditions in sports history and gave its name to the iconic *Vince Lombardi Trophy*.

## you gotta know

- Captured thirteen total league championships, including nine pre-merger titles and four [[super-bowl]] victories (*Super Bowl I*, *Super Bowl II*, *Super Bowl XXXI*, and *Super Bowl XLV*), establishing the standard of excellence rewarded by the *Vince Lombardi Trophy*.
- Operates under a unique community ownership model where hundreds of thousands of shareholders hold non-dividend, voting stock, ensuring the franchise remains permanently tied to Green Bay rather than relocated by a private owner.
- Dominance in the 1960s under legendary head coach *Vince Lombardi* produced five championships in seven seasons, powered by the famed *Packer Sweep* offensive play executed by Hall of Famers like *Bart Starr*, *Paul Hornung*, and *Jim Taylor*.
- Experienced a modern resurgence powered by consecutive Hall of Fame and future Hall of Fame quarterbacks *Brett Favre* and *Aaron Rodgers*, maintaining championship contention across three decades.
- Contested the famous 1967 *NFL Championship Game*, known as the *Ice Bowl*, defeating the Dallas Cowboys in sub-zero wind chills at *Lambeau Field* through a last-second quarterback sneak by *Bart Starr*.
- Originally sponsored by the *Indian Packing Company*, which provided five hundred dollars for jerseys and equipment in exchange for naming the athletic club after the canning enterprise.
- Holds the record for the most enshrined members in the *Pro Football Hall of Fame*, celebrating foundational figures such as *Don Hutson*, who revolutionized the wide receiver position in the 1930s and 1940s.

## summary

**Max Parrot**, born *Maxence Parrot*, is a celebrated Canadian professional snowboarder who achieved international prominence in the disciplines of slopestyle and big air. Raised in Bromont, Quebec, he emerged as one of the most innovative and technically daring freestyle snowboarders of his generation, winning eight gold medals at the *Winter X Games*. His triumphant victory in men's slopestyle at the 2022 Winter [[olympic-games]] in Beijing occurred just over three years after he was diagnosed with Hodgkin lymphoma, making his Olympic gold medal one of the most inspirational comeback stories in modern sports history.

## you gotta know

- Captured the gold medal in men's slopestyle at the 2022 Winter [[olympic-games]] in Beijing with a dominant second run score of 90.96, edging out rivals *Su Yiming* and teammate *Mark McMorris*.
- Diagnosed with stage 2A Hodgkin lymphoma in December 2018 at age twenty-four after discovering a suspicious bump on his neck, undergoing twelve grueling rounds of chemotherapy over six months before entering remission in mid-2019.
- Returned to elite competitive snowboarding a mere two months after finishing his cancer treatments, completing a stunning comeback by winning the big air gold medal at the *Winter X Games* in Norway in August 2019.
- Won the Olympic silver medal in slopestyle at the 2018 Winter [[olympic-games]] in Pyeongchang, South Korea, finishing narrowly behind American snowboarder *Red Gerard* in an exceptionally close final round.
- Added an Olympic bronze medal in big air at the 2022 Beijing Games, becoming one of Canada's most decorated multi-medalists in Olympic snowboarding history.
- Revolutionized competitive freestyle snowboarding progression by becoming the first rider ever to land consecutive triple corks in a slopestyle contest run and the first to successfully land a cab quadruple underflip 1620.
- Grew up skiing at a young age in the Eastern Townships before convincing his parents to let him switch to snowboarding at age nine, quickly mastering rails and kickers to ascend through the Canadian freestyle circuit.
- Earned the prestigious *Laureus World Sports Award for World Comeback of the Year* in 2021, recognizing his remarkable resilience and triumph over cancer.

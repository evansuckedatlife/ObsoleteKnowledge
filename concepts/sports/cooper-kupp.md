## summary

**Cooper Kupp**, fully named **Cooper Douglas Kupp**, is an American professional wide receiver who became one of the most prolific pass-catchers in the [[national-football-league]] while playing for the *Los Angeles Rams*. Drafted out of the *FCS* program at *Eastern Washington University*, he overcame modest draft expectations through elite route-running precision, football intelligence, and physical blocking in head coach *Sean McVay*'s offensive scheme. During the historic 2021 campaign, Kupp captured the rare receiving *Triple Crown*, won NFL *Offensive Player of the Year*, and was named Most Valuable Player of [[super-bowl]] LVI after catching the game-winning touchdown.

## you gotta know

- Achieved the receiving *Triple Crown* during the 2021 regular season by leading the league in receptions (145), receiving yards (1,947), and receiving touchdowns (16), becoming only the fourth player in the modern era to accomplish the feat alongside *Jerry Rice*, *Sterling Sharpe*, and *Steve Smith*.
- Awarded the *Pete Rozelle Trophy* as the Most Valuable Player of [[super-bowl]] LVI in February 2022, hauling in eight passes for ninety-two yards and two touchdowns, including the decisive one-yard score from *Matthew Stafford* with under two minutes remaining to defeat the Cincinnati Bengals.
- Selected by the *Los Angeles Rams* in the third round of the 2017 draft with the sixty-ninth overall pick after receiving zero scholarship offers from *FBS* college programs coming out of *Davis High School* in Yakima, Washington.
- Dominated college football at *Eastern Washington University*, winning the *Walter Payton Award* in 2015 and setting all-division college receiving records with 6,464 receiving yards and seventy-three touchdown catches over his four collegiate seasons.
- Comes from a celebrated three-generation football family in the [[united-states]]: his grandfather *Jake Kupp* played offensive line for the New Orleans Saints, and his father *Craig Kupp* played quarterback in the league for the Phoenix Cardinals and Dallas Cowboys.
- Suffered a torn *ACL* during week ten of the 2018 season, which forced him to miss the Rams' run to [[super-bowl]] LIII, where Los Angeles struggled offensively and lost to [[tom-brady]] and the New England Patriots.
- Instrumental on the Rams' game-winning drive in the 2021 divisional playoff round against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, catching a dramatic forty-four-yard deep pass from Stafford with seconds remaining to set up *Matt Gay*'s walk-off field goal.
- Recognized for redefining the modern hybrid slot receiver position, executing intricate option routes inside the numbers while frequently serving as a key run-blocker at the line of scrimmage in outside-zone running concepts.

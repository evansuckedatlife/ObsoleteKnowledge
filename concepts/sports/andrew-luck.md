## summary

**Andrew Luck**, full name **Andrew Austin Luck**, was an American professional football quarterback who played seven seasons for the *Indianapolis Colts* in the [[national-football-league]]. Widely regarded as the most complete quarterback prospect since [[peyton-manning]], he arrived in Indiana with monumental expectations and swiftly established himself among the premier passers in the [[united-states]]. Despite multiple Pro Bowl selections and remarkable postseason comebacks, persistent severe physical injuries prompted his shocking early retirement from professional athletics in 2019 at the age of twenty-nine.

## you gotta know

- Starred collegiately at *Stanford University*, finishing as the runner-up for the *Heisman Trophy* in both 2010 and 2011 while capturing the *Maxwell Award* and *Walter Camp Award*.
- Selected with the first overall pick in the 2012 draft by *Indianapolis*, stepping in as the successor to [[peyton-manning]] with the Colts and continuing the franchise quarterback tradition by guiding the team to three straight 11-5 seasons.
- Established a new league rookie record by passing for 4,374 yards during the 2012 campaign, demonstrating remarkable poise and tactical leadership behind an inexperienced roster.
- Engineered the second-largest comeback in postseason history during the 2013 wild-card round, overcoming a 38-10 second-half deficit to defeat the *Kansas City Chiefs* 45-44 with four total touchdowns.
- Led the entire [[national-football-league]] in passing touchdowns during the 2014 season with 40 scoring strikes, guiding the *Colts* to the AFC Championship Game against [[tom-brady]].
- Endured devastating injuries throughout his career due to chronic offensive line protection issues, suffering a lacerated kidney, torn abdominal muscles, and a severe shoulder labrum injury that sidelined him for the entire 2017 season.
- Earned the NFL Comeback Player of the Year Award in 2018 after throwing 39 touchdowns, before stunning the sports world by announcing his immediate retirement during the 2019 preseason to preserve his long-term health.

## summary

**Cam Newton** is an American football quarterback celebrated for his dynamic dual-threat athleticism, physical rushing power, and charismatic leadership in the *National Football League*. Rising to prominence by guiding the *Auburn Tigers* to a national title and winning the 2010 *Heisman Trophy*, he was selected first overall by the *Carolina Panthers* in the 2011 NFL Draft. During his 2015 MVP campaign, he spearheaded one of the most explosive offenses in modern football history and fundamentally redefined the quarterback position in the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Named the NFL Most Valuable Player and Offensive Player of the Year in 2015 after accounting for 45 total touchdowns and leading the *Carolina Panthers* to a 15-1 regular season.
- Led the Panthers to an appearance in *Super Bowl 50*, where his top-ranked scoring offense was stymied by a suffocating defense led by veteran quarterback [[peyton-manning]] and the *Denver Broncos*.
- Holds the NFL record for the most career rushing touchdowns by a quarterback (75), shattering previous benchmarks established by mobile signal-callers.
- Captured the 2010 *Heisman Trophy* and the BCS National Championship in his lone season starting for *Auburn University*, pulling off the famous "Cam-back" 24-point deficit win against the *Alabama Crimson Tide*.
- Named NFL Offensive Rookie of the Year in 2011 after becoming the first rookie quarterback in league history to throw for 4,000 yards while running for 14 touchdowns.
- Known for his signature celebratory gestures, popularizing the "Superman" shirt-pull celebration and mainstreaming the "dab" dance craze throughout the 2015 season.
- Signed with the *New England Patriots* in 2020 to replace legendary passer [[tom-brady]], playing under head coach [[bill-belichick]] and scoring 12 rushing touchdowns during his single season in Foxborough.
- Started his collegiate path at the *University of Florida* behind *Tim Tebow*, transferred to *Blinn College* to capture a NJCAA national title, and ultimately reemerged as a dominant collegiate superstar.

## summary

**Connor McDavid**, formally known as **Connor Andrew McDavid**, is a Canadian professional center and captain of the *Edmonton Oilers*, widely acclaimed as the preeminent player of his generation in [[ice-hockey]]. Selected first overall in the 2015 entry draft after earning exceptional player status in junior hockey, he quickly dominated the sport through his unprecedented combination of top-end skating velocity, puck-handling agility, and spatial vision. McDavid has accumulated numerous scoring championships and MVP awards, drawing frequent comparisons to legends like [[wayne-gretzky]] and cementing his legacy by winning the *Conn Smythe Trophy* as playoff MVP during the Oilers' run to the 2024 [[stanley-cup]] Finals.

## you gotta know

- Selected first overall by the *Edmonton Oilers* in the 2015 draft after gaining exceptional status at age fifteen to enter the *Ontario Hockey League* (*OHL*) early, starring for the *Erie Otters*.
- Won the *Conn Smythe Trophy* in 2024 as the most valuable player of the playoffs despite Edmonton falling in seven games to the Florida Panthers, joining *Reggie Leach* as the only non-goaltenders in modern history to win the award while playing for the losing team.
- Broke [[wayne-gretzky]]'s single-postseason record for assists during the 2024 playoffs, recording thirty-four assists to surpass Gretzky's previous mark of thirty-one set in 1988.
- Multiple-time recipient of the *Hart Memorial Trophy* as the league's Most Valuable Player, including a unanimous selection in 2021 after scoring 105 points in only fifty-six games during a pandemic-shortened schedule.
- Captured the *Art Ross Trophy* as regular season scoring leader multiple times, joining *Mario Lemieux*, [[wayne-gretzky]], and [[gordie-howe]] among the few skaters in league history to lead the league in scoring five or more times before turning twenty-eight.
- Recorded a career-high 153 points during the 2022–2023 campaign, winning his first *Maurice "Rocket" Richard Trophy* with sixty-four goals while leading the league in goals, assists, and points in the same season.
- Formed a lethal offensive duo with German center *Leon Draisaitl*, anchoring one of the most statistically efficient power-play units in the annals of North American professional sports.
- Suffered a catastrophic knee injury during the final game of the 2018–2019 season against the Calgary Flames, successfully rehabilitating a torn *PCL* and meniscus without undergoing invasive reconstructive surgery, as chronicled in the sports documentary *Whatever It Takes*.

## summary

**Lance Armstrong**, born **Lance Edward Armstrong**, is an American former professional road racing cyclist who achieved international celebrity by winning the *Tour de France* a record seven consecutive times from 1999 to 2005. After surviving advanced testicular cancer in the late 1990s, he founded the Livestrong Foundation and became a global symbol of athletic endurance and cancer survivorship. However, following exhaustive investigations by the United States Anti-Doping Agency that uncovered systematic blood doping, he was stripped of all seven titles in 2012 and banned for life from competitive sport.

## you gotta know

- Won the UCI Road World Championship road race in Oslo in 1993 at age twenty-one, becoming one of the youngest riders ever to win a world professional road championship.
- Diagnosed in October 1996 with stage III testicular cancer that had metastasized to his lungs, abdomen, and brain, undergoing brain surgery and aggressive chemotherapy before staging a miraculous recovery.
- Dominated the *Tour de France* across *[[france]]* for seven straight years from 1999 through 2005 as team leader of the US Postal Service Pro Cycling Team and Discovery Channel team.
- Founded the charity Livestrong (originally the Lance Armstrong Foundation) in 1997, popularizing the iconic yellow silicone wristband designed with Nike that raised over five hundred million dollars for cancer survivor programs.
- Competed in the 2000 Sydney *[[olympic-games]]*, winning a bronze medal in the men's individual road time trial, which the International Olympic Committee later revoked in 2013.
- Long accused of doping by investigative journalists *David Walsh* and *Pierre Ballester* in their book *L.A. Confidentiel*, as well as former teammate *Floyd Landis*, Armstrong aggressively utilized defamation lawsuits to silence whistleblowers.
- USADA published a landmark 2012 reasoned decision labeling his US Postal team's operation the most sophisticated, professionalized, and successful doping program that sport had ever seen.
- Publicly confessed to performance-enhancing drug use—including EPO, blood transfusions, and testosterone—during a widely watched two-part television interview with *Oprah Winfrey* in January 2013.

## summary

**Mike Weir** is a Canadian professional golfer who achieved international fame by winning the 2003 *Masters Tournament*, becoming the first Canadian man and only the second left-handed golfer to win a major championship. Hailing from Brights Grove, Ontario, he built a stellar career through perseverance on global developmental tours before establishing himself as an elite competitor on the *PGA Tour*. Known for his gritty determination, pinpoint accuracy, and imaginative short game, he captured eight tour victories to tie the national record. His green jacket triumph transformed him into one of Canada's most celebrated sporting heroes and a benchmark figure in modern professional golf.

## you gotta know

- Captured the 2003 *Masters Tournament* at Augusta National, edging *Len Mattiace* on the first hole of a sudden-death playoff with a steady bogey.
- Made history as the first Canadian male golfer to win a major championship and just the second left-handed player to capture a men's major, following *Bob Charles*.
- Received the iconic green jacket in Butler Cabin from defending champion [[tiger-woods]], marking a landmark moment for Canadian athletics.
- Tied the all-time record for *PGA Tour* victories by a Canadian golfer with eight, sharing the milestone with legendary ball-striker *George Knudson*.
- Defeated world number one [[tiger-woods]] in a dramatic singles match during the 2007 *Presidents Cup*, played before an ecstatic home crowd at the Royal Montreal Golf Club.
- Corresponded as a teenager with golf icon [[jack-nicklaus]], who wrote a letter encouraging the young Canadian never to switch to playing right-handed despite widespread advice.
- Honored in 2003 with the *Lou Marsh Trophy* as Canada's top athlete across all sports, an award previously bestowed on hockey royalty like [[mike-bossy]].
- Captained the International Team at the 2024 *Presidents Cup*, continuing his long-standing dedication to international team match play.

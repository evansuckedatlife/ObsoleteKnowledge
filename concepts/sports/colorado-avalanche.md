## summary

The **Colorado Avalanche** are an elite American professional *ice hockey* team based in Denver, Colorado, competing in the Central Division of the Western Conference. Originally founded as the *Quebec Nordiques* in 1972, the franchise relocated to Denver in 1995 and immediately swept the Florida Panthers to claim the title in their inaugural season. Known for a bitter, blood-feud rivalry with the Detroit Red Wings, the franchise has claimed three *Stanley Cup* titles, spanning triumphs in 1996, 2001, and 2022.

## you gotta know

- Relocated from Canada to Denver prior to the 1995–96 season, becoming the first NHL franchise to hoist the *Stanley Cup* in its very first year following a relocation.
- Led on the ice during their golden era by Hall of Fame center and captain *Joe Sakic* and dominant Swedish playmaker *Peter Forsberg*.
- Acquired legendary goaltender *Patrick Roy* from the Montreal Canadiens in a blockbuster 1995 trade, solidifying a championship core that redefined netminding excellence.
- Fueled an intense, violent rivalry with the Detroit Red Wings during the late 1990s and early 2000s, punctuated by the infamous "Brawl in Hockeytown" in March 1997.
- Captured their second championship in 2001, memorable for Joe Sakic instantly handing the silver trophy to retiring Hall of Fame blueliner *Ray Bourque* without hoisting it first.
- Returned to championship glory in 2022 by dethroning the two-time defending champion Tampa Bay Lightning, spearheaded by playoff MVP defenseman Cale Makar and star forward Nathan MacKinnon.
- The franchise's departure from Canada was catalyzed by the famous 1991 draft holdout of Eric Lindros, whose monumental trade haul provided the foundation of assets that built Denver's dynasty.

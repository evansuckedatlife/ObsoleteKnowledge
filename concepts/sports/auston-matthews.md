## summary

**Auston Matthews** is an American professional ice hockey center and captain of the Toronto Maple Leafs who is widely recognized as one of the greatest goal scorers in modern hockey history. Selected first overall in the 2016 NHL Entry Draft, he made immediate history with a four-goal debut before becoming a multiple-time winner of the Maurice "Rocket" Richard Trophy. Raised in the nontraditional hockey market of Arizona in the *United States*, he revitalized Canada's most scrutinized sports market and cemented his status alongside historic scorers like *Wayne Gretzky*.

## you gotta know

- Scored four goals in his NHL regular season debut on October 12, 2016, becoming the first player in modern league history to achieve that feat in their first game.
- Won the Hart Memorial Trophy as the league's most valuable player for the 2021–22 season after scoring 60 goals, the first American-born player to achieve the milestone in a single campaign.
- Surpassed his own personal and national records by netting 69 goals in the 2023–24 season, the highest single-season goal tally in the league since the mid-1990s.
- Chose an unorthodox developmental path for a top North American prospect by playing professional hockey for the ZSC Lions in Switzerland's National League A prior to his draft year.
- Named the 26th captain in Toronto Maple Leafs franchise history in August 2024, taking over the captaincy from veteran teammate *John Tavares*.
- Developed his signature elite shooting technique—featuring a deceptive curl-and-drag wrist release—under youth skill coach *Boris Dorozhenko* in Scottsdale, Arizona.
- Won the Calder Memorial Trophy as the NHL Rookie of the Year in 2017 after scoring 40 goals in his initial 82-game campaign.

## summary

The **Boston Red Sox** are an American professional baseball franchise based in Boston, Massachusetts, competing in Major League Baseball's American League East division. Founded in 1901 as one of the American League's eight charter franchises, the club plays its home games at historic *Fenway Park*, the oldest active ballpark in the majors. Renowned for its storied history, fervent fan culture, and heated rivalry with [[the-new-york-yankees]], the franchise famously overcame the eighty-six-year championship drought known as the *Curse of the Bambino* to capture four World Series titles in the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Famously sold transcendent slugger and pitcher [[babe-ruth]] to [[the-new-york-yankees]] in late 1919 under owner *Harry Frazee*, sparking an eighty-six-year title drought popularly dubbed the *Curse of the Bambino*.
- Plays home games at *Fenway Park*, opened in 1912, known across American sports for distinctive architectural quirks including the towering thirty-seven-foot left-field wall nicknamed the *Green Monster*, *Pesky's Pole* down the right-field line, and *The Lone Red Seat* marking *Ted Williams*'s longest home run.
- Hall of Fame left fielder *Ted Williams*, known as *The Splendid Splinter*, spent his entire nineteen-year career with the franchise, famously posting a .406 batting average in 1941, the last major league hitter to hit over .400 in a season.
- Staged an unprecedented postseason comeback in the 2004 American League Championship Series against [[the-new-york-yankees]], erasing a three-games-to-zero deficit behind *Dave Roberts*'s crucial stolen base, *David Ortiz*'s walk-off hits, and *Curt Schilling*'s *bloody sock* performance before sweeping the *St. Louis Cardinals* for their first championship since 1918.
- Endured legendary World Series heartbreaks in Game 7 of 1946, 1967 against *Bob Gibson*, 1975 despite *Carlton Fisk*'s iconic Game 6 walk-off homer off the foul pole, and Game 6 of 1986 against the *New York Mets* when *Mookie Wilson*'s ground ball rolled through first baseman *Bill Buckner*'s legs.
- Outfielder *Carl Yastrzemski* won the American League Triple Crown during the beloved 1967 *Impossible Dream* pennant run, carrying the team to the World Series following decades of postwar irrelevance.
- Entered the modern era with championships in 2004, 2007, 2013, and 2018, the last achieved during a franchise-record 108-win regular season led by manager *Alex Cora* and MVP outfielder *Mookie Betts*.
- Historic franchise figures include ace *Cy Young*, who threw the first perfect game in modern baseball history in 1904, hard-throwing right-hander *Roger Clemens*, and Hall of Fame designated hitter *David Ortiz*.

## connections

- [[the-new-york-yankees]] — fiercest historic rival in North American sports, contesting the American League East division and the monumental 2004 ALCS.
- [[babe-ruth]] — began his career as an elite left-handed pitcher and power hitter in Boston before being sold to New York in 1919.
- [[cal-ripken-jr]] — long-time American League peer and American League East adversary with the *Baltimore Orioles*.
- [[derek-jeter]] — captain of the rival Yankees who battled the Red Sox in numerous iconic regular season clashes and postseason series.
- [[mariano-rivera]] — Hall of Fame Yankee closer whom Boston famously solved during their miraculous 2004 ALCS comeback.
- [[mickey-mantle]] — mid-century Yankee slugger who frequently squared off against Boston legends *Ted Williams* and *Carl Yastrzemski*.
- [[united-states]] — foundational American sports institution shaping modern sports culture across New England and beyond.

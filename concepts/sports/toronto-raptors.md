## summary

The **Toronto Raptors** are a professional basketball franchise based in Toronto, Ontario, that competes in the Eastern Conference of the National Basketball Association. Founded in 1995 alongside the Vancouver Grizzlies as part of the league's international expansion into Canada, the **Raptors** became the sole Canadian team in the league following Vancouver's relocation to Memphis in 2001. The team reached the pinnacle of international basketball prominence by capturing their first league championship in 2019, galvanizing a passionate national fan base under the unifying slogan *We the North*.

## you gotta know

- Captured their first championship title in 2019 by defeating the Golden State Warriors in six games behind Finals Most Valuable Player *Kawhi Leonard*, becoming the first franchise located outside the [[united-states]] to win the Larry O'Brien Trophy.
- Advanced to the 2019 championship round on the strength of *Kawhi Leonard*'s dramatic Game 7 buzzer-beater against the Philadelphia 76ers, a baseline jumper that bounced four times on the rim before falling in the first Game 7 walk-off buzzer-beater in league history.
- Established an electrifying national profile during the early 2000s under high-flying superstar *Vince Carter*, whose iconic performance in the 2000 Slam Dunk Contest put the Canadian club on the global sporting map.
- Selected their distinctive nickname through a nationwide naming contest in 1994, riding the massive pop-culture popularity generated by *Steven Spielberg*'s blockbuster film *Jurassic Park*.
- Rebuilt into an annual perennial contender throughout the 2010s under team president *Masai Ujiri* and head coach *Dwane Casey*, driven by the celebrated backcourt partnership of *Kyle Lowry* and *DeMar DeRozan*.
- Drafted *Damon Stoudamire*, nicknamed *Mighty Mouse*, with their inaugural first-round pick in 1995, who went on to capture the Rookie of the Year Award while the club initially played home games inside the cavernous SkyDome.
- Competed through the 2018–19 championship campaign under rookie head coach *Nick Nurse*, who deployed unconventional box-and-one defensive schemes to neutralize opposing backcourt superstars.

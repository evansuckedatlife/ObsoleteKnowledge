## summary

The **Kentucky Derby** is an iconic American Grade I stakes horse race held annually on the first Saturday in May at *Churchill Downs* in Louisville, Kentucky. First contested in 1875, the one-and-a-quarter-mile race for three-year-old Thoroughbreds serves as the opening jewel of the American Triple Crown. Known colloquially as **The Run for the Roses** and **The Most Exciting Two Minutes in Sports**, it is the longest continuously held major sporting event in the *[[united-states]]* and a cornerstone of American sporting culture.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the first leg of the American Triple Crown of Thoroughbred Racing, preceding the *Preakness Stakes* at Pimlico and the *Belmont Stakes* in New York.
- Held at *Churchill Downs*, which was founded by *Meriwether Lewis Clark Jr.*, grandson of explorer *William Clark*, on land leased from his uncles John and Henry Churchill.
- Nicknamed **The Run for the Roses** because the winning horse is draped in an elaborate blanket of over 500 red roses, a tradition popularized by New York sports columnist *Bill Corum*.
- The inaugural 1875 race was won by *Aristides*, ridden by African American jockey *Oliver Lewis* and trained by former enslaved horseman *Ansel Williamson*; Black jockeys dominated the race's early decades, winning fifteen of the first twenty-eight runnings, including three victories by *Isaac Murphy*.
- The standard distance was originally one-and-a-half miles, matching the *Epsom Derby* in *[[england]]*, before being shortened in 1896 to its current distance of one-and-a-quarter miles (ten furlongs).
- Celebrated alongside cultural traditions such as the drinking of mint juleps from souvenir silver or julep cups and the singing of *Stephen Foster*'s ballad *My Old Kentucky Home* during the post parade.
- *Secretariat* set the enduring track record in 1973 with an astonishing time of 1:59.40, running each quarter-mile split faster than the previous one on his way to sweeping the Triple Crown.
- In 2019, *Maximum Security* became the first winner disqualified on race day for an in-race foul after causing interference around the final turn, elevating 65-1 longshot *Country House* to first place.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — premier equestrian tradition and longest continuously run sporting event in the nation.
- [[american-civil-war]] — Churchill Downs was developed during the *[[reconstruction-era]]* aftermath to revive Kentucky's antebellum equestrian economy.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — period during which African American jockeys like *Isaac Murphy* established legendary early records.
- [[british-empire]] — organized following *Meriwether Lewis Clark Jr.*'s visit to England's *Epsom Derby* and France's Grand Prix de Paris.
- [[france]] — inspired by French racing clubs and stakes systems studied during Clark's nineteenth-century European tour.
- [[larry-bird]] — fellow sports legend from the neighboring Ohio Valley state of Indiana whose collegiate championship overlapped with national sports prominence.
- [[lance-armstrong]] — represents the pinnacle of American endurance athletic prestige alongside major international racing fixtures.

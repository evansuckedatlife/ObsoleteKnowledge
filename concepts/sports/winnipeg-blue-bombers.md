## summary

The **Winnipeg Blue Bombers** are a storied professional Canadian football franchise based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, competing in the West Division of the Canadian Football League. Formed in 1930 through the amalgamation of local amateur sides, the **Blue Bombers** stand among the oldest continuously operating gridiron football clubs in North America and boast twelve Grey Cup championships. Their historic accomplishments and resilient prairie following make the team a foundational institution in the development of Canadian sports culture.

## you gotta know

- Claimed twelve Grey Cup championships across nine decades, achieving historic victories that include back-to-back modern titles in 2019 and 2021 under head coach *Mike O'Shea*.
- Made history in 1935 by becoming the very first team located in Western Canada to win the national Grey Cup championship, upsetting the Hamilton Tigers behind star import player *Fritzie Hanson*.
- Acquired their famous moniker in 1935 when local sports journalist *Vince Leah* compared the team's blue-clad defensive prowess to heavyweight boxing champion *Joe Louis*, widely celebrated as the *Brown Bomber*.
- Dominated the late 1950s and early 1960s under legendary head coach *Bud Grant*, who secured four championships between 1958 and 1962 before achieving legendary renown leading the Minnesota Vikings.
- Snapped an agonizing twenty-eight-year championship drought in November 2019, overpowering the Hamilton Tiger-Cats behind veteran quarterback *Zach Collaros* and dominant Canadian running back *Andrew Harris*.
- Featured legendary slotback *Milt Stegall*, who established the all-time Canadian Football League career touchdown record of 147 while earning legendary acclaim across fourteen memorable seasons in Winnipeg.
- Continued competition through pivotal periods including the [[world-war-ii]] era, when armed service personnel bolstered prairie rosters while traditional schedules were modified across the Dominion.

## summary

**Marie-Philip Poulin**, widely celebrated as **Captain Clutch**, is a legendary Canadian *ice hockey* forward and long-time captain of the Canadian national team. Renowned for her unmatched composure under pressure, she achieved international acclaim by scoring the gold-medal-winning goals in three separate *Winter Olympic* tournaments across three different decades. Her enduring dominance in international championships and professional women's leagues cements her status as one of the most consequential figures in modern winter sports history.

## you gotta know

- Earned the nickname **Captain Clutch** by scoring the decisive, game-winning goals in three separate *Winter Olympic* gold medal finals (2010, 2014, and 2022).
- Debuted at the Vancouver 2010 [[olympic-games]] as an eighteen-year-old phenom, scoring both goals in a 2–0 shutout against the *United States* to capture her first gold medal.
- Spearheaded a legendary comeback in the gold medal game at Sochi in 2014, scoring the tying goal with under a minute left in regulation before netting the overtime golden goal to defeat the Americans 3–2.
- Set a historic hockey benchmark at the Beijing 2022 [[olympic-games]] by scoring twice in a 3–2 gold medal victory, making her the only player in hockey history of any gender to score in four consecutive Olympic gold medal games.
- Led Boston University to multiple NCAA tournament appearances and the 2015 *Hockey East* championship, concluding her collegiate career as a top finalist for the *Patty Kazmaier Award*.
- Dominated professional club hockey in the *Canadian Women's Hockey League* (CWHL) with the *Montreal Canadiennes*, capturing two *Clarkson Cup* championships and winning multiple *Angela James Bowl* scoring titles and Most Valuable Player honors.
- Served as a pivotal organizing force in the *Professional Women's Hockey Players Association* (PWHPA), advocating for professional labor standards that led directly to the formation of the *Professional Women's Hockey League* (PWHL) and captaining *PWHL Montreal*.
- Honored as Canada's co-flag bearer alongside short-track speed skater *Charles Hamelin* during the opening ceremony of the Beijing 2022 Olympic Winter Games.

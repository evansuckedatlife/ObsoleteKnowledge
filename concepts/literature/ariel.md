## summary

**Ariel** is an airy, shape-shifting spirit in [[william-shakespeare]]'s late romance [[the-tempest]], composed around 1611 during the [[english-renaissance]]. Rescued by the exiled Milanese duke and sorcerer [[prospero]] from a twelve-year imprisonment inside a cloven pine tree, the spirit serves as the master's primary supernatural agent on an enchanted island in the Mediterranean. Ariel executes intricate illusions, stirs magical tempests, and manipulates shipwrecked courtiers, all while persistently negotiating for promised personal emancipation. Celebrated for evocative songs and otherworldly grace, the figure embodies the transformative power of theatrical art and stands in stark thematic contrast to the earthbound servant [[caliban]].

## you gotta know

- Enacts the opening tempest at the command of [[prospero]], boarding the royal vessel in flashes of flame resembling Saint Elmo's fire to divide and terrify the courtiers while preserving every passenger unharmed.
- Imprisoned for twelve agonizing years within a ruptured pine by the witch [[sycorax]], who punished the spirit for refusing to perform abhorrent sorceries before [[prospero]] arrived on the island to break the curse.
- Lures the grieving prince *Ferdinand* across the shores using bewitching music and ethereal songs, including *Come unto these yellow sands* and the haunting dirge *Full fathom five thy father lies*, which introduces the motif of a mystical sea-change.
- Manifests as an avenging Harpy amid thunder and lightning to snatch away a banquet from *Alonso*, *Antonio*, and *Sebastian*, denouncing them as "three men of sin" for their past betrayal of [[prospero]].
- Functions as the moral catalyst for the drama's resolution by expressing sympathy for the distraught Italian prisoners, observing that their suffering would move a spirit's heart "were I human," which persuades [[prospero]] to choose reconciliation over revenge.
- Frustrates the assassination plot devised by [[caliban]], the drunken jester *Trinculo*, and the butler *Stephano* by playing tunes invisibly upon a tabor and pipe and baiting them into a filthy marsh with glittering royal garments.
- Oversees the masque of Ceres, Iris, and Juno celebrating the betrothal of *Miranda* and *Ferdinand*, directing spirits who perform pastoral dances before the vision is abruptly dissolved.
- Receives final, unconditional freedom to fly into the winds and elements once [[prospero]] abjures his [[magic]], breaks his staff, and secures calm seas for the fleet's safe return journey to [[italy]].

## connections

- [[prospero]] — the exiled Duke of Milan whose esoteric arts Ariel dutifully serves in pursuit of promised liberty.
- [[caliban]] — the deformed island native whose bitter resentment and curses form a dramatic contrast to Ariel's ethereal compliance.
- [[the-tempest]] — the romance play by [[william-shakespeare]] centered on the enchanted island where Ariel executes music, tempests, and illusions.
- [[william-shakespeare]] — the English playwright who fashioned Ariel as an enduring embodiment of theatrical artifice and poetic imagination.
- [[english-renaissance]] — the cultural era whose court masques, pastoral drama, and humanist themes shape the staging of Ariel's spirits.
- [[poetry]] — the lyrical form of Ariel's musical airs, which rank among the most famous songs in English drama.
- [[magic]] — the supernatural craft exercised by [[prospero]] and channeled through Ariel to enchant the island and manipulate mortal minds.
- [[italy]] — the Mediterranean setting of the courtiers' origins in Naples and Milan, whose departure marks Ariel's ultimate liberation.

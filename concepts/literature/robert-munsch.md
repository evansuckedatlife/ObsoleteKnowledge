## summary

**Robert Munsch** is an American-born Canadian children's author and storyteller recognized as one of the best-selling picture-book writers in North American history. Renowned for his exaggerated vocal delivery, slapstick situations, and refrains born of improvised oral performances at daycares, he redefined modern juvenile literature. His enduring works, most notably the subversive fairytale *The Paper Bag Princess* and the perennial sentimental classic *Love You Forever*, have sold tens of millions of copies worldwide and profoundly shaped early-childhood literacy across Canada and the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Renowned for *The Paper Bag Princess*, illustrated by *Michael Martchenko*, which subverts chivalric tropes when Princess *Elizabeth* outsmarts a dragon to rescue Prince *Ronald*, only to reject him when he insults her unkempt appearance.
- Penned the bestselling tearjerker *Love You Forever*, which features the recurring lullaby *"I'll love you forever, I'll like you for always"*, originally written in grief after he and his wife suffered two stillbirths.
- Developed his distinctive writing process through live oral improvisation while working at preschools and daycares in Guelph, Ontario, telling stories hundreds of times before setting words to paper.
- Frequently partnered with illustrator *Michael Martchenko*, whose dynamic watercolor paintings provided the signature chaotic aesthetic for classic books like *Mud Puddle* and *The Dark*.
- Collaborated directly with real children across North America, naming protagonists after real youths he met and sending them manuscripts, as seen in *Stephanie's Ponytail*, *Angela's Airplane*, and *Thomas' Snowsuit*.
- Began his adult life training for seven years to enter the Jesuit priesthood before dedicating his career to early childhood education and narrative craft.
- Received the Order of Canada in 1999 and was inducted into Canada's Walk of Fame in recognition of his transformative cultural and literacy contributions.

## summary

**Charlton Heston**, born **John Charles Carter**, was an iconic American actor whose commanding physical presence and heroic baritone defined mid-century historical epics and dystopian cinema. Winning the Academy Award for Best Actor for the 1959 biblical spectacular *Ben-Hur*, he transitioned in the late 1960s and 1970s into the quintessential face of cerebral, apocalyptic [[science-fiction]] in the [[united-states]]. His performances captured the profound ideological anxieties, environmental fears, and civilizational exhaustion of the [[cold-war]] era.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Actor for playing Judah Ben-Hur in the 1959 epic directed by *William Wyler*.
- Portrayed Moses in *Cecil B. DeMille*'s massive biblical epic *The Ten Commandments*, solidifying his status as Hollywood's premier heroic patriarch.
- Anchored landmark speculative fiction as astronaut George Taylor in the 1968 classic *Planet of the Apes*, delivering the legendary despairing beach revelation before the ruined Statue of Liberty.
- Starred in 1971's *The Omega Man*, adapting *Richard Matheson*'s vampire-apocalypse novel *I Am Legend* to dramatize solitary biological survival.
- Uncovered the horrifying industrial secret behind institutional sustenance as detective Robert Thorn in the 1973 dystopian thriller *Soylent Green*.
- Trained extensively in classical theatre and frequently staged the works of [[william-shakespeare]], directing and starring in cinematic adaptations of *Antony and Cleopatra*.
- Served as president of the Screen Actors Guild and later became a prominent conservative political figure and president of the National Rifle Association.

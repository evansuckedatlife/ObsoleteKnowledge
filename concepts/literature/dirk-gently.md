## summary

**Dirk Gently** is the eccentric protagonist and titular framework of two comic mystery novels by British author *Douglas Adams*, originating with *Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency* in 1987. Operating on the core principle of the "fundamental interconnectedness of all things," Gently eschews conventional forensic deduction to resolve absurd paranormal crimes and metaphysical crises. The series represents a major landmark in comedic speculative fiction, synthesizing time travel, quantum mechanics, and literary parody within the detective genre.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Douglas Adams* following the immense international success of his radio series and book franchise *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*.
- Operates his agency under the belief that every event in the universe is causally linked, often billing clients for nonsensical expenses like beach vacations in the Bahamas under the guise of holistic investigation.
- Born under the name *Svlad Cjelli*, he first demonstrated psychic abilities at the University of Cambridge by accurately guessing exam papers, which resulted in his expulsion and imprisonment.
- Incorporates unused narrative concepts and characters that Adams originally developed for unproduced or rewritten episodes of *Doctor Who*, notably the legendary unfinished serial *Shada*.
- Solves a sprawling mystery in the debut novel that ties together an electric monk, the poetry of *Samuel Taylor Coleridge*, a dodo, and an alien ghost seeking to alter Earth's primordial past.
- Expanded in the 1988 sequel novel *The Long Dark Tea-Time of the Soul*, which pits Gently against Norse gods displaced into modern London, including an ailing *Odin* and a furious *Thor*.
- Left unfinished upon Adams's death in 2001, with the fragments of an incomplete third novel later compiled and published posthumously as *The Salmon of Doubt*.
- Adapted into a radio drama for the BBC, a 2010 television series starring *Stephen Mangan*, and a 2016 BBC America adaptation created by *Max Landis* starring *Samuel Barnett* and *Elijah Wood*.

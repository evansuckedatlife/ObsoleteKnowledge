## summary

**Coming-of-age** is a fundamental narrative archetype and genre convention wherein a protagonist moves from childhood or adolescence into adulthood, undergoing psychological, moral, and social transformation. These narratives, often called *bildungsromane* when they take novelistic form, trace the protagonist's journey through formative experiences, disappointments, and revelations that shape their adult identity. Coming-of-age stories resonate across all cultures and eras because they map the universal human transition from innocence to knowledge, from dependence to autonomy, and from illusion to a more complex understanding of self and world.

## you gotta know

- The German term *Bildungsroman* ("education novel") emerged in the late eighteenth century but the archetype is ancient, rooted in myths and religious texts about initiation.
- Central to the coming-of-age arc: the protagonist encounters a disorienting or traumatic event that forces growth; they must navigate between conflicting values or loyalties; they emerge changed, often sadder but wiser.
- Key works include *Great Expectations* (Dickens), *David Copperfield* (Dickens), *A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man* (Joyce), and *The Catcher in the Rye* (Salinger).
- The genre is not confined to children's literature; coming-of-age narratives appear across all genres and forms, from memoir to science fiction.
- Thematic preoccupations include loss of innocence, sexual awakening, identity formation, rebellion against authority, and the search for meaning and belonging.
- The contemporary young-adult (YA) boom has revitalised the coming-of-age form, making it one of publishing's most commercially successful categories.

## connections

- [[bildungsroman]] — the German literary form that crystallises the coming-of-age narrative into a distinct genre.
- [[childrens-literature]] — coming-of-age narratives are central to the appeal of children's and YA fiction, though not confined to it.
- [[first-love]] — often a pivotal component of coming-of-age narratives, marking the protagonist's romantic awakening.
- [[self-discovery]] — the thematic core of the coming-of-age arc, wherein the protagonist learns who they truly are.
- [[great-expectations]] — a canonical English coming-of-age novel that shaped the form in the Victorian era.
- [[the-catcher-in-the-rye]] — a twentieth-century coming-of-age masterpiece exploring adolescent alienation and identity.

## summary

**Pip** (Phillip Pirrip) is the protagonist of *Charles Dickens's* *Great Expectations*, an orphan boy whose desire to escape his humble origins and win the love of an unattainable girl drives him toward self-destruction. His journey from innocence through illusion to hard-won moral reckoning makes him one of literature's great coming-of-age figures, embodying the tension between ambition and authenticity.

## you gotta know

- An orphan raised by his sister and her blacksmith husband *Joe* in the Kent marshes, he works as an apprentice despite yearning for something grander.
- Falls in love with *Estella*, the beautiful, icy ward of the wealthy *Miss Havisham*, and becomes convinced this is his "great expectation"—his destined path to gentility.
- Receives an anonymous fortune (which he assumes comes from Miss Havisham) and abandons Joe to become a London gentleman, ashamed of his humble past.
- Discovers too late that his benefactor is actually *Magwitch*, an escaped convict he once helped as a child, destroying his illusions about the source and meaning of his expectations.
- His shame at Magwitch's identity and his obsessive devotion to Estella lead him toward ruin—emotionally, financially, and morally.
- Eventually reconciles with Joe and learns to value integrity over social aspiration; his growth involves accepting, not escaping, his past.

## connections

- [[great-expectations]] — the novel in which Pip's moral education unfolds.
- [[miss-havisham]] — the wealthy eccentric whose house and affection Pip mistakes for the source of his "great expectations."
- [[estella]] — the beautiful woman he loves but who is emotionally incapable of returning his affection; his pursuit of her mirrors his pursuit of false expectations.
- [[charles-dickens]] — Pip's creator, who uses him to explore social ambition and the costs of shame.

- [[magwitch]] — the convict whose rescue of Pip as a child echoes throughout; revealed as Pip's true benefactor.

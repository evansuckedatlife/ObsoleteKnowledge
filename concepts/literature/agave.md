## summary

**Agave** is a princess of *Thebes* in Greek mythology and dramatic literature, best remembered as the tragic mother of King *Pentheus* in *Euripides*' masterpiece *The Bacchae*. Driven into divine madness by her nephew, the god *Dionysus*, she joins the intoxicated maenads on Mount *Cithaeron* and unwittingly dismembers her own son under the delusion that he is a mountain lion. Her devastating recognition scene, in which her father *Cadmus* coaxes her out of frenzy to discover she is cradling her son's severed head, stands as one of the most celebrated and harrowing depictions of *anagnorisis* in classical [[ancient-greece|Greek]] [[tragedy]].

## you gotta know

- In *Euripides*' [[tragedy]] *The Bacchae*, she commits the ultimate act of unwitting filicide by dismembering her son, King *Pentheus*, while possessed by the bacchic frenzy of *Dionysus*.
- Carries the severed head of *Pentheus* back to the city of *Thebes* impaled upon her *thyrsus*, boasting proudly to the citizens that she has hunted and killed a magnificent wild lion with her bare hands.
- Experiences one of the most famous recognition scenes (*anagnorisis*) in classical [[ancient-greece|Greek]] drama, as her grief-stricken father *Cadmus* patiently prompts her to look up toward the sun and sky until her divine delusions dissolve and she realizes the bloody trophy she holds is her own child.
- Sinned against the god *Dionysus* alongside her sisters *Ino* and *Autonoe* by spreading slanderous rumors that their sister *Semele* had lied about sleeping with *Zeus* and had been killed for falsely claiming divine paternity.
- Participates with the ecstatic maenads in the ritual act of *sparagmos*—the violent tearing apart of living flesh—on Mount *Cithaeron*, ripping herds of cattle to shreds before turning upon the voyeuristic *Pentheus*, who had been spying on their rites disguised in women's clothing from atop a tall fir tree.
- Belongs to the cursed royal house of *Thebes* as the daughter of city founder *Cadmus* and goddess *Harmonia*, sharing her doomed family line with her unfortunate sisters and her grandnephew *Oedipus*.
- Sentenced to permanent exile from *Thebes* at the conclusion of *The Bacchae* by *Dionysus*, who appears *ex machina* to scatter the royal dynasty, prompting her despairing departure toward *Illyria* where later Roman mythographer *Hyginus* claims she murdered King *Lycoreses* to deliver the realm to her father.

## connections

- [[euripides]] — Athenian playwright who dramatized her ecstatic madness, filicide, and harrowing grief in his posthumous masterpiece *The Bacchae*.
- [[pentheus]] — her obstinate son whose refusal to honor the new god leads directly to his gruesome butchery at her hands.
- [[dionysus]] — her divine nephew who inflicts mania upon the Theban women to avenge her slander and prove his divinity.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the classical civilization whose theatrical and religious festivals gave dramatic form to her tragic legend.
- [[tragedy]] — the literary genre defined by her stark reversal of fortune, devastating guilt, and catastrophic recognition.
- [[sophocles]] — Athenian tragedian whose Theban dramas similarly chronicle the hereditary curses and divine retribution plaguing her lineage.
- [[cadmus]] — her venerable father who tenderly awakens her from bacchic mania and forces her to confront the horrific reality of her kill.

## summary

**Naturalism** is a literary movement that extends realism toward determinism and scientific materialism, depicting human beings as products of heredity, environment, and circumstance rather than moral agents exercising free will. Emerging in late nineteenth-century France with *Émile Zola*, naturalism sought to apply scientific method to fiction: observe society as a naturalist observes nature, dispassionately recording causes and effects. Naturalist fiction tends toward bleaker conclusions than realism: poverty, illness, and social forces overwhelm individual aspiration, and narrative often traces inevitable decline rather than moral improvement.

## you gotta know

- *Émile Zola*, the movement's theorist and leading practitioner, wrote the manifesto *Le Roman expérimental* (*The Experimental Novel*), arguing fiction could and should mirror scientific inquiry.
- Naturalist fiction focused on working-class life, urban degradation, sexual excess, and physiological detail—subjects considered too sordid for genteel literature.
- Key texts include Zola's *Nana* and *Germinal*, and American works like *Theodore Dreiser's* *Sister Carrie*, which traces a woman's descent into prostitution.
- Unlike realism, which aims at faithful representation, naturalism explicitly theorizes social and biological determination: individual choice becomes secondary to forces beyond the character's control.
- *Guy de Maupassant* represents a middle ground: his stories apply naturalist observation to middle-class and rural life with psychological precision and dark irony.
- Naturalism influenced Russian literature: *Dostoevsky* and others engaged with naturalist themes of urban alienation and psychological collapse.
- The movement intersected with visual art: naturalist painters like *Courbet* sought to depict ordinary people and landscape without idealization or sentimentality.
- Naturalist authors treated their characters clinically: rather than moral judgment, they offered scientific observation of how poverty, hunger, and biological drive override human aspiration.

## connections

- [[realism]] — naturalism evolved from realism but added explicit materialism and determinism to its methods and philosophy.
- [[guy-de-maupassant]] — French writer who combined naturalist observation with psychological subtlety and formal perfection.
- [[american-realism]] — American naturalist writers like *Dreiser* adapted the movement to American industrial capitalism and immigrant urban life.
- [[russian-literature]] — Russian writers engaged with naturalist themes of psychological dissolution and social determinism.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the historical context that generated naturalism's interest in working-class life and urban social forces.

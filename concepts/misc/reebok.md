## summary

**Reebok** is a historic multinational athletic footwear and apparel brand founded in England with roots tracing directly back to late nineteenth-century running innovations. Originally emerging from the pioneering spike manufacturing of **J.W. Foster and Sons**, the modern entity took its name from an Afrikaans term for the grey rhebok antelope. The enterprise grew into a dominant commercial sportswear powerhouse during the late twentieth century, largely by revolutionizing the aerobic exercise craze and securing marquee athletic sponsorships worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Traces its lineage back to 1895 in Bolton, Lancashire, when Joseph William Foster created some of the earliest spiked track running shoes.
- Founded under its modern corporate name in 1958 by grandsons Joe and Jeff Foster, taking inspiration from the swift South African *rhebok* antelope found in a dictionary.
- Displayed the British Union Jack flag prominently across its classic lifestyle shoes, cementing its identity within twentieth-century sportswear heritage.
- Exploded into the mainstream North American market in 1982 with the release of the *Freestyle*, the first athletic shoe specifically engineered and marketed for women's aerobics.
- Introduced the iconic inflatable *Reebok Pump* mechanism in 1989, allowing wearers to adjust ankle support via a miniature basketball-shaped bladder on the tongue.
- Propelled to massive basketball prominence during the 1991 NBA Slam Dunk Contest when Dee Brown visibly inflated his sneakers before executing a no-look dunk.
- Acquired by German sportswear rival Adidas in 2005 before being purchased by Authentic Brands Group in 2021.

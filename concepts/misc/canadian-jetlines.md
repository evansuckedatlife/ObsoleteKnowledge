## summary

**Canadian Jetlines** was an ultra-low-cost commercial airline headquartered in Mississauga, Ontario, established to challenge domestic carrier dominance in Canadian airspace. Conceived in 2013 and commencing scheduled passenger operations in September 2022 using an all-Airbus A320 fleet, the carrier sought to offer unbundled, discount point-to-point service across North America and the Caribbean. Plagued by intense market competition, high operational costs, and capital shortages, the airline abruptly ceased all flight operations in August 2024.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 2013 by aviation entrepreneurs aiming to replicate the bare-bones, unbundled pricing models popularized by European ultra-low-cost carriers like *Ryanair* and *easyJet*.
- Operated primarily out of *Toronto Pearson International Airport*, flying routes to popular leisure and vacation destinations within Canada, the [[united-states]], Mexico, and the Caribbean.
- Utilized a narrow-body fleet exclusively comprising leased *Airbus A320-200* passenger aircraft configured for single-class high-density seating.
- Faced severe regulatory hurdles and foreign ownership restriction debates before receiving its *Air Operator Certificate* (*AOC*) from *Transport Canada* in August 2022.
- Competed in an intensely consolidated domestic market historically dominated by legacy giants *Air Canada* and *WestJet*, as well as contemporaneous discount startups like *Swoop* and *Lynx Air*.
- Provided wet-lease and ad-hoc aircraft, crew, maintenance, and insurance (*ACMI*) charter services to international airlines alongside its scheduled scheduled passenger network.
- Suspended all flight services on August 15, 2024, following the mass resignation of its board members and executive leadership, subsequently filing for bankruptcy protection.

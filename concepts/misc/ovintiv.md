## summary

**Ovintiv** is a major North American hydrocarbon exploration and production corporation, created through the historical evolution of one of Canada's most powerful resource empires. Originally formed in 2002 as *Encana Corporation* through the blockbuster merger of *Alberta Energy Company* and *PanCanadian Petroleum*, it stood as an emblem of modern energy extraction. In 2020, the company rebranded as Ovintiv and controversially relocated its corporate domicile from Calgary to Denver in the [[united-states]] to access deeper pools of capital.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 2002 by the merger of two pioneer Canadian energy enterprises: *PanCanadian Petroleum* (rooted in the Canadian Pacific Railway's nineteenth-century land grants) and the *Alberta Energy Company*.
- Under longtime chief executive *Gwyn Morgan*, the company pioneered large-scale commercial unconventional shale extraction and hydraulic fracturing techniques in North America.
- Formerly ranked as the largest independent natural gas producer in North America before spinning off its oil sands holdings into *Cenovus Energy* in 2009.
- Rebranded from *Encana* to Ovintiv in early 2020 under CEO *Doug Suttles*, combining Latin-derived morphemes suggesting innovation and continuous motion.
- Sparked national political debate in Canada by exiting Calgary to establish its primary legal domicile and executive headquarters in Denver, Colorado.
- Holds core production assets across premier unconventional petroleum basins in the [[united-states]] and Canada, including the Permian Basin, the Anadarko Basin, and the Montney Formation.
- Trades publicly on both the Toronto Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange in [[new-york-city]].

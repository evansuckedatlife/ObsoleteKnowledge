## summary

The **Potash Corporation of Saskatchewan**, widely known as **PotashCorp**, was a major Canadian fertilizer and mineral producer headquartered in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. Established as a provincial Crown corporation in 1975 before its privatization in 1989, it became the world's largest producer of potash by capacity, alongside extensive nitrogen and phosphate operations. In 2018, the company concluded a massive cross-border merger with Calgary-based *Agrium* to form *Nutrien*, shaping modern international agribusiness and crop nutrition.

## you gotta know

- Created in 1975 by the provincial government of Saskatchewan under New Democratic Party Premier *Allan Blakeney* to secure public ownership and assert regulatory control over rich local potassium deposits.
- Privatized in 1989 by the progressive conservative administration of Premier *Grant Devine*, subsequently transforming into an aggressive global player traded publicly in Toronto and New York.
- Tapped into the expansive Middle Devonian Prairie Evaporite Formation beneath southern Saskatchewan, home to the richest and largest reserves of potassium chloride on Earth.
- Subject to a hostile $38.6 billion takeover bid by Anglo-Australian mining giant *BHP Billiton* in 2010, which was ultimately blocked by the Canadian federal government under the *Investment Canada Act* as lacking "net benefit."
- Controlled an influential export marketing cartel called *Canpotex* alongside fellow Canadian producers, negotiating global pricing contracts with buyers across China, India, and Latin America.
- Merged in January 2018 with retail agricultural supplier *Agrium* to create *Nutrien*, creating an integrated global agriculture input giant.

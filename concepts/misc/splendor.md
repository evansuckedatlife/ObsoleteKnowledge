## summary

**Splendor** is an engine-building and card-drafting board game designed by *Marc André* and illustrated by *Pascal Quidault*, published in 2014 by *Space Cowboys* in *[[france]]*. Players assume the roles of wealthy gem merchants during the European *[[renaissance]]*, acquiring mines, trade routes, and artisan workshops to amass prestige. Widely celebrated as a quintessential modern gateway game, it earned a nomination for the prestigious *Spiel des Jahres* and remains a staple in tabletop competition.

## you gotta know

- Designed by French board game designer *Marc André* and published in 2014 by the studio *Space Cowboys*, quickly becoming one of the most commercially successful European board games of the decade.
- Earned a nomination for the 2014 *Spiel des Jahres* and won the *Golden Geek Board Game of the Year* award, lauded for its simple rules, tactile poker chips, and deep engine-building mechanics.
- The gameplay revolves around collecting gem tokens representing five primary stones—emerald, sapphire, ruby, diamond, and onyx—as well as gold wild tokens.
- Players spend collected gem chips to purchase development cards arranged across three tiered decks, with each purchased card granting both a permanent discount bonus on future purchases and prestige points.
- Noble patron tiles award additional victory points automatically to the first player who amasses a specified combination of development card bonuses.
- The game triggers its end round immediately when any single player reaches fifteen prestige points, concluding after the current round finishes so all players have had an equal number of turns.
- Spawned an expansion pack titled *Cities of Splendor* in 2017, which introduced four modular variants that replace or augment the standard noble patrons and endgame objectives.
- A dedicated two-player spin-off, *Splendor Duel*, co-designed by *Bruno Cathala*, introduced an interactive central gem board with tactile scrolls, pearl resources, and multiple alternative win conditions.

## connections

- [[renaissance]] — the historical era of merchant capitalism and luxury gem trade that provides the thematic setting for the game.
- [[france]] — the country where designer *Marc André* and publishing house *Space Cowboys* developed and launched the title.
- [[geometry]] — underlying the spatial optimization and economic engine grids that govern card and resource collection.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the historical era represented by the maritime trade routes and overseas gem mines depicted on higher-tier development cards.
- [[stalemate]] — contrast in game termination, as the engine-building mechanics of *Splendor* prevent deadlock through steadily accumulating card discounts.

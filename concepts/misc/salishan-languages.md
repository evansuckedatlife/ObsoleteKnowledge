## summary

The **Salishan languages** form an indigenous language family indigenous to the Pacific Northwest region of North America, spanning Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and British Columbia. Renowned among linguists for their remarkable phonological complexity, heavy polysynthesis, and extensive consonant clustering, they challenge conventional cross-linguistic typological assumptions. Today, while critically endangered, they are the focus of extensive revitalization initiatives and foundational studies in morphological theory.

## you gotta know

- Indigenous to the Pacific Northwest Coast and Plateau cultural areas, comprising roughly twenty-three distinct languages divided into Coast Salish, Interior Salish, and Bella Coola (*Nuxalk*) branches, alongside the extinct *Tillamook*.
- Renowned in [[linguistics]] for extraordinary consonant inventories that include glottalized stops, ejectives, uvular consonants, and lateral fricatives, often paired with very small vowel systems.
- Famous for allowing long, vowelless obstruent clusters, most strikingly exemplified by *Nuxalk*, which permits entire grammatical sentences composed exclusively of voiceless consonants without any vowels or sonorants.
- Displays intense grammatical debate regarding parts of speech, as several prominent field linguists have argued that languages like *St'at'imcets* and *Nuxalk* lack a strict syntactic distinction between nouns and verbs, employing ubiquitous predicate-initial constructions instead.
- Relies heavily on polysynthetic morphology, using extensive systems of prefixes, infixes, and particularly lexical suffixes that incorporate concrete semantic meanings like body parts or natural entities directly into verb stems.
- Makes prolific use of reduplication across roots to signal grammatical categories including noun plurals, diminutives, continuous aspect, and distributive actions.
- Includes *Lushootseed*, historically spoken around Puget Sound by indigenous communities whose leader *Chief Sealth* gave his name to the city of Seattle.

## connections

- [[linguistics]] — landmark family in linguistic typology that reshaped theoretical understanding of phonotactics, syllable structure, and parts-of-speech universals.
- [[united-states]] — spoken across several states in the American Northwest, where tribal nations lead vigorous language reclamation programs.
- [[indian-removal]] — decimated historically by forced assimilation policies, boarding schools, and federal displacement of Native American communities.
- [[colonialism]] — targeted by colonial bans on indigenous speech in both Canada and the American West, driving widespread language endangerment.
- [[british-empire]] — disrupted through the establishment of British colonial administration and mission schools throughout nineteenth-century British Columbia.

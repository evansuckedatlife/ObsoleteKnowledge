## summary

**Ramadan** is the ninth month of the [[islamic-calendar]], observed by Muslims worldwide as a period of fasting, prayer, and spiritual reflection. During daylight hours, Muslims abstain from food, water, and other worldly indulgences as an act of devotion and self-discipline. It commemorates the revelation of the [[quran]] to the Prophet Muhammad and is one of the Five Pillars of Islam.

## you gotta know

- Ramadan is determined by the lunar calendar, so it shifts roughly 11 days earlier each solar year; the same month cycles through all seasons over 33 years.
- The fast (*sawm*) requires abstention from dawn to sunset; sick, elderly, pregnant, and menstruating individuals are exempted and may fast at another time.
- Pre-dawn meal is *suhoor*, breaking the fast is *iftar*; the iftar is often a communal celebration, reinforcing bonds within Muslim families and communities.
- Taraweeh prayers (special evening prayers) are performed throughout Ramadan, often reading through the entire Quran over the month.
- The Night of Power (*Laylat al-Qadr*) falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan and is believed to be when the Quran was first revealed; worship this night is considered especially meritorious.
- Ramadan emphasizes empathy for the poor and hungry; Muslims are encouraged to give *zakat* (alms) and perform acts of charity.
- The month concludes with *Eid al-Fitr*, a joyous festival and public holiday in Muslim-majority countries, marked by prayers, feasting, and gift-giving.

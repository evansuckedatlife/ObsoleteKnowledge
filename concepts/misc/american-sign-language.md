## summary

**American Sign Language** (**ASL**) is the complete, autonomous visual-gestural language developed and utilized by Deaf communities throughout the [[united-states]] and Anglophone Canada. Established in the early nineteenth century at the *American School for the Deaf* in Connecticut, the language grew from a rich synthesis of French Sign Language, regional indigenous sign systems, and domestic home signs. It serves as the primary linguistic medium of American Deaf culture and stands as a celebrated case study in formal modern [[linguistics]], demonstrating that full linguistic grammar operates independently of vocal articulation.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1817 at the *American School for the Deaf* in *Hartford, Connecticut*, by educator *Thomas Hopkins Gallaudet* and deaf French instructor *Laurent Clerc*.
- Derived primarily from *Langue des Signes Française* (*LSF*) brought by Clerc from [[france]], heavily merged with the indigenous sign variety of *Martha's Vineyard Sign Language* (*MVSL*) and regional home signs.
- Proved to be a genuine, rule-governed human language with autonomous morphology and syntax through the pioneering research of *William Stokoe* at *Gallaudet University* in the 1960s.
- Operates through five fundamental phonological parameters: *handshape*, *location*, *movement*, *palm orientation*, and *non-manual markers* such as facial expressions and head postures.
- Employs a topic-comment word order and utilizes three-dimensional signing space to establish spatial referents, pronominal indexing, and directional verb agreement.
- Suffered severe institutional suppression following the 1880 *Milan Conference*, which banned sign language in deaf education in favor of the *oralist* method championed by *Alexander Graham Bell*.
- Served as the focal rallying point of the historic 1988 *Deaf President Now* (*DPN*) student protest at *Gallaudet University*, culminating in the appointment of *I. King Jordan*.
- Features a standardized twenty-six-letter manual alphabet used for *fingerspelling* proper nouns, technical terms, and loanwords borrowed from English.

## connections

- [[linguistics]] — academic field revolutionized by Stokoe's discovery that visual-gestural modalities possess complete phonological and grammatical complexity.
- [[united-states]] — nation where the language developed, flourished, and gained institutional recognition within educational and cultural domains.
- [[france]] — source of *Langue des Signes Française*, the structural ancestor that provided roughly sixty percent of modern ASL vocabulary.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — broader twentieth-century social struggle that inspired the *Deaf President Now* protests and the modern Deaf rights movement.
- [[human-anatomy]] — bodily medium through which the hands, arms, torso, and facial musculature generate all linguistic signals.
- [[five-senses]] — sensory framework wherein vision entirely supplants hearing to receive and process linguistic communication.
- [[perception]] — cognitive domain governing the spatial visual parsing and non-linear grammar distinctive to signed communication.

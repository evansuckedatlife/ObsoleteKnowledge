## summary

The **Canadian National Railway** (*Compagnie des chemins de fer nationaux du Canada*), universally known as *CN*, is a major Canadian freight railway system spanning North America from the Atlantic to the Pacific and south to the Gulf of Mexico. Formed between 1918 and 1923 as a federal Crown corporation through the amalgamation of several financially bankrupt private rail networks, it served as a vital instrument of national integration and industrialization. Privatized in 1995, it remains the largest railway in Canada in terms of track mileage, revenue, and intermodal freight transport.

## you gotta know

- Created by the Government of Canada between 1918 and 1923 through orders-in-council that merged bankrupt lines, including the *Canadian Northern Railway*, the *Grand Trunk Railway*, the *Grand Trunk Pacific*, and the government-owned *Intercolonial Railway*.
- Established as a Crown corporation to avert nationwide transportation collapse after the boom in overbuilt competitive trackage collapsed during *[[world-war-i]]*.
- Pioneered early national telecommunications and mass media in Canada by creating the *CN Radio* network, which set up broadcast stations in its luxury railway hotels and served as the direct institutional predecessor of the *Canadian Broadcasting Corporation* (CBC).
- Constructed the iconic *CN Tower* in Toronto, Ontario, completed in 1976 as a telecommunications and observation tower that stood as the world's tallest free-standing terrestrial structure for more than three decades.
- Adopted its famous continuous-line "wet noodle" corporate monogram logo in 1960, designed by graphic artist *Allan Fleming*, widely hailed as a modernist masterpiece of corporate branding.
- Privatized in November 1995 through an initial public offering (IPO) under the government of Prime Minister *Jean Chrétien*, transitioning from public ownership to an investor-owned Class I freight operator.
- Expanded substantially into the *[[united-states]]* through strategic corporate acquisitions, notably purchasing the *Illinois Central Railroad* in 1999 to establish a tri-coastal network reaching the Gulf of Mexico.

## summary

**Poker** is a family of card wagering games centered on hand evaluation, probability, and strategic deception that crystallized in the early nineteenth-century *[[united-states]]*. Evolving from European card ancestors like French *poque* and Persian *as-nas*, the game spread rapidly along Mississippi River steamboats and throughout the frontier. Beyond its recreational status, the game holds deep intellectual significance as an applied foundation for mathematical game theory and the study of decision-making under conditions of imperfect information.

## you gotta know

- Participants place bets into a central pot based on the relative strength of their playing cards, with the accumulated wagers claimed either by revealing the best combination at showdown or by betting enough to induce all opponents to fold.
- Hand rankings reflect combinatorial probability, ascending from high card through one pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, flush, full house, four of a kind, straight flush, and the royal flush.
- *Texas hold 'em* developed as the dominant international variant, characterized by dealing two private hole cards to each player followed by five shared community cards dealt across three intervals known as the flop, turn, and river.
- The game coalesced in New Orleans and riverboat saloons along the Mississippi River in the 1820s, spreading across military camps during the *[[american-civil-war]]* and along frontier settlements.
- Mathematician *John von Neumann* conceived foundational principles of game theory during the mid-twentieth century specifically by analyzing the strategic dynamics of bluffing and concealed information in simplified card models.
- The *World Series of Poker*, established by *Benny Binion* in Las Vegas in 1970, propelled the game into a global spectator event, catalyzed by accountant *Chris Moneymaker*'s landmark televised championship in 2003.
- In addition to standard casino formats like Omaha and seven-card stud, social home card traditions include novelty games such as Cincinnati and [[polish|Polish]] poker.
- Competitive strategy relies heavily on decoding behavioral *tells*—subtle involuntary physical or verbal signals that betray card strength—countered by maintaining strict emotional detachment known as a "poker face."

## connections

- [[united-states]] — birthplace of the modern game, where nineteenth-century riverboat gambling and Nevada casinos codified international standards.
- [[american-civil-war]] — historical conflict whose encampments popularized five-card stud and draw among Union and Confederate soldiers alike.
- [[perception]] — psychological faculty exercised when interpreting an opponent's micro-expressions, posture, and nonverbal betting patterns.
- [[polish]] — term used to designate regional home-game card adaptations such as Polish poker or Polish draw.
- [[geometry]] — branch of mathematics related to the spatial configurations, combinatorial odds, and expected-value ratios used in bet sizing.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical rivalry during which defense analysts adapted poker-derived game-theoretic models to analyze nuclear deterrence.
- [[modernism]] — cultural era whose literature, cinema, and art frequently utilized high-stakes card games to symbolize existential risk and chance.

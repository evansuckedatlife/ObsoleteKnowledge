## summary

The **brain**, anatomically termed the **encephalon**, serves as the primary command center of the central nervous system across vertebrates and most bilaterian animals, situated within the protective bony cranium. It orchestrates vital involuntary physiological homeostasis, autonomic reflexes, and hormonal secretion while simultaneously integrating multifaceted sensory streams to generate motor commands, deliberate action, and conscious awareness. In humans, an estimated eighty-six billion neurons communicate across trillions of synaptic junctions supported by glial cells, making this organ the foundational biological substrate for cognition, memory, and subjective experience studied across medicine and science.

## you gotta know

- Anatomically partitioned into three fundamental regions—the forebrain (*prosencephalon*), midbrain (*mesencephalon*), and hindbrain (*rhombencephalon*)—with the expanded cerebrum divided into two bilateral hemispheres linked by the massive axonal tract of the *corpus callosum*.
- The outer cerebral cortex is divided into four major lobes: the frontal lobe for motor planning and executive decision-making, the parietal lobe for processing somatosensory information, the occipital lobe for visual perception, and the temporal lobe for audition and memory consolidation.
- Human language localization was classically established in its left hemisphere, featuring *Broca's area* in the inferior frontal gyrus for speech production and *Wernicke's area* in the superior temporal gyrus for speech comprehension, joined by the *arcuate fasciculus*.
- The subcortical limbic system orchestrates emotional reactions, motivation, and declarative memory formation, containing the fear-processing *amygdala*, the olfactory structures, and the seahorse-shaped *hippocampus*, which was famously illuminated by the study of surgical patient H.M.
- The *cerebellum*, situated inferior to the occipital lobes, accounts for roughly ten percent of total volume but over half of all neurons, coordinating motor precision, equilibrium, and procedural learning through distinctive, highly branched *Purkinje cells* and the internal white-matter *arbor vitae*.
- Arterial blood supply is safeguarded by an essential collateral circulatory ring known as the *circle of Willis*, which unites the basilar artery and internal carotid arteries at the base of the skull to maintain perfusion and mitigate stroke-induced ischemia.
- Enveloped and cushioned by three protective connective-tissue meninges—the tough outer *dura mater*, web-like *arachnoid mater*, and delicate inner *pia mater*—as well as circulating cerebrospinal fluid generated by the *choroid plexus* within its ventricular system.
- Microscopic cerebral capillaries are sealed by tight junctions (*zonula occludens*) and surrounded by astrocytic end-feet to constitute the *blood-brain barrier*, which strictly restricts the transit of polar solutes, circulating pathogens, and large macromolecules from the systemic bloodstream.

## connections

- [[human-anatomy]] — establishes the macroscopic cranial, vascular, and ventricular architecture that encapsulates and physically supports neural tissue.
- [[neurology]] — the specialized clinical discipline that investigates, diagnoses, and manages organic pathologies and lesions of the central nervous system.
- [[perception]] — the higher-order cognitive processing through which raw sensory afferents received by cortical regions are organized into conscious experience.
- [[five-senses]] — the traditional external sensory modalities whose peripheral receptor signals are conveyed via cranial and spinal nerves to dedicated primary cortices.
- [[linguistics]] — explores phonology, syntax, and semantics, mapping these structural elements of human communication onto specialized cerebral circuits.
- [[psychology]] — investigates human behavior, affect, and mental functions rooted in the neurochemical and structural dynamics of cerebral networks.

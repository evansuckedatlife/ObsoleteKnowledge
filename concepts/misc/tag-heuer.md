## summary

**TAG Heuer** is a luxury Swiss watchmaker celebrated for its high-precision chronographs and deep, historic ties to motorsport timing. Founded in 1860 by *Edouard Heuer* in St-Imier, Switzerland, the company pioneered dashboard timers and stopwatches before being acquired by *Techniques d'Avant Garde* in 1985 and subsequently joining the *LVMH* luxury conglomerate in 1999. In competitive trivia and history, it is renowned for supplying the first Swiss timepiece worn in human spaceflight and creating iconic, square-cased racing chronographs immortalised in cinema.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1860 under the name *Uhrenmanufaktur Heuer AG* by *Edouard Heuer*, patenting an oscillating pinion in 1887 that remains an industry standard in mechanical chronographs.
- The iconic *Monaco* chronograph, introduced in 1969 with an avant-garde square waterproof case and the pioneering automatic *Calibre 11* movement, was worn by *Steve McQueen* in the 1971 motorsport film *Le Mans*.
- Provided the stopwatch worn by *John Glenn* aboard *Friendship 7* during the *Mercury-Atlas 6* mission in 1962, making a Heuer timer the very first Swiss watch to enter orbit during the [[space-age]].
- Pioneered the *Carrera* chronograph in 1963, named by CEO *Jack Heuer* after the notoriously perilous *Carrera Panamericana* border-to-border rally across Mexico.
- Produced the *Autavia* starting in 1933 as a dashboard instrument for both automotive racing and aviation, later converted into a celebrated wristwatch line in 1962.
- Served as the official timekeeper for multiple Olympic Games throughout the 1920s and forged decades-long partnerships with *Formula One* racing teams like *McLaren*.
- Acquired in 1985 by the Luxembourg-based holding company *TAG* (*Techniques d'Avant Garde*), which combined its moniker to form the modern brand, before entering the *LVMH* portfolio alongside major luxury houses in [[france]].

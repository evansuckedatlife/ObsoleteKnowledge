## summary

**Boxing Day** is a prominent public holiday celebrated annually on December 26 across the [[united-kingdom]] and throughout numerous member nations of the former [[british-empire]]. Originating as a traditional occasion for distributing alms to the poor and granting servant classes monetary tokens or gift boxes following the festivities of *Christmas*, it has evolved over centuries into a major secular event marked by retail shopping, family gatherings, and premier athletic fixtures. In the Caribbean, particularly across the islands of the *Bahamas*, the date is internationally renowned as the vibrant primary staging ground for the exuberant, historic street masquerade festival of *Junkanoo*.

## you gotta know

- Observed annually on December 26, the day immediately following *Christmas Day*, functioning as an official statutory public holiday throughout [[england]], Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
- Derives historically from the British practice of tradespeople, domestic servants, and postal workers receiving a "Christmas box" containing gratuities, money, or food from wealthy masters on the day after Christmas in recognition of diligent year-round service.
- Connected ecclesiastical traditions trace the name to medieval church collection chests, which remained sealed through Advent and were opened on the feast day of *Saint Stephen* to disperse collected charitable alms directly to impoverished parishioners.
- Serves as the principal celebration date for *Junkanoo*, a world-famous street carnival in the *Bahamas* celebrating the cultural heritage of the [[african-diaspora]], wherein costumed revellers parade through Nassau playing goat-skin drums, cowbells, and whistles in a legacy dating back to enslaved people's granted holiday leaves.
- Celebrated across the Commonwealth with marquee athletic traditions, most prominently the Boxing Day Test match hosted at the *Melbourne Cricket Ground* in Australia, a prestigious full fixture program of *Premier League* football matches in the [[united-kingdom]], and the start of the prestigious *Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race*.
- Established as an official secular bank holiday in [[england]], Wales, and Ireland under the *Bank Holidays Act 1871*, introduced into Parliament by the noted British banker, polymath, and reformer *Sir John Lubbock*.
- Concurrently commemorated in the liturgical calendar as the Feast of *Saint Stephen*, the first Christian martyr, whose legendary patronage of charity and stoning in Jerusalem inspired the charitable themes celebrated on the feast.
- Celebrated in Ireland as *Wren Day* or *Lá Fhéile Stiofáin*, an ancient folk tradition where groups of young mummers known as *Wrenboys* historically hunted a wren, mounted it upon a holly bush, and paraded from house to house singing traditional verses for donations.

## summary

**Oneida**, officially known as the **Oneida Community**, was an influential perfectionist religious communal society established in 1848 in Oneida, New York, by *John Humphrey Noyes*. Originating in the revivalist fervor of upstate New York's "burned-over district," the commune became notorious for rejecting traditional marriage in favor of "complex marriage," communal parenting, and male continence. Though internal factionalism and legal threats led to the dissolution of the social experiment in 1880, its transformation into a major joint-stock flatware company cemented its legacy as one of the most successful economic enterprises to arise from 19th-century American utopianism.

## you gotta know

- Founded by *John Humphrey Noyes* following his expulsion from Yale Divinity School, based on the Perfectionist doctrine that the Second Coming of *Jesus Christ* had occurred in 70 CE, making complete liberation from sin achievable on Earth.
- Practiced *complex marriage* (or pantagamy), a theological system where every adult woman was considered married to every adult man, permitting sexual relations based on mutual consent while forbidding possessive romantic exclusivity.
- Regulated reproduction through *male continence* (*coitus reservatus*), a practice in which men refrained from ejaculation during intercourse to prevent unwanted pregnancies and liberate women from constant childbearing.
- Enforced social cohesion and internal discipline through *mutual criticism*, a structured tribunal in which an individual sat in silence while peers and elders delivered frank evaluations of their character flaws and selfish tendencies.
- Introduced an early eugenics program called *stirpiculture* in 1869, where a committee selected spiritually and physically exemplary couples for procreation, yielding 58 children who were raised collectively in the community nursery.
- Housed *Charles J. Guiteau*, the eventual assassin of President *James A. Garfield*, whose abrasive personality alienated the commune so severely that members mockingly referred to him as *Charles Gitout*.
- Sustained its treasury through industrial diversification, beginning with animal traps designed by *Sewell Newhouse* and silk thread before transitioning into precision silverware manufacturing under the name *Oneida Limited*.
- Operated from the communal brick *Oneida Mansion House*, a grand estate featuring communal dining halls, shared recreation areas, and specialized educational quarters that isolated children from exclusive parental influence.

## connections

- [[christianity]] — the theological tradition from which *John Humphrey Noyes* derived his millennial perfectionism and unconventional readings of the New Testament.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the national conflict during which the pacifist commune secured profitable manufacturing contracts while navigating shifting social orders.

- [[indian-removal]] — the historic dislocation of indigenous peoples, notably the *Oneida Nation* of the Haudenosaunee, whose ancestral New York homelands were reallocated to white settlers.

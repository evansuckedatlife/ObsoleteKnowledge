## summary

**Burning Man** is an annual nine-day gathering focused on community, radical self-expression, participatory art, and self-reliance, held in the remote Black Rock Desert of northern Nevada. Established in 1986 on a San Francisco beach before migrating to the desert playa, the event constructs a temporary metropolis called *Black Rock City* that vanishes completely upon conclusion. The gathering culminates on the penultimate evening with the ritualistic immolation of a monumental wooden effigy known simply as *The Man*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1986 by *Larry Harvey*, *Jerry James*, and associates on Baker Beach in San Francisco, marking the Summer Solstice by burning an eight-foot wooden effigy.
- Relocated in 1990 to the prehistoric lakebed (*playa*) of the Black Rock Desert in northwestern Nevada due to police intervention on California beaches and collaboration with the *San Francisco Cacophony Society*.
- Governed by the Ten Principles formulated by Larry Harvey in 2004, which include Radical Inclusion, Gifting, Decommodification, Radical Self-reliance, Radical Self-expression, Communal Effort, Civic Responsibility, Leaving No Trace, Participation, and Immediacy.
- Features a strict gift economy where no commerce, cash transactions, or advertising are permitted, with the sole exceptions of camp ice and coffee sold at the central pavilion.
- Utilizes an ambitious semi-circular urban design centered around the effigy, laying out themed radial avenues named after the dial of a clock face and annular streets.
- Culminates in two major ceremonial bonfires: the burning of the towering wooden *Man* on Saturday night, followed by the silent, contemplative burning of the non-denominational *Temple* on Sunday night.
- Operates under a rigorous environmental mandate known as *Leaving No Trace*, mobilizing volunteer teams to clean up every trace of matter out of place (*MOOP*) to restore the federal wilderness playa.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the country where the countercultural experiment was founded, organized, and hosted annually on federal public lands managed by the BLM.
- [[buckminster-fuller]] — the architect whose geodesic domes and principles of comprehensive anticipatory design serve as staple shelters across the desert encampments.
- [[modernism]] — the twentieth-century aesthetic and design movements that inspired both the event's radical avant-garde art installations and urban layout.
- [[existentialism]] — the philosophical tradition echoed in the community's core ethos of personal authenticity, immediate experience, and radical self-expression.
- [[architecture]] — the discipline exemplified by the temporary, high-wind kinetic structures and ceremonial temples erected and disassembled annually on the playa.
- [[daniel-burnham]] — the master urban planner whose sweeping civic grid designs find a surreal temporary parallel in the concentric layout of Black Rock City.

## summary

The **Mohawk** are the easternmost nation of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, traditionally inhabiting the Mohawk Valley of present-day upstate New York and southeastern Canada. Renowned as the *Keepers of the Eastern Door*, the Mohawk played a pivotal diplomatic and military role throughout North American colonial encounters. In popular global culture, the name also denotes the iconic hairstyle featuring shaved sides and an upright central strip of hair, as well as generations of high-steel ironworkers who constructed the skyline of modern metropolises.

## you gotta know

- Traditional self-designation is *Kanienʼkehá꞉ka*, meaning *People of the Flint*, reflecting their ancestral territories rich in chert and flint stone.
- Formed the foundational eastern flank of the original Five Nations of the Iroquois Confederacy alongside the Oneida, Onondaga, Cayuga, and Seneca.
- Allied primarily with Great Britain during the American Revolutionary War under the prominent leadership of diplomat and military officer *Joseph Brant* (*Thayendanegea*).
- Celebrated extensively in architectural history as skywalkers due to their daring work as structural ironworkers building landmark skyscrapers and bridges across *New York City*.
- Inspired the distinctive punk and warrior hairstyle where both sides of the head are shaved clean, leaving a conspicuous, upright strip of hair down the center.
- Experienced intense twentieth-century sovereignty disputes, notably the 1990 *Oka Crisis* in Quebec concerning the proposed expansion of a golf course onto sacred ancestral burial grounds.
- Traditional social organization is strictly matrilineal, with clan mothers choosing male chiefs and holding significant authority over political decision-making and land use.

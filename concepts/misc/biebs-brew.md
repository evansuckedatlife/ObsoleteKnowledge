## summary

**Biebs Brew** is a co-branded commercial cold brew coffee beverage launched in 2022 through a collaboration between Canadian pop musician *Justin Bieber* and the Canadian multinational quick-service restaurant chain *Tim Hortons*. Developed alongside a popular novelty pastry line dubbed *Timbiebs*, the product gained widespread consumer attention across Canada and the *[[united-states]]*. It stands as a notable modern case study in celebrity endorsement, mass-market franchising, and viral contemporary brand revitalization.

## you gotta know

- Officially debuted across North American franchises in June 2022 as an expansion of the blockbuster partnership with pop icon *Justin Bieber*.
- Formulated as a slow-steeped cold brew beverage infused with French vanilla flavor, brewed over sixteen hours using premium Arabica coffee beans.
- Followed the initial commercial success of *Timbiebs Timbits*, a trio of customized donut-hole pastry flavors released in late 2021 that significantly boosted franchise foot traffic.
- Accompanied by limited-edition retail promotional merchandise, including branded stainless steel thermal tumblers, tote bags, and fanny packs that triggered secondary market resale speculation.
- Coincided with parent corporation Restaurant Brands International attempting to modernize its core beverage offerings to compete against specialty cold coffee competitors.
- Reflected a growing trend in twenty-first-century fast-food marketing pairing high-profile recording artists with personalized signature menu items.

## summary

**Percy Bysshe Shelley** was an English Romantic poet and political radical whose visionary work explored freedom, love, and social justice. Living from 1792 to 1822, he scandalized his era with both his unconventional personal life and his revolutionary ideas encoded in lyric and dramatic verse. His death by drowning in Italy at just 29 cut short a career of profound influence on European Romanticism.

## you gotta know

- Wrote *Ozymandias* and *The Mask of Anarchy*, poems that confront power and mortality with unprecedented philosophical depth; *Ozymandias* stands as a meditation on the transience of human ambition and empire.
- Married twice: first to Harriet Westbrook (who died by suicide), then to Mary Shelley, author of *Frankenstein*, in an unconventional romance that scandalized Victorian England; his personal life was as transgressive as his politics.
- Expelled from Oxford University for publishing *The Necessity of Atheism*, a philosophical tract that openly challenged Christian doctrine, marking him as a dangerous radical in the eyes of establishment society.
- Championed republican ideals during the Napoleonic Wars and the restoration of monarchy; his poetry is shot through with calls for political liberation and solidarity with the oppressed, notably in *The Mask of Anarchy*.
- Developed a lyrical style of extraordinary intensity, using myth and natural imagery to probe the nature of consciousness and desire; his prosodic innovations influenced generations of Romantic and Victorian poets.
- Created major dramatic works including *Prometheus Unbound*, an epic reimagining of classical mythology as a parable of liberation and defiant idealism against tyranny.
- Drowned off the Italian coast, likely in a sailing accident, at age 29; his body was cremated on an Italian beach in a ceremony attended by fellow expatriate poets including Byron.

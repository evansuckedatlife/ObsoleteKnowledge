## summary

**Ramen** (*rāmen*) is an iconic Japanese noodle soup dish consisting of Chinese-style wheat noodles served in an intensely savory broth, seasoned with tare and garnished with varied meats and vegetables. Originating in late nineteenth-century port towns as a working-class food introduced by Chinese immigrants, it underwent extensive localization and post-war industrialization to become a cultural staple of modern *Japan*. Today, its vibrant spectrum of regional varieties and status as a culinary craft make it a global gastronomic phenomenon.

## you gotta know

- Emerged in treaty ports such as *Yokohama* in the late Meiji and Taisho eras, originally known by names such as *shina soba* or *chūka soba* before adopting its modern phonetic moniker.
- Composed of four fundamental architectural elements: springy wheat noodles treated with alkaline water (*kansui*), slow-simmered animal or seafood stock, an intensely concentrated seasoning sauce called *tare*, and aromatic finishing oils.
- Categorized traditionally into four main flavor profiles based on the seasoning base and soup density: soy sauce (*shōyu*), sea salt (*shio*), fermented soybean paste (*miso*), and rich, milky pork-bone broth (*tonkotsu*).
- Surged in national popularity following *World War II*, when widespread domestic rice shortages and an influx of cheap wheat flour imported by the *United States* occupation spurred street vendors to craft filling noodle soups.
- Revolutionized global shelf-stable food production in 1958 when entrepreneur *Momofuku Ando* invented flash-fried instant noodles, leading to the establishment of *Nissin Foods* and the debut of *Cup Noodles* in 1971.
- Features celebrated regional traditions throughout the islands, ranging from the hearty corn-and-butter *miso* soups of *Hokkaido* to the thin, firm noodles and unctuous broths of *Fukuoka*.
- Achieved international fine-dining acclaim in the twenty-first century, with visionary *Tokyo* establishments such as *Tsuta* and *Nakiryu* earning prestigious Michelin stars.

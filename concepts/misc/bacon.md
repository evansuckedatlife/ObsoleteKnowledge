## summary

**Bacon** is a cured meat prepared from various cuts of pork—most commonly the pork belly, loin, or side—and preserved through dry salting, brining, or smoking. Originating in antiquity as a method for preserving animal protein through long winters, it evolved into an iconic staple of English and American foodways. Celebrated for its potent savoury flavour generated by lipid oxidation and thermal browning, it is central to vernacular culinary traditions, linguistic idioms, and modern industrial food processing.

## you gotta know

- Prepared internationally in distinct regional styles: American streaky bacon is cut from the fatty pork belly, while British and Irish back bacon (or rashers) incorporates the leaner loin eye alongside a strip of belly meat.
- Traced back thousands of years to ancient agricultural communities in central China that cured pork bellies with sea salt, an early preservation technique that predates Roman preparations like *petaso*.
- Developed into a cornerstone of traditional culinary identity in *[[england]]* and the broader *[[united-kingdom]]*, serving as an indispensable component of the full English breakfast alongside eggs, sausage, and toast.
- Tied folklorically to the *Dunmow Flitch Trials* in Essex, a historical ritual dating to the Middle Ages wherein married couples who demonstrated total domestic harmony for a year were rewarded with a whole flitch (side) of cured pork.
- Gave rise to the popular English idiom "bring home the bacon," a phrase referencing economic provision that gained widespread currency in sports journalism following early twentieth-century boxing matches in the *[[united-states]]*.
- Generates its intense aromatic profile via the Maillard reaction and volatile degradation during frying, producing heterocyclic compounds like pyrazines, pyridines, and furans that vigorously stimulate human *[[perception]]* and the *[[five-senses]]*.
- Anchor ingredient of the classic BLT sandwich, a widely consumed luncheon dish in diner culture that is frequently modernized by the addition of sliced *[[avocado]]* to produce the "BLAT."

## connections

- [[england]] — nation where the curing of rashers and the historic *Dunmow Flitch Trials* established deep societal traditions.
- [[united-kingdom]] — country whose domestic breakfast tradition relies extensively upon regional back-bacon preparations.
- [[united-states]] — society where industrial commercialization and diner gastronomy established streaky pork belly as an everyday breakfast staple.
- [[avocado]] — buttery subtropical fruit commonly layered alongside cured pork strips in contemporary variations of the BLT sandwich.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical era of mechanised slaughterhouses, refrigerated transport, and modern supply chains that transformed regional cured meats into mass commodities.
- [[five-senses]] — physiological pathways through which human olfactory, gustatory, and auditory receptors process the sizzle and scent of cured meats.
- [[perception]] — cognitive and sensory evaluation responsible for recognizing the intense savoury and umami compounds released during thermal cooking.

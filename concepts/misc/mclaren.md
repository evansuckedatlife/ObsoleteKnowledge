## summary

**McLaren** is a British motor racing team and luxury sports car manufacturer founded in 1963 by New Zealand driver and engineer Bruce McLaren. Based in Woking, England, the outfit stands as one of the most successful constructors in Formula One history, alongside Ferrari and Williams. Celebrated for introducing revolutionary carbon-fiber chassis technology to grand prix competition and building the legendary Gordon Murray-designed McLaren F1 supercar, the company remains a cornerstone of international elite motorsport.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1963 by New Zealand racing driver *Bruce McLaren*, establishing its engineering base in *England* where it continues to operate.
- Won the Indianapolis 500 three times in the 1970s and dominated the North American Can-Am sports car series, winning five consecutive titles from 1967 to 1971.
- Revolutionized Formula One chassis construction in 1981 by introducing the *McLaren MP4/1*, the first racing car to feature a lightweight carbon-fiber composite monocoque.
- Fielded the dominant 1988 *MP4/4*, powered by Honda turbo engines and driven by *Ayrton Senna* and *Alain Prost*, which won 15 out of 16 races in a single championship season.
- Produced the groundbreaking *McLaren F1* road car in 1992, engineered by *Gordon Murray*, featuring a central driving position, gold-leaf engine bay lining, and a record top speed of 240.1 mph.
- Won the 24 Hours of Le Mans on its debut in 1995 with the *McLaren F1 GTR*, completing motorsport's informal Triple Crown alongside wins at Indianapolis and Monaco.
- Partnered with Mercedes-Benz from 1995 to 2014, securing driver titles with *Mika Häkkinen* and *Lewis Hamilton* before reviving its independent road-car division.

## summary

The **Bahamas**, officially the **Commonwealth of The Bahamas**, is an archipelagic nation within the Lucayan Archipelago in the western Atlantic Ocean, located northeast of Cuba and southeast of Florida. Celebrated as the initial landfall site of *Christopher Columbus* in the Americas in 1492, the islands experienced devastating Spanish slave-raiding of the indigenous *Lucayan* population followed by British settlement. Today, the nation is widely renowned for its rich Afro-Caribbean heritage, particularly the dynamic street festival of *Junkanoo*, standing as an enduring cultural hub of the wider *Caribbean* world.

## you gotta know

- Celebrates *Junkanoo*, a vibrant national festival and street parade showcasing elaborate crepe-paper costumes, goatskin drums, cowbells, and brass music on Boxing Day and New Year's Day, deeply rooted in the holiday traditions of the [[african-diaspora]].
- Served as the historic site of *Christopher Columbus*'s first landing in the Americas during the [[age-of-exploration]] in October 1492 on the island of *Guanahani*, which he promptly rechristened *San Salvador*.
- Became notorious throughout the Golden Age of Piracy as the base of the anarchic "Republic of Pirates" at *Nassau* on *New Providence Island*, harboring figures like *Blackbeard* (*Edward Teach*) and *Calico Jack Rackham* before governor *Woodes Rogers* reclaimed it for the British crown.
- Influxes of American Loyalists and enslaved laborers arrived following the [[american-revolution]], establishing plantation agriculture and solidifying the islands' predominantly African-descended demographic makeup.
- Operated as a major logistical hub for maritime smuggling in American conflicts, facilitating Confederate blockade running during the [[american-civil-war]] and liquor transport during American Prohibition.
- Attracted foundational ethnographic research in the early twentieth century by anthropologists and folklorists, notably *Franz Boas* and *Zora Neale Hurston*, who documented Bahamian folktales, dialect speech, and *rake-and-scrape* accordion music.
- Gained political independence from the [[british-empire]] in July 1973 under the leadership of Prime Minister *Lynden Pindling*, celebrated domestically as the father of the nation.

## connections

- [[african-diaspora]] — provides the demographic foundation, folktales, religious customs, and rhythmic heritage seen in the *Junkanoo* masquerade.
- [[british-empire]] — administered the territory as a strategic crown colony from 1718 until granting sovereign independence in 1973.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — witnessed its earliest American landfall in the Bahamian islands, initiating transoceanic European contact.
- [[colonialism]] — led to the rapid destruction of the native *Lucayan* population and the long era of plantation chattel slavery.
- [[united-states]] — adjacent continental neighbor closely linked through historic Loyalist resettlement, coastal blockade running, and tourism.
- [[spain]] — claimed the territory following the 1492 voyage and depopulated its native inhabitants to work mines in Hispaniola.

## summary

**Lexus** is the luxury vehicle division of the Japanese automaker *Toyota*, created in the late 1980s to compete directly against premium European marques such as *Mercedes-Benz* and *BMW*. Initiated under the clandestine codename *Circle F* or *Flagship 1* (*F1*) by Toyota chairman *Eiji Toyoda*, the brand debuted in 1989 with the flagship *LS 400* sedan. Renowned for meticulous engineering, whisper-quiet cabin acoustics, and high reliability ratings, it established a benchmark in international automotive manufacturing.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in 1983 when *Eiji Toyoda* challenged his engineers to build the world's best luxury car, an effort code-named *Project F1*.
- Launched publicly at the 1989 *North American International Auto Show* in Detroit with the V8-powered *LS 400* and the smaller *ES 250*.
- Renowned for its famous 1989 television commercial featuring a champagne glass pyramid balanced on the hood of an *LS 400* engine revving on a dynamometer at 145 mph without spilling a drop.
- Early masterstroke of customer relations occurred when a minor recall of early models involved technicians picking up cars directly from owners' homes and returning them washed and refueled.
- Headquartered in *Nagoya*, Japan, with premier assembly centered at the *Tahara* plant, celebrated for using master craftspeople known as *takumi*.
- Introduced the *RX 300* in 1998, widely credited with popularizing the luxury crossover SUV market segment.
- Pioneered the high-performance *F* marque, culminating in the carbon-fiber-bodied *LFA* supercar powered by an acoustic V10 engine co-developed with *Yamaha*.

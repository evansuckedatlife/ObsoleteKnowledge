## summary

A **corona** is the diffuse, outermost aura of extremely hot plasma that envelops the Sun and other stars, extending millions of kilometers into surrounding space. Readily visible to the naked eye only during a total solar eclipse as a pearly white crown, the solar corona possesses an astonishingly elevated temperature of several million kelvins despite overlying a photosphere that is orders of magnitude cooler. Beyond astrophysics, the term designates meteorological diffraction halos around celestial light sources as well as crown-shaped biological structures in human anatomy.

## you gotta know

- The coronal heating problem remains a paramount question in solar astrophysics, inquiring why the corona sizzles at one to three million kelvins while the underlying solar surface rests near 5,800 kelvins.
- Primary proposed mechanisms for coronal heating involve magnetic reconnection events known as nanoflares and the dissipation of magnetohydrodynamic waves, such as *Alfvén waves*, propagating outward along magnetic field lines.
- Unidentified bright green spectral lines spotted in the corona during late nineteenth-century eclipses were once attributed to an elusive new chemical element dubbed *coronium*, until physicists revealed in the 1930s that the emission sprang from iron ionized thirteen times (*Fe XIV*).
- The high temperature causes coronal plasma to expand continuously outward into interplanetary space, generating the supersonic solar wind and launching massive disruptions termed coronal mass ejections.
- French astronomer *Bernard Lyot* revolutionized solar observation in 1931 by inventing the coronagraph, a specialized telescope using an occulting disk to artificially blot out the glare of the solar surface.
- In meteorological optics, a corona describes small concentric rainbow-tinted rings produced by diffraction when light from the Sun or Moon shines through mist, fog, or thin clouds of uniform water droplets.
- In neuroanatomy, the *corona radiata* is a radiating crown of myelinated white-matter nerve fibers that projects between the internal capsule and the cerebral cortex.

## connections

- [[astronomy]] — the scientific discipline analyzing the stellar physics, magnetohydrodynamics, and spectral lines of the solar corona.
- [[perception]] — relates to the human visual experience of atmospheric diffraction rings and the viewing of eclipse crowns.
- [[space-age]] — era marked by satellite observatories and interplanetary probes, such as the *Parker Solar Probe*, designed to fly through coronal plasma.
- [[human-anatomy]] — provides the naming convention for crown-shaped biological structures such as the *corona radiata* and the *corona glandis*.
- [[neurology]] — studies brain pathology involving the white-matter tracts of the cerebral *corona radiata*.
- [[roman-empire]] — origin of the Latin word *corona*, which signified a ceremonial crown, laurel wreath, or garland.
- [[geometry]] — describes the toroidal topology, concentric circular rings, and radial streamline geometries formed by coronal magnetic loops.

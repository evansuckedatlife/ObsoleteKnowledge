## summary

**General Motors** is a multinational automotive conglomerate founded in 1908 by *William C. Durant* in *Flint*, *Michigan*. Long headquartered in *Detroit*, the company transformed the global [[industrial-revolution]] by pioneering the multi-brand corporate hierarchy, organized under legendary executive *Alfred P. Sloan* to provide "a car for every purse and purpose." Throughout the twentieth century, **GM** reigned as the world's largest automaker, introducing landmark engineering innovations, consumer credit models, and dynamic styling that defined modern industrial capitalism.

## you gotta know

- Formed by *William C. Durant* in 1908 through the consolidation of *Buick*, quickly followed by acquisitions of *Oldsmobile*, *Cadillac*, and *Oakland* (which later evolved into *Pontiac*).
- Revolutionized industrial management under longtime leader *Alfred P. Sloan*, who instituted decentralized operations with centralized financial controls and a graduated brand ladder.
- Created the *General Motors Acceptance Corporation* (*GMAC*) in 1919, popularizing consumer installment credit and auto loans across the middle class.
- Introduced modern automotive styling and the annual model change through design chief *Harley Earl*, who introduced tailfins inspired by the Lockheed P-38 Lightning fighter plane.
- Served as the target of the historic 1936 to 1937 Flint Sit-Down Strike, culminating in the formal recognition of the *United Auto Workers* (*UAW*) and reshaping American labor relations.
- Mobilized heavily during [[world-war-ii]] as a cornerstone of the "Arsenal of Democracy," producing airplane engines, tanks, trucks, and armaments for Allied forces.
- Prompted the famous confirmation hearing remark by nominee *Charles Erwin Wilson* in 1953: "for years I thought what was good for our country was good for General Motors, and vice versa."
- Underwent a historic Chapter 11 government-backed bankruptcy reorganization in 2009, eliminating historic nameplates such as *Pontiac*, *Saturn*, *Hummer*, and *Oldsmobile*.

## summary

**Motorola** was an American multinational telecommunications corporation founded in Chicago in 1928 as the *Galvin Manufacturing Corporation* by brothers *Paul Galvin* and *Joseph Galvin*. The company became an engine of twentieth-century technological modernity, pioneering commercial car radios, battlefield transceivers, spaceflight communications equipment, and the first handheld cellular telephone. Celebrated both for industrial design milestones and managerial paradigms such as *Six Sigma*, the firm dominated consumer electronics before its historic corporate breakup into enterprise and mobile successors.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Illinois to market battery eliminators before introducing an affordable automobile radio in 1930, blending *motor* with the popular phonograph suffix *-ola*.
- Developed critical battlefield radio equipment during global conflict, creating the portable *SCR-536* handie-talkie and the backpack-mounted *SCR-300* walkie-talkie for allied infantry forces.
- Engineered the specialized S-band transponder on board the *Apollo 11* lunar module, which relayed astronaut *Neil Armstrong*'s historic moon landing transmission back to Earth in 1969.
- Built the world's first portable handheld cellular phone, the *DynaTAC 8000X*, conceived by engineer *Martin Cooper*, who placed the inaugural public cellular call in Manhattan in 1973.
- Formalized the influential *Six Sigma* quality management framework during the 1980s under engineer *Bill Smith* and executive *Bob Galvin*, drastically reducing manufacturing defect rates.
- Launched the bestselling *StarTAC* clamshell phone in 1996 and the iconic ultra-thin aluminum *RAZR V3* in 2004, dominating the pre-smartphone mobile handset marketplace.
- Backed the ambitious *Iridium* satellite constellation in the 1990s, an engineering triumph that went into bankruptcy before the conglomerate split into *Motorola Solutions* and *Motorola Mobility*.

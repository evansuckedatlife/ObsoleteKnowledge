## summary

The **RCMP**, officially known as the **Royal Canadian Mounted Police**, is Canada's national and federal police service, possessing jurisdiction across federal, provincial, and municipal territories. Colloquially termed the **Mounties**, the service traces its institutional origins to the nineteenth-century **North-West Mounted Police**, established to assert territorial sovereignty across western frontiers. World-renowned for their formal red serge tunics and broad-brimmed Stetson hats, the force represents one of the most visible symbols of Canadian governance and frontier history.

## you gotta know

- Established in 1873 under Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald as the *North-West Mounted Police* to police the newly acquired Rupert's Land and Northwest Territories.
- Undertook the famous *March West* in 1874 to curb the illicit American whiskey trade and establish forts such as Fort Macleod.
- Encountered and negotiated with Lakota leader Sitting Bull when he fled northward into Canada following the Battle of the Little Bighorn.
- Granted the royal prefix in 1904 by King Edward VII in recognition of contributions during the Second Boer War, becoming the *Royal Northwest Mounted Police*.
- Merged with the Dominion Police in 1920 under modern legislation to establish the unified contemporary federal force.
- Famous for the ceremonial *Musical Ride*, a choreographed equestrian exhibition showcasing intricate cavalry drills and lance maneuvers set to music.
- Replaced its traditional federal intelligence role in 1984 following the McDonald Commission, which led to the creation of the civilian Canadian Security Intelligence Service.

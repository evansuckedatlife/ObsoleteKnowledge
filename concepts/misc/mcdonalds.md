## summary

**McDonald's** is an American multinational fast food corporation founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald in San Bernardino, California. Transformed into an international commercial empire by franchise agent Ray Kroc, the company pioneered the assembly-line *Speedee Service System* that defined modern industrial fast food. Operating tens of thousands of restaurants globally, its golden arches serve as an enduring cultural emblem of American commercial capitalism, globalization, and standardized service efficiency.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1940 in San Bernardino, California, by brothers *Richard and Maurice McDonald* as a drive-in barbecue restaurant.
- Introduced the *Speedee Service System* in 1948, adapting automobile factory assembly-line techniques to restaurant kitchens to mass-produce cheap hamburgers and fries.
- Transformed by milkshake mixer salesman *Ray Kroc*, who opened a franchise in Des Plaines, Illinois in 1955 and eventually bought out the founding brothers in 1961.
- Standardized its signature architectural motif, the *Golden Arches*, designed by architect Stanley Clark Meston in 1953, which became one of the world's most recognized corporate logos.
- Introduced the *Big Mac* in 1967, created by Pennsylvania franchisee *Michael James Delligatti*, which inspired the *Big Mac Index* published by *The Economist* to measure purchasing power parity.
- Served as a potent geopolitical symbol of Western commercial expansion when its first location in Moscow opened in Pushkin Square in January 1990 near the end of the *Cold War*.
- Inspired sociologist *George Ritzer*'s sociological concept of *McDonaldization*, describing the principles of efficiency, calculability, predictability, and control governing modern society.

## summary

**Air Canada** is the flag carrier and largest airline of Canada, headquartered in Montreal, Quebec. Founded by federal statute in 1937 as **Trans-Canada Air Lines** (*TCA*), the carrier was established under the Crown corporation model to unite the transcontinental expanse of the Dominion through commercial aviation. It grew into a founding member of the Star Alliance network and played a central historical role in pioneering flight safety innovations, polar routing, and civil aviation across North America.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1937 under the administration of transport minister *C. D. Howe* as *Trans-Canada Air Lines*, operating its inaugural passenger flight from Vancouver to Seattle on a Lockheed Electra.
- Underwent official rebranding to **Air Canada** in 1965 following parliamentary approval, adopting the stylized red maple leaf roundel insignia.
- Became the world's first airline to introduce fleet-wide computerized reservations when it commissioned the pioneering *ReserVec* system in the early 1960s.
- Experienced the famous 1983 *Gimli Glider* incident, wherein a Boeing 767 ran out of fuel midway through flight due to a metric conversion fueling error and glided safely to an abandoned Royal Canadian Air Force base in Manitoba.
- Fully privatized by the federal government in 1989, ending decades of Crown ownership, and later absorbed its primary domestic rival *Canadian Airlines International* in 2000.
- Suffered the catastrophic inflight fire aboard Flight 797 in 1983 at Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport, which led directly to sweeping global mandates for cabin smoke detectors and floor-level emergency lighting.
- Co-founded the Star Alliance global airline partnership in 1997 alongside carriers such as *Lufthansa*, *United Airlines*, *Scandinavian Airlines*, and *Thai Airways*.

## summary

**Rogers Communications** is a major Canadian telecommunications and mass media conglomerate headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, operating as one of Canada's dominant "Big Three" wireless and broadband providers alongside *Bell Canada* and *Telus*. Tracing its technological origins to the inventions of *Edward S. Rogers Sr.* and formally established in 1960 by *Ted Rogers*, the corporation oversees extensive operations in cellular networks, cable television, residential internet, and commercial sports entertainment. In academic quiz bowl and cultural history, it frequently appears in questions concerning continental telecommunications infrastructure, national sports broadcasting monopolies, corporate mergers, and significant technological network failures.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1960 by *Ted Rogers* when he purchased Toronto radio station CHFI-FM, expanding over several decades into a telecommunications giant through relentless acquisitions of regional cable franchises and cellular spectrum across Canada.
- Company roots trace back to patriarch *Edward S. Rogers Sr.*, who revolutionized radio technology in 1925 by patenting the world's first batteryless vacuum tube operating on alternating household electric current, creating Toronto radio station CFRB ("Canada's First Rogers Batteryless").
- Owns Major League Baseball's *Toronto Blue Jays* franchise and their home venue, *Rogers Centre*, the iconic downtown Toronto stadium celebrated upon its 1989 opening as the *SkyDome* for its pioneering motorized retractable roof.
- Suffered a catastrophic nationwide network disruption on July 8, 2022, when an erroneous internal routing configuration update during routine maintenance disabled cellular, home broadband, banking access, and emergency 911 services, paralyzing the *Interac* payment system throughout Canada.
- Completed a contentious twenty-six-billion-dollar acquisition of western Canadian competitor *Shaw Communications* in April 2023, following extensive antitrust reviews, parliamentary hearings, and formal approval by federal regulatory authorities.
- Holds sweeping national sports broadcasting authority through its media division *Sportsnet*, which in 2013 negotiated a landmark twelve-year, 5.2-billion-dollar agreement granting it exclusive Canadian multimedia distribution rights for the *National Hockey League*.
- Experienced severe boardroom turmoil in late 2021 when chairman *Edward Rogers III* initiated an executive purge of chief executive *Joe Natale*, precipitating a bitter public legal battle against his mother *Loretta Rogers* and sisters over control of the family trust.

## summary

**Enbridge** is a major North American multinational energy transportation company headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, best known for operating the world's longest and most sophisticated crude oil and liquid hydrocarbons pipeline network. Founded in 1949 as the *Interprovincial Pipe Line Company* following major oil strikes in Alberta, it transport substantial fractions of Canadian crude directly to refineries across the Midwestern and Gulf Coast regions of the *[[united-states]]*. In quiz bowl and economic history, it figures prominently in questions concerning continental infrastructure, energy geopolitics, environmental controversies such as the *Line 3* replacement and *Line 5* Straits of Mackinac disputes, and the 2010 *Kalamazoo River* oil spill.

## you gotta know

- Formed originally as the *Interprovincial Pipe Line Company* (IPL) in 1949 after the historic oil discovery at *Leduc No. 1* in Alberta, which transformed Canada into a major petroleum exporter.
- Operates the *Mainline* system, a massive pipeline trunk network carrying crude petroleum, synthetic crude, and natural gas liquids from Edmonton, Alberta, across the border into Chicago and beyond.
- Responsible for the July 2010 *Line 6B* rupture near Marshall, Michigan, which spilled over 840,000 gallons of heavy diluted bitumen into *Talmadge Creek* and the *Kalamazoo River*, becoming one of the costliest inland oil pipeline spills in American history.
- Operates *Line 5*, a twin underwater pipeline spanning the environmentally sensitive *Straits of Mackinac* connecting Lake Michigan and Lake Huron, which has sparked intense legal and diplomatic disputes between Michigan state authorities, Indigenous tribes, and the Canadian federal government over risks to the Great Lakes.
- Oversaw the contested *Line 3* replacement program through Minnesota and Wisconsin, which became a focal point of widespread environmental and Indigenous rights protests during the late 2010s and early 2020s.
- Adopted its current corporate name in 1998 as a portmanteau blending *energy* and *bridge*, reflecting corporate diversification into natural gas distribution and renewable power generation.
- Merged with Houston-based *Spectra Energy* in 2017 in a multi-billion-dollar transaction, creating one of the largest midstream energy operators across North America with extensive natural gas pipelines.

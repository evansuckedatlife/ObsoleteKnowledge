## summary

**Slack** is a cloud-based team communication and workflow platform developed by *Slack Technologies*, founded by *Stewart Butterfield*, *Eric Costello*, *Cal Henderson*, and *Seraph Henderson*. Originating in 2013 as an internal collaboration tool during the development of an unreleased online multiplayer game called *Glitch*, it quickly revolutionized enterprise collaboration through searchable, persistent chat channels. The platform was acquired by *Salesforce* in 2021 in one of the largest software acquisitions in history.

## you gotta know

- Developed internally by *Tiny Speck* to aid team communication while creating the defunct online game *Glitch*, before pivoting to the communication tool in 2013.
- The name was coined as an acronym for *Searchable Log of All Communication and Knowledge*, highlighting its central search capability.
- Co-founded by Canadian entrepreneur *Stewart Butterfield*, who previously co-founded the pioneering image-hosting service *Flickr*.
- Organizes conversations into public and private channels denoted by hashtags, alongside direct messaging and customizable notification preferences.
- Popularized the widespread corporate adoption of inline animated emojis, integrations with third-party developer APIs, and automated conversational bots.
- Went public on the *New York Stock Exchange* in June 2019 via a direct public offering under the ticker symbol *WORK*.
- Acquired by enterprise software giant *Salesforce* in July 2021 for approximately twenty-seven billion dollars to serve as its primary collaborative interface.

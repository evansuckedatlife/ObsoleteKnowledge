## summary

**Machi Koro** is a fast-paced tabletop engine-building card game designed by Japanese game creator *Masao Suganuma* and published in 2012 by *Grounding Inc.*. In the game, players act as newly elected mayors competing to develop a sleepy town into a bustling metropolis by purchasing civic establishments and rolling dice to collect revenue. Celebrated for its accessible blend of probability and engine construction, the game earned widespread international acclaim and a nomination for the prestigious 2015 *Spiel des Jahres*.

## you gotta know

- Designed by *Masao Suganuma* with cheerful, minimalist graphic artwork by *Noboru Hotta*, debuting in [[japan]] before being localized globally by *IDW Games* and *Pandasaurus Games*.
- Winning condition requires a player to be the first to build all four of their city's major landmark cards: the *Train Station*, *Shopping Mall*, *Amusement Park*, and *Radio Tower*.
- Employs color-coded cards that trigger revenue based on dice rolls: Blue cards earn coins on any player's turn, Green cards earn coins on your turn only, Red cards steal coins from the active roller, and Purple cards provide special abilities.
- Incorporates a crucial dice-upgrading threshold where players start rolling a single six-sided die (numbers 1–6) but gain the choice to roll two dice (numbers 2–12) upon constructing the *Train Station*.
- Relies on basic binomial probability, forcing players to choose between low-risk numbers near the center of the two-dice distribution (such as 7) and high-yield, low-frequency edge rolls.
- Expanded through major boxed supplements including *Machi Koro: Harbor* and *Machi Koro: Millionaire's Row*, which introduced variable card marketplaces and specialized luxury industries.
- Adapted into *Machi Koro Legacy* in 2019, an evolving campaign-style board game co-designed by Suganuma and board game designer *Rob Daviau*.

## summary

**Ashura**, also known as the **Day of Ashura**, is a major religious commemoration falling on the tenth day of *Muharram*, the opening month of the [[islamic-calendar]]. For Shia Muslims across the [[middle-east]] and the wider Islamic diaspora, the day represents the solemn emotional peak of an annual mourning cycle remembering the martyrdom of *Husayn ibn Ali* at the *Battle of Karbala* in 680 CE. For Sunni Muslims, the date is reverently observed as a day of voluntary fasting to celebrate God delivering the prophet *Moses* and the Israelites from the tyranny of the Egyptian Pharaoh. Its profound theological reverberations, rich liturgical traditions, and symbolic association with resistance against injustice make it one of the most historically significant dates in the practice of [[islam]].

## you gotta know

- Commemorates the martyrdom of *Husayn ibn Ali*, the grandson of the Prophet Muhammad, who was slain alongside his family and seventy-two companions by the Umayyad army of caliph *Yazid I* at the *Battle of Karbala*.
- Serves as the emotional climax of the ten-day mourning period of *Muharram*, culminating in widespread Shia mourning ceremonies collectively termed *azadari*, which include public weeping, laments, and rhythmic chest-beating known as *matam*.
- Features traditional street processions in which mourners carry elaborate replicas of Husayn's mausoleum, known as *ta'ziya* shrines or *tabuts*, accompanied by symbolic black banners, horse processions, and ceremonial standards called *alams*.
- Preserves a celebrated tradition of theatrical Passion plays, also termed *ta'ziyeh*, which are performed across Iran and Central Asia to dramatically reenact the betrayal, suffering, and deaths of the Karbala martyrs.
- Observed by Sunni Muslims with voluntary dawn-to-dusk fasting commemorating the parting of the Red Sea for the prophet *Moses*, traditionally paired with fasting on the preceding ninth day, *Tasu'a*, or the eleventh day.
- Connected in Ottoman and Turkish folk culinary tradition with the preparation and sharing of *aşure*, a sweet commemorative porridge of grains, pulses, and dried fruits said to have been cooked by Noah upon Mount Ararat.
- Involves the communal recitation of *Ziyarat Ashura*, a classical Shia liturgical supplication addressed to Husayn that affirms enduring loyalty to the *Ahl al-Bayt* and invokes divine condemnation upon their oppressors.
- Provokes ongoing religious debate regarding self-flagellation rituals known as *tatbir*, which have been largely denounced by modern Shia authorities and grand ayatollahs in favor of philanthropic blood donation drives.

## connections

- [[islam]] — the overarching faith within which the date marks a pivotal historical turning point and enduring theological divide.
- [[islamic-calendar]] — the twelve-month lunar reckoning whose initial month of *Muharram* places the holy day on its tenth date.
- [[middle-east]] — the historical and geographic heartland containing Karbala, Iraq, the central destination for millions of annual pilgrims.
- [[lunar-calendar]] — the astronomical system whose shorter year causes the commemoration to rotate continuously through all four solar seasons.
- [[judaism]] — the foundational Abrahamic faith whose Yom Kippur observances directly influenced the prophetic tradition of fasting on this day.
- [[five-pillars-of-islam]] — the core devotional pillars of the faith, informing the practice of voluntary fasting alongside obligatory Ramadan duties.

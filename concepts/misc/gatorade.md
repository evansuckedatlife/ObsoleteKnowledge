## summary

**Gatorade** is an American brand of electrolyte-replenishing sports beverages and nutritional products originally formulated in 1965 by a team of medical researchers at the *University of Florida*. Conceived to combat heat-related illnesses and rapid dehydration among collegiate athletes, the beverage established the modern commercial sports drink category by linking physiological science to athletic performance. Acquired by *Quaker Oats* and later *PepsiCo*, the brand became a global pop-culture phenomenon famous for high-profile athlete endorsements and the championship tradition of the victory bath.

## you gotta know

- Invented in 1965 by a research team led by nephrologist *Robert Cade*, alongside *Dana Shires*, *H. James Free*, and *Alejandro de Quesada*, for the Florida Gators football team.
- Formulated with water, carbohydrates (sucrose and glucose), and electrolytes (sodium and potassium) to restore glycogen reserves and maintain hydration under extreme exertion.
- Aided the Gators under head coach *Ray Graves* in winning the 1967 Orange Bowl, prompting opposing coach *Bobby Dodd* of Georgia Tech to attribute his team's defeat to not having the beverage.
- Licensed initially to *Stokely-Van Camp* in 1967 before being acquired by *Quaker Oats* in 1983, which expanded national distribution and launched the *Gatorade Sports Science Institute*.
- Generated substantial ongoing royalty revenues for the *University of Florida* and the inventors' *Gatorade Trust* through a long-standing intellectual property settlement.
- Popularized the "Gatorade shower" tradition in the mid-1980s, when *Jim Burt* and *Harry Carson* of the *New York Giants* dumped a cooler over head coach *Bill Parcells*.
- Dominated athletic marketing in 1991 through the iconic "Be Like Mike" advertising campaign featuring NBA superstar *Michael Jordan*, establishing standard athlete endorsement models.

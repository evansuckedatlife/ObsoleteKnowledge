## summary

**Porsche**, officially registered as **Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG**, is a world-renowned German automobile manufacturer specializing in high-performance sports cars, sedans, and luxury SUVs. Founded in Stuttgart in 1931 by automotive engineer *Ferdinand Porsche*, the brand attained legendary status for its rear-engine designs, precision engineering, and peerless pedigree in international motorsport. Today operating under the umbrella of the *Volkswagen Group*, it remains an enduring symbol of European industrial design and automotive performance.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1931 in Stuttgart by Austro-Hungarian-born engineer *Ferdinand Porsche*, originally serving as an independent automotive consulting and design bureau before building its own branded vehicles.
- Designed the iconic *Volkswagen Beetle* (*Käfer*) under government commission in the 1930s, whose air-cooled, rear-engine layout served as the mechanical architecture for early Porsche sports cars.
- Produced its first proprietary production model, the *Porsche 356*, developed and hand-built in Gmünd, Austria, under the leadership of Ferdinand's son, *Ferry Porsche*.
- Introduced the iconic rear-engine *Porsche 911* in 1964, styled by *Ferdinand Alexander "Butzi" Porsche*, which became one of the most celebrated and enduring sports car silhouettes in history.
- Dominated endurance motorsport, accumulating an all-time record 19 overall victories at the prestigious *24 Hours of Le Mans* with legendary racecars such as the *917*, *956*, *962*, and the hybrid *919*.
- Maintained a complex corporate and financial shareholding entanglement with *Volkswagen*, culminating in an audacious attempted takeover in 2008 that ultimately led to Porsche becoming an integrated brand of the *Volkswagen Group*.

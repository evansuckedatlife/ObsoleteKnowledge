## summary

**LG**, officially the *LG Corporation* and formerly known as *Lucky-Goldstar*, is a major South Korean multinational conglomerate (*chaebol*) headquartered in Seoul. Established in 1947 by entrepreneur *Koo In-hwoi*, the company played an instrumental role in the postwar industrial development and economic transformation known as the *Miracle on the Han River*. Today, it is recognized globally for its market leadership in consumer electronics, OLED display technology, petrochemicals, and electric vehicle lithium-ion battery manufacturing.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1947 as *Lak-Hui Chemical Industrial Corp.* (pronounced "Lucky"), producing South Korea's first domestic commercial face cream called *Lucky Cream*.
- Expanded into domestic home appliances and electronics in 1958 by establishing *Goldstar Co., Ltd.*, which produced the nation's first domestic radio, refrigerator, and television.
- Rebranded and merged its core operations under the unified brand name *LG* in 1995, pairing the abbreviation with the globally recognized marketing slogan "Life's Good".
- Operates as one of South Korea's massive family-controlled conglomerates known as *chaebols*, historically steered through multiple generations of the *Koo* family.
- World pioneer in display manufacturing via *LG Display*, serving as the predominant global producer of large-scale organic light-emitting diode (OLED) television panels.
- Spun off its chemical and energy storage enterprise into *LG Energy Solution*, becoming one of the world's largest suppliers of lithium-ion batteries for electric automobiles.
- Maintained a significant presence in the global smartphone industry, introducing dual-core processors and ultra-wide cameras before officially exiting the mobile phone market in 2021.

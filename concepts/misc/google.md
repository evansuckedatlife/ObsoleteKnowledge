## summary

**Google** is an American multinational corporate enterprise and technology platform founded in 1998 by *Larry Page* and *Sergey Brin* while doctoral candidates at *Stanford University*. Propelled by *PageRank*, an innovative graph-theoretical link analysis algorithm that ranked web pages based on citation structure rather than raw keyword frequency, the business evolved from an academic search engine into the preeminent index of human digital knowledge. Reorganized in 2015 under the holding conglomerate **Alphabet Inc.**, its ecosystem encompasses search indexing, targeted algorithmic advertising, the *Android* mobile operating system, planetary mapping, cloud enterprise infrastructure, and advanced artificial intelligence research.

## you gotta know

- Conceived under the initial research moniker *BackRub* because the underlying system systematically analyzed incoming backlink networks across the *World Wide Web*.
- Derives its name from a deliberate misspelling of *googol*, the mathematical figure representing the integer one followed by one hundred zeros, selected to symbolize organizing immense seas of information.
- Operated initially from the Menlo Park garage of *Susan Wojcicki*, who subsequently served as an early marketing executive and longtime chief executive of the subsidiary *YouTube*.
- Revolutionized corporate web monetization through *AdWords* and *AdSense*, deploying second-price sealed-bid auctions to pair search queries with sponsored contextual advertisements.
- Codified an unconventional corporate culture in its 2004 initial public offering prospectus through the celebrated informal maxim *"Don't be evil"*, later revised to *"Do the right thing"*.
- Acquired vital platforms including mobile OS developer *Android* in 2005, video repository *YouTube* in 2006, and applied neural network pioneer *DeepMind* in 2014.
- Entered everyday lexical usage as an English verb, creating modern verbal noun constructions where looking up information online is described using standard nominal morphology.

## summary

**SNC-Lavalin**, rebranded in 2023 as **AtkinsRéalis**, is a Montreal-headquartered global engineering, procurement, and construction conglomerate whose sprawling international portfolio helped shape major twentieth-century infrastructure projects. Founded through the merger of two prominent French-Canadian engineering pioneers, the firm expanded rapidly across hydroelectric, nuclear, transit, and resource sectors worldwide. In contemporary geopolitical history, the corporation became famous as the focal point of a seismic Canadian political corruption scandal in 2019 that implicated the highest echelons of government and prompted sweeping debates over corporate accountability and judicial independence.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 1991 through the high-stakes merger of *Surveyer, Nenniger & Chênevert* (established in 1911 by *Arthur Surveyer*) and *Lavalin* (founded in 1936 by *Jean-Paul Lalonde* and *Roméo Valois*).
- Played an architecturally vital role in iconic Canadian megaprojects, including the massive *James Bay Project* hydroelectric complex, the *Darlington Nuclear Generating Station*, and the *Confederation Bridge*.
- Anchored international growth during the era of postwar [[colonialism]] and resource exploitation, managing extractive mining ventures, oil infrastructure, and civil projects across Africa, South America, and the [[middle-east]].
- Implicated in massive international bribery investigations by the *Royal Canadian Mounted Police* regarding approximately forty-eight million Canadian dollars paid to public officials in *Libya* between 2001 and 2011 to secure lucrative civil contracts under *Muammar Gaddafi*.
- Triggered the explosive 2019 Canadian political crisis when former Attorney General and Justice Minister *Jody Wilson-Raybould* testified she was subjected to inappropriate partisan pressure from the Prime Minister's Office to grant the firm a deferred prosecution agreement.
- The resulting ethics investigation led federal Ethics Commissioner *Mario Dion* to rule that Prime Minister *Justin Trudeau* breached federal conflict-of-interest legislation by attempting to influence the prosecutorial process.
- Rebranded comprehensively in September 2023 under the moniker *AtkinsRéalis*, combining the lineage of British design and planning giant *WS Atkins* (acquired in 2017) to distance corporate operations from historical criminal proceedings.

## summary

**Maserati** is an Italian luxury sports car manufacturer founded in 1914 in Bologna by the Maserati brothers. Known for its iconic trident emblem inspired by the Fountain of Neptune in Piazza Maggiore, the marque forged its reputation in pre-war and early Formula One grand prix racing before transitioning into handcrafted grand tourers. Its storied history combines elite European motorsport triumphs with persistent financial upheavals through ownership by Citroën, De Tomaso, Fiat, and Stellantis.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Bologna, *Italy* in December 1914 by brothers Alfieri, Ettore, and Ernesto Maserati under the name *Officine Alfieri Maserati*.
- The emblem of the company is the trident, modeled by brother Mario Maserati after the statue of Neptune in Bologna's historic Piazza Maggiore.
- Won the prestigious Indianapolis 500 back-to-back in 1939 and 1940 with driver *Wilbur Shaw* piloting the legendary 8CTF single-seater, nicknamed the *Boyle Special*.
- Captured the Formula One World Championship in 1957 when *Juan Manuel Fangio* piloted the iconic *Maserati 250F* to his fifth driver title after a stunning comeback at the Nürburgring.
- Produced its first road-going grand tourer, the *A6 1500*, in 1947, which was styled by *Pininfarina* and set the template for the brand's production cars.
- Relocated its headquarters from Bologna to Modena in 1940 under the ownership of industrialist *Adolfo Orsi*, placing it in the heart of northern *Italy*'s Motor Valley.
- Introduced the *Ghibli*, designed by *Giorgetto Giugiaro*, in 1967, which became one of the defining grand tourers of the post-war sports car boom.

## connections

- [[italy]] — the nation where the marque was established, headquartered in Bologna and later Modena.
- [[mercedes-benz]] — chief racing adversary during grand prix racing in the 1930s and the 1954–1955 Formula One seasons.
- [[mclaren]] — fellow Formula One constructor and luxury high-performance supercar manufacturer.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the technological modernization that enabled early automotive engineering and competition.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict that forced the factory to produce spark plugs and military equipment for the Italian war effort.
- [[rome]] — ancient cultural origin of the Neptune motif and trident symbolism used as the marque's corporate crest.

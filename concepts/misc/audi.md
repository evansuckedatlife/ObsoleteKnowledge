## summary

**Audi**, operating corporately as **Audi AG**, is an internationally renowned German automotive manufacturer headquartered in Ingolstadt, Bavaria, celebrated for producing luxury passenger vehicles and advancing modern driveline technology. Formed through the historic consolidation of four Saxon vehicle manufacturers into the **Auto Union** consortium during the interwar era, the company established itself as a titan of European precision engineering. In motorsports history and modern consumer culture, it is renowned for popularizing permanent all-wheel drive via its groundbreaking *quattro* system and developing an influential design aesthetic characterized by minimalist industrial elegance.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Zwickau by pioneer engineer *August Horch* in 1909 after being forced out of his original motor works; because legal restrictions prevented him from reusing his surname—which means *listen* or *hark* in German—he translated the word into Latin as **Audi**.
- Utilizes an iconic emblem of four horizontally interlocking rings, which originally symbolized the 1932 merger that formed *Auto Union* from four Saxon motoring enterprises: *Horch*, *Audi*, *DKW*, and *Wanderer*.
- Revolutionized international rallying in 1980 by introducing the *quattro* permanent all-wheel-drive system, which dominated the World Rally Championship, rendered rear-wheel-drive competitors obsolete, and transformed road car engineering.
- Immortalized the advertising motto *"Vorsprung durch Technik"* (*Advancement through Technology*), a promotional slogan devised in 1969 by advertising executive *Hans Bauer* that entered worldwide popular culture.
- Endured severe wartime devastation and postwar expropriation when its original Saxon production plants were confiscated inside the Soviet occupation zone of East Germany, forcing executives to rebuild operations from scratch in Ingolstadt, West Germany.
- Acquired by the *Volkswagen Group* from Daimler-Benz in the mid-1960s, gradually transforming into the conglomerate's high-technology luxury flagship to compete against rivals Mercedes-Benz and BMW.
- Set a historic benchmark in endurance sports car racing by claiming thirteen overall victories at the *24 Hours of Le Mans* between 2000 and 2014, pioneering direct gasoline injection (*TFSI*), turbodiesel (*TDI*), and hybrid racing powertrains.

## summary

**Sennheiser**, legally organized as **Sennheiser electronic**, is an internationally acclaimed German manufacturer of premium audio transducers, professional microphones, wireless transmission equipment, and high-fidelity headphone systems. Established in Lower Saxony immediately following the devastation of [[world-war-ii]] by electrical engineer *Fritz Sennheiser*, the enterprise revolutionized acoustic engineering and human auditory [[perception]]. From creating the world's first open-back dynamic headphones to manufacturing iconic studio microphones, the brand remains a dominant technological force across broadcasting, filmmaking, live performance, and audiophile music reproduction.

## you gotta know

- Revered among sound engineers and audiophiles for high-end open-back dynamic reference headphones, most notably the *HD 600* and *HD 650*, celebrated for their neutral acoustic accuracy, modular design, and transparent soundstage reproduction.
- Engineered the revolutionary *HD 414* in 1968, the world's first open-back headphone model, which abandoned heavy closed earcups in favor of lightweight open foam cushions, becoming the best-selling headphone in history with over ten million units sold.
- Produced the celebrated *MD 421* large-diaphragm dynamic microphone, introduced in 1960 and utilized worldwide for tracking aggressive audio sources such as tom-toms, kick drums, brass instruments, and electric guitar cabinets.
- Developed the ubiquitous *MKH 416* shotgun condenser microphone, an interference-tube directional microphone revered as the premier global industry standard for film location sound, television field reporting, and commercial voiceover work.
- Handcrafted the mythical *Orpheus* (HE 90) in 1991, an ultra-luxury electrostatic headphone system paired with a dedicated vacuum-tube amplifier, later updated in 2015 as the *HE 1* featuring a motorized chassis carved from Italian Carrara marble.
- Founded in June 1945 in the municipality of Wedemark near Hanover under the original name *Laboratorium Wennebostel* (*Labor W*), originally manufacturing tube voltmeters and electronic measurement instruments before building microphones for *Siemens*.
- Stage technicians depend on advanced capsule engineering and supercardioid pickup patterns to suppress acoustic feedback loops that would otherwise trigger a piercing loudspeaker [[screech]] during amplified live theatre and arena concerts.

## connections

- [[germany]] — home country and manufacturing base where the company was founded in Wennebostel and continues to maintain international headquarters.
- [[perception]] — sensory and cognitive interpretation of acoustic vibrations shaped, measured, and enhanced through electroacoustic transducer design.
- [[world-war-ii]] — destructive conflict whose conclusion spurred *Fritz Sennheiser* and university colleagues to rebuild scientific enterprise out of rural Hanover.
- [[five-senses]] — biological perceptual system encompassing human audition, targeted directly by high-fidelity sonic equipment and electrostatic transducers.
- [[screech]] — unwanted high-frequency acoustic feedback spike suppressed on live performance stages through directional microphone polar patterns.
- [[modernism]] — industrial design movement whose functional aesthetics, clean geometry, and scientific minimalism influenced post-war German consumer electronics.

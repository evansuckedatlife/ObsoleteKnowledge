## summary

**Yu-Gi-Oh!** (often rendered **Yu-Gi-Oh** or **Yugioh**) is a Japanese multimedia franchise created by manga artist *Kazuki Takahashi*, spanning long-running manga, animated television series, and a globally dominant competitive trading card game. Premiering in Shueisha's *Weekly Shōnen Jump* in 1996, the story follows timid high schooler *Yugi Mutou*, who inadvertently releases an ancient, enigmatic Pharaoh's spirit after solving an Egyptian artifact called the *Millennium Puzzle*. The series evolved from exploring dark morality games into focusing primarily on *Duel Monsters*, developing into one of the highest-grossing media franchises in modern history.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Kazuki Takahashi*, who initially conceived the premise as a horror-tinged series where characters played sinister games of retribution (*Shadow Games*) that punished villainous opponents with psychological "Penalty Games."
- The protagonist *Yugi Mutou* shares his physical body with *Yami Yugi*, the nameless alter ego later unmasked as *Pharaoh Atem*, who ruled ancient Egypt three thousand years prior to the story.
- Centered on seven magical relics known as the *Millennium Items*, forged through the sacrifice of an entire village in *Kul Elna*, including the *Millennium Eye*, *Millennium Ring*, and *Millennium Rod*.
- The in-universe tabletop game *Duel Monsters* was inspired by *Richard Garfield's* real-world game *Magic: The Gathering* and was originally introduced under the name *Magic & Wizards*.
- Iconic signature cards of the original cast include Yugi's spellcaster *Dark Magician*, his ruthless corporate rival *Seto Kaiba's* three copies of *Blue-Eyes White Dragon*, and *Joey Wheeler's* *Red-Eyes Black Dragon*.
- Features the five-piece monster *Exodia the Forbidden One*, which grants an automatic win condition if all five individual body pieces are assembled in the player's hand simultaneously.
- Showcases the three legendary *Egyptian God Cards* introduced during the *Battle City* arc: *Slifer the Sky Dragon*, *Obelisk the Tormentor*, and *The Winged Dragon of Ra*.
- Officially certified by the *Guinness World Records* as the top-selling trading card game of all time, having sold tens of billions of cards worldwide under publisher Konami.

- [[tragedy]] — incorporates deep tragic narratives surrounding ancient sacrifices, lost identity, mourning, and the inevitable parting of souls in its finale.
- [[perception]] — utilizes optical illusions, hologram solid-vision projection arenas, and mind-altering sensory games within high-stakes matches.

## summary

**Crocs** is an American casual footwear company founded in 2002 in Boulder, Colorado, known for producing lightweight, perforated foam clogs. Designed originally as slip-resistant boating shoes made from an innovative injection-moulded polymer, the clogs provoked intense aesthetic polarization before becoming a global cultural and commercial phenomenon. The brand revolutionized casual footwear manufacturing and modern retail through ergonomic appeal among service workers, high-fashion runway subversions, and customizable accessory ecosystems.

## you gotta know

- Founded by *Scott Seamans*, *Lyndon Hanson*, and *George Boedecker Jr.* to market an injection-moulded foam clog originally engineered by the Canadian company *Foam Creations*.
- Unveiled its inaugural model, the *Beach*, at the 2002 Fort Lauderdale Boat Show in Florida, where all two hundred initial pairs sold out immediately due to their marine utility.
- Manufactured exclusively from *Croslite*, a proprietary closed-cell ethylene-vinyl acetate resin that warms and softens with body heat, resists bacterial growth, and repels moisture without absorbing odor.
- Features thirteen standardized ventilation holes across the upper and vamp of the classic clog model, designed for aerated drainage in nautical environments.
- Acquired the accessories enterprise *Jibbitz* in 2006, creating a lucrative market for decorative charms snapped into the shoe's ventilation ports to enable user personalization.
- Transitioned from derided utilitarian garden wear to high fashion through collaborations with Parisian design houses such as *Balenciaga*, designer *Christopher Kane*, and artist *Salehe Bembury*.
- Popularized among healthcare professionals, restaurant kitchen crews, and service industry workers who adopted the shoe for its non-marking, lightweight orthopedic shock absorption.

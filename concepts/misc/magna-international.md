## summary

**Magna International**, commonly known simply as **Magna**, is a Canadian multinational mobility technology company and one of the largest automotive parts suppliers on earth. Headquartered in Aurora, Ontario, the corporation was founded in 1957 by Austrian-born industrialist *Frank Stronach* as a small tool-and-die shop. Magna is unique among tier-one automotive suppliers for its subsidiary *Magna Steyr*, which possesses complete vehicle engineering and contract assembly capabilities for major global marques.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1957 by *Frank Stronach* in a Toronto garage under the name *Multimatic Investments Ltd.*, landing its first automotive contract producing sun visor brackets for General Motors in 1959.
- Merged with *Magna Electronics* in 1969 to form the modern corporate entity, expanding rapidly into stamped metal, seating, mirrors, and complex powertrain assemblies across North America.
- Operates *Magna Steyr*, an Austrian subsidiary based in Graz that assembles complete turnkey vehicles for automakers including the Mercedes-Benz G-Class, BMW 5 Series, Jaguar I-Pace, and Toyota GR Supra.
- Distinguished by Stronach's corporate philosophy of *Fair Enterprise*, formalised in an internal *Employee Charter* that allocates a set percentage of pre-tax profits to employees, management, and research.
- Mounted a major joint bid with Russian lender *Sberbank* in 2009 to acquire a majority stake in General Motors' European subsidiary *Opel/Vauxhall*, though GM abruptly scuttled the deal.
- Became embroiled in political and family disputes during the 2010s, culminating in Stronach founding the Austrian political party *Team Stronach* and later suing his daughter *Belinda Stronach* over trust assets.
- Serves as a primary hardware supplier for the automotive shift toward electrification, providing integrated electric drive systems, battery enclosures, and advanced driver-assistance sensors.

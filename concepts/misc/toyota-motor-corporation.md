## summary

**Toyota Motor Corporation** is a Japanese multinational automotive conglomerate headquartered in Toyota City, Aichi Prefecture. Established in 1937 by *Kiichiro Toyoda* as a spinoff of his family's automated loom enterprise, the company grew into one of the world's largest automotive conglomerates by vehicle production volume. Beyond its commercial scale, the enterprise fundamentally revolutionized modern manufacturing, supply chain logistics, and management philosophy worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1937 by *Kiichiro Toyoda* as an automotive division of the *Toyoda Automatic Loom Works*, an enterprise originally established by his father *Sakichi Toyoda*.
- Pioneered the *Toyota Production System* (*TPS*), a manufacturing philosophy formulated by *Taiichi Ohno* and *Eiji Toyoda* centered around lean manufacturing, *just-in-time* inventory, and continuous operational improvement known as *kaizen*.
- Introduced the concept of *jidoka*, or automation with a human touch, allowing assembly lines to automatically halt when errors occur to prevent defective output.
- Shifted spelling from the family name *Toyoda* to *Toyota* because writing it in Japanese katakana requires eight brush strokes, a number traditionally associated with good fortune and clarity.
- Headquartered in Koromo, which in 1959 formally changed its municipal name to *Toyota City* to honor the dominant local manufacturing powerhouse.
- Owns and manages a sprawling stable of major automotive subsidiaries and brands, including luxury marque *Lexus*, youth brand *Scion*, commercial truck manufacturer *Hino*, small-car maker *Daihatsu*, and corporate stakes in *Subaru* and *Mazda*.
- Surpassed American automotive giant *General Motors* in 2008 to claim the title of the world's largest automaker by annual sales volume.

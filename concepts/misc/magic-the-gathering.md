## summary

**Magic: The Gathering**, widely known as **MTG** or simply **Magic**, is a tabletop and digital collectible card game created by American mathematician *Richard Garfield* and released in 1993 by *Wizards of the Coast*. Recognized as the first modern trading card game, it casts players as duel-wielding wizards called *Planeswalkers* who cast spells, summon creatures, and tap land resources. The game revolutionized tabletop gaming through randomized booster packs, ongoing tournament play, and a rich fantasy multiverse.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *Richard Garfield* while a doctoral candidate in combinatorial mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania, originally pitched under the working title *Mana Clash*.
- Built around the fundamental *Color Pie*, which segments game mechanics and philosophical worldview across five colors: White (order), Blue (intellect), Black (ambition), Red (chaos), and Green (nature).
- Features the legendary *Power Nine*, a group of exceptionally potent cards printed in early sets (*Alpha*, *Beta*, and *Unlimited*), most iconically the zero-cost artifact *Black Lotus*.
- Implements a strict, deterministic rule set refined by the 1999 *Sixth Edition* overhaul, which introduced the *Stack*—a Last-In, First-Out resolution method drawn from computer science.
- Guarantees collector value through the controversial *Reserved List*, a corporate policy enacted in 1996 promising never to reprint certain early cards in tournament-legal form.
- Supported by competitive formats categorized into *Constructed* (such as Standard, Modern, Legacy, and Vintage) and *Limited* (Booster Draft and Sealed Deck), alongside the multiplayer format *Commander*.
- Grounded in an expansive narrative continuity focused on the plane of *Dominaria*, the *Weatherlight* airship saga, and iconic characters like *Urza*, *Mishra*, *Yawgmoth*, *Jace Beleren*, and *Liliana Vess*.

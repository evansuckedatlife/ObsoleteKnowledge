## summary

**Patek Philippe** is an ultra-luxury Swiss watch manufacturer founded in Geneva in 1839, widely considered among the most prestigious horological houses in the world. Established by Polish watchmakers *Antoni Patek* and *Franciszek Czapek*, the manufacture reached global fame after partnering with the French horologist *Adrien Philippe*, the inventor of the keyless winding mechanism. The house is revered for creating groundbreaking mechanical complications, historical royal timepieces, and some of the most expensive wristwatches and pocket watches ever sold at auction.

## you gotta know

- Founded initially in Geneva as *Patek, Czapek & Cie* before *Adrien Philippe* joined to patent the modern crown mechanism for stem winding and setting without a separate key.
- Acquired widespread imperial acclaim at the [[1851-great-exhibition-london]], where *Queen Victoria* and *Prince Albert* purchased pioneering keyless pendant and pocket watches.
- Created the famous *Henry Graves Supercomplication* in 1933, an ultra-complicated gold pocket watch featuring 24 distinct horological functions commissioned by an American banker.
- Released the iconic *Calatrava* dress watch line in 1932, drawing heavily on minimalist Bauhaus principles to define the archetype of classic luxury timepieces.
- Designed the steel sports watch *Nautilus* in 1976 with legendary designer *Gérald Genta*, featuring a distinctive porthole-inspired octagonal bezel.
- Developed the *Calibre 89* in 1989 to celebrate the firm's 150th anniversary, which held the record for decades as the world's most complicated mechanical watch with 33 complications.
- Owned continuously by the Swiss *Stern* family since 1932, maintaining an independent manufacture status distinct from modern luxury conglomerates.

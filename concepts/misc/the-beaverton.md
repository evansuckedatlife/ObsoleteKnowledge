## summary

**The Beaverton** is a Canadian satirical news website and television comedy series established in 2010 by *Laurent Noonan* and *Luke Gordon Field*. Mimicking the journalistic style of national broadsheets and broadcast networks, the publication lampoons Canadian politics, national identity, cultural clichés, and international relations. Often characterized as the northern counterpart to the American satire giant *The Onion*, it has evolved into a prominent voice in contemporary North American satire.

## you gotta know

- Founded initially in 2010 as an online publication by *Laurent Noonan* and *Luke Gordon Field* before expanding nationwide with a large team of contributors across Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver.
- Serves as a Canadian counterpart to *The Onion*, parodying traditional print journalism layout and headline conventions to mock national political scandals, parliamentary procedure, and everyday life in Canada.
- Adapted into a half-hour satirical television news program broadcast on *The Comedy Network* and *CTV* in 2016, hosted by *Emma Hunter* and *Miguel Rivas*.
- Frequently targets quintessential Canadian cultural tropes such as cold weather, polite stereotypes, universal healthcare debates, Tim Hortons, and standard national winter attire like the [[toque]].
- Published several bestselling satirical compilations, notably the 2017 humor anthology book *The Beaverton Presents Glorious and/or Free: The True History of Canada*.
- Garnered multiple nominations and wins at the *Canadian Screen Awards* for its sharp television writing, ensemble sketch acting, and parody of nightly news broadcasts.
- Its viral parody headlines have frequently duped unsuspecting readers and international social media accounts into believing absurd, fabricated Canadian political declarations were authentic news items.

## summary

**Gildan** (formally *Gildan Activewear*) is a Canadian multinational manufacturer of basic apparel, sportswear, and hosiery, celebrated globally as the preeminent supplier of blank garments for screen printing and promotional merchandise. Founded in 1984 in *Montreal, Quebec*, the corporation revolutionized the wholesale textile trade by developing vertically integrated, large-scale manufacturing facilities in Latin America and the Caribbean. It represents a quintessential case study of post–*Cold War* supply-chain optimization, market consolidation in commodity apparel, and brand acquisitions including the rescue of *American Apparel*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1984 by brothers *Glenn Chamandy* and *Greg Chamandy* in *Montreal*, initially established to supply fabric to their family-owned childrenswear business, *Harley Inc.*
- Revolutionized the imprintable apparel industry by introducing low-cost, durable blank heavyweight cotton T-shirts, notably the ubiquitous *Ultra Cotton* line.
- Relies on extensive vertical integration, controlling large-scale spinning mills and automated finishing plants located predominantly in *Honduras*, *Nicaragua*, and the *Dominican Republic*.
- Acquired the intellectual property and retail assets of bankrupt hipster clothing label *American Apparel* at auction in 2017 for approximately 88 million dollars.
- Expanded through serial corporate buyouts, absorbing prominent consumer and hosiery labels including *Gold Toe*, *Anvil Holdings*, *Comfort Colors*, and *Peds*.
- Engaged in a high-profile corporate governance battle in 2023–2024 when institutional shareholders mounted a successful proxy revolt to reinstate ousted co-founder *Glenn Chamandy*.

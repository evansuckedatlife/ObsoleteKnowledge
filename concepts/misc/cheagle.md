## summary

The **cheagle**, also known as the **Chihuahua-Beagle mix**, is a popular designer hybrid dog developed by crossing a purebred *Chihuahua* with a purebred *Beagle*. Originating in the late twentieth century within the [[united-states]] amid growing interest in companion crossbreeds, it unites the compact stature and watchful loyalty of toy breeds with the keen tracking drives of scenthounds. It is widely cherished among domestic pet owners for its playful temperament, expressive hound features, and adaptable suitability for apartment and family living.

## you gotta know

- Bred intentionally as a first-generation or multi-generation cross between two historic breeds recognized by kennel clubs in the [[united-states]]: the Mexican toy *Chihuahua* and the English scenthound *Beagle*.
- Exhibits an intermediate physical profile, typically blending the beagle's drooping pendant ears and squared muzzle with the chihuahua's prominent rounded eyes and slender frame.
- Displays heightened olfactory sensitivity rooted in the [[five-senses]] and sensory [[perception]], frequently inheriting the beagle's single-minded instinct to trace ambient ground scents.
- Weighs on average between 12 and 20 pounds and stands roughly 9 to 14 inches at the shoulder, placing it firmly in the small-to-medium companion dog category.
- Possesses a short, low-maintenance double coat that commonly inherits classic hound tricolor patterning (black, white, and tan), warm fawns, or contrasting piebald spots.
- Exhibits an affectionate, energetic, and vocal personality, often combining the melodious hunting bay of the beagle with the spirited alert bark of the chihuahua.
- Excluded from formal purebred registries like the American Kennel Club or the Kennel Club of the [[united-kingdom]], though recognized by hybrid organizations such as the International Designer Canine Registry.
- Demands patient positive-reinforcement training from an early age to manage typical scenthound stubbornness and the territorial guarding tendencies common to toy breeds.

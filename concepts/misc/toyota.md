## summary

**Toyota** is an industrial city located in northern Aichi Prefecture, *Japan*, and the operational heartland of the Japanese automotive industry. Formerly known as *Koromo*, the city renamed itself in 1959 to honor its primary employer, corporate benefactor, and engine of local development. Today, the municipality represents the ultimate manifestation of a company town, housing corporate headquarters, sprawling assembly complexes, and technical research facilities.

## you gotta know

- Originally named *Koromo*, a historic settlement and regional agricultural hub renowned during the Meiji and Taisho eras for flourishing silkworm cultivation and silk reeling factories.
- Faced severe economic distress following the collapse of the raw silk export market in the 1930s, prompting town leaders to invite *Kiichiro Toyoda* to establish automotive manufacturing plants on inexpensive local land.
- Officially changed its municipal name on January 1, 1959, from *Koromo* to *Toyota* to celebrate the extraordinary growth of [[toyota-motor-corporation]].
- Anchors the central manufacturing heart of the Chūkyō Industrial Area, featuring massive industrial assembly sites like the *Honsha*, *Motomachi*, and *Tsutsumi* plants.
- Houses the *Toyota Kaikan Museum*, which displays cutting-edge eco-friendly vehicle technologies, robotic manufacturing demonstrations, and concept automobiles to international visitors.
- Hosted matches during the 2019 Rugby World Cup at *Toyota Stadium*, an architecturally distinctive athletic arena designed by celebrated Japanese architect *Kisho Kurokawa*.
- Sits within Mikawa Province, the ancestral cradle of the *Tokugawa* clan who unified Japan under the Edo shogunate.

## connections

## summary

In formal logic, mathematics, and grammar, a **conjunction** functions as an indispensable connective that joins distinct constituents into a unified compound structure. In propositional calculus, the **logical conjunction** represents a truth-functional operator that yields a true evaluation if and only if every constituent conjunct is simultaneously true. In natural language [[linguistics]], conjunctions form a vital closed lexical class that connects words, phrases, and clauses, establishing syntactic hierarchy and discourse relationships across world languages.

## you gotta know

- In propositional logic, a truth table assigns truth to a conjunction only when all constituent propositions evaluate to true, failing if even a single conjunct is false.
- Represented across mathematical logic and computer science by symbols including the wedge, the ampersand, or simple multiplication in Boolean algebra.
- De Morgan's laws dictate that the negation of a conjunction is logically equivalent to the disjunction of the negations, mirroring set [[complement]] operations over intersections.
- In structural [[linguistics]], conjunctions are primarily classified into coordinating conjunctions linking grammatically equivalent units and subordinating conjunctions introducing dependent clauses.
- The conjunction fallacy, uncovered in behavioral economics and psychology, occurs when people mistakenly judge a compound conjunction as more probable than one of its individual components.
- In classical rhetoric and literary prose, polysyndeton intentionally multiplies conjunctions for rhythmic resonance, whereas asyndeton eliminates them to create dramatic urgency.
- In astronomy, an apparent conjunction takes place when two celestial bodies share the same right ascension or ecliptic longitude as observed from Earth.

## connections

- [[converse]] — conditional statements and their converses are analyzed in truth tables alongside conjunctions and material implications.
- [[complement]] — set intersection corresponds directly to logical conjunction, while set complements demonstrate negation across Boolean algebras.
- [[linguistics]] — conjunctions form the primary connective tissue in syntax, linking clauses, phrases, and coordinating syntactic structures.
- [[ancient-greece]] — Aristotle and early Stoic logicians first systematically analyzed connective particles and compound truth-evaluable propositions.
- [[geometry]] — geometric proofs rely upon conjoined premises and axiomatic conditions to establish deductive geometric theorems.
- [[enlightenment]] — pioneering thinkers such as Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz developed the algebraic treatment of conjunctions that preceded modern formal logic.

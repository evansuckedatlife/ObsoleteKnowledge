## summary

**Canada Day** is the national holiday of Canada, celebrated annually on July 1 to commemorate the federation of the North American colonies into a single dominion. Established under the *British North America Act* of 1867, the celebration marks the union of Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, and the Province of Canada into the new nation under the crown of the *[[british-empire]]*. Originally known as *Dominion Day*, the statutory holiday was officially renamed in 1982 alongside the patriation of the Canadian Constitution.

## you gotta know

- Celebrated on July 1 every year, marking the anniversary of the enactment of the *British North America Act* on July 1, 1867, which created the federal Dominion of Canada.
- Originally named *Dominion Day* (French: *Le Jour du Dominion*), reflecting Canada's constitutional status as a self-governing dominion within the *[[british-empire]]*.
- Renamed officially to **Canada Day** on October 27, 1982, following the patriation of the Constitution and the passage of the *Canada Act 1982* under Prime Minister *Pierre Trudeau*.
- In Quebec, July 1 is informally known as *Moving Day* (*Fête du déménagement*), because fixed residential apartment lease terms traditionally begin and terminate on this date rather than centering strictly on civic celebrations.
- In the province of Newfoundland and Labrador, the morning of July 1 is solemnly observed as Memorial Day, commemorating the catastrophic casualties suffered by the *Royal Newfoundland Regiment* at the Battle of Beaumont-Hamel on the opening day of the Battle of the Somme during *[[world-war-i]]*.
- The first major milestone celebration was the 1967 Canadian Centennial, which spurred an enormous wave of public infrastructure, the commissioning of the Centennial Flame on Parliament Hill, and the landmark *Expo 67* international exhibition in Montreal.
- Standard nationwide festivities include citizenship ceremonies, concerts on Parliament Hill in Ottawa, military flypasts by the *Snowbirds* aerobatic demonstration team, and widespread fireworks displays featuring the national red-and-white colors and the *[[canadian-flag]]*.

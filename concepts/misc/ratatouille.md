## summary

**Ratatouille** is a critically acclaimed 2007 American animated comedy-drama film produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Brad Bird, the film follows an ambitious young rat endowed with hyper-refined culinary gifts who forms an improbable cooking partnership with a garbage boy in a legendary Paris restaurant. Celebrated for its intricate gastronomic rendering and artistic ambition, the work stands as a poignant meditation on creativity, sensory delight, and the democratic nature of artistic genius.

## you gotta know

- Directed by Brad Bird, who took over creative direction from Jan Pinkava in 2005 to overhaul the story and visual pacing.
- Centers on the culinary adventures of **Remy**, a sewer rat with extraordinary olfactory and gustatory talents who idolizes the deceased celebrity chef *Auguste Gusteau*.
- Features the central slogan *Anyone can cook*, a philosophical maxim coined by *Gusteau* that serves as the moral core of the narrative.
- Centers on Remy secretly directing the clumsy culinary novice *Alfredo Linguini* by pulling his hair tufts beneath a tall *toque blanche* chef's hat.
- Climaxes with the preparation of a traditional Provençal layered vegetable dish, based on chef Thomas Keller's *confit byaldi* variation of the title stew, to appease an imposing critic.
- Depicts the austere, formidable food critic *Anton Ego*, voiced by Peter O'Toole, whose icy skepticism instantly melts upon tasting the titular rustic dish.
- Awarded the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and widely lauded for its exquisite atmospheric simulation of the culinary arts in *Paris*.

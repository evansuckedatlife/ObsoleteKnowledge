## summary

**Microsoft** is an American multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington, renowned for developing the personal computer operating systems and productivity software that came to define the digital age. Founded in 1975 by childhood friends *Bill Gates* and *Paul Allen* in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the company rose to global dominance through *MS-DOS* and the ubiquitous *Windows* graphical environment. As one of the foundational corporate giants of the modern computing revolution, **Microsoft Corporation** shaped software licensing, personal computing ubiquity, enterprise platforms, and landmark antitrust law in the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries.

## you gotta know

- Founded in April 1975 in Albuquerque, New Mexico, by *Bill Gates* and *Paul Allen* to develop and market a *BASIC* programming language interpreter for the *MITS Altair 8800* microcomputer.
- Secured a pivotal breakthrough in 1980 by partnering with *IBM* to provide the primary operating system for the *IBM Personal Computer*, licensing *86-DOS* from *Tim Paterson* of *Seattle Computer Products* and rebranding it as *MS-DOS*.
- Retained non-exclusive licensing rights to *MS-DOS* in the *IBM* agreement, an astute strategic decision that enabled the company to license the operating system to competing "PC clone" manufacturers and capture near-monopoly market share.
- Released the first version of *Windows* in 1985 as a graphical operating environment running on top of *MS-DOS*, followed by the massively popular *Windows 95*, which introduced the Start button, taskbar, and integrated 32-bit architecture.
- Bundled word processing, spreadsheet, and presentation applications into *Microsoft Office* (incorporating *Word*, *Excel*, and *PowerPoint*), establishing the de facto software standard across global business and educational institutions.
- Subject to the landmark federal antitrust case *United States v. Microsoft Corp.* in 1998 over the predatory bundling of the *Internet Explorer* web browser with *Windows* to undermine competitor *Netscape Navigator*.
- Expanded into consumer gaming and home entertainment with the launch of the *Xbox* console in 2001, anchoring a major video game publishing empire led by the *Halo* franchise and *Master Chief*.
- Underwent a major strategic pivot under third chief executive officer *Satya Nadella* starting in 2014, shifting focus toward the *Azure* cloud computing platform, cross-platform open-source integration, and substantial investments in artificial intelligence with *OpenAI*.

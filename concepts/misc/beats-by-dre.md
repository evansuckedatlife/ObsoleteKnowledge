## summary

**Beats by Dre** is an American consumer audio and electronics brand established in 2006 by legendary hip-hop producer *Dr. Dre* and record executive *Jimmy Iovine*. The company transformed high-end headphones from utilitarian studio monitors into prestigious fashion accessories and cultural status symbols characterized by bass-heavy acoustic profiles. Acquired by *Apple Inc.* in 2014 for three billion dollars, the brand stands as one of the most lucrative commercial mergers in music business history and provided the foundation for modern algorithmic music streaming.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in the mid-2000s when *Jimmy Iovine* and *Dr. Dre* lamented that audio piracy and cheap plastic earbuds were degrading the sound fidelity intended by record producers.
- Partnered originally with *Monster Cable* to manufacture and engineer the hardware for its debut model, the *Beats by Dr. Dre Studio* headphones, released in 2008.
- Engineered with a signature acoustic profile emphasizing heavy sub-bass response, which drew mixed reviews from audiophiles but captured mainstream pop, hip-hop, and athletic consumer demographics.
- Pioneered aggressive influencer marketing by distributing premium headphones to world-class athletes at the *2008 Beijing Olympics* and the *2012 London Olympics*, circumventing official sponsorship restrictions.
- Acquired music streaming pioneer *MOG* in 2012 to develop *Beats Music*, an algorithmic and curated subscription service designed by *Ian Rogers* and *Trent Reznor*.
- Purchased outright by *Apple Inc.* in May 2014 for three billion dollars, making *Dr. Dre* one of the wealthiest figures in global hip-hop history.
- Served as the corporate and conceptual launchpad for *Apple Music*, which absorbed the technology, curation architecture, and executive talent of the brand's streaming division in 2015.

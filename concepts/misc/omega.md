## summary

**Omega** is the twenty-fourth, final letter of the Greek alphabet, named to designate an open, long vowel sound in direct contrast with the short vowel *omicron*. Throughout intellectual history, Western religion, and modern mathematical science, the glyph stands as an iconic emblem of ultimate endings, boundary limits, and cosmic totality. Its uppercase glyph **Ω** and lowercase glyph *ω* provide essential standard notations across physics, algorithmic analysis, electrical engineering, and observational cosmology.

## you gotta know

- Denotes the final letter of the Greek writing system, translating literally as *great O* (*o mega*) to distinguish its phonetic length from *o mikron* (*little O*).
- Formulates the theological phrase *Alpha and Omega* in the Christian *Book of Revelation*, symbolizing the eternal, all-encompassing nature of the divine as the first and the last.
- Represents the *ohm*, the *SI* unit of electrical resistance named in tribute to German physicist *Georg Simon Ohm*, while an inverted glyph (*℧*, or mho) historically denoted electrical conductance.
- Serves in theoretical computer science as big *Ω* notation, defining an asymptotic lower bound on the computational complexity and run-time growth of algorithms.
- Functions in classical mechanics and physics as the standard lowercase variable *ω* for angular velocity, rotational frequency, and oscillatory rate in harmonic systems.
- Designates the cosmological density parameter in astrophysics, where uppercase *Ω* describes the ratio of actual cosmic mass-energy density to the critical density determining universal geometry.
- Identifies the total sample space in probability theory and mathematical statistics, encapsulating the comprehensive set of all possible experimental outcomes.

## summary

**PepsiCo** is an American multinational food, snack, and beverage corporation headquartered in *Purchase*, New York, established in 1965 through the consolidation of the *Pepsi-Cola Company* and snack manufacturer *Frito-Lay*. Founded within the [[united-states]], the corporate titan emerged as a dominant engine of mass consumer marketing, pioneering combative brand campaigns and shaping global food distribution networks. Its aggressive international diplomacy, landmark commercial agreements across the Iron Curtain, and massive retail presence established it as an enduring icon of modern corporate capitalism during the [[cold-war]] and the decades that followed.

## you gotta know

- The enterprise was created in 1965 under the leadership of beverage executive *Donald Kendall* and snack magnate *Herman Lay*, merging *Pepsi-Cola* with *Frito-Lay* to couple carbonated refreshments directly with salted packaged snacks.
- Flagship soft drink origins trace to 1893 in North Carolina, where pharmacist *Caleb Bradham* created a digestive formulation known as "Brad's Drink," rebranding it as *Pepsi-Cola* five years later to emphasize pepsin enzymes and kola nut flavoring.
- The corporation engaged in the famous "Cola Wars" against longtime rival *The Coca-Cola Company*, executing bold marketing maneuvers such as the televised, blind sip-testing "Pepsi Challenge" and defining demographic cohorts through the "Pepsi Generation" slogan.
- Corporate diplomacy breached the Iron Curtain in 1959 when vice president *Richard Nixon* guided Soviet leader *Nikita Khrushchev* toward a corporate kiosk at the American National Exhibition in Moscow, leading to a historic 1972 agreement that made the drink the first capitalist consumer good bottled inside the [[soviet-union]].
- Under the innovative tenure of chief executive officer *Indra Nooyi* from 2006 to 2018, the conglomerate embraced its "Performance with Purpose" paradigm, expanding sustainable practices and shifting consumer portfolios toward health-conscious nutrition.
- A bizarre 1989 trade renewal occurred when the hard-currency-strapped government of the [[soviet-union]] transferred seventeen decommissioned military submarines, a frigate, a cruiser, and an ocean tugboat to the firm to pay for syrup concentrate, prompting the corporate joke that it temporarily commanded a massive naval fleet.
- Legal lore enshrines the company in contract jurisprudence through *Leonard v. Pepsico, Inc.*, wherein a federal judge rejected an ambitious consumer's lawsuit claiming an AV-8 Harrier II jump jet advertised playfully in a television commercial for seven million promotional catalog points.

- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical development that instituted high-speed mechanized bottling, food preservation, and automated factory mass production.

## summary

**Automobili Lamborghini**, universally referred to as **Lamborghini**, is an Italian luxury sports car and performance SUV manufacturer based in Sant'Agata Bolognese. Established in 1963 by manufacturing magnate *Ferruccio Lamborghini*, the marque was founded to produce refined grand touring automobiles capable of rivaling *Ferrari* following a dispute over sports car clutches. The brand became a lasting icon of twentieth-century mechanical craft, pioneering the mid-engine rear-wheel-drive configuration for exotic automobiles and establishing a long aesthetic legacy defined by angular silhouettes and bullfighting motifs.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1963 by *Ferruccio Lamborghini*, an industrialist who built a thriving tractor company, *Lamborghini Trattori*, using surplus military vehicle engines and drivelines following *[[world-war-ii]]*.
- The company emblem features a raging bull inside a shield, chosen because *Taurus* was the founder's zodiac sign and inspired by his visit to the *Don Eduardo Miura* ranch in Seville, *[[spain]]*.
- The 1966 *Miura*, styled by *Marcello Gandini* at *Bertone*, became the first true modern production supercar by placing a transverse twelve-cylinder powerplant directly behind the passenger compartment.
- The radical *Countach*, unveiled in 1971 with forward-hinged scissor doors and sharp geometric bodywork, cemented the wedge silhouette that came to define avant-garde automotive *[[modernism]]*.
- Vehicle cockpits have long relied on handcrafted, hand-stitched automotive *[[leather]]* and premium hide treatments to line dashboards, seating surfaces, and steering assemblies.
- Following insolvency during the 1973 international oil crisis and multiple changes of ownership, the manufacturer was acquired in 1998 by *Audi*, a subsidiary of the *[[germany]]*-based *Volkswagen Group*.
- Flagship models such as the *Diablo*, *Murciélago*, *Aventador*, and *Revuelto* preserved naturally aspirated twelve-cylinder engines while the ten-cylinder *Gallardo* and *Huracán* drove modern commercial success alongside the *Urus*.

## connections

- [[italy]] — nation where the company was founded and where its principal engineering works remain in Emilia-Romagna.
- [[leather]] — primary material chosen for the upholstery, dashboard trim, and hand-finished interiors of its grand touring vehicles.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — era of mechanized engineering and precision metalworking that laid the foundation for modern internal combustion assembly.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict that left behind the surplus military components used by the founder to establish his early tractor operations.
- [[modernism]] — aesthetic movement emphasizing bold industrial geometry, functional aerodynamic form, and dramatic wedge proportions.
- [[germany]] — corporate home of the *Volkswagen Group* and *Audi*, which modernized the marque's manufacturing facilities after 1998.
- [[spain]] — country whose bullfighting tradition supplied the namesake pedigree for famous models including *Islero*, *Miura*, and *Murciélago*.

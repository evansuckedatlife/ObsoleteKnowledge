## summary

**Kellogg's**, historically incorporated as the **Kellogg Company** and reorganized into entities including **W.K. Kellogg Co**, is an American multinational food manufacturing icon celebrated for popularizing dry ready-to-eat breakfast cereal. Originating from the nineteenth-century health reform movement at the Battle Creek Sanitarium in Michigan, the business revolutionized the modern morning diet through industrial grain flaking and mass marketing. Its products, packaging innovations, and beloved corporate mascots fundamentally reshaped global food consumption and commercial advertising.

## you gotta know

- Originated in the 1890s at the *Battle Creek Sanitarium*, an internationally famous health spa directed by Dr. *John Harvey Kellogg*, a Seventh-day Adventist physician who advocated vegetarianism, biological living, and bland grain diets to suppress sexual urges.
- The iconic *Corn Flakes* were discovered accidentally in 1894 when wheat dough was left out overnight, became stale, and formed thin, crisp flakes when pushed through rolling cylinders rather than a single flat sheet.
- Founded commercially in 1906 by younger brother *Will Keith (W.K.) Kellogg* as the *Battle Creek Toasted Corn Flake Company*, after a bitter dispute over whether to add sugar to the recipe to satisfy consumer palates.
- Prompted a decades-long family legal feud between the brothers over commercial branding, culminating in court rulings granting W.K. exclusive rights to sell cereal bearing the famous flowing red script signature of *Kellogg's*.
- Pioneered groundbreaking marketing techniques, including printing full-color back-of-box premiums, giving away small toys inside cereal boxes, and printing standardized nutritional disclosures and vitamin fortification data.
- Introduced some of the most recognizable commercial advertising mascots in media history, notably *Tony the Tiger* for Frosted Flakes, *Snap, Crackle, and Pop* for Rice Krispies, and *Toucan Sam* for Froot Loops.
- Restructured in 2023 by splitting into two independent publicly traded corporations: *WK Kellogg Co*, retaining the legacy North American cereal portfolio, and *Kellanova*, focusing on global snack brands like Pringles and Cheez-It.

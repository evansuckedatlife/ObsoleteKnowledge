## summary

**Wendy's**, founded in Columbus, Ohio, in 1969 by Dave Thomas, is an international fast-food restaurant chain recognized for its square beef patties, sea-salt fries, and signature Frosty frozen dessert. Established on the promise of serving fresh, never frozen beef, the chain pioneered the modern quick-service drive-through window. It occupies an enduring place in American corporate culture, famed for its red-pigtail mascot, humorous advertising campaigns, and acerbic modern social media persona.

## you gotta know

- Founded on November 15, 1969, in Columbus, Ohio, by entrepreneur *Dave Thomas*, who named the restaurant after his fourth child, *Melinda Lou "Wendy" Thomas*.
- Famously prepares hamburgers with square beef patties to convey that the company "doesn't cut corners" and ensure meat extends beyond the round bun.
- Popularized the modern quick-service drive-through window system with a dedicated exterior speaker and pickup lane in 1970.
- Introduced the *Frosty* dairy dessert upon opening, formulated as a thick hybrid of chocolate and vanilla soft serve designed to be eaten with a spoon.
- Launched the iconic 1984 commercial campaign featuring actress *Clara Peller* shouting the catchphrase *"Where's the beef?"*, which entered American political lexicon.
- Founder Dave Thomas starred as the down-to-earth television spokesperson in more than eight hundred commercials during the 1990s.
- First major fast-food chain to introduce a nationwide salad bar concept (the *SuperBar*) in 1979, and later pioneered the popular 99-cent value menu structure.
- Developed an influential corporate social media presence characterized by sharp wit, rival-brand teasing, and informal internet pop-culture banter.

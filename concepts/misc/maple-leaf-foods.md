## summary

**Maple Leaf Foods** is one of Canada's largest consumer packaged food companies, headquartered in Mississauga, Ontario, and specializing in prepared meats, poultry, and plant-based protein products. Tracing its corporate roots back to late nineteenth-century agricultural consolidation, the company expanded through twentieth-century industrial food processing into an international supplier. It remains prominent in modern corporate studies both for its national branding and for its crisis management during a fatal foodborne illness outbreak in 2008.

## you gotta know

- Formed in its modern corporate structure in 1991 through the merger of *Canada Packers* and *Maple Leaf Mills*, consolidating major meat-packing and flour-milling entities.
- Traces foundational ancestry back to the 1861 establishment of the *William Davies Company*, which became the largest pork processor in the British Empire.
- Contributed heavily to Toronto acquiring the popular industrial moniker *Hogtown*, driven by Davies's immense central processing facilities.
- Supplied vast quantities of cured bacon and canned meats to Allied forces across the Atlantic during [[world-war-i]] and [[world-war-ii]].
- Acquired by the *McCain* family and the Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan during the 1990s, after which leadership reorganized meat-packing operations.
- Suffered a nationwide food safety catastrophe in 2008 when a listeriosis outbreak linked to contaminated deli slicers at its Toronto plant caused twenty-two deaths.
- Praised widely by public relations and business scholars for CEO *Michael McCain*'s rapid public apology, comprehensive recalls, and transparent accountability campaign following the 2008 outbreak.
- Invested heavily in alternative meat substitutes during the late 2010s by acquiring American plant-based brands such as *Lightlife* and *Field Roast*.

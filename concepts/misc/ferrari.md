## summary

**Ferrari** is an iconic Italian luxury sports car manufacturer and motorsport team founded by *Enzo Ferrari* in 1939 out of Alfa Romeo's racing division. Based in Maranello, [[italy]], the marque is internationally synonymous with elite performance engineering, striking aerodynamic styling, and dominant competitive participation in Formula One via *Scuderia Ferrari*. In cultural history and competitive quizzing, the company stands as a defining symbol of mid-century Italian industrial revitalization, automotive craftsmanship, and the prestige motorsport era.

## you gotta know

- Founded initially as *Auto Avio Costruzioni* in 1939 before producing the first true badged road and competition model, the Ferrari 125 S, in 1947.
- Features the renowned *Cavallino Rampante* (Prancing Horse) insignia, originally painted on the World War I fighter aircraft of Italian flying ace *Francesco Baracca*.
- Remains the oldest surviving and most successful team in Formula One World Championship history, having competed consecutively since the inaugural 1950 championship season.
- Long adorned in traditional racing red (*Rosso Corsa*), the national racing color historically assigned to Italian entrants by international motorsport governing bodies.
- Built luxury grand tourers and road-going supercars primarily to finance *Scuderia Ferrari*'s high-cost motorsport campaigns, according to *Enzo Ferrari*'s founding philosophy.
- Embroiled in a legendary mid-1960s rivalry with the American Ford Motor Company at the 24 Hours of Le Mans following a collapsed acquisition buyout.

- [[cold-war]] — coincided with the firm's golden era of transatlantic luxury exports and high-profile grand prix engineering dominance.

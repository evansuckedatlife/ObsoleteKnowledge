## summary

**Burger King** is an American multinational fast-food chain celebrated as one of the world's largest hamburger restaurant franchises. Founded in Jacksonville, Florida in 1953 as *Insta-Burger King*, the company reorganized in Miami under *James McLamore* and *David Edgerton*, pioneering commercial flame-broiling technology and creating the signature *Whopper* sandwich. Its enduring corporate rivalry with McDonald's and iconic advertising campaigns have cemented its place in modern global consumer culture.

## you gotta know

- Founded originally in 1953 in Jacksonville, Florida, by *Keith J. Kramer* and his wife's uncle *Matthew Burns*, who utilized a patented mechanical cooking grill called the *Insta-Broiler*.
- Restructured and rebranded in 1954 after Miami franchisees *James McLamore* and *David Edgerton* purchased the struggling parent company and engineered the gas-fired *flame-broiler*.
- Introduced its flagship signature sandwich, the *Whopper*, in 1957, designed by McLamore as a quarter-pound grilled beef patty with sesame-seed buns to outsell smaller competitive burgers.
- Launched the iconic 1974 marketing campaign slogan *Have It Your Way*, highlighting customizable sandwich assembly as a deliberate contrast to the rigid, factory-assembly style of rivals.
- Operates under the name *Hungry Jack's* in Australia due to a pre-existing trademark dispute with a takeaway food shop in Adelaide when expanding abroad in 1971.
- Promoted its brand in twentieth- and twenty-first-century pop culture via *The Burger King*, a caricature royal mascot portrayed in advertising ranging from vintage cartoon royalty to surreal camp figures.
- Participated aggressively in the twentieth-century commercial price-slashing and promotional marketing competition widely termed in corporate history as the *Burger Wars*.

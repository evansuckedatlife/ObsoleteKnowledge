## summary

**Intel Corporation**, originally founded as *Integrated Electronics*, is an American multinational corporation and technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Established in 1968 by semiconductor pioneers *Robert Noyce* and *Gordon Moore*, the company became a foundational pillar of Silicon Valley and the global microelectronics revolution. Intel is renowned for commercializing the world's first single-chip microprocessor, the *Intel 4004*, in 1971, and for designing the ubiquitous *x86* microprocessor architecture that powered the personal computer era.

## you gotta know

- Founded on July 18, 1968, in Mountain View, California, by *Robert Noyce*, co-inventor of the integrated circuit, and *Gordon Moore*, author of the famous empirical observation on semiconductor scaling known as *Moore's Law*.
- Joined immediately by executive *Andy Grove*, who later served as CEO and chairman, institutionalizing high-performance management philosophies and authoring the influential business strategy text *Only the Paranoid Survive*.
- Developed the *Intel 4004* in 1971, designed by *Federico Faggin*, *Ted Hoff*, and *Stanley Mazor* for the Japanese calculator manufacturer *Busicom*, marking the arrival of the commercial microprocessor.
- Released the *Intel 8086* and *8088* chips in the late 1970s, which established the dominant *x86* instruction set architecture and were chosen by *IBM* in 1981 for the revolutionary *IBM Personal Computer*.
- Launched the iconic *"Intel Inside"* brand cooperative marketing campaign in 1991, turning an internal electronic component into one of the most recognizable consumer trademarks in the world.
- Weathered a major quality control crisis in 1994 over the *Pentium FDIV bug*, an error in the floating-point math lookup table of early *Pentium* processors that forced a widespread product recall.
- Emerged during the technological competition of the [[cold-war]], helping cement the semiconductor dominance of the [[united-states]] against rising international competition from manufacturers in [[japan]].

## summary

**Iron Dome**, known in Hebrew as *Kipat Barzel*, is a mobile, all-weather air defense system deployed by Israel to intercept and destroy short-range rockets and artillery shells. Developed during the late 2000s primarily by Rafael Advanced Defense Systems and Israel Aerospace Industries with significant financial and technical backing from the United States, it became operational in 2011. The system has drawn global strategic scrutiny for its automated radar tracking algorithms, high reported interception success rates, and transformative impact on contemporary missile warfare.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in the aftermath of the 2006 Second Lebanon War, when thousands of Katyusha and short-range rockets fired by Hezbollah struck northern Israeli population centers, prompting Minister of Defense Amir Peretz to commission an indigenous interception platform.
- Engineered primarily by *Rafael Advanced Defense Systems* alongside *Israel Aerospace Industries*, with radar systems designed by *ELTA Systems* and command-and-control software created by *mPrest Systems*.
- Operates in three coordinated segments: an advanced multi-mission detection and tracking radar (*EL/M-2084*), a battle management and weapon control center (*BMC*), and missile firing units containing vertical launchers.
- Fires the *Tamir* interceptor missile, which utilizes steerable aerodynamic fins, electro-optical proximity sensors, and a blast-fragmentation warhead to destroy incoming threats mid-air.
- Utilizes predictive trajectory calculation algorithms that instantly determine whether an incoming projectile's impact point falls in an open field or a populated civilian zone, conserving costly interceptor missiles by intentionally ignoring harmless trajectories.
- Backed by extensive co-funding and joint production agreements with the *United States*, which provided billions in direct congressional appropriations and established co-production of Tamir interceptors through American defense contractor Raytheon.
- Complemented by naval variants called *C-Dome*, installed aboard Israeli Navy Sa'ar 6-class corvettes to safeguard offshore economic installations, gas fields, and coastal shipping routes.

- [[middle-east]] — central defensive military technology deployed across ongoing regional conflicts, border skirmishes, and urban defense operations.
- [[united-states]] — heavily subsidized by American defense budgets and co-manufactured in partnership with American defense firms like Raytheon.
- [[cold-war]] — directly inherits tactical and electronic guidance principles refined during late twentieth-century surface-to-air missile and ballistic defense competition.
- [[geometry]] — relies on complex mathematical algorithms, parabolic trajectory extrapolation, and three-dimensional coordinate targeting to achieve kinetic mid-air interceptions.
- [[perception]] — utilizes advanced active electronically scanned array (*AESA*) radar sensors to detect, track, and discriminate incoming micro-signatures against background radar clutter.
- [[space-age]] — operates on missile telemetry, rocket propulsion mechanics, and avionics guidance born out of mid-century rocketry programs.

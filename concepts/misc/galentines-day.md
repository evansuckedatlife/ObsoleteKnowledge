## summary

**Galentine's Day** (also written **Galentines Day**) is an unofficial secular holiday celebrated annually on February 13 dedicated to honoring female friendship, solidarity, and platonic sisterhood. Conceived as a fictional celebration on the American television comedy *Parks and Recreation*, the occasion centers on women gathering over brunch, exchanging heartfelt compliments, and eating waffles. Since its broadcast debut in 2010, the holiday has transformed into a bona fide cultural and commercial phenomenon throughout the [[united-states]] and across international social media communities.

## you gotta know

- Created by television writer Michael Schur and actress Amy Poehler for the sixteenth episode of *Parks and Recreation*'s second season, titled simply *Galentine's Day*, which aired on NBC in February 2010.
- Protagonist Leslie Knope, portrayed by Poehler, defines the holiday's central ritual as leaving husbands and boyfriends at home to gather female friends for breakfast food, specifically towering plates of waffles.
- The episode's narrative introduces signature gift-giving traditions, such as Leslie presenting her friends with personalized mosaics made from the crushed bottles of their favorite diet sodas and 5,000-word essays about their greatness.
- Commercial adoption rapidly accelerated during the mid-2010s as retailers, bakeries, florists, and greeting card companies began marketing February 13 merchandise specifically targeting female peer groups.
- Merriam-Webster officially added the term to its dictionary in 2022, defining it as a holiday observed immediately prior to Valentine's Day to celebrate women's friendships.
- Cultural commentators frequently analyze the holiday as an empowering feminist counterprogramming to the romantic pressures and consumerist expectations surrounding traditional Valentine's Day.
- The concept expanded beyond strictly female gatherings into broader secular observances celebrating non-romantic bonds, platonic affection, and community support among close friends.

## summary

**Costco** (officially **Costco Wholesale Corporation**) is an American multinational retail enterprise that pioneered the membership-only warehouse club format. Tracing its lineage to Sol Price's founding of **Price Club** in 1976 and James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman's establishment of the first eponymous warehouse in Seattle in 1983, the corporation grew into one of the largest retail organizations in the world. Characterized by strict inventory curation, pallet-based merchandising, rock-bottom gross margins, and a celebrated private label, it represents an iconic institutional model of late-twentieth-century consumer capitalism.

## you gotta know

- Emerged directly from the mentorship of retail pioneer Sol Price, who opened the inaugural **Price Club** inside a repurposed San Diego aircraft hangar in 1976 before his company executed a full merger with Sinegal's chain in 1993.
- Renowned for its signature food-court hot dog and soda combination, famously frozen at the price of one dollar and fifty cents since its introduction in 1985 as an unyielding corporate commitment to member value.
- Employs a disciplined merchandising strategy that caps retail markups at approximately fourteen to fifteen percent and stocks only about four thousand individual stock-keeping units (SKUs), compared to tens of thousands in conventional supermarkets.
- Derives the overwhelming majority of its net operating profit from recurring annual membership dues rather than standard product markups, incentivizing high volume and customer retention.
- Introduced the private-label brand *Kirkland Signature* in 1995, consolidating disparate house brands under a moniker honoring its former corporate headquarters in Kirkland, Washington.
- Operates its own dedicated poultry processing complex in Fremont, Nebraska, established to maintain complete supply-chain control over its popular four-dollar-and-ninety-nine-cent rotisserie chickens.
- Frequently analyzed in labor economics for paying wages and providing healthcare benefits significantly above conventional retail industry baselines, resulting in exceptionally low employee turnover rates.

## summary

**Jean Coutu** is a Canadian pharmacist and billionaire entrepreneur who revolutionized the retail pharmacy and consumer healthcare industry in Quebec and broader North America. In 1969, he opened a pioneering discount pharmacy in Montreal that evolved into the **Groupe Jean Coutu**, also known internationally as the **Jean Coutu Group**, combining professional prescription services with large-scale front-of-store discount retailing. A celebrated figure of Quebec's postwar business renaissance, he came to epitomize the rise of homegrown Francophone enterprise during the modern economic modernization of the province.

## you gotta know

- Founded his flagship discount pharmacy in 1969 on Montreal's Sainte-Catherine Street alongside partner *Louis Michaud*, adopting the commercial moniker *Pharm-Escomptes Jean Coutu*.
- Transformed traditional apothecary practices by introducing extended operating hours, aggressive price discounting on non-prescription goods, and broad selections of cosmetics, toiletries, and household merchandise.
- Popularized one of the most famous and durable advertising slogans in Canadian corporate history: *"On trouve de tout, même un ami, chez Jean Coutu"* ("You find everything, even a friend, at Jean Coutu").
- Emerged as a leading champion of *Québec inc.*, the mid-to-late twentieth-century economic movement where Francophone entrepreneurs built major national corporations following the Quiet Revolution.
- Expanded operations aggressively into the northeastern *United States*, acquiring major retail chains including Brooks Pharmacy and several hundred locations of the Eckerd drugstore network.
- Orchestrated a massive cross-border corporate transaction by swapping American retail holdings for a substantial ownership stake in the major American pharmacy corporation Rite Aid.
- Oversaw the sale of the retail pharmacy empire to Canadian food distribution and supermarket giant Metro Inc. in 2018 in a transaction valued at over four billion Canadian dollars.
- Established the Marcelle and Jean Coutu Foundation with his wife, donating substantial philanthropic endowments to healthcare initiatives, autism research centers, and university pharmacy faculties.

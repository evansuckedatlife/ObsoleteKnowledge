## summary

**President's Choice** is an influential Canadian private-label grocery brand created in the mid-1980s by Loblaw Companies Limited under executive *Dave Nichol*. Conceived to transcend the cheap, utilitarian reputation of standard generic supermarket lines, the brand pioneered upscale private-label retail by offering premium quality at lower prices than national brands. Its runaway commercial success transformed North American grocery marketing, revolutionised supermarket supply chains, and established cultural touchstones ranging from *The Decadent Chocolate Chip Cookie* to private-label sparkling wines like [[prosecco]].

## you gotta know

- Introduced in 1984 under the leadership of Loblaw executive *Dave Nichol*, who positioned the line as luxury items personally endorsed by the supermarket chain's president.
- Propelled to widespread fame by *The Decadent Chocolate Chip Cookie*, launched in 1988, which used real butter and maximum chocolate chips to outsell *Nabisco*'s *Chips Ahoy!* across Canada despite zero traditional television advertising.
- Promoted primarily through *Dave Nichol's Insider's Report*, a satirical, comic-strip-styled flyer inspired by the Californian grocer *Trader Joe's* newsletter that became a nationwide cultural phenomenon.
- Expanded private-label retail into international markets during the 1990s, licensing its product line to major supermarket chains in the [[united-states]], including *Jewel-Osco* and *D'Agostino*, and in the [[united-kingdom]].
- Launched the upscale *Memories of* culinary sauce line, beginning with *Memories of Szechwan*, capitalizing on the burgeoning consumer appetite for global cuisine during the late twentieth century.
- Developed early commercial private-label iterations of international delicacies and regional appellations, packaging European imports such as balsamic vinegar, olive oil, and Italian sparkling wine like [[prosecco]] for mainstream retail consumers.
- Served as a seminal business case study in retail branding, demonstrating how grocery chains could capture higher profit margins and strong customer loyalty through vertically integrated store brands rather than relying on legacy multinational conglomerates.

## connections

- [[prosecco]] — popular Italian sparkling wine bottled and sold under private labels, illustrating the brand's democratization of luxury European culinary imports.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — foundational shift toward mass consumer manufacturing, food preservation, and global supply chains that made national supermarket distribution feasible.
- [[united-states]] — neighbor nation where the private label was syndicated across several prominent regional grocery networks during the 1990s.
- [[united-kingdom]] — country whose retail landscape, notably *Marks & Spencer* and *Sainsbury's*, served as an early inspiration for premium own-brand grocery strategy.
- [[modernism]] — aesthetic and design movement whose packaging sensibilities influenced late twentieth-century corporate retail design and clean typographic marketing.

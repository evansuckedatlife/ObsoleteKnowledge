## summary

**Ubisoft** is a prominent French video game publisher and developer headquartered in Saint-Mandé, near Paris. Established in 1986 by five brothers of the Guillemot family in Brittany, the company grew into one of the largest video game publishers in the world. It is renowned for creating major open-world franchises such as *Assassin's Creed*, *Far Cry*, *Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell*, and *Rayman*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in March 1986 in Carentoir, *Brittany*, by the five *Guillemot* brothers (*Claude*, *Michel*, *Gérard*, *Christian*, and *Yves Guillemot*), with *Yves* becoming its longtime chief executive.
- Its best-selling and culturally defining property is *Assassin's Creed*, an open-world historical action-adventure series that recreates settings like the Third Crusade, Renaissance *Italy*, the *American Revolution*, and Revolutionary *France*.
- Created the mascot character *Rayman*, designed by animator *Michel Ancel*, who debuted in 1995 and later spawned the wildly popular *Raving Rabbids* spin-off franchise.
- Acquired the rights to produce military-themed tactical shooters under the name of American author *Tom Clancy*, resulting in influential series including *Rainbow Six*, *Ghost Recon*, and *Splinter Cell*.
- Popularized a ubiquitous design formula for open-world games—often dubbed the "Ubisoft tower" mechanic—where climbing vantage points unveils points of interest on a map.
- Operates major multinational development studios worldwide, notably *Ubisoft Montreal*, which led development on seminal titles like *Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time* and *Assassin's Creed II*.
- Fended off a high-profile hostile takeover attempt by French media conglomerate *Vivendi* between 2015 and 2018 by enlisting outside investors such as *Tencent*.

## connections

- [[france]] — the country where the Guillemot brothers founded the company and where its corporate headquarters resides.
- [[italy]] — the primary historical setting for early franchise milestones such as *Assassin's Creed II* featuring Ezio Auditore.
- [[american-revolution]] — the central conflict depicted in *Assassin's Creed III*, exploring colonial Boston and New York.
- [[united-states]] — home to author Tom Clancy, whose intellectual property license became a foundational pillar of Ubisoft's catalog.
- [[realism]] — an artistic and simulation aesthetic pursued across tactical series like *Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six*.

## summary

**Plasma** is one of the four fundamental states of matter, characterized by an ionized gas composed of freely moving ions, free electrons, and neutral particles. Often described as the most abundant visible state of matter in the universe, it dominates stellar bodies, interstellar media, and astrophysical jets. Understanding its electrodynamic behaviors has fueled pivotal advancements in controlled nuclear fusion, astrophysics, and modern semiconductor manufacturing.

## you gotta know

- First identified in a Crookes tube by *Sir William Crookes* in 1879 as radiant matter, and later formally named by chemist *Irving Langmuir* in 1928 due to its fluid, blood-like transport of charged entities.
- Exhibiting quasi-neutrality, the medium contains equal densities of positive and negative charges on macroscopic scales while remaining capable of conducting immense electric currents.
- Characterized fundamentally by collective behavior, meaning local electromagnetic disturbances affect distant particles due to long-range Coulomb interactions rather than simple binary collisions.
- Defined quantitatively by physical parameters such as the *Debye length*, which measures the distance over which electric charges screen external electrostatic fields.
- Governed on macroscopic scales by *magnetohydrodynamics* (MHD), a theoretical framework pioneered by Nobel laureate *Hannes Alfvén* that combines fluid dynamics and Maxwell's equations.
- Observed naturally in celestial phenomena studied in [[astronomy]], including the solar wind, the Sun's corona, auroras, lightning discharges, and neon signs.
- Serves as the operating medium inside magnetic confinement fusion reactors such as tokamaks and stellarators, designed to achieve clean energy generation.

## summary

***Mysterium*** is an unfinished, monumental multimedia liturgical composition conceived by the eccentric Russian composer and pianist *Alexander Scriabin*. Envisioned between 1903 and his sudden death in 1915, the work was designed not as an ordinary concert piece, but as a week-long mystical synesthetic rite to be performed at the foothills of the Himalayas in India. Driven by radical ideas of artistic synthesis and theosophical ecstasy, Scriabin believed the performance would dissolve human individuality and initiate a cosmic cataclysm that would usher in a higher spiritual age.

## you gotta know

- Envisioned as an all-encompassing *Gesamtkunstwerk* engaging the *[[five-senses]]*, incorporating orchestral music, massive choruses, liturgical chanting, interpretive dance, visual color projections, and orchestrated aromatic scents.
- Scriabin intended the ritual to be staged over seven days and nights inside an open-air, spherical temple reflected upon a sacred lake in India.
- Bound directly to Scriabin's severe synesthesia and harmonic theories, relying on his customized mystic chord and intended color-light mappings previously tested in *Prometheus: The Poem of Fire*.
- Intended to eradicate all separation between performers and spectators, turning all present into active participants of a universal spiritual transformation.
- Left completely unfinished upon Scriabin's sudden death from septicemia in 1915, surviving primarily as a poetic text and seventy pages of musical sketches for a preparatory prologue titled *Prefatory Action* (*Acte préalable*).
- Russian composer *Alexander Nemtin* spent nearly three decades reconstructing, harmonizing, and scoring these remaining fragments into a playable three-part orchestral cycle.

## connections

- [[modernism]] — the broader cultural and musical avant-garde movement characterized by radical harmonic exploration and esoteric grand narratives.
- [[perception]] — philosophical study central to Scriabin's efforts to systematically unite hearing, vision, and olfactory experience.
- [[five-senses]] — physical sensory modalities deliberately manipulated simultaneously within the planned ritual.
- [[soviet-union]] — political regime whose state musicians, notably *Alexander Nemtin*, later transcribed and performed the reconstructed manuscripts.
- [[world-war-i]] — global conflict breaking out during Scriabin's final months of work, which he interpreted as the beginning of his prophesied apocalyptic rebirth.

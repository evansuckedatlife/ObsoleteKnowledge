## summary

**Dollarama** is a prominent Canadian value retail corporation headquartered in *Montreal*, *Quebec*, operating as the country's largest and most dominant operator of dollar stores. Founded in 1992 by *Larry Rossy*, a third-generation retailer whose family previously operated *Rossy S. Inc.* department stores, the chain transformed the Canadian retail landscape by adopting an all-fixed-price discount model that expanded from a single converted storefront in *Matane* to over 1,500 locations nationwide. Renowned for its resilient unit economics, direct import sourcing from overseas manufacturers, and strategic multi-price tier restructuring, the corporation serves as a cornerstone of suburban and urban discount shopping throughout Canada.

## you gotta know

- Converted from a struggling variety store in *Matane*, *Quebec*, in April 1992 by *Larry Rossy*, who pivoted his family's century-old retail legacy into a dedicated fixed-price one-dollar emporium.
- Maintained a strict one-dollar maximum price cap for its first seventeen years before successfully breaking the dollar barrier in 2009 by introducing 1.25, 1.50, and 2.00 Canadian dollar price points to combat inflation and broaden its product catalog.
- Expanded through a disciplined real estate strategy that placed stores in strip malls, downtown street fronts, and grocery-anchored plazas across every Canadian province, deliberately avoiding costly warehouse superstores.
- Private-equity firm *Bain Capital* acquired an eighty percent majority stake in the enterprise in 2004 for over one billion Canadian dollars, steering the company through its blockbuster 2009 initial public offering on the Toronto Stock Exchange.
- Leverages direct procurement agreements with overseas manufacturing hubs, particularly in China, bypassing intermediary wholesalers to maintain gross profit margins exceeding thirty-five percent on seasonal, household, and dry food items.
- Expanded internationally into Latin American markets through a major controlling investment in *Dollarcity*, operating value storefronts across *El Salvador*, *Guatemala*, *Colombia*, and *Peru*.
- Features heavily in Canadian consumer routine alongside daily staples, sharing commercial strip plazas with fast-food giants like *[[dominos]]* and offering branded snacks and pantry supplies that anchor everyday household spending.

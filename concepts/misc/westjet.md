## summary

**WestJet** is Canada's second-largest commercial airline, headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, near Calgary International Airport. Founded in 1996 by *Clive Beddoe* and a group of western business partners as a low-cost carrier, it successfully dismantled the domestic passenger monopoly historically maintained by flag carrier Air Canada. The airline plays a central role in Canadian transcontinental transportation, leisure tourism across the Americas, and seasonal surges during civic holidays such as [[victoria-day]].

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1996 by *Clive Beddoe*, *Mark Hill*, *Tim Morgan*, and *Donald Bell* to establish an affordable, low-cost western alternative to Air Canada and Canadian Airlines.
- Commenced flight operations using three second-hand Boeing 737 aircraft serving five destinations in Western Canada: Calgary, Edmonton, Kelowna, Vancouver, and Victoria.
- Modeled its initial low-cost, point-to-point business structure and egalitarian company culture on Southwest Airlines in the [[united-states]].
- Operated for decades exclusively with an all-Boeing 737 fleet before diversifying into long-haul international routes using Boeing 787 Dreamliners and regional operations via WestJet Encore with De Havilland Dash 8-400 turboprops.
- Maintained a famous profit-sharing model where employees were called "WestJetters" and owned substantial company stock, contributing to its marketing campaign centred on friendly service.
- Acquired in 2019 by the private equity firm Onex Corporation in a multi-billion-dollar privatization deal, subsequently shifting core operational focus back toward its western Canadian strongholds.
- Experiences intense seasonal scheduling demands, especially during long weekend holiday getaways across Canada like [[victoria-day]] and transborder winter resort routes.

## summary

**Volkswagen** is a massive German multinational automotive manufacturer headquartered in Wolfsburg, Lower Saxony. Founded in 1937 under the German Labour Front to produce an affordable "people's car," it became the flagship brand of the Volkswagen Group, one of the world's largest automakers. It is globally recognized for iconic automotive designs such as the rear-engine *Beetle* and the *Golf*, as well as its central role in West Germany's post-war economic resurgence.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in 1930s *Germany* under Adolf Hitler's regime to fulfill the vision of a cheap, mass-produced "people's car" (*Volkswagen*), with engineering led by Austrian designer *Ferdinand Porsche*.
- The industrial hub of *Wolfsburg* was specifically founded in 1938 as the *Stadt des KdF-Wagens* to house factory workers and manufacture the nascent vehicle.
- Production was redirected to military transport vehicles like the *Kübelwagen* and *Schwimmwagen* during *World War II*, utilizing forced labour.
- Rebuilt from ruin following *World War II* under the direction of British Army officer Major *Ivan Hirst*, who restored factory operations to supply occupying Allied forces.
- Achieved phenomenal global success with the *Type 1*, colloquially known as the *Beetle* or *Bug*, promoted by *Doyle Dane Bernbach's* revolutionary American advertising campaign "Think Small."
- Introduced the *Volkswagen Golf* (marketed as the *Rabbit* in North America) in 1974, a front-wheel-drive hatchback that revitalized the company and defined the modern compact car class.
- Became embroiled in the massive 2015 "Dieselgate" emissions scandal, in which the company admitted to installing software defeat devices on millions of diesel vehicles to falsify clean emissions tests.

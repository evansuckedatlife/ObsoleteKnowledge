## summary

**Calvin Klein** is an acclaimed American fashion designer who founded his eponymous global lifestyle brand in *New York City* in 1968. Celebrated for pioneering an aesthetic of clean minimalism, understated elegance, and functional ready-to-wear tailoring, he transformed casual apparel into high-end luxury fashion. His revolutionary approach to designer denim and provocative advertising campaigns reshaped mass-market culture and modern visual branding throughout the late twentieth century.

## you gotta know

- Founded *Calvin Klein Inc.* in 1968 alongside childhood friend *Barry K. Schwartz* in [[new-york-city]] with an initial focus on women's coats and structured dresses.
- Revolutionized the denim market in the late 1970s by introducing tight-fitting designer jeans featuring his signature brand logo prominently stitched across the back pocket.
- Launched iconic and controversial advertising campaigns, notably the 1980 commercial featuring a teenage *Brooke Shields* reciting the line about nothing coming between her and her Calvins.
- Transformed men's and women's undergarments into desirable designer fashion during the 1980s and 1990s by displaying bold typography along elastic waistbands in advertisements shot by *Bruce Weber* and *Herb Ritts*.
- Popularized the minimalist, slip-dress aesthetic of 1990s [[modernism]], championing the waif-like look of supermodel *Kate Moss* and debuting the barrier-breaking unisex fragrance *CK One*.
- Studied apparel design at the *Fashion Institute of Technology* in Manhattan, receiving multiple *Coty American Fashion Critics' Awards* and *CFDA Awards* throughout his distinguished career.
- Sold his company to apparel conglomerate *Phillips-Van Heusen* (*PVH Corporation*) in 2002, cementing his legacy as a titan of minimalist commercial fashion.

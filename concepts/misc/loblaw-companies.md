## summary

**Loblaw Companies Limited** is Canada's largest food and pharmacy retailer, encompassing an extensive national network of supermarkets, hypermarkets, discount food marts, and drugstores. Established in 1919 by grocers *Theodore Pringle Loblaw* and *J. Milton Cork*, the enterprise transformed Canadian consumer shopping by pioneering the modern self-service grocery store format with open shelving, transparent pricing, and centralized checkouts. Governed for generations under the *Weston* family holding company *George Weston Limited*, the conglomerate is celebrated for distinctive private-label brands such as *President's Choice* and *No Name*, its transformational purchase of *Shoppers Drug Mart*, and its substantial influence over national food supply chains.

## you gotta know

- Established in Toronto in 1919 when *Theodore Pringle Loblaw* and *J. Milton Cork* unveiled the original *Loblaw Groceterias*, pioneering the self-service format where customers selected goods directly from shelves rather than waiting for counter clerks.
- Majority-controlled by the *Weston* dynasty via *George Weston Limited*, an enterprise established in 1882 by *George Weston* and massively expanded over the twentieth century by *W. Galen Weston* and *Galen G. Weston*.
- Revolutionized private-label consumer branding under marketing innovator *Dave Nichol*, introducing the stark, yellow-packaged *No Name* budget brand in 1978 and the premium *President's Choice* line in 1984.
- Completed the historic 2014 acquisition of *Shoppers Drug Mart* (known as *Pharmaprix* in Quebec) for over twelve billion Canadian dollars, fusing national retail grocery distribution with retail pharmacy networks and the *PC Optimum* loyalty rewards ecosystem.
- Maintains a diverse multi-tier retail banner portfolio catering to varied socioeconomic markets, including *No Frills*, *Real Canadian Superstore*, *Fortinos*, *Zehrs*, *T&T Supermarket*, and *Provigo*.
- Subjected to widespread public and regulatory scrutiny in 2017 after admitting involvement in an industry-wide commercial conspiracy to fix wholesale and retail packaged bread prices in Canada between 2001 and 2015.
- Stands alongside industrial manufacturing powers such as [[linamar]] as a dominant economic driver within Ontario and Canadian commerce, dictating national retail logistics and grocery distribution networks.

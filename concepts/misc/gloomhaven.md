## summary

**Gloomhaven** is a cooperative tactical fantasy board game designed by American board game designer *Isaac Childres* and published by *Cephalofair Games* in 2017. Renowned for its massive twenty-pound box, persistent legacy-style campaign, and card-driven deterministic combat, the game eschewed traditional dice rolling in favor of strategic hand management. It achieved unprecedented critical acclaim within modern tabletop gaming, holding the prestigious top ranking on *BoardGameGeek* for several consecutive years.

## you gotta know

- Designed by *Isaac Childres*, a former physics graduate student who crowdfunded the ambitious fantasy project on *Kickstarter* across multiple record-setting campaigns.
- Set in and around a rugged frontier port city where players assume the roles of wandering mercenaries, known colloquially as *drifters* or *Inox*, *Quatryls*, and other unique fantasy species.
- Utilizes an innovative dual-card tactical action mechanism where players select two ability cards per turn, executing the top half of one and the bottom half of the other according to numerical initiative.
- Replaces traditional random dice rolling with a customizable *attack modifier deck*, which players refine through earned perks and scenario accomplishments.
- Features an exhaustion clock mechanism whereby cards are permanently "lost" or temporarily discarded across resting phases, simulating character fatigue and urgency during dungeon crawls.
- Incorporates legacy and role-playing mechanics, including sealed character tuckboxes, stickers affixed directly to a cardboard world map, and individual secret *personal quests* that trigger character retirement.
- Spawned a standalone introductory prequel box titled *Gloomhaven: Jaws of the Lion*, an expansive 2023 sequel titled *Frosthaven*, and a turn-based video game adaptation.

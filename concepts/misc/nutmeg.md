## summary

**Nutmeg** is a pungent, aromatic culinary spice derived from the seed kernel of the tropical evergreen tree **Myristica fragrans**, indigenous originally only to the remote *Banda Islands* of eastern *Indonesia*. Prized across the ancient and early modern worlds for seasoning, food preservation, and supposed medicinal protection against epidemic disease, the spice drove violent maritime competition among European empires during the [[age-of-exploration]]. Today, it remains a beloved pantry staple celebrated globally in sweet baking, savory sauces, and classic spiced beverages.

## you gotta know

- Obtained from the inner seed kernel of *Myristica fragrans*, while the lacy reddish aril enveloping the shell is processed into the distinct sister spice *mace*.
- Grated fresh over dairy-heavy recipes, winter custards, bechamel sauces, holiday eggnogs, and vintage cocktails such as a spiced [[old-fashioned]].
- The *Dutch East India Company* (*VOC*), commanded by Governor-General *Jan Pieterszoon Coen*, orchestrated the brutal 1621 *Banda massacre*, killing, expelling, and enslaving thousands of indigenous islanders to enforce an absolute trade monopoly.
- Under the 1667 *Treaty of Breda*, the Dutch exchanged their territorial claim over *New Netherland*—including *New Amsterdam*, now [[new-york-city]]—to [[england]] in order to secure undisputed control over the small nutmeg-rich island of *Run*.
- Nicknamed the *Nutmeg State*, the region of Connecticut earned its moniker from folk legends of crafty Yankee peddlers who carved fraudulent wooden replicas of the seed to sell to unwitting buyers.
- Contains volatile organic compounds including *myristicin* and *elemicin*, which act as weak psychoactive deliriants and can cause nausea, dehydration, cardiovascular distress, and prolonged hallucinations when ingested in excessive, toxic quantities.
- French horticulturalist *Pierre Poivre* broke the Dutch monopoly in the late eighteenth century by smuggling viable plants to *Mauritius*, eventually fostering extensive plantations across the Caribbean island of *Grenada*.

## connections

- [[age-of-exploration]] — early Portuguese and Dutch maritime expeditions undertaken specifically to secure naval routes to the spice-producing Moluccas.
- [[colonialism]] — violent imperial monopolization and corporate state violence carried out by the Dutch East India Company against native island populations.
- [[british-empire]] — long-running maritime rival to the Netherlands that contested the spice trade and cultivated extensive plantations across the West Indies.
- [[new-york-city]] — major North American commercial center famously retained by the British when the Dutch traded Manhattan away at Breda for the island of *Run*.
- [[old-fashioned]] — classic cocktail tradition that incorporates grated aromatic garnishes to enrich aged whiskey and bitters.
- [[five-senses]] — intricate sensory activation governed by volatile essential oils stimulating the human olfactory and gustatory systems.

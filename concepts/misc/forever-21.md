## summary

**Forever 21** is an American fast-fashion retail chain founded in Los Angeles in 1984 by South Korean immigrants *Do Won Chang* and *Jin Sook Chang*. The brand became emblematic of late-twentieth and early twenty-first-century consumer culture by rapidly copying high-fashion runway trends and selling inexpensive apparel to teenagers and young adults. At its height, the company operated hundreds of sprawling retail stores across the globe before filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2019.

## you gotta know

- Founded originally in 1984 under the name *Fashion 21* in the Highland Park neighborhood of Los Angeles, initially catering primarily to the local Korean-American community.
- Rapidly expanded into massive suburban shopping malls across the *United States* and internationally, pioneering the retail model known as fast fashion alongside European competitors like *Zara* and *H&M*.
- Noted for printing the biblical citation *John 3:16* on the bottom of its bright yellow shopping bags, reflecting the devout Christian faith of its founding family.
- Faced dozens of copyright and trademark infringement lawsuits from major fashion designers, independent artists, and pop stars such as *Ariana Grande*.
- Relied on rapid turnover of low-cost garments produced by offshore manufacturing networks, attracting widespread scrutiny over labor conditions and textile waste.
- Suffered severe financial strain due to overaggressive international expansion and immense store footprints, culminating in a high-profile Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing in September 2019.
- Purchased out of bankruptcy in 2020 by a consortium comprising *Simon Property Group*, *Brookfield Property Partners*, and *Authentic Brands Group*.

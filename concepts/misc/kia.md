## summary

**Kia**, operating historically as **Kia Motors** and rebranded globally as **Kia Corporation**, is South Korea's oldest motor vehicle manufacturer and its second-largest automaker behind parent company Hyundai. Founded in 1944 near the end of [[world-war-ii]], the company takes its name from Sino-Korean characters meaning "to arise out of the Orient." From producing steel tubing and bicycles to building affordable passenger cars and innovative electric platforms, the enterprise represents the rapid industrial rise of South Korea in the global economy.

## you gotta know

- Founded in December 1944 as *Kyungsung Precision Industry*, initially manufacturing steel tubing and bicycle components before producing South Korea's first domestic bicycle, the *Samchuly*, in 1952.
- Transitioned through licensed industrial partnerships with foreign automakers, producing licensed Honda motorcycles in the 1960s, licensed Mazda commercial trucks such as the *Bongo*, and the Mazda-derived *Brisa* passenger sedan.
- Forced to halt passenger car manufacturing in 1981 under the military regime of *Chun Doo-hwan*, which mandated industrial rationalization, pivoting the firm toward light commercial vans before re-entering passenger markets in 1986.
- Partnered with *Ford* during the late 1980s and early 1990s to manufacture inexpensive compact cars, producing the widely exported *Kia Pride*, which was rebadged in North America as the *Ford Festiva* and *Ford Aspire*.
- Succumbed to massive corporate debt during the 1997 Asian financial crisis, declaring bankruptcy before rival *Hyundai Motor Company* acquired a 51 percent controlling interest in 1998 to establish the unified *Hyundai Motor Group*.
- Underwent a dramatic brand renaissance after hiring prominent German automotive designer *Peter Schreyer* in 2006, who established the brand's iconic corporate visual signature known as the *Tiger Nose* grille.
- Achieved widespread commercial success and critical acclaim in the twenty-first century with popular consumer models like the boxy *Soul*, the three-row *Telluride* SUV, the *Stinger* sports sedan, and the dedicated *EV6* electric crossover.

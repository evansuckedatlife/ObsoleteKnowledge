## summary

**GoPro** is an American consumer electronics and digital technology company founded in 2002 by *Nick Woodman*, famed for developing rugged, wearable high-definition action cameras. Designed to capture immersive, wide-angle point-of-view footage in extreme sporting environments, the device revolutionized consumer videography, drone cinematography, and social media broadcasting. The brand became a global standard for ruggedized personal documentation, transforming how humans record physical motion and aerial exploration.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *Nick Woodman* during a 2002 surfing expedition to Australia and Indonesia when he realized amateur athletes lacked accessible methods to photograph themselves while riding waves.
- Funded initially through sales of bead-and-shell belts from an old *Volkswagen* van and loans from family, resulting in the 2004 debut of the 35mm film *GoPro HERO*.
- Transitioned to digital recording in 2006 with the *Digital HERO*, followed by high-definition capture in the 2010 *HD HERO*, which featured a signature ultrawide 170-degree fisheye lens.
- Revolutionized video capture with proprietary stabilization software, most notably *HyperSmooth*, introduced in the *HERO7 Black* in 2018, which virtually eliminated external motorized gimbals.
- Pioneered user-generated viral marketing by encouraging athletes, skydivers, and motorsport drivers to upload first-person footage, accumulating millions of online views under brand sponsorship.
- Expanded briefly into drone manufacturing with the ill-fated 2016 *Karma* quadcopter, which was recalled for sudden battery disconnects before the company discontinued drone hardware in 2018.
- Utilized widely across scientific research, aerospace tracking, and wildlife conservation to document habitats and rocket launches from perspectives previously impossible for human camera operators.

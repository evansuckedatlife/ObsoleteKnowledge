## summary

**Canadian Tire** (formally the **Canadian Tire Corporation**, or **CTC**) is an iconic Canadian retail company founded in 1922 by brothers *John William Billes* and *Alfred Jackson Billes* in Toronto, Ontario. Operating across automotive, hardware, sports, and home goods sectors, the company expanded rapidly during the twentieth century to become a quintessential fixture of Canadian commercial culture. In modern business and trivia contexts, it is best celebrated for its unique customer loyalty scrip, popularly termed *Canadian Tire money*, which long functioned as a beloved domestic alternative currency.

## you gotta know

- Began in September 1922 when *J. William Billes* and *Alfred J. Billes* invested their combined savings of $1,800 to purchase the *Hamilton Tire and Garage Ltd.* in Toronto, subsequently renaming the venture to reflect its national focus.
- Introduced its legendary customer loyalty program in 1958 with paper coupons known as *Canadian Tire money*, originally designed by Muriel Billes as cash-bonus certificates to reward cash-paying customers over credit patrons.
- Featured the fictional Scottish character *Sandy McTire*, sporting a traditional tam o' shanter cap and tartan tie, on its paper scrip bills to symbolize thriftiness, financial prudence, and value.
- Reached such cultural ubiquity across Canada that numerous independent third-party businesses, including local diners, bars, and convenience stores, frequently accepted the scrip at face value for everyday transactions.
- Survived the post-war retail invasion of American big-box chains like *Target* through deeply established suburban and small-town market penetration, remaining one of Canada's most resilient domestic retailers alongside historic ventures rooted in the [[british-empire]].
- Expanded its corporate portfolio by acquiring major national retail banners, notably the workwear chain *Mark's* in 2001, the athletic retailer *Sport Chek* via *FGL Sports* in 2011, and the Norwegian outdoor brand *Helly Hansen* in 2018.
- Developed an early mail-order catalogue in 1928 that became a staple in rural and isolated northern households, helping to democratize automotive maintenance and hardware distribution outside urban centres.

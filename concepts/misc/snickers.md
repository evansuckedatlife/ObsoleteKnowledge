## summary

**Snickers** is an American confectionary candy bar composed of peanut-infused nougat topped with roasted whole peanuts and caramel, all enrobed in milk chocolate. Introduced in 1930 by *Frank Mars* and manufactured by *Mars, Incorporated*, it quickly rose to become one of the best-selling and most commercially dominant confections in global history. In cultural trivia and social history, it is notable for its memorable international branding transitions—having long been sold in the [[united-kingdom]] as the **Marathon bar**—its substantial caloric density that made it a fixture in military field rations, and its ubiquitous sports and television advertising campaigns.

## you gotta know

- Named after a prized racehorse owned by *Frank Mars* and his wife *Ethel* on their *Milky Way Farm* in Giles County, Tennessee, which passed away shortly before the confection entered production.
- Marketed and sold under the alternate name **Marathon bar** in the [[united-kingdom]] and the Republic of Ireland until 1990, when *Mars* unified its global brand identity.
- Built upon a layered interior architecture consisting of fluffy nougat whipped with peanut butter, a viscous blanket of golden caramel, and roasted whole peanuts encased in fluted milk chocolate.
- Utilized by the military forces of the [[united-states]] as a quick caloric supplement during foreign deployments, including distribution in combat ration packs during twentieth-century conflicts.
- Launched an acclaimed international marketing campaign in 2010 with the slogan *"You're Not You When You're Hungry,"* which debuted during the Super Bowl featuring comedic appearances by *Betty White*.
- Sparked viral internet commentary and satirical fact-checking in 2022 when social media users falsely claimed the manufacturer had smoothed over the confection's signature embossed chocolate surface texture.
- Anchored massive twentieth-century industrial food manufacturing operations at automated plants in Chicago, Illinois, and Hackettstown, New Jersey.
- Frequently crosses paths with athletics, street fashion, and pop culture, inspiring collaborative merchandise and novelty sneaker releases that pair the snack with athletic [[sneakers]].

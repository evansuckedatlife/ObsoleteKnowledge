## summary

**Popeyes**, officially known as **Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen**, is an American multinational fast-food chain renowned for its New Orleans-style spicy fried chicken, buttermilk biscuits, and Southern side dishes. Founded in Arabi, Louisiana, in 1972 by *Al Copeland*, the restaurant grew into a cultural and culinary juggernaut with thousands of global locations. It stands alongside competitors in popular culture as a quintessential purveyor of Louisiana Creole seasonings, red beans and rice, and signature fried poultry.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1972 in suburban New Orleans by entrepreneur *Al Copeland*, originally opening under the unsuccessful moniker *Chicken on the Run* before rebranding to capture consumer interest with spicy Cajun flavors.
- Named not after the cartoon sailor *Popeye the Sailor*, but rather after the hard-nosed fictional detective *Popeye Doyle*, played by *Gene Hackman* in the classic 1971 crime thriller film *The French Connection*.
- Famous for its signature side dishes rooted in Louisiana regional cuisine, including red beans and rice, Cajun fries, mashed potatoes with Cajun gravy, and flaky buttermilk biscuits.
- Sparked the viral nationwide "chicken sandwich wars" in August 2019 following the rollout of its crispy chicken sandwich, causing widespread sellouts, intense social media hype, and supply shortages across the [[united-states]].
- Operated for decades under parent company *AFC Enterprises* (*America's Favorite Chicken Company*), which also held rival chain *Church's Texas Chicken*, before being acquired by *Restaurant Brands International* in 2017.
- Featured animated endorsements in historical marketing using the cartoon *Popeye* character under license, despite Copeland's original movie inspiration, leading to lasting public confusion over its namesake.

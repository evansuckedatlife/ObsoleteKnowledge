## summary

**British Airways** is the flag carrier airline of the [[united-kingdom]], headquartered in London near its primary operational hub at Heathrow Airport. Formed in 1974 following the consolidation of state-owned carriers BOAC and BEA, the airline operated supersonic passenger flights with the Anglo-French *Concorde* throughout the late twentieth century. Privatized under the government of *Margaret Thatcher* in 1987, it later co-founded the global *Oneworld* airline alliance and merged with Spain's Iberia to establish the multinational holding group *International Airlines Group* (IAG).

## you gotta know

- Created by statutory consolidation of the *British Overseas Airways Corporation* (BOAC) and *British European Airways* (BEA) under the British Airways Board Act.
- Operated the flagship commercial supersonic transport *Concorde* alongside *Air France* from 1976 until the aircraft's retirement in 2003, flying transatlantic corridors between London, [[new-york-city]], and Washington.
- Long maintained extensive intercontinental routes retracing former colonial administrative paths developed across the [[british-empire]].
- Served as a high-profile flagship privatization program in 1987, championed by Prime Minister *Margaret Thatcher* to promote free-market industrial restructuring.
- Adopted the celebrated promotional moniker *"The World's Favourite Airline"* in high-budget global advertising campaigns throughout the 1980s and 1990s.
- Co-founded the *Oneworld* airline alliance in 1998 alongside American Airlines, Cathay Pacific, and Qantas to manage international codesharing.
- Survived significant early-twenty-first-century disruptions, culminating in its corporate combination with [[spain]]'s flag carrier *Iberia* in 2011 under IAG.

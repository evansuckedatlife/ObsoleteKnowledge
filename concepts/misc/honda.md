## summary

**Honda** is a multinational conglomerate founded in Hamamatsu, *Japan*, by mechanic and entrepreneur *Soichiro Honda* and business partner *Takeo Fujisawa* in 1948. Initially gaining international prominence for lightweight motorcycles like the ubiquitous *Super Cub*, it expanded into an automotive titan renowned for engineering efficiency and pioneering combustion technologies. In addition to mass-market passenger vehicles, the company has played a major role in global motorsport, power equipment manufacturing, and robotics.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1948 by *Soichiro Honda*, who began by affixing surplus two-stroke military radio generator engines to bicycles in post-war *Japan*.
- Achieved unprecedented global commercial reach with the *Super Cub*, introduced in 1958, which became the most produced motor vehicle in human history and was promoted in the *United States* through the groundbreaking advertising slogan "You meet the nicest people on a Honda."
- Revolutionized emissions standards during the 1970s energy crisis with the *Compound Vortex Controlled Combustion* (*CVCC*) engine, which famously met strict American Clean Air Act requirements without requiring a catalytic converter.
- Introduced iconic compact and mid-size passenger cars such as the *Civic* in 1972 and the *Accord* in 1976, cementing Japanese automotive manufacturing prominence in North American markets.
- Pioneered luxury Japanese automotive branding in 1986 by launching the *Acura* marque, predating rivals such as *Toyota*'s *Lexus* and *Nissan*'s *Infiniti*.
- Maintained a dominant legacy in Formula One racing as an engine supplier, powering championship-winning cars for legendary drivers such as *Ayrton Senna* and *Alain Prost* at *McLaren* during the late 1980s.
- Created the humanoid bipedal robot *ASIMO* in 2000, demonstrating cutting-edge advancements in mobility, artificial intelligence, and autonomous navigation.

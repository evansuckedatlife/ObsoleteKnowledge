## summary

**Adidas** is a German multinational corporation that designs and manufactures athletic footwear, apparel, and sporting accessories. Founded in 1949 by **Adi Dassler** in Herzogenaurach, [[germany]], the enterprise emerged from an acrimonious fraternal split that divided a family shoemaking shop into two global titans, Puma and Adidas. Renowned for its iconic three-stripe branding and revolutionary screw-in cleat engineering, the company transformed modern corporate sportswear endorsement, manufacturing automation, and twentieth-century urban street style.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 1949 after an irreconcilable feud between brothers Adolf (*Adi*) and Rudolf (*Rudi*) Dassler shattered their pre-war *Gebrüder Dassler Schuhfabrik*, prompting Rudolf to found rival firm Puma on the opposite side of the Aurach river.
- Named as a portmanteau blending the founder's nickname *Adi* and the first syllable of his surname *Dassler*, debunking the enduring urban legend and backronym that it stands for *All Day I Dream About Sport*.
- Secured global technological prominence at the 1954 FIFA World Cup Final in Bern, where the West German national football team defeated Hungary's *Golden Team* in the mud wearing Dassler's revolutionary lightweight shoes with screw-in studs.
- Created the famous three-stripe trademark after purchasing the design rights from Finnish sports brand Karhu in 1952 for two bottles of whiskey and a modest cash payment.
- Revolutionized hip-hop fashion and athletic apparel marketing in 1986 when American rap trio Run-DMC released the hit single *My Adidas*, leading directly to the first major corporate endorsement contract between a musical act and an athletic footwear company.
- Provided footwear for legendary African American sprinter Jesse Owens during the 1936 Berlin Summer Olympics, where Owens won four gold medals wearing handcrafted spiked track shoes supplied directly by the Dassler brothers.
- Developed the *Samba*, *Stan Smith*, and *Superstar* silhouette designs, which transitioned over decades from elite competitive tennis and football footwear into foundational staples of global streetwear.

## connections

- [[germany]] — home country where the Dassler family founded the original factory in the Bavarian town of Herzogenaurach.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict during which the original factory was commandeered for munitions production, deepening the brothers' mutual paranoia.
- [[acronym]] — linguistic construct falsely attributed to the corporate moniker via the popular backronym *All Day I Dream About Sport*.
- [[modernism]] — aesthetic and functionalist philosophy reflected in the minimalist three-stripe emblem and ergonomic industrial design.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era during which global athletics and Olympic shoe contracts served as high-stakes soft-power competition.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — systemic transformation of mechanized leather manufacturing and synthetic vulcanized rubber production.

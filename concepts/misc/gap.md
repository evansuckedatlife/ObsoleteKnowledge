## summary

**The Gap** is an influential American retail clothing and accessories corporation founded in 1969 by *Donald Fisher* and *Doris F. Fisher* in *San Francisco*, *California*. Established initially to cater to the countercultural youth market struggling to find well-fitting denim, the brand grew into a dominant force in casual apparel by defining mass-market American wardrobe staples through minimalist design and memorable advertising campaigns. Today, **Gap Inc.** oversees major apparel banners including *Old Navy*, *Banana Republic*, and *Athleta*, representing a key chapter in modern retail history.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1969 on Ocean Avenue in *San Francisco* by *Donald Fisher* and *Doris F. Fisher*, who raised initial capital to open a store selling LP vinyl records alongside *Levi's* denim jeans.
- Named after the concept of the "generation gap," reflecting the cultural divide between Baby Boomers and their parents during the late 1960s.
- Pioneered private-label casual apparel in 1974 by transitioning away from exclusively stocking third-party products to manufacturing its own signature jeans, pocket tees, and basics.
- Acquired *Banana Republic* in 1983 from founders *Mel Ziegler* and *Patricia Ziegler*, subsequently retooling the safari-themed surplus company into an upscale casual clothing chain.
- Launched the value-oriented spin-off *Old Navy* in 1994, which achieved one billion dollars in sales within four years, setting a record pace for a retail venture.
- Revolutionized fashion marketing in the 1990s through acclaimed television commercials directed by figures such as *Roman Coppola*, notably the "Khaki Swing" and "Khaki Soul" advertisements featuring Louis Prima tunes.
- Endured major cultural criticism in 2010 after abruptly redesigning its classic blue-box serif logo with a Helvetica typeface, prompting intense public backlash that forced the company to revert to its original emblem within one week.

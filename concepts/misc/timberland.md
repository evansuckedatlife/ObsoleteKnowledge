## summary

**The Timberland Company** (commonly known as **Timberland** or by the metonym **Timberlands**) is an American manufacturer and retailer of rugged outdoor footwear and apparel, founded by *Nathan Swartz* in Boston, Massachusetts. Best known for introducing its waterproof yellow nubuck work boot in 1973, the brand revolutionized outdoor gear manufacturing through injection-molding technology that fused soles directly to leather uppers without stitching. Beyond its New England blue-collar roots, the signature six-inch boot was adopted during the late twentieth century as an indelible style uniform within hip-hop culture, street fashion, and global urban identity.

## you gotta know

- Traces its lineage to 1952 when shoemaker *Nathan Swartz* purchased a half-interest in the *Abington Shoe Company* in Massachusetts, before buying out his partner and incorporating his sons into the business.
- Pioneered innovative injection-molding technology in 1965, fusing thick lugged synthetic soles directly into leather without stitching to produce truly waterproof footwear.
- Introduced the iconic six-inch waterproof nubuck leather boot in 1973 under the model name *Timberland*, whose runaway commercial success led the entire enterprise to adopt that corporate name in 1978.
- Manufactured from premium wheat-colored nubuck leather, rustproof hexagonal brass eyelets, taslan nylon laces, and four rows of heavy nylon stitching designed to resist rotting.
- Emerged unexpectedly in the late 1980s and 1990s as a vital fashion staple within the [[african-diaspora]] and the burgeoning hip-hop communities of [[new-york-city]], championed by artists such as *The Notorious B.I.G.*, *Wu-Tang Clan*, and *Nas*.
- Responded to urban subcultural adoption with initial corporate ambivalence under CEO *Jeffrey Swartz*, before embracing diverse urban consumers and pioneering major community service programs like the *Path of Service*.
- Acquired in 2011 by the global apparel conglomerate *VF Corporation*, joining major outdoor and lifestyle brands like *The North Face*, *Vans*, and *Supreme*.
- Experienced major resurgences in the 2020s through high-profile collaborations with luxury streetwear houses, finding an expansive new audience among youth demographics on [[tiktok]].

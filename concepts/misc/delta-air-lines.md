## summary

**Delta Air Lines**, known ubiquitously as **Delta**, is one of the oldest legacy commercial air carriers in the *[[united-states]]* and an architect of modern international civil aviation. Originating in 1925 as an agricultural crop-dusting venture in Macon, Georgia, it expanded passenger operations across the American South and pioneered the hub-and-spoke airline network model. Anchored at its fortress hub at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Delta grew through strategic corporate mergers and global alliances into one of the largest passenger airline corporations in the world.

## you gotta know

- Maintains its worldwide headquarters and primary fortress hub in Atlanta, Georgia, operating out of Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport.
- Founded in 1925 as *Huff Daland Dusters*, an agricultural aviation company created to combat boll weevil infestations destroying cotton crops destined for [[denim]].
- Renamed in 1928 after founder *C.E. Woolman* purchased its assets, naming the business after the Mississippi Delta region before launching passenger service in 1929.
- Contributed fleet capacity, logistics transport, and airframe modification to the military during *[[world-war-ii]]*, aiding the Allied aviation supply chain.
- Perfected the commercial hub-and-spoke transit system during the mid-twentieth century, routing passenger traffic through central nodes rather than point-to-point lines.
- Expanded its transcontinental and overseas network during the postwar aviation boom and the dawn of the commercial *[[space-age]]*.
- Formed the SkyTeam global airline alliance in 2000 as a founding member alongside Air France, Aeroméxico, and Korean Air.
- Merged with Northwest Airlines in 2008, absorbing extensive Asian transpacific corridors and domestic hubs to form the world's largest commercial carrier at the time.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the country where Delta was chartered, developed its transcontinental network, and operates as an essential national infrastructure carrier.
- [[denim]] — the ubiquitous cotton textile reliant on Southern cotton fields, which Delta's initial crop-dusting predecessor was incorporated to protect from pests.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global war during which Delta retrofitted combat aircraft and performed transport flying under contract for the War Department.
- [[cold-war]] — the geopolitical period that witnessed the deregulation of American civil aviation and the internationalization of transatlantic airline routes.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the overarching continuum of industrial engineering, internal combustion, and mass transport that gave rise to commercial flight.
- [[space-age]] — the era of rapid aerospace innovation that popularized jetliners like the Douglas DC-8 and Boeing 707 across Delta's trunk routes.
- [[new-york-city]] — a primary domestic and transatlantic gateway for Delta, anchored by extensive dual-hub terminal investments at JFK and LaGuardia.

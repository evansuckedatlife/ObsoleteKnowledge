## summary

**TC Energy**, formerly known as *TransCanada PipeLines Limited*, is a major North American energy infrastructure enterprise based in Calgary, Alberta. Established in 1951 by a special act of the Canadian Parliament, the corporation develops and operates extensive natural gas pipelines, liquids pipelines, and power generation facilities across Canada, the United States, and Mexico. It is globally famous in modern political history and regulatory debate as the corporate developer behind the contentious and ultimately canceled *Keystone XL* pipeline project.

## you gotta know

- Incorporated in 1951 to construct the *TransCanada Pipeline*, an immense domestic pipeline designed to transport natural gas from the *Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin* across Ontario and Quebec.
- The 1956 parliamentary debate over funding its initial pipeline sparked the infamous "Pipeline Debate", an intense legislative conflict in Ottawa that contributed to the fall of *Louis St. Laurent*'s Liberal government.
- Proposed the *Keystone Pipeline System* in the 2000s, including the controversial *Keystone XL* expansion intended to transport crude oil from Alberta's oil sands directly to refineries on the Gulf Coast of the [[united-states]].
- *Keystone XL* became a flashpoint of global environmental protest and executive dispute: rejected by *Barack Obama* in 2015, revived by *Donald Trump* in 2017, and permanently terminated when *Joe Biden* revoked its presidential permit in 2021.
- Rebranded from *TransCanada Corporation* to *TC Energy* in May 2019 to better reflect its expansive footprint across the United States and Mexico as well as varied energy assets.
- Operates the *Coastal GasLink* pipeline project in northern British Columbia, which drew nationwide controversy and indigenous rail blockades concerning hereditary *Wet'suwet'en* land rights.
- Holds significant nuclear assets, owning a major partnership stake in Ontario's *Bruce Power*, one of the largest operational commercial nuclear generating stations in the world.

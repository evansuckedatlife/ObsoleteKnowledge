## summary

**Southwest Airlines** (officially **Southwest Airlines Co.**) is a major American passenger air carrier founded in 1967 by *Herb Kelleher* and *Rollin King*, commencing flight operations out of *Texas* in 1971. By pioneering the high-frequency, low-cost carrier model, the company transformed the commercial aviation industry through single-model aircraft fleets, secondary airport operations, and rapid gate turnaround times. It remains famous in economic history for sparking widespread fare reductions dubbed the *Southwest Effect*, prompting fierce legislative clashes such as the *Wright Amendment*, and reshaping modern air travel logistics.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in 1967 when financial consultant *Rollin King* sketched a triangular route connecting *Dallas*, *Houston*, and *San Antonio* on a cocktail napkin during a meeting with attorney *Herb Kelleher*.
- Operated exclusively within the borders of *Texas* for its first eight years of service, intentionally avoiding the interstate jurisdiction of the federal *Civil Aeronautics Board* (CAB) to set its own low fares and flexible schedules.
- Standardized its entire operational fleet exclusively around the *Boeing 737*, maximizing maintenance efficiency, minimizing spare parts inventories, and allowing flight crews to move seamlessly between aircraft.
- Championed a *point-to-point* route network rather than the conventional *hub-and-spoke* structure used by legacy carriers, reducing passenger transfer times and limiting cascading delays across its system.
- Centered its early Dallas operations at *Dallas Love Field*, triggering the passage of the federal *Wright Amendment* in 1979, which severely restricted nonstop flights out of Love Field to protect nearby *Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport*.
- Cultivated an unorthodox corporate culture championed by *Herb Kelleher*, characterized by open unassigned seating, humorous safety briefings, casual flight attendant attire, and the trading ticker symbol LUV celebrating its Love Field origins.
- Experienced a catastrophic operational collapse during the December 2022 North American winter storm, when extreme weather cascaded into a complete breakdown of its outdated internal crew-scheduling software, forcing the cancellation of nearly 17,000 flights.

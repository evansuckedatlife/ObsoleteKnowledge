## summary

**American Airlines** is a premier commercial aviation carrier headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, recognized as the largest airline in the world by fleet size, scheduled passengers carried, and revenue passenger-kilometers. Tracing its lineage to the pioneering airmail routes of the late 1920s, the enterprise consolidated under investor *Errett Lobban Cord* and aviation pioneer *C. R. Smith* into an industry powerhouse. Operating through its parent corporation, the **American Airlines Group** (traded as **AAL**), the carrier pioneered computerized reservation infrastructure, hub-and-spoke scheduling, and customer loyalty rewards, fundamentally transforming modern global transit.

## you gotta know

- Traces its formal corporate birth to the 1930 consolidation of dozens of independent airmail transport operators into *American Airways*, rebranded four years later under the leadership of *C. R. Smith*.
- Commissioned the development of the venerable *Douglas DC-3* airliner in 1934, working directly with aeronautical engineer *Donald Douglas* to produce a passenger-friendly transport that made commercial passenger aviation profitable without government mail subsidies.
- Introduced the landmark *Semi-Automated Business Research Environment* (*SABRE*) reservation system in 1960 alongside *IBM*, revolutionizing global travel ticketing and establishing the blueprint for computerized inventory transactions.
- Conceived the world's first modern airline frequent-flyer loyalty program, *AAdvantage*, launched in 1981 to reward customer retention and subsequently adopted across the international airline industry.
- Maintained a long-standing corporate hub network centered at *Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport*, *Chicago O'Hare*, *Miami*, *Charlotte*, and *John F. Kennedy International Airport* in *New York City*.
- Navigated severe structural disruption following the September 11 terrorist attacks, where two of its flagship airliners (*Flight 11* and *Flight 77*) were hijacked by terrorists targeting the World Trade Center and the Pentagon.
- Underwent Chapter 11 corporate restructuring in 2011, culminating in a colossal 2013 merger with *US Airways* that created the modern incarnation of the carrier under CEO *Doug Parker*.
- Formed the prestigious global *oneworld* alliance alongside *British Airways*, *Cathay Pacific*, and *Qantas* in 1999 to manage international codeshare routing and reciprocal passenger service.

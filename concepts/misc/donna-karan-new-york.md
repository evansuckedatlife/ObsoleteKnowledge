## summary

**Donna Karan New York** is an influential American fashion house founded in 1984 by designer *Donna Karan* and her late husband *Stephan Weiss*. Renowned for revolutionizing executive womenswear through the concept of interchangeable, modular pieces, the brand codified the minimalist, functional aesthetic of corporate urban dress in the late twentieth century. Its diffusion line, *DKNY*, founded in 1989, became an emblem of metropolitan street style, branding the fast-paced energy of Manhattan for a global consumer base.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1984 after *Donna Karan* served as head designer for the classic sportswear label *Anne Klein*, departing to launch her independent label financed by the *Takihyo* group.
- Debuted in 1985 with the iconic *Seven Easy Pieces* collection, a capsule wardrobe centered on a black stretch bodysuit that could transform effortlessly from daywear to evening wear.
- Designed with the modern working woman in mind, prioritizing comfort, stretch fabrics, jersey knits, and tailored masculine blazers cut for female silhouettes.
- Launched the diffusion line *DKNY* in 1989 to offer a more accessible, casual, and denim-oriented collection inspired by the designer's daughter, *Gabby*.
- Built memorable advertising campaigns featuring high-contrast black-and-white photography shot by *Peter Lindbergh*, highlighting supermodels navigating the bustling streets of Manhattan.
- Went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 1996 and was later acquired by French luxury conglomerate *LVMH* (*Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton*) in 2001.
- *Donna Karan* stepped down as chief designer in 2015 to focus on her wellness lifestyle brand *Urban Zen*, after which *LVMH* sold the company to *G-III Apparel Group*.

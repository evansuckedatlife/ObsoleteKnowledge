## summary

**Azul** is a celebrated abstract strategy board game designed by *Michael Kiesling* and published in 2017 by *Plan B Games*. Players assume the role of artisan tile-layers competing to embellish the royal palace of *Évora* for King *Manuel I* of Portugal. Renowned for its elegant tile-drafting mechanics, spatial scoring system, and tactile resin pieces, the game won the prestigious *Spiel des Jahres* in 2018.

## you gotta know

- Designed by German game designer *Michael Kiesling*, a legendary board game author known for creating titles such as *Tikal* and *Torres* alongside *Wolfgang Kramer*.
- The theme draws direct inspiration from Portuguese and Moorish decorative ceramic tiles known as *azulejos*, which King *Manuel I* admired while visiting the *Alhambra* in southern *[[spain]]*.
- The core gameplay revolves around drafting colored resin tiles from central factory displays or the common center pool, requiring players to fill specific staging pattern lines.
- Unplaced or overflowing tiles fall directly onto the player's "floor line," incurring escalating negative penalty points that reflect broken and discarded materials.
- Scored tiles transfer at the end of each round to a five-by-five decorative grid, where they score points based on contiguous horizontal and vertical rows.
- Won the prestigious 2018 *Spiel des Jahres* (Game of the Year) and the *Deutscher Spiele Preis*, solidifying its reputation as a modern gateway gaming classic.
- Spawned multiple standalone sequels expanding on tiling themes, including *Azul: Stained Glass of Sintra*, *Azul: Summer Pavilion*, and *Azul: Queen's Garden*.

## summary

**DJI**, formally *SZ DJI Technology Co., Ltd.* or **Da-Jiang Innovations**, is a private Chinese technology enterprise headquartered in *Shenzhen*, *Guangdong*, that dominates the global civilian unmanned aerial vehicle and aerial photography marketplace. Founded in 2006 by *Frank Wang* from his dorm room at the *Hong Kong University of Science and Technology*, the corporation pioneered mass-market quadcopters, handheld camera stabilizers, and commercial imaging platforms. Its transformative *Phantom* and *Mavic* product lines established consumer drone flight as an omnipresent medium across filmmaking, search-and-rescue operations, agriculture, and modern surveillance, capturing over seventy percent of the worldwide market share.

## you gotta know

- The firm's flagship consumer breakthrough occurred with the release of the *Phantom 1* quadcopter in 2013, which integrated ready-to-fly flight controllers, GPS stabilization, and gimbals compatible with *GoPro* action cameras before introducing integrated proprietary cameras.
- The folding *Mavic* series, launched in 2016 with the *Mavic Pro*, revolutionized portable consumer aerial cinematography by collapsing motor arms into a compact chassis without sacrificing obstacle avoidance sensors or 4K video capabilities.
- Founder *Frank Wang* began building flight control modules while studying engineering, initially selling auto-pilot flight components to universities and hobbyists before scaling production in the manufacturing ecosystem of *Shenzhen*.
- Beyond drones, the company created the *Ronin* series of modular three-axis cinema gimbals and the *Osmo* line of handheld stabilized pocket cameras and action cameras, competing directly against traditional camera equipment makers.
- In enterprise aviation, the *Agras* agricultural octocopter series conducts automated crop spraying and multispectral field mapping across rural China, Southeast Asia, and global farmlands.
- The company faced intense geopolitical scrutiny from the *[[united-states]]*, resulting in the Department of Defense banning its hardware over data security concerns and the Department of Commerce adding the firm to the Entity List in 2020.
- A high-profile accidental landing of a consumer *Phantom* drone on the White House lawn in January 2015 catalyzed the firm's rollout of mandatory geofencing firmware, which programmatically blocks flight inside restricted airspaces.

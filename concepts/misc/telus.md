## summary

**Telus** is a major Canadian telecommunications conglomerate that provides wireless, broadband, voice, television, and digital healthcare services across Canada. Formed in 1990 by the provincial government of Alberta to privatize the *Alberta Government Telephones* commission, it grew rapidly through its pivotal 1999 merger with *BC Tel*. Alongside rivals *Rogers* and *Bell*, it anchors the national telecom oligopoly and is instantly recognizable in branding for its longstanding advertising campaigns featuring whimsical animals on minimalist white backgrounds.

## you gotta know

- Created in 1990 as a holding company to privatize *Alberta Government Telephones* (*AGT*), a public utility whose telephonic roots extended back to 1906.
- Merged with British Columbia's premier telecommunications provider, *BC Tel*, in 1999, creating Canada's second-largest telecommunications carrier and relocating dual headquarters between Edmonton and Vancouver.
- Part of Canada's dominant "Big Three" national wireless providers alongside *Rogers Communications* and *BCE Inc.* (*Bell Canada*).
- Highly recognizable across North America for its distinctive nature-themed marketing campaign, running since 2000 under the slogan "The Future is Friendly" featuring vibrant critters such as chameleons, pigs, and tree frogs.
- Diversified significantly into healthcare and digital health software through its subsidiary *Telus Health*, becoming Canada's largest provider of electronic medical records.
- Operates notable architectural landmarks including *Telus Sky* in Calgary, designed by Danish architect *Bjarke Ingels*, and *Telus Garden* in Vancouver.
- Expanded globally via *Telus International*, offering multilingual customer service, digital outsourcing, and AI data moderation solutions for tech enterprises worldwide.

## summary

**Father's Day** is an annual holiday honouring fatherhood, paternal bonds, and the cultural influence of fathers in society. First established in the early twentieth century within the *[[united-states]]* to complement the newly created Mother's Day, it overcame decades of congressional reluctance and commercial skepticism before obtaining permanent federal recognition. Celebrated in dozens of nations on the third Sunday in June or on Saint Joseph's Day, it forms a staple of modern celebratory calendars and consumer culture.

## you gotta know

- *Sonora Smart Dodd* of Spokane, Washington, is credited as the primary driving force behind the holiday, inspired while listening to a Mother's Day sermon in 1909 to honour her father *William Jackson Smart*, a veteran of the *[[american-civil-war]]*.
- The first statewide celebration occurred in Washington State on June 19, 1910, chose because June was the birth month of *Sonora Smart Dodd's* widowed father.
- An earlier one-time church memorial service was organized on July 5, 1908, by *Grace Golden Clayton* in Fairmont, West Virginia, to mourn 361 men killed in the 1907 *Monongah mining disaster*.
- President *Woodrow Wilson* endorsed the holiday by traveling to Spokane to speak in 1916, and President *Calvin Coolidge* supported a national observance in 1924, though both stopped short of issuing an official executive proclamation.
- Resistance to the holiday persisted for decades because many men viewed the sentimental cards and obligatory retail gifts as commercial exploitation lacking masculine dignity.
- President *Lyndon B. Johnson* issued the first presidential proclamation establishing the third Sunday in June as the official day of observance in 1966.
- President *Richard Nixon* signed Public Law 92-278 into law in 1972 during his re-election campaign, making the observance a permanent federal holiday in the *[[united-states]]*.
- In Roman Catholic traditions across Southern Europe and Latin America, paternal celebration has long been tied to *Saint Joseph's Day* (*San Giuseppe*) on March 19 within traditional *[[christianity]]*.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the country where civic campaigns in Washington and West Virginia established the modern secular holiday.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict in which *William Jackson Smart*, the widower who inspired *Sonora Smart Dodd*, served as an enlisted soldier.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-war transformation during which fraternal organizations and family commemorative holidays expanded across North America.
- [[christianity]] — the theological framework linking paternal honor to the biblical fifth commandment and Catholic feast days for *Saint Joseph*.
- [[etiquette]] — the social code governing greeting cards, familial ceremonies, and reciprocal domestic gift exchanges.
- [[new-york-city]] — headquarters of the Father's Day Council and major advertising agencies that commercialized the event in the 1930s.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict during which advertisers mobilized the holiday as a patriotic tribute to overseas military fathers.

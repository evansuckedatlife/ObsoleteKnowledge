## summary

**Air France**, formally styled as the **Compagnie Nationale Air France**, is the national flag carrier of France and a core operating subsidiary of the Air France–KLM Group. Formed in 1933 through the consolidation of several pioneering aviation ventures, the airline serves as a cultural and economic ambassador of the French Republic from its main hubs at Paris-Charles de Gaulle and Paris-Orly. It holds a storied position in aviation history as one of only two commercial operators of the supersonic *Concorde* airliner.

## you gotta know

- Created on October 7, 1933, via the formal merger of five pioneering early French civil airlines, notably including *Air Union* and *Société Centrale pour l'Exploitation de Lignes Aériennes*.
- Nationalized by the post-liberation French government in 1945 following the conclusion of *World War II*, establishing state stewardship over global routes across the French Union.
- Retained the iconic mythical winged seahorse emblem, affectionately nicknamed *la crevette* ("the shrimp"), inherited from predecessor firm *Air Orient*.
- Flew the supersonic passenger airliner *Concorde* alongside *British Airways* from 1976 until 2003, connecting Paris to New York and Dakar at twice the speed of sound.
- Suffered the fatal crash of *Concorde* Flight 4590 at Gonesse outside Paris in July 2000, which precipitated the premature retirement of supersonic passenger transport.
- Co-founded the *SkyTeam* global airline alliance in June 2000 in partnership with *Delta Air Lines*, *Aeroméxico*, and *Korean Air*.
- Executed a major transatlantic commercial merger in 2004 with Dutch flag carrier *KLM*, establishing one of the largest airline holding conglomerates in Europe.
- Endured the tragic loss of Flight 447 in June 2009 over the Atlantic Ocean en route from Rio de Janeiro to Paris due to pitot-tube sensor icing and subsequent aerodynamic stall.

## connections

- [[air-canada]] — fellow historic transatlantic flag carrier and rival partner in connecting European capitals with Francophone North America.
- [[france]] — sovereign home nation whose diplomatic, cultural, and colonial networks the flag carrier was chartered to serve.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict that devastated French civilian aircraft infrastructure, led to wartime Vichy reorganization, and prompted postwar state nationalization.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era defined by expanding intercontinental passenger transport, flag carrier prestige, and competitive jetliner modernization.
- [[belle-epoque]] — artistic and technological flowering of early French engineering that inspired early aviation pioneers such as Louis Blériot.
- [[modernism]] — avant-garde architectural and interior design aesthetic exemplified by Raymond Loewy's Concorde interiors and airport terminal lounges.

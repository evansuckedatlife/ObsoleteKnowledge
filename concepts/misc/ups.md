## summary

**UPS**, formally the **United Parcel Service**, is an American multinational package delivery and supply chain management company based in Sandy Springs, Georgia. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, by teenage entrepreneurs *James E. Casey* and *Claude Ryan*, it expanded from a local messenger service into the largest courier service in the world. Famous for its fleet of brown delivery package cars and uniforms, UPS transformed modern logistics, tracking technologies, and international intermodal freight transport.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1907 as the *American Messenger Company* by 19-year-old *James E. "Jim" Casey* and *Claude Ryan* using a borrowed $100.
- Renamed the *United Parcel Service* in 1919 after acquiring parcel delivery services outside Seattle and consolidating its brand identity.
- Famous for its distinctive signature color known as *UPS Brown* or *Pullman Brown*, chosen in 1919 because it mirrored luxurious Pullman sleeping cars and showed less dirt and road grime.
- Employs iconic custom-built step-in delivery vehicles known internally as "package cars," designed without doors on the cab side to enable drivers to exit quickly and safely.
- Developed an proprietary routing algorithm known as *ORION* (On-Road Integrated Optimization and Navigation), famous for minimizing dangerous and fuel-wasting left-hand turns against oncoming traffic.
- Operates its primary global air hub, *Worldport*, situated at Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport in Kentucky, capable of processing hundreds of thousands of packages per hour.
- Conducted the largest initial public offering (IPO) of the 20th century on the New York Stock Exchange in November 1999.

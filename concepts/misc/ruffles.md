## summary

**Ruffles** is a globally recognized brand of ridged potato chips manufactured and marketed by *Frito-Lay*, a major subsidiary of American food and beverage conglomerate *PepsiCo*. Deriving its name from the gathered, undulating fabric folds of a historical textile **ruffle**, the snack was introduced to North American consumers in the late 1950s using a patented mechanical cutting process designed to produce corrugated ridges that resist breakage when paired with heavy dips. In academic quiz bowl tournaments, popular culture competitions, and commercial business history, the brand frequently surfaces in questions concerning its ubiquitous advertising slogan "Ruffles Have Ridges," the emergence of cross-border culinary staples like "All-Dressed" seasoning, and the technological evolution of mid-century industrial food processing.

## you gotta know

- Promoted for decades under the celebrated commercial tagline "Ruffles Have Ridges," conceived to emphasize how its corrugated structural geometry provides greater rigidity and surface area than flat-cut potato chips when submerged into thick dips.
- Originated in 1958 when *C.E. Doolin*, the visionary co-founder of the *Frito Company*, purchased the brand trademark and specialized corrugated slicing patent from food technologist *Bernhardt Stahmer*, who had developed early undulating potato-slicing machines in the late 1940s.
- Formed a foundational product pillar of the modern snack conglomerate *Frito-Lay* following the landmark 1961 corporate merger between the *Frito Company* and *H.W. Lay & Company*, which subsequently joined with *Pepsi-Cola* in 1965 to create *PepsiCo*.
- Became a beloved cultural icon in Canada for popularizing the savory "All-Dressed" potato chip variety, a multi-flavor seasoning blend combining barbecue, ketchup, sour cream and onion, and salt-and-vinegar notes that later achieved cult culinary status in the American market.
- Introduced extensive product line innovations and texture variations over several decades, including *Ruffles Crispy Fries*, *Ruffles Double Dip*, *Ruffles Baked*, and *Ruffles Ridge Twists*, alongside top-selling domestic flavors such as "Cheddar & Sour Cream" and "Flamin' Hot."
- Formed prominent modern athletic partnerships to reach younger demographics, notably signing NBA superstar *Anthony Davis* in 2021 to a landmark endorsement deal that featured customized signature chip releases like "Lime and Jalapeño" and "Flamin' Hot BBQ."
- Derives its brand name directly from historical costume frills, drawing a vivid visual analogy to the ornamental pleated or ruffled fabric collars and lace trimmings that adorned European courtly attire from the Renaissance through the 18th century.
- Subject to rigorous food science engineering, utilizing specialized agricultural potato cultivars possessing high starch content and low reducing sugar levels to maintain structural ridge crispness and prevent scorching during industrial deep-frying.

## summary

**November** is the eleventh month of the Gregorian calendar and the concluding full month of meteorological autumn in the Northern Hemisphere. Deriving its name from the Latin word *novem*, meaning "nine," it originally marked the ninth month of the ancient Roman lunar and civic year before Julian reforms shifted its chronological position. Beyond its civil timekeeping role, the name represents the official radiotelephonic code word for the letter *N* within the international NATO phonetic alphabet, ensuring absolute clarity across maritime, aviation, and military voice telecommunications.

## you gotta know

- Retained its archaic Latin numerical root *novem* despite being displaced from the ninth to the eleventh position following the calendar expansions attributed to *Numa Pompilius* and *Julius Caesar*.
- Adopted globally as the standardized phonetic identifier for the letter *N* by the *International Civil Aviation Organization* and NATO to eliminate miscommunication over radio frequencies.
- Hosted significant religious observances and festivals in the [[roman-empire]], such as the *Ludi Plebeii* (Plebeian Games) in the Circus Flaminius and solemn banquets dedicated to *Jupiter*.
- Commemorates the cessation of hostilities on the Western Front of [[world-war-i]], celebrated internationally on November 11 as *Armistice Day*, *Remembrance Day*, or *Veterans Day*.
- Features prominent annual observational phenomena in [[astronomy]], including the *Leonid* meteor shower derived from comet *Tempel-Tuttle* and the *Andromedids*, both peaking during this month.
- Mandated under the federal statutes of the [[united-states]] as the month for holding nationwide presidential and congressional elections, scheduled on the Tuesday following the first Monday.
- Corresponds in Western astrology to the astrological signs of *Scorpio*, governing the month until November 21, and *Sagittarius*, commencing on November 22.
- Marked the historical moment in 2021 when international virologists and the *World Health Organization* officially named the [[omicron]] variant of concern.

## connections

- [[roman-empire]] — ancient civilization whose archaic ten-month calendar established the Latin etymological foundation for the month's name.
- [[astronomy]] — scientific field that tracks the Sun's transit through the zodiac and observes cyclical meteor showers occurring throughout the month.
- [[world-war-i]] — global conflict whose armistice went into effect at the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of this month in 1918.
- [[united-states]] — democratic nation whose major general elections and federal Thanksgiving holiday occur during this specific calendar period.
- [[omicron]] — coronavirus variant classified under Greek nomenclature by international health agencies during this month in 2021.
- [[nahuatl]] — indigenous Mexican language whose initial letter *N* is verbally transmitted in aviation telecommunications using the month's name.
- [[lunar-calendar]] — ancient timekeeping system whose natural lunar phases originally governed the month's length before solar calendar standardization.

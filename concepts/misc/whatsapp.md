## summary

**WhatsApp** is a cross-platform centralized instant messaging and voice-over-IP service created in 2009 by former Yahoo! engineers Jan Koum and Brian Acton. Acquired in 2014 by Facebook (now Meta Platforms) in a landmark tech transaction, it became the predominant consumer communication platform across much of Latin America, India, and Europe. Its implementation of default end-to-end encryption has made it a focal point in global debates concerning electronic surveillance, free expression, and telecommunications privacy.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 2009 by *Jan Koum*, a Ukrainian immigrant to California, alongside fellow former Yahoo! software engineer *Brian Acton*.
- Acquired by *Facebook* in February 2014 for approximately nineteen billion dollars, marking one of the largest technology acquisitions in history.
- Integrated the open-source *Signal Protocol* developed by *Open Whisper Systems* in 2016 to deliver default end-to-end encryption across all user messages and voice calls.
- Employs standard telephone numbers as primary user identifiers rather than traditional username-and-password account credentials.
- Emerged as the dominant digital communication backbone in developing economies, driving mobile commerce and peer-to-peer communication across India, Brazil, and Africa.
- Introduced ubiquitous UI conventions including single gray, double gray, and double blue checkmarks to denote sent, delivered, and read receipt statuses.
- Central subject of regulatory conflicts regarding platform accountability, government requests for backdoor decryption access, and viral election disinformation spreads.

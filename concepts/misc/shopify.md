## summary

**Shopify** is a leading multinational commerce platform founded in Ottawa, Canada, that provides digital infrastructure and software tools for online stores and retail point-of-sale systems. Created in two thousand six by German-Canadian programmers *Tobias Lütke*, *Daniel Weinand*, and *Scott Lake* after building their own custom software to sell snowboarding equipment online, the platform democratized internet retail by enabling independent merchants to build and manage digital storefronts without engineering expertise. Powering millions of businesses globally, it has grown into one of the most influential technological enterprises of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Founded in two thousand six in Ottawa, Ontario, after its creators found existing commercial e-commerce platforms inadequate for their online snowboard retailer *Snowdevil*.
- Built predominantly on the open-source *Ruby on Rails* framework, with founder *Tobias Lütke* serving as a core contributor to the software framework's early development.
- Created the open-source template language *Liquid* in two thousand six, which remains an industry standard for rendering dynamic storefront content.
- Launched the *Shopify App Store* in two thousand nine, establishing an extensive developer ecosystem that allowed third-party programmers to build and sell merchant extensions.
- Surpassed hundreds of billions of dollars in cumulative gross merchandise volume, competing directly against tech conglomerates by empowering merchant-owned branding rather than an aggregator marketplace.
- Introduced integrated merchant financial services, including payment gateway processing, inventory lending, and physical point-of-sale hardware terminals.
- Experienced dramatic worldwide expansion during global shifts toward electronic commerce, briefly becoming Canada's most valuable publicly traded corporation by market capitalization.

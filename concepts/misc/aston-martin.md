## summary

**Aston Martin** (formally **Aston Martin Lagonda**, or **Aston Martin Lagonda Global Holdings**) is an iconic British manufacturer of luxury grand tourers and high-performance sports cars, founded in 1913 by *Lionel Martin* and *Robert Bamford* in London, [[england]]. Famed for hand-finished coachbuilding, competitive racing heritage, and elegant grand touring design, the marque achieved enduring worldwide cultural fame through its long-standing cinematic association with fictional secret agent *James Bond*. Despite weathering seven separate bankruptcies across more than a century of existence, the brand persists as a leading global symbol of British bespoke craftsmanship and automotive prestige.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Henniker Mews, London, in 1913 by *Lionel Martin* and *Robert Bamford*, taking its name from Martin's competitive driving triumphs at the *Aston Hill Climb* in Buckinghamshire.
- Purchased in 1947 by English tractor and industrial magnate *Sir David Brown*, initiating the legendary "DB" model designation that defined the marque's most celebrated sporting roadsters and coupés.
- Catapulted into international cultural immortality when the silver birch *Aston Martin DB5*, equipped with imaginative espionage gadgets, debuted in the 1964 film *Goldfinger*.
- Achieved overall victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1959, when legendary drivers *Carroll Shelby* and *Roy Salvadori* piloted the green *Aston Martin DBR1* to a historic endurance triumph.
- Hand-assembles modern luxury models at headquarters in Gaydon, Warwickshire, competing for discerning collectors alongside elite haute horlogerie houses such as [[audemars-piguet]].
- Reached the technological cutting edge of road-going hypercars through the *Aston Martin Valkyrie*, an aerodynamic machine developed alongside *Adrian Newey* and *Red Bull Racing*.
- Survived numerous financial reorganizations and ownership eras, passing through stewardship by Ford's Premier Automotive Group before entering an investment era led by Canadian industrialist *Lawrence Stroll*.
- Expanded into the ultra-luxury sport utility vehicle sector with the *DBX*, manufactured at a purpose-built production facility located in St Athan, Wales.

## summary

A **discovered attack**, colloquially termed a **discovery**, is an essential tactical motif in chess where moving one piece or pawn unmasks a direct line of attack from a friendly long-range piece situated behind it. When the revealed target is the opposing king, the maneuver is designated a **discovered check**, which severely restricts defensive replies because the checked monarch must address the threat immediately. Because the moving piece can land on an otherwise undefended square to deliver independent threats, captures, or forks, this device frequently causes catastrophic material loss or rapid checkmate.

## you gotta know

- Arises whenever a player relocates an obstructing friendly piece off a file, rank, or diagonal, thereby unveiling an ambush from a queen, rook, or bishop lurking behind it.
- Generates devastating tactical tempo because the relocated piece can freely capture enemy material or deliver secondary threats while the opponent scrambles to counter the unmasked strike.
- Yields a *double check* when the moving piece itself also gives check alongside the unmasked piece, forcing the opposing king to move since neither blocking nor capturing can parry two checks at once.
- Enables the devastating tactical mechanism known as a *windmill* or see-saw, famously demonstrated by *Carlos Torre Repetto* against former world champion *Emanuel Lasker* in 1925.
- Featured centrally in classic games of chess literature, such as the immortal miniature victory by *Richard Réti* over *Savielly Tartakower* in Vienna in 1910 that culminated in a queen sacrifice and double check.
- Demands spatial visualization and acute board awareness, punishing players who leave pieces loosely aligned along open geometrical vectors.
- Formed a core tactical subject within the curriculum of elite twentieth-century chess academies, notably the state-sponsored training programs of the *Soviet school of chess*.

- [[geometry]] — relies directly on linear board mechanics, collinear sightlines, ray vectors, and angle alignments across ranks, files, and diagonals.
- [[perception]] — exploits cognitive blind spots and selective visual attention where a defender fails to perceive hidden threat rays masked by foreground pieces.

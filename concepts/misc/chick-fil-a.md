## summary

**Chick-fil-A** is an American fast-food restaurant chain specializing in pressure-fried chicken breast sandwiches, waffle potato fries, and distinctive dipping sauces. Founded in suburban Atlanta, Georgia, by *S. Truett Cathy*, the company grew into one of the largest and most profitable quick-service restaurant systems in the *United States*. The enterprise is equally noted for its corporate policies rooted in Southern evangelical Protestantism, including a chain-wide closure on Sundays.

## you gotta know

- Traces its operational origins to the *Dwarf Grill* (later *Dwarf House*), opened in 1946 in Hapeville, Georgia, near an Atlanta Ford assembly plant.
- Pioneered the commercial boneless fried chicken sandwich cooked under pressure in peanut oil, which founder *S. Truett Cathy* perfected during the early 1960s.
- Opened its first dedicated brand location in 1967 inside Atlanta's *Greenbriar Mall*, helping pioneer fast-food retail in suburban indoor shopping centers.
- Maintains an unwavering corporate mandate that all locations close on Sundays to allow employees a day of rest and religious worship.
- Popularized a multi-decade advertising campaign launched in 1995 featuring dairy cows brandishing misspelled signs pleading with customers to "Eat Mor Chikin."
- Enforces an unusual corporate franchise model where store operators pay a very low initial capital fee while the company retains ownership of real estate and equipment.
- Faced intense national consumer boycotts, counter-protests, and political scrutiny over executive charitable contributions to organizations opposing same-sex marriage.

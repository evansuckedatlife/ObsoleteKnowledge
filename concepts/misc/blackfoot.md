## summary

The **Blackfoot** people, unified historically as the **Blackfoot Confederacy** or **Niitsitapi** ("the original people"), are an Indigenous confederation of four culturally related nations inhabiting the northern Great Plains of Canada and the United States. Comprising the **Siksika**, Kainai, Northern Piikani, and Southern Piikani, their ancestral territory spanned southern Alberta, southwestern Saskatchewan, and northern Montana. Renowned for their equestrian bison-hunting culture, sophisticated ceremonial societies, and staunch defense of their homelands, they remained a dominant geopolitical power on the northwestern plains for centuries.

## you gotta know

- Formed by four allied divisions: the *Siksika* ("Black Foot", named for dark-dyed moccasins), the *Kainai* ("Blood"), the *Aapátohsipikani* (Northern Piikani in Alberta), and the *Aamsskáápipikani* (Southern Piikani in Montana).
- Speaks *Siksikáí’powahsin*, an Algonquian language whose deep structural divergence from eastern woodland relatives points to centuries of early residence on the plains.
- Built a nomadic seasonal subsistence strategy around vast herds of wild American bison (*tatanka*), harvesting meat, sinew, and hides while manufacturing winter provisions like *pemmican*.
- Commanded historic communal hunting sites for generations before acquiring Spanish horses, most famously preserving *Head-Smashed-In Buffalo Jump* in Alberta as an international heritage site.
- Maintained complex spiritual societies organized around sacred medicine bundles, beaver bundles, and the annual communal summer gathering known as the *Okan* (Sun Dance).
- Clashed with the Lewis and Clark Expedition in July 1806 along the Two Medicine River, leading to a fatal skirmish that represented the expedition's only deadly confrontation with Native peoples.
- Endured the horrific *Marias Massacre* (or *Baker Massacre*) in January 1870, when the U.S. Second Cavalry assaulted Chief *Heavy Runner*'s peaceful Piegan camp, killing over 170 mostly women, children, and elders.
- Confronted the tragic *Starvation Winter* of 1883–1884 after the collapse of plains bison herds, leading Chief *Crowfoot* (*Isapo-muxika*) and allied leaders to sign *Treaty 7* in Canada to ensure their people's survival.

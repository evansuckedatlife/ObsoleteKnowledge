## summary

**Taco Bell** is an American multinational fast-food restaurant chain founded in 1962 in Downey, California, by entrepreneur *Glen Bell*. Specializing in an Americanized variant of Mexican-inspired cuisine, the chain industrialized the production of crisp corn taco shells, burritos, and specialty items to build one of the largest quick-service restaurant networks in the world. Operating as a major subsidiary of Yum! Brands, the corporation represents a landmark case study in twentieth-century commercial franchising, food processing, and popular culture marketing.

## you gotta know

- Founder *Glen Bell* observed long lines of customers patronizing the Mitla Cafe across the street from his San Bernardino hot dog and hamburger stand, eventually reverse-engineering their hard-shell taco recipe.
- Bell developed a proprietary metal wire frying basket that held corn tortillas in a curved U-shape while submerged in boiling oil, enabling rapid pre-frying and assembly line efficiency.
- The inaugural official location opened in Downey, California, in 1962, featuring Spanish Mission-style adobe architecture complete with a functioning brass bell.
- The company grew exponentially through suburban franchise licensing across the Sunbelt during the 1960s and 1970s before being acquired by beverage and snack conglomerate PepsiCo in 1978.
- The chain introduced iconic branded signature menu items such as the *Crunchwrap Supreme*, the *Mexican Pizza*, and the 2012 *Doritos Locos Tacos*, which sold over a billion units.
- Advertising history includes the ubiquitous late-1990s promotional campaign featuring a talking Chihuahua voiced by *Carlos Alazraqui* uttering the slogan "¡Yo quiero Taco Bell!"
- Alongside sibling restaurant chains KFC and Pizza Hut, Taco Bell was spun off from PepsiCo in 1997 into Tricon Global Restaurants, later renamed Yum! Brands.
- In 2011, the company faced and successfully defended a high-profile class-action lawsuit in federal court alleging that its seasoned ground beef filling failed to meet minimum federal meat standards.

## summary

**Donair**, specifically the **Halifax donair**, is a regional Canadian street food dish originating in *Halifax*, *Nova Scotia*, derived from the Turkish *döner kebab* and Greek *gyro*. Created in the early 1970s by Greek immigrant *Peter Gamoulakos*, the dish adapts the traditional spit-roasted sandwich for maritime palates by swapping lamb for spiced ground beef and replacing garlic tzatziki with a distinctive sweet sauce made from sweetened condensed milk, vinegar, and garlic powder. Served hot inside a grilled Lebanese flatbread or pita, wrapped in aluminum foil, the savory and intensely sweet creation attained legendary status in Canadian culinary lore, eventually being designated the official food of Halifax in 2015.

## you gotta know

- Invented by Greek restaurateur *Peter Gamoulakos* at the *Velos Pizza* restaurant in *Bedford*, *Nova Scotia*, who found that local patrons initially rejected traditional Greek lamb gyros.
- The defining component is the signature donair sauce, a sweet and tangy emulsion created by vigorously whisking sweetened condensed milk, granulated sugar or garlic powder, and white vinegar until thickened.
- Prepared with heavily spiced, dense loaves of ground beef seasoned with paprika, cayenne pepper, onion powder, dried oregano, and salt, which are compressed to remove air pockets before being mounted on a vertical rotisserie spit.
- Shaved into thin ribbons as the exterior roasts, the beef is typically seared on a flat-top grill before being assembled into warm, water-misted pita bread with diced white onions and fresh tomatoes, strictly excluding lettuce.
- The city council of *Halifax Regional Municipality* formally declared the sandwich the city's official municipal food on December 8, 2015, following passionate civic debates over its cultural heritage.
- Spurred ubiquitous regional spin-offs across Eastern Canada, including donair pizza, donair egg rolls, donair poutine, and donair burgers found at virtually every local pizzeria competing with corporate delivery chains like *[[dominos]]*.
- The vertical spit technique descends directly from Ottoman Empire rotisserie meat traditions, which diffused globally into shawarma, al pastor, and döner variants.

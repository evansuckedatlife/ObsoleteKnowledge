## summary

**Target** is a prominent American big-box department store chain headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States. Established in 1962 by the *Dayton Company* as a discount department store concept, it carved out a distinctive market niche by marrying budget-friendly convenience with chic, design-forward merchandising known colloquially as "cheap chic". Recognized by its iconic red Bullseye logo and its canine mascot *Bullseye*, the brand is celebrated for democratizing modern design while serving as a case study in large-scale modern retail logistics.

## you gotta know

- Traces its lineage to the *Goodfellow Dry Goods* store founded in 1902 by *George Dayton*, which evolved into the prestigious Minneapolis department store chain *Dayton's*.
- Launched its first discount store in Roseville, Minnesota, in May 1962 under the direction of *Douglas Dayton*, debuting in the same year as retail competitors *Walmart* and *Kmart*.
- Developed the prominent marketing slogan "Expect More. Pay Less." to differentiate itself from traditional bare-bones discounters by partnering with high-profile fashion and industrial designers such as *Michael Graves*.
- Employs the globally recognized red three-ring "Bullseye" logo, accompanied since 1999 by *Bullseye*, an English Bull Terrier sporting the bullseye insignia painted over its left eye.
- Suffered an infamous data breach during the 2013 holiday shopping season affecting over forty million payment cards, leading to widespread overhauls of retail cybersecurity and payment terminal regulations in the [[united-states]].
- Executed a disastrous, short-lived expansion into Canada beginning in 2013, opening over one hundred stores before abruptly shutting down all Canadian operations in 2015 due to massive supply chain failures.
- Transformed suburban shopping culture in the late 20th century by pioneering elevated aesthetic merchandising, brightly lit wider aisles, and playful television advertising campaigns.

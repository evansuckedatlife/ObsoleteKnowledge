## summary

**Empire Company**, legally incorporated as **Empire Company Limited**, is a prominent Canadian corporate holding conglomerate headquartered in Stellarton, Nova Scotia. Anchored by its food retail subsidiary *Sobeys*, the corporation constitutes the second-largest grocery and retail food empire in Canada, operating more than fifteen hundred stores nationwide. Through a century of regional expansion, strategic mergers, and real estate development, it transformed a modest Maritime butcher route into a continental retail titan shaping Canadian commercial infrastructure.

## you gotta know

- Headquartered in the small town of Stellarton, Pictou County, Nova Scotia, where it remains one of the largest corporate enterprises based in Atlantic Canada.
- Controls *Sobeys*, the national supermarket chain originally founded in 1907 by *John William Sobey* as a horse-drawn meat delivery business before expanding into modern self-service grocery stores under *Frank H. Sobey*.
- Operates an extensive portfolio of distinct retail supermarket banners, including *Safeway*, *IGA*, *Foodland*, *FreshCo*, *Thrifty Foods*, and upscale specialty acquisitions such as *Farm Boy* and *Longo's*.
- Acquired the Canadian operations of the American grocery giant *Safeway* in 2013 for over five billion dollars, dramatically expanding its presence across Western Canada.
- Stocks extensive dairy selections across its supermarket delis, distributing specialty European products like Swiss [[emmental]] alongside wheels of Gouda imported from the [[dutch]] agricultural market.
- Competes aggressively in the convenient breakfast and ready-to-drink coffee retail segment, stocking packaged consumer products that contend with quick-service titans such as [[dunkin]].
- Held a major stake in Canadian commercial entertainment for decades through *Empire Theatres*, which operated movie exhibition houses across Canada before selling its screens to *Cineplex Entertainment* and *Landmark Cinemas* in 2013.
- Owns a significant controlling interest in *Crombie REIT*, an open-ended real estate investment trust that holds, leases, and develops commercial shopping centers anchored by corporate supermarkets.

## connections

- [[dunkin]] — retail beverage and breakfast competitor whose packaged grounds, beans, and breakfast goods are marketed directly in supermarket aisles.
- [[emmental]] — celebrated European cheese imported and sold across deli counters in specialized banners such as *Farm Boy* and *Sobeys*.
- [[dutch]] — nation of origin for popular imported specialty cheeses, including aged Gouda, widely merchandised across Atlantic and Canadian grocery counters.
- [[british-empire]] — historic maritime trading system that fostered early Scottish settlement and mercantile commerce in coastal Nova Scotia.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — era that introduced automated distribution, industrial refrigeration, and transcontinental railway logistics essential for modern grocery systems.
- [[united-states]] — neighboring market from which the conglomerate acquired *Safeway's* Canadian retail network and established cross-border produce supply chains.

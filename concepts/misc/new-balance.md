## summary

**New Balance** is an American athletic footwear and apparel manufacturer founded in Boston, Massachusetts, in 1906 by British immigrant *William J. Riley*. Originally established as the **New Balance Arch Support Company**, the enterprise focused on specialized orthopedic products before transforming in the mid-twentieth century into an innovator in competitive running shoes distinguished by multiple width offerings. Celebrated as an enduring symbol of American athletic engineering, the company is renowned for maintaining domestic factories in New England and the [[united-kingdom]] alongside its iconic grey suede runners and minimalist numeric model numbers.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1906 in Boston, Massachusetts, by *William J. Riley*, who reportedly formulated his flexible three-point arch support after observing how the three-toed claws of yard chickens provided perfect equilibrium and stability.
- Released the landmark *Trackster* in 1960, the first commercially produced running shoe manufactured with a rippled sole and offered in an unprecedented array of foot widths, making it an immediate favorite for collegiate track programs.
- Acquired on the day of the 1972 Boston Marathon by entrepreneur *Jim Davis*, who steered the six-person operation into a global sportswear empire while preserving the brand's core commitment to width sizing.
- Introduced the prestigious *990* silhouette in 1982, which became the first running shoe on the market to retail for one hundred dollars and established the brand's trademark muted grey suede aesthetic.
- Avoids naming shoes with descriptive titles, opting instead for a functional numeric naming convention (such as the 574, 990, 993, and 1500) to emphasize brand identity and engineering standards over individual shoe personas.
- Retains manufacturing facilities in the [[united-states]] across Massachusetts and Maine, as well as a celebrated heritage factory in Flimby, Cumbria, in the [[united-kingdom]], setting it apart from competitors reliant entirely on Asian contract facilities.
- Shifted from being colloquially stereotyped as practical footwear for suburban fathers into a high-fashion staple through partnerships with boutique designers, streetwear labels, and creative director *Teddy Santis*.

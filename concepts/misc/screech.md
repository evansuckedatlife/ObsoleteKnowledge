## summary

**Screech**, universally celebrated in Atlantic Canadian culture as **Newfoundland Screech**, is a potent, high-proof dark rum imported from Jamaica and bottled in the Canadian province of Newfoundland and Labrador. Originating from historic North Atlantic mercantile shipping networks established under the [[british-empire]], the liquor became embedded in maritime working-class heritage. Today, it is renowned as the ceremonial centerpiece of the comedic *Screech-in*, an elaborate initiation ritual in which visitors kiss a codfish and take a shot to receive honorary Newfoundland citizenship.

## you gotta know

- Forms the indispensable centerpiece of the traditional *Screech-in* welcoming ceremony, an interactive pub ritual where non-residents recite regional oaths, kiss a frozen or salted codfish directly on the mouth, and consume a generous measure of rum.
- Initiates undergoing the welcoming ceremony are asked by the presiding officiant *Is you a Newfoundlander?*, to which they must recite the customary dialect reply: *Deed I is, me cocky, and long may yer big jib draw!*
- Historical emergence dates to the maritime triangular trade under [[colonialism]], wherein vessels laden with salted cod from Newfoundland fisheries sailed to the Caribbean to feed enslaved populations, returning with casks of molasses and fiery unaged rum.
- Folkloric legend traces the brand's colorful name to an American soldier stationed in Newfoundland during [[world-war-ii]], whose piercing howl of pain upon tossing back the raw liquor led a startled local bystander to inquire what had caused the horrific sound.
- Historically imported in wooden hogsheads as high-proof Jamaican naval rum and sold in bulk, it was later standardized, blended, and bottled under crown monopoly regulations by the Newfoundland and Labrador Liquor Corporation.
- Demonstrates a vivid [[semantic-shift]] wherein an auditory word originally signifying an agonizing shriek or bird cry shifted through working-class slang into a permanent proper noun for dark liquor.
- Live sound operators managing amplification gear manufactured by [[sennheiser]] utilize acoustic feedback suppressors to prevent microphones from emitting a genuine, deafening audio feedback scream or screech.

## connections

- [[british-empire]] — historic maritime imperial power whose transatlantic navigation routes established trade exchanges between Canadian fisheries and Caribbean sugar plantations.
- [[colonialism]] — mercantilist economic framework that fostered the plantation economies, triangular shipping lanes, and rum production of the West Indies.
- [[scotch]] — counterpart Commonwealth distilled spirit whose tightly regulated malt traditions contrast with the naval molasses rum legacy of the North Atlantic.
- [[semantic-shift]] — linguistic evolution through which an onomatopoeic descriptor for an unbearable vocal noise became a permanent corporate and cultural brand.
- [[sennheiser]] — audio engineering titan whose microphones and stage equipment are engineered to suppress acoustic feedback resonance and high-frequency squeals.
- [[united-kingdom]] — imperial sovereign realm that administered the Dominion of Newfoundland prior to its entry into Canadian Confederation in 1949.

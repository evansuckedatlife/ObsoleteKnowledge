## summary

**Linamar Corporation** is a major Canadian diversified global manufacturing firm and precision-engineering enterprise headquartered in Guelph, Ontario. Founded in 1966 by Hungarian immigrant *Frank Hasenfratz*, the enterprise expanded from a one-man machining operation in a suburban basement into one of the preeminent automotive powertrain and industrial equipment suppliers in North America. The corporation is renowned in corporate business trivia for its distinctive portmanteau title honoring the founder's daughters and wife, as well as its high-profile ownership of the mobile access equipment brand *Skyjack* and agricultural manufacturer *MacDon*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1966 by *Frank Hasenfratz*, a skilled Hungarian machinist who escaped to Canada during the aftermath of the Hungarian Revolution of 1956 and launched a subcontract lathe venture in his family basement.
- The corporate moniker is a famous portmanteau coined from the first letters of Hasenfratz's two daughters, *Linda* and *Nancy*, and his wife, *Margaret*.
- Functions commercially across two overarching pillars: the Industrial division (encompassing agricultural machinery and aerial access platforms) and the Mobility division (machining advanced metallic assemblies for hybrid, electric, and internal combustion vehicle powertrains).
- Diversified away from strictly automotive manufacturing by acquiring *Skyjack* in 2002, becoming a global market leader in the production of motorized scissor lifts, boom platforms, and telehandlers used in commercial [[architecture]].
- Greatly expanded its agricultural harvesting machinery division in 2018 through the acquisition of Winnipeg-based *MacDon Industries*, supplying combine headers and windrowers across the [[united-states]] and international farming economies.
- Long directed by the founder's daughter *Linda Hasenfratz*, who took the helm as Chief Executive Officer in 2002 and guided its international expansion across North America, Europe, and Asia.
- Operates alongside peer automotive supplier *Magna International* and retail retail titan [[loblaw-companies]] as a foundation of Ontario's post-war commercial manufacturing economy, heavily reliant on integrated cross-border trade corridors.

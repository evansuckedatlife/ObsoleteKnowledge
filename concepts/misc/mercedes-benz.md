## summary

**Mercedes-Benz** is a German automotive marque established in 1926 following the merger of the pioneer companies founded by Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler. Renowned for engineering innovation, luxury passenger automobiles, and sustained domination in motorsport, the brand traces its lineage directly to the 1886 Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely recognized as the first internal combustion production automobile. Its trademark three-pointed star symbolizes Daimler's universal ambition for motorization across land, sea, and air.

## you gotta know

- Traces its lineage to *Karl Benz*, whose 1886 *Benz Patent-Motorwagen* is recognized as the world's first modern commercial automobile, financed and promoted by his wife *Bertha Benz*.
- The *Mercedes* brand name originated in 1901 from Austrian diplomat *Emil Jellinek*, who named racing cars designed by *Wilhelm Maybach* after his daughter, *Mercédès Jellinek*.
- Merged the companies of *Gottlieb Daimler* and *Karl Benz* in 1926 to form *Daimler-Benz AG*, consolidating production around the headquarters in Stuttgart, *Germany*.
- The three-pointed star logo was patented in 1909 to symbolize the founder's vision of engine dominance across three spheres: land, water, and air.
- Dominated international motorsport in the 1930s alongside Auto Union as the *Silver Arrows* (*Silberpfeile*), fielding technologically advanced grand prix machines.
- Suffered motorsport's darkest day at the 1955 24 Hours of Le Mans when a *Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR* crashed into the grandstands, prompting a three-decade withdrawal from racing.
- Pioneered foundational passenger safety innovations, including the rigid passenger cell with crumple zones in 1959 and the commercial introduction of anti-lock braking systems (*ABS*) in 1978.

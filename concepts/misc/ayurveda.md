## summary

**Ayurveda**, also known as **Ayurvedic medicine**, is an ancient system of holistic healing that originated on the Indian subcontinent over two millennia ago. Drawing its name from the Sanskrit roots *ayus* (life) and *veda* (knowledge), the discipline conceptualizes health as a harmonious equilibrium between mind, body, and cosmic elements. Renowned for its classical textual traditions, constitutional typology, and early surgical innovations, it remains one of the world's most influential traditional medical philosophies.

## you gotta know

- Grounded in the central doctrine of the *tridosha*, three biological energies derived from the five universal elements (*panchamahabhuta*): *Vata* (space and air, governing motion), *Pitta* (fire and water, governing metabolic digestion), and *Kapha* (water and earth, governing somatic structure).
- Codified within the foundational textual canon known as the *Brihat Trayi* (The Great Triad), comprising the *Charaka Samhita*, the *Sushruta Samhita*, and the later synthesis of the *Ashtanga Hridaya*.
- Celebrated for pioneering advanced surgical procedures documented in the *Sushruta Samhita*, which described early reconstructive rhinoplasty using forehead skin flaps, foreign-body extraction, and surgical instrumentation centuries before modern clinical medicine.
- Organized into eight traditional clinical branches (*ashtanga*), which encompass internal medicine (*kayachikitsa*), pediatrics (*kaumarbhritya*), surgery (*shalyatantra*), head and neck treatments (*shalakyatantra*), toxicology (*agadatantra*), rejuvenation (*rasayana*), virility (*vajikarana*), and psychiatry (*bhutavidya*).
- Relies for diagnostic assessment upon the eightfold clinical protocol (*ashtavidha pariksha*), in which the physician observes pulse (*nadi*), urine, stool, tongue, voice, touch, eyes, and general bodily habitus to assess internal *[[human-anatomy]]*.
- Employs *panchakarma*, an intensive detoxification and purification regimen consisting of five therapeutic cleansing actions designed to clear metabolic waste (*ama*) and restore balance.
- Shared conceptual affinities with the humoral doctrines developed in *[[ancient-greece]]* and practiced in the *[[middle-east]]*, exchanging botanical pharmacopeia and clinical observations across ancient trading networks.

## connections

- [[human-anatomy]] — biological framework evaluated in traditional diagnostics through physical pulse examination, tissue classification, and surgical treatises.
- [[five-senses]] — sensory faculties used by practitioners to observe symptoms and harmonized through therapeutic herbal applications and dietary therapies.
- [[perception]] — mental faculty emphasized in traditional diagnostic methodology, which stresses the clinician's attuned sensory awareness of physical signs.
- [[ancient-greece]] — civilization whose Hippocratic and Galenic humoral concepts closely parallel the constitutional humour theories of the classical Indian tradition.
- [[middle-east]] — region where translated Sanskrit medical works intersected with early Islamic medicine and Persian botanical scholarship.
- [[theravada-buddhism]] — spiritual tradition whose monastic communities helped preserve, disseminate, and practice traditional herbal therapeutics across South and Southeast Asia.

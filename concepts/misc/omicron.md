## summary

**Omicron** is the fifteenth letter of the Greek alphabet, functioning historically to represent the short close-mid back rounded vowel in classical antiquity. Its name derives from the Greek phrase *o mikron*, translating literally to "little o" to distinguish its phonetic length from *omega*, meaning "great o." In contemporary scientific disciplines, it serves prominently within the *Bayer designation* system of [[astronomy]], designates computational bounding algorithms in mathematics, and achieved worldwide prominence as a designated variant of concern during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## you gotta know

- Originated directly from the Phoenician consonant *ayin*, meaning "eye," whose glottal or pharyngeal sound was repurposed by early Greeks to represent the vowel sound /o/.
- Holds the numeric value of seventy (70) in the *Milesian* or Greek alphabetic numeral system, functioning as the symbol for that decade rank.
- Etymologically contrasted with *omega* (*ō mega*), which was appended to the Greek script to represent the long open-mid back rounded vowel sound /ɔː/.
- Utilized in [[astronomy]] to categorize stars within constellations according to apparent brightness, most famously designating *Omicron Ceti*, the prototypical long-period pulsating variable star named *Mira* by *Johannes Hevelius*.
- Applied in computational complexity theory and [[geometry]] as part of the *Bachmann-Landau* notation family, frequently paired with big-O notation to express asymptotic upper bounds on algorithmic running time.
- Selected by the *World Health Organization* in the month of [[november]] 2021 to designate the highly transmissible *B.1.1.529* lineage of SARS-CoV-2, deliberately skipping the letters *Nu* and *Xi* to avoid linguistic ambiguity and geopolitical friction.
- Transmitted into the Etruscan alphabet and subsequently into the Latin alphabet of the [[roman-empire]], providing the immediate ancestral glyph for the modern Latin letter *O*.
- Occupies the fifteenth position in standard Greek alphabetical sequence, immediately following the consonant *xi* and preceding the mathematical constant symbol *pi*.

## connections

- [[ancient-greece]] — Mediterranean culture that devised the vowel-inclusive classical alphabet and established the letter's graphic and phonetic values.
- [[astronomy]] — physical science that adopts Greek letters to catalog stellar brightness within constellations such as the variable giant *Omicron Ceti*.
- [[geometry]] — mathematical field where classical Greek characters denote planar angles, circular curves, and asymptotic complexity boundaries.
- [[linguistics]] — academic discipline examining phonological shifts, orthographic borrowing, and the transition from Semitic abjads to full alphabetic writing systems.
- [[roman-empire]] — classical power whose Latin script adapted the circular Etruscan letterform descended from archaic Greek epigraphy.
- [[november]] — calendar month during which global health authorities officially assigned this Greek letter to the surging coronavirus variant in 2021.

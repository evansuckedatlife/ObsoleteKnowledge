## summary

**Astronomy** is the scientific discipline that studies celestial objects, phenomena, and the structure of the universe itself. Rooted in ancient observation but rigorously developed through telescopes and physics, astronomy bridges pure observation and mathematical prediction. It stands as one of humanity's oldest sciences, central to calendars, navigation, and our understanding of reality.

## you gotta know

- Emerged from naked-eye observation in ancient Mesopotamia, Egypt, and Greece—used to track seasons, predict eclipses, and navigate.
- Revolutionized by the *telescope* (early 1600s), which revealed moons of *Jupiter*, phases of *Venus*, and countless stars invisible to the naked eye.
- Copernicus displaced Earth from the centre of the cosmos; Kepler described planetary orbits mathematically; Newton explained them through gravity.
- Spectroscopy allows astronomers to determine the chemical composition, temperature, and motion of distant stars and galaxies.
- Parallax measurement and the inverse-square law enabled calculation of distances to nearby stars and then to entire galaxies.
- Powers calendar-making: lunar cycles, solar years, and stellar positions regulate civil, religious, and agricultural timekeeping across cultures.
- Modern astronomy maps the universe's large-scale structure: galaxies cluster in cosmic webs, separated by vast voids of empty space.
- The *Doppler effect* in light reveals whether distant galaxies move toward or away from us, evidence that the universe itself is expanding.
- Black holes, neutron stars, and gravitational waves are predicted by Einstein's relativity and confirmed by modern observations—pushing the limits of physics itself.
- Exoplanet discovery (via transit photometry and radial velocity) has revealed thousands of worlds orbiting distant stars, reshaping our understanding of planetary formation.
- Radio astronomy, infrared telescopes, and space observatories (Hubble, James Webb) have opened new windows on the universe invisible to human eyes, revealing hidden stellar nurseries and ancient galaxies.

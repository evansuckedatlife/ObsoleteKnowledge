## summary

**Ravana** is the primary antagonist of the Hindu epic *Ramayana*, a demon king of extraordinary power and intellect whose abduction of *Sita* precipitates the entire narrative. Though fundamentally evil in his cosmic role, *Ravana* is portrayed with surprising complexity—a ruler proud of his accomplishments and deeply attached to his family. His defeat by *Rama* represents the triumph of *Dharma* (righteousness) over cosmic chaos and demonic chaos.

## you gotta know

- A demon king with ten heads and twenty arms, granting him supernatural strength and near-invincibility in battle, making him one of mythology's most formidable antagonists.
- Ruler of *Lanka*, a powerful kingdom across the ocean from India, commanding legions of rakshasas (demons) and vast treasures accumulated through conquest.
- Abducts *Sita*, the wife of *Rama* (*Vishnu's* incarnation), setting in motion the events of the *Ramayana* and the entire narrative of restoration and heroism.
- Of Brahmin heritage despite his demonic nature, originally obtained a boon of invulnerability through strict asceticism, making himself vulnerable only to man or monkey—a fatal loophole.
- A skilled warrior and strategist who commands vast armies and demonstrates considerable intellect, yet ultimately proves no match for *Rama* supported by divine grace and *Hanuman's* unfailing loyalty.
- Killed by *Rama* in the climactic battle that restores *Dharma* and retrieves the stolen *Sita*, vindicating righteousness over tyranny and chaos.
- Viewed by some traditions with nuance—as a figure tragic in his pride, devoted to his family and kingdom, yet ultimately imprisoned by his own ego, sin, and inability to resist temptation.
- His character demonstrates the Hindu concept that even demons possess intellect and complexity, yet their choices ultimately determine their fate in the cosmic order.

## connections

- [[rama]] — *Vishnu's* incarnation and *Ravana's* arch-nemesis, representing righteousness against demonic chaos.
- [[sita]] — the abducted wife whose seizure triggers the entire epic narrative.
- [[ramayana]] — the Hindu epic whose central plot revolves around *Ravana's* abduction and defeat.
- [[hanuman]] — the loyal monkey-devotee of *Rama* who proves instrumental in *Ravana's* downfall.
- [[vishnu]] — the supreme deity whose incarnation as *Rama* opposes *Ravana*.
- [[dharma]] — the cosmic order that *Ravana's* actions violate and that *Rama* restores through his victory.
- [[hinduism]] — the religious tradition that transmits *Ravana's* story as a moral and spiritual lesson.

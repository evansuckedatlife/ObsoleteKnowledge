## summary

**Avalon** is the mystical, otherworldly island in Arthurian legend where King *Arthur* is taken to heal from his mortal wounds after the Battle of Camlann. Shrouded in mist and magic, Avalon represents the threshold between the mortal realm and the divine, a place of eternal timelessness and restoration. It embodies the romantic ideal of a hidden sanctuary where legend itself never dies.

## you gotta know

- A mystical island hidden by enchantment, accessible only to those blessed with magical knowledge or divine favor, shrouded in perpetual mist.
- According to legend, *Arthur* is carried there after his final battle at Camlann to be healed by the magical arts and preserved for eternity.
- *Morgan le Fay* is often depicted as ruling Avalon, a realm of faeries and priestesses devoted to ancient magic and the old pagan ways.
- The location of Avalon has been a subject of endless speculation—some legends place it in Cornwall or Wales, others beyond the earthly plane entirely in the Otherworld.
- *Excalibur*, *Arthur's* legendary sword, is sometimes said to have been forged or enchanted in Avalon by its craftspeople, giving it its supernatural properties.
- *Arthur* is prophesied to return from Avalon one day to restore Britain when it is needed most—the concept of *Rex Quondam Rexque Futurus* ("the once and future king"), a messianic expectation that inspired real historical movements.
- Avalon represents the intersection of Celtic mythology and medieval Christian Arthurian romance, blending pre-Christian and Christian traditions.
- In some versions, Avalon is depicted as a place of eternal youth and timelessness where *Arthur* neither ages nor dies, living in a state of magical suspension.

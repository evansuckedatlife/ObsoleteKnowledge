## summary

**Anchises** was a mortal Trojan prince of Dardania whose union with the love goddess *Aphrodite* produced the legendary founding hero *Aeneas*. Celebrated across classical Greek and Roman epic literature, he became the timeless embodiment of filial devotion, ancestral piety, and patriarchal wisdom when his son carried him upon his shoulders away from the burning ruins of Troy during [[the-trojan-war]]. His eventual death in Sicily and his prophetic guidance of *Aeneas* from the Underworld form an essential foundation of Roman national mythology, anchoring the imperial destiny of the [[roman-empire]] directly to the royal house of ancient Troy.

## you gotta know

- Escaped the destruction of Troy on the back of his son *Aeneas*, who carried his elderly, crippled father through the burning streets while leading his young grandson *Ascanius* by the hand, creating an iconic scene immortalized in classical art as the supreme emblem of filial duty (*pietas*).
- Carried the sacred household gods (*Penates*) and the holy ancestral hearth fires out of the falling city, entrusted with their preservation because *Aeneas* could not handle the consecrated statues while his hands were stained with the bloodshed of [[the-trojan-war]].
- Seduced on *Mount Ida* by the Olympian goddess *Aphrodite*, who was compelled by [[zeus]] to experience mortal desire and visited the handsome prince disguised as a Phrygian princess, ultimately conceiving *Aeneas*.
- Struck and crippled by a thunderbolt hurled by [[zeus]] after boasting of his divine romance with *Aphrodite* during a banquet, an act of mortal hubris that left him impaired and dependent on others in his old age.
- Served as the venerable spiritual guide of the exiled Trojan fleet across the Mediterranean, mistakenly interpreting an oracle of [[apollo]] at Delos as directing them toward Crete before realizing their true ancestral destination was Italy.
- Died at Drepanum in western Sicily before the Trojan fleet reached the Italian mainland, prompting *Aeneas* to establish elaborate commemorative funeral games and ritual sacrifices in his memory a year later.
- Welcomed *Aeneas* into the Underworld Elysian Fields in the *Aeneid*, where his shade reveals the grand transmigration of souls and prophesies the future glory, triumphs, and rulers of the [[roman-empire]].
- Secretly pastured his own mares with the miraculous, divine horses given by [[zeus]] to King *Laomedon* of Troy, breeding a celebrated line of swift cavalry mounts mentioned in [[the-iliad]].

## connections

- [[the-trojan-war]] — the cataclysmic conflict whose final sack required him to flee his burning homeland on his son's back.
- [[the-iliad]] — the Homeric epic that chronicles his noble Dardanian lineage and the supernatural horses he bred.
- [[zeus]] — the king of the gods who struck him with a thunderbolt for bragging of his affair with *Aphrodite*.
- [[apollo]] — the god of prophecy whose cryptic oracles he attempted to interpret during the voyage toward Italy.
- [[hector]] — the champion of Troy and his royal second cousin through their shared descent from King *Dardanus*.
- [[paris]] — the Trojan prince whose judgment of the goddesses on *Mount Ida* precipitated the downfall of their civilization.
- [[roman-empire]] — the imperial civilization that revered him as a foundational patriarch and moral archetype of traditional piety.

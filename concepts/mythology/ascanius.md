## summary

**Ascanius**, also known as **Iulus**, was a legendary Trojan prince, the son of the hero *Aeneas* and the Trojan princess *Creusa*. After escaping the catastrophic destruction of his native city at the conclusion of the [[the-trojan-war|Trojan War]], he traveled across the Mediterranean to mainland [[italy|Italy]], where he led the surviving Trojans and founded the ancient settlement of *Alba Longa*. In Roman tradition and classical epic, he serves as the crucial genealogical bridge linking the fallen glory of Troy to the rise of the [[roman-empire|Roman Empire]], standing as the venerated patriarch of the imperial Julian lineage.

## you gotta know

- Fled the burning citadel of Troy as a small boy clinging to the hand of his father *Aeneas*, establishing an enduring archetype of generational continuity and filial piety celebrated throughout Western art.
- Known by the surname *Iulus*, an identity celebrated by Roman historians and poets to establish him as the eponymous founder of the patrician *gens Julia*, tracing the lineage of *Julius Caesar* and Emperor *Augustus* directly to the goddess *Venus*.
- Founded the historic Latin settlement of *Alba Longa* along the Alban ridge thirty years after the construction of Lavinium, fulfilling the prophecy of the miraculous white sow found suckling thirty piglets.
- Manifested a divine omen in *Vergil's* *Aeneid* when a harmless flame of celestial fire played across his hair, convincing his grandfather *Anchises* to embrace their fated exile.
- Impersonated by the god of love, *Cupid*, who temporarily assumed the prince's youthful appearance at a banquet in Carthage to instill Queen *Dido* with a passionate, doomed infatuation for his father.
- Unwittingly ignited the bitter war between the Trojan refugees and the native Latins by mortally wounding a pet stag cherished by *Silvia*, daughter of King *Latinus's* chief herdsman, after the Fury *Allecto* drove his hounds into a frenzy.
- Achieved his first battlefield triumph during the siege of the Trojan camp by shooting the insolent Rutulian champion *Numanus Remulus* through the head with a bow, prompting the god [[apollo|Apollo]] to descend from the heavens, commend his rising virtue, and bar him from further combat.

## connections

- [[the-trojan-war]] — the devastating conflict that destroyed his ancestral home and set his family on their fated journey westward.
- [[roman-empire]] — the imperial superpower whose rulers traced their divine lineage and political legitimacy back to his name.
- [[italy]] — the promised western land where he fought indigenous coalitions, succeeded his father, and founded Alba Longa.
- [[the-iliad]] — the foundational Homeric epic recounting the catastrophic siege of the Trojan city where his family held royal power.
- [[apollo]] — the Olympian deity who protected the Trojan remnants, guided their journey through prophecy, and praised the youth's archery.
- [[hector]] — his heroic royal kinsman whose battlefield prowess and tragic death defined the noble spirit of the Trojan resistance.
- [[paris]] — his uncle whose fateful judgment of the goddesses and abduction of Helen brought ruin to their dynasty.
- [[zeus]] — the king of the gods who ordained the inescapable destiny carrying his descendants from Troy to the hills of Latium.

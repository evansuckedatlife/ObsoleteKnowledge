## summary

**Eurydice** was an Auloniad oak-nymph whose sudden demise and agonizing second loss form the tragic centerpiece of the myth of *Orpheus*. After perishing from a venomous snakebite on her wedding day, her husband braved the subterranean horrors of the underworld, charming gods and monsters alike with his music to win back her life. The condition of her release—that *Orpheus* must not look back at her until reaching the upper realm—led to her permanent loss, cementing her narrative as the eternal symbol of love defeated by mortal frailty.

## you gotta know

- Married the legendary Thracian poet and musician *Orpheus*, whose divine artistry on the lyre was gifted and taught by the god *[[apollo]]*.
- Stepped fatally upon a venomous viper hidden within tall meadow grass while fleeing the aggressive advances of the pastoral beekeeper *Aristaeus*.
- Mourned by *Orpheus* with melodies so heart-rending that nymphs, wild beasts, and Olympian divinities wept, prompting his descent into the underworld through the cavern at Taenarum.
- Reclaimed conditionally after *Orpheus* charmed the chthonic judges, the Erinyes, and the three-headed hound *Cerberus*, bringing tears even to the eyes of *Persephone*.
- Subject to the absolute command that *Orpheus* must walk ahead without turning his eyes back to gaze upon her until both travelers stood under the sunlight.
- Lost permanently just paces away from the world of the living when *Orpheus*, seized by overwhelming anxiety and doubt, looked back to ensure she was following.
- Commemorated in classical literature through moving poetic depictions in Virgil's *Georgics* and Book X of Ovid's *Metamorphoses*.
- Reunited forever with *Orpheus* in the Elysian Fields after the grief-stricken singer was dismembered by Thracian Maenads during their ecstatic rites.

## connections

- [[apollo]] — the patron deity of music and prophecy whose artistic inspiration empowered *Orpheus* to charm the underworld for her sake.
- [[jason]] — the leader of the Argonauts whose seafaring quest for the Golden Fleece relied on her husband *Orpheus* to drown out the songs of the sirens.
- [[heracles]] — the heroic champion whose twelfth labor brought him into the underworld realm from which *Orpheus* sought to retrieve Eurydice.
- [[eurystheus]] — the Mycenaean king whose demand for the hound *Cerberus* forced *[[heracles]]* to tread the identical subterranean pathways traversed by *Orpheus*.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the ancient civilization within whose mystery cults and lyric poetry the journey to reclaim Eurydice held deep esoteric resonance.

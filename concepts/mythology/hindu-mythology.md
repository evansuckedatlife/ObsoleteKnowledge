## summary

**Hindu Mythology** encompasses the vast, ancient, and richly layered narrative tradition of the Indian subcontinent, preserved across sacred scriptures including the *Vedas*, *Upanishads*, *Puranas*, and the great epics *Mahabharata* and *Ramayana*. It articulates a cyclical universe shaped by *dharma* (cosmic order and righteousness), *karma* (cause and effect), and *samsara* (reincarnation), governed by major divine aspects such as the *Trimurti*. Unlike many classical mythologies whose worship ceased, Hindu mythological lore remains a living, evolving spiritual and cultural foundation for over a billion people worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Structured around the cosmic *Trimurti*, representing three primary functions of existence: *Brahma* the creator, *Vishnu* the preserver, and *Shiva* the destroyer or transformer.
- Centralizes the concept of *Avatars* (*descents*), primarily ten incarnations (*Dashavatara*) of *Vishnu* who enter the mortal world to vanquish evil and restore *dharma*, including *Rama* and *Krishna*.
- Features two monumental epic narratives: the *Ramayana*, detailing Prince *Rama*'s quest to rescue his wife *Sita* from the demon king *Ravana*, and the *Mahabharata*, chronicling the dynastic *Kurukshetra War*.
- Incorporates the *Bhagavad Gita*, a pivotal 700-verse philosophical dialogue spoken by *Krishna* to the Pandava warrior *Arjuna* on duty, devotion, and selfless action.
- Conceives of cosmic time as an endless cycle divided into four successive ages (*yugas*): *Satya Yuga*, *Treta Yuga*, *Dvapara Yuga*, and the present age of darkness, *Kali Yuga*.
- Revering the Divine Feminine through *Devi* or *Shakti*, manifesting both as gentle providers like *Lakshmi* and *Parvati* and as fierce cosmic warriors like *Durga* and *Kali*.
- Prominently features celestial beings including *devas* (gods), *asuras* (anti-gods or titans), *nagas* (serpent deities), *rakshasas* (demonic spirits), and *apsaras* (celestial nymphs).
- Employs sacred animals throughout divine iconography, including *Nandi* the bull mount of *Shiva*, the divine eagle *Garuda*, and sacred *horses* emerging from cosmic events like the churning of the ocean (*Samudra Manthan*).

## connections

- [[vishnu]] — the preserver deity whose ten avatars descend across cyclical ages to defeat evil.
- [[shiva]] — the transformer and destroyer in the *Trimurti*, embodying ascetic renunciation and cosmic dance.
- [[horses]] — sacred equines like *Uchchaihshravas* and rituals like the *Ashvamedha* play vital roles in the tradition.
- [[hermes-trismegistus]] — parallel esoteric archetype of deep cosmic wisdom and unity explored in comparative mystical philosophy.
- [[ancient-greece]] — Indo-European linguistic and mythological cousin sharing early pantheonic traits with the Vedic gods.
- [[middle-east]] — neighboring geographical and cultural sphere with ancient trade and ideological exchanges with the Indus region.

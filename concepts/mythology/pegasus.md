## summary

**Pegasus** is the legendary winged horse of Greek mythology, born from the blood of the slain Gorgon *Medusa* when she was beheaded by *Perseus*. This divine steed became the noble companion of the hero *Bellerophon*, enabling him to accomplish impossible feats, most famously the defeat of the *Chimera*. Pegasus transcends the role of mere beast, representing divine grace and the link between mortal heroism and celestial power.

## you gotta know

- Born from *Medusa's* blood when *Perseus* decapitated her during a difficult quest, making him a creature tied to both death and divine grace—a paradox central to his meaning.
- A winged horse with pure white plumage, capable of flight and divine speed beyond mortal comprehension, embodying grace and nobility in equine form.
- Tamed and ridden by the hero *Bellerophon*, who used him to defeat the three-headed *Chimera* that plagued the kingdom of Lycia, proving *Pegasus* essential to heroic success.
- Created the spring Hippocrene on Mount Helicon with a single hoof strike, a spring sacred to the Muses—firmly associating *Pegasus* with poetry, inspiration, and artistic genius.
- Eventually transcended to the heavens, where *Zeus* transformed him into a constellation still bearing his name, immortalizing him in the night sky as eternal recognition of his deeds.
- In some accounts, *Pegasus* is said to belong to the Muses themselves, serving as a symbol of poetic genius and artistic transcendence that inspires mortal creativity.
- Represented as the ideal of noble and divine power placed at the service of heroic virtue, a bridge between the divine realm and mortal accomplishment.
- The image of *Pegasus* became an enduring symbol in Western art and literature, representing the marriage of power and beauty with creative inspiration.

## connections

- [[medusa]] — the Gorgon from whose death *Pegasus* was born, linking nobility to monstrosity.
- [[bellerophon]] — the hero who tamed and rode *Pegasus* to accomplish his legendary deeds.
- [[chimera]] — the three-headed monster defeated by *Bellerophon* mounted upon *Pegasus*.
- [[poseidon]] — the god credited in some accounts with fathering *Pegasus* through *Medusa*.
- [[echidna]] — the mother of both *Medusa* and the *Chimera*, making her distantly ancestral to *Pegasus*.
- [[heracles]] — a fellow hero of legendary accomplishment, sharing the theme of divine-aided heroism.
- [[athens]] — the city where *Pegasus* became an important symbol in art and architecture.

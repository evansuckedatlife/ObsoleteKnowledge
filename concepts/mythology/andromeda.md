## summary

**Andromeda** was a legendary princess of *Aethiopia* in Greek mythology, the daughter of King *Cepheus* and Queen *Cassiopeia*. She is best known for the perilous ordeal in which she was chained to a coastal rock as a human sacrifice to appease a ferocious sea monster sent by the sea god *Poseidon*. Her rescue by the flying hero *Perseus*, who slew the leviathan and claimed her as his bride, remains one of the most famous archetype narratives of classical antiquity and Western romance. Following her earthly life, she was granted catasterism by the goddess *Athena*, who transformed her into the prominent northern constellation that still bears her name today.

## you gotta know

- Chained to a desolate sea crag as a sacrifice to a ravenous sea monster, she was spotted and saved by the hero *Perseus*, who killed the creature and took her as his wife.
- Condemned to die due to the hubris of her mother, Queen *Cassiopeia*, who angered the gods by boasting that she and her daughter were far fairer than the *Nereids*, the sea nymphs.
- Sentenced to exposure after the sea god *Poseidon* inundated the realm with floods and the sea monster *Cetus*, prompting the oracle of *Ammon* to decree that only her sacrifice would appease the divine fury.
- Watched her rescuer defeat the beast either through ferocious combat with his curved adamantine harpe sickle or by revealing the petrifying gaze of the severed head of *Medusa*.
- Sparked a violent brawl at her wedding banquet when her uncle and former fiancé *Phineus* attacked the hall, leading *Perseus* to turn *Phineus* and his fellow conspirators to stone with the Gorgon's head.
- Relocated with her husband across the Mediterranean to mainland Greece, dwelling in *Tiryns* and *Mycenae*, where she bore seven sons and two daughters who founded influential royal houses.
- Became the matriarch of the renowned Perseid dynasty, making her the direct great-grandmother of the Greek hero *Heracles* through her son *Electryon* and grandson *Amphitryon*.
- Placed in the heavens upon her death by the goddess *Athena*, forming the constellation *Andromeda*, depicted historically in celestial star maps as a maiden bound by her wrists with chains.

## connections

- [[perseus]] — the heroic slayer of Medusa who rescued her from the sea monster, defended her at their banquet, and wed her.
- [[medusa]] — the serpentine Gorgon whose severed head provided the deadly petrifying weapon that secured her salvation and marriage.
- [[athena]] — the Olympian goddess of wisdom who protected her husband during his quests and placed her among the stars.
- [[heracles]] — her most renowned dynastic descendant, inheriting her royal Perseid heritage through his mother and stepfather.
- [[zeus]] — the king of Mount Olympus and father of Perseus, standing as the divine patriarch of her marital family.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the classical civilization whose mythic storytellers, dramatists such as Euripides, and vase painters popularized her plight.
- [[the-iliad]] — Homer's monumental epic that references the legendary lineage of the Perseids descending from her marriage to Perseus.

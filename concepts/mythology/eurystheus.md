## summary

**Eurystheus** was the king of Tiryns, Mycenae, and the Argolid who commanded the legendary Twelve Labors performed by his cousin, the hero *[[heracles]]*. Born prematurely through the cunning interventions of *Hera*, who manipulated an oath made by *[[zeus]]*, he inherited royal primacy over the Peloponnese while the greatest warrior of myth was subordinated to his whim. His portrayal in classical literature as an insecure, cowardly despot illustrates the paradoxical inversion of true merit subjugated to institutional royal power.

## you gotta know

- Obtained the high kingship of Mycenae because *Hera* prolonged the labor of Alcmene while accelerating the birth of Eurystheus to seven months, outmaneuvering an oath sworn by *[[zeus]]*.
- Imposed the labors upon *[[heracles]]* as ritual penance ordained by the Delphic Oracle after the hero slaughtered his own children in a fit of divine madness.
- Disallowed two of the hero's triumphs on technical grounds: the Hydra because *Iolaus* used [[fire]] to cauterize the necks, and the Augean stables because *[[heracles]]* requested cattle as payment.
- Terrorized by the monstrous trophies fetched by *[[heracles]]*, leading him to commission a subterranean bronze storage jar (*pithos*) in which he cowered during royal audiences.
- Barred the champion from entering the gates of Mycenae, forcing him to exhibit his conquered monsters outside the walls while orders were conveyed through his herald *Copreus*.
- Dispatched *[[heracles]]* on increasingly perilous tasks across the known world, concluding with the harrowing mission to drag *Cerberus* out of the subterranean underworld.
- Pursued the surviving children of Heracles (*Heracleidae*) after the hero's death, demanding their expulsion from cities throughout *[[ancient-greece]]*.
- Slain in battle near the Scironian Rocks after attacking Athens during the reign of *[[theseus]]*, after which his severed head was presented to Heracles's mother, *Alcmene*.

## connections

- [[heracles]] — his divine cousin whom he subjected to twelve impossible labors intended to bring about the hero's destruction.
- [[fire]] — the cleansing element used by *Iolaus* against the Hydra, which Eurystheus cited to disqualify the second labor.
- [[zeus]] — the supreme ruler whose intended blessing for his son was intercepted by *Hera's* manipulation of Eurystheus's birth.
- [[theseus]] — the celebrated ruler of Athens who granted refuge to the persecuted *Heracleidae* and defeated Eurystheus's invading army.
- [[athena]] — the Olympian goddess whose protective presence guided *[[heracles]]* through every lethal ordeal commanded by the Mycenaean king.
- [[epimetheus]] — a mythic figure whose unthinking foolishness and administrative failure matched the petty cowardice of Eurystheus.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the mythic landscape whose political lore contrasted legitimate virtue with tyrannical hereditary authority.

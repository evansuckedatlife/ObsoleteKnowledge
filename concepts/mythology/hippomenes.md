## summary

**Hippomenes**, also identified in alternate traditions as *Melanion*, was a Boeotian suitor and hero celebrated in Greek mythology for winning the hand of the swift-footed huntress *Atalanta*. Confronted by her vow to marry only the man who could outrun her in a footrace, with immediate death the penalty for losing, he triumphed through divine stratagem rather than raw athletic prowess. Aided by the golden apples of *Aphrodite*, he distracted the huntress and secured victory, only to face a tragic transformation when they later offended the gods.

## you gotta know

- Famed for challenging *Atalanta*, a fierce virgin huntress devoted to *Artemis* who put suitors to death whenever she outran them.
- Received three magical golden apples from the goddess of love, *Aphrodite*, plucked from the sacred garden of the *Hesperides* or an orchard in *Cyprus*.
- Dropped the golden apples one by one during the race, exploiting *Atalanta*'s curiosity and desire so she repeatedly veered off course to pick them up.
- Successfully crossed the finish line first while *Atalanta* retrieved the final heavy apple, thereby sparing his own life and winning her as his bride.
- Incurred the wrath of *Aphrodite* by neglecting to give proper thanks and sacrifices to the goddess for her vital assistance in the contest.
- Desecrated a sacred temple of *Cybele* (or *Rhea*) when consumed by overwhelming divine madness and lust orchestrated as punishment by *Aphrodite*.
- Transformed alongside *Atalanta* into lions by the outraged goddess, reflecting the ancient belief that lions could never mate with each other, only with leopards.
- Bound in lion form to pull the ceremonial chariot of *Cybele* across the earth for eternity.

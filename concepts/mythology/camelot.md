## summary

**Camelot** is the legendary capital of King *Arthur's* kingdom, the idealized medieval court where chivalry, justice, and fellowship are meant to flourish. Though its precise location remains unknown and varies across retellings, Camelot symbolizes the height of knightly virtue and serves as the setting for the *Round Table* and the quests of the greatest knights. It represents not merely a place but a vision of an perfect society that ultimately cannot survive human fallibility and tragedy.

## you gotta know

- The royal seat and administrative center of *Arthur's* kingdom in medieval Arthurian legend, a place of legendary splendor and order.
- Home to the *Round Table*, where *Arthur* and his greatest knights gathered in fellowship and equality, with no head position to avoid hierarchy and jealousy.
- Court and kingdom presided over by *Arthur* with the counsel of the wizard *Merlin* in its founding, making it a center of both political and magical power.
- The setting for numerous tales of knightly adventure, courtly love, and quests for the *Holy Grail*, attracting the greatest heroes of the age.
- *Lancelot* and *Guinevere's* illicit romance, centered at Camelot, becomes the tragedy that tears the kingdom apart, as their affair violates the chivalric code.
- Described as a place of unsurpassed beauty and order, embodying the ideals of medieval chivalry, nobility, and courtly love, representing an almost impossible perfection.
- The fall of Camelot is precipitated by the revelation of Lancelot and Guinevere's affair and the resulting civil conflict, leading ultimately to Arthur's downfall and departure.
- The legend of Camelot has inspired countless literary and artistic works, representing an idealized vision of medieval kingship and knightly virtue that can never quite be sustained.

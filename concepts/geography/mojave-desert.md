## summary

The **Mojave Desert** is a hyper-arid rain-shadow ecoregion occupying southeastern *California*, southern *Nevada*, and portions of *Utah* and *Arizona* in the southwestern [[united-states]]. Defined primarily by the presence of the endemic *Joshua tree*, the desert encompasses dramatic elevation extremes, transitional ecotones between the *Great Basin* and *Sonoran* deserts, and intense seasonal temperature shifts. It holds substantial geopolitical and scientific importance as the location of *Death Valley*, major aerospace flight-testing installations, and rapid renewable solar energy developments.

## you gotta know

- Biologically delineated by the botanical range of the *Joshua tree* (*Yucca brevifolia*), an iconic arborescent monocot native almost exclusively to this arid ecoregion.
- Contains *Death Valley*, home to *Badwater Basin*, which at 282 feet below sea level is the lowest dry elevation in North America and holds the record for the highest reliably measured ambient air temperature on Earth.
- Traversed by the lower *Colorado River*, which supplies *Lake Mead* behind the historic *Hoover Dam*, providing municipal water and hydroelectric power across the region.
- Centers geographically around *Edwards Air Force Base*, the famous aerospace proving ground where *Chuck Yeager* broke the sound barrier and where numerous *NASA* Space Shuttle missions concluded.
- Encompasses *Las Vegas*, the entertainment and resort metropolis in *Nevada* that transformed a desert oasis into a global hospitality capital during the twentieth century.
- Features the dramatic tectonic geology of the *Mojave National Preserve*, including the *Kelso Dunes*, volcanic cinder cones, and extensive underground limestone caverns.
- Hosted early indigenous settlements including the *Mohave*, *Chemehuevi*, and *Serrano* peoples who utilized ancient trade networks connecting the interior desert to the Pacific coast.
- Houses massive utility-scale green energy projects, notably the *Ivanpah Solar Power Facility*, which uses extensive fields of heliostat mirrors focused on central solar thermal towers.

# Southeast Asia

## summary

**Southeast Asia** is a diverse subregion of *Asia* comprising eleven countries spanning the *Indochinese Peninsula* and the *Malay Archipelago*, with over 700 million people speaking hundreds of languages and practicing varied religions including *Buddhism*, *Islam*, *Hinduism*, and *Animism*. A historical crossroads of trade routes, great empires, and colonial powers, it remains strategically crucial for global commerce and geopolitics.

## you gotta know

- The region straddles the *Equator* and includes *Thailand*, *Vietnam*, *Cambodia*, *Laos*, *Myanmar*, *Malaysia*, *Singapore*, *Indonesia*, *Brunei*, *East Timor*, and the *Philippines*.
- The *Mekong River*, the region's largest, flows through six countries and supports vast populations dependent on its seasonal flooding and fish stocks; it is central to the region's ecology and culture.
- Historically, the region was dominated by powerful empires: the *Khmer Empire* (ruling *Cambodia*), the *Srivijaya* maritime empire, and later the *Ayutthaya* kingdom in *Thailand*; *Hindu-Buddhist* culture blended with *Islamic* influence in maritime areas.
- The region was colonized by *European* powers—*France* took *Indochina*, *Britain* *Burma* and *Malaya*, the *Netherlands* the *East Indies*, and *Spain* the *Philippines*—creating modern borders and extractive economies.
- *Vietnam* has been pivotal historically: unified in the 11th century, it resisted *Chinese* domination and *French* colonialism, and experienced devastating wars in the 20th century (with *France*, then the *U.S.*).
- The *Vietnam War* (1955–75) devastated the peninsula, killing millions and reshaping regional politics; neighboring *Cambodia* experienced further genocide under the *Khmer Rouge* (1975–79).
- Modern Southeast Asia is economically dynamic, home to rising powers like *Indonesia* and *Singapore*, and hosts critical sea lanes (the *Strait of Malacca*); regional cooperation occurs through *ASEAN* (founded 1967).

## summary

**Nairobi** is the capital and largest metropolis of Kenya, celebrated as the Green City in the Sun and the primary financial and diplomatic hub of East Africa. Founded in 1899 as a modest rail depot on the Uganda Railway, the settlement rapidly eclipsed the coastal port of *Mombasa* to become the administrative center of the British East Africa Protectorate and subsequently independent Kenya. It holds unique global distinction for hosting major United Nations headquarters and featuring a sprawling wildlife national park set directly against its gleaming downtown skyscraper skyline.

## you gotta know

- Derived its name from the Maasai phrase *Enkare Nyrobi*, which translates to *place of cool waters*, referring to the local *Nairobi River*.
- Founded in 1899 as a simple railway depot along the *Uganda Railway*, colloquially dubbed the *Lunatic Line*, which linked the port of *Mombasa* on the Indian Ocean to *Lake Victoria*.
- Replaced the historic coastal Swahili hub of *Mombasa* as the colonial capital of the British protectorate in 1907 due to its elevated, malaria-free climate.
- Experienced the *Mau Mau Rebellion* during the 1950s, which led colonial authorities to declare a state of emergency and launch *Operation Anvil* across the city to inter suspected insurgents.
- Hosts the global headquarters of the *United Nations Environment Programme* and the *United Nations Human Settlements Programme* in the leafy northern suburb of *Gigiri*, making it one of four principal UN global headquarters alongside [[new-york-city]].
- Contains *Nairobi National Park*, an extensive game reserve renowned for black rhino conservation whose open savanna plains sit against the backdrop of downtown office towers.
- Site of the devastating August 7, 1998, United States embassy bombing carried out by al-Qaeda, which occurred almost simultaneously with an attack in *Dar es Salaam*.
- Famous for prominent landmarks such as the saucer-topped *Kenyatta International Convention Centre*, the *Uhuru Park* green space defended by environmentalist *Wangari Maathai*, and the bustling informal settlement of *Kibera*.

## connections

- [[british-empire]] — established the settlement as a railway staging post before designating it the provincial capital of colonial Kenya.
- [[colonialism]] — served as the administrative core of white settler agricultural hegemony and the target of the *Mau Mau* anti-colonial uprising.
- [[world-war-i]] — functioned as a pivotal military supply base for British campaigns against German forces in neighboring East Africa.
- [[world-war-ii]] — mobilized troops, provisions, and air squadrons to combat Italian colonial forces during the East African Campaign.
- [[mozambique-channel]] — anchors the regional East African economic sphere whose oceanic trade routes extend south through the channel.
- [[mumbai]] — connected via Indian Ocean trade and the migration of thousands of South Asian railway laborers who stayed to build urban commerce.

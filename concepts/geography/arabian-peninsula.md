## summary

The **Arabian Peninsula** is the world's largest peninsula, encompassing roughly 1.25 million square miles and comprising Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait. Historically arid and sparsely populated, the peninsula is home to vast oil reserves that transformed its geopolitical significance in the twentieth century. It is also the birthplace of *Islam*, whose expansion from Mecca and Medina reshaped global civilization from the 7th century onward.

## you gotta know

- The largest peninsula on Earth; bordered by the Red Sea to the west, the Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean to the south, the Persian Gulf to the east, and the Levant to the north.
- Dominated by the *Arabian Desert* and *Rub' al Khali* (the Empty Quarter), one of the world's largest sand deserts; much of the interior receives less than four inches of annual rainfall.
- Home to *Mecca* and *Medina*, the two holiest cities in *Islam*; pilgrimage to Mecca remains one of the Five Pillars of Islamic faith.
- Inhabited historically by *Bedouin* peoples—semi-nomadic herders and traders who adapted to desert conditions and sustained trade routes including the *Silk Road*.
- Contains vast oil and natural gas reserves; Saudi Arabia alone holds roughly 17% of global proven oil reserves; petroleum wealth transformed the region from the 1930s onward.
- Central to Islamic history: the peninsula was home to Prophet Muhammad, the initial spread of Islam, and the early caliphates; remains theologically central to Islamic identity.
- Strategically positioned between Europe, Africa, and Asia; controls key maritime trade routes including the Strait of Hormuz, through which roughly one-third of global maritime oil passes.
- Linguistically united by Arabic; the Arabian Peninsula is the linguistic and cultural heartland of the Arab world.

## summary

**Beijing**, historically romanized as **Peking**, is the capital of [[china]] and one of the world's oldest and most populous megalopolises. Situated at the northern apex of the North China Plain, the city served as the imperial seat for the *Yuan*, *Ming*, and *Qing* dynasties before becoming the political center of the modern state. Renowned for monumental landmarks like the *Forbidden City*, the *Temple of Heaven*, and nearby sections of the *Great Wall*, it forms the enduring heart of Chinese governance, cultural heritage, and international diplomacy.

## you gotta know

- Functions as the political, educational, and cultural capital of [[china]], anchored along its historical north-south central axis by *Tiananmen Square* and the *Monument to the People's Heroes*.
- Encloses the *Forbidden City*, an immense imperial palace complex commissioned by the *Yongle Emperor* during the *Ming* dynasty that served as the home and ceremonial center of twenty-four emperors over five centuries.
- Hosted the 2008 Summer Olympic Games and the 2022 Winter Olympic Games, becoming the first municipality in modern Olympic history to stage both seasonal iterations of the global sporting competition.
- Guarded to the north and northwest by strategic mountain passes and heavily fortified sectors of the *Great Wall of China*, including the celebrated *Badaling*, *Mutianyu*, and *Simatai* military ramparts.
- Served as the imperial seat of *Kublai Khan* under the Mongol *Yuan* dynasty, when it was established on a grand rectilinear grid as *Dadu* and famously chronicled by the Venetian traveler *Marco Polo*.
- Marks the northern terminus of the ancient *Grand Canal*, the monumental artificial waterway engineering system constructed across successive imperial dynasties to transport tax grain and agricultural merchandise northward from the fertile Yangtze delta.
- Besieged during the anti-foreign *Boxer Rebellion* of 1900, when foreign diplomatic missions and native Christians withstood a fifty-five-day siege in the Legation Quarter until relieved by the expeditionary forces of the multinational *Eight-Nation Alliance*.
- Contains the limestone caverns of the *Zhoukoudian* archaeological complex in its southwestern mountainous outskirts, where excavations in the 1920s uncovered the prehistoric hominin fossils collectively classified as *Peking Man* (*Homo erectus pekinensis*).

## connections

- [[china]] — the East Asian nation for which the municipality has long functioned as the sovereign political, administrative, and cultural capital.
- [[ming-dynasty]] — the imperial ruling dynasty whose third emperor relocated the imperial court north from Nanjing and oversaw the construction of the imperial city.
- [[qing-dynasty]] — the Manchu-led empire that maintained the city as its imperial seat from 1644 until the fall of the monarchy in 1912.
- [[great-wall-of-china]] — the massive defensive barrier system constructed across the rugged mountain ridges flanking the city's vulnerable northern frontier.
- [[cold-war]] — the twentieth-century ideological confrontation that witnessed the Sino-Soviet split and *Richard Nixon*'s landmark 1972 diplomatic summit in the city.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the worldwide conflict during which the city suffered brutal military occupation by [[japan]] following the 1937 *Marco Polo Bridge Incident*.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial power whose expeditionary forces, allied with France, looted and destroyed the imperial *Old Summer Palace* during the *Second Opium War*.

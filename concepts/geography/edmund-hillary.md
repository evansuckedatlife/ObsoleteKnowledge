## summary

**Edmund Hillary** was a New Zealand mountaineer, polar explorer, and philanthropist who became world-renowned as the first person, alongside Sherpa *Tenzing Norgay*, to reach the summit of *Mount Everest*. Reaching the 29,032-foot crest on May 29, 1953, as part of the British Mount Everest Expedition led by *John Hunt*, Hillary achieved one of the definitive milestones in the history of mountaineering. Following this ascent, he led expeditions across Antarctica, navigated Himalayan river systems, and dedicated decades to improving education and healthcare for the *Sherpa* communities of Nepal.

## you gotta know

- Reached the summit of *Mount Everest* on May 29, 1953, ascending the formidable forty-foot vertical rock and ice prow near the apex now known internationally as the *Hillary Step*.
- News of the successful ascent reached London on June 2, 1953, coinciding with the coronation of Queen *Elizabeth II*, who immediately conferred a knighthood upon the New Zealand climber.
- Greeted his expedition teammate *George Lowe* upon descending to the South Col with the celebrated understated announcement: "Well, George, we knocked the bastard off."
- Served in the Royal New Zealand Air Force as a navigator aboard Catalina flying boats in the Pacific theatre during [[world-war-ii]] before focusing entirely on mountaineering.
- Led the New Zealand contingent of the Commonwealth Trans-Antarctic Expedition under *Vivian Fuchs*, reaching the South Pole overland in January 1958 using specially modified Ferguson farm tractors.
- Pioneered jet-boat exploration in South Asia in 1977, driving three vessels up the sacred Ganges River from the Bay of Bengal to its Himalayan headwaters in an expedition chronicled as *Ocean to Sky*.
- Founded the *Himalayan Trust* in 1960, financing and physically constructing dozens of schools, hospitals, bridges, and airfields throughout Nepal's remote Solukhumbu district.
- Appointed New Zealand's High Commissioner to [[india]] and Bangladesh in 1985, serving as a resident diplomat in New Delhi for four years.

- [[india]] — South Asian nation where Hillary prepared multiple alpine ventures, explored the sacred Ganges, and later served as High Commissioner.
- [[world-war-ii]] — global conflict during which Hillary gained operational navigational experience while serving in the Pacific theatre.
- [[australia]] — South Pacific neighbor and Commonwealth partner cooperating closely in Antarctic research and alpine exploration networks.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — historical tradition of global navigation and geographical discovery that inspired twentieth-century high-altitude and polar expeditions.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era during which dramatic polar treks and Himalayan ascents were elevated as emblems of national capability and resolve.

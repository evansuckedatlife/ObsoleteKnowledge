## summary

**Vietnam** is a Southeast Asian nation stretching along the eastern coast of Indochina, bordered by *China* to the north and Cambodia and Laos to the west. Shaped by centuries of Chinese influence, French colonialism, and Cold War conflict—most devastatingly the *Vietnam War* (1955–1975)—Vietnam embodies the geopolitics of 20th-century Asia. The *Mekong River Delta* in the south makes it one of the world's leading rice producers and a vital player in regional water diplomacy and trade.

## you gotta know

- The *Mekong Delta* in southern Vietnam is one of the world's richest agricultural regions, producing rice for both domestic consumption and export; it is Asia's rice bowl.
- Historically dominated by *China*, Vietnam resisted Chinese rule for nearly a millennium before establishing independence in 938 CE and maintaining it through a series of dynastic kingdoms.
- Colonized by *France* (1887–1954), becoming part of *French Indochina*; French rule extracted resources and instilled colonial rivalries that persisted after independence.
- The *Vietnam War* (1955–1975) was fought between the communist North (supported by the *Soviet Union* and *China*) and the non-communist South (supported by the *United States*). The war killed an estimated 2–3 million people.
- The *Tet Offensive* (1968) was a major turning point in the war, demonstrating North Vietnamese military strength and shifting American and global public opinion against the conflict.
- Unified under communist rule in 1975; since 1986, *doi moi* (economic reforms) have transformed Vietnam into a market-oriented economy while maintaining one-party political control.
- Today Vietnam is a key player in Southeast Asian geopolitics and economics, with significant agricultural output, manufacturing, and influence on regional trade and security.

## summary

The **Czech Republic**, officially known as *Czechia*, is a landlocked Central European nation bordered by *[[germany]]*, *Poland*, *Slovakia*, and *Austria*. Comprising the historical lands of *Bohemia*, *Moravia*, and Czech *Silesia*, its capital *Prague* stands as one of the continent's most enduring political and artistic capitals. The country's pivotal geopolitical position has made it a central stage for continental conflicts, ranging from the Protestant Reformation to twentieth-century struggles under totalitarianism.

## you gotta know

- Its capital, *Prague*, is celebrated for its historic Gothic, Baroque, and *[[renaissance]]* architecture, having miraculously escaped major destruction during *[[world-war-ii]]*.
- The *Elbe River* (Czech: *Labe*) originates in the northern *Krkonoše Mountains* before flowing across *[[germany]]* to empty into the North Sea near *Hamburg*.
- Erupted into continental war via the 1618 Defenestration of Prague, which directly precipitated the devastating *Thirty Years' War* across *[[europe]]*.
- Established as Czechoslovakia in 1918 following the collapse of the Austro-Hungarian Empire after *[[world-war-i]]*, championed by *Tomáš Garrigue Masaryk*.
- Betrayed in the 1938 *Munich Agreement*, in which Britain and *[[france]]* permitted Nazi annexation of the borderland *Sudetenland*.
- Witnessed the 1968 *Prague Spring*, a period of political liberalization crushed by an armed invasion orchestrated by the *[[soviet-union]]* during the *[[cold-war]]*.
- Transitioned peacefully away from communism in the 1989 *Velvet Revolution*, followed by the amicable 1993 "Velvet Divorce" with *Slovakia*.

## connections

- [[europe]] — the continental landmass where Czech lands have historically served as an intellectual crossroads.
- [[germany]] — primary western neighbour with whom it shares deep cultural, linguistic, and fluvial connections.
- [[cold-war]] — era of ideological confrontation during which the nation was trapped behind the Iron Curtain.
- [[soviet-union]] — superpower whose military forces invaded Czechoslovakia in 1968 to extinguish the Prague Spring.
- [[world-war-ii]] — catastrophic global war triggered in Central Europe following the dismemberment of the Czech state.
- [[world-war-i]] — conflict whose resolution dismantled the Habsburg monarchy and created the independent republic.
- [[renaissance]] — cultural epoch that flourished under Emperor *Rudolf II*, who made Prague an imperial center of arts.

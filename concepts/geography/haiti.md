## summary

**Haiti** is a sovereign nation occupying the western third of the island of Hispaniola in the Greater Antilles, sharing a land border with the Dominican Republic. Originally colonized by France as the brutally lucrative plantation colony of *Saint-Domingue*, it was transformed by the only successful slave revolt in world history, culminating in independence in 1804. Haiti stands as the first independent nation in Latin America and the Caribbean, the first modern state to permanently abolish slavery, and the second republic in the Americas.

## you gotta know

- Occupies mountainous terrain on Hispaniola, deriving its name from the indigenous Taíno word *Ayiti*, meaning "land of high mountains."
- Governed as a French colony under the name *Saint-Domingue*, becoming the wealthiest plantation colony in the world by producing half of Europe's coffee and sugar using hundreds of thousands of enslaved Africans.
- Sparked the Haitian Revolution in 1791 with a synchronized uprising at *Bois Caïman*, organized by spiritual leader *Dutty Boukman* and sustained by runaway slave communities.
- Led through brilliant military and diplomatic campaigns by *Toussaint Louverture*, a self-educated former slave who repelled invasions by both Britain and Spain while outmaneuvering French commissioners.
- Defeated the expeditionary army sent by *Napoleon Bonaparte* at the Battle of Vertières in 1803 under general *Jean-Jacques Dessalines*, who proclaimed independence on New Year's Day 1804.
- Endured severe international diplomatic isolation, including a decades-long refusal of recognition and an extortionate 1825 indemnity imposed by [[france]] that crippled national development.
- Built massive architectural monuments to defend its hard-won freedom, including the mountaintop *Citadelle Laferrière* and the ruined *Sans-Souci Palace* erected by King *Henri Christophe*.

## connections

- [[france]] — the colonial metropole against whose brutal plantation regime and armies the Haitian people revolted to achieve freedom.
- [[french-revolution]] — the European political upheaval whose rhetoric of universal human rights directly inspired and accelerated colonial anti-slavery unrest.
- [[colonialism]] — the global European system of imperial resource extraction and chattel slavery against which the Haitian Revolution dealt a historic blow.
- [[united-states]] — the second American republic, which feared the spread of slave revolts, delayed diplomatic recognition of Haiti until 1862, and occupied the country from 1915 to 1934.
- [[havana]] — fellow major Caribbean commercial city whose sugar planters profited immensely from the collapse of French Saint-Domingue's export trade.
- [[guyana]] — another Caribbean-connected South American territory shaped by European plantation slavery, indentured labor, and marronage.

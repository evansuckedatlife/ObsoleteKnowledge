## summary

**British Columbia**, commonly abbreviated **BC**, is the westernmost province of [[canada]], situated between the Pacific Ocean to the west and the Rocky Mountains of [[alberta]] to the east. It borders the [[united-states]] to the south along the 49th parallel and the Alaskan panhandle to the northwest. Formed from the union of mainland and Vancouver Island crown colonies before entering Canadian Confederation in 1871, the province is renowned for its alpine terrain, temperate coastal rainforests, and maritime trade. Victoria serves as the provincial capital on Vancouver Island, while the Greater Vancouver metropolitan area contains the vast majority of its population and serves as Canada's primary commercial portal to the Pacific Rim.

## you gotta know

- Victoria, situated at the southern tip of Vancouver Island, serves as the provincial capital, celebrated for the historic British Columbia Parliament Buildings, while Vancouver on the mainland is the largest city and primary commercial metropolis.
- Vancouver co-hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics alongside the alpine ski resort of Whistler, connected via the picturesque Sea to Sky Highway skirting Howe Sound.
- The Fraser River is the longest river in the province, originating near Mount Robson in the Rocky Mountains and cutting through the dramatic Fraser Canyon before meeting the Strait of Georgia south of Vancouver.
- Joined Canadian Confederation in 1871 as the sixth province under the condition that the federal government construct the Canadian Pacific Railway to physically link the Pacific coast with the eastern provinces.
- Haida Gwaii, formerly known as the Queen Charlotte Islands, is an archipelago off the northern coast celebrated for its rich indigenous Haida culture, monumental cedar totem poles at SGang Gwaay, and unique rainforest ecology.
- The Cariboo Gold Rush of the 1860s spurred rapid European settlement, prompting the construction of the Cariboo Wagon Road to boomtowns like Barkerville and expediting the formal consolidation of British colonial authority.
- The bloodless Pig War of 1859 arose over contested boundaries between British Vancouver Island authorities and United States troops on San Juan Island, eventually arbitrated by Kaiser Wilhelm I in favor of the Americans.
- The Okanagan Valley in the southern interior rain shadow is famous for its intensive fruit orchards, extensive wine country, and the legendary monster Ogopogo reputed to dwell in Okanagan Lake.

## connections

- [[alberta]] — eastern neighbor across the Rocky Mountain Continental Divide, linked by Kicking Horse Pass and Rogers Pass.
- [[canada]] — joined the confederation in 1871 on the condition that a transcontinental railway be completed within ten years.
- [[united-states]] — shares boundaries with Washington, Idaho, Montana, and Alaska, and resolved the San Juan and Alaska boundary disputes.
- [[british-empire]] — administered initially by the Hudson's Bay Company before Queen Victoria proclaimed it a crown colony in 1858.
- [[colonialism]] — marked by treaties, land disputes, and artistic traditions among First Nations like the Haida, Salish, and Kwakwaka'wakw.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — charted by Spanish mariners and British naval expeditions led by Captain James Cook and Captain George Vancouver.

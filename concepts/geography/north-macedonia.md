## summary

**North Macedonia**, officially known as the **Republic of North Macedonia** and formerly referred to diplomatically as the **FYROM** (Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia) or the **Republic of Macedonia**, is a landlocked nation located on the Balkan Peninsula in southeastern [[europe]]. The country gained independence following the peaceful dissolution of Yugoslavia in 1991, establishing its seat of government at Skopje along the Vardar River. Renowned for its ancient heritage, stunning tectonic lakes, and multiethnic society, the republic settled a historic decades-long diplomatic naming dispute with [[ancient-greece]] to secure wider European integration.

## you gotta know

- Resolved its protracted name dispute with neighboring Greece through the 2018 Prespa Agreement, which officially changed the country's constitutional name from Macedonia to North Macedonia.
- Capital city of Skopje underwent the controversial *Skopje 2014* urban redevelopment project, erecting dozens of monumental neoclassical buildings and colossal bronze equestrian statues.
- Contains the ancient Lake Ohrid, one of the oldest and deepest tectonic freshwater lakes in Europe, designated a UNESCO World Heritage site for its endemic biodiversity and medieval monasteries.
- Birthplace of Agnes Gonxha Bojaxhiu, world-renowned as Saint Mother Teresa of Calcutta, who was born to an Albanian family in Skopje in 1910 under the Ottoman Empire.
- Bordered by Serbia, Kosovo, Bulgaria, Greece, and Albania, acting as a historic crossroads connecting Central Europe with the ports of the [[mediterranean-sea]].
- Home to the ancient archaeological site of Stobi, a major urban hub under the [[roman-empire]] and early Christian episcopal seat in the province of Macedonia Salutaris.
- Emerged as an autonomous constituent republic within Josip Broz Tito's socialist federation of Yugoslavia following the turbulence of [[world-war-ii]] and the onset of the [[cold-war]].
- Experienced ethnic tensions between the Slavic Macedonian majority and ethnic Albanian minority that culminated in an armed conflict ended by the 2001 Ohrid Framework Agreement.

## connections

- [[europe]] — landlocked Balkan republic situated in the geographic heart of southeastern Europe.
- [[ancient-greece]] — southern neighbor whose cultural legacy and northern province sparked the historic naming controversy.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era during which the territory existed as the southernmost constituent republic of non-aligned Yugoslavia.
- [[roman-empire]] — imperial power that governed the regional roadway of the *Via Egnatia* crossing Macedonian territory.
- [[world-war-i]] — global conflagration during which the Macedonian Front (Salonica Front) ran across its southern boundary.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict during which local partisans fought Bulgarian and Axis occupation before joining federal Yugoslavia.
- [[mediterranean-sea]] — nearby maritime basin accessed historically via Aegean and Adriatic trade corridors.

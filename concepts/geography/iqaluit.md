## summary

**Iqaluit**, formerly known as **Frobisher Bay**, is the territorial capital and demographic center of the Canadian territory of Nunavut. Situated on the southern coast of Baffin Island along the shore of Koojesse Inlet, it serves as the administrative, cultural, and logistics hub for the eastern Canadian Arctic. Known for its rich Inuit heritage and challenging subarctic climate, the settlement anchors governance, air transport, and environmental research across northern [[canada]].

## you gotta know

- Designated as the capital of Nunavut when the territory officially came into existence on April 1, 1999, following the partition of the Northwest Territories under the Nunavut Land Claims Agreement.
- Originally named *Frobisher Bay* after English navigator *Martin Frobisher*, who sailed into the bay in 1576 while seeking the Northwest Passage for the [[british-empire]] and mistook iron pyrite for gold ore.
- Located on Baffin Island, which is the largest island in [[canada]] and the fifth-largest island in the world, opening eastward toward the northern reaches of the [[atlantic-ocean]].
- Expanded substantially during [[world-war-ii]] as an airfield codenamed *Crystal Two*, established as part of the Crimson Route to ferry American and Canadian combat aircraft to [[europe]].
- Served as a critical strategic monitoring base during the [[cold-war]], hosting a major installation on the Distant Early Warning (DEW) Line alongside an American military communication post.
- Chosen as the territorial capital in a 1995 plebiscite, in which regional voters selected the community over *Rankin Inlet*.
- Lacks any road or railway connections linking it to southern Canada, depending entirely upon commercial aviation and seasonal summer maritime sealift barges for cargo and freight.
- Renowned for its distinctive northern modernist architecture designed for permafrost and arctic gales, including the igloo-shaped *St. Jude's Anglican Cathedral* and the bright blue fiberglass *Nunavut Legislative Building*.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the sovereign nation encompassing Nunavut, with Iqaluit serving as its northernmost territorial capital.
- [[cold-war]] — the era that fueled post-war infrastructure and population growth through the Distant Early Warning radar network.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that founded the modern settlement as an allied aircraft staging station.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean basin connected to the settlement via Davis Strait and the Labrador Sea.
- [[british-empire]] — the maritime empire under whose banner Martin Frobisher originally explored the inlet in the sixteenth century.
- [[colonialism]] — the broader history of Arctic sovereignty patrols, federal administration, and Inuit relocations in northern North America.

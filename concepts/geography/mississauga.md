## summary

**Mississauga** is a major suburban city located on the shores of *Lake Ontario* in southern *Ontario*, immediately west of *Toronto*. As the seventh-most populous municipality in [[canada]], it forms an essential economic and transportation hub within the *Greater Toronto Area* and the broader *Golden Horseshoe* industrial region. It is widely renowned in civic governance for its long-serving mayor *Hazel McCallion* and as the home of *Toronto Pearson International Airport*, the country's busiest aviation gateway.

## you gotta know

- Governed for thirty-six consecutive years from 1978 to 2014 by the iconic mayor *Hazel McCallion*, who earned the widespread nickname *"Hurricane Hazel"* for her formidable political dominance and debt-free fiscal policies.
- Home to *Toronto Pearson International Airport*, the largest and busiest airport in [[canada]], handling tens of millions of international passengers and substantial trans-border cargo traffic annually.
- Site of the massive 1979 *Mississauga train derailment*, in which a freight train carrying toxic chlorine and explosive chemicals derailed, prompting the peaceful evacuation of more than two hundred thousand residents in North America's largest peacetime evacuation up to that era.
- Features the distinctive twisting residential skyscrapers known as the *Absolute World* towers, designed by architecture firm *MAD* and popularly nicknamed the *"Marilyn Monroe towers"* due to their hourglass, sinuous curvature.
- Situated on the traditional territory of the *Mississaugas of the Credit First Nation*, an *Anishinaabe* indigenous people who negotiated the *Toronto Purchase* with the [[british-empire]] in the late eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries.
- Traversed by the scenic *Credit River*, which empties into *Lake Ontario* at the historic lakeside village and recreational marina district of *Port Credit*.
- Functions as one of the largest corporate hubs in the country, hosting Canadian headquarters for numerous Fortune 500 aerospace, pharmaceutical, automotive, and technological enterprises.

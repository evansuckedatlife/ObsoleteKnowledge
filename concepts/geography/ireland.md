## summary

**Ireland** is an island nation off the western coast of Great Britain, occupying most of the island it shares with Northern Ireland. Home to one of Europe's oldest cultures, Ireland is defined by its Celtic heritage, literary tradition, and dramatic coastal landscapes. Today it is a sovereign republic and a major European economic power.

## you gotta know

- The Irish island has been inhabited since the Mesolithic period, with Celtic culture dominating from around 500 BCE, leaving a deep cultural imprint on language and mythology.
- Early Christian monasteries became centers of learning during Europe's medieval darkness, preserving classical texts and sending missionaries throughout Europe.
- Repeatedly invaded and colonized by England over centuries, beginning with Norman incursions in 1169 and hardening into direct English rule and suppression of Irish identity.
- The Great Famine (1845–1852) devastated the population, killing over one million and driving mass emigration, particularly to America, reshaping Irish society forever.
- Ireland gained independence from the British Empire in 1922 as the Irish Free State, though partition left six northern counties within the United Kingdom.
- The Irish language (Gaeilge) is experiencing cultural revival; Irish Gaelic and English are co-official languages of the republic, taught in schools nationwide.
- Dublin has emerged as a modern tech hub and cultural capital, famous for its literary legacy (Joyce, Wilde, Heaney), music, and historic Georgian architecture.
- Ireland is a member of the European Union and has achieved prosperity as a center for software and pharmaceutical companies.

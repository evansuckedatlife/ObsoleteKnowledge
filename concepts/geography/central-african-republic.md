## summary

The **Central African Republic**, officially known in French as the **République centrafricaine** and commonly abbreviated as the **CAR**, is a landlocked nation located in the geographical interior of Central Africa. Bordered by *Chad*, *Sudan*, *South Sudan*, the *Democratic Republic of the Congo*, the *Republic of the Congo*, and *Cameroon*, the state achieved its independence from *France* in 1960. Despite containing vast mineral wealth such as diamonds and uranium, the nation has experienced severe political volatility, prolonged civil strife, and dramatic institutional upheaval centered upon its capital city of *Bangui*.

## you gotta know

- Subjugated by the French empire during the Scramble for Africa in the late nineteenth century as the territory of *Ubangi-Shari* (*Oubangui-Chari*), subsequently becoming a constituent unit of *French Equatorial Africa*.
- Ruled during the 1960s and 1970s by military strongman *Jean-Bédel Bokassa*, who took power in a coup, proclaimed himself Emperor *Bokassa I*, and staged a lavish multimillion-dollar coronation in 1977 that bankrupted the national treasury.
- Landlocked completely within the African continent, with its capital and largest city, *Bangui*, situated along the northern bank of the *Ubangi River*.
- Ravaged in the twenty-first century by chronic internal warfare, notably following the 2012–2013 rebellion by the coalition known as the *Séléka*, which triggered retributive clashes with communal militias known as the *anti-balaka*.
- Endowed with substantial deposits of alluvial diamonds, gold, timber, and uranium, yet consistently ranking among the lowest countries in the world on the Human Development Index due to conflict and state collapse.
- Championed prior to sovereignty by pan-Africanist leader *Barthélemy Boganda*, who served as head of government and designed the national flag before dying in an unexplained airplane crash in 1959.
- Straddles a critical continental watershed, dividing drainage northward toward *Lake Chad* and the *Nile River* from drainage southward toward the *Congo Basin*.
- Preserves globally significant ecological habitats, including *Dzanga-Sangha Special Reserve* in the southwestern corner, home to dense populations of western lowland gorillas and forest elephants.

## connections

- [[france]] — the European colonial metropole that established the colony of Ubangi-Shari and maintained military sway post-independence.
- [[colonialism]] — the imperial era of conquest that partitioned the region into concessionary concessions that extracted rubber and ivory.
- [[cold-war]] — the global era of ideological contestation during which regional rulers balanced French patronage with eastern bloc ties.
- [[islam]] — the faith adhered to by northern communities, representing a significant component of the *Séléka* rebel coalition.
- [[christianity]] — the majority religion in the southern and western territories, from which the *anti-balaka* armed groups emerged.
- [[nile-river]] — the monumental river basin whose southwestern headwater streams trace into the northeastern borders of the country.

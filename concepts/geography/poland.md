## summary

**Poland** is a Central European sovereign nation bounded by the Baltic Sea to the north and the Sudeten and Carpathian mountain ranges to the south. Once the dominant power of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth alongside the Grand Duchy of Lithuania, it maintained one of the largest territorial realms in [[europe]] before being partitioned out of political existence in the late eighteenth century. Re-established after [[world-war-i]], the country became the primary battleground of [[world-war-ii]], suffered brutal dual occupation by Nazi [[germany]] and the [[soviet-union]], and subsequently sparked the regional unraveling of communist hegemony through the *Solidarność* movement during the [[cold-war]].

## you gotta know

- Capital and largest municipality is *Warsaw*, which was systematically razed by German forces following the 1944 Warsaw Uprising and meticulously rebuilt during the postwar era, while the southern royal capital of *Kraków* survived intact.
- Wiped off the political map of [[europe]] for 123 years following the three successive partitions of 1772, 1793, and 1795 conducted by the Russian Empire, the Kingdom of Prussia, and the Habsburg Monarchy, until regaining statehood in 1918 under *Józef Piłsudski*.
- Suffered the initial attack of [[world-war-ii]] on September 1, 1939, when German forces invaded without warning, followed on September 17 by the Soviet Red Army invading from the east in accordance with the secret protocols of the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact.
- Victim of the 1940 Katyn Massacre, in which the [[soviet-union]] secret police (*NKVD*) summarily executed nearly 22,000 captured Polish military officers, police officials, and members of the intellectual elite in Katyn Forest and other detention camps.
- Endured the darkest atrocities of the Holocaust on its occupied territory, where German authorities established notorious death camps including Auschwitz-Birkenau, Treblinka, Majdanek, and Sobibor, annihilating over three million Polish Jews.
- Sparked the peaceful collapse of Soviet satellite regimes across the Eastern Bloc through the independent trade union *Solidarność* (Solidarity), founded in 1980 at the Lenin Shipyard in *Gdańsk* by electrician *Lech Wałęsa*.
- Shifted geographically westward after [[world-war-ii]] when the Potsdam Conference moved its boundaries to the Oder-Neisse line, ceding its eastern *Kresy* borderlands to the [[soviet-union]] and acquiring former German territories in Pomerania and Silesia.
- Produced massive waves of wartime and postwar refugees, hundreds of thousands of whom migrated to North America and entered through maritime ports like [[pier-21]].

## connections

- [[europe]] — the continent in which this nation occupies the northern European Plain between the Baltic Sea and the Carpathian basin.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the devastating global conflict ignited by the German and Soviet invasions of Polish territory in September 1939.
- [[soviet-union]] — the bordering Eurasian power that annexed eastern Polish lands, executed thousands at Katyn, and imposed communist puppet governance after 1945.
- [[germany]] — the western neighbour whose militaristic expansionism initiated the partition, occupation, and near-total destruction of the Polish state.
- [[cold-war]] — the global confrontation during which the Warsaw Pact was ratified in the nation's capital, later dismantled largely by Polish civil resistance.
- [[world-war-i]] — the catastrophic war whose conclusion dissolved the partitioning empires and restored sovereign Polish independence in November 1918.
- [[pier-21]] — the Canadian immigration depot in Halifax that processed waves of Polish soldiers, political exiles, and displaced persons after 1945.

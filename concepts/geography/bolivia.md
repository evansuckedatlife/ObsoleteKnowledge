## summary

**Bolivia**, officially known as the **Plurinational State of Bolivia**, is a landlocked nation situated in the rugged heart of west-central [[south-america]]. Named in honor of the Venezuelan liberator *Simón Bolívar*, the country is defined by the stark contrasts between the towering volcanic peaks of the [[andes]], the high-altitude plateau of the *Altiplano*, and the vast tropical forests and savannas of the Amazon and Paraguay River basins. It holds enduring significance in global history and geography for its dual administrative capitals, its vibrant indigenous majority populations, and its unmatched mineral wealth spanning colonial silver to modern lithium reserves.

## you gotta know

- Features two distinct capital cities: *La Paz* functions as the de facto seat of government housing the executive and legislative branches at an elevation exceeding 3,600 meters, while *Sucre* remains the constitutional and judicial capital designated in the national constitution.
- Contains the *Salar de Uyuni*, the world's largest salt flat spanning over 10,000 square kilometers on the high *Altiplano*, renowned for its mirror-like brine surfaces and holding a massive share of the planet's identified lithium reserves.
- Anchored the economy of the Spanish Empire through the legendary silver mines of *Cerro Rico* above *Potosí*, a commercial hub that became one of the largest and wealthiest cities in the world under imperial [[colonialism]] despite immense human exploitation.
- Became entirely landlocked following its defeat in the nineteenth-century *War of the Pacific* against Chile, an enduring national grievance that led the country to retain a ceremonial navy stationed on the navigable waters of *Lake Titicaca*.
- Shares *Lake Titicaca* with neighboring Peru, celebrated as the highest commercially navigable body of water on Earth and revered in regional mythology as the sacred cradle of the Inca civilization.
- Suffered catastrophic territorial and personnel losses during the grueling 1930s *Chaco War* fought against Paraguay over disputed oil and river transport rights in the semi-arid Gran Chaco scrubland.
- Served as the focal point of a rural guerrilla uprising during the [[cold-war]] that culminated in the 1967 capture and summary execution of the Argentine Marxist revolutionary *Che Guevara* near the hamlet of *La Higuera*.

## connections

- [[south-america]] — the continent in which this landlocked Andean republic is geographically situated.
- [[andes]] — the vast mountain chain whose cordilleras frame the country and enclose its high *Altiplano*.
- [[brasilia]] — the modernist planned federal capital of Brazil, which borders the nation across its northern and eastern lowlands.
- [[spain]] — the Iberian monarchy that controlled the territory as Upper Peru before its 1825 independence.
- [[colonialism]] — the mercantile imperial system that exploited the forced labor of indigenous peoples in the mines of *Potosí*.
- [[cold-war]] — the ideological global conflict during which government forces supported by American intelligence defeated rural Marxist guerrillas.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the historical era of global maritime contact that initiated centuries of transatlantic bullion extraction.

## summary

**Iceland** is an island country located in the North [[atlantic-ocean]], situated directly on the volcanically active Mid-Atlantic Ridge just south of the Arctic Circle. First permanently settled by Norse seafarers in the late ninth century, the nation established the *Althing* in 930 CE, which stands today as one of the world's oldest parliamentary institutions. Characterized by a dynamic landscape where massive ice sheets contrast with active volcanic rifts, the country transitioned from centuries of union under the Danish crown into an independent republic during the mid-twentieth century. Today, it is renowned for its rich saga literature, geothermal energy infrastructure, and distinctive geographic isolation.

## you gotta know

- The capital and largest city is *Reykjavik*, home to the modernist *Hallgrimskirkja* church, the *Harpa* concert hall on the harbor, and the presidential residence at *Bessastadir*.
- Geologically defined by its position straddling the divergent plate boundary of the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, yielding vast geothermal energy, hot springs like *Geysir* and *Strokkur*, and the rift valley at *Thingvellir*.
- Covered extensively by glaciers, most prominently *Vatnajokull*, Europe's largest ice cap by volume, which conceals subglacial volcanoes such as *Grimsvotn* whose eruptions trigger massive glacial outburst floods known as *jokulhlaups*.
- Volcanism has repeatedly influenced global climate and aviation, notably during the deadly 1783 fissure eruption of *Laki* and the 2010 ash cloud from *Eyjafjallajokull* that halted air travel across Europe.
- The national legislature, the *Althing*, convened in 930 CE at *Thingvellir*, where the *Logsogumadur* (lawspeaker) recited the legal code from the *Logberg* (Law Rock) to gathered assemblies.
- Settled around 874 CE by Norse chieftain *Ingolfr Arnarson*, fostering a rich medieval literary culture that produced the *Icelandic Sagas* and preserved ancient mythology in the *Eddas*.
- Won the twentieth-century *Cod Wars* against [[england]], leveraging strategic Cold War naval importance to repeatedly expand its exclusive economic fishing boundaries out to two hundred nautical miles.
- The island of *Surtsey* formed off its southern coast between 1963 and 1967 through submarine volcanic eruptions, becoming an internationally protected biome for studying primary biological succession.

## connections

- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the surrounding marine basin whose *North Atlantic Current* moderates coastal climates and supports rich fishing waters.
- [[europe]] — the continent to which the island nation belongs culturally, historically, and through membership in the European Economic Area.
- [[cold-war]] — the geopolitical standoff during which the naval station at *Keflavik* monitored the strategic Greenland-Iceland-UK gap.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the conflict in which Allied forces pre-emptively occupied the island to safeguard transatlantic convoy routes from German submarines.
- [[england]] — the primary foreign rival during the four twentieth-century naval standoffs over North Atlantic fishing rights known as the *Cod Wars*.
- [[canada]] — the destination reached by medieval Norse voyagers from the island and later by nineteenth-century emigrants who founded *New Iceland*.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the historical era of oceanic voyages foreshadowed centuries earlier by Norse sailors traveling west from the island.

## summary

**Gros Morne National Park** is a Canadian federal preserve located on the rugged western coast of Newfoundland, protecting over seven hundred square miles of the *Long Range Mountains*. Celebrated as a UNESCO World Heritage site, the park offers one of the world's most dramatic terrestrial exposures of deep ocean crust and the Earth's oceanic mantle. Through its towering freshwater fjords, sheer glacier-carved cliffs, and barren alpine plateaus, the landscape serves as a premier geological textbook illustrating continental drift and tectonic plate collision.

## you gotta know

- Renowned geologically for the *Tablelands*, an arid, rust-colored plateau of exposed peridotite rock pushed up from the Earth's mantle during an ancient tectonic collision.
- Preserves a classic *ophiolite complex*, which provided groundbreaking field evidence verifying modern theories of plate tectonics and continental drift.
- Centered physiographically around *Gros Morne Mountain*, the second-highest peak on Newfoundland, whose name translates from French as *large solitary mountain* or *great gloomy hill*.
- Features *Western Brook Pond*, a landlocked, low-nutrient freshwater fjord carved by glaciers and enclosed by sheer rock precipices over two thousand feet high.
- Contains the coastal cliffs of *Green Point*, designated as the Global Boundary Stratotype Section and Point marking the boundary between the *Cambrian* and *Ordovician* geological periods.
- Encompasses part of the eroded remnants of the *Appalachian Mountain* chain, formed during the ancient tectonic assembly of the supercontinent *Pangaea*.
- Traversed along the coast by marine terraces and sea stacks shaped by post-glacial isostatic rebound and the relentless surf of the *Gulf of Saint Lawrence*.
- Inhabited historically by indigenous Maritime Archaic, Dorset, and Beothuk populations long before European fishermen, trappers, and settlers established coastal enclaves.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the federal nation that established the protected wilderness reserve in 1973 under its national parks system.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean whose sub-basin, the *Gulf of Saint Lawrence*, batters the western shoreline of the park.
- [[galapagos-islands]] — peer landmark of exceptional global natural heritage renowned for unveiling fundamental biological and geological mechanisms of Earth.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial authority that governed the colony and Dominion of Newfoundland before its twentieth-century confederation with Canada.
- [[france]] — the European power whose historic rights along the *French Shore* of western Newfoundland influenced regional toponymy and settlements.

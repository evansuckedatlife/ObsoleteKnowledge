## summary

**Vienna** is the capital of Austria and one of Europe's great imperial cities, perched on the Danube River at the crossroads of Central and Eastern Europe. For centuries it was the seat of the Habsburg Empire and one of the continent's most powerful political and cultural centers. Today it embodies the legacy of that lost empire—a city of grand palaces, classical music, and Austro-Hungarian refinement whose influence extended from Italy to Russia.

## you gotta know

- Located on the Danube River's northern bank, positioned as a bridge between Western and Eastern Europe.
- Capital of the Holy Roman Empire for centuries (from 1452 until the Empire's dissolution in 1806), then capital of the Austrian Empire and Austro-Hungarian Empire.
- The *Habsburg dynasty* ruled from Vienna for over 600 years, making it one of history's longest-reigning royal houses.
- Center of classical music's Golden Age: *Mozart*, *Beethoven*, *Brahms*, *Strauss* all composed in Vienna, which became synonymous with symphonic genius.
- The *Congress of Vienna* (1815) redrew Europe's political map after Napoleon's defeat, with Vienna's power at its zenith.
- Known for its architecture: the imperial palace of *Schönbrunn*, *St. Stephen's Cathedral*, and the *Hofburg Palace* represent centuries of artistic ambition.
- The *Austro-Hungarian Empire* was a multinational superpower whose tensions and eventual collapse triggered World War I.
- Post-empire Vienna became a center of modernist philosophy and psychology: *Sigmund Freud*, the *Vienna Circle*, *Ludwig Wittgenstein*.

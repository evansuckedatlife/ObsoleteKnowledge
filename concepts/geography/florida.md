## summary

**Florida**, widely known as the **Sunshine State**, is a low-lying peninsula in the southeastern United States bounded by the Gulf of Mexico, the Straits of Florida, and the Atlantic Ocean. First sighted and named by Spanish explorer *Juan Ponce de León* in 1513, the territory experienced centuries of colonial conflict before becoming the twenty-seventh U.S. state in 1845. Today, it ranks among the most populous states in the country, celebrated for its subtropical biodiversity, pivotal electoral history, major citrus production, and premier spaceflight facilities at *Cape Canaveral*.

## you gotta know

- Contains the settlement of *St. Augustine*, founded in 1565 by Spanish explorer *Pedro Menéndez de Avilés*, recognized as the oldest continuously inhabited European-established city in the continental United States.
- Home to the *Everglades*, a vast subtropical wetland ecosystem of sawgrass marshes and cypress domes famously popularized as the "River of Grass" by environmental advocate *Marjory Stoneman Douglas*.
- Served as the focal point of the disputed 2000 United States presidential election between *George W. Bush* and *Al Gore*, which culminated in intense legal disputes over punch-card ballots and the definitive Supreme Court decision *Bush v. Gore*.
- Serves as the historic launching ground of American human space exploration through the *Kennedy Space Center* on *Merritt Island*, which sent the Apollo missions to the Moon and launched the Space Shuttle fleet.
- Shaped by the three nineteenth-century *Seminole Wars*, in which indigenous Seminole coalitions, allied with escaped African slaves known as Black Seminoles, waged protracted guerrilla campaigns against federal removal under leaders like *Osceola*.
- The metropolis of *Miami* and the surrounding South Florida urban corridor developed into a global gateway and major center of Hispanic culture following mass migrations from the Caribbean, particularly after the 1959 Cuban Revolution and the 1980 *Mariel boatlift*.
- Terminated geographically by the *Florida Keys*, an elongated coral cay archipelago joined to the mainland by the scenic *Overseas Highway* and culminating at *Key West*.
- Subject to recurrent catastrophic meteorological events, including the devastating 1935 Labor Day hurricane, Hurricane Andrew in 1992, and Hurricane Ian in 2022.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the federal republic that acquired Florida from Spain and admitted it to statehood in 1845.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean flanking Florida's eastern coastline, dictating its maritime climate and hurricane exposure.
- [[spain]] — the European power that governed Florida across two imperial eras before relinquishing sovereignty under the Adams-Onís Treaty.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict in which Florida seceded as the third Confederate state, supplying beef and salt to southern armies.
- [[colonialism]] — the imperial rivalry that saw Spanish, British, French, and American forces battle over peninsular control and native land.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-Civil War period during which Florida was readmitted to the Union and became central to the disputed Compromise of 1877.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the historical era that initiated European cartography and settlement of the peninsula under early Spanish navigators.

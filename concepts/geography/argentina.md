## summary

**Argentina** is South America's second-largest nation by area, stretching from the Andes mountains in the west to the Atlantic coast in the east, encompassing the pampas grasslands that define its character. Known for its European immigration, political turbulence, and cultural vitality, Argentina has been both a regional power and a cautionary tale of economic mismanagement. Its distinctive tango culture, literary tradition, and geographic extremes make it a standout in Latin American studies.

## you gotta know

- Contains Mount Aconcagua, the highest peak in the Americas at 6,961 meters, located in the southern Andes on the border with Chile.
- The pampas are vast, flat grasslands that made Argentina an agricultural powerhouse, particularly for cattle ranching and grain production for export.
- Buenos Aires, Argentina's capital, is one of Latin America's most cosmopolitan cities and was shaped by massive European immigration from Italy, Spain, and Germany in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
- Argentina's economy has been volatile, marked by boom periods (especially in the early 20th century when it was one of the world's richest nations) and catastrophic collapses, most notably the 2001 financial crisis.
- Tango, the iconic music and dance form, emerged in Buenos Aires in the late 19th century and became a global symbol of Argentine culture and passion.
- Argentina experienced military dictatorships in the 20th century, most notoriously the 1976-1983 period that saw widespread human rights abuses and thousands of forced disappearances.
- The Falkland Islands, a British territory off Argentina's coast, have been a persistent point of dispute; Argentina invaded in 1982, leading to war with Britain.
- Argentina's literary tradition is among Latin America's strongest, producing figures like Jorge Luis Borges and Julio Cortázar who shaped world literature.
- The nation comprises 23 provinces plus Buenos Aires Federal District, spanning diverse regions from subtropical northeast to Patagonian south and Andean west.

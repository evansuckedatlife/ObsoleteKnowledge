## summary

The **Grand Canyon** (also known as the **Grand Canyon of the Colorado**) is an immense, steep-sided gorge carved by the Colorado River across northwestern Arizona in the [[united-states]]. Stretching approximately 277 miles in length, up to 18 miles in width, and attaining depths exceeding 6,000 feet, it exposes nearly two billion years of Earth's geological history through successive, undisturbed horizontal strata. Celebrated globally as a quintessential natural wonder, the chasm preserves rich ancestral Native American archaeology and represents a historic milestone in the American conservation movement.

## you gotta know

- Carved primarily by the persistent downcutting of the Colorado River over the last five to six million years, incising through the high elevation of the Colorado Plateau.
- Formally designated a national monument by President *Theodore Roosevelt* in 1908 before being elevated to a national park by an act of the [[united-states]] Congress in 1919.
- Stratigraphy famously reveals the *Great Unconformity*, an epic geological hiatus where Cambrian *Tapeats Sandstone* directly overlays Paleoproterozoic crystalline *Vishnu Basement Rocks*, omitting hundreds of millions of years of rock record.
- First observed by Europeans during the 1540 expedition led by the Spanish conquistador *Francisco Vázquez de Coronado*, when scouts under *García López de Cárdenas* stood upon the South Rim but were unable to descend to the water.
- The first recorded navigation through the entire gorge was accomplished in 1869 by the one-armed Civil War veteran and geologist *John Wesley Powell*, who documented the hazardous rapids in wooden dories.
- The canyon floor and surrounding plateau are the ancestral homeland of several Indigenous nations, including the *Havasupai*, *Hualapai*, *Navajo*, *Hopi*, and *Southern Paiute*, who maintain continuous spiritual and cultural ties to the land.
- Modern ecological conditions and water flows through the canyon are strictly governed by upstream discharge from *Glen Canyon Dam* and downstream impoundment near [[hoover-dam]].

## connections

- [[hoover-dam]] — concrete arch-gravity dam downstream that holds back *Lake Mead* and controls the lower Colorado River.
- [[gulf-of-california]] — historical marine destination where the Colorado River terminated prior to extensive modern water diversion.
- [[grand-coulee-dam]] — fellow monumental New Deal water management project on the Columbia River that reshaped Western hydrology.
- [[united-states]] — federal nation whose Department of the Interior administers the canyon as a premier national park.
- [[spain]] — European imperial power whose early sixteenth-century explorers were the first Westerners to document the canyon's existence.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — global era of geographical reconnaissance that propelled Spanish expeditions across the American Southwest.

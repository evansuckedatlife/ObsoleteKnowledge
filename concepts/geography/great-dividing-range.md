## summary

The **Great Dividing Range** forms a massive system of mountain ranges, plateaus, and rolling escarpments stretching more than thirty-five hundred kilometers down the entire eastern margin of the Australian landmass. Extending from the northern tip of Queensland through New South Wales and Victoria before terminating in western Victoria, it constitutes the world's fifth-longest terrestrial land-based range. This highland barrier dictates regional precipitation regimes, harbors endemic eucalyptus biomes, and acts as the crucial watershed separating coastal catchments from interior agricultural basins.

## you gotta know

- Contains the highest elevation point on the Australian continent, *Mount Kosciuszko*, situated within the subalpine *Snowy Mountains* of southern New South Wales.
- Acts as the primary drainage divide separating eastward rivers flowing directly into the Pacific Ocean from westward streams feeding the vital *Murray-Darling Basin*.
- Early European settlement at Sydney was confined along the coast for twenty-five years until the 1813 expedition of *Gregory Blaxland*, *William Lawson*, and *William Wentworth* traversed the *Blue Mountains*.
- Developed during the Carboniferous period around three hundred million years ago through protracted accretionary orogenies, then shaped into high plateaus through subsequent continental rifting.
- The southern sub-range known as the *Australian Alps* features true alpine ecosystems and hosts seasonal winter snowpack utilized for extensive hydroelectric generation in the *Snowy Mountains Scheme*.
- Runs parallel to the offshore waters of the *[[great-barrier-reef]]* along northern Queensland, intercepting moisture-laden trade winds that produce lush tropical rainforest pockets.
- Westward slopes transition into temperate savannas and pastoral downs that support national wool and wheat production along interior plains.

## connections

- [[australia]] — country whose eastern topography, climatic boundaries, and settlement history are dictated by the mountain system.
- [[british-empire]] — imperial authority that established penal colonies on coastal margins before expeditions surveyed routes through the highland passes.
- [[great-barrier-reef]] — adjacent marine wonder situated immediately offshore from the northern tropical section of the range.
- [[grassland-ecology]] — ecological regime characterizing the rain-shadowed western plains and interior downs lying beyond the divide.
- [[pacific-theater-ww2]] — regional wartime theater where coastal ranges served as crucial logistical centers and staging garrisons.
- [[colonialism]] — historical expansion through mountain gaps that dispossessed traditional Indigenous custodians across inland river valleys.

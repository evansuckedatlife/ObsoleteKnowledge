## summary

The **Palace of Westminster**, commonly known as the **Houses of Parliament**, is the meeting place of the House of Commons and the House of Lords, the bicameral legislature of the United Kingdom in [[england]]. Situated on the north bank of the River Thames in central London, the building stands as an internationally recognized icon of parliamentary democracy and constitutional governance. The present complex is a masterpiece of Perpendicular Gothic Revival architecture constructed following a catastrophic fire in 1834, embodying the height of Victorian civic design at the peak of the [[british-empire]].

## you gotta know

- Serves as the active seat of the Parliament of the United Kingdom, housing both the debating chambers of the Commons and Lords, as well as parliamentary committee rooms.
- Built largely in the nineteenth century according to the Perpendicular Gothic Revival designs of architect *Charles Barry*, with elaborate decorative interiors created by *Augustus Pugin*.
- Incorporates the medieval *Westminster Hall*, which dates back to 1097 under *William Rufus* and boasts a famed medieval timber hammerbeam roof installed during the reign of *Richard II*.
- Features the iconic *Elizabeth Tower* at its north end, which houses the great bell popularly known as *Big Ben*, alongside the taller *Victoria Tower* at the southwest corner where parliamentary archives are held.
- Replaced a rambling royal palace complex that had been largely incinerated during the Great Fire of 1834, an inferno caused by the burning of wooden exchequer tally sticks.
- Suffered extensive bomb damage during the Blitz of [[world-war-ii]], when the House of Commons chamber was completely destroyed by Luftwaffe incendiary bombs in May 1941.
- Target of the infamous *Gunpowder Plot* in 1605, when *Guy Fawkes* and fellow conspirators attempted to blow up the House of Lords during the State Opening of Parliament.

## connections

- [[england]] — the constituent nation whose ancient royal and parliamentary seat is situated here on the Thames.
- [[british-empire]] — the sprawling global dominion governed and legislated through statutes passed within these chambers.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the era of British technological and civic expansion that financed the lavish Victorian rebuilding of the complex.
- [[romanticism]] — the artistic movement whose nostalgic reverence for the medieval past spurred the building's Perpendicular Gothic styling.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict during which German air raids heavily damaged the complex and obliterated the Commons chamber.
- [[old-delhi]] — the historic capital city of the Mughal Empire whose administration was seized and placed under direct parliamentary rule from Westminster in 1858.

## summary

The **Caspian Sea** is the world's largest inland body of water, positioned at the geopolitical crossroads between Eastern [[europe]] and Central Asia. Classified hydrologically as an endorheic basin without natural oceanic outlets, it possesses both marine and lacustrine characteristics with brackish salinity. Celebrated historically for its wild beluga sturgeon caviar and modern vast petroleum and natural gas deposits, the basin forms a critical maritime hub in Eurasian transit and resource extraction.

## you gotta know

- Borders five sovereign littoral states: [[russia]], *Azerbaijan*, *Iran*, *Kazakhstan*, and *Turkmenistan*, which reached a historic legal division agreement in the 2018 *Aktau Convention*.
- Fed principally by the *Volga River*, which enters through the shallow northern basin and supplies around eighty percent of total inflow, alongside the *Ural*, *Kura*, and *Terek* rivers.
- Its southern coastline is bordered by the dramatic *Alborz Mountains* in northern Iran, historically giving the body its regional moniker, the **Mazandaran Sea**.
- Famous as the native habitat of the critically endangered *beluga sturgeon* (*Huso huso*), celebrated globally for yielding luxury black caviar, alongside the endemic *Caspian seal*.
- Major strategic urban centers along its perimeter include *Baku*, the energy capital of Azerbaijan on the *Absheron Peninsula*, alongside the Russian port city of *Astrakhan*.
- Extensive hydrocarbon extraction in fields like *Kashagan* and *Tengiz* generates substantial wealth while tying the region directly to fossil fuel pipelines and downstream [[carbon-emissions]].
- The basin experiences dramatic ecological strain from water diversion, industrial runoff, and severe evaporative water level drops exacerbated by global climate change.

## connections

- [[middle-east]] — southern littoral border along northern Iran connects the sea directly to West Asian politics.
- [[russia]] — controls the northern shores, linking the basin to internal riverine canal transport through the Volga.
- [[russian-empire]] — expanded southward across the Caucasus and Central Asia to secure complete control over Caspian commerce.
- [[soviet-union]] — previously shared dual-state legal dominion over the entire body with Iran prior to 1991.
- [[europe]] — the sea is conventionally defined as marking part of the eastern boundary separating Asia from the continent.
- [[black-sea]] — linked hydrologically and commercially to the Caspian via the *Volga-Don Canal* system.
- [[carbon-emissions]] — extensive exploitation of Caspian subsea oil and gas reserves feeds global hydrocarbon consumption and greenhouse releases.

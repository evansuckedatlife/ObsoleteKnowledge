# Caucasus

## summary

The **Caucasus** is a vast mountain range spanning the border between Europe and Asia, stretching from the *Black Sea* to the *Caspian Sea*. Extraordinarily geologically complex and culturally diverse, it is home to dozens of ethnic groups, languages, and religions in a relatively small space. The mountains have been contested between *Russia*, *Persia*, *Ottoman Empire*, and regional powers; modern geopolitics in the Caucasus remain volatile.

## you gotta know

- The Greater Caucasus range contains *Mount Elbrus* (18,510 feet), Europe's highest peak, and acts as the conventional boundary between Europe and Asia.
- Geographically, the region is divided into the Greater Caucasus (the main range), Lesser Caucasus (to the south), and the Caucasian lowlands.
- Populated by over 50 distinct ethnic groups speaking 100+ languages (Caucasian, Semitic, Indo-European, Turkic families); remarkable linguistic and cultural diversity compressed into one region.
- Russian expansion into the Caucasus began in the 16th century; the *Russo-Circassian War* (1763–1864) resulted in genocide and ethnic cleansing of the Circassian peoples.
- *Georgia* and *Azerbaijan* are two independent nations carved from the Caucasus; *Armenia*, *Abkhazia*, and *South Ossetia* also claim territory or autonomy.
- The *Caucasus conflict* (including Chechen wars, Georgian conflicts, Nagorno-Karabakh disputes) reflects overlapping claims, ethnic tensions, and great-power competition.
- The region is resource-rich (oil, natural gas) and strategically positioned; pipelines bypass *Russia*, making energy politics a flashpoint for regional conflict.

# Claude Monet

## summary

**Claude Monet** (1840–1926) was a French painter and founder of Impressionism, the revolutionary movement that prioritized light, color, and optical sensation over precise detail. Based in rural Normandy and Paris, Monet became obsessed with painting the same subjects under different conditions of light and atmosphere, most famously the Rouen Cathedral, haystacks, and water lilies. His radical approach to depicting fleeting visual impressions transformed painting into a vehicle for capturing moments of perception rather than static forms.

## you gotta know

- Born in Paris but grew up in Le Havre, where he discovered landscape painting and early Impressionist peers like Eugène Boudin.
- Pioneered the technique of *series painting*: returning obsessively to the same motif (haystacks, poplars, the Rouen Cathedral) to capture how light transforms its appearance across hours and seasons.
- His painting *Impression, Sunrise* (1872)—depicting the harbor at Le Havre at dawn—was mocked by critics; the term "Impressionism" was born as mockery but became the movement's name.
- Later life (1883 onward) centered at Giverny, where he built an elaborate Japanese garden with a water lily pond that occupied him for the final three decades.
- The water lilies series (1890s–1920s) grew increasingly abstract and immersive; these late works anticipate modernism and influenced abstract expressionists.
- Struggled with cataracts in old age, which altered his color perception and intensified the reds and oranges of his later paintings—once a medical tragedy, now celebrated as visionary.
- Allied with other Impressionists but outlasted them, continuing to innovate until his death.

## connections

- [[impressionism]] — the artistic movement he founded and exemplified, revolutionizing how Western art conceived light and perception.
- [[seine-river]] — the river he painted obsessively, especially at Rouen and in Paris.
- [[water-lilies]] — the monumental series that consumed his final decades and achieved transcendent abstraction.
- [[paris]] — his birthplace and the center of the Impressionist movement, though he retreated to Normandy.
- [[pierre-auguste-renoir]] — fellow Impressionist pioneer with whom he exhibited and shared artistic vision.
- [[japanese-aesthetics]] — Monet's Giverny garden and the water lilies reflect his passion for Japanese art and design.

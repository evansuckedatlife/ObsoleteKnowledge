## summary

**Ottawa** is the national capital of *Canada*, located in the eastern region of *Ontario* on the southern bank of the *Ottawa River*. Chosen by *Queen Victoria* as a strategic compromise between French and English populations, it developed from a rough logging encampment into the political heart of the Canadian federation. The city is renowned for its Victorian Gothic Parliament Buildings and the historic *Rideau Canal*, which transforms into the world's largest skating rink each winter.

## you gotta know

- Originally founded as *Bytown* in 1826 during the construction of the *Rideau Canal*, supervised by British military engineer *Colonel John By*.
- Selected as the permanent capital of the Province of Canada in 1857 by *Queen Victoria*, favored for its defensible inland location away from the *United States* border.
- Dominating the city skyline is *Parliament Hill*, home to the Gothic Revival *Centre Block* and the iconic *Peace Tower*, commemorating Canadian war service.
- Lies directly across the *Ottawa River* from *Gatineau*, *Quebec*, together forming the bilingual *National Capital Region*.
- The *Rideau Canal*, a UNESCO World Heritage site built to provide an alternate military supply route after the War of 1812, flows through the city centre.
- Sustained significant physical devastation during the great *Hull-Ottawa Fire* of 1900, which destroyed large swaths of residential and industrial timber districts.
- Site of the *National Gallery of Canada*, famous for housing Louise Bourgeois's monumental spider sculpture *Maman* in its outdoor plaza.
- Houses prominent scientific and cultural institutions, including the *Bank of Canada*, the *National Arts Centre*, and the central laboratory facilities of the National Research Council.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the federal nation for which it serves as the capital city and center of federal governance.
- [[ontario]] — the province in which the city is located, along its eastern boundary with Quebec.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial authority under which the city was planned, fortified, and declared capital by royal prerogative.
- [[nova-scotia]] — sister province whose delegates joined the political union governed from the capital upon confederation.
- [[united-states]] — neighbor to the south whose military threat during the nineteenth century dictated the city's inland location.
- [[france]] — nation whose linguistic and cultural legacy defines the adjacent Outaouais region across the provincial border.

## summary

**Guyana** is a sovereign nation situated on the northern coast of South America, bordered by the Atlantic Ocean to the north, Venezuela to the west, Suriname to the east, and Brazil to the south. Unique on the South American mainland for having English as its official language, it is culturally and historically tied to the Anglo-Caribbean community. Rich in dense rainforests, indigenous heritage, and substantial offshore mineral wealth, Guyana gained independence from the United Kingdom in 1966 and transitioned into a co-operative republic.

## you gotta know

- Known officially as the **Co-operative Republic of Guyana**, with its capital and principal commercial seaport situated at *Georgetown* at the mouth of the Demerara River.
- Originates geographically from an Amerindian term meaning "land of many waters," reflecting its extensive river networks including the Essequibo, Demerara, Berbice, and Corentyne.
- Features the dramatic *Kaieteur Falls* on the Potaro River, often cited as one of the world's largest single-drop waterfalls by volume of water falling over its sandstone cliff.
- Colonized initially by Dutch trading companies before formally ceding to the [[british-empire]] in 1814, becoming the crown colony of *British Guiana*.
- Transformed demographically following the abolition of slavery when British authorities brought hundreds of thousands of indentured laborers from [[india]] to work the sugar estates alongside Afro-Guyanese workers.
- Governed in the immediate post-independence era under the socialist and non-aligned leadership of *Forbes Burnham* and his rival *Cheddi Jagan*.
- Became the tragic site of the 1978 Jonestown mass murder-suicide, in which over 900 members of *Jim Jones*'s Peoples Temple cult perished at a remote jungle settlement in the country's northwest.
- Engaged in an enduring territorial dispute with Venezuela over the *Guayana Esequiba* region, which encompasses more than half of Guyana's recognized international landmass.

## connections

- [[south-america]] — the continent on whose northern Atlantic shoulder Guyana sits alongside Suriname and French Guiana.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial power that ruled British Guiana for over a century, leaving an administrative legacy of English law and cricket.
- [[india]] — the ancestral homeland of Indo-Guyanese citizens, who arrived in large numbers as indentured laborers across the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
- [[colonialism]] — the broader European system of maritime competition and plantation labor exploitation that defined the Guiana coastline.
- [[england]] — the core kingdom of the metropole that directed colonial commerce, trade tariffs, and political institutions until Guyana's 1966 independence.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean bounding Guyana's northern shores, beneath whose territorial waters massive modern petroleum reserves were recently discovered.
- [[haiti]] — a fellow member of the Caribbean Community (*CARICOM*), whose headquarters is permanently based in Guyana's capital of Georgetown.

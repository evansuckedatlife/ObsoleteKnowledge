## summary

**Arkansas** is a state in the south-central *United States*, occupying a pivotal geographic position between the *Mississippi River* to the east and the Ozark Plateau to the north. Named after the *Arkansa* people (a Siouan-speaking nation), the state encompasses diverse ecosystems from river valleys to mountains. Historically significant as a crossroads of American westward expansion, the Civil War, and Reconstruction, Arkansas remains economically tied to agriculture, timber, and the Mississippi River trade.

## you gotta know

- Located in the south-central United States; bordered by Oklahoma, Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Tennessee, Missouri, and the Mississippi River.
- Named for the *Arkansa* (or *Quapaw*) people, a Siouan-language group historically inhabiting the region; the name passed into European usage through French colonial records.
- The *Arkansas River* flows through the state eastward to meet the *Mississippi River*; the state's geography centers on river systems and their floodplains.
- Admission to the Union in 1836; slaveholding state that seceded during the *American Civil War* (1861–1865); site of significant conflict and brutal Reconstruction policies afterward.
- Contains the *Ozark Plateau* in the north and *Ouachita Mountains* in the west; the southern portions are flat Mississippi floodplain.
- Economically dependent historically on cotton cultivation (antebellum economy), timber (20th century), and river trade; modern economy diversified but agriculture and forestry remain significant.
- Home to natural resources including bauxite, natural gas, and lumber; Little Rock served as a flashpoint during the Civil Rights Movement (1957 school integration crisis).
- Forms part of the "Deep South" regionally; Arkansas culture, dialect, and history reflect Southern traditions and African American heritage.

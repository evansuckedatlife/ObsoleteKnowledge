## summary

**Climate science**, frequently pursued through the specialized planetary discipline of **climatology**, investigates the physical mechanisms, atmospheric dynamics, and long-term statistical patterns governing global weather systems. Spanning geological eras from ancient icehouse-greenhouse transitions to modern warming driven by the [[industrial-revolution]], the field synthesizes atmospheric chemistry, oceanography, and ecological feedback modeling. Understanding these complex planetary processes is essential for evaluating carbon cycles, polar cryosphere degradation, and accelerating changes across Earth's biosphere.

## you gotta know

- Ice core extraction from polar sheets in *Greenland* and *Antarctica*, such as at *Dome C* and the *Vostok Station*, yields hundreds of thousands of years of continuous atmospheric gas records.
- The Keeling Curve, established by geochemist *Charles David Keeling* at the *Mauna Loa Observatory* in 1958, provides the premier empirical benchmark tracking rising atmospheric carbon dioxide.
- Milankovitch cycles delineate how long-term orbital eccentricity, axial obliquity, and precession drive the cyclical advance and retreat of Quaternary planetary glaciations.
- The physics of the greenhouse effect was mathematically modeled by *Svante Arrhenius* in 1896, building upon earlier radiative absorption experiments by *Eunice Newton Foote* and *John Tyndall*.
- Coupled ocean-atmosphere teleconnections, prominently the *El Niño–Southern Oscillation* and the *Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation*, regulate global heat transfer across the [[atlantic-ocean]].
- The Orbis hypothesis identifies a pronounced drop in atmospheric carbon dioxide around 1610, linked to widespread agricultural abandonment and reforestation following European arrival via [[columbus]].
- Paleoclimatic reconstructions rely on proxy archives such as ocean sediment cores, fossil pollen, and dendrochronology, which has proved crucial in arid zones like the [[colorado-plateau]].
- General circulation models integrate radiative forcing estimates and dynamic feedback mechanisms to project shifts in polar vortex stability, sea level rise, and ecological disruption.

## connections

- [[industrial-revolution]] — initiated massive fossil fuel combustion that drives contemporary anthropogenic climate warming.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — hosts the vital thermohaline circulation system that redistributes equatorial warmth toward high latitudes.
- [[columbus]] — inaugurated transatlantic contact whose resulting demographic collapse contributed to early seventeenth-century atmospheric carbon sequestration.
- [[colorado-plateau]] — serves as a pivotal laboratory for dendrochronological research documenting prehistoric megadroughts and environmental stress.
- [[united-states]] — maintains influential meteorological tracking infrastructure, including long-term atmospheric monitoring observatories.
- [[europe]] — served as the historic birthplace of nineteenth-century thermodynamic physics and theoretical glacial geology.

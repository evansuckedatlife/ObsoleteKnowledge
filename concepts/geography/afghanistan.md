# Afghanistan

## summary

**Afghanistan** is a landlocked country in south-central Asia, a historical crossroads where empires have clashed and traded for millennia. Situated at the confluence of the *Silk Road*, it has been conquered by *Greeks* under *Alexander*, *Persians*, *Arabs*, *Mongols*, and *British* colonialists. Its mountainous terrain, diverse ethnic populations, and strategic position between the *Middle East*, South Asia, and Central Asia have made it perpetually contested and geopolitically significant.

## you gotta know

- The Hindu Kush and Pamir mountains dominate its geography, creating isolated valleys, harsh winters, and challenging logistics for invaders and inhabitants alike.
- The *Silk Road* passed through Afghanistan, making cities like *Samarkand* (nearby) and *Kabul* crucial trading hubs connecting East Asian, Persian, and European commerce.
- Ruled by the *Taliban* (Sunni Islamist movement) from 1996–2001; sheltered Osama bin Laden and *al-Qaeda*, leading to the 2001 U.S. invasion and two decades of foreign military presence.
- After 2021, the *Taliban* regained control; the country faces humanitarian crisis, economic collapse, and international isolation.
- Ethnically diverse: Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazaras, and Uzbeks have competing interests; tribal and regional loyalties often supersede national identity.
- Gold, lapis lazuli, and opium poppies have historically been sources of wealth and conflict; the opium trade now funds criminal networks and insurgent groups.
- The *Soviet Union* invaded in 1979 in support of a communist government, spawning a decade-long war that killed over one million and became a proxy battleground of the *Cold War*.

## connections

- [[pakistan]] — southern neighbor; Pakistan's military and intelligence services deeply involved in Afghan politics.
- [[mongolia]] — both conquered by Mongol empires; part of the Eurasian conquest narrative.
- [[iran]] — western neighbor; shares Persian linguistic and cultural ties; competing regional influence.
- [[soviet-union]] — invaded 1979; shaped modern Afghan conflict.
- [[silk-road]] — historic trade routes that enriched the region.
- [[middle-east]] — geopolitical region with which Afghanistan shares religious, ethnic, and political ties.

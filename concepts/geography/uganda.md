## summary

**Uganda** is a landlocked country in East Africa, bordered by *Kenya*, *Tanzania*, *Rwanda*, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and *South Sudan*. Straddling the equator and rich with volcanic soils and freshwater lakes—most notably *Lake Victoria*—Uganda has long been a crossroads of African trade, culture, and power. Its modern history is marked by colonialism, independence, political upheaval, and complex post-conflict recovery shaped by figures like *Idi Amin*.

## you gotta know

- A landlocked nation entirely within the watershed of the *Nile River*, which originates at *Lake Victoria* on its southern border.
- Shares the vast *Lake Victoria* with *Tanzania* and *Kenya*; the lake is the world's second-largest freshwater lake and supports millions.
- Home to the *East African Rift Valley*, characterized by dramatic escarpments, volcanic peaks, and rift lakes.
- Colonial possession of *Britain* until independence in 1962; shaped by protectorate rule and administrative divisions that prioritized resource extraction.
- Ruled by the brutal dictator *Idi Amin* from 1971–1979, whose regime killed an estimated 100,000–500,000 people and destabilized the entire region.
- Known for diverse wildlife including mountain gorillas in the southwestern forests, and savanna ecosystems that rival neighboring national parks.
- Post-conflict recovery has made it one of East Africa's more stable and economically dynamic nations, with growing agriculture, coffee exports, and tourism.
- The *Rwenzori Mountains* (Mountains of the Moon) in the southwest contain Uganda's highest peak, *Mount Stanley*, which sits on the border with the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
- Kampala is the capital and largest city, built on rolling hills overlooking *Lake Victoria*; it serves as a regional hub for East African commerce and culture.
- Culturally diverse with over 50 ethnic groups; the *Baganda* people and their kingdom represent the largest and historically most influential group, with their own traditional governance structures.

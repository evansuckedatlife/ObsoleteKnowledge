# Brazil

## summary

**Brazil** is the largest country in South America by both area and population, occupying nearly half the continent's landmass. A former Portuguese colony (1500–1822), it uniquely speaks Portuguese and absorbed millions of African slaves, creating a distinctive Afro-Portuguese culture. Its dominance of the *Amazon rainforest*, vast natural resources, and cultural influence make it a regional and global power.

## you gotta know

- The *Amazon rainforest* covers roughly 60% of the forest's total area; Brazil's sovereignty over this territory is simultaneously an ecological and geopolitical issue.
- Colonized by *Portugal* starting in 1500; developed as a sugar-producing colony dependent on enslaved labor (unlike Spanish America's emphasis on mining).
- Received roughly 46% of all African slaves transported across the Atlantic (over 4.9 million individuals), shaping Brazilian demographics, culture, language, and social inequality.
- Declared independence in 1822 under *Pedro I*; unlike Spanish American republics, Brazil retained its monarchy until 1889, creating a unique political trajectory.
- The Atlantic rainforest on its eastern seaboard is a distinct, highly biodiverse, and severely endangered ecosystem separate from the Amazon.
- *Carnival*, rooted in Portuguese Catholic tradition and Afro-Brazilian culture, is among the world's largest festivals, held annually in *Rio de Janeiro* and other cities.
- Modern *Brazil* is a middle power with significant soft power (football/soccer, music, culture); economic dependence on commodity exports (soybeans, iron ore) creates vulnerability.

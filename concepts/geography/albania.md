## summary

**Albania**, officially known in its native tongue as **Shqipëria**, is a sovereign nation situated in Southeastern [[europe]] on the Balkan Peninsula along the Adriatic and Ionian arms of the [[mediterranean-sea]]. Its capital and largest city is *Tirana*, while its deep history stretches from ancient Illyrian tribes through centuries of Ottoman rule and an intensely isolationist communist regime under *Enver Hoxha*. Today, Albania is recognized for its unique Indo-European language branch, stunning karst topography, and strategic maritime position across the Strait of Otranto directly facing [[italy]].

## you gotta know

- Its capital and largest city is *Tirana*, centered on the monumental *Skanderbeg Square*, which commemorates the nation's fifteenth-century national hero *Gjergj Kastrioti*.
- Bordered by Montenegro to the northwest, Kosovo to the northeast, North Macedonia to the east, Greece to the south, and maritime borders with [[italy]] across the Strait of Otranto.
- Governed for four decades following [[world-war-ii]] by hardline communist dictator *Enver Hoxha*, who broke relations with Yugoslavia, the [[soviet-union]], and [[china]], constructing over 173,000 concrete defensive bunkers across the country.
- Declared itself the world's first constitutionally atheist state in 1967 under *Hoxha*, banning all public and private religious observation until the fall of the regime in the early 1990s.
- Home to *Lake Ohrid* and *Lake Shkodra*, two of the oldest and deepest lakes in [[europe]], shared respectively with North Macedonia and Montenegro.
- Its national tongue, *Shqip*, forms an independent branch of the Indo-European linguistic family tree, descended from ancient Paleo-Balkan idioms such as Illyrian.
- Experienced the catastrophic 1997 pyramid scheme crisis, which sparked widespread civil unrest, armed rebellion, and United Nations military intervention to restore public order.
- The ancient archaeological site of *Butrint*, located near the southern city of *Sarandë*, preserves ruins from [[ancient-greece]] and the [[roman-empire]].

## connections

- [[mediterranean-sea]] — coastline borders both the Adriatic and Ionian basins of this vital sea.
- [[europe]] — continental landmass in which this Balkan state is located.
- [[italy]] — western maritime neighbor across the Strait of Otranto that invaded and occupied the nation in 1939.
- [[cold-war]] — historical era during which the country pursued radical ideological isolationism.
- [[soviet-union]] — ideological patron of the early Hoxha administration before the severe Soviet-Albanian split of 1961.
- [[roman-empire]] — ancient superpower that incorporated the region as the province of *Illyricum* and constructed the *Via Egnatia*.
- [[ancient-greece]] — ancient culture that founded coastal colonies such as *Epidamnos* (modern *Durrës*) and *Apollonia*.

## summary

**Prince Edward Island**, commonly abbreviated as **PEI** or **P.E.I.**, is an island province in eastern [[canada]] situated in the Gulf of Saint Lawrence within the [[atlantic-ocean]]. Renowned as the "Cradle of Confederation" for hosting the seminal 1864 *Charlottetown Conference*, the island is the smallest Canadian province by both total area and population. Beyond its constitutional legacy, the province is celebrated worldwide as the setting of *Lucy Maud Montgomery*'s 1808 novel *Anne of Green Gables* and is noted for its vivid red soil, potato agriculture, and maritime heritage.

## you gotta know

- Hosted the September 1864 *Charlottetown Conference*, where representatives of the British North American colonies first met to plan Canadian Confederation, although the island did not officially enter the union until 1873.
- Capital and largest city is *Charlottetown*, while *Summerside* serves as the second-largest incorporated city, both situated along the Northumberland Strait.
- Connected to mainland [[canada]] via New Brunswick by the 12.9-kilometer *Confederation Bridge*, which opened in 1997 across the Abegweit Passage as the world's longest bridge over ice-covered water.
- Celebrated as the pastoral setting for *Lucy Maud Montgomery*'s beloved novel *Anne of Green Gables*, centered on the fictional town of *Avonlea* and the preserved farmstead at *Cavendish*.
- Distinctive for its bright red soil and sea cliffs, caused by high concentrations of oxidized iron oxide (hematite) in the underlying Permian and Carboniferous sandstone bedrock.
- Generates a major portion of Canadian agricultural output through russet potato farming, alongside prominent aquaculture industries harvesting *Malpeque oysters* and blue mussels.
- Known originally to the native Mi'kmaq people as *Epekwitk* (meaning "cradle on the waves") and to French colonists of Acadia as *Île Saint-Jean* prior to British conquest during the Seven Years' War.
- Named in 1799 in honor of *Prince Edward, Duke of Kent*, commander-in-chief of British forces in North America and father of Queen Victoria.

## connections

- [[canada]] — smallest province in the dominion, formally joining in 1873 six years after the initial 1867 Confederation.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — surrounding oceanic basin whose Gulf of Saint Lawrence encloses the island and defines its maritime climate.
- [[quebec-city]] — destination of the October 1864 conference that built upon the diplomatic groundwork laid at *Charlottetown*.
- [[quebec]] — neighboring province across the Gulf of Saint Lawrence that originally formed part of the Saint Lawrence colonial trade network.
- [[british-empire]] — imperial power that annexed the island in 1763, renamed it in 1799, and administered its controversial absentee landlord land tenure system.
- [[colonialism]] — overarching geopolitical process that displaced the indigenous Mi'kmaq and expelled French Acadian settlers during the Great Upheaval.

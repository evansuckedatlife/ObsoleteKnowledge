## summary

**Nanjing**, historically anglicized as *Nanking*, is the capital of Jiangsu province in eastern [[china]] and one of the country's celebrated Four Great Ancient Capitals. Situated along the lower reaches of the *Yangtze River*, the city has repeatedly functioned as a national seat of political authority, cultural prestige, and economic vitality. It is universally prominent in modern history for the landmark 1842 treaty ending the First Opium War, its elevation as the capital of the Republic of China under the *Kuomintang*, and the catastrophic 1937 massacre perpetrated by the Imperial Japanese Army during the Second Sino-Japanese War.

## you gotta know

- Its name literally translates to *Southern Capital*, contrasting directly with the northern metropolis of *Beijing*.
- Served as the dynastic capital of the early *Ming dynasty* under the *Hongwu Emperor*, who constructed the colossal stone *City Wall of Nanjing* and the imperial *Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum* at the foot of *Purple Mountain*.
- Provided the site for the *Porcelain Tower of Nanjing*, a towering pagoda celebrated as one of the medieval wonders of the world before its destruction during the nineteenth-century *Taiping Rebellion*.
- Became the site of the 1842 *Treaty of Nanking*, the first of the unequal treaties between Qing-dynasty [[china]] and the [[british-empire]], which ceded Hong Kong Island and opened treaty ports.
- Chosen by the rebel leader *Hong Xiuquan* as *Tianjing* or the *Heavenly Capital* during the apocalyptic *Taiping Rebellion*, suffering immense devastation before imperial forces recaptured it in 1864.
- Established as the national capital of the Republic of China by *Sun Yat-sen* in 1912 and later confirmed during the *Nanjing Decade* under *Chiang Kai-shek*, who erected the grand *Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum*.
- Endured the horrific *Nanjing Massacre* in December 1937 following the city's fall to invading forces from [[japan]], an atrocity documented by foreign residents including *John Rabe* and *Minnie Vautrin* within the *Nanking Safety Zone*.
- Features the iconic *Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge*, a double-deck road-rail span completed in 1968 that became a major propaganda triumph of self-reliance during the [[cold-war]] era.

## connections

- [[china]] — served repeatedly as the imperial, republican, and cultural capital of the Chinese nation.
- [[world-war-ii]] — suffered one of the conflict's worst humanitarian catastrophes during the 1937 Japanese occupation.
- [[japan]] — captured the city during the Second Sino-Japanese War, sparking decades of diplomatic contention regarding war memory.
- [[british-empire]] — compelled the Qing court to sign the landmark 1842 treaty aboard the warship *HMS Cornwallis* off the city's riverbanks.
- [[cold-war]] — celebrated the construction of its monumental Yangtze River Bridge as a domestic triumph following the Sino-Soviet split.
- [[france]] — interacted diplomatically with the republican government based in the city throughout the interwar era.

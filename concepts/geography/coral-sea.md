## summary

The **Coral Sea** is a marginal sea of the South Pacific Ocean bounded by the northeastern coast of Australia, Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, and Vanuatu. Spanning nearly five million square kilometres, it is renowned for hosting the Great Barrier Reef, the largest coral reef system on Earth. The sea is also famous as the setting of the May 1942 Battle of the Coral Sea, a major strategic turning point in the Pacific theatre of the Second World War.

## you gotta know

- Bounded to the west by the coast of Queensland, [[australia]], to the north by New Guinea and the Solomon Islands, and to the east by Vanuatu and New Caledonia.
- Contains the Great Barrier Reef World Heritage area, encompassing thousands of individual reefs and coral cays supporting extraordinary marine biodiversity.
- Site of the historic Battle of the Coral Sea in May 1942, the first naval engagement in history where opposing aircraft carriers fought without visual sighting or direct surface fire.
- In the 1942 clash, allied naval forces from [[united-states]] and Australian fleets intercepted an imperial Japanese invasion fleet bound for Port Moresby, halting southward expansion.
- Marked the combat loss of the American aircraft carrier USS *Lexington* and the light Japanese carrier *Shōhō*, weakening Japanese carrier strength before the Battle of Midway.
- Characterized by warm tropical currents, frequent cyclonic activity between November and April, and complex underwater topography including deep trenches and oceanic plateaus.
- Encompassed significantly within Australia's Coral Sea Marine Park, one of the world's largest marine protected reserves designated to conserve pelagic fish and sea turtle habitats.

## connections

- [[australia]] — borders the eastern coastline of Queensland, providing ecological protection for the Great Barrier Reef and sustaining coastal fisheries.
- [[world-war-ii]] — served as the battleground for the pivotal May 1942 naval engagement that checked the southward advance of the Axis forces in Oceania.
- [[united-states]] — deployed aircraft carrier task forces under Admirals Frank Jack Fletcher and Chester W. Nimitz to counter Japanese naval offensives.
- [[japan]] — sought to isolate Australia and secure defensive perimeters across the Southwest Pacific during Operation MO.
- [[british-empire]] — framed regional defense and naval administration in Australasia and adjacent protectorates during the nineteenth and twentieth centuries.
- [[southeast-asia]] — adjoins the maritime passages connecting the Western Pacific through the Torres Strait to the Indonesian archipelago.

# Africa

## summary

**Africa** is the world's second-largest continent by area and population, home to 54 recognized nations and over one billion people of immense cultural, linguistic, and ethnic diversity. Cradled by human evolution and the birthplace of the world's oldest civilizations, Africa was subsequently subjected to European colonial partition, resource extraction, and the transatlantic slave trade—legacies that profoundly shaped its modern political and economic trajectories. Today Africa remains a continent of enormous potential, natural wealth, and geopolitical significance.

## you gotta know

- Africa is surrounded almost entirely by ocean: the *Atlantic Ocean* to the west, the *Indian Ocean* to the east, the *Mediterranean Sea* to the north, and the *Red Sea* to the northeast; only the *Suez Canal* links it to Asia by narrow waterway.
- The *Sahara Desert*, stretching across North Africa from the Atlantic to the Red Sea, is the world's largest hot desert and historically a major barrier to cross-continental contact, creating distinct North African and sub-Saharan regions.
- The *Nile River*, flowing northward through northeastern Africa into Egypt, is the world's longest river and has sustained Egyptian civilization for millennia; other major rivers include the *Congo River*, *Zambezi River*, and *Niger River*.
- Africa straddles the equator and comprises diverse climatic zones: tropical rainforests in Central Africa, savanna grasslands in East and Southern Africa, Mediterranean climates in the north, and vast deserts; *Mount Kilimanjaro* in Tanzania is Africa's highest peak.
- The transatlantic *slave trade* (15th–19th centuries) forcibly relocated approximately 12 million Africans to the Americas, devastating populations and societies and profoundly shaping global history, racial hierarchies, and modern inequality.
- European powers partitioned Africa during the *Age of Exploration* and the *Scramble for Africa* (roughly 1880–1920), establishing colonies, exploiting mineral wealth and agricultural production, and drawing arbitrary borders that persist today and underlie many modern conflicts.
- Africa is extraordinarily resource-rich: it possesses vast reserves of petroleum, gold, diamonds, copper, and other minerals; agricultural potential; and enormous freshwater reserves, yet much of this wealth has been extracted by foreign corporations and powers.
- Modern Africa comprises vast cultural diversity: over 2,000 languages are spoken, multiple religious traditions (Islam, Christianity, traditional religions, Judaism) coexist, and nations struggle with post-colonial state-building, economic development, and geopolitical positioning.

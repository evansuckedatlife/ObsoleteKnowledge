## summary

**Cape Canaveral** (briefly redesignated as **Cape Kennedy** from 1963 to 1973) is a low, barrier-island headland located in Brevard County along the central Atlantic coast of Florida in the [[united-states]]. Chosen in 1949 as the military's joint long-range missile proving ground, it quickly emerged as the epicentre of the Space Age and the primary launch complex for American crewed space exploration during the [[cold-war]]. Flanked by the Atlantic Ocean to the east and the Indian River Lagoon system to the west, it encompasses both the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station and the adjacent *Kennedy Space Center* on Merritt Island.

## you gotta know

- Selected by President Harry S. Truman in 1949 as the Joint Long Range Proving Ground, favoured for its unpopulated coastline, subtropical climate, and eastward orientation over the open [[atlantic-ocean]], which allowed rockets to drop spent stages safely into water and harness Earth's eastward rotational velocity.
- Hosted the launch of *Explorer 1*, America's first artificial satellite, on a Juno I rocket in January 1958, following the surprise Soviet launch of Sputnik.
- Served as the primary launch site for every American crewed orbital program, including Project Mercury (launching *Alan Shepard* on *Freedom 7* and *John Glenn* on *Friendship 7*), Gemini, Apollo, and the Space Shuttle fleet.
- Redesignated *Cape Kennedy* by President Lyndon B. Johnson six days after the November 1963 assassination of John F. Kennedy, an unpopular federal renaming that the Florida state legislature officially reversed in 1973 following local campaigns to restore its historic 400-year-old name.
- Named *Cabo Cañaveral* ("Cape of Reeds" or "Cape of Cane") by Spanish explorer *Juan Ponce de León* in 1513, making it one of the oldest surviving European place names on the North American continent.
- Features *Launch Complex 39* on adjacent Merritt Island, dominated by the massive 526-foot-tall Vehicle Assembly Building (*VAB*), designed to assemble the Saturn V moon rocket and Space Shuttle stack before crawler-transporters hauled them to Pads 39A and 39B.
- Witnessed the historic Apollo 11 mission departure on 16 July 1969, which carried astronauts *Neil Armstrong*, *Buzz Aldrin*, and *Michael Collins* to land on the Moon.
- Coexists with the *Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge*, an ecologically vibrant estuary providing sanctuary for West Indian manatees, sea turtles, and hundreds of migratory bird species adjacent to active rocket pads.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — serves as the country's preeminent gateway to space and historic military aerospace testing ground.
- [[cold-war]] — hosted the technological and geopolitical competition of the Space Race against the Soviet Union.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — provides the vast ocean drop zone and equatorial trajectory essential for orbital vehicle launches.
- [[soviet-union]] — rival superpower whose early orbital triumphs spurred the massive expansion of the space complex.
- [[world-war-ii]] — drew upon captured German rocketry expertise, including *Wernher von Braun*, to develop the cape's early launch vehicles.
- [[cape-of-good-hope]] — counterpart Atlantic headland celebrated in world history as a threshold of perilous exploration.

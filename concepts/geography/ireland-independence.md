## summary

The **Irish independence movement** was the political and military campaign that freed the greater part of Ireland from United Kingdom rule in the early twentieth century. Culminating in the **Irish War of Independence** (or **Anglo-Irish War**) from 1919 to 1921, the conflict pitted Irish republican guerrilla forces against British Crown forces and paramilitary police. The struggle achieved formal recognition through the Anglo-Irish Treaty, establishing the Irish Free State as a self-governing dominion and providing a pioneering model for twentieth-century anti-colonial liberation across the British Empire.

## you gotta know

- Ignited in earnest by the *Easter Rising* of 1916, when armed rebels led by *Patrick Pearse* and *James Connolly* seized Dublin landmarks such as the General Post Office and proclaimed an Irish Republic; the British execution of its leaders galvanized mass nationalist sentiment.
- The revolutionary party *Sinn Féin*, led by figures like *Éamon de Valera*, won an overwhelming landslide across Irish constituencies in the 1918 general election, boycotting the British Parliament in [[england]] to establish the revolutionary *First Dáil* in Dublin.
- The military phase, the *Irish War of Independence*, began on the same day the Dáil first convened in January 1919, when Irish Volunteers ambushed a Royal Irish Constabulary explosives shipment at *Soloheadbeg*, County Tipperary.
- The *Irish Republican Army* waged an asymmetric urban and rural guerrilla campaign orchestrated by director of intelligence *Michael Collins*, who established an elite counter-intelligence assassination unit nicknamed *The Squad*.
- British authorities deployed brutal counter-insurgency forces, notably the paramilitary *Black and Tans* and the *Auxiliary Division*, who engaged in reprisal burnings of Cork and civilian killings such as the 1920 *Bloody Sunday* massacre at Croke Park.
- Concluded with the *Anglo-Irish Treaty* signed in December 1921, which created the Irish Free State but mandated an oath of fidelity to the Crown and formalized the partition of six northern counties, triggering the tragic *Irish Civil War*.

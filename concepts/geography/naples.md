## summary

**Naples**, known in Italian as **Napoli** and originally founded by Greek settlers as **Neapolis**, is the regional capital of Campania and the third-largest municipality in [[italy]]. Situated on the scenic Gulf of Naples beside Mount Vesuvius in southern [[europe]], the city served for centuries as the thriving capital of independent southern realms, notably the Kingdom of Naples and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies. Celebrated worldwide for its distinctive culinary heritage, operatic traditions, and proximity to famous classical ruins, the metropolis remains an indispensable cultural center of the [[mediterranean-sea]].

## you gotta know

- Overlooked by Mount Vesuvius, the active stratovolcano that famously buried the Roman resort towns of Pompeii and Herculaneum in 79 CE under the [[roman-empire]].
- Celebrated as the birthplace of modern pizza, particularly the traditional pizza Margherita created to honor Queen Margherita of Savoy using tomato, mozzarella, and basil to mirror the Italian tricolor flag.
- Home to the prestigious *Teatro di San Carlo*, the world's oldest continuously active venue for public opera, inaugurated in 1737 long before La Scala in Milan.
- Founded during the eighth century BCE by colonists from [[ancient-greece]] as Parthenope, later reestablished as Neapolis ("New City"), making it one of Magna Graecia's most vital cultural outposts.
- Governed as an independent realm under Norman, Angevin, and Aragonese dynasties before spending centuries under the vice-regal administration of imperial [[spain]].
- Served as the southern pole of the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies under the House of Bourbon until conquered by Giuseppe Garibaldi's Expedition of the Thousand in 1860 during the unification of [[italy]].
- Features an extensive underground network of subterranean aqueducts, catacombs, and Greek-Roman tufa stone quarries excavated beneath the historic core.
- Preserves the celebrated miraculous relic of the liquefaction of the dried blood of its patron saint, *San Gennaro*, inside the Cathedral of the Assumption of Mary.

## connections

- [[italy]] — regional capital of Campania and the primary historic metropolis of the Italian south (*Mezzogiorno*).
- [[rome]] — major central Italian rival and political successor that absorbed Neapolitan territory into unified modern Italy.
- [[mediterranean-sea]] — major seaport and maritime hub commanding the Gulf of Naples and Tyrrhenian trade routes.
- [[roman-empire]] — prosperous cultural and resort haven where emperors and patricians built luxurious coastal villas.
- [[spain]] — imperial master whose Spanish viceroys ruled southern Italy from Naples throughout the early modern era.
- [[ancient-greece]] — founding civilization whose colonists originally established the city as Neapolis.
- [[renaissance]] — artistic crucible that fostered humanism and courtly culture under Alfonso V of Aragon.

## summary

**Kinshasa**, originally established under the colonial name **Léopoldville**, is the capital and largest metropolis of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Positioned along the southern bank of the expansive *Congo River* directly opposite the city of Brazzaville, it forms the closest pair of sovereign national capitals on Earth aside from Rome and Vatican City. Ranking as the most populous francophone urban agglomeration in the world, the megacity serves as the undisputed political, economic, intellectual, and musical center of Central Africa.

## you gotta know

- Lies immediately across the *Congo River* at the pool known as *Pool Malebo* from Brazzaville, making them the two closest national capitals separated by an international boundary.
- Hosted the historic 1974 heavyweight world championship boxing match billed as the *Rumble in the Jungle*, in which *Muhammad Ali* knocked out the heavily favored champion *George Foreman* at the *20th of May Stadium*.
- Founded in 1881 by the Welsh-American explorer *Henry Morton Stanley* as a colonial trading depot named **Léopoldville** to honor King *Leopold II* of Belgium during the brutal era of European [[colonialism]].
- Officially renamed **Kinshasa** in 1966 by authoritarian president *Mobutu Sese Seko* during his sweeping nationalist campaign of *Authenticité*, which mandated replacing European colonial toponyms with indigenous African names.
- Situated directly upstream from the treacherous *Livingstone Falls*, a series of massive cascades and cataracts that completely prevent maritime navigation downstream toward the [[atlantic-ocean]] and required the construction of the Matadi-Kinshasa Railway.
- Serves as the birthplace and global capital of *soukous* and *ndombolo* music traditions popularized by bandleaders like *Franco Luambo* and *Papa Wemba*, as well as the flamboyant designer-fashion subculture of high elegance known as *La Sape*.
- Identified by historical epidemiologists and geneticists as the early twentieth-century epicenter where the pandemic strain of the human immunodeficiency virus (*HIV-1* group M) first established human transmission networks along expanding Belgian colonial rail systems.

## connections

- [[colonialism]] — administrative center founded during King *Leopold II*'s exploitation of the Congo basin and expanded under Belgian rule.
- [[cold-war]] — strategic geopolitical prize during the tumultuous Congo Crisis that witnessed the rise of *Mobutu Sese Seko*.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — primary oceanic basin connected to the capital via the overland railway bypassing the *Congo River* rapids.
- [[france]] — leader of the international Francophone world whose capital Paris is surpassed in total French-speaking population by this African megacity.
- [[world-war-ii]] — transportation conduit for enriched uranium extracted from Katanga's Shinkolobwe mine destined for the Manhattan Project.
- [[lahore]] — fellow developing-world post-colonial metropolis with immense demographic scale and rich cultural influence.

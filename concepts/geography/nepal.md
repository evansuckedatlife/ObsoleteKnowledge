## summary

**Nepal**, officially the **Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal**, is a landlocked South Asian nation situated along the central spine of the Himalayas between [[india]] and [[china]]. Renowned for containing eight of the world's fourteen eight-thousander mountain peaks, including *Mount Everest*, the country spans dramatic elevation gradients from the subtropical southern plains of the Terai to glaciated Himalayan summits. In quiz bowl, Nepal is celebrated for its deep religious legacy as the birthplace of the Buddha at *Lumbini*, its unique non-quadrilateral national flag, the storied royal history of the *Shah dynasty*, its renowned elite *Gurkha* soldiers, and the architectural splendors of the *Kathmandu Valley*.

## you gotta know

- The capital and largest city is *Kathmandu*, situated in a highland valley that also contains the historic Newar royal cities of *Patan* and *Bhaktapur*, renowned for pagoda-style temples and UNESCO-listed Durbar squares.
- Contains the southern face and summit of *Mount Everest* (known domestically as *Sagarmatha* and across the northern border in [[china]] as *Chomolungma*), the highest mountain on Earth at 8,848.86 meters above sea level.
- Boasts eight of the fourteen worldwide mountain summits exceeding 8,000 meters, including *Kanchenjunga* on the eastern border, *Lhotse*, *Makalu*, *Cho Oyu*, *Dhaulagiri*, *Manaslu*, and the treacherous *Annapurna* massif.
- The southern lowland region, known as the *Terai*, features rich agricultural lands and biodiverse preserves such as *Chitwan National Park*, home to the greater one-horned rhinoceros and Bengal tiger, as well as the sacred site of *Lumbini*, recognized as the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama.
- Unified in the mid-eighteenth century by King *Prithvi Narayan Shah* of the *Gorkha Kingdom*, who initiated the Shah dynasty and established the legendary military traditions of the *Gurkhas*.
- Fought the Anglo-Nepalese War against the British East India Company, which concluded with the 1816 *Treaty of Sugauli*, establishing Nepal's modern territorial borders and inaugurating Gurkha recruitment into the armed forces of the [[british-empire]].
- Long dominated by the hereditary prime ministers of the *Rana dynasty* who relegated monarchs to figureheads, the country later suffered the shocking 2001 *Nepalese royal massacre* by Crown Prince *Dipendra*, followed by a Maoist civil war that culminated in the 2008 abolition of the monarchy.

## connections

- [[india]] — southern, eastern, and western neighbor that shares deep cultural, linguistic, and religious ties with Nepal across an open international border.
- [[china]] — northern neighbor bordering Nepal along the crest of the Himalayas, encompassing the Tibet Autonomous Region.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial force that clashed with Nepal in the Anglo-Nepalese War and formed enduring military ties recruiting Gurkha regiments.
- [[colonialism]] — the broader expansion of European dominion in South Asia that enveloped neighboring regions while Nepal preserved nominal sovereignty.
- [[southeast-asia]] — the neighboring Asian region connected historically through Buddhist monastic exchange, trade, and diaspora migrations.

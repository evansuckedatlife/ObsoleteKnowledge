## summary

**The Roaring Twenties** was the distinctive decade of the 1920s in the United States, marked by unprecedented prosperity, cultural exuberance, and social change. Following the end of World War I, American wealth and innovation sparked a revolution in arts, music, fashion, and urban life that defined the era. The decade dissolved into the Great Depression of 1929, making it a brief historical interlude of hedonism and artistic renaissance.

## you gotta know

- Emerged from wartime industrial boom and the prosperity of newly industrialized America.
- Characterized by speakeasies, jazz, and the *flapper* — a new kind of liberated woman with short hair, loose dresses, and social freedom.
- The *Great Gatsby* epitomizes the era's obsession with wealth, excess, and the hollowness beneath the glamour.
- Drove technological innovation: automobiles became mass-market, radio transformed communication, and electricity reshaped American homes.
- Prohibition (1920–1933) spawned organized crime and speakeasies, turning illegal drinking into a badge of modernity.
- Experienced a radical flowering of arts: *Harlem Renaissance* in music and literature, new cinema, *Art Deco* design.
- Collapsed catastrophically in October 1929 with the stock market crash that ushered in the *Great Depression*.
- Represented the final triumph of modernity over the Victorian era — a cultural break from tradition that defined the 20th century.

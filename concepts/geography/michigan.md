## summary

**Michigan**, nicknamed the **Great Lakes State** and the **Wolverine State**, is a state in the Midwestern region of the *United States* that borders *Canada*. It is the only U.S. state composed of two distinct landmasses: the industrial Lower Peninsula, shaped like a mitten, and the resource-rich, forested Upper Peninsula, connected across the Straits of Mackinac. Michigan became the global epicenter of automotive manufacturing during the *Industrial Revolution*, creating a legacy of labor activism, urban industry, and landmark musical innovation.

## you gotta know

- Composed of the Upper Peninsula (U.P.) and Lower Peninsula, which are physically joined by the five-mile-long *Mackinac Bridge* spanning the *Straits of Mackinac*.
- Borders four of the five Great Lakes—Superior, Michigan, Huron, and Erie—giving it the longest freshwater coastline of any political subdivision in the world.
- Gained its Upper Peninsula in 1836 as a congressional compromise resolving the bloodless border dispute with Ohio known as the *Toledo War*.
- Served as the historic cradle of the global automotive industry, pioneered in Detroit and surrounding towns by industrialists like *Henry Ford*, *William C. Durant*, and *Ransom E. Olds*.
- Transformed into the "Arsenal of Democracy" during [[world-war-ii]], mobilizing factories such as Ford's vast *Willow Run* plant to assemble B-24 Liberator bombers around the clock.
- Sparked groundbreaking labor rights struggles during the [[industrial-revolution]], including the pivotal 1936–1937 *Flint Sit-Down Strike* that established the *United Auto Workers* as a major collective bargaining force.
- Gave birth to the influential *Motown* sound founded by *Berry Gordy Jr.* in Detroit, producing timeless popular records by *Stevie Wonder*, *The Supremes*, and *Marvin Gaye*.
- Contains the historic car-free enclave of *Mackinac Island*, the vast dunes of *Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore*, and the remote wilderness of *Isle Royale National Park* in Lake Superior.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — sovereign nation in which the state was admitted to the Union in 1837 as the 26th state.
- [[canada]] — neighboring northern nation sharing extensive freshwater boundaries along the Detroit and St. Clair rivers.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — era of mechanization and assembly line mass production that made the state an automotive titan.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict during which the state's converted manufacturing plants earned it the title "Arsenal of Democracy."
- [[american-civil-war]] — conflict where the state sent numerous volunteer regiments, including the celebrated Michigan Cavalry Brigade led by *George Armstrong Custer*.

## summary

**Regina** is the capital city of the Canadian province of *Saskatchewan*, positioned in the vast prairie grasslands of south-central [[canada]]. Established along the modest waters of *Wascana Creek*, the settlement was originally designated by Indigenous Cree hunters as *Pile of Bones* before being rechristened in 1882 in tribute to the British sovereign, mirroring the imperial naming tradition seen in [[queensland]]. It is celebrated in national lore as the birthplace of North American social democracy, the judicial stage of the *North-West Rebellion*, and the home training base of the legendary *Royal Canadian Mounted Police*.

## you gotta know

- Originally designated by local Cree hunters as *Oskana kâ-asastêki*, commonly translated as *Pile of Bones*, in reference to massive heaps of bison skeletons accumulated from centuries of communal hunting.
- Renamed in 1882 by *Princess Louise, Duchess of Argyll*, who selected the Latin term for queen to honor her mother *Queen Victoria*, mirroring the monarchical derivation of [[queensland]].
- Functions as the historic training center and spiritual home of the *Royal Canadian Mounted Police*, hosting every recruit at the famed *Depot Division* established in the frontier era.
- Anchored by *Wascana Centre*, an expansive urban park landscaped around an artificial lake that encompasses the neoclassical *Saskatchewan Legislative Building* and the provincial university.
- Served as the judicial site for the 1885 high treason trial and hanging of Métis political leader *Louis Riel*, an event of enduring significance in the history of [[canada]].
- Endured the devastating *Regina Cyclone* on June 30, 1912, an intense F4 tornado that killed twenty-eight people and was survived by an unknown traveling actor named *Boris Karloff*.
- Staged the dramatic climax of the *On-to-Ottawa Trek* on Canada Day in 1935, when federal police clashed violently with striking workers in the *Regina Riot*.
- Witnessed the foundation of the *Co-operative Commonwealth Federation*, whose delegates drafted the landmark 1933 *Regina Manifesto* advocating democratic socialist reforms that paved the way for universal public healthcare.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the northern confederation in which the city serves as the provincial capital and legislative center of *Saskatchewan*.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial power whose colonial dominion surveyed the prairie townsite and christened it after *Queen Victoria*.
- [[queensland]] — Australian state that shares its royal titular tribute to the British sovereign *Victoria Regina*.
- [[colonialism]] — the western expansion that suppressed Indigenous autonomy, negotiated treaty agreements, and opened the prairies to European immigration.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the expansion of transcontinental railway lines by the *Canadian Pacific Railway* that dictated the settlement's geographic location.
- [[world-war-i]] — the overseas conflict in which countless prairie volunteers mobilized through local armories before deploying to trenches in [[europe]].
- [[world-war-ii]] — the wartime mobilization period during which local airfields contributed to the British Commonwealth Air Training Plan.

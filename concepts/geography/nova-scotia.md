## summary

**Nova Scotia** is one of the four Atlantic provinces of eastern *Canada*, consisting of a peninsula and *Cape Breton Island* surrounded by the *Atlantic Ocean*. Known for its deep maritime heritage and high tidal ranges along the *Bay of Fundy*, it served as a pivotal strategic theater between rival European empires in North America. Today, its capital and largest city, *Halifax*, functions as a major cultural and economic hub for maritime shipping and naval operations.

## you gotta know

- Its capital and largest city is *Halifax*, which features a massive natural harbor and was the staging ground for transatlantic convoys during major global conflicts.
- Bordered to the northwest by the *Bay of Fundy*, renowned for experiencing the highest tidal range in the world, often exceeding fifty feet.
- Originally inhabited by the Mi'kmaq people, the region became part of *Acadia* under French colonial rule before British conquest.
- The 1755 Great Upheaval, or expulsion of the Acadians, dispersed French settlers across the continent, eventually founding the Cajun culture of Louisiana.
- Connected to mainland *Canada* and neighboring *New Brunswick* solely via the narrow *Isthmus of Chignecto*.
- Site of the catastrophic 1917 *Halifax Explosion*, when the French cargo vessel *SS Mont-Blanc*, laden with munitions, collided with the Norwegian ship *SS Imo*, creating the largest human-made explosion before nuclear weapons.
- Cape Breton Island features the scenic *Cabot Trail* and was the summer home and laboratory site of inventor *Alexander Graham Bell* in Baddeck.
- Joined *New Brunswick*, *Quebec*, and *Ontario* as one of the original four founding provinces in Canadian Confederation in 1867.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the nation of which it is an eastern maritime province and founding confederation member.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean that almost entirely surrounds the province's peninsular coastline.
- [[scotland]] — the European nation whose Latin name means "New Scotland," inspiring the province's name and royal charter.
- [[british-empire]] — imperial power that established a naval citadel at *Halifax* and contested the region with *France*.
- [[france]] — former colonial power that established the settlement of *Port-Royal* and the fortress of *Louisbourg*.
- [[ontario]] — fellow founding province in 1867 confederation that forms the industrial interior of *Canada*.

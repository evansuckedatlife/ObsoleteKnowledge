## summary

**Oslo** is the capital and most populous city of Norway, situated at the northernmost terminus of the scenic Oslofjord in Northern [[europe]]. Founded in the Viking Age around the year 1040, the city was long known as **Christiania** and **Kristiania** after King *Christian IV* of Denmark-Norway rebuilt it following a devastating fire in 1624. Renowned as the historic venue for the annual awarding of the Nobel Peace Prize and the cradle of the 1993 *Oslo Accords* in international diplomacy, it represents one of Europe's major centers for maritime commerce, green urbanism, and Scandinavian culture.

## you gotta know

- Hosts the annual ceremony for the Nobel Peace Prize at *Oslo City Hall* (*Oslo rådhus*), distinguishing it from Stockholm, where the other Nobel Prizes are awarded.
- Renamed **Christiania** after *Christian IV* relocated and rebuilt the city in stone and brick beneath the walls of *Akershus Fortress* following the fire of 1624; the city adopted the spelling **Kristiania** in 1877 before officially restoring its ancient name in 1925.
- Safeguarded by the medieval fortress of *Akershus Castle*, which defended the city against multiple Swedish invasions and served as a royal residence and military base.
- Served as the secret diplomatic backdrop for the historic 1993 *Oslo Accords*, a landmark peace framework negotiated between the government of [[israel]] and the Palestine Liberation Organization under the mediation of Norwegian diplomats.
- Endured invasion and occupation during [[world-war-ii]] beginning in April 1940, during which the Norwegian collaborator *Vidkun Quisling* seized power and established a puppet regime headquartered in the capital.
- Features major artistic and cultural institutions, including the *Munch Museum*, dedicated to the expressionist painter *Edvard Munch*, and the *Vigeland Sculpture Park* inside Frogner Park, showcasing the bronze and granite works of *Gustav Vigeland*.
- Shelters famous polar exploration vessels, notably *Fridtjof Nansen* and *Roald Amundsen's* ship *Fram*, at the specialized maritime museums located on the *Bygdøy* peninsula.

## connections

- [[europe]] — the continent in whose northern Nordic region the city serves as a key economic, political, and cultural hub.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the war during which the city fell to German forces following Operation Weserübung, leading to the infamous collaborationist regime of *Vidkun Quisling*.
- [[cold-war]] — the geopolitical era in which Norway anchored the northern flank of the Western alliance, participating in European diplomatic security forums from its capital.
- [[israel]] — the Middle Eastern state that engaged in breakthrough back-channel negotiations leading to the 1993 peace accords named after the city.
- [[middle-east]] — the region profoundly impacted by the 1993 peace framework mediated and signed after secret discussions in this Norwegian capital.

## summary

The **Hudson's Bay Company** is a historic English and later British chartered corporation incorporated by royal charter in 1670 under King *Charles II*. Functioning as the de facto government over *Rupert's Land* for two centuries, the enterprise monopolized the lucrative fur trade across the vast drainage basin of Hudson Bay and extended commercial outposts into the continental interior of [[canada]]. Its extensive network of trading forts, maritime shipping routes, and frontier administration formed the foundational economic framework for British imperial dominance and territorial development across northern North America.

## you gotta know

- Granted a sweeping royal charter in 1670 bestowing commercial monopoly and administrative sovereignty over *Rupert's Land*, all lands draining into Hudson Bay.
- Established headquarters and maritime factories at river mouths on the bay, most notably *York Factory* on the Hayes River and *Fort Albany* on the Albany River.
- Engaged in an intense, violent commercial rivalry with the Montreal-based *North West Company*, culminating in the 1816 *Pemmican War* and their forced merger in 1821.
- Relied upon the *Made Beaver* token as a standard unit of trade and currency across trading posts when bartering European manufactured goods for indigenous pelts.
- Employed large numbers of *Orkneymen* from northern [[scotland]] as boatmen, labourers, and clerks suited to the harsh subarctic climate and river travel.
- Surrendered territorial sovereignty over *Rupert's Land* to the newly formed Dominion of [[canada]] in 1869 under the terms of the *Deed of Surrender*.
- Transitioned over the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries from a wilderness fur-trapping enterprise into a major Canadian department store retailer.

## connections

- [[canada]] — nation formed across the vast northern territories formerly governed and commercially organized by the corporation.
- [[british-empire]] — global imperial power that chartered the corporation to secure mercantile hegemony across North America.
- [[colonialism]] — imperial system of economic extraction, territorial claims, and institutional displacement of indigenous nations.
- [[great-slave-lake]] — northern body of water along whose shores the enterprise operated trading posts such as Fort Resolution.
- [[england]] — realm where the founding royal charter was issued to Prince *Rupert* and London merchant investors.
- [[scotland]] — source of generations of hardy voyageur recruits, clerks, and highland factors who staffed trading posts.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — navigational era that opened Hudson Strait and interior waterways to European mercantilism.

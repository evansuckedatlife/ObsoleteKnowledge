## summary

The **Great Barrier Reef** is the planet's most expansive coral reef ecosystem, encompassing nearly three thousand individual reefs and hundreds of continental islands off the northeastern coast of Queensland. Spanning across hundreds of thousands of square kilometers within the Coral Sea, it represents the largest individual biological structure fabricated entirely by living organisms. Recognized internationally as a critical global biodiversity sanctuary, it serves as a central barometer for marine conservation and anthropogenic climate impacts.

## you gotta know

- Visible from Earth orbit due to its gargantuan expanse, stretching more than twenty-three hundred kilometers along the northeastern Australian continental shelf.
- Composed of calcium carbonate structures secreted over millennia by colonies of tiny marine organisms known as scleractinian or stony coral polyps.
- Navigated by British cartographer Captain *James Cook*, whose flagship *HMS Endeavour* famously ran aground on its jagged labyrinths in 1770 during his first Pacific voyage.
- Sustains mutualistic symbioses between coral animals and photosynthetic dinoflagellates known as *zooxanthellae*, which supply hosts with vital metabolic energy and vivid pigmentation.
- Suffers from recurring mass coral bleaching events driven by marine heatwaves, where thermal stress causes corals to expel their symbiotic algae, leaving bare white skeletons.
- Menaced ecologically by population outbreaks of the corallivorous *crown-of-thorns starfish* (*Acanthaster planci*) alongside coastal agricultural pesticide and sediment runoff.
- Designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981, providing critical marine habitats for endangered green sea turtles, dugongs, and thousands of teleost fish species.
- The surrounding waters became the operational setting for the pivotal May 1942 naval engagement known as the *Battle of the Coral Sea* during *[[world-war-ii]]*.

## connections

- [[australia]] — commonwealth whose northeastern Queensland coastline borders and politically protects the marine marine park.
- [[british-empire]] — maritime power whose naval surveying vessels charted the dangerous passage through the inner reef barrier.
- [[pacific-theater-ww2]] — major combat theater featuring aircraft carrier clashes in adjacent waters during the defense of Port Moresby.
- [[great-dividing-range]] — terrestrial mountain backbone running parallel along the mainland coast whose river catchments drain into the reef lagoon.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — historical period that brought eighteenth-century European hydrographers into direct contact with the outer shoals.
- [[world-war-ii]] — global conflict encompassing naval maneuvering and defensive fortifications constructed across offshore coral cays.
- [[grassland-ecology]] — adjacent terrestrial biome whose livestock grazing and seasonal runoff directly influence nearshore marine water quality.

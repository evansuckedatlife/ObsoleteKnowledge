## summary

A **desert** is a barren biome characterized by hyper-arid climatic conditions, receiving typically less than 250 millimeters of precipitation annually. Spanning vast continental interiors, rain shadows, and polar expanses across the planet, these hostile landscapes drive extreme ecological adaptations and shaped early human migration. In quiz bowl, deserts frequently appear in questions on rain shadows, unique mineral formations, and ancient civilizations surviving along river corridors.

## you gotta know

- Defined strictly by aridity rather than temperature, meaning polar expanses like *Antarctica* and *Greenland* rank as the world's largest deserts.
- The *Sahara* in *North Africa* represents the largest subtropical hot desert, historically traversed by trans-Saharan salt and gold trading caravans connected to *[[egypt]]*.
- The *Outback* covering the vast majority of *[[australia]]* features extreme red-dune topography, including the *Great Victoria Desert* and sacred monoliths like *Uluru*.
- The *Atacama* in *[[south-america]]*, sheltered between the Chilean Coast Range and the *[[andes]]*, is recognized as the driest non-polar desert on Earth.
- Often formed by atmospheric Hadley cell circulation at subtropical latitudes or by rain shadow effects behind immense mountain barriers.
- Crucial desert river systems like the *[[nile-river]]* enabled flourishing riverine kingdoms by depositing fertile silt across otherwise uninhabitable terrain.
- Vulnerable to desertification driven by climate shifts and overgrazing, directly affecting pastoral societies across the *[[middle-east]]* and the African Sahel.

## connections

- [[australia]] — continental landmass predominantly covered by arid scrub and iconic interior sand deserts.
- [[egypt]] — historic civilization sustained entirely by a river oasis cutting through the hyper-arid eastern *Sahara*.
- [[middle-east]] — region defined by extensive hyper-arid landforms, such as the *Rub' al Khali* or Empty Quarter.
- [[nile-river]] — vital fluvial lifeline allowing urban civilization to flourish within an immense desert corridor.
- [[andes]] — massive cordillera responsible for creating the rain shadow of the hyper-arid *Atacama*.
- [[south-america]] — continent containing both hyper-arid coastal deserts and cold high-altitude plateaus.
- [[deep-south]] — humid subtropical region whose lush agricultural climate directly contrasts with arid desert zones.

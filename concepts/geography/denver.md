## summary

**Denver** (widely celebrated as the **Mile High City**) is the capital and largest city of Colorado, situated at the western edge of the Great Plains immediately east of the Front Range of the Rocky Mountains. Originating in 1858 as a provisional mining camp during the *Pikes Peak Gold Rush*, the settlement expanded at the junction of *Cherry Creek* and the *South Platte River* to become the financial, commercial, and transportation hub of the American West. The city is defined geographically by its high elevation, serving as a gateway to iconic alpine recreation, national parks, and ski resorts. In the modern era, Denver anchors a vibrant urban corridor sustained by federal administrative agencies, aerospace manufacturing, renewable energy research, and telecommunications technology.

## you gotta know

- Prominently known as the *Mile High City* because its official municipal elevation sits exactly 5,280 feet above sea level, famously marked on the steps of the Colorado State Capitol.
- Established in November 1858 by land speculator *William Larimer*, who named the fledgling settlement in honor of Kansas Territorial Governor *James W. Denver* to gain political patronage.
- Earned the unique historical distinction of being the only host city ever to reject the Olympic Games after being awarded the 1976 Winter Olympics, which voters rejected in a statewide ballot.
- Situated at the historic confluence of the *South Platte River* and *Cherry Creek*, which provided essential water resources for early miners and indigenous Arapaho traders.
- Secured long-term prosperity in 1870 through the completion of the *Denver Pacific Railway*, linking the isolated settlement to the First Transcontinental Railroad at Cheyenne, Wyoming.
- Features *Red Rocks Amphitheatre*, a renowned outdoor music venue built into monumental red sandstone geological formations in the nearby foothills of Morrison.
- Served by *Denver International Airport* (*DIA*), the largest airport by land area in the [[united-states]], renowned for its distinctive white fabric roof evoking the Rocky Mountains.
- Houses the historic *Denver Mint*, established in 1863, which remains one of the largest coin-manufacturing facilities in the world, stamping coinage with the iconic *D* mint mark.

- [[industrial-revolution]] — rapidly industrialized following the arrival of transcontinental rail links that connected regional mineral wealth to national markets.

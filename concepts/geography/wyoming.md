## summary

**Wyoming**, nicknamed the **Equality State** and the **Cowboy State**, is a landlocked western state in the [[united-states]] characterized by the dramatic convergence of the *Great Plains* and the *Rocky Mountains*. Admitted to the Union in 1890 as the forty-fourth state, it ranks as the least populous state in the nation despite encompassing vast expanses of rugged wilderness, high-altitude plateaus, and mineral-rich basins. In historical and cultural memory, it is celebrated as the crucible of the American frontier, home to the nation's premier national parks, and a political pioneer in extending universal suffrage to women.

## you gotta know

- Boasts the lowest total population of any state in the [[united-states]], alongside the second-lowest population density after Alaska, with its state capital and most populous city established at *Cheyenne*.
- Houses the vast majority of *Yellowstone National Park*, established by President *Ulysses S. Grant* in 1872 as the world's first national park, renowned for geothermal features like the *Old Faithful* geyser and the *Grand Prismatic Spring*.
- Contains *Grand Teton National Park*, which preserves the towering peaks of the *Teton Range* rising abruptly above the valley of *Jackson Hole*.
- Earned the official moniker **Equality State** because the Wyoming Territory granted women unconditional suffrage and the right to hold public office in 1869, later electing *Nellie Tayloe Ross* in 1924 as the first woman governor in American history.
- Features *Devils Tower*, a monolithic igneous intrusion that was proclaimed the first United States National Monument by *Theodore Roosevelt* in 1906.
- Encompasses *South Pass*, the broad, gentle saddle across the *Continental Divide* through the *Wind River Range* that served as the indispensable transit gateway for pioneers on the *Oregon Trail*, *California Trail*, and *Mormon Trail*.
- Dominates domestic energy production through massive sub-bituminous coal extraction in the *Powder River Basin*, alongside substantial reserves of crude oil, natural gas, trona, and uranium.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — sovereign nation within which this western state was admitted to the Union on July 10, 1890.
- [[yukon]] — fellow northern Cordilleran frontier territory renowned for resource booms, rugged mountain wilderness, and dramatic gold and mineral stampedes.
- [[rocky-mountains]] — major continental mountain system that defines the state's central and western topography via the *Tetons*, *Absarokas*, *Bighorns*, and *Wind River Range*.
- [[great-plains]] — expansive interior grasslands that cover the eastern third of the state and supported historic cattle ranching.
- [[canada]] — northern neighbor linked via continuous mountain wildlife corridors and shared conservation initiatives across the northern Cordillera.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — era of mechanized rail expansion and manufacturing that drove intense exploitation of the state's coal and mineral wealth.
- [[american-civil-war]] — conflict whose aftermath accelerated the construction of the *Transcontinental Railroad* through southern Wyoming along the route of the *Union Pacific*.

## summary

**The Maple Leaf** is an international passenger rail service operated jointly by Amtrak and VIA Rail Canada connecting Pennsylvania Station in `[[new-york-city]]` with Union Station in Toronto, Ontario. Traversing upstate New York, the Niagara River frontier, and southern Ontario, the service provides an essential overland link between the northeastern `[[united-states]]` and central `[[canada]]`. Traversing the scenic Hudson River Valley, the Mohawk River valley, and the Niagara gorge, the train serves as a vital corridor for business, cross-border tourism, and regional passenger transit.

## you gotta know

- Operates daily over a 544-mile route between Moynihan Train Hall at Pennsylvania Station in `[[new-york-city]]` and Toronto Union Station, completing the journey in approximately twelve and a half hours.
- Run as a collaborative international operation where an Amtrak crew operates the train south of the border and a VIA Rail crew assumes control between the international boundary and Toronto.
- Crosses the international frontier over the Niagara River via the Whirlpool Rapids Bridge, where passengers detrain with luggage for customs and immigration inspection.
- Travels along the *Empire Corridor* across New York State, serving major upstate urban centers including Albany-Rensselaer, Schenectady, Utica, Syracuse, Rochester, and Buffalo Exchange Street.
- On the Canadian side of the border, serves southern Ontario communities in the Golden Horseshoe region, including Niagara Falls, St. Catharines, Grimsby, and Oakville, before arriving in downtown Toronto.
- Named after the national arboreal emblem of `[[canada]]`, sharing its heritage with earlier namesake trains run by the Grand Trunk Railway and the Lehigh Valley Railroad during the `[[industrial-revolution]]`.
- Restored to daily operation in 1981 through bilateral negotiations between Amtrak and the Province of Ontario, reviving direct rail passenger connectivity between New York and Ontario after a multi-year lapse.
- Provides direct transfers at Toronto Union Station to transcontinental Canadian rail lines like VIA Rail's *The Canadian*, which carries passengers westward across the prairies through `[[winnipeg]]` to `[[vancouver]]`.

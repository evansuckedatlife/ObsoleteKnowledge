## summary

The **Colorado Plateau**, frequently categorized as the **Colorado Plateaus Province**, is a vast, high-altitude physiographic region encompassing approximately 130,000 square miles around the Four Corners in the western [[united-states]]. Distinguished by thick, uplifted sedimentary rock layers that remained remarkably undeformed during regional mountain-building events, the plateau has been incised into labyrinthine gorges by the *Colorado River* and its tributaries. Celebrated for its unique topography, canyon networks, and Indigenous heritage, the region serves as a premier terrestrial laboratory for geological, hydrological, and environmental study.

## you gotta know

- Covers extensive parts of *Utah*, *Arizona*, *New Mexico*, and *Colorado*, converging directly at the iconic *Four Corners* boundary monument.
- Boasts the highest density of protected federal parklands in North America, including the *Grand Canyon*, *Zion*, *Bryce Canyon*, *Arches*, and *Mesa Verde*.
- Rose dramatically as a single tectonic block during the Laramide Orogeny and subsequent Cenozoic uplifts without undergoing the severe crustal fracturing seen in the surrounding provinces.
- Features billions of years of exposed stratigraphy carved out by drainage incision, famously revealing the Great Unconformity where ancient basement rocks meet Paleozoic strata.
- Preserves elaborate stone architecture of the *Ancestral Puebloans*, including the sophisticated cliff dwellings of *Mesa Verde* and great houses of *Chaco Canyon*.
- Houses the hydrological lifeline of the American Southwest through the *Colorado River*, whose allocated waters are increasingly depleted by regional aridification.
- Provided the archaeological and biological wood specimens that enabled *A. E. Douglass* to establish the science of dendrochronology through the study of annual tree rings.
- Informs modern [[climate-science]] investigations evaluating historic multi-decade megadroughts, biological soil crust degradation, and changing moisture patterns.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — sovereign territory whose arid southwestern interior encompasses the geographic boundaries of the plateau.
- [[climate-science]] — relies on the plateau's living bristlecone pines and archaeological timbers to reconstruct ancient climatic conditions.
- [[colonialism]] — reshaped Indigenous territorial autonomy across the plateau through nineteenth-century federal treaties and mineral extraction.
- [[columbus]] — initiated the transatlantic contact that brought Spanish horses, livestock grazing, and European settlement onto the plateau.
- [[american-literature]] — inspired iconic environmental and regional writers including *John Wesley Powell*, *Edward Abbey*, and *Willa Cather*.
- [[mississippi-river]] — anchors the contrasting major watershed lying east of the Continental Divide beyond the Rocky Mountains.

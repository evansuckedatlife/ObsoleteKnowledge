## summary

**Liberia**, officially the **Republic of Liberia**, is a coastal nation located along the Upper Guinea coast of West Africa bordering the [[atlantic-ocean]], Sierra Leone, Guinea, and Ivory Coast. Established in the early nineteenth century through settlement projects sponsored by the *American Colonization Society*, it declared its independence in 1847, making it Africa's oldest modern republic. Its political trajectory, unique national identity, and historic social dynamics were deeply shaped by repatriated African American and freed Caribbean emigrants alongside diverse indigenous populations.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1822 as an organized settlement for free-born Black people and formerly enslaved people from the [[united-states]], orchestrated by the *American Colonization Society*.
- Declared sovereign independence on July 26, 1847, adopting a constitution and governance structure heavily modeled upon the constitutional framework of the [[united-states]].
- Chose its capital city name, *Monrovia*, in honor of U.S. President *James Monroe*, a prominent supporter of the colonization movement who secured congressional backing for the colony.
- Managed to preserve its national sovereignty alongside Ethiopia during the late-nineteenth-century Scramble for Africa, avoiding direct annexation under European [[colonialism]].
- Dominated politically for well over a century by the *True Whig Party*, which entrenched the authority of the Americo-Liberian minority over the indigenous majority until a violent military coup in 1980.
- Endured two devastating consecutive civil wars between 1989 and 2003 led by warlords like *Charles Taylor*, resulting in widespread destruction and deep humanitarian crises.
- Made African political history in 2005 by electing *Ellen Johnson Sirleaf*, who took office in 2006 as the African continent's first democratically elected female head of state.

## connections

- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean that forms the entire southern and southwestern coastline of the country.
- [[united-states]] — the nation where the *American Colonization Society* originated and from which freed settlers emigrated.
- [[colonialism]] — the European imperial partition of Africa that surrounded the republic while it maintained nominal independence.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict over slavery in North America that deeply influenced subsequent African emigration debates.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-Civil War period in the U.S. that coincided with continued diplomatic shifts regarding the republic.
- [[british-empire]] — the neighboring colonial power in Sierra Leone with which early border disputes frequently arose.

## summary

**Mumbai**, formerly and still colloquially known as *Bombay*, is the capital of the Indian state of Maharashtra and the preeminent commercial, financial, and entertainment capital of [[india]]. Originally an archipelago of seven marshy islands inhabited by indigenous Koli fishermen, the settlement was transformed through centuries of land reclamation into a sprawling peninsula jutting into the Arabian Sea. Renowned for its deep natural harbor, the vibrant Hindi film industry known as *Bollywood*, and striking Victorian Gothic architecture, the metropolis stands as one of the most populous urban areas on Earth.

## you gotta know

- Originally comprised the *Seven Islands of Bombay*, which were gradually unified through expansive land reclamation projects including the eighteenth-century *Hornby Vellard*.
- Transferred from Portuguese control to King *Charles II* of [[england]] in 1661 as part of the royal wedding dowry of *Catherine of Braganza*, who subsequently leased it to the *British East India Company*.
- Takes its contemporary official name from the patron goddess *Mumbadevi*, an incarnation of the mother goddess worshipped by the local *Koli* community.
- Boomed dramatically during the *American Civil War* in the 1860s, as the Union naval blockade of Confederate ports compelled British textile mills to import vast quantities of raw Indian cotton through its harbor.
- Celebrated for the iconic basalt ceremonial arch known as the *Gateway of India*, erected on the waterfront at *Apollo Bunder* to commemorate the 1911 visit of King *George V* and *Queen Mary*.
- Houses magnificent architectural monuments including the Victorian Gothic *Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus*, formerly *Victoria Terminus*, and the historic rock-cut caverns of *Elephanta Island*.
- Anchors the nation's financial institutions on *Dalal Street*, home to the *Bombay Stock Exchange*, while accommodating the vast informal economy of the *Dharavi* settlement and the legendary lunch-delivery network of the *Dabbawalas*.
- Endured the tragic terrorist attacks of November 2008, when gunmen launched coordinated assaults across the city targeting the *Taj Mahal Palace Hotel*, the *Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus*, and *Nariman House*.

## connections

- [[india]] — serves as the country's economic powerhouse, primary financial hub, and capital of the Hindi cinema industry.
- [[british-empire]] — grew into the premier commercial seaport of British India and served as the departure gate for the final British troops in 1948.
- [[colonialism]] — passed between Portuguese and British imperial ownership, evolving from an insular trade post into an imperial port metropolis.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — expanded into a global center of mechanized textile manufacturing, powered by cotton exports and modern deepwater docks.
- [[mozambique-channel]] — lies across the northern waters of the Indian Ocean, receiving historical merchant voyages originating from East African shores.
- [[nairobi]] — connected by migratory and trade pipelines through which Gujarati merchants emigrated to establish commercial networks in Kenya.

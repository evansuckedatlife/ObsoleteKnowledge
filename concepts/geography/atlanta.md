## summary

**Atlanta** is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Georgia, serving as the primary commercial, cultural, and transportation center of the American South. Established in the nineteenth century as a terminus for regional railroads, the city was devastated during the [[american-civil-war]] before orchestrating a dramatic rebirth during the [[reconstruction-era]] under the symbol of the rising phoenix. In the twentieth century, the metropolis emerged as the intellectual and strategic cradle of the [[civil-rights-movement]], and today it functions as a global economic powerhouse anchored by international enterprise and the world's busiest airport.

## you gotta know

- Rose to prominence as a vital rail nexus founded in 1837 at the intersection of two major lines, bearing the early names *Terminus* and *Marthasville* before its incorporation under its modern name in 1847.
- Served as a critical Confederate logistical and manufacturing center during the [[american-civil-war]] until Union forces under Major General *William Tecumseh Sherman* captured and torched the city in 1864, precipitating his devastating March to the Sea.
- Rebuilt rapidly during the [[reconstruction-era]] as civic promoters branded it the commercial capital of the "New South," formally adopting the mythical phoenix on the municipal seal to commemorate its ascent from the ashes.
- Formed the organizational epicenter of the American [[civil-rights-movement]], serving as the birthplace of Dr. *Martin Luther King Jr.*, the headquarters of the Southern Christian Leadership Conference, and the home of the historic *Atlanta University Center*.
- Operates *Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport*, an infrastructure marvel that transformed the city into an international crossroads and has consistently ranked as the busiest passenger airport in the world.
- Hosted the 1996 Summer Olympic Games, an international event that catalyzed sweeping infrastructure modernization and urban revitalization throughout downtown, though shadowed by the domestic terror bombing in *Centennial Olympic Park*.
- Anchors the corporate and financial landscape of the southeastern [[united-states]], housing the global headquarters of Fortune 500 giants including *The Coca-Cola Company*, *Delta Air Lines*, *The Home Depot*, and news network *CNN*.
- Flourished into an influential cultural capital of the modern era, gaining widespread recognition for its defining contributions to contemporary American music, hip-hop production, television broadcasting, and the theatrical film industry.

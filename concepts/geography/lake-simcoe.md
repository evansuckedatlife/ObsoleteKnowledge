## summary

**Lake Simcoe** is a prominent freshwater lake located in southern Ontario, nestled within the rolling terrain between Lake Huron's Georgian Bay and Lake Ontario. Historically known to the indigenous *Huron-Wendat* nation as *Ouentironk*, it was rechristened in 1793 by Upper Canada's first lieutenant governor, *John Graves Simcoe*, in loving memory of his father, Captain *John Simcoe*. Forming an indispensable central waterway of the historic *Trent-Severn Waterway*, the lake represents an essential ecological, recreational, and transport corridor within the Greater Golden Horseshoe region of [[canada]].

## you gotta know

- Serves as the vital central link in the 386-kilometer *Trent-Severn Waterway*, connecting the Bay of Quinte on *Lake Ontario* to Georgian Bay on *Lake Huron*.
- Renamed in 1793 by the colonial administrator *John Graves Simcoe* to commemorate his naval father, who served the [[british-empire]] during the Seven Years' War.
- Connected historically to Toronto by *Yonge Street*, one of the longest continuous thoroughfares in the world, originally surveyed as a military passage northward from *Lake Ontario* to the lake's southern shores at Holland Landing.
- Known originally in the *Huron-Wendat* language as *Ouentironk*, meaning "beautiful water", and celebrated archaeologically for the ancient *Mnjikaning Fish Weirs* at the lake's narrows near Orillia, constructed more than four millennia ago.
- Encloses several large islands including *Georgina Island*, which constitutes an Indian Reserve belonging to the Chippewas of Georgina Island First Nation, as well as the car-free *Thorah Island*.
- Renowned widely across North America as the self-proclaimed "Ice Fishing Capital of Canada", drawing thousands of winter anglers onto its frozen surface each winter to fish for yellow perch, lake trout, and whitefish.
- Grappled extensively throughout recent decades with agricultural phosphorus runoff and invasive species such as zebra mussels and spiny water fleas, culminating in Ontario's pioneering Lake Simcoe Protection Act.

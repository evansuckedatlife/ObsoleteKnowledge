## summary

**Switzerland** is a landlocked Alpine nation in central Europe known for its mountains, neutrality, political stability, and prosperity. Despite lacking colonial power or vast natural resources, Switzerland became one of the world's wealthiest and most stable nations through banking, precision manufacturing, and skilled diplomacy. Its federal system of cantons and linguistic diversity make it a unique European model of coexistence.

## you gotta know

- Switzerland occupies the largest portion of the Alps; Alpine geography defines its character, economy, and culture from dairy farming to tourism to hydroelectric power generation.
- The country comprises 26 cantons (federal states) with significant autonomy; its federal structure is a model of democratic decentralization and has been stable since 1848.
- Switzerland is officially multilingual, with four official languages: German (spoken by ~63% of the population), French (~23%), Italian (~8%), and Romansh (~0.5%).
- Switzerland maintained neutrality through both World Wars, though it was surrounded by Nazi Germany and faced pressure; this neutrality became its defining diplomatic stance and international identity.
- Swiss banking has been central to the nation's wealth and international influence; Swiss banks became repositories of global capital, though this role has been controversial and evolved in recent decades.
- The Swiss are known for precision engineering and manufacturing; brands like Rolex, TAG Heuer, and Victorinox represent the nation's reputation for quality and precision in watches and tools.
- Switzerland is home to several major international organizations including the United Nations' European headquarters (Geneva), the International Committee of the Red Cross, and numerous other global bodies.
- The Swiss political system operates through direct democracy with frequent referendums and initiatives, giving citizens unusual power over policy decisions at local, cantonal, and national levels.
- Switzerland's standard of living is consistently among the world's highest; it has no significant poverty, excellent healthcare and education, and strong labor protections.

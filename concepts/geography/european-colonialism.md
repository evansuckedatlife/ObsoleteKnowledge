## summary

**European Colonialism** was the centuries-long process through which European nations conquered, settled, and politically subordinated foreign territories and indigenous populations across the globe. Initiated in the late 15th century and accelerating through the Industrial Revolution, it transformed global trade, demography, and geopolitical boundaries. Driven by mercantile competition, religious expansion, and racialized ideologies, European empires extracted vast resources while establishing institutional networks that continue to shape modern global politics, economic inequality, and cultural geography.

## you gotta know

- Began in earnest during the late 15th century when maritime navigators from *[[spain]]* and Portugal explored sea routes across the *[[atlantic-ocean]]*, inaugurating the conquest of the Americas.
- Characterized by the catastrophic demographic collapse of indigenous populations across the Western Hemisphere due to introduced Old World pathogens, forced labor systems like the *encomienda*, and direct warfare.
- Fuelled the horrific Transatlantic Slave Trade, which forcibly displaced over twelve million enslaved Africans to power plantation monocultures producing sugar, tobacco, coffee, and cotton.
- Evolved from early mercantilist chartered enterprises like the Dutch and British East India Companies into direct state-administered bureaucratic regimes during the 19th century.
- Justified by ideological constructs of racial supremacy, paternalism, and the so-called "civilizing mission" (*mission civilisatrice*), which rationalized the dispossession of native inhabitants.
- Culminated in the rapid "Scramble for Africa" during the late 19th century, formalized at the 1884–1885 Berlin Conference where European powers partitioned almost the entire African continent.
- Penetrated continental interiors through state-backed geographic expeditions, such as those undertaken across *[[australia]]* by explorers like *[[ernest-giles]]*, opening new frontiers for extraction and pastoral settlement.
- Collapsed across Asia and Africa in the mid-20th century through wave upon wave of armed liberation struggles, civil disobedience, and negotiated decolonization following the devastation of World War II.

## connections

- [[colonialism]] — the broader historical and political practice of subjugation that European states institutionalized globally.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the early modern period of maritime discovery and navigation that launched overseas territorial conquests.
- [[british-empire]] — the largest global imperial system produced by this process, governing nearly a quarter of the world's land area.
- [[spain]] — the pioneering imperial power that conquered the Aztec and Inca empires and amassed vast American silver wealth.
- [[france]] — the major European colonial rival whose imperial domains spanned North America, the Caribbean, Africa, and Indochina.
- [[australia]] — the southern continent occupied by British settlers, where native populations were dispossessed during territorial expansion.
- [[ernest-giles]] — the frontier explorer who surveyed the arid Australian interior to map terrain for British colonial penetration.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the primary oceanic conduit for the triangular trade, naval power projection, and colonial migration.

## summary

**Khartoum** is the capital city of Sudan, situated at the historic confluence of the *White Nile* flowing north from Lake Victoria and the *Blue Nile* descending west from the Ethiopian Highlands. Founded in the early nineteenth century as an administrative outpost of Ottoman-Egyptian rule, the city grew into a major strategic crossroads linking the Saharan borderlands with the African interior. It is celebrated in global historiography for the nineteenth-century *Siege of Khartoum* during the Mahdist War and serves as the political and financial core of the modern nation.

## you gotta know

- Sited precisely at the *al-Mogran* confluence, where the sediment-laden *Blue Nile* joins the sluggish *White Nile* to form the unified trunk stream of the *Nile River*.
- Forms a sprawling tri-city metropolis with *Omdurman* to the west across the main Nile and *Khartoum North* (*Bahri*) across the Blue Nile.
- Founded in 1821 by *Ismail Pasha*, son of *Muhammad Ali Pasha* of *Egypt*, as a military garrison camp that replaced the historic Funj capital of *Sennar*.
- Besieged and captured in January 1885 by the forces of the religious reformer *Muhammad Ahmad*, known as the *Mahdi*, culminating in the death of British governor-general *Charles George Gordon*.
- Recaptured in 1898 by Anglo-Egyptian forces led by General *Herbert Kitchener* following the nearby Battle of *Omdurman*, establishing the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium.
- Rebuilt by British colonial planners following a street grid deliberately resembling the British Union Jack flag to facilitate artillery sightlines and administrative surveillance.
- Hosted the historic 1967 Arab League summit following the Six-Day War, where leaders issued the famous *Khartoum Resolution* containing the "Three No's": no peace, no recognition, and no negotiations.
- Endured significant structural devastation during factional urban fighting that erupted in April 2023 between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces.

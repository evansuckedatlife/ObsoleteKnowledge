## summary

**Ontario** is Canada's most populous province and leading economic powerhouse, situated in east-central *Canada* between the Great Lakes and Hudson Bay. Home to both the federal capital city of *Ottawa* and the nation's largest metropolis, *Toronto*, it anchors the country's manufacturing, financial, and political sectors. Its landscape transitions from the agricultural lowlands of the Golden Horseshoe to the mineral-rich forests and rocky expanses of the Canadian Shield.

## you gotta know

- Contains *Toronto*, the provincial capital and largest city in *Canada*, which serves as the anchor of the heavily urbanized *Golden Horseshoe* region.
- Contains the federal national capital of *Ottawa*, located in the eastern portion of the province along the Quebec border.
- Bounded to the south by four of the five Great Lakes—Superior, Huron, Erie, and Ontario—which it shares with the *United States*.
- Historically designated as *Upper Canada* following the Constitutional Act of 1791, receiving influxes of United Empire Loyalists following the American Revolution.
- Joined *Quebec*, *Nova Scotia*, and *New Brunswick* in 1867 as one of the four original provinces entering Canadian Confederation.
- Shares the spectacular natural landmark of *Niagara Falls* on the Niagara River with the American state of New York.
- Its vast northern expanse covers a significant portion of the *Canadian Shield*, an ancient geological formation rich in nickel, gold, and timber reserves.
- Home to major industrial corridors, historically known as the automobile manufacturing heartland of the nation centered in cities like Oshawa and Windsor.

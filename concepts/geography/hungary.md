## summary

**Hungary** (*Magyarország*) is a landlocked Central European nation occupying the *Carpathian Basin*, centered along the [[danube-river]] and dominated culturally and politically by its historic capital, *Budapest*. Founded in 895 CE under the chieftain *Árpád* and established as a kingdom under *Saint Stephen I* in 1000 CE, the realm served as a pivotal defensive bastion between Central [[europe]] and eastern empires. Following partition by the [[ottoman-empire]] after the 1526 *Battle of Mohács*, it was absorbed into the Habsburg sphere, later becoming an equal partner in the dual Austro-Hungarian Monarchy. In the twentieth century, the nation endured drastic territorial losses, foreign occupations, and communist hegemony before emerging as a modern democratic republic.

## you gotta know

- Capital city *Budapest* was created in 1873 by uniting *Buda* and *Óbuda* on the western bank of the [[danube-river]] with *Pest* on the eastern bank, joined by the iconic *Széchenyi Chain Bridge* and anchored by the neo-Gothic *Hungarian Parliament Building*.
- The *Magyars*, led by *Árpád*, entered the *Pannonian Basin* in 895 CE, establishing a *Finno-Ugric* language of the Uralic family that survives as an isolated non-Indo-European island, a core subject in historical [[linguistics]].
- The 1920 *Treaty of Trianon* after [[world-war-i]] drastically redrew national boundaries, depriving Hungary of over seventy percent of its territory and stranding more than three million ethnic Hungarians in neighboring states.
- The 1526 *Battle of Mohács* shattered medieval power when the armies of *Suleiman the Magnificent* crushed King *Louis II*, beginning 150 years of tripartite division among the [[ottoman-empire]], the Habsburgs, and *Transylvania*.
- The *Hungarian Revolution of 1956* was a nationwide popular uprising against Soviet domination that installed reformist *Imre Nagy* before being violently crushed by tanks of the [[soviet-union]] during the [[cold-war]].
- King *Stephen I* (*Szent István*) established the Christian kingdom around 1000 CE, receiving the sacred *Holy Crown of Hungary* from the Pope and permanently integrating the nation into Western [[christianity]].
- Renaissance monarch *Matthias Corvinus* led the country to a fifteenth-century cultural golden age, patronizing Italian humanists and curating the celebrated *Bibliotheca Corviniana*, one of Europe's largest libraries outside [[italy]].
- The sweeping steppe flatlands of the *Puszta* preserve equestrian and pastoral heritage at *Hortobágy National Park*, situated near *Lake Balaton*, Central Europe's largest freshwater lake.

## connections

- [[danube-river]] — the primary waterway that bisects the nation and flows directly through Budapest.
- [[ottoman-empire]] — imperial adversary that occupied central Hungary for 150 years after the Battle of Mohács.
- [[soviet-union]] — foreign power that occupied Hungary after [[world-war-ii]] and crushed the 1956 national revolution.
- [[austria]] — partner with whom Hungary formed the dual Austro-Hungarian Monarchy in 1867.
- [[world-war-i]] — conflict whose defeat led directly to the severe territorial dismemberment of the Treaty of Trianon.
- [[linguistics]] — discipline that studies the isolated Uralic origins and unique agglutinative grammar of the Hungarian tongue.
- [[christianity]] — faith adopted under Saint Stephen I, transitioning the early Magyars from pagan nomads into a Christian monarchy.

## summary

**Hells Canyon** is a ten-mile-wide river gorge situated along the border of eastern *Oregon*, western [[idaho]], and southeastern *Washington* in the *Pacific Northwest* region of the [[united-states]]. Carved over millions of years by the waters of the *Snake River*, the canyon plunges to a maximum depth of 7,993 feet from the riverbed to the summit of *He Devil Peak* in the *Seven Devils Mountains*, making it the deepest river gorge in *North America*. Forming the core of the *Hells Canyon National Recreation Area*, it represents a dramatic geographic boundary renowned for prehistoric indigenous settlements, remote wilderness, and prominent appearances in regional quiz-bowl questions.

## you gotta know

- Plunges deeper than the *Grand Canyon* of *Arizona*, holding the official record as the deepest river gorge in *North America* at nearly eight thousand feet.
- Formed by the swift waters of the *Snake River*, which flows northward through the gorge before joining the [[columbia-river]] in *Washington*.
- Bounded to the east by the dramatic, snow-capped peaks of the *Seven Devils Mountains* in [[idaho]] and to the west by the *Wallowa Mountains* of *Oregon*.
- Homeland of the *Nez Perce* people, who inhabited its temperate canyon floor during harsh winter months before *Chief Joseph* led his band across the swollen river during the 1877 Nez Perce War.
- Encountered by the 1811 overland Astor Expedition led by *Wilson Price Hunt*, whose starving fur traders found the steep basalt cliffs completely impassable by canoe.
- Site of the tragic 1887 Hells Canyon Massacre at *Deep Creek*, in which a gang of white horse thieves ambushed and murdered up to thirty-four Chinese gold miners.
- Altered ecologically in the twentieth century by the construction of the *Hells Canyon Complex*, three hydroelectric dams that permanently blocked salmon migration into the upper *Snake River* basin.
- Shaped geologically by extensive volcanic eruptions of *Columbia River Basalt* followed by tectonic uplift and the cataclysmic *Bonneville Flood* roughly fifteen thousand years ago.

## connections

- [[idaho]] — forms a rugged segment of the state's western border with eastern *Oregon*.
- [[united-states]] — geographic wonder preserved under federal protection within the national forest system.
- [[columbia-river]] — major river basin fed directly by the canyon's primary artery, the *Snake River*.

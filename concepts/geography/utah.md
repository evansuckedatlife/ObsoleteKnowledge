## summary

**Utah** is a landlocked state in the Mountain West subregion of the `[[united-states]]`, bordered by Colorado, Wyoming, Idaho, Arizona, Nevada, and New Mexico. Admitted to the Union as the forty-fifth state in 1896, it is celebrated for its dramatically diverse topography encompassing the Wasatch Range, the Colorado Plateau, and the arid Great Basin. The state holds a unique position in American history as the sanctuary established by Mormon pioneers, a vital crossroads of transcontinental rail, and a premier destination for scenic conservation.

## you gotta know

- Site of Promontory Summit, where the ceremonial *Golden Spike* was driven on May 10, 1869, uniting the Central Pacific and Union Pacific Railroads to complete North America's first transcontinental railroad.
- Settled heavily in 1847 by members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints led by *Brigham Young*, who migrated along the Mormon Trail to escape persecution and laid out Salt Lake City.
- Home to the famed "Mighty 5" national parks—*Zion*, *Bryce Canyon*, *Arches*, *Canyonlands*, and *Capitol Reef*—distinguished by stunning red rock formations, sandstone arches, and colorful hoodoos.
- Dominated geographically by the *Great Salt Lake*, the largest saltwater lake in the Western Hemisphere, which represents an endorheic remnant of the vast prehistoric pluvial *Lake Bonneville*.
- Statehood was delayed for nearly half a century due to intense federal opposition to Mormon polygamy, finally granted in 1896 after the church formally renounced the practice in the 1890 Manifesto.
- Contains the *Bonneville Salt Flats*, an expansive, flat salt pan in Tooele County renowned internationally as the venue for automotive land speed record attempts.
- Hosted the *2002 Winter Olympic Games* in Salt Lake City and the surrounding Wasatch mountain ski resorts, which also serve as the annual venue for the *Sundance Film Festival*.
- Anchors the *Four Corners Monument*, marking the only quadripoint in the nation shared with Colorado, New Mexico, and Arizona.

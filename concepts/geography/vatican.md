## summary

**The Vatican** is the world's smallest independent nation-state, a sovereign city nestled within Rome that serves as the spiritual and administrative center of the Roman Catholic Church. Established in its modern political form in 1929, it claims continuity from Saint Peter's burial site and the papacy's ancient seat. The Vatican is a unique entity: a religious institution with territorial sovereignty, diplomatic recognition, and profound influence on over a billion Catholics worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Covers approximately 110 acres (44 hectares) on the western bank of the Tiber River within Rome.
- The *Pope* is both its absolute monarch and the head of the worldwide Roman Catholic Church, making it a theocracy.
- Built upon the traditional burial site of *Saint Peter*, the apostle whom Jesus declared the "rock" upon which the Church would be built.
- *St. Peter's Basilica* is one of Christianity's holiest sites and a masterpiece of Renaissance architecture, begun in 1506 and completed in 1626.
- *Michelangelo* designed the basilica's dome and painted the ceiling of the *Sistine Chapel*, making the Vatican an artistic treasure.
- The *Papal States* ruled vast Italian territories for over a thousand years until Italy's unification in 1871 left the Pope trapped in the Vatican.
- The *Lateran Treaty* (1929) restored papal independence, creating Vatican City as a sovereign microstate with UN observer status.
- Houses one of the world's greatest libraries and the Secret Archives, repositories of centuries of Church documents and diplomatic correspondence.

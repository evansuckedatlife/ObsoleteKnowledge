## summary

**Oahu** is the third-largest of the Hawaiian Islands, located in the central Pacific within the region of *Oceania*. Known colloquially as "The Gathering Place," it is the most populous island in the state of Hawaii and hosts the state capital, *Honolulu*. In quiz bowl tournaments, it appears constantly in questions regarding the surprise Japanese attack on *Pearl Harbor*, volcanic landforms like *Diamond Head*, and royal landmarks of the former Hawaiian Kingdom.

## you gotta know

- Hosts the state capital and commercial center of *Honolulu*, along with the world-famous tourist beachfront district of *Waikiki*.
- Site of *Pearl Harbor*, a deep-water naval base whose devastating surprise attack by the Imperial Japanese Navy on December 7, 1941, brought the *United States* directly into *World War II*.
- Formed by two distinct coalesced shield volcanoes: the *Waianae Range* in the west and the *Koolau Range* in the east, separated by a central plateau.
- Prominent landmarks include *Diamond Head* (*Leahi*), a prominent volcanic tuff cone overlooking Honolulu, and *Punchbowl Crater*, home to the National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific.
- The North Shore of the island is celebrated globally as the epicenter of big-wave surfing, featuring legendary breaks such as the *Banzai Pipeline*, *Sunset Beach*, and *Waimea Bay*.
- Contains *Iolani Palace* in downtown Honolulu, the only royal palace used as an official state residence by a reigning monarch on United States soil, built by King *Kalakaua* in 1882.
- The overthrow of Queen *Liliuokalani* occurred here in 1893, led by foreign sugar planters and backed by American marines, precipitating the annexation of the Hawaiian Kingdom.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the country that annexed the island in 1898 and admitted it as part of the 50th state in 1959.
- [[oceania]] — the broad Pacific cultural and geographic realm whose Polynesian Triangle includes the Hawaiian Islands.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the conflict entered by the United States following the historic aerial strike on the island's naval installations at Pearl Harbor.
- [[colonialism]] — the commercial and political subjugation that overthrew the native Hawaiian monarchy in the late 19th century.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the period of Pacific exploration that brought British expeditions under Captain James Cook to the Hawaiian archipelago.

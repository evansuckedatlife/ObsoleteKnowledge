## summary

**Ellesmere Island** is the northernmost and tenth-largest island in the world, situated within the high Arctic archipelago of Nunavut in northern *Canada*. Known in Inuktitut as *Umingmak Nuna*, or land of the muskoxen, the island is characterized by rugged fjords, deep thermal permafrost, the towering Arctic Cordillera, and persistent ice shelves facing the Arctic Ocean. It holds enormous historical significance for circumpolar exploration, paleo-Arctic migrations, Cold War sovereignty operations, and pioneering high-latitude scientific research.

## you gotta know

- Ranked as the third-largest island in [[canada]] and the tenth-largest globally, bounded to the east across the narrow Nares Strait by Greenland.
- Home to *Alert*, the northernmost permanently inhabited settlement on Earth, which houses a weather station and military signals intelligence outpost.
- Features *Barbeau Peak*, the highest mountain in eastern North America and the high point of the glaciated British Empire Range.
- Contains *Quttinirpaaq National Park*, which encompasses dramatic polar desert ecosystems, thermal glaciers, and Lake Hazen.
- Known for key paleontological breakthroughs, including the 2004 discovery of *Tiktaalik roseae*, an essential transitional fossil linking fish and tetrapods.
- Inhabited by cold-adapted wildlife including polar bears, Arctic wolves, Peary caribou, and vast herds of shaggy muskoxen.
- Subject to Canadian Cold War sovereignty assertions during the 1950s, which involved controversial forced relocations of Inuit families to *Grise Fiord*.

## connections

- [[canada]] — northern sovereign nation whose territory of Nunavut includes the entire Arctic archipelago.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era when military listening posts and radar installations were established in the high Arctic.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — ocean connected to the island's eastern waters through Baffin Bay and the Davis Strait.
- [[british-empire]] — global power whose naval expeditions under *Sir George Nares* explored and mapped the northern coastline in the nineteenth century.
- [[united-states]] — partner in joint high-Arctic weather monitoring stations and early polar exploration expeditions.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict whose conclusion accelerated hemispheric defense planning and radar perimeter construction across polar approaches.

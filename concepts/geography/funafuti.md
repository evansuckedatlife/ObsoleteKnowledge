## summary

**Funafuti** is a coral atoll that serves as the official capital of the Polynesian island nation of Tuvalu in the south-central Pacific Ocean. Comprising dozens of tiny, low-lying islets surrounding a vast marine lagoon, the atoll contains more than half of Tuvalu's total citizenry within its primary settlement of *Vaiaku*. Funafuti is renowned in science for historic nineteenth-century drilling experiments that confirmed Charles Darwin's theories on coral reef development, and in contemporary world affairs as a frontline symbol of climate vulnerability and rising sea levels.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the national capital of *Tuvalu*, with administrative functions concentrated along the *Vaiaku* village on the eastern islet of *Fongafale*.
- Surrounds *Te Namo*, a massive turquoise lagoon measuring roughly eighteen kilometers long and fourteen kilometers wide, which is protected by a coral ring of thirty-three separate islets (*motu*).
- Provided empirical proof for *Charles Darwin*'s subsidence theory of atoll formation through the 1896–1898 Royal Society expeditions, which excavated the famous "Darwin's Drillhole" to test core rock compositions.
- Functioned as a heavily fortified forward airfield and anchorage for the *United States Navy* and Marine Corps during *World War II* to launch bomber strikes against Japanese bases in the Gilbert and Marshall Islands.
- Dominates national civilian activity through the *Funafuti International Airport*, an unusual airstrip built by wartime engineers that bisects Fongafale and serves as a public sports park when flights are absent.
- Faces severe existential peril from global climate change, as king tides (*valovalo*) and tropical storms routinely inundate its narrow landmass, which peaks at merely four to five meters above sea level.
- Suffered extensive ecological scarring from wartime military excavations known as the "borrow pits," large trenches where coral rock was scooped out to construct runways, creating stagnant saltwater pools later remediated by international aid.
- Houses the *Funafuti Conservation Area*, a marine reserve established in 1999 on the western rim of the lagoon to safeguard sea turtles, seabirds, and fragile coral ecosystems.

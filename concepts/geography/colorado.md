## summary

**Colorado** is a landlocked state in the western [[united-states]] encompassing the vast majority of the southern *Rocky Mountains* as well as the western reaches of the [[great-plains]]. Admitted to the Union in 1876—one century after the signing of the Declaration of Independence, earning it the enduring nickname the **Centennial State**—it is widely celebrated for its rugged alpine landscapes, extensive high-altitude plateaus, and deep river canyons. In geographic and historical competitions, the **State of Colorado** is renowned for the 1859 gold strike, its pivotal role in transcontinental rail transit, expansive federal public lands, and significant military-aerospace infrastructure.

## you gotta know

- Known universally as the *Centennial State* because it achieved statehood on August 1, 1876, exactly one hundred years after the adoption of the *United States Declaration of Independence*.
- Possesses the highest mean elevation of any state in the nation at over 6,800 feet, featuring *Mount Elbert*—the highest peak in the *Rocky Mountains*—and fifty-eight distinctive mountain summits rising above 14,000 feet, celebrated as "fourteeners."
- The state capital and largest metropolitan center is *Denver*, famously designated the "Mile High City" because an official benchmark on the steps of the state capitol sits precisely 5,280 feet above sea level.
- Sparked into rapid modern settlement by the 1858–1859 *Pikes Peak Gold Rush*, drawing tens of thousands of fortune seekers under the famous banner "Pikes Peak or Bust" and spurring the organization of the Colorado Territory in 1861.
- Site of the infamous November 1864 *Sand Creek Massacre*, where Colonel *John Chivington* led Colorado volunteer cavalry in an unprovoked dawn assault that slaughtered over two hundred peaceful *Cheyenne* and *Arapaho* villagers during the [[american-civil-war]].
- Houses crucial defense and aerospace installations along the Front Range, prominently including the *United States Air Force Academy* at Colorado Springs and the underground command center of NORAD excavated deep within *Cheyenne Mountain*.
- Marked by intense labor strife during the early twentieth century, culminating in the tragic 1914 *Ludlow Massacre*, where the state national guard and company guards attacked a tent colony of striking miners in the southern coalfields.
- Major headwaters for several prominent river systems of the American West, giving birth to the *Colorado River*, the *Rio Grande*, the *Arkansas River*, and the *South Platte River*.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the federal republic that purchased the eastern territory via the Louisiana Purchase and admitted Colorado as the thirty-eighth state.
- [[great-plains]] — the expansive grassland biome covering the eastern two-fifths of the state before terminating at the foothills of the Rockies.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict framing the mobilization of territorial troops who fought Confederate forces at Glorieta Pass and committed the atrocity at Sand Creek.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the political period during which congressional Republicans and President *Ulysses S. Grant* finalized statehood negotiations.
- [[kansas]] — the neighboring state whose territorial jurisdiction initially encompassed the eastern mining districts prior to separate territorial status.
- [[cold-war]] — the international standoff that spurred extensive regional defense contracting, nuclear triggers manufacturing at Rocky Flats, and Cheyenne Mountain operations.
- [[space-race]] — the aerospace boom that clustered satellite manufacturing, military tracking stations, and planetary science centers along the urban corridor.

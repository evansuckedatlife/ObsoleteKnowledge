## summary

**Okanagan Lake** is a large, deep fjord lake located in the southern interior of British Columbia, *Canada*. Formed by glacial activity during the last ice age, the lake anchors the arid, fruit- and wine-producing *Okanagan Valley*. In competition questions, it is famous both for hosting the major interior city of *Kelowna* and as the folkloric home of the lake monster *Ogopogo*.

## you gotta know

- Located in south-central British Columbia, surrounded by the dry terraces and benchlands of the *Okanagan Valley*, which form one of the premier viticultural and fruit-growing districts of *Canada*.
- The largest city along its shores is *Kelowna*, connected to the western shore via the *William R. Bennett Bridge*, a floating pontoon bridge.
- Renowned in Canadian folklore as the reputed habitat of *Ogopogo*, a cryptid lake monster derived from Indigenous *Syilx* stories of *Naitaka*, a sacred water spirit.
- Stretches roughly 135 kilometers (84 miles) in length from *Vernon* in the north down to *Penticton* at its southern outlet, where the *Okanagan River* drains toward the *Columbia River* system.
- Geologically classified as a fjord lake carved out by recurring Pleistocene glaciation, reaching depths of over 230 meters (750 feet) beneath an infill of glacial silt.
- *Rattlesnake Island*, located near *Peachland*, was historically developed by eccentric entrepreneur Eddie Haymour as a tourist park and is steeped in local legends regarding *Ogopogo* sightings.

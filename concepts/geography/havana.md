## summary

**Havana** is the capital, largest city, and primary commercial and cultural port of Cuba, situated on the island's northern coast along the Straits of Florida. Founded by the Spanish in the sixteenth century, the city grew into the essential staging ground for the West Indies treasure fleets before they crossed the Atlantic. In modern times, Havana became a focal point of international geopolitics, Latin American literature, and revolutionary transformation following the 1959 rise of *Fidel Castro*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1519 by conquistador *Diego Velázquez de Cuéllar* as **San Cristóbal de la Habana**, centered around a protected natural harbor on the Caribbean's northern edge.
- Served as the mandatory rendezvous point for the Spanish treasure fleet, or *Flota de Indias*, which gathered in the harbor before catching the [[gulf-stream]] back to [[spain]].
- Fortified heavily with classic colonial stone bastions, notably *Castillo de la Real Fuerza* and the fortress of *Morro Castle*, which guards the narrow entrance channel to Havana Bay.
- Captured by Great Britain during the Seven Years' War in 1762 before being returned to Spain in exchange for colonial Florida under the Treaty of Paris.
- Witnessed the catastrophic explosion of the armored cruiser USS *Maine* in its harbor in February 1898, which precipitated the Spanish-American War and direct military intervention by the [[united-states]].
- Anchored prominent architectural and civic landmarks, including the seafront promenade known as the *Malecón*, the baroque Havana Cathedral, and the neoclassical *El Capitolio*.
- Became the epicenter of socialist governance and Soviet economic partnership in the Western Hemisphere during the [[cold-war]], particularly during the Cuban Missile Crisis of 1962.

## connections

- [[spain]] — the imperial power that built the colonial city as its primary maritime gateway and transshipment hub in the Americas.
- [[cold-war]] — the global ideological conflict during which the city served as a vital regional bastion aligned with the [[soviet-union]].
- [[united-states]] — the neighboring northern power that fought a war over Cuba in 1898 and later imposed a comprehensive economic embargo on the island.
- [[gulf-stream]] — the oceanic current that exits the Florida Straits right outside the city's harbor, carrying outbound vessels north and east.
- [[haiti]] — neighboring Caribbean state whose massive slave uprising in the 1790s caused a dramatic boom in Havana's sugar economy.
- [[colonialism]] — the worldwide system of imperial governance and coerced plantation labor that built the wealth and stone architecture of Old Havana.

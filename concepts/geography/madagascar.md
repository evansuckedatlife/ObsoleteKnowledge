## summary

**Madagascar**, officially the **Republic of Madagascar**, is an island nation in the Indian Ocean situated off the southeastern coast of mainland Africa, across the Mozambique Channel. As the world's fourth-largest island, it fractured from the supercontinent Gondwana and the Indian tectonic plate tens of millions of years ago, giving rise to an extraordinary biodiversity hotspot where over ninety percent of wildlife is endemic. In quiz bowl, the island is famed for its unique ecological treasures such as lemurs and baobabs, its ancient settlement by Austronesian voyagers sailing across the Indian Ocean from [[southeast-asia]], the nineteenth-century central highland *Merina Kingdom*, and its subsequent subjugation under French [[colonialism]].

## you gotta know

- The capital and largest city is *Antananarivo*, located in the central highlands, which historically served as the royal seat of the *Merina Kingdom* and is dominated by the historic *Rova* palace complex.
- Renowned globally for extreme ecological endemism, hosting wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, including dozens of species of *lemurs*, the carnivoran *fossa*, vibrant chameleons, and giant *baobab* trees along the famed *Avenue of the Baobabs* near *Morondava*.
- Geologically isolated from mainland Africa by the *Mozambique Channel*, having split from Africa roughly 160 million years ago and later from the Indian landmass roughly 88 million years ago during the breakup of Gondwana.
- Settled in the first millennium CE primarily by *Austronesian* voyagers traveling by outrigger canoe from maritime [[southeast-asia]], resulting in the *Malagasy* language, which is closely related to the Barito languages of southern Borneo.
- The high-plateau *Merina Kingdom* unified much of the island in the early nineteenth century under King *Radama I*, who welcomed British Protestant missionaries, followed by Queen *Ranavalona I*, who fiercely expelled foreign influence and persecuted native Christians.
- Colonized by [[france]] in the 1890s following the Franco-Hova Wars, leading to the exile of Queen *Ranavalona III*, decades of colonial exploitation, and the bloody suppression of the *Malagasy Uprising* of 1947.
- Distinctive cultural and geological features include the dramatic jagged limestone karst landscapes of *Tsingy de Bemaraha*, premier global production of *bourbon vanilla* in the northeastern *Sava* region, and the traditional Merina reburial ceremony known as *famadihana* ("the turning of the bones").

## connections

- [[france]] — the colonial power that conquered the island in 1896, deposed the Merina monarchy, and ruled until Malagasy independence in 1960.
- [[colonialism]] — the global imperial expansion that led European powers to partition Africa and extinguish the sovereignty of the indigenous Malagasy state.
- [[southeast-asia]] — the ancient maritime ancestral homeland of the Austronesian navigators who first settled the island and founded the Malagasy ethnolinguistic identity.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — the era of European oceanic navigation during which Portuguese explorer *Diogo Dias* first sighted and recorded the island in 1500.
- [[india]] — the continental landmass with which Madagascar remained united long after detaching from mainland Africa, before parting ways in the Late Cretaceous.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean connected to the Indian Ocean via the Cape of Good Hope, sailing past which European merchant vessels called at Madagascar.

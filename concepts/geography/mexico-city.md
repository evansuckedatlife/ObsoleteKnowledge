## summary

**Mexico City**, known locally as **CDMX** or **Ciudad de México**, is the capital and largest metropolis of Mexico, situated at high elevation in the endorheic *Valley of Mexico*. Originally established as the Aztec island capital of **Tenochtitlan** on *Lake Texcoco*, it was conquered and rebuilt by the Spanish Empire to serve as the seat of the *Viceroyalty of New Spain*. Today, it is a global cultural and economic hub celebrated for its pre-Columbian ruins, colonial architecture, vibrant muralism, and immense urban scale.

## you gotta know

- Founded on an island in *Lake Texcoco* by the Mexica people in 1325 after spotting an eagle perched on a nopal cactus devouring a snake, an omen that now forms Mexico's national coat of arms.
- Conquered and razed in 1521 by Spanish conquistador *Hernán Cortés* during the [[age-of-exploration]] with help from indigenous allies like the Tlaxcalans, subsequently becoming the seat of the *Viceroyalty of New Spain* under [[colonialism]].
- Its historic heart surrounds the *Zócalo* (Plaza de la Constitución), bordered by the *Metropolitan Cathedral* and the *National Palace*, which displays famous historical murals painted by *Diego Rivera*.
- The archaeological site of the *Templo Mayor*, the central religious pyramid of the Aztecs dedicated to *Huitzilopochtli* and *Tlaloc*, was excavated directly adjacent to the *Zócalo* after its discovery by utility workers in 1978.
- Built on soft, saturated lakebed clay, causing the city to sink continuously through severe land subsidence as groundwater is extracted for its massive population.
- Home to *Chapultepec Castle*, which sits atop *Chapultepec Park* and served as the imperial residence of *Emperor Maximilian I* during the Second Mexican Empire before becoming the site of the Battle of Chapultepec.
- Features the historic borough of *Coyoacán*, famous as the home of artists *Frida Kahlo* (*La Casa Azul*) and *Diego Rivera*, as well as the exile and assassination location of *Leon Trotsky*.
- Hosted both the 1968 Summer Olympics—marked by the infamous *Tlatelolco massacre* shortly before the games began—and the FIFA World Cup finals at the iconic *Estadio Azteca* in 1970 and 1986.

## connections

- [[spain]] — colonial empire whose conquest of Tenochtitlan established the colonial capital.
- [[colonialism]] — historical imperial system under which the city governed the vast Viceroyalty of New Spain.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — global era of maritime navigation and conquest that brought European forces to the Americas.
- [[modernism]] — architectural and artistic movement that heavily shaped the city's 20th-century urban expansion and university campuses.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — body of water connecting the city's trade routes through Veracruz to European markets.
- [[miami]] — major North American commercial and financial hub closely tied to Latin American transit and cultural exchange.

## summary

The **buffalo**, scientifically classified as the **American bison** (*Bison bison*), is a massive bovine mammal indigenous to the grasslands and prairies of North America. As an ecological keystone species, it shaped the vegetative structure, nutrient cycling, and animal diversity of the Great Plains across the *[[united-states]]* and *[[canada]]*. The deliberate near-extermination of buffalo herds in the nineteenth century devastated Indigenous plains civilizations and fundamentally altered the geography of the continent.

## you gotta know

- Technically distinct from true Old World buffaloes (such as the African buffalo and Asian water buffalo), though colloquially termed *buffalo* across North America.
- Recognized as the official national mammal of the *[[united-states]]* following the passage of the National Bison Legacy Act in 2016.
- Numbered in the tens of millions before European settlement, roaming in great nomadic herds across the Great Plains between the Appalachian and Rocky Mountains.
- Functioned as a keystone species whose grazing patterns, wallowing depressions, and dung deposits sustained North American prairie ecology.
- Provided essential subsistence, clothing, shelter, bone tools, and spiritual foundation for Plains *[[native-american-peoples]]* including the Lakota, Cheyenne, and Comanche.
- Subjected to wholesale commercial slaughter in the late nineteenth century, actively abetted by the military and railroad expansion to force Indigenous nations onto reservations.
- Rescued from the brink of extinction through early conservation initiatives in Yellowstone National Park and private breeding herds.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the country where vast herds historically ranged and where the species is now the national mammal.
- [[canada]] — the northern range of the plains bison and the larger wood bison subspecies.
- [[mississippi-river]] — the major continental drainage basin whose western plains served as prime bison habitat.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict followed by accelerated westward rail expansion and subsequent commercial extermination of the herds.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-war era coinciding with intensive military campaigns and mass hide hunting on the Western plains.

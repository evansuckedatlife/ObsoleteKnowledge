## summary

The **A Train**, officially designated the *Eighth Avenue Express*, is a rapid transit route of the subway system in [[new-york-city]] within the [[united-states]]. Stretching from *Inwood* in northern *Manhattan* through *Brooklyn* and out to *Far Rockaway* and *Ozone Park* in *Queens*, it holds the distinction of having the longest single one-seat ride in the entire subway network. It attained widespread cultural immortality through *Billy Strayhorn*'s jazz standard *Take the "A" Train*, which captured its role as the direct conduit connecting midtown to the cultural epicenter of the *Harlem Renaissance*.

## you gotta know

- Inspired the signature jazz standard *Take the "A" Train*, composed by *Billy Strayhorn* in 1939 and recorded as the long-running theme song of the *Duke Ellington* orchestra.
- Operates the longest continuous route in the transit system, covering more than thirty-one miles on its southern terminal run to *Far Rockaway–Mott Avenue* on the *Rockaway Peninsula* near the [[atlantic-ocean]].
- Opened in September 1932 as the inaugural trunk line of the city-owned *Independent Subway System* (IND), designed explicitly to compete with the older private transit networks.
- Serves as the primary express artery running underneath *Eighth Avenue*, *Central Park West*, and *St. Nicholas Avenue* in *Manhattan*.
- Played a pivotal social role during the *Great Migration* and the [[civil-rights-movement]], providing swift transportation for African American commuters between residential *Harlem* and industrial jobs across lower *Manhattan* and *Brooklyn*.
- Crosses *Jamaica Bay* over a dedicated railway trestle, offering passengers broad panoramic views of tidal wetlands and coastal wildlife refuges.
- Originally celebrated in promotional materials and popular folklore for its rapid speeds and modern rolling stock, which bypassed dozens of local stops to transport uptown revelers swiftly to historic nightlife spots like the *Apollo Theater* and *Savoy Ballroom*.

## summary

**Agra** is an ancient and historic city located on the banks of the *Yamuna River* in the northern state of *Uttar Pradesh* within [[india]]. Renowned across the world as the imperial capital of the *Mughal Empire* during its golden age, the city showcases the pinnacle of Indo-Islamic architecture. It is internationally celebrated for housing the *Taj Mahal*, the white marble mausoleum commissioned by Emperor *Shah Jahan*, alongside monumental complexes such as the *Agra Fort* and the nearby deserted imperial city of *Fatehpur Sikri*.

## you gotta know

- Served as the vibrant capital of the *Mughal Empire* under emperors *Akbar*, *Jahangir*, and *Shah Jahan*, during which time it was known officially as *Akbarabad*.
- Home to the *Taj Mahal*, an exquisite funerary monument constructed from translucent *Makrana* marble, built by *Shah Jahan* to memorialize his beloved wife *Mumtaz Mahal*.
- Features the sprawling *Agra Fort*, a massive red sandstone citadel expanded by *Akbar* that served as the primary residence of the Mughal rulers until the capital moved to *Delhi*.
- Contains the tomb of *Itimad-ud-Daulah*, often referred to as the "Baby Taj," which pioneered the extensive use of intricate *pietra dura* stone inlay techniques.
- Located within short distance of *Fatehpur Sikri*, the short-lived red sandstone capital founded by *Akbar* to honor the Sufi saint *Salim Chishti*.
- Annexed into the territory of the *East India Company* in 1803 during the Second Anglo-Maratha War, becoming a key administrative center of the [[british-empire]] and early [[colonialism]] in northern South Asia.
- Forms the apex of India's world-famous "Golden Triangle" tourist circuit alongside the historic cities of *New Delhi* and *Jaipur*.

## connections

- [[india]] — the nation where the historic city sits along the plains of the *Yamuna River*.
- [[islam]] — the religious and cultural tradition that inspired the city's monumental mosques, gardens, and tombs.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial power that ruled the city following the decline of Mughal authority.
- [[colonialism]] — the era of European mercantile and administrative domination that reshaped the urban landscape.
- [[renaissance]] — the contemporary global epoch of flourishing arts and architecture that coincided with the Mughal apex.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the conflict during which the *Taj Mahal* was covered with scaffolding to disguise it from potential air attacks.

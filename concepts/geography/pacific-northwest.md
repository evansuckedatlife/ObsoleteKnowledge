## summary

**The Pacific Northwest** is a biogeographically and culturally distinctive region spanning the northwestern United States and southwestern Canada, bounded by the Pacific Ocean and defined by the Cascade and Rocky Mountain ranges. Characterized by temperate rainforests, dramatic coastlines, and rivers like the Columbia and Snake, the region has become a center for technology, logging, fishing, and outdoor recreation. Its geography profoundly shaped Native American cultures and attracted American expansion in the early 19th century.

## you gotta know

- The Pacific Northwest includes Washington, Oregon, and parts of California, Idaho, and British Columbia, unified by wet maritime climate and forest ecosystems.
- The Columbia River and its tributaries (especially the Snake River) form the region's hydrological backbone, supporting salmon fisheries and hydroelectric power.
- The Cascade Range separates the wet coastal regions from drier interior plateaus, creating distinct climatic and ecological zones.
- Dense temperate rainforests of the coastal region support old-growth Douglass fir and western hemlock, making logging historically central to the economy.
- Native American peoples, including the Salish, Chinook, and Nez Percé, developed complex societies based on salmon and cedar for thousands of years.
- Lewis and Clark's expedition (1804–1806) explored the region and reached the Pacific via the Columbia River, opening the region to American settlement.
- Modern Seattle and Portland have emerged as major tech and cultural hubs, attracting migration and investment since the late 20th century.

## summary

**Berlin** is the capital, largest city, and a constituent state of [[germany]], situated in northeastern Europe along the Spree and Havel rivers. Rising from its historic origins as the dual settlements of Berlin and Cölln, it grew into the royal residence of Prussia and the imperial center of unified Germany in 1871. In the twentieth century, the metropolis became the principal geopolitical stage of modern history, enduring heavy destruction during [[world-war-ii]], partition into Allied occupation sectors, forty years of ideological division marked by the *Berlin Wall*, and triumphant post-Cold War reunification.

## you gotta know

- Capital of [[germany]] and historic seat of Prussian kings and German emperors, centered architecturally around the neoclassical *Brandenburg Gate* and the grand boulevard *Unter den Linden*.
- Reunified as a federal city-state following the collapse of East German communism, with the federal parliament returning to the restored *Reichstag* building crowned with a glass dome designed by *Norman Foster*.
- Divided after [[world-war-ii]] into four Allied sectors, leaving West Berlin an isolated democratic enclave deep inside the Soviet occupation zone.
- Survived the 1948–1949 Soviet blockade through the *Berlin Airlift* (Operation Vittles), in which American and British transport aircraft flew essential coal and provisions into *Tempelhof* and *Gatow* airports.
- Bisected from August 1961 to November 1989 by the *Berlin Wall* (*Berliner Mauer*), erected by the East German regime to halt mass defection to the West.
- Served as the primary frontline arena of Cold War espionage and intelligence operations, symbolized globally by the border station *Checkpoint Charlie* and prisoner trades at the *Glienicke Bridge*.
- Renowned for *Museum Island* (*Museumsinsel*), a UNESCO World Heritage complex on the Spree River containing the *Pergamon Museum*, the *Altes Museum*, and the *Neues Museum* housing the bust of *Nefertiti*.
- Blossomed during the Weimar Republic into an avant-garde cultural capital celebrated for expressionist painting, cabaret, Bauhaus design, and the epic theater of *Bertolt Brecht*.

## connections

- [[germany]] — serves as both the federal capital and the most populous city of the united German republic.
- [[cold-war]] — stood as the central frontline symbol and flashpoint between NATO allies and the Warsaw Pact.
- [[world-war-ii]] — site of the final European military showdown during the Soviet storming of the city in April–May 1945.
- [[soviet-union]] — governed the eastern sector as the capital of East Germany and constructed the physical barriers dividing the city.
- [[united-states]] — provided security and diplomatic protection to West Berlin, commemorated by presidential speeches from *John F. Kennedy* and *Ronald Reagan*.
- [[enlightenment]] — served as the intellectual hub of King *Frederick the Great* and philosopher *Moses Mendelssohn*.
- [[belfast]] — parallel European city scarred by twentieth-century ideological and sectarian barriers that physically partitioned neighbourhoods.

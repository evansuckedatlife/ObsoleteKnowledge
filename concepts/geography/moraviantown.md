## summary

**Moraviantown**, initially established as **Fairfield**, is a historic indigenous settlement and First Nations reserve situated along the Thames River in southwestern Ontario, [[canada]]. Founded in 1792 by Christian Munsee Delaware refugees and missionary *David Zeisberger*, the community is most famous as the battleground of the 1813 Battle of the Thames during the War of 1812. The conflict resulted in the death of the Shawnee leader *Tecumseh*, an event that shattered his multi-tribal confederacy and decisively altered the geopolitics of North American indigenous sovereignty.

## you gotta know

- Site of the Battle of the Thames on October 5, 1813, where American forces decisively defeated a retreating British army and their allied indigenous warriors.
- Witnessed the death in combat of the renowned Shawnee war chief *Tecumseh*, bringing a permanent end to his pan-tribal confederacy against westward American territorial expansion.
- Delivered military renown to future American president *William Henry Harrison*, who leveraged his victory here alongside the Battle of Tippecanoe into his 1840 presidential campaign.
- Associated with Kentucky cavalry commander *Richard Mentor Johnson*, who gained national fame for allegedly killing *Tecumseh* in the fighting and later served as Vice President of the [[united-states]].
- Established in 1792 as a haven for Delaware converts by the Moravian Church following the brutal 1782 Gnadenhutten massacre in the Ohio Country.
- Burned to ashes by invading American troops following the battle, forcing surviving residents to rebuild across the river as New Fairfield, known today as the **Moravian of the Thames** reserve.
- Endures as home to the Delaware Nation at Moraviantown, preserving Munsee Delaware language, traditional governance, and ancestral land claims in southern Ontario.

## connections

- [[canada]] — encompasses the settlement within the modern municipal borders of Chatham-Kent in southern Ontario.
- [[british-empire]] — provided military alliance to *Tecumseh* before suffering defeat and abandoning the region in 1813.
- [[united-states]] — fought the War of 1812 here to extinguish indigenous confederate resistance along the northwestern frontier.
- [[american-revolution]] — generated the initial displacement of Moravian Delaware communities from the Ohio valley into Upper Canada.

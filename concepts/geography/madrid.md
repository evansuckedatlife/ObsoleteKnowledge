## summary

**Madrid** is the capital and largest city of Spain, located in the heart of the Iberian Peninsula at the geographical center of the country. Transformed from a modest fortress town into a major European capital during the Spanish Golden Age, Madrid became the seat of the Spanish monarchy and the cultural and intellectual hub of the Spanish Empire. Today, Madrid stands as a vibrant cosmopolitan center renowned for its art museums, architectural heritage, and role in European politics and culture.

## you gotta know

- Geographically centered on the Iberian Peninsula; strategic location contributed to its selection as Spain's capital by Philip II in 1561.
- Rose to prominence during Spain's Golden Age (16th–17th centuries) when the Spanish Empire was at its height; the Alcázar Palace served as the royal residence.
- Became the center of Spanish artistic and intellectual life; Miguel de Cervantes spent his final years here, and *Don Quixote* achieved its greatest success in Madrid.
- Home to the Prado Museum, one of Europe's finest art repositories, founded in 1819; contains masterworks by Velázquez, Goya, Bosch, and other Spanish and European painters.
- During the Spanish Civil War (1936–1939), Madrid endured a brutal three-year siege; the city's suffering became a symbol of the conflict and inspired artistic responses.
- Modern Madrid is Spain's largest metropolitan area and economic center; the city hosts European Union institutions and remains culturally dominant within Spain.
- The Alcázar Palace, residence of Spanish monarchs for centuries, was destroyed during the Civil War; the Royal Palace now serves as the official royal residence.
- Madrid's Retiro Park, established in the 17th century as royal grounds, is now a major public space exemplifying Madrid's transformation from monarchy-centered to democratic urban center.
- The Prado Museum contains works by Velázquez (including *Las Meninas*), Goya, and El Bosco; it is one of Europe's finest art repositories and reflects Spain's imperial cultural legacy.
- The Madrid metro system is one of Europe's largest and most efficient, constructed largely in the 20th century and essential to modern urban life.
- Modern Madrid balances its role as Spain's political capital with Barcelona's cultural rivalry; regional tensions between Madrid-centered Spanish nationalism and Catalan independence reflect broader Spanish divisions.

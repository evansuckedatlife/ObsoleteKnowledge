# Palestine

## summary

**Palestine** refers to a geographic and political region in the Levant, claimed and contested by Israelis and Palestinians. Historically the site of multiple ancient civilizations and religious traditions, it has been subject to competing claims and territorial disputes throughout the modern era. The territory today is divided among Israeli control, the Palestinian Authority in the West Bank, and various governance structures in the Gaza Strip, making it one of the world's most contentious geopolitical flashpoints.

## you gotta know

- Palestine encompasses the region west of the *Jordan River*, including the coastal plains, hills, and deserts of what is now Israel, the *West Bank*, and the *Gaza Strip*.
- The territory is sacred to three major religions: *Judaism* claims ancient kingdoms and the *Temple in Jerusalem*; *Christianity* venerates sites of Jesus's life; *Islam* holds the *Dome of the Rock* and *Al-Aqsa Mosque* as among its holiest shrines.
- The *Ottoman Empire* ruled Palestine for approximately four centuries (1517–1917) as part of the Levantine provinces; under Ottoman law, the territory was administered as part of greater Syria and northern Mesopotamia.
- In the late 19th century, the *Zionist movement* began organized Jewish immigration to Palestine, seeking to establish a Jewish homeland; this migration increased substantially under British rule after World War I.
- The British *Mandate for Palestine* (1920–1948), granted by the League of Nations after Ottoman collapse, promised both a Jewish national home and protection of Palestinian Arab rights—a fundamental contradiction that fueled conflict.
- The *1948 Arab-Israeli War* followed Israel's declaration of independence and resulted in the displacement of hundreds of thousands of Palestinians (the *Nakba*, meaning "catastrophe"), establishing a refugee crisis that persists.
- The *West Bank* and *Gaza Strip* remain disputed territories: the West Bank is partially governed by the Palestinian Authority under Israeli military control; Gaza is administered by Hamas and has been under Israeli blockade since 2007.
- The Israeli-Palestinian conflict involves competing territorial, national, and religious claims, with the status of Jerusalem, Israeli settlements, Palestinian refugees, and borders remaining unresolved despite decades of negotiations.

## summary

**Ankara**, historically recognized as **Angora** and during classical antiquity as **Ancyra**, is the capital city of Turkey and the nation's second-most populous urban center after *Istanbul*. Situated in central *Anatolia* along the rocky hills of the *Anatolian Plateau*, the city was selected by *Mustafa Kemal Atatürk* in 1923 to serve as the administrative, military, and diplomatic hub of the new secular republic following the disintegration of the *Ottoman Empire*. Today, it stands as a major center for national government, scientific research, and higher education, housing iconic landmarks such as the *Anıtkabir* mausoleum, the ancient *Temple of Augustus and Rome*, and world-renowned archaeological institutions.

## you gotta know

- Selected as the national capital of the newly declared Republic of Turkey in October 1923 by *Mustafa Kemal Atatürk*, deliberately replacing imperial *Istanbul* to signify a clean break with the collapsed *Ottoman Empire* after [[world-war-i]].
- Houses the colossal limestone mausoleum complex known as the *Anıtkabir*, constructed atop the hill of *Rasattepe* to entomb Atatürk, featuring the monumental *Lion Road* and an extensive ceremonial courtyard.
- Preserves the *Monumentum Ancyranum*, an inscription carved into the walls of the classical *Temple of Augustus and Rome* that represents the most complete surviving bilingual copy of the *Res Gestae Divi Augusti* from the [[roman-empire]].
- Serves as the historical namesake of several celebrated long-haired domestic animal breeds, including the *Angora goat* prized for luxurious *mohair*, the wool-bearing *Angora rabbit*, and the white *Angora cat*.
- Site of the decisive 1402 *Battle of Ankara*, in which the Central Asian conqueror *Timur* defeated and captured the Ottoman ruler *Sultan Bayezid I*, triggering a destructive decade-long civil war known as the *Ottoman Interregnum*.
- Home to the premier *Museum of Anatolian Civilizations*, located within restored 15th-century Ottoman bazaar buildings near the citadel, which displays priceless Neolithic treasures from *Çatalhöyük* and bronze artifacts from the *Hittites*.
- Initiated extensive modern forestry and agricultural modernization projects on the arid steppe, most notably the *Atatürk Forest Farm and Zoo*, which functions alongside regional botanical sites modeled on a classic [[arboretum]] to green the capital.
- Anchored historically by the hilltop *Citadel of Ankara* (*Ankara Kalesi*), an ancient fortress originally fortified by the *Galatians* and later expanded by successive Byzantine and Seljuk rulers overlooking the historic *Ulus* quarter.

## connections

- [[middle-east]] — serves as a dominant political and administrative center bridging southeastern Europe and western Asia.
- [[roman-empire]] — flourished as the thriving administrative capital of the Roman province of *Galatia*.
- [[world-war-i]] — became the military focal point of the Turkish National Movement after Allied forces occupied Istanbul following the conflict.
- [[europe]] — maintains crucial diplomatic, strategic, and treaty relationships across the continent through Turkish state institutions.
- [[cold-war]] — anchored southeastern NATO defense pacts and intelligence coordination countering the influence of the Soviet Union.
- [[arboretum]] — inspired central Anatolian urban greening initiatives that cultivate diverse collections of acclimated woody tree specimens.
- [[archipelago]] — contrasts the continental interior of Anatolia with Turkey's maritime frontier across the Aegean [[archipelago]].

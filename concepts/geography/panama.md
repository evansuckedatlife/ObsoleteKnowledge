## summary

**Panama** is a transcontinental nation situated on the narrow isthmus connecting North and Central America to *South America*. Renowned for the *Panama Canal*, which bisects the country to connect the *Atlantic Ocean* to the Pacific Ocean, it represents one of the most critical geopolitical choke points in international trade. The country transitioned from a Spanish colonial transit hub and a department of Colombia to an independent republic heavily shaped by modern maritime commerce.

## you gotta know

- Sits on the *Isthmus of Panama*, the narrow strip of land that formed roughly three million years ago, creating the land bridge between the Americas.
- Bisected by the 51-mile *Panama Canal*, completed by the *United States* in 1914 after a failed nineteenth-century French effort led by *Ferdinand de Lesseps*.
- Declared independence from Colombia in 1903 with naval and financial backing from the *United States* under *Theodore Roosevelt*.
- Administered along the canal strip via the *Panama Canal Zone*, until the 1977 *Torrijos-Carter Treaties* provided for full Panamanian sovereignty on December 31, 1999.
- Invaded by the *United States* in December 1989 in *Operation Just Cause* to depose de facto military ruler and indicted drug trafficker *Manuel Noriega*.
- Features the dense, roadless wilderness of the *Darién Gap* along its southern border with Colombia, interrupting the Pan-American Highway.
- Its capital, *Panama City*, lies at the Pacific entrance of the canal and contains the historic colonial district of *Casco Viejo*.
- The national currency is the *balboa*, pegged at parity with the United States dollar, honoring explorer *Vasco Núñez de Balboa*, who crossed the isthmus to sight the Pacific Ocean in 1513.

## connections

- [[south-america]] — the continent to its immediate southeast, separated politically by the rugged *Darién Gap*.
- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the body of water accessed on the northern Caribbean side through ports like *Colón*.
- [[united-states]] — imperial and commercial power that constructed the canal and occupied the Canal Zone for decades.
- [[spain]] — colonial empire that utilized the Camino Real across the isthmus to transport Incan silver from the Pacific to the Caribbean.
- [[colonialism]] — historical framework governing centuries of European and American exploitation of the strategic transit route.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — historical era marked by *Vasco Núñez de Balboa*'s historic crossing of the isthmus.

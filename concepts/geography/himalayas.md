## summary

The **Himalayas** form the world's highest mountain range, stretching across Asia for roughly 1,500 miles and serving as the geographical spine of South Asia. Home to Mount Everest and dozens of peaks exceeding 23,000 feet, they separate the Tibetan Plateau from the Indian subcontinent and act as a climatic barrier that shapes weather patterns across the continent. For centuries, these mountains have challenged explorers and mountaineers while remaining central to the cultures, religions, and geopolitics of the region.

## you gotta know

- Contains Mount Everest (29,032 feet), Earth's highest mountain; also K2, Kangchenjunga, and Lhotse among the world's tallest peaks.
- Formed by the collision and ongoing compression of the Indian and Asian tectonic plates, making it geologically young and still rising.
- Forms the source region for major rivers including the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Indus, which sustain billions of people across South Asia.
- Divides South Asia from Central Asia and the Tibetan Plateau; the range passes through or borders Pakistan, India, Nepal, Bhutan, and China.
- Home to the Sherpa people of Nepal, who have become legendary as mountaineering guides and porters on Everest expeditions.
- Site of numerous mountaineering achievements and tragedies; the 1953 Everest expedition represented a major postcolonial triumph for Britain and Nepal.
- Encompasses extreme ecological zones ranging from tropical forests in foothills to alpine tundra and permanent ice fields; contains unique flora and fauna adapted to high altitude.
- The range acts as a monsoon barrier, causing heavy precipitation on southern slopes while creating rain shadows that produce arid conditions on the Tibetan Plateau side.
- Historically significant for ancient trading routes including the Silk Road; Buddhist monasteries and pilgrimage sites dot the Himalayan valleys.
- Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay's 1953 Everest ascent marked the first confirmed summit of Earth's highest peak; subsequent expeditions have documented climate change effects including glacier retreat.

## connections

- [[mount-everest]] — the world's highest peak, located in the Himalayas between Nepal and China.
- [[k2]] — the second-highest mountain globally, situated in the Karakoram subrange adjacent to the main Himalayas.
- [[india]] — the subcontinent sheltered to the south by the range.
- [[nepal]] — a nation straddling the central Himalayas and home to Sherpa culture.
- [[pakistan]] — bordered by the Himalayas to the north, shaping its geography and history.
- [[ganges-river]] — originates in the Himalayan glaciers and flows across northern India.
- [[central-asia]] — separated from South Asia by the Himalayan barrier.

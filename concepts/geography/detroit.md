## summary

**Detroit** is the largest and most populous city in the state of Michigan, located along the Detroit River directly across the international border from Ontario, *Canada*. Celebrated worldwide as the historic cradle of the American automotive industry and the birthplace of the revolutionary *Motown* sound, the city transformed manufacturing during the twentieth century through mass assembly line production. Its trajectory from early French fur-trading outpost to industrial powerhouse, and its subsequent navigation of post-industrial contraction, make it a defining touchstone of American social and urban history.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1701 by the French explorer *Antoine de la Mothe Cadillac* as *Fort Pontchartrain du Détroit* to dominate Great Lakes fur trade routes.
- Known internationally as the *Motor City* due to housing the global headquarters and pioneering factories of Ford, General Motors, and Chrysler.
- Earned the moniker the *Arsenal of Democracy* during [[world-war-ii]] after manufacturing plants converted rapidly into mass producers of tanks, jeeps, and B-24 bombers.
- Served as the home of *Berry Gordy Jr.*'s *Motown Records*, which integrated American popular music through artists like *Stevie Wonder* and *The Supremes*.
- Acted as a major final terminus on the Underground Railroad, ferrying escaped enslaved people across the Detroit River into [[canada]].
- Experienced intense social upheaval and the 12th Street Riot in 1967, which accelerated white flight, suburban divestment, and municipal population decline.
- Underwent the largest municipal bankruptcy in United States history in 2013, followed by targeted downtown restructuring and revitalization initiatives.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — nation where the city developed into a core center of modern industrial manufacturing, organized labor, and culture.
- [[canada]] — neighboring sovereign country lying directly south across the international waterway, accessed via the Ambassador Bridge.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — global economic transition that reached an apex of assembly line mass production and labor organization here.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — twentieth-century struggle highlighted locally by the Great March on Detroit where *Martin Luther King Jr.* delivered an early version of his famous address.
- [[world-war-ii]] — total global conflict for which the city's automotive factories mobilized unprecedented industrial war materiel.
- [[france]] — European kingdom whose early eighteenth-century explorers established the original colonial fort and settlement.
- [[american-civil-war]] — conflict in which Michigan regiments mustered and anti-slavery networks coordinated transport across the Canadian border.

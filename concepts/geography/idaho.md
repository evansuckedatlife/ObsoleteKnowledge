## summary

**Idaho**, popularly known as the **Gem State**, is a mountainous inland state situated in the *Pacific Northwest* region of the [[united-states]]. Admitted to the Union in 1890 as the forty-third state, it is celebrated for its vast expanses of pristine wilderness, expansive volcanic plateaus, and thriving agricultural industry led by commercial potato farming. Characterized by the rugged peaks of the *Northern Rocky Mountains* in its panhandle and the crescent sweep of the *Snake River Plain* across its southern expanse, it occupies a pivotal place in American geography.

## you gotta know

- Contains the deepest river canyon in *North America*, [[hells-canyon]], carved along its remote western frontier by the *Snake River*.
- Capital and most populous metropolitan center is *Boise*, home to the distinctive blue turf of *Albertsons Stadium* and the scenic *Boise River Greenbelt*.
- Nicknamed for producing seventy-two different types of precious and semi-precious stones, including rare star garnets found almost exclusively within its borders.
- Contains the largest contiguous area of federally protected roadless wilderness in the lower forty-eight states, the *Frank Church–River of No Return Wilderness*.
- Features dramatic volcanic geology at *Craters of the Moon National Monument and Preserve*, where astronaut crews trained to identify lunar volcanic rocks.
- Crossed by the historic *Lewis and Clark Expedition*, which navigated the treacherous *Lolo Pass* with the vital guidance of *Sacagawea* and the *Shoshone*.
- Established as a formal territory by President *Abraham Lincoln* in 1863 during the [[american-civil-war]], following major gold rushes near *Pierce* and *Idaho City*.
- Encompasses a narrow northern panhandle bordered by *Washington*, *Montana*, and the international border with [[canada]] along the forty-ninth parallel.

## connections

- [[hells-canyon]] — immense river gorge marking the state's western border with *Oregon*.
- [[united-states]] — federal republic that admitted the territory into statehood on July 3, 1890.
- [[columbia-river]] — vast drainage basin whose largest tributary, the *Snake River*, traverses the entire state.
- [[canada]] — northern neighbor sharing an international border with the northern tip of the panhandle.
- [[american-civil-war]] — historical conflict that prompted congressional organization of the original territory.
- [[rocky-mountains]] — dominant mountain system that forms the spine and continental boundary of eastern portions of the state.

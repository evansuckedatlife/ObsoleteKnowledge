## summary

**Belarus**, officially the **Republic of Belarus** and historically referred to in English as **Byelorussia** or **White Russia**, is a landlocked Eastern European nation bordered by Russia, Ukraine, Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia. Formerly a constituent republic of the [[soviet-union]], it declared national sovereignty in 1990 and formal independence in 1991 following the dissolution of the Soviet state. Composed predominantly of flat lowlands, extensive marshlands, and dense pine and birch forests, Belarus occupies a strategic geopolitical transit corridor between Western [[europe]] and Russia, an exposed position that brought immense military devastation across successive centuries.

## you gotta know

- Minsk is the capital and largest city, situated on the Svislach River, almost completely rebuilt after [[world-war-ii]] in sweeping Soviet Neoclassical and Stalinist architectural styles along its central Independence Avenue.
- The Belovezha Accords were signed in December 1991 at a state dacha in the Belovezhskaya Pushcha forest by the leaders of Belarus, Russia, and Ukraine, effectively terminating the [[soviet-union]] and launching the Commonwealth of Independent States.
- Belovezhskaya Pushcha National Park, shared across the western frontier with Poland, protects Europe's largest remaining tract of primeval lowland forest and serves as the primary sanctuary for the European bison, or wisent.
- The Brest Fortress, located at the confluence of the Bug and Mukhavets rivers, earned the title of "Hero Fortress" for its garrison's desperate, protracted defense against the surprise German assault during Operation Barbarossa in 1941.
- Alexander Lukashenko has held the presidency continuously since 1994, retaining a largely state-controlled economy and authoritarian security apparatus that have led commentators and diplomats to label the nation "Europe's last dictatorship."
- The 1986 Chernobyl disaster in Soviet Ukraine showered roughly seventy percent of its radioactive fallout over Belarus, severely contaminating southern regions like Gomel and creating the Polesia State Radioecological Reserve.
- The Pripet Marshes, or Pinsk Marshes, encompass the broad floodplain of the Pripyat River in southern Polesia, constituting the largest wetland wilderness in Europe and posing a formidable barrier to invading armies throughout history.
- Architectural landmarks include the UNESCO-inscribed Mir Castle Complex and the residential palace of the noble Radziwiłł family in Nesvizh, illustrating Renaissance, Baroque, and defensive Gothic styles in the Grand Duchy of Lithuania.

## connections

- [[soviet-union]] — existed as the Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic, a founding member of the United Nations in 1945.
- [[world-war-ii]] — lost an estimated quarter of its entire population during the Nazi German occupation and brutal anti-partisan pacification campaigns.
- [[europe]] — situated within the Eastern European Plain along key transport routes between Moscow and Warsaw.
- [[cold-war]] — served as the heavily fortified Belorussian Military District on the western front of the Warsaw Pact.
- [[germany]] — invaded and devastated Belarusian lands during both world wars, inspiring fierce local partisan resistance movements.
- [[st-petersburg]] — imperial capital from which the Tsarist Russian Empire absorbed Belarusian territories during the eighteenth-century Partitions of Poland.

## summary

**Vancouver** is a bustling coastal seaport city situated in the Lower Mainland region of southwestern British Columbia, `[[canada]]`. Positioned between the Strait of Georgia and the rugged North Shore Mountains, it ranks as the most populous metropolitan area in western Canada and the country's primary Pacific commercial gateway. Renowned for its scenic natural setting, dense skyline, and deep Pacific Rim integration, the city functions as a global metropolis celebrated for livability, diverse immigrant communities, and sustainable urban design.

## you gotta know

- Named in honor of Captain *George Vancouver*, the British naval officer who explored and charted Burrard Inlet and the Pacific Northwest coast in 1792 on behalf of the `[[british-empire]]`.
- Originated around a makeshift tavern erected in 1867 by riverboat pilot *Gassy Jack* Deighton, giving rise to the surrounding settlement and current historic district of *Gastown*.
- Selected in the 1880s as the Pacific terminus of the transcontinental Canadian Pacific Railway, linking western ocean trade through `[[winnipeg]]` to the industrial markets of eastern Canada.
- Home to *Stanley Park*, a world-famous 1,000-acre public park occupying a densely forested peninsula surrounded by English Bay and Burrard Inlet, encircled by an iconic seawall and featuring totem poles at Brockton Point.
- Developed the influential urban planning model known internationally as *Vancouverism*, characterized by slender residential towers over commercial podiums, public view corridors, and extensive transit.
- Served as the host city for the *2010 Winter Olympic Games*, partnering with the nearby mountain resort community of Whistler to stage ice, sliding, and snow competitions.
- Operates as one of North America's foremost film and television production centers, widely designated "Hollywood North" due to its extensive studio infrastructure and varied regional landscapes.
- Contains an exceptionally diverse population, featuring one of the continent's oldest historic Chinatowns and large communities representing the broader Asia-Pacific diaspora.

## connections

- [[canada]] — the sovereign confederation in which it serves as the premier western seaport and economic engine of British Columbia.
- [[british-empire]] — global maritime empire represented by George Vancouver during his voyages of exploration.
- [[winnipeg]] — mid-continental rail hub connecting the Pacific port to eastern rail lines and industrial manufacturing centers.
- [[the-maple-leaf-train]] — cross-border eastern passenger route linked across the continent via transcontinental passenger services.
- [[united-states]] — adjacent southern neighbor sharing cross-border transit links along the Pacific Northwest corridor.
- [[world-war-ii]] — conflict that prompted coastal fortifications and the forced internment of Japanese-Canadians from the coast.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical era of steam navigation and transcontinental rail construction that transformed the settlement into a world port.

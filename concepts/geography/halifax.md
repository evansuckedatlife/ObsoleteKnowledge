## summary

**Halifax** (officially the **Halifax Regional Municipality**) is the provincial capital and largest metropolitan center of Nova Scotia, situated along the rugged southeastern coastline of [[canada]] facing the open waters of the [[atlantic-ocean]]. Built around one of the world's deepest and largest natural ice-free harbors, the settlement was founded in the mid-eighteenth century by the [[british-empire]] to serve as an imperial counterweight to the French fortress at Louisbourg. The port city quickly developed into a paramount British naval station and trade gateway, playing an indispensable logistical role in conflicts ranging from the [[american-revolution]] to the world wars. Halifax remains the cultural, economic, and maritime defense anchor of Atlantic Canada, celebrated for its historic waterfront, naval heritage, and vibrant academic institutions.

## you gotta know

- Devastated on December 6, 1917, by the catastrophic *Halifax Explosion*, an accidental wartime blast that occurred when the French munitions carrier *SS Mont-Blanc* collided with the Norwegian relief vessel *SS Imo*, killing approximately two thousand people.
- Sits along the shores of *Halifax Harbour*, an expansive, ice-free natural deepwater inlet guarded by the prominent star-shaped military stronghold atop *Citadel Hill* (*Fort George*).
- Served as the primary staging and coordination center for recovery efforts following the 1912 sinking of the *RMS Titanic*, with over one hundred recovered victims interred at the local *Fairview Lawn Cemetery*.
- Established in 1749 by British aristocrat *Edward Cornwallis* as an imperial garrison and naval base engineered to challenge French maritime supremacy in the North Atlantic.
- Functioned as the chief North American assembly point for allied transatlantic supply convoys during both [[world-war-i]] and [[world-war-ii]], sheltering merchant vessels from submarine wolfpacks.
- Absorbed thousands of civilian refugees and British loyalists following the conclusion of the [[american-revolution]], establishing a substantial community of United Empire Loyalists and Black Loyalists.
- Houses the Atlantic fleet headquarters of the Royal Canadian Navy at *CFB Halifax*, sustaining its centuries-long tradition as the foremost naval base in British North America.
- Welcomed dozens of diverted international flights and thousands of stranded commercial airline passengers at nearby *Halifax Stanfield International Airport* during *Operation Yellow Ribbon* in September 2001.

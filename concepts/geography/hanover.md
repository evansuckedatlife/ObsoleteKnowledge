## summary

**Hanover** is a major city in northern [[germany]] and the capital of the federal state of Lower Saxony, situated along the Leine River at the junction of the North German Plain and the Central German Uplands. Long celebrated as an influential seat of the House of Welf, the city gave its name to the royal *House of Hanover*, which ruled Great Britain in personal union for over a century following the Act of Settlement. In modern European geography and commerce, the city stands as a premier rail crossroads and global trade fair hub, famously hosting the annual industrial trade show *Hannover Messe*.

## you gotta know

- Capital and largest city of the German state of Lower Saxony (*Niedersachsen*), built along the banks of the river *Leine*.
- Entered a personal union with the British crown in 1714 when its elector, *George Louis*, ascended the British throne as King *George I*.
- The personal union with Great Britain ended in 1837 upon the accession of Queen *Victoria*, because Salic Law barred females from ruling the German kingdom.
- Polymath philosopher and mathematician *Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz* lived and worked in the city for four decades as court librarian and councillor.
- Featured the famed baroque *Herrenhausen Gardens*, one of the most meticulously preserved formal royal garden complexes in [[europe]].
- Suffered extensive devastation from Allied bombing raids during [[world-war-ii]], after which city planners pioneered modern post-war urban traffic concepts.
- Hosts the *CeBIT* technological exposition and *Hannover Messe*, long recognized among the world's most prominent commercial trade exhibitions.

## connections

- [[germany]] — modern federal republic in which the city serves as the regional capital of Lower Saxony.
- [[europe]] — continent whose dynastic, military, and economic developments the city helped shape over centuries.
- [[england]] — realm joined to the Hanoverian court in personal union throughout the eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries.
- [[british-empire]] — worldwide imperial domain governed by the Hanoverian royal monarchs from 1714 to 1901.
- [[enlightenment]] — philosophical and scientific movement enriched by Hanoverian court intellectual *Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz*.
- [[world-war-ii]] — twentieth-century global conflict that brought intensive strategic bombing and heavy destruction to the city's rail yards.
- [[world-war-i]] — European conflict that culminated in the dissolution of the historic Prussian province and German imperial monarchy.

## summary

**Cameroon**, officially the **Republic of Cameroon**, is a Central African nation situated at the crossroads of West and Central Africa along the Gulf of Guinea. Often described as *Africa in miniature*, the country contains every major climatic and ecological zone of the continent, from tropical rainforests and volcanic peaks to savanna and Sahelian steppes. Its capital is Yaoundé, while its economic engine and largest seaport is Douala on the *[[atlantic-ocean]]* coast.

## you gotta know

- Frequently termed *Africa in miniature* due to its unmatched diversity of ecosystems, topography, climates, and over 250 distinct ethnic groups.
- Borders *[[nigeria]]* to the west, *[[chad]]* to the northeast, the Central African Republic to the east, and Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, and the Republic of the Congo to the south.
- Lake Chad in the far north is bordered by four nations: *[[nigeria]]*, *[[niger]]*, *[[chad]]*, and Cameroon.
- Mount Cameroon, an active stratovolcano near the coast known locally as *Fako*, represents the highest peak in Central Africa and the Cameroon Volcanic Line.
- Lake Nyos and Lake Monoun are notorious volcanic crater lakes that experienced catastrophic limnic eruptions in the 1980s, releasing clouds of lethal carbon dioxide.
- Named from the Portuguese *Rio dos Camarões* (River of Prawns) by explorer Fernando Po after encountering swarms of ghost shrimp in the Wouri River.
- Experiencing prolonged internal tension known as the Anglophone Crisis, stemming from colonial partition between French and British administrative mandates following World War I.

## connections

- [[atlantic-ocean]] — the ocean bounding Cameroon's southwestern coastal plain along the Bight of Biafra.
- [[colonialism]] — the European imperial scramble that led to German colonization, followed by French and British League of Nations mandates.
- [[linguistics]] — field relevant to the country's dual official status of French and English and hundreds of indigenous languages.
- [[nigeria]] — its populous northwestern neighbour with whom it historically disputed the oil-rich Bakassi Peninsula.
- [[chad]] — northeastern neighbour sharing the basin and receding waters of Lake Chad.

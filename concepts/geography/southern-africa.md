# Southern Africa

## summary

**Southern Africa** is the southernmost subregion of the *African* continent, encompassing *South Africa*, *Namibia*, *Botswana*, *Zimbabwe*, *Lesotho*, *Eswatini*, and surrounding territories, characterized by the *Kalahari Desert*, vast mineral wealth, and a history of colonialism, racial segregation, and dramatic political transformation. The region's 21st-century trajectory has been shaped by the legacies of European settlement and apartheid.

## you gotta know

- *South Africa*, the region's largest and most developed country, was colonized by the *Dutch* (17th century) and then *British*, leading to a settler-colonial society dominated by *European* minorities over *African* majorities.
- The *Kalahari Desert* occupies much of *Botswana* and *Namibia*, creating arid landscapes inhabited by the *San people*; it is one of Africa's largest deserts and shaped human settlement patterns historically.
- *Apartheid* (1948–1994) was South Africa's systematic racial-segregation regime, enforcing racial hierarchy through law and violence; it provoked international isolation, internal resistance, and became a symbol of racism to the world.
- *Nelson Mandela*, a resistance leader imprisoned for 27 years, emerged as the face of anti-apartheid struggle; his release and election as president (1994) marked the transition to majority-rule democracy and became an international symbol of reconciliation.
- The region is rich in minerals: *South Africa* dominates gold and diamonds; *Namibia* and *Botswana* contain rich diamond deposits; this wealth attracted colonial exploitation and later drove geopolitics.
- The *Zambezi River* forms borders between countries and powered major infrastructure projects like the *Kariba Dam*; it is central to the region's ecology and hydroelectric power.
- Post-apartheid Southern Africa has experienced economic inequality, political instability in some countries (*Zimbabwe* under *Mugabe*), and continued social challenges; *South Africa* remains the region's economic powerhouse but faces inequality and corruption.

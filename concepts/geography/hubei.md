## summary

**Hubei** is a landlocked province in central [[china]], situated within the middle basin of the *Yangtze River* north of *Dongting Lake*. Its provincial capital and economic core is the sprawling megacity of *Wuhan*, historically created by merging the three ancient riverside settlements of *Wuchang*, *Hankou*, and *Hanyang*. Historically prominent as the heartland of the ancient state of *Chu* and the staging ground for the revolution that ended imperial rule, the province remains an essential transportation crossroads and manufacturing powerhouse.

## you gotta know

- The *Wuchang Uprising* of October 1911 erupted in its capital, igniting the *Xinhai Revolution* that rapidly overthrew the *Qing* dynasty and established the Republic of [[china]].
- Its capital city, *Wuhan*, is a massive conurbation centered at the confluence of the *Han River* and the *Yangtze River*, long celebrated as the thoroughfare of nine provinces due to its central transport links.
- The monumental *Three Gorges Dam*, the world's largest hydroelectric power station, spans the *Yangtze River* near the western prefecture-level city of *Yichang*.
- The *Wudang Mountains* in northwestern Hubei serve as a renowned sanctuary of *Daoism*, celebrated for complex cliffside temple architecture and the development of internal martial arts like *Tai Chi*.
- During the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods, the region formed the core of the state of *Chu*, developing distinct bronze casting techniques, ornate lacquerware, and the poetry of *Qu Yuan*.
- The famous *Tomb of Marquis Yi of Zeng*, excavated at *Suizhou*, yielded an extraordinary collection of sixty-five bronze chime bells cast in the fifth century BCE that demonstrate advanced acoustic engineering.
- Western Hubei contains the dense, biodiverse wilderness of *Shennongjia*, an ancient forest reserve famous in folklore for sightings of the legendary *Yeren* or wildman.
- The ancient walled city of *Jingzhou* served as a critical strategic prize fought over by *Liu Bei*, *Sun Quan*, and *Cao Cao* during the tumultuous *Three Kingdoms* era.

## connections

- [[china]] — the sovereign nation in which this central province forms a primary economic hub.
- [[world-war-ii]] — site of the grueling four-month *Battle of Wuhan* in 1938, where Chinese forces defended the wartime provisional capital against invading Japanese troops.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — transformed early modern Chinese industry when official *Zhang Zhidong* established the *Hanyang Iron Works* and *Hanyang Arsenal* in the late nineteenth century.
- [[colonialism]] — influenced the treaty port era through the foreign concessions forcibly established in *Hankou* following unequal treaties.
- [[southeast-asia]] — connected via extensive southern trade networks and cultural diffusion from the historic *Yangtze River* watershed.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — linked globally when its inland river ports opened to international steam navigation and European mercantile exchange.

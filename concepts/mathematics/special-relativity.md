## summary

**Special relativity** is Albert Einstein's 1905 theory of space, time, and motion at speeds comparable to light. It resolves the paradox posed by the [[michelson-morley-experiment]]—that the speed of light is constant in all reference frames—by redefining the geometry of spacetime itself. Mathematically, it unifies space and time into a four-dimensional manifold, revolutionising physics and mathematics alike.

## you gotta know

- The two postulates: the laws of physics are identical in all inertial frames, and the speed of light is constant (c ≈ 3 × 10^8 m/s) in every reference frame (independent of the observer's motion).
- Time dilation: moving clocks run slow by a factor of γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²); as v → c, γ → ∞, meaning time nearly stops at light speed.
- Length contraction: moving objects shrink along their direction of motion by the same factor γ; perpendicular dimensions are unaffected.
- The Lorentz transformation relates coordinates (x, y, z, t) in one inertial frame to coordinates in another, replacing Galilean transformations and preserving the speed of light.
- Einstein's mass-energy relation E = mc² shows mass and energy are interchangeable; rest energy alone exceeds any kinetic energy achievable from ordinary speeds.
- Spacetime is a 4-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifold with metric ds² = −c²dt² + dx² + dy² + dz²; geometry replaces force as the language of physics.
- Henri Poincaré independently derived Lorentz transformations and recognised the 4D structure; Einstein's insight was the physical interpretation and the mass-energy equivalence.
- Simultaneity is relative: events simultaneous in one frame are not simultaneous in another; this breaks the Newtonian assumption of universal time.
- The speed of light is a cosmic speed limit; nothing with mass can accelerate to light speed, only approach it asymptotically as energy approaches infinity.

## connections

- [[henri-poincare]] — proved Lorentz invariance and developed the mathematical framework for special relativity before Einstein's physical interpretation.
- [[albert-einstein]] — formulated the physical theory and derived E = mc² from it.
- [[michelson-morley-experiment]] — the null result that motivated special relativity; the absence of aether drift.
- [[lorentz-transformation]] — the mathematical transformation between inertial reference frames.
- [[spacetime]] — the unified 4-dimensional continuum of special relativity.
- [[calculus]] — the mathematical language for expressing rates and transformations in relativity.

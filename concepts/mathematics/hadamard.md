## summary

*Jacques Salomon* **Hadamard** was a preeminent mathematician from *[[france]]* whose influential career spanned complex analysis, analytic number theory, differential geometry, and partial differential equations. In 1896, he achieved international renown by proving the asymptotic distribution of primes, working independently alongside *Charles-Jean de la Vallée Poussin*. Beyond pure analysis, his deep inquiries into functional analysis, Cauchy problems, and mathematical psychology shaped twentieth-century science.

## you gotta know

- Independently proved the *[[prime-number-theorem]]* in 1896 by showing that the *Riemann zeta function* has no zeros along the vertical line where the real part equals one.
- Introduced the concept of *well-posed problems* in partial differential equations, demanding existence, uniqueness, and continuous dependence on initial data.
- Proved *Hadamard's inequality* for matrices, bounding the determinant by the product of the Euclidean norms of its column vectors.
- Named square matrices with entries in positive or negative one and mutually orthogonal rows, known as *Hadamard matrices*, which are essential to modern coding theory.
- Formulated the *Hadamard three-circle theorem*, establishing the convexity of the logarithm of the maximum modulus of an analytic function on concentric circles.
- Investigated chaotic dynamics in geodesic flows on surfaces of negative curvature, laying initial foundations for modern dynamical systems.
- Authored *The Psychology of Invention in the Mathematical Field*, analyzing how intuition, mental imagery, and unconscious incubation drive creative discovery.

## connections

- [[france]] — his homeland where he conducted research at the *Collège de France* and the *École Polytechnique*.
- [[prime-number-theorem]] — his landmark analytical breakthrough describing the asymptotic density of prime numbers.
- [[limit]] — analytic foundation required to examine asymptotic convergence and the zeros of the zeta function.
- [[linear-algebra]] — mathematical field using Hadamard determinants and orthogonal matrix structures.
- [[vector-space]] — underlying geometric setting for Hilbert spaces, orthogonal vectors, and functional transformations.
- [[exponential-function]] — vital functions governing entire complex functions and Hadamard factorization theorems.

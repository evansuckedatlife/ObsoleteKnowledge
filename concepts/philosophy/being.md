## summary

**Being** is the most fundamental concept in metaphysics and ontology: the abstract quality of existing or having reality. *Heidegger* made the question of Being itself central to philosophy, asking what it means "to be" rather than assuming the answer. In *Neoplatonism*, being flows hierarchically from *the One*, the ultimate principle from which all reality emanates. Being remains philosophy's most elusive and essential topic.

## you gotta know

- *Heidegger* reversed metaphysical tradition by making "the question of Being" (*die Seinsfrage*) the foundation of all philosophy — most philosophers assume what Being is rather than asking the question.
- For *Heidegger*, Being is not a thing but the condition enabling intelligibility; Being is revealed through human existence (*Dasein*), whose fundamental mode is being-in-the-world.
- *Parmenides* (ancient Greece) argued that "Being is" and "Non-being is not," making Being eternal, unchanging, indivisible, and absolute — a radical challenge to common sense and foundational to Western metaphysics.
- *Neoplatonism* developed a hierarchical metaphysics of being emanating from *the One*: the transcendent, ineffable source, followed by the divine intellect, the world-soul, and physical matter.
- The distinction between Being (abstract existence or intelligibility) and beings (concrete existing things) is central to ontology; medieval philosophers struggled with this distinction.
- Heidegger's concept of Being as a dynamic process of *revealing-and-concealing* (*aletheia*) differs from traditional substance metaphysics treating Being as static presence.
- Contemporary debates about Being explore whether it is a univocal concept applicable to all existing things or an equivocal term used differently across domains of reality.

## connections

- [[martin-heidegger]] — made Being (*Sein*) the central question in twentieth-century philosophy, arguing previous thinkers forgot or obscured the question of Being itself.
- [[phenomenology]] — philosophical method Heidegger developed to investigate how Being reveals itself through conscious experience and human existence.
- [[metaphysics]] — the broader discipline fundamentally concerned with asking what exists and what Being means in the most general sense.
- [[neoplatonism]] — ancient hierarchical metaphysics of Being emanating from *the One* as ultimate source, influencing medieval Christian theology.
- [[existentialism]] — built on Heidegger's Being-focused approach to philosophy, treating human existence (*Dasein*) as the privileged access point to Being.
- [[parmenides]] — pre-Socratic ancient Greek founder of Being-based metaphysics, arguing Being is eternal and unchanging.

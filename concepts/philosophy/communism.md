## summary

**Communism** is a political and economic ideology aiming to establish a classless society where the means of production are collectively owned and wealth is distributed according to need. Though rooted in *Marxist* theory, communism became primarily a political movement in the twentieth century, realizing various forms of state control through revolution and party apparatus. It emerged as a practical political force with *Lenin's* Russian Revolution, reshaping global politics and defining the Cold War era.

## you gotta know

- Theoretically derives from *Marx's* historical materialism — the claim that capitalism inevitably produces class conflict leading to its replacement by a communist society.
- *Lenin* adapted Marxism into a practical revolutionary strategy, arguing that the Communist Party would lead the working class to revolution rather than waiting for capitalism's internal collapse.
- The Soviet Union became the first major communist state (1922–1991), attempting to build communism through centralized state control and collective ownership.
- Communist states typically combine Marx's economic theory with authoritarian political control, creating totalitarian systems in practice despite communism's theoretical aim of stateless society.
- Communism and capitalism became the opposing ideologies defining the Cold War, with communist and capitalist powers competing for global influence.
- Different national forms emerged (Soviet communism, Maoist communism, Eurocommunism), each adapting theory to local conditions while maintaining commitment to common ownership and classless society.

## connections

- [[marxism]] — the theoretical and philosophical foundation of modern communism, providing historical and economic analysis of capitalism's inevitable collapse.
- [[lenin]] — architect of the first communist revolution and state, adapting Marxist theory into a practical strategy for seizing power.
- [[class-struggle]] — the central dynamic force communism claims drives historical change and justifies revolutionary action against capitalist classes.
- [[russian-revolution]] — the political event (1917) that realized communism as a practical state power, creating the Soviet Union as the first major communist regime.
- [[socialism]] — often conceived as an intermediate stage toward full communism, involving workers' control and state ownership during the transition period.
- [[totalitarianism]] — the political form communist states historically assumed in practice, despite theoretical aims of dissolving state power.
- [[capitalism]] — the opposing economic system communism seeks to replace through revolution and the abolition of private property and markets.

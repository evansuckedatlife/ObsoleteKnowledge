## summary

**Cogito ergo sum** is the foundational philosophical proposition formulated by [[descartes]] to establish an indubitable ground for human knowledge. First articulated in seventeenth-century [[france]], the insight affirms that the very act of doubting or thinking necessarily guarantees the immediate existence of the thinking subject. This principle launched the trajectory of modern [[rationalism]] and fundamentally reoriented Western philosophy around the conscious mind.

## you gotta know

- Appeared first in French as *Je pense, donc je suis* in the 1637 work *Discourse on the Method*, before appearing in Latin in *Principles of Philosophy*.
- Formulated as the culmination of radical methodological doubt, which systematically discarded sensory evidence, mathematical truths, and potential deception by an evil demon.
- Serves as the bedrock proposition in *Meditations on First Philosophy*, where [[descartes]] defines the self primarily as a thinking thing.
- Provided the immediate springboard for substance [[dualism]], conceptually severing the thinking mind from physical matter.
- Anticipated by *Saint Augustine* in *The City of God* through the ancient formulation *Si fallor, sum* ("If I am mistaken, I exist").
- Criticized by subsequent thinkers such as *Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz* and *David Hume*, who questioned whether the inference of a persistent substantial self goes beyond the immediate flow of thought.

## connections

- [[descartes]] — French philosopher who conceived the phrase as the cornerstone of his entire metaphysical system.
- [[rationalism]] — epistemological movement that prioritized innate ideas and intellectual deduction above sensory experience.
- [[dualism]] — ontological doctrine derived from the separation of the thinking mind from extended bodily matter.
- [[mind-body-problem]] — enduring philosophical dilemma concerning the relationship between mental states and physical processes.
- [[consciousness]] — subjective awareness whose immediate presence remains undeniable through the *cogito*.
- [[enlightenment]] — intellectual era that drew profound inspiration from Cartesian appeals to subjective reason.
- [[france]] — nation where Cartesian philosophy emerged and profoundly shaped modern intellectual traditions.

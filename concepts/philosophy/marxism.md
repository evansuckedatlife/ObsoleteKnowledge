# Marxism

## summary

**Marxism** is the political, economic, and philosophical ideology derived from *Karl Marx*'s writings, positing that capitalism contains internal contradictions that inevitably lead to its collapse and replacement by communism. Marxism combines historical materialism (the theory that material conditions and economic systems determine society), class analysis (society divided between owners and workers), and revolutionary praxis as the mechanism for social transformation.

## you gotta know

- Marx argued that capitalism's fundamental contradiction is between capital (concentrated in the hands of owners) and labor (performed by workers); capitalists accumulate wealth by extracting *surplus value*—the difference between what workers produce and what they are paid.
- *Historical materialism* claims that economic systems (feudalism, capitalism, communism) evolve in response to internal contradictions and technological change; this economic base determines the political and cultural superstructure, not the reverse.
- Marx distinguished between socialism (a transitional state where the working class controls the means of production) and communism (a classless, stateless society where resources are distributed according to need).
- Marxist theory predicts that capitalism will eventually collapse due to its contradictions: falling profit rates, increasing inequality, periodic crises, and the growing class consciousness of workers—culminating in proletarian revolution.
- *Class struggle* is the motor of history; Marx saw all history as driven by conflict between oppressed and oppressor classes (slave/master, serf/noble, worker/capitalist), with each revolution advancing material conditions.
- Marxism has been interpreted and adapted in many ways: *Leninism* (revolutionary vanguard party), *Stalinism* (socialism in one country), *Maoism* (peasant-based revolution), *Eurocommunism* (democratic socialism), and various academic Marxisms.
- Marxism remains contested; supporters argue it explains capitalist exploitation and inequality; critics argue its predictions have failed, its historical applications produced authoritarian regimes, and it reduces human society to economic determinism.

## summary

**Ballet Theatre**, officially known today as *American Ballet Theatre* (*ABT*), is a premier classical dance institution founded in *New York City* in 1939 by *Lucia Chase* and *Richard Pleasant*. Established as an ambitious American alternative to touring European companies, it became celebrated for maintaining classic 19th-century story ballets alongside bold innovations in *modern-dance* and theatrical choreography. Designated as America's National Ballet Company by an act of Congress, it has historically served as a vital home for pioneering choreographers including *Jerome Robbins*, *Antony Tudor*, *Agnes de Mille*, and *Twyla Tharp*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1939 by *Lucia Chase* and *Oliver Smith*, launching its historic inaugural season at the Center Theatre in *New York City* in January 1940 with Mikhail Fokine's *Les Sylphides*.
- Conceived under director *Richard Pleasant* with a democratic vision resembling a living museum of dance, dividing repertoire into classical, contemporary, and ethnic wings.
- Premiered landmark Americana masterworks such as Agnes de Mille's *Rodeo* and Jerome Robbins's breakout sailors-on-leave classic *Fancy Free*.
- Provided an artistic platform for British master *Antony Tudor*, who revolutionized dramatic psychological ballet with creations like *Pillar of Fire* and *Jardin aux Lilas*.
- Hosted world-renowned defecting Soviet dancers during the *cold-war*, famously serving as the artistic home and directorship base for virtuoso *Mikhail Baryshnikov*.
- Collaborated across popular styles by staging groundbreaking contemporary cross-genre crossovers, commissioning choreographers like *Twyla Tharp* to fuse classical technique with popular music.
- Elevated numerous iconic American principal dancers to international prominence, including *Nora Kaye*, *Cynthia Gregory*, and historic principal *Misty Copeland*.

## connections

- [[ballet]] — the foundational classical tradition that forms the bedrock of the company's repertoire and technique.
- [[modern-dance]] — the 20th-century movement vocabulary integrated into the company's repertory alongside classical forms.
- [[jerome-robbins]] — resident choreographer who debuted his breakthrough ballet *Fancy Free* with the company in 1944.
- [[twyla-tharp]] — modern choreographer whose crossover ballets like *Push Comes to Shove* brought a new voice to the troupe.
- [[billy-joel]] — pop singer-songwriter whose catalog was choreographed into the hit dance musical *Movin' Out* by Tharp.
- [[broadway-dancers]] — performers across commercial theatre whose technical lineage frequently intersects with classical repertory training.
- [[new-york-city]] — the metropolitan cultural center that has served as the company's permanent headquarters and principal stage.

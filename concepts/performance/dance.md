## summary

**Dance**, together with the universal human act of rhythmic **dancing**, is an expressive performing art defined by purposefully structured bodily movements unfolding through space, time, and physical energy. Spanning millennia across ancestral religious ceremonies, social recreation, and formal stage presentation, it evolved from courtly spectacles into rigorous classical idioms such as *[[ballet]]* and innovative twentieth-century forms like *[[modern-dance]]*. As both an independent aesthetic pursuit and a vital kinetic engine in opera and *[[musical-theatre]]*, it communicates complex narratives, emotional depths, and cultural identities without spoken language.

## you gotta know

- Formal theatrical choreography in Western culture was systematically codified during the seventeenth century under *King Louis XIV* of *[[france]]*, who founded the Académie Royale de Danse.
- Early twentieth-century pioneers including *Isadora Duncan* and *Ruth St. Denis* broke away from restrictive classical conventions, emphasizing natural locomotion, bare feet, and personal spiritual expression to initiate *[[modern-dance]]*.
- *[[martha-graham]]* established an influential training discipline rooted in the fundamental visceral opposition of bodily contraction and release, crafting visceral works exploring primal emotional states.
- *[[george-balanchine]]* transformed twentieth-century concert performance by pioneering neoclassical choreography, stripping away ornate narrative trappings to prioritize musical precision, clean lines, and athletic speed.
- Avant-garde visionary *[[merce-cunningham]]* emancipated stage movement from musical accompaniment, employing radical chance operations, everyday pedestrian action, and spatial decentralization.
- *[[alvin-ailey]]* synthesized modern technique, classical elegance, and African American cultural heritage, creating the 1960 American masterpiece *Revelations* set to spirituals and gospel hymns.
- Boundary-pushing choreographer *[[twyla-tharp]]* seamlessly bridged elite concert stages and popular commercial culture by blending classical rigors with jazz, pop music, and street movement.
- Narrative story works throughout history, exemplified by *Sergei Prokofiev's* classical score for *[[cinderella]]*, illustrate the art form's profound capacity to translate character, romance, and dramatic conflict into pure physical action.

## connections

- [[ballet]] — the highly technical, centuries-old performance discipline characterized by turnout, pointed feet, and codified positions.
- [[modern-dance]] — the expressive twentieth-century rebellion that prioritized gravity, floor contact, and personal emotional authenticity over academic restraint.
- [[martha-graham]] — the towering modern choreographer whose contraction-and-release technique redefined theatrical body movement.
- [[merce-cunningham]] — the post-modern master who decoupled movement from narrative structure and musical composition.
- [[alvin-ailey]] — the influential director whose company elevated African American cultural themes into global performance staples.
- [[twyla-tharp]] — the eclectic innovator who revolutionized crossover choreography between modern concert dance and Broadway productions.
- [[cinderella]] — the legendary rags-to-riches fairy tale adapted into full-length classical story ballets around the globe.
- [[musical-theatre]] — the multifaceted stage art that blends kinetic choreography, acting, and musical composition into unified storytelling.

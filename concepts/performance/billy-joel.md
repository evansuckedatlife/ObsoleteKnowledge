## summary

**Billy Joel**, known universally as *The Piano Man*, is one of the most successful American singer-songwriters, pianists, and composers of the 20th century. Born in the Bronx and raised on Long Island in *New York City*, his rich storytelling and narrative pop songs bridged rock, classical melody, and theatrical craft. His extensive song catalog crossed directly into elite concert dance and *musical-theatre* when director-choreographer *Twyla Tharp* conceived the Tony Award-winning Broadway dance musical *Movin' Out*, highlighting the versatility of modern American performance.

## you gotta know

- Achieved his breakout signature success in 1973 with the autobiographical storytelling ballad *Piano Man*, chronicling eccentric patrons at an executive lounge.
- Released critically acclaimed multi-platinum albums including *The Stranger* and *52nd Street*, the latter winning the Grammy Award for Album of the Year.
- Collaborated with legendary choreographer *Twyla Tharp* to create *Movin' Out*, a 2002 Broadway dance musical utilizing his songs to tell the story of Long Island youth during the Vietnam War.
- Won the Tony Award for Best Orchestrations alongside Stuart Malina for reinterpreting his music for the live dance ensemble of *Movin' Out*.
- Undertook a historic 1987 concert tour of the *Soviet-Union* during the *cold-war*, becoming one of the first major Western pop-rock artists to stage full arena concerts in Moscow and Leningrad.
- Composed the 2001 classical piano collection *Fantasies & Delusions*, recorded by pianist Richard Joo, demonstrating his deep immersion in traditional European classical composition.
- Established an unprecedented record-breaking monthly residency at Madison Square Garden, completing over 150 lifetime performances at the iconic arena.

## connections

- [[musical-theatre]] — the theatrical world where his pop compositions were adapted into narrative Broadway choreography in *Movin' Out*.
- [[twyla-tharp]] — innovative choreographer who used Joel's songbook as the direct kinetic score for her hit Broadway dance piece.
- [[ballet-theatre]] — classical institution long associated with Tharp's crossover choreography that shares performance lineage with her theatrical work.
- [[broadway-dancers]] — the virtuoso performers whose acrobatic physical skill brought his music to life without spoken dialogue in *Movin' Out*.
- [[new-york-city]] — his birthplace, thematic muse, and longtime artistic residence, celebrated throughout songs like *New York State of Mind*.
- [[united-states]] — the nation whose postwar working-class suburban experiences form the predominant lyrical subject of his oeuvre.
- [[cold-war]] — the geopolitical conflict during which his landmark 1987 Russian tour fostered cultural exchange across the Iron Curtain.

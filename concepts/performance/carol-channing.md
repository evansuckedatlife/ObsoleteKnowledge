## summary

**Carol Channing**, born **Carol Elaine Channing**, was an iconic American actress, singer, and comedienne whose outsized stage persona and unmistakable rasping contralto made her a legend of [[broadway-musicals]]. Born in Seattle and rising to national prominence in mid-century [[new-york-city]], she achieved theatrical immortality by originating indelible roles that shaped the trajectory of commercial [[musical-theatre]]. Her extraordinary stamina and dedication to live performance cemented her status as one of the most enduring figures in the entertainment history of the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Originated the role of *Dolly Gallagher Levi* in the 1964 *Jerry Herman* musical *Hello, Dolly!*, an interpretation so definitive that she played the part in more than five thousand performances over several decades.
- First catapulted to international stardom portraying the diamond-loving flapper *Lorelei Lee* in the 1949 Broadway production of *Gentlemen Prefer Blondes*, popularizing the song *"Diamonds Are a Girl's Best Friend"*.
- Defeated *Barbra Streisand* in *Funny Girl* to win the 1964 *Tony Award* for Best Actress in a Musical, sparking one of the most famous awards-season rivalries in theater history.
- Received a *Tony Award for Lifetime Achievement* in 1995 in recognition of her unprecedented longevity and dedication to touring regional venues across the nation.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress and won a Golden Globe for portraying *Muzzy Van Hossmere* in the 1967 musical film *Thoroughly Modern Millie*.
- Inducted into the *Grammy Hall of Fame* for the original Broadway cast recording of *Hello, Dolly!*, which famously unseated *The Beatles* from the top of the pop album charts.
- Performed as a passionate advocate for arts education throughout her later years, establishing the *Channing-Kullijian Foundation* to preserve arts programs in American public schools.

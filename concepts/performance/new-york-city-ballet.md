## summary

**New York City Ballet** (NYCB) is an American ballet company founded in 1948 by choreographer *George Balanchine* and Lincoln Kirstein, establishing New York as a global center for ballet innovation. The company pioneered a distinctly American approach to classical ballet: faster-paced, stripped of narrative ornamentation, and grounded in pure movement. It remains one of the world's most influential ballet institutions, defining 20th-century dance aesthetics.

## you gotta know

- Co-founded by *George Balanchine*, a Russian émigré choreographer who revolutionized ballet technique and aesthetics.
- Established in New York during the Cold War era, asserting American cultural leadership in classical dance.
- Created a signature style emphasizing musicality, speed, and geometric precision, departing from narrative-heavy Russian Imperial ballet.
- *Jerome Robbins* co-founded the company and served as ballet master, creating major works that blended classical training with American vernacular movement.
- Collaborated extensively with composer *Igor Stravinsky*, commissioning and premiering numerous ballets that defined the late-20th-century canon.
- Developed the "Balanchine body" — a training methodology emphasizing long lines, turnout, and the illusion of weightlessness.
- Known for rapid-fire choreography and rapid ballet speeds that pushed technical demands far beyond traditional European conventions.
- Remains a training ground for dancers who influence ballet companies globally, maintaining Balanchine's choreographic legacy.

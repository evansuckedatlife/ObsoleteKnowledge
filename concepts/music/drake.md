## summary

**Drake** is a Canadian rapper, singer, and cultural tastemaker whose atmospheric fusion of introspective hip-hop, contemporary R&B, and dancehall reshaped popular music throughout the 2010s and 2020s. First rising to public notice as an actor on the teen drama *Degrassi: The Next Generation*, he signed with Lil Wayne's Young Money Entertainment to release definitive albums including *Take Care* and *Nothing Was the Same*. Holding unprecedented records for the most charted songs in Billboard Hot 100 history, his prolific output and melodic delivery remain prominent benchmarks across modern cultural trivia.

## you gotta know

- Standout commercial powerhouse who shares the upper tier of international streaming records with contemporaries such as [[ed-sheeran]].
- Pioneered the ambient Toronto sound alongside longstanding producer Noah "40" Shebib, defined by downtempo drum loops, filtered keyboard pads, and moody confessional lyrics.
- Reached historic streaming milestones with ubiquitous singles like *God's Plan*, *One Dance*, and *Hotline Bling*, consistently dominating global radio programming.
- Signed to Lil Wayne's Young Money imprint following the influential 2009 mixtape *So Far Gone*, which fundamentally blurred the stylistic division between rapping and singing.
- Established his independent record label and lifestyle brand *OVO Sound* (October's Very Own), which spearheaded the global popularization of contemporary Canadian hip-hop.
- Engaged in high-profile, culture-defining hip-hop rivalries, most notably his multi-phase lyrical exchanges with Pusha T and a climactic 2024 feud with Kendrick Lamar.
- Regularly incorporates international styles such as UK drill, Afrobeats, and Caribbean dancehall over steady four-beat frameworks governed by [[4-time-signature]].

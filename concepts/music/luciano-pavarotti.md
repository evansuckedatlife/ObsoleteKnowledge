## summary

**Luciano Pavarotti** was an Italian tenor whose extraordinary voice and charismatic stage presence made him the most famous opera singer of the late twentieth century. Born in Modena, he dominated the tenor repertoire from the 1960s onward and brought opera to global popular audiences through recordings, television, and crossover performances. His signature role—Calaf in Puccini's *Turandot*—and his definitive recording of "Nessun dorma" remain defining moments in operatic recording history.

## you gotta know

- Made his professional debut in 1961 and rose to prominence through the 1960s and 1970s at La Scala Milan and other major houses.
- Possessed an exceptionally bright, easily soaring spinto tenor voice with uncommon staying power and projection.
- "Nessun dorma" (None Shall Sleep) from *Turandot* became his signature aria; his 1972 recording defined the role for modern audiences.
- Known for his gift for promotion: he used television and recordings to build a mass audience for opera at a time it was declining.
- Collaborated frequently with conductor Herbert von Karajan and soprano Joan Sutherland, creating legendary performances together.
- Performed the role of Rodolfo in *La Bohème* over 1,000 times throughout his career, making it second nature.
- A cultural ambassador who brought opera singers into collaboration with pop musicians and performed at major international events.

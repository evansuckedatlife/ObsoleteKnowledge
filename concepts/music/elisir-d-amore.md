## summary

***L'elisir d'amore*** (*The Elixir of Love*) is a two-act comic opera (*melodramma giocoso*) composed by the Italian master *Gaetano Donizetti* with a libretto by *Felice Romani*. Premiered in Milan, [[italy]], in 1832, it stands as one of the most frequently performed and enduring exemplars of the bel canto operatic tradition. The plot follows the poor, naive peasant *Nemorino*, who purchases a supposed magical love potion from a traveling charlatan to win the heart of the wealthy landowning beauty *Adina*.

## you gotta know

- Features the world-famous [[tenor]] romanza *Una furtiva lagrima* ("A furtive tear"), sung by *Nemorino* in Act II after he notices a tear in *Adina*'s eye and realizes she loves him.
- Libretto written by *Felice Romani*, who adapted it from *Eugène Scribe*'s French libretto for *Daniel Auber*'s opera *Le philtre*.
- Composed in great haste by *Gaetano Donizetti*, who reportedly wrote the entire score in just a few weeks before its triumphant debut at the Teatro della Canobbiana in Milan.
- Introduces the itinerant quack doctor *Dulcamara*, a classic bass-baritone buffo role who sells ordinary Bordeaux wine to villagers as a cure-all and magical love draught.
- Follows the rivalry between the shy *Nemorino* and the swaggering, conceited sergeant *Belcore*, who arrives with a military garrison to court *Adina*.
- Contains the dramatic turning point where *Nemorino* enlists as a soldier in *Belcore*'s regiment purely to earn twenty scudi to buy another bottle of the elixir.
- Concludes with *Adina* buying back *Nemorino*'s military contract and confessing her true love, while *Dulcamara* claims credit for the miracle and departs amid public celebration.

## summary

**Shania Twain** (born **Eilleen Regina Edwards**) is a Canadian singer-songwriter who revolutionized both country and mainstream pop music during the late twentieth century. Universally acclaimed as the "Queen of Country Pop," she dismantled commercial and stylistic boundaries by marrying traditional Nashville lyricism with massive arena-rock production techniques developed alongside producer *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*. Her landmark 1997 release *Come On Over* became the best-selling studio album by a female solo artist and the best-selling country album in global recorded music history, fundamentally altering the trajectory of modern pop crossover.

## you gotta know

- Achieved massive international crossover fame through multi-genre chart anthems including *"Man! I Feel Like a Woman!"*, *"That Don't Impress Me Much"*, and the acclaimed ballad *"You're Still the One"*.
- Her blockbuster 1997 album *Come On Over* sold more than forty million copies worldwide, earning double Diamond certification in the [[united-states]] and remaining the best-selling country record in history.
- Pioneered a punchy, arena-rock country sound in collaboration with producer and former husband *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*, renowned for producing hard-rock acts like *Def Leppard* and *AC/DC*.
- Scored her major breakthrough with the 1995 album *The Woman in Me*, which yielded hits such as *"Any Man of Mine"* and established her independence and bold visual style in music videos.
- Released the ambitious 2002 album *Up!*, which was issued in three distinct sonic configurations: a green disc for country, a red disc for pop-rock, and a blue disc for international dance mixes.
- Raised in severe poverty in Timmins, Ontario, she began singing in local community clubs at age eight to support her family before assuming custody of her younger siblings following her parents' deaths in 1987.
- Contracted Lyme disease in the early 2000s, causing severe dysphonia and vocal nerve damage that sidelined her touring career until extensive surgical reconstruction enabled her return.
- Recipient of five Grammy Awards, twenty-seven BMI Songwriter awards, and induction into the Canadian Music Hall of Fame and Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame.

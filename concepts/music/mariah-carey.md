## summary

**Mariah Carey** is an American singer, songwriter, record producer, and actress renowned for her astonishing five-octave vocal range, virtuosic whistle register, and influential fusion of pop, contemporary R&B, and hip-hop. Emerging in [[new-york-city]] in the early 1990s, she amassed nineteen number-one singles on the Billboard Hot 100 in the [[united-states]], surpassing all other solo artists in chart history. Referred to as the "Songbird Supreme" by Guinness World Records, her melismatic singing technique and perennial holiday anthem *All I Want for Christmas Is You* have secured her place as one of music's all-time iconic divas.

## you gotta know

- Holds the all-time record for the most Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles by a solo artist with nineteen chart-toppers, standing second only to The Beatles overall.
- Celebrated for an extraordinary five-octave vocal range categorized as a coloratura [[soprano]], distinguished by nimble melismatic runs and unprecedented mastery of the whistle register.
- Pioneered the modern pop and hip-hop crossover with her 1995 remix of *Fantasy*, which featured Ol' Dirty Bastard and set the template for subsequent collaborations between pop singers and rappers.
- Began her chart career with a historic run of five consecutive debut singles reaching number one on the Hot 100, beginning with her 1990 breakthrough track *Vision of Love*.
- Co-wrote and recorded the emotive ballad *One Sweet Day* with Boyz II Men in 1995, maintaining an uninterrupted sixteen-week reign atop the Hot 100 that stood as the longest in chart history for twenty-three years.
- Staged one of the greatest career resurgences in modern pop music with her 2005 multi-platinum album *The Emancipation of Mimi*, led by the Grammy-winning R&B masterpiece *We Belong Together*.
- Authored and performed the ubiquitous 1994 holiday classic *All I Want for Christmas Is You*, which routinely returns to number one worldwide every December and earned her the moniker the "Queen of Christmas".
- Inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2022, honoring her role as the primary writer or co-writer on eighteen of her nineteen chart-topping singles.

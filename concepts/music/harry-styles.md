## summary

**Harry Styles** (full name **Harry Edward Styles**) is an English singer, songwriter, and actor who rose to international prominence as a member of the boy band *One Direction* before launching a globally celebrated solo career. Emerging from the television vocal competition *The X Factor* in 2010, he transformed from a teen idol into an eclectic pop and rock star renowned for vintage musicality, flamboyant sartorial flair, and massive commercial appeal. His acclaimed solo albums, including *Fine Line* and *Harry's House*, cemented his reputation as one of the definitive cultural figures of modern British and American popular music.

## you gotta know

- Swept the Grammy Awards in 2023 with his third studio album *Harry's House*, winning both Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album while dominating charts in the [[united-states]].
- Achieved his biggest international chart triumph with the synth-pop lead single *As It Was*, which spent fifteen weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100 and broke global streaming records.
- Broke into the music industry in 2010 after auditioning as a solo contestant on *The X Factor* in [[england]], where judge Simon Cowell grouped him with four other contestants to form the pop sensation *One Direction*.
- Earned critical acclaim and his first Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance with the sunny funk-pop anthem *Watermelon Sugar*, praised for its infectious [[rhythm]] and summer groove.
- Debuted his solo recording career in 2017 with the epic, piano-driven rock ballad *Sign of the Times*, which drew favorable comparisons to British glam rock and topped the UK Singles Chart.
- Undertook the marathon *Love On Tour* from 2021 to 2023, performing nearly two hundred stadium and arena shows worldwide and establishing one of the top ten highest-grossing concert tours in music history.
- Branched into cinema with an acting role in Christopher Nolan's acclaimed [[world-war-ii]] epic *Dunkirk* in 2017, later securing leading parts in *Don't Worry Darling* and *My Policeman*.
- Broke traditional gender conventions in celebrity fashion by becoming the first solo male cover star of American *Vogue* in 2020, reflecting a broader trend of stylistic [[modernism]] through skirts and high-fashion tailoring.

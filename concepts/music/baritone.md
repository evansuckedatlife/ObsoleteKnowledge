## summary

A **baritone** is a male singing voice ranging roughly from the A below middle C to the A one octave above, sitting between the higher tenor and the lower bass in the male vocal spectrum. The baritone is one of the most common male voice types in Western classical music and opera, celebrated for its combination of vocal weight, dramatic resonance, and lyrical expressiveness. Baritones have historically sung both heroic leading roles and complex secondary characters, reflecting their vocal versatility and dramatic range.

## you gotta know

- Baritone ranges fall naturally into subtypes: *lyric baritone* (lighter, more agile, suited to romantic roles), *dramatic baritone* (heavier, powerful, for heroic antagonists and tragic figures), and *bass-baritone* (very low, overlapping with the bass register).
- The baritone voice is prized in opera for its dramatic intensity and warmth; famous operatic roles include Verdi's *Rigoletto* (a deformed jester) and *Count Almaviva* (a seductive aristocrat) from Mozart's *The Marriage of Figaro*.
- Unlike the soprano or tenor, which dominate opera's leading romantic roles, baritones often sing antagonists, fathers, mentors, and complex moral characters—roles requiring vocal weight and dramatic gravitas.
- Historical baritone roles have shifted in importance; in 18th-century opera, baritones were secondary, but by the 19th century (especially with Verdi), the baritone became a starring vocal type with major dramatic roles.
- Famous operatic baritones include *Tito Gobbi*, *Leonard Warren*, *Sherrill Milnes*, and *Dmitri Hvorostovsky*, who achieved superstar status comparable to the most celebrated tenors and sopranos.
- In musical theater, the baritone is the most common male singing voice and often plays the protagonist or love interest; in choral music, baritones form the bridge between the tenor's brightness and the bass's depth.
- The baritone voice sits at a natural resonance point in the male spectrum, making it suited to both powerful dramatic declaration and intimate lyrical expression.

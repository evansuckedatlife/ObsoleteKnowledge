## summary

**Bronze Age Greece** designates the advanced, interconnected third- and second-millennium BCE civilizations that flourished across mainland Greece, the Cycladic islands, and the island of *Crete*. Dominated in sequence by the maritime *Minoan civilization* and the militaristic, palace-centered *Mycenaean civilization*, this era established foundational networks of Mediterranean commerce, monumental cyclopean engineering, and early Aegean administration. The era provided the historical, archaeological, and cultural backdrop for ancient Greek heroic mythology, inspiring the monumental epics and royal lineages remembered throughout classical antiquity.

## you gotta know

- Divided archaeologically into three distinct regional cultures: Early, Middle, and Late Helladic on the mainland; Cycladic in the Aegean island archipelago; and Minoan on the island of *Crete*.
- Anchored in Crete by the Minoans, whose unfortified palace complexes at *Knossos*, *Phaistos*, and *Malia* exhibited sophisticated hydraulic engineering, vibrant frescoes, and the undeciphered administrative script *Linear A*.
- Characterized on the mainland by the Mycenaeans, who built heavily fortified citadels with massive "cyclopean" masonry at *Mycenae*, *Tiryns*, and *Pylos*, entered through monumental portals like the famous *Lion Gate*.
- Produced the earliest deciphered European written records in *Linear B*, an administrative syllabary preserved on unbaked clay tablets that linguist *Michael Ventris* demonstrated in 1952 to be an archaic dialect of Mycenaean Greek.
- Excavated during the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries by *Heinrich Schliemann* at Mycenae and *Tiryns* (where he unearthed shaft graves and the golden *Mask of Agamemnon*) and by *Sir Arthur Evans* at Knossos.
- Buried its warrior elites in dramatic architectural tombs, progressing from deep shaft graves to majestic beehive-shaped subterranean stone chambers known as *tholoi*, most notably the Treasury of Atreus.
- Collapsed catastrophically around 1200 BCE during the wider *Bronze Age Collapse*, causing the burning of the palace centers, the loss of literacy, and the onset of the centuries-long Greek Dark Ages.
- Preserved within the cultural memory of classical Greece as a legendary golden age of heroes, supplying the narrative settings for tales of *Minos*, the *Minotaur*, *Daedalus*, *Theseus*, and the Trojan War.

## connections

- [[ancient-greece]] — the classical Mediterranean civilization whose language, religion, and foundational myths originated in these Bronze Age cultures.
- [[bronze-age-collapse]] — the catastrophic systemic breakdown around 1200 BCE that obliterated Mycenaean palace administration and depopulated the Aegean.
- [[middle-east]] — the regional world of ancient empires like Egypt and Babylon with which Aegean elites traded copper, tin, gold, and luxury goods.
- [[poetry]] — the oral hexameter epic tradition through which Homeric bards transmitted memories of Mycenaean warfare, armor, and kingship.
- [[tragedy]] — the classical Athenian dramatic art form that derived its primary legendary plots from the dynasties of Mycenae and Thebes.
- [[bronze-inscriptions]] — a contemporaneous Eurasian practice of elite Bronze Age administrative and ritual writing on metallurgical artifacts.

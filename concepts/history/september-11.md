## summary

The **September 11 attacks**, frequently referred to as **September 11** or **9/11**, were four coordinated Islamist terrorist suicide attacks carried out by *al-Qaeda* against the [[united-states]] on the morning of September 11, 2001. Nineteen hijackers seized four commercial passenger jetliners, flying two into the Twin Towers of the *World Trade Center* in [[new-york-city]] and a third into the *Pentagon*, while the fourth crashed in a Pennsylvania field after passenger revolt. Killing 2,977 victims, the catastrophe fundamentally altered modern global history, triggering the international *War on Terror*, transforming American governance and surveillance, and prompting deep architectural re-evaluations of urban resilience and public memory.

## you gotta know

- Nineteen hijackers associated with the extremist group *al-Qaeda* commandeered four cross-country commercial flights departing from Boston, Newark, and Washington, D.C. to execute suicide strikes on major American landmarks.
- *American Airlines Flight 11* and *United Airlines Flight 175* struck the North and South Towers of the *World Trade Center* in lower Manhattan, igniting massive aviation-fuel infernos that caused both 110-story skyscrapers to collapse within two hours.
- *American Airlines Flight 77* struck the *Pentagon* in Arlington, Virginia, while *United Airlines Flight 93* crashed into an open field near Shanksville, Pennsylvania, after passengers stormed the cockpit to thwart an attack on the *United States Capitol*.
- The attacks claimed 2,977 innocent lives—surpassing *Pearl Harbor* as the deadliest assault on American soil—and caused catastrophic casualties among first responders, including 343 firefighters of the *Fire Department of the City of New York* (*FDNY*).
- President *George W. Bush* proclaimed a global *War on Terror*, articulating the *Bush Doctrine* of preemptive military action that led directly to the invasion of Afghanistan in October 2001 and the subsequent [[iraq-war]] in 2003.
- The disaster prompted the largest reorganization of the American federal government in half a century, producing the *USA PATRIOT Act*, the *Department of Homeland Security*, the *Transportation Security Administration*, and expanded surveillance programs authorized by the *Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court*.
- Federal authorities enacted the unprecedented ground stop *Operation Yellow Ribbon*, grounding all civilian air traffic across American airspace and closing Wall Street financial markets for six trading days, sparking sharp worldwide economic contractions.
- The obliteration of *Minoru Yamasaki*'s structural tube towers reshaped modern architectural engineering and memorial culture, culminating in *Daniel Libeskind*'s redevelopment master plan, the construction of the 1,776-foot *One World Trade Center* designed by architect [[david-childs]], and the subterranean reflecting pools of *Michael Arad*'s design *Reflecting Absence*.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — suffered the catastrophic domestic strikes that permanently transformed its foreign policy, intelligence apparatus, and societal landscape.
- [[new-york-city]] — lost its preeminent skyline landmark and financial district center, enduring a decade-long recovery and complex civic reconstruction.
- [[middle-east]] — experienced intense geopolitical destabilization and military interventions following the launch of the American-led global counterterrorism campaign.
- [[islam]] — became the focus of worldwide theological and political discourse surrounding militant fundamentalism versus traditional religious practice.
- [[cold-war]] — created the geopolitical crucible of the Soviet–Afghan war, during which foreign fighters formed the network that became *al-Qaeda*.
- [[modernism]] — lost an iconic structural expression when *Minoru Yamasaki*'s pioneering steel-column towers were destroyed, prompting a retreat from vulnerable minimalist mega-structures.
- [[david-childs]] — served as consulting and lead design architect for the rebuilding of *One World Trade Center*, integrating massive structural security into modern civic architecture.

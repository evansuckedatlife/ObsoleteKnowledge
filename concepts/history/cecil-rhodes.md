## summary

**Cecil Rhodes** was an English-born mining magnate, politician, and zealous imperialist who played an instrumental role in British expansion across southern Africa in the late nineteenth century. Serving as Prime Minister of the Cape Colony and founding both the *De Beers* diamond cartel and the *British South Africa Company*, he pursued an aggressive vision of British dominance stretching from Cape Town to Cairo. His aggressive policies helped trigger the Second Boer War, and his vast fortune endowed the prestigious *Rhodes Scholarship* at the University of Oxford.

## you gotta know

- Monopolized southern African diamond production by co-founding *De Beers Consolidated Mines* with financial backing from *N M Rothschild & Sons*.
- Founded the chartered *British South Africa Company* in 1889, utilizing a private military police force to annex territories north of the Limpopo River, later named Southern and Northern Rhodesia.
- Served as Prime Minister of the British Cape Colony from 1890 to 1896, introducing discriminatory legislation like the Glen Grey Act to push indigenous Africans off communal lands into wage labor.
- Champions of his memory cite his grand geopolitical ambition: building a continuous British-controlled "Cape to Cairo" railway and telegraph network spanning the African continent.
- Resigned his premiership in disgrace following the disastrous *Jameson Raid* of 1895–1896, an unsanctioned paramilitary attempt to overthrow the Boer South African Republic.
- Left an expansive will that created the *Rhodes Scholarship* at Oxford, intended to foster imperial leadership and transnational unity among English-speaking elites.
- Became the focal point of the international "Rhodes Must Fall" protest movement in the 2010s, which demanded the removal of his public statues and the decolonization of universities.

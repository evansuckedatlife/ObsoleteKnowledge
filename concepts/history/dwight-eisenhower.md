## summary

**Dwight D. Eisenhower** (1890–1969) was an American military commander and statesman who led the *Supreme Allied Command* in Europe during *World War II* and served as the 34th President of the United States (1953–1961). His presidency emphasized stability during the Cold War, infrastructure development, and a doctrine of "massive retaliation" against Soviet expansion.

## you gotta know

- Served as Supreme Commander of the Allied Forces in Europe during World War II, orchestrating the D-Day invasion and the final campaign against Nazi Germany.
- Popularized the phrase "military-industrial complex" in his farewell address, warning against the concentration of power in defense industries and the military.
- As President, launched the Interstate Highway System—one of the largest public works projects in history—nominally justified by Cold War defense needs.
- Endorsed the *domino theory* and the doctrine of "massive retaliation," which relied on nuclear weapons to deter Soviet aggression and communist expansion.
- Maintained a policy of "peaceful coexistence" with the Soviet Union while authorizing covert CIA operations in Iran and Guatemala to counter Soviet-backed movements.
- His "Open Skies" proposal to the Soviets in 1955 offered mutual air reconnaissance to reduce Cold War tensions (rejected by the USSR).
- Faced the *U-2 spy plane incident* in 1960, when a Soviet surface-to-air missile shot down an American reconnaissance aircraft, damaging Cold War thaw efforts.

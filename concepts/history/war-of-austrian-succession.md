## summary

The **War of the Austrian Succession** (1740–1748) was a sweeping pan-European and global conflict sparked by the accession of *Maria Theresa* to the hereditary Habsburg domains following the death of Emperor *Charles VI*. Contesting female succession despite the *Pragmatic Sanction of 1713*, rival European monarchs formed an aggressive coalition against Austria to dismember Habsburg territories, sparking widespread warfare across Central Europe, the Low Countries, Italy, and overseas colonial theaters. Marked by the rapid rise of the [[prussian-empire]] under *Frederick the Great* and intense imperial rivalry between the [[british-empire]] and [[france]], the war fundamentally altered the European balance of power and confirmed Prussian great-power status.

## you gotta know

- *Frederick the Great* seized the wealthy Austrian border province of *Silesia* without declaration in December 1740, initiating the Silesian Wars and catapulting the [[prussian-empire]] into the ranks of European great powers through early battlefield victories such as *Mollwitz* and *Chotusitz*.
- The war began following the death of Holy Roman Emperor *Charles VI*, whose *Pragmatic Sanction of 1713* sought to ensure that his Habsburg hereditary lands could be inherited by his daughter *Maria Theresa*, an arrangement rejected by opportunistic rivals upon his demise.
- The conflict featured the election of Bavarian Elector *Charles Albert* as *Charles VII* in 1742, briefly stripping the Holy Roman Imperial crown from the Habsburg dynasty for the only time in over three centuries until his death and the subsequent election of *Maria Theresa*'s husband, *Francis Stephen* of Lorraine (*Francis I*).
- Major continental combat centered heavily on the Austrian Netherlands, where French forces under Marshal *Maurice de Saxe* secured a renowned tactical victory over an allied British, Dutch, and Hanoverian army at the *Battle of Fontenoy* in 1745.
- Global combat spread across North America as *King George's War*, where New England provincial forces under *William Pepperrell* famously besieged and captured the strategic French citadel of *Fortress Louisbourg* on Cape Breton Island.
- Fighting erupted concurrently in India during the *First Carnatic War*, where French forces led by the Marquis *Joseph-François Dupleix* and *Bertrand-François Mahé de La Bourdonnais* captured the British settlement of *Madras* after the *Battle of Madras*.
- The conflict concluded with the 1748 *Treaty of Aix-la-Chapelle*, which restored nearly all global conquests to their status quo ante bellum—including swapping *Louisbourg* back to [[france]] in exchange for *Madras*—while guaranteeing Prussian possession of *Silesia*, leaving *Maria Theresa* determined to seek future revenge.

## connections

- [[prussian-empire]] — capitalized on the succession crisis to conquer *Silesia* and establish itself as a dominant militaristic power in Central Europe.
- [[france]] — supported Bavarian imperial ambitions and fought British-led forces across Flanders, North America, and India.
- [[british-empire]] — allied with Austria to counter French hegemony, funding continental armies while projecting naval power globally.
- [[spain]] — sought to expand Bourbon influence in northern Italy by challenging Austrian territorial control over Milan and Parma.
- [[russian-empire]] — entered late in support of Austria, marching troops toward the Rhine to hasten peace negotiations at Aix-la-Chapelle.
- [[enlightenment]] — coincided with the intellectual reign of enlightened despots, notably *Frederick II*, who applied rationalist administrative efficiency to wartime statecraft.
- [[colonialism]] — drove overseas proxy conflicts between European empires over trading posts and maritime dominance in North America and India.

## summary

The **Western Europe Campaign** (or **Western European Campaign**) was the massive Allied military offensive conducted from June 1944 to May 1945 that liberated occupied Western Europe and precipitated the fall of Nazi Germany. Initiated by the amphibious landings of [[operation-overlord]] along the Normandy coast of [[france]], the theater saw Allied armies break through entrenched German defenses, liberate Paris, and drive toward the Rhine River. Under the direction of Supreme Commander [[dwight-eisenhower]], Allied forces blunted Germany's final winter counteroffensive before advancing deep into the German heartland and linking with the [[soviet-union]]. By destroying the Wehrmacht's western armies and capturing the Ruhr industrial basin, the campaign brought an end to [[world-war-ii]] in Europe.

## you gotta know

- *Operation Overlord* and *D-Day* initiated the campaign on June 6, 1944, when Allied forces landed across five beachheads in Normandy to open a decisive second front in [[france]].
- The *[[battle-of-the-bulge]]* in December 1944 was Germany's surprise winter counteroffensive through the Ardennes, where encircled troops of the [[united-states]] resisted at Bastogne until relieved.
- The breakout from Normandy during *Operation Cobra* trapped tens of thousands of retreating German soldiers in the Falaise Pocket and enabled the liberation of Paris on August 25.
- *Operation Market Garden* in September 1944 was Field Marshal *Bernard Montgomery's* failed airborne attempt to seize Dutch bridges across the Rhine at Arnhem.
- Allied soldiers crossed the Rhine in March 1945 after seizing the intact *Ludendorff Bridge* at Remagen, trapping over three hundred thousand defenders in the Ruhr Pocket of western [[germany]].
- The *Red Ball Express* was an enormous continuous truck convoy system, operated predominantly by African American truck drivers, that supplied fuel and munitions across [[france]].
- Strategic debate pitted [[dwight-eisenhower]], who championed an advance along a broad front, against British field commanders favoring a concentrated spearhead toward the German capital.
- The campaign concluded militarily in late April 1945 when American patrols met soldiers of the [[soviet-union]] along the Elbe River near Torgau, cleaving remaining enemy territory.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that this campaign helped conclude by collapsing the European Axis.
- [[dwight-eisenhower]] — Supreme Allied Commander of the Allied Expeditionary Force who oversaw strategy and theater logistics.
- [[operation-overlord]] — the foundational amphibious invasion of Normandy that inaugurated the campaign.
- [[battle-of-the-bulge]] — the massive German winter counteroffensive in the Ardennes that depleted the Wehrmacht's remaining strategic reserves.
- [[france]] — the primary western theater where Allied forces broke German coastal defenses and liberated the civilian population.
- [[germany]] — the primary Axis power whose western defenses collapsed following the encirclement of the Ruhr Pocket.
- [[united-states]] — the Allied nation providing the bulk of motorized infantry, tactical air power, and theater supplies.
- [[soviet-union]] — the Allied power fighting on the Eastern Front that met Western armies along the Elbe River.

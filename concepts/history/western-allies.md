## summary

The **Western Allies** were the democratic coalition led primarily by the [[united-states]], the [[british-empire]], and the *Free French* forces that fought against the *Axis powers* during [[world-war-ii]]. Operating across the *Western Front*, the *Atlantic*, the *Mediterranean*, and the *Pacific*, they coordinated military strategy through the *Combined Chiefs of Staff* and high-level wartime summits. While allied with the [[soviet-union]] against [[germany]] and its partners, the Western Allies maintained a distinct political and strategic vision that shaped the post-war international order and laid the groundwork for the [[cold-war]].

## you gotta know

- Launched *Operation Overlord* on *D-Day* in June 1944 under Supreme Allied Commander *Dwight D. Eisenhower*, establishing a massive second front in Western Europe that liberated [[france]] and drove into western [[germany]].
- Agreed upon the *Europe First* strategy at the *Arcadia Conference* in late 1941, prioritizing the defeat of Nazi [[germany]] before committing full resources to crush the Empire of [[japan]].
- Executed the *Mediterranean* strategy through amphibious landings in North Africa (*Operation Torch*), Sicily (*Operation Husky*), and mainland [[italy]], grinding through fortified German defensive belts like the *Winter Line* and the *Gustav Line* to capture [[rome]].
- Waged the *Combined Bomber Offensive* against German industry, infrastructure, and cities, dividing daylight precision bombing by the [[united-states]] *Eighth Air Force* and nighttime area bombing by the *Royal Air Force Bomber Command*.
- Articulated global post-war democratic goals in the 1941 *Atlantic Charter* drafted by *Franklin D. Roosevelt* and *Winston Churchill*, establishing principles of national self-determination and free trade that later underpinned the *United Nations*.
- Sustained global allied combat operations through the immense industrial output of the [[united-states]] under *Lend-Lease*, funneling vast quantities of weapons, munitions, raw materials, and food to [[england]], the [[soviet-union]], and other allied nations.
- Maintained command coordination through the binational *Combined Chiefs of Staff*, creating an unprecedented degree of military integration between British and American armies, navies, and air corps.
- Clashed repeatedly with Soviet leader *Joseph Stalin* over post-war borders, Polish sovereignty, and the governance of occupied [[germany]] at wartime summits in *Tehran* and *Yalta*, frictions that directly precipitated the [[cold-war]].

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict in which the Western Allies mobilized to defeat the Axis powers.
- [[united-states]] — the industrial and military powerhouse that anchored the Western alliance after entering the war in 1941.
- [[british-empire]] — the global empire that resisted Axis aggression from the outset and served as the staging ground for Western European liberation.
- [[france]] — the fallen European power whose *Free French* military under *Charles de Gaulle* fought across North Africa and Western Europe.
- [[soviet-union]] — the Eastern Front partner that bore immense casualties fighting the *Wehrmacht* while maintaining an uneasy strategic coalition with the West.
- [[germany]] — the primary European Axis power whose complete unconditional surrender was the foremost operational objective of Western military campaigns.
- [[italy]] — the Axis nation where Allied armies fought protracted campaigns through mountainous defensive lines from 1943 to 1945.
- [[cold-war]] — the ensuing geopolitical conflict that emerged from ideological divisions between the Western Allies and the Soviet bloc.

## summary

**Grigory Orlov**, fully styled as **Count Grigory Grigoryevich Orlov**, was an influential Russian military officer, statesman, and the paramount favorite of Empress *Catherine the Great*. Together with his brothers, he orchestrated the bloodless palace coup of 1762 that toppled Emperor *Peter III* and elevated Catherine to the imperial throne. As a central figure of the [[russian-empire]] during the early Russian [[enlightenment]], he patronized scientific and economic reform, quelled the deadly 1771 Moscow plague riot, and famously presented the empress with the *Orlov Diamond*.

## you gotta know

- Masterminded the June 1762 coup d'état that deposed Tsar *Peter III*, securing the allegiance of the imperial guards regiments in Saint Petersburg to crown his lover *Catherine the Great* ruler of the [[russian-empire]].
- Fathered *Aleksey Bobrinsky*, Catherine's illegitimate son born shortly before the coup, who was raised in secrecy and later created a count, establishing a prominent Russian aristocratic lineage.
- Dispatched to Moscow in 1771 during a catastrophic outbreak of bubonic plague and the violent Moscow Plague Riot, where he exercised dictatorial powers to enforce quarantines, construct free public hospitals, and successfully halt the contagion.
- Presented the empress with the 189-carat *Orlov Diamond*, purchased from an Armenian merchant named *Shaffrass* after it was purportedly plundered from a temple in India, which Catherine mounted in the Imperial Sceptre of Russia.
- Co-founded the *Free Economic Society* in 1765 and served as its inaugural president, encouraging agricultural modernization and sponsoring an essay competition on the condition of Russian serfs.
- Commissioned the sprawling neoclassical *Gatchina Palace* as his personal residence and received the magnificent *Marble Palace* in Saint Petersburg as an imperial gift from Catherine in gratitude for his loyalty.
- Gradually lost political ascendancy and imperial favor in the early 1770s to *Grigory Potemkin*, eventually succumbing to severe dementia and mental illness before dying in Moscow in 1783.

## connections

- [[catherine-the-great]] — his royal mistress and sovereign whom he brought to power and served as chief advisor during her early reign.
- [[russian-empire]] — the imperial power whose court politics, military administration, and domestic affairs he directed.
- [[enlightenment]] — the philosophical and cultural movement he fostered in Russia through intellectual patronages and personal correspondence with *Jean-Jacques Rousseau*.
- [[potemkin]] — the brilliant military commander and statesman who superseded him as Catherine's favored companion and primary adviser.
- [[chesme]] — the decisive 1770 naval battle against the [[ottoman-empire]] commanded by his brother *Aleksei Orlov*.
- [[gatchina-palace]] — his vast Italianate estate outside Saint Petersburg designed by architect *Antonio Rinaldi*.
- [[marble-palace]] — the lavish Saint Petersburg palace faced with multi-colored marble erected for him by imperial decree.

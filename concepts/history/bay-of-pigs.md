## summary

The **Bay of Pigs Invasion**, also known as the battle of **Playa Girón**, was a disastrous April 1961 amphibious landing carried out by CIA-sponsored Cuban exiles attempting to overthrow the communist regime of *Fidel Castro*. Financed and orchestrated by the [[united-states]], the covert operation at the **Bay of Pigs** collapsed within three days on Cuba's southern coast when local militias and regular armed forces overwhelmed the invasion brigade. The failure profoundly humiliated the new administration of President *John F. Kennedy*, pushed Cuba irreversibly into an alliance with the [[soviet-union]], and set the geopolitical stage for the [[cuban-missile-crisis]] during the height of the [[cold-war]].

## you gotta know

- Kennedy accepted sole public responsibility for the humiliating disaster, famously remarking during a press conference that victory has a hundred fathers while defeat is an orphan.
- *Brigade 2506*, a CIA-trained paramilitary force of roughly 1,400 anti-Castro Cuban exiles, conducted the amphibious assault at *Playa Girón* and *Playa Larga* within the inlet.
- The operation unraveled early when an initial strike by U.S. *B-26 Invader* bombers disguised with Cuban insignia failed to eliminate *Fidel Castro*'s air fleet, prompting Kennedy to cancel subsequent air support to maintain plausible deniability.
- Cuban *Hawker Sea Fury* and *Lockheed T-33* aircraft sank the supply ships *Houston* and *Río Escondido*, marooning the invasion troops in coastal swamps without heavy ammunition or resupply.
- Originally approved by President *Dwight D. Eisenhower* as *Operation Pluto*, the plan was heavily revised and downscaled into *Operation Zapata* under CIA operations chief *Richard M. Bissell Jr.*
- Nearly 1,200 captured members of the brigade were imprisoned in Cuba until lawyer *James B. Donovan* negotiated their ransom and release twenty months later in exchange for 53 million dollars in baby food and medical supplies.
- The disaster prompted the *Taylor Commission* investigation, the dismissal of veteran CIA Director *Allen Dulles*, and the initiation of *Operation Mongoose* to destabilise the island through clandestine sabotage.

## connections

- [[cold-war]] — the overarching ideological struggle that motivated U.S. efforts to eliminate a Soviet-aligned outpost in the Western Hemisphere.
- [[cuban-missile-crisis]] — the direct 1962 confrontation triggered when Soviet leadership placed nuclear warheads on the island to deter another American invasion.
- [[soviet-union]] — the superpower ally whose economic and military backing solidified Cuba's socialist turn in the wake of the failed landing.
- [[united-states]] — the nation whose central intelligence apparatus organized, armed, and financed the botched operation.
- [[che-guevara]] — the Argentine revolutionary commander who helped organize Cuba's defense and publicly thanked Kennedy for politically cementing the revolution.
- [[assassination-of-jfk]] — the 1963 murder of the president whose handling of the invasion spawned lingering animosity among anti-Castro militant groups.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that shaped the careers and covert operational doctrines of the CIA planners who devised the assault.

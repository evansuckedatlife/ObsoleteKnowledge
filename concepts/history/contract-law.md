## summary

**Contract Law** is the legal body of rules and doctrines governing the formation, interpretation, performance, and enforcement of voluntary agreements between parties. Rooted in ancient civil jurisprudence and English common law, modern contract principles crystallized during the [[enlightenment]] and expanded rapidly to accommodate market transactions during the [[industrial-revolution]]. In American jurisprudence, constitutional protections such as the *Contracts Clause* elevated private agreements into foundational instruments of economic liberty and commercial stability.

## you gotta know

- Requires mutual assent, traditionally analyzed as a valid *offer* and an unequivocal *acceptance*, accompanied by bargained-for *consideration*.
- Common law tradition requires consideration, distinguishing enforceable contracts from gratuitous gifts, famously debated in landmark cases like *Hamer v. Sidway*.
- Traces foundational civil doctrines back to Roman jurisprudence compiled in [[constantinople]] within the *Corpus Juris Civilis* of *Justinian I*.
- Developed the modern doctrine of promissory estoppel, which permits reliance-based enforcement when one party changes position based on another's promise without formal consideration.
- Heavily shaped in the [[united-states]] by the *Contracts Clause* of the [[united-states-constitution]], which forbids states from passing laws impairing the obligation of contracts.
- Chief Justice *John Marshall* established strict constitutional protection for corporate charters and private agreements in *Dartmouth College v. Woodward* and *Fletcher v. Peck*.
- Codified modern sales rules in the United States through the *Uniform Commercial Code* (*UCC*) Article 2, standardizing commercial norms across states.

## connections

- [[united-states-constitution]] — Article I, Section 10 restricts state governments from retroactive interference with vested contractual duties and debts.
- [[enlightenment]] — philosophical notions of private property, legal rights, and the social contract directly shaped classic liberal contract doctrine.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — spurred the evolution of standard-form agreements, consumer warranties, and mass commercial transactions.
- [[constantinople]] — site where Emperor *Justinian I* oversaw the compilation of the *Digest* and *Institutes*, laying the framework for European civil contract systems.
- [[roman-empire]] — established primary concepts of consensual obligation, pacts, and remedies that underpin Western private law.
- [[england]] — supplied the common law precedent of *assumpsit*, consideration, and equitable remedies like specific performance.

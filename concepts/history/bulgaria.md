## summary

**Bulgaria** is a nation in Southeastern Europe occupying the eastern Balkan Peninsula, whose historical trajectory spans antiquity, imperial dominance, and prolonged foreign subjugation. Formed in 681 CE through an alliance between Slavic tribes and Bulgar nomads, the medieval state challenged the Byzantine Empire through the *First Bulgarian Empire* and *Second Bulgarian Empire*, spreading Slavic literacy and Orthodox *Christianity*. Conquered by the *Ottoman Empire* in the late fourteenth century, the country endured nearly five centuries of Ottoman rule before regaining autonomy in 1878, subsequently playing pivotal roles in the Balkan Wars, *World War I*, *World War II*, and the *Cold War*.

## you gotta know

- Joined the Central Powers in *World War I* to reverse territorial losses from the Second Balkan War, leading to defeat and the Treaty of Neuilly-sur-Seine.
- Aligned with the Axis Powers during *World War II* under Tsar Boris III, yet famously saved its domestic Jewish population from Nazi death camps while annexing southern Dobruja.
- Basil II earned the grim epithet *Boulgaroktonos* ("the Bulgar-Slayer") after crushing Tsar Samuel's army at the Battle of Kleidion in 1014 and blinding ninety-nine of every hundred captive soldiers.
- Adopted Eastern Orthodox *Christianity* in 864 under Boris I, who established the Preslav and Ohrid literary schools where the disciples of Saints Cyril and Methodius developed and propagated the early Cyrillic alphabet.
- Suffered five centuries of foreign domination under the *Ottoman Empire* that culminated in the 1876 April Uprising, whose violent Ottoman suppression caused international outrage and sparked the Russo-Turkish War of 1877–1878.
- Fell under the domination of the *Soviet Union* after a 1944 coup d'état, establishing the People's Republic of Bulgaria led for thirty-five years by communist dictator Todor Zhivkov until the regime's collapse in 1989.
- Crushed Western crusaders under Jean de Vienne and John the Fearless of [[burgundy]] alongside Ottoman forces at the fateful 1396 Battle of Nicopolis, extinguishing the last organized resistance of the *Second Bulgarian Empire*.

## connections

- [[ottoman-empire]] — subjugated the Bulgarian realm for nearly five centuries from the late 14th century until the 1878 Treaty of Berlin.
- [[world-war-i]] — member of the Central Powers whose defeat led to the abdication of Tsar Ferdinand I and major border surrenders.
- [[world-war-ii]] — Axis signatory that occupied Yugoslav and Greek territories but refused to declare war on the Soviets or deport domestic Jews.
- [[soviet-union]] — superpower patron that transformed postwar Bulgaria into one of the Warsaw Pact's most loyal satellite states.
- [[burgundy]] — western duchy whose crusading nobility under John the Fearless suffered catastrophic defeat at Nicopolis on the Danube.
- [[christianity]] — religious tradition adopted by Tsar Boris I that stimulated cultural independence and widespread Slavic scriptural literacy.
- [[cold-war]] — ideological era during which Todor Zhivkov's repressive regime closely mirrored Soviet domestic and foreign policies.
- [[roman-empire]] — ancient empire that governed the region as the frontier provinces of Moesia and Thrace prior to barbarian migrations.

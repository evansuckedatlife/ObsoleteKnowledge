## summary

**Hokkaido** is the northernmost and second-largest of the main islands of *Japan*, separated from Honshu across the deep Tsugaru Strait. Known historically to the Japanese as *Ezo* or *Yezo*, it was for centuries the autonomous homeland of the indigenous *Ainu* people before undergoing aggressive Japanese settlement and agricultural colonization during the Meiji era. The island developed into a vital northern economic and defensive frontier, marked by its cold climate, mountainous terrain, and distinct regional culture.

## you gotta know

- Historically designated as *Ezo* and primarily inhabited by the indigenous *Ainu*, who possessed a distinct language, animist spiritual tradition, and hunter-gatherer economy centered around salmon fishing and bear ceremonies.
- The southern Matsumae clan controlled early trade and political contact with the Ainu under the Tokugawa shogunate, which led to violent indigenous resistance including Shakushain's Revolt in 1669.
- Renamed **Hokkaido** in 1869 by explorer *Matsuura Takeshiro* following the Meiji Restoration, when the Meiji government established the Kaitakushi development commission to aggressively colonize and assimilate the region.
- Briefly hosted the Republic of Ezo in 1869, an unrecognised secessionist state established by Tokugawa naval loyalist *Enomoto Takeaki* and French military advisors at the star-shaped fort Goryokaku in Hakodate before its defeat.
- American agriculturalist *William S. Clark*, invited to lead the Sapporo Agricultural College, profoundly shaped the island's modern education and dairy farming, famously telling students, "Boys, be ambitious!"
- The island is connected to Honshu via the Seikan Tunnel, an engineering marvel completed beneath the Tsugaru Strait that ranks among the longest undersea railway tunnels in the world.
- The 1972 Winter Olympic Games were hosted in Sapporo, marking the first time the Winter Games were held in Asia and accelerating modern urban infrastructure across the prefecture.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the East Asian island nation of which this northern frontier forms the second-largest main island.
- [[russian-empire]] — the neighboring imperial power whose southward Pacific expansion spurred Japanese consolidation of the island.
- [[colonialism]] — the imperial expansion and assimilation policies practiced by the Meiji state against the indigenous Ainu.
- [[linguistics]] — the academic discipline that studies the endangered, language-isolate tongue spoken by the native Ainu.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global war during which naval bases and ports on the island faced Allied carrier raids.
- [[hiroshima]] — a major urban center on western Honshu whose industrial role contrasted with this agrarian frontier.

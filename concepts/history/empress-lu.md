## summary

**Empress Lü**, formally remembered as *Empress Gao of Han* or by her personal name **Lü Zhi**, was the fierce wife of the founding Han monarch *Emperor Gaozu* (*Liu Bang*) and the first woman to rule imperial [[china]] in all but name. Governing as empress dowager and grand empress dowager following her husband's death in 195 BCE, she exercised absolute authority across the reigns of three successive nominal emperors until her own death in 180 BCE. Her tenure stabilized the early Han administration and promoted agricultural recovery, though her legacy was famously darkened by the ruthless mutilation of rival concubines and the near-total purge of the Liu imperial clan in favor of her own kin.

## you gotta know

- Seized de facto command of the nascent Han dynasty after the death of her husband, *Liu Bang*, maintaining supreme imperial rule across fifteen years of de facto regency.
- Infamously ordered the horrific mutilation of her late husband's favorite concubine, *Consort Qi*, having her limbs severed, eyes gouged out, vocal cords poisoned, and dumping her into a latrine as a "human swine" (*renzhi*).
- Caused her traumatized son, *Emperor Hui*, to abandon state affairs and succumb to alcoholism and grief after forcing him to view the butchered *Consort Qi*.
- Engineered the poisoning of *Consort Qi*'s young son, *Liu Ruyi*, the Prince of Zhao, by sneaking poison into his bedchamber while her protective son was briefly away hunting.
- Appointed her nephews and brothers to paramount military commands and granted them hereditary kingdoms, breaching *Liu Bang*'s sacred oath that only members of the imperial Liu clan could be crowned kings.
- Maintained crucial fiscal moderation and rural stability by reducing burdensome land taxes, repealing the draconian Qin-era law banning private collections of canonical books, and preserving domestic peace through the *heqin* marriage alliance policy with the Xiongnu nomadic confederation.
- Precipitated the bloody *Lü Clan Disturbance* immediately upon her death in 180 BCE, wherein loyalist Han ministers and generals systematically exterminated every member of her family to restore the Liu lineage under *Emperor Wen*.

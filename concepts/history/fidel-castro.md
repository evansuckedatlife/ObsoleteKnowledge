## summary

**Fidel Castro** was a Cuban revolutionary, politician, and Marxist-Leninist statesman who governed *Cuba* as prime minister from 1959 to 1976 and subsequently as president until 2008. Overthrowing the authoritarian, American-backed military dictatorship of *Fulgencio Batista* in the *Cuban Revolution*, he established the first socialist state in the Western Hemisphere. Aligning closely with the [[soviet-union]] during the [[cold-war]], his regime withstood decades of diplomatic isolation, crippling economic trade embargoes, and covert destabilization campaigns directed by the [[united-states]]. His charismatic leadership, commitment to internationalist military intervention in Africa, and domestic reforms in education and healthcare made him one of the most polarizing and influential global figures of the twentieth century.

## you gotta know

- Spearheaded the revolutionary 26th of July Movement alongside Argentine physician *Che Guevara* and brother *Raúl Castro*, ultimately prompting dictator *Fulgencio Batista* to flee the country on New Year's Day in 1959.
- Sparked the perilous 1962 [[cuban-missile-crisis]] after secretly agreeing to station Soviet R-12 and R-14 nuclear ballistic missiles on the island, triggering a tense thirteen-day confrontation that brought humanity to the brink of nuclear war.
- Decisively repelled the April 1961 *Bay of Pigs Invasion*, personally commanding armed forces at *Playa Girón* against CIA-trained Cuban exile paramilitaries of Brigade 2506 and subsequently declaring the socialist character of the revolution.
- Survived hundreds of documented assassination plots orchestrated by the Central Intelligence Agency under covert initiatives like *Operation Mongoose*, famously evading exploding seashells, lethal thallium salts, poisoned cigars, and toxic wetsuits.
- Delivered his celebrated four-hour courtroom defense concluding with the phrase *History Will Absolve Me* during his 1953 trial following the catastrophic, failed armed assault on the *Moncada Barracks*.
- Embarked on the legendary 1956 expedition from Tuxpan, Mexico, aboard the overcrowded yacht *Granma*, surviving an initial ambush at *Alegría de Pío* to wage a successful mountain guerrilla war from the *Sierra Maestra*.
- Permitted the mass maritime migration known as the 1980 *Mariel Boatlift*, in which roughly 125,000 Cubans departed for Florida, fundamentally transforming demographic and political dynamics within the [[united-states]].

## connections

- [[cold-war]] — definitive global ideological struggle during which he aligned his island nation with the Eastern Bloc against American hegemony.
- [[soviet-union]] — indispensable economic, diplomatic, and military patron whose financial subsidies sustained the Cuban economy until its 1991 dissolution.
- [[united-states]] — neighboring superpower and principal adversary that imposed a punitive commercial embargo and sponsored repeated attempts at regime change.
- [[cuban-missile-crisis]] — critical standoff in October 1962 triggered by Soviet nuclear warhead installations that severely strained his relations with *Nikita Khrushchev*.
- [[bay-of-pigs]] — catastrophic 1961 CIA-backed invasion attempt whose rapid defeat consolidated his domestic popularity and pushed Cuba firmly into Marxist-Leninism.
- [[che-guevara]] — iconic Argentine guerrilla strategist and key lieutenant during the revolutionary insurgency who later served as president of the National Bank.
- [[spain]] — homeland of his immigrant father *Ángel Castro y Argiz*, maintaining uninterrupted diplomatic and trade relations with Havana throughout the embargo era.

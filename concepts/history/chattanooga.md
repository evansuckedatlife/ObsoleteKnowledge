## summary

**Chattanooga** is a strategic city in southeastern Tennessee situated along the Tennessee River, famed during the *American Civil War* as the vital rail gateway to the Confederate heartland. Following the disastrous Federal defeat at the *Battle of Chickamauga* in September 1863, the Union *Army of the Cumberland* retreated into the fortified city, where Confederate forces besieged them from the surrounding heights. The arrival of General *Ulysses S. Grant* to take unified command transformed the crisis into one of the most decisive Federal victories of the war. By breaking the siege in November 1863, Union forces secured the city as an impregnable base, opening the road for General *William Tecumseh Sherman*'s decisive campaign against Atlanta.

## you gotta know

- Charged up the steep face of *Missionary Ridge* on November 25, 1863, when Union soldiers under General *George H. Thomas*, sent only to take the lower rifle pits, spontaneously stormed all the way to the summit and routed General *Braxton Bragg*'s Confederate center.
- Witnessed the famed "Battle Above the Clouds" on November 24, 1863, in which Union forces led by General *Joseph Hooker* swept through thick mountain mist to seize *Lookout Mountain* and dominate the river valley below.
- Reopened vital supply lines through the establishment of the *Cracker Line*, an ingenious river-and-road transport corridor engineered by General *William F. "Baldy" Smith* that saved the starving Union garrison from imminent surrender.
- Served as the indispensable railway hub of the Western theater, where four essential lines intersected to connect Nashville, Atlanta, Memphis, and Knoxville, earning the city its reputation as the "Gateway to the Deep South."
- Prompted President *Abraham Lincoln* to create the unified *Military Division of the Mississippi* under General *Ulysses S. Grant*, leading to the relief of General *William Rosecrans* and the appointment of *George H. Thomas* to command the army.
- Functioned as the crucial marshaling ground and forward supply depot for General *William Tecumseh Sherman*'s massive army groups as they prepared for the 1864 Atlanta Campaign and the subsequent March to the Sea.
- Encircled by the distinct geographic loop of *Moccasin Bend*, whose surrounding cliffs and river narrows created formidable natural choke points that dictated the artillery positions and defensive perimeter during the two-month siege.

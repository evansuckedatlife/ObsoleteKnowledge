## summary

The **Great Purge**, also termed the **Great Terror** or the **Yezhovshchina**, was a sweeping state-sponsored campaign of political repression, mass executions, and imprisonment conducted in the [[soviet-union]] between 1936 and 1938 under *Joseph Stalin*. Orchestrated through the *NKVD* secret police, the terror aimed to consolidate autocratic control by eliminating Old Bolshevik party rivals, suspected counter-revolutionaries, ethnic minorities, and military officers. The campaign resulted in at least 700,000 recorded executions and sent over a million citizens into the *Gulag* labor camp system, deeply crippling Soviet institutions on the eve of [[world-war-ii]].

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Joseph Stalin* through the *NKVD* secret police chiefs *Genrikh Yagoda*, *Nikolai Yezhov*, and later *Lavrentiy Beria*, reaching its murderous peak during the *Yezhovshchina* period from 1937 to 1938.
- Centered publicly around three sensational *Moscow Trials*, which coerced fabricated confessions of treason, espionage, and Trotskyite-Zinovievite conspiracies from prominent revolutionary leaders like *Grigory Zinoviev*, *Lev Kamenev*, and *Nikolai Bukharin*.
- Decimated the upper command structure of the *Red Army* in 1937, leading to the summary execution of Marshal *Mikhail Tukhachevsky* and thousands of high-ranking commanders, severely weakening Soviet readiness against Nazi [[germany]].
- Institutionalized through *NKVD Order No. 00447*, which established extrajudicial three-person tribunals known as *troikas* to fulfill centralized execution and imprisonment quotas against former kulaks and political dissidents.
- Extended into extensive *national operations* that systematically targeted diasporic minority populations living along Soviet borders, including mass deportations and executions of ethnic Poles, Germans, Latvians, and Koreans.
- Triggered ostensibly by the December 1934 assassination of Leningrad party chief *Sergei Kirov*, an event *Stalin* exploited as pretext to hunt down alleged underground fifth columns and political opposition.
- Terminated abruptly in late 1938 when *Stalin* replaced *Yezhov* with *Beria*, executing *Yezhov* himself to pin the blame for excessive terror onto overzealous state security functionaries.

## connections

- [[soviet-union]] — the socialist state whose communist party apparatus, armed forces, and civil populace were devastated by the internal security apparatus.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict in which the Red Army suffered disastrous early defeats partly due to the recent liquidation of its senior officer corps.
- [[cold-war]] — the postwar geopolitical standoff whose ideological perceptions in the West were deeply shaped by exposure of Stalinist atrocities.
- [[russian-revolution]] — the 1917 Bolshevik upheaval whose original generation of revolutionary founders was systematically liquidated during the show trials.
- [[russian-empire]] — the predecessor tsarist state whose historical imperial borders and ethnic populations were aggressively reorganized and subjugated by the Soviet regime.
- [[germany]] — the aggressive fascist power whose impending threat spurred Stalinist paranoia regarding internal sabotage and espionage networks.

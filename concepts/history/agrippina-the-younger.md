## summary

**Agrippina the Younger** was a prominent Roman noblewoman and empress of the first century CE, infamous for her political ambition and ruthlessness in advancing her son's power. Born around 15 CE into one of Rome's most prominent families, she maneuvered her way to supreme influence as the wife of *Claudius* and mother of *Nero*. Her legacy is one of palace intrigue, alleged murder, and a son who would himself become one of Rome's most reviled emperors.

## you gotta know

- Granddaughter of Emperor *Augustus* and sister of the mad Emperor *Caligula*, she came from one of Rome's most powerful dynasties.
- Married her uncle *Claudius* (her mother's brother) around 49 CE—a politically ambitious marriage that made her empress.
- Allegedly poisoned *Claudius* with mushrooms to secure the throne for her son *Nero*, though accounts vary and historical certainty is elusive.
- Engineered the adoption of *Nero* as *Claudius*'s heir, sidelining the emperor's biological son *Britannicus*.
- Became one of the most powerful women in Roman history, with coins minted bearing her image alongside *Nero*'s.
- Attempted to maintain dominance over *Nero* after his accession, but he grew resentful of her influence and political interference.
- Murdered by *Nero* around 59 CE, allegedly after multiple assassination attempts, as he sought absolute control.

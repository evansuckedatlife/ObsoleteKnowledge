## summary

**Environmentalism** is a multifaceted political, philosophical, and social movement dedicated to preserving, protecting, and restoring the natural environment from the damaging consequences of human activity. Originating as an intellectual and civic reaction to the widespread industrial pollution and rapid resource exploitation of the *[[industrial-revolution]]*, it evolved from nineteenth-century wilderness preservation into an international crusade for sustainability, ecological health, biodiversity, and climate justice. Today, it constitutes a major force in multilateral diplomacy, statutory regulation, and democratic politics across the *[[united-states]]* and the modern global community.

## you gotta know

- Sparked modern mass consciousness in 1962 with the publication of *Silent Spring* by marine biologist *Rachel Carson*, which exposed the catastrophic ecological and human costs of synthetic pesticides like DDT.
- Traced philosophical roots to nineteenth-century *[[romanticism]]* and American transcendentalism, exemplified by *Henry David Thoreau*'s naturalistic reflections at Walden Pond and *John Muir*'s campaigns to establish national parks.
- Galvanised sweeping public concern in the *[[united-states]]* after high-profile industrial disasters in 1969, including the Cuyahoga River catching fire and the Santa Barbara oil spill, prompting the inaugural *Earth Day* in 1970.
- Catalysed landmark bipartisan federal environmental legislation signed by *Richard Nixon*, establishing the *Environmental Protection Agency* (EPA), the *Clean Air Act*, the *Clean Water Act*, and the *Endangered Species Act*.
- Broadened in the 1980s through the *environmental justice* movement, which demonstrated that hazardous waste facilities, toxic dumps, and chemical plants were disproportionately sited in low-income and minority neighborhoods.
- Internationalised through multilateral treaties to confront planetary degradation, such as the 1987 *Montreal Protocol* to halt ozone depletion and the 2015 *Paris Agreement* to limit greenhouse gas emissions.
- Institutionalised into parliamentary and presidential electoral politics through Green parties worldwide, notably influencing the *2000 U.S. presidential election* through the candidacy of consumer advocate *Ralph Nader*.
- Increasingly integrates indigenous ecological knowledge and climate resilience frameworks to address accelerating global warming, catastrophic weather events, deforestation, and the global biodiversity crisis.

## connections

- [[industrial-revolution]] — the historic transformation whose unchecked emissions, urban squalor, and resource exploitation provoked the ecological counter-movement.
- [[united-states]] — the nation where modern conservation policies, grassroots advocacy, and landmark federal regulatory agencies were pioneered.
- [[romanticism]] — the nineteenth-century artistic and intellectual movement that exalted pristine nature and directly challenged industrial mechanisation.
- [[cold-war]] — the tense geopolitical era when atomic fallout from atmospheric weapons testing raised public alarm over planetary contamination.
- [[dna]] — the biological breakthrough that provides the molecular tools for contemporary biodiversity conservation, genetics, and ecosystem monitoring.
- [[english-law]] — the legal tradition whose common-law doctrines of public nuisance and riparian rights provided early judicial remedies against industrial polluters.
- [[epidemic-response]] — the public health domain closely linked to environmental health, sanitary infrastructure, and the mitigation of zoonotic diseases.

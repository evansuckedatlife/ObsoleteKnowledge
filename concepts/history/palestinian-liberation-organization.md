## summary

The **Palestinian Liberation Organization** (**PLO**) is a secular nationalist political confederation established in 1964 to centralize Palestinian national resistance and advocate for an independent homeland. Under the longstanding chairmanship of *Yasser Arafat*, the coalition coordinated political diplomacy, social services, and guerrilla campaigns against [[israel]] throughout the [[cold-war]]. Recognized internationally as the official representative of the Palestinian people, it fundamentally reshaped diplomacy across the [[middle-east]] by renouncing armed militancy in 1993 to inaugurate direct peace negotiations.

## you gotta know

- Chaired from 1969 to 2004 by *Yasser Arafat*, whose leadership and iconic *keffiyeh* came to symbolize Palestinian nationalism, highlighted by his 1974 United Nations General Assembly address offering an olive branch alongside a freedom fighter's weapon.
- Exchanged the pivotal *Letters of Mutual Recognition* with Israeli Prime Minister *Yitzhak Rabin* in September 1993, formally recognizing the right of [[israel]] to exist in peace and renouncing terrorism in exchange for official recognition.
- Signed the bilateral [[oslo-accords]] on the White House lawn alongside Israeli and [[united-states]] leaders, creating the interim Palestinian National Authority to administer civil and security affairs across parts of the West Bank and Gaza Strip.
- Clashed violently with the Jordanian Armed Forces in the September 1970 civil war known as *Black September*, culminating in the leadership's expulsion to Lebanon until the 1982 Israeli invasion forced a maritime evacuation to Tunis.
- Founded in Jerusalem and Cairo in 1964 under Arab League auspices with *Ahmad Shukeiri* as its first chairman, operating as an umbrella coalition that soon came under the dominant control of Arafat's secular nationalist movement, *Fatah*.
- Designated unanimously at the 1974 Arab League summit in Rabat as the "sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people," bolstering its diplomatic standing against Jordanian administrative claims to the West Bank.
- Proclaimed the establishment of an independent State of [[palestine]] at an exile session in Algiers in 1988, adopting a declaration written by poet *Mahmoud Darwish* that implicitly recognized partition by accepting United Nations Security Council Resolution 242.
- Contained radical leftist member factions including the *Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine* (*PFLP*) and *Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine* (*DFLP*), which orchestrated international aircraft hijackings and armed attacks during the [[arab-israeli-conflict]].

## connections

- [[arab-israeli-conflict]] — regional warfare in which the organization served as the primary Palestinian political and military actor.
- [[middle-east]] — central geographic stage where the movement operated from bases in Jordan, Lebanon, and Tunisia.
- [[cold-war]] — international ideological conflict during which the movement aligned with Third World non-aligned powers and Eastern Bloc patrons.
- [[soviet-union]] — superpower that extended diplomatic patronage, military equipment, and tactical training to affiliated factions.
- [[united-states]] — global power that mediated bilateral peace negotiations and hosted the historic 1993 recognition accord.
- [[israel]] — sovereign state fought by the organization in decades of low-intensity war before mutual recognition.
- [[oslo-accords]] — framework agreements that established provisional self-governance under the Palestinian National Authority.

# Jewish Diaspora

## summary

The **Jewish Diaspora** refers to the dispersion of *Jewish* people from their ancestral homeland (*Israel*) into communities across the *Mediterranean*, *Middle East*, *Europe*, and eventually worldwide, beginning with the *Babylonian Exile* (6th century BCE) and accelerating after *Roman* conquests and expulsions. The diaspora became a defining feature of *Judaism*, creating distinct regional communities with diverse traditions, languages, and cultural expressions while maintaining religious and ethnic identity.

## you gotta know

- The *Babylonian Exile* (597–539 BCE) saw *King Nebuchadnezzar II* of *Babylon* conquer the *Kingdom of Judah*, exile its elite, and scatter *Jewish* populations; though many returned after *Persian* conquest, *Jewish* communities remained throughout the *Near East*.
- *Roman* subjugation and destruction of the *Second Temple* (70 CE) during the *Jewish-Roman Wars* accelerated diaspora; *Romans* expelled *Jews* from *Jerusalem* and suppressed rebellions, scattering communities throughout the *Roman Empire*.
- *Medieval Europe* saw *Jewish* communities flourish in some regions (*Spain* under *Islamic* rule, *Italy*, the *Rhineland*) but face persecution, forced conversions, and expulsions (England 1290, *France* multiple times, *Spain* 1492).
- *Ashkenazi Jews* (descendants of those in *Central* and *Eastern Europe*) developed *Yiddish* and distinct cultural practices; *Sephardic Jews* (from the *Mediterranean* and *Middle East*) retained *Ladino* and other traditions; *Mizrahi Jews* remained in *Arab* lands.
- *Diaspora* communities maintained religious practice, Torah study, and ethnic identity across centuries of dispersion and persecution, creating *Judaism* as a religion of diaspora (not tied to a single place) until *Zionism* emerged.
- *Pogroms* in *Eastern Europe* (especially *Russia*) and *anti-Semitic* policies drove mass *Jewish* migration to *North America* (1880s–1920s), creating large communities in *New York*, *Boston*, and other cities.
- The *Holocaust* (1941–45) murdered six million *Jews* and devastated *European* *Jewish* communities; the *Israeli* state's founding (1948) offered a homeland and redefined diaspora identity for many *Jews*.

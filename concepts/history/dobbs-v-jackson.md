# Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization

## summary

**Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization** was a landmark 2022 decision of the Supreme Court of the United States that eliminated the constitutional right to abortion. The 5–4 majority ruling authored by Justice *Samuel Alito* explicitly overruled both *Roe v. Wade* (1973) and *Planned Parenthood v. Casey* (1992), holding that the United States Constitution confers no right to obtain an abortion. The ruling returned regulatory authority over abortion entirely to individual state legislatures and the federal government, profoundly transforming the American legal and political landscape.

## you gotta know

- Arose as a direct challenge to Mississippi's *Gestational Age Act* of 2018, which prohibited nearly all abortions after fifteen weeks of gestational age.
- Subject to an unprecedented protocol breach in May 2022 when an early draft majority opinion was leaked to the news outlet *Politico*.
- Declared that the *14th-amendment* Due Process Clause does not protect abortion because the practice is neither explicitly mentioned nor deeply rooted in the nation's history and tradition.
- Overturned nearly fifty years of binding precedent established by *Roe v. Wade* and modified by the undue burden standard in *Planned Parenthood v. Casey*.
- Authored by Justice *Samuel Alito*, joined by Justices *Clarence Thomas*, *Neil Gorsuch*, *Brett Kavanaugh*, and *Amy Coney Barrett*, while Chief Justice *John Roberts* concurred only in the judgment upholding the Mississippi statute.
- Prompted a sharp joint dissent from Justices *Stephen Breyer*, *Sonia Sotomayor*, and *Elena Kagan*, warning of immediate harm to women's equality and liberty.
- Sparked a concurrent opinion by Justice *Clarence Thomas* arguing that the Court should reconsider all substantive due process precedents, explicitly citing *Griswold*, *Lawrence*, and *Obergefell*.
- Activated pre-existing "trigger laws" in numerous states that outlawed abortion immediately upon the decision's formal issuance on June 24, 2022.

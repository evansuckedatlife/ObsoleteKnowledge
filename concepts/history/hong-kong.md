## summary

**Hong Kong** (officially the **Hong Kong Special Administrative Region**, or **HKSAR**; Mandarin: *Xianggang*) is a global financial center and autonomous territory situated on the Pearl River Delta in southern [[china]]. Acquired by the [[british-empire]] following the First Opium War in 1841, the territory developed from a collection of coastal fishing villages and pirate harbors into one of the world's most dynamic commercial and shipping entrepôts. Governed under the "one country, two systems" framework following its return to Chinese sovereignty in 1997, the city retains a distinctive socio-political identity defined by British common law traditions, economic capitalism, and modern struggles over democratic self-determination.

## you gotta know

- Ceded in perpetuity to the [[british-empire]] under the 1842 Treaty of Nanking after the defeat of Qing dynasty forces in the First Opium War, an event inaugurating China's "Century of Humiliation."
- Expanded through successive imperial agreements, including the 1860 Convention of Peking that annexed the Kowloon Peninsula and the 1898 Second Convention of Peking that granted Britain a 99-year rent-free lease over the New Territories.
- Overrun by Imperial [[japan]] on "Black Christmas" in December 1941 following the Battle of Hong Kong, enduring a brutal three-year and eight-month military occupation during [[world-war-ii]] that devastated the local population.
- Developed into one of the prosperous "Four Asian Tigers" alongside Singapore, South Korea, and Taiwan during the post-war era, achieving exceptional growth through light manufacturing, export textiles, shipping, and global financial services.
- Returned to the sovereignty of the People's Republic of China on July 1, 1997, fulfilling the provisions of the 1984 Sino-British Joint Declaration signed by *Margaret Thatcher* and *Zhao Ziyang*.
- Structured under the constitutional framework of the Basic Law, which was intended to preserve the territory's capitalist market economy, legal independence, and individual civil liberties for a duration of fifty years until 2047.
- Shaken by large-scale civic unrest demanding universal suffrage and resisting political encroachment from Beijing, highlighted by the 2014 Umbrella Movement and the widespread 2019 protests against a proposed extradition bill, which triggered Beijing's enactment of a sweeping National Security Law in 2020.

## connections

- [[china]] — the sovereign nation from which the island was extracted by imperial force during the Qing era and to which it returned in 1997.
- [[british-empire]] — the maritime global empire that colonized and administered the territory as a crown colony and commercial outpost for over a century and a half.
- [[colonialism]] — the broader era of Western imperialist expansion and unequal treaties that carved out trading concessions along the East Asian seaboard.
- [[cold-war]] — the ideological conflict during which the colony served as an essential capitalist trade gateway, espionage center, and sanctuary adjoining the Communist mainland.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that brought British colonial rule to a temporary halt when Japanese forces overran the territory in late 1941.
- [[japan]] — the East Asian power whose wartime occupation inflicted severe famine, hyperinflation, and widespread atrocities on the civilian populace.

## summary

**Mehmed II** (1432–1481), known as *Mehmed the Conqueror*, was the Ottoman sultan who conquered Constantinople in 1453, ending the Byzantine Empire and establishing Ottoman dominance over the eastern Mediterranean. His military genius, determination, and strategic use of gunpowder artillery transformed the balance of power in the Eastern Hemisphere.

## you gotta know

- Conquered *Constantinople* in 1453 at age 21, ending over a thousand years of Byzantine imperial rule and making the city (renamed Istanbul) the Ottoman capital.
- Developed revolutionary siege tactics using massive cannons and bombardment, demonstrating that medieval fortifications could no longer withstand modern artillery.
- Captured the last remnants of the Byzantine Empire—the Despotate of Morea and other territories—consolidating Ottoman control of the Balkans and eastern Mediterranean.
- Expanded Ottoman dominance into Anatolia and the Balkans, subduing rebellious pashas and regional powers, making the empire a centralized bureaucratic state.
- Established the Ottoman sultanate as a global power that rivaled the largest European and Asian empires, reshaping Mediterranean geopolitics for centuries.
- Reorganized the Ottoman military and administration, creating the Janissaries as an elite corps and establishing the timar system of landholding and taxation.
- His conquest sent shockwaves through Christendom, spurring European powers to seek alternative trade routes (eventually driving the Age of Exploration).
- Known for his intellectual interests in art, architecture, and scholarship; commissioned major building projects including the Topkapi Palace, making Constantinople a center of Islamic and Ottoman culture.

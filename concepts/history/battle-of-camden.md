## summary

The **Battle of Camden** was a disastrous American defeat fought on August 16, 1780, north of Camden, South Carolina, during the Southern Campaign of the [[american-revolution]]. British regulars commanded by Lieutenant General *Charles Cornwallis* routed a numerically superior Patriot force led by Major General *Horatio Gates*. The catastrophe shattered American defenses in the Carolinas, destroyed Gates's military reputation, and led directly to *Nathanael Greene* taking command of the Southern Department.

## you gotta know

- Major General *Horatio Gates*, celebrated as the "hero of Saratoga", suffered a humiliating loss of prestige when he fled the battlefield on his horse *Tupper*, allegedly riding sixty miles to Charlotte in a single day and abandoning his army.
- The battle resulted in the death of Bavarian-born Major General *Johann de Kalb*, who remained behind commanding Continental regulars from Maryland and Delaware, suffering eleven wounds before being captured.
- *Gates* fatally deployed inexperienced North Carolina and Virginia militia on his left wing directly opposite veteran British regulars under Lieutenant Colonel *James Webster*; the militia broke and fled after a single British bayonet charge.
- The night before the engagement, *Gates* fed his exhausted and malnourished soldiers a quick meal of molasses, cornmeal, and rum, which triggered widespread dysentery among the troops during their overnight march.
- Lieutenant Colonel *Banastre Tarleton* led the British Legion cavalry in a devastating encirclement of the American flanks and pursued fleeing Patriots for more than twenty miles toward Hanging Rock.
- The crushing rout virtually annihilated the Continental Army in the southern theatre, prompting the Continental Congress to relieve *Gates* of command and appoint *Nathanael Greene* to lead the southern war effort.
- Both armies had unexpectedly collided around two in the morning along a road bounded by swamps and dense pine woods, which neutralized the Patriot numerical advantage before dawn.

## connections

- [[american-revolution]] — the broader war for independence in which Camden represented Britain's most complete battlefield victory of the Southern Campaign.
- [[george-washington]] — commander-in-chief of the Continental Army who had originally favored *Nathanael Greene* over *Gates* and oversaw the shift in command after the catastrophe.
- [[battle-of-yorktown]] — the decisive engagement of the Southern Campaign, where *Cornwallis* was ultimately surrounded after winning pyrrhic victories in the wake of Camden.
- [[guilford-courthouse]] — the costly battle fought seven months later by *Cornwallis* against *Gates*'s successor *Nathanael Greene*, which crippled British offensive capacity.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial power seeking to regain control of the southern colonies by cultivating Loyalist support and neutralizing Continental regulars.
- [[united-states]] — the fledgling republic whose conventional armed presence in the Deep South was nearly extinguished by the defeat.
- [[france]] — the foreign ally whose naval and financial contributions became increasingly critical as American land forces suffered major setbacks in the South.

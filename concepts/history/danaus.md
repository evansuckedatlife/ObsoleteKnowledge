## summary

**Danaus** was a legendary king of *Argos* in Greek mythology, the twin brother of *Aegyptus*, and the mythic progenitor of the *Danaans*, a foundational Homeric designation for the Greeks. Fleeing *Egypt* alongside his fifty daughters—the *Danaides*—aboard the first fifty-oared vessel, he usurped the throne of *Argos* and commanded his daughters to assassinate their forced suitor-cousins on their collective wedding night. This violent foundational tale inspired major works of classical literature, notably the tragic *Danaid Tetralogy* of *Aeschylus*, establishing an enduring cultural archetype of female resistance, political asylum, and terrifying subterranean retribution.

## you gotta know

- Commanded his fifty daughters, the *Danaides*, to murder their fifty cousins—the sons of his hostile twin brother *Aegyptus*—on their wedding night using concealed daggers.
- Saw only one daughter, *Hypermnestra*, defy his lethal edict by sparing her husband *Lynceus* because he honored her virginity, leading her father to prosecute her before an Argive tribunal that ultimately acquitted her.
- Suffered eternal infamy through his daughters' post-mortem punishment in *Tartarus*, where the murderous *Danaides* were condemned to carry water in perforated vessels or leaky sieves to fill an endlessly draining cistern.
- Fled ancestral lands in *Libya* and *Egypt* aboard a legendary fifty-oared *penteconter* constructed under the divine guidance of *Athena*, arriving as a desperate suppliant in *Argos*.
- Deposed King *Gelanor* to seize the Argive crown after the citizenry interpreted an omen—a wolf slaughtering a bull outside the city gates—as divine authorization for the bold foreign exile.
- Gave his name to the *Danaoi* or *Danaans*, an ethnonym famously employed by *Homer* in the *Iliad* to describe all Greek forces assembled before Troy, later echoed in *Virgil*'s warning regarding Greeks bearing gifts.
- Married off his remaining forty-nine daughters by staging an athletic footrace in *Argos*, offering them without bride price to whichever eligible Greek suitors could sprint to the finish line first.
- Formed the central dramatic figure of *The Suppliants* (*Hiketides*), the opening tragedy of *Aeschylus*'s surviving dramatic sequence examining the rights of political exiles and holy sanctuary.

## connections

- [[ancient-greece]] — the civilizational realm whose epic traditions and civic cults recognized him as the mythic founder of the Argive lineage.
- [[heracles]] — his ultimate heroic descendant through the lineage of his surviving daughter *Hypermnestra* and her husband *Lynceus*.
- [[agamemnon]] — the high king of Mycenae who commanded the Homeric Danaans in the Trojan War.
- [[diomedes]] — the valiant warrior who ruled the historic realm of *Argos* originally established by his mythic dynasty.
- [[tragedy]] — the Athenian dramatic genre exemplified by *Aeschylus*, who dramatized his flight from Egypt and subsequent royal arbitration.
- [[poetry]] — the epic medium through which *Homer* immortalized his name as a universal patronymic for Greek warriors.
- [[rome]] — the classical empire whose poets, including *Horace* and *Ovid*, celebrated *Hypermnestra*'s virtue and depicted the underworld torment of the Danaides.

## summary

**Daniel Morgan**, famously known across the frontier as **The Old Wagoner**, was an American brigadier general, frontiersman, and master tactician of the *American Revolution*. Revering irregular tactics, he organized elite corps of Virginia riflemen whose lethal marksmanship and skirmishing doctrine devastated British command structures in major Northern battles. His crowning military achievement came at the *Battle of Cowpens* in 1781, where he executed a textbook double envelopment that routed British light forces and decisively altered the strategic trajectory of the southern theater.

## you gotta know

- Engineered the tactical triumph at the *Battle of Cowpens* on January 17, 1781, inflicting a crushing double envelopment on Lieutenant Colonel *Banastre Tarleton* that stripped British forces of their mobile light cavalry and infantry.
- Devised a pioneering three-line defensive formation at Cowpens, ordering his frontline militia to fire only two deliberate volleys before safely withdrawing, which lured Tarleton's overconfident troops into a devastating trap against Continental regulars.
- Commanded *Morgan's Riflemen*, an elite corps of sharpshooters armed with grooved Pennsylvania long rifles who systematically targeted British officers and scouts to disrupt enemy command and control.
- Played an indispensable role at the *Battle of Saratoga* in 1777, where his hidden marksmen fatally wounded British General *Simon Fraser* at *Bemis Heights*, directly precipitating the surrender of General *John Burgoyne*.
- Survived a brutal punishment of five hundred lashes by the British Army during the French and Indian War for striking an arrogant British officer, frequently joking afterward that the drummer miscounted and still owed him one stroke.
- Stormed the ramparts during the winter assault on *Quebec City* in December 1775 after *Benedict Arnold* was wounded, fighting tenaciously through barricaded city streets until encircled and captured by British defenders.
- Mobilized federal militia units alongside Governor *Henry Lee III* to suppress the *Whiskey Rebellion* in western Pennsylvania in 1794, affirming his dedication to constitutional order under *George Washington*.
- Represented Virginia's 19th congressional district as a Federalist member of the *United States House of Representatives* from 1797 to 1799.

## summary

The **Inchon Landing**, formally designated as the **Battle of Inchon** and codenamed **Operation Chromite**, was an audacious amphibious assault conducted on September 15, 1950, during the Korean War. Planned and directed by General of the Army *Douglas MacArthur*, the operation landed the United States X Corps far behind enemy lines at the west-coast port city of Incheon. Overcoming monumental hydrographic obstacles and extreme tides, the surprise invasion relieved the besieged Pusan Perimeter, spearheaded the liberation of Seoul, and reversed the direction of the early war before subsequent counter-offensives prolonged the struggle.

## you gotta know

- Championed by General *Douglas MacArthur* despite near-unanimous skepticism from the Joint Chiefs of Staff and naval commanders, who warned that the port was a hydrographic nightmare due to thirty-foot tidal swings and narrow approach channels.
- Required landing craft to navigate the treacherous Flying Fish Channel (*Salee River*) and cross immense coastal mudflats, leaving assault troops with a narrow two-hour window at high tide to breach harbor defenses before running aground.
- Commenced with the neutralization of Wolmido (*Green Beach*), a heavily fortified island dominating the mouth of Incheon harbor, which was captured in the opening hours by the 3rd Battalion, 5th Marines.
- Executed under the field command of Major General *Edward Almond*, leading the unified assault of the United States X Corps, which was spearheaded by the 1st Marine Division under Major General *Oliver P. Smith*.
- Featured troops scaling tall stone harbor sea walls under enemy fire utilizing wooden scaling ladders equipped with iron grappling hooks at *Red Beach*.
- Broke the logistical spine of the North Korean Korean People's Army, triggering an immediate general retreat and enabling Lieutenant General *Walton Walker's* Eighth Army to break out from the Pusan Perimeter.
- Led directly to the bloody, street-by-street recapture of the South Korean capital of Seoul, culminating in a ceremonial restoration of the government under President *Syngman Rhee*.
- Created the strategic overconfidence that emboldened *MacArthur* to push United Nations divisions across the 38th parallel toward the Yalu River, prompting military intervention by [[china]].

## connections

- [[cold-war]] — the global confrontation between democratic and communist blocs that turned violent on the Korean Peninsula.
- [[united-states]] — the primary superpower providing naval, air, and ground forces under the United Nations Command in Korea.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict during which the Pacific amphibious doctrines and veterans used at Incheon were forged.
- [[china]] — the communist neighbor that launched a massive counter-offensive across the Yalu River after UN forces exploited their Inchon victory.
- [[soviet-union]] — the communist power that equipped North Korean armies with heavy armor and aircraft while avoiding direct naval engagement.
- [[japan]] — the neighboring archipelago that served as the primary logistical staging ground, naval anchorage, and repair hub for the assault force.

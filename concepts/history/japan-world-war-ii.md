## summary

**Japan in World War II** traces the empire's aggressive expansion across Asia and the Pacific from 1937 to 1945, its alliance with Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy, and its ultimate defeat by American forces. After the 1941 attack on [[world-war-ii|Pearl Harbor]], Japan fought a grueling island-by-island campaign across the Pacific against overwhelming industrial and technological disadvantage. The war's conclusion with atomic weapons and unconditional surrender reshaped Japan and the postwar world.

## you gotta know

- Invaded Manchuria in 1931 and China in 1937, establishing a vast Asian empire through military conquest.
- Signed the Tripartite Pact with Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy in 1940, formally aligning with the Axis powers.
- Launched the surprise attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941, bringing the United States into the war.
- Captured vast territories across Southeast Asia and the Pacific, but could not match American industrial production or naval capacity.
- Fought fanatically to the last soldier on remote islands like [[battle-of-iwo-jima|Iwo Jima]] and [[battle-of-okinawa|Okinawa]], refusing surrender.
- Faced devastating firebombing campaigns that destroyed major cities and killed hundreds of thousands of civilians.
- Surrendered unconditionally in August 1945 after the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki.

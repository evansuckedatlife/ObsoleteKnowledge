## summary

**Hillary Clinton**, born **Hillary Rodham Clinton**, is an American attorney, diplomat, and politician who has served at the highest levels of federal governance in the *United States*. After gaining national prominence as First Lady of Arkansas and subsequently First Lady of the United States alongside President *Bill Clinton*, she forged an independent political career as a United States Senator from New York and as the sixty-seventh Secretary of State under President *Barack Obama*. In 2016, she made history as the first woman nominated for the presidency by a major political party in the nation.

## you gotta know

- Emerged as an exceptionally active presidential spouse, chairing the *Task Force on National Health Care Reform* in 1993 to overhaul healthcare access across the country.
- Delivered an internationally lauded address at the 1995 *United Nations Fourth World Conference on Women* in Beijing, boldly declaring that human rights are women's rights and women's rights are human rights.
- Subjected to intense legal scrutiny during independent counsel *Kenneth Starr*'s investigations into the *Whitewater* land deal and the dismissals within the White House travel office.
- Elected in 2000 as the first female United States Senator representing *New York*, working extensively on economic rebuilding following the September 11 terrorist attacks on *New York City*.
- Waged a fierce, historic battle against *Barack Obama* in the 2008 Democratic presidential primary before conceding and serving as his chief diplomat from 2009 to 2013.
- Oversaw American diplomatic policy during the complex upheavals of the *Arab Spring* and helped negotiate crucial multilateral sanctions on Iran as Secretary of State.
- Won the popular vote by nearly three million ballots in the 2016 presidential contest against *Donald Trump*, though falling short of the required threshold in the *Electoral College*.

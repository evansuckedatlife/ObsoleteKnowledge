## summary

The **Caribbean**, historically known as the **West Indies** and centered on the **Caribbean Sea**, is an archipelagic region of the tropical Americas comprising the *Greater Antilles*, *Lesser Antilles*, and the *Lucayan Archipelago*. Following *Christopher Columbus*'s arrival in 1492 during the [[age-of-exploration]], the region became the first frontier of European [[colonialism]], where [[spain]], [[england]], and [[france]] established plantation regimes powered by the transatlantic slave trade. The near-total devastation of indigenous populations and the forced migration of millions of enslaved Africans forged dynamic Creole societies, while twentieth-century conflicts like the [[cuban-missile-crisis]] during the [[cold-war]] cemented the region's enduring geopolitical significance.

## you gotta know

- *Christopher Columbus* made his initial 1492 landfall in the *Bahamas* during the [[age-of-exploration]], initiating Spanish colonization across *Hispaniola* and causing the catastrophic collapse of indigenous *Taíno* and *Arawak* populations through disease and forced labor.
- Served as the primary theater of the 1962 [[cuban-missile-crisis]], when the [[soviet-union]] deployed nuclear missiles to *Cuba* under [[fidel-castro]], prompting an American naval blockade and bringing the [[cold-war]] superpowers to the brink of nuclear war.
- Became the epicentre of the Atlantic plantation economy, where millions of enslaved Africans were imported across the *Middle Passage* to cultivate sugarcane, transforming demographic structures and generating immense mercantile wealth for the [[british-empire]] and [[france]].
- Witnessed the *Haitian Revolution* (1791–1804), in which self-liberated enslaved people led by *Toussaint Louverture* defeated French colonial forces, influenced by the [[french-revolution]] to establish the world's first independent Black republic.
- Transformed into an American sphere of influence following the 1898 [[spanish-american-war]], precipitated by the explosion of the [[uss-maine]] in *Havana Harbor*, which yielded *Puerto Rico* to the [[united-states]] and led to decades of interventionism under the *Monroe Doctrine*.
- Formed the center of seventeenth- and eighteenth-century privateering and maritime banditry, where outlaw havens like *Port Royal* in *Jamaica* and *Nassau* in the *Bahamas* challenged the trade monopolies of [[spain]] and [[england]].
- Fostered distinct creolized cultures whose syncretic linguistic forms are foundational to modern [[linguistics]], while mid-twentieth-century outward migrations profoundly shaped artistic and political developments such as the [[harlem-renaissance]] in [[new-york-city]] and Caribbean-descended civil rights organizing.

## connections

- [[age-of-exploration]] — launched European colonization in the Americas following *Christopher Columbus*'s 1492 landfall in the region.
- [[cold-war]] — defined modern Caribbean geopolitics through ideological containment, revolutions, and the 1962 [[cuban-missile-crisis]].
- [[harlem-renaissance]] — drew critical intellectual leadership and artistic innovation from Caribbean immigrants arriving in [[new-york-city]].
- [[linguistics]] — analyzes Caribbean vernaculars like *Papiamento* and *Haitian Creole* as crucial case studies in creolization and grammar genesis.
- [[jazz]] — incorporated essential rhythmic foundations and syncopated styles carried through Afro-Caribbean maritime trade into *New Orleans*.
- [[colonialism]] — instituted brutal plantation monocultures and the transatlantic slave trade to extract wealth for competing European monarchies.
- [[french-revolution]] — unleashed egalitarian ideals that directly ignited the *Haitian Revolution* in the sugar colony of *Saint-Domingue*.

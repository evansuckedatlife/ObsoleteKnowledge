## summary

**George V** (*George Frederick Ernest Albert*) was King of the United Kingdom and the British Dominions, and Emperor of India, reigning from 1910 until his death in 1936. His eventful reign witnessed immense global and domestic upheavals across the [[british-empire]], including the constitutional confrontation over the House of Lords, the horrors of [[world-war-i]], the election of the first socialist Labour government, and the emergence of dominion sovereignty. He is celebrated for securing the Crown's stability and domestic popularity amidst the collapse of multiple European monarchies by severing dynastic German ties and founding the *House of Windsor*.

## you gotta know

- Renamed the British royal house from *Saxe-Coburg and Gotha* to the *House of Windsor* in 1917 to quell intense anti-German sentiment during [[world-war-i]], simultaneously relinquishing all German titles, heraldry, and peerages on behalf of his British relatives.
- Shared a startling physical resemblance with his maternal first cousin, Tsar *Nicholas II* of the [[russian-empire]], whom he controversial declined to rescue from revolutionary Russia in 1917 due to fears of domestic republican backlash and public unrest.
- Resolved the bitter constitutional crisis sparked by the "People's Budget" by reluctantly agreeing to create hundreds of Liberal peers if needed to pass the *Parliament Act 1911*, which permanently abolished the House of Lords' veto over legislation.
- Attended the magnificent 1911 *Delhi Durbar* with Queen *Mary*, making him the only reigning British Emperor of India to travel to the subcontinent, receive homage in person, and announce the shifting of the imperial capital to Delhi.
- Cemented the modern constitutional neutrality of the sovereign by facilitating the smooth transition to the first Labour government under *Ramsay MacDonald* in 1924 and encouraging conciliation during the 1926 General Strike.
- Formalized imperial decentralization through the landmark *Statute of Westminster 1931*, establishing the self-governing dominions as sovereign equals within the emerging British Commonwealth, united only by free allegiance to the British Crown.
- Inaugurated the annual royal *Christmas Broadcast* tradition on the radio in 1932, reading an address penned by *Rudyard Kipling* over the *BBC Empire Service* to speak directly to millions of citizens across the globe.
- Reached the line of succession only after the sudden 1892 death of his elder brother Prince *Albert Victor*, spent decades curating the world-renowned *Royal Philatelic Collection*, and was euthanized in 1936 by his physician *Lord Dawson of Penn* with lethal doses of morphine and cocaine.

## connections

- [[world-war-i]] — global conflict that defined his reign, during which he visited frontline trenches and stripped German relatives of British honors.
- [[british-empire]] — vast global domain he governed, which evolved during his reign into the Commonwealth of Nations through the *Statute of Westminster*.
- [[england]] — principal realm of his kingdom, where he cultivated a modest, duty-bound persona that anchored the monarchy in national affection.
- [[russian-empire]] — realm ruled by his close cousin Tsar *Nicholas II*, whose overthrow prompted George to deny him asylum to safeguard his own throne.
- [[germany]] — ancestral home of his paternal family, whose aggression in the Great War compelled him to cast off his family's historic German name.
- [[edward-viii]] — his eldest son and successor, whose marital indiscretions and erratic behavior the King accurately predicted would ruin him within a year.
- [[george-vi]] — his dutiful second son, who succeeded to the throne after the abdication of *Edward VIII* and sustained the royal traditions George established.

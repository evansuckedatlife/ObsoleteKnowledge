## summary

The **Battle of Berlin**, also remembered as the **Fall of Berlin**, was the final major offensive military campaign of the European theater of [[world-war-ii]]. Fought from mid-April to early May 1945 across eastern and central [[germany]], the massive assault culminated in the encirclement, street-by-street siege, and capture of the German capital by forces of the [[soviet-union]]. The battle shattered the remnants of Nazi power, precipitated the suicide of *Adolf Hitler* inside his fortified bunker, and ended the war in Europe, directly setting the territorial and political conditions for the subsequent division of the continent during the [[cold-war]].

## you gotta know

- Resulted directly in the suicide of *Adolf Hitler* alongside *Eva Braun* in the underground *Führerbunker* on April 30, 1945, followed two days later by the surrender of the city's defense garrison commanded by General *Helmuth Weidling*.
- Produced one of the most famous propaganda images of the twentieth century in *Yevgeny Khaldei*'s photograph *Raising a Flag over the Reichstag*, which depicted Red Army soldiers hoisting the hammer and sickle atop the ruined parliament.
- Began on April 16 across the Oder River with the bloody assault on the heavily fortified *Seelow Heights*, where German defenses inflicted massive Soviet casualties before Marshal *Georgy Zhukov* punched through toward the capital.
- Driven by a fierce internal race orchestrated by *Joseph Stalin*, who pitted *Zhukov*'s 1st Belorussian Front against Marshal *Ivan Konev*'s 1st Ukrainian Front to see which commander would encircle and seize the city center first.
- Defended on the German side by a fractured force of regular *Wehrmacht* units, desperate elderly recruits of the *Volkssturm*, teenage fighters of the *Hitler Youth*, and diehard foreign *Waffen-SS* volunteers from the *Charlemagne* division.
- Caused the destruction of the German Ninth Army trapped in the *Halbe Pocket* south of the city, where tens of thousands of troops and civilian refugees died attempting to break through westward to surrender to the [[united-states]] army.
- Saw intense close-quarters combat within the city's infrastructure, including the devastating flooding of the underground railway tunnels beneath the Landwehr Canal, drowning civilians and wounded soldiers sheltering in the subway stations.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — marked the final decisive engagement of the European theater, bringing about the unconditional surrender of Axis forces.
- [[soviet-union]] — committed more than two million soldiers and massive artillery formations in its climactic offensive against the Nazi regime.
- [[germany]] — experienced the destruction of its capital, the end of the Third Reich, and total Allied occupation following the battle.
- [[cold-war]] — arose out of the subsequent division of the captured capital into Western and Soviet administrative sectors.
- [[battle-of-stalingrad]] — represented the earlier turning point on the Eastern Front that reversed the German advance and initiated the Red Army's march west.
- [[operation-barbarossa]] — launched the Axis invasion of Russian territory in 1941, initiating the brutal four-year war that terminated in Berlin.
- [[united-states]] — halted its advancing western forces at the Elbe River, intentionally leaving the costly urban assault on Berlin to the Red Army.

## summary

**Georges Clemenceau**, celebrated across the nation by his moniker *Le Tigre* ("The Tiger"), was the formidable French radical statesman who twice governed the Third Republic as Prime Minister and led the country to triumph in *World War I*. Assuming office during the military and morale crisis of 1917, he galvanized the civilian population, ruthlessly suppressed defeatism, and unified the Allied strategic command under Marshal Ferdinand Foch. As the senior French delegate at the 1919 Paris Peace Conference, he relentlessly championed punitive territorial, financial, and military restrictions upon defeated *Germany* to guarantee the long-term defense of *France*.

## you gotta know

- Returned to the premiership in November 1917 at age seventy-six, rallying a demoralized nation through his uncompromising parliamentary declarations of total war: *"Je fais la guerre"*.
- Served as the dominant host of the Paris Peace Conference in 1919, insisting that the signing ceremony occur in the Hall of Mirrors at *Versailles* to reverse the German imperial proclamation of 1871.
- Clashed repeatedly with American President *Woodrow Wilson* and British Prime Minister *David Lloyd George*, demanding the military separation of the Rhineland and massive financial reparations.
- Survived an assassination attempt during the 1919 negotiations when an anarchist youth fired seven bullets at his vehicle, leaving a projectile permanently lodged near his ribs.
- Emerged as a passionate champion of *Alfred Dreyfus* during the Dreyfus Affair, publishing *Émile Zola*'s celebrated open letter *J'Accuse...!* in his radical journal *L'Aurore*.
- Earned his enduring nickname *Le Tigre* in the Chamber of Deputies for his razor-sharp oratorical duels and his destructive role in toppling conservative ministries.
- Suffered a bitter political defeat in the presidential election of 1920 to *Paul Deschanel*, entering retirement to write and issue grim warnings that Allied concessions would spark a renewed war within twenty years.

## connections

- [[france]] — sovereign republic he steered through internal political turmoil and total existential warfare.
- [[world-war-i]] — global conflagration in which his political leadership secured the final Allied military victory on the Western Front.
- [[germany]] — bordering rival against whom Clemenceau fought two bitter wars, spanning the Franco-Prussian War to the Great War.
- [[colbert]] — early modern administrative predecessor whose centralizing state traditions laid the historical foundations for French national mobilization.
- [[versailles]] — royal palace chosen by Clemenceau to dictate peace terms and avenge the nineteenth-century humiliation of France.
- [[napoleon-bonaparte]] — French military emperor whose autocratic legacy Clemenceau rejected in favor of uncompromising radical republicanism.
- [[united-states]] — transatlantic ally whose idealistic post-war diplomatic program Clemenceau resisted in defense of European security.

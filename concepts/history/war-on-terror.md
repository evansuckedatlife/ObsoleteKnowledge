## summary

The **War on Terror**, also known as the **Global War on Terrorism**, was an international military campaign spearheaded by the [[united-states]] following the *September 11 attacks* in 2001. Formally inaugurated by President *George W. Bush*, the extensive initiative sought to eliminate *al-Qaeda*, dismantle hostile extremist infrastructure, and prevent foreign regimes from harboring terrorists or developing weapons of mass destruction. Spanning multiple continents, the doctrine fundamentally reshaped international diplomacy, redefined global security protocols, and enticed American and allied armed forces into protracted counterinsurgency struggles throughout the [[middle-east]] and South Asia.

## you gotta know

- Inaugurated following the devastating *September 11 attacks* against [[new-york-city]] and the Pentagon, when *George W. Bush* declared before a joint session of Congress that the nation would make no distinction between terrorists and the states that harbor them.
- Initiated military hostilities in October 2001 through *Operation Enduring Freedom*, successfully toppling the *Taliban* regime in Afghanistan for granting sanctuary to *al-Qaeda* founder *Osama bin Laden*.
- Broadened its scope in March 2003 with the contentious invasion of Iraq during the [[iraq-war]], anchored by White House assertions that *Saddam Hussein* possessed weapons of mass destruction and maintained operational ties to transnational jihadist factions.
- Reorganized domestic intelligence and law enforcement apparatus within the [[united-states]], highlighted by the contentious passage of the *USA PATRIOT Act* and the creation of the *Department of Homeland Security*.
- Established clandestine rendition networks and extraterritorial detention centers, prominently at *Guantanamo Bay Naval Base*, which triggered widespread international condemnation regarding the legal status of *unlawful combatants* and the use of *enhanced interrogation techniques*.
- Culminated in a landmark tactical achievement in May 2011 when *United States Navy SEALs* executed *Operation Neptune Spear*, penetrating a compound in *Abbottabad*, Pakistan, to kill *Osama bin Laden* under the direction of President *Barack Obama*.
- Destabilized governance structures across the [[middle-east]], inadvertently creating governance vacuums that fueled sectarian civil wars and fostered the sudden emergence of the *Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant*.
- Ended its longest operational chapter in August 2021 with the hurried withdrawal of international forces from *Kabul*, culminating in the immediate return of the *Taliban* to power twenty years after their initial ouster.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the primary military and political architect behind the global coalition and its strategic objectives.
- [[middle-east]] — the central geopolitical region transformed by long-term military deployments, regime overthrows, and counterinsurgency operations.
- [[cold-war]] — the preceding global rivalry whose covert proxy funding in Afghanistan inadvertently fostered the origins of modern transnational militant groups.
- [[new-york-city]] — the primary site of the catastrophic *World Trade Center* destruction that directly triggered the campaign.
- [[islam]] — the global religion whose diverse communities experienced intensified geopolitical scrutiny, political tensions, and ideological conflicts throughout the era.
- [[iraq-war]] — the largest and most divisive theater of conventional invasion and occupation launched under the doctrine.
- [[september-11]] — the defining terrorist attacks that catalyzed the immediate declaration of the campaign.

## summary

**Russia** is a vast Eurasian nation spanning Eastern Europe and northern Asia, whose history encompasses the rise of Muscovy in the medieval period, expansion into the world's largest continuous territory, and emergence as a major military and political power. From early principalities through imperial expansion and into the Soviet era, Russia's development profoundly shaped European and global affairs, from the Napoleonic Wars to the Cold War. Its geographic expanse, resources, and strategic position made it a decisive actor in world history.

## you gotta know

- Emerged from medieval Kievan Rus and Mongol domination as the Principality of Muscovy, which gradually expanded and centralized under the Romanov dynasty beginning in 1613.
- Expanded eastward across the Urals into Siberia, then southward toward Central Asia, the Caucasus, and the Black Sea, creating the world's largest contiguous land empire by the 20th century.
- Played a pivotal defensive role in the Napoleonic Wars, particularly at the Battle of Leipzig and through the devastating 1812 invasion of its interior, which destroyed French forces.
- Industrialized late compared to Western Europe, remaining primarily agrarian and rigidly hierarchical under the tsars while revolutionary movements challenged autocratic rule from the 1800s onward.
- Underwent radical transformation through the Russian Revolution in 1917, becoming the world's first communist state and the Soviet Union.
- Suffered catastrophic losses during World War I and World War II but emerged as a superpower, competing with the United States throughout the Cold War.
- Maintained a multinational empire incorporating diverse ethnic groups, religions, and regional interests, which remained a source of internal tension and external geopolitical significance.

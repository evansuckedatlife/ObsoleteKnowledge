## summary

**Eugene V. Debs** (born **Eugene Victor Debs**) was a pioneering American trade unionist, orator, and industrial organizer from Terre Haute, Indiana, who revolutionized the strategy of the [[american-labor-movement]]. Disillusioned by the narrow craft focus of the *American Federation of Labor*, he founded the *American Railway Union* (ARU) as the nation's first successful industrial union, uniting skilled and unskilled railroad workers into a single powerful organization. His leadership of the dramatic 1894 *Pullman Strike* and subsequent federal imprisonment transformed him into America's foremost advocate for industrial democracy, working-class solidarity, and democratic socialism.

## you gotta know

- Began his career working as a locomotive fireman on the *Vandalia Railroad* before becoming national secretary and treasurer of the *Brotherhood of Locomotive Firemen* and editor of its magazine.
- Established the *American Railway Union* in 1893, proving that industrial unionism—organizing an entire industry regardless of specific trade or skill level—could win major concessions against powerful rail conglomerates like the *Great Northern Railway*.
- Led the ARU in a nationwide sympathy boycott in 1894 against *Pullman Palace Car Company* sleeper cars to support striking factory workers in the company town of *Pullman, Illinois*.
- Defied an omnibus federal court injunction issued under the *Sherman Antitrust Act*, prompting President *Grover Cleveland* to deploy federal troops over the objections of Illinois Governor *John Peter Altgeld*.
- Imprisoned for six months in the county jail at Woodstock, Illinois, for contempt of court, a landmark ruling upheld by the Supreme Court in *In re Debs* (1895).
- Underwent a political conversion while incarcerated at Woodstock, reading Karl Marx's *Capital* and socialist literature delivered by editor *Victor L. Berger*, concluding that industrial unionism alone could not defeat corporate monopoly without political socialism.
- Co-founded the *Social Democracy of America* and later helped establish the *Socialist Party of America* alongside *Morris Hillquit* and *Victor L. Berger*.
- Joined with radical labor leaders such as *William D. "Big Bill" Haywood*, *Mary Harris "Mother" Jones*, and *Daniel De Leon* in Chicago in 1905 to found the revolutionary *Industrial Workers of the World* (the Wobblies).

## summary

**Benjamin Netanyahu**, frequently referenced by his popular nickname **Bibi**, is an Israeli politician and the longest-serving prime minister in the country's history, dominating national politics as the chairman of the conservative *Likud* party. First rising to the premiership following the 1995 assassination of *Yitzhak Rabin*, he defeated *Shimon Peres* in 1996 by mobilizing conservative opposition against the concessions of the Oslo peace process. Across multiple non-consecutive tenures, his leadership fundamentally reshaped the modern [[middle-east]] through staunch security policies, free-market economic reforms, unyielding resistance to the Iranian nuclear program, and regional normalization under the 2020 Abraham Accords within the ongoing [[arab-israeli-conflict]].

## you gotta know

- Longest-serving prime minister in the history of the modern state of Israel, leading the country across multiple non-consecutive mandates (1996–1999, 2009–2021, and 2022–present) as the enduring head of the right-wing *Likud* party.
- First won the premiership in the 1996 direct election by defeating *Shimon Peres*, capitalizing on widespread public anxiety and security backlash following the 1995 assassination of Prime Minister *Yitzhak Rabin* by a right-wing extremist.
- Brokered the historic 2020 Abraham Accords alongside the [[united-states]], normalizing diplomatic, economic, and tourism relations with the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Sudan, and Morocco without granting Palestinian statehood.
- Vehemently opposed the 2015 multilateral Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action regarding Iran, bypassing diplomatic protocol to speak before a joint session of the [[united-states]] Congress to lobby against the agreement negotiated by the Obama administration.
- Indicted in 2019 by Attorney General *Avichai Mandelblit* on formal charges of bribery, fraud, and breach of trust in three separate corruption probes, titled Cases 1000, 2000, and 4000, making him the first sitting Israeli premier to face criminal trial.
- Championed extensive free-market privatization, deregulation, and welfare cuts as Finance Minister under *Ariel Sharon* between 2003 and 2005, before resigning from the cabinet to register his fierce opposition to Israel's unilateral withdrawal from the Gaza Strip.
- Served as a combat captain in the elite commando unit *Sayeret Matkal*, the same reconnaissance outfit once commanded by his older brother *Yonatan Netanyahu*, who became a national martyr as the only Israeli soldier killed during the 1976 Operation Entebbe.
- Influenced heavily by his father, *Benzion Netanyahu*, a prominent scholar of the Spanish Inquisition and former personal secretary to revisionist Zionist leader *Ze'ev Jabotinsky*, instilling in him a pessimistic historical outlook regarding Jewish survival.

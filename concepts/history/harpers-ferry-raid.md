## summary

The **Harpers Ferry raid** was an armed abolitionist assault led by *John Brown* from October 16 to 18, 1859, targeting the federal armory and arsenal at Harpers Ferry, Virginia. Brown intended to seize government munitions, arm enslaved laborers, and ignite an armed liberation movement across the American South to eradicate chattel slavery. Although militarily defeated within two days by local militia and federal troops led by *Robert E. Lee*, the raid and Brown's subsequent execution polarized the nation, acting as a direct catalyst for the *American Civil War*.

## you gotta know

- Led by radical abolitionist *John Brown* and an integrated force of twenty-one men, including five Black men and several of Brown's sons.
- Aimed to capture weapons from the federal arsenal to distribute to enslaved escapees, intending to establish a guerrilla stronghold in the Blue Ridge Mountains.
- Backed financially by a clandestine group of northern abolitionists known as the "Secret Six", including *Thomas Wentworth Higginson* and *Gerrit Smith*.
- Prominent abolitionist *Frederick Douglass* met secretly with Brown at a quarry in Chambersburg, Pennsylvania, but declined to participate, warning that an attack on the federal government was suicidal.
- The raid resulted in the death of *Heyward Shepherd*, a free African American railroad baggage handler who became the operation's first fatal casualty when shot by Brown's sentries.
- Brown and his surviving men were pinned down inside the armory's brick engine house, later celebrated in history as *John Brown's Fort*.
- A company of United States Marines under the command of Lieutenant Colonel *Robert E. Lee* and Lieutenant *J.E.B. Stuart* stormed the engine house, capturing Brown alive.
- Brown was convicted of treason against the Commonwealth of Virginia, murder, and inciting slave insurrection; his articulate courtroom statements and dignified execution on December 2, 1859, transformed him into an iconic martyr.

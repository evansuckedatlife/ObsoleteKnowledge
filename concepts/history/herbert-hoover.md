## summary

**Herbert Hoover** was the 31st President of the United States (1929–1933) whose administration collided catastrophically with the *Great Depression*, defining his presidency as a failure of leadership. A successful mining engineer and humanitarian relief coordinator during *World War I*, Hoover entered office as a cautious Republican at the moment the stock market crashed. His belief in limited government and refusal to implement aggressive federal relief made him seem callous to a nation in crisis, leading to his landslide defeat by *Franklin D. Roosevelt* in the election of 1932 and his eclipse from American politics.

## you gotta know

- Began his presidency in March 1929, just eight months before the stock market crash of October that triggered the Great Depression.
- Believed that excessive government intervention would weaken American self-reliance and capitalism; favored voluntary business cooperation over federal programs.
- Supported the Smoot-Hawley Tariff (1930), which raised import duties and is widely blamed for worsening international trade collapse and deepening the Depression.
- Authorized limited relief through the Reconstruction Finance Corporation (RFC) to prop up banks and railroads, but resisted direct aid to unemployed individuals.
- Largely rejected calls for public works programs and welfare expansion, a position that became politically toxic as unemployment exceeded 25%.
- Lost the [[election-of-1932|1932 election]] to *Franklin D. Roosevelt* in a landslide, ending Republican electoral dominance and ushering in the New Deal era.
- Lived until 1964, his reputation never recovering; "Hooverville" (shanytowns of the homeless) became a bitter monument to his name.

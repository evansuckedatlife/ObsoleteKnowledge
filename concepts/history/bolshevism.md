## summary

**Bolshevism** was the revolutionary Marxist political doctrine and theory of statecraft developed by *Vladimir Lenin* in the late *[[russian-empire]]* and realized in the founding of the *[[soviet-union]]*. Built upon the concept of a tightly disciplined vanguard party of dedicated professional revolutionaries, the doctrine rejected bourgeois parliamentary democracy and gradual social reform in favor of armed insurrection and the dictatorship of the proletariat. Its ultimate triumph in 1917 created the blueprint for twentieth-century state socialism, centralized economic planning, and Marxist-Leninist governance across the globe.

## you gotta know

- Formulated by *Vladimir Lenin* in seminal political texts like *What Is to Be Done?* (1902) and *The State and Revolution* (1917), adapting classical Marxist theory to an underdeveloped agrarian empire.
- Split definitively from moderate *Menshevism* over party membership rules, insisting that revolutionary success demanded a dedicated, secretive vanguard rather than an open mass organization.
- Propounded the operational mechanism of *democratic centralism*, which allowed internal debate before a vote but mandated total, unquestioning discipline from all members once policy was set.
- Championed radical peasant mobilization, breaking with orthodox Marxist assumptions that socialist revolutions could only occur in heavily industrialized Western European societies.
- Succeeded by seizing state power during the *October Revolution* of 1917, rallying mass public discontent with the famous revolutionary slogan *Peace, Land, and Bread*.
- Established *War Communism* during the Russian Civil War, characterized by extreme nationalization of industry, militarized labor discipline, grain requisitioning, and state-directed political terror.
- Transitioned pragmatically to the *New Economic Policy* (*NEP*) in 1921, temporarily tolerating small-scale private enterprise and market mechanisms to rescue the national economy from famine and ruin.
- Spread its revolutionary model through the *Communist International* (*Comintern*), founded in Moscow in 1919 to enforce theoretical conformity on foreign communist parties.

## connections

- [[bolsheviks]] — the political party and faction that put this revolutionary ideology into historical practice.
- [[soviet-union]] — the single-party socialist superpower founded, governed, and shaped by this political doctrine.
- [[russian-empire]] — the autocratic imperial state whose socioeconomic breakdown and wartime collapse allowed the ideology to triumph.
- [[world-war-i]] — the catastrophic military conflict that devastated imperial infrastructure and created ideal conditions for revolutionary takeover.
- [[germany]] — the imperial power whose wartime high command transported Lenin to Russia in a sealed train to subvert the Eastern Front.
- [[cold-war]] — the global ideological confrontation between the Western capitalist world and the state system generated by this doctrine.

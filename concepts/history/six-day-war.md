## summary

The **Six-Day War**, also known as the **1967 Arab-Israeli War** or the **June War**, was a swift and decisive armed conflict fought between June 5 and June 10, 1967, pitting *Israel* against an Arab coalition led by *Egypt*, *Jordan*, and *Syria*. Spurred by regional escalation, Egyptian military mobilization in the *Sinai Peninsula*, and the maritime blockade of the *Straits of Tiran*, the Israeli military launched preemptive airstrikes that crippled opposing air forces on the ground. The sweeping Israeli victory resulted in the occupation of the *Gaza Strip*, the *Sinai Peninsula*, the *West Bank*, *East Jerusalem*, and the *Golan Heights*, fundamentally reshaping the territorial map of the [[middle-east]] and defining the trajectory of the [[arab-israeli-conflict]].

## you gotta know

- Began with *Operation Focus* (*Mivtza Moked*), a surprise dawn airstrike by the Israeli Air Force that destroyed over three hundred Egyptian aircraft on their airfields, securing complete aerial superiority within hours.
- Precipitated by Egyptian President *Gamal Abdel Nasser*, who mobilized troops in the *Sinai Peninsula*, expelled *United Nations Emergency Force* peacekeepers, and closed the *Straits of Tiran* to Israeli navigation.
- Concluded with *Israel* seizing the *Sinai Peninsula* and *Gaza Strip* from *Egypt*, the *West Bank* and *East Jerusalem* from *Jordan*, and the *Golan Heights* from *Syria*, tripling its territorial control.
- Captured the *Old City* of *Jerusalem* on June 7, highlighted by paratroop commander *Mordechai Gur* declaring over the radio that the *Temple Mount* was in Israeli hands.
- Prompted the *United Nations Security Council* to draft *Resolution 242*, establishing the international diplomatic framework of "land for peace" that governed subsequent peace initiatives.
- Elicited the *Khartoum Resolution* from the *Arab League*, which proclaimed the unyielding "Three No's": no peace with *Israel*, no recognition of *Israel*, and no negotiations with *Israel*.
- Marred by the *USS Liberty* incident on June 8, where Israeli air and naval forces attacked an American intelligence collection ship in international waters, killing thirty-four crewmen.
- Accelerated by false intelligence disseminated by the [[soviet-union]] to *Syria* and *Egypt*, erroneously claiming that Israeli military divisions were massing on the Syrian border.

## connections

- [[arab-israeli-conflict]] — the broader geopolitical struggle in which this war served as the pivotal territorial turning point.
- [[middle-east]] — the region whose political boundaries, diplomatic dynamics, and refugee populations were reshaped by the outcome.
- [[cold-war]] — the global superpower rivalry that framed the clash through regional proxy alignments and diplomatic maneuvers.
- [[soviet-union]] — the communist superpower whose fabricated intelligence reports concerning Israeli troop concentrations catalyzed the initial crisis.
- [[united-states]] — the Western power whose naval surveillance vessel, the *USS Liberty*, was mistakenly struck by Israeli fire.
- [[yom-kippur-war]] — the 1973 follow-up conflict launched by *Egypt* and *Syria* in an attempt to recover the territories lost in 1967.
- [[suez-crisis]] — the 1956 armed clash that produced the *United Nations* peacekeeping presence and maritime guarantees revoked prior to the fighting.

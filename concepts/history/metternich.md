## summary

**Klemens von Metternich** (1773–1859) was an Austrian statesman and diplomat whose conservative vision dominated European politics for three decades after Napoleon's defeat. As the architect of the Concert of Europe and the post-Napoleonic order, he epitomized the conservative reaction against revolution and the principle of legitimacy in international affairs.

## you gotta know

- Became Austria's Foreign Minister in 1809 and chief diplomat, negotiating alliances, marriages, and peace settlements that shaped the Napoleonic Wars' outcome.
- Orchestrated the *Congress of Vienna* (1814–1815), the peace conference that reorganized Europe after Napoleon's defeat, establishing the Concert of Europe system.
- Championed the principle of *legitimacy*—the restoration of pre-revolutionary dynasties and territorial boundaries—as the foundation for European stability.
- Designed the *Concert of Europe*, a system of great-power consensus and collective security meant to prevent future revolutionary upheaval and maintain conservative order.
- Pursued a policy of *conservative internationalism*, using diplomacy, secret police, and suppression of nationalist movements to prevent the spread of revolutionary ideas.
- Opposed nationalist and liberal movements throughout Europe, viewing them as threats to established monarchies and the balance of power he had carefully constructed.
- Maintained influence over Austrian policy for nearly 40 years until the revolutions of 1848 forced his resignation, demonstrating the durability but ultimate fragility of his system.
- His conservative order preserved great-power stability for nearly a century, but his refusal to accommodate legitimate national aspirations sowed seeds of later conflict and European instability.

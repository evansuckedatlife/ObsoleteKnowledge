# Islamic Revolution

## summary

The **Islamic Revolution** (1978–1979) in Iran was a popular uprising that overthrew the autocratic *Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavi* and established an Islamic theocracy led by *Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini*. The revolution transformed Iran into an ideologically revolutionary state, altered the geopolitics of the Middle East, and inspired Islamic movements across the Muslim world, marking a seismic shift away from secular nationalism toward religious governance.

## you gotta know

- The Shah's regime (1953–1979), installed after a CIA-backed coup, pursued rapid modernization and Western alignment while suppressing political opposition, fostering resentment among religious scholars, bazaar merchants, workers, and intellectuals alienated by his authoritarianism.
- *Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini*, an exiled Shiite cleric, emerged as the revolution's spiritual leader; his doctrine of *velayat-e faqih* (guardianship of the jurist) proposed that religious scholars should hold ultimate political authority rather than secular rulers.
- Mass protests erupted in 1978, paralyzing the economy; strikes by oil workers, bazaaris (merchants), and public-sector employees crippled the regime; by January 1979, the Shah fled and Khomeini returned to Iran to a hero's reception.
- Khomeini consolidated power by neutralizing secular nationalists, liberals, and communist factions within the revolutionary coalition; the *hostage crisis* (November 1979–January 1981), when militants took 52 American hostages, rallied Iranians and isolated the new regime internationally.
- The revolution institutionalized Islamic law (Sharia), banned Western cultural products, required strict Islamic dress codes, and subordinated state power to a Supreme Leader answerable to religious councils, establishing the world's first modern theocracy.
- The revolution triggered the *Iran-Iraq War* (1980–88), as Iraq's Saddam Hussein attacked the unstable new regime; it also inspired Shiite uprisings across the region and transformed Iran into an anti-American, anti-Israeli regional power.

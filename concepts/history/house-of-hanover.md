## summary

The **House of Hanover** was a German royal dynasty that ruled [[england]] and later the [[british-empire]] from 1714 to 1901. Ascending to the throne after the death of Queen Anne, the last Stuart monarch, the Hanoverian dynasty brought with it a new line of succession and transformed Britain into a constitutional monarchy. The dynasty ultimately evolved into the [[house-of-windsor]] through marriage and continued as the reigning family of the modern [[united-kingdom]].

## you gotta know

- Inherited the [[england|English]] throne in 1714 through the Act of Settlement, which ensured a Protestant succession after the [[english-civil-war]] and religious turmoil.
- Founded by [[george-i]], a German prince who spoke little [[england|English]] and delegated much power to Parliament, strengthening constitutional governance.
- Produced [[george-iii]], the first Hanoverian king to be born in [[england]] and thoroughly English in education and sentiment.
- Reached its cultural and political apex under [[victoria]], whose long reign (1837–1901) defined the Victorian era and made Britain a global superpower.
- Maintained legitimacy through strategic marriages, with [[victoria]] marrying into various European royal houses and cementing Britain's diplomatic position.
- Officially changed its name to the [[house-of-windsor]] in 1917 during [[world-war-i]], abandoning the German name Hanover due to wartime sentiment against Germany.
- Oversaw Britain's transformation from an island kingdom to a global maritime empire controlling vast territories across Asia, Africa, and the Americas.
- The dynasty's relationship with Parliament evolved from the limited influence of early German-born kings to the constitutional monarchy model where the sovereign reigns but does not rule.
- Produced several capable administrators and military leaders who navigated European politics, colonial expansion, and industrial revolution, making Britain the world's leading power by the 19th century.

## connections

- [[house-of-stuart]] — the previous ruling dynasty; Hanover succeeded them through parliamentary succession.
- [[george-iii]] — presided over the [[american-revolution]] and the loss of the American colonies.
- [[victoria]] — embodied the height of British imperial power and gave her name to an era.
- [[england]] — the kingdom the dynasty ruled and transformed into an empire.
- [[house-of-windsor]] — the continuation of the Hanoverian line under a new name.
- [[british-empire]] — the global power that reached its zenith under Hanoverian rule.

## summary

**Chester Nimitz** was the American fleet admiral who served as Commander in Chief of the United States Pacific Fleet (*CINCPAC*) and Pacific Ocean Areas during *World War II*. Assuming command in the grim aftermath of the attack on *Pearl Harbor*, he directed the Allied naval campaign across the Pacific, orchestrating the pivotal victory at the *Battle of Midway* and the island-hopping offensive that systematically dismantled the maritime empire of *Japan*. Reaching the five-star rank of Fleet Admiral, he signed the Japanese Instrument of Surrender on behalf of the *United States* aboard the battleship USS *Missouri* in 1945.

## you gotta know

- Appointed Commander in Chief of the Pacific Fleet by President *Franklin D. Roosevelt* ten days after the devastating surprise strike on *Pearl Harbor*, formally raising his flag aboard the submarine USS *Grayling*.
- Masterminded the decisive American triumph at the *Battle of Midway* in June 1942, trusting naval cryptanalysts led by *Joseph Rochefort* at Station HYPO who broke the Japanese naval code JN-25.
- Implemented the dual-command rotation system between Admiral *Raymond A. Spruance* (Fifth Fleet) and Admiral *William F. Halsey Jr.* (Third Fleet), ensuring continuous operational pressure on Japanese forces while alternate staffs planned subsequent amphibious assaults.
- Coordinated the strategic island-hopping offensive across the Central Pacific, bypassing heavily fortified enemy strongholds to seize airfields and anchorages at Tarawa, Kwajalein, Saipan, Guam, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa.
- Advanced to the newly created five-star rank of Fleet Admiral in December 1944 alongside contemporaries *Ernest King*, *William Leahy*, and later *Halsey*.
- Signed the Instrument of Surrender ending hostilities with *Japan* on September 2, 1945, representing the American government while General *Douglas MacArthur* presided as Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.
- Supported early naval nuclear propulsion development under Admiral *Hyman G. Rickover* and was later honored as the namesake of the USS *Chester W. Nimitz*, the lead ship of the US Navy's nuclear supercarrier class.

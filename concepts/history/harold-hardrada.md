## summary

**Harald Hardrada** (*Harald Sigurdsson*), also known as *Harald III*, was King of Norway from 1046 until his death in 1066. Renowned throughout Europe as a formidable commander and mercenary in the Byzantine Varangian Guard, his epithet *Hardrada* translates from Old Norse as "hard ruler" or "stern counsel." His fatal invasion of northern Britain in September 1066 and subsequent defeat at the Battle of Stamford Bridge is traditionally viewed by historians as marking the definitive end of the Viking Age.

## you gotta know

- Fought at the Battle of Stiklestad in 1030 at age fifteen in defense of his half-brother, King *Olaf Haraldsson* (Saint Olaf), fleeing into exile after Olaf's defeat and death.
- Spent over a decade in Kievan Rus' under Prince *Yaroslav the Wise* before traveling to Constantinople to enlist in the elite imperial Varangian Guard.
- Campaigned extensively across the Mediterranean, fighting for the Byzantine Empire in Asia Minor, Sicily, southern Italy, and the Holy Land, amassing immense personal wealth.
- Returned to Scandinavia in 1045, compelling his nephew *Magnus the Good* to share the Norwegian throne before ruling as sole monarch following Magnus's death in 1047.
- Claimed the throne of England in 1066 upon the death of King *Edward the Confessor*, basing his assertion on an alleged mutual succession pact made between Magnus and Harthacnut.
- Formed an alliance with *Tostig Godwinson*, the estranged and exiled brother of the newly crowned Anglo-Saxon king *Harold Godwinson*.
- Won an initial victory against northern English earls at the Battle of Fulford before advancing toward York.
- Stunned by a surprise counter-march by *Harold Godwinson*, he was struck in the throat by an arrow and killed at the *Battle of Stamford Bridge* on September 25, 1066.

## connections

- [[england]] — realm he audaciously invaded in 1066 in an effort to re-establish the North Sea Empire.
- [[christianity]] — religion he championed in Norway by consolidating church dioceses and venerating his canonized half-brother, Saint Olaf.
- [[roman-empire]] — continuation in the medieval Byzantine Empire, whose eastern emperors Harald served as commander of the Varangian Guard.
- [[middle-east]] — region where Harald conducted military expeditions and guarded Christian pilgrim routes during his Byzantine service.
- [[france]] — origin territory of the Normans under William the Conqueror, who invaded southern Britain immediately following Harald's defeat.

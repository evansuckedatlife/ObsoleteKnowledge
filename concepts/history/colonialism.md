## summary

**Colonialism** is the political and economic system by which a metropolitan power controls and exploits distant territories and their populations, extracting resources and imposing governance structures. From the 15th century onward, European nations embarked on colonial expansion that reshaped the globe, establishing settlements, extracting wealth, and subjugating indigenous peoples. Colonialism created the modern international order while generating immense suffering and lasting structural inequality.

## you gotta know

- Began systematically during the Age of Exploration when European powers sought oceanic trade routes and territorial conquest.
- Spain and Portugal pioneered colonial empires in the Americas and Asia, dividing the world through treaties like Tordesillas and establishing extraction systems for gold, silver, and later sugar.
- Britain, France, and the Netherlands built rival empires, competing for colonial territory and resources across the Atlantic, Indian, and Pacific basins from the 1600s onward.
- Generated the triangular trade linking Europe, Africa, and the Americas: manufactured goods to Africa, enslaved people to the Americas, raw materials back to Europe.
- Justified through claims of civilizing missions and religious conversion, masking the fundamental purpose of wealth extraction and political control.
- Structured colonial economies as dependency systems: colonies produced raw materials and consumed European manufactures, enriching the metropole at colonial expense.
- Sparked resistance from indigenous populations and colonial subjects that intensified through the 18th and 19th centuries, eventually leading to independence movements and decolonization.

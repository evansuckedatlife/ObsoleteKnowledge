## summary

The **Dacian Wars** were two monumental military campaigns conducted between 101–102 CE and 105–106 CE in which Emperor *Trajan* led the legions of the [[roman-empire]] across the Danube to conquer the kingdom of *Dacia*, ruled by King *Decebalus*. Sparked by border insecurity, broken treaties, and Rome's desire to erase the humiliating subsidies paid under Emperor *Domitian*, the war concluded with the total defeat of Dacia and its transformation into an imperial province. The massive influx of plundered Dacian gold stabilized Roman state finances, funded immense architectural works across [[rome]], and propelled the empire to its absolute zenith of geographic expansion.

## you gotta know

- Commemorated in extraordinary narrative detail upon *[[trajans-column]]* in Rome, a thirty-meter freestanding marble monument featuring a spiraling helical relief that depicts legionary construction, river crossings, and combat scenes.
- Fought under Emperor *Trajan* to eliminate the persistent northern frontier threat posed by the Dacian king *Decebalus*, who had united mountain tribes and soundly defeated previous Roman expeditions in Moesia.
- Spanned the mighty Danube River via *Trajan's Bridge*, an unprecedented engineering marvel designed by the Syrian architect *Apollodorus of Damascus*, featuring massive stone piers and wooden segmental arches that facilitated Roman troop supply lines.
- Culminated in the siege and destruction of the heavily fortified mountain capital of *Sarmizegetusa Regia*, where Roman soldiers breached massive dry-stone walls and severed the defenders' water supply aqueducts.
- Ended dramatically when King *Decebalus* committed suicide with a curved Dacian blade (*falx* or *sica*) beneath an oak tree to prevent capture, after which his severed head was dispatched to Trajan and displayed upon the Gemonian Stairs in Rome.
- Yielded an astronomical quantity of royal plunder hidden beneath the diverted riverbed of the *Sargetia River*, which was betrayed to Roman captors by the Dacian nobleman *Bicilis*.
- Financed over four months of lavish public gladiatorial games in Rome, funded the construction of the colossal *Forum of Trajan* and *Trajan's Market*, and permanently shaped the romance linguistic identity of modern Romania through intensive imperial colonization.

## connections

- [[roman-empire]] — the Mediterranean superstate whose northern Danubian frontier was expanded and fortified by the campaigns, reaching its territorial peak under Trajan.
- [[rome]] — the imperial capital city whose urban topography was transformed through the construction of Trajan's Forum, markets, and celebratory monuments using Dacian booty.
- [[trajans-column]] — the celebrated triumphal column erected between the Greek and Latin libraries in Trajan's Forum to visually document the two campaigns.
- [[damascus]] — the Levantine birthplace of *Apollodorus of Damascus*, Trajan's master architect who designed the legendary Danube bridge and the imperial forum complex.
- [[hadrian]] — Trajan's cousin and imperial successor who served with distinction as a legionary commander during the wars before adopting a policy of frontier consolidation.
- [[nerva]] — the elderly Roman emperor who adopted Trajan as his heir and successor, ensuring a stable military transition that enabled the campaigns against Dacia.

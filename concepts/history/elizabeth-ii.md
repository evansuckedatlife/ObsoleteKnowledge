## summary

**Elizabeth II** was the queen regnant of the United Kingdom and other Commonwealth realms from 1952 until her death in 2022. Acceding to the throne upon the death of her father, King *George VI*, her reign of over seventy years was the longest verified reign of any British monarch and the longest recorded of any female head of state in world history. Her era encompassed the post-war reconstruction of Britain, the formal dissolution of the *[[british-empire]]*, and the navigation of the *[[cold-war]]* into the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Longest-reigning monarch in British history, celebrating her Platinum Jubilee in 2022 after reigning for 70 years and 214 days.
- Succeeded her father *George VI* in February 1952 while on a royal tour in Kenya, after his early death following years of declining health.
- Served during *[[world-war-ii]]* as an honorary second subaltern in the Auxiliary Territorial Service, training as a mechanic and truck driver.
- Oversaw the transition of former imperial dominions into the modern Commonwealth of Nations during rapid decolonization across Africa, Asia, and the Caribbean.
- Worked alongside fifteen British Prime Ministers during her reign, beginning with *Winston Churchill* in 1952 and concluding with *Liz Truss* in 2022.
- Dubbed 1992 her *annus horribilis* ("horrible year") following royal separations, widespread public controversy, and a major fire at Windsor Castle.
- Maintained a strict doctrine of political neutrality, serving primarily as a constitutional figurehead and continuity symbol through societal transformations in modern *[[england]]*.

## summary

**Dmitri of Uglich** was the youngest son of Tsar *Ivan the Terrible* and the last male heir of the storied Rurik dynasty. Exiled with his mother *Maria Nagaya* to the provincial town of Uglich following his father's death, the eight-year-old tsarevich died in 1591 under mysterious circumstances involving a severed throat. His death extinguished the dynasty's legitimate succession, cast suspicion on regent *Boris Godunov*, and triggered the chaotic political vacuum known as the Time of Troubles.

## you gotta know

- Born in 1582 to *Ivan the Terrible* and his uncanonical seventh wife, *Maria Nagaya*, leaving his legal legitimacy vulnerable to canon law disputes.
- Sent into internal exile to the town of *Uglich* in 1584 following the coronation of his weak-willed older half-brother, Tsar *Feodor I*.
- Found dead in the palace courtyard on May 15, 1591, with a fatal stab wound to his throat, causing an immediate riot in which local citizens killed suspected assassins.
- Investigated by a commission headed by boyar *Vasili Shuisky*, which officially concluded that Dmitri had accidentally stabbed himself during an epileptic seizure while playing a knife game called *tychka*.
- Widely believed by the Russian public to have been assassinated on the direct orders of the court regent and future tsar, *Boris Godunov*.
- Gave rise during the *Time of Troubles* to several pretenders claiming to be the miraculously surviving prince, most notably the crowned *False Dmitry I*.
- Canonized in 1606 by the *Russian Orthodox Church* as a holy passion-bearer after his relics were brought to Moscow by Tsar *Vasili Shuisky* to dispel impostor rumors.
- Dramatized famously as the phantom haunt and psychological torment of the usurping monarch in *Alexander Pushkin*'s historical drama and *Modest Mussorgsky*'s opera *Boris Godunov*.

## summary

**Hawaii** is an isolated volcanic archipelago in the central Pacific Ocean that developed a sophisticated Polynesian civilization and sovereign kingdom before becoming a focal point of Western expansion during the [[age-of-exploration]]. Annexed by the [[united-states]] in 1898 following an American-backed coup against its native monarchy, the territory played a central geopolitical role in trans-Pacific trade, sugar cultivation, and naval power. The island chain shaped modern global history as the site of the catastrophic 1941 surprise attack that thrust America into [[world-war-ii]], culminating in its admission as the fiftieth U.S. state in 1959.

## you gotta know

- Encountered in 1778 by British navigator *James Cook* during his third Pacific voyage, who named the archipelago the *Sandwich Islands* in honour of the Earl of Sandwich before being killed at Kealakekua Bay in 1779.
- Unified into a single sovereign realm in 1795 by *Kamehameha I*, who utilized Western cannons, muskets, and trade alliances to conquer rival island chieftains.
- Overthrown in an 1893 coup led by American sugar magnates and missionary descendants known as the Committee of Safety, aided by landing troops from the cruiser *USS Boston*.
- Ruled by its last reigning monarch, Queen *Liliuokalani*, who composed the beloved song *Aloha Oe* and attempted to promulgate a new constitution restoring voting rights to native subjects before being deposed and imprisoned in Iolani Palace.
- Condemned initially by President *Grover Cleveland* after the *Blount Report* determined the coup was an illegal act of war, though Cleveland was blocked from restoring the Queen, leading to the self-proclaimed Republic under *Sanford B. Dole*.
- Annexed via the 1898 *Newlands Resolution* during the Spanish-American War under President *William McKinley*, who recognized the archipelago's vital naval strategic position.
- Suffered the December 7, 1941, surprise attack on the naval base at Pearl Harbor by the Empire of Japan, provoking immediate entry by the [[united-states]] into [[world-war-ii]].
- Governed under martial law for three years following Pearl Harbor before achieving full statehood in 1959, leading to the *Apology Resolution* passed by Congress in 1993 acknowledging the illegal overthrow.

## connections

- [[age-of-exploration]] — the global era of European maritime navigation that brought Captain *James Cook* to the archipelago.
- [[colonialism]] — the imperialist dynamic through which white plantation oligarchs displaced the native Polynesian monarchical government.
- [[united-states]] — the federal republic that absorbed the islands through joint congressional resolution and admitted them as the fiftieth state.
- [[british-empire]] — the maritime power whose early naval contacts shaped initial island diplomacy, commemorated by the Union Jack in the state flag.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict ignited for America when naval facilities at Pearl Harbor were devastated by carrier-launched air strikes.
- [[cold-war]] — the postwar era of military expansion across the Pacific that accelerated Hawaii's political integration and statehood.

## summary

**Kenneth Starr** was an independent counsel whose investigations into President Bill Clinton's conduct consumed American politics in the 1990s and culminated in Clinton's impeachment. Appointed to investigate the [[whitewater]] land deal, Starr's mandate expanded dramatically to encompass the [[lewinsky-scandal]], resulting in the notorious Starr Report that detailed Clinton's affair with White House intern Monica Lewinsky. His work became emblematic of partisan investigation and raised lasting questions about the proper scope of independent counsel authority.

## you gotta know

- Born in 1946; was a federal judge and solicitor general before being appointed independent counsel in 1994.
- Initially tasked with investigating Whitewater, a controversial 1980s land investment involving the Clintons in Arkansas.
- His investigation broadened to include questions of Clinton's financial conduct, obstruction of justice, and personal behavior.
- Pursued aggressive legal tactics including grand jury testimony from presidential advisors and threatened legal action against the president.
- Expanded into investigating Clinton's relationship with Monica Lewinsky after a tip in 1997, making the scandal the focus of his inquiry.
- Released the Starr Report in September 1998, an explicit 445-page document detailing Clinton's affair that was published on the internet.

## summary

The **1920s**, widely celebrated as the **Roaring Twenties** and the **Jazz Age**, was a transformative decade characterized by rapid economic expansion, sweeping social change, and vibrant cultural dynamism across the Western world, particularly in the [[united-states]]. Following the trauma of [[world-war-i]], the era witnessed unprecedented urbanization, the nationwide enforcement of *Prohibition*, the rise of mass consumerism, and groundbreaking innovations in popular entertainment. However, this pervasive prosperity masked persistent agricultural distress, intense racial and nativist friction, and volatile financial speculation that ultimately triggered the catastrophic Wall Street crash of October 1929.

## you gotta know

- *Prohibition* went into nationwide effect in 1920 under the *Eighteenth Amendment* and the *Volstead Act*, fostering widespread bootlegging, speakeasies, and the violent rise of organized crime syndicates led by figures like *Al Capone*.
- The Wall Street crash on *Black Tuesday* (October 29, 1929) abruptly ended the era's economic expansion, triggering a global financial panic and initiating the worldwide *Great Depression*.
- Mass entertainment and consumer culture expanded through commercial radio broadcasting, assembly-line manufacturing perfected by *Henry Ford*, and the climax of the [[silent-film]] era with the 1927 sound feature *The Jazz Singer*.
- The [[harlem-renaissance]] blossomed in [[new-york-city]] as African American artists, writers, and musicians like *Langston Hughes*, *Zora Neale Hurston*, and *Duke Ellington* led a cultural movement centered on racial pride and creative autonomy.
- Expatriate writers of the "Lost Generation" defined [[modernism]] and transformed [[american-literature]], crafting celebrated portraits of disillusionment such as *The Great Gatsby* by *F. Scott Fitzgerald* and *The Sun Also Rises* by *Ernest Hemingway*.
- The 1925 *Scopes Monkey Trial* in Dayton, Tennessee, dramatized national divisions over religious fundamentalism and modernity as defense attorney *Clarence Darrow* sparred against *William Jennings Bryan* over teaching evolution.
- Nativist anxieties prompted the restrictive quotas of the *Immigration Act of 1924*, the controversial execution of Italian anarchists *Nicola Sacco* and *Bartolomeo Vanzetti*, and the rapid revival of the *Ku Klux Klan*.
- Conservative Republican administrations under *Warren G. Harding*, *Calvin Coolidge*, and *Herbert Hoover* championed tax reductions and deregulation, though Harding's term was tainted by the *Teapot Dome* bribery scandal.

## connections

- [[world-war-i]] — catalyzed the disillusionment of the Lost Generation and established the global economic dominance of the United States.
- [[harlem-renaissance]] — fostered a landmark flowering of African American literature, visual arts, and music centered in [[new-york-city]].
- [[jazz]] — soundtracked the urban nightlife, dancing flappers, and illicit speakeasy culture that gave the era its defining moniker.
- [[modernism]] — served as the foundational aesthetic movement transforming Western literature, visual art, and architecture in response to post-war realities.
- [[silent-film]] — reached its artistic and commercial zenith as a universal mass entertainment medium before transitioning to sound pictures.
- [[american-literature]] — produced enduring masterworks reflecting the disillusionment, hedonism, and excess of the post-war generation.
- [[soviet-union]] — consolidated power after the Russian Civil War, transitioning from Lenin's New Economic Policy to Stalin's first Five-Year Plan.
- [[germany]] — navigated the volatile instability of the Weimar Republic, from devastating hyperinflation to cultural flourishing and subsequent collapse.

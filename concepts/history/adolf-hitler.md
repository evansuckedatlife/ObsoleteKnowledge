## summary

**Adolf Hitler** was the Austrian-born dictator of Nazi Germany from 1934 to 1945 and the architect of one of history's worst genocides. Rising from obscurity through nationalist politics during the economic chaos of the *Weimar Republic*, he consolidated totalitarian power, rearmed Germany, and invaded neighboring countries, triggering *World War II*. His genocidal ideology and catastrophic leadership directly caused the deaths of tens of millions and the *Holocaust*.

## you gotta know

- Served as a soldier in *World War I* and later claimed the "stab-in-the-back" myth, blaming internal enemies for Germany's defeat.
- Published *Mein Kampf* in 1925, a rambling autobiography outlining his extreme nationalist ideology and antisemitic conspiracy theories.
- Rose to power partly through legal means: his Nazi Party won electoral support during the Great Depression, and he was appointed Chancellor in January 1933.
- Consolidated totalitarian control by eliminating internal party rivals during the 1934 purge known as the Night of the Long Knives.
- Openly defied the Treaty of Versailles by rearming, remilitarizing the Rhineland, and annexing *Austria* and *Czechoslovakia* without military consequence.
- Ordered *Operation Barbarossa* in 1941, his massive invasion of the *Soviet Union* that he viewed as a racial war for *Lebensraum* (living space).
- Committed suicide in his Berlin bunker in 1945 as the *Soviet* and Western armies converged, leaving a catastrophic legacy.

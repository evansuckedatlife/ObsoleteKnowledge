## summary

**Mardonius** was an elite Persian nobleman and supreme military commander during the Achaemenid Empire's campaigns against [[ancient-greece]] in the early fifth century BCE. A trusted cousin, son-in-law, and general to the Great Kings of Persia, he served as the foremost advocate for imperial expansion into mainland Europe. Following the Persian naval disaster at the Battle of Salamis, he assumed sole command of the remaining imperial land army, whose catastrophic defeat and his own death at the Battle of Plataea in 479 BCE permanently ended Persian attempts to conquer the Greek poleis.

## you gotta know

- Slain at the decisive Battle of *Plataea* in 479 BCE while conspicuously leading his troops on a white horse, where his battlefield death at the hands of the Spartan warrior *Arimnestus* shattered imperial morale and triggered the complete rout of the Persian ground forces.
- Entrusted with supreme command of the Persian occupation army by King *Xerxes I*, who retreated across the Hellespont into Asia Minor after the catastrophic Persian naval defeat at the Battle of Salamis in 480 BCE.
- Reoccupied and burned Athens a second time in 479 BCE, razing remaining temples and civic infrastructure to the ground after Athenian envoys rejected his diplomatic proposal for an autonomous alliance against *Sparta*.
- Acted as the chief warmonger and strategic architect of the second Persian invasion, aggressively lobbying *Xerxes I* to launch a massive punitive expedition across Europe despite the cautious counsel of royal elders like *Artabanus*.
- Subjugated Macedonia into a tributary vassal state and secured imperial hegemony over Thrace during his initial European offensive in 492 BCE, though a sudden storm off Mount *Athos* destroyed much of his fleet.
- Deposed local tyrants across the coastal cities of Ionia and instituted democratic governments in 492 BCE, a pragmatic administrative reform intended to conciliate rebellious Greeks following the Ionian Revolt.
- Reached the summit of imperial nobility as the son of *Gobryas*, one of the seven Persian aristocrats who helped *Darius the Great* seize the throne, and consolidated his royal ties by marrying Darius's daughter *Artazostre*.

## connections

- [[pausanias]] — Spartan regent and allied general who defeated the Persian army at *Plataea* and whose forces killed Mardonius in combat.
- [[persian-wars]] — foundational military conflict between the Achaemenid Empire and Greek city-states where Mardonius served as chief imperial commander.
- [[ancient-greece]] — collection of independent Hellenic city-states whose coordinated resistance thwarted Mardonius's campaign of conquest and subjugation.
- [[middle-east]] — imperial heartland of the Achaemenid realm from which Mardonius mobilized vast multi-ethnic infantry and cavalry contingents.
- [[xerxes-i]] — Persian emperor and cousin who authorized the grand invasion of Greece and left Mardonius in command of the occupation.
- [[darius-the-great]] — monarch and father-in-law who originally appointed Mardonius to lead the first Persian expedition into Thrace and Macedonia.
- [[roman-empire]] — subsequent Mediterranean power whose military historians analyzed the tactical misjudgments and supply vulnerabilities that sealed Mardonius's defeat.

## summary

The **Meiji Restoration**, or **Meiji Ishin**, also known historically as the **Meiji Renovation**, was the transformative 1868 political revolution in [[japan]] that overthrew the centuries-old *Tokugawa shogunate* and restored direct imperial governance under *Emperor Meiji*. Catalyzed by the sudden arrival of Western gunboat diplomacy and intensifying domestic economic crises, the event ended more than two centuries of strict isolationist policy and inaugurated an era of state-directed modernization, industrial expansion, and cultural westernization during the global [[industrial-revolution]]. By dismantling the hereditary *samurai* hierarchy and establishing a centralized bureaucratic administration supported by universal conscription, this radical restructuring converted an agrarian archipelago into a major industrial power, fundamentally reshaping Asian geopolitics and setting the stage for [[world-war-i]] and [[world-war-ii]].

## you gotta know

- Precipitated by the 1853 arrival of *Commodore Matthew Perry* and his "Black Ships" from the [[united-states]], which broke Japan's isolationist *sakoku* policy and forced the signing of unequal treaties.
- Culminated in the *Boshin War* (1868–1869), where modernizing samurai from the southwestern *Satcho Alliance* (*Satsuma* and *Choshu*) overthrew the final shogun, *Tokugawa Yoshinobu*.
- Articulated its modernizing vision in the 1868 *Charter Oath*, an imperial decree promising deliberative assemblies, the abandonment of obsolete customs, and the pursuit of international knowledge.
- Abolished the feudal *han* system in 1871, replacing daimyo domains with centralized prefectures and stripping the *samurai* of their stipends and sword-bearing privileges through the *Haitorei Edict*.
- Crushed the 1877 *Satsuma Rebellion* led by disaffected former imperial councilor *Saigo Takamori*, marking the bloody final stand of traditional warrior culture against the government's newly formed peasant conscript army at the *Battle of Shiroyama*.
- Dispatched the landmark *Iwakura Mission* (1871–1873), an extensive diplomatic voyage across the [[united-states]], [[england]], [[france]], and [[germany]] to observe Western legal and industrial institutions while seeking revisions to humiliating extraterritorial treaties.
- Advanced rapid modernization under the slogans *fukoku kyohei* ("enrich the country, strengthen the military") and *bunmei kaika* ("civilization and enlightenment"), building model facilities like the *Tomioka Silk Mill*.
- Established a constitutional monarchy with the 1889 *Meiji Constitution*, modeled on authoritarian imperial [[germany]], which created the *Imperial Diet* while enshrining the emperor's sacred supreme command.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the island nation fundamentally transformed from a decentralized feudal shogunate into a centralized modern empire.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the global transformation adopted through state-built telegraph lines, steam railways, and mechanized textile mills.
- [[united-states]] — the naval power whose gunboat expedition under *Commodore Matthew Perry* forced the collapse of Japanese isolationism.
- [[germany]] — the European nation whose authoritarian Prussian constitution and military doctrine served as the primary model for Meiji governance.
- [[france]] — the nation that shaped early Japanese civil and penal legal codes, judicial structures, and modern military dockyards.
- [[england]] — the maritime empire that assisted in training the Imperial Japanese Navy and building the country's first railway lines.
- [[china]] — the Qing empire defeated in the First Sino-Japanese War, transferring Taiwan to Japanese control and proving Japan's military modernization.
- [[russian-empire]] — the Eurasian great power defeated in the 1904–1905 war, marking the first modern Asian victory over a European empire.

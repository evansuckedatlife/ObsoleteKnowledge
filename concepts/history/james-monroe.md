# James Monroe

## summary

**James Monroe** (1758–1831) was the fifth *President of the United States* (1817–1825), whose presidency was defined by territorial expansion, the end of the *War of 1812*, and the *Monroe Doctrine*, a bold foreign policy declaration that asserted American interests in the Western Hemisphere. A *Founding Father* and diplomat who served under *Jefferson*, Monroe epitomized early American nationalism and continental ambition.

## you gotta know

- Monroe served as *Secretary of State* and *Secretary of War* under *Thomas Jefferson* and *James Madison*, establishing himself as a skilled diplomat and military administrator before his presidency.
- The *Monroe Doctrine* (1823) declared that the Western Hemisphere was no longer open to European colonization or interference by European powers; it positioned the United States as the dominant regional power and set the template for American foreign policy for centuries.
- Monroe's presidency saw the acquisition of *Florida* from *Spain* (1821), completing American control of the southeastern frontier and removing a source of Spanish military threat.
- The *Missouri Compromise* (1820) occurred under Monroe's watch, brokering a temporary peace between free and slave states by admitting *Missouri* as a slave state and *Maine* as free.
- Monroe opposed *Aaron Burr*'s western schemes and adventurism, positioning himself as a cautious steward of American expansion within constitutional bounds.
- His presidency was called the *"Era of Good Feelings,"* a period of national optimism, reduced partisan rancor, and economic growth following the War of 1812.
- Monroe believed in *Manifest Destiny* but pursued territorial expansion through diplomacy and purchase rather than conquest, setting a precedent that contrasted with some successors' militarism.

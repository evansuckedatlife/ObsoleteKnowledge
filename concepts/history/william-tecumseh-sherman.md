## summary

**William Tecumseh Sherman** (also recognized as **William T. Sherman**) was a preeminent Union general during the *American Civil War* whose strategic partnership with *Ulysses S. Grant* and operational mastery of total war decisively broke Confederate resistance. Appointed commander of the Military Division of the Mississippi in 1864, he directed wide-ranging joint offensives that captured the vital industrial and railway hub of *Atlanta*, an achievement that revived Northern political resolve and ensured the reelection of President *Abraham Lincoln*. His subsequent campaigns across Georgia and the Carolinas dismantled Southern logistical networks and civilian morale, establishing him as one of the earliest and most influential practitioners of modern psychological and scorched-earth warfare.

## you gotta know

- Executed the famed *March to the Sea* in late 1864, severing supply lines to march sixty thousand Union troops across Georgia from *Atlanta* to *Savannah*, living off the land while systematically destroying railways and infrastructure.
- Captured *Atlanta* in September 1864 after outmaneuvering Confederate forces under *Joseph E. Johnston* and *John Bell Hood*, a critical victory that boosted Northern morale and secured *Abraham Lincoln*'s reelection.
- Championed the doctrine of *total war*—or *hard war*—reasoning that lasting peace required demonstrating the harsh realities of conflict to civilian populations, later popularized in his 1879 remark that *"War is hell"*.
- Issued *Special Field Orders, No. 15* in Savannah in January 1865, temporarily redistributing hundreds of thousands of confiscated coastal acres to freed families in forty-acre plots, inspiring the phrase *"forty acres and a mule"*.
- Commanded the *Carolinas Campaign* in 1865, capturing *Columbia* and defeating Johnston at *Bentonville* before accepting the surrender of the largest remaining Confederate force at *Bennett Place*, North Carolina.
- Rejected post-war efforts to draft him for the presidency, stating in an 1884 telegram that defined the concept of a *Shermanesque statement*: *"If nominated I will not run; if elected I will not serve"*.
- Given the middle name *Tecumseh* in honor of the Shawnee leader, raised by politician *Thomas Ewing* after his father's death, and served as superintendent of a Louisiana military academy before the war.

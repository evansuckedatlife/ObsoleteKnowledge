## summary

**David Greenglass** was an American machinist and atomic spy who served in the *Manhattan Project* at *Los Alamos* during *World War II*. Recruited into espionage by his brother-in-law *Julius Rosenberg*, he delivered handwritten notes and technical sketches of high-explosive implosion lenses to the *Soviet Union*. His devastating testimony as the prosecution's star witness in 1951 resulted in the capital convictions and subsequent executions of his sister *Ethel Rosenberg* and Julius, an act of familial betrayal that remains one of the defining controversies of the early *Cold War*.

## you gotta know

- Testified as the central witness against his sister *Ethel Rosenberg* and brother-in-law *Julius Rosenberg*, providing the vital testimony that sent them to the electric chair at *Sing Sing* in 1951.
- Admitted publicly in 1996 to biographer *Sam Roberts* that he had committed perjury on the witness stand regarding Ethel typing his espionage notes, fabricating the claim under pressure to spare his wife *Ruth Greenglass* from prosecution.
- Stationed as an army corporal and skilled machinist at the secret *Los Alamos Laboratory* in *New Mexico*, where he had direct access to high-explosive lens molds developed for the *Fat Man* plutonium device.
- Transferred crude sketches and technical descriptions of the atomic implosion mechanism to Soviet courier *Harry Gold*, utilizing a severed half of a *Jell-O* box cover as a clandestine recognition signal.
- Assigned the Soviet operational codename *KALIBR* (*Caliber*) by his foreign handlers within the *NKVD*, who prized his access to technical specifications inside the weapons facility.
- Received a fifteen-year federal prison sentence after pleading guilty to espionage conspiracy, ultimately serving nine and a half years before his early release in 1960.
- Assumed the alias *David Matthews* upon his release, living an obscure life in *New York* while remaining permanently alienated from his nephews *Michael Meeropol* and *Robert Meeropol*.
- Exposed by the *Federal Bureau of Investigation* following the arrest and confession of British physicist *Klaus Fuchs*, serving as the critical domestic link that unraveled the Rosenberg espionage network.

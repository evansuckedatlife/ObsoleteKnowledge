## summary

The **Panic of 1893** was a catastrophic financial collapse in the [[united-states]] that initiated the deepest economic depression the nation faced prior to the 1930s. Sparked by railroad overbuilding, commercial bank failures, and an acute run on federal gold reserves, the panic paralyzed domestic commerce and produced widespread peacetime unemployment from 1893 until roughly 1897. It transformed American politics by shattering the conservative Democratic coalition under President *Grover Cleveland*, galvanizing the agrarian free-silver movement, and ushering in decades of Republican national dominance.

## you gotta know

- Stood as the most severe economic crisis in the [[united-states]] before the Great Depression, crippling the second administration of President *Grover Cleveland* and shaping the pivotal presidential election of 1896.
- Catapulted the agrarian "Free Silver" crusade into national politics, prompting bimetallists to seize the Democratic Party and inspiring *William Jennings Bryan* to deliver his famous *Cross of Gold* address.
- Precipitated nationwide labor turmoil and strikes, most famously seen when federal troops and marshals intervened to crush the 1894 *Pullman Strike* led by *Eugene V. Debs* and the *American Railway Union*.
- Inspired the 1894 protest march of *Coxey's Army*, in which hundreds of unemployed men led by *Jacob Coxey* marched to Washington to demand public works projects funded by paper money.
- Drained the federal treasury gold reserve beneath the critical statutory threshold of one hundred million dollars, forcing *Grover Cleveland* to convene a special congressional session to repeal the inflationary *Sherman Silver Purchase Act*.
- Compelled the executive branch to accept a controversial private bailout in 1895 arranged by financier *J.P. Morgan* and the European *Rothschild* banking syndicate, who supplied sixty-five million dollars in gold bullion to protect the nation's solvency.
- Erupted in early 1893 following the bankruptcy of the overextended *Philadelphia and Reading Railroad* and the sudden insolvency of the speculative *National Cordage Company* on Wall Street in [[new-york-city]].
- Remained closely guarded when *Grover Cleveland* underwent secret cancer surgery aboard the private yacht *Oneida* to remove a malignant jaw tumor, hiding his illness so pro-silver Vice President *Adlai Stevenson I* would not assume executive power.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the republic plunged into acute depression, widespread bank runs, mass industrial unemployment, and prolonged agricultural destitution.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the era of rapid corporate mechanization and speculative transcontinental railroad capitalization whose credit collapse triggered the panic.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the preceding epoch whose unresolved agrarian debt systems and deflationary pressures left rural farmers vulnerable to market failure.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict that created the national banking structure and paper greenback currency that framed the depression's monetary battles.
- [[new-york-city]] — the financial epicenter where equity crashes wiped out brokerage houses and Wall Street banks suspended gold shipments to rural institutions.
- [[united-states-constitution]] — the governing charter interpreted by the Supreme Court in *Pollock v. Farmers' Loan and Trust Company* when striking down the 1894 federal income tax.
- [[british-empire]] — the dominant global financial power whose nervous investors liquidated American securities and repatriated gold across the Atlantic, accelerating domestic panic.

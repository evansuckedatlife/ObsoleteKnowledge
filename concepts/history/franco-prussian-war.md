## summary

The **Franco-Prussian War**, also known as the **War of 1870**, was an armed conflict waged from July 1870 to May 1871 between the Second French Empire and a Prussian-led German coalition. Engineered by Chancellor *Otto von Bismarck*, the crisis was ignited by diplomatic friction over the Spanish succession and the provocative *Ems Dispatch*. The decisive Prussian victory led directly to [[german-unification]], the birth of the [[german-empire]], the fall of Emperor *Napoleon III*, and the annexation of *Alsace-Lorraine*, fundamentally altering the European balance of power and laying the groundwork for [[world-war-i]].

## you gotta know

- Triggered by diplomatic disputes over the Hohenzollern candidacy for the throne of [[spain]], escalated when *Otto von Bismarck* released the edited *Ems Dispatch*.
- The *Battle of Sedan* in September 1870 ended in the capture of Emperor *Napoleon III*, prompting the immediate collapse of the Second French Empire and the proclamation of the Third Republic.
- Prussian forces under Field Marshal *Helmuth von Moltke the Elder* dominated the field using coordinated railway mobilization and superior Krupp steel breech-loading artillery.
- The five-month *Siege of Paris* subjected civilians to winter starvation and bombardment, culminating in the January 1871 armistice and the radical uprising of the *Paris Commune*.
- The triumph of [[german-unification]] was consummated on French soil when King *William I* was proclaimed German Emperor in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles.
- Ended by the *Treaty of Frankfurt* in May 1871, which forced [[france]] to pay five billion francs and cede *Alsace* and *Lorraine*, igniting deep revanchism.
- The battle of *Gravelotte-St. Privat* was the bloodiest engagement of the conflict, sealing Marshal *François Achille Bazaine*'s army inside *Metz* until its surrender.
- French leader *Léon Gambetta* famously escaped besieged Paris in a hot-air balloon to rally republican armies in the provinces.

## connections

- [[germany]] — unified as a federal empire through shared military victory over French forces, securing Prussian dominance under the Hohenzollern crown.
- [[france]] — suffered swift defeat, ending the Second French Empire, establishing the Third Republic, and sparking the violent rise and fall of the *Paris Commune*.
- [[world-war-i]] — fueled decades later by French resentment over the loss of *Alsace-Lorraine* and the fragile diplomatic alignments forged by *Otto von Bismarck*.
- [[napoleon-bonaparte]] — his nephew, *Napoleon III*, sought to renew Bonapartist imperial glory but suffered catastrophic capture at Sedan and died in exile.
- [[spain]] — the succession crisis following Queen *Isabella II*'s deposition provided the catalyst when Prince *Leopold of Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen* was offered the Spanish throne.
- [[italy]] — took advantage of the withdrawal of French protection from the Papacy to enter [[rome]] and complete Italian national unification.
- [[russian-empire]] — used the breakdown of the French order to unilaterally renounce the naval restrictions in the Black Sea imposed after the Crimean War.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — transformed modern conflict through the integrated use of strategic railways, electric telegraphy, and Krupp breech-loading steel artillery.

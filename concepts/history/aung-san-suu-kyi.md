## summary

**Aung San Suu Kyi** is a Burmese politician, diplomat, and Nobel Peace Prize laureate who served as the de facto leader of Myanmar as State Counsellor from 2016 to 2021. The daughter of independence hero *General Aung San*, she rose to global prominence during the 1988 *8888 Uprising* as the leader of the *National League for Democracy* (*NLD*), enduring roughly fifteen years of house arrest imposed by the ruling military junta. Long celebrated internationally as a paragon of nonviolent resistance against authoritarian military control, her global standing was later severely tarnished by her defense of the military's violent campaign against the *Rohingya* minority before she was deposed and imprisoned again in the 2021 military coup.

## you gotta know

- Awarded the 1991 *Nobel Peace Prize* while under house arrest for her nonviolent struggle for democracy and human rights in Myanmar.
- Co-founded and led the *National League for Democracy* (*NLD*), which achieved a landslide victory in the 1990 general election that the military junta refused to honor.
- Spent approximately fifteen of the twenty-one years between 1989 and 2010 detained under house arrest at her lakeside family compound on *University Avenue* in Yangon.
- Barred from the presidency by the 2008 military-drafted constitution because her late husband and sons were foreign citizens, she governed instead through the newly created office of *State Counsellor* in 2016.
- Personally defended Myanmar against accusations of genocide regarding the *Rohingya* population at the *International Court of Justice* in The Hague in 2019, causing international outrage and the revocation of multiple human rights awards.
- Emerged as a national political figure during the *8888 Uprising* in August 1988 after returning to Burma from the *United Kingdom* to nurse her dying mother, *Khin Kyi*.
- Overthrown and detained by the military in the February 2021 coup orchestrated by Senior General *Min Aung Hlaing*, subsequently receiving lengthy prison sentences in closed-door trials.
- Her father, *General Aung San*, negotiated Burmese independence from the *[[british-empire]]* through the 1947 *Panglong Agreement* before being assassinated alongside six cabinet members months before formal sovereignty.

## connections

- [[british-burma]] — the colonial territory whose administrative boundaries and ethnic tensions shaped modern Burmese governance and her father's anti-colonial movement.
- [[state-law-and-order-restoration-council]] — the military junta that seized power during the 1988 protests, nullified the 1990 election, and kept her isolated under house arrest.
- [[burmese-people]] — the predominantly Bamar ethnic group that constituted her strongest base of popular electoral support throughout her political career.
- [[colonialism]] — the British imperial system that governed Burma as a province of British India, fostering ethnic divisions that fueled decades of post-independence civil war.
- [[cold-war]] — the global ideological conflict during which Burma maintained strict nonalignment while General *Ne Win* established an isolated military socialist dictatorship.
- [[china]] — the neighboring powerhouse that provided vital diplomatic shielding and economic investment to Myanmar under both military juntas and her civilian government.
- [[india]] — the neighboring regional democracy where her mother served as ambassador and where she studied politics before attending Oxford.
- [[buddhism]] — the spiritual and ethical tradition from which she drew concepts of selfless duty, meditation, and nonviolent resistance against state tyranny.

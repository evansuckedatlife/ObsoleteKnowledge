## summary

**Serbia** is a Balkan nation in southeastern Europe that played a pivotal role in the outbreak of World War I. After centuries of Ottoman domination, Serbia regained independence in the nineteenth century and emerged as a focal point of South Slavic nationalism. The 1914 assassination of *Archduke Franz Ferdinand* by a Serbian nationalist group triggered Austria-Hungary's ultimatum to Serbia, which precipitated the diplomatic crisis that ignited the First World War and reshaped the European map.

## you gotta know

- Serbia was under Ottoman rule for nearly five centuries before achieving autonomy and eventual independence in the nineteenth century.
- Pan-Slavic nationalism made Serbia a champion of Balkan independence and a rival to Austria-Hungary's multinational empire.
- The terrorist organization *Black Hand*, composed of Serbian nationalists and military officers, coordinated the 1914 assassination of *Archduke Franz Ferdinand* in Sarajevo.
- Austria-Hungary blamed Serbia for the assassination and issued a harsh ultimatum; Serbia's rejection triggered the alliance system that caused World War I.
- Russia supported Serbia, viewing it as a Slavic ally and a check on Austro-Hungarian expansion, deepening the great power tensions.
- Serbia's harsh response from Austria-Hungary drew in Russia, which mobilized to defend the Serbs, bringing France and Germany into the conflict.
- After World War I, Serbia expanded to become the core of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats, and Slovenes, later renamed Yugoslavia.

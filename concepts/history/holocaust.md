## summary

The **Holocaust** was the systematic, state-sponsored genocide of six million Jews perpetrated by Nazi Germany and its collaborators between 1933 and 1945, along with the murder of millions of others—Roma, disabled people, political prisoners, homosexuals, and others deemed racially or socially undesirable. It represents the most comprehensive and industrialized genocide in human history, a crime of unprecedented scale that fundamentally altered understanding of human capacity for evil. The Holocaust stands as a defining atrocity of modernity and a warning against ideology, totalitarianism, and unchecked state violence.

## you gotta know

- Began with antisemitic laws and exclusions after Hitler's rise to power in 1933, progressively stripping Jews of citizenship, property, and rights.
- Escalated during World War II with forced ghettoization of European Jews, particularly in Poland, where millions were concentrated in horrific conditions.
- Transformed into systematic extermination after the invasion of the Soviet Union in 1941 through mobile killing squads (Einsatzgruppen) and fixed death camps constructed specifically for mass murder.
- Operated death camps including Auschwitz-Birkenau, Treblinka, and Sobibor that used poison gas, starvation, disease, and brutal forced labor to murder millions.
- Depended on administrative systems, railroad transport, and extensive collaboration from local populations across occupied Europe—demonstrating how genocide required systematic infrastructure.
- Killed approximately six million Jews, along with three million Roma, two million disabled people, Soviet prisoners of war, and political and religious dissidents.
- Remained systematically concealed from world knowledge until liberation by Allied forces in 1944-1945, after which survivors provided testimony crucial to understanding the genocide.

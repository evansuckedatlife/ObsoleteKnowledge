## summary

**Jean-Baptiste Colbert** was the dominant French statesman and administrative reformer who served King *Louis XIV* as Controller-General of Finances from 1665 until his death in 1683. As the intellectual and executive architect of classical French mercantilism—a system frequently termed *Colbertism*—he subordinated domestic commerce, manufacturing, and foreign trade to the geopolitical power of the absolute crown. His systematic reorganization of French revenues, aggressive expansion of the royal navy, and generous patronage of royal cultural academies established the financial and institutional foundation of seventeenth-century French dominance.

## you gotta know

- Engineered the arrest and sensational treason trial of Superintendent of Finances *Nicolas Fouquet* in 1661, enabling the Sun King to abolish the superintendency and install Colbert as supreme financial manager.
- Formulated the economic strategy of *Colbertism*, an interventionist mercantilist system aimed at maximizing domestic bullion reserves through heavy import tariffs, export subsidies, and strict guild regulations.
- Created state-subsidized Royal Manufactures (*Manufactures royales*) to eliminate dependence on foreign luxury imports, founding the *Gobelins* tapestry manufactory and the Saint-Gobain royal mirror works.
- Built the French naval fleet (*Marine Royale*) into a formidable maritime force, constructing expansive royal arsenals and naval shipyards at Brest, Toulon, and Rochefort.
- Promulgated national legal codes to standardize French law, including the 1673 commercial *Code Marchand* and the drafting of the notorious 1685 *Code Noir*, which regulated colonial slavery.
- Established premier state intellectual bodies to burnish royal prestige, including the *Académie des sciences* in 1666 and the Royal Academy of Architecture in 1671.
- Financed titanic infrastructure and royal prestige ventures, underwriting the engineering of Pierre-Paul Riquet's *Canal du Midi* alongside the monumental expansion of the palace at *Versailles*.

## connections

- [[france]] — kingdom whose fiscal administration, naval doctrine, and state structures he reshaped under absolute monarchy.
- [[clemenceau]] — later French republican premier who inherited the centralized, interventionist administrative state pioneered by Colbert.
- [[enlightenment]] — intellectual era whose physiocratic and liberal economists mounted fierce critiques against Colbert's protective tariffs and state monopolies.
- [[colonialism]] — imperial endeavor directed by Colbert through state-chartered trading corporations in New France and the Caribbean.
- [[age-of-exploration]] — oceanic era whose trade routes Colbert sought to dominate through the French East India Company.
- [[versailles]] — palatial seat of the Sun King whose luxurious construction and royal art collections were funded through Colbert's treasury.

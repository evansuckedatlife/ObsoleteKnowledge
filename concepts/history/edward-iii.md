## summary

**Edward III**, often designated as **Edward III of England**, was the Plantagenet monarch who ruled [[england]] from 1327 to 1377, transforming a destabilized realm into the preeminent military power of medieval Europe. Assuming direct rule after toppling the regency that deposed his father, he claimed the crown of [[france]] following the confiscation of the *Duchy of Aquitaine*. This monumental dynastic dispute ignited the [[hundred-years-war]], marked by astonishing English tactical victories that reshaped European warfare. His half-century reign instituted the chivalric *Order of the Garter*, weathered the social and demographic devastation of the *Black Death*, and fostered the lasting parliamentary authority of the Commons.

## you gotta know

- Initiated the [[hundred-years-war]] in 1337 by challenging the accession of King *Philip VI* and claiming the throne of [[france]] through his mother, Queen *Isabella*, defying the Valois invocation of Salic law.
- Commanded English forces to a legendary triumph at the *Battle of Crécy* in 1346, where disciplined defensive formations deploying the [[longbow]] annihilated Genoese mercenary crossbowmen and charging French cavalry.
- Seized personal royal authority at age seventeen through a daring 1330 coup at Nottingham Castle, arresting and executing the regent *Roger Mortimer*, who had murdered his deposed father, King *Edward II*.
- Captured the crucial strategic port of *Calais* in 1347 following an eleven-month siege, establishing an invaluable continental military bridgehead and wool entrepôt that remained under English control for over two centuries.
- Secured the favorable *Treaty of Brétigny* in 1360 alongside his son, *Edward the Black Prince*, acquiring full territorial sovereignty over Aquitaine in exchange for a massive ransom for the captive King *John II*.
- Founded the celebrated *Order of the Garter* at Windsor Castle around 1348, uniting the realm's military aristocracy in a prestigious chivalric brotherhood modeled on Arthurian romance.
- Governed during the catastrophic arrival of the *Black Death* in 1348, promulgating the *Statute of Labourers* in 1351 to freeze soaring wages and enforce the obligations of [[medieval-feudalism]].
- Witnessed the dramatic expansion of parliamentary oversight during the *Good Parliament* of 1376, when the Commons instituted formal impeachments against corrupt courtiers and his influential mistress *Alice Perrers*.

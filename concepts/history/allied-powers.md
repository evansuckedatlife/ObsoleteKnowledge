## summary

The **Allied Powers**, frequently referenced simply as the **Allies**, were the decisive international military coalitions formed during the two global conflicts of the twentieth century to combat expansionist authoritarian alliances. In [[world-war-i]], the coalition evolved from the *Triple Entente* of Britain, [[france]], and the [[russian-empire]] to defeat the *Central Powers* led by imperial [[germany]]. During [[world-war-ii]], the partnership was reconstituted and led by the "Big Three"—the [[united-states]], the [[british-empire]], and the [[soviet-union]]—to dismantle the fascist *Axis Powers*. Their collective victories reshaped global borders, dismantled imperial and totalitarian regimes, established the *United Nations*, and set the geopolitical foundation for the [[cold-war]].

## you gotta know

- Orchestrated global grand strategy in [[world-war-ii]] through the "Big Three" leadership of *Franklin D. Roosevelt*, *Winston Churchill*, and *Joseph Stalin*, who coordinated military operations and political settlement terms at summit conferences in *Tehran*, *Yalta*, and *Potsdam*.
- Originated during [[world-war-i]] from the diplomatic framework of the *Triple Entente*, later expanding to encompass twenty-seven belligerent states including [[italy]], [[japan]], and the [[united-states]], which fought under the specific legal designation of an "Associated Power."
- Formalized broad political unity on New Year's Day 1942 through the *Declaration by United Nations*, in which twenty-six signatory countries pledged allegiance to the principles of the *Atlantic Charter* and agreed not to negotiate a separate peace.
- Designated [[china]] as one of the "Big Four" or "Four Policemen" under *Roosevelt's* vision for post-war security, recognizing its prolonged war of attrition against imperial forces across mainland Asia.
- Structured the institutional architecture of the post-war world order at allied conferences, creating the *United Nations Security Council*, establishing international economic systems at *Bretton Woods*, and organizing the international military tribunals at *Nuremberg*.
- Established a unified command structure on the Western Front in the spring of 1918 by appointing French Marshal *Ferdinand Foch* as supreme allied commander, whose coordinated *Hundred Days Offensive* shattered German defensive lines.
- Maintained wartime solidarity in the First World War through the 1914 *Pact of London*, while deploying secret diplomatic pacts like the *Treaty of London* and *Sykes-Picot Agreement* to partition spheres of influence in the [[middle-east]] and southern Europe.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — global conflict where the coalition mobilized industrial economies and armed forces to defeat the Axis powers.
- [[world-war-i]] — crucible conflict where the Entente powers expanded into a worldwide coalition to defeat the Central Powers.
- [[soviet-union]] — critical Eastern Front ally that bore immense demographic losses while capturing Berlin to end the European war.
- [[united-states]] — pivotal economic and military powerhouse that provided massive material aid through Lend-Lease before deploying overseas.
- [[british-empire]] — global maritime power that resisted early Axis dominance across the Commonwealth and sustained continuous multi-theater fighting.
- [[france]] — crucial founding member of both wartime coalitions, enduring trench warfare in 1914 and later mounting Free French resistance.
- [[cold-war]] — era of geopolitical tension and nuclear rivalry that erupted as the wartime coalition fractured along ideological lines.

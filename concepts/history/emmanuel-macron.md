## summary

**Emmanuel Macron** is a French politician who has served as the President of France since May 2017, having previously served as Minister of the Economy, Industry, and Digital Affairs under President *François Hollande*. Founding the centrist political movement *En Marche!*, he bypassed France's traditional center-left and center-right parties to win the presidency at age thirty-nine, becoming the youngest head of state in modern French history. His presidency has championed pro-business domestic economic reforms, deepened European integration, and active global diplomacy, while frequently encountering fierce domestic opposition over labor policy and the cost of living.

## you gotta know

- Created the centrist political movement *En Marche!* (later renamed *Renaissance*) in 2016, positioning it as an unaligned, progressive-liberal movement that broke the traditional post-war duopoly in French politics.
- Won the 2017 presidential election with over sixty-six percent of the runoff vote against far-right *National Front* leader *Marine Le Pen*, following a tumultuous campaign that saw both mainstream governing parties collapse.
- Rose rapidly through France's elite civil service and financial sector, graduating from the *École nationale d'administration* (ENA), working as an investment banker at *Rothschild & Cie*, and serving as senior economic adviser at the Élysée Palace.
- Confronted the widespread *Yellow Vests* (*Gilets Jaunes*) protest movement in late 2018, initially sparked by a proposed carbon fuel tax, which expanded into a nationwide revolt against economic inequality and presidential centralization.
- Secured re-election in 2022 by defeating *Marine Le Pen* a second time in the runoff, becoming the first French president in twenty years to win a consecutive second term under the Fifth Republic.
- Pushed through a contentious pension reform in 2023 raising the legal retirement age from 62 to 64, invoking Article 49.3 of the French Constitution to enact the measure without a formal parliamentary vote.
- Promoted the doctrine of European "strategic autonomy" in defense, technology, and energy, seeking closer strategic alignment with [[germany]] to position the European Union as an independent geopolitical actor.
- Called a surprise snap parliamentary election in June 2024 after heavy losses in the European Parliament elections, resulting in a fractured National Assembly divided among the left-wing *New Popular Front*, his centrist coalition, and the far-right *National Rally*.

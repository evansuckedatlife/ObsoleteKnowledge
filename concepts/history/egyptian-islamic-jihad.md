## summary

**Egyptian Islamic Jihad**, commonly known as **EIJ**, **Tanzim al-Jihad**, or simply **al-Jihad**, was a violent Islamist militant organization established in Egypt during the late 1970s with the ideological mission of toppling the secular Egyptian state. The group rose to international infamy by orchestrating the assassination of Egyptian President *[[anwar-sadat|Anwar Sadat]]* during a military parade in Cairo in October 1981 in retaliation for his peace treaty with Israel. Under the long-term stewardship of *Ayman al-Zawahiri*, the network integrated its operations into global jihadist circles in Afghanistan and Sudan, ultimately merging with *al-Qaeda* in 2001.

## you gotta know

- Carried out the high-profile assassination of President *[[anwar-sadat|Anwar Sadat]]* on October 6, 1981, when an assault squad led by artillery officer *Khalid al-Islambouli* leaped from an army truck and opened fire on the presidential reviewing stand during an annual military victory parade.
- Sprang from the intellectual framework of Cairo electrician *Muhammad Abd al-Salam Faraj*, whose widely circulated pamphlet *The Neglected Obligation* (*al-Farida al-Gha'iba*) argued that armed combat against apostate Muslim rulers was a mandatory personal duty.
- Drew theological sanction and spiritual guidance from the blind cleric Sheikh *Omar Abdel-Rahman*, who was tried and acquitted in the Sadat plot before later being convicted in the *[[united-states]]* for his role in the 1993 World Trade Center conspiracy.
- Brought prominence to *Ayman al-Zawahiri*, a Cairo surgeon who was imprisoned and tortured following Sadat's assassination, became the group's supreme emir, and formulated a strategy of targeting both the "near enemy" of apostate Arab regimes and the "far enemy" of Western powers.
- Executed severe terrorist attacks throughout the 1990s, including a massive vehicular suicide bombing against the Egyptian Embassy in Islamabad, Pakistan, in 1995 that killed sixteen people.
- Attempted to assassinate Egyptian President *Hosni Mubarak* in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, in June 1995, an operation carried out in close coordination with allied militant factions that triggered severe regional security crackdowns.
- Signed the 1998 declaration of the *World Islamic Front for Jihad Against Jews and Crusaders* issued by *Osama bin Laden*, which formalized a strategic alliance that culminated in the full merger into *Qaedat al-Jihad* in June 2001.

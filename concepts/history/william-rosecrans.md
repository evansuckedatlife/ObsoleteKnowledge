## summary

**William Rosecrans**, born **William Starke Rosecrans** and affectionately nicknamed **"Old Rosy"** by his soldiers, was a prominent *Union* general during the *American Civil War* who commanded the *Army of the Cumberland*. Renowned for his meticulous operational planning and logistical mastery in the Western Theater, he secured vital strategic victories at the *Battle of Stones River* and orchestrated the brilliant, nearly bloodless maneuver of the *Tullahoma Campaign*. Despite capturing the critical rail crossroads of *Chattanooga*, his career was permanently derailed by a disastrous tactical miscommunication at the *Battle of Chickamauga*, which resulted in his army being besieged and his eventual removal from high command.

## you gotta know

- Suffered a catastrophic defeat at the *Battle of Chickamauga* in September 1863 when a poorly worded dispatch inadvertently directed Brigadier General *Thomas J. Wood* to close a nonexistent gap, opening a massive breach that Confederate forces under *James Longstreet* exploited.
- Retreated in panic to *Chattanooga* following the collapse of his right flank, leaving his trapped troops besieged by *Braxton Bragg* until *Ulysses S. Grant* arrived with reinforcements to open the supply line and relieve him.
- Secured a grueling but crucial strategic victory at the *Battle of Stones River* near Murfreesboro, withstanding furious Confederate assaults on New Year's Eve 1862 and providing a vital boost to Northern morale after Fredericksburg.
- Conducted the masterclass *Tullahoma Campaign* in the summer of 1863, executing deceptive feints and rapid flanking maneuvers through atrocious mountain mud to force Bragg completely out of Middle Tennessee with minimal Union casualties.
- Triumphed early in the war at *Rich Mountain* in Western Virginia and subsequently defended northern Mississippi at *Iuka* and *Corinth*, though his abrasive personality and prickly correspondence caused bitter friction with Grant and *Edwin Stanton*.
- Carried a distinctive physical appearance with a prominent red nose and excitable demeanor that inspired his troops' affectionate nickname, while spending entire nights passionately discussing theology and philosophy as a deeply devout Catholic convert.
- Entered national politics following his military career, serving as Minister to Mexico, a United States Representative from California, and Register of the Treasury, having earlier declined an invitation to run as *Abraham Lincoln's* vice president.

## connections

- [[braxton-bragg]] — his chief Confederate adversary whom he outmaneuvered across Tennessee before meeting him in brutal combat at Stones River and Chickamauga.
- [[george-thomas]] — his stalwart subordinate whose defiant stand at Horseshoe Ridge prevented the total destruction of the Union army and earned him the name "Rock of Chickamauga."
- [[chattanooga]] — the vital rail junction and strategic mountain gateway to Georgia that his army successfully occupied before being surrounded within its defenses.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the conflict in which he proved to be one of the Western Theater's finest strategic thinkers, despite his ultimate downfall.
- [[ulysses-s-grant]] — his commanding general who grew impatient with his contentious communications, established the "Cracker Line" to lift the siege, and removed him from command.
- [[united-states]] — the republic he served as an army commander, diplomatic envoy to Mexico, and member of the House of Representatives.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-war transitional period during which he managed silver mining operations, rebuilt his personal fortunes, and served in the federal government.

## summary

The **Balfour Declaration** was a public statement issued by the British government on November 2, 1917, during *World War I*, announcing official support for the establishment of a "national home for the Jewish people" in *Palestine*. Penned by Foreign Secretary *Arthur Balfour* to Anglo-Jewish community leader *Lord Walter Rothschild*, the brief 67-word letter sought to rally international Jewish backing for the Allied cause while securing British strategic control over the region. Its contradictory commitments to Zionist aspirations and the civil and religious rights of existing non-Jewish communities profoundly reshaped the modern *Middle East*, laying the geopolitical groundwork for the *Arab-Israeli conflict*.

## you gotta know

- Issued on November 2, 1917, as an official letter from British Foreign Secretary *Arthur Balfour* addressed to *Lord Walter Rothschild*, a prominent leader of the British Jewish community and figurehead of the English Zionist Federation.
- Endorsed the creation of a "national home" for the Jewish people in *Palestine*, deliberately utilizing the vague phrase "national home" rather than explicitly promising a state in order to placate diverse diplomatic interests.
- Included an explicit safeguarding clause stipulating that "nothing shall be done which may prejudice the civil and religious rights of existing non-Jewish communities," though it pointedly avoided granting political or national rights to the Arab majority.
- Driven by intensive Zionist lobbying led by chemist *Chaim Weizmann*, who had gained crucial access to senior British ministers after developing a novel bacterial fermentation method to synthesize acetone for naval explosives.
- Directly contradicted earlier British wartime commitments, notably the *Hussein-McMahon Correspondence* that promised Arab independence, and the secret *Sykes-Picot Agreement* that carved up *Ottoman Empire* territory into French and British spheres of influence.
- Formalized into international law after the war at the 1920 *San Remo Conference* and subsequently embedded into the 1922 *League of Nations* Mandate for *Palestine* assigned to the *British Empire*.
- Fiercely opposed in the Cabinet by Secretary of State for India *Edwin Montagu*, the sole Jewish minister, who argued that creating a Jewish national home would provoke anti-Semitism and undermine the citizenship of assimilated Jews worldwide.

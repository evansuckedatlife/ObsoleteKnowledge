## summary

**Benito Mussolini** (1883–1945) was the founder of Italian fascism and the dictatorial leader of Italy from 1922 until his fall in 1943. Rising to power as a firebrand nationalist, Mussolini transformed Italy into a totalitarian fascist state, pursued aggressive imperial expansion in Africa and the Mediterranean, and led Italy as an Axis power alongside Nazi Germany during World War II. His grandiose vision of reviving a "Roman Empire" ended in military disaster, occupation, and his own execution by Italian partisans in 1945.

## you gotta know

- Born in Romagna in 1883; began as a socialist journalist before embracing violent nationalism during World War I.
- Founded the *Fasci Italiani di Combattimento* ("Italian Combat Squad") in 1919, the paramilitary organization that became the nucleus of fascism; these black-shirted squads intimidated opponents and seized power through violence and intimidation.
- Became Prime Minister in 1922 after the "March on Rome," a paramilitary demonstration of force that forced King Victor Emmanuel III to invite him to lead the government.
- Consolidated dictatorial power throughout the 1920s, eliminating parliamentary democracy, suppressing opposition parties and the press, and declaring himself "Il Duce" (The Leader).
- Invaded Abyssinia (Ethiopia) in 1935 and Albania in 1939; drew Italy closer to Nazi Germany, eventually forming the Axis alliance in 1936 (the Rome-Berlin Axis).
- Entered World War II in 1940 on Germany's side, expecting a quick victory; military failures in North Africa and the Mediterranean forced increasing dependence on German military support.
- Fell from power in July 1943 after military defeats and internal political betrayal; was rescued by German commandos and established a puppet fascist republic in northern Italy under German protection.
- Captured and executed by Italian partisans in April 1945 alongside his mistress, his body displayed publicly and mutilated in revenge.

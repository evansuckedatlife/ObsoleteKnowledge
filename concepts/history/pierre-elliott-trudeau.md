## summary

**Pierre Elliott Trudeau** was a transformative Canadian statesman who served as the 15th prime minister of Canada from 1968 to 1979 and from 1980 to 1984. A dominant and intellectual figure of the *Liberal Party of Canada*, he reshaped the nation's legal and social landscape through the patriation of the Constitution and the creation of the *Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms*. His aggressive defence of federalism, institutionalisation of bilingualism, and defiant stance against Quebec separatism defined modern Canadian politics throughout the late 20th century.

## you gotta know

- Entered federal politics under the mentorship of prime minister [[lester-b.-pearson]], serving as minister of justice where he famously decriminalised homosexual acts with the declaration that "there's no place for the state in the bedrooms of the nation."
- Swept into power during the 1968 federal election amidst an unprecedented wave of charismatic popular enthusiasm known as "Trudeaumania."
- Enacted the *Official Languages Act* in 1969, establishing English and French as equal official languages of the federal government and promoting institutional bilingualism across Canada.
- Invoked the *War Measures Act* during the *October Crisis* of 1970 following kidnappings by the militant separatist *Front de libération du Québec* (*FLQ*), famously responding to a journalist inquiring how far he would go with the phrase, "Just watch me."
- Led the federalist campaign against *René Lévesque* and the *Parti Québécois* in the 1980 Quebec independence referendum, securing a decisive victory for the "No" side.
- Achieved full constitutional sovereignty for Canada by patriating the Constitution from the [[british-empire]] via the *Constitution Act, 1982*, crucially entrenching the *Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms*.
- Introduced the controversial *National Energy Program* (*NEP*) in 1980 to increase federal control over oil and gas, provoking intense, long-lasting political alienation in western Canada, particularly Alberta.

## connections

- [[justin-trudeau]] — his eldest son, who followed in his footsteps to lead the *Liberal Party* and become prime minister in 2015.
- [[lester-b.-pearson]] — prime minister who recruited Trudeau into Parliament as one of the "Three Wise Men" and appointed him minister of justice.
- [[british-empire]] — imperial authority from which Trudeau severed Canada's final formal legal dependency through the patriation of the Constitution in 1982.
- [[cold-war]] — international ideological conflict during which Trudeau pursued an independent Canadian foreign policy and promoted nuclear de-escalation.
- [[france]] — European state with deep historical ties to Quebec, whose Gaullist interventions Trudeau firmly resisted in defence of Canadian sovereignty.
- [[united-states]] — adjacent superpower with whom Trudeau maintained complex relations, balancing continental defence with policies resisting American economic dominance.

## summary

The **Bronze Age Collapse** was a rapid, violent, and widespread systemic breakdown between roughly 1200 and 1150 BCE that shattered the palace economies and urban hubs of the Eastern Mediterranean and Near East. Spanning Greece, Anatolia, the Levant, and Mesopotamia, this crisis brought about the fall of the *Hittite Empire*, the destruction of cosmopolitan trade ports like *Ugarit*, and the obliteration of the Mycenaean citadels of *Bronze Age Greece*. Modern scholars analyze the collapse as a multifaceted systems crisis triggered by a deadly confluence of marauding *Sea Peoples*, severe multi-decade megadroughts, seismic disasters, social insurrections, and the fatal severance of international copper and tin trade networks.

## you gotta know

- Terminated multiple dominant ancient empires and urban centers almost simultaneously, obliterating the *Hittite Empire* in Anatolia, leveling the Syrian coastal kingdom of *Ugarit*, and toppling Mycenaean centers across Greece.
- Depicted vividly in Egyptian temple reliefs at Medinet Habu commissioned by *Ramses III*, which describe and illustrate land and sea battles against a predatory coalition of maritime invaders termed the *Sea Peoples*.
- Documented in tragic epistolary evidence from the clay tablets of *Ugarit*, which were baked hard when the city burned, preserving desperate diplomatic pleas from King Ammurapi noting that his own troops were absent and enemy ships were burning his towns.
- Plunged the Aegean region into several centuries of socio-economic contraction and illiteracy known as the *Greek Dark Ages*, marked by the total disappearance of the *Linear B* writing system.
- Precipitated the technological transition from the Bronze Age to the early *Iron Age*, as the breakdown of maritime trade severed access to imported tin, compelling metalsmiths to develop and refine indigenous ironworking.
- Reshaped the ethnopolitical landscape of the Levant, leading to the coastal settlement of groups like the *Peleset* (associated with the Philistines) and creating the political vacuum in which the kingdoms of Israel, Judah, and Aram emerged.
- Corroborated by modern paleoclimatology, including pollen profiles and speleothem data from Mediterranean caves, which demonstrate that prolonged, catastrophic drought caused widespread crop failure and famine across the basin.

## connections

- [[bronze-age-greece]] — the Aegean palace civilization whose fortified Mycenaean citadels were burned, abandoned, and depopulated during the crisis.
- [[middle-east]] — the expansive geopolitical region whose interconnected imperial powers and trade corridors experienced unprecedented devastation.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the classical Hellenic civilization that gradually developed centuries later out of the fragmented ruins and oral memories of the collapse.
- [[bronze-inscriptions]] — contemporaneous Bronze Age written records in China, reflecting metallurgical and political continuity during the era of Mediterranean collapse.
- [[judaism]] — the ancient Near Eastern religious tradition whose early biblical tribal confederations crystallized in the political vacuum left by the collapse.
- [[rome]] — the later Mediterranean empire whose foundational national myth in the *Aeneid* links its ancestry to heroic refugees fleeing the destruction of Bronze Age Troy.

## summary

The **Committee of Public Safety** (*Comité de salut public*) was the provisional executive governing body of [[france]] during the most radical and turbulent phase of the [[french-revolution]]. Created by the *National Convention* in April 1793 to protect the young republic against foreign invasion and domestic counter-revolution, the twelve-man committee quickly accumulated sweeping dictatorial powers. Under the leadership of radical [[jacobins]], most prominently [[robespierre]], it served as the administrative engine of the [[reign-of-terror]], centralizing wartime authority and ruthlessly eliminating political adversaries. Its dramatic collapse during the *Thermidorian Reaction* of July 1794 ended Jacobin rule and initiated the dismantling of the revolutionary emergency government.

## you gotta know

- Directed the [[reign-of-terror]] across [[france]], deploying the *Revolutionary Tribunal* and the guillotine to arrest hundreds of thousands and execute tens of thousands of suspected counter-revolutionaries, royalists, and political rivals.
- Dominated from July 1793 by [[robespierre]] alongside his close Montagnard allies *Louis Antoine de Saint-Just* and *Georges Couthon*, forming an austere ideological triumvirate that eliminated both moderate and ultra-radical political factions.
- Established originally in April 1793 under the leadership of [[danton]], who pursued a more cautious and conciliatory foreign defense policy before radical deputies ousted him from the governing body.
- Mobilized the entire French nation for total warfare through the celebrated *Levée en masse* decree, an unprecedented military conscription and industrial campaign organized by committee member *Lazare Carnot*.
- Concentrated full executive and administrative supremacy through the *Law of 14 Frimaire*, which eliminated ministerial autonomy and placed local revolutionary committees across the provinces under centralized Parisian oversight.
- Enforced strict wartime economic regulations through the *Law of the Maximum*, which set fixed price ceilings on grain and other essential staples while punishing black-market speculators with capital punishment.
- Maintained a contentious institutional rivalry with the *Committee of General Security*, a separate parliamentary body responsible for police intelligence and judicial referrals that ultimately conspired against Robespierrist dominance.
- Stripped of its dictatorial authority following the parliamentary coup of 9 Thermidor Year II (July 27, 1794), after which the *National Convention* restricted member tenures and curtailed its jurisdiction to military affairs and diplomacy.

## connections

- [[french-revolution]] — the monumental political convulsion that created the committee as an emergency wartime executive.
- [[reign-of-terror]] — the period of state-sanctioned violence and ideological purges directed by the twelve-man council.
- [[robespierre]] — the influential Jacobin deputy whose uncompromising moral vision and radical policies dominated the committee.
- [[danton]] — the early revolutionary leader who helped found the committee before falling victim to its purges.
- [[jacobins]] — the radical political club whose Montagnard faction controlled the committee throughout its dictatorial zenith.
- [[france]] — the embattled republic whose military defense, economy, and legal apparatus were placed under committee rule.
- [[enlightenment]] — the philosophical tradition whose concepts of civic virtue and the general will rationalized committee actions.
- [[napoleon-bonaparte]] — the young artillery officer who rose through the military ranks under the wartime administration of the committee.

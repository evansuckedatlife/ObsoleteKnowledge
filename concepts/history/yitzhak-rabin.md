## summary

**Yitzhak Rabin** was an Israeli military commander, diplomat, and statesman who served twice as Prime Minister of Israel (1974–1977 and 1992–1995). Widely celebrated as the *Israel Defense Forces* Chief of Staff who engineered the decisive victory in the 1967 *Six-Day War*, he later transformed into a determined peacemaker committed to resolving territorial hostilities in the [[middle-east]]. His courageous signing of the landmark [[oslo-accords]] alongside the [[palestinian-liberation-organization]] earned him the *Nobel Peace Prize*, though his pursuit of reconciliation provoked fierce domestic resistance that tragically ended with his 1995 assassination by a right-wing religious extremist.

## you gotta know

- Signed the historic 1993 [[oslo-accords]] on the South Lawn of the White House alongside Palestinian leader *Yasser Arafat* and President *Bill Clinton*, establishing interim Palestinian self-governance and earning the 1994 *Nobel Peace Prize* together with *Arafat* and Israeli foreign minister *Shimon Peres*.
- Assassinated on November 4, 1995, at a massive peace rally in *Tel Aviv*'s Kings of Israel Square by *Yigal Amir*, a radical right-wing law student fiercely opposed to territorial compromise in the [[israeli-palestinian-conflict]].
- Served as Chief of Staff of the *Israel Defense Forces* from 1964 to 1968, overseeing the planning and execution of the preemptive strikes during the 1967 *Six-Day War* that captured the West Bank, Gaza Strip, Sinai Peninsula, and Golan Heights.
- Concluded the historic 1994 peace treaty between [[israel]] and *Jordan* alongside *King Hussein* at the *Arava* border, terminating a forty-six-year state of belligerency and opening permanent diplomatic relations.
- Authorized the famous 1976 counter-terrorist commando raid known as *Operation Entebbe* in *Uganda*, successfully rescuing over one hundred Jewish and Israeli airline passengers held hostage by pro-Palestinian hijackers.
- Became the first native-born Israeli prime minister (a *sabra*), having been born in *Jerusalem* in 1922 during the era of the British Mandate of Palestine.
- Began his military career as an elite commander in the underground *Palmach* strike force, leading the *Harel Brigade* during the 1948 Arab-Israeli War to defend the critical supply corridor into besieged *Jerusalem*.
- Resigned from his first premiership in 1977 following the "Dollar Account Affair," an administrative scandal involving an illegal foreign currency bank account maintained in the [[united-states]] by his wife, *Leah Rabin*.

## connections

- [[oslo-accords]] — the landmark interim peace framework he signed with *Yasser Arafat* to establish Palestinian self-rule in the West Bank and Gaza Strip.
- [[palestinian-liberation-organization]] — the Palestinian nationalist body with which he exchanged letters of mutual recognition and negotiated the framework for self-administration.
- [[arab-israeli-conflict]] — the broader geopolitical struggle across the Levant in which he served as a battlefield general and later sought to resolve through territorial compromise.
- [[middle-east]] — the strategic region whose modern territorial borders and diplomatic alliances were profoundly reshaped by his military victories and peace treaties.
- [[assassination-of-anwar-sadat]] — a parallel political assassination of an Arab statesman who was murdered by religious extremists for negotiating peace agreements with [[israel]].
- [[united-states]] — the crucial superpower ally where he served as Israel's ambassador from 1968 to 1973 and whose diplomatic mediation fostered his historic accords.
- [[judaism]] — the religious tradition whose extremist fringes invoked radical interpretations of ancient legal concepts to justify his murder.

## summary

**Chandragupta Maurya** (c. 340–293 BCE) was the founder of the Mauryan Empire, the first pan-Indian empire to unify most of the Indian subcontinent under a single rule. Rising from humble origins to overthrow the fragmented *Nanda dynasty*, Chandragupta established a powerful centralized state backed by a vast army and sophisticated bureaucracy. His empire laid the foundation for Indian civilization, and his grandson *Ashoka* became perhaps the greatest ruler in Indian history.

## you gotta know

- Chandragupta was born into obscurity, possibly a member of a lower caste or merchant family; he rose through military service and political cunning.
- He was tutored by *Kautilya* (also known as *Chanakya*), a legendary political strategist and author of the *Arthashastra*, a treatise on statecraft and warfare that shaped Chandragupta's governance.
- Chandragupta defeated the *Nanda dynasty*, the rulers of the Ganges valley, establishing control of northern India around 321 BCE; this became the foundation of the Mauryan Empire.
- He built the largest empire India had seen to that point, controlling most of the Indian subcontinent except the southern Deccan; the empire stretched from the Arabian Sea to the Bay of Bengal.
- Organized the empire with a centralized bureaucracy, a vast standing army reportedly numbering 600,000, and an network of spies; the state controlled mines, agriculture, and trade to generate wealth.
- Made peace with the Greek *Seleucid Empire* (Alexander the Great's successor state in Asia), stabilizing India's northwestern frontier; this contact brought Greek culture into the Indian world.
- Abdicated in favor of his son *Bindusara* around 297 BCE and retired to a Jain monastery, reflecting the pluralistic spirituality of ancient Indian philosophy.

## connections

- [[mauryan-empire]] — the empire Chandragupta founded and ruled; it was the first pan-Indian state.
- [[ashoka]] — his grandson, who expanded and subsequently embraced Buddhism, making the Mauryan Empire a center of Buddhist civilization.
- [[kautilya]] — the legendary advisor and strategist who tutored Chandragupta and authored the *Arthashastra*.
- [[ancient-india]] — the civilization Chandragupta unified and centralized under imperial rule.
- [[india]] — the nation that traces its sense of unified subcontinent identity to Chandragupta's empire.
- [[buddhism]] — the religion that flourished under Chandragupta's successors, particularly Ashoka.
- [[seleucid-empire]] — the Greek successor state with which Chandragupta made peace, creating cultural exchange.

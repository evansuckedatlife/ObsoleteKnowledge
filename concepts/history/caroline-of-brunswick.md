## summary

**Caroline of Brunswick** was the spirited German princess who became Queen of the United Kingdom as the estranged consort of King *George IV*. Married reluctantly in 1795 to resolve the extravagant debts of the Prince of Wales, she lived in open separation and spent years traveling abroad in the Mediterranean. Her dramatic return to London in 1820 precipitated a major constitutional crisis and sensational parliamentary trial that galvanized public sympathy and anti-monarchical agitation across *England*.

## you gotta know

- Entered into an arranged dynastic union in 1795 with the future *George IV*, an event marked by immediate mutual revulsion, heavy drinking by the groom, and rapid separation after the birth of their only child.
- Mother to *Princess Charlotte of Wales*, whose immense public popularity and tragic death in childbirth in 1817 left the British monarchy facing an acute succession crisis.
- Subject of the secret 1806 *Delicate Investigation*, initiated under *George III* to scrutinize allegations that she had given birth to an illegitimate child named *William Austin*.
- Lived abroad in Italy for years with her companion and Italian courier *Bartolomeo Bergami*, prompting the British government to send the secretive *Milan Commission* to gather salacious evidence against her.
- Faced the notorious *Pains and Penalties Bill of 1820*, introduced by Prime Minister *Lord Liverpool* to strip her of royal status and dissolve the marriage on statutory grounds of adultery.
- Defended with oratorical brilliance in the House of Lords by Whig advocates *Henry Brougham* and *Thomas Denman*, forcing the government to withdraw the measure amid massive popular unrest.
- Transformed into an unlikely champion of radical reformers, working-class protesters, and Whig opponents of the Tory ministry who decried royal hypocrisy and despotism.
- Physically barred from entering Westminster Abbey by armed sentries on the orders of her husband during his opulent coronation in July 1821, dying mere weeks later.

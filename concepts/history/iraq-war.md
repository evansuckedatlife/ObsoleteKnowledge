# Iraq War

## summary

The **Iraq War** (2003–2011) was an invasion and occupation of *Iraq* by a *United States*-led military coalition, justified by claims that *Saddam Hussein* possessed weapons of mass destruction and posed a threat to global security. The war killed hundreds of thousands, destabilized the region, cost trillions in American resources, and became a defining tragedy of the early 21st century, shaping American foreign policy and domestic politics for decades.

## you gotta know

- *President George W. Bush* authorized the invasion following the September 11 attacks, linking Hussein to terrorism (a connection later proven baseless) and claiming Iraq possessed *weapons of mass destruction* (WMDs) that were never found.
- The initial invasion (March 2003) was swift and militarily successful; American forces defeated Hussein's army in weeks, but the occupation and insurgency that followed would consume far more resources and lives.
- The war's rationale was disputed even before the invasion: major allies (*France*, *Germany*, *Canada*) refused to participate; no WMDs were ever discovered; and investigations later concluded the intelligence was distorted or fabricated to justify the war.
- The occupation spawned a vicious insurgency fueled by sectarian tensions (*Sunni vs. Shia*), American counterinsurgency tactics (Abu Ghraib prison abuse, civilian casualties), and the power vacuum created by disbanding Hussein's military.
- The *Islamic State of Iraq and Syria* (ISIS) emerged from the remnants of Hussein's Ba'athist military and the chaos of the occupation, eventually seizing large parts of Iraq and neighboring *Syria* in 2014.
- American combat troops withdrew in 2011 under President *Barack Obama*, but the U.S. maintained military advisors and bases; the war fundamentally altered American credibility internationally and divided the American electorate.
- Estimates of Iraqi deaths range from 150,000 to 600,000 (directly from violence or war-related causes); millions became refugees; American casualties exceeded 4,400 killed and 30,000 wounded.

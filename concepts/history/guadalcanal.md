## summary

The **Battle of Guadalcanal**, also known as the **Guadalcanal Campaign** and codenamed **Operation Watchtower**, was the first major Allied land offensive against the Empire of [[japan]] during [[world-war-ii]]. Fought between August 1942 and February 1943 on and around the island of **Guadalcanal** in the Solomon Islands, the grueling multi-domain campaign permanently halted Japanese southward expansion toward Australia. By seizing and defending a vital airbase, Allied forces forced the Japanese military into a war of attrition that decisively transferred the strategic initiative to the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Commenced on August 7, 1942, when the 1st Marine Division under Major General *Alexander Vandegrift* executed amphibious landings and captured an unfinished Japanese airfield, naming it *Henderson Field* in honor of an aviator killed at the [[battle-of-midway]].
- Allied pilots who flew from Henderson Field formed the legendary *Cactus Air Force*, piloting *F4F Wildcats* and *SBD Dauntless* dive bombers to deny Japanese surface fleets daytime access to the surrounding seas.
- Japanese destroyers conducted high-speed nocturnal resupply and reinforcement missions down *The Slot* (New Georgia Sound), a tactic dubbed the *Tokyo Express* by the Allies and *Rat Transportation* by the Japanese navy.
- Ground engagements featured savage close-quarters fighting across the airfield perimeter, notably the *Battle of the Tenaru*, which shattered Colonel *Kiyonao Ichiki*'s regiment, and the desperate defense at the *Battle of Edson's Ridge* (*Bloody Ridge*).
- Marine Sergeant *John Basilone* was awarded the Medal of Honor for his heroic defense of the airfield perimeter on Bloody Ridge, holding off an entire Japanese regiment with machine guns.
- The waters north of the island were christened *Ironbottom Sound* because nearly fifty warships and hundreds of aircraft were sunk there across savage sea clashes including the *Battle of Savo Island*, the *Battle of Cape Esperance*, and the *Naval Battle of Guadalcanal*.
- Sinking of the light cruiser USS *Juneau* during the naval action cost the lives of nearly seven hundred sailors, including all five *Sullivan brothers* from Iowa, leading to changes in military assignment policies.
- Concluded in February 1943 after the secret Japanese evacuation codenamed *Operation Ke*, leaving U.S. Army and Marine forces under Major General *Alexander Patch* in complete control.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that framed the Allied island-hopping counter-offensive in the Pacific.
- [[japan]] — the imperial axis power whose elite carrier aviators and ground forces suffered irreplaceable losses in the campaign.
- [[united-states]] — the primary Allied nation that deployed naval, marine, and army units to secure the southern Pacific line of communication.
- [[battle-of-midway]] — the June 1942 naval victory that broke Japanese offensive carrier strength and made the invasion feasible.
- [[pacific-war]] — the broader maritime and amphibious struggle waged across Asia and the Pacific Ocean.
- [[operation-watchtower]] — the initial Allied operational plan to seize Japanese-held positions in the southern Solomon Islands.
- [[ironbottom-sound]] — the graveyard of sunken naval vessels between Guadalcanal, Savo Island, and Florida Island.

## summary

**Anarchism** is a political philosophy and movement advocating for the abolition of all involuntary, hierarchical authority—most centrally the state and coercive capitalism—in favor of self-governed voluntary cooperation. Emerging in its modern form during the nineteenth-century [[industrial-revolution]], it evolved from radical currents of the [[enlightenment]] and the [[french-revolution]] into a major force in international labor politics. It remains essential in quiz bowl for its historic association with fin-de-siècle political assassinations, revolutionary syndicalism, and its enduring imprint on speculative literature.

## you gotta know

- In 1901, *Leon Czolgosz*, an anarchist sympathizer inspired by *Emma Goldman*, assassinated President *William McKinley* in Buffalo, elevating *Theodore Roosevelt* to the presidency and triggering federal anti-radical legislation in the [[united-states]].
- French theorist *Pierre-Joseph Proudhon* was the first to adopt the label systematically, declaring that "property is theft!" in his 1840 treatise *What Is Property?* and establishing the philosophy of mutualism in [[france]].
- Russian activist *Mikhail Bakunin* led the collectivist faction in the First International, clashing with *Karl Marx* over Bakunin's warning that a "dictatorship of the proletariat" would create a despotic ruling bureaucracy across the [[russian-empire]].
- Prince *Peter Kropotkin* formulated anarcho-communism in *The Conquest of Bread* and published *Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution*, arguing that voluntary cooperation rather than cutthroat competition drives biological and social progress.
- Militants in the late nineteenth century championed "propaganda of the deed," carrying out political assassinations that killed French President *Sadi Carnot*, Empress *Elisabeth* of Austria, and King *Umberto I* of [[italy]].
- During the Spanish Civil War, the anarcho-syndicalist *CNT* and *FAI* mobilized over a million workers in [[spain]], enacting sweeping collectivization across Catalonia before being suppressed by Nationalist troops and Soviet-aligned communists.
- In speculative fiction, author *Ursula K. Le Guin* portrayed an ambiguous anarchist society on the moon Anarres in *The Dispossessed*, exploring both the promise and bureaucratic stagnation of non-authoritarian Odonianism.
- During the Russian Civil War, the guerrilla leader *Nestor Makhno* led an autonomous insurgent army in Ukraine, while the 1921 Kronstadt rebellion demanded free worker councils before both were crushed by the [[soviet-union]].

## connections

- [[industrial-revolution]] — created the factory conditions, wage labor disputes, and urban dislocation that stimulated mass syndicalist organizing.
- [[united-states]] — site of the Haymarket affair, the McKinley assassination, and the mass deportations of the First Red Scare.
- [[russian-empire]] — birthplace of prominent theorists *Bakunin* and *Kropotkin* and home to radical populist and nihilist undergrounds.
- [[spain]] — witnessed the largest historical experiment in libertarian socialism under the anarcho-syndicalist *CNT* during the 1930s.
- [[soviet-union]] — Bolshevik state that suppressed autonomous soviets, crushed *Makhno*'s insurgent forces, and repressed the Kronstadt sailors.
- [[existentialism]] — philosophical school whose emphasis on radical individual autonomy and rejection of external authority strongly parallels anarchist ethics.
- [[modernism]] — early twentieth-century artistic movement whose bohemian avant-garde frequently aligned with anti-statist critiques and radical cultural politics.

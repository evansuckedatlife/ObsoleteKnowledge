# Italy Campaign

## summary

The **Italian Campaign** (1943–1945) was the Allied invasion and liberation of Italy from Axis control, extending from Sicily through the Italian peninsula to the borders of Germany. The campaign was one of World War II's longest and costliest theaters, involving millions of troops, fighting through difficult terrain against entrenched German defenses, and demonstrating the challenges of amphibious invasion and mountain warfare while simultaneously destabilizing Italy's fascist regime.

## you gotta know

- Operation *Husky* (July 1943) saw American and British forces invade Sicily; rapid conquest led to the fall of *Benito Mussolini*'s fascist regime and a complex Italian surrender, complicated by German occupation of much of the peninsula.
- The *invasion of mainland Italy* began in September 1943 across the Strait of Messina; German forces under *Field Marshal Albert Kesselring* resisted fiercely, establishing defensive lines (*Gustav Line*, *Winter Line*) across the peninsula that halted Allied advance.
- The *Battle of Monte Cassino* (January–May 1944) became a symbol of the campaign's brutality: Allied forces assaulted a fortified height for months at enormous cost (over 100,000 casualties) before finally capturing it; Polish troops ultimately took the ruins.
- The *Salerno landings* (September 1943) nearly ended in disaster when German counterattacks almost drove the beachhead back into the sea; American reinforcements and naval gunfire support stabilized the landing and enabled the breakout.
- Fighting extended up the peninsula for nearly two years; by mid-1944, the focus shifted northward toward the *Gothic Line* and eventually the *Po Valley*; German forces fought a delaying campaign, inflicting maximum casualties to buy time for defense of Germany proper.
- The campaign tied down German forces that might otherwise have reinforced other theaters; Italy's surrender and shift to co-belligerent status complicated the political settlement of Southern Europe and left unresolved questions about Yugoslavia and Greece.

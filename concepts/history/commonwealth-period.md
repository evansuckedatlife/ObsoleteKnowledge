## summary

The **Commonwealth period** was the republican era from 1649 to 1660 when England, and later Scotland and Ireland, was governed without a monarch following the execution of King *Charles I*. Initially ruled by the unicameral *Rump Parliament* and a Council of State, the regime evolved into the military dictatorship of the *Protectorate* under *Oliver Cromwell* as Lord Protector. Marked by strict Puritan religious governance, sweeping naval and colonial expansion, and the brutal reconquest of Ireland, this transformative phase of English history ended with the collapse of republican authority and the Stuart Restoration in 1660.

## you gotta know

- Began in January 1649 immediately following the trial and public beheading of King *Charles I* outside the Banqueting House in Whitehall, an unprecedented act of regicide that abolished the monarchy and the House of Lords.
- First governed by the *Rump Parliament*, the purged remnant of the Long Parliament created by *Pride's Purge* under the authority of the New Model Army.
- Characterized by intense internal political radicalism, including challenges from democratic groups such as the *Levellers* and agrarian communitarians known as the *Diggers*.
- Led by *Oliver Cromwell*, the parliamentary forces launched devastating military campaigns in Ireland (1649–1650) and Scotland (1650–1651), resulting in severe confiscations of Catholic-owned land and mass casualties.
- Transformed in 1653 when Cromwell forcibly dismissed the Rump Parliament and instituted the *Instrument of Government*, England's first written constitution, making him *Lord Protector*.
- Imposed rigorous Puritan moral reforms throughout the country, including the suppression of traditional theater, festive holidays like Christmas, horse racing, and gambling.
- Collapsed rapidly following the death of Oliver Cromwell in 1658; his son *Richard Cromwell* proved unable to govern the military, prompting General *George Monck* to facilitate the 1660 return of King *Charles II*.

## connections

- [[england]] — the kingdom fundamentally transformed from an absolute monarchy into an experimental republic and protectorate.
- [[british-empire]] — the overseas imperial network expanded through aggressive naval acts, such as the *Navigation Acts*, and the conquest of Jamaica during the Western Design.
- [[christianity]] — the dominant religion whose radical Protestant and Puritan factions drove parliamentary policy and moral legislation.
- [[enlightenment]] — the philosophical tradition that later drew upon the republican political theories, contractual models, and debates on religious toleration born during the era.
- [[colonialism]] — imperial expansionist policies pursued during the Anglo-Dutch Wars and colonial land plantations in Ireland and the Caribbean.

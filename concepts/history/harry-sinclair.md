## summary

**Harry Sinclair** was an American oil industrialist and founder of the Sinclair Oil Corporation who served as a central figure in the notorious Teapot Dome scandal of the 1920s. Following [[world-war-i]], Sinclair secured fraudulent, non-competitive commercial drilling leases to federal naval petroleum reserves in Wyoming by bribing Secretary of the Interior *Albert B. Fall* with hundreds of thousands of dollars in Liberty Bonds and cash. His subsequent legal battles for conspiracy, contempt of the Senate, and jury tampering came to symbolize executive branch graft and corporate venality in the post-war [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Founded the Sinclair Oil Corporation in 1916 by consolidating various independent Midwestern petroleum holdings, establishing an iconic brand featuring the green *Dino* apatosaurus logo.
- Received exclusive, secret drilling rights to Naval Petroleum Reserve No. 3 at Teapot Dome, Wyoming, in April 1922 through his Mammoth Oil Company without competitive bidding.
- Transferred approximately three hundred thousand dollars in government Liberty Bonds and cash loans to Secretary of the Interior *Albert B. Fall*, who had persuaded President *Warren G. Harding* to transfer naval oil reserves from the Department of the Navy to the Department of the Interior.
- Exposed by a relentless congressional inquiry led by Montana Senator *Thomas J. Walsh*, uncovering the paper trail of bonds that had moved through the dummy Continental Trading Company.
- Refused to answer questions posed by the Senate committee regarding his corporate financial arrangements, leading directly to his indictment and conviction for contempt of the United States Senate.
- Hired the *William J. Burns* detective agency to shadow jurors during his 1927 criminal conspiracy trial, resulting in a mistrial and a six-month jail sentence for criminal contempt of court for jury tampering.
- Served six months of imprisonment in the District of Columbia jail in 1929 while working as the prison pharmacist, even as he was acquitted of the primary charge of bribing Fall.
- Built an extensive thoroughbred racing empire through Rancocas Stable, campaigning champion racehorses such as Kentucky Derby and Belmont Stakes winner *Zev*.

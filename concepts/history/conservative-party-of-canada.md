## summary

The **Conservative Party of Canada** is a federal political party in Canada situated on the centre-right of the national political spectrum. Formed in December 2003 through the unification of the *Canadian Alliance* and the *Progressive Conservative Party of Canada*, the party serves as the historic and contemporary rival to the federal *Liberal Party of Canada*. Governed federally under Prime Minister *Stephen Harper* from 2006 to 2015, the party combines fiscal conservatism, constitutional monarchism, and western Canadian populism within the traditional Westminster parliamentary framework.

## you gotta know

- Created in late 2003 when *Stephen Harper*, leader of the western-based *Canadian Alliance*, and *Peter MacKay*, leader of the *Progressive Conservatives*, signed an agreement to merge and resolve a decade of vote splitting on the Canadian right.
- Traces its lineage back to the founding coalition of Canadian Confederation in 1867, established by *Sir John A. Macdonald* and *Sir George-Étienne Cartier* as the original *Liberal-Conservative Party*.
- Absorbed deep regional roots in western populism that originated with the [[alberta-social-credit-party]], whose political heir *Preston Manning* founded the *Reform Party of Canada* in 1987 to challenge central Canadian dominance.
- Formed federal government under *Stephen Harper* across three terms, winning minority mandates in 2006 and 2008 before capturing a majority government in 2011, prioritizing tax cuts, criminal justice reform, and natural resource development.
- Triggered a major constitutional confrontation in December 2008 when Harper requested that the [[governor-general-of-canada]], *Michaëlle Jean*, prorogue Parliament to avoid a confidence vote that would have installed a Liberal-NDP coalition.
- Navigated historic twentieth-century conflicts under wartime leaders, notably *Sir Robert Borden*, who formed the Unionist government during [[world-war-i]] and introduced federal conscription.
- Led during the [[cold-war]] era by *John Diefenbaker*, who introduced the *Canadian Bill of Rights*, and later by *Brian Mulroney*, who negotiated the *Canada-United States Free Trade Agreement* and the *Acid Rain Treaty*.
- Elected *Pierre Poilievre* as leader in 2022, who reorganized the party's platform around working-class economic populism, anti-inflation policy, housing deregulation, and the abolition of the federal consumer carbon tax.

## connections

- [[alberta-social-credit-party]] — inherited the western populist voter base and political culture nurtured in Alberta by *Ernest Manning*, whose son *Preston Manning* launched the Reform movement that merged into the modern party.
- [[governor-general-of-canada]] — engaged in decisive constitutional moments with the viceroy, notably the 1926 King-Byng affair involving *Arthur Meighen* and the 2008 prorogation controversy under *Stephen Harper*.
- [[british-empire]] — historically championed imperial defense, British parliamentary traditions, and imperial trade preferences during the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
- [[world-war-i]] — led Canada through the conflict under Prime Minister *Sir Robert Borden*, who enacted the *Military Service Act* and secured independent Canadian signature at the Treaty of Versailles.
- [[world-war-ii]] — formed the official parliamentary opposition to *William Lyon Mackenzie King*, advocating for national mobilization and overseas conscription.
- [[cold-war]] — shaped Canada's defense and economic strategy under Progressive Conservative prime ministers, balancing North American defense integration with independent foreign policy initiatives.

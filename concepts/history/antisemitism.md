## summary

**Antisemitism** is systematic prejudice and hostility toward *Jews* and *Judaism*, rooted in religious persecution, conspiracy theories, and racialized ideology. From medieval Christian Europe to modern authoritarian regimes, it has motivated expulsions, pogroms, and culminated in the *Holocaust*, making it one of history's most destructive and enduring hatreds.

## you gotta know

- Medieval Christian Europe: *Jews* blamed for the death of *Jesus* via the "Christ-killer" trope; confined to ghettos and subjected to restrictions on occupations, land ownership, and citizenship rights.
- Theological basis: Christian doctrine held *Jews* collectively responsible for deicide; this "teaching of contempt" normalized centuries of legal discrimination and periodic violence.
- Expulsions and pogroms: Spain (1492), England (1290), France (multiple expulsions), and Eastern Europe expelled Jewish populations; periodic mob violence ("pogroms") in Russia and Poland killed thousands.
- 19th-century racial antisemitism: *Jews* reimagined as a racial rather than religious "other," fueling nationalist ideologies and pseudo-scientific (phrenological) theories of racial hierarchy.
- Emancipation backlash: As *Jews* gained legal rights in 19th-century Europe (French emancipation 1791, German emancipation 1871), antisemitism intensified among those fearing Jewish economic and political ascendancy.
- *Dreyfus Affair* (France, 1894–1906): antisemitic scapegoating of an innocent Jewish officer falsely accused of espionage; galvanized both *Zionism* as a response and international awareness of systemic institutional antisemitism.
- Nazi ideology: *Adolf Hitler* and the *Nazi Party* weaponized antisemitism as central state policy, combining racial pseudoscience with medieval blood-libel myths to justify genocide.
- *Holocaust*: Nazi systematic murder of ~6 million *Jews* (two-thirds of Europe's Jewish population) using industrial killing apparatus; defines 20th-century antisemitism's consequences.
- Persistent modern forms: conspiracy theories (Jewish control of banks/media), "New Antisemitism" conflating anti-*Zionism* with anti-Judaism, hate crimes in Western democracies, and resurgent far-right movements.

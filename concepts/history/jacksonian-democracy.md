## summary

**Jacksonian democracy** was an influential nineteenth-century political philosophy and mass movement in the [[united-states]] led by President *Andrew Jackson* that championed universal white male suffrage and celebrated the virtues of the common citizen over entrenched aristocratic elites. Emerging in the 1820s following the disputed [[election-of-1824]], the movement built the modern Democratic Party through innovative grassroots organizing, strict party loyalty, and nationwide campaign rallies. It fundamentally transformed the structure of American governance by strengthening executive authority and championing agrarian populism, while simultaneously executing brutal policies of indigenous displacement and fiercely defending the institution of slavery.

## you gotta know

- Expanded democratic participation by eliminating property requirements for voting, facilitating universal white male suffrage and sparking unprecedented voter engagement.
- Institutionalized the [[spoils-system]], defending partisan political patronage and rotation in office as vital tools to prevent the emergence of an entrenched, corrupt civil service.
- Waged the bitter *Bank War* against *Nicholas Biddle*, culminating in the 1832 veto of the charter for the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states]] and the transfer of federal deposits into state-level "pet banks."
- Rejected radical nullification doctrines during the [[nullification-crisis]] of 1832–1833, when *Jackson* prepared military force against South Carolina to preserve the primacy of the federal union.
- Enforced aggressive westward expansion by championing the [[indian-removal-act]] of 1830 and ignoring judicial resistance from the Supreme Court, directly causing the deadly [[trail-of-tears]].
- Fostered the creation of the *Whig Party* within the *Second Party System*, as disparate political opponents coalesced to resist executive overreach and what critics labeled executive tyranny under "King Andrew I."
- Precipitated severe financial instability through the 1836 *Specie Circular*, an executive directive requiring gold or silver coin for public land purchases that accelerated the devastating *Panic of 1837*.

## connections

- [[election-of-1824]] — the contested four-way race that galvanized *Jackson's* supporters to organize an unprecedented nationwide grassroots political coalition.
- [[corrupt-bargain]] — the alleged backroom deal between *John Quincy Adams* and *Henry Clay* that provided the movement's enduring anti-corruption rallying cry.
- [[spoils-system]] — the patronage mechanism championed by Jacksonians to rotate political allies into federal bureaucratic offices.
- [[second-bank-of-the-united-states]] — the central financial institution dismantled by *Jackson* during his crusade against concentrated monied monopolies.
- [[nullification-crisis]] — the major constitutional confrontation over tariffs where *Jackson* forcefully maintained federal authority over extreme states' rights theorists.
- [[indian-removal-act]] — the controversial 1830 legislation that authorized the expulsion of southeastern Native American tribes to lands west of the Mississippi River.
- [[trail-of-tears]] — the catastrophic forced march of the Cherokee and other nations that represented the darkest humanitarian toll of Jacksonian agrarian expansion.
- [[united-states-constitution]] — the constitutional order reinterpreted by Jacksonians through expansive assertions of presidential veto power and democratic mandates.

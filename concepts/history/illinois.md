## summary

**Illinois** is a Midwestern state of the *United States*, admitted to the Union in 1818, that served as an essential agricultural, commercial, and industrial crucible in modern American history. Centered on the boom metropolis of Chicago along Lake Michigan, the state evolved into the nation's premier transportation crossroads through canals, railroads, and grain terminals during the *Industrial Revolution*. Renowned as the "Land of Lincoln," it has produced transformative political leadership, labor movements, and civil rights organizing while serving as a microcosm of national electoral dynamics.

## you gotta know

- Admitted on December 3, 1818, as the 21st state, initially governing from Kaskaskia and Vandalia before establishing its permanent capital at Springfield in 1839.
- Hosted the famous 1858 Lincoln-Douglas debates, seven historic senatorial debates across the state that brought *Abraham Lincoln* national prominence over the issue of slavery expansion.
- Served as a critical Union stronghold during the *American Civil War*, supplying essential grain, logistical support, and leading generals including *Ulysses S. Grant*.
- Witnessed the 1871 Great Chicago Fire, a conflagration that leveled central Chicago and spurred a legendary era of urban planning and skyscraper architectural development.
- Stood at the epicenter of major nineteenth-century American labor struggles, including the 1886 Haymarket affair and the 1894 nationwide Pullman rail strike.
- Became the northern destination for hundreds of thousands of African Americans during the Great Migration, creating vibrant political and cultural centers that nurtured the Chicago Black Renaissance.
- Hosted pivotal civil rights activism, including the work of anti-lynching journalist *Ida B. Wells* and the 1966 Chicago Freedom Movement led by *Martin Luther King Jr.*
- Has sent prominent national figures to the *United States Senate*, including *Carol Moseley Braun*, the first African American woman elected to the Senate, and *Barack Obama*, elected the 44th president in 2008.

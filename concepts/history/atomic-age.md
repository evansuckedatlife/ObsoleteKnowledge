## summary

The **Atomic Age**, also known as the **Nuclear Age**, is the historical era and scientific epoch inaugurated by the discovery of nuclear fission and the development of the first atomic weapons. Initiated under the secret Allied *Manhattan Project* during [[world-war-ii]], the era opened publicly with the *Trinity* detonation and the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki in [[japan]]. It permanently reshaped modern global statecraft by initiating a decades-long nuclear arms race between the [[united-states]] and the [[soviet-union]]. Beyond military competition, the period fostered revolutionary advances in fundamental physics, geopolitical doctrines of mutual deterrence, and ambitious efforts to harness civilian nuclear power.

## you gotta know

- Began militarily on July 16, 1945, when the *Manhattan Project* conducted the *Trinity* test at Alamogordo, New Mexico, detonating a plutonium implosion device nicknamed *The Gadget* prior to the atomic strikes on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in [[japan]].
- The American nuclear monopoly dissolved in August 1949 when the [[soviet-union]] tested *RDS-1* (codenamed *Joe-1*) at Semipalatinsk, built rapidly using technical blueprints transmitted by espionage networks involving physicist *Klaus Fuchs* and courier *Harry Gold*.
- Grounded in [[quantum-mechanics]] and the December 1938 discovery of uranium fission by *Otto Hahn*, *Fritz Strassmann*, and *Lise Meitner*, which prompted *Albert Einstein* and *Leo Szilard* to warn President *Franklin D. Roosevelt* of possible weapons research in [[germany]].
- Escalated into thermonuclear weapon development during the [[cold-war]] through the *Teller-Ulam* configuration, unleashing explosive fusion energy in the American *Ivy Mike* test and the 50-megaton Soviet *Tsar Bomba* designed under *Andrei Sakharov*.
- Governed strategic doctrine through Mutually Assured Destruction (*MAD*), where vast arsenals of intercontinental ballistic missiles and bombers deterred direct superpower conflict while driving proxy wars, the Cuban Missile Crisis, and civil defense drills like *Duck and Cover*.
- Promoted civilian atomic electricity alongside weaponry, spurred by *Dwight D. Eisenhower*'s 1953 *Atoms for Peace* initiative, which funded commercial reactors like *Shippingport* and led to the creation of the International Atomic Energy Agency (*IAEA*).
- Unveiled by counterintelligence via the *Venona project*, an Allied decryption program that exposed Soviet infiltration of Los Alamos, precipitating the arrest of *Alan Nunn May*, the confession of *Klaus Fuchs*, and the 1953 execution of *Julius and Ethel Rosenberg*.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict that prompted the *Manhattan Project* and culminated in the atomic strikes on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
- [[cold-war]] — the ideological confrontation defined by the nuclear arms race, espionage, and the existential threat of mutual destruction.
- [[soviet-union]] — the rival superpower that broke the American atomic monopoly in 1949 using espionage networks to duplicate Western bomb designs.
- [[united-states]] — the superpower that pioneered wartime atomic weapons research at Los Alamos and directed the first operational nuclear strikes.
- [[quantum-mechanics]] — the theoretical physics framework explaining subatomic forces and enabling scientists to engineer nuclear fission and fusion reactions.
- [[japan]] — the nation subjected to the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, ending the Pacific War.
- [[germany]] — the nation whose 1938 discovery of nuclear fission provoked Allied fears of a rival atomic weapon program.

## summary

**Sitting Bull** (Lakota: **Tatanka Iyotake**) was a revered Hunkpapa Lakota holy man, supreme political leader, and principal organizer of indigenous resistance across the northern Great Plains during the American Indian Wars. He forged an unprecedented alliance of Lakota, Cheyenne, and Arapaho nations to defend the sacred Black Hills against encroachment by the [[united-states]], culminating in the defeat of federal troops at the 1876 Battle of the [[little-bighorn]]. A resolute defender of tribal sovereignty and traditional nomadic life, he resisted confinement on reservations by leading his followers into Canadian exile, toured with *Buffalo Bill's Wild West*, and was ultimately assassinated during a federal arrest triggered by panic surrounding the [[ghost-dance]] movement.

## you gotta know

- Orchestrated the massive indigenous coalition at the 1876 Battle of the [[little-bighorn]], where allied Lakota, Northern Cheyenne, and Arapaho warriors wiped out Lieutenant Colonel *George Armstrong Custer* and five companies of the [[seventh-cavalry]].
- Experienced a prophetic vision during a sacred Sun Dance ritual shortly before Little Bighorn, slashing his arms in sacrifice and foreseeing federal soldiers tumbling upside-down like grasshoppers directly into camp.
- Led thousands of Lakota followers north into *Canada* (the "Grandmother's Land"), maintaining tribal independence for four years until starvation and the collapse of the bison herds compelled his negotiated surrender in 1881.
- Toured North America and Europe with *Buffalo Bill Cody's Wild West* exhibition in 1885, earning fifty dollars a week signing autographs, meeting public figures, and using his celebrity to advocate for indigenous welfare.
- Assassinated on December 15, 1890, on the Standing Rock Indian Reservation when Indian Agency police, sent over fears he would sanction the messianic [[ghost-dance]], shot him during an armed standoff that presaged Wounded Knee.
- Elevated to the unprecedented position of supreme leader of the unified Lakota bands around 1868 to coordinate armed resistance against federal military forts along the Bozeman Trail.
- Inducted into the elite Strong Heart and Silent Eaters warrior societies in early adulthood after performing feats of valor in intertribal conflicts against the Crow and Assiniboine.
- Known in childhood by the birth name *Jumping Badger* and nicknamed *Slow* (*Húŋkešni*) due to his deliberate, patient temperament before taking his father's illustrious name after his first battle at age fourteen.

## connections

- [[little-bighorn]] — decisive 1876 engagement along the Greasy Grass River where his prophetic vision and unified tribal alliance destroyed the [[seventh-cavalry]].
- [[united-states]] — expanding continental power whose broken treaties, gold expeditions, and forced reservation policies he staunchly opposed throughout his life.
- [[colonialism]] — overarching geopolitical framework of westward settlement, territorial expropriation, and cultural eradication that devastated indigenous nations.
- [[ghost-dance]] — spiritual revitalization movement founded by *Wovoka*, whose promise of native renewal terrified federal authorities and prompted the fatal raid on his cabin.
- [[george-armstrong-custer]] — reckless cavalry officer whose surprise attack on the allied native encampment resulted in the total annihilation of his immediate command.
- [[lakota-sioux]] — Great Plains tribal confederacy comprising the Hunkpapa, Oglala, and other divisions whose autonomy and sacred lands he fought to defend.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — national period following the [[american-civil-war]] during which the federal government redirected seasoned military regiments westward to subdue independent tribes.

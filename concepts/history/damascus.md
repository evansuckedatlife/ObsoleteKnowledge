## summary

**Damascus**, known in Arabic as *Dimashq* and affectionately celebrated as the *City of Jasmine*, is the capital of Syria and one of the oldest continuously inhabited urban centers on Earth. Nestled at the eastern foothills of the Anti-Lebanon mountain range and irrigated by the vital *Barada River*, the oasis metropolis flourished as an imperial crossroad linking the Levant with Mesopotamia, Egypt, and the Mediterranean. Renowned as the historic seat of the *Umayyad Caliphate*, the city stands as a foundational monument of Islamic architecture, medieval trade, and turbulent modern Levantine statehood.

## you gotta know

- Home to the iconic *Umayyad Mosque* (the *Great Mosque of Damascus*), commissioned by Caliph *al-Walid I* in 705 CE upon the grounds of a former Christian basilica dedicated to *John the Baptist* and an earlier Roman temple of *Jupiter*.
- Selected in 661 CE by *Mu'awiya I* as the capital of the vast *Umayyad Caliphate*, serving as the administrative nerve center of an Islamic empire extending from the Iberian Peninsula to the borders of the Indus Valley until the Abbasid revolution in 750 CE.
- Features prominently in the New Testament as the destination of *Saul of Tarsus*, whose miraculous conversion to *[[christianity]]* occurred on the *Road to Damascus*, leading him to the ancient Roman thoroughfare known as the *Street Called Straight*.
- Achieved international renown along the [[silk-road]] for prized artisan goods, including intricate patterned damask textiles and the legendary metallurgical craft of *Damascus steel*, celebrated for its distinctive watered patterns and superior cutting edge.
- Served as the administrative capital and military bastion of the Ayyubid sultan *Saladin* during the Crusades, whose stone mausoleum rests in the garden immediately adjacent to the northwest corner of the Umayyad Mosque.
- Ruled by the [[ottoman-empire]] for four centuries from 1516 until the First World War, functioning as the chief staging and marshalling hub for the annual imperial *Hajj* pilgrimage caravan journeying to Mecca.
- Transformed into a modern twentieth-century ideological battleground, witnessing the short-lived Arab Kingdom of Syria under King *Faisal I*, the subsequent French Mandate, and its emergence as the power base of the Syrian [[baath-party]] under [[hafez-al-assad]].

## connections

- [[middle-east]] — the broader geopolitical region in which the city has functioned as an indispensable cultural, commercial, and political capital for millennia.
- [[islam]] — the world religion under whose early Umayyad caliphs the city reached the height of its imperial prestige and architectural achievement.
- [[ottoman-empire]] — the imperial dynasty that conquered the city under Sultan *Selim I* and administered it as an essential provincial capital and pilgrimage route.
- [[silk-road]] — the vast trans-Eurasian trade network that funneled raw materials, spices, and crucible steel into the bustling covered souks of the old city.
- [[arab-israeli-conflict]] — the enduring twentieth-century regional confrontation that made the city a central hub of frontline military command and Arab diplomacy.
- [[dacian-wars]] — the conflict whose master military engineer, *Apollodorus of Damascus*, hailed from the city before designing monumental works for Emperor *Trajan*.
- [[rome]] — the ancient empire that annexed the city under *Pompey the Great* in 64 BCE and designed its classical grid plan and fortified civic walls.

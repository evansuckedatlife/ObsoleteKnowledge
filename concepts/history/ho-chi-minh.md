## summary

**Ho Chi Minh** was a Vietnamese communist revolutionary, anticolonial nationalist, and statesman who led the struggle for Vietnamese independence against French colonial rule and American military intervention. Born *Nguyen Sinh Cung*, he founded the Indochinese Communist Party and the *Viet Minh* independence coalition, later serving as Prime Minister and President of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam. Widely venerated domestically as *Uncle Ho*, his strategic vision, guerrilla warfare doctrine, and diplomatic maneuvering established the foundations of a unified, socialist Vietnam.

## you gotta know

- Traveled the world in his youth as a ship galley cook under the alias *Nguyen Tat Thanh*, living in London, New York, and Paris, where he embraced Marxist-Leninist thought and co-founded the French Communist Party in 1920.
- Petitioned Allied leaders under the name *Nguyen Ai Quoc* at the 1919 Paris Peace Conference, advocating for self-determination for the people of French Indochina in response to *Woodrow Wilson*'s Fourteen Points.
- Founded the *Viet Minh* independence front in 1941 to fight Japanese occupation and French colonial administrators during the Pacific War, receiving initial intelligence assistance from the American OSS.
- Declared the independence of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam in Hanoi's Ba Dinh Square on September 2, 1945, deliberately quoting the American Declaration of Independence.
- Directed the First Indochina War against French imperial forces, culminating in General *Vo Nguyen Giap*'s decisive victory at the Siege of Dien Bien Phu in 1954 and the partition of Vietnam at the Geneva Accords.
- Oversaw the development of the Ho Chi Minh Trail, an elaborate logistics network through Laos and Cambodia that supplied the National Liberation Front (*Viet Cong*) throughout the Vietnam War.
- Died of heart failure in Hanoi in September 1969 before seeing national reunification; following the fall of Saigon to North Vietnamese forces in 1975, the southern metropolis was officially renamed Ho Chi Minh City.

## summary

**Land reform**, also known as **agrarian reform**, denotes the statutory redistribution of agricultural property from privileged landholders and vast estates to tenant farmers, peasants, and landless laborers. Spanning ancient antiquity to modern revolutions across Latin America, Eastern Europe, and East Asia, these policies sought to dismantle feudal hierarchies, alleviate rural poverty, and eliminate coercive tenancy. In modern political economy, agrarian restructuring served as a central battleground during the `[[cold-war]]`, operating alternately as a socialist revolutionary catalyst or a capitalist bulwark against communist insurgency.

## you gotta know

- In the Mexican Revolution, peasant general *Emiliano Zapata* issued the 1911 *Plan de Ayala* under the rallying cry *Tierra y Libertad*, demanding the restitution of ancestral village lands seized by *hacienda* estates; this struggle shaped Article 27 of the 1917 Constitution and later enabled President *Lázaro Cárdenas* to distribute millions of hectares into communal *ejidos*.
- Bolshevik leader *Vladimir Lenin* secured peasant loyalty during the 1917 Russian Revolution through the *Decree on Land*, which abolished private estate ownership without compensation and placed agrarian parcels under the control of peasant communes under the slogan *Peace, Land, and Bread*, long before forced collectivization in the `[[soviet-union]]`.
- In post-World War II `[[japan]]`, Allied occupation authorities guided by agricultural expert *Wolf Ladejinsky* enacted sweeping land-to-the-tiller legislation that extinguished tenant subjugation, establishing an egalitarian smallholder model duplicated in Taiwan and South Korea to prevent peasant radicalization during the `[[cold-war]]`.
- Following victory in 1949, the newly established government of `[[china]]` under *Mao Zedong* promulgated the Agrarian Reform Law of 1950, inciting mass peasant tribunals to conduct violent *struggle sessions* against rural landlords and redistribute arable soil prior to nationwide collectivization.
- Guatemalan President *Jacobo Árbenz* enacted Decree 900 in 1952 to expropriate fallow corporate holdings and transfer acreage to indigenous Mayan peasants, triggering sharp retaliation from the *United Fruit Company* and a CIA-backed coup d'état orchestrated by the `[[united-states]]`.
- Near the close of the `[[american-civil-war]]` and the dawn of the `[[reconstruction-era]]`, Union General *William Tecumseh Sherman* issued Special Field Orders No. 15 to settle emancipated families along the southern coast, popularizing the promise of *forty acres and a mule* before President *Andrew Johnson* revoked the order and restored plantations to white planters.
- In the late Roman Republic, plebeian tribune *Tiberius Gracchus* passed the *Lex Sempronia Agraria* in 133 BCE to reclaim state-owned land held illegally by wealthy patricians and distribute small plots to dispossessed citizens, provoking a mob of conservative senators to beat him to death in the `[[roman-empire]]`.

## connections

- [[cold-war]] — motivated global superpowers to finance land redistribution or suppress rural agrarian revolts.
- [[soviet-union]] — nationalized aristocratic estates during the 1917 revolution before replacing independent family holdings with state collectives.
- [[colonialism]] — entrenched dual agricultural systems of wealthy export plantations and dispossessed native populations that subsequent reformers targeted.
- [[french-revolution]] — dismantled seigneurial feudal privileges in 1789 and auctioned confiscated church lands to cultivate a loyal smallholder peasantry.
- [[japan]] — transformed impoverished rural tenants into prosperous, politically conservative landowners under postwar American occupation directives.
- [[china]] — mobilized millions of rural peasants against the traditional landowning gentry to consolidate communist state authority.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — transformed national labor markets when rural dispossession and agricultural consolidation drove displaced farmers into manufacturing cities.

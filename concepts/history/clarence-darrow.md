## summary

**Clarence Darrow** was an acclaimed American defense attorney, civil libertarian, and orator who became the central legal figure in several of the early twentieth century's most iconic courtroom battles. Practicing primarily in Chicago, *Darrow* championed progressive causes, labor unions, and individual rights while staunchly opposing capital punishment. His eloquent, hours-long closing arguments reshaped modern criminal defense, death penalty jurisprudence, and legal *realism* throughout the *United States*.

## you gotta know

- Defended *Nathan Leopold* and *Richard Loeb* in their infamous 1924 Chicago murder trial, delivering an impassioned twelve-hour closing speech that saved both young men from the gallows and became a landmark text in capital punishment defense.
- Opposed *William Jennings Bryan* in the 1925 *Scopes Monkey Trial* in Dayton, Tennessee, defending biology teacher *John T. Scopes* and subjecting biblical literalism to devastating cross-examination.
- Defended the *American Railway Union* and its leader *Eugene V. Debs* following the 1894 *Pullman Strike*, establishing his reputation as the foremost legal advocate for organized labor.
- Secured the acquittal of *Ossian Sweet*, a Black physician in Detroit charged with murder in 1925 after defending his newly purchased home against an armed white mob, marking an early milestone for racial justice and the *civil-rights-movement*.
- Successfully defended labor leader *William "Big Bill" Haywood* against conspiracy charges surrounding the 1905 assassination of former Idaho governor *Frank Steunenberg*.
- Embraced a determinist philosophy that viewed criminal actions through the lenses of heredity, social environment, and poverty rather than innate malice, prefiguring modern sociological and psychological approaches in criminal law.

## summary

The **Glorious Revolution**, also remembered as the **Revolution of 1688** or the **Bloodless Revolution**, was the November 1688 deposition of King *James II* of [[england]], *Scotland*, and *Ireland* and his replacement by his Protestant daughter *Mary II* and her Dutch husband *William III* of Orange. Triggered by anxieties over the birth of a Catholic heir and *James II*'s autocratic suspension of penal laws, the invasion established a constitutional monarchy bound by the rule of law. It permanently settled the supremacy of the English Parliament over the crown and reshaped European alliances against *Louis XIV*'s [[france]].

## you gotta know

- Overthrew the Catholic monarch *James II* in 1688 after the birth of his son, *James Francis Edward Stuart*, threatened to establish an enduring Catholic dynasty on the English throne.
- The *Immortal Seven*, a cross-party conspiracy of English nobles and bishops, drafted a secret invitation urging the Dutch Stadtholder *William of Orange* to mount a military expedition to defend English liberties and the Protestant faith.
- Resulted in the joint coronation of *William III* and *Mary II* after Parliament promulgated the landmark Declaration of Rights, enacted into law as the *Bill of Rights 1689*.
- Prompted the immediate collapse of royal administration in the American colonies, inspiring rebellions such as *Leisler's Rebellion* in New York and the overthrow of royal [[governor]] *Sir Edmund Andros* in Boston.
- Established the foundation for modern parliamentary sovereignty, curbing the royal dispensing power, mandating regular parliaments, and inaugurating the *Toleration Act 1689* for nonconformist Protestants.
- Triggered fierce armed resistance in *Scotland* and *Ireland*, giving rise to the long-running *Jacobite* rebellions led by *John Graham of Claverhouse* at the Battle of *Killiecrankie* and *James II* at the Battle of the *Boyne*.
- Facilitated a major financial revolution in London modeled on Amsterdam, including the founding of the *Bank of England* and a permanent national debt to finance European wars.

## connections

- [[england]] — the seat of the Stuart monarchy where parliament permanently established its constitutional supremacy over the crown.
- [[british-empire]] — the global imperial system whose colonial governance was reshaped when colonial assemblies asserted rights against the royal crown.
- [[enlightenment]] — the philosophical epoch shaped by *John Locke*, whose *Two Treatises of Government* defended the moral and constitutional legitimacy of the overthrow.
- [[france]] — the Catholic realm ruled by *Louis XIV* that sheltered the exiled Stuarts and fought the Grand Alliance across the Nine Years' War.
- [[christianity]] — the religious framework whose sectarian Protestant-Catholic divisions drove the succession crisis and subsequent exclusion laws.
- [[governor]] — the colonial executive office held by figures like *Sir Edmund Andros*, who was deposed by colonists following news of the invasion.
- [[united-states-constitution]] — the foundational American document that incorporated protections directly adapted from the English *Bill of Rights 1689*.

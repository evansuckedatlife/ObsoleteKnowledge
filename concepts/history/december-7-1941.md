## summary

**December 7 1941** was the date of the surprise aerial and naval raid launched by the Imperial Japanese Navy against the United States naval installation at *Pearl Harbor* in Hawaii, an operation universally remembered as the **Attack on Pearl Harbor**. The strike disabled much of the American Pacific Fleet's surface force, killed over two thousand service members, and shattered the prevailing domestic isolationism in the [[united-states]]. The catastrophe precipitated immediate American entry into [[world-war-ii]], transforming the European and Pacific conflicts into a unified global war.

## you gotta know

- Incited President *Franklin D. Roosevelt* to address a joint session of Congress the following day, famously branding the event a date which will live in infamy before securing a formal declaration of war.
- Planned under the leadership of Admiral *Isoroku Yamamoto* and directed at sea by Vice Admiral *Chuichi Nagumo*, deploying six aircraft carriers and more than three hundred and fifty warplanes across two strike waves.
- Devastated the vessels moored along *Battleship Row*, culminating in the explosive loss of the *USS Arizona*, which sank with 1,177 crew members and remains an offshore national shrine.
- Spared all three operational aircraft carriers of the American Pacific Fleet, including *USS Enterprise* and *USS Lexington*, which were absent on deployment missions at sea and thus preserved American carrier aviation doctrine.
- Left crucial shore installations largely intact, including the base's submarine pens, repair drydocks, and massive aboveground oil storage tank farms, allowing the United States Navy to reconstitute operations rapidly.
- Triggered reciprocal declarations of war against the [[united-states]] four days later by Nazi *Germany* and Fascist *Italy*, fully entangling the American armed forces across both the European and Pacific theaters.
- Formed one component of a sweeping Japanese offensive, accompanied by simultaneous military assaults on Malaya, Hong Kong, the Philippines, Guam, and Wake Island.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the worldwide conflict that expanded into an all-out total war across multiple continents as a result of the Hawaiian strike.
- [[japan]] — the empire whose expansionist ambitions in East Asia and desire to secure petroleum reserves motivated the preemptive operation.
- [[united-states]] — the country whose neutrality was terminated overnight, prompting total industrial mobilization and global military deployment.
- [[pearl-harbor]] — the naval base on the island of Oahu that served as the home anchorage for the Pacific Fleet and the strike's target.
- [[battleship]] — the traditional naval capital ship whose vulnerability to carrier-based naval aviation was conclusively demonstrated during the attack.
- [[cold-war]] — the postwar global confrontation whose security architecture and forward American military posture emerged from wartime mobilization.

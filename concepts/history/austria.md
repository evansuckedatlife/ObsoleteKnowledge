## summary

**Austria** is a European nation whose history spans from medieval principalities to the multi-ethnic *Austro-Hungarian Empire*, one of Europe's great powers, and finally to its modern republican state. Centered on Vienna and the Danube Valley, Austria rose through dynastic marriages and military prowess, particularly under the *House of Habsburg*, to dominate Central Europe for centuries. The empire's dissolution after *World War I* reduced Austria to a landlocked nation, which later fell under *Nazi* domination, but emerged after *World War II* as a neutral, prosperous republic.

## you gotta know

- The *House of Habsburg* ruled Austria (and much of Central Europe) from 1276 onward, building an empire through strategic marriages rather than conquest alone.
- Vienna, Austria's capital on the *Danube River*, became one of Europe's most cultured and powerful cities, home to imperial courts and later major composers like Mozart and Beethoven.
- The *Austro-Hungarian Empire* (1867–1918) was a dual monarchy spanning vast territories across Central and Eastern Europe, containing dozens of ethnic groups united only by the Habsburg crown.
- Austria played central roles in European power politics: opposing *Napoleon Bonaparte* through the *Coalition Wars*, and both competing with and allying with Prussia/Germany.
- The *Austrian Empire* was reorganized as the *Austro-Hungarian Empire* in 1867 to appease Hungarian nationalism, weakening imperial coherence just as nationalism itself undermined multi-ethnic empires.
- After *World War I*, the empire dissolved; Austria became a small republic, later annexed by Nazi Germany (1938), liberated by the Allies (1945), and declared permanently neutral during the *Cold War*.

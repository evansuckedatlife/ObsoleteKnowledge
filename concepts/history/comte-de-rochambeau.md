## summary

**Comte de Rochambeau** (*Jean-Baptiste Donatien de Vimeur*) was a distinguished French nobleman and general who commanded the *Expédition Particulière*, an elite land army dispatched across the Atlantic by *King Louis XVI* during the [[american-revolution]]. Operating in close partnership with General [[george-washington]], he combined veteran European siegecraft with Continental troops to encircle and capture the British army under *Lord Cornwallis* at the decisive Siege of *Yorktown* in 1781. His exemplary military discipline, logistical resourcefulness, and selfless deference to American leadership averted alliance friction, delivering the landmark victory that shattered the political resolve of the [[british-empire]] and established the independence of the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Commanded allied siege operations alongside General [[george-washington]] at the Siege of *Yorktown* in 1781, utilizing classical European parallel trenches, sap lines, and heavy artillery to compel the capitulation of *Lord Cornwallis*.
- Led the *Expédition Particulière*, transporting approximately 5,500 disciplined French regular soldiers across the Atlantic Ocean to establish an essential allied naval and military base at *Newport*, *Rhode Island*, in July 1780.
- Subordinated his immense European rank and four decades of military experience entirely to [[george-washington]], faithfully observing royal instructions from *Versailles* to accept American supreme command and avoid alliance friction.
- Persuaded Washington to abandon a frontal assault against the fortifications of [[new-york-city]], arguing instead for an audacious joint march south to *Virginia* timed to meet the incoming fleet of *Admiral de Grasse*.
- Advanced substantial sums of hard French silver coin from his expeditionary military treasury in *Philadelphia* to pay restive Continental soldiers, averting mutiny and restoring morale before the long march to *Virginia*.
- Enforced strict discipline in *New England* winter quarters, forbidding looting and paying local farmers in gold and silver specie, which disarmed local prejudices against French Catholic troops.
- Built his martial reputation across Europe in the *War of the Austrian Succession* and *Seven Years' War*, rising through combat merit and surviving severe wounds at *Minorca* and *Clostercamp*.
- Survived the [[french-revolution]] after being elevated to Marshal of [[france]], enduring imprisonment in the *Conciergerie* during the *Reign of Terror* and escaping the guillotine only when *Maximilien Robespierre* was overthrown.

## connections

- [[george-washington]] — allied commander in chief with whom he planned the march south and executed the decisive siege of Yorktown.
- [[american-revolution]] — global conflict in which his expeditionary force provided the professional regular army needed to achieve victory.
- [[france]] — kingdom whose royal military he served for over forty years, directing crown soldiers and matériel across the Atlantic.
- [[british-empire]] — imperial antagonist whose army under Cornwallis capitulated at Yorktown, collapsing British political support for the American war.
- [[united-states]] — young republic whose sovereignty and independence were firmly secured through his military leadership and financial support.
- [[new-york-city]] — British headquarters whose formidable defenses he persuaded Washington to bypass in favor of the Chesapeake campaign.
- [[french-revolution]] — domestic political upheaval that elevated him to Marshal of France before subjecting him to arrest during the Terror.
- [[napoleon-bonaparte]] — French ruler who recognized his lifetime of distinguished military service by restoring his pension and awarding him honours.

## summary

The **Colorado Territory** was an organized incorporated territory of the [[united-states]] that existed from February 28, 1861, until Colorado gained statehood on August 1, 1876. Carved from portions of the Kansas, Nebraska, Utah, and New Mexico territories, it was established following the *Pikes Peak Gold Rush* of 1858–1859. The territory played a crucial role during the [[american-civil-war]] and was defined by mineral extraction, territorial expansion, and devastating conflicts with indigenous *Cheyenne* and *Arapaho* nations.

## you gotta know

- Created when President *James Buchanan* signed its organic act on February 28, 1861, shortly before the secession crisis erupted into the [[american-civil-war]].
- Established in response to the *Pikes Peak Gold Rush*, which brought tens of thousands of prospectors carrying the slogan *Pikes Peak or Bust* into the ancestral lands of the *Ute*, *Cheyenne*, and *Arapaho*.
- Replaced the provisional and extra-legal *Territory of Jefferson*, an unrecognised governance structure set up by miners to regulate claims and civic order across the Rocky Mountains.
- Raised the *1st Colorado Volunteer Infantry Regiment*, which checked Confederate advances into the American West at the pivotal 1862 *Battle of Glorieta Pass* in New Mexico, often called the *Gettysburg of the West*.
- Witnessed the infamous *Sand Creek Massacre* in November 1864, in which the *3rd Colorado Cavalry* commanded by *John Chivington* massacred peaceful *Cheyenne* and *Arapaho* villagers under the protection of *Black Kettle*.
- Governed by territorial officials including *William Gilpin*, who financed Union troops using unauthorised federal drafts, and *John Evans*, who was forced to resign following congressional condemnation of Sand Creek.
- Developed an urban spine along the Front Range centered on *Denver*, which eclipsed early rival settlements such as *Auraria* and territorial capitals *Colorado City* and *Golden*.
- Admitted to the Union as the thirty-eighth state on August 1, 1876, earning Colorado the enduring nickname of the *Centennial State* because its statehood coincided with the centenary of the Declaration of Independence.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — the expanding federal republic that created and administered the western territory through territorial governors.
- [[american-civil-war]] — the civil conflict during which territorial volunteer units repelled Confederate expansion in the Southwest.
- [[colonialism]] — the structural displacement of indigenous nations across the Great Plains to facilitate mineral extraction and settlement.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — the post-war federal period during which territorial politics and railroads set the stage for admission to statehood.
- [[colorado-amendment-2]] — the later statewide constitutional controversy rooted in the civic and political institutions developed from territorial statehood.

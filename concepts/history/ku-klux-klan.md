## summary

The **Ku Klux Klan** is a terrorist organization founded in 1866 by white Southerners determined to resist Reconstruction and Black political power. Operating through violence, intimidation, and murder, the KKK enforced a reign of terror against freedmen and their white allies, becoming the primary instrument of white resistance to emancipation and equal rights. Its ideology of white racial supremacy and methods of organized violence established a template for American extremism that persisted across generations.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1866 in Pulaski, Tennessee, by Confederate veterans seeking to restore white dominance after the Civil War's end.
- Used masked night riders, cross-burning, whipping, and murder to terrorize Black voters and prevent their participation in politics during Reconstruction.
- Successfully suppressed Black political participation across the South through coordinated campaigns of violence and intimidation in the late 1860s and 1870s.
- The first Klan operated through cells with no central authority, giving individual chapters autonomy to decide targets and methods while maintaining plausible deniability at the leadership level.
- Federal suppression (notably under President *Ulysses S. Grant*) largely broke the organization's power by the 1880s, though it never entirely disappeared; smaller cells persisted into the early 20th century.
- Experienced a major revival in the 1920s, expanding beyond the South and incorporating anti-immigrant, anti-Catholic, and anti-Jewish ideology alongside white supremacy, bringing membership to an estimated 4 million at its peak.
- The second Klan explicitly modeled itself on the original, using the same symbolic imagery (robes, burning crosses) to evoke historical precedent and legitimacy.
- Served as the cultural model for American fascism and extremism throughout the 20th century, with its imagery and tactics inspiring later hate groups and domestic terrorism.

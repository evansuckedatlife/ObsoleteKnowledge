## summary

**Latin America** refers to the Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking regions of the Americas, comprising the former colonial territories of *Spain* and *Portugal* in Central America, South America, and the Caribbean. Born from conquest, colonialism, and eventually liberation, Latin America developed a distinct cultural and political identity shaped by indigenous civilizations, European settlement, African slavery, and waves of independence. The region emerged as a major geopolitical arena where European and American imperial ambitions continually collided.

## you gotta know

- Encompasses roughly 650 million people across 20 countries, united by Spanish or Portuguese language heritage and colonial Spanish or Portuguese rule, though indigenous languages remain widely spoken.
- The region's civilization represents a fusion: indigenous American societies, European (primarily Spanish and Portuguese) colonizers, and African slaves forcibly brought to the Americas through the transatlantic slave trade.
- Spanish conquest and colonization (16th-18th centuries) created an enormous wealth extraction system that enriched Europe while impoverishing indigenous populations and reshaping continental demographics.
- Independence movements in the early 19th century, led by figures like *Simón Bolívar* and *José de San Martín*, overthrew Spanish and Portuguese rule across the region through military campaigns and revolutions.
- The *Monroe Doctrine* (1823) asserted American opposition to European interference in the region, establishing the Western Hemisphere as within the American sphere of influence and presaging American hemispheric dominance.
- The region became a battleground of Cold War politics, with the United States intervening repeatedly to prevent communist influence in what it deemed its strategic backyard through military aid and coups.

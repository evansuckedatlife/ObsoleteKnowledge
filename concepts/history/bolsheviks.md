## summary

The **Bolsheviks** were a radical Marxist faction of the *Russian Social Democratic Labour Party* (*RSDLP*) that seized state power during the *October Revolution* of 1917. Led by *Vladimir Lenin* and *Leon Trotsky*, the faction transformed broad popular discontent with the provisional government of the crumbling *[[russian-empire]]* into a coordinated seizure of critical infrastructure and governmental authority. After winning the brutal Russian Civil War, the party dismantled all rival political organizations, establishing single-party communist rule and founding the *[[soviet-union]]*.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 1903 at the *Second Congress of the RSDLP* in Brussels and London, taking their name from the Russian word for "majority" after winning a crucial vote against the rival *Mensheviks*.
- Maintained a professional underground apparatus of agitators and organizers who operated clandestinely while avoiding arrest and Siberian exile by the tsarist secret police, the *Okhrana*.
- Gained political control of the *Petrograd Soviet* in the autumn of 1917, providing *Leon Trotsky* with the military-revolutionary platform needed to orchestrate the seizure of power.
- Overthrew the Russian Provisional Government during the *October Revolution*, storming the *Winter Palace* and arresting cabinet ministers while proclaiming a government of workers and peasants.
- Shut down the democratically elected *Constituent Assembly* by force in January 1918 after finishing second to the *Socialist Revolutionary Party*, extinguishing multiparty democracy.
- Signed the separate *Treaty of Brest-Litovsk* with *[[germany]]* in March 1918, conceding substantial imperial territories in order to exit the fighting of *[[world-war-i]]*.
- Organized the victorious *Red Army* under the military leadership of *Leon Trotsky*, defeating the counter-revolutionary *White Movement* and foreign interventionist armies during the Russian Civil War.
- Renamed themselves the *Russian Communist Party* in 1918, which later evolved into the all-powerful *Communist Party of the Soviet Union*.

## connections

- [[bolshevism]] — the vanguard Marxist doctrine and governing philosophy that directed the party's actions.
- [[soviet-union]] — the socialist state created and monopolized by the party from its inception until its dissolution.
- [[russian-empire]] — the imperial monarchy whose autocratic rule, social inequality, and collapse enabled the party's ascent.
- [[world-war-i]] — the devastating conflict whose military disasters and economic collapse fatally undermined the Russian imperial and provisional regimes.
- [[germany]] — the imperial enemy that concluded a punitive separate peace treaty with the party at Brest-Litovsk.
- [[cold-war]] — the protracted geopolitical rivalry fought between Western powers and the superpower governed by the party.

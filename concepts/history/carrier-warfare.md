## summary

**Carrier Warfare** is a form of naval combat centered on aircraft carriers, which deploy embarked strike planes, dive bombers, and fighters to project military power over vast oceanic distances. Developed experimentally in the interwar era, it supplanted the traditional big-gun battleship during *World War II* as the definitive doctrine of command of the seas. Carrier strike groups subsequently served as the foundational instrument of forward power projection and deterrent diplomacy throughout the Cold War and into the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- The Battle of the Coral Sea in May 1942 was the first naval engagement in history where opposing surface warships neither saw each other nor exchanged gunfire directly.
- Decisively demonstrated its dominance at the Battle of Midway in June 1942, where the United States Navy sank four fleet carriers of the Imperial Japanese Navy, altering the trajectory of the Pacific War.
- The British Royal Navy pioneered modern carrier attack doctrine during the Battle of Taranto in November 1940, crippling the Italian battle fleet with obsolete biplane torpedo bombers.
- Japan executed the devastating carrier raid against Pearl Harbor in December 1941, illustrating the immense operational range and surprise capability of massed naval air wings.
- Shifted maritime tacticians away from the Mahanian doctrine of big-gun line-of-battle clashes toward coordinated fleet air defense, radar interception, and long-range anti-ship strikes.
- Postwar technological revolutions introduced jet propulsion, angled flight decks, steam catapults, and the optical landing system to manage heavier, faster naval aircraft.
- Supercarriers powered by nuclear reactors became the premier instrument of power projection for the United States Navy throughout the Cold War and modern regional crises.

## connections

- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflict across the Pacific and Atlantic where aircraft carriers eclipsed the battleship.
- [[japan]] — the naval power whose *Kido Butai* demonstrated carrier force before suffering devastating carrier losses at Midway and Leyte Gulf.
- [[united-states]] — the nation that built vast carrier task forces to conquer the Pacific and maintains carrier strike groups today.
- [[cold-war]] — the multi-decade confrontation where carrier battle groups projected deterrence across the Mediterranean, Atlantic, and Pacific.
- [[world-war-i]] — the earlier conflict that witnessed the initial conversion of merchant hulls and cruisers into experimental seaplane tenders.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — the metallurgical and manufacturing transformation that enabled mass industrial production of armored flattops and naval aircraft.

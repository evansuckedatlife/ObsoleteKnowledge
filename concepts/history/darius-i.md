## summary

**Darius I**, commonly known as **Darius the Great**, was the third King of Kings of the [[achaemenid-empire]], ruling from 522 BCE until his death in 486 BCE. Ascending the throne amid widespread rebellion, he consolidated imperial authority and expanded Persian dominion to its greatest territorial extent, spanning from the Indus Valley in the east to Thrace and Egypt in the west. His reign transformed the sprawling empire through an innovative administrative structure, monumental construction at *Persepolis*, and the initiation of the Greco-Persian Wars against [[ancient-greece]].

## you gotta know

- Dispatched a massive expeditionary force across the Aegean Sea to punish Athens and Eretria for aiding the *Ionian Revolt*, which culminated in a famous Persian defeat against Athenian hoplites at the *Battle of Marathon* in 490 BCE.
- Reorganized the vast empire into approximately twenty governed administrative provinces known as *satrapies*, each overseen by a regional governor (*satrap*), a military commander, and royal inspectors famously dubbed the "King's Eyes and Ears."
- Commissioned the monumental *Behistun Inscription* carved high upon a cliff face in modern western Iran, which proclaimed his divine mandate in Old Persian, Elamite, and Babylonian, ultimately providing the key to the modern decipherment of cuneiform writing.
- Initiated construction of the grand ceremonial capital at *Persepolis*, renowned for its monumental Gate of All Nations and the Apadana palace depicting subjugated delegations bearing tribute, while also refurbishing the imperial administrative capital at *Susa*.
- Greatly enhanced communication, trade, and military logistics across the empire by paving the fifteen-hundred-mile *Royal Road* between *Sardis* and *Susa* and completing a maritime waterway connecting the Nile River to the Red Sea.
- Stabilized imperial commerce and tax collection by introducing a uniform, state-backed monetary system anchored by the high-purity gold *daric* coin and the silver *siglos*.
- Legitimized his imperial authority through constant appeals to the supreme creator god *Ahura Mazda*, integrating Persian royal ideology deeply with the ethical traditions of [[zoroastrianism]].
- Came to power in 522 BCE by orchestrating a palace coup alongside six Persian conspirators to execute *Gaumata*, an alleged Magian usurper who had impersonated *Cambyses II*'s deceased brother *Bardiya* (known in Greek accounts as *Smerdis*).

## connections

- [[cyrus-the-great]] — imperial founder whose dynasty he restored, securing his lineage by marrying *Cyrus*'s daughters *Atossa* and *Artystone*.
- [[xerxes-i]] — his designated son and successor who inherited the empire and led the second Persian invasion of Greece.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the collection of Mediterranean city-states whose support for the *Ionian Revolt* provoked his military expeditions.
- [[middle-east]] — the geographic core of his empire, unified under centralized bureaucracy, standard taxation, and royal roads.
- [[zoroastrianism]] — the religion whose dualistic concepts and veneration of *Ahura Mazda* pervaded his royal decrees and rock inscriptions.
- [[achaemenid-empire]] — the imperial Persian state that reached its greatest administrative organization and territorial reach under his direction.
- [[silk-road]] — early overland trade networks that flourished due to his standard currency, security, and imperial communication routes.

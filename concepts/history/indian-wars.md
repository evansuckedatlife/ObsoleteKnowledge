## summary

The **Indian Wars** were a series of armed conflicts between the *United States* and *Native American* tribes spanning from the early 19th century through the 1890s. As American expansion moved westward, the federal government and settlers violently dispossessed indigenous peoples of their ancestral lands. These wars resulted in displacement, genocide, and the destruction of Native American societies and cultures.

## you gotta know

- Began in earnest after the *War of 1812* as American settlers moved across the *Mississippi River* into indigenous territories, breaking treaties and igniting conflicts with established tribal nations.
- Driven by [[Manifest Destiny]], the ideology that American expansion across the continent was inevitable and justified by Providence, framing indigenous peoples as obstacles to national destiny.
- Major conflicts included wars against the [[Cherokee]], [[Creek]], [[Seminole]], [[Sioux]], [[Cheyenne]], [[Comanche]], Apache, Navajo, and other tribes across multiple decades; some lasted years and involved thousands of combatants.
- Featured legendary figures like *Sitting Bull*, *Geronimo*, and *Crazy Horse* who led indigenous resistance against American military campaigns; their tactical brilliance and courage earned respect even from American military opponents.
- *Andrew Jackson* forced the *Trail of Tears* (1838–1839), the devastating forced removal of southeastern tribes (Cherokee, Creek, Chickasaw, Choctaw, and Seminole) to western reservations in Oklahoma, killing thousands from disease and starvation.
- Culminated in massacres like [[Sand Creek]] (1864, Colorado militia killed Cheyenne and Arapaho families) and [[Wounded Knee]] (1890, U.S. 7th Cavalry killed Lakota Sioux), which symbolized the final suppression of Native resistance.
- Resulted in the creation of the reservation system, confining surviving Native Americans to marginal lands; this policy destroyed their traditional hunting and agricultural ways of life, creating cycles of poverty and cultural disruption lasting generations.

## summary

**Imperial Japan** was the Japanese nation during the period of rapid industrialization and imperial expansion from the *Meiji Restoration* (1868) through World War II. Driven by nationalist ideology and a desire for continental power, Japan transformed into a militaristic state that sought dominance over East Asia. This era fundamentally reshaped the region's geopolitics and left a legacy of conflict and suffering.

## you gotta know

- Rose rapidly to great-power status after the *Meiji Restoration* embraced Western technology and military reforms, transforming a feudal society into an industrial military power within decades.
- Built a powerful navy and army, defeating the *Russian Empire* in the Russo-Japanese War (1904–1905) and gaining respect as a military power—the first time an Asian nation defeated a European power, shocking the global order.
- Pursued expansionism across East Asia: captured *Korea* (1910) and annexed it, invaded *Manchuria* (1931), and waged the Second Sino-Japanese War against *China* (1937–1945), killing millions of Chinese civilians.
- Developed an increasingly militaristic government, with military factions gaining dominance over civilian politics through the 1920s and 1930s; assassinated moderate politicians to impose military control.
- Allied with Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy in the Axis pact (1936), viewing the alliance as serving its imperial ambitions in Asia while Germany expanded in Europe; Italy and Germany never effectively coordinated with Japan.
- Initiated the Pacific War by attacking *Pearl Harbor* on December 7, 1941, destroying American battleships and bringing the United States into World War II; the surprise assault unified American public opinion against Japan.
- Surrendered unconditionally in September 1945 after atomic bombings of *Hiroshima* and *Nagasaki*, totaling approximately 200,000 immediate deaths plus hundreds of thousands more from radiation sickness; subsequently occupied and democratized by American forces under General *Douglas MacArthur*.

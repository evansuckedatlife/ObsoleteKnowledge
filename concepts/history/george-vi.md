## summary

**George VI** (born **Prince Albert**, known privately as **Bertie**) reigned as King of the United Kingdom and the Dominions of the *British Commonwealth* from December 1936 until his death in 1952. Thrust unexpectedly onto the throne following the abdication crisis of his brother *Edward VIII*, he overcame a severe stammer to serve as an enduring symbol of British resilience throughout *World War II*. His wartime leadership alongside Prime Minister *Winston Churchill*, marked by his refusal to flee London during the Blitz, restored moral authority to the crown and guided the *British Empire* toward its modern Commonwealth incarnation.

## you gotta know

- Ascended the throne unexpectedly in December 1936 after his older brother, *Edward VIII*, abdicated to marry American divorcee *Wallis Simpson*, sparking a major constitutional crisis and forcing the shy prince into monarchical duty.
- Struggled with a severe stammer and received unorthodox speech therapy from Australian specialist *Lionel Logue*, whose coaching enabled him to deliver crucial radio broadcasts to the empire, notably his 1939 wartime address.
- Refused to evacuate London during the Blitz, remaining at *Buckingham Palace* with his family through nine aerial bombing hits and frequently touring bomb-damaged ruins across the working-class *East End*.
- Maintained an intimate working relationship with Prime Minister *Winston Churchill*, meeting for private weekly lunches throughout *World War II* to discuss war strategy and sustain civilian morale.
- Created the *George Cross* and *George Medal* in 1940 to honour civilian gallantry under enemy assault, famously awarding the collective *George Cross* to the besieged island of *Malta* in 1942.
- Relinquished the title *Emperor of India* following Indian and Pakistani independence in August 1947, becoming recognized as the first formal *Head of the Commonwealth* under the 1949 *London Declaration*.
- Saw naval combat during *World War I* as a turret officer aboard *HMS Collingwood* during the 1916 *Battle of Jutland*, making him the last British monarch to witness active military battle.
- Suffered a strict Victorian upbringing under his father *King George V*, who forced the naturally left-handed prince to write right-handed, and later played competitive tennis in the 1926 *Wimbledon Championships*.

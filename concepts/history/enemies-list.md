## summary

The **Enemies List**, known formally in internal White House correspondence as the **Master list of Nixon's political opponents** or simply **Nixon's enemies list**, was a secret catalog of political adversaries compiled during the administration of President *Richard Nixon*. Designed to systematically direct federal agencies—most notably the Internal Revenue Service—to harass, audit, and sanction prominent critics of the administration, its existence was dramatically exposed during the 1973 Watergate hearings. The document stands as one of the most glaring symbols of the abuse of executive authority and presidential paranoia in modern American political history.

## you gotta know

- Compiled primarily by White House counsel *John Dean* in 1971, building upon earlier memoranda initiated by presidential aide *Charles Colson*.
- Stated its explicit objective in a leaked memorandum as determining how the administration could "use the available federal machinery to screw our political enemies."
- Target list grew from an initial memorandum naming twenty key political figures into an expanded roster containing hundreds of politicians, journalists, labor leaders, and celebrity activists.
- Highlighted leading Democratic politicians, including senators *Edward Kennedy*, *George McGovern*, *Edmund Muskie*, and representative *Shirley Chisholm*.
- Singled out prominent media critics and reporters, such as *Daniel Schorr* of CBS News, *Carl Bernstein*, *Bob Woodward*, and syndicated columnist *Jack Anderson*.
- Targeted cultural icons and Hollywood celebrities who opposed the Vietnam War, including actors *Paul Newman* and *Jane Fonda*, quarterback *Joe Namath*, and author *Norman Mailer*.
- Revealed publicly by *Daniel Schorr* during a live television broadcast of the Senate Watergate Committee hearings, where Schorr read the list aloud on air and discovered his own name on it in real time.
- Served as direct evidential support for Article II of the Articles of Impeachment against *Richard Nixon*, which charged the president with unlawful abuse of executive power and weaponization of the IRS.

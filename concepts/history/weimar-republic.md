## summary

The **Weimar Republic**, officially designated the *Deutsches Reich* and known retrospectively as **Weimar Germany**, was the democratic government of [[germany]] established in November 1918 following the collapse of the imperial Hohenzollern monarchy at the close of [[world-war-i]]. Governed under a progressive national constitution framed in the city of Weimar, the fledgling state instituted universal suffrage, proportional representation, and an eight-hour workday while fostering a celebrated flourishing of avant-garde culture. Despite its progressive institutional reforms, the republic was severely crippled by reparations, ruinous economic shocks, and virulent opposition from anti-democratic factions, ultimately collapsing in 1933 when [[adolf-hitler]] was appointed chancellor and dismantled constitutional democracy.

## you gotta know

- Replaced the imperial German Empire following the Kiel mutiny, the German Revolution of 1918, and the abdication of Kaiser *Wilhelm II*, after which Social Democrat *Philipp Scheidemann* proclaimed the republic from a Reichstag window.
- Overthrown and replaced in 1933 by [[adolf-hitler]] and the National Socialist regime, which exploited the mysterious Reichstag fire to pass the *Reichstag Fire Decree* and the dictatorial *Enabling Act* (*Ermächtigungsgesetz*).
- Suffered catastrophic hyperinflation in 1923 when the government printed paper marks to finance passive resistance against the Franco-Belgian occupation of the industrial *Ruhr* valley, destroying the savings of the middle class.
- Plagued from its inception by the right-wing *Dolchstoßlegende* ("stab-in-the-back" myth), which falsely blamed socialists, liberals, and Jews for the military defeat and castigated the republican signatories of the *Treaty of Versailles* as the "November Criminals."
- Experienced a dazzling cultural explosion during the "Golden Twenties" (*Goldene Zwanziger*), transforming Berlin into a vibrant capital of [[modernism]] through the *Bauhaus* school of architecture, *Bertolt Brecht*'s theater, and expressionist [[silent-film]] masterpieces.
- Governed increasingly by emergency decree rather than parliamentary legislation under *Article 48* of the Weimar Constitution, a loophole heavily exploited by presidential cabinets under the aging field marshal *Paul von Hindenburg*.
- Stabilized during the mid-1920s through the diplomatic and financial stewardship of *Gustav Stresemann*, who halted hyperinflation with the temporary *Rentenmark*, negotiated the American-funded *Dawes Plan*, and secured the 1925 *Locarno Treaties*.
- Undermined by violent paramilitary street brawls and political terrorism, including the right-wing *Kapp Putsch*, Hitler's failed 1923 *Beer Hall Putsch*, and the assassinations of foreign minister *Walther Rathenau* and *Matthias Erzberger* by the nationalist *Organisation Consul*.

## connections

- [[germany]] — the nation transformed into a progressive yet vulnerable federal republic between imperial collapse and Nazi totalitarianism.
- [[world-war-i]] — the catastrophic conflict whose military defeat, imperial collapse, and punitive armistice directly established the republic.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the global conflagration launched by the Third Reich after destroying the republic's democratic institutions.
- [[modernism]] — the innovative artistic and architectural movement championed by Weimar creators across literature, theater, and design.
- [[silent-film]] — the revolutionary cinematic art form elevated by German Expressionist masterpieces like *Metropolis* and *The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari*.
- [[soviet-union]] — the isolated socialist power with which Weimar established secret military cooperation and diplomatic recognition via the 1922 *Treaty of Rapallo*.
- [[fascism]] — the militant ultra-nationalist ideology whose paramilitary stormtroopers systematically subverted and dismantled the republic's parliamentary democracy.

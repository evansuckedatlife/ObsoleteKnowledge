## summary

**George Herbert Walker Bush** (1924–2018), popularly known as **George H.W. Bush** or "Bush the Elder," served as the 41st President of the United States (1989–1993). His single term coincided with the end of the Cold War and witnessed the collapse of the Soviet Union, the Gulf War, and the beginning of economic recession. His presidency marked the transition from Cold War geopolitics to a unipolar American order.

## you gotta know

- Served as Ronald Reagan's Vice President for eight years (1981–1989), loyally supporting supply-side economics and a hard anti-communist foreign policy.
- His presidency saw the fall of the *Berlin Wall*, the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and the end of the Cold War—events largely beyond his control but that defined his era.
- Ordered Operation *Desert Storm* (1991), the Gulf War against Iraq after Saddam Hussein's invasion of Kuwait—a swift military victory that briefly boosted his approval ratings.
- Famously promised "no new taxes" during his 1988 campaign ("Read my lips"), then agreed to tax increases as part of a deficit-reduction deal—a political misstep that damaged his credibility.
- Ended his presidency with high approval but faced a stubborn recession that contributed to his defeat in 1992 by Bill Clinton and independent candidate Ross Perot.
- His son *George W. Bush* later became the 43rd President, making them the second father-son presidential pair in American history (after John and John Quincy Adams).
- Selected Indiana Senator *Dan Quayle* as running mate, a controversial choice that fueled jokes about his readiness for the presidency.

## summary

**Medieval Europe** refers to the historical period roughly spanning from the fall of the [[roman-empire]] in the 5th century to the emergence of the Renaissance in the 14th–15th centuries. Characterized by feudalism, the dominance of the [[christianity|Christian]] church, and decentralized political authority, this era saw the rise of kingdoms, the Crusades, and the development of chivalric culture. The period was marked by both cultural achievement and suffering, including the devastating [[black-death]] that reshaped European society.

## you gotta know

- Emerged from the collapse of [[roman-empire|Roman]] authority in Western Europe, with Germanic kingdoms fragmenting the continent into feudal territories.
- Witnessed the rise of the [[christianity|Christian]] church as the primary unifying force and greatest landowner, with the [[papal-authority|Pope]] wielding immense temporal power.
- Organized society through feudalism, a system of mutual obligations between lords and vassals that dominated social and economic relations.
- Launched the [[crusades]], military expeditions to the [[holy-land]] driven by religious zeal and economic ambition, fundamentally reshaping Mediterranean power dynamics.
- Endured the [[black-death]] in the 14th century, which killed roughly one-third of Europe's population and triggered massive social upheaval and labor shortages.
- Gradually developed the conditions for its own transformation through urbanization, trade, and intellectual revival that culminated in the [[renaissance]].
- Produced distinctive cultural achievements including Gothic architecture, illuminated manuscripts, choral music, and a rich oral tradition of folklore and legend.
- Witnessed the rise and consolidation of strong national kingdoms in [[france]], [[spain]], and [[england]], replacing the fragmented feudal order with centralized monarchies.
- Experienced periodic conflicts between temporal and spiritual authority, most notably between emperors and popes, which shaped the development of political thought and governance.

## connections

- [[crusades]] — a defining military and religious movement of the medieval period, reshaping Christendom and the [[muslim-world]].
- [[black-death]] — the catastrophic plague that fundamentally altered medieval European society and economy.
- [[reconquista]] — the centuries-long [[spain|Spanish]] effort to expel [[islam|Islamic]] rulers from the Iberian Peninsula.
- [[papal-authority]] — the temporal and spiritual power that provided coherence and structure to medieval Christendom.
- [[renaissance]] — the intellectual and cultural revival that emerged from and eventually transcended the medieval world.
- [[feudalism]] — the social and political system that organized medieval European society.

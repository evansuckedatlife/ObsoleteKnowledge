## summary

**Hindu nationalism**, widely articulated through the political philosophy of **Hindutva**, is an influential political and cultural ideology that conceptualizes the nation of *India* primarily through the lens of indigenous Hindu identity and civilization. Developing in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries in reaction to British colonial subjugation and communal political divisions, the movement asserts that true Indian national identity derives from shared ancestral heritage, sacred geography, and traditional culture. Today, it stands as one of the dominant forces in modern South Asian politics, guiding major parties and reshaping legal, social, and constitutional debates.

## you gotta know

- Synthesized into a modern political doctrine by *Vinayak Damodar Savarkar* in his influential 1923 treatise *Hindutva: Who is a Hindu?*, defining nationhood through common blood, culture, and sacred homeland.
- Institutionalized organizationally with the founding of the *Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh* in 1925 by physician *K. B. Hedgewar* as a volunteer paramilitary cultural organization.
- Clashed sharply with the secular civic nationalism espoused by *Jawaharlal Nehru* and the pluralist vision of the *Indian National Congress* during the twentieth century.
- Catapulted into widespread global attention through the agitation led by *L. K. Advani* to construct a temple at the birthplace of the deity *Rama* in *Ayodhya*, culminating in the 1992 demolition of the sixteenth-century *Babri Masjid*.
- Produced fringe militant offshoots, including extremist *Nathuram Godse*, who assassinated *Mohandas Gandhi* in 1948 over grievances regarding the partition of British India.
- Operates through an extensive coalition of affiliated civic, educational, and religious wings known collectively as the *Sangh Parivar*, which includes the *Vishwa Hindu Parishad*.
- Achieved unprecedented modern governance through the electoral triumphs of the *Bharatiya Janata Party*, leading coalitions under Prime Minister *Atal Bihari Vajpayee* and later *Narendra Modi*.

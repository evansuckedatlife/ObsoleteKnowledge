## summary

The **Congo Free State** (1885–1908) was the personal property of King *Leopold II* of *Belgium*, a massive African territory granted to him privately by the *Berlin Conference* (1884–1885). Under Leopold's rule, the territory became a byword for colonial exploitation and atrocity: forced labor in rubber extraction, massacre, mutilation, and disease killed an estimated 10 million Africans. International outcry over these horrors eventually forced Belgium to annex the territory in 1908 and assume direct control, though exploitation continued under Belgian administration until independence in 1960.

## you gotta know

- The Congo was not officially a colony but a personal fiefdom: Leopold II acquired it as private property through diplomatic maneuvering at the Berlin Conference, treating it as his personal estate rather than Belgian territory.
- Leopold's sole interest was profit: the Congo's rubber and ivory were extracted through a brutal system of forced labor; Africans were coerced, beaten, and killed; villages that failed to meet rubber quotas were razed; limbs were severed as punishment and proof of death.
- Rubber exploitation was catastrophically destructive: the demand for rubber created incentives for maximum extraction; the Congo-Kasai region's economy was shattered; disease (sleeping sickness) spread as populations were displaced and concentrated; violence became endemic.
- International journalists, missionaries, and activists documented the atrocities: *E. D. Morel* and *George Washington Williams* exposed the horrors; the *Congo Reform Association* publicized the crimes; photographs of mutilated Congolese victims shocked Europe.
- Under mounting international pressure, Belgium annexed the *Congo Free State* in 1908, assuming direct colonial control as the *Belgian Congo*; this did not end exploitation but subjected it to nominal metropolitan oversight.
- The *Congo Free State* exemplified late-nineteenth-century imperialism at its most predatory: African sovereignty erased, resources extracted for foreign profit, populations decimated, and governance reduced to coercion and violence.

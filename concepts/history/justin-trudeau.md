## summary

**Justin Trudeau** is a Canadian politician who became the 23rd prime minister of Canada in 2015, leading the *Liberal Party of Canada*. The eldest son of former prime minister [[pierre-elliott-trudeau]], he initially gained national attention for his charismatic campaign style, gender-balanced cabinet, and progressive policy agenda. His premiership has been shaped by domestic reforms such as cannabis legalisation and carbon pricing, as well as complex diplomatic negotiations with the [[united-states]] and domestic political controversies.

## you gotta know

- Swept to power in the 2015 federal election, ending nearly a decade of *Conservative Party* rule under *Stephen Harper* and leading the *Liberals* from third place in Parliament directly to a majority government.
- Appointed Canada's first federal gender-balanced cabinet in 2015, famously justifying the decision to reporters by remarking, "Because it's 2015."
- Championed landmark domestic reforms including the federal legalisation of recreational cannabis via the *Cannabis Act* in 2018 and the implementation of a national carbon-pricing framework through the *Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing Act*.
- Faced major political controversies, notably the *SNC-Lavalin affair* in 2019 concerning alleged political interference in a criminal prosecution that prompted the resignation of Attorney General *Jody Wilson-Raybould*, and the *WE Charity* controversy in 2020.
- Invoked the *Emergencies Act* in February 2022 for the first time since its 1988 passage to clear the *Freedom Convoy* protests that blockaded Ottawa and border crossings, an action legally derived from the older *War Measures Act* deployed by his father [[pierre-elliott-trudeau]].
- Navigated significant international turbulence, renegotiating the *North American Free Trade Agreement* (*NAFTA*) into the *United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement* (*USMCA*) during tense discussions with the administration of the [[united-states]].
- Governed via minority administrations after the 2019 and 2021 elections, sustained from 2022 to 2024 through a formal confidence-and-supply agreement with the *New Democratic Party* (*NDP*) led by *Jagmeet Singh*.

## connections

- [[pierre-elliott-trudeau]] — his father and iconic Canadian prime minister, making them the first father-and-son pair to hold the office.
- [[peoples-party-of-canada]] — right-wing populist party founded by former minister *Maxime Bernier*, which emerged as a vocal opponent of Trudeau's pandemic mandates.
- [[lester-b.-pearson]] — predecessor Liberal prime minister whose minority governments established Canada's modern welfare state and traditional multilateral foreign policy.
- [[united-states]] — southern neighbour and dominant trading partner with whom Trudeau renegotiated continental trade under *USMCA*.
- [[france]] — key international partner in the *Francophonie* and European diplomacy, reflecting shared bilingual and transatlantic ties.
- [[culture]] — prominent focus of his political messaging emphasizing multiculturalism, reconciliation with Indigenous peoples, and Canadian national identity.

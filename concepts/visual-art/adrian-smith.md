## summary

**Adrian Smith**, formally known as **Adrian D. Smith**, is an eminent American architect widely celebrated for designing some of the most technologically ambitious and visually striking supertall skyscrapers in modern history. After spending three prolific decades as a leading design partner at the Chicago office of Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM), he co-founded Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill Architecture, spearheading monumental developments that reshaped urban skylines across the *[[middle-east]]*, Asia, and the *[[united-states]]*. Smith's architectural legacy is defined by his ability to harmonize high-performance engineering, regional cultural symbolism, and dramatic vertical *[[abstraction]]*, cementing his status as the foremost skyscraper architect of the 21st century.

## you gotta know

- Architect of the world's tallest building: Smith designed the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, which opened in 2010 at 828 meters (2,717 feet), breaking all previous records for freestanding architectural height.
- Floral inspiration and wind engineering: The Burj Khalifa's spiraling, stepped Y-shaped floor plan was inspired by the desert flower *Hymenocallis* and engineered with progressive setbacks to confuse and dissipate turbulent high-altitude wind vortexes.
- Pagoda-inspired design of the Jin Mao Tower: Completed in 1999 in Shanghai, China, this 88-story tower elegantly incorporates traditional Chinese pagoda proportions and the auspicious number eight into an advanced steel-and-glass structural system.
- Mastermind behind the Jeddah Tower: Designed through AS+GG in Saudi Arabia, this ongoing megatall project was conceived to surpass the Burj Khalifa as the first human-built structure to exceed one kilometer (3,281 feet) in height.
- Leadership at Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM): As a design partner in Chicago from 1980 to 2006, he contributed extensively to global civic architecture, corporate headquarters, and high-density urban planning.
- Pioneering Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill Architecture: Established in Chicago in 2006 with Gordon Gill and Robert Forest, the firm specializes in energy-positive, sustainable skyscrapers and holistic zero-carbon urban master plans.
- Record-breaking residential architecture: Smith designed Central Park Tower on Billionaires' Row in *[[new-york-city]]*, which rises 1,550 feet to stand as the tallest residential building by roof height in the world.

## connections

- [[middle-east]] — region that provided the patronage and urban stage for his most ambitious supertall towers, including the Burj Khalifa.
- [[united-states]] — his homeland and the birthplace of the Chicago skyscraper tradition that formed his foundational practice.
- [[modernism]] — architectural tradition of steel, glass, and structural expressionism that his skyscrapers inherit and technologically advance.
- [[new-york-city]] — major global metropolis where his firm designed the monumental Central Park Tower.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical catalyst that generated the elevator, structural steel, and curtain-wall technologies central to his towers.
- [[abstraction]] — aesthetic principle reflected in the clean geometric tapering, aerodynamic sculpting, and non-figurative lines of his supertall structures.

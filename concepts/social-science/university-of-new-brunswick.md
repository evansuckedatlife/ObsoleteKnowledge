## summary

The **University of New Brunswick** (widely designated **UNB**) is a historic public university maintaining campuses in Fredericton and Saint John, New Brunswick. Founded in 1785 by United Empire Loyalists who fled the newly formed United States, it stands as the oldest English-language university in Canada and among the earliest public institutions of higher learning in North America. Known for establishing Canada's first engineering school, sustaining the country's oldest continuous literary journal, and cultivating prominent public figures, UNB remains a foundational anchor of Maritime intellectual and cultural life.

## you gotta know

- Established in 1785 as the *Academy of Arts and Sciences* by seven United Empire Loyalists who petitioned the newly minted colonial governor *Thomas Carleton*.
- Incorporated by provincial charter in 1800 as the **College of New Brunswick** and subsequently granted a royal charter by King William IV in 1828 under the title *King's College*, before adopting its current provincial designation in 1859.
- Home to the historic *Old Arts Building* (Sir Howard Douglas Hall) on the Fredericton campus, erected between 1826 and 1827, which remains the oldest operational university building in Canada.
- Initiated Canada's first university-level engineering curriculum in 1854 when civil engineer *Styleman Chesterton* began delivering specialized lectures in surveying and mechanics.
- Fostered the historic literary community that founded *The Fiddlehead* in 1945 under poet and historian *Alfred Bailey*, which continues to operate as Canada's longest-running literary periodical.
- Supported extensively by the Canadian-British media proprietor *Max Aitken, Lord Beaverbrook*, who served as UNB's long-time chancellor and gifted numerous residences, library collections, and the provincial law school.
- Operates advanced contemporary research centers in geodesic science and software, including the pioneering Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity located on the Fredericton campus.

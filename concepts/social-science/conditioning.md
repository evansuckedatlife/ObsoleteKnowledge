## summary

**Conditioning** is the foundational behavioural learning process through which environmental stimuli, actions, and consequences become systematically associated to modify an organism's responses over time. Formally established in the early twentieth century through laboratory research in the [[soviet-union]] and the [[united-states]], it encompasses two primary paradigms: **classical conditioning**, which pairs involuntary physiological reflexes with previously neutral environmental antecedents, and **operant conditioning**, which shapes voluntary behaviour through reinforcement and punishment. As the core mechanism of [[behaviorism]], conditioning provided objective, quantifiable principles that transformed psychology, establishing enduring empirical laws vital to understanding learning, habit formation, phobias, and [[developmental-psychology]].

## you gotta know

- *Ivan Pavlov* established the principles of classical conditioning while studying canine digestion, demonstrating that repeatedly pairing a neutral stimulus—such as a metronome or buzzer—with meat powder caused dogs to salivate to the neutral sound alone.
- *B. F. Skinner* systematized operant conditioning using automated chambers, proving that voluntary emitted actions are shaped and maintained by environmental consequences rather than elicited mechanically by preceding stimuli.
- *John B. Watson* and *Rosalie Rayner* demonstrated conditioned fear in the controversial 1920 *Little Albert* experiment, pairing a white rat with the clang of a steel bar until the infant's phobia generalized to other furry objects.
- *Edward Thorndike* formulated the *Law of Effect* with puzzle box experiments on cats, showing that responses followed by satisfying consequences are stamped in and become far more likely to recur.
- Operant reinforcement schedules govern response rates; while continuous reinforcement enables rapid acquisition, intermittent schedules—especially variable-ratio schedules seen in gambling—produce the highest response rates and greatest resistance to extinction.
- *Martin Seligman* and *Steven Maier* discovered learned helplessness by subjecting dogs to inescapable electric shocks; when later placed in shuttle boxes where escape was possible, the conditioned animals passively whimpered, providing a behavioral model for clinical [[depression]].
- *John Garcia* identified conditioned taste aversion, or the *Garcia effect*, showing that rats develop strong aversions to novel flavors paired with radiation-induced nausea across multi-hour delays, revealing evolutionary biological preparedness that contradicted strict temporal contiguity.
- *Robert Rescorla* and *Allan Wagner* mathematically modeled classical conditioning via prediction error, formalizing associative strength to explain *Leon Kamin*'s blocking effect, in which an established conditioned stimulus prevents learning to an added redundant cue.

## connections

- [[behaviorism]] — the dominant twentieth-century school of psychology that positioned conditioned reflexes and reinforcement as the primary mechanisms governing behavior.
- [[b-f-skinner]] — the Harvard psychologist who pioneered operant conditioning, designed Skinner boxes, and developed radical behaviorist philosophy.
- [[cognitive-science]] — the interdisciplinary field that challenged associationist conditioning paradigms by demonstrating the role of internal mental representations and expectations.
- [[developmental-psychology]] — the discipline examining how conditioned habits, parental reinforcement schedules, and social environments shape childhood behavioural growth.
- [[psychoanalysis]] — the competing clinical tradition founded by *[[sigmund-freud]]* that emphasized dynamic unconscious conflict over observable conditioned reflexes.
- [[soviet-union]] — the geopolitical home of *Pavlov*'s imperial and Soviet reflexology laboratories, which framed conditioning as materialist physiology.
- [[united-states]] — the primary academic center where comparative animal laboratories and radical behaviorist conditioning programs flourished.

## summary

The **Royal Bank of Canada** (commonly known as RBC) is a Canadian multinational financial services corporation and the largest commercial bank in Canada by both assets and market capitalization. Founded in 1864 in Halifax, Nova Scotia, as the Merchants' Bank of Halifax, it expanded alongside Canadian confederation and transatlantic trade networks across the [[british-empire]]. In financial history and comparative economics, it exemplifies the resilience of Canada's nationwide branch-banking system, standing as a permanently designated Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB).

## you gotta know

- Established in 1864 by a syndicate of Nova Scotian maritime merchants as the *Merchants' Bank of Halifax* to finance regional shipping, timber, and mercantile operations.
- Secured a federal charter in 1869 following Canadian Confederation and adopted its current corporate title in 1901 to reflect its transcontinental domestic expansion and international ambitions.
- Shifted its corporate headquarters to Montreal in 1907, commissioning the landmark modernist complex *Place Ville Marie* in 1962, which served as a major architectural symbol of Canadian commerce before operations shifted toward Toronto.
- Provided essential commercial underwriting and credit for the transcontinental railways, mineral extraction, and western agricultural expansion of the late North American [[industrial-revolution]].
- Pioneered early overseas branch banking throughout the Caribbean and Latin America in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, becoming a dominant commercial lender in Cuba, Puerto Rico, and Trinidad before political revolutions and nationalizations.
- Maintained stability during severe economic disruptions, famously avoiding insolvency during the Great Depression and navigating the 2008 global financial crisis without direct government bailouts.
- Expanded its cross-border retail and private banking footprint in the [[united-states]], highlighted by its high-profile acquisition of Los Angeles-based *City National Bank*.

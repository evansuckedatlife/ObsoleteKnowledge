## summary

**Toronto Metropolitan University** (commonly abbreviated **TMU**, and historically known as **Ryerson University**) is a major public research university located in the urban core of Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1948 as the **Ryerson Institute of Technology**, the school developed from a vocational post-war training institute into an urban comprehensive university distinguished for its media production, engineering, and experiential entrepreneurship. In 2022, the institution officially changed its name to distance its identity from *Egerton Ryerson*, a key architect of the Canadian Indian residential school system, marking a prominent modern milestone in Canadian higher education reconciliation.

## you gotta know

- Officially renamed from *Ryerson University* to *Toronto Metropolitan University* in April 2022 following the binding recommendations of the university's Standing Strong Task Force.
- Originally named in honor of *Egerton Ryerson*, an influential 19th-century educator and Methodist minister whose statue on campus was pulled down by demonstrators in June 2021 due to his role in promoting [[colonialism]] through residential schooling.
- Anchored directly in downtown Toronto along Yonge Street, featuring the eight-story Student Learning Centre designed by the architecture firm *Snøhetta*, which opens toward the bustling commercial hub of Yonge-Dundas Square.
- Adapted the historic *Maple Leaf Gardens* arena—the legendary home of the Toronto Maple Leafs hockey franchise—into the *Mattamy Athletic Centre*, preserving the heritage roof structure over contemporary university athletic courts and an ice rink.
- Pioneered a globally recognized model of university-based venture incubation through the *DMZ* (originally the Digital Media Zone), which supports high-growth tech startups.
- Houses the *Ted Rogers School of Management*, Canada's largest undergraduate business school, named after broadcasting mogul *Ted Rogers* and located minutes from the financial district of the [[toronto-stock-exchange]].
- Maintains premier programs in journalism, film, and media production through The Creative School, heavily influencing Canadian national broadcasting and contemporary popular [[culture]].

## connections

- [[university-of-toronto]] — neighboring urban research institution located mere blocks west with which it shares cross-institutional transit corridors and downtown civic space.
- [[toronto-stock-exchange]] — prominent financial market located south along Bay Street where alumni of the Ted Rogers School of Management frequently establish careers.
- [[colonialism]] — historical structure of dominion and assimilation that characterized the residential school proposals authored by the university's original namesake.
- [[native-americans]] — Indigenous communities whose political mobilization and truth-seeking efforts forced the reassessment of the institution's public symbols.
- [[world-war-ii]] — military conflict whose conclusion prompted the 1948 establishment of the institute to provide practical technical education for returning Canadian soldiers.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — epoch of technological acceleration that established the vocational and polytechnic educational traditions upon which the school was initially founded.
- [[culture]] — dynamic social domain heavily mediated by TMU's documentary, communications, and media design faculties across Canada.

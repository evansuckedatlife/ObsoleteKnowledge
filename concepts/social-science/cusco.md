## summary

**Cusco** was the capital and spiritual heart of the Inca Empire, situated high in the Peruvian Andes. Founded according to legend by *Manco Capac*, it served as the administrative and ceremonial center from which the Incas governed their vast domain. The city's strategic location, sophisticated urban planning, and role as the seat of the Sapa Inca made it the undisputed center of one of pre-Columbian America's greatest civilizations.

## you gotta know

- Located in the southern highlands of Peru at an elevation of over 11,000 feet, making it one of the highest cities in the world.
- Served as the capital of the Inca Empire from its founding until the Spanish conquest in the early 16th century.
- The name *Cusco* (or *Cuzco*) means "navel of the world" in Quechua, reflecting its central importance to Incan cosmology.
- Built on a site that the Incas believed was sacred, with origin myths connecting it to the wanderings of *Manco Capac* and *Mama Ocllo*.
- Featured sophisticated urban planning organized in a grid pattern, with *Manco Capac's* palace at the center.
- The Temple of the Sun (*Qorikancha*) was Cusco's most important religious structure, housing gold-plated walls and sacred objects.
- Housed the royal court and served as the base for the Sapa Inca's imperial administration across the vast empire.
- Largely destroyed by Spanish conquistadors under *Pizarro* but incorporated into the Spanish colonial city that still stands today.
- Connected to the wider empire through *Qhapaq Ñan*, the vast network of roads radiating from Cusco to all four quarters of Tawantinsuyu.
- Served as both a ceremonial center where imperial rituals occurred and a practical administrative hub for collecting tributes and managing provinces.
- Archaeological evidence shows Cusco housed elite craft workers, priests, military officials, and merchants, making it a cosmopolitan center for an empire.

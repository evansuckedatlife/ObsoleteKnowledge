## summary

**18th-century Britain**, often termed **Georgian Britain**, encompassed the transformative era between the Act of Union in 1707 and the close of the Napoleonic conflicts. The period witnessed the birth of modern parliamentary governance under the Hanoverian monarchs, the emergence of the Scottish *Enlightenment*, and the initial domestic mechanization that ignited the *Industrial Revolution*. Internationally, imperial expansion across South Asia and the Atlantic positioned the British state as a premier maritime empire, reshaping global commerce while provoking profound crises like the American War of Independence.

## you gotta know

- Defined politically by the Hanoverian succession in 1714, inaugurating the reigns of *George I*, *George II*, and *George III*, which established a Whig oligarchy and the office of the Prime Minister under *Robert Walpole*.
- Hosted the intellectual flowering of the Scottish *Enlightenment*, producing groundbreaking philosophy from *David Hume* and classical political economy from *Adam Smith*, whose 1776 masterwork *The Wealth of Nations* analyzed market specialization and the division of labour.
- Sparked the early *Industrial Revolution* through pivotal inventions such as *James Watt*'s refined steam engine, *James Hargreaves*'s spinning jenny, and the widespread development of turnpike and canal networks across *England* and Scotland.
- Underwent severe imperial upheaval through the loss of the Thirteen Colonies in the *American Revolution*, followed by an aggressive pivot toward imperial consolidation in the *Middle East* and the Indian subcontinent led by the East India Company.
- Fostered the institutionalization of colonial scholarship, notably exemplified by *Sir William Jones*, who served as a Supreme Court judge in Calcutta, founded the Asiatic Society of Bengal, and posited the common ancestry of the Indo-European tongues, establishing comparative methodologies later applied to categorize *agglutinative languages*.
- Developed the fiscal-military state, utilizing the Bank of England and a national public debt system to finance global warfare against *France* across the Seven Years' War and the War of the Spanish Succession.
- Witnessed intense domestic debates over parliamentary reform, radicalism sparked by the *French Revolution*, the abolitionist activism of *William Wilberforce* and *Olaudah Equiano*, and early enclosures that displaced rural laborers into urban industrial work.

## summary

The **Bank of Canada** is the central bank of Canada, responsible for formulating national monetary policy, issuing bank notes, and maintaining domestic financial stability. Founded in 1934 during the depths of the Great Depression under Prime Minister *R. B. Bennett*, it initially operated as a privately owned corporation before being nationalized as a Crown corporation in 1938. As a key institutional actor alongside bodies like the [[canadian-radio-broadcasting-commission]], it exemplifies the mid-twentieth-century expansion of state apparatus designed to secure economic and cultural autonomy from the [[british-empire]] and the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Established by the *Bank of Canada Act* of 1934 following the recommendations of the 1933 *Macmillan Commission*, chaired by British jurist *Lord Macmillan*.
- Nationalized in 1938 under the Liberal government of Prime Minister *William Lyon Mackenzie King*, turning it into a wholly state-owned Crown corporation.
- Its first governor was *Graham Towers*, who led the institution for two decades through the Great Depression, [[world-war-ii]], and post-war reconstruction.
- The 1961 *Coyne Affair*—a public conflict between Governor *James Coyne* and Prime Minister *John Diefenbaker* over restrictive monetary versus expansionary fiscal policy—culminated in Coyne's resignation and codified the bank's operational independence.
- In 1991, under Governor *John Crow*, the bank pioneered formal inflation targeting alongside Finance Minister *Michael Wilson*, adopting a 1-to-3 percent annual inflation control target range centered at 2 percent.
- Headquartered in Ottawa, Ontario, in a modernist building complex designed by architect *Arthur Erickson* that surrounds the original 1930s neoclassical structure.
- Serves as the fiscal agent for the federal government, managing the national public debt and administering the *Exchange Fund Account* for foreign exchange reserves.

## connections

- [[supply-and-demand]] — the core market forces influenced by the bank's overnight policy interest rate and monetary management.
- [[john-maynard-keynes]] — economic theorist whose macro-level stabilization ideas heavily influenced the bank's counter-cyclical mandate.
- [[canadian-radio-broadcasting-commission]] — fellow depression-era regulatory body created by *R. B. Bennett* to assert federal oversight.
- [[authority]] — the institutional power and monetary sovereignty exercised by central banks over national currency.
- [[british-empire]] — the imperial financial and legal sphere from which Canada deliberately separated its sovereign fiscal machinery.
- [[world-war-ii]] — the major conflict during which the bank managed wartime financing, credit controls, and Victory Loan drives.

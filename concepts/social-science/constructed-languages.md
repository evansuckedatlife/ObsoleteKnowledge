## summary

**Constructed languages**, commonly known as conlangs, are linguistic systems whose phonology, vocabulary, and grammar are deliberately engineered by an individual or committee rather than emerging organically through cultural evolution. Originating during the philosophical debates of the seventeenth-century *Enlightenment* before flourishing in modern internationalist and artistic movements, conlangs serve varied intellectual purposes ranging from scientific modeling and universal communication to literary worldbuilding. Their systematic design provides cognitive and sociological insights into grammatical limits, human utopian aspirations, and the mechanics of language acquisition.

## you gotta know

- Broadly divided into three main functional categories: engineered or philosophical languages (loglangs), international auxiliary languages (auxlangs), and artistic or fictional languages (artlangs).
- Early modern predecessors include the philosophical languages of *John Wilkins* and *Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz*, which attempted to create universal taxonomic classifications mirroring pure human thought.
- *Esperanto*, devised in 1887 by Polish ophthalmologist *L. L. Zamenhof* under the pseudonym *Doktoro Esperanto*, became the most successful international auxiliary language, amassing thousands of native speakers and an active global culture.
- Predated by *Volapük*, an international language created in 1879 by German Catholic priest *Johann Martin Schleyer*, which collapsed rapidly due to dialect factionalism and complex agglutinative morphology.
- Fictional worldbuilding revolutionized artlangs through the work of Oxford philologist *J. R. R. Tolkien*, who constructed elaborate historical language families including *Quenya* and *Sindarin* before composing his high fantasy fiction.
- Modern cinematic linguistics has created popular artlangs engineered by linguists like *Marc Okrand* (Klingon for *Star Trek*) and *David J. Peterson* (Dothraki and High Valyrian for *Game of Thrones*).
- Distinguished between *a priori* languages, whose lexicon and syntax are invented entirely from scratch without existing natural source models, and *a posteriori* languages, which derive roots from natural families such as Romance or Germanic tongues.
- Engineered languages such as *Loglan* and its derivative *Lojban* were specifically built to test the *Sapir-Whorf hypothesis* by removing syntactic ambiguity and examining whether logical grammar alters human cognition.

## connections

- [[linguistics]] — the scientific field whose structural categories, typologies, and phonetics provide the toolkit for language engineering.
- [[enlightenment]] — the intellectual epoch that birthed early philosophical attempts to construct perfect, mathematically precise rational languages.
- [[sociolinguistics]] — the subfield that analyzes community formation, political ideology, and speaker identity among speech groups like Esperantists.
- [[creoles]] — natural languages created unconsciously through contact communities, often contrasted with deliberately planned conlangs.
- [[conversational-implicature]] — pragmatic phenomena that logical constructed languages attempt to formalize or eradicate through explicit syntactic markers.
- [[soviet-union]] — state that initially supported Esperanto as the language of world proletarian revolution before brutally suppressing its movement during the purges.

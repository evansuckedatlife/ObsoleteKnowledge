## summary

**Turkey**, located at the crossroads of Europe and Asia in the region historically called *Anatolia*, has been a crucial site of human civilization for millennia. From the emergence of agriculture and early settlements to the rise of the Ottoman Empire, the territory has generated foundational developments in social, political, and technological history. Modern Turkey, founded in 1923, occupies a geopolitically strategic position between Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, bridging diverse cultural and religious traditions.

## you gotta know

- Anatolia is one of the world's primary centers of agricultural domestication, where wheat and barley cultivation emerged around 10,000 BCE, spurring the Neolithic Revolution.
- *Göbekli Tepe*, a 9,500-year-old ceremonial temple site in southeastern Turkey, is among the world's oldest known monumental structures, predating agriculture and urbanism.
- *Çatalhöyük*, a sprawling early agricultural settlement (7500-5700 BCE) in central Anatolia, is one of the world's oldest cities and reveals complex early urban social organization.
- The region hosted successive civilizations: Hittites, Greeks, Persians, Hellenistic kingdoms, Romans, Byzantines, and finally the *Ottoman Empire*, which dominated for over 600 years.
- The Ottoman Empire, centered in Anatolia, became one of history's longest-lasting and most culturally influential empires, governing three continents at its peak.
- Modern Turkey was founded by *Mustafa Kemal Atatürk* in 1923 from Ottoman remnants, undergoing rapid modernization and secularization while remaining culturally Islamic and geopolitically contested.

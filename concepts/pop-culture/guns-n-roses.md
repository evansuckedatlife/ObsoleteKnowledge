## summary

**Guns N' Roses** is an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles, California, in 1985. Anchored by frontman *Axl Rose* and lead guitarist *Slash*, the group revitalized mainstream rock with their raw, blues-infused blend of heavy metal and punk attitude. Their 1987 debut, *Appetite for Destruction*, remains the best-selling debut album of all time in the United States, cementing their place as rock legends.

## you gotta know

- Formed by the merger of members from *Hollywood Rose* and *L.A. Guns*, establishing the classic lineup of *Axl Rose*, *Slash*, *Izzy Stradlin*, *Duff McKagan*, and *Steven Adler*.
- Released *Appetite for Destruction* in 1987, which sold over 30 million copies worldwide and featured evergreen anthems like *Welcome to the Jungle*, *Paradise City*, and *Sweet Child o' Mine*.
- *Sweet Child o' Mine* became the band's sole number-one single on the US Billboard Hot 100, driven by *Slash*'s iconic opening guitar riff.
- Simultaneously released two massive, ambitious studio albums in 1991: *Use Your Illusion I* and *Use Your Illusion II*, debuting at numbers two and one on the Billboard 200.
- Produced elaborate cinematic epics and music videos, most famously for the multi-movement rock ballad *November Rain*.
- Underwent a decade-long saga of lineup changes and production delays before *Axl Rose* released the long-awaited album *Chinese Democracy* in 2008.

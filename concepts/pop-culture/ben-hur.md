## summary

**Ben-Hur** is a legendary historical epic originating in the 1880 novel *Ben-Hur: A Tale of the Christ* by *Lew Wallace* and immortalized by the 1959 Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer film directed by *William Wyler*. Set in the first-century Roman province of Judea, the story follows the Jewish prince *Judah Ben-Hur*, who is betrayed into slavery by his Roman friend *Messala* before seeking retribution and encountering the ministry of *Jesus of Nazareth*. The 1959 cinematic adaptation won a record-breaking eleven Academy Awards and remains a towering landmark of Hollywood spectacle.

## you gotta know

- Written by Union general *Lew Wallace* during the Reconstruction era, the original 1880 novel became one of the bestselling American literary works of the nineteenth century.
- The definitive 1959 film stars *Charlton Heston* as *Judah Ben-Hur*, with *Stephen Boyd* portraying his childhood friend turned ruthless Roman tribune, *Messala*.
- The narrative revolves around the famous nine-minute chariot race sequence at the Circus of Antioch, filmed without computer effects and considered one of cinema's greatest action set pieces.
- Depicts Judah's miraculous survival as a galley slave, earning the favor and eventual adoption of Roman consul *Quintus Arrius* after saving his life during a naval battle.
- Interweaves Judah's fictional pursuit of vengeance with key moments in the life of Christ, including Christ offering him water in Nazareth and the ultimate crucifixion on Golgotha.
- Preceded by a celebrated 1925 silent film adaptation starring *Ramon Novarro*, which established the technical template for large-scale arena racing on screen.
- Won eleven Oscars at the 32nd Academy Awards, establishing a record later tied only by *Titanic* in 1997 and *The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* in 2003.

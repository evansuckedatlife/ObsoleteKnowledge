## summary

**Grimes** is the artistic moniker of Canadian musician, singer, songwriter, and visual artist *Claire Boucher*. Emerging from the experimental Montreal DIY music scene in the early 2010s, she achieved critical breakthrough with her third studio album *Visions* in 2012. Her work synthesizes elements of dream pop, synth-pop, electronic dance music, science fiction, and industrial aesthetics into an idiosyncratic avant-garde pop catalog.

## you gotta know

- Rose to international prominence with the 2012 release of *Visions*, which was recorded entirely on Apple's *GarageBand* during a period of self-imposed isolation and spawned the singles *Genesis* and *Oblivion*.
- Released the critically lauded 2015 LP *Art Angels*, an ambitious self-engineered album highlighting pop songcraft, dancefloor energy, and fierce feminist independence against industry producer norms.
- Explored dystopian transhumanist and climate catastrophe concepts on her 2020 concept album *Miss Anthropocene*, personifying an anthropomorphic goddess of climate change.
- Voiced the pop star character *Lizzy Wizzy* and contributed music to the futuristic soundtrack of the high-profile open-world role-playing game *Cyberpunk 2077*.
- Maintains a distinct visual arts practice, illustrating her own album covers, creating digital animations, and directing several conceptual music videos.
- Drew immense mainstream media scrutiny due to her high-profile romantic relationship with tech entrepreneur *Elon Musk*, with whom she shares three children bearing unconventional names.
- Embraced artificial intelligence, generative software, and decentralized technology in musical production, notably launching open-source voice software allowing musicians to legally utilize her vocal likeness.

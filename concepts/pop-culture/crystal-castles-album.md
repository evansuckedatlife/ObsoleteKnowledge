## summary

**Crystal Castles (album)** is the groundbreaking self-titled debut studio album by Canadian electronic duo *Crystal Castles*, released internationally in March 2008 by *Last Gang Records* and *Lies Records*. Defined by its raw, chaotic collision of glitched-out 8-bit synthesizer patches, pulverizing drum machines, and visceral vocals, the record stood as a defiant counterpoint to the polished dance music of its era. It captured the hedonistic spirit of late-2000s indie sleaze, garnered widespread critical adulation, and established the band as leading pioneers of underground electropunk and witch house music.

## you gotta know

- Features the signature track "Alice Practice", a volatile piece of lo-fi digital noise built from a covertly recorded microphone check by singer *Alice Glass*.
- Displays provocative album cover art showcasing an altered photograph of pop superstar *Madonna* sporting a severe black eye, created by artist *Trevor Brown* and encapsulating the record's anti-establishment attitude.
- Built largely around vintage home console and arcade hardware, utilizing hacked synthesizer cartridges and sound chips from old *Atari* and *Nintendo* systems to produce distinctive abrasive chiptune textures.
- Includes the celebrated breakthrough single "Crimewave", a collaborative sonic transformation and reworking of an original track by the Los Angeles noise-rock outfit *HEALTH*.
- The hypnotic dance track "Untrust Us" heavily loops a pitch-shifted vocal sample of the track "Dead Womb" by Canadian dance-punk duo *Death from Above 1979*, turning it into an eerie, danceable electronic mantra.
- Sparked heated controversies across online chip-music forums when listeners recognized uncredited melodic samples taken directly from tracking songs by artists *Lo-bat* and *Covox*.
- Gained immense critical acclaim from influential publications such as *Pitchfork* and *NME*, with the latter naming it one of the top one hundred greatest albums of the 2000s decade.
- Proved foundational to the evolution of contemporary underground electronic music, acting as a crucial stylistic bridge connecting bloghouse, noise rock, hyperpop, and witch house.

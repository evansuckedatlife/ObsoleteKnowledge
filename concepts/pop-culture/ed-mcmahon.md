## summary

**Ed McMahon** was an iconic American television announcer, host, and sidekick whose thirty-year tenure on NBC's *The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson* set the enduring standard for late-night sidekicks. Renowned for his booming voice, affable laugh, and effortless comedic chemistry with *Johnny Carson*, McMahon served as the host's indispensable sounding board and master of ceremonies from 1962 until 1992. Beyond late-night television, he established himself as a versatile broadcaster, hosting the influential talent competition *Star Search* and co-hosting *TV's Bloopers & Practical Jokes*.

## you gotta know

- Introduced host *Johnny Carson* for three decades on *The Tonight Show* with the nightly drawn-out proclamation, "Heeeeere's Johnny!", which became one of the most famous catchphrases in broadcasting history.
- Served as the archetypal late-night television second banana, anchoring the couch with booming laughter, impeccable timing, and reliable comic support during Carson's opening monologues.
- First partnered with *Johnny Carson* on television in 1957 as the announcer on the daytime television game show *Who Do You Trust?* before moving with Carson to *The Tonight Show* in 1962.
- Hosted the nationally syndicated amateur talent competition *Star Search* from 1983 to 1995, helping launch the careers of future stars including *Britney Spears*, *Justin Timberlake*, *Beyoncé*, and *Dave Chappelle*.
- Served as a decorated United States Marine Corps naval aviator during *World War II* and was later recalled to active duty as an artillery spotter pilot during the Korean War, retiring with the rank of colonel.
- Partnered with *Dick Clark* to co-host the popular NBC entertainment reality series *TV's Bloopers & Practical Jokes* throughout the 1980s.
- Became widely associated in the public imagination with the *Publishers Clearing House* sweepstakes, though he was actually the longtime celebrity spokesperson for their direct rival, *American Publishers Mall*.

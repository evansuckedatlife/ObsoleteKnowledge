## summary

**Orphan Black** is an acclaimed Canadian science fiction thriller television series created by screenwriter *Graeme Manson* and director *John Fawcett*, originally broadcast from 2013 to 2017 on *Space* in Canada and *BBC America* internationally. The narrative follows *Sarah Manning*, a streetwise English con artist who witnesses the suicide of a woman identical to herself and gradually uncovers that she is one of dozens of genetically identical human clones created by a shadowy scientific movement. Renowned for its gripping blend of corporate espionage, biological ethics, and dark humor, the series achieved widespread critical acclaim for star *Tatiana Maslany*, whose tour-de-force performance portraying more than a dozen distinct clone characters earned her an *Emmy Award* and established the show as a landmark achievement in modern serialized television.

## you gotta know

- Starred *Tatiana Maslany*, who portrayed more than ten distinct clones across five seasons—including punk hustler *Sarah Manning*, neurotic suburban soccer mom *Alison Hendrix*, brilliant dreadlocked geneticist *Cosima Niehaus*, manic Ukrainian assassin *Helena*, and corporate executive *Rachel Duncan*.
- Propelled *Maslany* to widespread industry acclaim, culminating in an *Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series* in 2016 after years of passionate advocacy from critics and the show's dedicated fan community, dubbed the *Clone Club*.
- Pioneered groundbreaking visual effects techniques utilizing the *Technodolly*, a specialized motion-control camera system that permitted repeatable [[camera-movement]] across multiple takes so Maslany could physically interact with herself in continuous shots.
- Explored profound scientific and ethical questions surrounding human cloning, genetic patenting, eugenics, and reproductive autonomy through rival shadowy organizations known as the *Dyad Institute*, *Neolution*, and the military-run *Project Castor*.
- Addressed philosophical themes of identity and personal agency through literary allusions, naming every episode of each season after works by *Charles Darwin*, *Francis Bacon*, *William Blake*, *George William Algernon Ellis*, and *Donna Haraway*.
- Filmed entirely in and around *Toronto*, *Ontario*, serving as a signature flagship for Canadian television production alongside national cultural institutions like the [[osheaga-festival]].
- Expanded into a broader media franchise, including graphic novels, audio dramas voiced by Maslany, and the 2023 spin-off series *Orphan Black: Echoes* starring *Krysten Ritter*.

## summary

**Ariana Grande**, born **Ariana Grande-Butera**, is an American singer, songwriter, and actress recognized as one of the most commercially successful and influential pop vocalists of the twenty-first century. Renowned for her expansive four-octave soprano vocal range, melismatic agility, and mastery of the upper whistle register, she began her career on Broadway and television before transitioning into an international music superstar who seamlessly blended classic R&B influences with contemporary trap-pop production. In quiz-bowl circles and popular culture, she is celebrated for record-breaking studio albums like *Thank U, Next* and *Sweetener*, high-profile musical tributes to legendary divas, and starring as *Glinda* in the cinematic adaptation of *Wicked*.

## you gotta know

- Rose to mainstream adolescent prominence starring as the eccentric aspiring performer *Cat Valentine* on the *Nickelodeon* television sitcom *Victorious* and its crossover series *Sam & Cat*, after launching her professional stage career on Broadway in the musical *13*.
- Earned consistent critical comparisons to vocal icons such as *Mariah Carey* and *Whitney Houston* due to her soaring vocal acrobatics, light-lyric soprano flexibility, and effortless command over the whistle tone.
- Delivered a widely praised, emotionally charged performance of *(You Make Me Feel Like) A Natural Woman* at the televised 2018 funeral service of soul legend [[aretha-franklin]] in Detroit.
- Organized and headlined the historic *One Love Manchester* benefit concert in June 2017 to raise humanitarian relief funds for families following a deadly terrorist bombing outside her arena concert in England.
- Became the first solo artist in the history of the *Billboard* Hot 100 to occupy the entire top three positions concurrently in 2019, claiming the summit with *7 Rings*, *Break Up with Your Girlfriend, I'm Bored*, and *Thank U, Next*.
- Released multiple chart-topping, critically acclaimed studio albums including *Yours Truly*, *Dangerous Woman*, *Positions*, and *Eternal Sunshine*, the latter exploring concepts of heartbreak through references to the classic film *Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind*.
- Collaborated with premier European fashion houses including [[armani]] and *Givenchy*, establishing her signature high ponytail, oversized streetwear hoodies, and thigh-high boots as enduring fashion staples of the 2010s.
- Portrayed *Glinda the Good* alongside *Cynthia Erivo* as *Elphaba* in the major motion-picture adaptation of the musical *Wicked*, returning directly to her foundational musical theater background.

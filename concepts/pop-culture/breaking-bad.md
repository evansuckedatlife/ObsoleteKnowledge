## summary

**Breaking Bad** is an acclaimed American neo-Western crime drama television series created by *Vince Gilligan* that aired on AMC from 2008 to 2013. Set and filmed in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the narrative follows *Walter White*, an underpaid and disheartened high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with terminal lung cancer who turns to manufacturing methamphetamine alongside former student *Jesse Pinkman*. Widely celebrated as one of the greatest television dramas in history, the series examines the corrupting influence of pride, moral rationalization, and the psychological descent of an ordinary man into a ruthless criminal mastermind.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Vince Gilligan* with the explicit dramatic goal of taking a protagonist and transforming him into an antagonist, famously described as turning "Mr. Chips into Scarface."
- Stars *Bryan Cranston* as *Walter White* (who adopts the street pseudonym *Heisenberg*) and *Aaron Paul* as his troubled partner *Jesse Pinkman*.
- Co-stars *Anna Gunn* as Walter's wife Skyler, *RJ Mitte* as Walter Jr., *Dean Norris* as DEA agent brother-in-law Hank Schrader, *Betsy Brandt* as Marie, and *Bob Odenkirk* as criminal lawyer Saul Goodman.
- Notable for introducing formidable antagonists and cartel kingpins, most prominently *Giancarlo Esposito* as fast-food entrepreneur *Gustavo Fring* and *Jonathan Banks* as fixer *Mike Ehrmantraut*.
- Acclaimed for its distinctive cinematic craftsmanship, extensive use of wide-angle desert landscapes, handheld camera work, time-lapse sequences, and symbolic color palettes assigned to characters.
- Won 16 Primetime Emmy Awards across its five seasons, including four Lead Actor Emmys for Cranston, three Supporting Actor Emmys for Paul, and two Outstanding Drama Series awards.
- Spawned a critically praised media franchise that includes the prequel spin-off series *Better Call Saul* and the 2019 sequel film *El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie*.

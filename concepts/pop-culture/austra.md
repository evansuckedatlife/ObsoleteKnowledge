## summary

**Austra** is a Canadian synthpop and electronic music project formed in Toronto in 2009, founded and led by vocalist, songwriter, and producer *Katie Stelmanis*. Renowned for blending dramatic, classically trained operatic vocals with driving darkwave rhythms and pulsating club beats, the band established an influential presence in modern indie electronic music. Their critically acclaimed debut album *Feel It Break* earned widespread praise for its theatrical gothic atmosphere and introspective lyricism.

## you gotta know

- Fronted by *Katie Stelmanis*, who was trained as an opera singer in the Canadian Children's Opera Chorus and previously played in the riot grrrl punk outfit *Galaxy*.
- Released their breakthrough debut studio album *Feel It Break* in 2011 via Domino Recording Company and Paper Bag Records to widespread critical acclaim.
- *Feel It Break* was shortlisted for the prestigious 2011 Polaris Music Prize and nominated for a Juno Award for Electronic Album of the Year.
- Produced standout atmospheric club singles including *Lose It*, *The Beat and the Pulse*, and *Spellwork*, noted for eerie synthesizer arpeggios and soaring vocal vibrato.
- Released their sophomore album *Olympia* in 2013, incorporating live acoustic instrumentation, complex percussion, and more upbeat house-music influences.
- Followed with politically charged conceptual records, including 2017's *Future Politics*, which addressed economic anxiety and technological accelerationism, and 2020's baroque-pop LP *HiRUDiN*.
- Named after the goddess of light and the dawn in Baltic and Latvian mythology, reflecting the heritage of *Katie Stelmanis*.

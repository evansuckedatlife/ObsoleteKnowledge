## summary

**Baby** is a 2010 bubblegum pop and R&B single recorded by Canadian singer *Justin Bieber* alongside rapper *Ludacris*, serving as the lead track for his debut studio album *My World 2.0*. Produced by *Tricky Stewart* and *The-Dream*, the record propelled Bieber from an internet sensation into an inescapable global teen idol whose infectious doo-wop inspired hook dominated international airwaves. It remains one of the defining cultural touchstones of early 2010s digital fandom, early streaming video milestones, and viral social media visibility.

## you gotta know

- Features an instantly recognizable chorus built around the repeated refrain of *baby, baby, baby, oh*, evoking mid-century Motown and contemporary R&B vocal harmonies.
- Debuted at number five on the Billboard Hot 100 in the [[united-states]], marking Bieber's highest-charting entry at that point and securing multiplatinum certification across several continents.
- Includes a guest rap verse performed by *Ludacris*, which grounded the track across urban contemporary and pop radio formats during a major transition era in mainstream commercial broadcasting.
- Became the most-viewed video on YouTube in July 2010 after overtaking *Lady Gaga*'s video for *Bad Romance*, a title it held for over two years until being surpassed by *Psy*'s *Gangnam Style*.
- Gained widespread notoriety on digital platforms for holding the Guinness World Record for the most disliked video on YouTube for several consecutive years, reflecting early web polarization.
- Filmed its music video inside a bowling alley and arcade setting at Universal CityWalk, featuring cameo appearances by contemporary teen artists such as *Drake*, *Tinashe*, and *Lil Twist*.
- Co-written by *Christina Milian*, who helped shape the song's melodic contours alongside Bieber, *Christopher Stewart*, and *Terius Nash*.

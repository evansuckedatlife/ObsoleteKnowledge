## summary

**Hilo** is the largest settlement and county seat of Hawaii County, situated on the lush, rainy eastern coast of the Island of Hawaii. Renowned for its rich Polynesian heritage, historic sugar plantation economy, and profound vulnerability to Pacific tsunamis, the city serves as a major gateway to *Hawaii Volcanoes National Park*. In popular culture and cultural history, the city is world-famous as the host of the annual *Merrie Monarch Festival*, the premier competitive hula festival honoring Hawaiian indigenous arts.

## you gotta know

- Recognized as the cultural heart of Hawaiian performing arts by hosting the prestigious week-long *Merrie Monarch Festival* every spring since 1964.
- Named in honor of *King David Kalakaua*, known as the "Merrie Monarch", who revitalized native Hawaiian music, language, and hula traditions.
- Located along the slopes of two massive volcanoes, the active shield volcano *Mauna Loa* and the dormant volcanic peak *Mauna Kea*.
- Experienced catastrophic devastation from two historic Pacific tsunamis in 1946 and 1960, which reshaped the coastal geography and led to the creation of the *Pacific Tsunami Museum*.
- Developed into a major commercial and agricultural port town during the 19th and early 20th centuries driven by the sugarcane plantation boom.
- Absorbed substantial waves of Asian immigrant laborers, particularly from [[japan]], the Philippines, and China, profoundly shaping local cuisine, language, and social culture.
- Serves as the administrative center for premier global astronomical observatories situated atop the summit of *Mauna Kea*.

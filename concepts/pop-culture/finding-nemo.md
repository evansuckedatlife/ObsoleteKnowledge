## summary

**Finding Nemo** is an acclaimed 2003 American computer-animated adventure film produced by *Pixar Animation Studios* and released by *Walt Disney Pictures*. Directed by *Andrew Stanton*, the story follows an overprotective clownfish named Marlin who embarks on an epic oceanic quest alongside an amnesiac regal blue tang named Dory to find his captured son. Celebrated for technical breakthroughs in digital lighting and aquatic physics, the film won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and stands as a modern storytelling benchmark.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Andrew Stanton* with co-direction by *Lee Unkrich*, featuring a prominent voice cast led by *Albert Brooks*, *Ellen DeGeneres*, and *Alexander Gould*.
- Follows the anxious clownfish Marlin across the Pacific Ocean to Sydney Harbour after his adventurous son Nemo is netted by a scuba-diving dentist.
- Introduced memorable comedic supporting characters, including the vegetarian shark Bruce, the laid-back sea turtle Crush, and the aquarium Tank Gang.
- Marked significant advances in three-dimensional computer graphics, notably in simulating realistic light absorption, subsurface scattering, and murky ocean currents.
- Garnered four Academy Award nominations, becoming the first Pixar production to capture the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.
- Spawned a commercially lucrative multimedia franchise, including theme park attractions and a highly successful 2016 sequel, *Finding Dory*.

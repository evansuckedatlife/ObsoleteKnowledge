## summary

**Fendi** is a venerable Italian luxury fashion house celebrated worldwide for its artisanal leather goods, pioneering fur craftsmanship, ready-to-wear collections, and cultural trendsetting. Founded in [[rome]] in 1925 as a modest boutique workshop by *Adele Casagrande* and her husband *Edoardo Fendi*, the company expanded under the stewardship of their five visionary daughters into an internationally dominant luxury empire. Recognized by its iconic inverted double-F logo and historic accessories like the *Baguette* bag, the brand represents the pinnacle of twentieth-century Roman craftsmanship and modernist high-fashion glamour.

## you gotta know

- Created the iconic *Baguette* handbag in 1997, designed by *Silvia Venturini Fendi* to be tucked comfortably under the arm like a loaf of French bread, widely celebrated as the world's very first "It-bag".
- Cemented the *Baguette*'s legendary status in modern pop culture through the television series *Sex and the City*, immortalized when protagonist *Carrie Bradshaw* corrected a street robber by declaring that her accessory was not a mere bag, but a *Baguette*.
- Forged an unprecedented fifty-four-year creative partnership with German designer *Karl Lagerfeld*, who joined the house in 1965 and directed its fur and women's ready-to-wear collections until his death in 2019.
- Commissioned Lagerfeld to conceive the revolutionary inverted "Zucca" double-F monogram, an emblem originally standing for "Fun Fur" that became one of the most recognizable luxury motifs in graphic history.
- Expanded from a family boutique into an international fashion powerhouse through the collaborative leadership of the five Fendi sisters—*Paola*, *Anna*, *Franca*, *Carla*, and *Alda*—each overseeing distinct branches of the brand's production and design.
- Introduced the celebrated *Peekaboo* handbag in 2008, an architectural accessory designed with a classic turn-lock that casually hangs open to display a contrasting interior lining.
- Staged an audacious, record-breaking runway show atop the *Great Wall of China* in 2007, spending an estimated ten million dollars to construct an eighty-eight-meter catwalk across the ancient monument.
- Transitioned into the modern global luxury conglomerate era by joining the French multinational group *LVMH*, later appointing British designer *Kim Jones* to lead artistic direction alongside the founding family.

## connections

- [[italy]] — represents the national homeland whose long traditions of leather artisanal craft and textile manufacturing anchor the brand.
- [[rome]] — serves as the historic birthplace, headquarters, and constant architectural muse for the fashion house, which funded the restoration of the *Trevi Fountain*.
- [[france]] — represents the modern corporate home of parent conglomerate *LVMH* as well as the birthplace of haute couture runway traditions.
- [[modernism]] — reflected in *Karl Lagerfeld*'s structural garment silhouettes, dynamic graphic monograms, and avant-garde reinterpretation of traditional furs.
- [[world-war-ii]] — disrupted the young family business and precipitated post-war industrial reorganization under the leadership of the five sisters.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — transformed twentieth-century luxury production by enabling mechanized leather tanning, global distribution networks, and synthetic textile blends.

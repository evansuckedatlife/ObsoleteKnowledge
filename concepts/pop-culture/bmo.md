## summary

**BMO** is a living video game console, computer, and loyal roommate created by Pendleton Ward for the American animated television series *Adventure Time*. Voiced by South Korean voice actress *Niki Yang*, the character resembles a teal handheld gaming device evocative of a cross between a Nintendo Game Boy and a Macintosh computer. Within modern television animation, the character is renowned for embodying childlike innocence, surreal existential identity crises, and a rich imaginative inner world that subverts traditional mechanical sidekick tropes.

## you gotta know

- Resides in the Tree House alongside main protagonists *Finn the Human* and *Jake the Dog*, serving variously as their entertainment console, alarm clock, chef, and friend.
- Created originally by the inventor *Moe* (*Moseph Mastro Giovanni*) as the sole unit designed specifically to understand and experience fun and love among thousands of utilitarian MO models.
- Engages in elaborate solo alter-ego routines in front of mirrors, most famously conversing with an imaginary reflection named *Football* whom the character occasionally trades places with.
- Voiced with a distinct accent and pitch by *Niki Yang*, who also voiced *Lady Rainicorn* on the same show.
- Possesses fluid gender pronouns throughout the series, being referred to interchangeably as "he," "she," or "it" by different companions.
- Stars as the central protagonist in the standalone special episode *BMO*, which served as the premiere installment of the *Adventure Time: Distant Lands* miniseries on HBO Max.
- Plays retro 8-bit and 16-bit video games on its built-in monitor, including fictional titles like *Conversation Parade* and *Master's Order*.
- Survives far into the distant dystopian future of the Land of Ooo, eventually becoming the self-proclaimed *King of Ooo* and custodian of past relics in the series finale *Come Along With Me*.

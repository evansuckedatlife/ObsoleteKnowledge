## summary

**Avengers: Infinity War** is a 2018 American superhero blockbuster produced by Marvel Studios and directed by *Anthony and Joe Russo*. Serving as the culmination of ten years and eighteen preceding films in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), the film depicts Earth's Mightiest Heroes uniting with the *Guardians of the Galaxy* to prevent the cosmic warlord *Thanos* from collecting the six Infinity Stones. Renowned for its unprecedented crossover scale, somber cliffhanger ending, and commercial dominance, it became the first superhero movie to gross over two billion dollars worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Directed by the *Russo brothers* and written by *Christopher Markus* and *Stephen McFeely*, serving as the direct precursor to *Avengers: Endgame* (2019).
- Positions the warlord *Thanos*, portrayed through motion capture by *Josh Brolin*, as the functional protagonist driving the narrative across multiple galactic locations.
- Centers on the quest to assemble the six *Infinity Stones* (Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time, and Soul) into the golden *Infinity Gauntlet* to eradicate half of all life in the universe.
- Splits an expansive ensemble cast across disparate battlefronts, including combat in *New York City*, deep space on *Titan*, and an alien planetary siege in the fictional African kingdom of *Wakanda*.
- Features the dramatic death of *Vision*, *Loki*, and *Gamora*, culminating in the infamous *"Snap"* that turns half of the universe's population to dust.
- Received acclaim for its pacing and structural balancing of dozens of major heroes, including *Iron Man* (Robert Downey Jr.), *Thor* (Chris Hemsworth), and *Captain America* (Chris Evans).
- Held the record for the highest-grossing worldwide opening weekend in film history at the time, generating over 640 million dollars during its premiere.

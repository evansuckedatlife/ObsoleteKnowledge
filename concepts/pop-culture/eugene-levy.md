## summary

**Eugene Levy** is an acclaimed Canadian actor, comedian, and writer renowned for his deadpan comedic delivery, expressive bushy eyebrows, and mastery of flustered patriarch roles. Rising to prominence through the groundbreaking sketch comedy series *Second City Television* (*SCTV*), he became an international fixture through frequent improvisational collaborations with *Christopher Guest* and his turn as Noah Levenstein in the *American Pie* film franchise. In the 2010s, he achieved widespread critical acclaim by co-creating and starring as Johnny Rose in the multi-Emmy-winning sitcom *Schitt's Creek*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved his breakout success in the 1970s and 1980s as a core cast member and writer on *SCTV*, creating memorable characters such as newsman Earl Camembert and accordion bandleader Stan Shmenge.
- Starred in all four theatrical releases of the *American Pie* franchise as Jim's understanding, awkwardly candid father, Noah Levenstein.
- Co-wrote and starred in *Christopher Guest*'s celebrated mockumentary satires, including *Waiting for Guffman*, *Best in Show*, *A Mighty Wind*, and *For Your Consideration*.
- Received an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Song alongside Christopher Guest for writing "A Kiss at the End of the Rainbow" from *A Mighty Wind*.
- Co-created the hit CBC and Pop TV comedy series *Schitt's Creek* with his son *Dan Levy*, starring as the formerly wealthy video store magnate Johnny Rose.
- Swept all major comedy categories at the 72nd Primetime Emmy Awards with *Schitt's Creek*, winning Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series.
- Hosted the Apple TV+ travel documentary series *The Reluctant Traveler*, visiting destinations across Europe, Asia, and North America.

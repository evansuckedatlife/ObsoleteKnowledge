## summary

**Guts**, widely feared and renowned across dark fantasy fiction under the ominous epithet **The Black Swordsman**, is the central protagonist of *Kentaro Miura's* acclaimed Japanese manga series *Berserk*. Born from the corpse of a hanged woman on a bloodied medieval battlefield, he survives an unforgiving upbringing as a wandering mercenary only to become entangled in the tragic cosmic ambitions of his charismatic commander *Griffith*. Renowned for his superhuman endurance, indomitable will, and massive iron broadsword, he endures as an iconic figure of modern sequential literature exploring trauma, fatalism, and the defiant struggle against destiny.

## you gotta know

- Wields the monstrous *Dragon Slayer*, a titanic blade forged by the blacksmith *Godo* that is described in the text as far too large, thick, heavy, and crude to be called an ordinary sword, functioning essentially as a massive heap of iron.
- Marked upon his neck by the cursed *Brand of Sacrifice* during the catastrophic demonic ritual known as the *Eclipse*, drawing relentless tides of vengeful spirits, astral specters, and demonic apostles whenever night falls.
- Rose through the ranks of the mercenary company known as the *Band of the Hawk* to become its lethal raid captain, forging intimate, pivotal relationships with commander *Griffith* and fellow officer *Casca*.
- Severed his own left forearm with a broken sword blade and lost his right eye while attempting in vain to rescue *Casca* from demonic assault during the horrifying betrayal that concluded the *Golden Age* narrative arc.
- Equipped with a custom mechanical prosthetic arm constructed of steel that houses a hidden repeating crossbow mount, a magnetic palm clasp for two-handed sword handling, and an explosive concealed cannon barrel.
- Donned the cursed *Berserker Armor*, a dwarven relic that numbs the user to mortal pain and punctures fractured bones with internal metal barbs, granting terrifying supernatural strength at the cost of gradual sensory degradation and psychological decay.
- Journeyed across corrupted lands accompanied by an eclectic second family of companions, including the playful elf *Puck*, the apprentice witch *Schierke*, the juvenile thief *Isidro*, and former holy knights *Farnese* and *Serpico*.

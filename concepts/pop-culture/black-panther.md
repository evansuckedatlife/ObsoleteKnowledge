## summary

**Black Panther** is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by *Marvel Comics*, created by writer-editor *Stan Lee* and artist *Jack Kirby*. Debuting in *Fantastic Four* #52 in July 1966, the character is monarch and protector of the fictional African nation of *Wakanda*, an technologically advanced kingdom hidden from the outside world. As the first superhero of African descent in mainstream American comic books, the character stands as a landmark figure in comic history and modern popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Real name is **T'Challa**, king of the uncolonized, isolationist African nation of *Wakanda*, which derives its futuristic technological supremacy from the rare extraterrestrial metal *vibranium*.
- Created by *Stan Lee* and *Jack Kirby* in 1966 during the American civil rights movement, predating the founding of the political *Black Panther Party*, leading Marvel to briefly rename him the *Black Leopard* to avoid confusion before reverting.
- Draws superhuman strength, speed, stamina, and heightened senses from the mystical, heart-shaped herb cultivated exclusively in Wakanda, coupled with ancestral empowerment from the panther goddess *Bast*.
- Governs alongside elite royal bodyguards known as the *Dora Milaje*, an all-female warrior order, and collaborates closely with his genius sister, the inventor princess *Shuri*.
- Portrayed by actor *Chadwick Boseman* in the *Marvel Cinematic Universe*, debuting in *Captain America: Civil War* (2016) and headlining the acclaimed 2018 film *Black Panther*, which earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Picture.
- Features landmark comic storylines including *Don McGregor*'s celebrated multi-issue arc *"Panther's Rage"*, *Christopher Priest*'s defining satirical run, and award-winning author *Ta-Nehisi Coates*'s expanded political world-building series.

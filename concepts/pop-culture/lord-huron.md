## summary

**Lord Huron** is an American indie rock band formed in Los Angeles in 2010, initially conceived as a solo recording project by singer-songwriter, visual artist, and Michigan native *Ben Schneider*. Named after Lake Huron, where Schneider spent formative childhood summers composing folk melodies, the musical ensemble blends rich acoustic folk traditions, surf rock guitars, sweeping orchestral harmonies, and evocative narrative songwriting. The band is widely recognized for constructing intricate fictional lore, pulp-fiction mythologies, and cinematic concept albums that explore mortality, spectral hauntings, existential yearning, and the vanishing American frontier.

## you gotta know

- Founded originally as a solo recording project by front-man *Ben Schneider*, who independently recorded the breakthrough early extended plays *Into the Sun* and *Mighty* before recruiting longtime childhood friends into a full touring ensemble.
- Achieved widespread mainstream recognition and multi-platinum commercial success with the melancholic ballad *The Night We Met*, which went viral after appearing in popular television soundtracks.
- Released their acclaimed debut studio album *Lonesome Dreams* in 2012, designed around the fictional western adventure pulp novels of imaginary traveling author *George Ranger Johnson*.
- Expanded their narrative world-building on the 2015 follow-up album *Strange Trails*, introducing recurring fictional characters, phantom greaser gangs, and mid-century subcultures such as the *World Ender*.
- Released the retro noir-inspired concept record *Long Lost* in 2021, centered around the fictional *Whispering Pines Studios* and lost mid-twentieth-century country and lounge vocalists.
- Developed the surreal variety television streaming web series *Alive from Whispering Pines*, featuring fictional ghost host *Tubbs Tarbell* to introduce new musical compositions and thematic lore.
- Composed the evocative original film score for the 2022 mystery drama *The Starling Girl*, further cementing Schneider's reputation as a cinematic composer.
- Maintained a dedicated touring core consisting of guitarist *Tom Renaud*, bassist *Miguel Briseño*, and drummer *Mark Barry*, combining close four-part vocal harmonies with evocative soundscapes.

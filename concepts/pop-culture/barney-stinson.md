## summary

**Barney Stinson**, whose full canonical name is **Barnabus Stinson**, is a manipulative, hyperactive, suit-obsessed bachelor portrayed by actor *Neil Patrick Harris* on the *CBS* television sitcom *How I Met Your Mother*. Appearing across all nine seasons from 2005 to 2014, he serves as the amoral, wingman foil to central romantic protagonist *Ted Mosby*, dedicating his boundless resources to seduction schemes, laser tag, and magic tricks. The character evolved into the series' definitive breakout icon, earning multiple Primetime Emmy nominations and exerting a major influence on twenty-first-century network sitcom dialogue.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed throughout the series by *Neil Patrick Harris*, an openly gay performer whose exuberant and nuanced portrayal of an aggressively womanizing heterosexual bachelor received widespread critical acclaim.
- Defined by a rigid personal sartorial code, constantly urging his social circle to "Suit up!" and refusing to appear in casual street clothes except under extreme physical duress.
- Authored several mock lifestyle manuals within the sitcom's universe that were subsequently published as real-world bestsellers, most prominently *The Bro Code* and the elaborate deception manual *The Playbook*.
- Popularized a series of rapid-fire conversational catchphrases that entered mainstream television vernacular, including "Legen—wait for it—dary!," "Challenge accepted!," and "True story."
- Worked for years in an intentionally ambiguous, lucrative corporate executive post at *Goliath National Bank*, eventually revealed to be an acronym standing for "Provide Legal Exculpation And Sign Everything" (*P.L.E.A.S.E.*).
- Developed a complex, multi-season romantic relationship with Canadian television news reporter *Robin Scherbatsky*, leading to an engagement and a wedding weekend that formed the entire narrative spine of the final season.
- Connected directly to Canadian musical history when his fiancee's teen-pop past as *Robin Sparkles* was scrutinized in a retrospective documentary featuring musicians from [[barenaked-ladies]].
- Masked deep psychological trauma resulting from childhood paternal abandonment, which fueled his desperate competitive drive, fear of emotional intimacy, and obsession with personal mythology.

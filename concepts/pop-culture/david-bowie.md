## summary

**David Bowie** was an influential English singer, songwriter, actor, and cultural icon whose career was defined by continuous reinvention and theatrical audacity. Rising to prominence during the early 1970s glam rock explosion with his alter ego *Ziggy Stardust*, he fundamentally transformed popular music by merging theatrical presentation with diverse musical genres. His vast body of work spanned rock, soul, electronic art-pop, and industrial experimentation, leaving an indelible imprint on modern visual culture and popular aesthetics.

## you gotta know

- Achieved his first major commercial breakthrough with the 1969 single *Space Oddity*, which coincided with the Apollo 11 moon landing and introduced his fictional astronaut *Major Tom*.
- Pioneered the theatrical heights of glam rock through his flamboyant, extraterrestrial persona *Ziggy Stardust* and the acclaimed 1972 concept album *The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars*.
- Recorded the legendary *Berlin Trilogy*—comprising the albums *Low*, *“Heroes”*, and *Lodger*—collaborating with *Brian Eno* to explore ambient textures and electronic minimalism during the *Cold War*.
- Adopted multiple conceptual personae across his musical journey, including *Aladdin Sane*, *Halloween Jack*, and the controversial, austere *Thin White Duke*.
- Reached global commercial superstardom in 1983 with the dance-rock album *Let's Dance*, produced by *Nile Rodgers* and featuring hit singles like *Modern Love*.
- Enjoyed a distinguished cinematic career, starring as the goblin king *Jareth* in the fantasy film *Labyrinth* and an alien wanderer in *The Man Who Fell to Earth*.
- Concluded his career with the haunting, avant-garde jazz-rock album *Blackstar*, released just two days before his death in 2016 as a carefully orchestrated artistic farewell.

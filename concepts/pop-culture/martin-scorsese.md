## summary

**Martin Scorsese** is an acclaimed American film director, producer, and screenwriter central to the New Hollywood cinema movement that emerged in the late 1960s and 1970s. Raised in the Little Italy neighborhood of *New York City*, he became internationally renowned for hard-hitting, character-driven dramas exploring Italian-American identity, Catholic guilt, machismo, crime, and moral corruption. His technical mastery, energetic pacing, and tireless advocacy for international film preservation have cemented him as one of the most influential filmmakers in cinematic history.

## you gotta know

- Directed seminal masterworks including *Taxi Driver* (1976), which won the Palme d'Or at Cannes, *Raging Bull* (1980), and *Goodfellas* (1990).
- Won the Academy Award for Best Director and Best Picture for the Boston-set crime thriller *The Departed* (2006) after numerous celebrated nominations.
- Established legendary creative partnerships with actor *Robert De Niro*, collaborating across nine feature films, and later with *Leonardo DiCaprio* on major productions such as *The Aviator* (2004) and *The Wolf of Wall Street* (2013).
- Worked throughout his career with editor *Thelma Schoonmaker*, whose rhythmic, rapid cutting defined the distinctive cadence of his filmography.
- Examined intense spiritual turmoil and religious conviction in controversial projects such as *The Last Temptation of Christ* (1988), *Kundun* (1997), and *Silence* (2016).
- Founded The Film Foundation in 1990 and the World Cinema Project in 2007 to restore, preserve, and distribute deteriorating motion pictures globally.
- Pioneered dynamic stylistic techniques, frequently combining freeze frames, voice-over narrations, rapid tracking shots, and eclectic pop and rock music soundtracks.

## summary

**She-Hulk: Attorney at Law**, also cataloged as **She-Hulk (TV series)**, is a 2022 American superhero legal comedy miniseries developed by head writer *Jessica Gao* for *Disney+*. Based on the *Marvel Comics* character created by *Stan Lee* and *John Buscema*, the program stars *Tatiana Maslany* as an ambitious attorney who balances her specialized courtroom practice with her accidental transformation into a six-foot-seven green powerhouse. Blending workplace sitcom conventions with meta-fictional fourth-wall breaks, the show satirized corporate franchise storytelling, online misogyny, and superhero melodrama.

## you gotta know

- Focuses on *Jennifer Walters*, a deputy district attorney who acquires superhuman strength after cross-contamination with the gamma-irradiated blood of her cousin *Bruce Banner* following a vehicular collision.
- Employs self-referential fourth-wall breaks where the protagonist speaks directly to viewers, drawing heavily from *John Byrne*'s iconic 1989 comic series *The Sensational She-Hulk*.
- Stars *Tatiana Maslany* portraying both the grounded, insecure civilian lawyer and her super-powered alter ego, navigating dating, social expectations, and career pressures at law firm *GLK&H*.
- Features legal storylines centered on obscure superhuman clients, including the parole hearing of *Emil Blonsky* (*Abomination*) and an intellectual property dispute involving mystical practitioner *Donny Blaze*.
- Climaxed in a radically subversive season finale titled *Whose Show Is This?*, in which the heroine escapes the Disney+ interface into the real-world production offices to rewrite her own plot before an artificial-intelligence producer named *K.E.V.I.N.*.
- Reintroduced *Charlie Cox* as *Matt Murdock* (*Daredevil*) into the *Marvel Cinematic Universe*, highlighting an affectionate romantic and courtroom rivalry between two hero-attorneys.
- Sparked widespread popular discourse surrounding computer-generated imagery workflows, contemporary feminist cultural satire, and deliberate parody of reactionary online review campaigns.

## summary

**Rowan Atkinson**, full name **Rowan Sebastian Atkinson**, is an acclaimed English comic actor, writer, and physical satirist whose work reshaped television comedy across the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries. Born in County Durham, [[england]], he achieved global renown for creating and portraying the eccentric, largely non-verbal character *Mr. Bean* as well as the sharp-tongued historical schemer *Edmund Blackadder*. His screen achievements stand as one of the most commercially successful and influential exports of British television, bridging modern visual satire with the heritage of classic [[slapstick-comedy]] and [[physical-comedy]].

## you gotta know

- Created and starred as the childish, suit-wearing title character in *Mr. Bean*, an international television sensation celebrated for minimalist dialogue and expressive pantomime that captivated global audiences without linguistic barriers.
- Portrayed the cynical, social-climbing protagonist *Edmund Blackadder* across four historical incarnations of the celebrated BBC television sitcom *Blackadder*, culminating in the poignant trenches of [[world-war-i]] in *Blackadder Goes Forth*.
- Parodied the quintessential British secret agent cinematic genre as the bumbling yet earnest MI7 operative in the international box-office hit film trilogy *Johnny English*.
- Reprised his *Mr. Bean* persona in a widely watched comic performance during the London 2012 Olympic Games opening ceremony, miming along to the synthesizer theme from *Chariots of Fire*.
- Gained his initial national breakthrough on the BBC topical sketch series *Not the Nine O'Clock News*, earning a British Academy Television Award alongside writers and performers who transformed modern satire.
- Performed on the West End stage to critical acclaim as the pickpocket mentor *Fagin* in the musical *Oliver!*, and displayed dramatic versatility as the titular French detective *Jules Maigret* in television adaptations.
- Earned an undergraduate degree in electrical engineering at Newcastle University followed by a Master of Science degree at The Queen's College, Oxford, where he began developing his rubber-faced performance style with the Oxford Revue.
- An avid sports car enthusiast and historic vintage automotive racer who famously drove, crashed, and subsequently restored an ultra-rare McLaren F1 supercar.

- [[england]] — native homeland and cultural landscape providing the setting for his iconic historical satires and parodies of eccentric British gentility.

- [[ryan-long]] — contemporary satirical sketch performer whose short-form character comedy draws on the rich sketch and physical performance lineage Atkinson pioneered.

## summary

**Dota 2**, expanding historically from **Defense of the Ancients 2**, is a premier multiplayer online battle arena title developed and published by *Valve* in 2013. Evolving from a community-made modification for Blizzard's *Warcraft III*, the game pits two teams of five players against each other as they manage resources, select distinct heroes, and coordinate strategies to obliterate the opposing team's ancient stronghold. Celebrated for its staggering mechanical depth, high competitive ceiling, and multimillion-dollar tournament scene, it remains a cornerstone of the global competitive gaming landscape.

## you gotta know

- Directed by the pseudonymous lead designer *IceFrog*, who guided the original *Defense of the Ancients: Allstars* modification before being hired by *Valve* to forge a standalone sequel on the Source engine.
- Matches pit the *Radiant* against the *Dire* on a single symmetrical map partitioned by a river and divided into three primary lanes protected by defensive towers.
- Features a roster exceeding one hundred and twenty distinct heroes, categorized across core attributes of Strength, Agility, and Intelligence, all made entirely free to play from the outset.
- Pioneered modern crowdfunded esports prize pools through the *Compendium* and *Battle Pass*, allowing community purchases to inflate *The International* tournament purse past forty million dollars.
- Hosts an intricate economic and tactical metagame centered on gold acquisition, experience points, last-hitting enemy creeps, lane equilibrium, denying, and dynamic itemization.
- Served as a historic testbed for artificial intelligence research when *OpenAI Five* defeated top-tier human world championship teams in 2019 using deep reinforcement learning.
- Showcased iconic championship moments in competitive lore, such as the famous million-dollar *Echo Slam* and Na'Vi's tactical fountain hook maneuver.

- [[counter-strike]] — *Valve*'s foundational tactical shooter that shares its Steam distribution ecosystem and global tournament circuit.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — parallel competitive genre that shaped team-based electronic sports alongside strategy arenas.
- [[united-states]] — the home base of *Valve Corporation* in Bellevue, Washington, where development and tournament infrastructure originated.
- [[dragon-quest]] — foundational Japanese fantasy RPG whose party systems, mana management, and stat metrics distant ancestors of MOBA hero design.

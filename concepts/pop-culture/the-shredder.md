## summary

**The Shredder**, whose true identity is *Oroku Saki*, is the iconic arch-nemesis of the *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles* and the supreme leader of the criminal syndicate known as the *Foot Clan*. Created by comic book artists *Kevin Eastman* and *Peter Laird*, he first appeared in *Mirage Studios'* gritty debut issue in 1984 as a ruthless martial arts master clad in bladed armor and a menacing kabuto helmet. Across comic books, animated television series, and live-action films, he represents the quintessential vengeful warlord whose personal feud with *Master Splinter* and the turtles drives the franchise's enduring narrative.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in May 1984 in *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles* #1, where *Kevin Eastman* conceived the villain's signature bladed design while looking at a metal cheese grater.
- Born in *Japan* as *Oroku Saki*, his fierce vendetta stems from the ancient feud between his older brother *Oroku Nagi* and *Hamato Yoshi*, the martial artist tied directly to the mutation of *Splinter*.
- Heads the *Foot Clan*, an underground ninja crime syndicate operating primarily out of *New York City*, which parodies the assassin order known as *The Hand* from Marvel Comics.
- Wears ornate samurai-inspired battle armor featuring razor-sharp gauntlets, shin guards, a flowing cape, and a metal mask concealing most of his scarred facial features.
- Originally died in the very first comic book issue after falling off a roof with a live thermite grenade, but proved so immensely popular that he was repeatedly resurrected via mutagen, cloning, and mystical arts.
- Portrayed in the seminal 1987 animated television series as a comedic, bumbling commander frequently barking orders at mutant henchmen *Bebop and Rocksteady* alongside the alien warlord *Krang*.
- Underwent a radical biological transformation into *Super Shredder* in the 1991 live-action film *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles II: The Secret of the Ooze*, played by professional wrestler *Kevin Nash*.

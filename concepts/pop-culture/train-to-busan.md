## summary

**Train to Busan**, titled in South Korea as **Busanhaeng**, is an internationally acclaimed 2016 action-horror disaster film directed by *Yeon Sang-ho* and starring *Gong Yoo*, *Ma Dong-seok*, and *Jung Yu-mi*. Debuting in the Midnight Screenings section at the 2016 Cannes Film Festival in *[[france]]*, the film chronicles the plight of passengers trapped aboard a high-speed KTX passenger train during a sudden nationwide zombie epidemic. Celebrated for its intense claustrophobic suspense, emotional storytelling, and biting social critique of corporate self-interest, the film became a record-breaking international blockbuster that revitalized the global zombie genre.

## you gotta know

- Follows cynical, self-absorbed fund manager Seok-woo (*Gong Yoo*) and his young, estranged daughter Su-an (*Kim Su-an*) as their routine morning train journey from Seoul to Busan descends into a desperate fight for survival.
- Features a breakout, universally lauded performance by *Ma Dong-seok* (Don Lee) as Sang-hwa, a working-class hero whose bare-knuckle brawling against zombie hordes propelled him to global action-star fame.
- Portrays corporate executive Yong-suk (*Kim Eui-sung*) as a ruthless villain who repeatedly manipulates crowds and sacrifices conductors, crewmen, and fellow passengers to ensure his own survival.
- Re-envisions classic horror mechanics by presenting infected victims who sprint at blinding speeds, climb over one another in swarms, and lose predatory awareness in pitch darkness when traveling through railway tunnels.
- Confines the vast majority of its narrative action within narrow passenger railcars, employing tight *[[mise-en-scene]]*, kinetic *[[camera-movement]]*, and rhythmic *[[film-editing]]* to maximize tension.
- Functions as an allegory for modern South Korean social inequities and institutional failure, evoking sharp cultural parallels to the government's response to the 2014 MV *Sewol* ferry disaster.
- Expanded into a multimedia cinematic universe under Yeon's direction, including the animated prequel *Seoul Station* and the standalone post-apocalyptic sequel *Peninsula*.

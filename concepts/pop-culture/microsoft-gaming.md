## summary

**Microsoft Gaming** is the multinational interactive entertainment and video game division of *Microsoft*, managing the global *Xbox* brand, console hardware, and cloud gaming platforms. Established as an umbrella division to consolidate first-party development studios and publishing arms, it represents one of the largest video game publishers in world history. Through massive strategic acquisitions including *Mojang*, *ZeniMax Media*, and *Activision Blizzard*, it oversees legendary gaming properties such as *Halo*, *Gears of War*, *Minecraft*, *The Elder Scrolls*, *Fallout*, and *Call of Duty*.

## you gotta know

- Entered the home console market in 2001 with the release of the original *Xbox*, challenging established Japanese console powerhouses *Sony* and *Nintendo*.
- Revolutionized console multiplayer gaming and digital distribution through the launch of *Xbox Live* in 2002, popularizing matchmaking, gamer tags, and digital achievements.
- Established *Bungie*'s sci-fi epic *Halo: Combat Evolved* and its hero *Master Chief* as the flagship console system-seller and definer of modern two-analog-stick first-person shooters.
- Achieved widespread commercial success with the *Xbox 360* generation, fostering competitive multiplayer games, independent downloadable titles via *Xbox Live Arcade*, and early online services.
- Pioneered modern digital subscription models in interactive entertainment by launching *Xbox Game Pass* in 2017, often described as the "Netflix of video games."
- Purchased Swedish development studio *Mojang* and its global sandbox cultural phenomenon *Minecraft* in 2014 for 2.5 billion dollars, pioneering cross-platform community operations.
- Completed a historic 7.5 billion dollar acquisition of *ZeniMax Media* in 2021, bringing *Bethesda Softworks* and premier franchises like *The Elder Scrolls*, *Fallout*, and *Doom* into its studio portfolio.
- Finalized a landmark 68.7 billion dollar buyout of *Activision Blizzard* in 2023, the largest gaming transaction ever recorded, acquiring blockbuster IPs such as *Call of Duty*, *Warcraft*, *Diablo*, and *Overwatch*.

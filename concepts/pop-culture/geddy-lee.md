## summary

**Geddy Lee** is a celebrated Canadian musician, singer, and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist, bassist, and keyboardist for the progressive rock power trio *Rush*. Renowned for his distinct high-register countertenor vocals and intricate, percussive bass lines, he formed a legendary rhythmic and conceptual partnership with guitarist *Alex Lifeson* and lyricist-drummer *Neil Peart*. His virtuosic multi-instrumental stage presence and expansive catalog of conceptually ambitious rock albums earned him induction into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame* in 2013.

## you gotta know

- Formed the quintessential progressive rock trio *Rush* alongside lifelong friend and guitarist *Alex Lifeson*, solidified when virtuosic drummer *Neil Peart* joined in 1974.
- Renowned for simultaneously performing complex syncopated bass lines on his *Fender Jazz Bass*, operating foot-pedal synthesizers, playing keyboards, and singing lead vocals during live concerts.
- Provided soaring vocal work and innovative bass grooves on landmark rock albums such as *2112*, *A Farewell to Kings*, *Hemispheres*, *Permanent Waves*, and *Moving Pictures*.
- Born *Gershon Eliezer Weinrib* in Toronto, Ontario, to Polish Jewish Holocaust survivors who survived the concentration camps of *Auschwitz* and *Bergen-Belsen* during [[world-war-ii]].
- Adopted his stage moniker from the heavily accented pronunciation of his given name Gary by his mother *Mary Weinrib*.
- Replaced traditional on-stage amplifier cabinets during later concert tours with whimsical household appliances, including commercial rotisserie chicken roasters and coin-operated washing machines.
- Authored the comprehensive 2018 organological study *Geddy Lee's Big Beautiful Book of Bass*, documenting the cultural history and construction of vintage electric bass guitars.

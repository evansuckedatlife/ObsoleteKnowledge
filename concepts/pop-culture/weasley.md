## summary

The **Weasley family** (popularly known as the **Weasleys**) is a prominent fictional dynasty of red-haired British wizards in *J. K. Rowling*'s *Harry Potter* fantasy series. Residing at a crooked, magically expanded country home called *The Burrow* outside Ottery St Catchpole in Devon, [[england]], the family provides the emotional anchor, domestic warmth, and moral counterweight to pure-blood wizard supremacy. Through their son *Ron Weasley*, the lifelong companion of *Harry Potter*, and his siblings, the clan stands at the center of the climactic resistance against *Lord Voldemort*.

## you gotta know

- Headed by parents *Arthur Weasley*, an eccentric employee at the Ministry of Magic fascinated by non-magical technology, and *Molly Weasley* (née *Prewett*), a fiercely protective matriarch.
- Ranked among the *Sacred Twenty-Eight* British pure-blood families, yet shunned and branded *blood traitors* by aristocratic houses like the Malfoys due to their staunch defense of non-magical Muggle-borns.
- Comprises seven siblings, each sorted into Gryffindor house: *Bill*, *Charlie*, *Percy*, identical twins *Fred* and *George*, *Ron*, and youngest child and only daughter *Ginny*.
- Represented centrally by *Ron Weasley*, Harry Potter's closest friend, who excels at wizard chess, endures insecurities regarding secondhand possessions, and eventually destroys Salazar Slytherin's locket Horcrux.
- Known for *Fred* and *George Weasley*, mischievous pranksters who invent magical novelties, drop out of Hogwarts during Dolores Umbridge's regime, and establish the wildly profitable shop *Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes* in Diagon Alley.
- Features *Ginny Weasley*, an accomplished Quidditch chaser who was possessed by Tom Riddle's enchanted diary in her first year and later marries *Harry Potter*.
- Experiences wartime loss during the climactic Battle of Hogwarts, where *Fred Weasley* is killed in an explosion and *Molly Weasley* famously duels and slays Death Eater *Bellatrix Lestrange*.
- Resides at *The Burrow*, a multi-story, ramshackle dwelling held upright by magic that serves as a temporary headquarters for the resistance organization known as the *Order of the Phoenix*.

## connections

- [[england]] — the geographical setting where the family resides in Devon and around which the British wizarding society is centered.
- [[warhammer]] — fellow British pop-culture staple created by tabletop game designers that shares aesthetic traditions of miniature British fantasy worldbuilding.
- [[british-empire]] — historic state whose boarding school culture and domestic social stratifications heavily satirize and inform class dynamics within the narrative.
- [[william-shakespeare]] — classic English dramatist whose comedies, domestic family intrigues, and twin-based mistaken identities influenced British literary fantasy tropes.
- [[tragedy]] — classical dramatic genre whose themes of familial loss, sacrifice, and violent conflict characterize the wartime climax of the characters' arcs.
- [[realism]] — literary tradition juxtaposed against fantasy conventions in depictions of the family's domestic poverty, hand-me-down robes, and economic struggles.

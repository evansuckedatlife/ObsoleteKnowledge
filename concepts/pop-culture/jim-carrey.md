## summary

**Jim Carrey**, born **James Eugene Carrey**, is a Canadian-American actor, comedian, and writer celebrated for his kinetic physicality, elastic facial contortions, and explosive improvisational style. After rising through Toronto comedy circuits and the sketch series *In Living Color*, he achieved unprecedented commercial dominance in 1994 with three consecutive blockbusters: *Ace Ventura: Pet Detective*, *The Mask*, and *Dumb and Dumber*. In subsequent decades, he expanded beyond manic slapstick into deeply philosophical dramatic performances that examined surveillance, celebrity, memory, and personal identity.

## you gotta know

- Dominated the global box office in 1994 with a rare trifecta of major comedic hits, establishing a brand of rubber-faced kinetic clowning that defined 1990s Hollywood cinema.
- Became the first screen actor in Hollywood history to secure a $20 million upfront salary for a single motion picture with the dark satire *The Cable Guy* in 1996.
- Won consecutive Golden Globe Awards for Best Actor for his dramatic work in *Peter Weir's* media satire *The Truman Show* and *Miloš Forman's* biographical drama *Man on the Moon*.
- Starred opposite *Kate Winslet* as the withdrawn and grieving *Joel Barish* in *Michel Gondry's* surreal, non-linear romantic masterpiece *Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind*.
- Practiced extreme, controversial immersion while portraying eccentric avant-garde comic *Andy Kaufman*, refusing to break character on set as detailed in the documentary *Jim & Andy: The Great Beyond*.
- Emerged from working-class origins in Ontario, Canada, dropping out of school at age sixteen to support his family while performing impersonations and stand-up at Toronto's *Yuk Yuk's*.
- Exhibited profound mastery of physical stuntwork and facial pantomime, synthesizing classic silent clown traditions with contemporary cartoon sensibility in roles such as *The Grinch* and *Dr. Robotnik*.

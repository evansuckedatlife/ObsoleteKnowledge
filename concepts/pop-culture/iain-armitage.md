## summary

**Iain Armitage** is an American actor who gained widespread critical acclaim and international fame as a child star portraying the boy genius *Sheldon Cooper* on the long-running television sitcom *Young Sheldon*. Emerging initially through theater criticism on the internet before securing major television roles, he also delivered a breakthrough dramatic performance as *Ziggy Chapman* in HBO's celebrated drama series *Big Little Lies*. His work established him as one of the most prominent child performers on prime-time television in the United States during the late 2010s and early 2020s.

## you gotta know

- Rose to international prominence playing the title role of young *Sheldon Cooper* in the CBS sitcom *Young Sheldon*, a prequel spin-off to *The Big Bang Theory*, running from 2017 to 2024.
- First captured public attention at age four by publishing theatrical reviews under the moniker *Iain Loves Theatre*, discussing Broadway stage productions in [[new-york-city]].
- Played *Ziggy Chapman*, the sensitive son of *Jane Chapman* (played by *Shailene Woodley*), in the award-winning HBO dramatic series *Big Little Lies*.
- Voiced the enthusiastic police pup *Chase* in the feature-length animated film *PAW Patrol: The Movie* (2021).
- Portrayed a young version of *Brian Walls* in the 2017 film adaptation of *Jeannette Walls*'s bestselling memoir *The Glass Castle*.
- The son of stage actor *Euan Morton* and theatrical producer *Lee Armitage*, embedding him deeply in American theatrical and performing arts traditions.
- Earned multiple nominations for the *Young Artist Awards* and *Critics' Choice Television Awards* for his nuanced portrayal of an eccentric child prodigy growing up in East Texas.

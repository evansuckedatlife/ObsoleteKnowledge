## summary

**Billy Ray Cyrus** is an American country music singer, songwriter, and actor whose debut single transformed contemporary country radio into a commercial crossover powerhouse in the early 1990s. Born in Flatwoods, Kentucky, he spearheaded a nationwide line-dancing craze before pivoting to a durable career in television and entertainment. He later achieved renewed global success by co-starring in Disney Channel's *Hannah Montana* alongside his daughter *Miley Cyrus* and guest featuring on one of the longest-running number-one hits in *Billboard* chart history.

## you gotta know

- Rose to international superstardom with his 1992 signature crossover hit *"Achy Breaky Heart"*, originally composed by *Don Von Tress*, which fueled a massive global resurgence in country line dancing.
- Released the landmark debut album *Some Gave All* (1992), which spent seventeen consecutive weeks at number one on the *Billboard* 200 chart and earned multi-platinum certification.
- Famously sported a signature mullet hairstyle during the peak of his 1990s country recording career, cementing an enduring visual trope in American pop culture iconography.
- Starred as the fictionalized father *Robby Ray Stewart* alongside his real-life daughter *Miley Cyrus* on the wildly influential Disney Channel series *Hannah Montana* from 2006 to 2011.
- Earned his first career *Billboard* Hot 100 number-one single in 2019 after delivering a guest verse on the record-shattering remix of *Lil Nas X*'s country-trap hybrid track *"Old Town Road"*.
- Garnered two Grammy Awards for his contribution to *"Old Town Road"*, winning Best Pop Duo/Group Performance and Best Music Video.
- Earned his start as a television leading man in the medical drama *Doc* (2001–2004), portraying Montana physician *Clint Cassidy* adjusting to life in an urban clinic.

## summary

**Blizzard Entertainment** is an influential American video game developer and publisher founded in 1991, celebrated for creating landmark franchises that redefined multiplayer gaming, digital worldbuilding, and global electronic sports. Emerging from Southern California, the studio became an industry titan by delivering meticulously polished titles across strategy, role-playing, and online competitive genres. Its major intellectual properties—most notably *Warcraft*, *StarCraft*, and *Diablo*—established foundational standards for modern online culture, community engagement, and digital distribution.

## you gotta know

- Founded in February 1991 under the original name *Silicon & Synapse* by three UCLA graduates: *Michael Morhaime*, *Allen Adham*, and *Frank Pearce*.
- Revolutionized the real-time strategy landscape with the release of *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* in 1994, which quickly spawned the groundbreaking *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* and *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos*.
- Released *StarCraft* in 1998, a science-fiction strategy classic that became a national cultural phenomenon and the cornerstone of the modern professional [[esports]] industry in South Korea.
- Popularized the isometric action role-playing genre with *Diablo* in 1997 and *Diablo II* in 2000, which introduced procedural dungeon generation, randomized loot tables, and dark gothic aesthetics.
- Developed the record-breaking massively multiplayer online role-playing game *World of Warcraft* in 2004, which peaked at over twelve million concurrent subscribers and reshaped global internet culture.
- Launched the proprietary online gaming service *Battle.net* in 1996 with *Diablo*, pioneering integrated matchmaking, anti-cheat mechanisms, and digital community lobbies.
- Created the team-based hero shooter *Overwatch* in 2016, which won numerous game-of-the-year awards and established a franchised city-based professional league.
- The player-created *Warcraft III* custom map *Defense of the Ancients* (*DotA*) established the modern multiplayer online battle arena genre, deeply influencing competitive team gaming.

## connections

- [[esports]] — elevated competitive video gaming into a mass-market global spectacle through professional *StarCraft* broadcasts and the *Overwatch League*.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — entered the hero-based competitive shooter market with the widely celebrated release of *Overwatch*.
- [[counter-strike]] — rival pillar in competitive team multiplayer gaming and early LAN tournament history alongside *StarCraft*.
- [[minecraft]] — shared massive influence over persistent player-driven online virtual spaces and modern PC gaming communities.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow landmark fantasy property that dominated popular culture and modern role-playing video game expectations.
- [[united-states]] — nation of origin where the studio developed its core software franchises and maintained its Irvine headquarters.

## summary

**Avatar** is a 2009 American epic science-fiction film written, directed, co-produced, and co-edited by *James Cameron*. Set in the mid-twenty-second century on the habitable moon *Pandora*, the story follows paraplegic Marine *Jake Sully*, who infiltrates the indigenous *Na'vi* species using a remotely operated bioengineered body before turning to defend their biosphere from human resource exploitation. Recognized as a technological watershed for motion capture, stereoscopic 3D projection, and photorealistic CGI, it surpassed Cameron's own *Titanic* to become the highest-grossing film of all time.

## you gotta know

- Conceived and directed by *James Cameron*, who delayed production for over a decade waiting for computer-generated imagery technology to match his cinematic vision.
- Follows *Jake Sully* (played by *Sam Worthington*), a paralyzed former Marine sent to replace his deceased twin brother in the RDA corporation's mining expedition on *Pandora*.
- Depicts the conflict over *Unobtainium*, a priceless room-temperature superconductor mineral buried beneath the ancestral *Hometree* of the *Omaticaya* Na'vi clan.
- Co-stars *Zoe Saldana* as the Na'vi warrior princess *Neytiri*, who teaches Jake the customs, language, and spiritual interconnectedness of Pandora's biosphere, governed by the deity *Eywa*.
- Pioneered groundbreaking performance capture methods, facial muscle tracking, and stereoscopic virtual camera systems developed specifically for the production by Cameron and *Wētā Digital*.
- Garnered nine Academy Award nominations, winning three Oscars for Best Art Direction, Best Cinematography, and Best Visual Effects.
- Spawned a multi-film franchise starting with the massive box-office sequel *Avatar: The Way of Water* (2022), which explored the marine ecosystems of Pandora.

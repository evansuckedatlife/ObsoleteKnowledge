## summary

**Klaus** is a 2019 animated holiday comedy-drama film written and directed by Spanish animator *Sergio Pablos* in his feature directorial debut, produced by *The SPA Studios* and released worldwide by *Netflix*. Serving as an inventive, grounded origin story for the folklore of *Santa Claus*, the film follows a selfish postal cadet exiled to a frozen northern island who inadvertently sparks a cultural revolution of kindness alongside a reclusive toy-maker. Praised by critics as a landmark technical and narrative triumph, it revitalized interest in feature-length hand-drawn animation through pioneering lighting technology.

## you gotta know

- Conceived and directed by veteran Disney animator *Sergio Pablos*—the original creator of the *Despicable Me* franchise—who sought to demonstrate how traditional 2D hand-drawn animation could evolve if computer CGI had not eclipsed it in the late 1990s.
- Centers on *Jesper Johansen*, the lazy, entitled heir to a wealthy postal dynasty voiced by *Jason Schwartzman*, who is banished by his father to the remote Arctic outpost of *Smeerensburg* with a quota of stamping six thousand letters or losing his inheritance.
- Depicts *Smeerensburg* as a dark, perpetually snowbound island paralyzed by a centuries-old blood feud between two miserable clans, the *Krums* (led by *Mr. Ellingboe*) and the *Ellingboes* (led by *Mrs. Krum*).
- Details Jesper's fateful encounter with the titular character *Klaus*, an imposing yet gentle reclusive woodsman voiced by *J. K. Simmons*, whose house is packed with handmade wooden toys he stopped distributing following the death of his beloved wife.
- Shows how the pair's covert nightly deliveries of toys inspire local children to write letters, attend school with the cynical fishmonger-turned-teacher *Alva*, and perform selfless deeds, steadily disarming the town's ancient cycle of hatred.
- Features technical breakthroughs by *The SPA Studios* and French studio *Les Films du Poisson Rouge*, who engineered proprietary volumetric lighting and texturing software called *KLaS* to give hand-drawn drawings the volumetric depth and subsurface scattering of 3D models.
- Swept the 47th Annie Awards by winning seven categories including Best Animated Feature, won the BAFTA Award for Best Animated Film, and earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature.

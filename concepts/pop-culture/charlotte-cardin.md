## summary

**Charlotte Cardin** is an acclaimed Canadian pop, electro, and jazz-influenced singer-songwriter hailing from Montreal, Quebec. After gaining public attention as a finalist on the televised vocal competition *La Voix*, she established an international following through bilingual releases that merge electro-soul rhythms with intimate lyrical introspection. Her celebrated full-length recordings *Phoenix* and *99 Nights* earned her multiple Juno Awards, establishing her as one of contemporary Francophone Canada's leading musical voices.

## you gotta know

- Rose to regional fame in 2013 as a finalist on the inaugural season of Quebec's television singing competition *La Voix*.
- Released debut solo EPs *Big Boy* in 2016 and *Main Girl* in 2017, generating viral streaming success across North America and Europe.
- Issued her debut studio album *Phoenix* in 2021, which held the number-one position on the Canadian Albums Chart for multiple consecutive weeks.
- Won four major honors at the 2022 Juno Awards, including Artist of the Year, Single of the Year for "Meaningless," and Album of the Year.
- Writes and performs comfortably in both French and English, blending traditional chanson poetics with contemporary pop and rhythm-and-blues production.
- Released her critically acclaimed second studio album *99 Nights* in 2023, accompanied by popular tracks such as "Confetti" and "Jim Carrey."
- Worked professionally as a fashion model before concentrating entirely on songwriting and touring.

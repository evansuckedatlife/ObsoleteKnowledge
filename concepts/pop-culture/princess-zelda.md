## summary

**Princess Zelda**, often referred to simply as **Zelda**, is the titular royal heroine of *Nintendo*'s flagship fantasy adventure franchise, created by *Shigeru Miyamoto* and *Takashi Tezuka* in *[[japan]]* in 1986. While players directly control the swordsman *[[link]]*, Zelda serves as the sovereign ruler or crown princess of Hyrule and the mortal reincarnation of the goddess Hylia. Possessing the sacred Triforce of Wisdom, she is an indispensable figure in quiz-bowl questions on video game lore, celebrated both as a recurring captive and as a formidable magical sorceress.

## you gotta know

- Namesake of the franchise and bearer of the Triforce of Wisdom, regularly allying with *[[link]]* to seal away the dark warlock *[[ganon]]*.
- Named after *Zelda Fitzgerald*, the American novelist and spouse of *F. Scott Fitzgerald*, because creator *Shigeru Miyamoto* found the name alluring and noble.
- Disguised herself as the athletic Sheikah youth **Sheik** in the 1998 classic *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time*, playing the lyre and teaching essential warp melodies.
- Appeared as the spunky pirate captain **Tetra** in the 2002 seafaring release *The Wind Waker*, unaware of her royal bloodline until the King of Red Lions unveils her destiny.
- Established as the mortal reincarnation of the goddess Hylia in the 2011 prequel *Skyward Sword*, bound to an endless cycle of rebirth against the primordial demon Demise.
- Commands divine magic in the *Super Smash Bros.* fighting franchise, wielding spells named after Golden Goddesses like Din's Fire, Farore's Wind, and Nayru's Love.
- In *Breath of the Wild* and *Tears of the Kingdom*, she awakens sealing light after decades of scholarly struggle and undergoes draconification to restore the Master Sword across millennia.
- Finally gained top-billed playable status in a mainline title in 2024's *Echoes of Wisdom*, wielding the Tri Rod to replicate objects and monsters across a fractured Hyrule.

## connections

- [[japan]] — country where Nintendo created, developed, and published the groundbreaking action-adventure series.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow landmark fantasy open-world adventure game that featured exclusive Zelda-themed weapons and armor on the Nintendo Switch.
- [[minecraft]] — ubiquitous sandbox game featuring an official Nintendo-themed texture and mash-up pack that faithfully reconstructs Hyrule castles and dungeons.
- [[professor-layton]] — fellow celebrated puzzle-heavy Nintendo console series originating in Japan that emphasizes deduction, antiquities, and ancient relics.
- [[prozd]] — voice actor and popular internet sketch comedian whose satirical gaming shorts frequently spoof classic fantasy tropes, silent protagonists, and princess rescues.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming sphere in which Zelda and her alter ego Sheik have served as prominent tournament fighter choices in *Super Smash Bros.*.

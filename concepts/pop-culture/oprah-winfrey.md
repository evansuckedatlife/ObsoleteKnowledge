## summary

**Oprah Winfrey**, often known simply as **Oprah**, is an American talk show host, media mogul, actress, and philanthropist who revolutionized daytime television and became one of the most influential cultural figures in modern history. Rising from poverty in rural Mississippi and early career broadcasting in Nashville and Baltimore, she transformed Chicago's low-rated morning broadcast into *The Oprah Winfrey Show*, which aired nationally from 1986 to 2011 as the highest-rated daytime talk show in television history. Through her syndicated program, production company *Harpo Studios*, cable channel *OWN*, and influential cultural initiatives like *Oprah's Book Club*, she reshaped American media, popularised intimate confessional interviewing, and became the first Black female multi-billionaire in North America.

## you gotta know

- Hosted *The Oprah Winfrey Show* across twenty-five seasons from 1986 to 2011, transforming daytime talk television by pioneering the intimate, confessional talk style dubbed *Oprahfication*.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress for her film debut as *Sofia* in *Steven Spielberg*'s 1985 adaptation of *Alice Walker*'s novel *The Color Purple*, later returning to produce both the acclaimed Broadway musical and the 2023 musical film version.
- Founded *Harpo Productions* in 1986, becoming only the third woman in American entertainment history to own her own television and film production studio, which produced acclaimed films such as *Beloved* and television phenomena like *Dr. Phil*.
- Launched *Oprah's Book Club* in 1996, single-handedly reviving sales for classic and contemporary novels such as *Toni Morrison*'s *Song of Solomon*, wielding unparalleled gatekeeping influence across modern [[american-literature]].
- Exercised enormous political influence through her endorsement of *Barack Obama* in the 2008 Democratic primary, which political scientists estimated delivered over one million votes in a pivotal victory for the [[civil-rights-movement]] legacy.
- Conducted monumental, record-breaking broadcast interviews, including her 1993 live conversation with *Michael Jackson* that drew ninety million viewers and her bombshell 2021 interview with *Prince Harry* and *Meghan Markle*.
- Co-founded the *Oxygen* cable network, launched *O, The Oprah Magazine*, established the *Oprah Winfrey Network* (*OWN*), and founded the *Oprah Winfrey Leadership Academy for Girls* in South Africa through extensive philanthropic giving.

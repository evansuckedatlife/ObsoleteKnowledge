## summary

**The Godfather** is a seminal 1972 American crime drama film directed by *Francis Ford Coppola*, adapted from *Mario Puzo*'s bestselling 1969 novel of the same name. Chronicling the powerful Italian-American Corleone crime syndicate from 1945 to 1955, the narrative charts the transformation of war hero *Michael Corleone* from reluctant outsider to ruthless mob patriarch. Frequently ranked among the greatest and most influential films in cinematic history, it won three Academy Awards—including Best Picture and Best Actor for *Marlon Brando*—and established a new paradigm for American gangster storytelling.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Francis Ford Coppola* and produced by *Albert S. Ruddy*, revitalizing Hollywood's New Hollywood era alongside cinematographer *Gordon Willis*.
- *Marlon Brando* plays the aging don *Vito Corleone*, crafting iconic mannerisms with cotton balls in his cheeks, a raspy whisper, and an unhurried, calculating demeanor.
- *Al Pacino* delivered a breakthrough performance as *Michael Corleone*, whose moral descent from decorated war veteran to calculating Mafia don forms the movie's tragic emotional spine.
- Features the famous baptism sequence, an extraordinary masterclass in parallel film editing that crosscuts between *Michael* renouncing Satan in church and the brutal simultaneous executions of rival Five Families bosses.
- Contains widely quoted lines such as "I'm gonna make him an offer he can't refuse" and the notorious image of film producer *Jack Woltz* waking up next to the severed head of his prized racehorse *Khartoum*.
- Won three Oscars at the 45th Academy Awards—Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Actor—which *Marlon Brando* famously declined by sending activist *Sacheen Littlefeather* in protest of Hollywood's treatment of Native Americans.
- Spawned *The Godfather Part II* in 1974, which made history as the first sequel to win the Academy Award for Best Picture.

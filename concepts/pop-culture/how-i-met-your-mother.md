## summary

**How I Met Your Mother** is an American television sitcom created by *Craig Thomas* and *Carter Bays* that aired on CBS from 2005 to 2014 across nine seasons and 208 episodes. Set predominantly in Manhattan, the series uses an elaborate framing device in which an older protagonist narrates his youth to his children in the future year 2030. It became an essential cultural touchstone of twenty-first-century broadcast comedy, noted for its innovative non-linear narrative structure, recurring gags, and cinematic continuity.

## you gotta know

- Framed around *Ted Mosby*, voiced in older narration by *Bob Saget* and portrayed in live action by *Josh Radnor*, recounting the sprawling story of his twenties and thirties to his teenage son and daughter.
- Follows a close-knit group of five friends living in [[new-york-city]]: architect *Ted*, aspiring artist *Lily Aldrin*, law student turned judge *Marshall Eriksen*, Canadian broadcast journalist *Robin Scherbatsky*, and eccentric playboy *Barney Stinson*.
- Renowned for distinct running motifs and catchphrases, notably *Barney*'s catchphrases such as "legendary" and "suit up," the Playbook of seduction schemes, the Bro Code, the Slap Bet officiated by *Lily*, and the symbolic yellow umbrella.
- The core ensemble spent their leisure hours in a booth at *MacLaren's Pub*, an Irish bar situated directly underneath *Ted* and *Marshall*'s apartment, modeled after the real-world New York establishment *McGee's*.
- Famously broke traditional multi-camera sitcom boundaries established by predecessors like [[i-love-lucy]] by shooting without a live studio audience to accommodate rapid-fire scene transitions, flashbacks, unreliable narration, flash-forwards, and visual gags.
- Introduced the titular mother character, *Tracy McConnell*, played by *Cristin Milioti*, at the climax of the eighth season before centering the entire ninth season on the weekend of *Barney* and *Robin*'s ill-fated wedding in Far Rockaway.
- Concluded with an intensely debated series finale in 2014, in which *Tracy*'s terminal illness is revealed, reframing *Ted*'s entire twenty-five-year retrospective confession as an implicit request for his children's blessing to pursue *Robin*.

## summary

**Iron Man**, the alter ego of billionaire weapons manufacturer **Tony Stark** (formally **Anthony Edward Stark** and occasionally hailed as the **Golden Avenger**), is a superhero created for Marvel Comics by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber, Don Heck, and Jack Kirby. Debuting in 1963, Stark uses his extraordinary mechanical intellect to design sophisticated armored suits that sustain his damaged heart and protect the globe. The character became the cultural anchor of twenty-first-century comic book cinema following Robert Downey Jr.'s seminal cinematic performance in 2008.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in *Tales of Suspense* #39 in March 1963, originally conceived by *Stan Lee* as an explicit challenge to make an arrogant, capitalist weapons manufacturer appealing to anti-war, youth counterculture readers during the height of geopolitical conflict.
- Suffers catastrophic shrapnel wounds to the chest following an explosive trap and is captured by warlord *Wong-Chu* during an inspection tour in Vietnam, an origin retroactively updated in modern comics to the Gulf War and Afghanistan.
- Constructs the bulky grey Mark I armor alongside Nobel-winning co-captive physicist *Ho Yinsen*, who heroically sacrifices his life to buy Stark the critical seconds needed to charge the suit's weapons.
- Sustains his life through an electrified chest plate and magnetic arc reactor, designed to prevent metal fragments from migrating into his heart tissue while powering his lethal repulsor rays.
- Stands as a co-founding member of the *Avengers* alongside Thor, the Hulk, Ant-Man, and the Wasp in *The Avengers* #1 in September 1963, routinely acting as the team's chief financier and technological benefactor.
- Explored deep psychological vulnerability in the acclaimed 1979 story arc *Demon in a Bottle*, written by *David Michelinie* and *Bob Layton*, which confronted his crippling struggle with alcoholism.
- Serves as the ideological antagonist to Captain America in the landmark 2006 comic crossover *Civil War*, championing government oversight under the controversial Superhero Registration Act.
- Served as the foundation for the *Marvel Cinematic Universe* through the 2008 film directed by *Jon Favreau*, concluding Stark's narrative arc with his universe-saving sacrifice against Thanos in *Avengers: Endgame*.

## summary

**Dallas** is an iconic American prime-time television soap opera created by *David Jacobs* that aired on CBS from April 1978 to May 1991. Revolving around the affluent, feuding Ewing family—owners of the independent oil company *Ewing Oil* and the sprawling cattle ranch *Southfork*—the series captivated global audiences with its ruthless business scheming and turbulent personal relationships. It redefined serialized television drama, popularizing the cliffhanger format and influencing decades of television worldwide.

## you gotta know

- The central antagonist and breakout antihero of the series was *J.R. Ewing*, portrayed with charismatic villainy by *Larry Hagman*, whose greedy machinations and trademark Stetson hat made him a worldwide cultural icon.
- The Season 3 finale cliffhanger in March 1980, titled "A House Divided," concluded with J.R. being shot by an unseen assailant, initiating the historic pop-culture sensation and promotional catchphrase "Who shot J.R.?".
- The resolution episode, "Who Done It?", aired on November 21, 1980, and revealed that J.R.'s sister-in-law and mistress *Kristin Shepard*, played by *Mary Crosby*, fired the gun; the broadcast drew over eighty million American viewers and became one of the most-watched television episodes in history.
- The core emotional dynamic of the Ewing dynasty pitted J.R. against his morally upright younger brother *Bobby Ewing*, played by *Patrick Duffy*, whose marriage to *Pamela Barnes*, played by *Victoria Principal*, sparked a bitter family feud reminiscent of Romeo and Juliet.
- The series sparked one of the most famous retcons in television history when Bobby Ewing, killed off at the end of Season 8, was restored to life in the Season 9 finale when Pamela awakened to find him in the shower, erasing the entire previous season as a dream.
- The family patriarch and matriarch were *Jock Ewing*, played by *Jim Davis*, and *Miss Ellie Ewing*, played by *Barbara Bel Geddes*, whose ranch *Southfork* in Parker, Texas, became a major international tourist attraction.
- The franchise spawned the successful spin-off series *Knots Landing*, which followed outcast brother *Gary Ewing* and ran for fourteen seasons, as well as a 2012 revival on TNT that brought back Hagman, Duffy, and Linda Gray alongside a new generation of Ewings.

## summary

**Beyoncé** is an American singer, songwriter, producer, and cultural icon who rose to international fame in the late 1990s as the lead vocalist of the R&B girl group *Destiny's Child* before launching an era-defining solo career. Renowned for her mezzo-soprano vocal agility, demanding live choreography, and ambitious audiovisual concept albums, she has accumulated thirty-two Grammy Awards, more than any other artist in history. Her creative evolution across pop, soul, trap, and dance music transformed modern album rollouts and established her as a preeminent voice of twenty-first-century Black feminism throughout the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Rose to international stardom alongside *Kelly Rowland* and *Michelle Williams* in *Destiny's Child*, delivering chart-topping global anthems including *Say My Name*, *Survivor*, and *Bootylicious*.
- Launched her blockbuster solo career with the 2003 multi-platinum album *Dangerously in Love*, which yielded the timeless worldwide hit *Crazy in Love* featuring her future husband, rapper *Jay-Z*.
- Created a viral pop-culture touchstone with the 2008 single *Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)*, featuring an iconic black-and-white dance video modeled after the choreography of *Bob Fosse*.
- Revolutionized the music industry's standard promotional models in December 2013 by dropping her self-titled visual album *Beyoncé* overnight without any prior warning or marketing campaign.
- Released the critically acclaimed 2016 audiovisual concept album *Lemonade*, which explored marital infidelity, generational trauma, African diasporic traditions, and Black resilience across the American South.
- Became the first Black woman to headline the *Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival* in 2018, celebrating historically Black colleges and universities (*HBCUs*) in a monumental performance documented in the concert film *Homecoming*.
- Embarked on an expansive three-act cultural reclamation project beginning with the dance and ballroom-inspired 2022 album *Renaissance* and continuing with the 2024 country-oriented *Cowboy Carter*.
- Became the center of a viral 2018 pop-culture whodunit after comedian *Tiffany Haddish* publicly revealed that an unnamed actress had bitten the singer on the face at a private Hollywood gathering, giving rise to the widespread [[bitten]] internet mystery.

## summary

**Ganon**, often introduced in his mortal Gerudo form as **Ganondorf**, is the primary antagonist of *[[nintendo]]*'s *The Legend of Zelda* video game franchise created in *[[japan]]*. Across centuries and alternate timelines, he embodies the Triforce of Power, continually seeking to conquer the kingdom of Hyrule and overthrow *[[princess-zelda]]* and the legendary hero *[[link]]*. Serving as a quintessential archetype of cyclical villainy in gaming, he represents the eternal hatred of the ancient demon king Demise reincarnated to challenge each new generation.

## you gotta know

- First appeared in the original 1986 *The Legend of Zelda* on the Famicom Disk System in *[[japan]]* as a blue, boar-like sorcerer who steals the Triforce of Power and kidnaps *[[princess-zelda]]*.
- The human incarnation named *Ganondorf Dragmire* was introduced in *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* (1998), depicted as the sole male born every hundred years to the desert-dwelling Gerudo tribe who becomes their king.
- His defeat in *Ocarina of Time* fractures the franchise narrative into three divergent chronological timelines, each tracing different fates of Hyrule depending on whether the hero *[[link]]* triumphs as a child, triumphs as an adult, or falls in battle.
- In *The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker* (2002), he displays rare depth and philosophical melancholy, lamenting how the harsh winds of the desert brought death while the winds of Hyrule brought life, driving his desperate invasion before being impaled in stone beneath the Great Sea.
- Manifests in *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild* (2017) as *Calamity Ganon*, a primal, formless malice that possesses Hyrule's ancient mechanical Guardians and Divine Beasts, devastating the kingdom in the Great Calamity.
- The prequel *Skyward Sword* (2011) establishes his cosmological origin through the demonic curse of Demise, decreeing that an incarnation of demonic hatred will endlessly pursue the blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero.
- In competitive fighting games, he appears as a heavy clone fighter of Captain Falcon in *Super Smash Bros. Melee*, later gaining his own distinct moveset featuring the Sword of the Creator and dark sorcery.

## connections

- [[link]] — the eternal bearer of the Triforce of Courage whose destiny is to thwart Ganon across millennia.
- [[princess-zelda]] — the mortal incarnation of the goddess Hylia holding the Triforce of Wisdom who seals Ganon away.
- [[nintendo]] — the Kyoto-based gaming giant in *[[japan]]* that established Ganon as one of the premier villains in entertainment.
- [[japan]] — the nation whose game development golden age gave rise to Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka's fantasy mythology.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow landmark fantasy action role-playing game whose open-world design was heavily compared to Ganon's world in *Breath of the Wild*.
- [[minecraft]] — ubiquitous sandbox title featuring official crossover mash-up packs depicting Hyrule and Ganon's ominous stronghold.

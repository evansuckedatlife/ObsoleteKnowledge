## summary

**Lost Girl** is a Canadian supernatural drama television series created by M. A. Lovretta that originally aired on Showcase from 2010 to 2015. The series follows *Bo Dennis*, a bisexual succubus who discovers her supernatural heritage within an underground society of mythological beings known as the Fae. Blending folklore, urban fantasy, and procedural investigation, the program earned acclaim for its sex-positive themes and prominent representation of queer relationships.

## you gotta know

- Created by *M. A. Lovretta*, who later developed the cult space-opera television series *Killjoys*.
- Centers on *Bo Dennis*, portrayed by *Anna Silk*, a succubus who feeds on the sexual life energy of humans and initially struggles with guilt over inadvertently killing her high school sweetheart.
- Introduces the secret division of the Fae into two politically opposed factions, the *Light Fae* and the *Dark Fae*, with Bo asserting herself as an unaffiliated, neutral renegade.
- Features *Kenzi Malikov*, played by *Ksenia Solo*, a sharp-tongued human grifter who acts as Bo's best friend, moral compass, and business partner in running a private investigation firm.
- Features prominent romantic arcs with the Light Fae wolf shapeshifter *Dyson* and human physician *Lauren Lewis*, cementing the show's landmark status in LGBTQ+ genre fiction.
- Delves into the lore of the *Blood King* and ancient underworld deities, drawing directly upon Celtic, Norse, and Greco-Roman mythology.
- Aired for five seasons spanning seventy-seven episodes, concluding in 2015 with a finale resolving Bo's true ancestry and the identity of her father, the ancient deity *Hades*.

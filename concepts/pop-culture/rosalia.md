## summary

**Rosalía** is a Spanish singer, songwriter, producer, and musician acclaimed for modernizing traditional flamenco music through contemporary urban, electronic, and avant-pop styles. Born *Rosalía Vila Tobella* in Sant Esteve Sesrovires, Catalonia, she established international critical acclaim with her concept albums and boundary-pushing vocal arrangements. Her innovative synthesis of classical Hispanic traditions and cutting-edge global production has made her one of the most prominent Spanish-language artists of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Trained formally in flamenco and music production at the *Escola Superior de Música de Catalunya*, studying under master *Chiqui de Jerez*.
- Gained international breakthrough acclaim with her 2018 sophomore concept album *El Mal Querer*, structured around the fourteenth-century Occitan romance *Flamenca*.
- Delivered massive international streaming hits by blending urban reggaeton with art-pop, notably collaborating with *J Balvin* on *Con Altura*.
- Released the critically lauded 2022 studio album *Motomami*, which explored themes of transformation, fame, vulnerability, and dual identity across eclectic rhythms.
- Incorporates signature lyrical and visual motifs inspired by traditional Andalusian vocal ornamentation, motorcycle culture, Japanese pop aesthetics, and Catholic iconography.
- Won multiple *Grammy Awards* and Latin Grammy Awards, becoming the first artist to earn a Best New Artist Grammy nomination singing entirely in Spanish.
- Expanded her musical versatility through high-profile collaborations with international artists such as *The Weeknd*, *Billie Eilish*, *Travis Scott*, and *Tokischa*.

## summary

**Alex Lifeson** (born **Aleksandar Živojinović**) is a Canadian musician, songwriter, and producer best known as the guitarist and co-founder of the progressive rock power trio *Rush*. Alongside bassist *Geddy Lee* and drummer-lyricist *Neil Peart*, he anchored the band from its 1968 inception in Toronto until its final tour in 2015. Celebrated for his innovative chord voicings, arpeggios, textural guitar synths, and blistering solos, *Lifeson* played an indispensable role in shaping progressive hard rock across North America and the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Co-founded *Rush* in 1968 with drummer *John Rutsey* and bassist *Jeff Jones*, quickly inviting childhood friend *Geddy Lee* to take over lead vocals and bass.
- Pioneered the use of complex suspended and resonant chords, notably the "F-sharp major with open B and high E strings" chord frequently dubbed the "Alex Lifeson chord" (prominently heard in "Hemispheres" and "Far Cry").
- Composed the sweeping, iconic acoustic and electric guitar movements on conceptual epics such as "2112", "Cygnus X-1", and "La Villa Strangiato", the latter featuring one of rock's most celebrated multi-part guitar solos.
- Delivered the famously explosive guitar solo on the radio staple "Limelight" from the 1981 landmark album *Moving Pictures*, showcasing his mastery of whammy bar technique and emotional phrasing.
- Delivered a notoriously surreal acceptance speech at the 2013 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony, repeating only the phrase "blah, blah, blah" with varied theatrical inflections for several minutes.
- Released the solo album *Victor* in 1996 and later formed the project *Envy of None* in 2021, showcasing atmospheric alternative and electronic rock styles.
- Is an avid aviation enthusiast and licensed, instrument-rated private pilot who frequently flew himself between tour stops.
- Made notable comedy appearances in Canadian television, playing an exaggerated, kidnapped version of himself in *Trailer Park Boys*.

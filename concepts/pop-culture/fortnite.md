## summary

**Fortnite** is a landmark multiplayer online video game developed by Epic Games and released in 2017 in the *[[united-states]]*. While originally conceived as a cooperative base-building survival title known as *Fortnite: Save the World*, the game achieved unprecedented global ubiquity through its free-to-play standalone mode, **Fortnite Battle Royale**. By merging fast-paced tactical gunplay with instant architectural construction, competitive live-service updates, and massive cross-media promotional spectacles, the title redefined modern interactive entertainment and transformed competitive digital culture.

## you gotta know

- Developed by Epic Games under lead design director *Darren Sugg* and Epic CEO *Tim Sweeney*, launching its breakout free-to-play battle royale mode in September 2017 after the sudden commercial ascent of *PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds*.
- Matches drop one hundred players from an aerial *Battle Bus* onto a shrinking island map surrounded by a lethal approaching storm, forcing contestants into elimination combat until a solitary survivor or squad claims a *Victory Royale*.
- Features a signature structural building mechanic utilizing wood, stone, and metal gathered via a harvesting pickaxe, enabling players to fabricate high-speed defensive towers, ramps, and tactical fortifications mid-firefight.
- Pioneered the mainstream live-service economic framework of the seasonal *Battle Pass*, monetizing cosmetic outfits, harvesting tools, gliders, and celebratory dance emotes such as the *Floss* without selling competitive gameplay advantages.
- Cultivated a massive competitive [[esports]] presence highlighted by the 2019 *Fortnite World Cup* at Arthur Ashe Stadium in *[[new-york-city]]*, where sixteen-year-old American competitor *Kyle "Bugha" Giersdorf* won the three-million-dollar solo championship.
- Transformed into a virtual metaverse venue staging live interactive concerts by musical artists including *Travis Scott*, *Marshmello*, and *Ariana Grande*, alongside film trailer premieres and digital community events.
- Introduced the *Zero Build* permanent mode in 2022 to remove defensive construction mechanics entirely, revitalizing player retention by emphasizing pure movement, positioning, and traditional shooting fundamentals.
- Integrated expansive third-party intellectual property crossovers spanning global franchises, incorporating skins ranging from superhero icons and anime characters to musical legends like *[[freddie-mercury]]* through the rhythm mode *Fortnite Festival*.

## connections

- [[esports]] — elevated tournament prize pools, streaming viewership records on Twitch, and arena-scale global competitions.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — drew heavy inspiration from competitive shooter lineage while distinguishing itself through third-person camera perspective and instant environmental building.
- [[counter-strike]] — shares foundational heritage within competitive multiplayer shooter scenes and skin-trading economies.
- [[minecraft]] — popularized procedural voxel resource gathering and rapid crafting systems that inspired *Fortnite*'s architectural construction engine.
- [[freddie-mercury]] — Queen frontman whose likeness, iconic yellow jacket, and vocal anthems entered the game's rhythm-based *Fortnite Festival* mode.
- [[united-states]] — home nation of developer Epic Games, headquartered in Cary, North Carolina, and primary hub of its initial esports infrastructure.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow landmark open-world title representing fantasy role-playing immersion and sprawling modding ecosystems.

## summary

**Rihanna**, born **Robyn Rihanna Fenty**, is a Barbadian singer, songwriter, actress, and billionaire businesswoman who became one of the most commercially successful and influential recording artists of the twenty-first century. After relocating to the [[united-states]] as a teenager, she released an uninterrupted sequence of era-defining pop, dancehall, and contemporary R&B anthems including *Umbrella*, *Diamonds*, and *Work*. Alongside her transformative musical catalog and acclaimed album *Anti*, she revolutionized modern cosmetics and fashion through her inclusive brand *Fenty Beauty* and was declared an official National Hero of Barbados.

## you gotta know

- Achieved fourteen number-one singles on the *Billboard* Hot 100 chart, placing her among the top chart-topping performers in popular music history alongside *The Beatles* and *Mariah Carey*.
- Launched *Fenty Beauty* in 2017 in partnership with luxury conglomerate *LVMH*, debuting with forty foundation shades to address systemic shade exclusion and prompting an industry-wide transformation known as the *Fenty Effect*.
- Headline performer for the *Super Bowl LVII* halftime show in 2023, commanding the stage suspended on floating platforms while visibly pregnant and drawing one of the largest worldwide broadcast audiences in television history.
- Designated a *National Hero of Barbados* with the title *The Right Excellent* by Prime Minister *Mia Mottley* in November 2021 as Barbados officially severed its ties to the British Crown to become a republic.
- Released the landmark 2016 studio album *Anti*, earning universal critical praise for abandoning conventional commercial dance-pop formulas in favor of experimental soul, dub, and psychedelic production.
- Ventured prominently into cinematic acting and luxury design, co-starring in major ensemble releases such as *Ocean's 8* and *Battleship* and founding the inclusive lingerie line *Savage X Fenty*.
- Discovered in Saint Michael, Barbados, by producer *Evan Rogers* before traveling to [[new-york-city]] to audition for *Jay-Z*, who reportedly signed her to *Def Jam Recordings* on the spot past midnight.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — nation where she relocated at age sixteen to begin her professional recording career and achieve unprecedented international commercial dominance.
- [[new-york-city]] — metropolitan hub where she secured her original record deal at *Def Jam* and established herself as a celebrated icon at the annual *Met Gala*.
- [[british-empire]] — historic colonial power that governed Barbados for centuries until the island nation established its full republic status, coinciding with her national hero appointment.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — pivotal twentieth-century campaign whose cultural legacy opened mainstream American media institutions to Black female artistic leadership and business ownership.
- [[modernism]] — aesthetic current of formal disruption reflected in her innovative cross-genre musical shifts, high-concept music videos, and boundary-pushing fashion statements.
- [[realism]] — artistic sensibility that informed the candid lyricism, raw vocal vulnerability, and unvarnished personal themes across her later records.

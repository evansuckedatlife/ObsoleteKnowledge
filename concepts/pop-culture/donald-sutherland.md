## summary

**Donald Sutherland** was a towering Canadian actor whose versatile career spanned six decades across cinema and television. Known for his distinctive voice, piercing eyes, and remarkable range, he gained international fame in countercultural classics such as *The Dirty Dozen*, *M*A*S*H*, and *Klute*. Sutherland brought nuanced psychological complexity to roles ranging from traumatized fathers in *Ordinary People* to authoritarian antagonists like President Coriolanus Snow in *The Hunger Games* franchise.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed the tyrannical President Coriolanus Snow across four installments of *The Hunger Games* film series, introducing his commanding presence to modern global audiences.
- Starred as the anti-establishment surgeon Captain Benjamin Franklin *Hawkeye* Pierce in Robert Altman's satirical 1970 anti-war masterpiece *M*A*S*H*.
- Gained his international breakout role playing Vernon Pinkley, one of the convicts recruited for a suicide mission, in Robert Aldrich's 1967 war classic *The Dirty Dozen*.
- Received critical acclaim for his haunting performance opposite Mary Tyler Moore as grieving father Calvin Jarrett in Robert Redford's Best Picture-winning drama *Ordinary People*.
- Collaborated with Jane Fonda in the 1971 neo-noir thriller *Klute*, portraying the reserved private detective John Klute.
- Led Nicolas Roeg's celebrated 1973 psychological horror masterpiece *Don't Look Now* opposite Julie Christie as a father mourning a drowned daughter in Venice.
- Received an Academy Honorary Award in 2017 celebrating his lifetime contributions to motion pictures despite never having received a competitive Oscar nomination.

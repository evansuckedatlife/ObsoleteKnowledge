## summary

**Khaby Lame** is a Senegalese-born Italian social media personality who became the most followed creator on the global video-sharing platform *TikTok*. Rising to fame during the 2020 lockdowns in Chivasso, *[[italy]]*, he captivated worldwide audiences by reacting wordlessly to absurd life-hack videos with exasperated common sense. His universally understood pantomime revived the universal appeal of *[[silent-film]]* traditions for the short-form mobile video era.

## you gotta know

- Surpassed American dancer *Charli D'Amelio* in June 2022 to become the most followed creator on *TikTok*, amassing over one hundred and sixty million followers.
- Created a signature viral visual template by reacting to overcomplicated internet *life hacks* with deadpan silence, performing the task simply, and extending both palms in an exasperated shrug.
- Utilized wordless pantomime and expressive facial gestures, intentionally avoiding spoken dialogue to transcend language barriers and achieve immediate international resonance.
- Lost his job as a factory machine operator in northern *[[italy]]* during the early coronavirus pandemic, prompting him to start recording comedy sketches in his bedroom.
- Emulated classic comedic forebears of *[[physical-comedy]]* and *[[slapstick-comedy]]*, regularly citing screen icons *[[charlie-chaplin]]* and *[[buster-keaton]]* as inspirations for his non-verbal humor.
- Served as a guest juror at the prestigious *Venice International Film Festival* in 2022 to evaluate short digital film submissions.
- Formally granted Italian citizenship in August 2022 in his hometown of Chivasso after living in the country since infancy.

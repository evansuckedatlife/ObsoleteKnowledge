## summary

**Danny Elfman** is an acclaimed American composer, singer, and songwriter celebrated for his distinctive, gothic-orchestral film scores and his leadership of the new wave band *Oingo Boingo*. He is widely celebrated for his decades-long creative collaboration with director *Tim Burton*, producing iconic scores featuring whimsical choirs, brass-heavy rhythms, and macabre themes. Across a career bridging rock music and Hollywood cinematic orchestration, his contributions to modern media have earned major honors including a Grammy Award and multiple Academy Award nominations.

## you gotta know

- Fronted the eclectic rock band *Oingo Boingo* from the late 1970s through the mid-1990s, penning cult favorite tracks such as *Dead Man's Party* and *Weird Science*.
- Began his seminal scoring collaboration with director *Tim Burton* on the comedy *Pee-wee's Big Adventure*, establishing an eccentric orchestral voice combining circus music and dark romanticism.
- Composed the instantly recognizable, carnivalesque opening theme for the long-running animated television sitcom *The Simpsons*, performed with iconic choral motifs.
- Provided both the orchestral score and the singing voice for the character *Jack Skellington* in the animated classic *The Nightmare Before Christmas*.
- Defined the sound of cinematic comic book heroism by composing the driving, gothic orchestral themes for *Batman* and *Batman Returns*, as well as *Sam Raimi*'s *Spider-Man*.
- Earned Academy Award nominations for his work on *Good Will Hunting* and *Men in Black*, showcasing a compositional range extending well beyond gothic fantasy.
- Traveled to *France* and West Africa in his youth, absorbing global musical traditions and percussion styles that later influenced his syncopated, rhythm-heavy Hollywood orchestrations.

## summary

**Dexter** is an American television drama series that aired on *Showtime* from 2006 to 2013, adapted from *Jeff Lindsay*'s 2004 novel *Darkly Dreaming Dexter*. The series stars *Michael C. Hall* as **Dexter Morgan**, a Miami Metro Police Department forensic blood spatter analyst who secretly leads a double life as a vigilante serial killer. The critically acclaimed show examined morality, psychopathy, and suburban disguise during the Golden Age of Television.

## you gotta know

- Developed for television by *James Manos Jr.*, based on the novel series created by crime author *Jeff Lindsay*.
- Stars *Michael C. Hall* as *Dexter Morgan*, an emotionally detached forensic blood spatter specialist working for the fictional *Miami Metro Police Department*.
- Follows the *Code of Harry*, a strict set of moral survival rules taught by his adoptive police officer father *Harry Morgan* to channel his violent urges exclusively toward unpunished murderers.
- Refers to his homicidal impulse as his *Dark Passenger*, born from early childhood trauma when he witnessed his mother's brutal murder in a shipping container.
- Key characters include his foster sister and detective *Debra Morgan* (played by *Jennifer Carpenter*) and his suspicious colleague Sergeant *James Doakes*, famed for the catchphrase "Surprise, motherfucker!"
- Season 4 earned widespread acclaim for *John Lithgow*'s guest performance as the elusive and terrifying *Trinity Killer* (*Arthur Mitchell*).
- Continued with the 2021 revival miniseries *Dexter: New Blood*, addressing the widely criticized lumberjack ending of the original eighth season.

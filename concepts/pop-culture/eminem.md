## summary

**Eminem** is the stage name of *Marshall Mathers*, an American rapper, songwriter, and record producer who stands as one of the best-selling and most technically revered musical artists in modern history. Emerging from the underground rap battles of *Detroit*, *Michigan* under the mentorship of producer *Dr. Dre*, he shattered commercial boundaries as a white artist in a Black-dominated genre. Through technical dexterity, controversial shock-rap humor, and deeply vulnerable autobiographical storytelling, he became the defining hip-hop icon of the turn of the millennium.

## you gotta know

- Achieved colossal critical and commercial acclaim with the successive albums *The Slim Shady LP*, *The Marshall Mathers LP*, and *The Eminem Show*, making him the top-selling musical artist of the 2000s in the *United States*.
- Won the *Academy Award for Best Original Song* for *Lose Yourself* from the semi-autobiographical motion picture *8 Mile*, marking the first time a rap song received an Oscar.
- Created the maniacal, bleach-blonde alter ego **Slim Shady** to express grotesque satire, comic-book violence, and nihilistic humor distinct from his serious autobiographical persona.
- Celebrated for virtuoso technical rhyming mechanics, bending vowel pronunciations to assemble intricate multisyllabic rhymes and speed-flow cadences on tracks like *Rap God*.
- Co-founded *Shady Records*, signing and developing major multi-platinum hip-hop talents such as *50 Cent* and his childhood *Detroit* rap ensemble *D12*.
- Conquered severe prescription drug dependency following a near-fatal 2007 overdose, detailing his sobriety, grief, and artistic reinvention on albums like *Relapse* and *Recovery*.
- Popularized the cultural and lexicographical term "stan" via his haunting hit single *Stan*, dramatizing an unhealthily obsessed fan writing letters to his idol.

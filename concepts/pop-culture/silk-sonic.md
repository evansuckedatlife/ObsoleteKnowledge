## summary

**Silk Sonic** is an American musical superduo formed in 2021 by pop vocalist *Bruno Mars* and singer-drummer *Anderson .Paak*. Channeling the warm, luxurious production of 1970s Philadelphia soul, funk, and vintage rhythm and blues, the project revitalized traditional analog arrangements and live musicianship within contemporary mainstream pop. Their debut single *Leave the Door Open* became a massive critical and commercial triumph, sweeping the major categories at the *Grammy Awards* and establishing the group as a premier retro-soul showcase.

## you gotta know

- Conceived in 2017 during the European leg of *Bruno Mars*'s *24K Magic World Tour*, where opening act *Anderson .Paak* began improvising backstage jam sessions that laid the groundwork for their shared vintage sound.
- Named by iconic *Parliament-Funkadelic* bassist *Bootsy Collins*, who christened the duo and served as the guest narrator and host on their studio material.
- Released their chart-topping breakout single *Leave the Door Open* in March 2021, which reached number one on the *Billboard Hot 100* and won four *Grammy Awards*, including *Record of the Year* and *Song of the Year*.
- Issued their debut studio record *An Evening with Silk Sonic* in November 2021, crafted in collaboration with producer *D'Mile* and featuring arrangements defined by orchestral glockenspiels, live horn sections, and analog bass grooves.
- Adopted a flamboyant 1970s stage aesthetic across all public performances, sporting matching leisure suits, aviator sunglasses, and retro gold chains while executing synchronized dance choreography.
- Staged an extended residency titled *An Evening with Silk Sonic at Park MGM* in Las Vegas, enforcing a mandatory phone-free policy to recreate the communal intimacy of historic supper-club reviews.
- Chose to withdraw their celebrated debut project from consideration for the 2023 *Grammy Awards*, declining to campaign further after dominating the ceremony during the previous cycle.

## summary

**Bad Guy** is a genre-bending electropop and trap-pop single released in 2019 by American singer-songwriter *Billie Eilish*. Serving as the fifth single from her record-breaking debut studio album *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?*, the track catapulted *Eilish* to global superstardom and redefined the sonic textures of contemporary commercial pop music. It remains celebrated in music trivia for ending the longest-running number-one reign in American chart history and sweeping the major categories at the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards.

## you gotta know

- Performed by *Billie Eilish* and co-written with her brother and primary producer *Finneas O'Connell*, who recorded and engineered the track in their modest childhood bedroom studio in Highland Park, Los Angeles.
- Dethroned the monolithic nineteen-week chart run of *Lil Nas X*'s "Old Town Road" in August 2019, securing *Eilish* her first number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100 in the [[united-states]].
- Swept both Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the 2020 Grammy Awards, making the eighteen-year-old *Eilish* the youngest artist ever to sweep all four general field Grammy categories in a single evening.
- Characterized by its skeletal, subversive production arrangement that relies almost entirely on a driving synthesized bassline, snapping finger clicks, whispered layered vocals, and an irreverent spoken-word "duh" preceding the instrumental bass drop.
- Features an abrupt, menacing tempo change in its outro, dropping into an ominous half-time trap groove driven by pitch-shifted vocals where *Eilish* taunts that she is "only good at being bad."
- Accompanied by a surreal, saturated music video directed by *Dave Meyers*, featuring memorable visual motifs including yellow prison jumpsuits, a gushing nosebleed, a wall of floating cereal, and *Eilish* cruising on a tiny red miniature car.
- Spawned a widely discussed July 2019 remix featuring Canadian pop superstar *Justin Bieber*, whose promotional single artwork famously featured a childhood photograph of *Eilish* surrounded by fan posters of *Bieber*.

## summary

**Sorcery of Thorns** is a 2019 young adult fantasy novel written by American author *Margaret Rogerson* and published by *Margaret K. McElderry Books*. Set in an alternate, gothic-infused historical kingdom reminiscent of nineteenth-century *[[england]]*, the narrative centers on an apprentice librarian raised among living, sentient grimoires who must uncover a conspiracy that threatens both the great libraries and the realm. Praised for its lush atmospheric worldbuilding, bibliophile themes, and dynamic character relationships, the standalone novel became a *New York Times* bestseller and a prominent title in contemporary romantic fantasy.

## you gotta know

- Follows the protagonist *Elisabeth Scrivener*, an orphan raised as a foundling inside the *Summershall Great Library*, who aspires to become a sword-wielding *Warden* tasked with guarding perilous magical texts.
- Operates on the unique magic system where grimoires are conscious magical organisms that whisper, rattle their chains, possess distinct personalities, and transform into monstrous demonic entities called *Maleficts* if mistreated or damaged.
- Involves Elisabeth being framed for the sabotage of *Summershall* and the murder of the library's Director, *Katrien*, following the catastrophic release of a high-ranking, lethal class of grimoire.
- Features the aloof high-society sorcerer *Nathaniel Thorn*, whose family carries a grim bloodline legacy, and his sardonic demon companion *Silas*, who appears as an immaculate servant in exchange for years of Nathaniel's lifespan.
- Examines themes of prejudice and indoctrination, as Elisabeth was taught all sorcery is inherently evil and demonic, only to realize the sorcerers and Wardens are entangled in institutional corruption orchestrated by Chancellor *Ashcroft*.
- Culminates in an assault on the capital city of *Austermeer*, where Elisabeth wields ancestral Warden blades and allies with awakened library grimoires to prevent the summoning of the archdemon *Katzenjammer*.
- Expanded in 2023 with the companion novella *Mysteries of Thorn Manor*, which follows Elisabeth, Nathaniel, and Silas navigating the magical eccentricities of the Thorn family estate after the events of the novel.

## connections

- [[american-literature]] — forms part of the prominent contemporary wave of young adult fantasy fiction produced by American novelists in the late 2010s.
- [[england]] — serves as the primary geographic, architectural, and cultural template for the fictional kingdom of *Austermeer* and its class hierarchy.
- [[enlightenment]] — echoed in the novel's thematic tension between institutional preservation of classified knowledge, academic science, and superstitious fear.
- [[industrialization]] — mirrors the urban development, social stratification, and municipal expansion depicted within the capital city setting.
- [[renaissance]] — reflected in the historical aesthetics of classical scholarship, illuminated manuscripts, and antiquarian bookbinding traditions.
- [[united-states]] — publishing origin of the work and home base of author *Margaret Rogerson*.

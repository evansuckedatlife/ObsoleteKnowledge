## summary

**Kendrick Lamar** is an American rapper, songwriter, and producer widely regarded as one of the most influential and accomplished lyricists in the history of hip-hop. Raised in Compton, California, his intricate narrative concept albums blend jazz, funk, soul, and spoken word into searing portraits of systemic oppression, spiritual conflict, and personal accountability. In 2018, he made history as the first non-classical, non-jazz musician to be awarded the Pulitzer Prize for Music.

## you gotta know

- Won the 2018 Pulitzer Prize for Music for his commercially and critically acclaimed fourth studio album *DAMN.* (2017), featuring the chart-topping hit single "HUMBLE."
- Released the landmark concept album *good kid, m.A.A.d city* (2012), subtitled "A Short Film by Kendrick Lamar," which documented adolescent survival amidst gang violence in Compton.
- Created the critically revered *To Pimp a Butterfly* (2015), a monumental socio-political jazz-rap fusion addressing racism, depression, and survivor's guilt, anchored by the civil rights anthem "Alright."
- Released the deeply introspective, therapy-focused double album *Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers* (2022), confronting generational trauma, fatherhood, infidelity, and societal accountability.
- Secured his major breakthrough with the independent release *Section.80* (2011), an ambitious thematic project exploring the crack cocaine epidemic's generational toll on 1980s youth.
- Curated and produced the critically praised soundtrack album *Black Panther: The Album* (2018) for Marvel Studios, featuring the Oscar-nominated collaboration "All the Stars."
- Dominated a widely publicized 2024 lyrical hip-hop feud with Drake, culminating in record-breaking diss tracks such as "Euphoria" and the historic Billboard Hot 100 number-one single "Not Like Us."
- Co-founded the creative communications company and media collective pgLang alongside his longtime manager and artistic collaborator Dave Free.

## connections

- [[civil-rights-movement]] — twentieth-century Black liberation struggle whose rhetoric, spirituals, and political goals echo directly through his anthem "Alright."
- [[united-states]] — nation whose racial inequities, institutional police violence, and inner-city economic policies form the core subject matter of his discography.
- [[american-literature]] — tradition of high narrative realism and confessional poetry reflected in Lamar's complex album structuring and Pulitzer Prize recognition.
- [[existentialism]] — philosophical current concerning free will, identity, faith, and moral dread permeating the reflective songs across *DAMN.* and *Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers*.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — cinematic device utilized heavily in his award-winning music videos, such as the Last Supper staging in "HUMBLE."
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic narrative form structuring the fatalistic street narratives and family losses recounted across *good kid, m.A.A.d city*.
- [[reconstruction-era]] — formative post-Civil War historical period whose unfulfilled racial justice promises set the structural stage for modern African American inner-city struggles.

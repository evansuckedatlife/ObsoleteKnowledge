## summary

**Hideo Kojima** is a renowned Japanese video game director, designer, screenwriter, and producer widely regarded as one of the medium's defining auteurs. Born in Tokyo in 1963, he pioneered the stealth-action genre during his decades-long tenure at Konami, creating the globally celebrated *Metal Gear* franchise. Renowned for weaving elaborate cinematic narratives with geopolitical philosophy, metafictional trickery, and cutting-edge mechanical design, Kojima fundamentally reshaped how video games synthesize cinema and interactive storytelling.

## you gotta know

- Created the seminal 1987 stealth title *Metal Gear* on the MSX2 home computer, inventing stealth mechanics to circumvent hardware limitations that prevented multiple moving sprites on screen.
- Directed *Metal Gear Solid* in 1998 for the Sony PlayStation, a watershed masterpiece that popularized cinematic cutscenes, full voice acting, and complex three-dimensional tactical espionage.
- Infamous for boundary-pushing fourth-wall breaks, notably the boss battle against *Psycho Mantis*, who read the player's memory card and forced players to switch controller ports.
- Subverted audience expectations in *Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty* by replacing protagonist *Solid Snake* with rookie operative *Raiden*, predicting modern internet culture, memes, and disinformation.
- Explored themes of nuclear deterrence, proxy conflicts, deterrence theory, and espionage deeply rooted in the history of the [[cold-war]].
- Left Konami in 2015 following a highly publicized corporate rift during the completion of *Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain*.
- Re-established Kojima Productions as an independent studio to release *Death Stranding* in 2019, starring actor *Norman Reedus* in an eerie, post-apocalyptic delivery simulation exploring global reconnection.

## summary

**Crimes of the Future** is a 2022 speculative science fiction body horror film written and directed by Canadian auteur *David Cronenberg*, starring *Viggo Mortensen*, *Léa Seydoux*, and *Kristen Stewart*. Set in a decaying post-industrial future where humanity has largely ceased to feel physical pain or contract infectious diseases, the narrative follows a celebrated performance artist whose rapid biological mutations become the centerpiece of avant-garde surgical spectacles. Premiering in competition at the Cannes Film Festival, the film stands as a provocative philosophical meditation on human evolution, ecological ruin, and the shifting boundaries between art and bodily autonomy.

## you gotta know

- Written and directed by *David Cronenberg*, marking his highly anticipated return to the original body horror genre after more than two decades exploring psychological drama and crime fiction.
- Stars *Viggo Mortensen* in his fourth collaboration with Cronenberg as *Saul Tenser*, a quiet performance artist afflicted with "Accelerated Evolution Syndrome," which causes his body to spontaneously generate novel, non-functional internal organs.
- Features *Léa Seydoux* as *Caprice*, a former trauma surgeon and Tenser's creative and domestic partner, who tattoos and theatrically excises his newly grown organs before live audiences using a biomorphic surgical console known as the *Sark*.
- Popularized the widely quoted philosophical catchphrase "Surgery is the new sex," coined within the narrative to articulate how surgical penetration and biological modification have replaced conventional eroticism in a desensitized society.
- Co-stars *Kristen Stewart* as *Timlin*, a skittish, infatuated investigator working alongside *Wippet* (*Don McKellar*) at the National Organ Registry, an improvised government bureau created to catalog and regulate anomalous human mutations.
- Centers on a subversive dissident faction led by *Lang Dotrice* (*Scott Speedman*), who have surgically re-engineered their digestive tracts to consume synthetic industrial plastics, treating bodily mutation as humanity's necessary adaptation to a poisoned planet.
- Culminates in Tenser submitting to Dotrice's philosophical vision by voluntarily ingesting a synthetic, toxic purple candy bar, peacefully welcoming his body's physiological evolution while seated in his biomechanical *Breakshuck* chair.
- Reuses the title of Cronenberg's low-budget, underground 1970 feature film, though the 2022 production features a completely unrelated plot and was developed from a screenplay originally titled *Painkillers* written in the late 1990s.

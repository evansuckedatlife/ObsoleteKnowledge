## summary

**Moonlight** is a 2016 American coming-of-age drama film written and directed by *Barry Jenkins*, adapted from *Tarell Alvin McCraney*'s unpublished semi-autobiographical play *In Moonlight Black Boys Look Blue*. Structured across three distinct chapters of the protagonist's life, the film portrays the emotional, physical, and psychological growth of a young Black man growing up in the Liberty City neighborhood of Miami. Universally celebrated for its tender lyricism, rich color palette, and empathetic treatment of queer Black identity, *Moonlight* won three Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Supporting Actor for *Mahershala Ali*.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Barry Jenkins* and shot on location in Liberty City, Miami, where both Jenkins and playwright *Tarell Alvin McCraney* grew up independently under similar circumstances.
- Structured into three chronological acts titled "i. Little", "ii. Chiron", and "iii. Black", tracking the protagonist *Chiron* played by three separate actors: *Alex Hibbert* (childhood), *Ashton Sanders* (adolescence), and *Trevante Rhodes* (adulthood).
- Follows Chiron's childhood relationship with *Juan* (*Mahershala Ali*), a compassionate local drug dealer who becomes a protective father figure despite unintentionally supplying drugs to Chiron's addicted mother *Paula* (*Naomie Harris*).
- Features *Jharrel Jerome* and *André Holland* as *Kevin*, Chiron's childhood friend, romantic awakening, and adult confidant whose reunion at an Atlanta diner drives the third act's resolution.
- Shot by cinematographer *James Laxton* with an expressive visual approach featuring lush oceanic blues, saturated neon lights, fluid 360-degree Steadicam turns, and high-contrast skin tones.
- Utilizes an innovative musical score composed by *Nicholas Britell*, combining classical orchestral chamber music with the Southern hip-hop technique of "chopped and screwed" pitch-shifting and tempo-slowing.
- Made cinema history at the 89th Academy Awards when presenters *Faye Dunaway* and *Warren Beatty* mistakenly announced *La La Land* as Best Picture due to an envelope mix-up, before *La La Land* producer *Jordan Horowitz* corrected the error and brought the *Moonlight* team to the stage.
- First LGBTQ-themed film and first film featuring an entirely Black leading cast to win the Academy Award for Best Picture.

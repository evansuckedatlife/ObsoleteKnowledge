## summary

**Ash Ketchum** is the determined ten-year-old anime protagonist of the global *Pokémon* media franchise created by *Satoshi Tajiri* and developed by *Game Freak* in [[japan]]. Debuting on television screens in 1997 alongside his loyal electric rodent partner *Pikachu*, he served for twenty-five years as the primary fictional viewpoint character and worldwide ambassador for competitive creature collecting and battling. His long, stubborn trajectory from reckless novice to world champion made him one of the most recognizable cultural icons in modern television history.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in the anime premiere episode *Pokémon - I Choose You!* in 1997, receiving a rebellious *Pikachu* from Professor Oak after oversleeping on the morning he was eligible to become a Pokémon Trainer.
- Named *Satoshi* in [[japan]] after franchise creator *Satoshi Tajiri*, while his persistent rival *Gary Oak* was named *Shigeru* after legendary game designer *Shigeru Miyamoto*.
- Chanted the English franchise slogan *"Gotta catch 'em all!"*, although he famously prioritized forging deep emotional bonds with his partners over maximizing raw numerical captures.
- Released or left several of his most potent companions in nature or training, most poignantly letting go of his beloved *Butterfree* across the sea and leaving *Charizard* in the Charicific Valley.
- Suffered iconic and agonizing tournament defeats across many regions, including losing at the Indigo Plateau due to *Charizard's* refusal to obey him and dropping the Kalos League finals to Alain's Mega Charizard X.
- Captured his first official regional Pokémon League title in the tropical Alola region during the *Sun & Moon* anime series, over two decades after his televised journey began.
- Reached the absolute pinnacle of the competitive battling world in 2022 by defeating Galar Champion Leon in the Masters Eight Tournament of the World Coronation Series to claim the title of Monarch.
- Retired from his role as the franchise's mainline animated protagonist in 2023 alongside *Pikachu*, concluding a celebrated 1,200-episode narrative arc across nine contiguous series.

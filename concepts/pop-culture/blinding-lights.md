## summary

**Blinding Lights** is a globally acclaimed synthwave and synth-pop single recorded by Canadian singer *The Weeknd* (*Abel Tesfaye*), released in November 2019 as the second single from his fourth studio album, *After Hours*. Co-written and co-produced alongside Swedish pop masterminds *Max Martin* and *Oscar Holter*, the track synthesizes 1980s retro-futurism, pulsing synthesizers, and kinetic melodrama into one of the most commercially successful recordings in modern music history. In 2021, *Billboard* designated the song as the number-one greatest hit of all time on the Hot 100 chart after it spent a record-shattering ninety weeks on the tally.

## you gotta know

- Set the historic record for the longest-charting song on the *Billboard* Hot 100 at ninety weeks, spending a record-breaking fifty-seven weeks inside the top ten.
- Propelled by a brisk tempo of 171 beats per minute and an energetic drum-machine rhythm that channels early 1980s pop icons such as *A-ha*, *Depeche Mode*, and *Prince*.
- The signature aesthetic of the era features *The Weeknd* adopting a cinematic alter-ego dressed in an iconic red tailored [[blazer]], leather gloves, and facial bandages depicting grotesque physical degradation.
- Directed by *Anton Tammi*, the surreal music video showcases a manic night in downtown Las Vegas and Los Angeles, characterized by disorienting neon reflections, high-speed car cruising, and erratic hallucinations.
- Served as the triumphant centerpiece of *The Weeknd*'s solo halftime performance at Super Bowl LV in Tampa, Florida, where dozens of masked, red-coated clones danced through an illuminated golden mirror labyrinth.
- Co-producer *Max Martin* secured another epochal number-one pop standard, reinforcing his decades-long dominance of Western popular songwriting.
- Gained immense early momentum in 2020 through a viral synchronized dance trend on the short-form social video platform *TikTok*, expanding its demographic reach worldwide during early quarantine shutdowns.

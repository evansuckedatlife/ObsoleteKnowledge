## summary

**Captain Marvel** is the superhero moniker shared by several prominent figures in American comic book history, most notably the classic Fawcett Comics hero *Billy Batson* (later rebranded by DC Comics as *Shazam*) and Marvel Comics' cosmic hero *Carol Danvers*. First created in 1939 by artist C. C. Beck and writer Bill Parker for Fawcett, the original character outsold all competitors during the Golden Age of Comics before trademark disputes led Marvel Comics to secure the name for its own alien champion *Mar-Vell*. Today, the title represents both a milestone in copyright law and a premier Marvel superhero who commands cosmic energy and galactic defense.

## you gotta know

- Fawcett Publications introduced the original hero in *Whiz Comics* #2 in February 1940, starring young orphan *Billy Batson*, who transforms into an adult champion upon speaking the magic acronym *Shazam*.
- The original character's magical acronym derives from six mythological figures: *Solomon*, *Hercules*, *Atlas*, *Zeus*, *Achilles*, and *Mercury*, blending Hellenic and classical lore into a modern superhuman.
- National Comics (now DC Comics) sued Fawcett in 1941 for copyright infringement, alleging that the character was a direct imitation of *Superman*, resulting in a prolonged legal battle that ended Fawcett's comics line.
- Marvel Comics trademarked the vacant name in 1967 with the debut of *Mar-Vell*, a Kree alien military officer created by Stan Lee and Gene Colan who defects to protect humanity.
- *Carol Danvers*, an officer in the *United States* Air Force, debuted in 1968, gained powers after an explosion melded her genetics with Kree DNA, and became *Ms. Marvel* before officially adopting the name *Captain Marvel* in 2012.
- The 1982 graphic novel *The Death of Captain Marvel* by Jim Starlin broke narrative conventions by having *Mar-Vell* perish not in battle, but from terminal cancer.
- DC Comics officially resolved decades of public confusion in 2011 by rebranding their original magical character permanently as *Shazam*, while Marvel brought *Carol Danvers* to the screen portrayed by *Brie Larson*.

## summary

**Ought** was a critically acclaimed Canadian post-punk band formed in Montreal, Quebec, in 2011 by vocalist and guitarist *Tim Darcy*, keyboardist *Matt May*, bassist *Ben Stidworthy*, and drummer and violinist *Tim Keen*. Active throughout the 2010s, the quartet became celebrated for their angular guitar riffs, kinetic rhythm section, and Darcy's nervous, declamatory vocal delivery. Their breakout debut album, *More Than Any Other Day* (2014), established them as a pivotal force in the international art-punk and post-punk revival before their eventual disbandment in 2021.

## you gotta know

- Formed in Montreal by four international musicians and university students living in a shared apartment during the tumultuous 2012 Quebec student strikes, which deeply shaped their communal rehearsal space and political sensibility.
- Achieved widespread international recognition with their 2014 debut studio LP, *More Than Any Other Day*, released on Montreal's storied independent label *Constellation Records* to universal acclaim.
- Fronted by vocalist *Tim Darcy*, whose intense, stream-of-consciousness singing and spoken-word delivery drew frequent comparisons to *David Byrne* of *Talking Heads*, *Mark E. Smith* of *The Fall*, and *Richard Hell*.
- Penned the defining indie rock anthem "Today More Than Any Other Day", a dynamic composition that transforms mundane thoughts about grocery shopping and iced coffee into an ecstatic, cathartic celebration of fragile everyday resilience.
- Released their abrasive, brooding second album *Sun Coming Down* in 2015, featuring the driving, anxiety-laden post-punk single "Beautiful Blue Sky" and its sardonic deconstruction of casual social pleasantries.
- Signed to *Merge Records* for their ambitious third full-length album, *Room Inside the World* (2018), which integrated lush art rock textures, vibraphone, drum machines, and a 70-person choir on the single "Desire".
- Announced their amicable breakup in November 2021, prompting *Tim Darcy* and *Ben Stidworthy* to immediately form the post-punk trio *Cola* alongside former *U.S. Girls* drummer *Evan Cartwright*.

## summary

**James Earl Jones** was an acclaimed American actor of stage and screen whose commanding presence and resonant bass voice made him one of the most revered performing artists in modern history. Over a career spanning seven decades, he earned an EGOT—winning competitive Emmy, Grammy, and Tony Awards alongside an Honorary Academy Award. He achieved worldwide cultural immortality by providing the menacing voice of *Darth Vader* in the *Star Wars* franchise and the noble king *Mufasa* in *The Lion King*.

## you gotta know

- Provided the indelible, booming voice for cinema's most iconic antagonist, *Darth Vader*, across the *Star Wars* saga, beginning with *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope* in 1977.
- Voiced the wise monarch *Mufasa* in Disney's 1994 animated masterpiece *The Lion King*, later reprising the royal vocal performance in the 2019 photorealistic remake.
- Won the Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play for creating the role of boxer *Jack Jefferson* in *The Great White Hope*, later receiving an Academy Award nomination for its 1970 film adaptation.
- Won a second competitive Tony Award in 1987 for originating the role of garbage collector *Troy Maxson* in playwright *August Wilson*'s seminal drama *Fences*.
- Overcame a severe childhood stutter by reading poetry aloud and studying vocal mechanics under high school English teacher *Donald Crouch*.
- Performed extensively in classical stage works of *[[william-shakespeare]]*, winning widespread critical acclaim for his portrayals of *Othello*, *King Lear*, and *Claudius*.
- Debuted on the silver screen as the B-52 bombardier Lieutenant *Lothar Zogg* in *Stanley Kubrick*'s 1964 satirical masterpiece *Dr. Strangelove*.
- Honored in 2022 when Broadway's historic *Cort Theatre* on West 48th Street was officially renamed the *James Earl Jones Theatre* in recognition of his lifetime theatrical contributions.

## summary

**Gabe Newell**, often known by the community moniker **Gaben**, is an American video game developer, entrepreneur, and co-founder and president of the Valve Corporation. After a lucrative early career working at Microsoft on the Windows operating system, Newell co-founded Valve in 1996 and oversaw the creation of revolutionary titles such as *Half-Life* and the global distribution platform Steam. His innovations in digital distribution, competitive community gaming, and narrative game design fundamentally reshaped the global entertainment software industry.

## you gotta know

- Spent thirteen years at Microsoft as an early producer on Windows operating systems, using his accumulated wealth to co-found Valve Corporation in 1996 with fellow ex-employee *Mike Harrington*.
- Oversaw the development and 1998 release of *Half-Life*, which revolutionized the [[first-person-shooter]] genre through uninterrupted first-person narrative and scripted environmental storytelling.
- Spearheaded the development and 2003 launch of Steam, a digital storefront and distribution network that grew to dominate PC gaming worldwide.
- Championed open community modifications, leading Valve to acquire and commercialize landmark grassroots projects such as [[counter-strike]], *Team Fortress*, and *Dota 2*.
- Oversaw the development of the innovative *Source* game engine and acclaimed original franchises including *Portal* and *Left 4 Dead*.
- Became a celebrated internet icon and folk hero across gaming culture, frequently affectionately referenced by players as *Gaben*.
- Inducted into the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences Hall of Fame in 2013 for his transformative contributions to video game distribution and design.

## connections

- [[first-person-shooter]] — core video game genre revolutionized by his company's flagship release *Half-Life* and its sequel *Half-Life 2*.
- [[counter-strike]] — legendary competitive tactical franchise incubated, acquired, and continuously maintained under his corporate leadership at Valve.
- [[esports]] — global competitive gaming industry largely catalyzed and powered by Valve ecosystem titles like *Counter-Strike* and *Dota 2*.
- [[minecraft]] — massively popular sandbox game whose creator *Markus Persson* cited Valve's developer-first indie ethos and distribution models.
- [[skyrim]] — iconic fantasy role-playing game that leveraged Newell's Steam platform for distribution and community modification via Steam Workshop.
- [[united-states]] — home country where he attended Harvard University, worked for Microsoft in Washington state, and established Valve headquarters.

## summary

**Dragon Age** is a dark fantasy role-playing video game franchise created by Canadian developer *BioWare* and published by *Electronic Arts*, debuting in 2009 with **Dragon Age: Origins**. Set on the tumultuous continent of *Thedas*, the series is renowned for its intricate moral dilemmas, tactical party-based combat, detailed geopolitical lore, and character-driven storytelling. Alongside open-world fantasy landmarks like [[skyrim]], the franchise stands as a cornerstone of modern western interactive role-playing narratives.

## you gotta know

- Created by *BioWare* under lead writer *David Gaider* as a spiritual successor to the studio's legendary *Baldur's Gate* series, prioritizing rich party banter, romantic relationships, and world-state continuity across sequels.
- **Dragon Age: Origins** (2009) tasks the player, a newly inducted Grey Warden, with uniting the kingdom of Ferelden against the Fifth Blight, an invasion of monstrous darkspawn led by an Archdemon named Urthemiel.
- Centers on the Grey Wardens, an elite, secretive order that drinks darkspawn blood in a lethal initiation ritual known as the Joining to gain the supernatural ability to sense and permanently slay Archdemons.
- Features prominent recurring companions, including the cynical swamp witch Morrigan (daughter of the mysterious sorceress Flemeth), the idealistic royal bastard Alistair, the Chantry spy Leliana, and the story-spinning dwarven rogue Varric Tethras.
- Explores complex socio-political conflicts, primarily the brutal systemic friction between the religious Chantry, the magic-suppressing Templar Order, and the Circle of Magi, whose practitioners are vulnerable to demonic possession from the mystical dreamscape known as the Fade.
- **Dragon Age: Inquisition** (2014) earned numerous Game of the Year awards by placing players in command of the Inquisition to close the catastrophic breach in the Veil, a metaphysical barrier separating reality from the Fade.
- Introduced the pivotal twist surrounding party companion Solas, an apostate elven mage revealed at the conclusion of *Inquisition* and its *Trespasser* expansion to be Fen'Harel, the ancient elven god of rebellion known as the Dread Wolf.

## connections

- [[skyrim]] — fellow landmark high-fantasy role-playing game of the seventh console generation that prioritized open-world exploration compared to BioWare's companion-driven narrative focus.
- [[christianity]] — primary cultural and theological model for the Chantry, with its singular deity the Maker, its martyred female prophet Andraste, and its scripture the Chant of Light.
- [[tragedy]] — narrative framework governing the sacrifice required of Grey Wardens and the recurring fall of ancient elven and dwarven civilizations.
- [[colonialism]] — thematic underpinning of the historical subjugation of native elves by human empires, forcing elves into urban ghettos called alienages or nomadic Dalish clans.
- [[middle-east]] — aesthetic and geographic inspiration for the Tevinter Imperium and the desert realm of the Anderfels.
- [[ancient-greece]] — mythological parallel for the pantheon of the Evanuris, the tyrannical ancient elven rulers worshiped as gods before their imprisonment.

## summary

**William Shatner** is a Canadian actor, author, director, and recording artist who achieved worldwide fame as Captain James T. Kirk of the starship *Enterprise* in the groundbreaking science-fiction franchise *Star Trek*. Known for his flamboyant cadence and Shakespearean theatricality, Shatner evolved into an indelible pop-culture icon across seven decades on stage, screen, and television.

## you gotta know

- Originated the role of Captain James T. Kirk in Gene Roddenberry's 1966 television series *Star Trek: The Original Series*, later reprising the character across seven feature films ending with *Star Trek Generations*.
- Began his professional acting career as a classically trained Shakespearean actor at Canada's Stratford Shakespeare Festival under legendary theatrical director Tyrone Guthrie.
- Developed a distinctively theatrical speaking style characterized by dramatic, unpredictable pauses and staccato cadence, widely parodied and celebrated throughout popular culture.
- Won two Emmy Awards and a Golden Globe portraying the eccentric, litigation-obsessed attorney Denny Crane on David E. Kelley's legal dramas *The Practice* and *Boston Legal*.
- Popularized a spoken-word musical style through his 1968 debut album *The Transformed Man*, featuring idiosyncratic readings of songs like "Rocket Man" and "Mr. Tambourine Man", later collaborating with Ben Folds on 2004's *Has Been*.
- Starred as veteran police officer T. J. Hooker in the 1980s television police drama series *T. J. Hooker*, and served as the host of the reality television series *Rescue 911*.
- Became the oldest living person to travel into space in October 2021, flying aboard a Blue Origin suborbital spaceflight at the age of ninety.

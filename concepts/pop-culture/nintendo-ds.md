## summary

The **Nintendo DS** is a dual-screen handheld video game console developed and manufactured by Nintendo, first released in 2004 in North America and *[[japan]]*. Designed under company president *Satoru Iwata* as an unconventional "third pillar" alongside the Game Boy Advance and GameCube, it introduced mass-market touch controls, built-in wireless connectivity, and an integrated microphone. It became one of the best-selling gaming devices of all time, fundamentally transforming casual gaming and expanding interactive media to global non-traditional demographics.

## you gotta know

- Succeeded the Game Boy line as Nintendo's flagship portable device, debuting its signature clamshell form factor with dual LCD screens working in tandem.
- Featured a resistive lower touch screen manipulated via stylus or thumb, establishing tactile interaction years before capacitive smartphone dominance.
- Became the second best-selling video game console in history behind Sony's PlayStation 2, moving more than one hundred and fifty million units worldwide.
- Launched the immensely popular Touch! Generations software line aimed at casual audiences, including smash hits *Brain Age: Train Your Brain in Minutes a Day!* and *Nintendogs*.
- Included built-in Wi-Fi and local wireless ad-hoc multiplayer, popularized by the pre-installed visual messaging utility *PictoChat* and *Mario Kart DS*.
- Backward compatible with Game Boy Advance cartridges through a dedicated secondary slot, which also accommodated peripherals like the *Guitar Hero: On Tour* grip.
- Iterated across multiple redesigns, including the sleeker *DS Lite*, camera-equipped *DSi*, and the larger-display *DSi XL*, before leading directly to the stereoscopic 3DS and eventually influencing the hybrid architecture of the [[nintendo-switch]].

## connections

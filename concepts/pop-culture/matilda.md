## summary

**Matilda** is a renowned 1988 children's novel written by British author *Roald Dahl* and illustrated by Quentin Blake, following an extraordinary child prodigy who discovers she possesses telekinetic powers. Set in an English village, the story contrasts Matilda's brilliance and gentle demeanor against the cruel negligence of her parents and the tyrannical reign of her school headmistress. Celebrated for its subversive humor, advocacy of literacy, and fierce celebration of intellect, the work remains one of the enduring masterpieces of late twentieth-century youth fiction.

## you gotta know

- Revolves around *Matilda Wormwood*, a five-year-old child prodigy whose intellect, moral sense, and love of reading are ridiculed by her crude parents.
- Pits the heroic schoolteacher *Miss Jennifer Honey* against the terrifying, Olympic hammer-throwing headmistress *Miss Agatha Trunchbull*.
- Features memorable retributions engineered by Matilda, including bleaching her father's hair blonde with hydrogen peroxide and gluing his hat to his head.
- Includes the famous school assembly scene where classmate *Bruce Bogtrotter* is forced to eat an entire massive chocolate cake as punishment.
- Depicts Matilda unlocking telekinesis to manipulate chalkboard chalk, terrifying Trunchbull by impersonating the ghost of Miss Honey's deceased father, *Magnus*.
- Concludes with Miss Honey adopting Matilda while the corrupt Wormwood family flees the authorities to Spain.
- Adapted into an acclaimed 1996 feature film directed by Danny DeVito and a multi-award-winning stage musical by the Royal Shakespeare Company with music by Tim Minchin.

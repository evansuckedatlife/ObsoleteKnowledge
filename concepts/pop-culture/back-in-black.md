## summary

**Back in Black** is the landmark 1980 hard rock album and title track released by Australian band *AC/DC*, produced by *Robert John "Mutt" Lange* following the sudden death of their original frontman *Bon Scott*. Featuring newly recruited vocalist *Brian Johnson* alongside lead guitarist *Angus Young* and rhythm guitarist *Malcolm Young*, the record served as an uncompromising memorial packaged in an all-black sleeve that evolved into one of the best-selling studio albums in music history. The title song remains one of the most recognizable guitar anthems ever recorded, celebrated for its muscular opening riff and propulsive beat.

## you gotta know

- Created in the immediate shadow of catastrophe after singer *Bon Scott* died in London in February 1980, prompting the band to soldier on with *Brian Johnson*, formerly of *Geordie*.
- Features the iconic title track driven by *Angus Young*'s staccato E-D-A chord progression, consistently cited as a masterclass in hard rock rhythm and minimalistic guitar tone.
- Opens with the funeral tolling of a custom bronze bell on the track *Hells Bells*, setting an ominous yet celebratory tone of grief transmuted into stadium hard rock.
- Recorded at Compass Point Studios in Nassau, Bahamas, where the band endured tropical storms and power failures while crafting their lean, bone-dry audio aesthetic.
- Produced by *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*, whose exacting standards regarding vocal overdubs, snare dynamics, and rhythmic precision elevated hard rock production into a global pop benchmark.
- Stands as one of the top-selling albums of all time globally, having earned a certified 25-times platinum rating in the [[united-states]] alone.
- Spawned multiple classic rock radio staples including *You Shook Me All Night Long*, *Shoot to Thrill*, and *What Do You Do for Money Honey*.

## connections

- [[england]] — nation where the group recruited *Brian Johnson* and where rehearsals commenced following *Bon Scott*'s death in London.

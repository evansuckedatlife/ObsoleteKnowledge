## summary

**Captain America** is a fictional patriotic superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby. First introduced in *Captain America Comics* #1 in March 1941, the frail youth *Steve Rogers* is transformed into a peak-human super-soldier via the experimental Super-Soldier Serum to fight the Axis powers during *World War II*. Frozen in suspended animation near the end of the conflict, he was revived decades later in the modern era, serving as the enduring leader and moral compass of the Avengers.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in December 1940 with a cover famously depicting him punching *Adolf Hitler* in the jaw, published nearly a year before the *United States* formally entered *World War II*.
- In his origin story, rejected recruit *Steve Rogers* volunteers for Project: Rebirth in *New York City*, where scientist *Dr. Abraham Erskine* administers the serum before being assassinated by a Nazi spy.
- Wields a concave, nearly indestructible disc-shaped shield composed of a unique fictional alloy of vibranium and steel, designed to absorb kinetic impacts and ricochet with unerring accuracy.
- Fought alongside his teen sidekick *Bucky Barnes* against primary antagonist the *Red Skull* (*Johann Schmidt*), head of the Nazi terrorist division Hydra.
- Fell into the icy waters of the North Atlantic after preventing a drone rocket launch, entering cryogenic suspended animation until his body was recovered by the *Avengers* in *The Avengers* #4 in 1964.
- Renounced his mantle and adopted the identity *Nomad* in a famous 1974 storyline reflecting real-world disillusionment with the American government following the Watergate scandal.
- Portrayed by actor *Chris Evans* in the Marvel Cinematic Universe across a trilogy of standalone films and multiple team-up blockbusters.

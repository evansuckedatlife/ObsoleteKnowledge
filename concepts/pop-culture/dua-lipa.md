## summary

**Dua Lipa** is an English and Albanian singer, songwriter, and cultural entrepreneur whose sophisticated dance-pop revitalized vintage disco and funk for modern global audiences. Born in *London* to Kosovo Albanian parents, she cultivated a loyal online following before achieving commercial breakthrough with her self-titled 2017 debut. Her worldwide prominence reached new heights with the 2020 masterpiece *Future Nostalgia*, an exhilarating synth-pop and disco record that captured the global cultural mood and earned her status as a premier pop visionary.

## you gotta know

- Catapulted to international superstardom with the female-empowerment breakup manual *New Rules*, whose viral choreography video garnered billions of online views.
- Spearheaded the contemporary disco-pop revival with her sophomore album *Future Nostalgia*, producing massive worldwide chart-topping singles such as *Don't Start Now*, *Physical*, and *Levitating*.
- Received the prestigious *Grammy Award for Best New Artist* in 2019 alongside *Best Dance Recording* for the collaborative hit *Electricity* with *Silk City*.
- Contributed the sparkling disco single *Dance the Night* to the soundtrack of the 2023 blockbuster film *Barbie*, also making a cameo appearance in the movie as Mermaid Barbie.
- Founded the multifaceted editorial and lifestyle platform *Service95*, hosting the high-profile international interview podcast *Dua Lipa: At Your Service* and an influential global book club.
- Granted Albanian citizenship in 2022 by President *Bajram Begaj* for elevating Albanian arts and culture on the global stage through philanthropic work.
- Teaming up with legendary music veteran *Elton John* on *Cold Heart (PNAU Remix)*, achieving a career-defining transatlantic number-one single.

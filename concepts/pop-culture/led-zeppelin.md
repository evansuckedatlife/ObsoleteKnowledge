## summary

**Led Zeppelin** was an English rock band formed in London in 1968, comprising guitarist *Jimmy Page*, singer *Robert Plant*, bassist and keyboardist *John Paul Jones*, and drummer *John Bonham*. Fusing electrified Chicago blues, Celtic folk melodies, and thunderous rhythmic grooves, the group became one of the most innovative and commercially colossal acts in musical history. Their groundbreaking catalog and monumental stadium performances fundamentally established the templates for hard rock and heavy metal throughout the 1970s.

## you gotta know

- Formed by virtuoso guitarist *Jimmy Page* following the dissolution of *The Yardbirds*, initially performing under the temporary name *The New Yardbirds* before adopting a moniker suggested by *Keith Moon* of *The Who*, who quipped the venture would plummet like a "lead balloon."
- Produced entirely by *Jimmy Page*, whose innovative studio techniques—such as backward echo, natural room reverb, and playing his *Gibson Les Paul* with a violin bow on *Dazed and Confused*—revolutionized rock production.
- Refused to release standard radio singles in the United Kingdom, deliberately pioneering the concept of *album-oriented rock* (*AOR*) and compelling millions of listeners to experience their records as complete artistic statements.
- Released their untitled fourth studio album in 1971 (universally designated as *Led Zeppelin IV*), featuring four enigmatic occult runes representing the individual musicians and containing the eight-minute epic *Stairway to Heaven*.
- Propelled by the explosive percussion of *John Bonham*, whose immense dynamic control, intricate bass drum triplets, and driving groove earned him acclaim as one of rock's most influential drummers; the group disbanded immediately after his death in 1980.
- Established the archetype of the hard-rock frontman through *Robert Plant*, whose soaring vocal range, charismatic stage swagger, and fascination with mythology and the writings of *J.R.R. Tolkien* enriched tracks like *Ramble On*.
- Set unprecedented box-office attendance records across global tours, traveling aboard a private converted *Boeing 720* airliner nicknamed *The Starship* and shattering previous attendance benchmarks set by *The Beatles*.

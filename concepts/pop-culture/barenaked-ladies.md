## summary

**Barenaked Ladies**, frequently abbreviated as **BNL**, is an acclaimed Canadian alternative rock band formed in Scarborough, Ontario, in 1988 by lead vocalists and songwriters *Ed Robertson* and *Steven Page*. Renowned for their infectious acoustic-driven pop melodies, hyper-literate wordplay, lightning-fast vocal delivery, and comedic stage banter, the group evolved from busking grassroots origins into an international multi-platinum headlining act. In contemporary popular culture and quiz bowl, they are celebrated for their chart-topping single "One Week," their devoted touring following, and composing the theme song for the television sitcom *The Big Bang Theory*.

## you gotta know

- Captured the number-one spot on the *Billboard* Hot 100 in the [[united-states]] in 1998 with their breakthrough hit single "One Week," celebrated for its dense, breathless barrage of rapid-fire pop-culture name-drops.
- Established originally as an acoustic duo by high school classmates *Ed Robertson* and *Steven Page*, later solidifying their classic lineup with bassist *Jim Creeggan*, keyboardist *Kevin Hearn*, and drummer *Tyler Stewart*.
- Achieved early Canadian music history with their self-released 1991 five-track demo cassette, known colloquially as *The Yellow Tape*, which became the first independent release ever certified platinum in Canada.
- Wrote and performed the high-tempo scientific chronicle "The History of Everything," which served as the official opening theme song for all twelve seasons of the sitcom *The Big Bang Theory*.
- Endured a major lineup transition in 2009 when founding co-frontman and distinctive tenor vocalist *Steven Page* amicably parted ways with the band to embark on a solo career.
- Cultivated a passionate live concert culture characterized by spontaneous freestyle comedic raps, an audience tradition of hurling Kraft macaroni boxes during the chorus of "If I Had $1000000," and annual fan cruises.
- Featured on television in the eighth season of *How I Met Your Mother*, appearing as musical talking heads discussing the fictional Canadian teen-pop sensation engaged to [[barney-stinson]].
- Center of an iconic pop-culture debate in the comedy series *Community*, where characters passionately argue over the band's musical importance and artistic legacy.

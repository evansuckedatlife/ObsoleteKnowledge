## summary

**Metroid** is a pioneering science-fiction action-adventure video game franchise created by Nintendo designers *Makoto Kano*, *Hiroji Kiyotake*, and *Gunpei Yokoi*. Debuting on the Family Computer Disk System in 1986, the series follows interstellar bounty hunter *Samus Aran* as she explores desolate alien worlds and battles the predatory bio-engineered organisms known as Metroids. Renowned for its atmospheric isolation and non-linear level design, the franchise co-founded the influential *Metroidvania* gameplay genre alongside Konami's *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*.

## you gotta know

- Directed and produced by key members of Nintendo R&D1 in 1986, heavily inspired by Ridley Scott's sci-fi film *Alien*, borrowing dark biomechanical aesthetics and naming the recurring dragon-like villain *Ridley*.
- Features one of gaming's earliest and most iconic female protagonists, revealing *Samus Aran* beneath her powered Power Suit only upon completing the original game quickly.
- Released *Super Metroid* on the Super Nintendo Entertainment System in 1994, widely hailed as a timeless masterpiece of environmental storytelling, atmospheric mood, and speedrunning mechanics.
- Transitioned seamlessly into three dimensions with *Metroid Prime* in 2002, developed by Texas-based *Retro Studios*, which translated backtracking exploration into a first-person adventure format.
- Established structural genre conventions revolving around acquiring traversal upgrades—such as the *Morph Ball*, *Screw Attack*, and *Ice Beam*—to unlock previously impassable terrain.
- Continued the side-scrolling narrative canon through *Metroid Fusion* on the Game Boy Advance in 2002 and the critically lauded *Metroid Dread* on the Nintendo Switch in 2021.
- Chronicles enduring battles across the galaxy against the ruthless *Space Pirates*, the bio-mechanical supercomputer *Mother Brain*, and the ancient avian race known as the *Chozo*.

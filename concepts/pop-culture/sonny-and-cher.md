## summary

**Sonny & Cher** were an American pop music and television entertainment duo formed by husband and wife *Sonny Bono* and *Cherilyn Sarkisian*. Emerging from the mid-1960s Los Angeles music scene, they initially gained worldwide fame through soaring folk-pop hits before successfully transitioning into beloved primetime variety television personalities. Their trademark dynamic paired musical warmth with self-deprecating domestic banter, creating a versatile comedic template that influenced twentieth-century celebrity entertainment across the *[[united-states]]*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved global commercial superstardom in 1965 with the international chart-topping single *"I Got You Babe"*, which defined mid-1960s pop-rock optimism and sold over a million copies.
- Hosted the wildly popular CBS primetime variety program *The Sonny and Cher Comedy Hour* from 1971 to 1974, which garnered twelve Emmy nominations and revived their career.
- Established a signature comic dynamic rooted in the traditions of *[[vaudeville]]*, contrasting Sonny's short stature and earnest earnestness with Cher's towering glamour and biting deadpan barbs.
- Began their professional recording career under the early stage name *Caesar and Cleo* in 1964, releasing short-lived singles such as *"Baby Don't Go"* and *"The Letter"*.
- Apprenticed under legendary record producer *Phil Spector* at Gold Star Studios, performing background harmonies on historic girl-group recordings including the Ronettes' classic *"Be My Baby"*.
- Continued their broadcast partnership amicably on television with *The Sonny and Cher Show* after their high-profile 1975 divorce, demonstrating remarkable commercial resilience.
- Launched two profoundly divergent second acts: Cher evolved into an Oscar-winning dramatic star and legendary pop icon, while Sonny became mayor of Palm Springs and a member of the *[[united-states]]* House of Representatives.

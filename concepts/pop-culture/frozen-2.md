## summary

**Frozen II** (marketed as **Frozen 2**) is a 2019 American computer-animated musical fantasy film produced by *Walt Disney Animation Studios* and directed by *Chris Buck* and *Jennifer Lee*. Serving as the direct sequel to the 2013 blockbuster [[frozen]], the film follows Queen *Elsa*, her sister *Anna*, the iceman *Kristoff*, the snowman *Olaf*, and the reindeer *Sven* as they venture into an enchanted mist-veiled forest to uncover the origins of Elsa's magical powers. Achieving historic commercial success in the [[united-states]] and across the globe, it grossed over 1.45 billion dollars while exploring mature themes of ecological stewardship, historical reparations, and indigenous reconciliation.

## you gotta know

- The narrative centers on uncovering Arendelle's imperialist past, revealing that King *Runeard*, Elsa and Anna's grandfather, constructed a dam to weaken the resources of the indigenous *Northuldra* people before treacherously initiating war against them.
- Features an acclaimed soundtrack composed by *Kristen Anderson-Lopez* and *Robert Lopez*, highlighted by Elsa's soaring anthem *Into the Unknown*, which received an Academy Award nomination, and Kristoff's humorous 1980s soft-rock tribute *Lost in the Woods*.
- Elsa tames the four classical elemental spirits guarding the *Enchanted Forest*: *Gale* the wind spirit, *Bruni* the fiery salamander, the towering stone *Earth Giants*, and the *Nokk*, a fierce mythological aquatic stallion.
- At the mystical glacier of *Ahtohallan*, Elsa sings the triumphant self-actualization ballad *Show Yourself* and discovers that she is the fifth spirit bridging nature and humanity, a divine gift granted because their mother Queen *Iduna* selflessly saved Prince *Agnarr*.
- Anna undergoes profound emotional maturity through the song *The Next Right Thing*, coping with grief after Elsa freezes in Ahtohallan and Olaf dissolves, before orchestrating the destruction of Runeard's dam to right historical injustices.
- At the conclusion of the story, Elsa chooses to abdicate the royal throne to protect the wild natural realm, and Anna is crowned the new Queen of Arendelle, solidifying their complementary leadership.
- The production team negotiated an unprecedented formal agreement with the indigenous *Sámi* parliaments of Scandinavia, creating an advisory group named the *Verddet* to ensure respectful, authentic depictions of nomadic Sámi culture.

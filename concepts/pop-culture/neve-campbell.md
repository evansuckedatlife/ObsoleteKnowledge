## summary

**Neve Campbell** is a Canadian actress who gained international renown as one of the defining screen figures of 1990s popular cinema and television. She achieved iconic status as the ultimate "final girl" *Sidney Prescott* in director *Wes Craven*'s satirical horror franchise *Scream*, beginning in 1996. Beyond her genre-defining horror performances, she established herself in dramatic television on *Party of Five* and in boundary-pushing cinema like the neo-noir erotic thriller *Wild Things*.

## you gotta know

- Rose to global prominence portraying the resilient protagonist *Sidney Prescott* across multiple installments of the meta-horror franchise *Scream*, revitalizing the modern slasher genre.
- Gained her breakthrough television role as the orphaned teenager *Julia Salinger* in the critically acclaimed Fox family drama *Party of Five*, running from 1994 to 2000.
- Starred alongside *Robin Tunney*, *Fairuza Balk*, and *Rachel True* as high school outcasts practicing witchcraft in the 1996 cult supernatural film *The Craft*.
- Broke away from her wholesome television persona by playing the conniving *Suzie Toller* opposite *Denise Richards* and *Kevin Bacon* in the 1998 erotic thriller *Wild Things*.
- Trained extensively as a classical ballet dancer at the National Ballet School of Canada before career-ending physical injuries redirected her ambition toward acting.
- Co-wrote, produced, and starred in the 2003 dance drama *The Company*, directed by *Robert Altman*, drawing directly upon her personal background in professional ballet.
- Earned critical praise in later television roles, notably as political campaign manager *LeAnn Harvey* in the acclaimed Netflix political thriller *House of Cards*.

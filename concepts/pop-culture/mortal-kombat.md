## summary

**Mortal Kombat** is an influential American fighting game franchise created by *Ed Boon* and *John Tobias* for *Midway Games* in 1992. Renowned for its distinctive digitized sprites of live actors and graphic violence, the game became a cultural phenomenon and a primary catalyst for congressional hearings on video game violence. Its dark martial-arts fantasy lore and signature finishing moves established an enduring multimedia brand across arcade machines, consoles, film adaptations, and [[esports]] tournaments.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Ed Boon* and *John Tobias* at *Midway Games* in Chicago, originally conceived as an action vehicle starring martial artist *Jean-Claude Van Damme*.
- Introduced *Fatalities*, brutal end-of-match finishing moves executed via precise directional button sequences upon hearing the iconic command *"Finish Him!"*
- Its graphic depiction of blood and decapitations prompted 1993 United States Senate hearings led by *Joe Lieberman* and *Herb Kohl*, which directly led to the establishment of the *Entertainment Software Rating Board* (ESRB).
- Centers on an interdimensional tournament between martial artists defending *Earthrealm* against the sorcerer *Shang Tsung*, the four-armed Shokan champion *Goro*, and the imperial warlord *Shao Kahn* of *Outworld*.
- Features staple rivalry matchups between ninja combatants, most notably the hellspawn specter *Scorpion* with his spear attack and battle cry *"Get over here!"* alongside the cryomancer *Sub-Zero*.
- Paved the way for subsequent arcade fighting mainstays like *Tekken* and modern competitive fighting gaming within professional [[esports]].
- Spawned popular glitch-turned-characters and rumors, including the hidden green ninja *Reptile* and the red-clad ninja *Ermac*, who arose from an arcade diagnostic error macro.

## connections

- [[esports]] — headline franchise featured prominently in international fighting game tournament circuits like EVO.
- [[united-states]] — home country where Midway Games developed the title and where congressional hearings created the ESRB.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — contemporary genre that joined fighting games in 1990s legislative scrutiny over interactive violence.
- [[counter-strike]] — fellow pioneer in competitive tactical and arcade gaming that helped define multiplayer gaming culture.
- [[japan]] — origin nation of primary arcade competitors such as *Capcom* and *Namco*.
- [[ms-pac-man]] — predecessor arcade cabinet sensation that defined North American coin-op commercial success.

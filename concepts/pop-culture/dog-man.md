## summary

**Dog Man** is the titular canine-human superhero of a wildly popular comedic graphic novel series created by American author and illustrator *Dav Pilkey*, first published in 2016 as a spin-off of *Captain Underpants*. The character was created when an explosion injured police officer *Knight* and his police hound *Greg the Dog*, leading surgeons to save them by stitching Greg's head onto Knight's body. Blending traditional crime-fighting duties with irrepressible canine instincts, the series combines playful physical humor with deeply touching storylines about empathy, redemption, and chosen family.

## you gotta know

- Framed within Pilkey's universe as the creative work of elementary school students *George Beard* and *Harold Hutchins*, who invented the hero in their treehouse.
- His origin stems from a bomb planted by the feline villain *Petey the Cat*, which incapacitated Officer Knight's head and Greg's body, prompting life-saving hybrid surgery.
- Incorporates Pilkey's hallmark "Flip-O-Rama" technique, an interactive feature where readers flip pages back and forth rapidly to animate slapstick action sequences.
- Series titles frequently parody famous works of classical literature, such as *A Tale of Two Kitties*, *Lord of the Fleas*, *For Whom the Ball Rolls*, *Grime and Punishment*, and *Mothering Heights*.
- Centres emotionally on the reform of *Petey the Cat*, who abandons supervillainy to become a loving, conscientious father to his clone son, *Li'l Petey*.
- Forms the crime-fighting trio known as the *Supa Buddies*, adopting the superhero mantle **The Bark Knight** alongside Li'l Petey as *Cat Kid* and the gentle artistic robot *80-HD* as *Lightning Dude*.
- Promotes literacy and emotional growth for reluctant readers, inspired by Pilkey's personal experiences growing up with ADHD and dyslexia.
- Consistently tops international children's bestseller lists, leading to the successful educational spin-off comic series *Cat Kid Comic Club*.

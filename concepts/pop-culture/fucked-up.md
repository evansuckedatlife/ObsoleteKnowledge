## summary

**Fucked Up** is an acclaimed Canadian hardcore punk band formed in Toronto, Ontario, in 2001. Celebrated for transforming abrasive punk rock into ambitious narrative concept albums, the group won the prestigious Polaris Music Prize in 2009 for their sprawling opus *The Chemistry of Common Life*. Fronted by charismatic vocalist *Damian Abraham*, the band stands as one of the most creatively inventive and critically lauded alternative music acts of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Formed in Toronto, Ontario, in 2001, building an initial reputation through a massive catalog of independent seven-inch vinyl records, singles, and thematic releases.
- Fronted by vocalist *Damian Abraham* (also known as *Father Damian*), whose abrasive, gravelly growls contrast sharply with complex three-guitar arrangements and melodic textures.
- Won the prestigious Polaris Music Prize in 2009 for their second studio album, *The Chemistry of Common Life*, beating out mainstream pop and rock competitors across Canada.
- Known for expansive conceptual narrative records, most notably their celebrated 2011 rock opera *David Comes to Life*, which tells a tragic love story set in Thatcherite [[england]].
- Embarked on the ambitious ongoing *Zodiac* series of extended plays, issuing experimental, long-form musical compositions named after astrological animals.
- Established a rich lineage in Canadian independent music, frequently appearing on critical lists alongside peers such as *Arcade Fire* and historical forebears like *The Tragically Hip*.

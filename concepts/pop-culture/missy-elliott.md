## summary

**Missy Elliott** (born *Melissa Arnette Elliott*, also known professionally as **Missy "Misdemeanor" Elliott**) is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer who transformed the sonic and visual landscape of modern hip-hop and R&B. Rising out of Portsmouth, Virginia alongside childhood collaborator *Timbaland*, she introduced syncopated polyrhythms, futuristic digital textures, and playful, body-positive video concepts. As the first female hip-hop artist inducted into both the Songwriters Hall of Fame and the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, she stands as one of the most prolific creative forces in American popular music.

## you gotta know

- Partnered with legendary producer *Timbaland* (Timothy Mosley) to craft a signature sonic palette defined by syncopated off-kilter percussion, vocal sound effects, and experimental bass arrangements.
- Debuted her groundbreaking solo album *Supa Dupa Fly* in 1997, which premiered at number three on the Billboard 200 and produced the iconic hit single *The Rain (Supa Dupa Fly)*.
- Captured worldwide visual attention with the music video for *The Rain*, directed by *Hype Williams*, dancing energetically while wearing an inflated black patent-leather garbage bag suit against an extreme fisheye lens.
- Penned and produced seminal hits for prominent artists across the 1990s and 2000s, including *Aaliyah* (*One in a Million*), *Monica*, *Ciara*, *SWV*, and *Destiny's Child*.
- Released a continuous sequence of critically lauded, Platinum-certified albums including *Da Real World*, *Miss E... So Addictive*, and *Under Construction*.
- Reached the top of global charts with innovative singles utilizing backwards vocal masking, onomatopoeic lyrics, and diverse world rhythms, such as *Get Ur Freak On* and *Work It*.
- Stepped back from intense touring after being diagnosed in 2008 with Graves' disease, an autoimmune thyroid disorder that affected her motor skills, before making an acclaimed live return at the 2015 Super Bowl.
- Inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2019 and became the first female rapper inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame during her inaugural year of eligibility in 2023.

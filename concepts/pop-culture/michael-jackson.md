## summary

**Michael Jackson**, heralded globally as the "King of Pop," was an American singer, songwriter, and dancer whose unparalleled showmanship transformed modern music and entertainment. Beginning his career in childhood as the energetic frontman of *The Jackson 5*, he evolved into a solo global phenomenon whose record-breaking album *Thriller* remains the best-selling studio record of all time. Through groundbreaking music videos, revolutionary dance moves like the moonwalk, and sonic innovation across pop, soul, rock, and funk, he fundamentally reshaped the global music industry and visual media.

## you gotta know

- Started his career as a child prodigy leading *The Jackson 5* on the *Motown* label, producing chart-topping classic hits like "I Want You Back", "ABC", and "I'll Be There".
- Released *Thriller* in 1982, produced by *Quincy Jones*, which yielded seven top-ten singles including "Billie Jean" and "Beat It", cementing its position as the best-selling album in history.
- Introduced his signature dance move, the "moonwalk", to an audience of tens of millions during the 1983 televised special *Motown 25: Yesterday, Today, Forever* while performing "Billie Jean".
- Transformed the music video into an ambitious narrative art form with the 14-minute cinematic epic *Thriller*, directed by *John Landis*, breaking racial barriers on the newly launched *MTV* network.
- Followed with blockbuster studio records including *Off the Wall*, *Bad*, *Dangerous*, and *HIStory*, continuously redefining contemporary pop and R&B production standards.
- Co-wrote the multi-artist charity anthem "We Are the World" in 1985 with *Lionel Richie*, which raised tens of millions of dollars for humanitarian famine relief in Africa.
- Popularized iconic visual fashion staples recognized across the globe, such as the single white rhinestone glove, military-style braided jackets, aviator sunglasses, and fedora hats.
- Inducted twice into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame—first as a member of *The Jackson 5* in 1997, and subsequently as a solo artist in 2001.

## summary

**Joey Tribbiani** (*Joseph Francis Tribbiani Jr.*) is a fictional character portrayed by actor *Matt LeBlanc* on the seminal American television sitcom *Friends* (1994–2004) and its spin-off series *Joey*. An endearing, food-obsessed, and promiscuous Italian-American actor living in *[[new-york-city]]*, he serves as the primary comic relief and emotional anchor among the show's six central companions. The character became a cultural phenomenon of late-twentieth-century broadcast television, celebrated for his iconic pickup lines, dim-witted innocence, and unwavering loyalty to his friends.

## you gotta know

- Famous for his trademark romantic catchphrase, *"How you doin'?"*, which entered mainstream English vernacular as one of the most widely recognized television quotes of the 1990s.
- Portrayed across ten seasons as a struggling stage and screen performer whose career breakthrough occurs when cast as neurosurgeon *Dr. Drake Ramoray* on the fictionalized daytime soap opera *Days of Our Lives*.
- Defined by an insatiable appetite, fiercely guarding his meals under the personal rule *"Joey doesn't share food!"*, with a special devotion to pizza, meatball subs, and jam.
- Shares an iconic domestic partnership in a Greenwich Village apartment with his best friend *Chandler Bing*, famously raising a chick and a duck while lounging in matching leather recliners.
- Demonstrates deep affection for a beloved stuffed penguin named *Hugsy*, whom he cherishes as his bedtime pal and finds impossible to sleep without.
- Possesses an unusually large extended family based in Queens, including seven sisters—*Gina*, *Tina*, *Dina*, *Mary Angela*, *Mary Therese*, *Veronica*, and *Cookie*—who share his boisterous personality.
- Underwent a controversial romantic storyline in later seasons with *Rachel Green* after inadvertently proposing to her with an engagement ring that fell out of *Ross Geller*'s jacket pocket in the hospital.

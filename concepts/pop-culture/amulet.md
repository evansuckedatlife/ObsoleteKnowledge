## summary

**Amulet** is a critically acclaimed fantasy graphic novel series created by Japanese-American author and illustrator *Kazu Kibuishi*, published by Graphix, an imprint of Scholastic. Debuting in 2008 with *The Stonekeeper*, the nine-volume saga follows young siblings Emily and Navin Hayes as they journey into an alternate universe called Alledia to rescue their kidnapped mother. The series achieved massive commercial success and critical acclaim, becoming a perennial fixture on middle-grade bestseller lists and a major influence on twenty-first-century comic book storytelling.

## you gotta know

- Created, written, and illustrated by *Kazu Kibuishi*, who concluded the sweeping nine-volume overarching narrative in 2024 with the publication of *Waverider*.
- Opens with a domestic *tragedy*, as protagonist Emily Hayes witnesses the traumatic death of her father in a car crash before inheriting a magical heirloom from her great-grandfather Silas Charnon.
- Blends classic western fantasy tropes with the artistic pacing and cinematic aesthetic of Japanese animation, heavily inspired by the work of *Hayao Miyazaki*.
- Follows Emily's journey as she assumes the perilous mantle of a Stonekeeper, struggling against the malevolent, sentient voice within her titular talisman that seeks complete psychological control.
- Features a sprawling, mechanical fantasy world complete with sentient walking houses, anthropomorphic animals, airships, and the villainous Elf King.
- Highlighted by major literary bodies, earning recurring placements on the New York Times Best Seller list and the American Library Association's Great Graphic Novels for Teens.
- Adapted into widely celebrated classroom curriculum texts that fueled the mainstream recognition of comic book formats within contemporary youth literacy.

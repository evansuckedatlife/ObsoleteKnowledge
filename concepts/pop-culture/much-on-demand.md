## summary

**MuchOnDemand** (frequently abbreviated as **MOD**) was a daily live afternoon music video and youth culture television program broadcast across Canada on [[muchmusic]] from 2001 until 2010. Originating from the landmark glass-fronted street-level studio at 299 Queen Street West in downtown Toronto, the show functioned as the quintessential Canadian counterpart to MTV's American series *Total Request Live*. It played a central role in millennial Canadian pop culture by integrating real-time viewer voting, daily crowds of teenage fans lining the public sidewalk, and promotional press appearances by leading global recording stars. The series stands as a definitive document of turn-of-the-century music broadcasting before online on-demand streaming dismantled afternoon video countdown programming.

## you gotta know

- Aired live every weekday afternoon as the flagship daily broadcast of the [[muchmusic]] network, directly succeeding earlier interactive request formats such as *Dial-Style*, *MuchUSA*, and *Live@Much*.
- Broadcast directly out of the open-concept studio at 299 Queen Street West in Toronto, allowing spectators gathered on Queen Street to be seen through large ground-floor windows and actively brought into live broadcasts.
- Relied upon an interactive multimedia voting mechanism where Canadian viewers submitted requests and voted for top daily music videos through landline telephones, early web portals, and mobile text messaging.
- Launched and solidified the public profiles of prominent Canadian video jockeys, including *Rick Campanelli* (widely known as *Rick the Temp*), *George Stroumboulopoulos*, *Leah Miller*, *Devon Soltendieck*, *Sarah Taylor*, and *Matte Babel*.
- Functioned as an essential promotional tour stop in the Canadian market for global entertainment acts, hosting chaotic appearances and sidewalk fan mobs for *Britney Spears*, *Eminem*, *Destiny's Child*, *Backstreet Boys*, and *Green Day*.
- Strictly fulfilled Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC) domestic content quotas by regularly spotlighting domestic acts such as *Sum 41*, *Simple Plan*, *Billy Talent*, *Avril Lavigne*, and *K-os*.
- Hosted special extended editions and fan rallies in the lead-up to the annual *MuchMusic Video Awards*, serving as the daily staging ground for live outdoor stage performances and celebrity interviews.
- Rebranded in 2010 under the revised title *New.Music.Live.*, marking an effort to adapt to shifting adolescent viewing habits before linear television music programming waned in favor of digital streaming.

## connections

- [[muchmusic]] — the parent television specialty channel that conceived, packaged, and aired the program from its downtown Toronto studios.
- [[murdoch-mysteries]] — long-running Canadian television drama produced in Toronto that shared heritage with the CHUM media network.
- [[music-from-big-pink]] — landmark recording that established Canadian rock identity, paving the way for the domestic artist culture championed by the show.
- [[united-states]] — origin of the American music television industry and MTV, whose daily *Total Request Live* served as the direct commercial competitor.
- [[camera-movement]] — dynamic production philosophy pioneered by the channel that rejected stationary studio camera desks in favor of roving shoulder rigs.
- [[i-love-lucy]] — classic broadcast model whose formal multi-camera television studio tradition was radically inverted by the freeform street-level environment.

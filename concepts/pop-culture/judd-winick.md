## summary

**Judd Winick** is an American comic book writer, cartoonist, and television personality who first rose to national prominence as a cast member on MTV's reality series *The Real World: San Francisco* in 1994. He received widespread critical praise for his autobiographical graphic novel *Pedro and Me*, which documented his deep bond with roommate and AIDS educator *Pedro Zamora*. Winick transitioned into a prominent mainstream superhero writer for DC Comics, scripting influential story arcs across *Green Lantern*, *Green Arrow*, and *Batman*, most notably resurrecting *Jason Todd* as the antihero *Red Hood*.

## you gotta know

- Gained public celebrity in 1994 on *The Real World: San Francisco*, where his close friendship with HIV-positive educator *Pedro Zamora* propelled him into prominent public health activism following Zamora's death.
- Created the acclaimed graphic memoir *Pedro and Me: Friendship, Loss, and What I Learned* in 2000, garnering an Eisner Award nomination, a Robert F. Kennedy Journalism Award, and national educational praise for demystifying the AIDS epidemic.
- Penned the pivotal 2005 *Batman* comic storyline *Under the Hood*, in which *Jason Todd*—the second Robin who had been murdered by the Joker in 1988—returned to life as the vengeful vigilante *Red Hood*.
- Adapted his own comic storyline into the feature-length animated film *Batman: Under the Red Hood* in 2010, which received widespread praise for its dramatic tension and emotional gravity.
- Authored a notable run on *Green Lantern* that featured the character *Terry Berg*, an assistant whose brutal assault in a hate crime storyline earned the book a GLAAD Media Award for addressing anti-LGBTQ+ violence.
- Created and executive-produced the animated television series *The Life and Times of Juniper Lee* for Cartoon Network, which ran from 2005 to 2007 and followed a preteen protector of a mystical dimension.
- Scripted the twenty-four-issue biweekly comic maxiseries *Justice League: Generation Lost* from 2010 to 2011, following former international heroes tracking the resurrected villain *Maxwell Lord*.
- Launched the bestselling children's graphic novel series *Hilo* in 2015, chronicling the comedic and imaginative adventures of an amnesiac extraterrestrial robot boy on Earth.

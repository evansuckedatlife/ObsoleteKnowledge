## summary

**A Haunting** is an American paranormal anthology docudrama television series that premiered in 2005 on the *Discovery Channel* before moving to *Destination America* and *Travel Channel*. Developed from two popular television specials produced in 2002, the program dramatizes purported eyewitness accounts of ghost hauntings, demonic possessions, and poltergeist activity across the [[united-states]]. It is widely recognized for popularizing modern supernatural docudrama tropes, combining moody re-enactments, dramatic cinematic pacing, and grave narration.

## you gotta know

- Premiered as a full television series in October 2005 following the viral ratings success of two initial two-hour specials, *A Haunting in Connecticut* and *A Haunting in Georgia*, which aired on the *Discovery Channel* in 2002.
- Narrated throughout the vast majority of its multi-decade broadcast run by character voice actor *Anthony D. Call*, whose solemn, ominous delivery became an iconic sonic trademark of 2000s cable television docudramas.
- Utilizes cinematic dramatizations alongside direct interview segments with eyewitnesses, local paranormal investigators, Catholic demonologists, and self-described psychic mediums.
- Frequently features cases investigated by prominent paranormal celebrities *Ed and Lorraine Warren*, including the famous Snedeker family mortuary case and the Smurl family infestation in West Pittston, Pennsylvania.
- Popularized a rigid episodic taxonomy of escalating malevolence: beginning with gentle sensory oddities, advancing to physical terrorization and poltergeist disruption, and concluding in direct demonic infestation or possession.
- Inspired high-profile Hollywood feature films, including the 2009 psychological horror film *The Haunting in Connecticut* starring *Virginia Madsen*, loosely adapted from the series' inaugural special.
- Featured eerie scoring and atmospheric cinematic techniques that echoed classic suspense television, utilizing handheld framing, dark practical lighting, and stylized visual distortion to represent unseen entities.

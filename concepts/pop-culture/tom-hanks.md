## summary

**Tom Hanks** is a legendary American actor, director, and producer widely celebrated as one of the most bankable, universally admired screen performers in cinema history. Rising from 1980s television comedies into dramatic supremacy throughout the 1990s and beyond, he earned consecutive Academy Awards for Best Actor for *Philadelphia* and *Forrest Gump*. Often termed "America's Dad" and modern heir to [[james-stewart]], Hanks has profoundly shaped historical storytelling regarding the [[united-states]] in twentieth-century warfare, space exploration, and democratic resilience.

## you gotta know

- Won back-to-back Academy Awards for Best Actor for portraying gay lawyer Andrew Beckett battling HIV/AIDS in *Philadelphia* (1993) and the titular Alabama innocent traveling through decades of American history in *Forrest Gump* (1994).
- Began his screen career in comedy, achieving breakout recognition on the television sitcom *Bosom Buddies* before starring in smash hit feature films like *Splash* and *Big*.
- Forged a historic artistic partnership with director *Steven Spielberg*, starring in monumental works including *Saving Private Ryan*, *Catch Me If You Can*, *The Terminal*, and *Bridge of Spies*.
- Produced landmark historical television miniseries depicting military history, most notably *Band of Brothers* and *The Pacific*, chronicling [[world-war-ii]].
- Voiced the heroic pull-string cowboy doll *Sheriff Woody* across four acclaimed installments of Pixar's groundbreaking *Toy Story* animated franchise.
- Portrayed real-life American survival figures in tense cinematic biographies, including astronaut *Jim Lovell* in *Apollo 13*, merchant mariner *Richard Phillips* in *Captain Phillips*, and heroic aviator *Chesley Sullenberger* in *Sully*.
- Received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016 in recognition of his extensive artistic and philanthropic contributions to American cultural life.

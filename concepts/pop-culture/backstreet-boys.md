## summary

**Backstreet Boys** is an American vocal harmony group formed in Orlando, Florida, in 1993, comprising *Nick Carter*, *Howie Dorough*, *AJ McLean*, *Brian Littrell*, and *Kevin Richardson*. Shaped by the songwriting of Swedish mastermind *Max Martin* and the management of *Lou Pearlman*, the group became the top-selling boy band in global music history, tallying more than 100 million records sold worldwide. Their tight five-part vocal arrangements and synchronized staging spearheaded the late-1990s worldwide teen pop resurgence alongside peers such as *NSYNC* and *Britney Spears*.

## you gotta know

- Their defining 1999 single "I Want It That Way" from the multi-platinum album *Millennium* reached number one in over twenty-five countries and collected three Grammy Award nominations.
- The quintet built their massive international commercial foundation in mainland Europe—especially within Germany, Austria, and the UK—several years before attaining mainstream commercial recognition in their native United States.
- The memorable music video for "Everybody (Backstreet's Back)," directed by *Joseph Kahn*, cast the members as classic movie monsters such as a vampire, a mummy, and a werewolf trapped in a gothic haunted manor.
- The members brought a sweeping civil lawsuit against manager *Lou Pearlman* and his firm *Trans Continental Records* in 1998, exposing deceptive management agreements where Pearlman received the lion's share of their multimillion-dollar revenue.
- The airport tarmac and hangar sequence for "I Want It That Way," filmed at Los Angeles International Airport, became one of the quintessential visual monuments of MTV's *Total Request Live* golden age.
- Both of their landmark studio albums, *Millennium* (1999) and *Black & Blue* (2000), achieved platinum certifications in their first weeks of release in the US, with *Millennium* selling over 1.13 million units within seven days.
- Unlike traditional manufactured studio acts, the group grounded their vocal identity in tight five-part a cappella harmony, taking early stylistic inspiration from gospel traditions and contemporary R&B vocal quartets like *Boyz II Men*.

## summary

**Elliot Page** is an Oscar-nominated Canadian actor, producer, and prominent LGBTQ+ activist who gained international fame for his breakout role in the 2007 comedy-drama film *Juno*. Known for his sharp, emotionally resonant screen presence, he has starred in major Hollywood blockbusters including *Inception* and the *X-Men* franchise, as well as the hit television adaptation *The Umbrella Academy*. Page is recognized globally as one of the most prominent openly transgender men in the entertainment industry.

## you gotta know

- Achieved widespread critical acclaim and an Academy Award nomination for Best Actress for playing the witty, pregnant title teenager in *Jason Reitman*'s indie hit *Juno* (2007).
- Portrayed *Kitty Pryde* (Shadowcat) in the superhero films *X-Men: The Last Stand* (2006) and *X-Men: Days of Future Past* (2014).
- Starred as the brilliant architecture student *Ariadne* in *Christopher Nolan*'s mind-bending psychological thriller *Inception* (2010), designing dream worlds for corporate espionage.
- Came out publicly as a transgender man in December 2020, sharing his transition on social media and subsequently appearing on the cover of *Time* magazine in March 2021 as the first openly trans man to do so.
- Played *Vanya Hargreeves* in the television series *The Umbrella Academy*, a narrative arc that was affirmatively updated in season three to reflect his transition as the character *Viktor Hargreeves*.
- Published the candid, bestselling 2023 memoir *Pageboy*, detailing his experiences with Hollywood exploitation, body dysphoria, mental health, and queer self-discovery.
- Co-directed and produced the 2019 documentary *There's Something in the Water*, highlighting the devastating environmental racism impacting Indigenous and Black Canadian communities in Nova Scotia.

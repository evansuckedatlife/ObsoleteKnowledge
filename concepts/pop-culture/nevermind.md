## summary

**Nevermind** is the landmark second studio album by American rock band *Nirvana*, released on September 24, 1991, by DGC Records. Produced by *Butch Vig*, the record catapulted alternative rock, grunge, and Pacific Northwest counterculture from underground obscurity straight into mainstream global consciousness. Driven by its explosive lead single *Smells Like Teen Spirit*, the album radically transformed the modern music landscape, famously displacing *Michael Jackson*'s *Dangerous* from the summit of the Billboard 200 chart.

## you gotta know

- Propelled *Nirvana*—comprising frontman *Kurt Cobain*, bassist *Krist Novoselic*, and newly recruited drummer *Dave Grohl*—into reluctant global megastardom.
- Spawned monumental alternative rock anthems including *Smells Like Teen Spirit*, *Come as You Are*, *Lithium*, and *In Bloom*.
- Recorded primarily at Sound City Studios in Van Nuys, California, under producer *Butch Vig*, who utilized polished dynamic tracking and vocal double-tracking.
- Surpassed all commercial expectations of label executives, knocking *Michael Jackson*'s *Dangerous* off the number-one spot on the Billboard 200 in January 1992.
- Features one of the most iconic cover photographs in history, depicting an infant underwater swimming toward a dollar bill hooked on a fishing line.
- Ended the commercial dominance of 1980s hair metal and synth-dominated pop radio, inaugurating the widespread corporate embrace of the 1990s alternative rock movement.
- Concludes with the haunting, acoustic-driven track *Something in the Way*, as well as the unlisted noisy hidden track *Endless, Nameless*.

## summary

**Hack-and-slash** is a video game and tabletop role-playing subgenre centered on fast-paced, real-time melee combat against large waves of enemies. Originating in tabletop gaming to describe campaigns focused purely on violence over storytelling, it evolved into an arcade and 3D action staple defined by intricate combos, acrobatic movement, and weapon mastery. Key franchises such as *Devil May Cry*, *God of War*, and *Dynasty Warriors* established it as a cornerstone of modern gaming action.

## you gotta know

- Began as a tabletop role-playing term for dungeon crawls that prioritized monster-slaying and loot over deep narrative or character interaction.
- Transitioned to digital arcades through classic cooperative titles such as *Gauntlet* and fantasy side-scrollers like *Golden Axe*.
- Evolved into complex 3D "character action" games following *Hideki Kamiya*'s breakthrough 2001 release *Devil May Cry*.
- Distinguishes itself from standard action-adventure games by emphasizing stylish combo meters, juggles, and precise dodging or parrying mechanics.
- Subgenres range from the massive battlefield skirmishes of *Dynasty Warriors* to the dark, loot-driven isometric dungeons of *Diablo*.
- Influential modern titles like *Bayonetta*, *Ninja Gaiden*, and the early Greek saga of *God of War* pushed the limits of combat speed and mechanical precision.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the country where developers like *Capcom* and *PlatinumGames* revolutionized the modern 3D character-action formula.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — a parallel action genre that emphasizes ranged gunplay rather than acrobatic melee combos.
- [[skyrim]] — open-world fantasy role-playing game that incorporates melee dungeon exploration alongside deep worldbuilding.
- [[minecraft]] — sandbox survival title featuring simple melee weapon combat against nighttime waves of monsters.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming sphere closely tied to mechanical mastery and high input speed.
- [[grime]] — hyper-kinetic musical genre matching the rhythmic intensity and adrenaline of fast-paced action gameplay.

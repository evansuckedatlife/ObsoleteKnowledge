## summary

**Jimmy Kimmel** is an American television host, comedian, writer, and producer who has anchored the late-night talk show *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* on ABC since 2003. Born in Brooklyn, [[new-york-city]], and raised in Las Vegas, he established the longest-running late-night hosting tenure among contemporary network broadcasters in the [[united-states]]. His satirical style blends biting political commentary, celebrity pranks, and high-profile emceeing duties for major entertainment awards ceremonies.

## you gotta know

- Host and executive producer of *Jimmy Kimmel Live!*, which premiered on ABC in January 2003 and evolved into an iconic fixture of modern American television.
- Hosted the prestigious Academy Awards telecast multiple times, steering Hollywood's biggest night through major television moments including the infamous 2017 Best Picture envelope mishap.
- Gained early television prominence on Comedy Central as the co-host of the game show *Win Ben Stein's Money* and the irreverent sketch series *The Man Show*.
- Popularized recurring comedic segments such as *Celebrity Mean Tweets*, *Unnecessary Censorship*, and an elaborate mock feud with actor *Matt Damon*.
- Shifted toward poignant social commentary in 2017 by delivering heartfelt monologues advocating for healthcare access following his infant son *Billy's* open-heart surgery.
- Began his entertainment career as a morning radio disc jockey, performing on stations in Phoenix, Seattle, and Los Angeles before transitioning to broadcast television.
- Engaged in an April Fools' Day tradition in 2022 by trading late-night talk shows with rival network host [[jimmy-fallon]].

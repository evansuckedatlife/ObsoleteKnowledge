## summary

**PinkPantheress**, the professional stage moniker of *Victoria Beverley Walker*, is an English singer, songwriter, and producer who rose to international prominence in the early 2020s through viral music clips shared on social media. She is celebrated for pioneering a bedroom-pop revival of early-2000s British electronic genres, seamlessly fusing drum and bass, 2-step garage, and jungle breakbeats with wistful, diary-like pop melodies. Her breakout collaborations and critically acclaimed projects transformed her into one of the definitive alternative pop voices of Generation Z.

## you gotta know

- Achieved global viral recognition in 2021 through short previews of tracks like *Just for Me* and *Pain*, recorded in her university dorm room using sampled drum and bass rhythms.
- Scored a massive worldwide chart hit alongside American rapper *Ice Spice* on the remix of *Boy's a Liar Pt. 2*, which reached the upper echelon of the *Billboard Hot 100* in the [[united-states]].
- Won the prestigious *BBC Sound of 2022* industry poll, marking her rapid ascent from anonymous internet producer to mainstream critically acclaimed artist.
- Released her debut mixtape *To Hell with It* in 2021, distinguished by remarkably brief track lengths that rarely exceed two minutes yet showcase dense melodic hooks.
- Draws sonic inspiration from turn-of-the-century UK electronic dance movements that flourished across [[england]], repurposing nostalgic samples with contemporary bedroom-pop vocal intimacy.
- Released her full-length debut studio album *Heaven Knows* in 2023, expanding her aesthetic into gothic romance, indie pop, and dance-pop arrangements.

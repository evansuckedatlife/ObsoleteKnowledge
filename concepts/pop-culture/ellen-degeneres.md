## summary

**Ellen DeGeneres** is an American comedian, television host, actress, and producer who pioneered queer visibility on mainstream network television during the late 1990s. After gaining national prominence as a stand-up comic, she starred in the acclaimed sitcom *Ellen*, where her character's public coming-out made television history. She subsequently hosted the syndicated daytime variety talk show *The Ellen DeGeneres Show* for nearly two decades, winning dozens of Daytime Emmy Awards and cementing her status as an enduring entertainment icon.

## you gotta know

- Began her career performing stand-up comedy in clubs during the early 1980s, gaining national acclaim following a 1986 appearance on *The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson*, who invited her to the couch on her very first visit.
- Starred as bookstore owner *Ellen Morgan* in the *ABC* sitcom *Ellen*, making television history in the 1997 two-part landmark broadcast "The Puppy Episode" by coming out as gay simultaneously in fiction and in real life.
- Voiced the delightfully forgetful Pacific regal blue tang *Dory* in *Pixar*'s acclaimed animated feature *Finding Nemo* (2003) and its direct sequel *Finding Dory* (2016).
- Launched *The Ellen DeGeneres Show* in 2003, a massively popular daytime talk program famous for celebrity interviews, audience dancing, and her sign-off motto, "Be kind to one another."
- Hosted the Academy Awards ceremony twice, notably orchestrating a record-shattering celebrity selfie at the 86th Academy Awards in 2014 that temporarily crashed *Twitter*.
- Awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom by President *Barack Obama* in 2016 in recognition of her courage and profound contributions to LGBTQ+ civil rights.
- Concluded her daytime talk show in 2022 following extensive workplace toxicity allegations that challenged her wholesome public image.

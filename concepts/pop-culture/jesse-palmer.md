## summary

**Jesse Palmer**, born **Jesse James Palmer**, is a Canadian television host, sports commentator, and former professional football quarterback who rose to national prominence across North American broadcast media. After playing college football for the *Florida Gators* and spending five seasons in the *National Football League*, he transitioned into television entertainment, notably starring in 2004 as the lead on the fifth season of *The Bachelor*. He subsequently established himself as a versatile network anchor, sports analyst, and the permanent host of the entire *Bachelor* entertainment franchise.

## you gotta know

- Starred as the titular romantic lead in season five of ABC's hit reality television series *The Bachelor* in 2004, becoming the franchise's first professional athlete bachelor.
- Named the permanent host of *The Bachelor* and *The Bachelorette* in 2021, succeeding longtime franchise host *Chris Harrison*.
- Played collegiate quarterback for the *University of Florida Gators* from 1997 to 2000 under legendary head coach *Steve Spurrier*.
- Drafted into the *National Football League* in 2001 by the *New York Giants*, serving primarily as backup quarterback behind *Kerry Collins*.
- Finished his athletic career with the *San Francisco 49ers* and the *Montreal Alouettes* of the *Canadian Football League* before retiring from competitive football in 2007.
- Emerged as a prominent college football studio and game analyst for *ESPN* and *ABC*, providing commentary on flagship broadcasts including college football bowl matchups.
- Hosted an extensive portfolio of broadcast lifestyle and culinary programs, including Food Network's *Holiday Baking Championship* and *Spring Baking Championship*.
- Contributed as a special correspondent and lifestyle reporter on national morning television for ABC's flagship program *Good Morning America* based in *[[new-york-city]]*.

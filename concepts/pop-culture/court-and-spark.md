## summary

**Court and Spark** is the acclaimed sixth studio album by Canadian singer-songwriter *Joni Mitchell*, released in January 1974 by Asylum Records. Marking a watershed transition from her earlier acoustic confessional folk style toward jazz-fusion and sophisticated pop arrangements, the record became the greatest commercial success of her career. Exploring romantic ambiguity, emotional independence, and California lifestyles, it is revered as a touchstone of 1970s popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Reached number two on the Billboard 200 in the [[united-states]] and earned four Grammy Award nominations, including Album of the Year, achieving double-platinum sales.
- Showcases the backing of the jazz-fusion group *L.A. Express*, featuring woodwind player *Tom Scott*, guitarist *Larry Carlton*, and drummer *John Guerin*, whose polished sound reshaped Mitchell's music.
- Includes the breezy hit single *Help Me*, Mitchell's only top-ten single on the Billboard Hot 100, which articulates the anxiety and intoxication of entering a passionate romantic liaison.
- Features the autobiographical classic *Free Man in Paris*, written about music executive *David Geffen* and his exhaustion with commercial industry demands while longing for European holiday leisure.
- Features the punchy rocker *Raised on Robbery*, narrating an aggressive, lonely woman soliciting a businessman inside the lounge of an empire hotel.
- Integrates Mitchell's signature open and alternate guitar tunings with elaborate vocal harmonizations and complex chord progressions drawn from contemporary jazz.
- Concludes with a humorous, virtuosic vocalese cover of the *Annie Ross* and *Wardell Gray* standard *Twisted*, featuring cameo spoken lines from comedy duo *Cheech & Chong*.
- Paved the way for Mitchell's increasingly daring avant-garde and jazz explorations on subsequent masterpieces like *The Hissing of Summer Lawns*, *Hejira*, and *Mingus*.

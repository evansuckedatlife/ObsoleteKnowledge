## summary

**Metric** is a Canadian indie rock and synth-pop band formed in 1998 by frontwoman and keyboardist *Emily Haines* and guitarist *James Shaw*. Emerging from the vibrant turn-of-the-millennium alternative explosion with additional members *Joshua Winstead* and *Joules Scott-Key*, the group forged a celebrated sound uniting post-punk guitar grit with sleek electronic new wave. With multiple Juno Awards, landmark crossover anthems, and prominent film soundtrack contributions, they remain one of the most critically respected and commercially durable bands of the modern indie era.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1998 in Toronto by songwriting partners *Emily Haines* and *James Shaw*, who developed their early sound while living in lofts across Montreal, London, and New York.
- Lived in a communal artist loft in Brooklyn in the early 2000s alongside future members of celebrated indie contemporaries *Yeah Yeah Yeahs* and *TV on the Radio*.
- Maintained deep collaborative roots with the acclaimed Canadian indie collective *Broken Social Scene*, in which *Emily Haines* and *James Shaw* frequently performed as core contributors.
- Broke into international critical consciousness with their 2003 debut album *Old World Underground, Where Are You Now?*, featuring post-punk staples *Combat Baby* and *Dead Disco*.
- Achieved major global commercial and critical acclaim with their 2009 LP *Fantasies*, which produced iconic alternative radio hits including *Help I'm Alive* and *Gimme Sympathy*.
- Provided the musical identity for the fictional band The Clash at Demonhead in the 2010 film *Scott Pilgrim vs. the World*, penning the breakout fan-favorite anthem *Black Sheep*.
- Secured multiple *Juno Awards*, including honors for Group of the Year and Alternative Album of the Year, while maintaining complete creative control via their own independent imprint *MMI*.
- Collaborated with legendary rock pioneer *Lou Reed* on the title track of their 2012 studio album *Synthetica*, cementing their stature across generations of alternative music.

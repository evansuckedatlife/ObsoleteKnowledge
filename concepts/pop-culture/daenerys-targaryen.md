## summary

**Daenerys Targaryen**, also known as the **Mother of Dragons** and the **Khaleesi**, is a central protagonist and claimant to the Iron Throne in *George R. R. Martin*'s epic fantasy series *A Song of Ice and Fire* and its television adaptation *Game of Thrones*. Exiled as an infant to the continent of Essos following the downfall of her ruling dynasty, she evolves from a timid political pawn into a formidable conqueror who commands three hatched dragons and vast liberation armies. Her dramatic narrative arc explores themes of monarchical legitimacy, revolutionary justice, imperial conquest, and the corrupting nature of absolute power, culminating in a devastating descent into catastrophic violence and [[tragedy]].

## you gotta know

- Hatched three petrified dragon eggs on the funeral pyre of her husband *Khal Drogo*, emerging unburnt from the flames alongside her newly born dragons named *Drogon*, *Rhaegal*, and *Viserion*.
- Built an overwhelming military coalition across Essos by acquiring and emancipating the disciplined eunuch slave soldiers known as the *Unsullied* in the city of Astapor, alongside a mercenary sellsword army called the *Second Sons*.
- Governed the city of Meereen in Slaver's Bay as an abolitionist monarch, where her attempts to uproot systemic slavery triggered guerrilla resistance from a masked aristocratic insurgency called the *Sons of the Harpy*.
- Wed the Dothraki warlord *Khal Drogo* in an arranged diplomatic marriage engineered by her abusive older brother *Viserys Targaryen* and the merchant prince *Illyrio Mopatis*, earning the Dothraki title of *Khaleesi*.
- Carried an extensive formal title reciting her royal heritage, notably proclaiming herself *Daenerys Stormborn*, *Breaker of Chains*, *Queen of the Andals and the First Men*, and *Lady of the Seven Kingdoms*.
- Allied with *Jon Snow* to confront the supernatural existential threat of the *White Walkers* in the North, sacrificing dragons in the war before marching south against the reigning queen *Cersei Lannister*.
- Succumbed to grief, betrayal, and ancestral paranoia during the final siege of King's Landing, using *Drogon* to incinerate the surrendered city and its civilian population in firestorm devastation before being assassinated by *Jon Snow*.

## connections

- [[tragedy]] — her character trajectory mirrors classic classical and dramatic falls, turning an idealistic liberator into a catastrophic tyrant through isolated hubris.
- [[american-literature]] — stands as one of the most widely recognized and scrutinized characters created in modern American genre fiction.
- [[colonialism]] — her interventionist campaigns across the cities of Slaver's Bay evoke complex political allegories of foreign regime change and cultural imposition.
- [[england]] — the dynastic struggles of her ancestral house in Westeros draw direct historical inspiration from the fifteenth-century Wars of the Roses.
- [[william-shakespeare]] — her descent into paranoid autocracy and subsequent regicide bears close structural parallels to tragic figures like *Macbeth*.
- [[skyrim]] — popularized high-fantasy dragon lore and draconic warfare in contemporary popular culture alongside major contemporary video game epics.

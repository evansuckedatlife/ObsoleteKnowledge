## summary

**Jordan Peele** is an acclaimed American filmmaker, screenwriter, producer, and comedian who fundamentally revitalized the cinematic landscape of modern psychological horror and social thriller. After gaining nationwide recognition as an ingenious sketch comic, he transitioned into directing with *Get Out* (2017), which explored racial tension and exploitation through terrifying allegory. His visionary mastery of suspense, symbolic design, and visual technique has established him as one of the most significant auteurs of twenty-first-century American cinema.

## you gotta know

- Made history with his directorial debut *Get Out* (2017), winning the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay and becoming the first African American screenwriter to win the category.
- Formed the influential comedy duo *Key & Peele* with *Keegan-Michael Key*, creating five seasons of Peabody Award-winning sketches that dissected race, codeswitching, and culture.
- Directed the acclaimed horror film *Us* (2019), which utilized doppelgängers known as the *Tethered* to explore societal neglect and class oppression beneath the surface of the *United States*.
- Directed the grand sci-fi spectacle and horror film *Nope* (2022), examining cinematic spectacle, exploitation, and the historical erasure of Black artists in Hollywood.
- Developed his early improvisational comedy credentials on Fox's *Mad TV* alongside future collaborator Key, mastering physical comedy and character transformation.
- Popularized the potent cultural concept of the *Sunken Place*, an indelible visual metaphor for systemic racial subjugation and psychic paralysis.
- Operates the production company *Monkeypaw Productions*, producing groundbreaking genre projects including *BlacKkKlansman*, the 2021 reboot of *Candyman*, and *Monkey Man*.

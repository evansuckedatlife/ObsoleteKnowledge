## summary

**Murdoch Mysteries** is a long-running Canadian period mystery television drama series adapted from the detective novel series created by British-Canadian author *Maureen Jennings*. Debuting in 2008, the series follows police detective *William Murdoch* as he solves intricate homicides in late Victorian and Edwardian Toronto using visionary, proto-scientific investigative methods that foreshadow modern forensics. Broadcast domestically on Citytv and subsequently CBC Television, and distributed across the United States under the title **The Artful Detective**, the program is celebrated for its warm blend of historical fiction, social commentary, and lighthearted steampunk invention.

## you gotta know

- Stars Canadian actor *Yannick Bisson* as Detective *William Murdoch*, a devout Roman Catholic investigator in Station House No. 4 possessing a methodical mind and an inventive engineering intellect.
- Co-stars *Helene Joy* as pathfinding coroner and physician *Dr. Julia Ogden*, *Thomas Craig* as blunt Yorkshire-born *Inspector Thomas Brackenreid*, and *Jonny Harris* as eager constable and aspiring novelist *George Crabtree*.
- Centers on early forensic science and proto-technological innovations, depicting Murdoch's early use of fingerprinting (termed "finger marks"), blood-spatter analysis, surveillance wiretapping, and early ballistics tests.
- Regularly weaves prominent historical personalities into fictional homicide cases, including appearances by *Nikola Tesla*, *Sir Arthur Conan Doyle*, *Alexander Graham Bell*, *Thomas Edison*, and *Winston Churchill*.
- Originated from three 2004-2005 made-for-television films starring *Peter Outerbridge* as Murdoch before being completely recast and rebooted as an episodic hour-long series in 2008.
- Transitioned from its original commercial broadcaster Citytv to public broadcaster CBC Television for its sixth season in 2012, expanding into one of the highest-rated and longest-running dramas in Canadian history.
- Routinely explores late 19th-century social and cultural tensions, including anti-Catholic prejudice in Victorian Toronto, women's suffrage, labor rights struggles, and the emergence of modern municipal infrastructure.

## summary

**Cyberpunk 2077** is an open-world action role-playing video game developed and published by the Polish studio *CD Projekt Red*, released globally in December 2020. Based on the classic tabletop role-playing system created by *Mike Pondsmith*, the game places players into the cybernetically augmented body of mercenary *V* navigating the hyper-capitalist, crime-ridden metropolis of *Night City*. Though its initial launch was marred by severe technical glitches on older home consoles, comprehensive overhauls, the celebrated expansion *Phantom Liberty*, and a hit anime adaptation turned it into one of the most remarkable redemption stories in modern gaming history.

## you gotta know

- Features Hollywood star *Keanu Reeves* as *Johnny Silverhand*, an iconic cybernetic rockerboy and anti-corporate terrorist who exists as a digital construct trapped inside the protagonist's neural implant.
- Adapted directly from *Mike Pondsmith*'s pen-and-paper tabletop system *Cyberpunk 2020*, retaining the rich lore of the *Free State of Northern California* and corporate warfare between *Arasaka* and *Militech*.
- Faced one of the most controversial launches in video game history in December 2020 due to crippling bugs, crashes, and poor frame rates on eighth-generation consoles like the *PlayStation 4* and *Xbox One*.
- The severity of its launch state led *Sony Interactive Entertainment* to take the unprecedented step of pulling the game entirely from the *PlayStation Store* for over six months while offering unconditional digital refunds.
- Experienced an enormous resurgence in critical standing and player engagement following the release of the 2022 companion anime series *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners*, animated by Japan's *Studio Trigger*.
- Implements hybrid role-playing mechanics combining open-world driving, deep dialogue trees, stealth hacking protocols, and fluid gunplay characteristic of a modern [[first-person-shooter]].
- Released a massive 2.0 system overhaul alongside the 2023 spy-thriller expansion *Phantom Liberty*, set in the lawless walled district of *Dogtown* and starring *Idris Elba* as FIA sleeper agent *Solomon Reed*.
- Marked the final major franchise production developed on *CD Projekt Red*'s proprietary *REDengine*, with the studio officially transitioning to *Epic Games*' *Unreal Engine 5* for future installments.

## summary

**Assassin's Creed** is an acclaimed open-world action-adventure and stealth video game franchise published by Ubisoft and primarily developed by its Montreal studio. Debuting in 2007, the series depicts an ancient, covert war between the Assassin Brotherhood, who champion personal liberty and free will, and the Templar Order, who seek world peace through totalitarian control. By marrying parkour traversal and social stealth with elaborate digital recreations of eras like the Third Crusade in the [[middle-east]] and the Italian [[renaissance]], the franchise became a landmark commercial and cultural phenomenon in interactive entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Originally developed under creative director Patrice Désilets as a planned spin-off to *Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time* before evolving into a standalone historical franchise.
- Employs a science-fiction framing narrative centered on the Animus, a virtual reality device that decodes genetic memories to let modern protagonists experience their ancestors' lives.
- Introduced the Syrian assassin Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad in the 2007 debut title, following his quest across Jerusalem, Damascus, and Acre during the Third Crusade in the [[middle-east]].
- Achieved immense critical and commercial popularity through the Italian [[renaissance]] trilogy starring nobleman Ezio Auditore da Firenze, set across Florence, Venice, and [[rome]].
- Incorporates iconic parkour locomotion, the leap of faith dive into haystacks, and wrist-mounted hidden blades used for precision assassinations.
- Weaves historical crises around powerful precursor artifacts called Pieces of Eden, left behind by an ancient superhuman species known as the Isu.
- Expanded its historical canvas to the American Revolution in the [[united-states]], pirate voyages in the Caribbean, revolutionary [[france]], Victorian [[england]], Ptolemaic Egypt, and [[ancient-greece]].
- Transitioned into an action role-playing game framework starting with *Assassin's Creed Origins* in 2017, emphasizing expansive maps, leveling systems, and mythic encounters.

## connections

- [[middle-east]] — setting of the Third Crusade where the foundational conflict between Hashashin and Crusaders unfolds.
- [[renaissance]] — cultural golden age in [[italy]] that serves as the backdrop for Ezio Auditore's quest against the Borgia papacy.
- [[ancient-greece]] — Peloponnesian War setting of *Assassin's Creed Odyssey*, featuring mercenary conflicts between Athens and Sparta.
- [[france]] — historical setting of *Assassin's Creed Unity*, set during the bloody turmoil of the French Revolution.
- [[england]] — backdrop for *Assassin's Creed Syndicate* in Victorian London as well as the Viking invasions of *Assassin's Creed Valhalla*.
- [[united-states]] — backdrop for *Assassin's Creed III*, featuring protagonist Connor Kenway during the American Revolutionary War.
- [[italy]] — cradle of Renaissance humanism where multiple core entries explore secret societies influencing civic life.

## summary

**Dr. Dre**, born **Andre Young**, is a pioneering American record producer, rapper, and audio entrepreneur from Compton, California. As a key architect of West Coast hip-hop and the G-funk sound, he rose to fame with the seminal gangsta rap group N.W.A before achieving massive solo success with his 1992 debut album *The Chronic*. Beyond his musical productions that launched the careers of Snoop Dogg, Eminem, and 50 Cent, he transformed modern consumer audio hardware as the co-founder of Beats Electronics.

## you gotta know

- Co-founded Beats by Dr. Dre with Jimmy Iovine in 2006, leading to a monumental three-billion-dollar acquisition by Apple in 2014.
- Pioneered the G-funk subgenre characterized by slow, hypnotic grooves, heavy basslines, melodic synthesizers, and samples from P-Funk collectives.
- Rose to national prominence as the primary sonic architect and producer for the groundbreaking, controversial hip-hop group N.W.A alongside Eazy-E and Ice Cube.
- Released the definitive 1992 solo album *The Chronic* on Death Row Records, followed by his multi-platinum 1999 follow-up album *2001*.
- Discovered and launched the careers of global hip-hop icons including Snoop Dogg, Eminem, 50 Cent, and Kendrick Lamar via Aftermath Entertainment.
- Started his musical journey in the mid-1980s as a DJ with the electro-hop group World Class Wreckin' Cru in Los Angeles.
- Headlined the 2022 Super Bowl LVI Halftime Show at SoFi Stadium alongside Snoop Dogg, Mary J. Blige, Eminem, and Kendrick Lamar.

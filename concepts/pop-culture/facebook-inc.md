## summary

**Facebook Inc.** is an American multinational technology and social media conglomerate founded in 2004 by *Mark Zuckerberg* alongside fellow Harvard College students. Originating as a collegiate networking platform, the service rapidly transformed digital telecommunications, personal information sharing, and modern public discourse across the globe. Rebranded in 2021 as *Meta Platforms*, its operational history encompasses the rise of algorithmic social networking, ubiquitous internet surveillance, and massive corporate consolidations across Silicon Valley.

## you gotta know

- Founded in a Harvard dormitory in February 2004 by *Mark Zuckerberg*, *Eduardo Saverin*, *Dustin Moskovitz*, and *Chris Hughes*, originally titled *Thefacebook*.
- Expanded from an exclusive network for elite universities to the general public in 2006, swiftly eclipsing incumbent early networks such as *MySpace* and *Friendster*.
- Acquired competing mobile photo-sharing application *Instagram* in 2012 for one billion dollars and global messaging client *WhatsApp* in 2014 for nineteen billion dollars.
- Played a central role in the landmark 2018 *Cambridge Analytica* scandal, which revealed the improper harvesting of personal demographic data for electoral manipulation.
- Popularized fundamental design patterns of the modern social internet, including the persistent user feed, the algorithmic wall, and the universal social "Like" button.
- Rebranded corporate operations under the moniker *Meta Platforms* in October 2021 to signal strategic pivoting toward virtual reality, immersive digital hardware, and the metaverse.

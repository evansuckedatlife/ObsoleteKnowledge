## summary

**Blue (Joni Mitchell album)** is the landmark fourth studio album by Canadian singer-songwriter *Joni Mitchell*, released in June 1971 by Reprise Records. Widely recognized as a pinnacle of confessional songwriting and modern popular music, the record explores the emotional devastation of romantic dissolution, psychological vulnerability, personal independence, and creative exhaustion. Through unorthodox open guitar tunings, intricate Appalachian dulcimer arrangements, and extraordinary vocal expressiveness, the album set a lasting benchmark for artistic introspection in post-sixties lyricism.

## you gotta know

- Recorded following an extended hiatus from live performing in 1970, during which *Joni Mitchell* traveled through Europe with an Appalachian dulcimer and lived in a hippie cave community in Matala, Crete.
- Includes the universally covered Christmas-tinged ballad *River*, which uses the melancholy piano motif from *Jingle Bells* to frame profound self-blame and romantic loss.
- Features the exuberant single *Carey*, directly inspired by her travels with the free-spirited American expatriate *Cary Raditz* in the Greek Mediterranean.
- Draws on the painful disintegration of her high-profile personal relationships with singer-songwriters *Graham Nash* and *James Taylor*, providing the raw emotional core for songs like *My Old Man* and *A Case of You*.
- Showcases prominent instrumental contributions from *James Taylor* on acoustic guitar, *Stephen Stills* on bass and guitar, and *Sneaky Pete Kleinow* on pedal steel.
- Features *A Case of You*, widely hailed by music critics as one of the finest love songs ever written, showcasing her poetic mastery and sweeping vocal range.
- Concludes with the devastatingly sparse track *The Last Time I Saw Richard*, which critiques domestic complacency and romantic disillusionment in a smoky romantic barroom setting.
- Regularly placed in the top tier of all-time greatest record surveys, ranking number three on *Rolling Stone*'s 2020 revision of the 500 Greatest Albums of All Time.

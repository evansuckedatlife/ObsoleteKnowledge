## summary

**Alec Baldwin** (*Alexander Rae Baldwin III*) is an American actor, comedian, and producer renowned for his versatile performances spanning theatre, cinema, and broadcast television. Emerging as a charismatic leading man in late twentieth-century Hollywood, he gained enduring cultural prominence through his critically acclaimed turn as network executive *Jack Donaghy* on the satirical sitcom *30 Rock* and his record-setting comedic runs on *Saturday Night Live*. His commanding presence, distinctive baritone delivery, and deft transitions between dramatic gravity and razor-sharp satire have made him one of the most recognizable figures in modern American entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Starred as corporate executive *Jack Donaghy* alongside creator *Tina Fey* in the acclaimed NBC satirical sitcom *30 Rock* (2006–2013), winning two Primetime Emmy Awards, three Golden Globe Awards, and seven consecutive Screen Actors Guild Awards.
- Holds the all-time record for hosting *Saturday Night Live* seventeen times between 1990 and 2017, frequently returning for memorable recurring sketches such as *Pete Schweddy* and later portraying *Donald Trump* during the 2016 presidential election cycle.
- Delivered an iconic, profane seven-minute motivational monologue as *Blake* in the 1992 film adaptation of *David Mamet's* play *Glengarry Glen Ross*, popularizing catchphrases such as *A-B-C: Always Be Closing* and *coffee's for closers*.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor for his role as veteran Las Vegas casino boss *Shelly Kaplow* in the 2003 romantic drama *The Cooler*.
- Established himself as a prominent cinematic lead and character actor in notable films including *The Hunt for Red October*, *Beetlejuice*, *The Departed*, *The Aviator*, and multiple installments of the *Mission: Impossible* franchise.
- Featured as the brand ambassador in a high-profile commercial campaign alongside *Dan Marino* during Super Bowl 50 to promote the voice-activated smart assistant [[alexa]].
- Involved in the tragic October 2021 on-set shooting death of cinematographer *Halyna Hutchins* during rehearsals for the independent period [[western-film]] *Rust* in Santa Fe County, New Mexico.

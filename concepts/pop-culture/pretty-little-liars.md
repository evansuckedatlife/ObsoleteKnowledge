## summary

**Pretty Little Liars** is an American teen drama mystery television series developed by *I. Marlene King*, based on the popular novel series of the same name by *Sara Shepard*. Running on *ABC Family* (later *Freeform*) from 2010 to 2017 across seven seasons, the show became a defining cultural phenomenon of 2010s youth television and social media engagement. It follows four estranged high school girls in the fictional town of **Rosewood**, Pennsylvania, whose lives unravel when a mysterious stalker threatens to expose their darkest secrets following the disappearance of their clique leader.

## you gotta know

- Centers on four teenage protagonists known as the Liars: *Spencer Hastings* (*Troian Bellisario*), *Aria Montgomery* (*Lucy Hale*), *Hanna Marin* (*Ashley Benson*), and *Emily Fields* (*Shay Mitchell*).
- Catalyzed by the mysterious disappearance of queen bee *Alison DiLaurentis* (*Sasha Pieterse*) during a Labor Day weekend sleepover, whose apparent murder exposes the group's underlying fractures.
- Antagonized by a sinister, omnipresent blackmailer known simply as *"A"*, who torments the group with anonymous text messages ending in signature taunts and secrets only Alison knew.
- Broke multiple television records as one of the most tweeted-about series in broadcast history, popularizing real-time interactive fandom speculation and viral mystery sleuthing.
- Revealed multiple major iterations of the primary antagonist across its run, including the *"Original A"* (*Mona Vanderwaal*), *"Big A"* (*CeCe Drake* / *Charlotte DiLaurentis*), and the ultimate villain *"A.D."* (*Alex Drake*).
- Featured intricate melodramatic plotlines involving secret twins, institutionalizations at the haunting *Radley Sanitarium*, faked deaths, and suburban conspiracies.
- Spawned multiple spin-off series, including *Ravenswood* (2013), *Pretty Little Liars: The Perfectionists* (2019), and the *Max* horror reboot *Pretty Little Liars: Original Sin* (2022).

## summary

**Donkey Kong**, known universally by the abbreviation **DK**, is a foundational arcade video game and multimedia franchise conceived by legendary designer *Shigeru Miyamoto* and published by Nintendo in 1981. Serving as the groundbreaking title that saved Nintendo's American division and established the platforming genre, it introduced an eponymous ape alongside the heroic carpenter *Jumpman*, who was soon rechristened as *Mario*. With its pioneering multi-screen vertical traversal, expressive character animation, and comedic narrative structure, the game revolutionized global interactive entertainment and remains an indelible cornerstone of popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Created in 1981 by a young *Shigeru Miyamoto* under the mentorship of *Gunpei Yokoi*, originally developed to repurpose surplus, unsold coin-op arcade cabinets of the failed title *Radar Scope*.
- Established the fundamental mechanics of the platformer genre, introducing running, climbing, jumping over rolling barrels, and wielding protective hammers across four distinct construction-site girder screens.
- Served as the debut appearance of both the title character and *Mario*, originally dubbed *Jumpman*, who sought to rescue the captive heroine *Lady* (later known as *Pauline*).
- Derived its peculiar English name from *Miyamoto*'s belief that "donkey" conveyed stubborn, goofy obstinacy in an English dictionary, paired with "Kong" to evoke classical cinema's *King Kong*.
- Sparked a landmark corporate copyright lawsuit from *Universal Studios* against Nintendo in 1982, in which attorney *John Kirby* famously established that King Kong was in the public domain, saving Nintendo millions.
- Revolutionized the arcade industry by integrating narrative cutscenes and character-driven motivation, replacing the abstract space shooting formulas common in early electronic games with visual storytelling.
- Re-emerged as a high-stakes competitive phenomenon featured in the acclaimed 2007 documentary *The King of Kong: A Fistful of Quarters*, which chronicled the rivalry between *Steve Wiebe* and *Billy Mitchell*.
- Re-invented in 1994 by British developer *Rare* with *Donkey Kong Country*, which utilized groundbreaking pre-rendered Silicon Graphics 3D sprites to revitalize the Super Nintendo console.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the nation where Nintendo and designer *Shigeru Miyamoto* conceived the game, kickstarting a golden era of global video game exports.
- [[united-states]] — the massive overseas coin-op market where the arcade cabinet became an unprecedented commercial sensation and pop-culture craze.
- [[slapstick-comedy]] — the visual humor, rolling barrels, and exaggerated cartoon falls that inspired the game's playful character interactions.
- [[physical-comedy]] — the slapstick timing, expressive pantomime, and chest-thumping animations central to the giant ape's personality.
- [[esports]] — the high-score arcade leaderboard culture and competitive scene that developed around mastering the game's unforgiving kill-screen mechanics.
- [[cold-war]] — the late-twentieth-century geopolitical era during which commercial electronics and cross-Pacific video game hardware transformed global leisure.

## summary

**Commander Shepard**, commonly known simply as **Shepard**, is the customizable protagonist and central heroic figure of BioWare's science-fiction action role-playing video game trilogy *Mass Effect*, introduced in 2007. Serving as an elite human soldier in the Systems Alliance navy and commander of the stealth starship SSV *Normandy*, the character becomes the first human appointed to the Citadel Council's elite peacekeeping corps, the Spectres. Shepard is celebrated in modern interactive fiction as a defining gaming icon whose moral decisions determine the fate of galactic civilizations confronting the ancient synthetic threat of the Reapers.

## you gotta know

- Voice acting duties are split between two celebrated performers, with *Mark Meer* voicing the male iteration and *Jennifer Hale* providing the acclaimed performance for female Shepard, affectionately known to fans as *FemShep*.
- Player choices are mediated through a dynamic morality system measuring *Paragon* alignment, emphasizing diplomacy and idealism, against *Renegade* alignment, rewarding ruthless pragmatism and decisive action.
- The default visual likeness for the male version was scanned directly from Dutch model *Mark Vanderloo*, leading the gaming community to nickname that specific appearance *Sheploo*.
- Begins the trilogy tracking the treasonous Turian operative *Saren Arterius* and his gargantuan synthetic flagship *Sovereign*, revealing that sentient machines intend to purge all advanced organic life.
- Suffers fatal decompression during an ambush by the mysterious *Collectors* in the opening moments of *Mass Effect 2*, only to be resurrected through the multi-billion-credit *Lazarus Project* funded by the human-survivalist organization *Cerberus* and the enigmatic *Illusive Man*.
- Popularized the widely quoted promotional catchphrase: *"I'm Commander Shepard, and this is my favorite store on the Citadel."*
- Unites a diverse multi-species coalition aboard the *Normandy SR-2* to conduct a daring suicide mission through the Omega-4 Relay into the galactic core.
- Culminates the trilogy during the battle for Earth in *Mass Effect 3*, confronting the *Catalyst* atop the Citadel to select the ultimate fate of synthetic and organic life via the *Destroy*, *Control*, or *Synthesis* conclusions.

## connections

- [[skyrim]] — peer blockbuster Western role-playing game celebrated for profound player agency and heroic protagonist mythmaking.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — genre whose real-time gunplay conventions heavily influenced *Mass Effect*'s mechanical evolution from tactical RPG to cinematic third-person shooter.
- [[conan-obrien]] — comedian and late-night host whose popular video game reviews highlighted romance options and moral dilemmas in expansive RPGs.
- [[existentialism]] — philosophical doctrine reflected in the cyclical extinction agenda of the Reapers and Shepard's individual burden of existential responsibility.
- [[minecraft]] — landmark contemporary title illustrating the broad spectrum of twentieth-first-century interactive world-building and narrative agency.
- [[united-states]] — principal home base for publisher Electronic Arts and the primary production market for modern big-budget role-playing spectacles.

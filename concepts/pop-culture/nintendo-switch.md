## summary

The **Nintendo Switch** is an eighth-generation hybrid video game console developed by Nintendo, unveiled worldwide in March 2017. Engineered to seamlessly transition between a stationary television console and a portable handheld device, it represents the synthesis of the company's historical home console and portable hardware divisions. Powered by strong first-party software and wide third-party backing, it ranks among the most commercially successful gaming systems in history.

## you gotta know

- Utilizes a unique hybrid design centered on a tablet unit that docks to output high-definition video to a television, or docks detached Joy-Con controllers for handheld and tabletop play.
- Launched alongside *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild*, which earned universal critical acclaim and redefined open-world action-adventure game design.
- Features modular *Joy-Con* controllers equipped with advanced haptic feedback termed *HD Rumble*, an infrared motion camera, and gyroscope-driven motion sensing.
- Surpassed one hundred and forty million hardware units sold globally, overtaking legendary systems like the Sony PlayStation 4 and the Nintendo Game Boy.
- Replaced the financially disappointing Wii U era while unifying the separate handheld market previously dominated by the [[nintendo-ds]] and 3DS.
- Received hardware revisions including the dedicated portable *Switch Lite* and an enhanced model featuring a vivid seven-inch *OLED* display screen.
- Set massive software milestones with record-breaking titles such as *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe*, *Super Smash Bros. Ultimate*, and the pandemic-era cultural phenomenon *Animal Crossing: New Horizons*.

## connections

- [[nintendo-ds]] — foundational dual-screen predecessor whose touch input and portable philosophy helped shape Nintendo's hardware identity.
- [[japan]] — country of design, manufacture, and home market for Nintendo and its engineering leadership.
- [[skyrim]] — *Bethesda*'s open-world role-playing masterpiece famously featured in early promotional reveals to showcase heavy third-party support.
- [[minecraft]] — immensely popular building sandbox that achieved massive multi-million unit sales on the portable hybrid platform.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming circuits regularly hosted on the platform for titles like *Super Smash Bros.* and *Splatoon*.

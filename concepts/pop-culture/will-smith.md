## summary

**Will Smith** is an American actor, producer, and hip-hop artist who achieved unparalleled global crossover success across television, popular music, and blockbuster cinema. First rising to fame as a Grammy-winning rapper and sitcom star, he became one of Hollywood's most bankable leading men in history before capturing the Academy Award for Best Actor.

## you gotta know

- Began his entertainment career as the rapper The Fresh Prince alongside DJ Jazzy Jeff, earning the inaugural Grammy Award for Best Rap Performance in 1989 for "Parents Just Don't Understand".
- Starred in the beloved NBC television sitcom *The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air*, establishing his signature screen charisma, comic timing, and emotional dramatic range.
- Dominated the summer blockbuster box office in the mid-1990s with consecutive global smash hits *Bad Boys*, *Independence Day*, and *Men in Black*.
- Became the only actor in cinema history to star in eight consecutive films that grossed over one hundred million dollars domestically in the United States.
- Earned critical acclaim and Academy Award nominations for dramatic portrayals of real-life figures, playing boxing champion Muhammad Ali in *Ali* and struggling salesman Chris Gardner in *The Pursuit of Happyness*.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Actor in 2022 for his performance as Richard Williams, father of Venus and Serena Williams, in the biographical sports drama *King Richard*.
- Overshadowed his Oscar triumph during the 94th Academy Awards ceremony by walking onstage and slapping comedian Chris Rock following an unscripted joke about his wife, Jada Pinkett Smith.

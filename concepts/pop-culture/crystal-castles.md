## summary

**Crystal Castles** was an influential Canadian electronic and experimental synth-punk duo formed in Toronto, Ontario, in 2006 by multi-instrumentalist producer *Ethan Kath* and vocalist-songwriter *Alice Glass*. Renowned for their confrontational stage presence, melancholic melodies, and abrasive fusion of 8-bit chiptune samples with distorted industrial beats, the project became a foundational pillar of late-2000s indie sleaze and pioneered the dark, reverb-drenched aesthetic of witch house. The group achieved international cult status and critical acclaim across three self-titled albums before disintegrating in the mid-2010s amid public legal disputes and abuse allegations.

## you gotta know

- Formed after *Ethan Kath* discovered *Alice Glass* singing in a local noise-punk band called *Fetus Fatale* and invited her to record vocals over his electronic tracks.
- Breakthrough track "Alice Practice" was originally a casual microphone sound-check secretly recorded by the engineer, which *Kath* later uploaded online without *Glass* initially knowing it was being released.
- Derived their moniker from a line in a 1980s animated television commercial for a *She-Ra: Princess of Power* toyline that stated: "The fate of the world is safe in *Crystal Castles*."
- Cemented their underground reputation with their 2008 self-titled debut *[[crystal-castles-album]]*, which popularized an aggressive, crushed-bit electronic sound across international indie rock clubs.
- Released their sophomore effort *Crystal Castles (II)* in 2010, featuring the international hit single "Not in Love" with guest vocals by *Robert Smith* of the iconic English rock band *The Cure*.
- Developed a notorious reputation for chaotic and unpredictable live shows, where *Alice Glass* frequently climbed scaffolding, stagedived into hostile crowds, and sang through distorted megaphones amid blinding strobe lights.
- Faced prominent intellectual property controversies within the 8-bit tracking community over uncredited and unlicensed chip-music samples taken from artists such as *Covox* and *Lo-bat*.
- *Glass* officially departed the group in October 2014; *Kath* recruited vocalist *Edith Frances* to release the 2016 album *Amnesty (I)*, but the project ceased following *Glass* coming forward in 2017 with detailed accounts of severe personal and professional abuse by *Kath*.

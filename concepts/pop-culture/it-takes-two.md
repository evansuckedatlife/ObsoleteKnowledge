## summary

**It Takes Two** is a 2021 cooperative action-adventure platform game developed by Hazelight Studios and published by Electronic Arts under the EA Originals program. Directed by Swedish-Lebanese filmmaker Josef Fares, the game mandates two-player split-screen cooperation to guide an estranged couple trapped inside handcrafted dolls through fantastical domestic environments. Acclaimed for its boundless genre hybridization and narrative pacing, it achieved widespread critical acclaim and won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2021.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Josef Fares*, the outspoken cinematic creator who previously pioneered cooperative storytelling mechanics in *Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons* and *A Way Out*.
- Strictly designed as a two-player cooperative experience with no single-player option, utilizing split-screen presentation even during online play and providing a free *Friend's Pass* allowing one owner to invite an unpaid partner.
- Follows bickering married couple *Cody* and *May*, whose plan to divorce causes their young daughter *Rose* to weep onto two handmade figures, magically trapping their minds inside yarn and clay dolls.
- Mentored and tormented throughout their journey by *Dr. Hakim*, an anthropomorphic, heavily accented relationship self-help book dubbed *The Book of Love* who forces them into collaborative trials.
- Renowned for dispensing with uniform gameplay mechanics, instead granting each player distinct, complementary abilities per chapter, including nail-and-hammer traversal, gravity-shifting boots, time reversal, and magnetic polarities.
- Features a notoriously dark, controversial narrative sequence in which Cody and May brutally dismember and drop Rose's cherished plush toy, *Cutie the Elephant*, falsely believing their daughter's heartbreak and tears will undo the spell.
- Swept major honors across the industry, winning Game of the Year, Best Multiplayer Game, and Best Family Game at the 2021 Game Awards ceremonies.

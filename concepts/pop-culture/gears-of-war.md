## summary

**Gears of War** is a seminal military science fiction third-person shooter franchise created by *Epic Games* and lead designer *Cliff Bleszinski*, debuting on the *Xbox 360* in 2006. Set on the subterranean-threatened planet *Sera*, the series follows scarred soldier *Marcus Fenix* and *Delta Squad* in a desperate war of attrition against the subterranean reptilian *Locust Horde*. Renowned for standardizing modern over-the-shoulder cover mechanics, active reload systems, and chainsaw-bayonet combat, it became a defining pillar of seventh-generation console gaming and competitive multiplayer.

## you gotta know

- Popularized dynamic stop-and-pop tactical gameplay via an over-the-shoulder perspective, redefining cover shooters throughout the late 2000s alongside peer tactical titles like [[counter-strike]].
- Features the signature *Lancer Assault Rifle*, a fictional combat weapon outfitted with an underslung chainsaw bayonet utilized for gory melee executions.
- Introduced the *Active Reload* timing minigame, rewarding players who tap reload at a precise threshold with accelerated reload speeds and temporary damage bonuses.
- Follows protagonist *Marcus Fenix*, voiced by *John DiMaggio*, alongside comrades *Dominic Santiago*, *Damon Baird*, and *Augustus Cole* under the *Coalition of Ordered Governments* (*COG*).
- Chronicled pivotal lore events including *Emergence Day* (*E-Day*), when the subterranean *Locust Horde* suddenly surfaced to eradicate humanity, and the deployment of the devastating orbital laser known as the *Hammer of Dawn*.
- Acquired by *Microsoft Studios* in 2014, shifting development to *The Coalition*, which produced mainline sequels starring Marcus's son *JD Fenix* and *Kait Diaz*.
- Supported significant professional [[esports]] tournaments throughout multiple iterations, especially in North American and Latin American competitive circuits.

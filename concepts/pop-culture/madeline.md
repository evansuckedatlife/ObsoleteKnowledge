## summary

**Madeline** is a classic children's picture book franchise created by Austrian-American author and illustrator *Ludwig Bemelmans*, first published in 1939. Set in a Parisian boarding school run by the nun *Miss Clavel*, the rhyming stories chronicle the misadventures of the smallest and boldest of twelve young schoolgirls. Celebrated for its expressive expressionistic watercolor renderings of landmarks across *[[france]]*, the original book and its sequels earned prestigious honors including the Caldecott Medal and remain enduring staples of twentieth-century [[american-literature]].

## you gotta know

- Every story famously begins with the rhyming couplet: *In an old house in Paris that was covered with vines / lived twelve little girls in two straight lines.*
- The title heroine is the smallest of the twelve girls, distinguished by her fearlessness, fiery red hair, and willingness to scoff at mice and tigers at the zoo.
- In the inaugural 1939 book, *Madeline* awakens in agony in the middle of the night and is rushed by ambulance to the hospital to have her appendix removed.
- Upon returning to school, her surgical scar becomes an object of intense envy among her classmates, prompting all the other girls to cry and demand their own operations.
- The guardian who oversees the girls and famously senses nightly emergencies with the line *Something is not right!* is the Catholic nun *Miss Clavel*.
- The sequel *Madeline's Rescue* introduced the beloved stray canine *Genevieve*, who saves the title character from drowning in the Seine and won the 1954 Caldecott Medal.
- Other notable characters include *Pepito*, the mischievous son of the Spanish ambassador who lives next door and is known initially as the *Bad Hat*.
- *Ludwig Bemelmans* painted real architectural landmarks of Paris across the background plates, including the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame, the Tuileries Garden, and the Place de la Concorde.

## summary

**Chromeo** is an acclaimed electro-funk musical duo formed in Montreal, Canada, in 2002 by childhood friends *David Macklovitch* and *Patrick Gemayel*. Known affectionately by their stage monikers *Dave 1* and *P-Thugg*, the group spearheaded the 21st-century revival of vintage synth-funk, disco, and 1980s Minneapolis sound. With self-aware, humorous lyricism exploring romantic neuroses alongside irresistible dance grooves, they earned a devoted international following and widespread critical respect across modern dance music.

## you gotta know

- Formed by childhood best friends *David "Dave 1" Macklovitch* (lead vocals, guitar) and *Patrick "P-Thugg" Gemayel* (keyboards, synthesizers, talkbox), who frequently joke about their collaboration being the only successful Arab-Jewish partnership in modern pop culture.
- Achieved widespread critical and commercial breakthrough with their 2007 sophomore album *Fancy Footwork*, whose title track became an electro-house and indie-dance anthem that defined the bloghouse era.
- Cultivated a distinct visual and stage identity characterized by tailored suits, chrome accents, vintage talkbox mouth tubes, and signature illuminated keyboard stands modeled after female legs.
- Relies exclusively on authentic vintage analog synthesizers, drum machines, and talkbox effects, channeling musical influences such as *Prince*, *Zapp*, *Cameo*, *Hall & Oates*, and *Rick James*.
- Released prominent hit records including *Jealous (I Ain't With It)*, *Needy Girl*, *Bonafied Lovin'*, *Over Your Shoulder*, and *Night by Night*, celebrating blue-eyed soul and infectious dancefloor hooks.
- Expanded their sonic palette with their fourth studio album *White Women* (2014), which entered the top 15 of the Billboard 200 chart in the [[united-states]] and featured guest appearances by *Solange*, *Toro y Moi*, and *Ezra Koenig*.
- Earned a prestigious Grammy Award nomination for Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical for their fifth full-length record, *Head Over Heels* (2018).
- Features notable academic and musical pedigrees outside the group, as *Dave 1* completed a PhD in French literature at Columbia University in [[new-york-city]], while his younger brother *Alain Macklovitch* (*A-Trak*) is an acclaimed DMC World Champion DJ and founder of *Fool's Gold Records*.

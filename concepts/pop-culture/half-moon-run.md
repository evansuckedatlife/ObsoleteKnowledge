## summary

**Half Moon Run** is an acclaimed Canadian indie rock band formed in 2009 in the Mile End district of Montreal, Quebec. Renowned for their intricate multi-instrumental virtuosity, complex percussion arrangements, and sweeping three-part vocal harmonies, the ensemble merges indie pop, progressive folk, and art rock into deeply evocative soundscapes. Their ambitious songcraft and magnetic live performances have earned them widespread international praise, multiple *Juno Awards*, and a prominent position in contemporary North American alternative music.

## you gotta know

- Formed after multi-instrumentalists *Devon Portielje* from Ottawa and *Conner Molander* and *Dylan Phillips* from British Columbia answered rehearsal advertisements in Montreal, later adding multi-instrumentalist *Isaac Symonds* to solidify their live lineup.
- Broke out internationally with their 2012 debut studio album *Dark Eyes*, championed by the restless, syncopated lead single *Full Circle*, which became a signature alternative radio hit across North America and Europe.
- Celebrated for their dynamic live concerts where members rapidly rotate between instruments, notably with drummer *Dylan Phillips* executing complex drum beats with his right hand while playing piano melodies with his left.
- Toured extensively across [[england]], Europe, and Australia as headliners and as direct support for major global acts including *Mumford & Sons*, *Of Monsters and Men*, and *City and Colour*.
- Won the *Juno Award for Adult Alternative Album of the Year* in 2020 for their third studio effort, *A Blemish in the Great Light* (2019), noted for its buoyant, textured orchestration and acoustic warmth.
- Captured another *Juno Award* in 2022 for their exploratory EP *The Covet*, showcasing their ability to self-produce atmospheric indie folk during periods of international touring lockdowns.
- Released the reflective studio album *Salt* in 2023, meticulously resurrecting and polishing unfinished song ideas and tape recordings gathered over their initial twelve years of collaboration.
- Cultivated an enduring connection to the Montreal music community, drawing aesthetic inspiration from the city's rich history of bilingual literary, post-rock, and chamber-pop traditions.

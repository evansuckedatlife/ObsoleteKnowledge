## summary

**Stellaris** is a grand strategy and 4X space simulation video game developed by *Paradox Development Studio* and released in 2016. Players guide an interstellar civilization from its first steps past faster-than-light travel through diplomacy, exploration, galactic warfare, and empire management. It is celebrated in competitive and casual gaming for its vast emergent storytelling, sweeping technological trees, and complex galactic politics.

## you gotta know

- Developed and published by *Paradox Interactive*, premiering on *Windows*, *macOS*, and *Linux* in May 2016 before later console ports.
- Adopts the classic 4X gameplay model of *eXplore, eXpand, eXploit, and eXterminate* within a dynamically generated galaxy featuring thousands of celestial bodies.
- Allows players to design custom species using ethics, civics, and traits ranging from xenophilic pacifist democracies to hive minds and fanatic purifiers.
- Features prominent mid-game and late-game crises, such as the extra-dimensional *Unbidden*, the cybernetic *Contingency*, and the ravenous *Prethoryn Scourge*.
- Introduces massive megastructures, including Dyson spheres, ring worlds, and orbital habitats that fundamentally alter planetary development.
- Incorporates an expansive *Galactic Community* mechanic that models interstellar resolutions, bureaucratic reform, sanctions, and imperial ascension.
- Features deep archaeology, anomaly investigations, and precursor empire event chains that flesh out ancient galactic history.

## summary

**Tyra Banks** is an American supermodel, television producer, talk show host, author, and entrepreneur who gained worldwide fame during the 1990s as a barrier-breaking fashion trailblazer. She established historic milestones as the first African American woman to grace the covers of the *Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue* and *GQ*, while serving as a signature *Victoria's Secret Angel*. Banks subsequently transformed herself into a television mogul by creating and executive producing the global reality franchise *America's Next Top Model* and hosting her own daytime talk show.

## you gotta know

- Began her professional modeling career in Southern California before moving to Europe and booking an extraordinary 25 runway shows during her first Paris Fashion Week in 1991.
- Broke major racial barriers in the modeling industry by becoming the first Black woman featured on the covers of the *Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue* in 1996 and 1997.
- Created, executive produced, and hosted the reality competition series *America's Next Top Model* (ANTM) starting in 2003, setting off an international format craze with dozens of foreign editions.
- Coined pervasive pop culture neologisms on television, most famously "smize" (smiling with one's eyes) and "flawsome" (embracing one's personal flaws as awesome).
- Hosted the syndicated daytime series *The Tyra Banks Show* from 2005 to 2010, earning two consecutive Daytime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Informative Talk Show.
- Delivered an iconic 2007 television retort against body-shaming tabloid photographers while wearing the photographed swimsuit on air, concluding with the fiery retort: "Kiss my fat ass!"
- Expanded her acting resume by starring as the fashion doll *Eve* alongside *Lindsay Lohan* in the 2000 television fantasy *Life-Size* and playing *Zoe* in the film *Coyote Ugly*.
- Authored the 2011 young-adult novel *Modelland*, a fantasy tale about a magical fashion academy that reached the top of *The New York Times* Best Seller list.

## summary

**Killing Eve** is a British spy thriller television series produced by *Sid Gentle Films* for *BBC America* and *BBC iPlayer*, adapted from the *Villanelle* novella series by *Luke Jennings*. Developing a gripping psychological cat-and-mouse game across Europe, the show centers on the mutual obsession between an unfulfilled British intelligence officer and an extravagant, sociopathic assassin. Praised for its black comedy, stylistic flair, and subversive feminist lens, the series won critical acclaim and major acting honors for leads *Sandra Oh* and *Jodie Comer*.

## you gotta know

- Created for television with its first season led by head writer *Phoebe Waller-Bridge*, establishing a trademark blend of sharp wit, stylish murder, and tension.
- Stars Canadian actress *Sandra Oh* as *Eve Polastri*, an uninspired MI5 officer who becomes recruited into an off-the-books MI6 task force tracking a female assassin.
- Features *Jodie Comer* as *Villanelle* (born *Oxana Vorontsova*), a multilingual, fashion-obsessed Russian psychopathic hitwoman operating on behalf of a shadowy cabal called *The Twelve*.
- Co-stars *Fiona Shaw* as the dry, enigmatic MI6 Russia desk chief *Carolyn Martens* and *Kim Bodnia* as Villanelle's mentor and handler *Konstantin Vasiliev*.
- Earned *Sandra Oh* a Golden Globe Award and *Jodie Comer* an Emmy Award and BAFTA for their electrifying dual performances.
- Explored espionage operations spanning European locales across *[[england]]*, *[[france]]*, *[[italy]]*, and Russia, echoing classic international spy tropes with modern psychological depth.

## connections

- [[england]] — the headquarters of British intelligence agencies MI5 and MI6 where *Eve Polastri* works and conducts her investigations.
- [[france]] — primary initial home base of the stylish assassin *Villanelle*, whose luxurious Parisian apartment defines her decadent lifestyle.
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic tradition informing the self-destructive spiral of obsession and ruin that consumes the two central characters.
- [[cold-war]] — the historical era of geopolitical rivalry that laid the foundation for modern European intelligence networks and fiction.
- [[soviet-union]] — the historical state backdrop connecting veteran intelligence operatives like *Konstantin* and *Carolyn* across historical espionage pacts.
- [[italy]] — the scenic European backdrop for key assassinations and pivotal confrontations between *Eve* and *Villanelle*.

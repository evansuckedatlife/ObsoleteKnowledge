## summary

**Legally Blonde** is a 2001 American legal comedy film directed by *Robert Luketic* and adapted from the novel of the same name by *Amanda Brown*. Starring *Reese Witherspoon* as the fashion-merchandising sorority president **Elle Woods**, the story follows her journey to *Harvard Law School* to win back her ex-boyfriend, only to discover her innate brilliance and vocation for trial advocacy. The film was both a critical and commercial smash hit, spawning sequels, a Broadway musical adaptation, and enduring status as a feminist touchstone in modern popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Stars *Reese Witherspoon* in her breakout titular role as sorority queen and aspiring attorney *Elle Woods*, alongside her pet Chihuahua named *Bruiser Woods*.
- Elle gains admission to *Harvard Law School* after scoring a 179 on the Law School Admission Test (LSAT) and submitting an unconventional video essay directed in a hot tub.
- Features the famous courtroom revelation centered on the chemical properties of ammonium thioglycolate in perm maintenance, proving defendant *Brooke Taylor-Windham* innocent of murder because washing perm curls within forty-eight hours ruins them.
- Popularized the flirtatious body-language routine dubbed the *bend and snap*, which Elle teaches to salon manicurist *Paulette Bonafonté*.
- Co-stars *Luke Wilson* as supportive attorney *Emmett Richmond*, *Matthew Davis* as Elle's status-obsessed ex-boyfriend *Warner Huntington III*, and *Victor Garber* as the unscrupulous *Professor Callahan*.
- Adapted into an acclaimed 2007 Broadway musical with music and lyrics by *Laurence O'Keefe* and *Nell Benjamin*, which was broadcast nationally on MTV.
- Followed by the 2003 political comedy sequel *Legally Blonde 2: Red, White & Blonde*, which relocates Elle to Washington, D.C. to pass a federal bill banning animal cosmetic testing.

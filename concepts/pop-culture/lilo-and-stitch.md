## summary

**Lilo & Stitch** is a 2002 American animated science fiction comedy-drama film written and directed by *Chris Sanders* and *Dean DeBlois* for *Walt Disney Feature Animation*. Set on the Hawaiian island of Kauaʻi, it tells the story of a lonely Native Hawaiian girl named *Lilo Pelekai* and her adoption of an escaped, genetically engineered extraterrestrial fugitive known as **Experiment 626**, whom she names **Stitch**. Celebrated for its watercolor backgrounds, authentic cultural depiction, and emotional exploration of grief and unconventional family bonds, the film remains one of the defining triumphs of Disney's post-Renaissance era.

## you gotta know

- Directed and conceived by *Chris Sanders*, who personally provided the high-pitched, mischievous guttural voice of the titular alien *Stitch*.
- Revolves centrally around the traditional Hawaiian cultural concept of *ʻohana*, famously summarized in the film's core theme: "*ʻOhana* means family, and family means nobody gets left behind or forgotten."
- Distinguished visually by its hand-painted watercolor background layouts, a delicate artistic technique Disney had not utilized in a feature animation since *Dumbo* in 1941.
- Features a prominent soundtrack saturated with the music of rock-and-roll legend *Elvis Presley*, of whom Lilo is an avid fan and impersonator.
- Includes a climactic chase sequence originally animated with a hijacked Boeing 747 navigating through downtown Honolulu skyscrapers, which was hastily redesigned after September 11 into an alien spaceship navigating a mountain canyon.
- Produced primarily at Disney's satellite animation studio located inside *Disney-MGM Studios* in Orlando, Florida.
- Spawned a massive multi-media franchise including several direct-to-video sequels, television series, and an internationally successful anime spin-off series in *Japan*.

## summary

**Professor Farnsworth**, known formally as **Hubert J. Farnsworth** or **Professor Hubert Farnsworth**, is the senile, mad scientist patriarch of the animated science-fiction sitcom *Futurama*, created by *Matt Groening* and *David X. Cohen* in the *[[united-states]]*. As the founder and proprietor of the intergalactic delivery company Planet Express, he regularly dispatches his crew on lethally dangerous courier missions across the cosmos. Masterfully voiced by *Billy West*, the character is an enduring fixture of pop-culture quiz bowls, celebrated for his apocalyptic scientific inventions, geriatric mood swings, and complete disregard for workplace safety.

## you gotta know

- Instantly recognizable by his thick coke-bottle spectacles, slippers, white lab coat, stooped posture, and ubiquitous ironic entrance catchphrase, "Good news, everyone!"
- Founder and CEO of Planet Express, an interplanetary delivery service based in 31st-century *[[new-york-city]]* (specifically New New York), which he operates solely to bankroll his erratic scientific experiments.
- Actually the distant multi-great-nephew of protagonist *Philip J. Fry*, who was cryogenically frozen in 1999 and revived a thousand years later to discover the elderly professor as his only living relative.
- Named by creator *Matt Groening* after *Philo Farnsworth*, the pioneering American inventor who successfully demonstrated the first fully electronic television transmission.
- Designed the green Planet Express delivery ship and its revolutionary propulsion system, which moves the entire universe around the vessel rather than moving the ship through space.
- Engineered countless preposterous or catastrophic inventions, including the Fing-Longer, the Smell-O-Scope, the Doomsday Device, the Parallel Universe Box, and the forward-only time machine.
- Maintains a volatile personal and corporate rivalry with *Mom* (Carol Miller), the ruthless industrialist head of MomCorp who weaponized their past romance to control planetary robotics.
- Teaches intermittently as a tenured professor at Mars University and created a 12-year-old clone named Cubert to inherit his patents and scientific legacy.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — country where creators Matt Groening and David X. Cohen developed the animated series for Fox and Comedy Central.
- [[new-york-city]] — geographic setting of the show, upon whose historical ruins the futuristic metropolis of New New York and Planet Express headquarters stand.
- [[slapstick-comedy]] — comedic genre underpinning Farnsworth's explosive laboratory mishaps, sudden bodily failures, and violent slapstick escalations.
- [[physical-comedy]] — performance tradition evident in his tottering movement, sudden bouts of public nudity, and spontaneous narcoleptic collapses.
- [[prozd]] — voice actor and popular entertainment commentator who frequently celebrates classic cartoon voice acting traditions exemplified by Billy West and satirizes animation archetypes.
- [[professor-layton]] — fellow iconic fictional professor whose polite English dignity and meticulous intellect stand in hilarious contrast to Farnsworth's chaotic dementia.

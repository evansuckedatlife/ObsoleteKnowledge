## summary

**Wham!** (frequently referenced simply as **Wham**) was an English pop duo formed in Bushey in 1981 by vocalist and songwriter *George Michael* and instrumentalist *Andrew Ridgeley*. Emerging from the British post-disco dance scene, the group achieved massive transatlantic commercial success with buoyant post-punk pop, upbeat Motown-inspired soul, and glamorous music videos. They became one of the defining acts of the 1980s music landscape before staging an amicable farewell concert at Wembley Stadium in 1986.

## you gotta know

- Composed of lead vocalist, principal songwriter, and producer *George Michael* (born *Georgios Kyriacos Panayiotou*) alongside co-writer and guitarist *Andrew Ridgeley*, who met as schoolboys at Bushey Meads School.
- Released the perennial holiday classic *Last Christmas* in December 1984, which raised funds for Ethiopian famine relief alongside *Band Aid* and eventually hit number one on the UK Singles Chart decades later.
- Reached global superstardom with the 1984 studio album *Make It Big*, which topped album charts in both [[england]] and the [[united-states]].
- Scored multiple international chart-toppers with upbeat dance tracks such as *Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go*, *Freedom*, and the energetic Motown homage *I'm Your Man*.
- Scored the iconic soft-rock ballad *Careless Whisper*, noted for its prominent descending saxophone riff played by *Steve Gregory*, credited in North America to *Wham! featuring George Michael*.
- Made international diplomatic and cultural history in April 1985 by becoming the first major Western pop act to tour the People's Republic of China during the late [[cold-war]], performing landmark concerts in Beijing and Guangzhou.
- Performed at the historic *Live Aid* charity concert at Wembley Stadium in July 1985, where *George Michael* joined *Elton John* on lead vocals for *Don't Let the Sun Go Down on Me*.
- Concluded their career at the height of popularity in June 1986 with a farewell concert titled *The Final* before 72,000 fans at Wembley Stadium, launching *George Michael*'s solo career with the subsequent album *Faith*.

## connections

- [[england]] — nation of origin where the duo met at secondary school, emerged out of Hertfordshire, and achieved their initial breakthrough on television.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical backdrop against which the duo achieved their groundbreaking 1985 diplomatic visit and performance tour across communist China.
- [[united-states]] — primary foreign pop market where the duo dominated *Billboard* pop and adult contemporary charts throughout the mid-1980s.
- [[soviet-union]] — rival socialist superpower whose youth pop culture during the late-Soviet era was deeply shaped by illicit Western pop cassettes including the duo's music.
- [[japan]] — major Asian recording market where the duo enjoyed immense commercial sales, television advertising sponsorships, and sold-out arena tours.

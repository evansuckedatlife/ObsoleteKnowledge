## summary

**Michael Bublé** is a multi-Grammy-winning Canadian singer and songwriter who spearheaded the twenty-first-century revival of traditional pop, big band swing, and the Great American Songbook. Rising from humble beginnings in British Columbia, he gained international recognition in the early 2000s through lush arrangements inspired by mid-century vocal jazz. His warm crooning delivery and festive recordings have cemented his status as a permanent seasonal staple in global pop culture.

## you gotta know

- Born in Burnaby, British Columbia, in 1975, he grew up listening to jazz records supplied by his Italian grandfather, a plumber who traded home repairs for stage gigs and vocal lessons.
- Achieved his breakout moment after singing at the wedding of former Canadian prime minister Brian Mulroney's daughter, which led directly to an introduction to Grammy-winning record producer *David Foster*.
- Scored breakout commercial success with his 2005 album *It's Time*, featuring popular signature covers alongside the original hit ballad *Home*.
- Topped international music charts with the 2007 album *Call Me Irresponsible* and the 2009 record *Crazy Love*, featuring the infectious pop single *Haven't Met You Yet*.
- Released the landmark 2011 album *Christmas*, which has sold over sixteen million copies worldwide and consistently re-enters holiday charts every December.
- Known for an easygoing, self-deprecating stage persona heavily indebted to mid-twentieth-century nightclub banter, often performing tribute arrangements of Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin, and Bobby Darin.
- Holds dual citizenship in Canada and *Italy*, frequently honoring his heritage through bilingual concerts and traditional Italian folk standards.

## summary

**Mission: Impossible** (frequently styled **Mission Impossible**) is a celebrated American multimedia espionage franchise that began as an innovative 1960s television series before evolving into one of the most successful action film franchises in history. Created by Bruce Geller, the property is famous for its intricate undercover operations, ticking-clock tension, hyper-realistic latex disguises, and Lalo Schifrin's iconic 5/4-time musical theme. Under the stewardship of producer and star Tom Cruise, the cinematic installments became an international benchmark for death-defying practical stunts and kinetic action filmmaking.

## you gotta know

- Created by television producer Bruce Geller, the original CBS series aired from 1966 to 1973, chronicling the clandestine operations of the elite Impossible Missions Force (IMF) under leaders Dan Briggs and Jim Phelps.
- Famous for its recurring framing device in which the team leader receives an undercover assignment via a disposable audio recording that concludes with the warning that it will "self-destruct in five seconds."
- Renowned for its thrilling title theme composed by Lalo Schifrin, written in an uncommon, driving 5/4 time signature that mimics the Morse code for the initials M and I.
- Launched in cinemas in 1996 with Brian De Palma's blockbuster adaptation, which established the signature heist sequence of Ethan Hunt suspended horizontally inches above a sound-and-pressure-sensitive vault floor.
- Starred Tom Cruise as relentless field agent Ethan Hunt, with Cruise famously performing his own extreme stunts, including climbing the exterior glass of Dubai's Burj Khalifa in *Ghost Protocol*.
- Relies heavily on high-tech narrative deception, particularly lifelike latex masks coupled with digital voice modulation chips, allowing IMF agents to secretly impersonate enemy operatives.
- Featured acclaimed directing turns by visionary filmmakers with contrasting visual styles, including John Woo, J. J. Abrams, Brad Bird, and Christopher McQuarrie.
- Gained widespread critical adoration with films like *Fallout* (2018) for championing tangible, real-world practical effects, halo jumps, and helicopter pursuits over computer-generated imagery.

## connections

- [[cold-war]] — global twentieth-century ideological struggle between the [[united-states]] and the [[soviet-union]] that served as the geopolitical backdrop for the original television series.
- [[united-states]] — nation whose clandestine intelligence operations the fictional Impossible Missions Force represents abroad.
- [[film-editing]] — rhythmic cutting technique indispensable to orchestrating the franchise's elaborate heist sequences and synchronized countdown climaxes.
- [[camera-movement]] — dynamic cinematographic technique used by Brian De Palma and Christopher McQuarrie to track Ethan Hunt through intricate physical choreography.
- [[mise-en-scene]] — spatial and visual arrangement of sets, lighting, and props exemplified in the pristine Langley vault infiltration and dramatic shadow-filled safe houses.
- [[france]] — European setting for spectacular practical pursuit sequences throughout Paris in *Mission: Impossible – Fallout*.
- [[silent-film]] — foundational cinematic tradition whose heritage of physical stunt performances (exemplified by [[buster-keaton]]) directly inspired Cruise's approach to action spectacles.

## summary

**Dan Levy**, born **Daniel Levy**, is an acclaimed Canadian actor, writer, producer, and director best known for co-creating and starring in the hit comedy series *Schitt's Creek*. Rising to prominence as a television personality on MTV Canada, Levy achieved international stardom by portraying the sarcastic yet deeply vulnerable character *David Rose*. In 2020, he made television history by sweeping the four major comedy categories at the Primetime Emmy Awards in a single year for his work on the show's final season.

## you gotta know

- Co-created the critically lauded CBC and Pop TV sitcom *Schitt's Creek* alongside his father, legendary comedian *Eugene Levy*, developing the premise around an ultra-wealthy family suddenly forced to relocate to a rundown rural town.
- Starred as the high-fashion, pansexual gallerist *David Rose*, whose romantic relationship with business partner *Patrick Brewer*, played by *Noah Reid*, earned widespread praise for portraying queer romance without homophobia or tragedy.
- Set a historic milestone at the 72nd Primetime Emmy Awards in 2020 by winning awards for acting, writing, directing, and producing for the sixth season of *Schitt's Creek*, leading an unprecedented nine-Emmy sweep for a comedy series.
- Began his television career in 2006 as a co-host on *MTV Live* and *The After Show* on MTV Canada, where his witty commentary on reality programs alongside *Jessie Cruickshank* developed a passionate national following.
- Co-hosted the culinary competition *The Great Canadian Baking Show* for its first two seasons alongside *Julia Chan*, showcasing his warm on-screen persona and signature comedic timing.
- Made his feature film directorial debut with the 2024 Netflix romantic comedy-drama *Good Grief*, which he also wrote and starred in as an artist navigating the sudden death of his husband in Paris.
- Authored the best-selling retrospective book *Best Wishes, Warmest Regards: The Story of Schitt's Creek* alongside his sister and co-star *Sarah Levy*, celebrating the show's cultural impact and devoted fanbase.

## summary

**Funeral** is the landmark debut studio album by Canadian indie rock band *Arcade Fire*, released in September 2004 on Merge Records. Produced amid the sudden passings of several band members' close relatives, the record channels profound grief, youth, familial memory, and community catharsis through lush baroque-pop and soaring art-rock orchestration. Universally lauded by music critics, it became a defining touchstone of 2000s alternative rock and catapulted the Montreal collective to global prominence.

## you gotta know

- Titled *Funeral* because several members of the band had suffered the bereavement of close relatives during its recording sessions in Montreal.
- Features iconic multi-part thematic suites, most famously the four distinct installments titled *Neighborhood* (*#1 (Tunnels)*, *#2 (Laïka)*, *#3 (Power Out)*, and *#4 (7 Kettles)*).
- Propelled by the soaring, communal anthem *Wake Up*, known for its wordless choral refrains, which became a staple of live arena shows and major festival performances.
- Led the mid-2000s boom of baroque pop and orchestral indie rock, incorporating non-traditional rock instrumentation including accordions, violins, violas, cellos, and xylophones.
- Earned widespread critical acclaim and a Grammy Award nomination for Best Alternative Music Album, helping establish independent music label *Merge Records* as a commercial powerhouse.
- Frequently ranked alongside iconic works of Canadian music such as [[fully-completely]] by *The Tragically Hip* and Polaris-winning releases by [[fucked-up]].

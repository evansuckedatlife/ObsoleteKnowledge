## summary

**Ozark** is an American neo-noir crime drama television series created by *Bill Dubuque* and *Mark Williams* that streamed on *Netflix* for four seasons from 2017 to 2022. The series stars *Jason Bateman* as *Marty Byrde*, a mild-mannered financial planner who uproots his family from Chicago to the Lake of the Ozarks in Missouri after an illicit money-laundering operation for a Mexican drug cartel turns lethal. Celebrated for its unrelenting suspense, signature cold-blue visual palette, and riveting performances by Bateman, *Laura Linney*, and *Julia Garner*, the show stands as a defining work of modern prestige television exploring moral corruption and familial survival in rural America.

## you gotta know

- Centers on financial advisor *Marty Byrde*, who must launder hundreds of millions of dollars through small lakefront cash businesses to satisfy the ruthless *Navarro cartel* after his corrupt business partner is summarily executed.
- Co-stars *Laura Linney* as *Wendy Byrde*, Marty's politically astute wife whose arc transforms her from a reluctant accomplice into a cunning, cutthroat political strategist willing to sacrifice her own relatives to protect their growing empire.
- Propelled actress *Julia Garner* to stardom for her portrayal of *Ruth Langmore*, a fiercely loyal, foul-mouthed local trailer park native whose tragic trajectory as Marty's protégée earned her three Primetime Emmy Awards.
- Features iconic regional antagonists including Missouri heroin producers *Jacob and Darlene Snell*, unhinged cartel boss *Omar Navarro*, zealous FBI investigator *Roy Petty*, and chilling cartel attorney *Helen Pierce*.
- Directed frequently by series lead *Jason Bateman*, who helmed the pilot and several critical chapter finales, capturing the 2019 Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Directing for a Drama Series for the episode "Reparations".
- Employs a renowned visual aesthetic distinguished by heavily desaturated blue-green color grading, claustrophobic framing, and fluid camera movements that contrast stark natural beauty with industrial criminal pragmatism.
- Concluded in 2022 with a critically discussed two-part final season consisting of fourteen episodes, culminating in an unsparing ending that reflects the corrupting power of dynastic American wealth.

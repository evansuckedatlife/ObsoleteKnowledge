## summary

**Grime** is an electronic music and hip-hop genre that developed in East London in the early 2000s. Characterized by rapid 140-beat-per-minute tempos, syncopated breakbeats, and aggressive MC delivery, the style grew out of pirate radio culture and UK garage. It remains one of modern Britain's most distinctive musical movements, launching global acts such as *Dizzee Rascal*, *Skepta*, and *Stormzy*.

## you gotta know

- Emerged from Bow in East London around 2001, pioneered by pirate radio stations like *Rinse FM* and crew collectives like *Roll Deep*.
- Typically adheres to a fast, rigid tempo of around 140 beats per minute with syncopated sub-bass lines, drawing heavily on UK garage, dancehall, and jungle.
- *Dizzee Rascal* propelled the genre into mainstream critical acclaim with his 2003 debut album *Boy in da Corner*, which won the prestigious *Mercury Prize*.
- Producer and MC *Wiley*, often nicknamed the "Godfather of Grime", established its signature sonic identity through his early "eski beat" instrumentals.
- The movement saw an international resurgence in the 2010s driven by *Skepta*'s *Konnichiwa* and *Stormzy*'s *Gang Signs & Prayer*, the latter becoming the first grime album to reach number one on the UK Albums Chart.
- In 2017, the political grassroots movement *Grime4Corbyn* mobilized young urban voters across the country during the United Kingdom general election.

## summary

**Desi Arnaz** was a Cuban-American musician, actor, comedian, and pioneering television producer who fundamentally reshaped the American entertainment industry. As the co-creator and co-star of the groundbreaking sitcom *I Love Lucy*, he portrayed the fictional bandleader *Ricky Ricardo* opposite his real-life wife *Lucille Ball*. Behind the camera as the head of *Desilu Productions*, Arnaz pioneered the multi-camera setup filmed on 35mm film in front of a live studio audience, creating the technical template for network television production and syndication.

## you gotta know

- Co-created and starred in *I Love Lucy* (1951–1957), popularizing the character of *Ricky Ricardo* alongside his iconic musical rendition of *Babalu* and his frustrated catchphrases.
- Engineered the revolutionary three-camera setup with cinematographer *Karl Freund*, using overhead studio lighting and 35mm motion picture film to record sitcoms live with high visual fidelity.
- Founded *Desilu Productions* with *Lucille Ball*, which grew into an independent television empire that produced hits like *The Untouchables* and later provided studio production for *Star Trek* and *Mission: Impossible*.
- Insisted on shooting on high-quality film rather than lower-resolution kinescope, shrewdly securing full ownership rights of the finished episodes and inadvertently inventing television reruns and lucrative syndication.
- Popularized the Latin big band and conga line dance craze in the United States during the late 1930s and 1940s as the frontman of the *Desi Arnaz Orchestra*.
- Fled to the United States from Santiago de Cuba with his family after the 1933 Cuban Revolution stripped his wealthy political family of their property and influence.
- Broke unprecedented cultural barriers on American television by portraying a loving, successful intercultural marriage and insisted on writing Ball's real-life pregnancy into the show in 1952.

## summary

**Undertale** is an acclaimed 2015 indie role-playing video game developed almost entirely by American creator and composer *Toby Fox*. The narrative charts the journey of a human child who tumbles into the Underground, an expansive realm sealed beneath the earth populated by exiled monsters. Celebrated for subverting conventional role-playing tropes, the game features a witty narrative and an unforgettable chiptune score, allowing players to resolve every encounter peacefully without killing anyone.

## you gotta know

- Developed independently by *Toby Fox* using the *GameMaker: Studio* engine after securing funding through a modest 2013 Kickstarter campaign that wildly exceeded its financial target.
- Employs an inventive combat mechanic that synthesizes turn-based dialogue menus with real-time bullet-hell dodging, allowing players to talk, flatter, or spare monsters instead of attacking.
- Implements three drastically distinct narrative routes based on player conduct: the compassionate True Pacifist route, the flexible Neutral route, and the grimly devastating Genocide route.
- Deconstructs classic genre conventions by revealing that typical combat acronyms like EXP and LV represent "Execution Points" and "Level of Violence" rather than heroic milestones.
- Populated by memorable supporting characters such as the comedic skeleton duo *Sans* and *Papyrus*, the protective goat-mother *Toriel*, the armored guard *Undyne*, and the mechanical host *Mettaton*.
- Features the energetic boss battle composition "Megalovania," a track originally arranged by *Fox* for an *EarthBound* fan modification that evolved into a ubiquitous global internet meme.
- Drew substantial stylistic and thematic inspiration from Japanese game series, most notably *Shigesato Itoi's* eccentric masterpiece *EarthBound* (*Mother 2*), *Touhou Project*, and *Shin Megami Tensei*.
- Presented symbolically to *Pope Francis* during a 2016 Vatican summit by internet creator *MatPat* as an exemplar of empathy and moral decision-making in digital art.

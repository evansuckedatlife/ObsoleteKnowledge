## summary

**Bitten** is a 2001 urban fantasy novel written by Canadian author *Kelley Armstrong*, serving as the inaugural entry in her long-running *Women of the Otherworld* series. The narrative chronicles the internal conflict and supernatural tribulations of protagonist *Elena Michaels*, the world's only known surviving female werewolf, as she struggles between human domesticity and pack obligations. Adapted into an international television series that aired from 2014 to 2016, the work helped define twenty-first-century paranormal fiction by blending procedural mystery with gritty supernatural pack politics.

## you gotta know

- Introduces protagonist *Elena Michaels*, an orphan bitten and turned into a werewolf against her will by her deceptive former lover, *Clayton Danvers*.
- Establishes *Stonehaven*, a secluded ancestral country estate outside *Bear Valley*, New York, which serves as the fortified rural sanctuary for the ruling North American werewolf pack.
- Centers on Elena's desperate attempts to assimilate into human society as a professional photographer in Toronto while concealing her lycanthropy from her human boyfriend, *Philip Sorrentino*.
- Follows a deadly crisis when renegade werewolves, known as mutts, orchestrate a coordinated campaign of murders that threatens to expose the secretive supernatural order to humanity.
- Subverts traditional monster lore by establishing that the physiological trauma of the werewolf bite and subsequent shapeshifting had historically proven fatal to all female victims except Elena.
- Explores pack leadership through the philosophical, aristocratic alpha *Jeremy Danvers*, who emphasizes strict discipline, territorial isolation, and harsh retribution against rogue werewolves.
- Adapted into a three-season Canadian television drama developed by *Daegan Fryklind*, starring *Laura Vandervoort* as Elena, which was broadcast globally on *Space* and *Syfy*.
- Shares its title phonetically with the sensational 2018 entertainment media frenzy involving an altercation where an unnamed actress bit pop singer [[beyonce]] at a private reception.

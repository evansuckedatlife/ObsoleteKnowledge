## summary

**Speed** is a 1994 American action thriller film directed by *Jan de Bont* in his feature directorial debut, starring *Keanu Reeves*, *Sandra Bullock*, and *Dennis Hopper*. The plot centers on Los Angeles SWAT officer *Jack Traven*, who must board a public transit bus armed with a hidden bomb configured to detonate if the vehicle drops below 50 miles per hour. Celebrated for its pure high-concept premise, breathtaking practical stunt work, and relentless narrative momentum, the blockbuster captured two Academy Awards and cemented Sandra Bullock's status as a top Hollywood star.

## you gotta know

- Directed by Dutch cinematographer *Jan de Bont*, who brought the visual kineticism of his camera work on *Die Hard* and *The Hunt for Red October* to his directorial debut.
- Centered on an indelible high-concept cinematic premise: a commuter bus rigged to arm once it accelerates to 50 miles per hour and blow up if it drops below that speed.
- Propelled *Sandra Bullock* to international fame as *Annie Porter*, a plucky passenger who must take the steering wheel after the regular bus driver is inadvertently shot.
- Features *Dennis Hopper* delivering a campy, menace-filled villain turn as extortionist *Howard Payne*, a retired Atlanta police officer seeking a multimillion-dollar pension payout.
- Deploys an escalating three-act structure centered on trapped conveyances, progressing from an office elevator shaft to city bus 2525 and concluding in an uncompleted subway tunnel.
- Won Academy Awards for Best Sound and Best Sound Effects Editing at the 67th ceremonies, prevailing against prestigious big-budget competitors.
- Benefited from an uncredited screenplay overhaul by *Joss Whedon*, who penned almost all of the sharp banter, character quirks, and quotable one-liners.

## summary

**Legendborn** is a bestselling young adult contemporary fantasy novel written by American author *Tracy Deonn*, published in 2020 in the [[united-states]]. The narrative deconstructs and reimagines the classic British Arthurian cycle within the modern American South, following sixteen-year-old *Bree Matthews* as she uncovers a secretive collegiate order of demon-hunting knights while grieving her mother's sudden death. Celebrated for its incisive handling of generational trauma, institutional racism, and historical memory, the novel achieved widespread critical acclaim and won the *Coretta Scott King/John Steptoe Award for New Talent*.

## you gotta know

- Follows sixteen-year-old protagonist *Bree Matthews*, who enrolls in a selective residential high school program hosted at the *University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill* following the unexpected death of her mother, *Faith*.
- Introduces the *Order of the Round Table*, an ancient collegiate secret society whose members, the titular *Legendborn*, inherit the ancestral spirits and martial powers of King Arthur and his original twelve knights through magical bloodlines.
- Contrasts the insular, lineage-obsessed European sorcery of the Order with *Rootcraft*, a deeply spiritual African American ancestral magic practiced through communion with the earth, memory, and the spirits of enslaved forebears.
- Features a tense dynamic and emerging romantic triangle involving *Nick Davis*, the noble and compassionate golden-boy Scion of King Arthur, and *Selwyn Kane*, a cynical, demon-tainted *Kingsmage* bound by oath to defend the bloodline.
- Reinterprets demonic entities called *Shadowborn*, who feed upon human emotion and breach reality during catastrophic events, requiring the Order to wage an invisible war while systematically wiping the memories of civilian witnesses.
- Delivers a major subversion of traditional Arthurian lore when *Bree* discovers that she is not merely an outside medium possessing Root magic, but the true direct descendant and reincarnated vessel of King Arthur himself.
- Earned prestigious literary honors including the 2021 *Coretta Scott King/John Steptoe Award for New Talent* and a nomination for the *Hugo Award* for Best Young Adult Novel (the *Lodestar Award*), launching a continuing series known as the *Legendborn Cycle*.

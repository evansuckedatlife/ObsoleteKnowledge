## summary

**Catherine O'Hara** (born **Catherine Anne O'Hara**) is an acclaimed Canadian-American actress, comedian, and writer widely celebrated for her virtuoso character acting, eccentric vocal delivery, and mastery of improvisational performance. After launching her career with Toronto's The Second City and the seminal sketch comedy program *SCTV*, she became a beloved presence in international cinema through films such as *Beetlejuice* and *Home Alone*. Her career reached an extraordinary cultural zenith with her Emmy-winning portrayal of Moira Rose on the sitcom *Schitt's Creek*, cementing her reputation as one of the most inventive comedic actors of her generation.

## you gotta know

- Won widespread acclaim and a Primetime Emmy Award for starring as the flamboyant, fallen socialite Moira Rose in the comedy series *Schitt's Creek*, famous for her theatrical vocabulary, rotating collection of wigs, and idiosyncratic mid-Atlantic cadence.
- Portrayed Kate McCallister, the frantic and desperately determined mother of Macaulay Culkin's character, in the blockbuster holiday classics *Home Alone* (1990) and *Home Alone 2: Lost in New York* (1992).
- Enjoyed a long and fruitful creative partnership with director Tim Burton, starring as the pretentious avant-garde sculptor Delia Deetz in *Beetlejuice* (1988) and its sequel, while also voicing the stitched rag doll Sally in *The Nightmare Before Christmas* (1993).
- Began her professional comedic career in 1974 with the Toronto branch of The Second City theatrical troupe, serving as an understudy for Gilda Radner before joining the main stage cast.
- Served as an original cast member and primary writer for the groundbreaking sketch comedy series *Second City Television* (*SCTV*), originating memorable satirical personas such as the sultry entertainer Lola Heatherton.
- Established an enduring, decades-long collaborative partnership with fellow Canadian comedian Eugene Levy, co-starring with him on *SCTV*, across multiple Christopher Guest films, and throughout *Schitt's Creek*.
- Showcased her brilliant gift for unscripted dialogue in Christopher Guest's ensemble mockumentary films, playing the washed-up dog owner Cookie Fleck in *Best in Show* and the neurotic folk singer Mickey Crabbe in *A Mighty Wind*.
- Developed a signature brand of expressive physical comedy, ranging from her hilarious running gag of mismatched gait in *Best in Show* to the possessed calypso dinner dance to Harry Belafonte's music in *Beetlejuice*.

## summary

**Aloy** is the fictional protagonist of the post-apocalyptic action role-playing video game series *Horizon*, developed by Dutch studio *Guerrilla Games* and published by *Sony Interactive Entertainment*. Introduced in *Horizon Zero Dawn* (2017) and returning in *Horizon Forbidden West* (2022), she navigates a lush thirty-first-century Earth dominated by colossal animalistic machines. Raised as a pariah by the matriarchal Nora tribe, she uncovers the technological downfall of past civilization to save humanity from catastrophic ecological extinction.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Guerrilla Games* and brought to life through the voice acting and motion capture performance of *Ashly Burch* alongside the visual likeness of Dutch actress *Hannah Hoekstra*.
- Raised as a shunned outcast by her surrogate father *Rost* within the Sacred Lands of the isolationist, matriarchal Nora tribe due to her mysterious birth with no known mother.
- Discovers as a young child an augmented-reality scanning device known as a *Focus* inside subterranean ancient ruins, allowing her to interact with forgotten technologies and scan the weaknesses of predatory machines.
- Competes in the Nora coming-of-age ritual called the *Proving* to overcome her exile, only to survive a deadly ambush by the *Eclipse* cult that sparks her broader global journey.
- Learns through exploration of subterranean military facilities that she is a genetic clone of twenty-first-century roboticist *Dr. Elisabet Sobeck*, engineered by the terraforming artificial intelligence *GAIA*.
- Employs precision archery, tripwires, ropecasters, and spear strikes to disable machine components and override their internal programming to convert hostile beasts into mounts or allies.
- Crossed over into other major video game franchises as a playable guest character, appearing in *Genshin Impact* as an exclusive Cryo-element hunter and in *Monster Hunter: World*.

## connections

- [[skyrim]] — open-world role-playing predecessor whose expansive wilderness design, archery mechanics, and lore exploration heavily inspired *Horizon*.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — foundational game genre mastered by developer *Guerrilla Games* through their *Killzone* series prior to designing *Horizon*.
- [[esports]] — modern competitive gaming sphere adjacent to the high-level speedrunning and combat trial communities centered on action role-playing games.
- [[japan]] — country housing the corporate headquarters of publisher *Sony Interactive Entertainment*, which positioned her as a flagship gaming mascot.
- [[united-states]] — post-collapse geographic setting of her adventures, spanning real-world landmarks across Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, and California.
- [[existentialism]] — philosophical perspective echoed in her struggle to forge personal identity and responsibility in defiance of deterministic cloned origins.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — artistic design principle embodied in the contrast between primitive tribal textiles and mechanical animal anatomy.

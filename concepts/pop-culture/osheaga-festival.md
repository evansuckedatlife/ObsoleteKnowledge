## summary

The **Osheaga Music and Arts Festival**, widely known as **Osheaga** or the **Osheaga Festival**, is an annual multi-day indie music and visual arts festival held at *Parc Jean-Drapeau* on *Île Sainte-Hélène* in *Montreal*, *Quebec*. Founded in 2006 by promoter *Nick Farkas* under *Evenko*, the event has expanded from a modest gathering of twenty-five thousand attendees into Canada's premier destination music festival, drawing over one hundred and thirty thousand spectators across a summer weekend. Showcasing an eclectic blend of international headliners, emergent indie rock bands, hip-hop icons, and electronic artists alongside site-specific visual installations, the festival plays a vital role in showcasing Montreal as an international cultural capital with deep ties to both North American and Francophone musical traditions.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 2006 by *Nick Farkas* of *Evenko*, who sought to establish a world-class European-style outdoor festival in Canada inspired by gatherings such as *Glastonbury* and *Coachella*.
- Takes place on the scenic island of *Île Sainte-Hélène* at *Parc Jean-Drapeau*, an iconic venue built for the historic *Expo 67* worlds fair in *Montreal*.
- Named after a historical *Mohawk* or *Iroquoian* settlement name, *Osheaga*, which regional folklore also links to oral traditions describing European explorer *Jacques Cartier* meeting Indigenous peoples at the *Lachine Rapids*.
- Features multiple outdoor stages named after the city and historic motifs, including the *River Stage* (*Scène de la Rivière*), *Mountain Stage* (*Scène de la Montagne*), and *Green Stage* (*Scène Verte*), designed to allow alternating continuous performances without overlapping sound bleed.
- Prominently showcases visual arts alongside sound, commissioning large-scale sculptures, interactive murals, and digital exhibits from local and international contemporary artists throughout the festival park grounds.
- Has hosted legendary headliners spanning indie rock and global pop including *Radiohead*, *Eminem*, *Kendrick Lamar*, *The Cure*, *Billie Eilish*, and *Arcade Fire*, whose hometown connection highlights the vibrant indie rock boom of Quebec.
- Successfully adapted during major site renovations between 2017 and 2018 by temporarily relocating to adjacent *Île Notre-Dame* before returning to an expanded, purpose-built amphitheatre space.

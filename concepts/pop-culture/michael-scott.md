## summary

**Michael Scott** is a fictional character and the central protagonist of the NBC mockumentary comedy series *The Office*, portrayed by actor *Steve Carell*. Serving as the Regional Manager of the Scranton, Pennsylvania branch of the fictional paper supply company *Dunder Mifflin*, he embodies the archetype of the well-meaning yet hopelessly oblivious and desperate-to-be-liked middle manager. Carell's layered, empathetic performance turned cringe comedy into a mainstream television triumph, earning widespread critical acclaim and cementing the character as one of modern American comedy's definitive figures.

## you gotta know

- Adapted from the British character *David Brent*, created and played by *Ricky Gervais* in the original BBC series of *The Office*, before being retooled into a warmer, more endearing American boss.
- Serves as the Regional Manager of *Dunder Mifflin* Scranton, frequently derailing workplace productivity with inappropriate staff seminars, improvisational theatrics, and childish pranks.
- Hosts the annual "Dundies" awards banquet at the local Chili's restaurant, handing out cringeworthy, customized novelty trophies to keep office morale artificially elevated.
- Known for his signature catchphrase "That's what she said!", which he impulsively shouts at any turn of phrase containing double entendres or accidental innuendo.
- Wrote, directed, and starred in the legendary amateur action thriller movie *Threat Level Midnight*, playing heroic secret agent *Michael Scarn* alongside his bewildered employees.
- Founded the short-lived, rival paper venture *The Michael Scott Paper Company* in Season 5 alongside colleagues *Pam Beesly* and *Ryan Howard*, successfully forcing a lucrative corporate buyout.
- Promised a third-grade class college tuition in the infamously uncomfortable episode "Scott's Tots", returning a decade later only able to distribute replacement laptop lithium batteries.
- Departed the television series in Season 7 after becoming engaged to fellow eccentric HR representative *Holly Flax*, moving to Colorado to build a quiet life and raise a family.

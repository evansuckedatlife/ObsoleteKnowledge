## summary

**Nintendo** is a Japanese entertainment company founded in 1889 that evolved into one of the world's most influential video game publishers. The company rescued the video game industry from collapse in 1983 and has since defined entire genres and franchises—from *Super Mario* to *The Legend of Zelda* to *Pokémon*. Nintendo's commitment to innovation, game design excellence, and accessible gameplay has made it synonymous with video gaming itself.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1889 as a playing card manufacturer; transitioned to video games in the 1970s when it acquired arcade games and later created its own hits like *Donkey Kong* (1981).
- Saved the video game industry after the 1983 North American market crash by releasing the *Nintendo Entertainment System* (NES) in 1985 with *Super Mario Bros.*, a game that redefined what console gaming could be.
- Created iconic franchises that span decades: *The Legend of Zelda* (1986), *Metroid* (1986), *Pokémon* (1996), each defining entire subgenres of gaming.
- Pioneered the D-pad controller and the cross-shaped directional pad, which became the standard for game controllers and influenced every major gaming platform thereafter.
- Known for rigorous game design philosophy emphasizing fun and accessibility over raw technical power; many Nintendo games are considered masterpieces of ludic design.
- Maintained profitability and cultural relevance across multiple console generations (NES, SNES, Game Boy, GameCube, Wii, Switch) while competitors have come and gone.
- The company's internal R&D team, led by legendary designers like Shigeru Miyamoto, has become a model for creative vision in an industry often driven by graphics and horsepower.

## connections

- [[pokemon]] — the monster-catching franchise that became a multimedia empire and one of Nintendo's most successful exports, extending Nintendo's influence far beyond gaming into collectibles, anime, and merchandise.
- [[super-mario]] — the plumber who saved the video game industry and became Nintendo's iconic mascot, representing the company's philosophy of character-driven, accessible game design.
- [[the-legend-of-zelda]] — the adventure-game franchise that pioneered non-linear exploration and puzzle-based world design, influencing countless games across all platforms.
- [[japan]] — Nintendo's home country; the company embodies Japanese game design philosophy, precision manufacturing, and minimalist aesthetic principles that contrast with Western maximalism.
- [[arcade-gaming]] — Nintendo's early roots in arcade cabinets before moving to home consoles; the company's arcade legacy remains foundational to its identity.
- [[video-game-industry]] — as a dominant force, Nintendo shaped the entire ecosystem of gaming, from controller design to publishing models to how the industry values game preservation.

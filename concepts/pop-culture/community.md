## summary

**Community** is an American television sitcom created by *Dan Harmon* that originally aired on NBC and Yahoo! Screen from 2009 to 2015. Set at the fictional Greendale Community College in Colorado, the series follows an eclectic study group of disparate misfits who form an unlikely bond while navigating academic absurdity. Celebrated for its intense meta-humor, genre deconstructions, and cinematic parodies, the show developed an enduring cult following and became a landmark of twenty-first-century meta-television.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Dan Harmon*, who based the premise on his personal experience attempting to reconcile with his girlfriend by enrolling at Glendale Community College in California.
- Centers on disbarred lawyer *Jeff Winger* (*Joel McHale*), who forms a Spanish study group as a pretext to romance classmate *Britta Perry* (*Gillian Jacobs*), inadvertently assembling seven eccentric students.
- Renowned for its ambitious concept and tribute episodes, including high-stakes campus paintball championships, blanket fort civil wars, stop-motion Christmas specials, and multi-timeline roll-of-the-die narratives.
- The ensemble cast features *Danny Pudi* as *Abed Nadir*, a pop-culture-obsessed film student whose meta-commentary frames the reality of the show, alongside *Donald Glover*, *Alison Brie*, *Yvette Nicole Brown*, *Gillian Jacobs*, *Jim Rash*, *Ken Jeong*, and *Chevy Chase*.
- Fostered the viral rallying cry *"six seasons and a movie"*, which originated in season two as Abed's plea for *The Cape* before becoming the fanbase's organizing slogan for the show's own survival.
- Suffered extensive network battles, low Nielsen ratings, and the firing of showrunner *Dan Harmon* for season four (commonly dubbed the "gas leak year") before his reinstatement for season five.
- Served as a breakout directorial proving ground for *Anthony Russo* and *Joe Russo*, who directed numerous foundational episodes including the first paintball epic, *"Modern Warfare"*.

## summary

**The Big Bang Theory** is an American television sitcom created by *Chuck Lorre* and *Bill Prady*, which aired on CBS from September 2007 to May 2019 across twelve seasons. Set in Pasadena, California, the series chronicles the lives of physicists *Sheldon Cooper* and *Leonard Hofstadter*, their aerospace engineer and astrophysicist colleagues, and their aspiring actress neighbor *Penny*. The program became a dominant ratings juggernaut in the [[united-states]], bringing geek culture, academic satire, and conventional multicamera sitcom formats to unprecedented global mainstream popularity.

## you gotta know

- Centered on Caltech physicists *Leonard Hofstadter* (*Johnny Galecki*) and *Sheldon Cooper* (*Jim Parsons*), living across the hall from waitress and actress *Penny* (*Kaley Cuoco*).
- Popularized the character *Sheldon Cooper*, whose rigid routines, lack of social intuition, and catchphrase *"Bazinga!"* became ubiquitous cultural touchstones.
- Featured core supporting characters *Howard Wolowitz*, an MIT-trained aerospace engineer, and *Rajesh Koothrappali*, an astrophysicist suffering from selective mutism around women.
- Expanded its ensemble in later seasons to include neurobiologist *Amy Farrah Fowler* (*Mayim Bialik*) and microbiologist *Bernadette Rostenkowski* (*Melissa Rauch*).
- Employed actual scientists and consultants, notably UCLA professor of physics *David Saltzberg*, to ensure complex chalkboard equations and scientific dialogue remained genuine.
- Garnered four Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series for *Jim Parsons*'s breakout performance.
- Concluded in May 2019 after 279 episodes, surpassing *Cheers* to become the longest-running multi-camera comedy series in American television history.
- Spawned a lucrative television franchise, including the successful prequel spinoff *Young Sheldon*.

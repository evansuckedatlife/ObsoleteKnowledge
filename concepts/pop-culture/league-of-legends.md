## summary

**League of Legends** (commonly abbreviated as **LoL**) is a multiplayer online battle arena (*MOBA*) video game developed and published by *Riot Games*, officially released in 2009 in the [[united-states]]. Inspired directly by the community-developed *Warcraft III* modification *Defense of the Ancients*, the title pits two opposing squads of five powerful avatars, known as champions, against each other across a symmetrical map titled *Summoner's Rift*. The game emerged as a cornerstone of the modern gaming landscape, fundamentally establishing the free-to-play live-service business model and driving the global explosion of organized [[esports]].

## you gotta know

- Developed under the leadership of *Brandon Beck* and *Marc Merrill*, recruiting veteran *Defense of the Ancients* creators *Steve Feak* (*Guinsoo*) and *Steve Mescon* (*Pendragon*) to craft a standalone commercial iteration of the genre.
- Matches take place primarily on *Summoner's Rift*, where two teams of five players maneuver across three lanes—top, mid, and bottom—defended by defensive turrets and waves of computer-controlled *minions*, aiming to destroy the rival base structure known as the *Nexus*.
- Features a roster exceeding 160 distinct champions categorized into specific tactical archetypes such as assassins, marksmen, mages, bruisers, and supports, each possessing unique passive talents and active abilities.
- Anchors the annual *League of Legends World Championship* (widely known as *Worlds*), one of the most-watched competitive sporting spectacles on earth, where international regional champions compete to lift the celebrated *Summoner's Cup*.
- South Korean mid-laner *Lee Sang-hyeok*, celebrated universally under the handle *Faker*, became the defining icon of professional play, winning multiple world championships with *T1* (formerly *SK Telecom T1*) and earning the moniker the *Unkillable Demon King*.
- Transcended traditional gaming through major multimedia expansions, notably the critically acclaimed animated television series *Arcane*, which chronicles the tragic estrangement of sisters *Vi* and *Jinx* amid social upheaval between the cities of *Piltover* and *Zaun*.
- Cultivated massive pop-culture crossovers through in-game musical collectives, including the virtual K-pop girl group *K/DA* and the virtual hip-hop crew *True Damage*, performing original tracks voiced by mainstream international artists.

- [[first-person-shooter]] — contrasting dominant genre of team-based competitive play that evolved parallel spectator ecosystems.
- [[minecraft]] — contemporary late-2000s PC release that revolutionized internet video streaming and gaming creator communities.
- [[skyrim]] — fantasy gaming landmark from the same era that helped drive the 2010s mainstream popularization of high-fantasy aesthetics.
- [[united-states]] — country where developer *Riot Games* was founded in Los Angeles, California.

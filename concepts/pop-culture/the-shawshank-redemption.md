## summary

**The Shawshank Redemption** is a 1994 American drama film written and directed by *Frank Darabont*, adapted from the 1982 novella *Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption* by *Stephen King*. Starring *Tim Robbins* as the wrongfully convicted banker *Andy Dufresne* and *Morgan Freeman* as his contraband-smuggling confidant *Ellis Boyd "Red" Redding*, the narrative chronicles decades of perseverance, spiritual resilience, and quiet brotherhood inside a brutal Maine penitentiary. Despite modest initial theatrical box-office returns, the feature earned seven Academy Award nominations and gained legendary status through cable television broadcasts and home video, becoming one of the most beloved cinematic works in American cultural history.

## you gotta know

- Centers on *Andy Dufresne*, a quiet Portland banker sentenced in 1947 to two consecutive life terms at Shawshank State Penitentiary for the murders of his wife and her lover, crimes he steadfastly maintains he did not commit.
- Narrated from the intimate perspective of *Red*, an inmate serving life who operates as the prison's resourceful smuggler, providing Andy with a rock hammer and a poster of *Rita Hayworth*.
- Features Andy utilizing his financial expertise to provide tax advice to corrupt guards and Warden *Samuel Norton*, securing protection while laundering kickbacks from prison labor programs under the alias *Randall Stephens*.
- Climax reveals Andy's escape after chipping through a concrete cell wall over nineteen patient years, crawling through five hundred yards of foul sewage pipes before emerging into a rainstorm to wash away his captivity.
- Depicts the tragic parole experience of elderly prison librarian *Brooks Hatlen*, whose inability to adjust to modern life outside walls leads him to carve "Brooks was here" into a rooming-house beam before committing suicide.
- Highlights a pivotal moment of defiance when Andy broadcasts the duet "Canzonetta sull'aria" from *Mozart's* opera *The Marriage of Figaro* over the prison loudspeakers, offering the yard a fleeting glimpse of transcendent beauty.
- Concludes with Red violating his parole following Andy's hidden instructions to travel to Buxton, Maine, locating buried cash and a letter beneath an obsidian stone, leading to their reunion in *Zihuatanejo*, Mexico.

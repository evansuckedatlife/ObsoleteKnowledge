## summary

**Bella Swan**, born **Isabella Marie Swan** and later known as **Bella Cullen**, is the primary protagonist and narrator of *Stephenie Meyer's* bestselling *Twilight* fantasy romance series. Relocating from sunny Phoenix to the overcast logging town of Forks, Washington, the clumsy human teenager falls into an all-consuming romance with the centuries-old vampire *Edward Cullen*. Her narrative trajectory across four blockbuster novels and their cinematic adaptations sparked a transformative commercial wave in twenty-first-century young-adult publishing and global fan culture.

## you gotta know

- Moves from Arizona to live with her father, police chief *Charlie Swan*, in the perpetually rainy setting of *Forks, Washington*, where she enrolls as an unassuming junior at the local high school.
- Possesses a distinctively quiet, introspective temperament and notorious physical clumsiness, but exhibits a rare innate mental shield that prevents telepathic vampires like *Edward Cullen* and *Aro* from reading her thoughts.
- Becomes entangled in an iconic literary love triangle between the glittering, brooding vampire *Edward Cullen* and her childhood friend *Jacob Black*, a Native Quileute youth who transforms into a giant shapeshifting wolf.
- Sunk into debilitating clinical depression during the second book, *New Moon*, after Edward abruptly abandons her to keep her safe from predatory vampires, turning to adrenaline-seeking behavior to evoke hallucinations of his voice.
- Marries Edward in the final novel, *Breaking Dawn*, on the explicit condition that she will subsequently be transformed into an immortal vampire to remain with him forever.
- Survives a catastrophic pregnancy with a rapidly growing human-vampire hybrid daughter named *Renesmee*, undergoing venomous transformation in childbirth to save her life.
- Awakens as an exceptionally powerful newborn vampire whose natural mental shield expands into a defensive force field capable of protecting her entire coven from psychic execution by the ruling *Volturi*.
- Portrayed by actress *Kristen Stewart* opposite *Robert Pattinson* across five lucrative film adaptations between two thousand eight and two thousand twelve.

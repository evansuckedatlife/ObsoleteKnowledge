## summary

**Marvin Gardens** is a notable property space on the standard American *Monopoly* board game, situated in the yellow color group between the Water Works utility and the Go to Jail corner space. It is famous in popular culture as the only property on the classic Atlantic City-themed board that was misspelled, deriving its real-world origin from *Marven Gardens*, an upscale planned community located in Margate City, New Jersey. As the most expensive and lucrative yellow property, it plays a vital strategic role in mid-to-late tabletop gameplay.

## you gotta know

- Forms the yellow property set alongside Atlantic Avenue and Ventnor Avenue on the classic American *Monopoly* board.
- Derived from *Marven Gardens*, a residential neighborhood in Margate City, New Jersey, combining the names of nearby *Margate* and *Ventnor*.
- Misspelled with an "in" instead of "en" when *Charles Darrow* copied the board layout during the Great Depression before patenting the game with Parker Brothers.
- Parker Brothers formally acknowledged the historic misspelling in 1995 and apologized to the residents of Margate City, though the spelling on the board was kept intact for tradition.
- Stands as a premier investment tile on the third side of the board, frequently visited by players rolling out of the high-traffic Jail space.
- Requires an initial purchase price of $280, commanding steep rents once developed with houses and hotels to bankrupt opponents.

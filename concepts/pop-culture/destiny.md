## summary

**Destiny** is an online multiplayer first-person shooter franchise developed by *Bungie* and originally published by *Activision*. First released in 2014, the series incorporates science-fantasy worldbuilding, cooperative strikes, competitive multiplayer, and multi-player raids within a persistent shared-world environment. It pioneered the modern console "looter-shooter" genre and remains influential for popularizing games-as-a-service seasonal content models.

## you gotta know

- Developed by *Bungie* as their first major franchise after departing the *Halo* series and published initially under a ten-year partnership with *Activision*.
- Set in a mythic science-fiction solar system where resurrected warriors called *Guardians* wield the paracausal light of a celestial entity known as the *Traveler*.
- Players choose between three core character classes: the heavily armored *Titan*, the agile *Hunter*, and the mystic *Warlock*.
- Features high-difficulty, six-player end-game cooperative missions known as *Raids*, beginning with the influential *Vault of Glass* in 2014 and later *The Taken King* expansion.
- Transitioned into *Destiny 2* in 2017, which adopted a free-to-play model and introduced evolving annual seasonal narrative expansions like *Forsaken* and *The Witch Queen*.
- Popularized hybridized gameplay combining tight precision shooting mechanics with role-playing progression loops pioneered by action RPGs like [[diablo]].
- The overarching saga revolves around the cosmic conflict between the paracausal forces of the *Light* and the *Darkness*, culminating in *The Final Shape*.

## connections

- [[first-person-shooter]] — core gameplay genre defining its combat mechanics, weapon handling, and perspective.
- [[diablo]] — structural progenitor for its color-coded tiered loot system, stat-driven character builds, and repeatable dungeon farming.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow major open-ended role-playing milestone that shaped modern gaming expectations of exploration and lore hunting.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming sphere adjacent to its player-versus-player arena mode, *The Crucible*, and high-stakes *Trials of Osiris*.
- [[counter-strike]] — tactical multiplayer shooter classic whose competitive weapon balancing traditions contrast with Destiny's space-magic gunplay.
- [[united-states]] — home country of developer *Bungie*, based in Bellevue, Washington.

## summary

**FromSoftware**, officially known as **FromSoftware Inc.** or simply **From Software**, is an acclaimed Japanese video game development studio founded in November 1986 in Tokyo, [[japan]]. Originally established to build business productivity applications and agricultural databases, the company transitioned into interactive entertainment during the 1990s, ultimately revolutionizing the industry through demanding action role-playing games characterized by environmental storytelling, uncompromising difficulty, and intricate architecture. Under the visionary creative leadership of director *Hidetaka Miyazaki*, the studio pioneered the globally renowned "Soulslike" subgenre, establishing a new design standard for modern gaming.

## you gotta know

- Launched its game development career in December 1994 with *King's Field*, a moody, first-person dungeon crawler for the original *Sony PlayStation* that established its penchant for grim atmospheres and lethal traps.
- Achieved substantial domestic and international success across the late 1990s and 2000s with the *Armored Core* franchise, a series of complex, fast-paced mecha action simulations emphasizing deep mechanical customization.
- Sparked a global design revolution with the 2009 release of *Demon's Souls*, which defied prevailing casual gaming trends by emphasizing deliberate stamina management, punishing combat, and innovative asynchronous multiplayer phantoms.
- Mastered interconnected world design with *Dark Souls* in 2011, earning universal praise for its seamless three-dimensional vertical map of *Lordran*, minimalist lore delivery, and melancholic reflections on entropy.
- Collaborated with *Sony Interactive Entertainment* on the 2015 cosmic horror masterpiece *Bloodborne*, shifting the combat tempo toward hyper-aggressive gun parries in a Victorian city inspired by gothic literature.
- Captured The Game Award for Game of the Year in 2019 with *Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice*, a Sengoku-era shinobi adventure that replaced conventional role-playing progression with an exacting sword-deflection posture mechanic.
- Achieved unprecedented commercial and critical triumph with *Elden Ring* in 2022, partnering with fantasy author *George R.R. Martin* to construct the vast *Lands Between*, which won numerous Game of the Year accolades.
- Celebrated for its signature environmental narrative philosophy, which deliberately leaves history fragmented across item descriptions and architectural remnants to encourage collaborative player interpretation.

## connections

- [[japan]] — home country where the studio operates its headquarters and drew inspiration from historical eras like the Sengoku period.
- [[skyrim]] — landmark Western role-playing game frequently contrasted with the studio's works regarding world design, narrative transparency, and combat philosophy.
- [[frodo-baggins]] — mythic fantasy protagonist whose grueling, solitary trek through hostile, corrupted realms closely mirrors the experience of the *Tarnished*.
- [[tragedy]] — central dramatic mode informing its somber game narratives, in which decaying kingdoms collapse under the weight of pride and divine curses.
- [[frogger]] — classic arcade ancestor that established the fundamental gaming virtue of timing-based hazard avoidance and severe penalties for simple errors.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — interactive game genre sharing deep technical considerations regarding spatial awareness, hitboxes, precision player reflexes, and combat encounter pacing.
- [[fubar]] — military slang term fittingly capturing the chaotic, catastrophic outcomes that frequently await unprepared players in brutal boss arenas.

## summary

**Knife Party** is an Australian electronic dance music duo formed in 2011 by producers *Rob Swire* and *Gareth McGrillen*, best known as the central creative forces behind the influential drum and bass band *Pendulum*. Operating across dubstep, electro house, and moombahton, the project achieved international stardom with aggressive bass drops, theatrical vocal samples, and explosive festival anthems during the peak of the 2010s dance music boom. Their work defined the sonic landscape of modern electronic music festivals and became a prominent fixture of internet meme culture and competitive gaming soundtracks.

## you gotta know

- Founded as a side project by *Rob Swire* and *Gareth McGrillen* while *Pendulum* went on an extended touring hiatus, allowing both musicians to experiment freely with club-driven four-on-the-floor tempos and aggressive American-style dubstep.
- Derived their project name from the track "Knife Prty" off the critically acclaimed 2000 alternative metal album *White Pony* by the American band *Deftones*.
- Skyrocketed to viral fame with their 2011 debut extended play *100% No Modern Talking*, highlighted by the iconic track "Internet Friends" featuring a cold robotic voice announcing: "You blocked me on Facebook, and now you're going to die."
- Followed with the commercially dominant 2012 EP *Rage Valley*, containing festival mainstays like "Centipede" and "Bonfire", the latter of which was memorably synced to *Walter White* and *Walter White Jr.* revving sports cars in the fifth season of *Breaking Bad*.
- Released their 2013 EP *Haunted House*, which earned widespread acclaim for the vocally driven club hit "LRAD" and broke into the top ten on the Billboard 200 album sales chart.
- Delivered their sole full-length studio LP *Abandon Ship* in 2014, showcasing diverse genre parodies, disco influences, and hard electro tracks such as "Boss Mode" and "Begin Again".
- Maintained immense cross-pollination with competitive video gaming and online montage communities, where their ferocious bass drops became standard audio backing for montage clips in titles like [[counter-strike]].

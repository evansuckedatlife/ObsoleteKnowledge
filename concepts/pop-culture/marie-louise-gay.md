## summary

**Marie-Louise Gay** is an acclaimed Canadian author and illustrator of children's literature, renowned internationally as the creator of the whimsical *Stella and Sam* picture book series. Born in Quebec City and long established in Montreal, she crafts delicate, watercolor-and-collage narratives that explore early childhood curiosity, family affection, and the untamed imagination of youth. Her prolific career across French and English publishing has earned multiple Governor General's Literary Awards and international acclaim, cementing her status as a foundational voice in contemporary Canadian visual storytelling.

## you gotta know

- Created the beloved *Stella and Sam* picture book franchise, beginning in 1999 with *Stella, Star of the Sea*, which depicts an imaginative, red-headed elder sister guiding her skeptical younger brother through the wonder of seaside wonders.
- Won the Governor General's Literary Award in two distinct disciplines, earning the prize for illustration in 1987 for *Rainy Day Magic* and later winning the children's literature text award in 2000 for *Stella, Star of the Sea*.
- Illustrated and authored the delightful *Caramba* series, chronicling the gentle identity struggles of a plump cat who discovers he cannot fly despite belonging to an aerial feline species.
- Penned the energetic *Roslyn Rutabaga* books, capturing the stubborn determination and eccentric schemes of a little rabbit who yearns to dig the biggest hole in the world.
- Gained sustained global recognition as Canada's nominee for the Hans Christian Andersen Award and the Astrid Lindgren Memorial Award, the highest international honors in juvenile illustration.
- Co-wrote and illustrated an acclaimed sequence of semi-autobiographical middle-grade travel novels with her husband, novelist *David Homel*, including *Travels with My Family* and *The Travelling Circus*.
- Appointed an Officer of the Order of Canada in recognition of her profound influence on childhood literacy, bilingual publishing, and graphic illustration.

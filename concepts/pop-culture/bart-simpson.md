## summary

**Bart Simpson**, whose full legal name is **Bartholomew Jo-Jo Simpson**, is the mischievous, skateboard-riding ten-year-old eldest child on the long-running animated sitcom *The Simpsons*, created by cartoonist *Matt Groening*. Debuting on television in 1987 in animated shorts on *The Tracey Ullman Show* before launching his own half-hour series on the *Fox* network in 1989, he quickly ignited a worldwide cultural phenomenon known as "Bartmania." In academic media studies and quiz bowl tournaments, he is celebrated as a transformative touchstone of television satire whose rebellious defiance of authority permanently reshaped American broadcast comedy.

## you gotta know

- Voiced continuously since his 1987 inception by actress *Nancy Cartwright*, who originally auditioned for the role of his intellectual younger sister *Lisa Simpson* before switching to the mischievous boy.
- Sparked fierce cultural and political controversy during the early 1990s through "Bartmania," leading several public schools to ban student apparel displaying his likeness and catchphrases like "Underachiever and proud of it, man."
- Renowned for an arsenal of early-season catchphrases that saturated popular culture across the [[united-states]], including "Eat my shorts," "¡Ay, caramba!," and "Don't have a cow, man."
- Torments school principal *Seymour Skinner* and fourth-grade teacher *Edna Krabappel* at *Springfield Elementary School* with disruptive pranks, making prank phone calls to *Moe's Tavern*, and leaving spray-painted graffiti signed by his alter ego **El Barto**.
- Serves as the frequent target of physical strangulation by his short-tempered father *Homer Simpson*, an enduring sight gag rooted directly in the classic animation traditions of [[physical-comedy]] and [[slapstick-comedy]].
- Engages in an ongoing, operatic feud with former television clown sidekick *Sideshow Bob* (*Kelsey Grammer*), who repeatedly breaks out of prison to plot elaborate, murderous revenge schemes against him.
- Portrayed in an in-universe television movie parody titled *Blood on the Blackboard: The Bart Simpson Story* by guest star *Neil Patrick Harris*, who would later achieve fame playing [[barney-stinson]].
- Conceived by creator *Matt Groening* as an anagram of the word "brat" while waiting in an office lobby to pitch animation ideas to veteran television producer *James L. Brooks*.

- [[physical-comedy]] — kinetic performance mode driving his relentless practical jokes, slingshot attacks, and frantic escapes from authority figures.
- [[i-love-lucy]] — foundational television sitcom whose sanitized mid-century family domesticity was radically deconstructed by the animated series.
- [[barney-stinson]] — prime-time comedy protagonist, connected when actor *Neil Patrick Harris* played Bart in a third-season television movie dramatization.

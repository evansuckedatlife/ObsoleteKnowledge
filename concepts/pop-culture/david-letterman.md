## summary

**David Letterman** is an influential American television host, comedian, and writer who redefined the late-night talk show format through ironic detachment, absurdist visual stunts, and unconventional interviews. Broadcasting for thirty-three years across NBC and CBS, Letterman established an enduring comedic template that bridged mid-century television traditions with modern anti-comedy. His legacy in the [[united-states]] includes pioneering segments like the *Top Ten List* and championing alternative comedic talent across thousands of broadcasts taped in [[new-york-city]].

## you gotta know

- Hosted *Late Night with David Letterman* on NBC from 1982 to 1993, followed by *Late Show with David Letterman* on CBS from 1993 to 2015, taping out of the historic Ed Sullivan Theater.
- Popularized deadpan signature segments including the nightly *Top Ten List*, *Stupid Pet Tricks*, *Stupid Human Tricks*, and throwing household objects off the roof of the theater to watch them smash on the pavement.
- Engaged in famous, highly adversarial on-air encounters, including volatile interviews with actor *Crispin Glover*, wrestler *Jerry Lawler* alongside performance artist *Andy Kaufman*, and a notoriously uncomfortable 1994 appearance by pop icon *Madonna*.
- Suffered a high-profile corporate setback during the 1992 late-night transition wars when NBC selected *Jay Leno* over him to replace the retiring *Johnny Carson* on *The Tonight Show*, prompting Letterman to jump to CBS.
- Broadcasted the first major late-night comedy episode after the September 11 attacks, delivering an emotional, widely praised monologue reflecting on the resilience of [[new-york-city]].
- Began his television career in Indianapolis as an eccentric radio announcer and television weatherman, once famously congratulating a tropical storm on being upgraded to a hurricane and inventing fictional hailstone sizes.
- Returned to long-form interviewing in 2018 with the Netflix series *My Next Guest Needs No Introduction with David Letterman*, sporting an unkempt retirement beard and conducting in-depth conversations with global leaders and cultural figures.

## summary

**Nathan Drake**, commonly nicknamed **Nate Drake**, is the charismatic treasure hunter and primary protagonist of the *Uncharted* video game series developed by [[naughty-dog]]. First appearing in the 2007 title *Uncharted: Drake's Fortune*, he was created by game director *Amy Hennig* and brought to life through the voice and motion-capture performance of *Nolan North*. Recognized by his witty banter, half-tucked henley shirt, and archaeological escapades across the globe, Drake became one of the defining mascots of the modern console generation and a celebrated archetype of cinematic adventure gaming.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in *Uncharted: Drake's Fortune* (2007) for the *PlayStation 3*, embarking on an expedition to uncover the lost golden city of *El Dorado* using a tracking coordinate engraved on the coffin of Sir *Francis Drake*.
- Portrayed across all mainline entries by voice and performance-capture actor *Nolan North*, whose improvisational humor, vulnerability, and relatable reactions established Drake as an everyman counterweight to superhuman action heroes.
- Wears a silver ring suspended on a leather cord around his neck, engraved with the Latin maxim *Sic Parvis Magna* ("Greatness from small beginnings") and the date *29 Januarie 1596*, belonging to his purported ancestor Sir *Francis Drake*.
- Supported on his global treasure hunts by his veteran mentor and surrogate father *Victor "Sully" Sullivan*, his fellow adventurer *Chloe Frazer*, and investigative journalist *Elena Fisher*, who ultimately becomes his wife and partner.
- Unearths lost mythological civilizations across four primary campaigns, including the Himalayan kingdom of *Shambhala* and the *Cintamani Stone* in *Uncharted 2: Among Thieves*, the desert necropolis *Iram of the Pillars* in *Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception*, and the pirate colony *Libertalia* in *Uncharted 4: A Thief's End*.
- Revealed in *Uncharted 4: A Thief's End* to have been born **Nathan Morgan**, an orphaned runaway raised in *Saint Francis Boys' Home* who changed his surname alongside his brother *Samuel Morgan* after recovering their deceased historian mother's research notebooks.
- Depicted in youth flashbacks learning pickpocketing and climbing in Cartagena, Colombia, and surviving a brush with death at the hands of villainous collector *Gabriel Roman*, cartel leader *Zoran Lazarević*, and occultist *Katherine Marlowe*.
- Portrayed by *Tom Holland* in the 2022 live-action cinematic adaptation directed by *Ruben Fleischer*, introducing the character's swashbuckling origin to mainstream theater audiences.

## summary

**Astroworld** is the critically and commercially acclaimed third studio album by American rapper and producer Travis Scott, released on August 3, 2018, by Grand Hustle Records, Epic Records, and Cactus Jack. Named after the defunct Six Flags AstroWorld theme park in Houston, Texas, the album is celebrated for its psychedelic hip-hop production, elaborate sonic world-building, and seamless multi-part song structures. The project also lent its name to an annual Houston music festival founded by Scott, which became the site of an infamous crowd crush tragedy in November 2021.

## you gotta know

- Debuted at number one on the US *Billboard* 200 chart and spawned the diamond-certified, chart-topping single "SICKO MODE" featuring Drake.
- Takes its conceptual identity and nostalgic title from Six Flags AstroWorld, an amusement park in Houston that was demolished in 2005 to Scott's enduring dismay.
- Features prominent guest contributions and instrumentation from a wide array of artists, including Stevie Wonder, Philip Glass, Kid Cudi, Frank Ocean, The Weeknd, and James Blake.
- Employs sudden beat switches, distortion, lush synthesizers, and carnival-themed sonic motifs to recreate the feeling of amusement park rides through sound.
- Received a nomination for Best Rap Album at the 61st Annual Grammy Awards, where its loss to Cardi B's *Invasion of Privacy* sparked significant online discussion.
- Promoted with giant inflatable golden replicas of Travis Scott's head positioned on landmarks and record stores across the country, as photographed by David LaChapelle for the cover art.
- Lent its name to an annual music festival in Houston that suffered a catastrophic crowd collapse on November 5, 2021, resulting in ten deaths and hundreds of injuries.

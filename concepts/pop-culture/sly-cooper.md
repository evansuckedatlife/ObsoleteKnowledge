## summary

**Sly Cooper** is a celebrated stealth-action platformer video game franchise developed by the American studio Sucker Punch Productions and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. Debuting in 2002 on the PlayStation 2 with *Sly Cooper and the Thievius Raccoonus*, the series chronicles the exploits of an aristocratic anthropomorphic raccoon thief who robs international syndicates alongside his lifelong gang. Renowned for its cel-shaded graphic-novel aesthetics, mid-century crime-caper atmosphere, and charismatic ensemble dynamics, the franchise stands as an iconic touchstone of sixth-generation video game history.

## you gotta know

- Created by American developer Sucker Punch Productions, who later achieved acclaim for the *Infamous* series and *Ghost of Tsushima*, before stewardship transferred to Sanzaru Games for the fourth installment.
- Focuses on the Cooper Gang, composed of titular rogue **Sly Cooper**, the brains and wheelchair-bound demolitions expert Bentley the turtle, and the enthusiastic brawn Murray the pink hippopotamus.
- Drives its narrative via the *Thievius Raccoonus*, an ancient family grimoire detailing centuries of master-thief maneuvers developed by Sly's ancestors, stolen by the villainous Fiendish Five syndicate during Sly's childhood.
- Distinguished its presentation using vibrant, cel-shaded rendering, comic-book panel cinematic cutscenes, and a jazzy, film-noir musical score that evokes classic detective fiction.
- Centers on a chivalric thief code decreeing that members of the Cooper lineage steal exclusively from other master criminals rather than innocent civilians.
- Developed an enduring romantic and adversarial dynamic between Sly and Inspector Carmelita Montoya Fox, an impassioned Interpol agent perpetually pursuing the gang with a shock pistol.
- Evolved gameplay mechanics significantly in sequels *Sly 2: Band of Thieves* (2004) and *Sly 3: Honor Among Thieves* (2005) by abandoning linear stages in favor of sprawling open-hub heists across Paris, Venice, and Prague.
- Pioneered accessible stealth-platforming mechanics, such as the context-sensitive cane latch, shadow-based concealment, pickpocketing, and multi-character coordination during complex, episodic robbery finales.

## summary

**Arcane** is a critically acclaimed adult animated science fantasy action-adventure television series created by Christian Linke and Alex Yee for Netflix. Produced by French animation studio *Fortiche Production* in partnership with *Riot Games*, the show is set in the fictional universe of the popular multiplayer online battle arena game *League of Legends*. Premiering in November 2021, the series earned universal praise for its groundbreaking blend of hand-painted 2D textures with 3D animation, complex moral ambiguity, socio-political worldbuilding, and emotional character tragedy.

## you gotta know

- Focuses on the fractured bond between two orphaned sisters from the undercity, *Vi* (voiced by *Hailee Steinfeld*) and *Powder*, who after a devastating tragedy descends into madness to become the unhinged anarchist *Jinx* (voiced by *Ella Purnell*).
- Depicts the simmering class warfare between the opulent, progressive utopian metropolis of *Piltover* and the impoverished, polluted, crime-ridden subterranean district of *Zaun*.
- Centers its narrative engine on the scientific breakthrough of *Hextech*, engineered by scientists *Jayce Talis* and *Viktor*, which harnesses raw magical energy through technological machinery, provoking ethical dilemmas and geopolitical escalation.
- Produced by *Fortiche Production*, a Parisian studio famed for its signature aesthetic uniting traditional digital matte painting with stylized three-dimensional CGI character rigging.
- Became the first streaming animated series to win the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Animated Program, winning four awards at the 74th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards.
- Features an eclectic soundtrack highlighted by the global hit theme song *Enemy* performed by *Imagine Dragons* and rapper *JID*.
- Explores complex supporting characters including the Zaunite crime baron *Silco*, the wealthy councilor *Mel Medarda*, and the aristocratic investigator *Caitlyn Kiramman*.

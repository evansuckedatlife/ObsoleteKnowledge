## summary

**Ottery St Catchpole** is a fictional semi-magical civil parish and village situated along the River Otter in Devon, [[england]], created by *J. K. Rowling* in the *Harry Potter* fantasy universe. Designed as a prime example of integrated wizarding and Muggle cohabitation under the Statute of Secrecy, the settlement is renowned as the regional setting for iconic magical households including *The Burrow* and the *Lovegood House*. The village exemplifies the pastoral, eccentric topography characteristic of twentieth-century British fantasy literature.

## you gotta know

- Named after the real-world Devon market town of *Ottery St Mary*, drawing inspiration from the tranquil rural landscapes and architectural heritage of southwest England.
- Serves as the residential location of *The Burrow*, the ramshackle, multiple-story homestead of the *Weasley* family supported precariously by continuous domestic charms.
- Located within walking distance of *Stoatshead Hill*, a prominent geographic landmark used as the departure site for a Portkey transporting wizards to the Quidditch World Cup.
- Houses notable non-conformist wizarding residents including the eccentric publisher *Xenophilius Lovegood* and his daughter *Luna*, who reside in a distinctive chess-rook-shaped tower.
- Illustrated as an exemplar of the International Statute of Wizarding Secrecy of 1689, wherein magical enclaves quietly shared communal territory with oblivious Muggle neighbors.
- Home to additional magical lineages including the *Fawcett* family and the *Diggory* family, whose patriarch *Amos* and son *Cedric* commute from the nearby countryside.
- Functions narratively as an emotional sanctuary, moral center, and domestic retreat contrasting sharply with institutional settings like *Hogwarts* and the *Ministry of Magic*.

## summary

**Lara Croft** is a British archaeologist, adventurer, and aristocrat who serves as the central protagonist of the celebrated *Tomb Raider* video game franchise. Conceived by *Toby Gard* at the British studio *Core Design* and debuting in 1996, she quickly became a global pop-culture phenomenon and a groundbreaking female action lead in interactive media. Navigating subterranean ruins, lethal traps, and supernatural forces with her signature dual pistols, she exemplifies the late-twentieth-century intersection of video games, cinema, and global celebrity.

## you gotta know

- Introduced in the 1996 title *Tomb Raider*, pioneering 3D action-adventure game design, acrobatic platforming, and puzzle-solving across historical ruins.
- Portrayed in live-action cinematic adaptations by Academy Award winners *Angelina Jolie* in the early 2000s and *Alicia Vikander* in the 2018 reboot.
- Characterized visually by her signature twin semi-automatic pistols, turquoise tank top, cargo shorts, climbing axes, and long braided ponytail.
- Holds multiple *Guinness World Records*, including recognition as the most recognized female character in video game history and the most successful video game heroine.
- Reimagined in a critically acclaimed 2013 gritty survival reboot trilogy developed by *Crystal Dynamics*, which explored her traumatic formative shipwreck on the lost island of *Yamatai*.
- Born into nobility as the Countess of Abbingdon, operating out of *Croft Manor*, where players frequently practiced agility maneuvers and trapped the loyal butler *Winston* in the walk-in freezer.
- Originally conceived during development under the working name *Laura Cruz*, an adventurous South American mercenary, before being reworked into an English aristocrat.

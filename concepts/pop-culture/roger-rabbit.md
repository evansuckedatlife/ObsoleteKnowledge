## summary

**Who Framed Roger Rabbit** is a 1988 American fantasy comedy-mystery film directed by *Robert Zemeckis* that ingeniously combines live-action photography with traditional animation. Set in an alternate 1947 Hollywood where animated cartoon characters coexist with humans, the story follows cynical private detective *Eddie Valiant* as he attempts to exonerate an anxious cartoon rabbit framed for murder. The production stands as a technical and historical milestone, revitalizing American animation and uniting rival studio icons on screen for the first time.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Robert Zemeckis* and produced by *Steven Spielberg*'s Amblin Entertainment alongside Walt Disney Pictures, adapting *Gary K. Wolf*'s novel *Who Censored Roger Rabbit?*.
- Stars *Bob Hoskins* as the gritty, toon-hating private eye *Eddie Valiant*, whose performance against empty space set a benchmark for physical acting alongside animated characters.
- Features the voice of *Charles Fleischer* as the hyperactive title character *Roger Rabbit*, and *Kathleen Turner* as his alluring, misunderstood femme fatale wife *Jessica Rabbit*.
- Introduces the terrifying villain *Judge Doom*, played by *Christopher Lloyd*, who schemes to dismantle the public transit system and exterminate toons using a caustic chemical mixture called "The Dip."
- Achieved unprecedented legal agreements allowing characters from rival animation houses, including *Warner Bros.* and *Disney*, to appear together in shared scenes featuring *Mickey Mouse*, *Bugs Bunny*, *Donald Duck*, and *Daffy Duck*.
- Directed animated sequences under master animator *Richard Williams*, who insisted on interacting shadows, moving live-action camera movement, and tactile props to sell the illusion.
- Triggered the historical period known as the Disney Renaissance by proving the massive critical and commercial appetite for high-craft animation.

## summary

**tick, tick... BOOM!** is an autobiographical musical composed by *Jonathan Larson*, detailing the crushing artistic anxieties, financial privation, and dread of aging experienced by an aspiring Broadway composer. Originally performed by Larson as a solo rock monologue in [[new-york-city]] in 1990, the piece explores creative desperation beneath the shadow of the ongoing AIDS epidemic. Following Larson's tragic death on the eve of *Rent*'s off-Broadway premiere, playwright *David Auburn* restructured the show into a celebrated three-actor piece, subsequently adapted into an acclaimed 2021 feature film directed by *Lin-Manuel Miranda*.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *Jonathan Larson* under working titles such as *Boho Days*, premiered as a solo performance piece at New York's Second Stage Theater in September 1990.
- Follows the protagonist *Jon*, a Greenwich Village diner waiter at the Moondance Diner approaching his 30th birthday while desperately striving to finish writing his dystopian sci-fi musical *Superbia*.
- Features poignant numbers such as "30/90," the Sondheim tribute "Sunday," and the reflective cabaret ballad "Why," detailing Jon's bond with his childhood friend Michael.
- Portrays the looming toll of the HIV/AIDS epidemic across New York's bohemian artistic community, mirroring the real-world milieu Larson later immortalized in *Rent*.
- Reconfigured after Larson's sudden 1996 death from an aortic dissection into a three-person chamber musical by playwright *David Auburn*, opening Off-Broadway in 2001.
- Adapted into a 2021 Netflix musical drama film directed by *Lin-Manuel Miranda*, featuring an Academy Award-nominated lead performance by *Andrew Garfield*.
- Deeply showcases the mentorship of legendary Broadway composer *Stephen Sondheim*, whose real-life encouraging phone message to Larson is faithfully preserved in the finale.

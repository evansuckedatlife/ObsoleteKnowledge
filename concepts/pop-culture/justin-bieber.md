## summary

**Justin Bieber** is a Canadian singer and pop icon who skyrocketed to global superstardom during the late 2000s and 2010s after being discovered on YouTube. Emerging as a teen idol with chart-topping bubblegum pop hits, he successfully navigated commercial turbulence and media scrutiny to transition into contemporary pop and R&B. His career reflects modern viral celebrity culture, record-breaking streaming statistics, and widespread influence across twenty-first-century mainstream music.

## you gotta know

- Achieved breakout international fame with the signature 2010 pop single *Baby*, featuring rapper *Ludacris*, which became an early viral phenomenon and generated record-setting music video viewership.
- Discovered in 2007 by talent manager *Scooter Braun* through amateur acoustic performance covers uploaded to YouTube, leading directly to a signing partnership with R&B star *Usher* at Raymond Braun Media Group and Island Records.
- Released the multi-platinum studio debut *My World 2.0* in 2010, establishing a massive and dedicated global fanbase known colloquially as *Beliebers*.
- Reinvented his musical image toward electronic dance music and mature contemporary R&B with the 2015 critically acclaimed album *Purpose*, which spawned three consecutive Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles: *What Do You Mean?*, *Sorry*, and *Love Yourself*.
- Featured alongside *Daddy Yankee* on the 2017 remix of *Despacito* by *Luis Fonsi*, tying the then-record for the longest run at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 chart.
- Collaborated across hip-hop, electronic, and pop scenes, including prominent team-ups with artists such as [[kanye-west]] on the remix of *Runaway Love*, DJ *Skrillex*, and DJ *Diplo* under the duo *Jack Ü*.
- Subject of the hit 2011 3D concert-documentary film *Justin Bieber: Never Say Never*, which set box-office records for concert films in North America.

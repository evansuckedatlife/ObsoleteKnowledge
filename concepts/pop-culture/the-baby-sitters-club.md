## summary

**The Baby-Sitters Club** is an influential American children's and young adult novel franchise created by *Ann M. Martin* and published by Scholastic between 1986 and 2000. Set in the fictional town of Stoneybrook, Connecticut, the series follows an entrepreneurial group of middle school girls who operate a neighborhood childcare enterprise while confronting family issues, friendship rivalries, and growing up. Selling hundreds of millions of copies worldwide, the franchise transformed modern middle-grade publishing and established enduring benchmarks for commercial serialization.

## you gotta know

- Conceived and inaugurated by author *Ann M. Martin*, who wrote the first thirty-five titles before delegating subsequent volumes to trusted ghostwriters working from detailed character bibles.
- Revolves around four founding eighth-grade members: headstrong president *Kristy Thomas*, stylish vice president *Claudia Kishi*, reliable secretary *Mary Anne Spier*, and treasurer *Stacey McGill*.
- Expands its core roster over time to include alternate members such as California transplant *Dawn Schafer*, junior officer *Mallory Pike*, and younger friend *Jessi Ramsey*.
- Operates out of *Claudia Kishi*'s bedroom on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday afternoons to take advantage of her private, landline telephone and stash of junk food.
- Tackles serious adolescent and health realities for a young audience, including *Stacey McGill*'s management of type 1 diabetes, parental divorce, death, and blended families.
- Adapted across multiple media formats, including an acclaimed graphic novel line by *Raina Telgemeier*, a 1995 theatrical feature, and a critically praised 2020 Netflix streaming adaptation.

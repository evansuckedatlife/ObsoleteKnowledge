## summary

**Mattel** is an American multinational toy manufacturing and entertainment enterprise founded in 1945 by *Harold "Matt" Matson*, *Ruth Handler*, and *Elliot Handler*. Headquartered in El Segundo, California, it grew into one of the highest-grossing toymakers in the world through ubiquitous global brands such as *Barbie*, *Hot Wheels*, *Fisher-Price*, *Masters of the Universe*, and *American Girl*. The corporation played a pioneering role in postwar consumer culture by advertising toys directly to children via television.

## you gotta know

- Founded in a southern California garage in 1945, initially producing picture frames and dollhouse furniture constructed from scrap materials before pivoting entirely to toys.
- *Ruth Handler* revolutionized the industry in 1959 by introducing *Barbie*, a three-dimensional teenage fashion doll named after her daughter *Barbara*, inspired by the German adult novelty doll *Bild Lilli*.
- Introduced *Hot Wheels* in 1968, conceived by *Elliot Handler* to compete directly with Matchbox die-cast vehicles by utilizing friction-reducing axles and vibrant candy-colored *Spectraflame* paint schemes.
- Disrupted traditional retail by sponsoring *The Mickey Mouse Club* in 1955, establishing modern direct-to-consumer toy marketing on national broadcast television rather than targeting parents exclusively.
- Ventured into home video gaming during the early console era with the *Intellivision* in 1979 to rival the Atari 2600, later manufacturing licensed accessories such as the infamous *Power Glove* for the Nintendo Entertainment System in North America.
- Acquired major competitors and legacy brand portfolios including *Fisher-Price* in 1993, the *American Girl* doll line in 1998, and card games like *Uno*.
- Experienced unprecedented contemporary media success when Greta Gerwig directed the live-action blockbuster comedy *Barbie* in 2023, starring Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling.

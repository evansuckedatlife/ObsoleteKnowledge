## summary

**Capcom** is a Japanese video game developer and publisher renowned for pioneering foundational genres in modern gaming. Founded in Osaka in 1979 as a manufacturer of electronic game machines, the company became an arcade titan before successfully conquering home consoles worldwide. Its landmark franchises, ranging from competitive fighting games to survival horror, have exerted immense worldwide influence across interactive media and competitive *esports*.

## you gotta know

- Revolutionized competitive fighting games with the 1991 release of *Street Fighter II*, codifying combo systems, special move inputs, and tournament culture.
- Coined and defined the survival horror genre in 1996 with the launch of *Resident Evil* on the PlayStation, directed by *Shinji Mikami*.
- Created the iconic action-platformer franchise *Mega Man*, known for non-linear boss selection and challenging mechanic-driven level design.
- Pioneered the character-action and hack-and-slash genre with the stylish combat mechanics of *Devil May Cry*.
- Developed the massively successful cooperative hunting RPG series *Monster Hunter*, which became an enduring social phenomenon in *Japan*.
- Originating as I.R.M. Corporation, the company adopted the name *Capcom* as an abbreviation of *Capsule Computers* to convey enclosed entertainment hardware.
- Expanded its global footprint into professional competitive circuits through the annual *Capcom Cup*, a centerpiece of the fighting game community.

## connections

- [[japan]] — country of origin where the studio was established and where its primary development headquarters remain in Osaka.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming scene largely established around early tournament play for Capcom's flagship fighting game releases.
- [[counter-strike]] — fellow titan of the competitive multiplayer landscape that shared tournament stages alongside Capcom's fighters.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — genre contrasting with Capcom's traditional arcade brawlers, though later adopted in games like *Resident Evil 7*.
- [[united-states]] — vital international market where Capcom established major arcade distribution centers and suburban tournament circuits.

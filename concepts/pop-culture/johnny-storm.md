## summary

**Johnny Storm**, also known by the superhero alias the **Human Torch** or his full name **Jonathan Storm**, is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by *Marvel Comics*. Created by writer *Stan Lee* and artist *Jack Kirby*, he debuted in *The Fantastic Four* #1 in November 1961 as a founding member of Marvel's foundational superhero family. Endowed with pyrokinesis, flight, and plasma generation following exposure to cosmic rays, his brash temperament and youthful exuberance revolutionized dynamic character writing in Silver Age superhero storytelling.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in November 1961 alongside sister *Sue Storm*, team leader *Reed Richards*, and pilot *Ben Grimm* in *The Fantastic Four* #1, igniting the Silver Age of *Marvel Comics*.
- Transforms into a fiery, plasma-engulfed state by proclaiming his iconic catchphrase and battle cry: Flame on!
- Gained pyrokinetic superpowers after a cosmic radiation storm bombarded an experimental, unauthorized orbital spaceflight captained by scientist *Reed Richards*.
- Possesses the ability to engulf his body in fiery plasma, achieve supersonic aerodynamic flight, absorb extreme heat, and unleash an all-consuming nova burst blast.
- Reimagined the golden-age moniker of the original android *Human Torch*, *Jim Hammond*, who had been introduced in 1939 by *Timely Comics*.
- Maintained a celebrated, prank-filled comic rivalry with teammate *Ben Grimm*, known as the *Thing*, alongside a famous, enduring friendship with *Peter Parker*, the *Amazing Spider-Man*.
- Resides and operates from the *Baxter Building*, an iconic high-tech headquarters located in midtown *[[new-york-city]]*.
- Portrayed in feature films by actors *Chris Evans* in 2005 and 2007, *Michael B. Jordan* in 2015, and *Joseph Quinn* in subsequent cinematic adaptations.

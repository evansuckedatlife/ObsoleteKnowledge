## summary

**Holly Black** is a prominent contemporary fantasy and children's literature author from the *[[united-states]]*, best known for creating rich modern fairy-tale mythologies and dark urban fantasy worlds. She gained widespread fame alongside illustrator *Tony DiTerlizzi* as the co-creator of *The Spiderwick Chronicles* series before achieving massive critical and commercial success with young adult novels such as *The Folk of the Air* trilogy. Her writing is celebrated in modern *[[american-literature]]* for blending folkloric whimsy with chilling, cutthroat court politics.

## you gotta know

- Co-created the bestselling multi-volume children's fantasy franchise *The Spiderwick Chronicles* alongside illustrator *Tony DiTerlizzi*, which debuted in 2003 with *The Field Guide* and follows the *Grace* children as they discover a secret field guide to magical creatures.
- Penned the bestselling *The Folk of the Air* trilogy, beginning with *The Cruel Prince* in 2018, revolving around the mortal girl *Jude Duarte* navigating the dangerous, manipulative faerie court of *Elfhame* and her fraught dynamic with Prince *Cardan Greenbriar*.
- Launched her published career in 2002 with the gritty urban fantasy novel *Tithe: A Modern Faerie Tale*, which established her signature dark, eerie depiction of fae folk entangled in contemporary American life and was followed by *Valiant* and *Ironside*.
- Received a *Newbery Honor* in 2014 for her middle-grade horror and mystery novel *Doll Bones*, which centers on three children returning a bone-china doll made from human ashes to an unmarked grave.
- Authored the *Curse Workers* urban fantasy trilogy, featuring protagonist *Cassel Sharpe* in an alternate America where criminal syndicates exploit practitioners of magical touch-curses.
- Frequently collaborates with author *Cassandra Clare*, co-authoring the five-book middle-grade fantasy series *Magisterium*, which opens with *The Iron Trial*.
- Explored adult dark fantasy with her 2022 novel *Book of Night*, which focuses on shadow-manipulating magic known as gloaming within underworld crime rings.

## summary

**Ed Asner** was an acclaimed American character actor and labor leader whose seven Primetime Emmy Awards made him the most honored male performer in television history. He gained national prominence during the 1970s portraying gruff newsman *Lou Grant* on the hit CBS sitcom *The Mary Tyler Moore Show*, later transitioning the character into an acclaimed hour-long dramatic spinoff of the same name. Later generations widely recognize his distinctive voice as the widowed protagonist *Carl Fredricksen* in the 2009 Pixar animated film *Up* and his holiday turn as Santa Claus in *Elf*.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed news director *Lou Grant* on *The Mary Tyler Moore Show*, famously telling *Mary Richards* in the pilot episode, "You've got spunk... I hate spunk."
- Achieved the rare television feat of winning Primetime Emmy Awards for playing the exact same character in both a comedy series (*The Mary Tyler Moore Show*) and an hour-long drama (*Lou Grant*).
- Voiced the elderly, balloon-flying widower *Carl Fredricksen* in the Academy Award-winning Pixar animated feature *Up*.
- Won additional Primetime Emmy Awards for his acclaimed dramatic performances in groundbreaking miniseries, including *Rich Man, Poor Man* and *Roots*.
- Served two tumultuous terms as president of the *Screen Actors Guild* (SAG) in the 1980s, where his outspoken political activism led to national controversy.
- Played Santa Claus opposite *Will Ferrell* in the beloved 2003 Christmas comedy film *Elf*.
- Began his professional stage career in Chicago, performing alongside foundational figures of *The Second City* improvisational comedy troupe before moving into episodic television.

## summary

**Solar Opposites** is an American adult animated science fiction sitcom created by Mike McMahan and Justin Roiland that debuted on the streaming platform Hulu in 2020. The narrative centers on a dysfunctional family of extraterrestrial refugees from the destroyed planet Shlorp who crash-land in suburban Indiana, oscillating between utter revulsion and manic fascination toward human society. Highly regarded for its hyper-violent [[slapstick-comedy]], relentless meta-humor, and high-concept narrative balance, the series is equally renowned for its serialized dramatic secondary plotline, "The Wall."

## you gotta know

- Follows four Shlorpians—the stern scientist Korvo, the hedonistic pop-culture enthusiast Terry, and their respective younger replicants Yumyulack and Jesse—alongside their slug-like pet, the Pupa, whose ultimate biological destiny is to terraform Earth.
- Originated as a creative collaboration between Mike McMahan and Justin Roiland following their work on *Rick and Morty*, sharing aesthetic design traits while focusing more explicitly on family sitcom tropes and suburban community satire.
- Features the critically celebrated, self-contained dramatic subplot known as "The Wall," in which human beings shrunken by Yumyulack construct an elaborate, grim post-apocalyptic society inside a bedroom terrarium system.
- "The Wall" storyline functions as an intense pastiche of prestige post-apocalyptic television dramas, depicting violent class struggle, religious zealotry, democratic revolution, and brutal authoritarian regimes led by figures like Tim and Cherie.
- Expanded its world-building in later seasons with the introduction of the "SilverCops," a sprawling space-opera secondary arc tracking human neighbor Glen as he joins an intergalactic, corrupt interstellar police force.
- Recast its co-lead protagonist Korvo with British actor Dan Stevens starting in season four, explaining the abrupt vocal transformation in-universe via a voice-restoration ray malfunction that the characters nonchalantly ignore.
- Explores absurd parodies of American consumerism, commercial branding, convenience store food staples like the [[slurpee]], Halloween traditions, and private country clubs.
- Received praise for subverting suburban sitcom archetypes by having the extraterrestrial protagonists solve domestic disputes using reality-warping sci-fi gadgets, mutant clones, and chaotic bio-weaponry.

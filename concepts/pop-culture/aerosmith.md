## summary

**Aerosmith**, frequently dubbed **The Bad Boys from Boston**, is an American hard rock band formed in Boston, Massachusetts, in 1970 by lead singer *Steven Tyler*, lead guitarist *Joe Perry*, bassist *Tom Hamilton*, rhythm guitarist *Brad Whitford*, and drummer *Joey Kramer*. Emphasizing a swaggering, blues-based guitar sound punctuated by flamboyant stage theatricality and sharp rhythmic hooks, they became the best-selling American hard rock group in music history. Their dramatic late-1980s comeback set enduring industry precedents for mainstream longevity, MTV music video dominance, and genre-crossing collaboration.

## you gotta know

- Scored foundational mid-1970s breakthrough anthems with the soaring power ballad *Dream On*, the funk-infused rocker *Walk This Way*, and the swaggering riff-driven classic *Sweet Emotion*.
- Spearheaded the modern convergence of mainstream rock and hip-hop in 1986 through their historic cross-genre collaboration with *Run-D.M.C.* on the *Walk This Way* remake, revolutionizing MTV programming.
- Achieved one of the most successful commercial resurgences in popular music history during the late 1980s and early 1990s with multi-platinum albums *Permanent Vacation*, *Pump*, and *Get a Grip*.
- Dominated early-1990s MTV video rotation with a celebrated trilogy of cinematic narrative music videos—*Cryin'*, *Amazing*, and *Crazy*—featuring breakout actress *Alicia Silverstone*.
- Scored their first *Billboard* Hot 100 number-one single in 1998 with the power ballad *I Don't Want to Miss a Thing*, written by *Diane Warren* for the blockbuster disaster film *Armageddon*.
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2001, having accumulated four Grammy Awards and worldwide record sales exceeding 150 million copies.
- Sustained a historic creative and personal partnership between *Steven Tyler* and *Joe Perry*, collectively dubbed the *Toxic Twins* due to their notorious lifestyle and explosive artistic chemistry.

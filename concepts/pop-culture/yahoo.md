## summary

**Yahoo** is an American web services provider and digital media pioneer founded in January 1994 by Stanford graduate students *Jerry Yang* and *David Filo*. Originally created as a hierarchically organized web directory, it evolved into one of the defining web portals of the early dot-com era, providing search, email, news, and financial information to hundreds of millions of users worldwide. Yahoo played an instrumental role in shaping the commercial Internet, online advertising models, and modern digital pop culture.

## you gotta know

- Founded initially under the title *Jerry and David's Guide to the World Wide Web* before adopting its memorable name in April 1994 and incorporating in 1995.
- The name was officially backronymed as "Yet Another Hierarchical Officious Oracle," while also playing on the uncivilized creatures depicted in *Jonathan Swift's* *Gulliver's Travels*.
- Became famous for its ubiquitous marketing campaigns featuring the distinctive three-note yodel signature, which cemented the brand across global radio and television.
- Structured early web navigation as a human-curated directory of links categorized by subject, contrasting sharply with the automated algorithmic page crawls that later supplanted it.
- Pioneered essential web services of the 1990s and 2000s, including *Yahoo! Mail*, *Yahoo! Messenger*, *Yahoo! Finance*, *Yahoo! Games*, and the user-generated question platform *Yahoo! Answers*.
- Made historic investments in international tech ventures, most notably acquiring a forty percent stake in Chinese e-commerce giant *Alibaba* in 2005 for one billion dollars.
- Following years of corporate reorganizations and declining search market share, the core Internet business was acquired by telecommunications firm *Verizon Communications* in 2017.

## summary

**Among Us** is an online multiplayer social deduction game developed and published by the American indie game studio *Innersloth*, initially released in June 2018. Set in outer space environments, players are randomly assigned the roles of Crewmates or hidden Impostors, tasked respectively with completing ship maintenance or covertly assassinating the crew. Despite modest early reception, the game experienced an unprecedented viral resurgence in mid-2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic, transforming into a global cultural and digital phenomenon.

## you gotta know

- Developed by *Innersloth*, a tiny independent studio consisting originally of creators *Marcus Bromander*, *Forest Willard*, and *Amy Liu*.
- Directly inspired by the traditional parlor game *Mafia* (or *Werewolf*) as well as the classic science fiction horror film *The Thing*.
- Achieved explosive mainstream popularity in 2020 through live-streaming platforms like Twitch and YouTube, propelled by content creators during pandemic lockdown isolations.
- Centers on asymmetric gameplay where Crewmates complete tasks around maps like *The Skeld* while Impostors sabotage systems, navigate vents, and murder players.
- Core gameplay pivots entirely on text or voice "Emergency Meetings," during which players debate, accuse, and vote to eject suspected perpetrators into space.
- Spawned massive internet meme culture, popularizing gaming slang terms including "sus" (suspicious) and the phrase "venting" into the global vernacular.
- Hosted notable public outreach events, including a record-breaking Twitch livestream by American politicians *Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez* and *Ilhan Omar* to encourage voter turnout.

## summary

**Nimona** is an acclaimed science-fantasy graphic novel and animated feature film created by American cartoonist *ND Stevenson*. Originating as an experimental webcomic while the author was an art student, the story follows a mischievous, punk shapeshifting sidekick who partners with disgraced knight-turned-villain *Lord Ballister Blackheart*. Combining retro-futuristic medieval aesthetics with subversive humor, it is widely celebrated for its deconstruction of classic hero-villain tropes and its resonant exploration of queer, non-conforming, and trans identity.

## you gotta know

- Began in 2012 as a widely read webcomic serialized on Tumblr before being published in 2015 as a bestselling graphic novel by HarperCollins.
- Received prestigious literary recognitions, earning an *Eisner Award* for Best Digital Comic and becoming a National Book Award Finalist in Young People's Literature.
- Follows the titular shapeshifter who insists on serving as henchperson to *Lord Ballister Boldheart* (formerly Blackheart) in their battle against the shadowy, authoritarian *Institution of Law Enforcement and Heroics*.
- Features a rich romantic rivalry between *Ballister* and the realm's premier champion *Sir Ambrosius Goldenloin*, confronting deeply ingrained institutional propaganda.
- Underwent a turbulent screen adaptation journey, being canceled mid-production when Disney shuttered Blue Sky Studios in 2021 before being rescued by Annapurna Pictures and Netflix.
- Released as an acclaimed feature-length 3D animated film in 2023, voiced by *Chloë Grace Moretz* as the title character and *Riz Ahmed* as *Ballister*.
- Garnered an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature, receiving universal acclaim for its fluid stylized visuals, punk-rock soundtrack, and heartfelt celebration of fluidity.

## summary

**Avengers: Endgame** is a monumental 2019 superhero epic directed by *Anthony and Joe Russo*, serving as the grand culmination of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's twenty-two-film Infinity Saga. Chronicling the surviving heroes' desperate "time heist" to reverse the universal genocide caused by *Thanos*, the film synthesized narrative threads spanning over a decade of interconnected Hollywood franchise filmmaking. Upon release, it shattered numerous box-office records, briefly surpassing *Avatar* to become the highest-grossing film of all time worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Directed by the *Russo brothers* and written by *Christopher Markus* and *Stephen McFeely*, serving as the direct sequel to 2018's *Avengers: Infinity War*.
- Grossed over 2.798 billion dollars globally, becoming the fastest film in history to cross multiple box office milestones and briefly holding the all-time gross record until *Avatar* re-releases.
- Features an ensemble cast including *Robert Downey Jr.* as *Tony Stark*, *Chris Evans* as *Steve Rogers*, *Chris Hemsworth* as *Thor*, and *Mark Ruffalo* as *Bruce Banner*.
- Centered on the surviving Avengers utilizing the Quantum Realm to travel back through MCU history to retrieve the six Infinity Stones before *Thanos* could collect them.
- Features the iconic climax at the ruins of the Avengers Compound, punctuated by *Captain America* lifting the mystical hammer *Mjolnir* and *Tony Stark* declaring *"I am Iron Man"* while wielding the Infinity Stones.
- Earned critical acclaim for its emotional resolution of long-running character arcs, concluding with the heroic sacrifice of *Iron Man* and *Captain America* retiring into the past.
- Received an Academy Award nomination for Best Visual Effects and represented the zenith of the modern Hollywood shared-universe cinematic model.

## summary

**Life Is Strange 2** is a 2018 episodic narrative adventure video game developed by the French studio *Dontnod Entertainment* and published by *Square Enix*. Written by *Jean-Luc Cano* and directed by *Michel Koch* and *Raoul Barbet*, the game chronicles the harrowing cross-country odyssey of Mexican-American brothers **Sean Diaz** and his younger brother **Daniel Diaz**, who manifests telekinetic powers following a fatal encounter with police in Seattle. Departing from the time-travel mechanics of its predecessor, the game was acclaimed for its poignant social realism, cinematic visual storytelling, and nuanced moral choice architecture governing brotherhood and education.

## you gotta know

- Follows sixteen-year-old artist *Sean Diaz* as he protects and mentors his nine-year-old brother *Daniel*, whose emerging telekinesis is triggered by emotional distress.
- Traces an epic southward fugitive journey from suburban Seattle, Washington through Oregon, California, Nevada, and Arizona, aiming to reach their ancestral hometown of *Puerto Lobos* in Mexico.
- Replaces direct superpower gameplay by placing the player in the role of the unpowered older sibling Sean, whose daily behavioral examples shape Daniel's hidden morality and brotherly trust meters.
- Preceded by a standalone playable teaser demo titled *The Awesome Adventures of Captain Spirit*, starring a creative neighbor boy named *Chris Eriksen*.
- Explores acute contemporary sociopolitical issues including police violence, racial profiling, border enforcement, counterculture communities, and religious cultism across rural America.
- Features five distinct narrative endings that diverge radically based on whether Sean prioritized teaching Daniel survivalist pragmatism or civic morality, culminating at the Mexican border wall.
- Showcases a haunting acoustic indie-folk score composed by *Jonathan Morali* of the French band *Syd Matters*, complemented by licensed tracks from artists such as *Sufjan Stevens* and *First Aid Kit*.

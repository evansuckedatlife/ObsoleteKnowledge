## summary

**Cities: Skylines** is a genre-defining city-building simulation video game developed by the Finnish studio *Colossal Order* and published by *Paradox Interactive* in 2015. Offering deep economic, zoning, and infrastructural modeling, the game emerged as the definitive critical and commercial successor to the *SimCity* franchise following the troubled launch of *SimCity* (2013). Renowned for its flexible road-building mechanics, granular traffic algorithms, and extensive community modding ecosystem, the game stands as one of the most successful urban management simulations in gaming history.

## you gotta know

- Developed by a small team at *Colossal Order* after their previous specialized transportation simulators, *Cities in Motion* and *Cities in Motion 2*.
- Features the distinctive in-game social media mascot *Chirper*, a blue bird that broadcasts humorous, real-time citizen complaints and civic commentary mimicking modern internet platforms.
- Emphasizes detailed transportation design, requiring players to manage traffic congestion through complex interchanges, one-way systems, roundabouts, and hierarchical road classification.
- Incorporates extensive civic infrastructure management, demanding careful placement of water pumping stations, sewage outflow pipes, electricity grids, and healthcare networks to maintain citizen health.
- Achieved unprecedented commercial vitality through full integration with the *Steam Workshop*, allowing players to download hundreds of thousands of custom buildings, vehicles, visual filters, and behavioral modifications.
- Expanded through numerous major downloadable expansions including *After Dark* (adding leisure and day-night cycles), *Snowfall* (adding winter weather and tram transit), *Mass Transit* (adding ferries, blimps, and monorails), and *Green Cities*.
- Spurred a high-profile 2023 sequel, *Cities: Skylines II*, which significantly increased map scale, simulated climatic weather seasons, and updated citizen life simulation.
- Frequently utilized by actual civic planners, urban design educators, and civil engineers to illustrate land use allocation, highway network layout, and the environmental impacts of urban sprawl.

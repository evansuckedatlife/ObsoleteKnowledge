## summary

**Dishonored** is an acclaimed stealth-action video game developed by the French studio *Arkane Studios* and published by *Bethesda Softworks* in 2012. Set in the plague-ridden, whale-oil-powered metropolis of *Dunwall*, the game casts players as the framed royal protector *Corvo Attano* on a quest for vengeance against the conspirators who murdered the reigning empress. Famed for its immersive sim design, emergent gameplay mechanics, and atmospheric world-building, it stands as a landmark release in modern digital interactive entertainment.

## you gotta know

- It was directed by industry veterans *Raphaël Colantonio* and *Harvey Smith*, featuring visionary art direction co-helmed by *Viktor Antonov*, famed for his previous architectural work on dystopian game environments.
- The game is set in *Dunwall*, an industrial port capital heavily inspired by Victorian [[england]], running on volatile refined trans-oceanic whale oil while crumbling under an epidemic of virulent rat plague.
- Players control *Corvo Attano*, the royal bodyguard framed for the assassination of *Empress Jessamine Kaldwin* and the kidnapping of her young daughter and legal heir, *Lady Emily*.
- The protagonist is branded by a mysterious, aloof deity known as the *Outsider*, who bestows supernatural abilities including short-range teleportation through *Blink*, temporal manipulation via *Bend Time*, and vermin possession.
- The game features a dynamic *Chaos* system, where killing adversaries and sowing rampant destruction raises environmental infestation and drives the story toward a darker, cynical conclusion.
- The campaign can be completely traversed without killing a single target, providing intricate non-lethal resolutions for every primary assassination target on the conspiracy hit list.
- Musical and voice talents featured in the production include *Michael Madsen* as the remorseful assassin *Daud*, *Chloë Grace Moretz* as young *Emily*, and an atmospheric score composed by *Daniel Licht*.

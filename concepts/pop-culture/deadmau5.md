## summary

**deadmau5** is the professional moniker of *Joel Zimmerman*, an influential Canadian electronic music producer and DJ who became one of the defining figures of the late-2000s global electronic dance music explosion. Recognized for his oversized, glowing mouse helmet and mastery of progressive house and electro music, he achieved international mainstream stardom while maintaining outspoken independence from commercial pop conventions. A prominent technology and gaming enthusiast, Zimmerman has also left a lasting cultural imprint on digital gaming spaces such as [[minecraft]].

## you gotta know

- Performs wearing a signature oversized rodent helmet called the *mau5head*, which features glowing eyes and mouth displays synchronized with his live visual concert production.
- Adopted his stage name after discovering an actual dead mouse inside his computer chassis while replacing a video card, subsequently using the shortened pseudonym *deadmau5* in online IRC chatrooms due to username character limits.
- Released definitive progressive house anthems including *Ghosts 'n' Stuff* (featuring *Rob Swire*), *Strobe*, *Some Chords*, and *I Remember* (with *Kaskade*), which became touchstones of modern electronic music.
- Founded his own independent record label, *mau5trap*, in 2007, launching or advancing the careers of prominent electronic artists including *Skrillex*, *Feed Me*, and *REZZ*.
- Received a unique, permanent tribute in the sandbox video game [[minecraft]], where creator *Markus "Notch" Persson* coded the game so that Zimmerman's player avatar is the only character rendered with prominent mouse ears.
- Pioneer of elaborate festival stage architecture, touring globally with the *Cube* series of modular LED performance structures, culminating in the complex kinetic *Cube V3*.
- Engaged in an internationally publicized trademark dispute with the *Walt Disney Company* in 2014 over attempts to register his mouse logo in the [[united-states]], which was ultimately resolved through an amicable settlement.

## summary

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is a 2018 American computer-animated superhero film directed by *Bob Persichetti*, *Peter Ramsey*, and *Rodney Rothman*, and produced by *Columbia Pictures* and *Sony Pictures Animation*. The story tracks Brooklyn teenager *Miles Morales*, who gains arachnid abilities and joins forces with five displaced alternate-reality heroes to thwart *Kingpin* from collapsing the multiverse. Widely acclaimed for blending classic comic-book printing techniques with cutting-edge digital rendering, the film revolutionized modern animation and claimed the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

## you gotta know

- Introduced Afro-Latino teenager *Miles Morales* (voiced by *Shameik Moore*) as a cinematic protagonist, adapting the comic character created by Brian Michael Bendis and Sara Pichelli.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2019, breaking a six-year consecutive winning streak held by Walt Disney Animation Studios and Pixar.
- Pioneered a groundbreaking visual language incorporating Ben-Day dots, halftone shading, chromatic aberration, split-screen panels, and hand-drawn line overlays onto computer models.
- Animated Miles Morales at twelve frames per second ("on twos") while his veteran mentor Peter B. Parker moved at twenty-four ("on ones") to physically illustrate Miles's awkwardness.
- Culminates in the celebrated "leap of faith" sequence set to the song *"What's Up Danger"*, where an upside-down camera framing depicts Miles ascending rather than falling.
- Assembles an imaginative cast of alternate-dimension counterparts, including *Gwen Stacy*, *Spider-Man Noir*, *Peni Parker*, and the cartoon pig *Peter Porker* (*Spider-Ham*).
- Features *Liev Schreiber* voicing the colossal crime boss *Wilson Fisk*, whose tragic obsession with restoring his deceased family drives him to build a reality-fracturing particle accelerator.

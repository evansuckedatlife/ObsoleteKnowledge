## summary

**Chris Rock** is an American stand-up comedian, actor, writer, and director renowned for his incisive, high-energy commentary on race relations, politics, class, and romantic relationships. Emerging from the comedy club circuit of [[new-york-city]] and a tenure on *Saturday Night Live*, he achieved legendary status with acclaimed HBO comedy specials like *Bring the Pain*. A multi-time Grammy and Emmy Award winner, Rock has profoundly influenced generations of modern comedians and entertainers, including [[childish-gambino]], through his razor-sharp delivery and social satire.

## you gotta know

- Broke into the upper echelon of stand-up comedy with his 1996 HBO special *Bring the Pain*, which won two Emmy Awards and featured famous routines on racial politics, family, and marriage.
- Cast as a repertory player on *Saturday Night Live* in 1990 alongside *Chris Farley*, *Adam Sandler*, and *David Spade*, where he created characters such as talk-show host *Nat X*.
- Created, produced, and narrated the hit semi-autobiographical sitcom *Everybody Hates Chris*, based on his turbulent childhood years growing up in the Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood of Brooklyn.
- Hosted the annual Academy Awards ceremonies in 2005 and 2016, and became the center of a globally publicized incident at the 94th Academy Awards in 2022 when *Will Smith* slapped him on stage.
- Wrote, directed, and starred in critically acclaimed comedy feature films, including the political satire *Head of State* and the show-business commentary *Top Five*.
- Voiced the zebra *Marty* in the blockbuster animated film franchise *Madagascar*, showcasing his distinctive vocal delivery and comedic timing.
- Starred opposite [[christian-bale]] as fellow World War I veteran *Milton King* in *David O. Russell*'s 2022 historical mystery film *Amsterdam*.

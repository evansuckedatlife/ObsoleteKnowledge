## summary

**Kanye West**, legally known as **Ye**, is an American rapper, producer, and fashion designer whose groundbreaking work fundamentally reshaped hip-hop and twenty-first-century popular culture. Rising to prominence as an in-house producer for Roc-A-Fella Records, he shattered commercial rap conventions with his introspective lyricism, soulful sampling, and avant-garde stylistic evolutions. His critically celebrated discography, outspoken public persona, and lucrative fashion ventures have made him one of the most influential and polarizing artists of modern times.

## you gotta know

- Gained early recognition as a producer crafting sped-up soul samples for *Jay-Z*'s acclaimed 2001 album *The Blueprint*, before launching a solo career with the critically lauded 2004 debut *The College Dropout*.
- Recorded his breakthrough single *Through the Wire* with his jaw wired shut following a near-fatal 2002 car accident, an origin story that established his tenacious creative reputation.
- Won widespread critical acclaim with the progressive orchestral rap album *Late Registration* in 2005 and stadium-oriented synth-pop record *Graduation* in 2007, famously defeating *50 Cent* in a high-profile sales battle.
- Pioneered the mainstream adoption of Auto-Tune and vulnerable melodic themes in hip-hop on the 2008 album *808s & Heartbreak*, directly influencing the next generation of alternative rap and pop performers.
- Released the 2010 masterpiece *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy*, which received near-unanimous critical praise for its lavish maximalist production and complex themes of celebrity excess.
- Partnered directly with prominent pop peers, guest-starring on the hit remix of *E.T.* alongside [[katy-perry]] and recruiting [[justin-bieber]] for the remix of *Runaway Love*.
- Interrupted *Taylor Swift*'s acceptance speech at the 2009 MTV Video Music Awards to advocate for *Beyoncé*, sparking an infamous cultural controversy and long-running media narrative.
- Founded the influential fashion and sneaker brand *Yeezy*, which achieved massive global commercial impact through landmark corporate partnerships with *Nike* and *Adidas*.

## connections

- [[katy-perry]] — recorded the Billboard Hot 100 number-one collaboration *E.T.* during their shared peak of pop-chart dominance.
- [[justin-bieber]] — joined forces on the *Runaway Love* remix with *Raekwon* and maintained close artistic ties during recording sessions.
- [[kelly-clarkson]] — contemporary multi-Grammy winner whose ascent during the mid-2000s paralleled his own breakthrough on American radio.
- [[united-states]] — birthplace and principal arena for his musical innovations, business ventures, and cultural controversies.
- [[new-york-city]] — central hub where he earned production acclaim at Roc-A-Fella Records and debuted major fashion collections.

## summary

**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the *Duffer Brothers* for Netflix, debuting in 2016. Set during the 1980s in the fictional suburban town of Hawkins, Indiana, the narrative follows the disappearance of a young boy and the emergence of a telekinetic girl who aids his friends against sinister supernatural incursions. The show became a global streaming phenomenon, celebrated for its nostalgic synthesis of genre homages, emotional camaraderie, and atmospheric horror.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Matt and Ross Duffer*, drawing heavy inspiration from the works of *Steven Spielberg*, *Stephen King*, and *John Carpenter*.
- Focuses on the friendship group of *Mike Wheeler*, *Dustin Henderson*, *Lucas Sinclair*, and *Will Byers*, whose sudden vanishing triggers the central mystery.
- Introduces *Eleven*, a mysterious telekinetic runaway subjected to abusive government testing at the *Hawkins National Laboratory*.
- Explores an ominous shadow dimension known as the *Upside Down*, home to predatory horrors such as the *Demogorgon*, the *Mind Flayer*, and *Vecna*.
- Leverages tabletop gaming culture, particularly *Dungeons & Dragons*, as a thematic lens through which the characters name and understand terrifying extradimensional threats.
- Revitalized interest in 1980s pop culture, driving vintage soundtrack hits like Kate Bush's *Running Up That Hill* back to international chart pinnacles.
- Stars an ensemble cast including *Winona Ryder*, *David Harbour*, *Millie Bobby Brown*, and *Finn Wolfhard*.

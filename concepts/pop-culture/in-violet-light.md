## summary

**In Violet Light** is the eighth full-length studio album by the seminal Canadian rock band *The Tragically Hip*, released in June 2002 on Universal Music. Recorded at the historic Compass Point Studios in Nassau, Bahamas, and produced by veteran British engineer *Hugh Padgham*, the record presents a refined, brooding sonic landscape that balances tight alternative rock hooks with evocative, poetic lyricism. It cemented the group's reputation for pairing intricate regional and literary imagery with muscular, arena-filling musicianship.

## you gotta know

- Features the celebrated lineup of lead vocalist and lyricist *Gord Downie*, lead guitarist *Rob Baker*, rhythm guitarist *Paul Langlois*, bassist *Gord Sinclair*, and drummer *Johnny Fay*.
- Track listing includes prominent radio singles such as "The Darkest One", "Silver Jet", "Throwing Off Glass", and the evocative opening track "Vaccination Scar".
- The music video for "The Darkest One" achieved legendary Canadian cultural status by featuring actors *Mike Smith*, *Robb Wells*, and *John Paul Tremblay* in character from the television series *Trailer Park Boys*, alongside hockey icon *Don Cherry* delivering fried chicken.
- Recorded in the Bahamas under the direction of *Hugh Padgham*, the acclaimed British producer celebrated internationally for his engineering work with *The Police*, *Phil Collins*, and *XTC*.
- Debuted at number two on the Canadian Albums Chart and earned platinum certification, reflecting the band's continued commercial power across North America and within the [[united-states]] touring circuit.
- Embraces a rich lyrical aesthetic of twilight, introspective melancholy, and regional memory, exemplified by tracks like "Dire Wolf" and "A Beautiful Thing".
- Released with an interactive bonus software package titled *In Violet Light Online*, allowing fans early digital access to bonus audio tracks, studio web streams, and desktop media.

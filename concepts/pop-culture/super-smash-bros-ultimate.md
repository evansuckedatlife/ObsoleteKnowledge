## summary

**Super Smash Bros. Ultimate** is a celebrated crossover platform fighting game developed by *Bandai Namco Studios* and *Sora Ltd.* and published by *Nintendo* for the *Nintendo Switch* console in 2018. Directed by franchise creator *Masahiro Sakurai*, the game fulfilled the ambitious promise that "Everyone is Here!" by assembling every playable character in the series' history alongside prominent new guest fighters. It became the best-selling fighting game of all time and a cornerstone title within contemporary [[esports]] and global competitive gaming culture.

## you gotta know

- Directed by legendary designer *Masahiro Sakurai*, who spearheaded an unprecedented licensing effort in [[japan]] and abroad to include third-party icons alongside classic *Nintendo* mascots.
- Promoted under the slogan *"Everyone is Here!"*, featuring all sixty-three fighters from previous franchise entries alongside eleven new base roster combatants at launch.
- Expanded its roster to eighty-nine total fighters through two expansive post-launch downloadable *Fighters Passes*, culminating with the addition of *Sora* from *Kingdom Hearts* in October 2021.
- Included the protagonist *Steve* from [[minecraft]] as a high-profile DLC fighter, whose unique block-placing and resource-crafting mechanics reshaped the competitive tournament meta.
- Set a historic commercial record by selling over thirty-four million copies worldwide, overtaking *Street Fighter II* as the best-selling individual fighting game release in video game history.
- Maintained a massive global scene in [[esports]], headlining premier tournaments such as *Evolution Championship Series* (*EVO*), *Super Smash Con*, and *Genesis* across the [[united-states]] and [[japan]].
- Introduced the sprawling single-player campaign *World of Light*, where players navigate a vast world map collecting *Spirits* to rescue captured heroes from the cosmic entity *Galeem*.

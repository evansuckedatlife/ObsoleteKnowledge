## summary

**Ed Robertson** is a Canadian musician, singer, and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter for the alternative rock band *Barenaked Ladies*. Formed in Scarborough, Ontario, in the late 1980s alongside childhood friend *Steven Page*, the group gained international renown for witty pop hits and improvised stage banter. Robertson notably composed and performed the theme song for the hit American television sitcom *The Big Bang Theory*, cementing his imprint on contemporary pop culture.

## you gotta know

- Co-founded *Barenaked Ladies* in 1988 with *Steven Page*, initially performing as an acoustic duo at local open-mic nights across Ontario before recruiting a full ensemble.
- Co-wrote the group's breakthrough international hit single *"One Week"* in 1998, which reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in the *[[united-states]]* and featured notoriously rapid-fire lyrical delivery.
- Wrote and recorded the iconic theme song for the sitcom *The Big Bang Theory* in 2007 after show creators *Chuck Lorre* and *Bill Prady* attended a concert where he improvised a freestyle rap on scientific history.
- Became the band's sole primary frontman and chief vocal leader following the departure of longtime co-lead vocalist *Steven Page* in 2009.
- Collaborated in 2013 with Canadian astronaut *Chris Hadfield* while Hadfield was aboard the International Space Station, co-writing and recording *"I.S.S. (Is Somebody Singing?)"*, one of the first musical collaborations staged between Earth and orbit.
- Contributed musical compositions and voice cameo work to gaming and animated media projects, including voice acting in the machinima web series *Red vs. Blue*.
- An avid video gamer and gadget enthusiast who frequently discussed video game titles and tech culture in media appearances and stage banter.

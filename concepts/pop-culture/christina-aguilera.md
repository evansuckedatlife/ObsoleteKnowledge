## summary

**Christina Aguilera** is an influential American singer, songwriter, and television personality who rose to global stardom during the late-1990s teen pop explosion. Renowned for her powerful four-octave soprano vocal range, dynamic belting ability, and virtuosic melisma, she established herself as one of the preeminent vocalists of her generation. Often adopting the provocative artistic alter ego **Xtina**, she broke away from bubblegum pop norms to explore themes of female empowerment, sexuality, and domestic vulnerability, shaping modern pop music across multiple decades.

## you gotta know

- Broke into mainstream superstardom in 1999 with her chart-topping debut single *Genie in a Bottle*, which earned her the *Grammy Award for Best New Artist* and placed her alongside *Britney Spears* at the forefront of the late-1990s teen pop resurgence in the [[united-states]].
- Collaborated with *P!nk*, *Mýa*, and *Lil' Kim* on the blockbuster 2001 remake of the *Labelle* track *Lady Marmalade* for the musical film *Moulin Rouge!*, earning a worldwide number-one hit and critical acclaim for her climactic vocal riffs.
- Shed her wholesome teen image with her landmark 2002 studio album *Stripped*, introducing her alter ego **Xtina** through the controversial lead single *Dirrty*, the self-acceptance anthem *Beautiful* written by *Linda Perry*, and the defiant feminist hit *Fighter*.
- Celebrated for a formidable four-octave vocal range and extensive use of melismatic runs, drawing stylistic inspiration from soul legends such as *Etta James*, *Aretha Franklin*, and *Billie Holiday*.
- Began her professional performing career as a child on *Star Search* before joining the cast of *The All-New Mickey Mouse Club* alongside future pop icons *Justin Timberlake*, *Britney Spears*, and actor *Ryan Gosling*, subsequently recording *Reflection* for the Disney animated film *Mulan*.
- Embraced vintage mid-century big band, jazz, blues, and [[vaudeville]] aesthetics on her ambitious 2006 double album *Back to Basics*, which spawned the hit singles *Ain't No Other Man* and *Candyman*.
- Transitioned to television as an original celebrity coach on the hit singing competition series *The Voice* on NBC, mentoring winning contestants across multiple seasons while releasing collaborations such as *Moves Like Jagger* with *Maroon 5* and *Say Something* with *A Great Big World*.
- Recorded the Latin pop and Spanish-language albums *Mi Reflejo* in 2000 and *Aguilera* in 2022, earning multiple Latin Grammy Awards and honoring her Ecuadorian heritage from her father's lineage.

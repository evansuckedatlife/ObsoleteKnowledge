## summary

**Mattea Roach** is a Canadian writer, podcaster, and trivia champion who captured international attention during an extraordinary 23-game winning streak on the syndicated television game show *Jeopardy!* in 2022. Emerging as the most successful Canadian contestant in the show's history, they amassed over $560,000 in regular-season winnings and captivated audiences with expressive, animated gameplay. Their remarkable performance placed them among the elite competitors of modern trivia culture, bridging traditional academic knowledge with contemporary media presence.

## you gotta know

- Achieved a 23-game winning streak on *Jeopardy!* in April and May 2022, ranking fifth in consecutive regular-season games won in show history.
- Set the record as the highest-earning and longest-running Canadian contestant on the American quiz program.
- Earned $566,402 in regular-season play and later secured the runner-up position in the inaugural 2023 *Jeopardy! Masters* tournament behind *James Holzhauer*.
- Grew up in Halifax, Nova Scotia, and worked as a freelance tutor and writer in Toronto after studying sexual diversity studies and political science.
- Hosted the current-affairs podcast *The Backbench* for Canadaland, showcasing political commentary, sharp debate, and public-affairs interviewing.
- Stood out for an engaging, highly visible stage manner, frequently talking through thought processes, reacting candidly to clues, and demonstrating vast recall across history and literature.

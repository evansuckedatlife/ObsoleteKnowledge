## summary

**ND Stevenson**, born in 1991 and formerly known professionally as **Noelle Stevenson** before publishing as **Nate Stevenson**, is an American cartoonist, graphic novelist, and animation showrunner celebrated for vibrant character-driven fantasy and queer storytelling. He first gained widespread attention while still an undergraduate at the *Maryland Institute College of Art* by creating the beloved webcomic *Nimona*, which evolved into an award-winning graphic novel and an acclaimed animated feature. Stevenson later served as the executive producer and showrunner of *She-Ra and the Princesses of Power*, establishing himself as a vital and transformative creative voice in contemporary visual media and comics.

## you gotta know

- Created the hit fantasy-sci-fi webcomic *Nimona* (2012–2015), chronicling an impulsive, punk-rock shapeshifting girl who offers her services as a minion to the disgraced scientist villain *Lord Ballister Blackheart* to fight the corrupt *Institution of Law Enforcement and Heroics*.
- Became one of the youngest authors ever nominated for the *National Book Award* for Young People's Literature when the published graphic novel edition of *Nimona* was selected as a finalist in 2015, later earning an *Eisner Award* for Best Graphic Album.
- Co-created the celebrated comic series *Lumberjanes* (2014–2020) for *BOOM! Studios* with *Shannon Watters*, *Grace Ellis*, and *Brooklyn A. Allen*, following five diverse girls at *Miss Qiunzella Thiskwin Penniquiqul Thistle Crumpet's Camp for Hardcore Lady-Types* solving supernatural mysteries and shouting feminist slogans.
- Developed, executive produced, and served as showrunner for the acclaimed *DreamWorks Animation* reboot *She-Ra and the Princesses of Power* (2018–2020) on *Netflix*, earning widespread critical praise and a *Peabody Award* nomination for its emotional nuance, deep ensemble cast, and explicit LGBTQ+ romance between *Adora* and *Catra*.
- Authored and illustrated the intimate, vulnerable graphic memoir *The Fire Never Goes Out: A Memoir in Pictures* (2020), assembling yearly mini-comics, sketches, and personal journal essays charting his rise to prominence alongside severe depression, anxiety, and self-discovery.
- Chronicled his personal gender transition, top surgery, and exploration of non-binary identity through the autobiographical 2020 webcomic essay *The Weight of Them*, published on Substack prior to adopting the professional byline ND Stevenson.
- Co-wrote issues of *Marvel Comics*' *Runaways* during the 2015 *Secret Wars* crossover event, writing adolescent superheroes confronting institutional authority and high school pressure.
- Celebrated the release of the Academy Award-nominated 2023 animated film adaptation of *Nimona*, directed by *Nick Bruno* and *Troy Quane*, which was salvaged and completed by *Annapurna Pictures* and *Netflix* following the closure of *Blue Sky Studios*.

## connections

- [[neil-gaiman]] — elder fantasy author and comic writer whose boundary-pushing serialized graphic novels paved the way for literary recognition of creator-owned comics.
- [[united-states]] — nation where he was born in Columbia, South Carolina, pursued his art education, and created his published graphic novels and animated series.
- [[american-literature]] — overarching literary landscape in which his graphic novels, memoirs, and youth comics have been canonized and critically awarded.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — narrative technique employed throughout his visual storytelling to express internal transformation, emotional vulnerability, and bodily dysphoria through dynamic shapeshifting.
- [[modernism]] — twentieth-century artistic tradition characterized by the breakdown of traditional heroic binaries and genre deconstruction, echoed in Stevenson's antiheroic narratives.

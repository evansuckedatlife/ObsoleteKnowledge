## summary

**Alvvays** is a Canadian indie pop and dream pop band formed in 2011, originating from Prince Edward Island and Cape Breton before establishing themselves in Toronto, Ontario. Fronted by vocalist and guitarist *Molly Rankin*, the group won international acclaim for their shimmering jangle pop melodies, wry lyrical storytelling, and lush wall-of-sound guitar textures. Combining nostalgic guitar hooks reminiscent of 1980s indie rock with biting modern wit, they have become one of the most celebrated alternative acts of their generation across three studio albums.

## you gotta know

- Achieved widespread international breakthrough with the irresistible 2014 jangle-pop anthem "Archie, Marry Me", propelled by *Molly Rankin*'s satirical yet tender vocals addressing student debt and matrimonial apathy.
- Released their self-titled debut album *Alvvays* in 2014 through Polyvinyl and Transgressive Records, receiving widespread praise for sparkling singles such as "Adult Diversion" and "Next of Kin".
- Followed with their sophomore album *Antisocialites* in 2017, which earned the Juno Award for Alternative Album of the Year and featured dreamy melancholic favorites including "In Undertow" and "Dreams Tonite".
- Released their third LP *Blue Rev* in 2022 to universal acclaim, named after a sugary Canadian caffeinated malt cooler beverage favored during the band members' teenage years.
- Earned a Grammy Award nomination for Best Alternative Music Performance for the track "Belinda Says", an orchestral shoegaze song referencing pop singer *Belinda Carlisle* and the hit single "Heaven Is a Place on Earth".
- Formed around the musical partnership of frontwoman *Molly Rankin*—daughter of *John Morris Rankin* of the acclaimed Celtic folk group *The Rankin Family*—and guitarist *Alec O'Hanley*, who previously played in the indie outfit *Two Hours Traffic*.
- Developed a signature audio aesthetic layering vintage synthesizers, jangly Rickenbacker and Fender guitars, and noisy shoegaze feedback inspired by groups like *The Smiths*, *Teenage Fanclub*, *The Primitives*, and *My Bloody Valentine*.
- Experienced major setbacks prior to recording *Blue Rev*, including the theft of a computer containing demo files from Rankin's apartment and a basement rehearsal flood that destroyed vintage equipment.

## summary

**My Heart Will Go On**, recorded by Canadian singer *Celine Dion*, is the iconic power ballad serving as the main theme song to *James Cameron*'s blockbuster 1997 disaster romance film *Titanic*. Composed by *James Horner* with lyrics by *Will Jennings*, the song achieved monumental commercial and critical acclaim across the world. It became one of the best-selling physical singles in recorded music history and swept major honors at both the Academy Awards and Grammy Awards.

## you gotta know

- Performed by *Celine Dion* as the central love theme for the 1997 epic film *Titanic*, starring *Leonardo DiCaprio* and *Kate Winslet*.
- Written by composer *James Horner* and lyricist *Will Jennings*, featuring a signature tin whistle opening played by Irish instrumentalist *Eric Rigler*.
- Directed by *James Cameron*, who originally opposed having any pop song with lyrical vocals over the film's closing credits before hearing Dion's secret demo.
- Captured the Academy Award for Best Original Song and swept four major Grammy Awards, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year.
- Reached number one on music charts across more than twenty nations, including the *[[united-states]]*, the United Kingdom, and *[[france]]*.
- Included on Dion's fifth English-language studio album, *Let's Talk About Love*, which propelled the record to sell over thirty million copies worldwide.
- Stood as a defining musical staple of late-1990s pop culture, solidifying the cinematic scale and commercial triumph of Hollywood event blockbusters.

## summary

**Austin Butler** is an American film and stage actor who rose from early-career television roles into international prominence with his transformative biographical performances. He achieved widespread critical acclaim for portraying rock-and-roll icon Elvis Presley in Baz Luhrmann's 2022 musical drama *Elvis*, a role that earned him a Golden Globe Award, a BAFTA Award, and an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor. Known for his dedicated method immersion, magnetic screen presence, and physical adaptability, Butler has established himself as a sought-after leading man across both prestige historical epics and auteur-driven science fiction.

## you gotta know

- Won the Golden Globe Award and the BAFTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role for embodying Elvis Presley in Baz Luhrmann's biographical extravaganza *Elvis*.
- Began his professional career as a teen actor on the Disney Channel and Nickelodeon, appearing on series like *Hannah Montana*, *Zoey 101*, *Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide*, and *Switched at Birth*.
- Portrayed Manson Family member Charles "Tex" Watson in Quentin Tarantino's acclaimed 2019 period film *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*.
- Earned critical acclaim on Broadway playing Don Parritt opposite Denzel Washington in the 2018 revival of Eugene O'Neill's classic drama *The Iceman Cometh*.
- Portrayed the psychopathic, bald Harkonnen na-baron Feyd-Rautha in Denis Villeneuve's cinematic adaptation *Dune: Part Two*.
- Starred as real-life World War II bomber pilot Major Gale "Buck" Cleven in the Apple TV+ miniseries *Masters of the Air*.
- Gained widespread media attention for maintaining a lingering southern cadence in his speaking voice for months after filming completed on *Elvis*.

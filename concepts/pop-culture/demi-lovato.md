## summary

**Demi Lovato** is an American pop vocalist, songwriter, and actor who rose from child stardom on the Disney Channel to become one of the most powerful and influential vocalists of contemporary popular music. Renowned for their dynamic multi-octave range and emotionally vulnerable delivery, they have scored multi-platinum hits including "Skyscraper", "Heart Attack", and "Cool for the Summer". Throughout a career marked by immense commercial triumphs and harrowing personal tribulations, they have become a prominent cultural advocate for mental health awareness, substance abuse recovery, and LGBTQ+ rights.

## you gotta know

- Gained widespread fame starring as the aspiring musician *Mitchie Torres* opposite the *Jonas Brothers* in the 2008 Disney Channel original musical film *Camp Rock*, performing the signature anthem "This Is Me".
- Starred as the title character *Sonny Munroe* on the Disney Channel television sitcom *Sonny with a Chance* from 2009 to 2011, alongside releasing the successful pop-rock debut studio album *Don't Forget*.
- Released the landmark comeback single "Skyscraper" in 2011 from the album *Unbroken*, demonstrating raw vocal power and emotional resilience following an initial stay in residential treatment for bulimia and self-harm.
- Scored major commercial pop radio hits throughout the mid-2010s, including the dramatic ballad "Heart Attack", the confident electropop summer smash "Cool for the Summer", and the soulful title track of the 2015 album *Confident*.
- Earned critical acclaim and their first Grammy Award nomination for Best Pop Vocal Album with *Confident*, followed by the blues-infused 2017 album *Tell Me You Love Me*, which featured the six-times platinum lead single "Sorry Not Sorry".
- Suffered a publicized near-fatal opioid overdose in July 2018, an event courageously addressed in the devastating single "Sober" and documented in the candid 2021 documentary series *Demi Lovato: Dancing with the Devil*.
- Pivoted back to guitar-heavy pop-punk and hard rock roots with the 2022 studio album *Holy Fvck*, and subsequently released *Revamped* in 2023, which reimagined career-defining pop hits as hard rock anthems featuring guitarists *Slash* and *Nita Strauss*.
- Began their entertainment career as a child actor playing *Angela* on the public television children's series *Barney & Friends* in the early 2000s alongside fellow future pop star *Selena Gomez*.

## summary

**Magnezone** is a fictional dual-type *Electric/Steel* creature within the *Pokémon* franchise created by *Satoshi Tajiri* and *Ken Sugimori* for *Nintendo* and *Game Freak* in [[japan]]. First introduced in the 2006 fourth-generation video games *Pokémon Diamond* and *Pearl*, it serves as the ultimate evolutionary stage of the Generation I monster *Magnemite*, evolving from *Magneton* when exposed to unique magnetic fields. The species is celebrated in competitive [[esports]] for its exceptional Special Attack stat and its defining ability *Magnet Pull*, which allows it to trap and eliminate opposing Steel-type combatants with lethal mechanical efficiency.

## you gotta know

- Introduced in Generation IV (*Pokémon Diamond* and *Pearl*), expanding the original evolutionary line of *Magnemite* and *Magneton* after a decade of evolutionary stagnation.
- Evolved originally by leveling up *Magneton* within specific geographic regions with intense magnetic anomalies, such as *Mt. Coronet* in the *Sinnoh* region and *Chargestone Cave* in *Unova*, before later games permitted the straightforward use of a *Thunder Stone*.
- Defined in tournament [[esports]] circles by its ability *Magnet Pull*, which forbids opposing non-Ghost Steel-type creatures from switching out, establishing it as the premiere "Steel-trapper" in competitive metagames.
- Displays an unmistakable science-fiction visual motif resembling a saucer-shaped unidentified flying object, featuring a large central red ocular receptor, two lateral electromagnets, and an aerial radar dish on its dorsal plate.
- Carries an extraordinary defensive typing of *Electric* and *Steel*, which grants resistance to twelve different attack types and total immunity to Poison, offset primarily by a devastating quadruple vulnerability to Ground-type maneuvers.
- Frequently outfitted in high-level play with items such as *Choice Specs* or *Choice Scarf* alongside moves like *Thunderbolt*, *Flash Cannon*, and *Volt Switch* to swiftly dismantle defensive pillars like *Skarmory*, *Corviknight*, and *Ferrothorn*.
- According to in-game lore and fictional research logs, scientists attempted repeatedly to induce its evolution artificially through lab-generated electromagnetism, but such human experiments routinely failed until regional planetary forces were harnessed.

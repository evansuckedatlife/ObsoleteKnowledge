## summary

**Rogue One: A Star Wars Story** is a 2016 American epic space-opera film directed by *Gareth Edwards* that serves as an immediate narrative prequel to the original 1977 *Star Wars* film. The plot follows a disparate group of Rebel fighters led by *Jyn Erso* on a desperate mission to steal the design schematics for the Galactic Empire's superweapon, the *Death Star*. Celebrated for its darker, militaristic tone and sacrifice-driven narrative, the film demonstrated the viability of standalone anthology films within major cinematic franchises.

## you gotta know

- Depicts the events directly preceding *Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope*, explaining how the Rebellion acquired the plans revealing the *Death Star*'s fatal thermal exhaust port.
- Stars *Felicity Jones* as *Jyn Erso*, whose father *Galen Erso*, played by *Mads Mikkelsen*, intentionally engineered a structural flaw into the superweapon as an act of sabotage.
- Features *Diego Luna* as Rebel intelligence officer *Cassian Andor*, a character whose backstory was subsequently explored in the critically acclaimed streaming television series *Andor*.
- Includes the comedic and deadpan reprogrammed Imperial security droid *K-2SO*, brought to life via motion-capture performance by *Alan Tudyk*.
- Culminates in a massive, multi-tiered ground and space battle on the tropical citadel planet of *Scarif*, resulting in the deaths of all primary squad members.
- Concludes with a widely celebrated horror-infused action sequence featuring *Darth Vader* slaughtering Rebel soldiers in a dimly lit corridor to retrieve the data tape.
- Employed cutting-edge visual effects to recreate the likenesses of actors from the 1977 original, notably digital recreations of *Peter Cushing* as *Grand Moff Tarkin* and *Carrie Fisher* as *Princess Leia*.

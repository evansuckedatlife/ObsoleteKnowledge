## summary

**Ken Jennings** is an American game show contestant, author, and television host best known for holding the longest winning streak in the history of the syndicated quiz show *Jeopardy!*. Emerging from Washington state in 2004, his legendary seventy-four-game run transformed competitive trivia from a niche hobby into prime-time cultural viewing across the *[[united-states]]*. Following decades as an author, trivia advocate, and tournament champion, he succeeded longtime host *Alex Trebek* as the permanent host of *Jeopardy!*.

## you gotta know

- Set the all-time regular-play *Jeopardy!* record in 2004 by winning seventy-four consecutive games and accumulating over two and a half million dollars before losing on a question about *H&R Block*.
- Clinched the definitive title of trivia supremacy by defeating fellow champions *Brad Rutter* and *James Holzhauer* in the televised 2020 primetime tournament *Jeopardy! The Greatest of All Time*.
- Became the official permanent solo host of syndicated *Jeopardy!* after sharing hosting duties with actress *Mayim Bialik* following the death of beloved host *Alex Trebek*.
- Competed in the landmark 2011 exhibition match against *IBM*'s artificial intelligence computer system *Watson*, famously writing on his final wager the humorous surrender note, *I for one welcome our new computer overlords*.
- Author of bestselling non-fiction books including *Brainiac*, which chronicles American trivia subcultures, *Maphead*, exploring geography enthusiasts, and *Planet Funny*, examining comedic history.
- Appeared as a resident trivia savant nicknamed *The Professor* on the American game show reboot of *The Chase* alongside other legendary quiz veterans.
- Competed in collegiate quiz bowl at *Brigham Young University*, where he captained the team and honed the rapid buzzer technique that powered his historic television career.

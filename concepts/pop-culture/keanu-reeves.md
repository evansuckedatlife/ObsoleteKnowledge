## summary

**Keanu Reeves** is a Canadian actor who became one of the defining screen presences of late-twentieth and early-twenty-first-century popular cinema. Emerging from teen comedies and independent dramas in the late 1980s, he evolved into a premier action star celebrated for his physical commitment and understated charisma. His iconic portrayals across *The Matrix* franchise and the *John Wick* film series cemented his status as a cinematic legend and cultural icon.

## you gotta know

- Starred as Neo (Thomas Anderson), the cybernetic messiah known as "The One," in the Wachowskis' groundbreaking science-fiction classic *The Matrix* (1999) and its subsequent sequels.
- Reinvigorated the modern gun-fu action subgenre portraying the titular grieving, lethal retired assassin in *John Wick* (2014) and its blockbuster franchise expansions.
- Broke out alongside Alex Winter in the time-travel comedy *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure* (1989), playing the lovable slacker musician Ted "Theodore" Logan.
- Established his action credentials alongside Patrick Swayze in Kathryn Bigelow's cult surf-heist thriller *Point Break* (1991), portraying undercover FBI agent Johnny Utah.
- Anchored Jan de Bont's pulse-pounding action hit *Speed* (1994) opposite Sandra Bullock, playing LAPD SWAT officer Jack Traven aboard a bomb-rigged transit bus.
- Portrayed the DC comic book exorcist and occult detective John Constantine in the supernatural thriller *Constantine* (2005).
- Known for his work in independent and dramatic cinema, including Gus Van Sant's *My Own Private Idaho* (1991) and Kenneth Branagh's Shakespeare adaptation *Much Ado About Nothing* (1993).
- Voiced and provided motion capture for the rebellious rocker Johnny Silverhand in the high-profile open-world video game *Cyberpunk 2077* (2020).

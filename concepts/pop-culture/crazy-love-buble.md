## summary

**Crazy Love** is the fourth major-label studio album by Canadian vocalist *Michael Bublé*, released in October 2009 by 143 Records and Reprise Records. Built around a polished synthesis of traditional pop standards, contemporary adult contemporary ballads, and energetic big-band swing, the record cemented Bublé's global commercial standing as the preeminent mid-century revivalist of his generation. The album achieved widespread multi-platinum success across international markets, topping the Billboard 200 chart and earning the Grammy Award for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Album.

## you gotta know

- Released in October 2009, debuting at number one on the US *Billboard* 200 and achieving multi-platinum sales figures across the [[united-states]], Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
- Launched the massive worldwide lead single "Haven't Met You Yet", an upbeat pop-swing original co-written by Bublé that reached the top ten in numerous countries and served as his defining contemporary radio anthem.
- Borrows its album title from its soulful, big-band interpretation of *Van Morrison*'s classic 1970 romantic ballad "Crazy Love", recorded with vocal harmonies provided by the a cappella group *Naturally 7*.
- Produced by a distinguished team of veteran music producers including *David Foster*, *Bob Rock*, and *Humberto Gatica*, who combined lush orchestral arrangements with crisp, modern acoustic pop dynamics.
- Honored at the 53rd Annual Grammy Awards in 2011 by winning the award for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Album, marking Bublé's third career victory in that prestigious category.
- Features vibrant guest appearances, notably a dynamic rhythm-and-blues duet with *Sharon Jones and the Dap-Kings* on a cover of Dinah Washington and Brook Benton's "Baby (You've Got What It Takes)".
- Includes ambitious covers of timeless American standards such as the *Eagles*' rock ballad "Heartache Tonight", *Cry Me a River* featuring a sweeping cinematic orchestral score, and *Georgia on My Mind*.
- Reissued in November 2010 as a deluxe two-disc "Hollywood Edition," which introduced the newly recorded hit pop single "Hollywood" alongside several bonus live tracks recorded during his world arena tour.

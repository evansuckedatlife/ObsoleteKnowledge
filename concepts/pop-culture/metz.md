## summary

**METZ** is a Canadian noise rock and post-punk band formed in Ottawa in 2007 and based in Toronto, composed of guitarist *Alex Edkins*, bassist *Chris Slorach*, and drummer *Hayden Menzies*. Noted for their blistering volume, percussive intensity, and kinetic live performances, the trio revitalized hardcore and noise aesthetics within the twenty-first-century alternative rock landscape. Through acclaimed releases on the storied indie label Sub Pop and multiple nominations for prestigious music honors, they have established themselves as an essential touchstone of modern heavy post-punk.

## you gotta know

- Formed in Ottawa, Ontario, in 2007 before relocating to Toronto, establishing their core three-piece lineup through intense club tours across North America.
- Signed to the legendary independent record label *Sub Pop* in 2012, aligning themselves with the aesthetic heritage of pioneering grunge and underground alternative acts.
- Released their self-titled debut album *METZ* in 2012, earning universal critical praise and a nomination for the prestigious *Polaris Music Prize*.
- Cultivated an uncompromising signature sonic palette based on overdriven bass tones, dissonant guitar feedback, and relentless, mechanical drum patterns.
- Collaborated with legendary recording engineer *Steve Albini* at his Electrical Audio studio in Chicago to capture their abrasive analog sound for the 2017 album *Strange Peace*.
- Explored increasingly nuanced dynamic range, melodic texture, and thematic depth on later studio records such as *Atlas Vending* and *Up On Gravity Hill*.
- Maintained a fierce DIY ethos throughout their international touring career, frequently headlining alternative festivals while sharing bills with heavy metal and indie rock peers.

## summary

**21 Guns** is an alternative rock power ballad recorded by the American band *Green Day*, released in May 2009 as the second single from their eighth studio album, *21st Century Breakdown*. Composed by singer and guitarist *Billie Joe Armstrong*, the track repurposes military funeral ceremony customs as an emotional plea for exhaustion, surrender, and mutual peace in a fractured sociopolitical climate. The composition garnered major international radio acclaim, achieved double-platinum status in the [[united-states]], earned two Grammy nominations, and became a centerpiece of the stage musical adaptation of *American Idiot*.

## you gotta know

- Derives its title from the ceremonial military honor of the twenty-one gun salute, employing the martial tradition to symbolize surrender and laying down weapons.
- Forms a pivotal thematic climax on the concept record *21st Century Breakdown*, which tracks the angst of fictional protagonists *Christian* and *Gloria*.
- Featured an acclaimed promotional music video directed by *Marc Webb*, showing the band performing inside a bullet-ridden room amid floating white debris.
- Selected for the motion picture soundtrack of director *Michael Bay*'s action blockbuster *Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen* in 2009.
- Earned Grammy Award nominations for *Best Rock Song* and *Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocals* at the 52nd Annual Grammy Awards.
- Adapted by composer *Tom Kitt* for the 2010 Broadway musical production of *American Idiot*, featuring cast members *John Gallagher Jr.* and *Rebecca Naomi Jones*.
- Built upon a driving progression that shifts from delicate acoustic strumming to distorted power chords and an anthem-like falsetto vocal delivery.

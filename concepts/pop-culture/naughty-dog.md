## summary

**Naughty Dog**, operating formally as **Naughty Dog LLC**, is a premier American video game developer based in Santa Monica, California, and a flagship first-party studio of *Sony Interactive Entertainment*. Established in 1984 by *Andy Gavin* and *Jason Rubin* under the original name **JAM Software**, the company evolved from bedroom software coders into one of the most commercially successful and critically revered game development houses in history. Celebrated for technical breakthroughs and cinematic narrative depth, the studio created enduring video game franchises including *Crash Bandicoot*, *Jak and Daxter*, *Uncharted*, and *The Last of Us*.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1984 by childhood friends *Andy Gavin* and *Jason Rubin* as **JAM Software** ("Jason and Andy's Magic"), publishing early titles on the Apple II before officially changing the company banner to **Naughty Dog** in 1989.
- Scored their breakthrough commercial phenomenon on the original *PlayStation* with *Crash Bandicoot* (1996), engineering custom memory hacks and vibrant 3D platforming that transformed the titular marsupial into the de facto mascot of Sony's gaming platform.
- Pioneered seamless, loading-screen-free open-world streaming on the *PlayStation 2* with *Jak and Daxter: The Precursor Legacy* (2001) and its darker action-oriented sequels, cementing Sony's acquisition of the studio that same year.
- Revolutionized interactive cinematic storytelling on the *PlayStation 3* with the *Uncharted* series, beginning with *Drake's Fortune* (2007) and peaking with *Uncharted 2: Among Thieves* (2009), introducing swashbuckling adventurer [[nathan-drake]].
- Directed by *Bruce Straley* and *Neil Druckmann* to create the post-apocalyptic narrative masterpiece *The Last of Us* (2013), tracking the cross-country survival journey of *Joel* and the immune adolescent *Ellie* amid a fungal *Cordyceps* outbreak.
- Released the controversial, critically lauded sequel *The Last of Us Part II* (2020), which won more than 300 Game of the Year awards while challenging audience empathy through dual perspectives and visceral depictions of cycles of grief and revenge.
- Employs a unique corporate structure historically lacking formal producers or middle management, relying instead on horizontal peer critique and custom proprietary game engines designed to push Sony hardware to its computational limits.
- Expanded their gaming universes into mainstream prestige entertainment, collaborating on the 2022 *Uncharted* feature film and co-producing the multi-Emmy-winning *HBO* television adaptation of *The Last of Us* led by *Craig Mazin* and *Neil Druckmann*.

## connections

- [[nathan-drake]] — the iconic globetrotting protagonist created by the studio to headline their blockbuster *Uncharted* series.
- [[camera-movement]] — technical and cinematic discipline that the studio revolutionized through virtual camera rigging and dynamic staging during playable action sequences.
- [[film-editing]] — visual pacing and scene composition techniques borrowed from Hollywood cinema to blend in-engine gameplay into seamless narrative experiences.
- [[united-states]] — country where the studio was founded and has operated continuously across Southern California for over four decades.
- [[western-film]] — American cinematic genre whose morality, dusty frontier landscapes, and visual motifs directly influenced both the *Uncharted* series and *The Last of Us*.
- [[esports]] — competitive multiplayer sector that incorporated the studio's competitive online modes from *Uncharted 2* through *The Last of Us: Factions*.

## summary

**The 5th Wave** is a best-selling 2013 post-apocalyptic science fiction novel by American author *Rick Yancey*, launching a celebrated young adult trilogy. The story depicts sixteen-year-old *Cassie Sullivan* navigating a devastated landscape following a systematic, multi-tiered alien invasion orchestrated by shadowy extraterrestrials known as *The Others*. Balancing intense survivalist prose with psychological suspense, the work became a major commercial success, earning widespread praise for revitalizing invasion literature before receiving a 2016 film adaptation in the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- The invasion unfolds across carefully staggered phases: an electromagnetic pulse wipes out technology, tsunamis drown coasts, weaponized avian flu decimates populations, and snipers pick off survivors.
- The titular fifth wave involves alien consciousnesses infiltrating human bodies as covert sleeper agents called *Silencers*, erasing all trust among surviving human refugees.
- Protagonist *Cassie Sullivan*, armed with an *M16* rifle and her younger brother's teddy bear, embarks on a desperate trek across Ohio to rescue her brother *Sammy* from a military installation.
- Introduces *Evan Walker*, a mysterious farm boy who rescues *Cassie* after she is shot, but whose true identity as an infiltrated alien sleeper operative complicates their alliance.
- Reveals that *Camp Haven*, ostensibly a United States military refuge protecting children, is actually a lethal fifth-wave training camp tricking children into executing uninfected survivors.
- The novel was followed by two sequels, *The Infinite Sea* and *The Last Star*, which concluded *Yancey's* overarching apocalyptic saga.
- Adapted into a 2016 feature film directed by *J Blakeson* and starring *Chloë Grace Moretz* as *Cassie Sullivan*.

## summary

**Million Dollar Baby** is a 2004 American sports drama film directed, co-produced, and scored by *Clint Eastwood*, starring *Hilary Swank*, *Eastwood*, and *Morgan Freeman*. Adapted from stories by *F.X. Toole* (the pen name of veteran boxing cutman *Jerry Boyd*), the narrative chronicles an underappreciated trainer who reluctantly mentors an impoverished amateur female boxer toward world championship contention. Celebrated for its somber emotional gravity, austere *film-editing*, and sensitive exploration of assisted suicide, the film won four Academy Awards including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actress, and Best Supporting Actor.

## you gotta know

- Swept major categories at the 77th Academy Awards in 2005, earning Best Picture for director *Clint Eastwood*, Best Actress for *Hilary Swank*, and Best Supporting Actor for *Morgan Freeman*.
- Centers on *Maggie Fitzgerald*, a thirty-one-year-old waitress from rural Missouri who relentlessly lobbies stubborn Los Angeles gym proprietor *Frankie Dunn* to oversee her professional boxing development.
- Features *Frankie Dunn* christening *Maggie* with the Gaelic phrase *Mo Chúisle*, meaning "my pulse" or "my darling," which is stitched in gold across her emerald-green fight robe.
- Shifts unexpectedly from an uplifting sports comeback into an agonizing [[tragedy]] during a welterweight title bout in Las Vegas against the notoriously dirty champion *Billie "The Blue Bear"*, portrayed by actual world champion boxer *Lucia Rijker*.
- Climax pivots on an illegal punch delivered after the round bell, causing *Maggie* to fall awkwardly against an unpadded wooden corner stool and fracture her cervical vertebrae into permanent quadriplegia.
- Explores raw themes of [[existentialism]] and mercy when *Maggie*, facing amputations and despair in a medical rehabilitation facility, asks *Frankie* to disconnect her ventilator and administer a fatal dose of adrenaline.
- Screenplay was adapted by *Paul Haggis* from the short story collection *Rope Burns: Stories from the Corner* published under the pseudonym *F.X. Toole*.
- Co-stars *Morgan Freeman* as *Eddie "Scrap-Iron" Dupris*, an ex-prizefighter blinded in his hundred-and-ninth bout who manages *Frankie*'s gym and supplies the film's mournful voiceover narration.

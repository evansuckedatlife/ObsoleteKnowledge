## summary

**Hogsmeade**, formally known as **Hogsmeade Village**, is the picturesque, all-magical settlement in the Scottish Highlands that serves as a vital weekend respite and cultural center for students of the nearby Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry in modern British fantasy literature. Founded historically by the medieval wizard *Hengist of Woodcroft*, the community is renowned in lore as the only entirely non-Muggle town in the British Isles, bustling with thatched cottages, iconic sweetshops, and underground passages. Its presence across novels, global film franchises, and themed immersive attractions has established it as a foundational touchstone of twenty-first-century fantasy geography and contemporary popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Third-year Hogwarts students and older are granted permission to visit the village on select weekends, provided they possess a signed consent form from a parent or guardian.
- Home to *Honeydukes*, a renowned confectioner famous throughout the wizarding realm for novelty treats like *Chocolate Frogs*, *Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Beans*, and *Sugar Quills*.
- Serves as the site of *The Three Broomsticks*, a bustling inn and tavern run by *Madam Rosmerta* where patrons regularly gather over mugs of butterbeer and gillywater.
- Contains the notoriously haunted *Shrieking Shack*, a derelict house connected via a subterranean tunnel to the *Whomping Willow* on Hogwarts grounds, originally constructed to conceal the monthly werewolf transformations of *Remus Lupin*.
- Anchors *The Hog's Head*, a disreputable, dingy tavern operated by *Aberforth Dumbledore* that secretly served as the clandestine founding venue for the underground student defense organization known as *Dumbledore's Army*.
- Connected to Hogwarts castle by seven secret passageways, several of which were mapped by the *Marauders* and navigated by *Harry Potter* using the *Invisibility Cloak*.
- Historically hosted the bloody *1612 Goblin Rebellion*, whose leaders established an insurgent headquarters inside one of the village's local public houses.

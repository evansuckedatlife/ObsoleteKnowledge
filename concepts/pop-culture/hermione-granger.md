## summary

**Hermione Granger** is one of the primary fictional protagonists of *J. K. Rowling*'s *Harry Potter* fantasy series, first appearing in the 1997 novel *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone*. Born to non-magical Muggle dentists, she is a remarkably gifted witch whose formidable intellect, encyclopedic mastery of spells, and unwavering moral compass make her an indispensable ally to *Harry Potter* and *Ron Weasley*. Portrayed by actress *Emma Watson* in the film adaptations, she stands as one of the most prominent cultural archetypes of female academic prowess and loyalty in modern literature.

## you gotta know

- Sorted into Gryffindor house at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, despite the Sorting Hat seriously considering her for Ravenclaw due to her razor-sharp intellect.
- Utilized a magical Time-Turner during her third school year to attend multiple overlapping academic classes simultaneously, which she later used to save *Sirius Black* and *Buckbeak*.
- Championed non-human magical rights by founding the Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare (S.P.E.W.) to advocate for enslaved house-elves.
- Endured vicious prejudice and blood-purism bigotry from villainous wizards who labeled her with the derogatory slur *"Mudblood"*.
- Served as the organizational mastermind and co-founder of *Dumbledore's Army*, secretly instructing classmates in defensive spells using enchanted Galleon coins.
- Performed a Memory Charm (*Obliviate*) upon her own parents prior to the Second Wizarding War to protect them from Death Eaters, erasing their memories of her existence.
- Destroyed *Helga Hufflepuff's* cursed cup with a basilisk fang inside the Chamber of Secrets during the climactic Battle of Hogwarts.
- Married *Ron Weasley* in adulthood and eventually rose through the Ministry of Magic to become the Minister for Magic.

## connections

- [[england]] — the homeland where she was raised by Muggle parents and attended school in the Scottish Highlands.
- [[william-shakespeare]] — playwright whose romance *The Winter's Tale* provided the classical inspiration for her first name.
- [[ancient-greece]] — ancient culture whose mythology and linguistic traditions provided the roots for many classical spells she mastered.
- [[roman-empire]] — historic civilization whose Latin language forms the incantations of almost every spell Hermione casts.
- [[realism]] — narrative grounding that informs her pragmatic, rule-abiding approach to magical problems.

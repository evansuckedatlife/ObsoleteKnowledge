## summary

**Amy Wong** is a fictional character in the animated science-fiction television sitcom *Futurama*, created by Matt Groening and developed by David X. Cohen. Voiced by Lauren Tom, she is an engineering intern at the Planet Express delivery company and the heiress to the vastly wealthy Western Hemisphere of Mars. Her character blends broad [[slapstick-comedy]] and high-energy romance with sharp satire of youthful hedonism, academic procrastination, and generational wealth.

## you gotta know

- Introduced in the pilot episode of *Futurama* as a clumsy Martian-born undergraduate student and longtime intern at Planet Express under Professor Hubert Farnsworth.
- Voiced throughout the series by actress Lauren Tom, who infused the character with a distinct mix of cheerful slang, Cantonese profanity substitutions, and comedic timing.
- Daughter of Leo and Inez Wong, mega-wealthy cattle ranchers who own the western half of Mars and continuously pressure her to marry and provide grandchildren.
- Frequently involved in over-the-top [[physical-comedy]] and sight gags, regularly tripping, dropping expensive spaceship parts, or causing accidental workplace catastrophes.
- Enters a long-term, loving relationship with Kif Kroker, the long-suffering green amphibious lieutenant of Zapp Brannigan, eventually marrying him and mothering their brood of offspring.
- Holds a PhD in applied physics from Mars University, finally completing her dissertation on interstellar planetary motion despite recurring comic distractions.
- Visual character design deliberately channels Japanese [[anime]] aesthetics and colorful comic archetypes, sporting a distinctive pink sweatsuit, dark pigtails, and exaggerated expressive reactions.

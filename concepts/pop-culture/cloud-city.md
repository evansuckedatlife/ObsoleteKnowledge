## summary

**Cloud City** is an iconic floating metropolis and tibanna gas mining colony hovering within the atmosphere of the gas giant Bespin, created by George Lucas for the film *The Empire Strikes Back*. Introduced in 1980, it serves as the backdrop for several of the most dramatic confrontations in cinematic history, including the betrayal orchestrated by its administrator and the carbonite freezing of a major protagonist. Its stark aesthetic transition from gleaming, utopian architectural corridors to dark industrial bowels represents one of modern cinema's most famous set designs.

## you gotta know

- Introduced in *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980) as a saucer-shaped floating colony suspended high above the gas planet *Bespin*.
- Governed by the charming scoundrel *Lando Calrissian*, who won administration of the mining outpost in a game of sabacc.
- Sustains its lucrative luxury and industrial economy through the extraction, processing, and refining of valuable tibanna gas.
- Serves as the site where *Han Solo* is betrayed by *Lando Calrissian* and frozen in a block of carbonite for collection by the bounty hunter *Boba Fett*.
- Hosts the climactic lightsaber duel where *Darth Vader* severs the right hand of *Luke Skywalker* and delivers the revelation that he is Luke's father.
- Features a striking contrast designed by *Norman Reynolds*, transitioning from elegant, sunlit white corridors to sinister, steam-filled subterranean industrial chambers.
- Staffed and patrolled by distinctive cybernetically enhanced aides such as *Lobot* and blue-clad Bespin Wing Guards.

## summary

**Liam Neeson** (born **William John Neeson**) is an acclaimed Northern Irish actor known for his commanding screen presence, deep gravelly voice, and versatile transition from prestige dramatic biographical roles to late-career international action stardom. He achieved worldwide critical recognition and an Academy Award nomination for his portrayal of industrialist *Oskar Schindler* in *Steven Spielberg's* masterwork *Schindler's List*. Later in life, Neeson redefined his screen legacy as a bankable action hero through the smash-hit *Taken* franchise, establishing a prolific archetype of weathered, vengeful protective fathers.

## you gotta know

- Born in Ballymena, County Antrim, Northern Ireland, he worked as a forklift operator for Guinness and trained as an amateur boxer before joining the Lyric Players' Theatre in Belfast.
- Breakthrough dramatic role came playing the title character *Oskar Schindler* in the 1993 Holocaust drama *Schindler's List*, earning him nominations for the Oscar, BAFTA, and Golden Globe for Best Actor.
- Portrayed Jedi Master *Qui-Gon Jinn*, mentor to *Obi-Wan Kenobi*, in *George Lucas's* 1999 space opera blockbuster *Star Wars: Episode I – The Phantom Menace*.
- Re-invented himself at age fifty-five as an elite action icon starring as former CIA operative *Bryan Mills* in the 2008 thriller *Taken*, immortalizing his iconic telephone monologue: "I have a particular set of skills."
- Starred as Irish revolutionary leader *Michael Collins* in *Neil Jordan's* 1996 historical biopic, winning the prestigious Volpi Cup for Best Actor at the Venice Film Festival.
- Portrayed the legendary villain *Ra's al Ghul* (Henri Ducard) in *Christopher Nolan's* 2005 superhero film *Batman Begins*, serving as mentor and antagonist to Bruce Wayne.
- Provided the majestic, resonant voice of the lion deity *Aslan* throughout *The Chronicles of Narnia* film trilogy.

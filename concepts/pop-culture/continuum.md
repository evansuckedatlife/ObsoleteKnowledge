## summary

**Continuum** is the acclaimed third studio album by American singer-songwriter *John Mayer*, released in September 2006 through Columbia Records. The recording marked a decisive stylistic transformation away from acoustic pop toward traditional blues, Memphis soul, and roots rock, developed in partnership with the *John Mayer Trio*. Honored with the Grammy Award for Best Pop Vocal Album, the record achieved enduring commercial success while establishing Mayer as a serious modern guitar virtuoso. Within television pop culture, the name also identifies the Canadian time-travel police procedural drama created by *Simon Barry* that aired from 2012 to 2015.

## you gotta know

- Features the socially conscious lead single *'Waiting on the World to Change'*, which earned the Grammy Award for Best Male Pop Vocal Performance and addressed generational political disillusionment.
- Recorded alongside legendary session masters bassist *Pino Palladino* and drummer *Steve Jordan*, who co-produced the project following their collaboration on the live blues record *Try!*.
- Anchored by the slow-burning blues ballad *'Gravity'*, which Mayer has repeatedly described as the most vital and foundational composition of his musical life.
- Showcases a faithful blues-rock interpretation of *Jimi Hendrix*'s classic ballad *'Bold as Love'*, displaying intricate chord embellishments and vintage tube amplification.
- Features enduring fan favorites and radio staples such as *'Slow Dancing in a Burning Room'*, *'Belief'*, and the wistful acoustic meditation on family mortality, *'Stop This Train'*.
- Earned a coveted nomination for Album of the Year at the 49th Annual Grammy Awards, competing against works by the *Dixie Chicks* and *Justin Timberlake*.
- Shares its title with the Canadian television series starring *Rachel Nichols* as city protector *Kiera Cameron*, who travels from a corporate-dominated 2077 to contemporary Vancouver while tracking the radical insurgent group *Liber8*.

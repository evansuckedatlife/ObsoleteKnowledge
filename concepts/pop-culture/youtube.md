## summary

**YouTube** is an American online video-sharing and social media platform launched in 2005 that fundamentally revolutionized the distribution, consumption, and creation of global audiovisual media. Founded by former *PayPal* employees *Chad Hurley*, *Steve Chen*, and *Jawed Karim*, the website decentralized broadcast entertainment by allowing ordinary users to upload, view, and monetize their own digital content. As the second-most visited website in the world and an indispensable cultural repository, it has transformed celebrity, citizen journalism, participatory culture, and contemporary media economics.

## you gotta know

- Created in February 2005 in San Mateo, California, and acquired by *Google* in November 2006 for 1.65 billion dollars, quickly developing into the primary global clearinghouse for streaming video.
- Commenced public operations with the historic nineteen-second video titled *Me at the zoo*, uploaded by co-founder *Jawed Karim* on April 23, 2005, showing him standing in front of elephants at the *San Diego Zoo*.
- Launched the transformative *YouTube Partner Program* in 2007, which introduced shared advertisement revenue for creators and effectively invented modern online influencer culture as a viable full-time vocation.
- Pioneered the viral spread of international pop music, exemplified in 2012 when South Korean artist *Psy*'s music video for *Gangnam Style* became the first upload to surpass one billion views, breaking the platform's view counter.
- Fostered enormous creative subcultures surrounding video game streaming and "Let's Play" videos, catapulting creators like *PewDiePie* to global prominence while driving massive player engagement for games like [[minecraft]].
- Faced major legal and industry battles over digital copyright, culminating in the deployment of the automated *Content ID* system in 2007 to identify, track, and monetize copyrighted music and video material.
- Expanded beyond desktop browsing into a pervasive media ecosystem comprising live streaming, mobile applications, subscription television services, and the short-form vertical video format *YouTube Shorts* to compete with *TikTok*.

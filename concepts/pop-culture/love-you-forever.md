## summary

**Love You Forever** is an internationally acclaimed 1986 children's picture book written by Canadian-American author *Robert Munsch* and illustrated by *Sheila McGraw*. Published by *Firefly Books*, the tender narrative chronicles the life of a young boy and the enduring devotion of his mother, anchored by an emotional, cyclical bedtime lullaby. Having sold over thirty million copies worldwide, it stands as a celebrated classic of modern [[american-literature]] and North American family culture.

## you gotta know

- Features the universally recognized lullaby refrain: "I'll love you forever, / I'll like you for always, / As long as I'm living, / My baby you'll be."
- Follows a mother as she cares for her son across distinct developmental milestones, from an infant to a chaotic toddler, an unruly nine-year-old, an eccentric teenager, and finally an adult living across town.
- Reaches its emotional climax when the elderly mother becomes too frail to finish singing her customary song, prompting her grown son to cradle her in his arms and sing: "As long as I'm living, / My mommy you'll be."
- Concludes cyclically with the grown son returning home to hold his newborn baby daughter in the nursery, singing the lullaby to begin the generational bond anew.
- Conceived by *Robert Munsch* as a private song of personal mourning after he and his wife suffered two consecutive stillborn children in 1979 and 1980, before he decided to incorporate the verse into a children's story.
- Rejected by Munsch's regular publisher *Annick Press* because editors found the imagery of a mother climbing into her adult son's bedroom via a ladder bizarre and unsettling, leading to its release with *Firefly Books*.
- Read aloud in an emotional, theatrical performance by character *Joey Tribbiani* during the first birthday party of baby Emma in an episode of the popular television sitcom *Friends*.

## summary

**Eddie Murphy** is a transformative American comedian, actor, and producer whose explosive breakout on *Saturday Night Live* revitalized the late-night sketch show in the early 1980s. Emerging as one of the biggest box-office draws of the decade, he headlined blockbuster film franchises including *Beverly Hills Cop*, *48 Hrs.*, and *Coming to America*. His stand-up specials *Delirious* and *Raw* redefined live comedy, while his later voice work as *Donkey* in the *Shrek* series and his Oscar-nominated turn in *Dreamgirls* showcased his extraordinary vocal and dramatic range.

## you gotta know

- Joined the cast of *Saturday Night Live* in 1980 at just nineteen years old, widely credited by critics and producers with single-handedly saving the show from cancellation after the departure of the original cast.
- Created memorable *Saturday Night Live* characters such as *Mr. Robinson*, *Gumby*, *Buckwheat*, and a sharp recurring impression of *Stevie Wonder*.
- Starred as Detroit police detective *Axel Foley* in the 1984 action-comedy *Beverly Hills Cop*, solidifying his position as a preeminent global movie superstar.
- Released two seminal stand-up concert films, *Delirious* (1983) and *Raw* (1987), the latter of which remains the highest-grossing stand-up comedy concert film of all time.
- Demonstrated exceptional range playing multiple prosthetically disguised characters within a single film in comedies such as *Coming to America* and *The Nutty Professor*.
- Provided the fast-talking voice of *Donkey* throughout the *Shrek* animated franchise and voiced the dragon *Mushu* in Disney's animated feature *Mulan*.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor and won a Golden Globe Award for his dramatic portrayal of soul singer *James "Thunder" Early* in the 2006 musical *Dreamgirls*.
- Received the prestigious Mark Twain Prize for American Humor in 2015 from the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts.

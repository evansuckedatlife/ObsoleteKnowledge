## summary

**Mac DeMarco** (born **Vernor Winfield MacBriare Smith IV**) is a Canadian singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer who emerged as a defining figure of 2010s "slacker rock" and "bedroom pop." Celebrated for his laid-back songwriting, woozy analog guitar tones, and irreverent stage presence, he popularized a vintage, DIY aesthetic that inspired a generation of independent musicians. After recording breakthrough projects in Montreal and [[new-york-city]], he gained an expansive global audience across the [[united-states]] and abroad.

## you gotta know

- Popularized a distinct, modulated guitar sound crafted by running vintage electric guitars through a *Boss VB-2 Vibrato* pedal into analog tape machines.
- Broke into critical and commercial indie stardom with his acclaimed 2012 album *2* and its 2014 follow-up *Salad Days*, which featured signature singles like "Ode to Viceroy," "My Kind of Woman," and "Chamber of Reflection."
- Concluded the final track of his 2015 mini-album *Another One* by reciting his actual home address in Far Rockaway, Queens, [[new-york-city]], inviting listeners to visit him for a cup of coffee.
- Based his most famous track, "Chamber of Reflection," around an infectious, pitch-warped synthesizer loop sampled from *Shigeo Sekito*'s 1975 instrumental composition "The Word II."
- Released the sprawling, 199-track instrumental marathon *One Wayne G* in 2023, named in honor of Canadian ice hockey legend *Wayne Gretzky*.
- Performed under the moniker *Makeout Videotape* in Vancouver and Montreal before adopting his own name upon signing with the independent record label *Captured Tracks*.
- Produced and engineered his early catalog independently in home apartments using an eight-track *Fostex A-8* reel-to-reel recorder and vintage analog synthesizers.

## connections

- [[new-york-city]] — provided the vibrant creative home where he lived, composed his peak studio material, and directly addressed his neighborhood listeners.
- [[united-states]] — the epicenter of his independent record distribution with *Captured Tracks* and the primary territory for his extensive concert tours.
- [[modernism]] — reflected in his ironic detachment, collage-like pastiche of soft-rock genres, and skepticism toward mainstream music industry conventions.
- [[realism]] — embodied in his plainspoken lyrical focus on modest domestic routines, financial anxiety, and interpersonal vulnerability.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — employed throughout his deliberately lo-fi, surreal promotional videos and home-recorded VHS footage.
- [[charlie-chaplin]] — mirrors his self-effacing physical comedy, theatrical absurdity, and gap-toothed clowning persona during live performances.

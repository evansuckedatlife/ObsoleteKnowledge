## summary

The **Arctic Monkeys** are an English rock band formed in High Green, a suburb of Sheffield, in 2002. Fronted by lyricist, lead vocalist, and guitarist *Alex Turner*, alongside guitarist *Jamie Cook*, drummer *Matt Helders*, and bassist *Nick O'Malley*, the quartet emerged as one of the defining indie rock acts of the twenty-first century. Renowned for their sharp observational songwriting, explosive garage rock rhythms, and subsequent evolutions into desert rock and baroque lounge pop, they achieved unprecedented viral acclaim through internet file-sharing before dominating international charts.

## you gotta know

- Their debut studio album, *Whatever People Say I Am, That's What I'm Not* (2006), became the fastest-selling debut album in British music history, driven by explosive singles like *I Bet You Look Good on the Dancefloor* and *When the Sun Goes Down*.
- Initially cultivated a massive grassroots following through fan-distributed demos on the internet and file-sharing networks, becoming one of the first major music groups to achieve global prominence via online word-of-mouth rather than traditional record label marketing.
- Followed their debut with the blistering 2007 album *Favourite Worst Nightmare*, which featured aggressive hits such as *Brianstorm* and the haunting closing ballad *505*.
- Collaborated with *Josh Homme* of *Queens of the Stone Age* on their third album, *Humbug* (2009), recorded partly in the Mojave Desert, signaling a moody shift toward psychedelic and stoner rock textures.
- Reached commercial superstardom in the United States and worldwide with *AM* (2013), an LP infusing hip-hop beats, R&B grooves, and heavy guitar riffs on tracks like *Do I Wanna Know?* and *R U Mine?*.
- Radically reinvented their sonic palette with *Tranquility Base Hotel & Casino* (2018) and *The Car* (2022), pivoting toward piano-driven lounge music, vintage synthesizers, strings, and cinematic crooning.
- Frontman *Alex Turner* also gained widespread critical acclaim for his retro-pop side project *The Last Shadow Puppets*, formed alongside musician *Miles Kane*.

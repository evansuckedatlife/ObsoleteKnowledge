## summary

**Eric Andre** (born *Eric Samuel André*) is an American comedian, actor, television host, and writer acclaimed as the creator and host of the surreal parody series *The Eric Andre Show* on *Adult Swim*. Pioneering an aggressive blend of nihilistic anti-comedy, shocking hidden-camera street pranks, and visceral studio vandalism, he revolutionized alternative television humor by deconstructing the conventional celebrity talk-show format. In addition to his cult-favorite television program, he has distinguished himself as a dynamic live performer, prominent voice actor in major animated features, and creator of the 2021 prank comedy film *Bad Trip*.

## you gotta know

- Created and hosted *The Eric Andre Show*, which debuted on Cartoon Network's late-night programming block *Adult Swim* in 2012, featuring fellow stand-up comic *Hannibal Buress* as his unflappable, deadpan co-host.
- Every episode traditionally opens with Andre executing a violent, maniacal rampage across the set—smashing desks, destroying musical equipment, breaking cameras, and tearing through synthetic drywall—before an instantaneous cut to a calm, seated monologue.
- Celebrity guests invited onto the program were intentionally kept uninformed regarding the bizarre studio conditions, enduring sweltering room temperatures, putrid scents hidden in the floorboards, collapsing chairs, live animals, and unsettling interview lines.
- Starred in, co-wrote, and produced the 2021 hidden-camera narrative film *Bad Trip*, which embedded real-world prank stunts and unsuspecting public reactions within a scripted comedic road trip alongside *Lil Rel Howery* and *Tiffany Haddish*.
- Spawned ubiquitous viral internet memes, most notably the "Who Killed Hannibal?" sketch, wherein Andre repeatedly shoots his co-host on camera before turning straight to the audience to lamentably ask who committed the crime.
- Earned a formal degree in music performance from the *Berklee College of Music* in Boston, graduating in 2005 as a double-bass player before abandoning jazz to pursue stand-up comedy and performance art in [[new-york-city]].
- Built a substantial career as a voice actor for major studio animation, voicing the villainous hyena *Azizi* in *The Lion King* (2019), the personal demon *Luci* in *Disenchantment*, and the quirky inventor *Mark Bowman* in *The Mitchells vs. the Machines*.

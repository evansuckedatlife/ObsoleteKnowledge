## summary

**Kenneth Oppel** is an acclaimed Canadian author best known for his richly imaginative works of young-adult fantasy, speculative fiction, and children's literature. He achieved international prominence with the *Silverwing* saga, an epic anthropomorphic fantasy series centered on a colony of silver-haired bats, as well as the alternate-history steampunk *Airborn* trilogy. His compelling narratives frequently blend historical technological wonders with biological curiosity, ethical science, and coming-of-age peril.

## you gotta know

- Achieved widespread international fame with *Silverwing* (1997), following the young bat Shade on a dangerous seasonal migration across North America, which launched a successful multi-book series and an animated television adaptation.
- Won the Governor General's Literary Award for Children's Literature and the Michael L. Printz Honor for *Airborn* (2004), an alternate-history adventure set aboard luxury transoceanic zeppelins.
- Continued the airborne world of protagonist Matt Cruse and heiress Kate de Vries in bestselling sequels *SkyBreaker* (2005) and *Starclimber* (2008).
- Penned the gothic horror prequel series *The Apprenticeship of Victor Frankenstein*, beginning with *This Dark Endeavor* (2011) and *Such Wicked Intent* (2012), exploring the youthful descent of Mary Shelley's iconic character.
- Authored the *Overthrow* trilogy (*Bloom*, *Hatch*, *Thrive*), a post-apocalyptic science fiction narrative concerning alien botanical spores that terrorize Earth.
- Published *Half Brother* (2010), an acclaimed realistic novel exploring the ethical boundaries of animal language experiments and family dynamics with a chimpanzee.
- Penned *The Nest* (2015), a haunting psychological novella exploring parental dread, chronic illness, and disturbing insectoid bargains, illustrated by Jon Klassen.

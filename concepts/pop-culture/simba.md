## summary

**Simba** is a fictional anthropomorphic lion who serves as the central protagonist of Disney's 1994 animated feature *The Lion King*. Created by screenwriters Irene Mecchi, Jonathan Roberts, and Linda Woolverton, the character navigates guilt, exile, and royal duty across the African Pride Lands. Culturally enduring and heavily modeled on Prince Hamlet from *[[william-shakespeare]]*'s dramatic canon, the character anchors one of the highest-grossing animated properties and stage adaptations in global entertainment history.

## you gotta know

- Protagonist of the 1994 animated musical feature *The Lion King*, introduced as the heir apparent to King *Mufasa* of Pride Rock before being driven into self-imposed exile.
- Tricked by his villainous uncle *Scar* into believing he caused *Mufasa*'s death during a wildebeest stampede gorgeously animated with early computer assistance.
- Befriended during his wilderness exile by the meerkat *Timon* and warthog *Pumbaa*, adopting their carefree philosophy encapsulated in the Swahili phrase *Hakuna Matata*.
- Encouraged to confront his ancestral destiny by the mandrill shaman *Rafiki* and the ghostly apparition of his father, reflecting classic Shakespearean conventions.
- Voiced across various life stages by actors Jonathan Taylor Thomas (singing by Jason Weaver) as a cub, and Matthew Broderick (singing by Joseph Williams) as an adult.
- Overthrows *Scar* in a climactic duel atop burning Pride Rock, restoring ecological and social equilibrium to his kingdom through the *Circle of Life*.
- Adapted directly into Julie Taymor's groundbreaking 1997 Broadway stage musical, which pioneered transformative puppetry, mask craft, and physical theater.
- Re-imagined in Jon Favreau's 2019 photorealistic computer-animated remake, voiced by Donald Glover as an adult and JD McCrary as a cub.

## connections

- [[william-shakespeare]] — direct literary progenitor whose tragic masterpiece *Hamlet* heavily shaped the character's familial betrayal and spiritual confrontation.
- [[tragedy]] — classical dramatic genre informing the prince's grief, unearned culpability, and narrative burden.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — cinematic technique utilized when *Simba* gazes into a reflective pool and perceives his father's visage.
- [[situational-comedy]] — comedic format reflected in the carefree domestic banter between *Simba*, *Timon*, and *Pumbaa*.
- [[united-states]] — home country of Walt Disney Feature Animation where the character was developed and animated.
- [[simu-liu]] — fellow Disney tentpole franchise lead who also portrayed an exiled son confronting an intimidating patriarch.

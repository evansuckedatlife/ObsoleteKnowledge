## summary

***Jaws*** is a 1975 American thriller film directed by *Steven Spielberg*, adapted from *Peter Benchley*'s bestselling 1974 novel about a man-eating great white shark terrorizing a coastal town. Widely recognized as the first modern Hollywood "summer blockbuster," the film established nationwide saturation booking and aggressive television marketing as industry standards. Its masterful suspense, groundbreaking score, and inventive cinematic techniques fundamentally reshaped modern entertainment and popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Steven Spielberg* and starring *Roy Scheider* as police chief *Martin Brody*, *Robert Shaw* as shark hunter *Quint*, and *Richard Dreyfuss* as oceanographer *Matt Hooper*.
- Centers on the fictional resort community of *Amity Island*, where local authorities face a lethal great white shark and resist closing public beaches ahead of the lucrative Fourth of July weekend.
- Plagued by notorious production delays and technical malfunctions with the mechanical animatronic shark, nicknamed *Bruce* by the crew.
- Spielberg turned mechanical failures into an artistic triumph by keeping the creature largely offscreen, building dread through point-of-view underwater tracking shots and barrel floats.
- Features an iconic, two-note alternating score composed by *John Williams*, which earned an Academy Award and became shorthand for impending danger.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Film Editing for *Verna Fields*, whose precise pacing and suspense-building cuts heightened the visceral impact of the predatory attacks.
- Became the highest-grossing film of all time upon release until *Star Wars* surpassed it two years later.

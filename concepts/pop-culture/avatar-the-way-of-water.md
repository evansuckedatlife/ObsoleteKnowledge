## summary

**Avatar: The Way of Water** is an epic science fiction film released in 2022, directed, co-written, and co-produced by *James Cameron* as the long-awaited sequel to his record-breaking 2009 blockbuster *Avatar*. Returning to the lush alien moon of Pandora, the film follows *Jake Sully* and *Neytiri* as they flee human military encroachment to seek refuge among the oceanic reef clan known as the *Metkayina*. Celebrated for groundbreaking underwater performance-capture technology and immersive visual worldbuilding, the film grossed over 2.3 billion dollars worldwide to rank among the highest-earning motion pictures ever made.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *James Cameron*, marking his return to the world of Pandora thirteen years after the release of the original 2009 film.
- Required years of technological development to invent new performance-capture systems capable of accurately recording actors underwater without surface optical distortion.
- Stars *Sam Worthington* as *Jake Sully*, *Zoe Saldaña* as *Neytiri*, and *Sigourney Weaver* playing the adolescent Na'vi character *Kiri*.
- Follows the *Sully* family's relocation from the Pandoran rainforests to the coastal archipelago of the *Metkayina*, led by *Tonowari* and *Ronal*.
- Focuses on the renewal of military operations by the Earth corporation RDA, who deploy recombinant clones with the memories of deceased soldiers, including *Colonel Miles Quaritch*.
- Features deeply emotional subplots involving *Payakan*, an outcast whale-like creature called a *Tulkun*, highlighting ecological themes of anti-whaling and conservation.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects and became the first film released during the post-pandemic era to cross two billion dollars at the global box office.

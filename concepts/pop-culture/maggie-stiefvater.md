## summary

**Maggie Stiefvater** is an American young adult author, artist, and musician, best known for her atmospheric urban fantasy novels and mythology-infused fiction. She achieved international recognition with *The Wolves of Mercy Falls* series and garnered widespread critical acclaim for *The Raven Cycle* tetralogy and *The Scorpio Races*. Recognized for lyrical prose and character-driven worldbuilding, she ranks among the most influential voices in modern twenty-first-century *young adult fiction*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved widespread commercial success with her breakout werewolf trilogy *The Wolves of Mercy Falls*, initiated by the *New York Times* bestseller *Shiver* (2009).
- Penned the celebrated four-book fantasy series *The Raven Cycle* (2012–2015), comprising *The Raven Boys*, *The Dream Thieves*, *Blue Lily, Lily Blue*, and *The King's Raven*, centered on Welsh mythology in Virginia.
- Wrote *The Scorpio Races* (2011), a standalone fantasy inspired by Celtic water horse legends (*each-uisge*) that earned a prestigious *Michael L. Printz Honor*.
- Set much of *The Raven Cycle* in the fictional town of Henrietta, Virginia, following psychic protagonist *Blue Sargent* and a group of elite prep school boys searching for the sleeping Welsh king *Glendower*.
- Multi-talented artist who illustrates cover art, composes musical companion soundtracks on bagpipes and piano, and maintains a passion for restoring vintage rally cars.
- Continued the universe of *The Raven Cycle* through *The Dreamer Trilogy*, focusing extensively on the dream-manifesting character *Ronan Lynch*.
- Authored the graphic novel *Swamp Thing: Twin Branches* for DC Comics alongside artist *Morgan Beem*.

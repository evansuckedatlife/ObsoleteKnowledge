## summary

**King Triton**, also known simply as **Triton**, is the regal sea king and sovereign ruler of the underwater kingdom of *Atlantica* in *Disney*'s 1989 animated feature *The Little Mermaid*. Wielding a magical golden trident that commands the ocean and channels immense destructive energy, he acts as the protective yet authoritarian father to seven mermaid daughters, most notably *Ariel*. Rooted loosely in the oceanic deities of *[[ancient-greece]]*, the character remains an enduring pop-culture figure embodying patriarchal power, fierce paternal devotion, and eventual emotional reconciliation.

## you gotta know

- Commands the seven seas as the absolute monarch of *Atlantica* and wields an enchanted golden trident capable of manipulating ocean currents, projecting blasts of magic, altering anatomy, and controlling maritime weather.
- Introduced to international audiences in the 1989 animated classic *The Little Mermaid*, which launched the Disney Renaissance in the *[[united-states]]*; the character was famously voiced by comedic actor *Kenneth Mars*, whose theatrical baritone defined the role, and later portrayed by *Javier Bardem* in the 2023 live-action adaptation.
- Harbors an intense suspicion and protective hostility toward surface-dwelling humanity, an animosity revealed in prequel media such as *The Little Mermaid: Ariel's Beginning* to stem from the tragic death of his beloved wife, *Queen Athena*, who was struck by a human pirate vessel.
- Triggers the narrative crisis when he discovers his youngest daughter *Ariel*'s hidden grotto of sunken human artifacts; enraged by her rescue of *Prince Eric*, he uses his trident to obliterate her collection, an act of authoritarian temper that drives her directly to the treacherous sea witch *Ursula*.
- Demonstrates selfless paternal redemption by signing *Ursula*'s binding contract to take *Ariel*'s place as a prisoner, willingly surrendering his crown and trident before being magically withered into a helpless polyp in the villain's grotesque aquatic garden.
- Featured prominently in interactive media, most notably as an ally and world leader in the Disney-Square crossover role-playing game *[[kingdom-hearts]]*, where he guards the Keyhole of *Atlantica*, recognizes the cosmic nature of the Keyblade, and sternly warns *Sora* against disturbing the natural order of foreign worlds.
- Serves as an adaptation of ancient mythological archetypes, deriving his lineage and oceanic dominion from the merman son of *Poseidon* in the mythos of *[[ancient-greece]]*, reimagined within modern family animation as a study in stubborn parental authority yielding to unconditional love.
- Father to seven musical daughters whose names all begin with the letter A—*Aquata*, *Andrina*, *Arista*, *Attina*, *Adella*, *Alana*, and *Ariel*—whose court concerts under royal composer *Sebastian* showcase the cultural prestige of his aquatic court.

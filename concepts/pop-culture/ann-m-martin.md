## summary

**Ann M. Martin** is an acclaimed American children's and young-adult fiction author, best known as the creator and primary writer of *The Baby-sitters Club* series. Born in Princeton, New Jersey, in 1955, she began her literary career working in educational publishing before launching a publishing phenomenon that defined middle-grade literature throughout the late twentieth century. Her prolific work has sold hundreds of millions of copies worldwide and earned significant critical praise, including a Newbery Honor for her 2002 novel *A Corner of the Universe*.

## you gotta know

- Created *The Baby-sitters Club* series in 1986, originally slated as a four-book miniseries beginning with *Kristy's Great Idea*.
- Set the beloved series in the fictional suburb of Stoneybrook, Connecticut, centering on an entrepreneurial group of middle-school friends managing a neighborhood care business.
- Personally wrote the first thirty-five novels of *The Baby-sitters Club* before managing a team of collaborative ghostwriters as the franchise expanded past two hundred titles.
- Awarded a prestigious Newbery Honor in 2003 for *A Corner of the Universe*, an evocative period novel exploring mental illness, neurodivergence, and family secrets.
- Authored several other acclaimed stand-alone works and series, including the *Main Street* collection and the canine perspective novel *A Dog's Life: The Autobiography of a Stray*.
- Formed the philanthropic Ann M. Martin Foundation to support animal welfare organizations and promote youth literacy initiatives across the *United States*.
- Graduated from Smith College in Massachusetts, where she studied early childhood education and psychology before moving to *New York City* to enter the editorial trade.

## summary

The **Criterion Collection** is an American home-video distribution company dedicated to publishing important classic and contemporary films from around the world in definitive, restored editions. Founded in 1984 by *Robert Stein*, *Aleen Stein*, and *Joe Medjuck*, the company pioneered crucial technical standards in consumer media, including the letterbox format, supplementary audio commentaries, and exhaustive archival restorations. It remains a gold standard among cinephiles, preserving seminal art-house, foreign, and independent cinema for physical media and streaming audiences.

## you gotta know

- Established in 1984 as a home-video venture initially producing LaserDiscs, launching famously with spine number 1 (*Jean Renoir*'s *The Rules of the Game*) and spine number 2 (*King Kong*).
- Revolutionized home-video presentation by introducing the running audio commentary track on their 1984 LaserDisc release of *King Kong*, recorded by film historian *Ronald Haver*.
- Popularized the letterbox presentation format for wide-screen releases, preserving the director's original cinematic composition and aspect ratio on home television screens.
- Standardized the inclusion of comprehensive archival bonus features, including essays by renowned film critics, restored alternate cuts, deleted scenes, and contemporary filmmaker interviews.
- Transitions through media formats from LaserDisc to DVD in 1998, Blu-ray Disc in 2008, and 4K Ultra HD in 2021, alongside launching *The Criterion Channel* standalone streaming service in 2019.
- Distinctive packaging design features custom cover art and individual spine numbering, turning its physical releases into prestigious collector objects.
- Maintains deep holdings of world cinema luminaries, publishing extensive libraries of *Akira Kurosawa*, *Ingmar Bergman*, *Federico Fellini*, *François Truffaut*, and *Yasujirō Ozu*.

## summary

**Adele**, born **Adele Laurie Blue Adkins**, is an English singer-songwriter internationally celebrated for her powerful mezzo-soprano voice, raw emotional authenticity, and deeply personal torch ballads. Graduating from the BRIT School for Performing Arts & Technology in 2006, she rapidly transformed into one of the 21st century's best-selling musical artists, generating astronomical physical and digital sales during an era of industry-wide streaming transition. Her critically lauded studio albums have earned dozens of major accolades, including multiple Grammy Awards for Album of the Year and an Academy Award.

## you gotta know

- Broke onto the international stage with her 2008 debut album *19*, which featured breakout acoustic soul singles *Hometown Glory* and *Chasing Pavements*, securing her the Grammy Award for Best New Artist.
- Catapulted to unprecedented global superstardom with her 2011 sophomore album *21*, which sold over 31 million copies worldwide and spawned immortal chart-topping heartbreak anthems *Rolling in the Deep* and *Someone Like You*.
- Swept all six of her nominated categories at the 54th Annual Grammy Awards in 2012, tying the record for the most Grammy wins by a female artist in a single evening.
- Composed and performed *Skyfall*, the Oscar-winning, Golden Globe-winning, and Grammy-winning theme song for the 2012 *James Bond* film of the same name.
- Broke modern American single-week album sales records with her 2015 release *25*, selling 3.38 million copies in its debut week in the [[united-states]], spearheaded by the record-shattering lead single *Hello*.
- Released her deeply vulnerable fourth studio album *30* in 2021, chronicling her divorce, maternal anxieties, and fame through the acclaimed global lead single *Easy on Me*.
- Anchored the critically acclaimed, long-running residency *Weekends with Adele* at the Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas, setting records for live vocal performance box-office receipts.

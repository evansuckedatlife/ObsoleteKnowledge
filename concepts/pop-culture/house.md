## summary

**House**, widely distributed and popularized under the title **House, M.D.**, is a celebrated American medical procedural television series created by *David Shore* that ran for eight seasons on the Fox network from 2004 to 2012. The series stars British actor *Hugh Laurie* as the misanthropic, cane-wielding diagnostic prodigy Dr. *Gregory House*, who leads a specialized team of physicians unraveling bizarre medical mysteries at the fictional Princeton-Plainsboro Teaching Hospital in New Jersey. Subverting the sentimental tropes of traditional medical dramas, the program earned widespread critical acclaim, captured five Primetime Emmy Awards, and became the most-watched television show on the planet in 2008.

## you gotta know

- Explicitly conceived as a contemporary medical reimagining of *Arthur Conan Doyle*'s *Sherlock Holmes*, pairing House with his loyal oncologist confidant Dr. *James Wilson*, played by *Robert Sean Leonard*.
- Centers on House's severe addiction to the opioid painkiller Vicodin and his abrasive worldview, both driven by chronic pain from a past muscle infarction in his right thigh.
- Codified ubiquitous pop-culture maxims, most notably House's cynical philosophy that "everybody lies" and the frequent clinical dismissal that "it's never lupus," an ailment diagnosed only once across the series.
- Showcased a career-defining performance by *Hugh Laurie*, whose flawless American accent in his self-taped audition famously deceived director *Bryan Singer* into believing he was an American native.
- Chronicled high-stakes fellowship dynamics featuring an evolving diagnostic team that included *Jennifer Morrison* as Cameron, *Jesse Spencer* as Chase, *Omar Epps* as Foreman, and *Olivia Wilde* as the enigmatic "Thirteen."
- Reached a critical zenith with the harrowing season four finale duology "House's Head" and "Wilson's Heart," which tracked House's struggle to recall clues from a fatal bus accident that claimed Wilson's partner *Amber Volakis*.
- Experienced an abrupt plot development in season five when the sudden suicide of fellow Dr. *Lawrence Kutner* was written into the narrative following actor *Kal Penn*'s departure for the Obama administration.
- Concluded its eight-year broadcast with the series finale "Everybody Dies," in which House fakes his own death to spend Wilson's remaining terminal months traveling across the country.

## summary

**Jimi Hendrix** was an American rock guitarist, singer, and songwriter who revolutionized the sonic possibilities of the electric guitar. Active prominently during the late 1960s counterculture movement in the [[united-states]] and [[england]], he pioneered expressive uses of amplifier feedback, fuzz distortion, and wah-wah pedals. Widely regarded by critics and musicians as one of the most influential instrumentalists in the history of popular music, his performances redefined rock performance and recording studio experimentation.

## you gotta know

- Headlined the legendary 1969 *Woodstock Festival*, delivering a radical, politically charged psychedelic interpretation of *"The Star-Spangled Banner"* that reflected domestic turmoil during the [[cold-war]] era.
- Achieved international commercial stardom in [[england]] alongside bassist *Noel Redding* and drummer *Mitch Mitchell* in the power trio known as *The Jimi Hendrix Experience*.
- Set his Fender Stratocaster electric guitar on fire on stage during the 1967 *Monterey Pop Festival*, an iconic act of theatrical rock showmanship.
- Recorded landmark rock studio albums including *Are You Experienced*, *Axis: Bold as Love*, and the sprawling psychedelic double LP *Electric Ladyland*.
- Famous for playing standard right-handed electric guitars turned upside down and restrung to accommodate his left-handed technique.
- Began his professional career touring on the Black theatrical circuit known as the *Chitlin' Circuit*, backing rhythm and blues legends such as the *Isley Brothers* and *Little Richard*.
- Died tragically in London in September 1970 at age 27 from barbiturate-related asphyxia, joining the mythologized cultural canon of the *27 Club*.

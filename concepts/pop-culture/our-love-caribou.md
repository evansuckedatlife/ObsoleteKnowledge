## summary

**Our Love** is the sixth studio album by Canadian electronic musician, producer, and mathematician *Dan Snaith*, released under his moniker *Caribou* in October 2014 by *Merge Records* and *City Slang*. Composed and engineered in Snaith's home studio in London following years of touring his breakthrough record *Swim*, the album bridges intimate, bittersweet indie-pop songwriting with sleek, club-ready house, UK garage, and contemporary R&B rhythms. Renowned for its warm synthesizer arrangements, emotional vulnerability, and immaculate sound design, the record received a nomination for the 2015 Polaris Music Prize and stands as a masterwork of 2010s electronic music.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the direct successor to Snaith's acclaimed 2010 LP *Swim*, shifting away from fluid, aquatic psychedelia toward warmer, deeply personal meditations on romantic partnership, aging, and fatherhood.
- Anchored by the celebrated opening single "Can't Do Without You", a soaring dance anthem built on a filtered, looping vocal sample that swells into an ecstatic festival crescendo.
- Features guest vocal and collaborative contributions from Canadian synth-pop songwriter *Jessy Lanza* on the minimalist, sensuous electronic ballad "Second Chance".
- Incorporates experimental cello flourishes and brass textures arranged and performed by longtime collaborator and Canadian avant-garde saxophonist *Colin Stetson*.
- Infuses infectious rhythmic elements, syncopated drum programming, and heavy low-end basslines developed during Snaith's parallel underground dancefloor club project, *Daphni*.
- Garnered widespread universal acclaim from major music critics upon release, earning a Best New Music distinction from *Pitchfork* and appearing on numerous 2014 album-of-the-year lists alongside contemporaries like [[ought]].
- Recognized with a nomination for Best Dance/Electronic Album at the 58th Annual Grammy Awards in 2016 and shortlisted for Canada's prestigious 2015 Polaris Music Prize.

## summary

**Electronic Arts**, widely abbreviated as **EA**, is a prominent American video game corporation founded in May 1982 by *Trip Hawkins* and headquartered in Redwood City, California. An early pioneer of the home computer game industry, EA pioneered treated game developers as artistic talents before growing into one of the largest third-party publishers in the world. The company is internationally recognized for its dominant sports simulation brands, expansive action-adventure franchises, and massive multiplayer online services.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1982 by former Apple executive *Trip Hawkins*, who initially envisioned software designers as "software artists" and packaged early games in album-style gatefold sleeves.
- Dominates the global sports video game market through its *EA Sports* label, responsible for blockbuster annual franchises such as *Madden NFL*, *FIFA* (now *EA Sports FC*), and *NHL*.
- Pioneered early PC gaming successes such as *M.U.L.E.*, *Pinball Construction Set*, and *The Bard's Tale*, before expanding aggressively into home console publishing.
- Acquired numerous legendary game development studios, including *Maxis* (creators of *SimCity* and *The Sims*), *BioWare* (creators of *Mass Effect* and *Dragon Age*), and *DICE* (creators of *Battlefield*).
- Operates major titles in the competitive shooter sphere, publishing the *Battlefield* franchise and *Respawn Entertainment*'s battle royale smash hit *Apex Legends*.
- Sparked widespread regulatory debate over digital microtransactions and loot boxes in the late 2010s following the monetization design of *Star Wars Battlefront II*.
- Heavily influenced popular music discovery throughout the 2000s and 2010s by licensing indie rock, hip-hop, and alternative tracks for in-game soundtracks across its sports titles.

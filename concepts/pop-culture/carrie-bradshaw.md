## summary

**Carrie Bradshaw** is the fictional protagonist, narrator, and central perspective of the HBO television series *Sex and the City*, brought to life by actress Sarah Jessica Parker. Originating in a semi-autobiographical newspaper column written by Candace Bushnell, she operates as a Manhattan relationship essayist whose weekly dispatches dissect the romantic and sexual tribulations of modern single women. Celebrated for her eclectic designer wardrobe, stiletto shoe fixation, and candid narrative voice, she remains a definitive pop-culture archetype of turn-of-the-millennium urban glamour.

## you gotta know

- Narrates the serialized storylines of *Sex and the City* through the weekly relationship column she pens for the fictional broadsheet *The New York Star*, structuring episodes around speculative questions about modern romance.
- Sustains a tumultuous, multi-year romantic saga with elusive corporate financier John James Preston, universally referred to by his nickname *Mr. Big*.
- Maintains a tight-knit inner circle of three Manhattan confidantes: the sexually liberated PR mogul Samantha Jones, the idealistic art dealer Charlotte York, and the pragmatic attorney Miranda Hobbes.
- Cultivates an extravagant footwear obsession centered on luxury designer stilettos, most famously showcasing designs by Manolo Blahnik, Christian Louboutin, and Jimmy Choo.
- Enters a celebrated secondary engagement with artisanal furniture craftsman Aidan Shaw, though their romance collapses due to her lingering attachment to her former lover.
- Popularized daring high-low fashion ensembles orchestrated by costume designer Patricia Field, including the iconic vintage tulle tutu worn during the show's opening title sequence.
- Embraces sophisticated metropolitan wardrobe staples, often sporting luxurious *cashmere* cardigans, tailored vintage jackets, and couture evening gowns across Manhattan.
- Earned actress Sarah Jessica Parker two Primetime Emmy Awards and four Golden Globe Awards across six television seasons, two feature films, and the revival series *And Just Like That...*.

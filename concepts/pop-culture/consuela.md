## summary

**Consuela** is a recurring animated character in the American satirical sitcom *Family Guy*, created by *Seth MacFarlane* and voiced by actor *Mike Henry*. Introduced in the sixth season episode "Believe It or Not, Joe's Walking on Air" in 2007, she is depicted as a stubbornly uncooperative, stoic Latina domestic maid. Known for her catchphrase *"No, no, no"*, she became one of the series' most recognizable recurring gag characters while drawing both popularity and critique for her reliance on broad ethnic caricature.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in season six of *Family Guy* in 2007, initially working for the Griffin family before appearing as an independent maid and nanny across the fictional town of Quahog, Rhode Island.
- Voiced by series regular *Mike Henry*, who based her distinctive vocal cadence and speech rhythm on a real-life domestic worker employed by his friend.
- Famous for her signature dialogue pattern of stubbornly refusing simple requests with a flat, drawn-out *"No, no, no..."*, followed by a refusal to leave or clean appropriately.
- Frequently depicted carrying or obsessively hoarding household spray bottles of lemon-scented cleaning products, especially Lemon Pledge, insisting that employers supply them.
- Prominently featured in the parody episode "Road to the Multiverse", where she appears across alternate dimensions, as well as an extended plotline where she temporarily adopts superhero Peter Griffin.
- Often serves as a vehicle for the show's hallmark cutaway gags and absurd non sequiturs rather than driving serialized plot arcs.
- Has drawn critical analysis from media scholars and advocacy groups for embodying reductive, stereotypical representations of immigrant domestic workers in American media.

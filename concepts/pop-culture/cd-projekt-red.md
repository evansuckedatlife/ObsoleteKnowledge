## summary

**CD Projekt Red** (commonly abbreviated as **CDPR**), a video game development studio founded in 2002 in Warsaw, Poland, is the primary development division of the holding company **CD Projekt**. The studio achieved international renown for its ambitious role-playing adaptations of Andrzej Sapkowski's fantasy saga *The Witcher* and the futuristic open-world epic *Cyberpunk 2077*. Celebrated for mature, morally ambiguous storytelling, rich world-building, and intricate quest design, the company became an emblem of Eastern European creative prominence in global media.

## you gotta know

- Achieved monumental critical and commercial success with the 2015 fantasy title *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt*, which chronicles the monster hunter Geralt of Rivia as he searches for his surrogate daughter Ciri across the war-ravaged continent.
- Released the ambitious open-world science-fiction game *Cyberpunk 2077* in 2020, set in the neon-lit megalopolis of Night City and co-starring actor Keanu Reeves as the digitized anarchist rocker Johnny Silverhand.
- Founded by entrepreneurs Marcin Iwiński and Michał Kiciński, who began their business during the 1990s by importing, pirating, and legally translating Western PC software on CD-ROMs for Polish consumers.
- Established a beloved consumer reputation by operating their sister digital storefront GOG.com, championing digital preservation and strictly releasing all studio games without digital rights management copy protection.
- Developed the proprietary REDengine software suite to power titles such as *The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings* and *The Witcher 3* before transitioning to Epic Games' Unreal Engine 5 for upcoming releases.
- Produced two acclaimed expansions for their third Witcher installment: the Faustian narrative *Hearts of Stone* featuring the enigmatic villain Gaunter O'Dimm, and the sunny Mediterranean-inspired *Blood and Wine* set in the duchy of Toussaint.
- Weathered a disastrous, highly publicized technical launch for *Cyberpunk 2077* on older consoles, gradually redeeming the title through extensive system updates, the anime adaptation *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners*, and the acclaimed spy-thriller expansion *Phantom Liberty* starring Idris Elba.

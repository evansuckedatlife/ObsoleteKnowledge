## summary

**Game of Thrones** is an American fantasy drama television series created by David Benioff and D. B. Weiss for HBO, adapting George R. R. Martin's epic fantasy novel series *A Song of Ice and Fire*. Airing from 2011 to 2019 across eight seasons, it chronicles the violent dynastic civil wars among noble houses vying for the Iron Throne of Westeros while an ancient icy threat looms from the far north. The series became an unparalleled global pop-culture phenomenon, acclaimed for its political intrigue and subversive narrative tension before concluding with a deeply polarizing final season.

## you gotta know

- Premiered on HBO in April 2011, establishing an ensemble cast featuring Peter Dinklage as *Tyrion Lannister*, Emilia Clarke as *Daenerys Targaryen*, and Kit Harington as *Jon Snow*.
- Draws extensive historical inspiration from the fifteenth-century English dynastic conflicts known as the Wars of the Roses, pitting equivalents of the House of Lancaster and House of York against each other in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos.
- Achieved unprecedented Emmy Award success, amassing 59 Primetime Emmy Awards across its run, the most for any scripted primetime television drama series in history.
- Famously subverted standard fantasy conventions through shocking, sudden character executions, most notably the beheading of protagonist *Eddard Stark* in the first season and the brutal massacre of the Northern army at the "Red Wedding" in season three.
- Faced immense production challenges and narrative divergence after surpassing the published material in Martin's novel sequence following the conclusion of its fifth season.
- Prompted major debate in television criticism regarding graphic sexual violence, gratuitous nudity coined as "sexposition," and a hurried six-episode eighth season in 2019 that drew substantial viewer backlash.
- Spawned a wider multimedia franchise, including the prequel television adaptation *House of the Dragon*, which centers on the ancestral civil war of House Targaryen.

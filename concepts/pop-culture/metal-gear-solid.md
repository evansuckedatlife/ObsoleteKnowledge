## summary

**Metal Gear Solid** is a seminal 1998 stealth-action video game developed by *Konami* and directed by *Hideo Kojima* for the *Sony PlayStation*. Set during an alternate post-Cold War era on the remote Alaskan island of *Shadow Moses*, the game follows elite operative *Solid Snake* as he infiltrates a nuclear weapons facility seized by the rogue special forces unit *FOXHOUND*. Widely credited with pioneering the modern stealth genre, it revolutionized interactive storytelling by merging cinematic presentation with complex geopolitical philosophy and subversive fourth-wall-breaking mechanics.

## you gotta know

- Revolutionized video game storytelling by popularizing the tactical espionage action formula, encouraging stealthy evasion and environmental awareness over mindless gunplay.
- Masterminded by Japanese director *Hideo Kojima*, whose deep passion for international cinema infused the game with extended voice-acted cutscenes, dynamic camera angles, and sweeping musical scores.
- Follows legendary operative *Solid Snake*, famously voiced in English by *David Hayter*, who must neutralize his genetic twin *Liquid Snake* and disable the nuclear-equipped walking battle tank *Metal Gear REX*.
- Features celebrated postmodern boss encounters, most notably against the telepathic psychic *Psycho Mantis*, who reads the player's memory card, vibrates the controller with his mind, and requires switching the controller into port two to evade his attacks.
- Reveals the overarching conspiracy of *Les Enfants Terribles*, a clandestine cloning program designed to replicate the genetic profile of the twentieth century's greatest soldier, *Big Boss*.
- Employs the two-way *Codec* radio interface for extensive tactical, scientific, and philosophical consultations with supporting characters like *Colonel Roy Campbell*, *Mei Ling*, and weapons designer *Hal "Otacon" Emmerich*.
- Hinges dramatically upon the genetic bioweapon *FOXDIE*, an engineered retrovirus programmed by virologist *Naomi Hunter* to induce instantaneous cardiac arrest in specific target individuals.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the nation where Konami and Hideo Kojima engineered the franchise, establishing global milestones for cinematic video game design.
- [[cold-war]] — the ideological standoff whose lingering nuclear stockpiles, secret deterrence treaties, and proxy wars define the game's geopolitical narrative.
- [[mega-man]] — fellow landmark Japanese action gaming franchise featuring technologically augmented warriors combating rogue robotic weapon systems.
- [[film-editing]] — the cinematic staging, pacing, and visual continuity principles Kojima adapted to craft immersive real-time cutscenes.
- [[united-states]] — the superpower whose shadowy defense establishment, black-budget black-ops, and nuclear deterrence strategies drive the narrative conflict.
- [[soviet-union]] — the former communist state whose dissolution left rogue mercenaries, advanced weaponry, and orphaned ballistic technologies up for grabs.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — the dominant combat genre whose aggressive combat design was subverted by Kojima's emphasis on non-lethal stealth and camouflage.

## summary

**Due South** is an acclaimed Canadian-American police comedy-drama television series created by *Paul Haggis* that aired across four seasons between 1994 and 1999. The narrative chronicles the adventures of an impossibly polite, morally incorruptible Royal Canadian Mounted Police constable and his deaf lip-reading half-wolf companion who relocate to urban *Chicago* to solve a paternal murder, inadvertently becoming permanent liaisons to the local police department. By juxtaposing frontier tracking ingenuity against cynical big-city policing, the series achieved widespread international syndication, garnered fifteen *Gemini Awards*, and established itself as one of the most successful cultural exports in Canadian broadcasting history.

## you gotta know

- Follows Constable *Benton Fraser* (portrayed by *Paul Gross*), an impeccably mannered Mountie who arrives in *Chicago* dressed in full formal red serge uniform and a Stetson hat on the trail of the conspirators who murdered his father.
- Fraser is permanently accompanied by *Diefenbaker*, a deaf white half-wolf who lip-reads English and Inuktitut, disdains manual sled-dog labor, displays a voracious appetite for sweet pastries, and frequently intervenes to save his master's life.
- Across the first two seasons, Fraser partners with *Detective Raymond Vecchio* (played by *David Marciano*), a cynical, Italian-American urban cop who drives a green 1971 Buick Riviera and serves as a world-weary foil to Fraser's earnest idealism.
- In seasons three and four, following cast departures, Fraser partners with *Detective Stanley Raymond Kowalski* (played by *Callum Keith Rennie*), an undercover officer assigned to assume Vecchio's identity while the real Vecchio conducts an undercover mob operation.
- Created and developed by *Paul Haggis*, who later earned widespread Hollywood prominence by writing *Million Dollar Baby* and writing and directing *Crash*, both of which won Academy Awards for Best Picture.
- Fraser regularly consults the ghost of his murdered father, *Bob Fraser* (played by legendary Canadian actor *Gordon Pinsent*), who appears wearing a flannel coat to dispense cryptic paternal wisdom and investigate unresolved frontier corruption.
- Renowned for Fraser's hyper-attentive tracking techniques inherited from his Inuit upbringing, which include licking street pavement to identify automotive fluids, sniffing trash to isolate suspects, and measuring wind currents with damp fingers.
- Notable for a soundtrack highlighting prominent contemporary Canadian musical artists such as *Sarah McLachlan*, *The Tragically Hip*, and *Stan Rogers*, culminating in a series finale where the protagonists embark into the Arctic in search of the Northwest Passage.

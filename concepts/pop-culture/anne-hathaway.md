## summary

**Anne Hathaway** is an acclaimed American actress celebrated for her emotional intensity, versatile range, and vocal talent across both Hollywood studio productions and legitimate theater. Rising to international prominence in family comedies during the early 2000s, she quickly transitioned into prestige drama, auteur-driven cinema, and blockbuster genre films. Her career has garnered numerous accolades, including an Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress, establishing her as one of the most prominent screen performers of her generation.

## you gotta know

- Achieved her breakout role playing Mia Thermopolis in the Disney coming-of-age comedy *The Princess Diaries* (2001) opposite Julie Andrews, later reprising the royal character in its 2004 sequel.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for portraying the impoverished factory worker Fantine in Tom Hooper's film adaptation of the musical *Les Misérables* (2012), famously singing "I Dreamed a Dream" live in a single take.
- Starred as the beleaguered junior assistant Andy Sachs opposite Meryl Streep's Miranda Priestly in the acclaimed satirical comedy-drama *The Devil Wears Prada* (2006).
- Earned her first Academy Award nomination for Best Actress portraying Kym Buchman, an estranged woman on furlough from rehab, in Jonathan Demme's naturalistic family drama *Rachel Getting Married* (2008).
- Portrayed the DC Comics character Selina Kyle, also known as Catwoman, in Christopher Nolan's superhero epic *The Dark Knight Rises* (2012), and later reunited with Nolan to play astronaut scientist Dr. Amelia Brand in *Interstellar* (2014).
- Co-hosted the 83rd Academy Awards ceremony in 2011 alongside actor James Franco, an event whose awkward tonal dynamic drew substantial public and media scrutiny.
- Showcased classical stage pedigree in *[[new-york-city]]* by starring as Viola in a 2009 Shakespeare in the Park production of *Twelfth Night* by *[[william-shakespeare]]*, sharing a name with the historic English playwright's wife.
- Demonstrated early dramatic depth as rodeo queen Lureen Newsome Twist in Ang Lee's landmark neo-western romance *Brokeback Mountain* (2005) alongside Heath Ledger and Jake Gyllenhaal.

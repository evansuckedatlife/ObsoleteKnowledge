## summary

**Twitter**, rebranded commercially as **X**, is an influential American microblogging and social networking service created in 2006 by *Jack Dorsey*, *Noah Glass*, *Biz Stone*, and *Evan Williams*. Originally characterized by a strict 140-character limitation on posts known as tweets, the platform revolutionized global public discourse, journalism, and digital communication by enabling rapid real-time commentary. It quickly emerged as a central public square for breaking news, grassroots political organizing, celebrity announcements, and viral internet culture.

## you gotta know

- Created in San Francisco in March 2006 as an SMS-based dispatch utility, sending its inaugural message when co-founder *Jack Dorsey* posted "just setting up my twttr."
- Popularized the hashtag metadata tag prefixed with the pound symbol, an organizational indexing innovation first suggested on the service in 2007 by technologist *Chris Messina*.
- Doubled its iconic character limit in November 2017 from 140 characters to 280 characters, before later introducing paid options that allowed long-form essays and multimedia articles.
- Acquired in October 2022 by entrepreneur *Elon Musk* for roughly 44 billion dollars, prompting major restructuring, significant staff reductions, and a formal rebranding to **X** in July 2023.
- Served as an indispensable coordination tool during major international political upheavals, including the 2010s Arab Spring revolutions, the global MeToo movement, and widespread racial justice protests.
- Pioneered the blue checkmark verification badge in 2009 following a lawsuit by baseball manager *Tony La Russa*, a public trust marker that was later transformed into a paid monthly subscription system.
- Enacted permanent suspensions of prominent figures, notably barring incumbent president *Donald Trump* following the January 6 Capitol riot in 2021 before his account was reinstated in late 2022.
- Commemorated server strain during its early hypergrowth through the Fail Whale error illustration designed by *Yiying Lu*, which depicted eight birds lifting a stranded white whale.

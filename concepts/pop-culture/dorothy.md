## summary

**Dorothy**, fully known as **Dorothy Gale**, is the quintessential American fairytale protagonist created by author *L. Frank Baum* in his 1900 children's novel *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*. Transported by a cyclone from rural Kansas to the magical Land of Oz, she embarks on an odyssey along the yellow brick road alongside her dog *Toto* to seek an audience with the Wizard. Her innocence, resourcefulness, and unyielding determination to return home have cemented her status as one of the most celebrated heroines in world literature and cinema.

## you gotta know

- Swept away alongside her little dog *Toto* when a violent cyclone tears through their Kansas farmstead, depositing their farmhouse directly atop the *Wicked Witch of the East*.
- Traveled the yellow brick road wearing magical footwear—silver shoes in the original *L. Frank Baum* novel, famously changed to ruby slippers in the 1939 film adaptation starring *Judy Garland* to exploit three-strip Technicolor.
- Befriended three companion seekers who mirror core human virtues: the *Scarecrow* searching for a brain, the *Tin Woodman* seeking a heart, and the *Cowardly Lion* pleading for courage.
- Liquidated the tyrannical *Wicked Witch of the West* by throwing an ordinary bucket of water over her to extinguish the burning broom of the Scarecrow.
- Unmasked the Great and Powerful Wizard of Oz as an ordinary humbug and charlatan who arrived from Omaha via hot-air balloon.
- Discovered that the power to journey home resided within her all along, returning to Kansas by clicking her magical heels together three times while chanting that there is no place like home.
- Featured across fourteen canonical Oz books penned by *L. Frank Baum*, where she eventually settled permanently in the Emerald City and was granted the royal rank of Princess of Oz by *Princess Ozma*.

## summary

**Like a Prayer** is a landmark 1989 pop-rock song and title track by American singer-songwriter *Madonna*, co-written and co-produced with *Patrick Leonard*. Released as the lead single from her acclaimed fourth studio album of the same name, the track achieved worldwide commercial dominance while revolutionizing the thematic ambition of mainstream dance-pop. Renowned for its audacious synthesis of spiritual gospel ecstasy, romantic devotion, and sensual pop confession, the song remains one of the most culturally significant and debated artistic statements in modern music history.

## you gotta know

- Features an impassioned backing arrangement by the *Andraé Crouch Choir*, whose soaring gospel harmonies elevate the track's dramatic transition from a somber, organ-led prelude into an ecstatic dance-pop anthem driven by syncopated percussion.
- Accompanied by a groundbreaking and controversial music video directed by *Mary Lambert*, depicting Madonna witnessing a racially motivated crime, receiving the stigmata, and dancing joyously amid a nocturnal field of burning crosses.
- Portrays Madonna seeking spiritual sanctuary in a Catholic church where a statue of a Black saint—widely interpreted as *Saint Martin de Porres* or an African-American *Saint Benedict the Moor*—comes alive to share a passionate, sacred kiss.
- Sparked fierce international outrage from conservative religious organizations and the Vatican, prompting *Pope John Paul II* to publicly condemn the video and encourage a Roman Catholic boycott of Madonna's concerts and merchandise.
- Led directly to multinational corporation Pepsi canceling an unprecedented five-million-dollar endorsement contract with Madonna and withdrawing their television advertisement, despite Madonna keeping the multi-million dollar advance.
- Explores complex theological and personal dualities by deliberately fusing traditional Christian liturgical imagery, divine supplication, and romantic desire into a single ecstatic devotional metaphor.
- Showcases a distinctive funk-driven bassline played by bassist *Guy Pratt* alongside subtle, blues-inflected rhythm guitar overdubs contributed by *Prince*, who collaborated extensively with Madonna during the album's production sessions.
- Topped singles charts in more than twenty countries, including the United States Billboard Hot 100, the United Kingdom, and Canada, cementing Madonna's critical reputation for visionary artistic reinvention.

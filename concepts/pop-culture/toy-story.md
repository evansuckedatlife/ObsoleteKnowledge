## summary

**Toy Story** is a groundbreaking 1995 animated feature directed by *John Lasseter* and produced by *Pixar Animation Studios* for *Walt Disney Pictures*. Released in the [[united-states]], it holds historic significance as the first entirely computer-animated feature-length film, revolutionizing the global film industry and establishing 3D digital animation as a dominant cinematic medium. The narrative explores the secret lives of toys through the fraught rivalry and eventual friendship between a traditional pull-string cowboy and a delusional space ranger action figure. Its critical and commercial triumph transformed *Pixar* into an animation powerhouse and earned an *Academy Special Achievement Award* for technical innovation and narrative excellence.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *John Lasseter*, the feature stars *Tom Hanks* as the vintage pull-string cowboy *Sheriff Woody* and *Tim Allen* as the high-tech spaceman action figure *Buzz Lightyear*.
- Celebrated as the first fully computer-animated feature film in history, requiring sophisticated CGI techniques and proprietary rendering technology developed by *Pixar* under executive producer *Steve Jobs*.
- Features the signature opening song *"You've Got a Friend in Me"*, composed and performed by *Randy Newman*, which became an enduring motif across the entire franchise.
- Centers on the crisis of ownership and obsolescence when young boy *Andy Davis* receives *Buzz Lightyear* for his birthday, threatening *Woody's* position as the favorite toy.
- Depicts the terrifying suburban house of *Sid Phillips*, a sadistic neighbor who tortures toys by dismembering them and grafting mismatched pieces together into monstrous mutant creations.
- Concludes with a dynamic chase sequence back to the moving van involving the remote-control car *RC*, a firework rocket strapped to *Buzz*, and a triumphant descent that *Woody* famously terms falling with style.
- Evolved directly out of *Lasseter's* 1988 *Academy Award*-winning short *Tin Toy*, which convinced *Disney* executives that a full-length digital narrative was technically feasible.
- Nearly canceled during production following the infamous *Black Friday Reel* screening in late 1993, where *Disney* executive notes had turned *Woody* into an abrasive, unlikable bully before an emergency rewrite salvaged the project.

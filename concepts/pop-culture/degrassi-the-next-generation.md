## summary

**Degrassi: The Next Generation**, commonly abbreviated as **DTNG** or simply **Degrassi**, is an acclaimed Canadian teen drama series created by *Linda Schuyler* and *Yan Moore* that aired from 2001 to 2015. Serving as the fourth installment of the long-running *Degrassi* franchise, the show follows students at Toronto's fictional Degrassi Community School as they confront complex social, ethical, and personal dilemmas. Acclaimed for its unflinching social [[realism]] and diverse ensemble cast, the series attained worldwide popularity and launched several major international entertainment careers.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in October 2001 on CTV and Noggin's The N, directly bridging past franchise history with a new generation by centering on *Emma Nelson* (*Miriam McDonald*), the teenage daughter of *Spike* from [[degrassi-junior-high]].
- Brought back original cast members including *Snake* (*Stefan Brogren*), *Joey Jeremiah* (*Pat Mastroianni*), and *Spike* (*Amanda Stepto*) as teachers, parents, and mentors to anchor intergenerational continuity.
- Launched the acting career of *Aubrey Graham* (later world-famous recording artist *Drake*), who portrayed charismatic student athlete *Jimmy Brooks* across eight seasons.
- Produced the harrowing 2004 episode *"Time Stands Still"*, in which ostracized student *Rick Murray* brings a handgun to school, fatally shoots student *Jimmy Brooks*—paralyzing him—and is killed in the struggle, marking one of television's most poignant depictions of school violence.
- Retained the signature aesthetic established in the 1980s by outfitting high school students in accessible, contemporary casualwear—including hoodies, sneakers, and vintage [[denim-jacket]] pieces—rather than polished haute couture.
- Tackled pressing social topics with empathetic candor, highlighting storylines regarding LGBTQ+ self-discovery (notably through *Marco Del Rossi*), mental illness, abortion, and eating disorders.
- Shifted in later seasons to a continuous telenovela-style daily broadcast format on TeenNick and MuchMusic, concluding its fourteen-season run before briefly continuing on Netflix as *Degrassi: Next Class*.

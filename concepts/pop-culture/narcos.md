## summary

**Narcos** is an acclaimed crime drama television series created by *Chris Brancato*, *Carlo Bernard*, and *Doug Miro* that debuted on Netflix in 2015. Filmed on location in Colombia, the first two seasons chronicle the meteoric rise and violent downfall of notorious Medellín Cartel kingpin *Pablo Escobar* through the perspective of DEA agent *Steve Murphy*. Lauded for blending authentic archival footage with gritty serialized storytelling, the show expanded into multiple seasons and the spin-off series *Narcos: Mexico*.

## you gotta know

- Chronicled the life and illicit drug empire of Colombian drug lord *Pablo Escobar*, portrayed by Brazilian actor *Wagner Moura*.
- Narrated from the perspective of real-life Drug Enforcement Administration agent *Steve Murphy*, played by actor *Boyd Holbrook*, alongside partner *Javier Peña*, played by *Pedro Pascal*.
- Integrated real historical documentary reels and archival photographs seamlessly alongside dramatized sequences to anchor its historical authenticity.
- Depicted the brutal conflict between Colombian law enforcement, rival cartels like the Cali Cartel, paramilitary groups, and American foreign intervention.
- Utilized predominantly Spanish-language dialogue despite being financed and distributed by an American streaming company for a global audience.
- Transitioned focus in its third season to the sophisticated financial networks and government corruption of the Cali Cartel following Escobar's death.
- Spawned the acclaimed companion spin-off series *Narcos: Mexico*, exploring the genesis of modern Mexican drug trafficking under *Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo*.

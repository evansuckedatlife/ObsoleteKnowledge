## summary

**Counter-Strike** is a team-based tactical first-person shooter developed by Valve, originally released as a *Half-Life* mod in 2000 before becoming a standalone phenomenon. It emphasizes economy management, team coordination, and precise gunplay over individual reflexes, fundamentally shaping competitive esports. Counter-Strike became the longest-running esports title and inspired generations of tactical shooters.

## you gotta know

- Started in 2000 as a grassroots *Half-Life* mod created by players Minh "Gooseman" Le and Jess Cliffe before Valve recognized its potential and commercialized it into a standalone phenomenon.
- The game's core innovation: buy rounds and economy management, where teams must collectively decide weapons and utility each round, creating strategic depth absent from most shooters and rewarding coordination over individual reflexes.
- Two opposing teams — Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists — compete in bomb-planting (Terrorists plant, Counter-Terrorists defend) or hostage-rescue scenarios rather than deathmatch, emphasizing objective-based play.
- Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (CS:GO, released 2012) became the definitive competitive esports title, spawning a multibillion-dollar professional scene with international tournaments and sponsorship ecosystems rivaling traditional sports.
- The game's longevity (dominant for over two decades) comes from its balance between accessibility and skill ceiling — easy to learn basic mechanics, brutally hard to master spray patterns, positioning, and team coordination.
- Iconic maps like *Dust II* became as recognizable to esports fans as football stadiums, defining tactical gameplay through level design and sightlines that force strategic engagement points.
- Valve's support via rank-based matchmaking and the Steam marketplace for weapon skins created both competitive infrastructure and cosmetic incentives, legitimizing esports as a career path.

## summary

**First-person shooter** (FPS) is a video game genre defined by gameplay presented from the player's perspective looking through the protagonist's eyes, with guns or weapons as the primary interaction. Beginning with *Doom* (1993) on PC, the genre expanded to dominate both hardcore PC and console gaming, creating massive franchises and defining esports. FPS games emphasize reflexes, spatial awareness, and weapon mastery in both campaign and multiplayer contexts.

## you gotta know

- *Doom* (1993) pioneered the genre on PC, establishing the first-person viewpoint and fast-paced gunplay as the template, fundamentally changing how games could be played and designed.
- *Quake* (1996) popularized internet-based multiplayer FPS gaming, creating the infrastructure for competitive esports through LAN tournaments and online deathmatch modes.
- Consoles reshaped FPS design: *Halo* (2001) on Xbox proved FPS games could work with controller input, not just mouse and keyboard, introducing controller-friendly mechanics like aim assist and shield systems.
- Call of Duty (2003 onwards) became the dominant mainstream franchise, emphasizing cinematic campaigns with Hollywood production values and accessible multiplayer designed for casual players.
- Competitive FPS design splits between arcade (fast, high respawn rate, emphasis on reflexes) and tactical (economy, teamwork, limited lives per round) styles, appealing to different player psychology.
- The genre's appeal rests on immediacy and agency — the player *is* the protagonist, experiencing action in real time without abstraction or turn-based delays, creating an unmediated sense of presence.
- Modern FPS games integrate progression systems, cosmetics, and battle passes, evolving from pure gameplay focus to live-service ecosystems with seasonal content and cosmetic monetization strategies.

## connections

- [[call-of-duty]] — the dominant mainstream FPS franchise shaping commercial gaming and console culture.
- [[halo]] — pioneering console FPS that redefined genre design for controller-based play.
- [[counter-strike]] — tactical FPS emphasizing economy and teamwork over arcade action.
- [[doom]] — the foundational PC shooter that established the genre's grammar and perspective.
- [[esports]] — FPS games (especially Counter-Strike and Call of Duty) became pillars of competitive gaming infrastructure.
- [[video-game-genre]] — FPS as one of gaming's primary taxonomies alongside RPGs, strategy, and action-adventure.
- [[multiplayer-games]] — FPS design fundamentally shaped team-based and competitive multiplayer gaming.

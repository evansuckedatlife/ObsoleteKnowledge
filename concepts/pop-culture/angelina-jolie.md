## summary

**Angelina Jolie** is an acclaimed American actress, filmmaker, and humanitarian whose versatile screen career spans major Hollywood blockbusters and prestige character studies. Widely recognized as one of the most prominent cultural icons of her generation, she won an Academy Award for her performance in *Girl, Interrupted* before transitioning into blockbuster action roles and directing feature films. Beyond cinema, she has served extensively as a Special Envoy for the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees, advocating globally for displaced populations.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her portrayal of the charismatic sociopath Lisa Rowe in the 1999 psychological drama *Girl, Interrupted*.
- Cemented her status as an international box office action star by portraying video game archaeologist Lara Croft in *Lara Croft: Tomb Raider* and *Lara Croft: Tomb Raider – The Cradle of Life*.
- Directed acclaimed historical and wartime dramas including *In the Land of Blood and Honey*, the World War II biographical survival film *Unbroken*, and the Cambodian drama *First They Killed My Father*.
- Embodied the Disney villainess Maleficent in the blockbuster fantasy reimagining *Maleficent* and its sequel, earning praise for her theatrical and commanding performance.
- Dedicated more than two decades of public service to humanitarian work, serving as a Goodwill Ambassador and later Special Envoy for the UNHCR on over sixty field missions worldwide.
- Advocated extensively for global child protection, women's health, and refugee rights, frequently sharing international humanitarian forums with young leaders such as poet [[amanda-gorman]].
- Earned an Academy Honorary Award (the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award) in 2013 in recognition of her tireless global advocacy on behalf of displaced refugees.

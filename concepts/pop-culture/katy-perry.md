## summary

**Katy Perry**, born **Katheryn Elizabeth Hudson**, is an American pop singer and songwriter who defined the sound and aesthetics of mainstream pop music during the late 2000s and 2010s. Known for her campy, candy-colored visual imagery and hook-laden anthems, she achieved unprecedented commercial success with chart-dominating studio albums. Her historic string of number-one singles solidified her place as one of the best-selling and most recognizable musical figures of the modern era.

## you gotta know

- Broke into mainstream fame with the controversial, provocative 2008 single *I Kissed a Girl*, followed by *Hot n Cold*, from her breakthrough pop-rock album *One of the Boys*.
- Achieved historic chart dominance with her 2010 album *Teenage Dream*, which generated five Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles—*California Gurls*, *Teenage Dream*, *Firework*, *E.T.*, and *Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)*—tying the record set by *Michael Jackson*'s *Bad*.
- Collaborated directly with [[kanye-west]] on the hit single *E.T.*, whose remixed version topped the Billboard charts and featured a high-concept sci-fi music video.
- Originally began her career performing contemporary Christian music, releasing a self-titled gospel album under her birth name *Katy Hudson* in 2001 before reinventing her stage persona.
- Headlined the Super Bowl XLIX halftime show in 2015, delivering a high-energy visual spectacle featuring giant mechanical props and viral internet sensation *Left Shark*.
- Continued her commercial reign with the 2013 album *Prism*, highlighted by the diamond-certified empowering stadium anthems *Roar* and *Dark Horse*.
- Served as a high-profile judge on the revived broadcast of the long-running television vocal competition *American Idol*.

## connections

- [[kanye-west]] — teamed up on the chart-topping 2011 single *E.T.* and shared overlapping radio supremacy throughout the early 2010s.
- [[kelly-clarkson]] — fellow *American Idol* affiliate and pop-rock contemporary who influenced the late-2000s radio climate Perry entered.
- [[justin-bieber]] — generational contemporary with whom she shared dominant positions across worldwide pop radio and social media followings.
- [[united-states]] — her home country, source of her cultural motifs, and primary arena for her chart records and stadium tours.

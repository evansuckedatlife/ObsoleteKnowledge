## summary

**Elvis Presley**, universally crowned as "The King of Rock and Roll," was an American singer and cultural icon whose fusion of country, rhythm and blues, and gospel music transformed global popular culture during the mid-twentieth century. Rising from rural Mississippi to international super-stardom, his charismatic vocal delivery and provocative physical performances galvanized youth culture across the [[united-states]]. His record-breaking 1956 national television broadcasts became defining milestones in entertainment history, breaking post-war social conventions.

## you gotta know

- Recorded his breakthrough early tracks in 1954 at *Sam Phillips's* *Sun Studio* in Memphis, Tennessee, including *That's All Right*.
- Managed by the shrewd and domineering *Colonel Tom Parker*, who orchestrated unprecedented merchandising, television exposure, and exclusive studio contracts.
- Scored his first national number-one single in 1956 with *Heartbreak Hotel*, followed immediately by an astonishing string of chart-topping hits like *Hound Dog* and *Don't Be Cruel*.
- His September 1956 debut on *The Ed Sullivan Show* attracted over sixty million viewers, cementing one of the most legendary television moments in American broadcasting history.
- Caused moral panic among conservative critics due to his suggestive hip gyrations, prompting television producers to notoriously film him exclusively from the waist up.
- Drafted into the United States Army in 1958, serving in [[germany]] at the peak of his career to burnish his mainstream patriotic credentials.
- Staged a triumphant career resurgence with the televised *'68 Comeback Special*, donning black leather to reclaim his raw musical roots after years of formulaic Hollywood musicals.
- Headlined historic, sold-out Las Vegas residencies during the 1970s and performed the 1973 global satellite concert broadcast *Aloha from Hawaii*.

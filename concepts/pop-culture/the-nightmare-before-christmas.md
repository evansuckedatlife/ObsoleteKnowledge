## summary

**The Nightmare Before Christmas** (also known as **Tim Burton's The Nightmare Before Christmas**) is a 1993 American stop-motion animated musical dark fantasy film directed by Henry Selick and produced and conceived by Tim Burton. The film follows *Jack Skellington*, the revered "Pumpkin King" of *Halloween Town*, who stumbles into *Christmas Town* and impulsively decides to take over the festive holiday, resulting in disastrous and humorous consequences. Treasured for its macabre visual aesthetic, revolutionary stop-motion craftsmanship, and unforgettable song suite by Danny Elfman, the work remains an enduring cinematic classic spanning both Halloween and Christmas traditions.

## you gotta know

- Follows the charismatic skeletal leader *Jack Skellington*, who suffers existential ennui with his annual spooky duties before accidentally discovering the portal to *Christmas Town* through the holiday door trees in the Hinterlands.
- Directed by stop-motion pioneer Henry Selick, despite Tim Burton receiving dominant possessory title billing due to Burton having written the original 1982 poem and created the foundational character sketches while an animator at Disney.
- Features composer Danny Elfman providing the iconic singing voice for *Jack Skellington* in addition to composing the entire musical score and penning memorable showstoppers such as *This Is Halloween* and *What's This?*.
- Demanded an arduous production schedule spanning over three years, where twenty individual soundstages were utilized to capture the fluid, precise 24-frames-per-second miniature armature movements of hundreds of distinct puppets.
- Showcases the loving ragdoll *Sally*, voiced by Catherine O'Hara, whose patchwork construction and premonitions of disaster serve as the emotional conscience and grounding foil to *Jack's* obsessive hubris.
- Originally deemed too dark and strange for Disney's family-oriented core banner, prompting the studio to originally release it theatrically under its adult-oriented Touchstone Pictures imprint.

## summary

**Eva Marie Saint** is an esteemed American actress of stage, film, and television celebrated for her delicate emotional transparency, intelligence, and poise. Emerging during the Golden Age of live television before transitioning to cinematic stardom, she won an Academy Award for her stunning screen debut in *On the Waterfront*. Across a distinguished career spanning more than seven decades, she captivated audiences by subverting traditional feminine archetypes, collaborating with legendary filmmakers including *Elia Kazan*, *Alfred Hitchcock*, and *Fred Zinnemann*.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her very first feature film role as *Edie Doyle* in *Elia Kazan's* *On the Waterfront*, providing the gritty dockside drama with its tender emotional center opposite *Marlon Brando*.
- Improvised one of the most famous romantic gestures in cinema history during *On the Waterfront* when she accidentally dropped a white glove, which *Marlon Brando* organically picked up, slipped over his own hand, and stroked, creating an unforgettable moment of naturalistic intimacy.
- Starred as the sophisticated, cool-headed double agent *Eve Kendall* in *Alfred Hitchcock's* quintessential Cold War suspense masterpiece *North by Northwest*, working from a witty and kinetic original script written by [[ernest-lehman]].
- Reimagined completely by *Alfred Hitchcock* for *North by Northwest*, who famously took her to *Bergdorf Goodman* in [[new-york-city]] to personally select her chic wardrobe, intentionally subverting her pure girl-next-door persona into an alluring, ambiguous femme fatale.
- Trained extensively at the celebrated *Actors Studio* under *Lee Strasberg*, mastering the principles of Method acting to bring psychological nuance and unvarnished emotional [[realism]] to mid-century American screen acting.
- Portrayed the loyal, beleaguered wife *Celia Pope* in *Fred Zinnemann's* groundbreaking urban narcotics drama *A Hatful of Rain*, earning a nomination for the BAFTA Film Award for Best Foreign Actress.
- Appeared opposite *Paul Newman* in *Otto Preminger's* sprawling historical epic *Exodus*, playing a widowed nurse swept into the volatile aftermath of World War II and the birth of modern Israel.
- Enjoyed extraordinary career longevity across theater, television, and film, winning a Primetime Emmy Award in 1990 for the miniseries *People Like Us* and later portraying *Martha Kent* in *Superman Returns*.

## connections

- [[new-york-city]] — the creative epicenter where she studied at the Actors Studio, succeeded on Broadway, and filmed key location scenes for *On the Waterfront*.
- [[realism]] — the naturalistic performance aesthetic she championed through her dedicated practice of psychological Method acting.
- [[cold-war]] — the historical era and geopolitical backdrop of espionage tensions explored in her iconic starring vehicle *North by Northwest*.
- [[film-editing]] — the post-production art that assembled her naturalistic takes and iconic improvised moments into cinematic history.

## summary

**La La Land** is an acclaimed 2016 American musical romantic comedy-drama film written and directed by *Damien Chazelle*, starring *Ryan Gosling* and *Emma Stone*. Set in modern-day Los Angeles, the movie follows a passionate jazz pianist and an aspiring actress pursuing their artistic ambitions while falling in love. Renowned for its vibrant Cinemascope visuals, sweeping continuous tracking shots, and bittersweet exploration of romance versus professional sacrifice, the film received fourteen Academy Award nominations and stands as a major modern revival of Hollywood's classic studio musical.

## you gotta know

- Tied the all-time record for the most Academy Award nominations in history with fourteen, matching *All About Eve* and *Titanic*, ultimately winning six Oscars including Best Director.
- Infamously involved in the 89th Academy Awards ceremony envelope blunder, where presenters *Faye Dunaway* and *Warren Beatty* mistakenly read it as Best Picture before *Moonlight* was crowned.
- Opens with the breathtaking musical sequence *Another Day of Sun*, staged in an unbroken long take amidst a gridlocked traffic jam on a Los Angeles highway overpass.
- Features original music composed and orchestrated by *Justin Hurwitz*, highlighted by the melancholic Oscar-winning piano theme and duet *City of Stars*.
- Showcases virtuoso dance duets performed by *Emma Stone* and *Ryan Gosling*, including a twilight routine overlooking the San Fernando Valley in *A Lovely Night*.
- Draws extensive stylistic and choreographic inspiration from golden-age Hollywood musicals, particularly *Singin' in the Rain*, and French New Wave masterworks by *Jacques Demy* like *The Umbrellas of Cherbourg*.
- Concludes with an emotionally devastating, dialogue-free *Epilogue* fantasy ballet that presents an alternate timeline where the lovers stayed together instead of chasing separate careers.

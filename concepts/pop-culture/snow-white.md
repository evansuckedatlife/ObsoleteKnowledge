## summary

**Snow White** is a foundational European folktale published by the Brothers Grimm in their 1812 collection *Kinder- und Hausmärchen* under the German title *Schneewittchen*. The story recounts the plight of an innocent maiden whose murderous, vanity-crazed stepmother seeks her execution after an omniscient magic mirror declares the young princess the fairest in the realm. Adapted in 1937 as Walt Disney's *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*, the narrative became the world's first full-length cel-animated feature, redefining cinematic history and global commercial animation.

## you gotta know

- Published by Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm in [[germany]] in 1812 as Tale 53, synthesized from oral folklore variants recorded across Central Europe and Hesse.
- Centers on the obsessive vanity of the Evil Queen, who daily interrogates a truth-telling magic mirror that famously declares the young maiden "fairest of them all."
- In the original Grimm text, the jealous Queen orders a huntsman to take the child into the forest, kill her, and return with her lungs and liver to be consumed, though the huntsman pities her and substitutes a young boar's organs.
- Depicts the protagonist seeking refuge deep within the woods in a diminutive cottage inhabited by seven mining dwarfs who extract gold and gems from the mountains.
- Features three distinct assassination attempts by the disguised Queen in the Grimm tradition: suffocating the heroine with bodice laces, brushing her hair with a poisoned comb, and finally feeding her a poisoned apple.
- Rescued after falling into a deathlike slumber inside a glass coffin, awakened not by a romantic kiss in the literary tale, but by a servant stumbling while carrying the casket, which dislodges the toxic apple bite from her throat.
- Walt Disney revolutionized modern cinema with the 1937 film adaptation *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*, which defied industry skepticism dubbed "Disney's Folly" to become an extraordinary commercial and critical triumph.
- Disney gave the seven dwarfs distinctive individual personalities—Doc, Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, Bashful, Sneezy, and Dopey—which subsequently supplanted all earlier folkloric traditions in global popular memory.

## connections

- [[germany]] — ancestral birthplace of the Brothers Grimm and setting for the primary written collection of the folktale.
- [[tragedy]] — classical literary form inverted by the fairy tale's descent into betrayal and death followed by comic restoration.
- [[silent-film]] — early cinematic medium that featured several live-action adaptations of the story that directly inspired Walt Disney as a young viewer.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — narrative device demonstrated through the poisoned apple, the transparent glass coffin, and the prophetic magic mirror.
- [[film-editing]] — technical discipline essential to pacing the tension and suspense of the animated feature's climactic woodland storm sequence.
- [[renaissance]] — cultural epoch whose artistic conventions, costume silhouettes, and pictorial iconography heavily influenced theatrical stagings of the fairy tale.

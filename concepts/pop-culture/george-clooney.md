## summary

**George Clooney** is an acclaimed American actor, film director, producer, and political activist who rose to prominence in the mid-1990s as one of Hollywood's leading classical movie stars. First gaining national attention as Dr. Doug Ross on the medical television drama *ER*, he transitioned into an award-winning cinematic career characterized by charismatic heist comedies, political thrillers, and nuanced dramatic performances. He is among the few figures in American cinema to receive Academy Awards in both acting and producing categories.

## you gotta know

- Rose to fame as pediatrician Dr. Doug Ross on the long-running NBC medical drama *ER* from 1994 to 1999, earning multiple Emmy and Golden Globe nominations.
- Anchored Steven Soderbergh's star-studded ensemble caper *Ocean's Eleven* (2001) as master thief Danny Ocean, reprising the role across two commercially successful sequels.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his portrayal of cynical CIA operative Bob Barnes in the geopolitical espionage thriller *Syriana* (2005).
- Directed and co-wrote the historical drama *Good Night, and Good Luck.* (2005), filmed in high-contrast black-and-white to dramatize Edward R. Murrow's journalistic challenge to Senator Joseph McCarthy.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Picture as a producer of the historical hostage thriller *Argo* (2012) alongside Ben Affleck and Grant Heslov.
- Collaborated extensively with the Coen brothers on screwball and satirical comedies, starring in what the filmmakers playfully dubbed their "idiot trilogy": *O Brother, Where Art Thou?*, *Intolerable Cruelty*, and *Burn After Reading*.
- Prominently engaged in international political activism and human rights advocacy, serving as a United Nations Messenger of Peace and co-founding the Not On Our Watch project to address humanitarian crises in Darfur.

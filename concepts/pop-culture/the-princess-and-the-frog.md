## summary

**The Princess and the Frog** is a 2009 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and directed by *John Musker* and *Ron Clements*. Loosely based on the 2002 novel *The Frog Princess* by *E. D. Baker* and the classic *Brothers Grimm* fairy tale, the film is set against the vibrant backdrop of 1920s Jazz Age New Orleans. Introducing *Tiana*, Disney's first African American princess, the production signaled a celebrated temporary return to traditional hand-drawn animation while exploring themes of hard work, personal integrity, and romantic humility.

## you gotta know

- Follows *Tiana*, a hardworking New Orleans waitress voiced by *Anika Noni Rose*, who labors at two exhausting diner jobs to achieve her late father *James's* lifelong dream of opening a fine-dining establishment named "Tiana's Palace".
- Features the arrogant and broke Prince *Naveen* of Maldonia, voiced by *Bruno Campos*, who visits the Crescent City for Mardi Gras and falls victim to the shadowy schemes of voodoo bokor *Dr. Facilier*, transforming Naveen into an amphibian.
- Subverts classic fairy tale tropes when Tiana kisses the enchanted frog prince at debutante *Charlotte La Bouff's* masquerade ball, only to be transformed into a frog herself because she is not an actual royal princess.
- Depicts their survival journey through the treacherous Louisiana bayou, befriending a jazz-trumpet-playing alligator named *Louis* and an eccentric Cajun firefly named *Raymond* (Ray), who pines romantically for the evening star *Evangeline*.
- Sends the protagonists to seek the guidance of *Mama Odie*, a wise 197-year-old voodoo priestess voiced by *Jenifer Lewis*, who advises them through the gospel-style showstopper "Dig a Little Deeper" to understand what they truly need rather than what they want.
- Highlights the sinister villainy of *Dr. Facilier*, known as the "Shadow Man" (voiced by *Keith David*), whose theatrical villain song "Friends on the Other Side" showcases shadowy occult pacts that culminate in his dramatic demise when his spirit debts come due.
- Features an acclaimed musical score composed by *Randy Newman*, weaving authentic Dixieland jazz, blues, gospel, and zydeco across memorable anthems such as "Almost There" and "Down in New Orleans".

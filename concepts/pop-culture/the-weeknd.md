## summary

**The Weeknd**, the stage name of Canadian singer, songwriter, and producer **Abel Tesfaye**, is one of the most commercially successful and critically acclaimed pop icons of the twenty-first century. He initially gained an underground cult following in 2011 by releasing a series of enigmatic, genre-bending mixtapes that revolutionized contemporary alternative R&B with moody, atmospheric production and hedonistic songwriting. He subsequently transitioned into global pop superstardom, defining the sound of modern synth-pop and disco-inflected arena music through record-shattering hits such as *Blinding Lights* and conceptual cinematic album rollouts.

## you gotta know

- Rose to fame anonymously on YouTube in late 2010 before releasing the seminal 2011 mixtape trilogy *House of Balloons*, *Thursday*, and *Echoes of Silence*.
- Pioneered the sound of dark, alternative R&B alongside producers like *Doc McKinney* and *Illangelo*, influencing a decade of moody hip-hop and atmospheric pop.
- Scored his mainstream commercial breakthrough with his 2015 sophomore studio album *Beauty Behind the Madness*, propelled by chart-topping singles *Can't Feel My Face* and *The Hills*.
- Released the 2020 magnum opus *After Hours*, featuring the mega-smash hit *Blinding Lights*, which spent a historic 90 weeks on the Billboard Hot 100 chart and became the top-performing song in Billboard chart history.
- Embodied a bruised, red-jacketed, heavily bandaged alter ego throughout the *After Hours* promotional cycle as a surreal satire of Hollywood celebrity culture and cosmetic surgery obsession.
- Headlined the prestigious Super Bowl LV Halftime Show in February 2021, funding millions of dollars of his own money to produce an elaborate funhouse-style performance in Tampa.
- Announced plans to retire his musical moniker following the completion of an overarching conceptual album trilogy spanning *After Hours*, *Dawn FM*, and *Hurry Up Tomorrow*.

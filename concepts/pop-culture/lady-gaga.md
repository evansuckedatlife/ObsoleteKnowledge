## summary

**Lady Gaga**, born **Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta** (often credited simply as **Stefani Germanotta**), is an American singer, songwriter, and actress celebrated for her vocal prowess, avant-garde visual reinvention, and theatrical pop anthems. Emerging from the Manhattan club scene, she exploded to global superstardom in 2008 with her debut album *The Fame* and its reissue *The Fame Monster*, producing genre-defining electropop hits like *Just Dance*, *Poker Face*, and *Bad Romance*. She subsequently expanded her cultural dominance into critical acclaim in film and traditional pop, winning thirteen Grammy Awards and an Academy Award.

## you gotta know

- Adopted her stage name from the 1984 Queen song *Radio Ga Ga* while developing her musical identity in the Lower East Side performance art circuit.
- Established a signature culture of visual provocation and conceptual fashion, epitomized by her raw flank-steak meat dress worn at the 2010 MTV Video Music Awards.
- Released the landmark 2011 self-empowerment anthem and album *Born This Way*, celebrated widely as an anthem for the LGBTQ+ community and selling over one million copies in its first week.
- Formed an enduring creative partnership with legendary jazz crooner Tony Bennett, producing two collaborative Great American Songbook records, *Cheek to Cheek* and *Love for Sale*.
- Transitioned into elite screen acting by portraying *The Countess* in *American Horror Story: Hotel*, for which she won a Golden Globe Award.
- Starred alongside Bradley Cooper as *Ally Maine* in the 2018 remake of *A Star Is Born*, winning the Academy Award for Best Original Song for the duet *Shallow*.
- Emerged from the same late-2000s New York City alternative music scene alongside fellow future pop vanguard [[lana-del-rey]].

## connections

- [[lana-del-rey]] — contemporary New York singer-songwriter whose introspective vintage ballads served as the stylistic counterweight to Gaga's high-concept dance-pop.
- [[united-states]] — the native cultural and commercial entertainment landscape she radically disrupted throughout the late 2000s and 2010s.
- [[new-york-city]] — her lifelong home, training ground at NYU's Tisch School of the Arts, and the backdrop for her burlesque beginnings.
- [[vaudeville]] — the variety-theater and burlesque tradition strongly embedded in her early stage shows and visual camp.
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic mode informing her dark thematic concepts in *The Fame Monster* and her role in *A Star Is Born*.
- [[modernism]] — art movement emphasizing bold formal experimentation and reinvention reflected in her Haute Couture collaborations.

## summary

**Better Call Saul** is an American television drama series created by *Vince Gilligan* and *Peter Gould* that aired on AMC from 2015 to 2022 across six seasons. Serving as both a prequel and a sequel to *Breaking Bad*, the show chronicles the moral degeneration of earnest public defender *Jimmy McGill* into the flamboyant, morally bankrupt criminal defense attorney known as *Saul Goodman*. Acclaimed for its meticulous character development, inventive cinematography, and deep exploration of institutional and personal ethics, it stands as one of television's greatest critical accomplishments.

## you gotta know

- Stars *Bob Odenkirk* as *Jimmy McGill*, tracing his long, agonizing transition from a striving lawyer seeking brotherly approval into the slick cartel-adjacent advocate *Saul Goodman*.
- Explores Jimmy's fraught, emotionally abusive relationship with his brilliant, mentally ill elder brother *Chuck McGill*, played by *Michael McKean*, who suffers from electromagnetic hypersensitivity.
- Co-stars *Rhea Seehorn* as *Kim Wexler*, Jimmy's close confidante, law partner, and eventual wife, whose own ethical compromises mirror and accelerate Jimmy's descent.
- Runs a parallel crime narrative tracking former Philadelphia police officer *Mike Ehrmantraut*, played by *Jonathan Banks*, as he enters the orbit of drug kingpin *Gustavo Fring*, played by *Giancarlo Esposito*.
- Introduces charismatic antagonist *Lalo Salamanca*, portrayed by *Tony Dalton*, whose cat-and-mouse struggle against Fring destabilizes Albuquerque's criminal underworld.
- Incorporates black-and-white flash-forwards set in Omaha, Nebraska, where Jimmy hides under the alias *Gene Takavic* while managing a shopping mall Cinnabon bakery.
- Earned 53 Primetime Emmy Award nominations across its six-season run, garnering historic critical praise despite never winning a single trophy.

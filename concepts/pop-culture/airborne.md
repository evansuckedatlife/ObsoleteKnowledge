## summary

**Airborne** (often cited as **Airborne (1993 film)**) is an American teen sports comedy-drama film released in 1993, directed by *Rob Bowman* in his feature directorial debut. The film follows *Mitchell Goosen*, a laid-back Southern California surfer and inline skater who is sent to live in snowy Cincinnati, Ohio, where he must navigate high school hockey rivalries and culture shock. Celebrated as a quintessential artifact of 1990s extreme-sports youth culture, the film is revered for its kinetic downhill rollerblading sequences and for providing early breakout screen roles for *Jack Black* and *Seth Green*.

## you gotta know

- Centers on California teenager *Mitchell Goosen* (played by *Shane McDermott*), whose zoologist parents embark on a research sabbatical to Australia, forcing him to spend a semester in cold Cincinnati.
- Features an early comedic performance by *Jack Black* as *Augie*, a loudmouthed, leather-jacket-wearing high schooler who delivers manic, memorable lines.
- Stars *Seth Green* as Mitchell's quirky, insecure cousin *Wiley*, who guides Mitchell through the hostile social hierarchy of Central High School.
- Features the climax race down the treacherous, winding hill road known as "Devil's Backbone", pitting street skaters against a rival prep school crew in a high-stakes downhill inline-skating duel.
- Directed by *Rob Bowman*, who went on to become an acclaimed director and producer on *The X-Files* television series and its 1998 feature film adaptation.
- Features *Chris Conrad* as *Jack*, the antagonistic captain of the school hockey team, and *Brittney Powell* as *Nikki*, Jack's sister and Mitchell's romantic interest.
- Captured the early-1990s rollerblading boom, employing stunt skaters, dynamic camera rigs, and rapid [[film-editing]] to shoot authentic high-speed downhill action.
- Filmed on location in Cincinnati, Ohio, showcasing iconic local landmarks including Riverfront Stadium, the Suspension Bridge, and Mount Adams.

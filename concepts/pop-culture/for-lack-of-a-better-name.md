## summary

**For Lack of a Better Name** is the fourth studio album by Canadian electronic music producer and DJ *deadmau5* (Joel Zimmerman), released in September 2009 by mau5trap and Ultra Records. Combining intricate progressive house, driving electro rhythms, and cinematic ambient breakdowns, the record was crucial in transitioning modern dance music from club undergrounds into global stadium arenas. Centered on the universally celebrated progressive anthem "Strobe", the album solidified Zimmerman's reputation as a master audio engineer and one of the defining electronic artists of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Features the monumental closing track "Strobe", widely regarded by electronic music critics, fellow producers, and listeners as one of the crowning masterpieces of progressive house music.
- Delivered the massive international crossover single "Ghosts 'n' Stuff", featuring powerhouse vocals from *Rob Swire* of the electronic rock band Pendulum, which reached number one on the US Billboard Dance Club Songs chart.
- Constructed in substantial part as a seamless continuous mix, reflecting Zimmerman's background in live DJing and club performances rather than isolated commercial singles.
- Includes popular festival cuts such as "FML", "Moar Ghosts 'n' Stuff", "Hi Friend!" featuring *MC Flipside*, and the glitch-infused techno piece "Bot".
- Marked a major turning point in deadmau5's career, establishing his signature LED-lit "mau5head" helmet as an iconic visual emblem and elevating him to headliner status at major global festivals.
- Followed closely on the critical momentum generated by his breakthrough 2008 commercial album *Random Album Title*, cementing his dominance across both dancefloors and digital charts.
- Became an ubiquitous cultural soundtrack within gaming communities and early internet streaming, frequently appearing in competitive [[esports]] highlight reels and YouTube montages.
- Released during the fertile 2009–2010 era of Canadian musical innovation, sharing contemporary cultural resonance with major domestic crossover releases like Broken Social Scene's [[forgiveness-rock-record]].

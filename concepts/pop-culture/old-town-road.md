## summary

**Old Town Road** is a viral country-trap single released in 2018 by American rapper *Lil Nas X*, which ignited intense cultural debate across the music industry. The song achieved historic commercial success after climbing social video platforms, culminating in a record-shattering nineteen-week reign at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 chart. Fusing traditional country tropes with contemporary Southern trap percussion, the track serves as a landmark case study in Internet-era virality, racial boundary-policing within genre categories, and grassroots independent music distribution in the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Recorded by *Montero Lamar Hill* under the moniker *Lil Nas X* after purchasing an instrumental beat produced by Dutch musician *YoungKio* for thirty dollars on an online marketplace.
- Relies on a prominent, recurring banjo and acoustic guitar loop sampled directly from the ambient track *32 Ghosts IV* by industrial rock band *Nine Inch Nails*, crediting *Trent Reznor* and *Atticus Ross* as co-writers.
- Sparked national controversy in March 2019 when *Billboard* quietly removed the track from its Hot Country Songs chart on the grounds that it did not embrace enough elements of modern country music.
- Rebounded dramatically after veteran country musician *Billy Ray Cyrus* recorded guest vocals for an official remix, providing institutional legitimacy that fueled its ascent to the top of mainstream charts.
- Broke the all-time record for the longest continuous run at number one on the Billboard Hot 100, surpassing the sixteen-week marks previously held by *Mariah Carey* and *Boyz II Men* for *One Sweet Day* and *Luis Fonsi* for *Despacito*.
- Pioneered viral promotion on the short-form video platform *TikTok*, where user-generated memes utilizing the song's beat drop helped establish a novel pipeline for breaking global hit records.
- Won two Grammy Awards in 2020 for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance and Best Music Video, cementing its transformation from an independent novelty into a certified multi-platinum cultural touchstone.

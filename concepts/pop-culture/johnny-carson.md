## summary

**Johnny Carson** (*John William Carson*) was an American television host, comedian, and writer who hosted NBC's *The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson* for thirty years from 1962 to 1992. Known affectionately as the "King of Late Night," he established the definitive template of the American late-night television talk show, featuring a topical monologue, celebrity sofa interviews, and diverse novelty entertainment. His enduring cultural influence shaped comedy, political satire, and public discourse in the *[[united-states]]* across three pivotal decades of the twentieth century.

## you gotta know

- Introduced every evening by his sidekick *Ed McMahon* shouting the iconic drawn-out catchphrase *"Heeeeere's Johnny!"*, backed by *Doc Severinsen* and the NBC Orchestra.
- Famous for recurring comedic sketches and alter egos, most notably *Carnac the Magnificent*, a turbaned psychic who held sealed envelopes to his forehead to divine the answers before opening them to read the questions.
- Possessed the power to launch national stand-up comedy careers with a single gesture, famously inviting promising performers like *Jerry Seinfeld*, *Roseanne Barr*, and *Ellen DeGeneres* to sit on his couch after their sets.
- Began his performing career as a teenage magician known as "The Great Carsoni," honing sleight-of-hand card tricks, deadpan timing, and ventriloquism before transitioning to radio and broadcast television.
- Handled live broadcast mishaps and monologue groans with legendary self-deprecating wit, often executing a signature golf swing or playfully reprimanding his audience when jokes flopped.
- Concluded his historic television tenure on May 22, 1992, in an emotional final broadcast watched by an estimated fifty million viewers, featuring musical guest *Bette Midler*.
- Maintained a notoriously private, reclusive personal life off-camera in Malibu, California, rarely granting interviews or making public appearances following his retirement.

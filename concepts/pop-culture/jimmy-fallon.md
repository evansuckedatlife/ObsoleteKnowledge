## summary

**Jimmy Fallon** is an American television host, comedian, actor, and singer who has hosted NBC's flagship franchise *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon* since 2014. Born in Brooklyn, [[new-york-city]], he first rose to national prominence as an energetic cast member on *Saturday Night Live*. Renowned for his musical parodies, celebrity parlor games, and infectious positivity, he revitalized modern late-night television for digital and viral audiences across the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Named the sixth permanent host of *The Tonight Show* in 2014, succeeding *Jay Leno* and returning the storied NBC broadcast to its historic home in [[new-york-city]].
- Spent six seasons as a prominent cast member on *Saturday Night Live* from 1998 to 2004, co-anchoring the satirical *Weekend Update* desk alongside *Tina Fey*.
- Succeeded *Conan O'Brien* as the host of NBC's *Late Night with Jimmy Fallon* in 2009, enlisting the acclaimed hip-hop band *The Roots* as his house band.
- Revolutionized late-night television formats by emphasizing viral internet clips, musical parodies, celebrity party games, and lip-sync battles that spawned spinoff programs.
- Known during his sketch comedy career for a notorious tendency to break character and laugh during live television sketches.
- Starred in Hollywood comedy feature films during the mid-2000s, including *Taxi* and the romantic baseball comedy *Fever Pitch*.
- Orchestrated a historic 2022 April Fools' Day television stunt in which he and ABC late-night rival [[jimmy-kimmel]] swapped hosting roles on each other's shows.

## summary

**Golden Sun** is a celebrated Japanese role-playing video game developed by Camelot Software Planning and published by Nintendo for the Game Boy Advance in 2001. Set in the fantasy world of Weyard, the game follows Isaac and his companions as they journey across continents to prevent powerful antagonists from lighting four elemental lighthouses and breaking the seal on Alchemy. Celebrated for its lush pseudo-3D visual presentation, intricate puzzle design, and rich musical score by Motoi Sakuraba, it stands as one of the definitive handheld RPGs of its era.

## you gotta know

- Developed by Camelot Software Planning and conceived alongside its direct 2002 sequel *Golden Sun: The Lost Age*, which allowed players to transfer game data and party items via a password or link cable.
- Centers on the magic-wielding protagonist Isaac and his allies Garet, Ivan, and Mia, who practice the art of Psynergy to manipulate environmental elements and cast spells.
- Takes place on the flat, disc-shaped world of Weyard, whose stability is maintained by the balanced elements of Venus (Earth), Mars (Fire), Jupiter (Wind), and Mercury (Water).
- Features an innovative Djinn collection system, where dozens of elemental spirits can be tracked down, assigned to characters to alter character classes, and unleashed to unleash devastating summon spells.
- Requires players to cast Psynergy spells outside of combat to solve intricate overworld and dungeon environmental puzzles, utilizing abilities such as Move, Frost, Growth, and Mind Read.
- Pits the party against the antagonistic Mars Adepts Saturos and Menardi, who kidnap Isaac's companions Jenna and Kraden while attempting to restore Alchemy to Weyard.
- Acclaimed for pushing Game Boy Advance hardware capabilities using pre-rendered 3D battle animations, rotational camera effects during summons, and a lavish orchestral-style soundtrack.

## summary

**Lando Calrissian** is a smooth-talking galactic entrepreneur, cardsharp, and military commander in the *Star Wars* cinematic saga created by *George Lucas* in the [[united-states]]. Debuting in *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980), he serves as the Baron Administrator of the floating tibanna-gas mining facility *Cloud City* before redeeming his forced betrayal of *Han Solo* to become a pivotal general in the Rebel Alliance. Celebrated for his charismatic wit, stylish capes, and pioneering representation as the first major Black character in the franchise, he remains an enduring icon of American space opera.

## you gotta know

- Originated by actor *Billy Dee Williams* in *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980) and *Return of the Jedi* (1983), returning decades later in *The Rise of Skywalker* (2019).
- Portrayed in his younger, debonair smuggling years by *Donald Glover* in the 2018 heist film *Solo: A Star Wars Story*.
- Commanded the *Millennium Falcon* as its original owner before losing the iconic Corellian freighter to his old friend *Han Solo* in a high-stakes game of *sabacc*.
- Governed *Cloud City*, a floating luxury metropolis and gas-mining outpost hovering in the atmosphere of the gas giant planet *Bespin*.
- Forced into an agonizing bargain by *Darth Vader* to surrender *Luke Skywalker* and *Han Solo*, only to turn against the Empire and organize a daring city-wide evacuation.
- Promoted to General in the Rebel Alliance, leading the fighter assault inside the superstructure of the second *Death Star* during the climactic *Battle of Endor*.
- Accompanied throughout his tenure on *Bespin* by his loyal cyborg aide-de-camp *Lobot*, whose neural cybernetic implant managed the city's complex computer communications.

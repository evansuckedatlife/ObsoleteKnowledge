## summary

**Spirited Away** (*Sen to Chihiro no Kamikakushi*) is a 2001 Japanese animated fantasy film written and directed by *Hayao Miyazaki* and produced by *Studio Ghibli*. The story follows ten-year-old Chihiro Ogino, who wanders into an abandoned amusement park that doubles as a supernatural bathhouse for spirits (*kami*) and must labor under the sorceress Yubaba to rescue her transformed parents. Celebrated for its lush hand-drawn animation, deep folkloric symbolism, and emotional depth, it became a global critical triumph and stood as the highest-grossing film in the history of *[[japan]]* for nearly two decades.

## you gotta know

- Directed by legendary filmmaker *Hayao Miyazaki*, who conceived the coming-of-age journey to give real ten-year-old girls a heroine who overcomes challenges without traditional superhero powers.
- Follows the transformation of Chihiro, who is stripped of her identity and renamed *Sen* after signing an exploitative employment contract with bathhouse proprietor *Yubaba*.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 75th Academy Awards in 2003, making it the first hand-drawn and non-English-language film to capture the trophy.
- Features the iconic spirit *No-Face* (*Kaonashi*), a lonely mask-wearing phantom who mirrors the greed and corruption of the bathhouse until calmed by Chihiro's purity.
- Focuses on Chihiro's relationship with *Haku*, a young river spirit able to morph into a serpentine white dragon, who ultimately remembers his lost identity as the Kohaku River.
- Uses potent *[[visual-metaphor]]* to critique environmental pollution and rampant greed, vividly demonstrated when Chihiro cleanses a foul "stink spirit" that reveals itself as an ancient, sludge-covered river god.
- Earned the Golden Bear at the 2002 Berlin International Film Festival, sharing the prestigious top prize with the live-action drama *Bloody Sunday*.

## summary

**Neil Young** is a Canadian singer-songwriter and guitarist whose distinctive high tenor voice, deeply personal lyricism, and ragged, distorted guitar work helped define folk-rock and proto-grunge. Rising to international fame during the late 1960s and 1970s through his solo releases, his work with *Buffalo Springfield*, and his volatile partnership in *Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young*, he established a legendary reputation for artistic independence. Often revered as the *Godfather of Grunge*, his prolific catalog bridges gentle acoustic balladry with raw, feedback-drenched rock and roll.

## you gotta know

- Scored his sole Billboard Hot 100 number-one single with the acoustic ballad *Heart of Gold* from his landmark 1972 album *Harvest*.
- Wrote the haunting protest anthem *Ohio* immediately following the 1970 Kent State shootings, performing it with *Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young*.
- Backed frequently throughout his career by the garage rock band *Crazy Horse*, producing iconic guitar-driven albums including *Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere* and *Rust Never Sleeps*.
- Released the renowned early 1970s *Ditch Trilogy*—comprising *Time Fades Away*, *On the Beach*, and *Tonight's the Night*—deliberately swerving away from mainstream pop commercialism toward raw grief and disillusionment.
- Earned the lasting moniker *Godfather of Grunge* after his heavily distorted guitar style on albums like *Ragged Glory* and *Mirror Ball* deeply influenced 1990s alternative bands such as *Nirvana* and *Pearl Jam*.
- Founded the annual *Bridge School Benefit* concert alongside his former wife *Pegi Young* in 1986 to support children with severe physical and speech impairments.
- Advocated persistently for high-resolution digital audio formats, leading to his development and launch of the *PonoMusic* player and music download store in 2014.

## summary

**Ms. Marvel** is a superhero mantle in Marvel Comics, most prominently associated in modern popular culture with **Kamala Khan**, a Pakistani-American teenager from Jersey City created by Sana Amanat, G. Willow Wilson, and Adrian Alphona in 2013. Kamala debuted as Marvel's first Muslim character to headline her own solo comic book series, gaining widespread critical acclaim for blending classic adolescent superhero tropes with authentic contemporary family and cultural life. Gaining polymorphous shapeshifting abilities following exposure to Terrigen Mist, she adopts the hero persona inspired by her idol, *Carol Danvers*.

## you gotta know

- Created in 2013 by editor Sana Amanat, writer G. Willow Wilson, and artist Adrian Alphona, premiering in *Captain Marvel* #14 before starring in her own solo series in 2014.
- An Inhuman teenager residing in Jersey City who discovers her dormant superpowers after sneaking out of her house and walking into a cloud of Terrigen Mist.
- Her primary signature power is "embiggening"—polymorphic elasticity allowing her to stretch, enlarge, or compress her body, limbs, and fists to superhuman proportions.
- Originally idolized *Carol Danvers*, who had previously fought crime under the title Ms. Marvel before formally ascending to the title of Captain Marvel.
- Balances high-stakes superhero duties with her Pakistani immigrant heritage, religious traditions, school life at Coles Academic High School, and family expectations.
- Served as a founding member and leader of the teenage superhero team the *Champions* alongside *Miles Morales* and *Nova* (*Sam Alexander*).
- Adapted into television and cinema within the Marvel Cinematic Universe, portrayed by Iman Vellani in the 2022 Disney+ streaming series and the 2023 feature film *The Marvels*.

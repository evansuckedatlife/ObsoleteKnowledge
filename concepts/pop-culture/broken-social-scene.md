## summary

**Broken Social Scene** is an acclaimed Canadian indie rock collective formed in Toronto in 2001 by core songwriters *Kevin Drew* and *Brendan Canning*. Renowned for its fluid membership that swelled to over a dozen musicians on stage and in the studio, the group spearheaded the early-2000s resurgence of Canadian indie music. Their lush, orchestral arrangements and communal aesthetic established them as one of the most influential baroque-pop and post-rock ensembles of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Formed initially as an ambient, mostly instrumental duo by *Kevin Drew* and *Brendan Canning*, who released the minimalist debut record *Feel Good Lost* in 2001 before expanding into a maximalist community.
- Broke into international critical acclaim with their landmark 2002 sophomore album *You Forgot It in People*, which won the Juno Award for Alternative Album of the Year and remains a touchstone of 2000s indie rock.
- Functioned as a collaborative supergroup whose roster launched or amplified the careers of solo star *Feist* (*Leslie Feist*), *Emily Haines* and *James Shaw* of *Metric*, and *Amy Millan* and *Evan Cranley* of *Stars*.
- Co-founded the influential independent record label *Arts & Crafts* with music executive *Jeffrey Remedios*, creating an institutional home that supported their expansive roster and shaped North American music distribution.
- Released a self-titled 2005 album featuring the intricate, polyrhythmic fan favorite *"7/4 (Shoreline)"* and the chaotic anthem *"Ibi Dreams of Pavement (A Better Day)"*.
- Enlisted *Tortoise* multi-instrumentalist *John McEntire* to produce their acclaimed 2010 release *Forgiveness Rock Record*, which was recorded in Chicago and Toronto.
- Staged high-energy live performances characterized by interlocking horn arrangements, shared lead vocals, wall-of-sound electric guitars, and multiple drummers playing simultaneously.

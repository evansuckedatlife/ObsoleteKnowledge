## summary

A **Cardinal** is a senior ecclesiastical official and prince of the Catholic Church who serves as a direct counselor to the Bishop of *Rome*. Organized collectively within the **College of Cardinals**, these dignitaries govern the global church during a papal interregnum and assemble in solemn conclave to elect each successive pontiff. Distinguished by their scarlet vestments symbolizing a commitment to defend the faith even unto the shedding of blood, they occupy a prominent position in religious history, global diplomacy, and popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Assembles in a strictly sequestered papal conclave within the Sistine Chapel upon the death or resignation of a pope to cast secret ballots until a two-thirds majority is achieved.
- Signals the outcome of papal voting to the world through smoke emitted from a temporary chimney, where black smoke (*fumata nera*) indicates an inconclusive ballot and white smoke (*fumata bianca*) announces the election of a new pontiff.
- Wears distinct scarlet choir dress that includes a cassock, a silk sash known as a *fascia*, a small skullcap called a *zucchetto*, and a four-cornered peaked cap known as a *biretta*.
- Received the historical *galero*, a broad-brimmed scarlet hat with elaborate hanging tassels that was suspended above a deceased prelate's tomb until it decayed into dust.
- Occupies one of three distinct ministerial orders within the hierarchy, classified in ascending precedence as cardinal deacons, cardinal priests, and cardinal bishops.
- Exercised immense geopolitical influence across early modern Europe, exemplified by figures such as Cardinal Richelieu and Cardinal Mazarin who served as chief ministers directing the foreign policy of *France*.
- Inspired the common name of the North American red songbird and several professional sports franchises due to the striking red coloration of ecclesiastical vestments.
- Faces an age limit instituted by Pope Paul VI in 1970 that revokes the right to cast a vote in papal elections once an individual reaches eighty years of age.

## connections

- [[christianity]] — the worldwide religious tradition whose primary ecclesiastical institution is guided by the sacred college.
- [[rome]] — the historic episcopal see whose governance and elections remain entrusted to these senior churchmen.
- [[italy]] — the nation housing the Vatican enclave and historical territories where clerical princes exerted sovereign rule.
- [[renaissance]] — the cultural era during which wealthy Italian dynasties like the Medicis and Borgias dominated high clerical offices.
- [[france]] — the sovereign monarchy where influential clerical statesmen consolidated centralized royal authority throughout the seventeenth century.
- [[castlevania]] — the gothic video game franchise whose fictional narratives frequently invoke ecclesiastical authority, inquisitions, and scarlet robes.
- [[cashmere]] — the luxury wool fiber historically favored for crafting warm winter vestments, sashes, and ceremonial capes worn by high-ranking clerics.

## summary

**Jean-Marc Vallée** (credited also as **Jean-Marc Vallee**) was an acclaimed Canadian filmmaker, screenwriter, and editor renowned for his intimate, visually raw character studies and sensory directorial style. After breaking out in Quebec with the coming-of-age drama *C.R.A.Z.Y.*, he earned international prominence by helming the Academy Award-winning drama *Dallas Buyers Club* and the wilderness biography *Wild*. He subsequently revolutionized prestige television directing by helming entire limited series for HBO, most notably the cultural phenomenon *Big Little Lies* and the psychological southern gothic mystery *Sharp Objects*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved critical international acclaim with *C.R.A.Z.Y.* (2005), a French-Canadian coming-of-age feature about a young gay man navigating family expectations and faith in 1970s Montreal, driven by an iconic rock soundtrack.
- Directed *Dallas Buyers Club* (2013), an indie biographical drama about AIDS activist *Ron Woodroof* that earned six Academy Award nominations and garnered historic Oscar wins for stars *Matthew McConaughey* and *Jared Leto*.
- Directed the 2014 biographical survival drama *Wild*, which earned Academy Award nominations for lead actress *Reese Witherspoon* and supporting actress *Laura Dern*.
- Revolutionized modern prestige cable television by solo-directing the entire seven-episode first season of HBO's *Big Little Lies* (2017), winning two Primetime Emmy Awards including Outstanding Directing for a Limited Series.
- Directed all eight episodes of HBO's critically lauded psychological miniseries *Sharp Objects* (2018), starring *Amy Adams* as an investigative reporter confronting trauma in her Missouri hometown.
- Pioneered a naturalistic filmmaking philosophy that eliminated artificial lighting rigs, tripods, and formal rehearsals, opting instead for handheld digital cameras and available natural light to maximize actor intimacy.
- Regularly served as his own film editor, frequently utilizing the pseudonym *John Mac McMurphy* to craft associative, memory-driven montages, sonic motifs, and rapid-fire flashbacks.
- Passed away unexpectedly from a cardiac arrhythmia in December 2021 at his cabin outside Quebec City at the age of fifty-eight, prompting widespread mourning across the film industry.

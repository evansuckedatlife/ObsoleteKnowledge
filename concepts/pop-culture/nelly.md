## summary

**Nelly** is an American rapper, singer, and entrepreneur whose infectious, melodic vocal cadence and Midwestern twang brought St. Louis hip-hop into the mainstream during the early 2000s. Emerging as a solo artist following work with his group the *St. Lunatics*, his record-shattering 2000 debut album *Country Grammar* firmly shifted rap's commercial focus beyond the traditional East Coast-West Coast binary. Recognized for his signature face bandage, sports jerseys, and genre-crossing country-rap collaborations, he became one of the best-selling rap artists in music history.

## you gotta know

- Debuted with the Diamond-certified 2000 album *Country Grammar*, propelled by massive hit singles including the title track, *E.I.*, and *Ride wit Me*.
- Released his blockbuster follow-up album *Nellyville* in 2002, featuring the chart-topping party anthem *Hot in Herre*, produced by *The Neptunes*.
- Earned critical acclaim and a Grammy Award for the romantic duet *Dilemma*, featuring *Kelly Rowland* of *Destiny's Child*, which topped the Billboard Hot 100 for ten weeks.
- Famously pioneered the modern fusion of hip-hop and country music with the 2004 remix of *Over and Over* featuring *Tim McGraw*, followed years later by his appearance on *Florida Georgia Line*'s record-breaking remix of *Cruise*.
- Achieved a historic industry feat in September 2004 by releasing two distinct solo albums simultaneously, *Sweat* and *Suit*, which debuted at number one and number two on the Billboard 200 chart.
- Formed the hip-hop collective *St. Lunatics* alongside childhood friends *Ali*, *Murphy Lee*, *Kyjuan*, and *City Spud*, celebrating St. Louis culture and regional slang.
- Established successful fashion and lifestyle business ventures, including the urban streetwear lines *Vokal* and *Apple Bottoms*.

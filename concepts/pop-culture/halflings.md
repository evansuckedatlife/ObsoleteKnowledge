## summary

**Halflings** are a foundational race of diminutive humanoids in modern fantasy literature and tabletop role-playing games, popularized originally as **Hobbits** by British author *J. R. R. Tolkien*. Characterized by their short stature, furry feet, peaceful agricultural lifestyle, and love of domestic comfort, they serve as unconventional protagonists whose unassuming virtue triumphs over cosmic evil. The term halfling was widely adopted across fantasy systems, such as *Dungeons & Dragons*, to avoid trademark conflicts while preserving Tolkien's pastoral ideals.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *J. R. R. Tolkien* in his 1937 novel *The Hobbit*, where they are described as peace-loving **Shire-folk** roughly half the height of an average human.
- Central to the narrative quest of *The Lord of the Rings*, where *Frodo Baggins* and *Samwise Gamgee* bear the One Ring to Mount Doom.
- Renamed generically as halflings in fantasy media like *Dungeons & Dragons* following intellectual property claims by the Tolkien Estate.
- Portrayed with distinct physical traits: barefoot mobility, leathery soles, curly brown hair, exceptional stealth, and a resistance to magical corruption.
- Embody an idealized vision of pastoral, pre-industrial *England*, explicitly contrasted with the smoke and mechanization of the *Industrial Revolution*.
- Commonly adapted in role-playing rulebooks into lightfoot subraces favoring rogue and ranger classes due to high dexterity and luck.
- Replaced the classic martial epic hero with a domestic, peace-oriented protagonist who values good food, friendship, and quiet rural life over military conquest.

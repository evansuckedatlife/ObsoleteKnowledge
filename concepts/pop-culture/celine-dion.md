## summary

**Celine Dion** (often styled with French orthography as **Céline Dion**) is a Canadian vocalist renowned as the "Queen of Power Ballads," celebrated for her extraordinary five-octave vocal range, technical precision, and dramatic belting. Emerging from Charlemagne, Quebec, as a child star in the Francophone market, she attained worldwide fame after winning the 1988 *Eurovision Song Contest* for Switzerland and successfully crossing over into English-language pop. She transformed the economics of live performance through her record-breaking Las Vegas residencies and provided the soundtrack to one of Hollywood's greatest box-office triumphs.

## you gotta know

- Belted the multi-platinum worldwide anthem *"My Heart Will Go On"*, composed by *James Horner* and lyricist *Will Jennings* as the love theme for *James Cameron*'s blockbuster film *Titanic*, which swept the Academy Awards and Grammy Awards.
- Won the 1988 *Eurovision Song Contest* in Dublin representing Switzerland with the French-language entry *"Ne partez pas sans moi"*, winning by a razor-thin single-point margin over the United Kingdom in one of the most famous finishes in contest history.
- Pioneered the modern era of the Las Vegas entertainment residency with *A New Day...* at the custom-built *Colosseum at Caesars Palace*, running from 2003 to 2007 and establishing a lucrative performance model subsequently adopted by major pop artists.
- Guided from age twelve by her manager and future husband *René Angélil*, who famously mortgaged his home to finance her debut studio album *La voix du bon Dieu* before orchestrating her global career.
- Broke into the mainstream Anglophone pop market with her 1990 debut English album *Unison*, followed shortly by her Grammy-winning duet with *Peabo Bryson* on the title track for Disney's animated feature *Beauty and the Beast*.
- Collaborated with legendary composer and rock dramatist *Jim Steinman* on the epic, operatic track *"It's All Coming Back to Me Now"*, an elaborate centerpiece on her 1996 multi-million-selling album *Falling into You*.
- Announced in 2022 her diagnosis with Stiff-Person Syndrome, a rare neurological condition that causes progressive muscle rigidity and severe spasms, culminating in a triumphantly received live performance of *Édith Piaf*'s *"Hymne à l'amour"* from the Eiffel Tower during the 2024 Paris Olympic opening ceremony.

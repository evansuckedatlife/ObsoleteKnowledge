## summary

**Atanarjuat: The Fast Runner** is a 2001 epic drama directed by *Zacharias Kunuk*, celebrated as the first feature-length fiction film written, directed, and acted entirely in the Inuktitut language. Produced by the Isuma filmmaking collective based in Igloolik, Nunavut, the work dramatizes an ancient Inuit oral legend set in the eastern Arctic concerning an evil spirit that unleashes jealousy, deceit, and generational murder upon an isolated community. Renowned as a monumental achievement of Indigenous cinema, it won the Caméra d'Or at the Cannes Film Festival in [[france]] and was voted the greatest Canadian film of all time in a landmark national critics' survey.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Zacharias Kunuk* and written by the Inuit elder *Paul Apak Angilirq*, who gathered and synthesized variations of the ancient legend from community elders before his death during production.
- Features an iconic, harrowing centerpiece sequence in which the naked protagonist *Atanarjuat*, portrayed by *Natar Ungalaaq*, flees across jagged spring sea ice to escape ambushers after the murder of his brother.
- Awarded the prestigious Caméra d'Or for best debut feature at the 2001 Cannes Film Festival, bringing unprecedented global critical acclaim in [[france]] to Arctic storytelling.
- Surpassed the longstanding critical benchmark set by filmmaker [[atom-egoyan]] when the Toronto International Film Festival voted it the single greatest Canadian movie ever made in its 2015 poll.
- Created through *Igloolik Isuma Productions*, an Inuit-led collaborative media company co-founded by Kunuk and cinematographer *Norman Cohn* dedicated to preserving oral heritage.
- Shot on location in the Canadian Arctic utilizing widescreen digital video equipment customized to operate reliably in subzero polar conditions without freezing.
- Concludes by rejecting cyclical revenge in favor of community reconciliation, holding wrongdoers accountable through public confession and customary exile.

- [[tragedy]] — explores how unchecked ambition, marital jealousy, and curses unravel sacred family obligations and bring catastrophe upon an entire clan.

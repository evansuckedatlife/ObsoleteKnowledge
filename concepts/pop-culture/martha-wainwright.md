## summary

**Martha Wainwright** is a critically acclaimed Canadian-American singer-songwriter celebrated for her emotionally raw lyricism, theatrical vocal power, and unconventional blend of folk, indie rock, and cabaret. Born into the prominent *Wainwright*-*McGarrigle* musical dynasty in New York City and raised in Montreal, she emerged from the shadow of her famous relatives as a fiercely independent artist confronting familial conflict and romantic turmoil. Her work is celebrated across the international music press for its visceral vulnerability, black humor, and melodic sophistication, establishing her as an enduring voice in contemporary song.

## you gotta know

- Achieved underground fame with her searing 2005 breakout song *Bloody Mother Fucking Asshole*, a cathartic indictment of patronizing male music critics and a fiery declaration of independence from her father.
- Born to American folk satirist *Loudon Wainwright III* and Canadian folk icon *Kate McGarrigle*, and is the younger sister of singer-songwriter *Rufus Wainwright* and half-sister of *Lucy Wainwright Roche*.
- Released her eponymous debut studio album *Martha Wainwright* in 2005, earning international acclaim for dramatic ballads such as *Factory* and *When the Day Is Short*.
- Explored lush pop orchestrations and electro-folk textures on subsequent albums including *I Know You're Married But I've Got Feelings Too* in 2008 and *Come Home to Mama* in 2012.
- Honored her francophone roots with the 2009 live concert album *Sans Fusils, Ni Souliers, à Paris: Martha Wainwright's Piaf Record*, showcasing dramatic interpretations of songs popularized by *Édith Piaf*.
- Founded the independent community arts venue and cultural cafe *Ursa* in the Mile End district of Montreal, serving as a hub for local musicians and writers.
- Authored the revelatory 2022 autobiographical memoir *Stories I Might Regret Telling You*, offering an unvarnished account of divorce, maternal grief, and life within a turbulent musical clan.

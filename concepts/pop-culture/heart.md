## summary

**Heart** is an American rock band formed in the early 1970s, fronted by the sister duo of lead vocalist *Ann Wilson* and guitarist *Nancy Wilson*. Originating in the Pacific Northwest, the group broke gender barriers in hard rock by combining heavy electric guitar riffs with acoustic folk traditions. Over a career spanning five decades, the ensemble produced multiple platinum albums and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013.

## you gotta know

- Fronted by **The Wilson Sisters**, *Ann Wilson*, known for her operatic vocal power, and *Nancy Wilson*, renowned for dynamic acoustic and electric guitar instrumentation.
- Breakthrough debut album *Dreamboat Annie* (1975) yielded major international hit singles including "Magic Man" and "Crazy on You".
- Signature 1977 anthem "Barracuda" was composed in anger after an industry rumor fabricated an incestuous relationship between the sisters for publicity.
- Heavily influenced by British folk and blues-rock pioneers, notably *Led Zeppelin*, whom the sisters famously honored with an emotional performance of "Stairway to Heaven" at the Kennedy Center Honors.
- Underwent a dramatic commercial resurgence during the mid-1980s with power ballads such as "These Dreams", "What About Love", and "Alone".
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013, celebrated for opening pathbreaking avenues for female bandleaders in heavy rock music.
- Regularly incorporated literary, mystical, and acoustic storytelling motifs into their music, blending hard rock dynamics with folk balladeering traditions.

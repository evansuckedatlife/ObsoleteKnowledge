## summary

The **Nintendo 3DS** is an eighth-generation handheld video game console produced by Nintendo, released in *Japan* and worldwide in 2011. Serving as the direct successor to the Nintendo DS family, its defining technological feature was autostereoscopic 3D display, which allowed users to perceive glasses-free 3D depth. Through hardware revisions and a software library that sustained Nintendo during the commercial struggles of the Wii U, the platform cemented a vital chapter in modern portable gaming history.

## you gotta know

- Launched in early 2011 with an autostereoscopic top screen that produced stereoscopic 3D effects without requiring specialized glasses.
- Equipped with a stereoscopic 3D depth slider on the side of the upper display, allowing players to adjust the intensity of the depth effect or turn it off entirely.
- Introduced the Circle Pad analog controller above the traditional directional pad to allow smooth 360-degree directional movement in handheld games.
- Featured outward-facing dual digital cameras capable of taking 3D photographs and driving augmented reality minigames via bundled physical AR cards.
- Endured an initially sluggish commercial launch caused by high pricing and a sparse software catalog, prompting a dramatic price drop in August 2011 and the *Nintendo 3DS Ambassador Program* of downloadable legacy games for early adopters.
- Supported background local communication through the *StreetPass* protocol, which let sleep-mode consoles exchange player avatars, game data, and minigame progress whenever users walked past one another in public.
- Spawned multiple hardware redesigns across its lifespan, including the larger *3DS XL*, the budget-focused slate-styled *2DS* that removed the hinge and 3D display, and the *New Nintendo 3DS* with an added C-Stick and upgraded CPU.
- Maintained complete backwards compatibility with physical game cartridges and digital software from the original Nintendo DS generation.

## connections

- [[nintendo-64]] — received acclaimed stereoscopic 3D remasters on the handheld, including *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D* and *Star Fox 64 3D*.
- [[nintendo-entertainment-system]] — ancestor platform whose historic library was distributed on the 3DS via the Virtual Console service and the Ambassador Program.
- [[japan]] — home nation of manufacturer Nintendo, where the portable console achieved enormous sales fueled by social multiplayer phenomena like *Monster Hunter*.
- [[minecraft]] — ported to the upgraded New Nintendo 3DS hardware edition in 2017 to bring voxel sandbox survival to the twin-screen format.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — genre represented on the hardware by specialized portable entries, frequently utilizing the touchscreen or the Circle Pad Pro accessory for aiming.

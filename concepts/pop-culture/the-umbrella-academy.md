## summary

**The Umbrella Academy** is an acclaimed superhero comic book and multimedia franchise created and written by musician *Gerard Way* and illustrated by Brazilian artist *Gabriel Bá*. Published by *Dark Horse Comics* beginning in 2007, the story centers on an estranged, dysfunctional family of adopted sibling superheroes who reunite following the mysterious death of their eccentric billionaire benefactor, *Sir Reginald Hargreeves*. Renowned for its surrealist gothic tone, eccentric dark humor, and catastrophic time-travel paradoxes, the property achieved widespread global popularity through an award-winning live-action television adaptation developed by *Steve Blackman*.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Gerard Way*, lead singer of rock band *My Chemical Romance*, alongside acclaimed comic artist *Gabriel Bá*, winning the prestigious 2008 Eisner Award for *The Apocalypse Suite*.
- Follows seven extraordinary individuals born simultaneously on October 1, 1989, to mothers worldwide who had exhibited no prior signs of pregnancy that morning.
- Trained from childhood by the cold, alien industrialist *Sir Reginald Hargreeves* (known publicly as *The Monocle*), who designated his adoptive children by numerical rank rather than names.
- Features a wildly eclectic core cast, including the gorilla-bodied strongman *Luther* (Number One), the reality-warping *Allison* (Number Three), the drug-addled clairvoyant *Klaus* (Number Four), and the space-jumping assassin *Five*.
- Highlights *Vanya* (later *Viktor*) *Hargreeves* (Number Seven), initially believed to possess no supernatural powers, whose repressed acoustic telekinesis unwittingly triggers the apocalypse.
- Introduced bizarre antagonists and temporal agencies, such as the temporal-management syndicate *The Temps Aeviternity* (The Commission) and sadistic hitmen *Hazel and Cha-Cha*.
- Adapted by Netflix into a four-season hit television series starring *Elliot Page*, *Tom Hopper*, *David Castañeda*, *Emmy Raver-Lampman*, *Robert Sheehan*, and *Aidan Gallagher*.

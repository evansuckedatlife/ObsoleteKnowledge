## summary

**Diary of a Wimpy Kid** is a comedic children's fiction and graphic novel series created by American author and cartoonist *Jeff Kinney*. Originating as an online comic on FunBrain in 2004 before being published in print in 2007, the books follow the diary entries and notebook doodles of middle school student **Greg Heffley**. The franchise became a global publishing phenomenon, celebrated for its cynical humor, handwritten font, and depiction of suburban adolescent angst.

## you gotta know

- Written and illustrated by *Jeff Kinney*, the series presents itself as the handwritten journal of middle schooler *Greg Heffley*, complete with simple line drawings.
- Follows the self-absorbed, status-conscious *Greg Heffley* as he navigates middle school social hierarchies, family life, and his frequent schemes for popularity.
- Introduces iconic recurring gags, most famously the dreaded *Cheese Touch*, a social curse passed around a rotting slice of Swiss cheese on the school basketball court.
- Features Greg's earnest, naive best friend *Rowley Jefferson*, who often succeeds effortlessly despite Greg's manipulative attempts to coach him.
- Depicts the dysfunctional dynamics of the Heffley household, notably older heavy-metal drummer brother *Rodrick* and pampered younger toddler *Manny*.
- Adapted into a successful live-action feature film series by 20th Century Fox starting in 2010 starring *Zachary Gordon*, as well as subsequent animated adaptations.
- Has sold over 275 million copies worldwide, ranking among the bestselling book series of all time in modern juvenile literature.

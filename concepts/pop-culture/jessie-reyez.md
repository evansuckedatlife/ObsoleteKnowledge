## summary

**Jessie Reyez**, born **Jessica Reyez**, is an acclaimed Colombian-Canadian singer and songwriter celebrated for her distinctively raspy vocal delivery, unfiltered emotional vulnerability, and visceral fusion of contemporary R&B, hip-hop, and pop. Emerging from Toronto's creative arts initiatives, she gained widespread recognition with her haunting 2016 single "Figures" and her breakthrough debut extended play *Kiddo*. Known for tackling institutional abuse, mental health struggles, toxic romance, and the immigrant experience, she has won multiple Juno Awards and received a Grammy Award nomination.

## you gotta know

- Raised in Toronto by Colombian immigrant parents, initially busking in public squares and working as a bartender in Miami before returning to enroll in Toronto's *The Remix Project* arts incubator.
- Broke into international prominence with the raw acoustic heartbreak ballad "Figures," earning widespread critical acclaim for her abrasive, dynamic vocal delivery and emotional intensity.
- Released the landmark 2017 song and short film "Gatekeeper," which confronted sexist power dynamics and predatory sexual coercion in the commercial music recording industry ahead of the #MeToo movement.
- Co-wrote massive global pop anthems for major international recording artists, including *Calvin Harris* and *Dua Lipa's* smash hit "One Kiss" as well as *Sam Smith's* "Promises."
- Received a Grammy Award nomination for Best Urban Contemporary Album for her sophomore project *Being Human in Public* at the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards.
- Released her full-length debut studio album *Before Love Came to Kill Us* in 2020, charting in the top five of the US Billboard Top R&B Albums chart and featuring prominent contributions from *Eminem*.
- Embarked on major international arena and stadium concert tours as an opening performer for superstar artists such as *Billie Eilish* and *Sam Smith*.

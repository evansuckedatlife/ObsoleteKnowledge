## summary

**Bowser**, also recognized by the title *King Koopa*, is a fictional character, conqueror, and the primary antagonist of the *Super Mario* video game franchise published by *Nintendo* in *[[japan]]*. Debuting in the 1985 NES title *Super Mario Bros.*, he rules over the turtle-like Koopa species and perpetually attempts to seize power by kidnapping *Princess Peach* and invading the *Mushroom Kingdom*. Recognized by his spiked shell, horned visage, and fire-breathing attacks, he is among the most famous and recognizable villainous characters in the history of interactive entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Created by legendary Japanese video game designer *Shigeru Miyamoto*, who initially sketched the villain as an ox inspired by the Ox King from the animated film *Alakazam the Great* before character designer *Yoichi Kotabe* revised his anatomy into a fire-breathing turtle.
- Named after *gukbap*, a Korean comfort soup known as *kuppa* in Japanese, reflecting *Shigeru Miyamoto*'s habit of considering names inspired by popular Korean culinary dishes including *bibimbap* and *bulgogi*.
- Defined landmark three-dimensional boss battles in *Super Mario 64*, where players must run behind him, grab him by his spiked tail using directional analog stick rotations, and throw him into floating boundary bombs.
- Reappears across an enormous variety of gaming genres beyond classic platformers, serving as a heavy heavyweight racer in the *Mario Kart* series, an imposing fighter in *Super Smash Bros.*, and an athletic rival in *Mario Tennis*.
- Transformed into fearsome alternate incarnations, including the skeletal undead form *Dry Bowser* that reassembles after falling into lava, and the titanic, spiky colossus *Giga Bowser* first encountered in *Super Smash Bros. Melee*.
- Portrayed in lighthearted role-playing games such as *Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars* and *Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story* as a blustering, prideful, and comedic anti-hero who frequently forms reluctant truces with *Mario*.
- Led a large family of reptilian commanders known as the *Koopalings*, originally presented as his children in *Super Mario Bros. 3* before being narratively distinguished from his single acknowledged son and heir, *Bowser Jr.*
- Featured prominently in computer animation and cinema, voiced by *Jack Black* in the 2023 hit *The Super Mario Bros. Movie*, which featured the viral piano ballad *Peaches*.

## summary

**No Time to Die** is a 2021 spy film produced by Eon Productions, marking the twenty-fifth installment in the long-running *James Bond* cinematic franchise. Directed by American filmmaker *Cary Joji Fukunaga*, the film serves as the definitive swan song and emotional climax for actor *Daniel Craig*'s five-film tenure as MI6 secret agent 007. It earned widespread critical praise and box office success for deconstructing traditional spy genre formulas through poignant themes of mortality, legacy, and romantic vulnerability.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Cary Joji Fukunaga*, who became the first American to helm an official Eon Productions *James Bond* feature film.
- Concludes the serialized five-film narrative arc of *Daniel Craig*, which began with *Casino Royale* in 2006 and revitalized the character with gritty realism.
- Features *Rami Malek* as the villainous terrorist *Lyutsifer Safin*, who wields *Heracles*, a programmable DNA-targeted nanoweapon developed in a clandestine biological laboratory.
- Introduced *Lashana Lynch* as *Nomi*, a younger British agent who inherits the 007 codename following Bond's temporary retirement in Jamaica.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Original Song for its melancholic title track, performed and co-written by American pop singer *Billie Eilish* and her brother *Finneas O'Connell*.
- Co-written by English writer-actor *Phoebe Waller-Bridge*, brought aboard to inject sharp wit, emotional complexity, and modern agency into the female characters.
- Marked an unprecedented franchise milestone by depicting the heroic death of James Bond, who sacrifices his life under a barrage of cruise missiles to ensure the safety of his lover *Madeleine Swann* and daughter *Mathilde*.

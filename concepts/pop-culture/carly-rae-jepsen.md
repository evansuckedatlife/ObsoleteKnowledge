## summary

**Carly Rae Jepsen** is a Canadian singer-songwriter who achieved worldwide commercial dominance with her infectious 2011 bubblegum pop smash hit *Call Me Maybe*. After finishing in third place on the fifth season of *Canadian Idol*, she transitioned from regional folk-pop to become an internationally recognized pop icon. While initially categorized as a viral sensation, her subsequent critically acclaimed records established her as an enduring darling of indie music critics and a celebrated pioneer of modern synth-pop.

## you gotta know

- Rose to global prominence when pop singer *Justin Bieber* and actress *Selena Gomez* tweeted praise for *Call Me Maybe*, causing the single to go viral online and top charts in dozens of countries.
- Released *Call Me Maybe* as the lead single from her major-label EP *Curiosity* and sophomore studio album *Kiss*, which also featured the *Owl City* duet *Good Time*.
- Rebuilt her artistic identity with the critically lauded 2015 record *Emotion*, a 1980s-inspired synth-pop masterpiece featuring collaborations with *Dev Hynes*, *Rostam Batmanglij*, and *Sia*.
- Made her Broadway theatrical debut in 2014, performing the title role in *Rodgers + Hammerstein's Cinderella* at the Broadway Theatre in *New York City*.
- Established a signature tradition of releasing companion albums of unreleased material from her recording sessions, beginning with *Emotion: Side B* in 2016.
- Earned an enthusiastic cult following in indie and queer communities, celebrated for her vibrant, earnest lyricism about romantic infatuation and melodic craftsmanship.
- Followed up her breakout decade with the dual releases *The Loneliest Time* in 2022 and *The Loveliest Time* in 2023, expanding into disco, funk, and electronic dance arrangements.

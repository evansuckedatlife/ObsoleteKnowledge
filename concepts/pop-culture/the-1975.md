## summary

**The 1975** is an English pop rock and indie band formed in Wilmslow, Cheshire, in 2002, fronted by charismatic lead vocalist and lyricist *Matty Healy*. Known for blending eighties sophisti-pop, post-punk, electronic textures, and hook-heavy synth-pop, the quartet achieved international acclaim for dissecting modern alienation, romance, and digital culture. Their sprawling, genre-defying studio albums and conceptual live performances have established them as one of Britain's most distinctive twenty-first-century rock acts.

## you gotta know

- Comprises vocalist and guitarist *Matty Healy*, lead guitarist *Adam Hann*, bassist *Ross MacDonald*, and drummer and producer *George Daniel*.
- Adopted their moniker from handwritten poetic notations discovered by *Healy* in the back of an antique copy of Jack Kerouac's *On the Road*, dated "1 June, The 1975".
- Broke into mainstream prominence with their 2013 self-titled debut album, generating infectious alternative anthems such as *Chocolate*, *Sex*, and *Robbers*.
- Released the landmark 2016 album *I Like It When You Sleep, for You Are So Beautiful yet So Unaware of It*, which topped charts in both the United Kingdom and America.
- Emphasized social commentary, internet anxiety, and political polarization on their 2018 record *A Brief Inquiry into Online Relationships*, frequently compared to Radiohead's *OK Computer*.
- Followed with expansive concept records *Notes on a Conditional Form* and *Being Funny in a Foreign Language*, the latter supported by the acclaimed *At Their Very Best* theatrical tour.

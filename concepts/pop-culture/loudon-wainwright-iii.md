## summary

**Loudon Wainwright III** is an American singer-songwriter, humorist, and actor who emerged during the early 1970s folk revival as one of the celebrated "new Dylans." Known for his mordant wit, autobiographical candor, and theatrical live performances, he achieved mainstream chart success with the 1972 novelty hit *"Dead Skunk"*. Across more than two dozen albums, he chronicled family dysfunction, aging, and generational grief, earning a 2010 Grammy Award for his tribute project *High Wide & Handsome: The Charlie Poole Project*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved his biggest commercial pop single with the 1972 novelty song *"Dead Skunk"* (subtitled *"Dead Skunk in the Middle of the Road"*), which climbed into the Billboard top twenty.
- Hailed early in his career as one of several singer-songwriters designated a "new Bob Dylan" due to his acoustic guitar work, wry narrative lyricism, and biting social observation.
- Patriarch of an acclaimed musical family: father to singer-songwriters *Rufus Wainwright*, *Martha Wainwright*, and *Lucy Wainwright Roche*, with many family conflicts aired publicly in song.
- Married folk musician *Kate McGarrigle* in 1973, with whom he had Rufus and Martha before their divorce, and later fathered Lucy with *Suzzy Roche* of *The Roches*.
- Played the recurring role of singing surgeon *Captain Calvin Spalding* on the third season of the television series *M\*A\*S\*H*, performing several original tunes on the program.
- Won the 2010 Grammy Award for Best Traditional Folk Album for *High Wide & Handsome*, an expansive tribute commemorating rural North Carolina banjo pioneer *Charlie Poole*.
- Wrote the candid 2017 memoir *Liner Notes: On Parents & Children, Exes & Excess, Death & Making a Life*, exploring his privileged upbringing and relationship with his father, *Life* magazine columnist *Loudon Wainwright Jr.*

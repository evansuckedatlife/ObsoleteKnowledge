## summary

**Bungie** is a prominent American video game developer celebrated for revolutionizing the *first-person shooter* genre on home consoles and pioneering modern online multiplayer ecosystems. Founded in Chicago in 1991 by *Alex Seropian* and *Jason Jones*, the studio initially made its mark developing games for the Apple Macintosh. After its landmark acquisition by *Microsoft*, the studio created the universally influential *Halo* franchise before independently spearheading the shared-world live-service shooter with *Destiny*.

## you gotta know

- Created *Halo: Combat Evolved* as the flagship launch title for Microsoft's original Xbox console in 2001, proving that complex *first-person shooter* mechanics could thrive on gamepads.
- Popularized modern console multiplayer conventions on *Xbox Live* with *Halo 2*, introducing matchmaking playlists, party systems, and clan lobbies.
- Pioneered early 3D storytelling, terminal lore, and network play on the Macintosh platform with the cult classic *Marathon* trilogy.
- Developed the tactical real-time strategy series *Myth*, renowned for its realistic physics engines, terrain elevation advantages, and dark fantasy lore.
- Negotiated a high-profile split from parent company Microsoft in 2007, completing *Halo 3: ODST* and *Halo: Reach* before handing the series to *343 Industries*.
- Launched the shared-world online looter-shooter *Destiny* in 2014, establishing persistent live-service cooperative raids and evolving seasonal narratives.
- Entered into an agreement to be acquired by *Sony Interactive Entertainment* in 2022 to bolster cross-platform live-service game development.

## connections

- [[esports]] — competitive gaming ecosystem where early Bungie titles served as premier showcase competitions on televised circuits like Major League Gaming.
- [[counter-strike]] — contemporary multiplayer tactical shooter that defined competitive PC gaming while Bungie dominated competitive console arenas.
- [[capcom]] — peer industry titan renowned for arcade mastery whose console releases competed alongside Bungie's seminal titles.

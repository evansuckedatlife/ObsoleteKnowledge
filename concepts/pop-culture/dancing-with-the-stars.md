## summary

**Dancing with the Stars**, frequently abbreviated as **DWTS**, is an American reality television dance competition series that premiered on ABC in June 2005. Adapted from the British BBC television series *Strictly Come Dancing*, the show pairs celebrities from sports, television, film, and music with professional ballroom and Latin dancers to perform choreographed routines before a panel of judges and a voting public. As a dominant prime-time entertainment franchise across multiple decades, the show revived ballroom dance in global popular culture and became a hallmark of twenty-first-century broadcast television.

## you gotta know

- Celebrities and their professional partners compete each week in distinct traditional styles, including the tango, cha-cha-cha, waltz, quickstep, and paso doble, culminating in the high-stakes, unconstrained freestyle round during seasonal finales.
- The coveted grand prize awarded to the winning couple is the *Mirrorball Trophy*, a glittering disco-ball statuette that has become an iconic emblem of television victory.
- Longtime original judges included British ballroom champions *Len Goodman* and *Bruno Tonioli*, alongside American dancer-choreographer *Carrie Ann Inaba*, whose distinct critiques, scoring paddles, and Goodman's trademark score of "ten" defined the show's broadcast identity.
- The series was hosted for twenty-eight seasons by *Tom Bergeron*, renowned for his quick wit and smooth handling of live broadcasts, co-hosting alongside figures including *Lisa Canning*, *Samantha Harris*, and *Brooke Burke-Charvet*, before later hosting tenures by *Tyra Banks* and *Alfonso Ribeiro*.
- Professional dancers who achieved widespread individual fame through the program include *Derek Hough*, who holds the record with six Mirrorball victories before becoming a judge, *Julianne Hough*, *Cheryl Burke*, *Mark Ballas*, and *Valentin Chmerkovskiy*.
- Athlete contestants have historically dominated the competition, beginning with NFL Hall of Fame running back *Emmitt Smith*, who won Season 3 alongside Cheryl Burke, establishing a long-standing precedent for sports stars transitioning into prime-time entertainment.
- High-profile celebrity contestants from iconic television dramas have competed, including *Priscilla Presley*, known for her role in the prime-time soap opera [[dallas]], who appeared during Season 6.
- In 2022, after thirty seasons on ABC, the franchise made broadcast history by shifting to *Disney+*, becoming the first live competition series to stream on an American subscription video-on-demand platform before returning to a simulcast format.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — reflects American network broadcast traditions, uniting figures from professional sports, music, and Hollywood into live prime-time spectacle.
- [[dallas]] — featured prominent cast members from the prime-time soap opera, such as *Priscilla Presley*, as well as Texas sports royalty like *Emmitt Smith*.
- [[dan-levy]] — represents the golden age of American reality and competition programming that Levy covered, analyzed, and satirized as an MTV host.
- [[vaudeville]] — carries forward theatrical traditions of variety entertainment, live musical performance, physical stagecraft, and popular showmanship.
- [[physical-comedy]] — incorporates energetic theatrics, expressive choreography, and lighthearted mishaps during rehearsals and live routines.

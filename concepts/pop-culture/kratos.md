## summary

**Kratos**, widely known as the **Ghost of Sparta**, is the central protagonist of Sony Computer Entertainment's flagship action-adventure video game franchise *God of War*, created by David Jaffe at Santa Monica Studio. Debuting on the PlayStation 2 in 2005, the character begins as a vengeance-crazed Spartan warrior tricked by *Ares* into slaughtering his own family, driving him to systematically overthrow the Olympian pantheon. Decades later, a critically acclaimed 2018 reinvention relocated him to Midgard within Norse mythology as a stoic, aging father attempting to escape the cycle of patricide and deicide.

## you gotta know

- Bound permanently to the *Blades of Chaos*, twin chain-bladed daggers seared into his forearms by the god *Ares* as a token of his oath of servitude.
- Earned the title **Ghost of Sparta** when a village oracle cursed him to bear the pale white ashes of his murdered wife *Lysandra* and daughter *Calliope* bonded to his skin.
- Overthrows the original Greek god of war *Ares* at the climax of the first game, briefly ascending Mount Olympus as the new titular deity before being betrayed by *Zeus*.
- Discovers in *God of War II* and *God of War III* that he is a demigod son of *Zeus*, culminating in his release of *Hope* to mankind after decimating the Olympian gods with the *Blade of Olympus*.
- Voiced across the Greek saga by Terrence C. Carson and subsequently portrayed via performance capture by Christopher Judge in the 2018 Norse reboot and its 2022 sequel *God of War Ragnarök*.
- Wields the frost-imbued *Leviathan Axe*, forged by the Huldra brothers *Brok* and *Sindri*, while guiding his son *Atreus* across the Nine Realms to fulfill the final wishes of his late wife *Faye*.
- Contends with Norse deities including *Baldur*, *Freya*, *Thor*, and the manipulative *Odin*, while grappling with the prophecy that *Atreus* is destined to become *Loki*.

## summary

**Gran Turismo** is an acclaimed series of racing simulation video games developed by *Polyphony Digital* and published by *Sony Interactive Entertainment* for *PlayStation* consoles. Conceived and directed by *Kazunori Yamauchi*, the franchise debuted in *Japan* in 1997 and became known worldwide as "The Real Driving Simulator." It revolutionized the racing genre through meticulous vehicular physics, extensive licensed car rosters, and technical graphic benchmarks across multiple hardware generations.

## you gotta know

- Created by game designer *Kazunori Yamauchi*, who spent five years developing the original 1997 release with a small internal team known as *Polys Entertainment*.
- Subtitled "The Real Driving Simulator," setting the competitive standard for vehicular physics, realistic handling, and audio design on home consoles.
- Features dual primary gameplay styles: the open Arcade Mode and the comprehensive Simulation Mode, later titled GT Mode, which requires earning competition licenses and tuning purchased production cars.
- Remains the highest-selling video game franchise under the *PlayStation* brand banner, having surpassed 90 million units sold across worldwide markets.
- Launched the innovative *GT Academy* driver discovery program in collaboration with *Nissan*, which trained skilled digital sim racers to become professional real-world motorsport competitors like *Jann Mardenborough*.
- Pioneered digital car preservation by partnering directly with automotive manufacturers, featuring obscure historical concept cars alongside popular production vehicles and dedicated Vision GT prototypes.
- Transitioned into regulated competitive motorsport through *Gran Turismo Sport*, which established an official partnership with the *Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile* (*FIA*) for sanctioned global esports championships.
- Adapted into a 2023 biographical sports drama feature film directed by *Neill Blomkamp*, dramatizing the true racing career of *Jann Mardenborough*.

## summary

**Freddie Mercury**, born **Farrokh Bulsara**, was a British singer, songwriter, and charismatic showman who achieved worldwide renown as the lead vocalist and primary creative engine of the rock band Queen. Renowned for his operatic four-octave vocal range, theatrical songwriting, and kinetic stage presence, he shattered traditional conventions of popular music and stadium entertainment throughout the late twentieth century. His enduring compositions and magnetic showmanship established him as one of the most celebrated and influential vocalists in the history of global rock music.

## you gotta know

- Born in the British protectorate of Zanzibar to Parsi parents from India, attending English boarding school St. Peter's in Panchgani before fleeing political turmoil during the 1964 Zanzibar Revolution to settle in Middlesex, *[[england]]*.
- Formed the rock group Queen in London in 1970 alongside guitarist *Brian May* and drummer *Roger Taylor*, later joined by bassist *John Deacon*, adopting his flamboyant stage surname from mythological poetry.
- Composed Queen's magnum opus, *Bohemian Rhapsody* (1975), an audacious six-minute progressive suite that combined a cappella intro, operatic melodrama, hard-rock cadenzas, and balladry into a chart-topping sensation.
- Authored numerous international anthems that remain staples of worldwide sporting events, political rallies, and arena gatherings, including *We Are the Champions*, *Somebody to Love*, *Killer Queen*, and *Crazy Little Thing Called Love*.
- Delivered an electrifying performance at the *Live Aid* charity concert at Wembley Stadium in London on July 13, 1985, widely hailed by critics and musicians as the single greatest live rock performance in television and music history.
- Maintained a private domestic life in London alongside longtime companion *Mary Austin* and partner *Jim Hutton*, while maintaining an adventurous persona across vibrant nightlife scenes in Munich and *[[new-york-city]]*.
- Diagnosed with HIV/AIDS in 1987, continuing to record music through severe physical decline until releasing a public confirmation of his illness just twenty-four hours before passing away on November 24, 1991, at age forty-five.
- Commemorated posthumously with the 1992 *Freddie Mercury Tribute Concert for AIDS Awareness* at Wembley Stadium, establishing the Mercury Phoenix Trust and inspiring the Oscar-winning 2018 biographical film *Bohemian Rhapsody*.

## summary

**Homer Simpson** is the bumbling, well-meaning patriarch of the animated sitcom *The Simpsons*, created by *Matt Groening* for the Fox Broadcasting Company in the *[[united-states]]*. Voiced by actor *Dan Castellaneta*, he serves as a satirical embodiment of the American working-class blue-collar everyman, characterised by impulsive gluttony, laziness, and unexpected flashes of deep familial love. Since his debut on *The Tracey Ullman Show* in 1987, he has become one of the most widely recognised cultural figures in television history.

## you gotta know

- Works as the bumbling, frequently incompetent safety inspector in Sector 7-G at the *Springfield Nuclear Power Plant*, owned by the ruthless billionaire *C. Montgomery Burns*.
- Renowned for iconic catchphrases, most famously his annoyed grunt "D'oh!", which was formally inducted into the *Oxford English Dictionary* in 2001.
- Resides at 742 Evergreen Terrace in the town of *Springfield* with his wife *Marge Simpson* and children *Bart*, *Lisa*, and *Maggie Simpson*.
- Frequently frequents *Moe's Tavern*, run by bartender *Moe Szyslak*, drinking *Duff Beer* alongside drinking companions *Barney Gumble*, *Carl Carlson*, and *Lenny Leonard*.
- Voiced continuously by *Dan Castellaneta*, who initially modeled his vocal cadence on actor *Walter Matthau* before pitching it into a more energetic, expressive comic voice.
- Often participates in extreme *[[physical-comedy]]* and classic *[[slapstick-comedy]]*, such as falling repeatedly down Springfield Gorge or choking Bart while exclaiming "Why you little...!".
- Traveled into outer space in the critically praised fifth-season episode "Deep Space Homer", inadvertently releasing potato chips into zero gravity before saving the mission with an inanimate carbon rod.

## connections

- [[slapstick-comedy]] — classic tradition of comic violence and absurd pratfalls that defines much of his physical animation.
- [[physical-comedy]] — comedic style utilized throughout his exaggerated bodily injuries, stunts, and pain reactions.
- [[i-love-lucy]] — foundational television sitcom model that established family-based situation comedy tropes in the *[[united-states]]*.
- [[united-states]] — nation whose suburban culture, television obsessions, and working-class archetypes he parodies.
- [[charlie-chaplin]] — iconic comic master whose pioneering slapstick techniques influenced generations of screen comedy.
- [[vaudeville]] — historical variety stage format whose comic timing and archetypes laid the groundwork for modern animated farce.

## summary

**Instagram** (commonly shortened to **IG** or **Insta**) is an American photo and video sharing social networking service created by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger in 2010. Launched initially on iOS before expanding globally, the service transformed mobile digital photography, online communication, and commercial influencer culture. Acquired by Facebook in 2012, it grew into one of the most culturally dominant media platforms of the twenty-first century.

## you gotta know

- Launched on October 6, 2010, by co-founders *Kevin Systrom* and *Mike Krieger*, quickly amassing one million registered users within its first two months.
- Acquired by *Mark Zuckerberg* and *Facebook* (now *Meta Platforms*) in April 2012 for approximately one billion dollars in cash and stock, a landmark tech acquisition that cemented mobile-first social networking.
- Originally restricted all uploads to a strict square 1:1 aspect ratio with a 640-pixel width to mimic Kodak Instamatic and Polaroid cameras, a defining constraint until rectangular orientations were permitted in August 2015.
- Distinguished in its early era by nostalgic vintage photo filters such as *Valencia*, *X-Pro II*, *Earlybird*, and *Lo-Fi*, which democratized stylized mobile aesthetics.
- Introduced the ephemeral twenty-four-hour *Stories* feature in August 2016 to counter rival platform *Snapchat*, followed by long-form video hub *IGTV* in 2018 and short-form video feed *Reels* in 2020 to compete directly with *TikTok*.
- Began development under the name *Burbn*, a prototype HTML5 multi-feature check-in application inspired by Systrom's love of fine whiskeys, before being radically simplified into a pure photo-sharing platform.
- Co-founders Systrom and Krieger abruptly resigned from the company in September 2018 amid escalating conflicts regarding platform autonomy and algorithmic integration with parent company leadership.

## summary

**Cloud Strife** is a fictional protagonist created by game developer *Square* (now *Square Enix*) as the central hero of the landmark 1997 role-playing video game *Final Fantasy VII*. Designed by *Tetsuya Nomura*, he is immediately recognized across global popular culture by his spiky blond hair, purple-blue SOLDIER uniform, and oversized Buster Sword. Cloud's complex narrative arc—shifting from an arrogant, emotionally detached mercenary into a traumatized hero confronting manufactured memories—made him an enduring icon of Japanese interactive fiction and video game history.

## you gotta know

- Debuted as the protagonist of *Final Fantasy VII* (1997) on the Sony PlayStation, a title instrumental in popularizing Japanese role-playing games in the West.
- Instantly recognizable by his signature weapon, the massive, broad-bladed *Buster Sword*, inherited from his deceased comrade *Zack Fair*.
- Initially presents himself as an aloof, former elite operative of *Shinra's* private military force *SOLDIER*, working for the eco-terrorist group *AVALANCHE*.
- His central psychological conflict revolves around a shattered identity, severe memory distortion, and trauma caused by mako poisoning and human experimentation.
- Engages in an iconic, fateful rivalry with the fallen, god-complex antagonist *Sephiroth*, culminating in the defense of the planet from an apocalyptic Meteor.
- Features prominently in extensive spin-off media across the *Compilation of Final Fantasy VII*, including the computer-animated film *Advent Children* (2005) and a modern multi-part remake series.
- Crossed over into broader gaming culture as a playable guest fighter in Nintendo's *Super Smash Bros.* series and the *Kingdom Hearts* franchise.

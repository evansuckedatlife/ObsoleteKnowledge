## summary

**Diablo** is a dark fantasy action role-playing game series developed by *Blizzard Entertainment* and originally created by *David Brevik* and *Blizzard North*. Debuting in 1996, the franchise pioneered point-and-click isometric combat, procedural dungeon generation, and randomized loot grinding within the gothic mortal realm of *Sanctuary*. Its combination of atmospheric storytelling and addictive character progression defined the modern ARPG genre and influenced game design for decades.

## you gotta know

- Created by *David Brevik*, *Max Schaefer*, and *Erich Schaefer* at *Condor* (later renamed *Blizzard North*) and released in late 1996.
- Centers on the eternal conflict between the *High Heavens* and the *Burning Hells*, fought over the mortal world of *Sanctuary*, created by the rogue angel *Inarius* and demon *Lilith*.
- The titular prime evil, **Diablo**, is the **Lord of Terror**, who alongside his brothers *Baal* (Lord of Destruction) and *Mephisto* (Lord of Hatred) seeks to corrupt humanity.
- *Diablo II* (2000) cemented the franchise's legacy with iconic classes like the *Necromancer*, five acts of dark fantasy landscapes, and persistent multiplayer on *Battle.net*.
- Popularized color-coded loot rarity (normal, magic, rare, unique/legendary) that became the universal template for looter-shooters like [[destiny]].
- Features beloved recurring non-player characters, notably the scholar *Deckard Cain*, famous for his iconic greeting, "Stay awhile and listen!"
- *Diablo III* (2012) set PC sales records despite controversies surrounding its early real-money auction house, followed by *Diablo IV* in 2023.

## connections

- [[christianity]] — foundational theological source for its gothic imagery of archangels, demonic legions, cathedrals, and fallen angels.
- [[destiny]] — popular shooter franchise directly indebted to Diablo's randomized tiered loot and repeatable boss farming formula.
- [[skyrim]] — seminal fantasy role-playing counterpart renowned for expansive lore and dungeon crawling traditions.
- [[tragedy]] — narrative framework characterizing the grim fates of its heroes, such as the original warrior who drives Diablo's soulstone into his own forehead.
- [[united-states]] — country of origin for developer *Blizzard Entertainment*, based in Irvine, California.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming ecosystem closely tied to Blizzard's adjacent multiplayer properties and speedrunning communities.

## summary

**Ms. Pac-Man** is a seminal 1982 arcade maze game developed by *General Computer Corporation* and published by *Midway Games*. Originating as an unauthorized enhancement kit titled *Crazy Otto*, the game introduced sophisticated ghost behavioral algorithms, four alternating multi-color mazes, and moving fruit bonuses. Widely recognized as one of the greatest video games ever made, it surpassed its Japanese predecessor in North American popularity and became a foundational landmark of the Golden Age of Arcade Games.

## you gotta know

- Began development at MIT by Doug Macrae, Kevin Curran, and *General Computer Corporation* (GCC) as an enhancement speed-up conversion board for *Pac-Man* called *Crazy Otto*.
- Purchased by *Midway Games* without the initial involvement of Japanese original copyright holder *Namco*, who had conceived the original character designed by *Toru Iwatani*.
- Replaced deterministic ghost patterns from the original title with semi-randomized AI behaviors, significantly elevating strategic difficulty and competitive depth.
- Introduced four distinct labyrinth layouts with varied tunnel configurations and color palettes, replacing the single repetitive maze of the 1980 original.
- Upgraded static bonus items to animated fruits, pretzels, and treats that bounce through the maze corridors before exiting through escape tunnels.
- Features narrative theatrical cutscenes charting the courtship, wedding, and delivery of a baby by a stork to the titular protagonist and her bow-wearing partner.
- Replaced the orange ghost *Clyde* with *Sue*, retaining *Blinky*, *Pinky*, and *Inky* alongside the signature power pellets and blue vulnerable phases.

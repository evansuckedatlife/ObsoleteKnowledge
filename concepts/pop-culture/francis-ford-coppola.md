## summary

**Francis Ford Coppola** is a seminal American film director, producer, and screenwriter who emerged as a central figure of the New Hollywood filmmaking renaissance of the 1970s in the [[united-states]]. Renowned for his operatic visual scale, mastery of cinematic drama, and exploration of familial corruption, he directed landmark works including *The Godfather* trilogy and *Apocalypse Now*. Coppola has earned five Academy Awards and remains widely celebrated as one of the most influential and audacious auteur filmmakers in modern cinema history.

## you gotta know

- Directed *The Godfather* (1972) and *The Godfather Part II* (1974), adapting Mario Puzo's novel into an epic [[tragedy]] chronicling the multi-generational rise and moral decay of the Corleone Mafia family.
- Directed the harrowing Vietnam War masterpiece *Apocalypse Now* (1979), inspired by Joseph Conrad's *Heart of Darkness*, surviving an infamously catastrophic and legendary production in the Philippines.
- Won the prestigious Palme d'Or at the Cannes Film Festival twice, receiving the honor for *The Conversation* (1974) and *Apocalypse Now* (1979).
- Founded the pioneering independent studio American Zoetrope in Northern California alongside fellow filmmaker George Lucas to bypass Hollywood studio control.
- Early in his career, won an Academy Award for co-writing the screenplay of the World War II biographical military drama *Patton* (1970).
- Directed other celebrated works across varied genres, including *The Outsiders* (1983), *Rumble Fish* (1983), *Bram Stoker's Dracula* (1992), and the self-financed epic *Megalopolis* (2024).
- Serves as the patriarch of a renowned Hollywood cinematic dynasty that includes his daughter Sofia Coppola, nephew Nicolas Cage, and sister Talia Shire.

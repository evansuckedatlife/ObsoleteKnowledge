## summary

**Sheryl Sandberg** is an American business executive, billionaire, and author who served as the chief operating officer of *Meta Platforms* (formerly *Facebook*) from 2008 to 2022. She was the chief architect of the company's hyper-targeted digital advertising apparatus, transforming an unprofitable campus network into one of the most lucrative corporate empires in world history. Through her best-selling 2013 book *Lean In: Women, Work, and the Will to Lead*, she emerged as a prominent yet intensely debated champion of corporate feminism, while later facing public scrutiny over platform governance and user data privacy.

## you gotta know

- Built the monetization strategy of *Facebook* after being recruited from *Google* by founder *Mark Zuckerberg*, pioneering the targeted mobile advertising engine that drove the platform's multi-billion-dollar valuation.
- Penned the bestselling non-fiction manifesto *Lean In* in 2013, which urged professional women to assert themselves in boardrooms, seek leadership posts, and challenge internal self-doubt, spurring the international non-profit foundation *LeanIn.Org*.
- Began her public policy career as Chief of Staff to United States Secretary of the Treasury *Lawrence Summers* during the *Bill Clinton* presidency, helping oversee American financial assistance during the *Asian financial crisis*.
- Appointed to Facebook's board of directors in 2012, making her the first woman to serve on the company's corporate governing body.
- Faced intense congressional and journalistic criticism during the fallout from the 2018 *Cambridge Analytica* scandal, which revealed pervasive vulnerabilities in user privacy safeguards and election oversight.
- Addressed personal bereavement, trauma, and psychological resilience in her 2017 memoir *Option B*, co-written with organizational psychologist *Adam Grant* following the unexpected death of her husband *Dave Goldberg*.
- Resigned from her executive post as Chief Operating Officer of *Meta* in 2022, redirecting her public endeavors toward philanthropic foundations and maternal health advocacy.

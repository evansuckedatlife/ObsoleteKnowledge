## summary

**Hacksaw Ridge** is a 2016 biographical war drama directed by *Mel Gibson* that chronicles the military service of *Desmond Doss*, an American combat medic during *World War II*. Doss, an adherent of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, served as a conscientious objector who refused to carry firearms or kill enemy combatants. The film is celebrated for its visceral portrayal of the Battle of Okinawa and received six Academy Award nominations, winning for Best Sound Mixing and Best Film Editing.

## you gotta know

- Depicts the real-life service of *Desmond Doss*, portrayed by *Andrew Garfield*, who was awarded the Medal of Honor for his extraordinary heroism in *World War II*.
- Centers on Doss's religious convictions rooted in *Christianity*, which led him to serve as a non-combatant medic while refusing to handle a rifle or train on Saturdays.
- Highlights his court-martial trial at Fort Jackson, where Doss defended his constitutional right to serve unarmed before deploying to the Pacific theater against *Japan*.
- Depicts the brutal combat atop the Maeda Escarpment, a heavily fortified sheer cliff face nicknamed the eponymous title location.
- Details Doss's solo rescue of 75 wounded American soldiers overnight while dodging enemy patrols, repeatedly praying, "Lord, please help me get one more."
- Marks the major directorial comeback of *Mel Gibson*, receiving extensive critical acclaim for its graphic *realism* and spiritual themes.
- Won the Academy Award for Best Film Editing for *John Gilbert*, whose pacing juxtaposed peaceful domestic life in Virginia with horrifying, chaotic combat choreography.

## summary

**Professor Layton**, born **Hershel Layton**, is the titular gentleman archaeologist and puzzle solver of the acclaimed adventure game franchise created by *Akihiro Hino* and developed by *Level-5* in *[[japan]]*. Operating out of a stylized, nostalgic vision of *[[england]]*, the character is renowned for unraveling municipal conspiracies, historical secrets, and folklore through deductive reasoning and mathematical brainteasers. Debuting on the Nintendo DS, Layton emerged as one of modern gaming's definitive intellectual protagonists, recognized for his iconic top hat, courteous etiquette, and methodical approach to problem-solving.

## you gotta know

- Instantly identified by his towering brown silk top hat, orange-accented overcoat, and signature moral aphorism: "A true gentleman leaves no puzzle unsolved."
- Works as a distinguished professor of archaeology at Gressenheller University in London, traveling across *[[england]]* alongside his spirited young apprentice, *Luke Triton*.
- Debuted in the 2007 Nintendo DS adventure *Professor Layton and the Curious Village*, unraveling the mystery of the Golden Apple in the automaton-filled hamlet of St. Mystere.
- Concluded his original narrative trilogy in *The Unwound Future*, a poignant time-travel tale detailing the origin of his top hat and his tragic separation from his late beloved, Claire Foley.
- Explored in an expansive prequel trilogy starting with *The Last Specter*, charting his initial encounter with Luke and his assistant Emmy Altava against the criminal mastermind Jean Descole.
- Utilized hundreds of brainteasers, sliding blocks, and logic riddles created by celebrated Japanese puzzle scholar *Akira Tago*, author of the influential *Atama no Taisō* book series.
- Starred alongside Capcom's premier defense attorney in the crossover title *Professor Layton vs. Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney*, merging puzzle exploration with dramatic medieval witch-trial cross-examinations.
- Expanded the family legacy through his sleuthing daughter Katrielle in *Layton's Mystery Journey*, preceding his announced return in *Professor Layton and the New World of Steam*.

## connections

- [[japan]] — country where game creator Akihiro Hino and studio Level-5 designed and developed the puzzle-adventure franchise.
- [[england]] — nation providing the visual setting, period architecture, and Edwardian gentlemanly manners central to Layton's characterization.
- [[linguistics]] — academic discipline echoed in the riddles, lateral-thinking wordplay, and cryptograms central to the gameplay.
- [[princess-zelda]] — fellow beloved Nintendo platform franchise celebrated for intricate environmental puzzles, hidden relics, and ancient architectural secrets.
- [[prozd]] — voice actor and comedy creator whose sketches regularly parody the eccentric mechanics, dramatic pauses, and dialogue tropes of detective and puzzle games.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow acclaimed adventure game where players investigate ancient ruins, decipher mechanism puzzles, and uncover lost archaeological lore.

## summary

**Quentin Tarantino** is an acclaimed American filmmaker, screenwriter, and producer celebrated for his stylized violence, nonlinear storytelling, and hyper-literate pop-culture dialogue. Emerging in the 1990s as a leading auteur of independent cinema in the *[[united-states]]*, he achieved instant prominence with crime classics like *Reservoir Dogs* and *Pulp Fiction*. His signature cinephilic style draws extensively from spaghetti westerns, martial arts films, blaxploitation, and mid-century genre cinema.

## you gotta know

- Won two *Academy Awards for Best Original Screenplay* for the nonlinearly structured crime masterpiece *Pulp Fiction* and the revisionist historical western *Django Unchained*.
- Gained initial fame while working as a clerk at the *Video Archives* rental store in Manhattan Beach, absorbing decades of international genre cinema.
- Known for assembling ensemble casts featuring recurring collaborators such as *Samuel L. Jackson*, *Uma Thurman*, *Harvey Keitel*, *Tim Roth*, and *Christoph Waltz*.
- Pioneered alternative historical cinema where oppressed groups exact cathartic cinematic vengeance, notably in *Inglourious Basterds* and *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*.
- Collaborated for over two decades with the brilliant editor *Sally Menke*, whose precise sense of rhythm defined his trademark tension and abrupt violent bursts.
- Consistently champions analog filmmaking, shooting exclusively on photochemical film stock and organizing roadshow presentations in ultra-widescreen formats like *Ultra Panavision 70*.
- Declared an intention to retire from feature filmmaking after completing his tenth full-length directorial project to protect his cinematic legacy.

## summary

**k.d. lang**, born *Kathryn Dawn Lang* in 1961, is a Canadian pop, country, and adult contemporary singer-songwriter renowned for her extraordinary mezzo-soprano vocal range and distinctive styling. Initially gaining international attention with her cowpunk ensemble *The Reclines*, she challenged country music conventions before transitioning into lush, orchestral torch pop. As one of the first major music artists to come out openly as a lesbian in the early 1990s, she stands as a seminal cultural figure in LGBTQ+ representation, animal rights advocacy, and modern popular vocal performance.

## you gotta know

- Achieved global commercial and critical stardom with her 1992 studio album *Ingénue*, which showcased polished pop balladry and featured the Grammy-winning single *Constant Craving*.
- Formed her breakthrough backing group *The Reclines* in 1983 as a spirited homage to legendary country trailblazer *Patsy Cline*.
- Publicly came out as a lesbian in 1992 in *The Advocate*, cementing her status as an early, prominent queer icon in mainstream music.
- Stirred major media sensation with an iconic 1993 *Vanity Fair* cover photographed by *Herb Ritts*, featuring her dressed in a men's suit in a barbershop chair being shaved by supermodel *Cindy Crawford*.
- Delivered an acclaimed, show-stopping performance of *Leonard Cohen*'s *Hallelujah* during the opening ceremony of the 2010 Winter Olympic Games in Vancouver.
- Recorded the collaborative album *A Wonderful World* in 2002 with jazz and pop legend *Tony Bennett*, earning widespread acclaim and another Grammy Award.
- Faced radio boycotts across conservative country stations in North America following her participation in PETA's controversial "Meat Stinks" vegetarian campaign.

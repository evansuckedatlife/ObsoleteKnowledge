## summary

**Heartstopper** is a British coming-of-age graphic novel series and television adaptation created by author and illustrator *Alice Oseman*. Originating as a self-published webcomic on Tumblr and Tapas in 2016, the story chronicles the blossoming romance between secondary school students *Charlie Spring* and *Nick Nelson*. Celebrated for its wholesome, optimistic portrayal of queer teenage youth alongside delicate treatments of mental health, it became an international cultural phenomenon following its 2022 streaming debut.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Alice Oseman*, who first featured the lead characters as background figures in her 2014 debut novel *Solitaire*.
- Focuses on the relationship between *Charlie Spring*, an openly gay and anxious overthinker, and *Nick Nelson*, a popular, soft-hearted rugby player who gradually discovers his bisexuality.
- Set primarily at the fictional Truham Grammar School for Boys and the nearby Higgs School for Girls in Kent, [[england]].
- Features an ensemble supporting cast that broadens LGBTQ+ representation, including the transgender teenager *Elle Argent*, her romantic interest *Tao Xu*, and lesbian couple *Tara Jones* and *Darcy Olsson*.
- Adapted into an acclaimed live-action Netflix television series starring *Joe Locke* as Charlie and *Kit Connor* as Nick, premiering in April 2022.
- Distinguished stylistically by dynamic cartoon leaves, electric sparks, and animated flourishes that visually externalize the characters' inner emotional states and romantic turbulence.
- Addresses heavy thematic issues such as homophobic bullying, eating disorders, anorexia nervosa, and psychiatric hospitalization with compassionate, empathetic realism.

## connections

- [[england]] — the country where Truham Grammar School is located and where the narrative takes place.
- [[modernism]] — literary movement whose focus on interiority influenced contemporary coming-of-age graphic novels.
- [[realism]] — artistic tradition reflected in the series' grounded depiction of teenage life and mental health.
- [[united-states]] — major television market where the adaptation gained massive international fandom.
- [[france]] — the destination of a pivotal school trip in the narrative's third volume.

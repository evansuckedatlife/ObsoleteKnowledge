## summary

**North of 60** is a Canadian television drama series that aired on CBC Television from 1992 to 1997, set in the fictional subarctic Dene community of Lynx River, Northwest Territories. Created by *Wayne Grigsby* and *Barbara Samuels*, the series is renowned for its groundbreaking, authentic depiction of modern Indigenous life, tackling complex community dynamics, environmental issues, and cross-cultural tensions. By prioritizing Indigenous actors, directors, and screenwriters, the program became a cultural milestone in Canadian broadcasting and inspired a sequence of subsequent television films.

## you gotta know

- Set in the fictional remote community of Lynx River, situated north of the 60th parallel in Canada's Northwest Territories, and filmed largely in Bragg Creek, Alberta.
- Focused on the partnership and tensions between RCMP officer *Eric Blake* (played by *John Oliver*) and band constable *Michelle Kenidi* (played by Dene actress *Tina Keeper*).
- Featured veteran actor *Tom Jackson* as the charismatic and enigmatic community figure *Peter Kenidi*.
- Addressed contemporary social realities in northern Indigenous communities, including band politics, residential school trauma, alcoholism, land preservation, and economic development.
- Provided high-profile early career roles and sustained exposure for numerous Indigenous artists, including *Gordon Tootoosis*, *Tantoo Cardinal*, and *Wilma Pelly*.
- Garnered numerous Gemini Awards throughout its six-season run, including a 1997 Best Actress win for *Tina Keeper*.
- Continued its narrative beyond its 1997 series finale with several successful made-for-television continuation movies, such as *In the Blue Ground* and *Distant Drumming*.

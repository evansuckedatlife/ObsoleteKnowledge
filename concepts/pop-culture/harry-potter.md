## summary

**Harry Potter** is a fantasy media franchise originating from the seven-volume novel series written by British author *J. K. Rowling* between 1997 and 2007. The narrative follows the titular adolescent wizard and his companions *Ron Weasley* and *Hermione Granger* at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry as they confront the dark wizard *Lord Voldemort*. It remains one of the best-selling book series in publishing history and spawned a massive global entertainment ecosystem including blockbuster films, stage plays, and themed attractions.

## you gotta know

- Focuses on the orphan wizard known as the **Boy Who Lived**, marked by a lightning bolt scar received when *Lord Voldemort's* killing curse rebounded upon him.
- Set primarily at Hogwarts School in the Scottish Highlands, where students are sorted into four distinct houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, and Slytherin.
- Begins with *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* (published in the *United States* as *The Sorcerer's Stone*) and concludes with *The Deathly Hallows*.
- Features magical artifacts called Horcruxes, soul fragments created through murder that grant *Voldemort* immortality until methodically hunted and destroyed.
- Adapted by Warner Bros. into an eight-part cinematic franchise starring *Daniel Radcliffe*, *Rupert Grint*, and *Emma Watson*.
- Draws heavily on classical mythology and English boarding school literature traditions, weaving Latinate spells, mythological beasts, and moral coming-of-age arcs.
- Explores profound themes of prejudice, institutional corruption, death, and sacrificial love, reflecting allegorical conflicts of modern European history.

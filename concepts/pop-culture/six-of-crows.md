## summary

**Six of Crows** is a bestselling 2015 young adult fantasy novel written by American author *Leigh Bardugo* and the opening installment of a widely acclaimed duology set within her expansive *Grishaverse*. Set primarily in the maritime trade metropolis of Ketterdam, the story follows teenage criminal prodigy *Kaz Brekker* and his crew of marginalized outcasts as they orchestrate an impossible heist into the impenetrable Ice Court of Fjerda. Celebrated for its intricate point-of-view plotting, morally gray protagonists, and atmospheric world-building, the novel stands as a defining masterwork of modern fantasy literature.

## you gotta know

- Authored by *Leigh Bardugo* as an expansion of her *Grishaverse*, following her initial *Shadow and Bone* trilogy but shifting focus to the grimy, criminal underworld of Ketterdam, inspired by Dutch Golden Age Amsterdam.
- Revolves around criminal mastermind *Kaz Brekker*, known across the slums of the Barrel as "Dirtyhands", who is commissioned to retrieve scientist *Bo Yul-Bayur* from the heavily fortified Fjerdan stronghold known as the Ice Court.
- Features an iconic ensemble crew of six distinct, traumatized outcasts: criminal leader *Kaz*, acrobatic Suli spy *Inej Ghafa*, sharpshooter *Jesper Fahey*, Heartrender Grisha *Nina Zenik*, Fjerdan drüskelle witch-hunter *Matthias Helvar*, and runic demolitionist *Wylan Van Eck*.
- Centers on the catastrophic threat posed by *jurda parem*, an illicit synthetic drug that magnifies Grisha magical abilities a thousandfold while inducing fatal physical addiction and delirium.
- Formatted as a high-stakes, clockwork heist novel drawing structural inspiration from classic caper cinema like *Ocean's Eleven*, utilizing shifting third-person limited perspectives across its ensemble.
- Followed by the bestselling 2016 sequel novel *Crooked Kingdom*, concluding the Ketterdam duology before integrating into Netflix's television adaptation *Shadow and Bone*.
- Popularized the famous mutual oath exchanged by the crew members: "No mourners, no funerals", encapsulating their fatalistic survival ethos within the cutthroat criminal underworld.

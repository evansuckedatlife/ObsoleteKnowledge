## summary

**Celeste** is a celebrated 2018 precision platformer video game created by Canadian developers Maddy Thorson and Noel Berry with art by the Brazilian studio Extremely OK Games. The narrative follows Madeline, a young woman who resolves to scale the fictional Celeste Mountain while confronting severe anxiety, depression, and a supernatural manifestation of her own self-doubt. Renowned for its exacting gameplay, generous accessibility options, and poignant depiction of mental health struggles, the game achieved universal critical acclaim and became an iconic landmark of independent game development.

## you gotta know

- Players guide the protagonist through demanding obstacle courses using a simple move set comprising jumping, clinging to surfaces with limited stamina, and an eight-directional mid-air dash that recharges upon landing or collecting glowing green crystals.
- The narrative revolves around mental illness and emotional vulnerability; Madeline copes with panic attacks by visualizing a feather that floats up and down with steady rhythmic breathing.
- The primary antagonist is a mirror-world doppelgänger named Badeline—credited in-game as Part of You—who represents the heroine's internalized anxiety and fear of failure before the two reconcile and unite their powers.
- The journey across the mountain features encounters with quirky supporting figures, including the selfie-taking Seattle traveler Theo, the elderly recluse Granny Celia, and the tragic ghostly hotelier Mr. Oshiro.
- Beyond the main campaign, the title offers extensive challenging content in the form of collectible strawberries, alternate B-Side and C-Side cassette levels, and the punishing free expansion chapter titled *Farewell*.
- The game pioneered thoughtful accessibility through its Assist Mode, which allows players to modify game speed, toggle infinite stamina, or grant invincibility without punitive judgment.
- Features a critically lauded synth-driven soundtrack composed by Lena Raine, which utilizes dynamic musical layering, reversed piano motifs, and evolving melodic themes to reflect Madeline's internal psychological state.
- Originated as a minimalist four-day game jam prototype built for the PICO-8 virtual machine in 2015 before undergoing full development into a commercial release.

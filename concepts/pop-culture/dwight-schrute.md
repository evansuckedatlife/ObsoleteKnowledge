## summary

**Dwight Schrute**, born **Dwight Kurt Schrute III**, is a celebrated fictional television character portrayed by *Rainn Wilson* on the American mockumentary comedy series *The Office*, which aired on NBC from 2005 to 2013. Serving as the devoted Assistant to the Regional Manager and top paper salesman at *Dunder Mifflin* in *Scranton, Pennsylvania*, he combines aggressive authoritarianism, survivalist vigilance, and unwavering obedience to his manager *Michael Scott*. Defined by his eccentric beet-farming heritage, fierce office rivalry with *Jim Halpert*, and turbulent romance with *Angela Martin*, he evolved from an abrasive antagonist into one of the most recognizable and beloved comedic icons in modern television.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed across all nine seasons of *The Office* by actor *Rainn Wilson*, sporting a trademark mustard short-sleeved dress shirt, large aviator glasses, and a center-parted hairstyle while fiercely defending his corporate authority.
- Operates *Schrute Farms*, a sixty-acre working beet farm and agritourism bed-and-breakfast inherited from his Pennsylvania Dutch ancestors, which he runs alongside his socially uncalibrated cousin *Mose Schrute*.
- Serves as the constant target of elaborate workplace pranks executed by desk mate *Jim Halpert*, including having his desk supplies encased in gelatin, being duped by coded messages from the future, and being wrapped in wrapping paper.
- Possesses an encyclopedic devotion to martial arts, medieval weaponry, and science fiction lore, holding a black belt in Goju-Ryu karate and concealing dangerous weapons such as nunchucks and crossbows throughout the office.
- Maintains a long-running, tumultuous secret romance with conservative accountant *Angela Martin*, progressing from clandestine meetings in corporate supply closets and rigid mating contracts to their eventual marriage in the series finale.
- Held the post of volunteer sheriff's deputy for Lackawanna County with deadly seriousness until resigning in shame after providing his boss *Michael Scott* with clean urine to pass an impending corporate drug screening.
- Orchestrated the infamous fire drill in the episode "Stress Relief," causing mass panic, *Stanley Hudson's* heart attack, and an anarchic CPR training session during which he cut the face off a medical dummy in imitation of *The Silence of the Lambs*.
- Conceived as the American counterpart to *Gareth Keenan* (played by *Mackenzie Crook*) from the original British series created by *Ricky Gervais* and *Stephen Merchant*, with Wilson infusing the character with unique German folkloric traditions like dressing as *Belsnickel*.

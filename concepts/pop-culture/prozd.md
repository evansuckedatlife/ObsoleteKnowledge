## summary

**ProZD**, the widely recognized online handle of **SungWon Cho**, is an American voice actor, digital comedian, and tabletop gaming reviewer based in the *[[united-states]]*. He first attained viral fame on Vine and YouTube by writing and performing rapid-fire micro-sketches that satirized ubiquitous anime tropes, role-playing video game archetypes, and board gaming quirks. Cho successfully translated his grassroots internet success into a prominent mainstream voiceover career across high-profile video games, animated series, and English anime localizations.

## you gotta know

- Achieved widespread internet renown through deadpan, low-budget comedy videos on Vine and YouTube that affectionately mocked role-playing games, anime conventions, and board games.
- Created beloved recurring sketch personas such as the pompous anime antagonist King Dragon, the exasperated detective Archibald, and long-suffering gamers dealing with nonsensical side quests.
- Established a dedicated following as an influential board game tastemaker, producing in-depth reviews and unboxings of modern designer tabletop games such as *[[settlers-of-catan]]* and *[[dominion]]*.
- Voiced prominent characters in major video games, notably portraying Ratatoskr in *God of War Ragnarök*, the robotic hunter FL4K in *Borderlands 3*, and Holger in *Fire Emblem Warriors: Three Hopes*.
- Provided character voices for popular American animated programs and films, including Detective Gregory in *Craig of the Creek*, *Lackadaisy*, and *Oddballs*.
- Performed major dubbing roles in prominent anime releases broadcast in the *[[united-states]]*, including *Ranking of Kings*, *My Hero Academia*, *Vinland Saga*, and *One Piece*.
- Actively advocates for diversity, fair compensation, and union representation across the entertainment and voiceover industries.

## summary

**Brendan Fraser**, born **Brendan James Fraser**, is an American-Canadian actor celebrated for his charismatic work across blockbuster action-adventure films, broad physical comedies, and deeply moving dramatic cinema. After rising to global fame during the 1990s and 2000s in box-office hits like *The Mummy* and *George of the Jungle*, he later achieved a historic Hollywood comeback known colloquially as the "Brenaissance". His career reached a critical pinnacle in 2023 when he won the Academy Award for Best Actor, cementing his status as one of his generation's most enduring and versatile screen performers.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of *Charlie*, a reclusive, six-hundred-pound English teacher seeking redemption with his estranged daughter, in *Darren Aronofsky*'s 2022 psychological drama *The Whale*.
- Achieved international box-office superstardom as dashing legionnaire and adventurer *Rick O'Connell* in director *Stephen Sommers*'s blockbuster action-adventure hits *The Mummy* (1999) and *The Mummy Returns* (2001).
- Showcased brilliant comedic timing and expressive [[physical-comedy]] in early career breakthroughs, notably starring as a resurrected prehistoric human in *Encino Man* (1992) and the clumsy jungle protector in *George of the Jungle* (1997).
- Demonstrated early dramatic depth alongside legendary actors, earning critical praise as gardener *Clayton Boone* opposite *Ian McKellen* in *Gods and Monsters* (1998) and as *Alden Pyle* opposite *Michael Caine* in *The Quiet American* (2002).
- Formed part of the celebrated ensemble cast of *Crash* (2004) playing Los Angeles District Attorney *Rick Cabot*, which won the Academy Award for Best Picture.
- Stepped away from leading roles for years due to debilitating physical injuries requiring multiple spinal and vocal cord surgeries from performing his own movie stunts, alongside personal grief and industry isolation.
- Disclosed in 2018 that he had been sexually assaulted in 2003 by *Philip Berk*, the former president of the Hollywood Foreign Press Association, an experience that severely affected his mental health and career trajectory.
- Re-entered mainstream television and prestige cinema in the late 2010s, voicing and portraying *Cliff Steele* (*Robotman*) in the DC superhero series *Doom Patrol* and appearing as attorney *W.S. Hamilton* in *Martin Scorsese*'s *Killers of the Flower Moon* (2023).

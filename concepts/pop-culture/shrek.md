## summary

**Shrek** is a landmark 2001 American computer-animated fantasy comedy film directed by Andrew Adamson and Vicky Jenson, produced by DreamWorks Animation and loosely based on William Steig's 1990 picture book. Chronicling a reclusive, swamp-dwelling green ogre who embarks on a reluctant quest alongside an irrepressible talking donkey to rescue an enchanted princess for a scheming nobleman, the film became an unprecedented box-office and cultural sensation. Celebrated for its subversive parody of classic fairy-tale tropes, satirical bite, and iconic pop soundtrack, it reshaped the landscape of Western feature animation.

## you gotta know

- Won the inaugural Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2002, beating out Nickelodeon's *Jimmy Neutron: Boy Genius* and Pixar's *Monsters, Inc.* to establish DreamWorks as a premier animation powerhouse.
- Originated as a project for Chris Farley, who recorded nearly all of the lead character's dialogue before his untimely death in 1997, after which fellow *Saturday Night Live* alumnus Mike Myers re-recorded the role with a signature Scottish brogue.
- Features Eddie Murphy as the fast-talking, waffle-loving Donkey, delivering an improvised, high-energy vocal performance that became one of the most acclaimed supporting comedy roles in cinematic history.
- Centers on the tyrannical Lord Farquaad of Duloc, voiced by John Lithgow, whose diminutive stature, architectural vanity, and forced eviction of magical creatures served as a pointed satire of Disney leadership.
- Subverts the traditional romantic rescue narrative when Princess Fiona, voiced by Cameron Diaz, is revealed to carry a nighttime ogre curse herself and chooses true love's form over conventional royal perfection.
- Boasts a defining pop and alternative rock soundtrack, notably featuring Smash Mouth's hit "All Star" over the opening mud-bath credits and a poignant cover of Leonard Cohen's "Hallelujah" performed by John Cale.
- Spawned a massive multi-billion-dollar multimedia franchise including three theatrical sequels, television specials, theme park attractions, the Broadway musical *Shrek The Musical*, and the acclaimed spin-off series *Puss in Boots*.
- Selected in 2020 for preservation in the United States National Film Registry by the Library of Congress, honored as being "culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant."

## summary

**Elden Ring** is an acclaimed 2022 action role-playing game developed by Japanese studio *FromSoftware* and directed by *Hidetaka Miyazaki*, featuring worldbuilding contributions from American fantasy author *George R. R. Martin*. Set in the vast, shattered realm of the Lands Between, the game challenges players to claim Great Runes and ascend as the Elden Lord following the shattering of the titular artifact. Celebrated for its revolutionary open-world design, demanding combat, and rich environmental storytelling, it won numerous Game of the Year honors and achieved monumental commercial success.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Hidetaka Miyazaki*, expanding upon the exacting gameplay formulas established in his prior masterpieces *Demon's Souls*, *Dark Souls*, and *Bloodborne*.
- Featured worldbuilding and historical lore created in collaboration with *George R. R. Martin*, the renowned author of the *A Song of Ice and Fire* fantasy novel series.
- Casts the player as a *Tarnished*, an exile summoned back to the *Lands Between* to restore cosmic order following the shattering of the titular ring by *Queen Marika the Eternal*.
- Introduced an expansive, seamless open-world format featuring a spectral steed named *Torrent*, subterranean labyrinths, and sprawling "legacy dungeons" such as *Stormveil Castle*.
- Features notoriously challenging boss encounters against demigod shardbearers, most famously *Malenia, Blade of Miquella* and *General Radahn*.
- Received near-universal critical acclaim upon its February 2022 release, sweeping major industry accolades including Game of the Year at *The Game Awards 2022*.
- Expanded in June 2024 with the record-breaking, critically celebrated expansion *Shadow of the Erdtree*, which explored the enigmatic realm of the Land of Shadow.

## connections

- [[japan]] — home nation of developer *FromSoftware* and its influential creative director *Hidetaka Miyazaki*.
- [[skyrim]] — seminal fantasy role-playing game that set open-world standards which *Elden Ring* expanded upon a decade later.
- [[electronic-arts]] — fellow gaming giant representing the major commercial landscape of contemporary global game publishing.
- [[minecraft]] — legendary open-world title sharing *Elden Ring*'s emphasis on open-ended exploration, discovery, and player agency.
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic literary tradition reflected in the game's grim narrative arcs, fallen heroes, and cursed demigods.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — prominent gaming genre contrasting with *Elden Ring*'s deliberate, melee-focused third-person action combat.

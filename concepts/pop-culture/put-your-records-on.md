## summary

**Put Your Records On** is a critically acclaimed 2006 neo-soul and pop song by English singer-songwriter *Corinne Bailey Rae*. Released as the second single from her eponymous debut studio album, the track achieved international commercial success and earned nominations for *Record of the Year* and *Song of the Year* at the Grammy Awards. Its breezy acoustic arrangement and empowering lyrical themes established it as an enduring summer anthem of self-acceptance and unhurried optimism.

## you gotta know

- Written by *Corinne Bailey Rae* alongside producers *John Beck* and *Steve Chrisanthou* at a converted barn studio in Yorkshire, *[[england]]*.
- Opens with an effortless acoustic guitar strum and gentle funk bassline, delivering the famous introductory exhortation to three little birds and relaxed hair.
- Reached number two on the *UK Singles Chart* and crossed over into the top twenty on the American *Billboard Hot 100*.
- Features lyrics that celebrate natural beauty, self-expression, and personal liberation, encouraging young women to unwind and trust their identity.
- Accompanied by a music video directed by *Sam Brown*, showcasing Bailey Rae cycling through rural English country roads alongside friends during high summer.
- Experienced a massive global resurgence in 2020 after American indie artist *Ritt Momney* released an ethereal synthpop cover that went viral on digital platforms.
- Endures as a standard of mid-2000s British acoustic soul, cementing Bailey Rae's critical breakthrough alongside contemporaries like *Amy Winehouse* and *Joss Stone*.

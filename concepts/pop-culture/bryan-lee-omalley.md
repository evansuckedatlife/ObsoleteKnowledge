## summary

**Bryan Lee O'Malley** is a prominent Canadian cartoonist, graphic novelist, and musician celebrated as the creator of the influential *Scott Pilgrim* comic series. Born in Ontario in 1979, he reshaped twenty-first-century comic book storytelling by merging North American alternative comix with Japanese manga layouts, indie music culture, and video game iconography. His energetic visual style, witty slice-of-life dialogue, and poignant explorations of young adulthood have earned him international acclaim and major multimedia adaptations.

## you gotta know

- Authored and illustrated the six-volume graphic novel epic *Scott Pilgrim* (2004–2010), following an aimless Toronto bassist who must defeat the "Seven Evil Exes" of enigmatic rollerblader *Ramona Flowers*.
- Saw his signature work adapted into *Edgar Wright's* celebrated 2010 live-action feature *Scott Pilgrim vs. the World*, an arcade-style beat-'em-up video game, and the 2023 animated Netflix series *Scott Pilgrim Takes Off*.
- Rooted the fictional world of *Scott Pilgrim* in genuine Toronto landmarks, setting memorable scenes across real municipal locations including *Lee's Palace*, *Sneaky Dee's*, *Casa Loma*, and the historic bargain store *Honest Ed's*.
- Integrated overt video game mechanics directly into his graphic storytelling, featuring floating combat health meters, extra lives, save states, and defeated foes dissolving into piles of monetary coins.
- Wrote and illustrated the 2014 full-color graphic novel *Seconds*, following a talented young restaurant chef named *Hazel* who receives supernatural mushrooms that let her undo her daily missteps.
- Scripted the satirical graphic comic series *Snotgirl* in collaboration with illustrator *Leslie Hung*, exploring social media anxieties, allergic reactions, and murder mystery through fashion influencer *Lottie Person*.
- Recorded and self-released low-fidelity indie-pop and acoustic rock songs under the musical alias **Kupek**, channeling a personal musical history that informed his fictional garage bands like *Sex Bob-omb*.
- Published his debut graphic novel *Lost at Sea* through Oni Press in 2003, presenting an emotional, melancholic road-trip narrative centered on an introspective adolescent named *Raleigh*.

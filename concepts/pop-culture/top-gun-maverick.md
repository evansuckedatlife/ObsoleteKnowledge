## summary

**Top Gun: Maverick** is a 2022 American aviation action drama directed by *Joseph Kosinski*, serving as the high-octane legacy sequel to the iconic 1986 film *Top Gun*. Produced in the [[united-states]] and starring *Tom Cruise* as veteran naval aviator *Captain Pete "Maverick" Mitchell*, the film follows the insubordinate test pilot as he is called back to train a cohort of elite *TOPGUN* graduates for a perilous strike against an unsanctioned uranium enrichment facility. Revered for its breathtaking practical aerial cinematography, visceral sound engineering, and emotional storytelling, the production earned widespread critical adulation and served as a landmark box office phenomenon that revitalized theatrical moviegoing.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Joseph Kosinski* from a screenplay developed by *Christopher McQuarrie*, *Ehren Kruger*, and *Eric Warren Singer*, arriving thirty-six years after *Tony Scott's* original blockbuster.
- *Tom Cruise* strictly mandated the avoidance of green-screen computer graphics for flight sequences, requiring actors to endure grueling five-month flight training courses and film while pulling up to eight Gs in real *Boeing F/A-18 Super Hornet* fighter jets.
- Explores a poignant emotional arc between the protagonist and *Lieutenant Bradley "Rooster" Bradshaw* (*Miles Teller*), the resentful son of Maverick's late radar intercept officer *Nick "Goose" Bradshaw*.
- Features a climatic, multi-phase precision strike mission requiring low-altitude navigation through a heavily defended mountainous canyon followed by a steep vertical climb, evoking classic cinematic attack sequences.
- Marks an emotional, tear-filled on-screen reunion between Mitchell and his former rival *Admiral Tom "Iceman" Kazansky*, played by *Val Kilmer*, who communicated using typed text and digital voice replication following his real-life battles with throat cancer.
- Achieved staggering commercial triumph by grossing nearly 1.5 billion dollars worldwide, becoming the second-highest grossing film of 2022 and earning praise from director *Steven Spielberg* for preserving the theatrical cinematic experience.
- Nominated for six Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Adapted Screenplay, ultimately winning the Oscar for Best Sound for its immersive cockpit audio landscape.
- Accompanied by an epic musical score composed by *Hans Zimmer*, *Harold Faltermeyer*, and pop icon *Lady Gaga*, who penned the original theme ballad *Hold My Hand*.

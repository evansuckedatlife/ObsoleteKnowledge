## summary

**Kelly Clarkson** is an American singer, songwriter, and television personality who rose to prominence after winning the inaugural season of the reality singing competition *American Idol* in 2002. Known for her powerful, versatile vocal range, she successfully bridged mainstream pop, pop-rock, and country across decades of commercial acclaim. Her evolution from talent show victor to multi-Grammy-winning recording artist and daytime talk show host established her as an enduring fixture of American popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Won the first season of *American Idol* in 2002, instantly launching her career with the coronation single *A Moment Like This*, which broke the record for the biggest leap to number one on the Billboard Hot 100.
- Released the landmark pop-rock sophomore album *Breakaway* in 2004, which earned two Grammy Awards and yielded definitive international hits including *Since U Been Gone*, *Behind These Hazel Eyes*, and *Because of You*.
- Fought renowned creative battles with record executive *Clive Davis* over artistic control and darker musical themes on her self-penned 2007 studio album *My December*.
- Rebounded to pop radio dominance with the 2009 smash *My Life Would Suck Without You* and the 2011 anthem *Stronger (What Doesn't Kill You)*, both reaching number one on the Billboard Hot 100.
- Expanded successfully into country music, collaborating with artists such as *Jason Aldean* on *Don't You Wanna Stay* and releasing critically lauded country-infused pop records.
- Built a second career as a prominent television personality, serving as a coach on NBC's *The Voice* and hosting her eponymous syndicated daytime program *The Kelly Clarkson Show*.
- Performed a critically celebrated rendition of *My Country, 'Tis of Thee* at the second presidential inauguration of *Barack Obama* in Washington, D.C.

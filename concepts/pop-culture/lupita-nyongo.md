## summary

**Lupita Nyong'o**, in full **Lupita Amondi Nyong'o**, is a Kenyan-Mexican actress and author who rose to global acclaim with her breakthrough performance in the 2013 biographical drama *12 Years a Slave*. Born in Mexico City to prominent Kenyan political figures and educated at Hampshire College and the Yale School of Drama, she won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her film debut. Celebrated for her versatility across prestige drama, horror cinema, and blockbuster franchises in the *[[united-states]]*, she has also achieved distinction on the Broadway stage and as a bestselling children's author.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her harrowing feature film debut portraying the enslaved woman *Patsey* in *Steve McQueen's* historical drama *12 Years a Slave*.
- Became the first Kenyan actress and the first Mexican-born actress to win an Academy Award in any acting category.
- Delivered a masterclass dual performance as both the protective mother *Adelaide Wilson* and her monstrous scissor-wielding doppelgänger *Red* in *Jordan Peele's* 2019 psychological horror film *Us*.
- Portrayed the Wakandan War Dog spy and royal love interest *Nakia* in the Marvel Cinematic Universe blockbusters *Black Panther* and *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever*.
- Provided motion capture and voice acting for the CGI pirate queen *Maz Kanata* across the *Star Wars* sequel trilogy, beginning with *The Force Awakens*.
- Earned a Tony Award nomination for Best Actress in a Play in 2016 for her role as *The Girl* in *Danai Gurira's* Liberian Civil War stage drama *Eclipsed*.
- Wrote the 2019 bestselling picture book *Sulwe*, an autobiographical story exploring colorism and self-love that was named a Coretta Scott King Illustrator Honor book.
- Starred as the terminally ill protagonist *Samira* navigating an alien invasion with her therapy cat *Frodo* in the 2024 horror prequel *A Quiet Place: Day One*.

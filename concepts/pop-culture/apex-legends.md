## summary

**Apex Legends** is a free-to-play squad-based battle royale hero shooter developed by *Respawn Entertainment* and published by *Electronic Arts* in February 2019. Set within the frontier science-fiction universe of the *Titanfall* franchise, the game features competitive matches where squads select distinct characters known as Legends, each possessing complementary tactical, passive, and ultimate abilities. Renowned for its fluid movement mechanics, rapid momentum, and influential non-verbal communication systems, it transformed the landscape of the modern *[[first-person-shooter]]* genre and established itself as an elite platform for international *[[esports]]*.

## you gotta know

- Released as a complete surprise on February 4, 2019, eschewing traditional marketing and trailers in favor of an influencer-driven launch that garnered over 50 million players within its opening month.
- Standardized the non-verbal "Smart Comms" contextual ping system, which allows players to communicate enemies, loot, pathways, and tactics without requiring voice chat, subsequently copied across the industry.
- Categorizes its diverse roster of playable Legends into specialized gameplay classes, including Assault, Skirmisher, Recon, Support, and Controller, featuring fan-favorite launch characters such as *Wraith*, *Bloodhound*, *Lifeline*, and *Gibraltar*.
- Innovated the battle royale format by introducing mobile respawn beacons and character recovery banners, enabling teammates to revive permanently eliminated squad members long after a fight concludes.
- Takes place in the rugged Outlands following the Frontier War, where combatants compete in the televised bloodsport known as the Apex Games, overseen by the mercenary commander *Kuban Blisk*.
- Runs on a heavily upgraded and customized version of the Source engine, producing distinctive high-speed movement techniques such as slide-jumping, wall-bouncing, zipline-hopping, and tap-strafing.
- Hosts the multi-tiered Apex Legends Global Series (ALGS), a professional competitive circuit that culminates in massive international LAN tournaments with million-dollar prize pools.

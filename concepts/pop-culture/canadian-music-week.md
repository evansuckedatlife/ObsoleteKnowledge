## summary

**Canadian Music Week** (CMW) is a prominent annual entertainment industry conference and multi-venue music festival founded in 1981 and held in Toronto, Ontario. Spanning several days, the event brings together thousands of international music business executives, tech developers, broadcasters, and artists for panel discussions, networking, and club showcases. Regarded as one of Canada's most vital cultural summits, it serves as a premiere launching pad for emerging talent seeking international exposure and distribution deals.

## you gotta know

- Founded in 1981 in Toronto, Ontario, originally conceived as an annual gathering uniting radio broadcasters, record label executives, and independent artists.
- Combines a daytime business and digital media conference with a nighttime multi-venue festival across dozens of live clubs and concert halls throughout Toronto.
- Frequently interfaces with major domestic cultural organizations, complementing celebrations surrounding the *Juno Awards* and the [[canadian-music-hall-of-fame]].
- Hosts prestigious industry awards programs, including the *Canadian Indie Awards* (the Indies) and the *Canadian Music and Broadcast Industry Awards*.
- Focuses heavily on the evolving mechanics of the global music business, addressing copyright law, streaming revenue models, digital marketing, and independent touring.
- Operates an annual "Spotlight Country" program, inviting international delegations of managers, promoters, and label reps from overseas markets to foster bilateral trade.
- Has showcased early career performances by countless major acts, including *Broken Social Scene*, *Billy Talent*, *The Tragically Hip*, and international touring stars.

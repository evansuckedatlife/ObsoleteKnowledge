## summary

**Childish Gambino** is the musical alter ego of American actor, writer, director, and musician *Donald Glover*, whose cross-disciplinary work bridges hip-hop, funk, television, and cinema. Generating his stage name from an online *Wu-Tang Clan* name generator, Glover rose from internet sketch comedy and sitcom writing to become a multi-hyphenate cultural visionary. Through the award-winning television series *Atlanta* and groundbreaking musical releases like the viral, Grammy-sweeping track *This Is America*, he has cemented his status as a premier artistic voice exploring contemporary Black American life.

## you gotta know

- Released the monumental 2018 single and music video *This Is America*, which debuted at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and became the first rap song to win both Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards.
- Directed by *Hiro Murai*, the music video for *This Is America* employed startling choreography and abrupt gunfire as a powerful critique of gun violence, entertainment distractions, and anti-Black racism in the [[united-states]].
- Created, wrote, produced, and starred in the surreal FX comedy-drama series *Atlanta*, winning two Golden Globe Awards and two Primetime Emmy Awards for portraying music manager *Earnest "Earn" Marks*.
- Began his television career when *Tina Fey* hired him at age twenty-three as a staff writer on the NBC comedy *30 Rock*, drawing comedic influence from stand-up icons such as [[chris-rock]].
- Played the endearing, pop-culture-obsessed former high school quarterback *Troy Barnes* on the cult-favorite NBC comedy series *Community* alongside *Danny Pudi*'s character *Abed Nadir*.
- Transitioned toward psychedelic soul, R&B, and funk on the 2016 studio album *“Awaken, My Love!”*, which featured the multi-platinum, falsetto-driven single *Redbone*.
- Portrayed the young smuggler *Lando Calrissian* in the 2018 film *Solo: A Star Wars Story* and provided the speaking voice of adult *Simba* in the 2019 photorealistic remake of *The Lion King*.

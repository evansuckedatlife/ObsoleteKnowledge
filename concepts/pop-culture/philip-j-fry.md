## summary

**Philip J. Fry**, voiced by actor *Billy West*, is the central protagonist of the animated science-fiction television sitcom *Futurama*, created by *Matt Groening* and *David X. Cohen*. An unmotivated twentieth-century pizza delivery boy from *New York City*, he accidentally stumbles into a cryogenic pod on New Year's Eve 1999 and awakens exactly one thousand years later in the retro-futuristic metropolis of *New New York*. Working as an interplanetary delivery boy for his distant nephew's company, *Planet Express*, his endearing dim-wittedness, loyalty, and heart anchor the series through bizarre cosmic adventures.

## you gotta know

- Accidentally frozen in cryogenic suspension on December 31, 1999, awakening on December 31, 2999 to start a new life as a delivery courier for the *Planet Express* cargo service.
- Forms an iconic comedic trio with the cyclopean spaceship captain *Turanga Leela*, who serves as his enduring romantic interest, and the hedonistic, foul-mouthed robot *Bender Bending Rodríguez*.
- Discovered to be his own biological grandfather due to an accidental temporal paradox occurring in the Emmy-winning episode *Roswell That Ends Well*.
- Possesses a unique genetic immunity to the psionic mind-attacks of the evil *Brain Spawn* because his incestuous grandfather paradox left him lacking the *Delta brainwave*.
- Starred in acclaimed emotional storylines that balance humor with pathos, such as the poignant, heart-wrenching tribute to his abandoned twentieth-century dog *Seymour* in *Jurassic Bark*.
- His visual design—featuring a red jacket, blue jeans, and unkempt reddish hair—was inspired directly by *James Dean* in the classic film *Rebel Without a Cause*.

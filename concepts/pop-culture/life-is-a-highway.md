## summary

**Life Is a Highway** is an exhilarating heartland rock anthem written and originally recorded by Canadian musician *Tom Cochrane*, released in 1991 as the lead single from his breakthrough solo album, *Mad Mad World*. Born from Cochrane's visceral emotional reaction to humanitarian crises he witnessed while visiting West Africa, the song translates existential hardship into a universally uplifting message of perseverance. It achieved international chart success upon release and attained enduring pop-cultural immortality through a multi-platinum 2006 country-pop cover by American band *Rascal Flatts*.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *Tom Cochrane* following an emotionally grueling trip to Mozambique and Kenya with the humanitarian relief organization *World Vision*, where he witnessed intense poverty, famine, and sickness.
- Written initially under the title "Love Is a Highway" as an unfinished demo, which Cochrane reworked in an all-night studio session as an uplifting psychological anchor to pull himself out of post-trip depression.
- Defined by its infectious harmonica intro, propulsive guitar riffs, driving rhythm section, and Cochrane's raspy, earnest vocal delivery declaring "life is a highway, I wanna ride it all night long."
- Reached number one on the Canadian RPM singles chart, hit number six on the United States Billboard Hot 100, and earned Cochrane four *Juno Awards* in 1992, including Single of the Year and Album of the Year.
- Re-recorded in 2006 by American country group *Rascal Flatts* for the soundtrack of the *Pixar* animated blockbuster *Cars*, introducing the anthem to a vast new generation of younger listeners.
- Earned massive commercial acclaim in the *Rascal Flatts* rendition, which peaked at number seven on the Billboard Hot 100, topped country radio charts, and achieved sextuple-platinum certification.
- Features in the original music video Cochrane performing along a deserted desert highway near Alberta and Saskatchewan, accompanied by evocative imagery of classic American automobiles and roadside diners.
- Celebrated as one of the most enduring Canadian rock songs ever composed, leading to Cochrane being inducted into the Canadian Music Hall of Fame and having a section of Manitoba Provincial Trunk Highway 390 officially renamed "Life Is a Highway."

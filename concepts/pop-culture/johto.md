## summary

**Johto** is a fictional region introduced in the second generation of the *Pokémon* franchise, serving as the primary setting for *Pokémon Gold*, *Silver*, and *Crystal*, as well as their remakes *HeartGold* and *SoulSilver*. Designed as an immediate western neighbor to the earlier Kanto region, it is heavily modeled on the Kansai and Chūbu regions of central [[japan]], embodying a distinctly traditional, historic Japanese aesthetic. Its creation expanded the franchise's worldbuilding through mythic lore, architectural motifs rooted in classical Japanese heritage, and groundbreaking gameplay innovations that solidified the global cultural reach of Japanese video games.

## you gotta know

- Introduced in the 1999 Game Boy Color titles *Pokémon Gold* and *Silver*, directed by Satoshi Tajiri and featuring music and programming heavily shaped by Junichi Masuda and Satoru Iwata.
- Geographically and culturally based on the Kansai region of [[japan]], with Ecruteak City reflecting historical Kyoto through its traditional pagoda architecture and Bell Tower, and Goldenrod City paralleling modern Osaka.
- Connected directly to Kanto via the Tohjo Falls and Magnet Train, allowing players in the post-game campaign to traverse back across the original region and face *Red* atop Mt. Silver.
- Anchored by legendary creature lore centered on the Tower Duo, *Ho-Oh* and *Lugia*, who once nested upon the Tin and Brass Towers before the latter burned down and forced Lugia to the Whirl Islands.
- Features the legendary beasts *Raikou*, *Entei*, and *Suicune*, roaming creatures said to have perished in the burning tower fire and been resurrected by *Ho-Oh*.
- Introduced seminal gameplay mechanics to the franchise, including a real-time internal clock governing day-and-night cycles, distinct Pokémon breeding centers with egg hatching, held items, and the *Steel* and *Dark* elemental types.
- Governed academically by *Professor Elm* in New Bark Town, who tasks the protagonist with studying Pokémon evolution and investigating the enigmatic *Team Rocket* revival.
- Re-explored with full audiovisual enhancements in the 2009 Nintendo DS remakes *Pokémon HeartGold* and *SoulSilver*, which allowed companion creatures to walk alongside the trainer throughout the region.

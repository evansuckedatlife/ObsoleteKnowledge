## summary

**AC/DC** is an Australian hard rock band formed in Sydney in 1973 by Scottish-born brothers *Malcolm Young* and *Angus Young*. Renowned for their driving power-chord riffs, stomping four-on-the-floor rhythms, and electrifying stage antics featuring Angus in his signature British schoolboy uniform, they became one of the best-selling music acts in history. Their seminal 1980 release *Back in Black* stands among the top-selling albums worldwide, solidifying their status as monumental architects of arena rock across the [[united-states]] and the globe.

## you gotta know

- Pioneered a stripped-down, blues-infused brand of high-voltage hard rock defined by the rhythmic precision of rhythm guitarist *Malcolm Young* and blistering pentatonic solos from lead guitarist *Angus Young*.
- Endured the tragic death of charismatic original lead vocalist *Bon Scott* in February 1980, after which they recruited English singer *Brian Johnson* from the band Geordie and recorded their monumental comeback.
- Released *Back in Black* in 1980 as a tribute to *Bon Scott*, featuring the roaring opening toll of a custom bronze bell on "Hells Bells" and timeless rock staples like "You Shook Me All Night Long".
- Produced definitive 1970s international breakthrough albums with legendary producer *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*, including *Highway to Hell* (1979), whose title track remains one of rock's most famous anthems.
- Adopted their name from the electrical abbreviation for alternating current/direct current after sister *Margaret Young* noticed the letters on a sewing machine, symbolizing their raw sonic energy.
- Maintained an unyielding visual identity centered on *Angus Young* wearing a traditional British prep school uniform, duckwalking across stadium stages while headbanging uncontrollably.
- Experienced massive resurgence during the 1990s with the chart-topping single "Thunderstruck" from *The Razors Edge* (1990), produced by *Bruce Fairbairn*.
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2003, with global album sales exceeding 200 million copies, standing alongside British heavy rock innovators who emerged out of [[england]].

## connections

- [[england]] — homeland of the Glasgow-born Young brothers and longtime home of second frontman *Brian Johnson*.
- [[united-states]] — pivotal market where *Back in Black* earned 25x Platinum certification and broke attendance records.
- [[british-empire]] — historical migration pathways that brought the Young family from Scotland to Australia in the 1960s.

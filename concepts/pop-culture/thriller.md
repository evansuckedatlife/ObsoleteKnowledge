## summary

**Thriller** is the monumental sixth studio album by American singer-songwriter *Michael Jackson*, released on Epic Records in November 1982, as well as its iconic title track and music video. Produced by *Quincy Jones*, the album obliterated racial barriers in mainstream pop broadcasting, set enduring commercial records as the bestselling album in global history, and fundamentally transformed the music video into a cinematic art form. Its fusion of pop, rock, funk, post-disco, and horror aesthetics made it a defining commercial phenomenon of the late twentieth century.

## you gotta know

- Stands as the bestselling album of all time worldwide, certified over thirty times Platinum in the [[united-states]] with estimated worldwide sales surpassing seventy million copies.
- Produced an unprecedented seven Billboard Hot 100 top-ten singles, including "Beat It," "Billie Jean," "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," and the horror-themed title track "Thriller."
- Enlisted master producer *Quincy Jones* and British songwriter *Rod Temperton*, who penned the title track and arranged its signature theatrical elements.
- Features legendary horror icon *Vincent Price*, who delivered the famous closing spoken-word rap and chilling theatrical laughter.
- Accompanied by a transformative fourteen-minute horror short film directed by *John Landis*, showcasing elaborate zombie creature choreography and cinematic [[film-editing]].
- Broke deep-seated racial segregation barriers on early MTV, forcing the burgeoning cable network to broadcast Black artists in heavy rotation.
- Yielded the historic March 1983 television performance of "Billie Jean" on *Motown 25: Yesterday, Today, Forever*, where Jackson debuted his signature moonwalk dance step.

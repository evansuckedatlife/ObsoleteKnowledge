## summary

**Giorgio Armani** is an acclaimed Italian fashion designer renowned for revolutionizing luxury menswear and womenswear in the late twentieth century through his softly tailored, deconstructed suits. Founding his eponymous fashion house in Milan in 1975, he stripped rigid padding and stiff interlinings from traditional tailoring to create a fluid, relaxed silhouette that defined executive elegance. His international breakthrough exploded after designing the impeccably sleek wardrobe for Richard Gere in the 1980 film *American Gigolo*, transforming modern cinematic styling and red-carpet glamour.

## you gotta know

- Revolutionized global tailoring by removing structural shoulder pads, canvas interfacings, and stiff lining from men's suits, popularizing an unconstructed drape that came to symbolize understated luxury and power dressing.
- Achieved global fame and cultural ubiquity after designing the wardrobe for Richard Gere's character Julian Kaye in Paul Schrader's stylish neo-noir *American Gigolo* in 1980.
- Founded Giorgio Armani S.p.A. in Milan in 1975 alongside his architectural and business partner Sergio Galeotti, having previously worked as a window dresser at *La Rinascente* department store and as a designer under *Nino Cerruti*.
- Pioneered red-carpet relationships with Hollywood celebrities in the 1980s and 1990s, opening a Beverly Hills boutique and dressing stars such as Jodie Foster and Michelle Pfeiffer for high-profile Academy Award broadcasts.
- Expanded his luxury empire into sub-labels across varying price points, creating *Emporio Armani*, *Armani Exchange*, high-end cosmetics, and ultra-exclusive haute couture under *Armani Privé*.
- Became the first fashion designer to ban models with a body mass index (BMI) under 18 from his runway presentations following the tragic death of model Ana Carolina Reston in 2006.
- Expanded into architectural hospitality by opening luxury *Armani Hotels* in prestigious landmarks including the Burj Khalifa in Dubai and Via Manzoni in Milan.

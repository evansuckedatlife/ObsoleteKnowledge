## summary

**Loewe** is a luxury Spanish fashion house founded in Madrid, renowned as one of the world's oldest continuously operating purveyors of luxury leather goods and ready-to-wear. Established originally by a cooperative of Spanish leather artisans, the brand gained international prominence after Hessian craftsman *Enrique Loewe Roessberg* consolidated the workshop under his name in 1872. Today operating under the French luxury conglomerate *LVMH*, the house has earned critical acclaim in modern pop culture and contemporary high fashion for surrealist designs, artistic runway concepts, and iconic handbag silhouettes.

## you gotta know

- Founded in Madrid, *Spain* in 1846 by a group of Spanish leather craftsmen, making it older than many iconic luxury peers like *Louis Vuitton* and *Hermès*.
- Joined in 1872 by German merchant craftsman *Enrique Loewe Roessberg*, who unified the artisan collective into a consolidated luxury firm bearing his surname.
- Appointed as the Official Supplier to the Royal Court of Spain in 1905 by *King Alfonso XIII*, earning Queen Victoria Eugenie's patronage and cementing its aristocratic status.
- Acquired in 1996 by the French multinational luxury goods conglomerate *LVMH*, integrating historic Iberian craftsmanship into the global fashion market alongside Parisian couture.
- Entered a major modern renaissance following the 2013 appointment of Northern Irish designer *Jonathan Anderson* as creative director, celebrated for playful, architectural silhouettes such as the geometric "Puzzle Bag".
- Reached the summit of global fashion influence in recent years, dressing pop icons like *Beyoncé* for the *Renaissance World Tour* and *Rihanna* for the Super Bowl LVII halftime show with surrealist, sculptural breastplates.
- Renowned for its distinctive anagram logo, historically styled after branding irons used for cattle and leather hides, famously redesigned by typography and graphic design legends *Vicente Vela* and later *M/M Paris*.

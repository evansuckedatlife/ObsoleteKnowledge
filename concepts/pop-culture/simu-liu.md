## summary

**Simu Liu** is a Canadian actor, author, and stunt performer who rose to global prominence as the title superhero in Marvel Studios' 2021 blockbuster *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*. Born in Harbin, China, and raised in Ontario, he transitioned from corporate accounting into television acting, gaining widespread acclaim on the CBC television *[[situational-comedy]]* *Kim's Convenience*. Known for his martial arts athleticism, social media presence, and vocal advocacy for Asian representation in Western cinema, he has emerged as a high-profile Hollywood leading man.

## you gotta know

- Gained breakout television recognition portraying Jung Kim, the estranged, athletic son of Korean-Canadian store owners on the CBC television *[[situational-comedy]]* *Kim's Convenience* from 2016 to 2021.
- Cast as Shang-Chi in the Marvel Cinematic Universe's *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings* (2021), marking the first Asian superhero lead in Marvel Studios history.
- Transitioned into stunt performance, background acting, and stock photography modeling after being laid off from his position as an accountant at Deloitte in Toronto.
- Publicly campaigned on Twitter for Marvel to develop an Asian superhero film years before being officially cast, a social media interaction that later became viral franchise lore.
- Performed extensive martial arts choreography and wirework for *Shang-Chi*, drawing praise for intricate hand-to-hand fight sequences on a runaway transit bus and scaffolding in Macau.
- Portrayed one of the competing variations of Ken (Tourist Ken) alongside Ryan Gosling and Margot Robbie in Greta Gerwig's 2023 blockbuster fantasy comedy *Barbie*.
- Published an autobiographical family memoir in 2022 titled *We Were Dreamers: An Immigrant Superhero Origin Story*, chronicling his family's immigration journey and generational reconciliation.
- Voiced characters in animated films and voice-acted in video games, while remaining an active voice on media diversity, diaspora storytelling, and Asian-American cultural representation.

## summary

**Home (Michael Bublé song)** is a poignant pop and traditional pop ballad recorded by Canadian vocalist *Michael Bublé*, released in 2005 as the second single from his studio album *It's Time*. Co-written by Bublé alongside *Amy Foster-Gillies* and *Alan Chang*, the song depicts the emotional ache and deep longing of a touring performer wishing to return to his loved one. The single became an enormous worldwide crossover hit, topping the adult contemporary charts in Canada and the *[[united-states]]*.

## you gotta know

- Released in early 2005 as the signature original hit from Bublé's multi-platinum studio album *It's Time*, produced by music industry veteran *David Foster*.
- Topped the *Billboard Adult Contemporary* chart in the *[[united-states]]*, solidifying Bublé's standing as a premier contemporary crooner capable of creating original pop standards.
- Conceived during a grueling European touring schedule while Bublé was deeply missing his then-fiancée, actor *Debbie Timuss*, who provided backing vocals on the original studio track.
- Opens with an intimate acoustic guitar riff accompanied by contemplative piano lines before building into an emotionally swelling orchestral string arrangement.
- Features prominent reflective lyrics capturing nomadic loneliness in foreign cities, opening with the memorable lines "Another summer day has come and gone away in Paris and Rome."
- Reached the top ten on the *UK Singles Chart* and won the prestigious *Juno Award* for Single of the Year in 2006.
- Covered notably by American country music artist *Blake Shelton* in 2008, whose version reached number one on the *Billboard Hot Country Songs* chart.

## summary

**Dan Aykroyd** is a Canadian-American comedian, actor, screenwriter, and musician who rose to prominence as an original cast member on *Saturday Night Live* in 1975. Renowned for his rapid-fire delivery, encyclopedic monologues, and sharp characterizations, he co-created and starred in monumental cinematic comedies such as *The Blues Brothers* and *Ghostbusters*. His versatile contributions to sketch comedy, screenwriting, and character acting established him as an enduring architect of modern American screen humor.

## you gotta know

- Rose to national fame as one of the original "Not Ready for Prime-Time Players" on *Saturday Night Live* alongside *John Belushi*, *Chevy Chase*, and *Gilda Radner*.
- Famously portrayed President *Richard Nixon*, fast-talking pitchman *Bass-O-Matic* salesman *Irwin Mainway*, and alien patriarch *Beldar Conehead* during his formative sketch television career in *[[new-york-city]]*.
- Formed the rhythm-and-blues tribute band and comedy duo *The Blues Brothers* as "Elwood Blues" with his close friend and creative partner *John Belushi*.
- Co-wrote and starred in the 1980 musical comedy film *The Blues Brothers*, directed by *John Landis*, which became a cult classic celebrated for elaborate car chases and authentic musical performances.
- Conceived, co-wrote, and starred in the 1984 supernatural comedy smash *Ghostbusters* as Dr. Raymond Stantz, drawing on his lifelong family interest in spiritualism and the paranormal.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor for portraying the loyal, gentle son Boolie Werthan in the 1989 drama *Driving Miss Daisy*.
- Co-founded the commercial entertainment venue chain *House of Blues* in 1992 to celebrate and preserve African-American musical traditions and blues heritage.

## summary

**J. K. Rowling** is a British author, philanthropist, and film producer best known for creating the world-renowned *Harry Potter* fantasy book series. Born in Gloucestershire, [[england]], she conceived the story of the orphaned wizard while delayed on a train journey from Manchester to London in 1990. Her seven-book sequence grew into one of the most commercially successful and influential media franchises in global publishing and cinematic history.

## you gotta know

- Authored the record-breaking seven-volume *Harry Potter* series, which sold more than 500 million copies worldwide and was adapted into an eight-film blockbuster franchise.
- Conceived the central premise of a young boy discovering he is a wizard while stuck on a delayed train between Manchester and London King's Cross in 1990.
- Wrote initial chapters of *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* while living on state welfare as a single mother in Edinburgh, Scotland, frequently drafting in local cafes.
- Adopted the pen name *Robert Galbraith* to publish an acclaimed contemporary crime fiction series centered on private investigator *Cormoran Strike*.
- Co-wrote and produced the *Fantastic Beasts* prequel film series and collaborated on the stage play *Harry Potter and the Cursed Child*.
- Founded the international children's charitable organization *Lumos*, dedicated to ending the institutionalization of vulnerable children worldwide.
- Drew substantial critical attention and public controversy in later years for her outspoken public commentary regarding transgender issues and gender identity legislation.

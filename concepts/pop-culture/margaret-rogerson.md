## summary

**Margaret Rogerson** is an acclaimed American young adult fantasy author known for her lush, atmospheric worldbuilding and subversions of traditional folklore. Rising to prominence in contemporary [[american-literature]], she writes intricate standalone and series fantasy that frequently interrogates the perils of artistic devotion, forbidden enchantment, and dangerous fae courts. Her critically acclaimed bestsellers have established her as an influential voice in twenty-first-century speculative fiction across the [[united-states]].

## you gotta know

- Debuted in 2017 with the *New York Times* bestselling novel *An Enchantment of Ravens*, following a human portrait painter named *Isobel* who creates art for immortal fair folk.
- Authored the gothic high-fantasy hit *Sorcery of Thorns* in 2019, centered on an apprentice librarian named *Elisabeth Scrivener* who tends to sentient, dangerous grimoires that transform into monsters if damaged.
- Published *Vespertine* in 2021, exploring a grim fantasy realm inspired by medieval and [[renaissance]] themes, where an unyielding nun bonds with an ancient, malevolent revenant.
- Released *Mysteries of Thorn Manor* in 2023 as a companion novella to *Sorcery of Thorns*, satisfying widespread reader acclaim for her character dynamics.
- Holds a degree in anthropology from *Miami University* of Ohio, an academic background that deeply informs her cultural worldbuilding and societal magic systems.
- Consistently praised by critics for witty, bantering dialogue, original non-traditional romance arcs, and lush descriptions of sensory aesthetic craft.

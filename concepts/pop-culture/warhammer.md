## summary

**Warhammer** (encompassing **Warhammer Fantasy** and its futuristic counterpart **Warhammer 40,000**) is a British franchise of miniature wargames, fictional universes, and multimedia lore created by *Games Workshop*. Originating in Nottingham, [[england]], in 1983 through the design work of *Bryan Ansell*, *Richard Halliwell*, and *Rick Priestley*, the franchise combines tabletop tactical combat with assembling and painting detailed plastic and pewter miniatures. Renowned for pioneering the bleak, satirical sci-fi subgenre known as *grimdark*, it represents one of the most influential hobby gaming properties in modern pop culture.

## you gotta know

- Launched in 1983 as *Warhammer Fantasy Battle*, a tabletop ruleset that drew heavily upon high fantasy, Renaissance history, and dark humor, before expanding into the grimdark science-fiction universe *Warhammer 40,000* (*40K*) in 1987.
- Produced by *Games Workshop*, a company founded by *Steve Jackson*, *John Peake*, and *Ian Livingstone* that operates dedicated retail hobby shops and publishes modeling literature through *White Dwarf* magazine.
- Established the aesthetic and literary archetype of *grimdark*, popularized by the famous franchise tagline: "In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war."
- Centers its science-fiction narrative around the *Imperium of Man*, a decaying, xenophobic, theocratic empire ruled nominally by the desiccated, psychic *God-Emperor of Mankind* entombed upon the *Golden Throne*.
- Features genetically engineered supersoldiers known as *Space Marines* (or *Adeptus Astartes*), organized into legendary chapters like the *Ultramarines*, *Blood Angels*, and the traitorous legions turned to the cosmic forces of *Chaos*.
- Details the foundational historical tragedy known as the *Horus Heresy*, a galaxy-spanning civil war ignited when the favored Warmaster *Horus Lupercal* fell to Chaos and led half the Space Marine legions against the Emperor.
- Replaced the original fantasy setting in 2015 with *Warhammer Age of Sigmar* following the apocalyptic *End Times* narrative event, introducing a dynamic, planar mythological cosmology.
- Spawned acclaimed video game adaptations across diverse genres, including real-time strategy hits like *Dawn of War*, tactical turn-based titles, and co-operative action games like *Vermintide* and *Space Marine*.

## connections

- [[england]] — country of origin where *Games Workshop* established its headquarters and miniature manufacturing foundry in Nottingham.
- [[settlers-of-catan]] — landmark modern hobby board game that, along with miniature wargaming, catalyzed the global tabletop gaming resurgence.
- [[dominion]] — designer tabletop game whose engine-building mechanics share the hobbyist board game ecosystem cultivated by specialized hobby stores.
- [[ticket-to-ride]] — influential modern Eurogame representing the mainstream family board game movement that developed parallel to tabletop wargames.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — prominent video game genre that frequently adapted the visceral weaponry, power armor, and gothic battlegrounds of the franchise.
- [[weasley]] — fellow British pop-culture icon reflecting England's distinctive late-twentieth-century fantasy literature and worldbuilding heritage.

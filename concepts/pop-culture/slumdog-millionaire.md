## summary

**Slumdog Millionaire** is a 2008 British drama film directed by *Danny Boyle* and written by *Simon Beaufoy*, adapted from Indian author *Vikas Swarup*'s novel *Q & A*. The film chronicles the life of *Jamal Malik*, an eighteen-year-old orphan from the Dharavi slums of Mumbai, who surprises the nation by answering every question correctly on the Indian television game show *Kaun Banega Crorepati*. Celebrated for its kinetic editing, propulsive score, and poignant depiction of destiny and urban inequality, the film became an international box-office sensation and swept eight Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Director.

## you gotta know

- Swept eight Academy Awards at the 81st Oscars, including Best Picture, Best Director for *Danny Boyle*, Best Adapted Screenplay, and two statuettes for composer *A. R. Rahman*.
- Structured around a nonlinear narrative framing device where *Jamal Malik*, brutally interrogated by police under suspicion of cheating, recounts formative life traumas that explain how he acquired the answer to each trivia question.
- Launched the international screen careers of British-Indian lead actor *Dev Patel*, who portrayed the adolescent Jamal, and Indian actress *Freida Pinto*, who played his lifelong love *Latika*.
- Featured a kinetic, genre-blending musical score composed by *A. R. Rahman*, whose infectious celebratory dance anthem "Jai Ho" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and became a worldwide chart-topping crossover hit.
- Adapted faithfully in core dramatic premise from Indian diplomat *Vikas Swarup*'s celebrated 2005 debut novel *Q & A*, while streamlining characters and shifting the setting to Mumbai.
- Shot on location in Mumbai using pioneering lightweight digital cameras (*Silicon Imaging SI-2K*), enabling dynamic handheld cinematography deep within the labyrinthine alleyways of the Dharavi slums.
- Concludes with an exuberant, choreographed Bollywood-style dance sequence on a Mumbai railway station platform during the closing credits, honoring traditional Indian popular cinema.

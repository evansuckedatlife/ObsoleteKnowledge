## summary

**Glass Animals** is an English psychedelic pop and indie rock band formed in Oxford in 2010, fronted by producer, singer, and songwriter *Dave Bayley* alongside childhood friends *Drew MacFarlane*, *Edmund Irwin-Singer*, and *Joe Seaward*. The group achieved global stardom through their atmospheric production, syncopated hip-hop percussion, and narrative-driven lyricism, transitioning from niche underground critical darlings to stadium headliners. They are best known for their record-breaking sleeper hit single *Heat Waves*, which mounted the longest climb to number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in chart history and cemented their status as a defining mainstream crossover act of the early 2020s.

## you gotta know

- Formed by *Dave Bayley*, who studied neuroscience at university before committing to music, which heavily influenced the atmospheric, sensory-rich textures of their early compositions.
- Released the landmark viral smash *Heat Waves* in 2020, a melancholy reflection on longing that climbed to the top of the United States charts after an unprecedented 59-week ascent.
- Released their debut album *Zaba* in 2014, featuring tropical trip-hop grooves, lush organic percussion, and the breakthrough track *Gooey*, which brought the band significant alternative radio play.
- Recorded the concept album *How to Be a Human Being* in 2016, building every song around an eccentric character inspired by secret recordings *Bayley* made of strangers while on tour.
- Faced potential dissolution in 2018 when drummer *Joe Seaward* suffered a near-fatal bicycle collision that caused complex skull fractures and temporary loss of speech and movement, leading to an extensive recovery period.
- Followed their global breakthrough with the introspective autobiographical album *Dreamland* in 2020, immersing their sound in 1990s and early-2000s electronic nostalgia.
- Released the space-themed album *I Love You So F***ing Much* in 2024, exploring existential isolation, romance, and cosmic imagery inspired by *Bayley* experiencing a sensory overload during a stormy retreat.

## summary

**Norman Jewison** was an acclaimed Canadian film director, producer, and founder of the Canadian Film Centre whose career spanned more than four decades across Hollywood and international cinema. Celebrated for addressing controversial social and political themes with commercial flair, he directed landmark works including *In the Heat of the Night*, *Fiddler on the Roof*, and *Moonstruck*. His humanistic, genre-spanning filmography earned forty-six Academy Award nominations, twelve Oscars, and the prestigious Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award in 1999.

## you gotta know

- Directed the groundbreaking 1967 racial murder mystery *In the Heat of the Night*, which starred *Sidney Poitier* and *Rod Steiger* and won the Academy Award for Best Picture.
- Nominated three times for the Academy Award for Best Director: for *In the Heat of the Night* (1967), *Fiddler on the Roof* (1971), and *Moonstruck* (1987).
- Helmed the popular 1966 Cold War satire *The Russians Are Coming, the Russians Are Coming*, ridiculing American Red Scare hysteria.
- Pioneered multi-screen cinematic storytelling in the 1968 stylish heist thriller *The Thomas Crown Affair*, starring *Steve McQueen* and *Faye Dunaway*.
- Directed high-profile musical and dramatic adaptations, including the 1973 rock opera *Jesus Christ Superstar* and the 1984 military racial drama *A Soldier's Story*.
- Founded the Canadian Film Centre (CFC) in Toronto in 1988 to cultivate Canadian screenwriting, acting, and filmmaking talent.
- Directed *Denzel Washington* in the 1999 biographical sports drama *The Hurricane*, chronicling the wrongful imprisonment of boxer *Rubin Carter*.
- Honored by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences with the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award in 1999 for his lifelong achievements as a creative producer.

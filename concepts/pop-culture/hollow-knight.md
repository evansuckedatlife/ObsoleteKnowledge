## summary

**Hollow Knight** is a critically acclaimed 2017 Metroidvania action-adventure video game developed and published by the independent Australian studio *Team Cherry*. Players control **The Knight**, a silent, nameless warrior exploring the vast, ruined underground kingdom of *Hallownest* populated by anthropomorphic insects. Renowned for its hand-drawn art, haunting atmospheric score, tight combat, and subtle environmental storytelling, it is widely considered one of the finest indie video games ever produced.

## you gotta know

- Follows **The Knight**, a small insectoid wanderer wielding a blade known as a *Nail*, traversing the labyrinthine subterranean ruins of *Hallownest*.
- Developed by the core trio of *Ari Gibson*, *William Pellen*, and composer *Christopher Larkin* at Adelaide-based indie developer *Team Cherry* following a successful Kickstarter campaign.
- Explores a kingdom devastated by the *Infection*, a supernatural plague of orange light that robs insects of their minds and binds them to the ancient moth deity known as *The Radiance*.
- Centers on the tragic legacy of the *Pale King*, who created empty artificial beings forged from the substance of *Void* in an attempt to contain the contagion.
- Employs souls-like death mechanics where dying leaves behind a hostile *Shade* that holds the player's accumulated *Geo* currency until defeated.
- Features intricate non-linear exploration, platforming gauntlets like the *Path of Pain*, and demanding multi-phase boss fights against warriors like *Hornet* and the *Hollow Knight*.
- Highly anticipated for its long-awaited narrative sequel focusing on the needle-wielding princess-protector, titled *Hollow Knight: Silksong*.

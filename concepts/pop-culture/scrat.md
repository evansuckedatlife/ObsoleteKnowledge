## summary

**Scrat** is a fictional prehistoric saber-toothed squirrel created by Blue Sky Studios who serves as the breakout comic mascot of the animated *Ice Age* film franchise. Obsessed to the point of existential mania with securing and burying a single elusive acorn, he perpetually stumbles through parallel B-plots that humorously trigger cataclysmic geographical changes across the prehistoric world. Developed entirely without spoken dialogue, the character stands as one of modern computer animation's greatest tributes to the golden age of classical cinematic physical comedy.

## you gotta know

- Voiced throughout the film franchise by Blue Sky Studios co-founder and director *Chris Wedge*, who performed his character's iconic panting, squeaking, grunts, and shrieks of agony.
- Designed as a hybrid paleontological creature combining the characteristics of a squirrel and a saber-toothed cat with enormous bulging eyes and bushy fur.
- Driven by a single-minded, Sisyphean fixation on an acorn, whose routine burial attempts inadvertently trigger continental drift, glacial avalanches, planetary alignment shifts, and tectonic ruptures.
- Functions entirely as a pantomime character without verbal dialogue, relying strictly on body language, facial contortions, and orchestral musical cues.
- Headlined several acclaimed animated theatrical short films, including the Academy Award-nominated *Gone Nutty* in 2002 and the time-traveling romp *No Time for Nuts* in 2006.
- Encountered a romantic rival and sultry flying squirrel love interest named *Scratte* in the 2009 installment *Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs*, forcing him to choose between romantic passion and botanical nuts.
- Became the subject of an extended copyright dispute with artist *Ivy Silberstein*, which concluded shortly before Disney closed Blue Sky Studios, culminating in a poignant 2022 farewell short where he finally eats the acorn.

## summary

**Winona Ryder** is an acclaimed American actress who became an iconic cultural figure of Generation X through a succession of eccentric, melancholic, and rebellious roles in the late 1980s and 1990s. Renowned for collaborating with visionary directors such as Tim Burton and Martin Scorsese, she earned critical praise and consecutive Academy Award nominations before mounting a celebrated career renaissance in the 2010s on television.

## you gotta know

- Achieved breakout stardom playing goth teenager Lydia Deetz in Tim Burton's 1988 dark supernatural comedy *Beetlejuice*, later reprising the role in the 2024 sequel.
- Cemented her alternative cultural icon status starring as Veronica Sawyer opposite Christian Slater in Michael Lehmann's 1989 black comedy *Heathers*.
- Collaborated again with Tim Burton as Kim Boggs in the fantasy romance *Edward Scissorhands*, acting alongside Johnny Depp in a modern fairytale setting.
- Earned back-to-back Academy Award nominations in the mid-1990s: Best Supporting Actress for Martin Scorsese's *The Age of Innocence* and Best Actress as Jo March in Gillian Armstrong's adaptation of *Little Women*.
- Starred in Bram Stoker's *Dracula* as Mina Harker alongside Gary Oldman, and co-produced and starred in the 1999 psychological drama *Girl, Interrupted* as Susanna Kaysen.
- Named Winona Laura Horowitz at birth in Minnesota, her godfather was counterculture LSD advocate Timothy Leary, reflecting her family's beatnik artistic circles.
- Achieved a massive career resurgence beginning in 2016 starring as the fiercely determined mother Joyce Byers in the hit science-fiction series *Stranger Things*.

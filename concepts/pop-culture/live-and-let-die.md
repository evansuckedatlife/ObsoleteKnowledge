## summary

**Live and Let Die** is a 1973 British spy film directed by *Guy Hamilton*, serving as the eighth entry in the long-running *James Bond* cinematic franchise and adapting the 1954 novel of the same name by *Ian Fleming*. The film marks the debut of actor *Roger Moore* as the fictional MI6 agent *007*, taking over the iconic mantle from *Sean Connery*. Set against the backdrop of 1970s geopolitical tensions and urban crime, the narrative follows Bond as he unravels an illicit international heroin-distribution conspiracy orchestrating the murders of British undercover agents across the *[[united-states]]* and the Caribbean.

## you gotta know

- Introduced *Roger Moore* in his first official screen performance as secret agent *James Bond*, initiating a suave, lighter, and more humorously detached era that lasted across seven consecutive films.
- Featured an Oscar-nominated, chart-topping title track composed by *Paul McCartney* and his band *Wings*, orchestrated by legendary *Beatles* producer *George Martin*, which became one of the most celebrated rock anthems in cinema history.
- Adapted narrative elements heavily influenced by contemporary American blaxploitation cinema, moving the franchise away from traditional *[[cold-war]]* European espionage into urban Harlem and bayou settings.
- Starred *Yaphet Kotto* in a memorable dual role as Caribbean prime minister *Dr. Kananga* and his alter ego, New York drug lord *Mr. Big*, who plots to monopolize the heroin trade by distributing free narcotics.
- Cast *Jane Seymour* in her breakthrough screen role as *Solitaire*, a mystical tarot reader whose clairvoyant abilities guide Kananga until she loses her powers after being seduced by Bond.
- Introduced the eccentric, tobacco-spitting Louisiana lawman *Sheriff J. W. Pepper*, played by *Clifton James*, who proved popular enough with audiences to reappear in *The Man with the Golden Gun*.
- Achieved a Guinness World Record during production for a thrilling speed-boat jump sequence through the Louisiana bayous, leaping a distance of 110 feet across a roadway.
- Marked the first *James Bond* film in which *007* does not order his signature vodka martini shaken, not stirred, instead requesting bourbon while visiting *[[new-york-city]]*.

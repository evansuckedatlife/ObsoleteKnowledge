## summary

**Doctor Who** is an iconic British science-fiction television programme produced by the BBC, recognized as the longest-running science-fiction television series in broadcast history since its 1963 premiere. The series chronicles the journeys of **The Doctor**, an eccentric extraterrestrial Time Lord from the planet *Gallifrey* who roams across space and time to defend civilizations from galactic tyrants. Traveling inside the **TARDIS**, a sentient time machine permanently shaped like a British police call box, the Doctor relies on intellect, moral courage, and various human companions to triumph over hostile alien species.

## you gotta know

- Debuted on BBC television on November 23, 1963, premiering with the serial *An Unearthly Child* starring *William Hartnell* as the First Doctor.
- Introduced the narrative mechanism of "regeneration" in 1966 to explain recasting when Hartnell's health declined, allowing the Time Lord to transform physically and mentally while retaining past memories.
- Traverses the universe inside the **TARDIS** (*Time and Relative Dimension in Space*), whose broken "chameleon circuit" froze its exterior as a 1960s London police telephone box that is dimensionally transcendental ("bigger on the inside").
- Achieved legendary status during the classic era under *Tom Baker* (the Fourth Doctor), famous for his bohemian demeanor, multi-colored knit scarf, wide-brimmed fedora, and fondness for offering *Jelly Babies*.
- Regularly battles iconic extraterrestrial adversaries, most famously the hateful cybernetic mutants known as *Daleks* from Skaro (who screech "Exterminate!"), the emotionless *Cybermen*, the predatory *Weeping Angels*, and fellow rogue Time Lord *The Master*.
- Refuses conventional firearms in favour of ingenious non-violent problem-solving gear, most prominently the ubiquitous *sonic screwdriver* and identification-spoofing *psychic paper*.
- Revived successfully in 2005 by executive producer *Russell T Davies*, featuring successive modern incarnations portrayed by *Christopher Eccleston*, *David Tennant*, *Matt Smith*, *Peter Capaldi*, and *Jodie Whittaker* (the first woman cast in the leading role).
- Pioneered electronic music on television through its iconic theme score, written by *Ron Grainer* and realized by *Delia Derbyshire* at the innovative *BBC Radiophonic Workshop*.

## connections

- [[doctor-strange]] — American pop-culture counterpart also introduced in 1963 who similarly wields arcane, reality-bending lore under the title of an eccentric Doctor.
- [[england]] — home country of the BBC and the cultural wellspring that defines the series' distinctive British tone and eccentric humor.
- [[british-empire]] — historic global imperial power whose post-colonial anxieties and historical expeditions heavily influenced early time-travel serials.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical era whose anxieties over nuclear destruction and totalitarian uniformity directly shaped the creation of villains like the Daleks.
- [[world-war-ii]] — historical conflict repeatedly visited in the series, serving as a primary thematic model for the Daleks' fascistic ideology.
- [[modernism]] — mid-century aesthetic movement that inspired the experimental electronic tape music developed by the BBC Radiophonic Workshop.

## summary

**Dark** is an acclaimed German science fiction thriller television series co-created by *Baran bo Odar* and *Jantje Friese* that streamed on *Netflix* from 2017 to 2020. Set in the gloomy, fictional town of *Winden*, the plot begins with the mysterious disappearance of young children and unfurls across multiple generations linked by a wormhole in a nearby subterranean cave system. Renowned for its intricate narrative architecture, deterministic philosophy, and melancholic mood, the series stands as a landmark in complex speculative fiction and international prestige television.

## you gotta know

- Follows four interconnected families—the *Kahnwalds*, *Nielsens*, *Dopplers*, and *Tiedemanns*—whose generational secrets, romantic betrayals, and genealogical paradoxes drive the labyrinthine narrative.
- Revolves around interconnected thirty-three-year intervals connecting the years 1888, 1921, 1953, 1986, 2019, and 2052, governed by an antique brass time machine built by clockmaker *H.G. Tannhaus*.
- Centers on the protagonist *Jonas Kahnwald*, who discovers that his older wandering self is *The Stranger* and that his ultimate incarnation is the disfigured temporal warlord *Adam*, leader of the cult *Sic Mundus*.
- Sets *Adam* in an ideological war against the elderly *Claudia Tiedemann*, known as the "White Devil," who manipulates the causal loop across decades while searching for an elusive path to break the cycle.
- Triggers its core bootstrap paradox when *Mikkel Nielsen* vanishes into the cave portal in 2019, emerges in 1986, grows up under the alias *Michael Kahnwald*, and ultimately fathers *Jonas*.
- Expands in its final season to incorporate an alternate parallel world governed by *Eva*, revealing that both dying realities originated from a tragic grief experiment conducted by *Tannhaus* in the real world.
- Features prominent recurring motifs including yellow raincoats, the Latin motto *Sic Mundus Creatus Est*, the *Emerald Tablet*, and the ominous *Winden Nuclear Power Plant* that precipitates the apocalypse.

## connections

- [[germany]] — historical and cultural setting of the production, reflecting national environmental debates surrounding nuclear energy and provincial claustrophobia.
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic structure where characters make desperate, agonized choices to avert catastrophes that those very choices end up causing.
- [[existentialism]] — philosophical engine of the narrative, probing determinism and *Arthur Schopenhauer*'s maxim that human beings can do what they will but cannot will what they will.
- [[christianity]] — source of religious symbolism, including the opposing leaders named *Adam* and *Eva*, the thirty-three-year lunar-solar cycles, and themes of original sin.
- [[cold-war]] — geopolitical backdrop shaping the 1986 timeline and the pervasive sense of nuclear disaster haunting twentieth-century Europe.
- [[dark-souls]] — companion dark speculative work centered on endless doomed cycles of recurrence, temporal decay, and agonizing sacrifices to maintain or break reality.

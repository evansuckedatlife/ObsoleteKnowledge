## summary

**Red Bull** is a carbonated functional energy drink formulated with caffeine, taurine, B-complex vitamins, and sucrose, manufactured by the Austrian conglomerate **Red Bull GmbH**. Founded in 1984 by Austrian entrepreneur *Dietrich Mateschitz* and Thai businessman *Chaleo Yoovidhya*, the beverage was modified from the popular Thai tonic *Krating Daeng* to appeal to Western consumers during the late *[[cold-war]]* era. The brand revolutionized modern corporate lifestyle marketing by largely eschewing traditional television advertising in favor of extreme sports ownership, media creation, and competitive *[[esports]]*.

## you gotta know

- Adapted directly from the Southeast Asian tonic *Krating Daeng*, which translates literally to red gaur, an uncarbonated energy drink originally marketed toward manual laborers, transport workers, and long-distance drivers in Thailand.
- Promoted globally through the ubiquitous, rhyming advertising slogan promising that the beverage gives you wings, which prompted a high-profile class-action false-advertising legal settlement in the *[[united-states]]*.
- Controls a dominant empire in international motorsport, owning two active *Formula One* constructors, notably *Red Bull Racing*, which captured multiple World Drivers' Championships with *Sebastian Vettel* and *Max Verstappen*.
- Built a multi-club global association football network, establishing and managing competitive soccer clubs including *RB Leipzig* in *[[germany]]*, *FC Red Bull Salzburg* in Austria, and the *New York Red Bulls* in *[[new-york-city]]*.
- Pioneered signature proprietary extreme sports spectacles such as *Red Bull Rampage*, a dangerous premier freeride mountain-biking competition, and the *Red Bull Flugtag*, where teams pilot homemade human-powered flying machines off water piers.
- Sponsored the historic 2012 *Red Bull Stratos* mission, wherein Austrian base jumper *Felix Baumgartner* rode a helium balloon into the stratosphere and broke the sound barrier in unassisted human freefall.
- Established an expansive commercial footprint across competitive *[[esports]]*, financing premier tournament circuits, fielding elite professional teams, and running dedicated training facilities for titles in the *[[first-person-shooter]]* genre such as *[[counter-strike]]*.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — major Western marketplace where consumer protection lawsuits challenged the company's literal interpretation of its winged advertising claims.

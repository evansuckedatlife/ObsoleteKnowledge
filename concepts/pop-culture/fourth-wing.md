## summary

**Fourth Wing** is a runaway bestselling romantic fantasy novel published in 2023 by American author Rebecca Yarros, serving as the debut entry in *The Empyrean* series. Set in the war-torn nation of Navarre, the narrative chronicles twenty-year-old Violet Sorrengail as she is thrust into the ruthless Riders Quadrant at Basgiath War College, where cadets must bond with deadly dragons or die trying. The novel achieved global publishing dominance, fueling the meteoric popularization of the "romantasy" commercial publishing subgenre in contemporary [[american-literature]].

## you gotta know

- Centers on protagonist Violet Sorrengail, a physically frail scholar trained to enter the quiet Scribe Quadrant, whose mother, the commanding general of Navarre, forcibly orders her into the brutal and lethal Riders Quadrant.
- Introduces Basgiath War College, an elite military academy where cadets endure brutal hazing, lethal training trials, and tests of survival including crossing the treacherous "Parapet" walkway just to gain entrance.
- Features a signature dragon-bonding system where cadet riders risk incineration during "Threshing"; Violet improbably bonds two distinct dragons: the diminutive, golden-feathered Andarna and the massive, feared black dragon Tairn.
- Explores a high-stakes enemies-to-lovers romantic subplot with Xaden Riorson, the wing leader of Fourth Wing whose rebellious father led an insurrection against Navarre's crown, leaving Xaden and other children marked by treason relic scars.
- Characters channel raw elemental magic through their bonded dragons known as "signets"; Violet develops the devastating ability to manifest and command raw lightning strikes.
- Propelled to the top of international bestseller lists in the [[united-states]] and worldwide through massive organic virality on the social media platform TikTok (BookTok).
- Followed by immediate blockbuster commercial sequels, including *Iron Flame* (2023) and *Onyx Storm*, expanding Navarre's dark geopolitical secrets and the external threat posed by venin and wyvern creatures.

## connections

- [[american-literature]] — landmark 2020s commercial fiction phenomenon that redefined modern mass-market publishing and bestseller records.
- [[united-states]] — home country of author Rebecca Yarros and the principal launching pad for the novel's initial publishing release.
- [[skyrim]] — shares high-fantasy tropes including dragon lore, dangerous mountain keeps, and combat between elemental magic-wielders.
- [[tragedy]] — incorporates harrowing casualties, fatal betrayals, and systemic wartime sacrifice throughout cadet training.
- [[ancient-greece]] — echoes classical martial training academies like Spartan agoges and mythical tales of bonds forged with divine beasts.
- [[realism]] — intentionally contrasts heightened fantasy magic with the realistic physical toll of chronic illness and joint hypermobility mirrored in its heroine.

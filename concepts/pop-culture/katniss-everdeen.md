## summary

**Katniss Everdeen** is the fictional protagonist and narrator of *Suzanne Collins*'s dystopian trilogy *The Hunger Games*, published between 2008 and 2010. Hailing from the impoverished coal-mining region of District 12 in the nation of Panem, she volunteers for a televised gladiatorial death match to save her younger sister, *Primrose*. Known as *The Girl on Fire* and *The Mockingjay*, she inadvertently ignites a nationwide revolution against the totalitarian rule of the Capitol and *President Coriolanus Snow*, becoming one of the most defining heroines in modern speculative fiction.

## you gotta know

- Voluntarily takes the place of her younger sister *Primrose Everdeen* as tribute during the 74th annual Hunger Games reaping ceremony.
- Expertly uses a bow and arrow, a survival skill learned from her deceased father to illegally hunt in the off-limits forests outside District 12.
- Forces the Capitol to declare dual victors by staging a suicide pact with poisonous nightlock berries alongside fellow tribute *Peeta Mellark*.
- Earned the moniker *The Girl on Fire* after stylist *Cinna* designs dazzling flame-themed costumes that captivate Capitol audiences.
- Re-enters the arena during the 75th Hunger Games (the Third Quarter Quell) and destroys the forcefield by firing an electrified arrow into the arena roof.
- Becomes the reluctant figurehead, dubbed *The Mockingjay*, for the rebellion orchestrated by *District 13* and its calculating leader *President Alma Coin*.
- Executes *President Coin* instead of the deposed *President Snow* during a public execution, recognizing Coin's willingness to perpetuate tyrannical cycles.
- Portrayed by actress *Jennifer Lawrence* in the blockbuster four-film cinematic adaptation released by Lionsgate.

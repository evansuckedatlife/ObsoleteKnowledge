## summary

**Man! I Feel Like a Woman!** is a landmark country-pop crossover single recorded by Canadian singer-songwriter *Shania Twain* and released in 1999 as part of her blockbuster 1997 album *Come On Over*. Written by *Twain* and producer *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*, the song became a global feminist anthem known for its infectious spoken intro and celebratory embrace of female empowerment. Its immense commercial success helped redefine mainstream country music and firmly cemented *Twain* as an international pop phenomenon in the [[united-states]] and worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Opens with the signature spoken catchphrase "Let's go, girls!", which became one of the most famous and widely recognized vocal tags in modern popular music.
- Served as a cornerstone single on *Come On Over*, which went on to become the best-selling studio album by a female artist in music history.
- Won the *Grammy Award for Best Female Country Vocal Performance* in 2000 and earned a nomination for *Record of the Year*.
- Featured an iconic music video directed by *Paul Hunter* that visually paid homage to *Robert Palmer*'s 1985 video for "Addicted to Love," with *Twain* backed by a stone-faced, all-male band dressed in identical attire.
- Employs an assertive lyrical narrative championing carefree female independence, nocturnal liberation, and freedom from patriarchal constraints.
- Achieved multi-platinum sales globally and became a staple across sports arenas, drag performances, karaoke stages, and pop-culture celebrations.

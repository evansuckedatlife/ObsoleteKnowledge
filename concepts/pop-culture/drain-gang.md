## summary

**Drain Gang** (formerly known by monikers including **Gravity Boys**, **Shield Gang**, and **Smörgasboard**) is an influential Swedish artistic collective and musical group founded in *Stockholm* in 2013. Composed of vocalists *Bladee*, *Ecco2K*, and *Thaiboy Digital* alongside producers *Whitearmor* and *Yung Sherman*, the group pioneered an ethereal, post-digital fusion of cloud rap, emo rap, ambient trance, and hyperpop. Through an uncompromising DIY internet ethos, esoteric lyricism, and cutting-edge fashion design, the collective cultivated a dedicated worldwide cult following that transformed contemporary underground music.

## you gotta know

- Established in *Stockholm*, *Sweden* around 2013 by childhood collaborators who initially uploaded raw music files under aliases like **Gravity Boys** before formalizing their collective identity.
- Defined a groundbreaking sonic signature anchored by *Whitearmor*'s crystalline synthesizers, polyrhythmic trap 808s, and heavily autotuned, melancholic vocal textures.
- Led creatively by prolific frontman *Bladee*, whose expansive catalog—including projects like *Eversince*, *Red Light*, and *The Fool*—charts an evolution from despairing alienation to spiritual transcendence.
- Pioneered a distinct post-internet visual aesthetic merging Y2K computer iconography, luxury Scandinavian fashion, fantasy video games, and cyber-spirituality.
- Maintained a sister collective relationship with fellow *Stockholm* visionary *Yung Lean* and his *Sad Boys* crew, frequently sharing producers and releasing collaborative albums like *Trash Island* and *D&G*.
- Left a massive imprint on the late-2010s soundcloud rap and hyperpop movements, directly influencing artists across underground digital microgenres and mainstream avant-garde pop.
- Fostered acclaimed solo endeavors within the group, notably *Ecco2K*'s ethereal art-pop milestone *E* and *Thaiboy Digital*'s anthemic solo output on *Legendary Member*.

- [[existentialism]] — pervasive lyrical theme examining numbness, isolation, hyper-connectivity, and the search for spiritual meaning in the digital age.
- [[minecraft]] — foundational aesthetic touchstone whose nostalgic pixelated worlds and sandbox creativity informed the collective's early internet culture.
- [[counter-strike]] — emblem of Swedish LAN gaming culture and online youth communities that shaped the group's adolescent social sphere.
- [[esports]] — deeply tied to the visual language, digital distribution channels, and gaming aesthetics that underpinned the collective's merchandise and artwork.
- [[eminem]] — serves as an aesthetic opposite, contrasting traditionalist lyrical rap with Drain Gang's heavily pitch-corrected, impressionistic vocal melodies.
- [[dua-lipa]] — represents the mainstream end of European electronic dance production, which draws indirect inspiration from underground sound experiments.

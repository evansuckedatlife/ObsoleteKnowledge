## summary

**Alexisonfire** is an influential Canadian post-hardcore band formed in St. Catharines, Ontario, in 2001. Celebrated for pioneering a dynamic vocal interplay that balanced raw screaming with soaring melodic counterpoints, the group became a defining act of the 2000s alternative rock and emo scenes. Their distinctive sound earned multiple platinum certifications in Canada and international acclaim, cementing their legacy as leaders in modern heavy music.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 2001 by guitarists *Dallas Green* and *Wade MacNeil*, bassist *Chris Steele*, drummer *Jesse Ingelevics*, and lead screamer *George Pettit*.
- Derived their unusual band name from contortionist and adult performer *Alexis Fire*, adding the suffix "on fire" after an early trademark dispute.
- Built a signature three-vocalist dynamic contrasting *George Pettit*'s aggressive shrieks, *Dallas Green*'s soulful clean vocals, and *Wade MacNeil*'s gritty harmonies.
- Released their critically acclaimed self-titled debut album in 2002, featuring the breakout single *Pulmonary Archery*.
- Achieved widespread mainstream breakthrough with the 2004 sophomore album *Watch Out!* and their 2006 platinum-selling magnum opus *Crisis*, which drew lyrical inspiration from real-world historical disasters like the Great Lakes Blizzard of 1977.
- Clean vocalist and rhythm guitarist *Dallas Green* established an internationally successful acoustic-folk solo project under the moniker *City and Colour*.
- Announced a high-profile hiatus and farewell tour in 2011 before reuniting for festival appearances and releasing their fifth studio album, *Otherness*, in 2022.

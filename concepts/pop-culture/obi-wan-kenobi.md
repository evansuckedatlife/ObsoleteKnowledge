## summary

**Obi-Wan Kenobi** is a fictional Jedi Master in the *Star Wars* franchise created by American filmmaker *George Lucas*. Introduced as a solitary desert hermit in 1977 and later explored as a central protagonist of the prequel trilogy, he bridges classical mythological mentorship with the fall of a galactic republic. The character remains an enduring touchstone of American science-fiction cinema, celebrated for embodying noble stoicism, self-sacrifice, and master swordsmanship.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed in the original film trilogy by legendary English actor *Sir Alec Guinness*, who earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor for *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope*.
- Played as a younger Jedi Knight and general throughout the prequel trilogy by Scottish actor *Ewan McGregor*, who later reprised the role in a self-titled streaming television series.
- Trained under Jedi Master *Qui-Gon Jinn*, whom he avenged by defeating the Sith Lord *Darth Maul* on Naboo after his master fell in combat.
- Served as the direct mentor and surrogate brother to *Anakin Skywalker*, whose tragic turn to the dark side culminated in their fateful duel on the volcanic planet Mustafar.
- Adopted the pseudonym *Ben Kenobi* while living in exile on Tatooine to secretly watch over and protect the young *Luke Skywalker*.
- Introduced the foundational lore of the Force, famously describing it as an energy field created by all living things that surrounds and binds the galaxy together.
- Allowed himself to be struck down by his former pupil aboard the Death Star, becoming one with the Force and continuing to guide heroes as a spiritual apparition.

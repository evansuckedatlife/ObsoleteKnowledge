## summary

**Mega Man**, known in *Japan* as *Rockman*, is a legendary platforming action video game series created by *Capcom* and launched on the *Nintendo Entertainment System* in 1987. The saga depicts a brave robotic youth engineered by the benevolent *Dr. Thomas Light* to thwart the world-conquering ambitions of the mad scientist *Dr. Albert Wily*. Revered for its nonlinear stage selection, punishing platform challenges, and celebrated chip-tune soundtracks, the franchise established foundational gameplay standards that helped define the side-scrolling action genre.

## you gotta know

- Established an innovative nonlinear gameplay structure that allows players to select any of the initial *Robot Master* stages in arbitrary order from a central hub screen.
- Introduced the signature weapon-copying mechanic, whereby defeating a boss awards their special attack armaments, establishing an intricate rock-paper-scissors weakness cycle across the entire roster.
- Developed by a celebrated creative team at *Capcom*, including character artist and producer *Keiji Inafune*, lead game designer *Akira Kitamura*, and composer *Manami Matsumae*.
- The character's signature blue armor was chosen specifically to maximize detail and tonal variation within the strict color palette limitations of the 8-bit *Nintendo Entertainment System*.
- Conceived originally in lore as a humble domestic laboratory assistant named *Rock*, paired with his sister android *Roll*, who volunteered for weaponized cybernetic conversion when Wily reprogrammed industrial service robots for war.
- Expanded the core cast in subsequent titles by introducing the faithful canine transformable support unit *Rush*, the elusive scarf-wearing elder brother *Proto Man*, and rival creation *Bass*.
- Spawned several celebrated narrative spin-offs and successor sub-series, including the futuristic *Mega Man X*, the role-playing series *Mega Man Battle Network*, and the exploratory *Mega Man Legends*.

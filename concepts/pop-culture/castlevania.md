## summary

**Castlevania**, created by Konami and known domestically in *Japan* as **Akumajō Dracula**, is an influential gothic horror action-adventure video game series inaugurated in 1986. The franchise chronicles the perennial generational crusade waged by the Belmont dynasty of vampire hunters against Count *Dracula*, who periodically reawakens to unleash supernatural devastation upon humanity. Renowned for its exacting platforming, classical-rock musical scores, and nonlinear exploratory design, the series co-founded the foundational "Metroidvania" genre.

## you gotta know

- Centers on the heroic Belmont bloodline, whose members wield the consecrated whip known as the *Vampire Killer* alongside secondary consecrated weapons such as holy water, boomerangs, and silver daggers.
- Revolutionized video game design with the 1997 masterpiece *Symphony of the Night*, introducing role-playing progression, equippable weapons, and map exploration that established the industry term "Metroidvania".
- Follows the tragic half-vampire protagonist *Alucard*, the dhampir son of Count *Dracula* and a mortal woman named Lisa, who awakens from torpor to foil his father's vengeful war against humankind.
- Features iconic multi-generational protagonists who brave the demonic citadel, including *Simon Belmont*, his ancestor *Trevor Belmont*, and the agile martial artist *Richter Belmont*.
- Evolved under the longtime artistic stewardship of producer Koji Igarashi, who established the series' intricate timeline, interconnected castle structures, and gothic aesthetic sensibilities.
- Achieved widespread critical acclaim for its haunting musical compositions scored by Michiru Yamane, Kinuyo Yamashita, and other Konami composers, yielding enduring themes like *Vampire Killer* and *Bloody Tears*.
- Inspired a celebrated animated television adaptation produced for Netflix, depicting the struggle of Trevor, the magician *Sypha Belnades*, and Alucard against the forces of darkness.

## connections

- [[japan]] — the nation where developer Konami conceived the franchise and produced its foundational installments.
- [[skyrim]] — the landmark dark-fantasy video game that shares thematic conventions of dungeon exploration, supernatural curses, and vampire lore.
- [[cardinal]] — the high ecclesiastical office whose institutional authority, religious iconography, and gothic vestments are evoked throughout the series' church lore.
- [[tragedy]] — the classical dramatic mode embodied in Dracula's mournful descent into monstrous tyranny following the execution of his human wife.
- [[modernism]] — the artistic movement whose twentieth-century forms stand in stylistic contrast to the neo-gothic romanticism championed by the franchise.
- [[carrie-bradshaw]] — the contemporary late-twentieth-century pop-culture protagonist who emerged in the same broadcast era during the franchise's television and gaming peak.

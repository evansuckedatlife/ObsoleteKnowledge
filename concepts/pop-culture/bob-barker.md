## summary

**Bob Barker** was a celebrated American television personality who achieved legendary status as the longtime host of the CBS daytime game show *The Price Is Right* from 1972 until his retirement in 2007. Over five decades on network airwaves, his genial wit, signature long-stemmed microphone, and impeccable comic timing made him a reassuring fixture of daily broadcast entertainment. A passionate animal rights advocate, Barker permanently embedded himself in American popular consciousness through his daily sign-off and high-profile public philanthropy.

## you gotta know

- Hosted *The Price Is Right* continuously for thirty-five years, steering the program through thousands of pricing games including *Plinko*, *The Clock Game*, and the *Showcase Showdown*.
- Concluded every single broadcast with his iconic animal welfare advocacy catchphrase: "Help control the pet population. Have your pets spayed or neutered."
- Began his national television hosting career in 1956 as the charismatic frontman of the audience-participation stunt program *Truth or Consequences*, a post he held for eighteen years.
- Grew up on the Rosebud Indian Reservation in South Dakota, where he was an enrolled member of the *Sioux* tribe through his mother's lineage.
- Enlisted in the United States Navy as a naval aviation fighter pilot during [[world-war-ii]], completing flight training before the conflict concluded.
- Made a memorable, self-parodying comedic film cameo in the 1996 sports comedy *Happy Gilmore*, engaging in a profane, bare-knuckle brawl with *Adam Sandler* on a golf course.
- Served for two decades as the master of ceremonies for both the *Miss USA* and *Miss Universe* pageants before dramatically resigning in 1988 over organizers' refusal to eliminate animal fur coats from prize packages.
- Won nineteen Daytime Emmy Awards, including fourteen for Outstanding Game Show Host, culminating in a Lifetime Achievement Emmy Award in 1999.

## summary

**George Lucas** is an American film director, screenwriter, producer, and entrepreneur whose monumental cinematic sagas fundamentally transformed Hollywood production, visual effects, and merchandising. After breaking out with the dystopian science fiction feature *THX 1138* and the nostalgic coming-of-age hit *American Graffiti*, he created the era-defining *Star Wars* space opera franchise and co-created the *Indiana Jones* adventures with *Steven Spielberg*. Beyond directing, he pioneered modern digital filmmaking, computer animation, and cinematic sound by founding groundbreaking companies such as *Industrial Light & Magic* (*ILM*), *Lucasfilm*, *Skywalker Sound*, and *THX*.

## you gotta know

- Created the iconic *Star Wars* epic space-opera saga, writing and directing the 1977 original film *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope* as well as the prequel trilogy beginning with *The Phantom Menace*.
- Co-created the globetrotting *Indiana Jones* film series starring *Harrison Ford*, developing the story concepts alongside frequent creative collaborator *Steven Spielberg*.
- Founded *Industrial Light & Magic* (*ILM*) in 1975 to invent custom motion-control miniature cameras and digital visual effects, revolutionizing modern visual spectacles.
- Established *Lucasfilm* and later created the computer graphics division that was spun off and incorporated as *Pixar Animation Studios* under *Steve Jobs*.
- Directed the critical and commercial triumph *American Graffiti* in 1973, capturing early 1960s California cruising culture and earning five Academy Award nominations.
- Earned his start under mentor *Francis Ford Coppola*, collaborating on the New Hollywood indie studio *American Zoetrope* after studying cinema at the *University of Southern California*.
- Championed pioneering digital motion-picture photography and nonlinear digital [[film-editing]], directing *Attack of the Clones* as the first major studio feature shot entirely on high-definition digital cinema cameras.

- [[western-film]] — American genre that served as a core narrative template for frontier planets, scoundrels, and desert standoffs in his space operas.
- [[cold-war]] — historical backdrop influencing the technological optimism, political subtext, and imperial motifs of his science fiction works.

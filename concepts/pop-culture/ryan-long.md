## summary

**Ryan Long** is a Canadian comedian, sketch satirist, filmmaker, and podcaster whose viral video characters and sharp cultural commentary made him a prominent voice in modern digital comedy. Originally establishing his comedic presence in Toronto before relocating to [[new-york-city]], he gained international attention by producing fast-paced, independently distributed internet video shorts that satirize contemporary socio-political polarization, media posturing, and cultural orthodoxy. His comedic approach blends guerrilla street interviews, mockumentary realism, and rapid-fire visual gags, representing an innovative twenty-first-century evolution of topical [[slapstick-comedy]] and [[physical-comedy]] within the independent digital creator economy.

## you gotta know

- Rose to widespread viral fame in 2020 with high-concept satirical video sketches such as "When Woke and Racist People Agree on Everything", which garnered tens of millions of views across online video platforms.
- Hosts the popular independent weekly comedy podcast *The Boyscast with Ryan Long*, featuring comedic cultural commentary, industry banter, and improvisational riffing with recurring guests.
- Released independent full-length stand-up comedy specials, including *White Immigrant* (2022), exploring his experiences as a Canadian immigrant comic adjusting to American metropolitan life.
- Created, directed, and starred in satirical broadcast comedy series in Canada, including *Ryan Long Is Challenged* and *Torontopia* on BiteTV, earning multiple Canadian Comedy Award nominations.
- Gained early underground prominence as the lead vocalist and guitarist for the Toronto-based ska-punk band *The Johnstones*, touring widely and directing satirical music videos broadcast on MuchMusic.
- Developed an agile digital production workflow, writing, shooting, editing, and releasing multiple polished character sketches each week without relying on traditional broadcast network backing.
- Regularly produces deadpan "man on the street" interview videos in major metropolitan public spaces, deploying absurd inquiries to highlight ideological contradictions and public eccentricities.
- Operates a successful independent digital subscription model and comedy production company, bypassing traditional Hollywood studio gatekeepers to fund stand-up tours and creative projects.

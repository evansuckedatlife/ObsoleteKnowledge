## summary

**Alexey Pajitnov** is a Soviet-born computer engineer and video game designer celebrated worldwide as the original creator of *Tetris*, one of the most popular and influential video games in history. Born **Alexey Leonidovich Pajitnov** in Moscow, he conceived and programmed the falling-block puzzle title in 1984 while conducting speech-recognition and artificial intelligence research at the *Dorodnitsyn Computing Centre* of the Soviet Academy of Sciences. Because all intellectual property created by state employees was owned by the government under the [[soviet-union]], he did not receive royalties for his masterpiece until founding *The Tetris Company* in the [[united-states]] more than a decade later. His enduring design established foundational principles of addictive casual gaming, spatial reasoning puzzles, and cross-platform electronic entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Created *Tetris* in June 1984 on an indigenous *Electronika 60* computer, drawing inspiration from classical pentomino board games and simplifying the shapes into falling four-square polyominoes.
- Gained worldwide fame after *Nintendo* bundled *Tetris* with its revolutionary *Game Boy* handheld console in [[japan]] and North America, selling tens of millions of cartridges globally.
- Coined the title *Tetris* by inventing a portmanteau of the Greek numerical prefix *tetra-* (denoting the four square blocks forming each falling piece) and *tennis*, his favorite sport.
- Earned no initial royalties or financial compensation for his invention because software copyrights were seized by the Soviet foreign trade monopoly *Elektronorgtechnica* (*Elorg*) during the [[cold-war]].
- Became the focal point of an international licensing battle when Dutch entrepreneur *Henk Rogers* travelled secretly to Moscow to outmaneuver rival Western publishers and secure console rights.
- Collaborated with sixteen-year-old student *Vadim Gerasimov* and colleague *Dmitry Pavlovsky* to port the game to *MS-DOS* on the *IBM PC*, sparking wild underground piracy across the Eastern Bloc.
- Emigrated to the [[united-states]] in 1991 with the personal help of *Henk Rogers*, eventually reclaiming his original game copyright in 1996 and co-founding *The Tetris Company* to manage the global brand.
- Designed innovative puzzle games at *Microsoft* between 1996 and 2005, publishing critically praised titles including the puzzle adventure *Pandora's Box* and the tile-matching Xbox classic *Hexic*.

## connections

- [[soviet-union]] — birth nation where he conducted scientific research and created his most celebrated game under communist state property laws.
- [[cold-war]] — tense international geopolitical environment that restricted his personal earnings and spurred complex espionage-like copyright negotiations in Moscow.
- [[united-states]] — western nation where he settled in 1991, reclaimed his commercial software rights, and built a successful corporate career at *Microsoft*.
- [[japan]] — home of *Nintendo*, the hardware manufacturer whose handheld console transformed his cerebral puzzle design into a global cultural phenomenon.
- [[minecraft]] — modern gaming sensation that carries forward his design philosophy of simple geometric shapes, procedural block arrangement, and spatial creativity.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming scene that embraced his foundational puzzle creation through the enduring annual *Classic Tetris World Championship*.
- [[tetris]] — masterwork falling-block puzzle game that defined his career and remains an immortal benchmark of elegant video game architecture.

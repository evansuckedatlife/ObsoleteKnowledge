## summary

***Make Way for Ducklings*** is a beloved American children's picture book written and illustrated by *Robert McCloskey*, first published in 1941 by Viking Press. Awarded the prestigious 1942 *Caldecott Medal*, the story follows a pair of mallards who search for an ideal nesting place and eventually raise their ducklings in the heart of *Boston*. Renowned for its sepia-toned lithographic drawings, the work stands as an enduring classic of mid-twentieth-century urban storytelling and public art.

## you gotta know

- Won the 1942 *Caldecott Medal* for distinguished American picture book illustration, cementing *Robert McCloskey*'s reputation as a master author-illustrator.
- Follows *Mr. and Mrs. Mallard*, who scout potential homes in *Boston* before nesting on an island in the Charles River basin.
- Features the famous rhyming names of the eight ducklings: *Jack*, *Kack*, *Lack*, *Mack*, *Nack*, *Ouack*, *Pack*, and *Quack*.
- Showcases the friendly Boston police officer *Michael*, who feeds peanuts to the ducks and halts bustling city automobile traffic on *Beacon Street* so the family can safely cross.
- Ends with the duckling family happily establishing their permanent home upon the pond in the historic *Boston Public Garden*.
- Illustrated with distinct brown sepia-toned lithographs drawn directly onto zinc plates by McCloskey, who famously kept live ducklings in his New York apartment for anatomical study.
- Commemorated by a celebrated set of bronze statues crafted by sculptor *Nancy Schön* in the *Boston Public Garden*, which are frequently dressed in seasonal attire and sports uniforms.

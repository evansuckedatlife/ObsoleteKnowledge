## summary

**Netflix** is an American multinational entertainment and media company that pioneered the transition of home video consumption from physical mail-order DVD rentals to global subscription video-on-demand streaming. Founded in 1997 in Scotts Valley, California, by *Reed Hastings* and *Marc Randolph*, the company disrupted traditional broadcast television models and theatrical release windows. Through its data-driven recommendation algorithms and aggressive financing of original television series and feature films, it catalyzed the modern digital streaming era and transformed worldwide viewing habits.

## you gotta know

- Started in 1997 as a flat-fee DVD-by-mail rental business that eliminated late fees, outcompeting and precipitating the eventual bankruptcy of retail giant *Blockbuster*.
- Introduced its video-on-demand streaming service in 2007, originally offered as a free value-add feature for its established physical disc rental subscribers.
- Launched its original programming era in 2013 with the political drama *House of Cards*, directed by *David Fincher*, popularizing the cultural practice of binge-watching by releasing entire seasons simultaneously.
- Produced massive global cultural hits including the supernatural 1980s homage *Stranger Things*, the historical drama *The Crown*, and the record-breaking South Korean survival drama *Squid Game*.
- Revolutionized algorithmic recommendations via the 2006–2009 *Netflix Prize*, an open competition offering one million dollars to improve its collaborative filtering software.
- Expanded into prestige cinema production, garnering numerous Academy Award nominations for auteur-driven films such as *Alfonso Cuarón*'s *Roma* and *Martin Scorsese*'s *The Irishman*.
- Restructured its global business model in the 2020s by introducing lower-cost ad-supported subscription tiers and enforcing strict measures against multi-household password sharing.

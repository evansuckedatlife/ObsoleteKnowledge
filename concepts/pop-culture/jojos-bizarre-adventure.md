## summary

**JoJo's Bizarre Adventure** is an influential Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hirohiko Araki, serialized since 1987 in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* and later in *Ultra Jump*. Spanning multiple generations, the narrative follows successive descendants of the heroic Joestar bloodline as they confront supernatural villains across the globe. Celebrated for its flamboyant aesthetic, dramatic character posing, inventive tactical combat, and extensive references to Western popular music, the franchise has become a landmark of Japanese visual culture with widespread international anime, gaming, and fashion adaptations.

## you gotta know

- Created by Hirohiko Araki and first published in 1987 in Shueisha's *Weekly Shōnen Jump*, making it one of the longest-running and highest-selling manga series in publication history.
- Structured into distinct, chronologically sequential story parts, each centered on a different protagonist nicknamed "JoJo," beginning with Jonathan Joestar in Victorian [[england]] and continuing through Jotaro Kujo, Giorno Giovanna, and Jolyne Cujoh.
- Introduced the groundbreaking supernatural power system of "Stands" in its third story arc, *Stardust Crusaders*, replacing earlier *Hamon* sun-breathing martial arts with physical manifestations of a user's fighting spirit.
- Features the perennial antagonist *Dio Brando*, a vampire who steals Jonathan Joestar's body and casts a multi-generational curse of conflict over the entire Joestar family lineage.
- Takes place across varied global settings, including Victorian [[england]], interwar Europe and America, Egypt, suburban [[japan]] in Morioh, and organized-crime syndicates throughout [[italy]] in *Golden Wind*.
- Famous for naming numerous characters, abilities, and Stands after prominent Western rock musicians, bands, and albums, including *Queen*, *Led Zeppelin*, *AC/DC*, *King Crimson*, and *The Beatles*.
- Renowned for its distinctive art style, characterized by exaggerated anatomical poses, high-fashion sensibilities inspired by classical European sculpture, and vibrant shifts in color palettes during dramatic sequences.
- Adapted into a widely acclaimed television anime series by David Production beginning in 2012, which sparked widespread internet meme culture and global pop-cultural visibility.

## connections

- [[japan]] — home country of creator Hirohiko Araki, publisher Shueisha, and the setting for storylines such as *Diamond Is Unbreakable*.
- [[england]] — the historical Victorian setting of the original arc *Phantom Blood*, detailing the origins of Jonathan Joestar and Dio Brando.
- [[italy]] — primary geographical and cultural setting for the fifth part, *Golden Wind*, tracking Giorno Giovanna's rise within the Passione mafia.
- [[johto]] — contemporary Japanese fictional pop-culture territory from the same era of serialized multimedia expansion and stylized creature manifestations.
- [[tragedy]] — narrative mode underpinning the doomed struggles, generational sacrifices, and noble deaths throughout the Joestar bloodline.

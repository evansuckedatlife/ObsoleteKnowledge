## summary

**Baldur's Gate 3** is a landmark turn-based role-playing video game developed and published by the Belgian studio *Larian Studios*, released in full in August 2023. Set in the celebrated *Dungeons & Dragons* Forgotten Realms universe along the Sword Coast, the game provides an unprecedented digital translation of the tabletop 5th Edition ruleset, emphasizing dynamic environmental physics, emergent player choice, and cinematic narrative branching. It achieved overwhelming critical acclaim and commercial success, sweeping Game of the Year honors at every major gaming ceremony and establishing a new high watermark for modern role-playing design.

## you gotta know

- Developed over six years under the direction of *Larian Studios* founder *Swen Vincke*, utilizing feedback from a three-year early access release to refine its expansive world and intricate systemic reactivity.
- Achieved an unprecedented clean sweep of major Game of the Year accolades in 2023, capturing top honors at the Golden Joystick Awards, The Game Awards, the D.I.C.E. Awards, the Game Developers Choice Awards, and the BAFTA Games Awards.
- Anchored around a narrative premise where the player protagonist and their diverse companions are infected with mind flayer (illithid) parasites, sparking a desperate race for a cure that uncovers a conspiracy driven by the cult of the Absolute.
- Features three primary antagonists known as the Chosen of the Dead Three: General *Ketheric Thorm* (voiced by Academy Award winner *J.K. Simmons*), arch-politician *Enver Gortash* (voiced by *Jason Isaacs*), and the bloodthirsty shapeshifter *Orin the Red* (voiced by *Maggie Robertson*).
- Renowned for deep companion storylines and full-performance motion capture, with actor *Neil Newbon* earning multiple international acting trophies for his portrayal of the flamboyant elven vampire spawn *Astarion*.
- Directly honors the legacy of [[baldurs-gate]] by reintroducing legendary veteran party members, including the stalwart druid *Jaheira* and the beloved berserker ranger *Minsc*, alongside his "miniature giant space hamster" *Boo*.
- Offers an optional, customizable origin character named *The Dark Urge*, a deeply amnesiac protagonist tormented by violent, murderous impulses tied directly to the legacy of *Bhaal*, the Lord of Murder.

## connections

- [[baldurs-gate]] — foundational computer role-playing forebear whose Forgotten Realms lore, legendary heroes, and narrative world it carries forward.
- [[skyrim]] — fellow fantasy role-playing titan whose open-ended questing design, lore immersion, and critical ubiquity helped define the modern genre landscape.
- [[william-shakespeare]] — English playwright whose dramatic rhetoric, soliloquies, and moral complexities strongly color the game's theatrical vocal performances.
- [[tragedy]] — dramatic mode central to the catastrophic character backstories, hubristic villains, and moral failure states available throughout the campaign.
- [[minecraft]] — revolutionary sandbox title that, like this game's systemic combat engines, emphasized player agency, spatial problem-solving, and emergent gameplay.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — contrasting gaming genre whose mechanical conventions and twitch reflexes stand opposite this game's tactical, turn-based tabletop deliberation.

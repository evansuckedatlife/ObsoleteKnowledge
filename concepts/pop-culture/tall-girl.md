## summary

**Tall Girl** is an American teen romantic comedy film directed by *Nzingha Stewart* and released on Netflix in 2019. The story follows sixteen-year-old *Jodi Kreyman*, whose self-consciousness over her stature dominates her high school social life until a romance with a Swedish exchange student prompts her journey toward self-acceptance. The film became an enduring subject of internet memes and social media commentary for framing height disparity as an existential adolescent struggle.

## you gotta know

- Stars dancer and actress *Ava Michelle* as *Jodi Kreyman*, a 6-foot-1-and-a-half-inch high school student living in suburban New Orleans who endures constant mocking from peers.
- Spawned the widely mocked opening voiceover monologue, in which the protagonist dramatically laments: "You think your life is hard? I'm a high school junior wearing size 13 Nikes. Men's size 13 Nikes."
- Features a romantic triangle involving *Jack Dunkleman*, played by *Griffin Gluck*, a lifelong friend who carries his schoolbooks in a milk crate to stand taller beside her.
- Introduces Swedish foreign exchange student *Stig Mohlin*, played by *Luke Eisner*, whose tall frame temporarily attracts Jodi before his shallow behavior is exposed.
- Co-stars *Sabrina Carpenter* as Jodi's glamorous older sister *Harper*, an experienced pageant winner who encourages Jodi to embrace personal confidence.
- Produced a 2022 sequel, *Tall Girl 2*, which centers on Jodi landing the lead role in the school musical while contending with sudden popularity and performance anxiety.

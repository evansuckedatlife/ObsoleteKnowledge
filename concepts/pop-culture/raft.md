## summary

**Raft** is a popular oceanic survival sandbox video game developed by *Redbeet Interactive* and published by *Axolot Games*. Released in early access in 2018 before reaching full launch in 2022, the title strands players on a tiny floating platform amidst an endless open sea. It gained widespread acclaim and massive streaming viewership for combining resource management, base-building, and cooperative exploration with maritime hazards.

## you gotta know

- Begins with the player floating adrift upon a four-square wooden pallet equipped only with a plastic hook tied to a length of rope.
- Employs a core gameplay loop centered around slinging the hook to snag flotsam, plastic barrels, wooden planks, and floating foliage carried by ocean currents.
- Constantly threatens player survival through dehydration, hunger, and an ever-present aggressive great white shark nicknamed *Bruce* by the community.
- Allows players to expand their humble drifting debris into multi-story floating palatial fortresses equipped with crop plots, water purifiers, grills, and engine rooms.
- Features a rich post-apocalyptic lore uncovered across story islands like the abandoned luxury ship *Vasagatan*, the floating dome city of *Tangaroa*, and *Utopia*.
- Incorporates extensive underwater scavenging, requiring players to dive along coral reefs surrounding uncharted tropical atolls to mine scrap metal, clay, and sand.
- Represents a notable milestone within the oceanic survival crafting genre alongside contemporaries such as *Subnautica* and *Stranded Deep*.

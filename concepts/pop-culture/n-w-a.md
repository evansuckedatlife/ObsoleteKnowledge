## summary

**N.W.A** (an abbreviation for **Niggaz Wit Attitudes**) was an influential American hip hop group formed in Compton, California, in 1987, widely credited with popularizing gangsta rap and West Coast hip hop. Comprising *Eazy-E*, *Dr. Dre*, *Ice Cube*, *MC Ren*, and *DJ Yella*, the group gained national notoriety for their raw, explicit lyricism addressing police brutality, systemic racism, and inner-city street realities. Their seminal 1988 album *Straight Outta Compton* revolutionized the music industry and sparked intense free-speech debates across the United States.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 1987 in Compton by drug dealer turned music entrepreneur *Eazy-E* (Eric Wright), who co-founded Ruthless Records with manager Jerry Heller.
- Core lineup featured lyricists *Ice Cube* (O'Shea Jackson) and *MC Ren*, musical architect and producer *Dr. Dre* (Andre Young), producer *DJ Yella*, and frontman *Eazy-E*.
- Released their breakthrough studio album *Straight Outta Compton* in August 1988, which went platinum with virtually zero radio airplay or MTV support.
- The controversial protest anthem *"Fuck tha Police"* led to a formal warning letter from the Assistant Director of the FBI to Ruthless Records in 1989.
- During a 1989 concert in Detroit, local police ordered the group not to perform *"Fuck tha Police"*, leading to an altercation and backstage detainment when they defied the ban.
- *Ice Cube* departed the group acrimoniously in late 1989 over financial exploitation and unpaid royalties, igniting a famous feud immortalized in his 1991 diss track *"No Vaseline"*.
- Disbanded in 1991 following *Dr. Dre*'s departure to co-found Death Row Records with Suge Knight, leading to monumental solo careers for its members and induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2016.

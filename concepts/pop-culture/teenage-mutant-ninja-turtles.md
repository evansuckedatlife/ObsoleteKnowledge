## summary

**Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles** is an American multimedia entertainment franchise created by comic artists *Kevin Eastman* and *Peter Laird* in 1984. Originally debuting as an affectionate, gritty parody of popular superhero comic conventions, the property follows four anthropomorphic reptile brothers trained in ninjutsu who defend New York City from subterranean sewers. Propelled by animated television series, action figures, and feature films, the franchise expanded into a multi-billion-dollar pop culture empire.

## you gotta know

- Originated in 1984 as a self-published, black-and-white comic book through Mirage Studios in Dover, New Hampshire, using a tax refund and personal loan.
- Created as a fond satire of popular superhero comics of the early 1980s, specifically referencing Frank Miller's run on *Daredevil*, Marvel's *New Mutants*, and Dave Sim's *Cerebus*.
- Names the four protagonist brothers after masters of the Italian *Renaissance*: *Leonardo* (katanas), *Donatello* (bo staff), *Michelangelo* (nunchucks), and *Raphael* (sai).
- Mentored by the mutated humanoid rat sensei *Master Splinter*, whose name and function deliberately parody Daredevil's martial arts mentor, Stick.
- Battles the villainous *Shredder* (Oroku Saki), leader of the criminal *Foot Clan*, a direct satirical play on Daredevil's ninja syndicate, The Hand.
- Transformed into a global phenomenon via the 1987 animated television series, which introduced distinctive colored bandanas, a passionate love of pizza, and lighter comedic tones.

## summary

**Candice Bergen** is an acclaimed American actress, author, and photojournalist celebrated for her sharp comedic timing and pioneering roles in broadcast television. She rose to global fame as the acerbic, trailblazing investigative journalist on the hit CBS sitcom *Murphy Brown*, a role that garnered her five Primetime Emmy Awards and sparked a national debate on modern family structures during the 1992 presidential race. Her versatile career spans dramatic cinema, satirical television, and groundbreaking appearances as one of the definitive early hosts of late-night comedy.

## you gotta know

- Anchored the long-running sitcom *Murphy Brown* from 1988 to 1998 (and a 2018 revival), portraying a fiercely independent recovering alcoholic who hosts the fictional investigative newsmagazine *FYI*.
- Became a central flashpoint in American political culture in 1992 when Vice President *Dan Quayle* publicly criticized her television character for choosing to bear a child as an unwed mother, prompting a blistering satirical rebuttal in the subsequent season premiere.
- Achieved historical comedy status as the first woman to host *Saturday Night Live*, leading the fourth episode of its inaugural 1975 season, and eventually becoming the first female member of the legendary Five-Timers Club.
- Grew up in Hollywood royalty as the daughter of celebrated ventriloquist *Edgar Bergen*, famously sharing her childhood home and family limelight with his wooden dummy *Charlie McCarthy*, whom she playfully dubbed her brother.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress for her comedic performance in Alan J. Pakula's *Starting Over*, memorably portraying a self-absorbed ex-wife who performs a hilariously off-key musical ballad.
- Co-starred alongside Jack Nicholson in Mike Nichols's controversial drama *Carnal Knowledge* and portrayed pioneering real-life photojournalist *Margaret Bourke-White* in Richard Attenborough's Oscar-winning epic *Gandhi*.
- Enjoyed a later television triumph as the formidable, aristocratic senior law partner *Shirley Schmidt* opposite William Shatner and James Spader on the legal comedy-drama *Boston Legal*.
- Published two critically praised autobiographical memoirs, *Knock Wood* and *A Fine Romance*, reflecting with wry self-awareness on her complex family upbringing, Hollywood privilege, and mid-career resurgence.

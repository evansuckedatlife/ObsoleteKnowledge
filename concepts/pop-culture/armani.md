## summary

**Giorgio Armani**, celebrated across global culture by the single mononym **Armani**, is an Italian fashion designer whose eponymous luxury fashion house fundamentally revolutionized international tailoring and sartorial elegance in the late twentieth century. Founded in *Milan* in 1975 alongside his partner *Sergio Galeotti*, the label systematically stripped away rigid internal padding, stiff horsehair linings, and bulky construction to pioneer the unconstructed, relaxed power suit for both men and women. In quiz-bowl competitions and design history, he is renowned for merging haute couture with Hollywood cinema, establishing the definitive minimalist aesthetic for modern red-carpet fashion and transforming contemporary celebrity styling.

## you gotta know

- Revolutionized modern menswear and womenswear during the mid-1970s by introducing the deconstructed jacket, discarding burdensome internal padding to achieve a fluid, elegant drape that moved naturally with the wearer.
- Catapulted to international celebrity status after supplying the entire luxury wardrobe for actor *Richard Gere* in the 1980 neo-noir film *American Gigolo*, an influential cinematic showcase that redefined late-twentieth-century masculine glamour.
- Built an expansive fashion and lifestyle empire spanning haute couture (*Armani Privé*), diffusion apparel lines (*Emporio Armani* and *Armani Exchange*), signature cosmetics, luxury interior decor, and boutique hotels in cities like Dubai and Milan.
- Championed a restrained, minimalist color palette defined by subtle neutral tones, becoming especially famous for inventing and popularizing greige—a sophisticated hybrid tone fusing warm beige and cool grey.
- Became the first high-fashion designer to ban runway models with a Body Mass Index below eighteen in 2006, responding decisively to global health debates surrounding eating disorders within the modeling industry.
- Outfitted legendary musical icons and performing artists across five decades, providing bespoke formal gowns, touring wardrobes, and custom ceremonial outfits for legendary divas including [[aretha-franklin]] and pop superstars like [[ariana-grande]].
- Designed the official ceremonial uniforms for the Italian national athletic delegations at multiple Summer and Winter Olympic Games, consolidating his status as an official ambassador of Italian craft.

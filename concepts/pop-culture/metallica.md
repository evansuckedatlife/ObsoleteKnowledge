## summary

**Metallica** is an American heavy metal band formed in Los Angeles in 1981 by vocalist and rhythm guitarist *James Hetfield* and drummer *Lars Ulrich*. Renowned as the commercial and musical vanguard of thrash metal, the group expanded the boundaries of heavy rock through complex time signatures, aggressive speed, and deeply introspective lyrics. With over one hundred million albums sold worldwide and induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, they stand as one of the most influential and enduring musical acts in modern popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Formed in 1981 in California after *Lars Ulrich* placed an advertisement in the local classified paper *The Recycler*, quickly recruiting rhythm guitarist and lead vocalist *James Hetfield*.
- Regarded alongside *Megadeth*, *Slayer*, and *Anthrax* as one of the "Big Four" foundational acts responsible for creating and popularizing the thrash metal subgenre.
- Released their critically acclaimed debut album *Kill 'Em All* in 1983, followed by landmark records *Ride the Lightning* and *Master of Puppets*, the latter widely celebrated as a definitive masterpiece of heavy music.
- Suffered a profound personal tragedy in 1986 when virtuoso bassist *Cliff Burton* was killed in a tour bus accident in Sweden, after which *Jason Newsted* joined on bass.
- Achieved unprecedented mainstream crossover success with their self-titled 1991 release, commonly known as *The Black Album*, which produced global radio hits such as *Enter Sandman* and *Nothing Else Matters*.
- Pioneered the use of cinematic storytelling in metal music videos with the 1989 release of *One*, which incorporated dialogue and clips from the anti-war film *Johnny Got His Gun*.
- Ignited an industry-wide legal confrontation in 2000 by suing the peer-to-peer file-sharing platform *Napster*, fundamentally reshaping modern debates over digital copyright and distribution.
- Documented their severe internal collapse and subsequent psychological rehabilitation during the making of the 2003 album *St. Anger* in the acclaimed documentary film *Some Kind of Monster*.

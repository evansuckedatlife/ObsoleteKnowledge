## summary

**All Too Well** is an acclaimed narrative power ballad by American singer-songwriter *Taylor Swift*, originally released on her fourth studio album, *Red* (2012). Co-written with *Liz Rose* and produced with *Nathan Chapman*, the track chronicles the painful dissolution of an intense romantic relationship through intensely detailed autobiographical vignettes. Widely lauded by music critics as Swift's magnum opus, its enduring popularity culminated in the 2021 release of **All Too Well (10 Minute Version)**, which achieved unprecedented commercial success as the longest song ever to top the US Billboard Hot 100.

## you gotta know

- Features vivid, nostalgic storytelling anchored by sensory details such as a forgotten scarf left at an ex-partner's sister's home and slow dancing in the refrigerator light.
- Written spontaneously during a studio band rehearsal in 2011 as a raw twenty-minute cathartic ad-libbed jam session following a difficult emotional breakup.
- Co-written with veteran Nashville songwriter *Liz Rose*, who helped Swift pare down the sprawling original improvisation into a focused five-and-a-half-minute album cut for *Red*.
- Regarded by numerous publications, including *Rolling Stone*, *Pitchfork*, and *Billboard*, as the definitive lyrical benchmark of Swift's songwriting catalogue and one of the century's finest breakup songs.
- Re-recorded in November 2021 for *Red (Taylor's Version)* as a complete ten-minute unabridged release featuring additional narrative verses detailing age disparities and emotional detachment.
- Set a historic record by becoming the longest song in history to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100, surpassing the nearly fifty-year record previously held by *Don McLean's* *American Pie*.
- Accompanied by a self-directed romantic drama short film starring actors *Sadie Sink* and *Dylan O'Brien*, which won the MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year and a Grammy Award for Best Music Video.

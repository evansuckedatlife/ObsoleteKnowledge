## summary

**Jeff Kinney** (*Jeffrey Patrick Kinney*) is an American author, cartoonist, game designer, and bookseller celebrated for creating the blockbuster illustrated children's book series *Diary of a Wimpy Kid*. Combining handwritten journal entries with minimalist, expressive cartoon sketches, his stories chronicle the anxieties, moral missteps, and awkward humor of middle-school life through protagonist *Greg Heffley*. Since debuting in print in 2007, the series has sold hundreds of millions of copies worldwide, transformed intermediate-grade literacy, and spawned an enduring multi-media entertainment franchise.

## you gotta know

- Conceived *Diary of a Wimpy Kid* over eight years before initially publishing daily online installments on the educational gaming website FunBrain, where it accumulated tens of millions of views.
- Released the first physical *Diary of a Wimpy Kid* hardcover in 2007 with Abrams Books, quickly climbing *The New York Times* children's bestseller list and remaining a fixture for years.
- Illustrated the novels using a clean, comic-strip aesthetic presented on lined notebook pages, capturing the voice and perspective of middle-school antihero *Greg Heffley*.
- Introduced iconic running gags and plot elements that permeated popular culture, most famously the playground dread of the "Cheese Touch" and the heavy metal aspirations of older brother *Rodrick*'s band, *Löded Diper*.
- Authored the companion book *The Wimpy Kid Do-It-Yourself Book* and launched the spinoff series *Diary of an Awesome Friendly Kid*, narrated from the perspective of Greg's cheerful best friend *Rowley Jefferson*.
- Co-founded the widely popular commercial virtual gaming world *Poptropica* in 2007, designing immersive, story-driven digital islands for children.
- Served as an executive producer and screenwriter on several live-action and animated film adaptations produced by 20th Century Fox and Disney+.
- Operates the independent bookstore and community hub *An Unlikely Story* in Plainville, Massachusetts, demonstrating a hands-on commitment to local literary culture.

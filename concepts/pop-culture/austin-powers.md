## summary

**Austin Powers** is an American spy comedy film series conceived by and starring *Mike Myers*, who plays both the eponymous swinging British secret agent and his arch-nemesis *Dr. Evil*. Spanning three feature films released between 1997 and 2002, the franchise parodies 1960s espionage thrillers, particularly the *James Bond* cinematic series and countercultural British mod aesthetics. It became a global pop-culture phenomenon celebrated for its catchphrases, double entendres, elaborate musical sequences, and gross-out humor.

## you gotta know

- Created and written by Canadian comedian *Mike Myers*, who based the title character on British pop icons, swinging London archetypes, and spy thrillers like *Casino Royale* and the *Matt Helm* series.
- Centers on *Austin Danger Powers*, a flamboyant 1960s British spy who is cryogenically frozen alongside his archenemy, *Dr. Evil*, and thawed out decades later in the 1990s to resume their rivalry.
- Features *Mike Myers* portraying multiple distinct characters across the trilogy, most notably Austin Powers, *Dr. Evil*, *Fat Bastard*, and *Goldmember*.
- Launched the cinematic trilogy with *Austin Powers: International Man of Mystery* (1997), followed by *The Spy Who Shagged Me* (1999) and *Austin Powers in Goldmember* (2002), all directed by *Jay Roach*.
- Popularized iconic comedic phrases, physical gestures, and running gags such as Dr. Evil holding his pinky finger to his mouth, Demanding *"one million dollars"*, and referring to his clone as *Mini-Me* (played by *Verne Troyer*).
- Starred prominent female leads playing Austin's secret agent partners, including *Elizabeth Hurley* as Vanessa Kensington, *Heather Graham* as Felicity Shagwell, and *Beyoncé Knowles* as Foxxy Cleopatra.
- Prominently incorporated swinging sixties music, Burt Bacharach cameos, and lavish spy spoof production design, drawing heavily on cinematic satire and physical farce.

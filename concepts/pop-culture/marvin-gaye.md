## summary

**Marvin Gaye** was an American singer, songwriter, and producer whose soulful tenor and visionary social commentary earned him the monikers *"Prince of Motown"* and *"Prince of Soul"*. Rising to prominence in the 1960s with iconic singles like *"I Heard It Through the Grapevine"* and sparkling duets with *Tammi Terrell*, Gaye fought *Motown* founder *Berry Gordy* to gain complete creative autonomy, releasing the groundbreaking 1971 concept masterpiece *What's Going On*. His profound fusion of jazz-inflected orchestral soul, political protest, environmental awareness, and sensual vulnerability fundamentally transformed modern rhythm and blues across the *[[united-states]]*.

## you gotta know

- Recorded the monumental 1971 concept album *What's Going On*, addressing the *Vietnam War*, racial injustice, poverty, police brutality, and ecological decay from the perspective of a returning African-American soldier.
- Scored iconic 1960s *Motown* hits including *"How Sweet It Is (To Be Loved by You)"*, *"Ain't That Peculiar"*, and his timeless 1968 version of *Norman Whitfield*'s *"I Heard It Through the Grapevine"*.
- Formed one of soul music's most celebrated vocal partnerships alongside *Tammi Terrell*, scoring indelible hits such as *"Ain't No Mountain High Enough"* and *"Your Precious Love"* before her tragic death from a brain tumor in 1970.
- Revolutionized multi-track vocal arrangement techniques by recording his own lead, backing, and counter-melodic falsetto harmonies in layered stereo tracks, creating an intimate, choral tapestry.
- Embraced raw sexual politics and romantic intimacy on the chart-topping 1973 album *Let's Get It On*, establishing a sleek blueprint for the modern neo-soul and bedroom-pop genres.
- Released the painfully intimate 1978 double album *Here, My Dear*, written as a contractual settlement to fund alimony payments to his ex-wife *Anna Gordy Gaye*, chronicling the collapse of their marriage with unflinching honesty.
- Staged a triumphant international comeback in 1982 with the worldwide smash single *"Sexual Healing"*, winning his first two *Grammy Awards* before being tragically shot and killed by his father, *Marvin Gay Sr.*, on April 1, 1984.

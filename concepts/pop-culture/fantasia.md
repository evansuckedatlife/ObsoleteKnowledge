## summary

**Fantasia** is a pioneering 1940 American animated anthology film produced by *Walt Disney* and released through *RKO Radio Pictures*. Structured as eight distinct animated segments synchronized to classical music selections performed by the Philadelphia Orchestra under conductor *Leopold Stokowski*, the work broke away from conventional narrative animation to merge abstract fine art with popular cinema. Marked by its premier sound innovation, *Fantasound*, it remains an ambitious landmark in experimental animation and twentieth-century American culture.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by *Walt Disney* as an extravagant showcase to revitalize the popularity of *Mickey Mouse* through the classic segment *"The Sorcerer's Apprentice"*.
- Featured the *Philadelphia Orchestra* conducted by maestro *Leopold Stokowski*, with musicologist *Deems Taylor* providing live-action narrative introductions.
- Pioneered *Fantasound*, an elaborate stereophonic sound reproduction system that preceded modern multi-channel cinematic surround sound.
- Explored daring abstract and mythic visuals, ranging from *J.S. Bach's* *Toccata and Fugue in D minor* to the prehistoric evolutionary sequences set to *Igor Stravinsky's* *The Rite of Spring*.
- Showcased terrifyingly expressive character animation in *Modest Mussorgsky's* *"Night on Bald Mountain"*, featuring the towering Slavic winged demon *Chernabog*.
- Met with initial commercial difficulties upon theatrical release due to expensive sound installation equipment and disrupted overseas European markets during World War II.

## summary

**Arceus** is a mythical creature in the *Pokémon* media franchise created by Game Freak and Nintendo in *[[japan]]*, revered within the fictional canon as the supreme creator deity of the cosmos. Introduced in the fourth-generation titles *Pokémon Diamond* and *Pearl* (2006), it is described in ancient Sinnoh folklore as "The Original One," having emerged from an egg before the universe existed. It remains one of the most powerful and lore-heavy entities in interactive entertainment, serving as the namesake and focal point of the historical open-world adventure *Pokémon Legends: Arceus* (2022).

## you gotta know

- Revered in fictional cosmogony as the creator god who hatched from an egg in nothingness and shaped the entire universe with its thousand arms.
- Created the primordial Creation Trio consisting of Dialga to govern time, Palkia to govern space, and Giratina to govern antimatter and the Distortion World.
- Formed the Lake Guardians consisting of Uxie, Mesprit, and Azelf to bestow the core sentient virtues of knowledge, emotion, and willpower upon all living things.
- Possesses the signature battle ability Multitype, which dynamically alters its elemental typing and physical coloration based on the elemental Plate or Z-Crystal it holds.
- Commands the exclusive signature move Judgment, an attack that changes its elemental damage type to mirror whatever Plate the user is holding.
- Originally programmed to be encountered in *Pokémon Diamond*, *Pearl*, and *Platinum* at the Hall of Origin using the Azure Flute item, which Game Freak never distributed officially during generation four due to perceived complexity.
- Served as the titular divine antagonist and protagonist of the twelfth animated film *Arceus and the Jewel of Life* (2009), seeking vengeance against humanity after an ancient betrayal.
- Stands as the primary catalyst and framing presence of *Pokémon Legends: Arceus*, pulling the modern protagonist back through a spacetime rift into the ancient frontier region of Hisui.

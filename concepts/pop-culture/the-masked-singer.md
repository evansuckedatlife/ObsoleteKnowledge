## summary

**The Masked Singer** is an American reality television singing competition franchise that premiered on the Fox network in January 2019. Adapted from the South Korean television format *King of Mask Singer*, the program features celebrities performing vocal numbers in elaborate, full-body costumes and head-to-toe disguises that entirely conceal their identities from the studio audience, host, and panel of judges. Combining musical variety, mystery guessing-game dynamics, and spectacle, the show quickly grew into a massive broadcast ratings hit and a global pop-culture phenomenon.

## you gotta know

- Adapted by executive producer Craig Plestis from the original Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation (MBC) South Korean variety format *King of Mask Singer* (*Bongmyeon Ga-wang*).
- Hosted continuously by Nick Cannon, with an eccentric, rotating celebrity detective panel that has prominently included Robin Thicke, Jenny McCarthy Wahlberg, Ken Jeong, Nicole Scherzinger, and Rita Ora.
- Employs extravagant, couture-grade disguises engineered by renowned costume designers like Marina Toybina, featuring whimsical characters ranging from the Monster and Peacock to the Alien and Rottweiler.
- Enforces draconian security and nondisclosure protocols on set, compelling participants to wear anonymity hoodies reading "Don't Talk to Me", face visors, and gloves whenever moving through studio backstage corridors.
- Involves cryptic video clues, vocal distortions during pre-recorded interview packages, and post-performance cross-examinations designed to challenge the judging panel and viewers at home to deduce each contestant's true identity.
- Crowned hip-hop artist and producer T-Pain, disguised as the "Monster," as the inaugural season winner, a victory widely noted for rehabilitating his vocal reputation by proving his natural singing ability without Auto-Tune.

## summary

**Justina Ireland** is a contemporary American author of young adult and middle-grade speculative fiction, best known for her alternate history zombie novel *Dread Nation*. Born in 1985 in the [[united-states]], she served in the military before transitioning to creative writing and becoming an outspoken advocate for racial diversity and inclusion in children's publishing. Her works frequently intertwine historical traumas such as chattel slavery and the [[american-civil-war]] with fantastical horrors, offering incisive commentary on structural racism and systemic oppression in American history.

## you gotta know

- Authored the acclaimed 2018 alternate history novel *Dread Nation*, which follows teenager *Jane McKeene* navigating an alternate post-Civil War society where the dead rose at the Battle of Gettysburg.
- In the world of *Dread Nation*, the passage of the Native and Negro Reeducation Act conscripts Black and Indigenous children into combat academies to become combatants called Attendants against the undead hordes.
- Published the direct sequel to *Dread Nation* titled *Deathless Divide* in 2020, chronicling *Jane McKeene* and *Katherine Deveraux* as they seek refuge in California.
- Served in the [[united-states]] Army as a young adult, later drawing upon her military discipline and background in weapon mechanics to detail tactical combat scenes in her novels.
- Contributed prominently to the *Star Wars: The High Republic* multimedia initiative, writing pivotal works such as *A Test of Courage*, *Out of the Shadows*, and *Mission to Disaster*.
- Received a Coretta Scott King Honor and earned nominations for both the Andre Norton Award and the Bram Stoker Award for *Dread Nation*.
- Wrote the middle-grade fantasy novel *Ophie's Ghosts*, which explores post-World War I racial violence, ghosts, and memory through a young girl working in a Pittsburgh manor.

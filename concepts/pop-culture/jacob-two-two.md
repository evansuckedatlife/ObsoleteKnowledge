## summary

***Jacob Two-Two*** is a beloved Canadian children's literary franchise created by acclaimed author *Mordecai Richler*, originating in the 1975 classic *Jacob Two-Two Meets the Hooded Fang*. The stories follow the pint-sized youngest child of five who must repeat everything he says twice just to be heard by busy grown-ups and older siblings. The series is celebrated for its satirical humor, empathetic depiction of childhood indignities, and enduring presence across film, stage, and animated television.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by prominent novelist *Mordecai Richler* as a bedtime story for his youngest son, *Jacob Richler*, marking the author's celebrated foray into children's fiction.
- Centers on titular protagonist *Jacob Two-Two*, nicknamed because he is two plus two plus two years old, has two older brothers and two older sisters, and says everything twice to get noticed.
- Debuted in the 1975 book *Jacob Two-Two Meets the Hooded Fang*, which pits Jacob against a terrifying wrestling villain in the surreal, child-hating dungeon of *Slimers' Island*.
- The inciting incident begins when Jacob attempts to buy two pounds of firm, ripe tomatoes from a grocer, who accuses him of impudence for repeating himself.
- Rescued in the first novel with the aid of the secret child resistance network called *Child Power*, led by agents *Shapiro* and *O'Toole*.
- Subsequent installments by Richler include *Jacob Two-Two and the Dinosaur* (1987) and *Jacob Two-Two's First Spy Case* (1995).
- Adapted into two feature films (in 1978 and 1999) as well as an internationally broadcast Canadian animated television series produced by *Nelvana*.

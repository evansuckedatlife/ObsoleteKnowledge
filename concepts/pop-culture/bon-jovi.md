## summary

**Bon Jovi** is an American rock band formed in Sayreville, New Jersey, in 1983, led by frontman and namesake singer *Jon Bon Jovi*. Rising to global superstardom with their breakthrough 1986 album *Slippery When Wet*, the group became one of the quintessential arena rock and pop-metal acts of the late twentieth century. With enduring anthems depicting blue-collar perseverance, romance, and resilience, the band has sold more than one hundred million records worldwide and was inducted into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved colossal commercial breakthrough with their third studio album, *Slippery When Wet* (1986), spending eight weeks at number one on the Billboard 200.
- Recorded the definitive working-class rock anthem *Livin' on a Prayer*, following the fictional blue-collar couple *Tommy and Gina* struggling through economic hardship.
- Co-written extensively with famed songwriter *Desmond Child* and lead guitarist *Richie Sambora*, who was famous for his melodic solos and talk-box guitar work.
- Dominated international airplay and MTV rotation with chart-topping singles such as *You Give Love a Bad Name*, *Wanted Dead or Alive*, and *Bad Medicine*.
- Successfully navigated shifting musical landscapes into the 1990s and 2000s, scoring global smash hits with the power ballad *Always* and the comeback anthem *It's My Life*.
- Inducted into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame* in 2018, reuniting on stage with former longtime guitarist *Richie Sambora* and bassist *Alec John Such*.
- Frontman *Jon Bon Jovi* established the *Soul Kitchen* community restaurant foundation in New Jersey, serving meals to in-need diners on a pay-what-you-can basis.

## connections

- [[united-states]] — birthplace and cultural home of the group, deeply woven into their thematic focus on American working-class dreams.
- [[new-york-city]] — major metropolitan center across the Hudson River from their native New Jersey, where their earliest studio sessions were recorded at the Power Station.
- [[industrial-revolution]] — historical emergence of the modern manufacturing working class celebrated in narrative songs like *Livin' on a Prayer*.
- [[realism]] — narrative lyrical style detailing the economic anxieties of factory workers, strikes, and everyday financial strife.
- [[western-film]] — cinematic genre heavily borrowed in the visual styling, acoustic guitars, and outlaw cowboy motifs of *Wanted Dead or Alive*.

## summary

**Michael J. Fox** is a Canadian-American actor, author, and advocate who rose to international fame in the 1980s as one of Hollywood's most beloved screen icons. Renowned for playing *Marty McFly* in *Robert Zemeckis*'s science fiction blockbuster *Back to the Future* trilogy and the ambitious young conservative *Alex P. Keaton* on the sitcom *Family Ties*, he established himself through impeccable comic timing and energetic performance. After being diagnosed with early-onset Parkinson's disease in 1991, he became a prominent public advocate and philanthropist through the Michael J. Fox Foundation, leading global research efforts into treatments and a cure.

## you gotta know

- Skyrocketed to household stardom portraying teenage Republican *Alex P. Keaton* on the NBC sitcom *Family Ties*, earning three consecutive Primetime Emmy Awards between 1986 and 1988.
- Cemented pop culture immortality as *Marty McFly* in the time-traveling adventure *Back to the Future*, replacing original actor *Eric Stoltz* weeks into filming and shooting both the movie and *Family Ties* simultaneously.
- Reprised *Marty McFly* in *Back to the Future Part II* and *Back to the Future Part III*, popularizing iconic pop-culture imagery such as the hoverboard, the DeLorean time machine, and power-lacing sneakers.
- Starred in a series of popular comedies and high-profile features throughout the late 1980s and 1990s, including *Teen Wolf*, *The Secret of My Success*, *Doc Hollywood*, and *The American President*.
- Returned to episodic television as New York City deputy mayor *Mike Flaherty* on the hit political sitcom *Spin City*, winning an Emmy and three Golden Globe Awards before retiring from full-time series work in 2000 due to advancing Parkinson's symptoms.
- Disclosed his 1991 diagnosis of Parkinson's disease publicly in 1998 and founded the Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research in 2000, which has raised more than two billion dollars for biomedical investigations.
- Transitioned to critically acclaimed guest appearances in dramatic television series, earning praise and Emmy nominations for memorable recurring roles in *The Good Wife*, *Boston Legal*, *Curb Your Enthusiasm*, and *Rescue Me*.
- Adopted the middle initial "J." as an homage to veteran character actor *Michael J. Pollard*, deliberately choosing it over his legal initial "A." to avoid sounding like "Michael, A Fox."

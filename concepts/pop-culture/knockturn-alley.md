## summary

**Knockturn Alley** is a notorious, shadowed commercial thoroughfare located in wizarding London, branching directly off the bustling wizarding district of *Diagon Alley* in *[[england]]*. Revered by practitioners of the Dark Arts and shunned by respectable wizards, the grim corridor is packed with illicit purveyors of sinister curiosities, dangerous poisons, and cursed artifacts. Functioning within modern British fantasy literature as an eerie urban underworld, the alley is renowned as a primary haunt of *Death Eaters* and dark wizards throughout the *Harry Potter* saga.

## you gotta know

- Branching off the brighter avenues of *Diagon Alley*, this seedy pedestrian lane serves as the premier commercial center for Dark Arts paraphernalia, dangerous charms, black-market potions, and forbidden contraband in wizarding *[[england]]*.
- Its name is a playful literary pun on the word "nocturnally," mirroring the phonological wordplay of *Diagon Alley* ("diagonally") conceived by author *J. K. Rowling*.
- Anchored by its most famous and sinister storefront, *Borgin and Burkes*, an antique emporium founded by *Caractacus Burke* and *Mr. Borgin* that deals in cursed relics such as the *Hand of Glory*, the *Opal Necklace*, and dark magical curios.
- Served as the workplace of a young *Tom Marvolo Riddle* (the future *Lord Voldemort*) following his graduation from *Hogwarts*, where he used his charm to persuade wealthy collectors to part with ancient magical heirlooms, including the locket of *Salazar Slytherin* and the cup of *Helga Hufflepuff*.
- First entered by *Harry Potter* in *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* when he accidentally mispronounces his destination while traveling by Floo Powder from *The Burrow*, coughing on fireplace soot and arriving unceremoniously in *Borgin and Burkes*.
- Features the pivotal *Vanishing Cabinet* at *Borgin and Burkes*, which forms a secret magical transit passage linked to a twin cabinet inside the *Room of Requirement* at *Hogwarts*, exploited by *Draco Malfoy* in *Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince* to smuggle *Death Eaters* into the castle.
- Populated by shady denizens, hags, and peddlers, including merchants offering flesh-eating slug repellent, shriveled human heads, poisoned candles, and venomous spider parts in dark doorways.

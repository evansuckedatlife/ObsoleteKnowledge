## summary

**Markus Persson**, universally recognized in digital culture by his online moniker **Notch**, is a Swedish video game programmer and designer who created the revolutionary sandbox title [[minecraft]]. Born in Stockholm in 1979, he established the development studio *Mojang Specifications* alongside *Carl Manneh* and *Jakob Porsér* to support the game's explosive organic growth following its initial 2009 alpha release. His pioneering vision of procedurally generated voxel worlds transformed independent game development, culminating in the 2014 sale of *Mojang* to American technology titan *Microsoft* for 2.5 billion dollars.

## you gotta know

- Conceived, programmed, and released the original public alpha build of [[minecraft]] in May 2009 on the indie developer forum *TIGSource*, originally referring to the project as *Cave Game*.
- Drew primary design inspiration from *Zachary Barth's* open-source block-building title *Infiniminer*, alongside classic simulation mechanics from *Dwarf Fortress* and Peter Molyneux's *Dungeon Keeper*.
- Co-founded the Swedish independent game studio *Mojang* in 2010 to handle the title's commercial explosion, serving as its figurehead and lead developer until stepping aside in late 2011 to transfer design authority to *Jens Bergensten*.
- Finalized a blockbuster 2.5 billion dollar acquisition with *Microsoft* in September 2014, departing *Mojang* entirely and using his fortune to purchase an opulent residential estate in Beverly Hills, California in the [[united-states]].
- Spent his early career building web games at *King* (the subsequent creator of *Candy Crush*) and honed his procedural generation skills during rapid 48-hour *Ludum Dare* hackathons, creating miniature games like *Prelude of the Chambered* and *Minicraft*.
- Announced an ambitious science-fiction space simulation titled *0x10c* in 2012 that planned to feature an emulated 16-bit virtual processor, though the project was permanently shelved due to creative fatigue.
- Found himself distanced from official promotional retrospectives and tenth-anniversary celebrations orchestrated by *Microsoft* in 2019 following years of contentious, conspiratorial, and inflammatory public social media remarks.

## connections

- [[minecraft]] — landmark sandbox video game he independently conceptualized, coded, and transformed into the best-selling game of all time.
- [[united-states]] — destination market where corporate buyer *Microsoft* integrated his intellectual property and where he relocated a substantial portion of his wealth.
- [[skyrim]] — acclaimed fantasy role-playing game whose parent publisher *Bethesda Softworks* entered a widely publicized trademark lawsuit against him over his card game *Scrolls*.
- [[counter-strike]] — legendary multiplayer title reflecting the grassroots European modding and PC gaming culture that informed his early programming years.
- [[settlers-of-catan]] — foundational resource-management board game whose elegant physical systems influenced modern Euro-style video game mechanics.
- [[esports]] — broader competitive ecosystem that expanded alongside the sandbox streaming culture and video content revolution sparked by his software.

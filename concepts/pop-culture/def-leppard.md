## summary

**Def Leppard** is an iconic English rock band formed in Sheffield in 1977 that emerged as a cornerstone of the New Wave of British Heavy Metal before conquering global pop charts in the 1980s. Masterminded musically alongside pioneering producer *Robert John "Mutt" Lange*, the group developed a revolutionary sound defined by immaculate multitrack vocal harmonies, glistening guitar hooks, and electronic rhythm technology. With diamond-certified blockbuster albums such as *Pyromania* and *Hysteria*, they remain one of the best-selling music acts in history and a defining symbol of arena rock supremacy.

## you gotta know

- Anchored by longtime frontman *Joe Elliott*, bassist *Rick Savage*, guitarist *Phil Collen*, drummer *Rick Allen*, and late founding guitarist *Steve Clark*, with former *Dio* and *Whitesnake* guitarist *Vivian Campbell* completing the modern lineup.
- Achieved an extraordinary triumph of perseverance when drummer *Rick Allen* lost his left arm in a catastrophic car crash on New Year's Eve 1984, returning to perform with a custom-engineered electronic drum kit utilizing foot pedals to trigger snare sounds.
- Produced the diamond-selling 1983 breakthrough album *Pyromania*, which sold over ten million copies in the [[united-states]] behind massive MTV and radio staples including "Photograph" and "Rock of Ages".
- Released the colossal 1987 magnum opus *Hysteria*, which yielded seven hit singles including the iconic anthem "Pour Some Sugar on Me", the soaring power ballad "Love Bites" (their sole Billboard Hot 100 number one), and the rhythmic smash "Animal".
- Pioneered an obsessively polished recording technique with producer *Mutt Lange*, who painstakingly layered dozens of background vocal tracks and synchronized acoustic guitars with sampled percussion to achieve a pristine pop sheen.
- Overcame the tragic death of principal songwriter and guitarist *Steve Clark* from alcohol poisoning during the making of their 1992 album *Adrenalize*, which nevertheless debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 driven by the hit "Let's Get Rocked".
- Formed in the industrial hub of Sheffield under the original moniker *Atomic Mass* by students *Rick Savage*, *Pete Willis*, and *Tony Kenning*, before *Joe Elliott* joined and suggested the name adapted from his fictional schoolboy band Deaf Leopard.
- Inducted into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame* in 2019 by *Queen* guitarist *Brian May*, celebrating a historic recording catalog that boasts over one hundred million records sold worldwide.

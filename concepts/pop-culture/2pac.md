## summary

**2Pac**, born *Lesane Parish Crooks* and later named *Tupac Amaru Shakur*, was an iconic American rapper, songwriter, poet, and actor whose socially conscious and emotionally volatile music defined 1990s hip-hop. Emerging from a family deeply immersed in the *Black Panther Party*, he achieved global stardom through diamond-certified records and magnetic cinematic performances before his unsolved murder in Las Vegas in September 1996. Widely cited among the most influential cultural figures of the late 20th century in the [[united-states]], his artistic output bridges raw socio-political activism with tragic outlaw mythmaking.

## you gotta know

- Born in East Harlem, [[new-york-city]], to mother *Afeni Shakur*, a prominent activist in the *Black Panther Party* who successfully defended herself in the historic Panther 21 trial.
- Rose to prominence as a dancer and MC for the alternative hip-hop group *Digital Underground* before releasing his solo debut album, *2Pacalypse Now*, in 1991.
- Earned critical acclaim as a dramatic screen actor, starring in seminal films such as *Ernest Dickerson's* *Juice*, *John Singleton's* *Poetic Justice*, and *Gridlock'd*.
- Survived being shot five times during an armed robbery at Quad Recording Studios in Manhattan in 1994, an event that catalyzed the deadly East Coast–West Coast hip-hop rivalry.
- Released *Me Against the World* in 1995 while serving a prison sentence at Clinton Correctional Facility, making him the first artist to top the Billboard 200 while incarcerated.
- Signed with *Suge Knight's* *Death Row Records* upon his release, subsequently releasing *All Eyez on Me*, the first solo double-album in commercial rap history.
- Adopted the pseudonym *Makaveli* after Renaissance political philosopher *Niccolò Machiavelli*, releasing the posthumous landmark album *The Don Killuminati: The 7 Day Theory*.
- Fatally shot in a drive-by shooting on the Las Vegas Strip on September 7, 1996, succumbing to internal injuries six days later at age 25.

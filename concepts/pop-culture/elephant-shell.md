## summary

**Elephant Shell** is the critically acclaimed debut studio album by Canadian indie rock band *Tokyo Police Club*, released in April 2008 by *Saddle Creek Records* in the *[[united-states]]* and *Memphis Industries* in the United Kingdom. Emerging from the vibrant post-punk revival of the late 2000s, the record is celebrated for its urgent tempos, jangly guitar hooks, and concise, youthful songwriting. Its energetic singles garnered substantial critical praise and licensing across indie media, television, and video game soundtracks.

## you gotta know

- Released in April 2008 as the highly anticipated full-length follow-up to *Tokyo Police Club*'s breakout 2006 extended play, *A Lesson in Crime*.
- Produced by *Peter Katis*, a renowned producer famous for his work with seminal indie rock acts such as *The National* and *Interpol*.
- Supported by notable indie rock singles including *"Tessellate"*, *"In a Cave"*, and *"Graves"*, each highlighting driving basslines and rapid vocal phrasing.
- Issued on the influential Omaha-based independent label *Saddle Creek Records* for North American distribution, marking one of the label's major international signings.
- Featured widely in video game culture, with the standout track *"Tessellate"* appearing on popular interactive soundtracks, notably within racing and rhythm franchises.
- Met with widespread critical acclaim from music outlets such as *Pitchfork*, *NME*, and *Rolling Stone*, which praised its infectious energy and tight musicianship.
- Clocked in at just under twenty-eight minutes across eleven tracks, epitomizing the band's trademark brevity and punchy song arrangements.

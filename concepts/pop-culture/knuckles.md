## summary

**Knuckles the Echidna** is a fictional red anthropomorphic echidna created by *Takashi Yuda* for the *Sega* video game franchise originating in [[japan]]. Introduced in the 1994 platformer *Sonic the Hedgehog 3*, he serves as the hot-tempered yet fiercely honorable guardian of the *Master Emerald* on the floating landmass of *Angel Island*. Renowned across gaming culture for his spiked fists, gliding ability, and early role as a rival manipulated by *Doctor Ivo Robotnik*, he became one of the medium's defining antiheroes and sidekicks.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in the 1994 game *Sonic the Hedgehog 3* as an antagonist tricked into opposing the protagonist before becoming a permanent ally in *Sonic & Knuckles*.
- Serves as the sole remaining guardian of the mystical *Master Emerald*, a massive gemstone that keeps his ancestral floating home, *Angel Island*, suspended in the sky.
- Possesses two distinct spiked knuckles on each glove that grant him immense physical strength, allowing him to smash through solid stone, scale sheer walls, and burrow underground.
- Glides horizontally through the air by trapping air underneath his dreadlock-like head quills, contrasting with the high-speed running mechanics of *Sonic*.
- Voiced in modern Hollywood live-action adaptations by *Idris Elba*, appearing in *Sonic the Hedgehog 2* and his own eponymous spin-off television miniseries.
- Formed the treasure-hunting gameplay branch of *Sonic Adventure* (1998) and *Sonic Adventure 2* (2001), which featured expansive exploratory stages accompanied by dynamic hip-hop themes performed by *Hunnid-P*.
- Originally designed with a Jamaican color motif before developers settled on red, green, and yellow sneakers paired with an indigenous tribal lineage connected to the ancient *Nocturnus* and *Knuckles* clans.

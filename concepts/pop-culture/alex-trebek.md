## summary

**Alex Trebek** was a celebrated Canadian-American television personality who hosted the syndicated game show *Jeopardy!* for more than thirty-six seasons from 1984 until his death in 2020. Renowned for his calm demeanor, cultured authority, and unflappable delivery, he became an enduring cultural icon in North American broadcasting. His stewardship transformed *Jeopardy!* into a premier televised showcase of academic trivia, general knowledge, and intellectual prowess across the [[united-states]] and beyond.

## you gotta know

- Hosted the daily syndicated revival of *Jeopardy!* from September 1984 to November 2020, presiding over more than 8,200 episodes.
- Earned eight Daytime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Game Show Host and received a Lifetime Achievement Award from the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences.
- Began his broadcasting career in Canada with the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (*CBC*), hosting news broadcasts, sports coverage, and programs like *Reach for the Top*.
- Emceed several other notable American game shows prior to and concurrent with *Jeopardy!*, including *The Wizard of Odds*, *High Rollers*, *Battlestars*, and *Classic Concentration*.
- Set a Guinness World Record in 2014 for the most game show episodes hosted by the same presenter on the same program.
- Publicly announced his diagnosis of stage IV pancreatic cancer in March 2019, continuing to tape episodes through treatments with candid resilience until shortly before his passing in November 2020.
- Authored the best-selling 2020 memoir *The Answer Is...: Reflections on My Life*, chronicling his career, philosophies, and backstage history.

## summary

**Kim's Convenience** is an acclaimed Canadian television sitcom and stage play created by *Ins Choi*, centered on a Korean-Canadian family operating a corner grocery store in Toronto's Regent Park neighborhood. Debuting on *CBC Television* in 2016 and later achieving international acclaim via streaming, the series explores immigrant family dynamics, generational divides, and urban multiculturalism. The show is celebrated for its warm comedic realism and for launching stars such as *Paul Sun-Hyung Lee* and *Simu Liu* into global prominence.

## you gotta know

- Adapted by playwright *Ins Choi* from his award-winning 2011 Toronto Fringe Festival theatrical production of the same name.
- Stars *Paul Sun-Hyung Lee* as the stubborn, opinionated patriarch *Mr. Sang-il Kim* ("Appa") and *Jean Yoon* as his pious wife *Mrs. Umma Kim*.
- Features *Simu Liu* as the estranged son *Jung*, who works at *Handy Car Rental*, and *Andrea Bang* as the artistic daughter *Janet*, who studies photography.
- The narrative revolves around the everyday life inside the family's Toronto convenience store, showcasing interactions with eclectic neighborhood regulars and classic Canadian retail staples like *[[ketchup-chips]]*.
- Broadcast for five seasons on the *CBC* from 2016 to 2021, picking up numerous Canadian Screen Awards before abruptly ending when the showrunners departed.
- Helped break groundbreaking ground for East Asian representation on North American television, preceding major Hollywood roles for its cast including *Simu Liu* in *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*.

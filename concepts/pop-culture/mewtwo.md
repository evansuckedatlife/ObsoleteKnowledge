## summary

**Mewtwo** is a fictional creature and legendary psychic entity from the Japanese multimedia franchise *Pokémon*, introduced in the 1996 video games *Pokémon Red and Green*. Created through clandestine gene-splicing experiments using the DNA of the mythical ancestor *Mew*, the character is defined by immense psychic power and a profound philosophical crisis regarding its unnatural creation. As the tragic antagonist of the franchise's inaugural feature film and an enduring tournament fighter in competitive gaming, this engineered titan remains one of the most recognizable icons in interactive entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Created within the lore of the Kanto region by researchers on Cinnabar Island who subjected pregnant ancestor *Mew* to brutal gene-splicing experiments, leading to the destruction of the island laboratory.
- Serves as the ultimate post-game challenge in the original Game Boy titles, hidden deep within Cerulean Cave and catchable only by seasoned trainers utilizing the infallible Master Ball.
- Holds the penultimate entry in the original Kanto Pokédex as species number one hundred and fifty, immediately preceding its elusive genetic progenitor *Mew*.
- Starred as the central antagonist of the 1998 theatrical release *Pokémon: The First Movie*, rebelling against *Giovanni* and Team Rocket before delivering a famous monologue on the value of life.
- Entered the competitive fighting game circuit as an unlockable combatant in the 2001 platform fighter *Super Smash Bros. Melee*, later returning across subsequent franchise installments.
- Received two distinct Mega Evolutions in the sixth-generation titles *Pokémon X and Y*, diverging into a physical combatant and a specialized telepathic powerhouse.
- Wields the exclusive signature move *Psystrike*, an esoteric psychic assault that calculates damage against the physical defense of its target rather than special resilience.

## summary

**Brooklyn Nine-Nine**, frequently abbreviated as **B99**, is an American television police procedural sitcom created by *Dan Goor* and *Michael Schur* that aired from 2013 to 2021 across eight seasons. Set within a fictional police precinct in [[new-york-city]], the series chronicles the cases and comedic antics of a talented but childlike detective and his diverse cohort of colleagues. Celebrated for its warm ensemble chemistry and quick-witted writing, the show earned critical acclaim and multiple awards while addressing contemporary social and institutional issues.

## you gotta know

- Revolves around the workplace clash and evolving mutual respect between immature ace detective *Jake Peralta*, played by *Andy Samberg*, and his stoic, openly gay commanding officer *Captain Raymond Holt*, played by *Andre Braugher*.
- Created by comedy veterans *Michael Schur* and *Dan Goor*, who previously collaborated on the hit workplace comedy *Parks and Recreation*.
- Won two Golden Globe Awards in its first season, capturing Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy and Best Actor for *Andy Samberg*.
- Feature popular annual holiday episodes centering on the competitive *"Halloween Heist"*, in which precinct members scheme to crown the ultimate human/genius.
- Developed an ensemble of eccentric detectives including hyper-competitive *Amy Santiago*, intimidating *Rosa Diaz*, food-obsessed *Charles Boyle*, and bureaucrat-slacker duo *Hitchcock* and *Scully*.
- Canceled by Fox after five seasons in 2018, only to be resurrected within twenty-four hours by NBC following an overwhelming public campaign by devoted fans.
- Re-evaluated its portrayal of law enforcement in its final season, directly addressing racial profiling, systemic police corruption, and the aftermath of the 2020 Black Lives Matter protests.

## connections

- [[new-york-city]] — vibrant urban metropolis whose outer borough of Brooklyn provides the setting, local flavor, and diverse jurisdiction for the squad.
- [[slapstick-comedy]] — visual comedic tradition evident in the show's rapid-fire pratfalls, exaggerated arrests, and chaotic interrogation room stunts.
- [[physical-comedy]] — foundational performance style mastered by *Andy Samberg* and *Joe Lo Truglio* through broad body humor and kinetic office chases.
- [[vaudeville]] — historical theatrical lineage whose comedic timing, eccentric double acts, and absurdity inform the precinct's banter.
- [[i-love-lucy]] — pioneering television comedy prototype that established the enduring multi-character workplace and domestic sitcom conventions used in modern broadcast television.
- [[civil-rights-movement]] — historical struggle for equality that echoes through *Captain Holt*'s trailblazing backstory confronting institutional racism and homophobia in the police force.

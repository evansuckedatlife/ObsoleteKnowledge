## summary

**Anonymous** is a decentralized, leaderless international hacktivist collective and internet subculture that originated in the early 2000s on the imageboard 4chan. Operating without a central governing structure, its participants collaborate on coordinated digital campaigns ranging from mischievous online trolling to major political leaks and distributed denial-of-service cyberattacks against powerful corporations and state agencies. Widely recognized by the stylized Guy Fawkes mask, the collective has played a substantial role in modern digital activism, transparency crusades, and global anti-censorship protests.

## you gotta know

- Adopted the smiling, stylized Guy Fawkes mask popularized by Alan Moore and David Lloyd's graphic novel *V for Vendetta* as its universal visual avatar and protest uniform.
- Known for its signature institutional motto: "We are Anonymous. We are Legion. We do not forgive. We do not forget. Expect us."
- Originated around 2003 on the English-language imageboard 4chan, where the default system label "Anonymous" assigned to untagged posters coalesced into a collective identity.
- Gained mainstream political prominence in 2008 with Project Chanology, targeting the Church of Scientology with prank faxes, distributed denial-of-service attacks, and global street protests.
- Defended the whistleblowing organization WikiLeaks in 2010 through Operation Payback, directing network strikes against financial giants such as Visa, MasterCard, and PayPal after they cut off payment processing.
- Assisted activists during the 2011 Arab Spring uprisings by executing denial-of-service attacks on authoritarian state websites and providing online communication workarounds in Tunisia and Egypt.
- Participated actively in the 2011 Occupy Wall Street demonstrations in *[[new-york-city]]*, providing technical infrastructure, video broadcasts, and boots on the ground in Zuccotti Park.
- Suffered significant operational disruption when splinter groups such as LulzSec were compromised by federal law enforcement through the cooperation of prominent hacker Sabu, also known as Hector Monsegur.

## summary

**Frodo Baggins** is the diminutive hobbit protagonist of *J.R.R. Tolkien*'s high-fantasy epic *The Lord of the Rings*, published in three volumes across 1954 and 1955 in [[england]]. Entrusted with the malevolent *One Ring* forged by the *Dark Lord Sauron*, he undertakes an agonizing pilgrimage across *Middle-earth* to cast the artifact into the volcanic fires of *Mount Doom*. His arduous quest stands as one of the most influential heroic narratives in modern English-language literature, celebrated for replacing triumphant martial conquest with quiet endurance, moral fortitude, and the devastating psychic costs of duty.

## you gotta know

- Inherited the *One Ring* and the pastoral subterranean estate of *Bag End* from his eccentric cousin and adoptive guardian *Bilbo Baggins* in the peaceful rural enclave of the *Shire*.
- Journeyed across *Eriador* to the elven haven of *Rivendell*, where the *Council of Elrond* formally established the nine-member *Fellowship of the Ring* and appointed him as the quest's official *Ring-bearer*.
- Sustained a grave, lingering spiritual wound at *Weathertop* when the *Witch-king of Angmar*, leader of the *Nazgûl*, stabbed him in the shoulder with a cursed *Morgul-knife* whose poisoned tip worked toward his heart.
- Traveled into the hostile wilderness alongside his devoted gardener *Samwise Gamgee*, whose steadfast loyalty, physical resilience, and practical optimism sustained him as the malevolent talisman eroded his will.
- Accepted the treacherous guidance of the corrupted creature *Gollum*, formerly known as *Sméagol*, whose obsessive longing for his lost trinket mirrored the tragic psychological ruin threatening Frodo himself.
- Succumbed to the overpowering dominion of the ring at the ultimate precipice of *Mount Doom*, claiming the treasure as his own before Gollum severed his finger with his teeth and inadvertently tumbled into the fiery chasm.
- Led the nonviolent restoration of his homeland during the *Scouring of the Shire*, strictly forbidding vengeful retribution against defeated invaders and offering clemency to the traitorous wizard *Saruman*.
- Departed mortal existence forever from the coastal haven of the *Grey Havens*, boarding a white elven ship destined for the *Undying Lands* of *Tol Eressëa* to seek healing for his incurable trauma.

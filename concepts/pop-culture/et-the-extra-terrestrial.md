## summary

**E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial** is a landmark 1982 American science fiction film directed by *Steven Spielberg* and written by *Melissa Mathison*. Depicting the tender friendship between a lonely suburban boy named Elliott and a benevolent alien stranded on Earth, the movie captured global audiences with its emotional intimacy and technical wonder. It surpassed *Star Wars* to become the highest-grossing film of all time during its original release in the *[[united-states]]* and stands as a defining cultural milestone of late twentieth-century cinema.

## you gotta know

- Directed by *Steven Spielberg*, who drew deeply upon the emotional pain and loneliness of his own parents' divorce while developing the story.
- Stars *Henry Thomas* as the young protagonist Elliott, alongside *Drew Barrymore* as his little sister Gertie and *Dee Wallace* as their mother Mary.
- Features groundbreaking creature animatronics and practical special effects created by Italian artist *Carlo Rambaldi*.
- Set against the backdrop of 1980s American suburban cul-de-sacs, contrasting childhood imagination with menacing, shadowy government scientists led by the character Keys.
- Produced the iconic cinematic image of a bicycle carrying Elliott and the alien silhouetted against a luminous full moon, which became the logo for Amblin Entertainment.
- Features a sweeping orchestral score composed by *John Williams*, which won an Academy Award for Best Original Score.
- Popularized the famous tagline "E.T. phone home" and caused a massive commercial surge in sales for Hershey's confectionery *Reese's Pieces*.

## summary

**Mario Kart** is an iconic go-kart racing video game franchise developed and published by Nintendo, originating with the 1992 release of **Super Mario Kart** on the Super Nintendo Entertainment System. The series pioneered the mascot kart racing genre by combining responsive arcade driving mechanics with chaotic item-based combat drawn from the *Super Mario* universe. Renowned for its accessible pick-up-and-play multiplayer design, deceptive competitive depth, and massive worldwide sales, it stands as one of the most enduring and commercially successful franchises in interactive entertainment history.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in 1992 on the Super Nintendo Entertainment System with **Super Mario Kart**, developed under producer *Shigeru Miyamoto* and directors *Tadashi Sugiyama* and *Hideki Konno* using the Mode 7 graphics scaling technology.
- Introduced iconic offensive and defensive item pick-ups, most notoriously the winged Spiny Shell, commonly called the *Blue Shell*, which automatically tracks down and detonates upon the race leader.
- Features *Rainbow Road* as its traditional climactic circuit, a treacherous, floating track completely devoid of guardrails that serves as the final trial in the Special Cup.
- Transitioned the franchise into full 3D polygonal graphics and four-player split-screen multiplayer with the groundbreaking 1996 Nintendo 64 release *Mario Kart 64*.
- Experimented with dual-character cooperative vehicles on the Nintendo GameCube in the 2003 title *Mario Kart: Double Dash!!*, allowing players to switch roles between driver and weapon handler.
- Released *Mario Kart Wii* in 2008, popularizing motion steering via the bundled Wii Wheel accessory and introducing motorbikes that could execute speed-boosting wheelies.
- Culminated in the astronomical commercial juggernaut *Mario Kart 8* and its Nintendo Switch reissue *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe*, which introduced anti-gravity vertical racing and became the best-selling title on the Switch platform.

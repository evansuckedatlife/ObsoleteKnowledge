## summary

**Divergent** is a dystopian young adult science-fiction novel published in 2011 by American author *Veronica Roth*, inaugurating a commercially dominant literary trilogy set in a post-apocalyptic Chicago. The narrative envisions a segregated society structured around five rigid factions dedicated to cultivating isolated human virtues in the aftermath of widespread civil collapse. Achieving international acclaim amidst the twenty-first-century boom of youth speculative fiction, the work explores the tension between state-mandated social sorting and individual identity, dramatized through the trials of an anomaly whose psychological aptitude cannot be contained.

## you gotta know

- It was written by *Veronica Roth* during her undergraduate college winter break and rapidly climbed national bestseller lists in the [[united-states]], spawning two sequels titled *Insurgent* and *Allegiant*.
- The setting organizes urban civilization into five virtue-based factions: *Abnegation* for the selfless, *Dauntless* for the brave, *Erudite* for the intellectual, *Amity* for the peaceful, and *Candor* for the honest.
- The narrative follows sixteen-year-old *Beatrice Prior*, who renames herself *Tris* after leaving her native *Abnegation* home to undergo the brutal, combat-heavy initiation rites of *Dauntless*.
- An individual whose aptitude test shows inconclusive allegiance across multiple factions is termed an anomaly of this title, marked as a lethal threat to social cohesion because their consciousness cannot easily be controlled by administrative serums.
- *Tris* falls in love with her stern initiation instructor *Tobias Eaton*, widely known by the alias *Four* due to his psychological profile exhibiting only four personal fears during mental simulations.
- The overarching political conflict erupts when the power-hungry *Erudite* leader *Jeanine Matthews* uses a mind-controlling transmitter serum to turn the *Dauntless* warrior caste into an oblivious execution squad targeting *Abnegation*.
- It was adapted into a major 2014 Hollywood feature film starring *Shailene Woodley* as *Tris* and *Theo James* as *Four*, accompanied by extensive soundtrack releases and box-office success.

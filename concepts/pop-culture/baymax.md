## summary

**Baymax** is a synthetic healthcare companion and superhero created by *Steven T. Seagle* and *Duncan Rouleau* for Marvel Comics, fundamentally redesigned as an inflatable personal healthcare robot in Walt Disney Animation Studios' twenty-fourteen feature film *Big Hero 6*. Built by robotics prodigy *Tadashi Hamada*, he is designed to comfort, diagnose, and treat ailing humans with soothing gentleness before being upgraded into an armor-clad flying hero by Tadashi's younger brother, *Hiro Hamada*. Embodying empathy and technological optimism, the character stands as a beloved modern icon of compassionate nonviolent heroism and grief counseling.

## you gotta know

- Conceived and constructed by student inventor *Tadashi Hamada* at the *San Fransokyo Institute of Technology* as an inflatable vinyl healthcare companion activated by the spoken expression of physical distress.
- Deactivates only when a treated patient explicitly utters the discharge affirmation phrase, *I am satisfied with my care*.
- Operates primarily using a green healthcare software chip programmed with over ten thousand medical procedures, diagnostic algorithms, and warm bedside manners.
- Befriends fourteen-year-old robotics genius *Hiro Hamada* following Tadashi's tragic death in an arson explosion, serving as an emotional anchor and therapeutic companion to help the grieving boy cope with loss.
- Outfitted by Hiro with high-tech carbon-fiber battle armor, rocket thrusters, wings, and a red combat program chip to hunt down the masked villain *Yokai*, who stole Hiro's microbots.
- Serves as the compassionate moral center of the superhero squad *Big Hero 6*, repeatedly preventing Hiro from surrendering to vengeful rage and abandoning his brother's noble pacifist ideals.
- Sacrifices his robotic physical body inside an unstable interdimensional portal to propel Hiro and the rescued daughter of industrialist *Alistair Krei* back to safety using his rocket fist.
- Restored to life after Hiro discovers that Baymax clutched Tadashi's original green healthcare programming chip within his detached rocket gauntlet, allowing him to be faithfully rebuilt.

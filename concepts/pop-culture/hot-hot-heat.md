## summary

**Hot Hot Heat** was an influential Canadian indie rock and dance-punk revival band formed in 1999 in Victoria, British Columbia. Fronted by lead singer and keyboardist *Steve Bays*, the quartet emerged alongside the early 2000s garage rock post-punk revival across North America and the *[[united-states]]*. Renowned for their frantic synth-driven energy, staccato vocal delivery, and melodic pop hooks, they became best known for their breakout 2002 album *Make Up the Breakdown*.

## you gotta know

- Achieved commercial and critical acclaim with their 2002 full-length debut *Make Up the Breakdown*, released on the legendary indie label *Sub Pop* and produced by *Jack Endino*.
- Scored their most enduring international hit single with "Bandages", an infectious dance-punk track whose rapid lyrical hook and quirky melody drove substantial alternative radio airplay.
- Charted additional prominent rock singles such as "Talk to Me, Dance with Me" and the melodic follow-up "No, Not Now", cementing their signature blend of art-punk keyboards and indie pop.
- Released their major-label follow-up album *Elevator* on *Sire Records* in 2005, which featured popular alternative tracks including "Goodnight Goodnight" and "Middle of Nowhere".
- Originally formed as an aggressive hardcore noise-punk outfit before adopting a new wave and synth-infused dance-rock sound when *Steve Bays* moved to lead vocals.
- Featured core long-term members including bassist *Dustin Hawthorne*, drummer *Paul Hawley*, and guitarist *Dante DeCaro*, the latter of whom later departed to join fellow Canadian indie group *Wolf Parade*.
- Concluded their recording career with a self-titled fifth studio album in 2016, officially disbanding shortly after its release.

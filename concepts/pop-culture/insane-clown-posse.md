## summary

**Insane Clown Posse** (commonly abbreviated **ICP**) is an American hip-hop duo founded in Detroit, Michigan, in 1989 by rappers *Joseph Bruce* and *Joseph Utsler*, performing under the stage personas *Violent J* and *Shaggy 2 Dope*. Famous for their theatrical face paint, carnivalesque horrorcore lyrics, and an elaborate overarching cosmology called the *Dark Carnival*, the duo cultivated one of the most dedicated independent music followings in the [[united-states]]. Their grassroots empire spawned the multi-day *Gathering of the Juggalos* festival and the signature concert tradition of dousing audiences with generic *Faygo* soda.

## you gotta know

- Structured their primary musical discography around the *Dark Carnival*, an elaborate conceptual mythology depicting a purgatorial carnival where souls face judgment through six distinct album releases termed *Joker's Cards*.
- Completed the original six-album *Joker's Card* cycle spanning from 1992 to 2002, which encompassed *Carnival of Carnage*, *The Ringmaster*, *Riddle Box*, *The Great Milenko*, *The Amazing Jeckel Brothers*, and *The Wraith: Shangri-La*.
- Cultivated a passionate subculture of fans known as *Juggalos* and *Juggalettes*, identifiable by clown face paint, running *Hatchetman* logos, and the ubiquitous greeting chant "whoop whoop."
- Generated a widely circulated viral video and lasting internet meme with their 2009 song *Miracles*, noted for its earnest bewilderment over natural phenomena and the famous query regarding how magnets function.
- Mounted an extended legal challenge alongside the American Civil Liberties Union against the Federal Bureau of Investigation, which had classified their fanbase as a hybrid street gang in a 2011 national threat report.
- Maintained extraordinary commercial independence by founding *Psychopathic Records*, allowing them to manage their merchandise, produce feature films, and run their own professional wrestling promotion, *Juggalo Championship Wrestling*.
- Created a major media controversy in 1997 when Disney subsidiary Hollywood Records abruptly recalled and canceled their breakthrough album *The Great Milenko* mere hours after release, prompting a bidding war won by Island Records.

## summary

**Money Heist** (*La casa de papel*) is a Spanish heist crime drama television series created by *Álex Pina* that became an international pop-culture phenomenon following its acquisition by *Netflix*. Set in Madrid, the series traces two elaborate, multi-day assaults meticulously planned and coordinated by a mastermind known as the *Professor*—first on the Royal Mint of Spain and later on the Bank of Spain. Noted for its non-linear narrative, iconic red jumpsuits, and Salvador Dalí masks, the production stands as one of the most widely watched non-English television series in streaming history.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Álex Pina* and originally broadcast on the Spanish terrestrial network *Antena 3* before finding worldwide acclaim when *Netflix* re-cut and distributed the series globally.
- Features code-named robbers who use major international city names, with the volatile and impulsive narrator *Tokyo* (*Úrsula Corberó*) serving as the narrative core alongside *Berlin*, *Nairobi*, *Rio*, *Denver*, *Helsinki*, *Oslo*, and *Moscow*.
- Centers on the brilliant, socially awkward criminal strategist *Sergio Marquina*, known as the *Professor*, who orchestrates operations from an external command hideout while manipulating police negotiators *Raquel Murillo* and *Alicia Sierra*.
- Popularized the Italian anti-fascist partisan anthem *"Bella ciao"*, which became a viral charting single and modern protest anthem across various global demonstrations after being repeatedly sung by characters in the show.
- Distinctive visual iconography includes bright red hooded jumpsuits and caricature masks of surrealist painter *Salvador Dalí*, which were widely adopted by real-world political protestors, activists, and sports supporters.
- Focuses thematic weight on anti-systemic financial rebellion and Robin Hood-style social redistribution, framing the minting of untraceable currency rather than the theft of existing savings as an ideological challenge to central banking systems.
- Concludes the first major plot arc with the 11-day seizure of the Royal Mint, wherein hostages and captors wear matching attire to confound law enforcement tactical snipers and special forces.
- Spawned significant franchise expansion, including the spinoff series *Berlin* centered on *Pedro Alonso*'s character, as well as the 2022 South Korean adaptation *Money Heist: Korea – Joint Economic Area*.

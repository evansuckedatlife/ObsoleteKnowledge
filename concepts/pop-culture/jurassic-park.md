## summary

**Jurassic Park** is an iconic American science-fiction franchise that began with *Michael Crichton*'s 1990 techno-thriller novel and achieved global immortality through *Steven Spielberg*'s 1993 film adaptation. Set on the remote Central American island of *Isla Nublar*, the narrative recounts the disastrous failure of an extravagant amusement park populated with de-extinct dinosaurs cloned from fossilized amber. The film revolutionized visual effects through its pioneering synthesis of computer graphics and animatronics, setting a new technical benchmark for blockbuster cinema.

## you gotta know

- Authored in 1990 by *Michael Crichton* as a cautionary tale detailing corporate greed, commercial bioethics, and the cascading unpredictability of complex genetic systems.
- Directed by *Steven Spielberg* in 1993, starring *Sam Neill*, *Laura Dern*, *Jeff Goldblum*, and *Richard Attenborough*, shattering box-office records to become the highest-grossing film of its era.
- Revolutionized the Hollywood film industry by blending photorealistic computer-generated imagery from *Industrial Light & Magic* with life-size practical animatronics engineered by *Stan Winston*.
- Popularized chaos theory and mathematical unpredictability through the dialogue of eccentric mathematician *Ian Malcolm*, immortalizing the phrase "life finds a way."
- Dominated the technical categories at the Academy Awards, winning Oscars for Best Visual Effects, Best Sound, and Best Sound Effects Editing for *Gary Rydstrom*'s innovative creature sound design.
- Fundamentally modernized public perceptions of dinosaurs, popularizing paleontological theories that depicted them as agile, intelligent, warm-blooded forebears of birds rather than sluggish lizards.
- Expanded into a multi-billion-dollar global media franchise encompassing cinematic sequels such as *The Lost World*, video games, theme-park rides, and the *Jurassic World* revival series.

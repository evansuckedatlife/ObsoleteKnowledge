## summary

**Aretha Franklin**, born **Aretha Louise Franklin** and universally acclaimed as the **Queen of Soul**, was an American singer, songwriter, and pianist recognized as one of the preeminent vocalists in the history of popular music. Emerging from a youth immersed in gospel traditions at her father's Detroit church, she signed with *Atlantic Records* in 1967 to unleash an unmatched sequence of commercial and critical masterpieces that fused religious emotional ecstasy with secular rhythm and blues. In quiz-bowl tournaments and twentieth-century cultural studies, she is venerated for her transcendent vocal range, her pioneering feminist and racial-justice anthems, and her achievement as the very first woman inducted into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame*.

## you gotta know

- Formally crowned with the enduring title of the *Queen of Soul* by Chicago disc jockey *Pervis Spann* during a 1967 performance at the *Regal Theater*, an honorific that defined her public persona for the remainder of her life.
- Transformed *Otis Redding*'s composition *Respect* into a towering, Grammy-winning national anthem of black pride and feminist empowerment in 1967, memorably adding the spelled-out hook and backed by her sisters' vocal arrangements.
- Cultivated her extraordinary keyboard technique and vocal delivery at *New Bethel Baptist Church* in Detroit under the guidance of her father, the celebrated minister and civil rights activist Reverend *C. L. Franklin*.
- Stepped in on twenty minutes of notice at the 1998 Grammy Awards to deliver a breathtaking, critically lauded performance of *Puccini*'s operatic aria *Nessun dorma*, filling in for an indisposed *Luciano Pavarotti*.
- Made history in 1987 by becoming the inaugural female performer inducted into the *Rock and Roll Hall of Fame*, later receiving the *Presidential Medal of Freedom* in 2005 for her exceptional contributions to American culture.
- Performed at the inaugurations of three different United States presidents—*Jimmy Carter*, *Bill Clinton*, and *Barack Obama*—most famously sporting a custom grey felt hat with a Swarovski-crystal bow during the 2009 inauguration.
- Honored at her passing in August 2018 with an eight-hour televised memorial celebration in Detroit, where twenty-first-century pop star [[ariana-grande]] performed a signature rendition of *(You Make Me Feel Like) A Natural Woman*.

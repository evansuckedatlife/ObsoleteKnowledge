## summary

**Raya and the Last Dragon** is a 2021 American computer-animated fantasy adventure film produced by *Walt Disney Animation Studios* and directed by *Don Hall* and *Carlos López Estrada*. Set in the fractured mythical realm of *Kumandra*, the narrative follows a lone warrior princess who embarks on an arduous quest to track down the legendary water spirit *Sisu* in order to repair a broken divine gem and eradicate a mindless, petrifying pestilence known as the *Druun*. Acclaimed for its vibrant homage to Southeast Asian textiles, architecture, and martial arts, the film received critical praise and an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature.

## you gotta know

- Takes place on a fantasy landmass shaped like a dragon that is partitioned into five warring tribal lands named after parts of a dragon's body: Heart, Fang, Talon, Spine, and Tail.
- Features the voice talents of *Kelly Marie Tran* as the determined martial artist protagonist *Raya* and *Awkwafina* as the self-deprecating water dragon *Sisu*, with *Gemma Chan* voicing her antagonistic childhood rival *Namaari*.
- Depicts its existential antagonist not as a sentient villain with personal malice, but as the *Druun*, a faceless, undulating dark plague born of human selfishness that petrifies all living beings into stone.
- Premiered simultaneously in physical movie theaters and via paid streaming on *Disney+ Premier Access* in early 2021 due to public gathering limitations during the global coronavirus pandemic in the *[[united-states]]*.
- Features meticulously choreographed hand-to-hand and bladed combat inspired by traditional Southeast Asian disciplines, particularly the Filipino weapon-based martial arts of *Arnis* and *Eskrima* and Indonesian *Silat*.
- Drew extensive anthropological inspiration from cultures across Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Laos, guided by a collaborative advisory panel termed the *Southeast Asia Story Trust*.
- Contended at the 94th Academy Awards for Best Animated Feature, earning a competitive nomination alongside top international animated films before ultimately conceding the statuette to Disney's *Encanto*.

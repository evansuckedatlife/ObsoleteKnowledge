## summary

**Highlander** is a 1986 British-American fantasy action film directed by *Russell Mulcahy* that launched an extensive multimedia franchise encompassing sequels, television spin-offs, and novels. The narrative chronicles the centuries-spanning conflict among immortal warriors who can die only by decapitation, culminating in a modern-day showdown in *New York City*. Celebrated for its stylish transitions, energetic rock soundtrack by *Queen*, and iconic tagline, the work remains an influential cult classic of fantasy cinema.

## you gotta know

- Popularized the legendary catchphrase *"There can be only one"*, uttered as immortal warriors duel to be the last survivor.
- Stars *Christopher Lambert* as **Connor MacLeod**, a 16th-century Scottish clansman who discovers his immortality after surviving a mortal wound in battle.
- Features *Sean Connery* as *Juan Sánchez-Villalobos Ramírez*, an ancient Egyptian-Spanish immortal who mentors MacLeod in swordsmanship and the rules of *The Game*.
- Features *Clancy Brown* as the villainous *Kurgan*, a brutal warrior pursuing the ultimate omniscient power known as *The Prize*.
- Renowned for dramatic visual match cuts and ambitious [[camera-movement]] that fluidly transition between 16th-century Scotland and 1980s [[new-york-city]].
- Features an iconic, bombastic rock score and songs composed specifically for the production by the British band *Queen*, including *"Princes of the Universe"* and *"Who Wants to Live Forever"*.
- When an immortal is decapitated, the victor absorbs their essence, knowledge, and power through an explosive, atmospheric phenomenon known as the *Quickening*.
- Succeeded by multiple sequels and a popular 1990s television series starring *Adrian Paul* as fellow clansman *Duncan MacLeod*.

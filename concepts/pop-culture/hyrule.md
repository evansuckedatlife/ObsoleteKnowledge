## summary

**Hyrule** is the fictional fantasy kingdom that serves as the primary setting for Nintendo's acclaimed action-adventure video game franchise *The Legend of Zelda*. Created by Japanese video game designers *Shigeru Miyamoto* and *Takashi Tezuka* and introduced in the 1986 *Nintendo Entertainment System* title, the realm is characterized by its recurring cycle of divine creation, political collapse, and mythical rebirth. It stands as one of the most culturally significant and geographically influential virtual worlds in gaming history, pioneering modern open-world exploration.

## you gotta know

- Created by the three Golden Goddesses (*Din*, *Nayru*, and *Farore*), who departed the world leaving behind the *Triforce*, an omnipotent golden relic granting the wishes of whoever touches it.
- Inhabited primarily by the elf-like *Hylians*, alongside diverse regional species including the aquatic *Zora*, the rocky mountainous *Gorons*, the desert-dwelling *Gerudo*, and the arboreal *Kokiri*.
- Centers around a perpetual, epoch-spanning mythical struggle involving the hero *Link*, the royal princess *Zelda*, and the demon king *Ganon* (or *Ganondorf*), representing Courage, Wisdom, and Power respectively.
- Features iconic, recurring physical landmarks such as *Death Mountain*, *Lake Hylia*, *Hyrule Castle*, the *Lost Woods*, and the *Temple of Time*.
- Depicted in varied historical and cataclysmic states across diverging timelines, including a submerged ocean world in *The Wind Waker* and an apocalyptic, nature-reclaimed wilderness in *Breath of the Wild*.
- Governed by a divine monarchy whose royal crest incorporates the triangular *Triforce* held aloft by the wings of the celestial *Loftwing* bird.
- Radically revolutionized environmental design and non-linear open-world mechanics in modern gaming through the vast sandbox geography of *Breath of the Wild* (2017) and *Tears of the Kingdom* (2023).

## connections

- [[japan]] — the nation where creators *Shigeru Miyamoto* and *Takashi Tezuka* conceptualized the world for the Kyoto-based company *Nintendo*.
- [[skyrim]] — another landmark fantasy gaming realm renowned for broad environmental exploration, open landscapes, and deep medieval worldbuilding.
- [[minecraft]] — the wildly popular sandbox game that shares Hyrule's emphasis on open-ended exploration, crafting, and player spatial freedom.
- [[esports]] — the competitive gaming ecosystem that developed around Nintendo speedrunning communities mastering Hyrule's intricate game engines.
- [[ancient-greece]] — the mythological tradition whose pantheons, divine relics, and epic heroic journeys parallel the legendary cosmology of Hyrule's Golden Goddesses.
- [[tragedy]] — the narrative dramatic tradition that echoes through Hyrule's recurring historical catastrophes, fallen kingdoms, and ruined civilizations.

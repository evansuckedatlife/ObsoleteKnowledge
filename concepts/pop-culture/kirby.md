## summary

**Kirby** is a fictional video game character and the primary protagonist of the eponymous multimedia franchise developed by *HAL Laboratory* and published by *Nintendo*. Created in *Japan* in 1992 by game designer *Masahiro Sakurai*, the spherical pink hero is celebrated for his cheerful demeanor and his ability to inhale enemies, swallow objects, and gain their physical powers. Widely recognized as one of gaming's most enduring and universally recognizable mascots, he has appeared in dozens of platformers, racing titles, spin-offs, and competitive fighting games across four decades.

## you gotta know

- Created by legendary developer *Masahiro Sakurai* at age nineteen for the 1992 Game Boy title *Kirby's Dream Land*, originally serving as a round placeholder sprite named *Twinkle Popo* until the studio fell in love with his minimalist design.
- Famously acquires his iconic Copy Ability in the 1993 NES sequel *Kirby's Adventure*, allowing him to swallow inhaled enemies and transform into forms possessing swords, fire breath, ice magic, or martial arts strikes.
- Inhabits the whimsical realm of *Dream Land* on the ring-shaped *Planet Popstar*, where he frequently protects peaceful citizens from recurring antagonists such as the gluttonous monarch *King Dedede*, the chivalrous swordsman *Meta Knight*, and cosmic entities like *Nightmare* and *Dark Matter*.
- Named in honor of American attorney *John Kirby*, who successfully defended *Nintendo* against *Universal Studios* in the landmark 1984 copyright infringement lawsuit alleging *Donkey Kong* infringed upon *King Kong*.
- Serves as an omnipresent core combatant throughout the *Super Smash Bros.* fighting game franchise, prominently featured as the sole surviving hero who rescues all other Nintendo characters in the expansive "World of Light" adventure mode of *Super Smash Bros. Ultimate*.
- Voiced continuously since the 1999 debut of *Super Smash Bros.* by Japanese voice actress *Makiko Ohmoto*, who provides his distinctive squeaks, cheerful cheers, and expressive nonverbal vocalizations.
- Transitioned into complete three-dimensional platforming with the critically acclaimed 2022 Nintendo Switch release *Kirby and the Forgotten Land*, introducing the "Mouthful Mode" mechanic enabling him to stretch over cars, vending machines, and light bulbs.

## summary

**Ringo Starr** is an English musician, singer, songwriter, and actor who gained worldwide fame as the drummer for the legendary rock band *The Beatles*. Born *Richard Starkey* in Liverpool, he anchored the most culturally influential pop group of the twentieth century with his steady, idiosyncratic, and groove-oriented percussion. Beyond his work in the Fab Four, he developed a prolific solo career, pioneered his touring supergroup the *All-Starr Band*, and narrated beloved children's television programs.

## you gotta know

- Joined *The Beatles* in August 1962, replacing original drummer *Pete Best* right before the group achieved monumental international breakthrough.
- Sang lead vocals on several of the band's most recognizable and charming anthems, including *Yellow Submarine* and *With a Little Help from My Friends*.
- Penned notable original compositions for the group, most famously *Octopus's Garden* and *Don't Pass Me By*, while receiving shared writing credits on tracks like *Flying*.
- Pioneered a distinctive drumming technique characterized by leading with his left hand while playing a right-handed kit, producing an unmistakable dragging fill style.
- Launched a successful solo career following the group's 1970 breakup, scoring chart-topping global hits with singles such as *Photograph*, *You're Sixteen*, and *It Don't Come Easy*.
- Entertained younger generations as the on-screen storyteller and narrator for the classic British children's television series *Thomas & Friends* and *Shining Time Station*.
- Formed the rotating concert ensemble *Ringo Starr & His All-Starr Band* in 1989, touring consistently for decades alongside notable veteran rock performers.
- Endured serious childhood illnesses in Liverpool, including peritonitis and pleurisy, spending years hospitalized before taking up percussion in a convalescent hospital band.

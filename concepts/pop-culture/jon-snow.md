## summary

**Jon Snow**, born **Aegon Targaryen**, is a central heroic protagonist in *George R. R. Martin*'s epic fantasy novel series *A Song of Ice and Fire* and its acclaimed television adaptation *Game of Thrones*. Raised in the northern stronghold of Winterfell as the purported bastard son of *Lord Eddard Stark*, he joins the ancient military brotherhood of the *Night's Watch* to defend the realm of *Westeros* from supernatural threats beyond the Wall. His complex journey through martial leadership, resurrection, political betrayal, and hidden royal lineage makes him an archetype of honor grappling with bleak moral ambiguity.

## you gotta know

- Repeatedly told the defining phrase *"You know nothing, Jon Snow"* by the free folk wildling spearwife *Ygritte*, with whom he shares a doomed romance in the icy wilderness.
- Accompanied by *Ghost*, an albino direwolf with crimson eyes that serves as his fierce guardian and mystical warg companion reflecting his Stark heritage.
- Wields *Longclaw*, an ancestral Valyrian steel bastard sword with a carved white stone wolf's head pommel, gifted to him by Lord Commander *Jeor Mormont*.
- Rises to become the 998th Lord Commander of the *Night's Watch*, forging a controversial alliance with the wildlings that leads to his mutinous assassination by his own sworn brothers.
- Restored to life by the Red Priestess *Melisandre*, an existential resurrection that releases him from his vows and sets him on the path to reclaim Winterfell from *Ramsay Bolton*.
- Crowned King in the North by northern lords following the brutal *Battle of the Bastards*, leading the collective defense against the apocalyptic *Night King*.
- Revealed to be the legitimate son of *Rhaegar Targaryen* and *Lyanna Stark*, making him the rightful heir to the *Iron Throne* before his ultimate exile beyond the Wall after executing *Daenerys Targaryen*.

- [[skyrim]] — the epic fantasy video game sharing his northern aesthetic of permafrost, ancient ruins, and legendary dragons.
- [[existentialism]] — the philosophical struggle against cosmic nihilism mirrored in the looming winter and cold undead horde.
- [[joker]] — a dark thematic contrast representing agentic chaos and nihilism opposed to Jon's rigid, self-sacrificing moral duty.

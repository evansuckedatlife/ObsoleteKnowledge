## summary

**Morgan Freeman** is an iconic American actor, director, and narrator whose deep baritone voice, calm authority, and extraordinary screen presence have established him among the most revered performers in cinema history. Emerging from acclaimed stage work and public educational television in the 1970s, he rose to international superstardom through celebrated roles in *Driving Miss Daisy*, *Glory*, *The Shawshank Redemption*, and *Million Dollar Baby*. Renowned for embodying dignity, quiet moral strength, and profound wisdom, he has become one of modern Hollywood's most recognized cultural figures.

## you gotta know

- Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for portraying world-weary former boxer Eddie "Scrap-Iron" Dupris in Clint Eastwood's 2004 boxing drama *Million Dollar Baby*.
- Received an Academy Award nomination for his universally beloved performance as convicted lifer Ellis Boyd "Red" Redding in *The Shawshank Redemption* (1994), also serving as the film's iconic narrator.
- Portrayed God with warm humor and white-suited gravitas in the box-office comedy hit *Bruce Almighty* (2003) and its companion film *Evan Almighty* (2007), cementing his status as cinema's definitive divine voice.
- Played Lucius Fox, the principled Wayne Enterprises executive and tactical gadget armorer, throughout Christopher Nolan's blockbuster *The Dark Knight* cinematic trilogy.
- Earned critical acclaim as Sergeant Major John Rawlins alongside Denzel Washington in the 1989 historical drama *Glory*, chronicling the heroic exploits of the 54th Massachusetts Infantry Regiment during the [[american-civil-war]].
- Originated the role of soft-spoken Southern chauffeur Hoke Colburn on the stage before reprising it in the 1989 feature film adaptation of *Driving Miss Daisy*, winning a Golden Globe Award.
- Gained early career national television exposure on the PBS children's series *The Electric Company*, portraying popular recurring characters including Easy Reader, Mel Mounds, and Vincent the Vegetable Vampire.
- Portrayed South African president Nelson Mandela in Clint Eastwood's 2009 sports drama *Invictus*, depicting how the 1995 Rugby World Cup helped unite a divided post-apartheid nation.

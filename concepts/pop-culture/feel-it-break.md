## summary

**Feel It Break** is the critically acclaimed debut studio album by Canadian electronic music outfit *Austra*, released in May 2011 by *Domino Recording Company* and *Paper Bag Records*. Written and led by operatically trained vocalist and composer *Katie Stelmanis*, the album blends dark synth-pop, new wave, industrial percussion, and dramatic, gothic-tinged electronic arrangements. The record established the group as a prominent voice in modern indie-pop and earned a nomination for the prestigious *Polaris Music Prize*.

## you gotta know

- Written and performed by *Katie Stelmanis*, who drew upon her classical training with the *Canadian Children's Opera Company* before moving into punk and electronic composition.
- Features prominent contributions from core collaborators *Maya Postepski* on drums and programming and *Dorian Wolf* on bass guitar.
- Released internationally to critical acclaim in May 2011, securing a spot on the short list for the 2011 *Polaris Music Prize* alongside records by *Arcade Fire* and *Destroyer*.
- Anchored by standout singles such as "Lose It," "Beat and the Pulse," and "Spellwork," which juxtapose icy synthesizers against resonant, sweeping vocal melodies.
- Explores lyrical themes of alienation, romantic longing, psychological despair, and queer identity through an evocative, dramatic performance style.
- Received the award for Electronic Album of the Year at the 2012 Juno Awards, confirming its influence across the contemporary North American indie landscape.
- Frequently cited alongside contemporaries in the early-2010s dark synth-pop resurgence, including *The Knife*, *Zola Jesus*, and *Fever Ray*.

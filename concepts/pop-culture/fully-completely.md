## summary

**Fully Completely** is the critically acclaimed third studio album by Canadian rock band *The Tragically Hip*, released in October 1992 through MCA Records. Recorded in London with British producer *Chris Tsangarides*, the album achieved diamond certification in Canada and solidified the Kingston, Ontario group as an indispensable pillar of Canadian cultural identity. Renowned for frontman *Gord Downie*'s evocative storytelling, the album weaves poignant Canadian historical events, literary portraits, and cultural folklore into anthemic, hard-driving alternative rock.

## you gotta know

- Released in October 1992 by *The Tragically Hip*, reaching number one on Canadian charts and eventually earning Canadian diamond sales certification for over one million copies sold.
- Recorded at *Battery Studios* in London, [[england]], alongside producer *Chris Tsangarides*, who brought a polished, guitar-driven hard rock sonic punch to the band's rootsy sound.
- Filled with vivid historical and cultural references, notably the unjust wrongful conviction of *David Milgaard* commemorated in the track *Wheat Kings*.
- Features the iconic single *Courage (for Hugh MacLennan)*, which directly tributes Canadian novelist *Hugh MacLennan* and cites his Governor General's Award-winning novel *The Watch That Ends the Night*.
- Explores hockey folklore and local tragedy in *Fifty-Mission Cap*, chronicling the disappearance and fatal plane crash of 1951 Toronto Maple Leafs defenseman *Bill Barilko*.
- Prefigured subsequent historic waves of internationally recognized Canadian alternative rock albums, including [[funeral-arcade-fire]] and Polaris Prize winner [[fucked-up]].

## summary

The **Justice League**, originally known as the **Justice League of America** or **JLA**, is the flagship superhero ensemble created by writer *Gardner Fox* for DC Comics in 1960. Traditionally anchored by the "Big Seven"—*Superman*, *Batman*, *Wonder Woman*, *The Flash*, *Green Lantern*, *Aquaman*, and *Martian Manhunter*—the team unites the world's most formidable champions to confront cosmic, mystical, and global crises. As an enduring pop-culture institution, the group codified the modern team-crossover format, pioneered multiverse storytelling, and inspired numerous animated and cinematic adaptations.

## you gotta know

- Debuted in *The Brave and the Bold* #28 in March 1960, repelling the planetary invasion of *Starro the Conqueror*, an event that sparked the Silver Age comic renaissance.
- Conceived as a modern reimagining of the Golden Age *Justice Society of America*, the wartime superhero alliance that had disbanded during the postwar publishing slump.
- Operating headquarters have historically transitioned from a secret cave in *Happy Harbor*, Rhode Island, to an orbiting satellite base and the lunar *Watchtower*.
- Established DC's famous *Multiverse* construct through annual summer crossover stories with Earth-Two's heroes, beginning with the 1963 landmark *Crisis on Earth-One!*.
- Underwent an iconic comedic transformation in 1987 under writers *Keith Giffen* and *J. M. DeMatteis*, popularizing lighthearted workplace camaraderie featuring *Blue Beetle*, *Booster Gold*, and *Guy Gardner*.
- Reclaimed blockbuster commercial dominance in 1997 under writer *Grant Morrison*, who restored the core mythic lineup in *JLA* to battle divine and existential threats as modern gods.
- Formed the central subject of *Justice League: Generation Lost*, a high-stakes 2010 comic maxiseries scripted by *Judd Winick* focusing on hunting the resurrected mastermind *Maxwell Lord*.
- Adapted into widely celebrated television projects, particularly the *Bruce Timm*-helmed animated series *Justice League* and *Justice League Unlimited* in the early 2000s.

## summary

**Conan O'Brien**, frequently known mononymously as **Conan**, is an American comedian, writer, television host, and podcaster renowned for redefining late-night comedy across four decades. After distinguished writing tenures on *Saturday Night Live* and *The Simpsons*, he succeeded *David Letterman* as host of NBC's *Late Night with Conan O'Brien* in 1993, developing a celebrated style combining highbrow wit, surreal absurdity, and energetic self-deprecation. Following a high-profile scheduling controversy during his brief 2009–2010 tenure on *The Tonight Show*, he transitioned to cable with TBS's *Conan* and subsequently built the successful modern digital media brand *Team Coco*.

## you gotta know

- Hosted NBC's *Late Night with Conan O'Brien* from 1993 to 2009, surviving severe early critical hostility to establish an innovative, youth-oriented television institution.
- Penned some of the most critically revered episodes in television history during his time on *The Simpsons*, including the acclaimed musical satire *"Marge vs. the Monorail"* and *"Homer Goes to College"*.
- Served as a core writer on *Saturday Night Live* between 1988 and 1991, co-creating recurring sketches such as *"Mr. Short-Term Memory"* and the elevator-set *"The Girl Watchers"*.
- Collaborated for decades with dry-witted comic sidekick *Andy Richter* and bandleader *Max Weinberg*, who directed the versatile house band *The Max Weinberg 7*.
- Departed NBC in 2010 during the sensationalized *Tonight Show* conflict after network executives sought to return *Jay Leno* to late-night programming, sparking the nationwide *"I'm with Coco"* fan mobilization.
- Cultivated legendary recurring bits including remote on-location investigations, *Triumph the Insult Comic Dog* performed by *Robert Smigel*, the *Masturbating Bear*, and a recurring prank wherein actor *Paul Rudd* substituted promotional clips with footage from the 1988 film *Mac and Me*.
- Reached millions of digital viewers through his comedic *Clueless Gamer* series, where his genuine lack of gaming skill provided comedic commentary on popular video games.
- Launched the flagship podcast *Conan O'Brien Needs a Friend* in 2018, leading to the creation of the *Team Coco* podcast network later acquired by SiriusXM.

## summary

**Aquaman** is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by *DC Comics*, created by *Paul Norris* and *Mort Weisinger*. Debuting in *More Fun Comics* #73 in November 1941, he is the telepathic ruler of Atlantis and monarch of the Earth's oceans, possessing superhuman strength, marine telepathy, and aquatic physiology. Long an iconic founding member of the *Justice League of America*, the character evolved from a lighthearted Golden Age adventurer into a brooding, regal warrior who defends oceanic ecosystems against surface pollution and political warfare.

## you gotta know

- Created during the Golden Age of Comic Books by writer *Mort Weisinger* and artist *Paul Norris*, debuting in *More Fun Comics* alongside *Green Arrow*.
- Born *Arthur Curry* to human lighthouse keeper *Tom Curry* and Atlantean queen *Atlanna*, making him the rightful heir to the underwater throne of *Atlantis*.
- Possesses the unique ability to communicate with and command marine life via telepathy, often depicted visually as concentric sonar ripples emanated from his forehead.
- Wields the magical *Trident of Poseidon* (or *Trident of Neptune*), a divine relic capable of manipulating water, unleashing energy bolts, and commanding ocean currents.
- Founding member of the *Justice League of America*, standing alongside *Superman*, *Batman*, *Wonder Woman*, *The Flash*, *Green Lantern*, and *Martian Manhunter*.
- Underwent a gritty 1990s reinvention penned by writer *Peter David*, in which he lost his left hand to piranhas and replaced it with a harpoon spearhead.
- Contends with major archenemies including his half-brother *Ocean Master* (*Orm Marius*) and the high-tech mercenary *Black Manta* (*David Hyde*).
- Adapted into modern live-action cinema within the *DC Extended Universe*, portrayed by actor *Jason Momoa* in *Batman v Superman*, *Justice League*, and his standalone 2018 film.

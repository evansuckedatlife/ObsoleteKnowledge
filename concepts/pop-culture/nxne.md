## summary

**North by Northeast**, commonly abbreviated as **NXNE**, is an annual music and arts festival held each June in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1995 as a Canadian counterpart and sibling festival to Austin's renowned South by Southwest (SXSW), the gathering showcases emerging indie rock, hip-hop, and electronic musicians alongside film screenings, comedy, and digital media panels. It stands as a vital cultural platform in North American live entertainment, drawing thousands of touring performers, music industry scouts, and international audiences across dozens of downtown club venues.

## you gotta know

- Established in 1995 through an official partnership with the directors of South by Southwest, adopting a similar multiday multi-venue urban showcase format.
- Traditionally anchored at iconic live music venues across Queen Street West, Kensington Market, and Yonge-Dundas Square in central Toronto.
- Expanded over decades from an indie-rock platform into an expansive multi-arts summit featuring interactive technology summits, visual art, comedy shows, and an independent film festival.
- Attracted notable headliners and breakthrough performances across genres, hosting artists such as *The Flaming Lips*, *Post Malone*, *Run the Jewels*, *St. Vincent*, *De La Soul*, *Iggy and the Stooges*, and *Chvrches*.
- Sparked significant local controversy in 2015 regarding a scheduled performance by rapper *Action Bronson* at Yonge-Dundas Square, culminating in a public petition that led organizers to relocate the concert to a private venue.
- Briefly transitioned into a consolidated festival village format at the Port Lands in 2016 and 2017 before returning to its foundational downtown club-hopping roots to champion grassroots live venues.
- Cancelled in-person festivities during the 2020 and 2021 coronavirus pandemic before staging a full live return in 2022 emphasizing accessible low-cost admission wristbands to revitalize local nightlife.

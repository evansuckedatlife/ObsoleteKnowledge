## summary

**Kendall Jenner** is an American fashion model, television personality, and businesswoman who gained worldwide recognition through the reality broadcast *Keeping Up with the Kardashians*. Emerging from Los Angeles in the *[[united-states]]*, she leveraged unprecedented social media visibility into a career as one of the world's most lucrative high-fashion runway models. Her rise exemplifies the twenty-first-century transformation of celebrity, where digital connectivity and legacy fashion institutions in hubs like *[[new-york-city]]* intertwine.

## you gotta know

- Rose to fame as a child on the *E!* network reality television series *Keeping Up with the Kardashians*, which debuted in 2007 and documented the lives of her blended family.
- Named the world's highest-paid model by *Forbes* magazine in 2017, unseating veteran supermodel *Gisele Bündchen* who had held the top spot continuously since 2002.
- Debuted on international high-fashion runways in 2014, walking for prominent fashion houses including *Marc Jacobs*, *Chanel*, *Givenchy*, *Fendi*, and *Balmain*.
- Founded the award-winning beverage enterprise *818 Tequila* in 2021, expanding her commercial portfolio from brand endorsements into independent spirits manufacturing.
- Starred in a controversial 2017 *Pepsi* television commercial that was pulled from circulation within days after widespread public criticism for trivializing contemporary civil rights protests.
- Walked in multiple editions of the televised *Victoria's Secret Fashion Show*, cementing her status as an internationally recognizable commercial supermodel.
- Ranked consistently among the top ten most followed individuals on *Instagram*, pioneering the era of the *social media supermodel* alongside peers *Gigi Hadid* and *Bella Hadid*.

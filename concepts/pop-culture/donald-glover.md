## summary

**Donald Glover**, also known by his musical stage name **Childish Gambino**, is an acclaimed American actor, writer, comedian, director, and musician. Rising to prominence as a writer on *30 Rock* and starring as Troy Barnes on the NBC sitcom *Community*, he established himself as a versatile creator before creating and starring in the groundbreaking FX series *Atlanta*. Through his musical alter ego, Glover won multiple Grammy Awards, achieving cultural and critical milestones by examining race, violence, and identity in contemporary America.

## you gotta know

- Created, wrote, produced, directed, and starred in the acclaimed surreal comedy-drama series *Atlanta*, winning two Primetime Emmy Awards for acting and directing.
- Released the landmark 2018 single and viral music video *This Is America*, which addressed systemic gun violence and racial injustice, winning Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards.
- Began his professional television writing career at age twenty-three on Tina Fey's NBC sitcom *30 Rock* while working alongside the comedy troupe Derrick Comedy.
- Starred as the endearing former high school quarterback Troy Barnes in Dan Harmon's cult comedy series *Community* from 2009 to 2014.
- Portrayed a young Lando Calrissian in the 2018 film *Solo: A Star Wars Story* and voiced adult Simba in the 2019 photorealistic remake of *The Lion King*.
- Generated his hip-hop stage moniker using an online Wu-Tang Clan name generator while a student at New York University's Tisch School of the Arts.
- Released major studio albums including *Camp*, *Because the Internet*, and the funk-infused *Awaken, My Love!*, which featured the hit single *Redbone*.

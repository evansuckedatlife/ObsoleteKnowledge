## summary

**Kevin James** is an American comedian, actor, and screenwriter celebrated for his prominent roles in network television and broad commercial comedy films. Born on Long Island in the *[[united-states]]*, he achieved national prominence starring as parcel delivery driver *Doug Heffernan* on the long-running *CBS* sitcom *The King of Queens*. Known for blending self-deprecating suburban warmth with nimble *[[slapstick-comedy]]*, he built a durable career bridging classic American sitcom archetypes with physical cinematic blockbusters.

## you gotta know

- Starred for nine seasons from 1998 to 2007 as the lead character on the acclaimed sitcom *The King of Queens*, earning an *Emmy Award* nomination for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series.
- Displayed exceptional agility and athletic *[[physical-comedy]]* despite his stocky frame, drawing frequent critical comparisons to classic television performers like *Jackie Gleason*.
- Headlined the commercial blockbuster film *Paul Blart: Mall Cop* in 2009 and its 2015 sequel, portraying a dedicated, Segway-riding security officer defending a suburban shopping mall.
- Maintained a prolific collaborative creative partnership with comedy star *Adam Sandler*, co-starring in major studio films including *I Now Pronounce You Chuck & Larry*, *Grown Ups*, and *Pixels*.
- Provided the voice of *Frankenstein* in the successful animated film franchise *Hotel Transylvania* produced by *Sony Pictures Animation*.
- Began his entertainment career on the Long Island stand-up circuit in *[[new-york-city]]* before making appearances on late-night talk shows hosted by *David Letterman* and *Jay Leno*.
- Became a widespread internet meme decades into his career after a vintage 1998 promotional photograph of him smirking with hands tucked into his pockets went viral as a visual shorthand for cheeky nonchalance.

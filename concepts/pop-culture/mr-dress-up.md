## summary

**Mr. Dressup** is a beloved Canadian children's television series created by and starring American-born performer *Ernie Coombs*, broadcast on *CBC Television* from 1967 to 1996. Developed after Coombs worked alongside *Fred Rogers* on *Misterogers* in Pittsburgh and Toronto, the program emphasized creative imagination, craft projects, music, and gentle storytelling. Anchored by the magical *Tickle Trunk* and puppet companions *Casey* and *Finnegan*, the show became a foundational cultural touchstone of North American public broadcasting.

## you gotta know

- Starred Maine-born puppeteer and actor *Ernie Coombs*, who originally moved to Canada in the early 1960s with *Fred Rogers* to produce children's programming for the CBC.
- Featured the magical *Tickle Trunk*, a large patterned chest that resisted opening until Coombs tickled its lock, providing costumes and props for imaginative role-playing sketches.
- Co-starred beloved puppet characters created and operated by Australian-born puppeteer *Judith Lawrence*, including the child puppet *Casey* and their silent anthropomorphic dog companion *Finnegan*.
- Included secondary woodland puppets operated by Lawrence, such as *Alligator Al*, the owl *Aunt Bird*, and *Chester the Crow*.
- Focused on calm, unhurried, commercial-free segments highlighting drawing, construction paper arts and crafts, folk songs, storytelling, and gentle social-emotional lessons.
- Lawrence retired her puppet characters in 1989 to keep their integrity intact, prompting the series to introduce new puppet friends such as *Truffles*, *Granny*, and *Chester*.
- Ran for nearly four thousand episodes over twenty-nine years, earning Coombs the Order of Canada in 1996 for his lifelong contributions to children's media.

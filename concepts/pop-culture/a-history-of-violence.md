## summary

**A History of Violence** is a critically acclaimed 2005 neo-noir psychological thriller directed by *David Cronenberg*, adapted from the 1997 graphic novel by *John Wagner* and *Vince Locke*. Starring *Viggo Mortensen*, *Maria Bello*, *Ed Harris*, and *William Hurt*, the film examines the precarious nature of rural American domesticity when a small-town diner owner's lethal hidden past is violently unearthed. It stands as a pivotal transition in *Cronenberg's* auteur career away from bodily surrealism toward grounded, unflinching interrogations of human brutality and cinematic bloodshed.

## you gotta know

- Stars *Viggo Mortensen* as *Tom Stall*, an unassuming small-town diner proprietor in Indiana who brutally dispatches two homicidal drifters during an attempted robbery.
- Adapted by screenwriter *Josh Olson* from the *DC Comics* / *Vertigo* imprint graphic novel of the same title, earning an Academy Award nomination for Best Adapted Screenplay.
- The sudden nationwide media coverage draws the attention of Philadelphia mobsters led by *Carl Fogarty*, who insists *Tom* is actually a notorious former enforcer named *Joey Cusack*.
- Marked the end of major studio home video transitions, famously noted as the final major Hollywood feature released commercially on the *VHS* tape format in the [[united-states]].
- Features a critically lauded, Oscar-nominated supporting performance by *William Hurt* as *Joey's* estranged, theatrical crime-boss brother *Richie Cusack*.
- Noted for its sudden, unvarnished bursts of visceral action, subverting traditional Hollywood heroics by highlighting grotesque anatomical injury and long-term psychological fallout.
- Concludes with an ambiguous, dialogue-free family dinner scene that scrutinizes whether a domestic life built on deceit and bloodshed can ever truly be restored.

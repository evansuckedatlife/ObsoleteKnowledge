## summary

**Harvest** is the fourth solo studio album by Canadian-American musician *Neil Young*, released in February 1972 by Reprise Records. Featuring the backing group *The Stray Gators*, the LP blends rustic country-folk, delicate acoustic ballads, and brooding orchestral arrangements recorded with the *London Symphony Orchestra*. Anchored by the chart-topping hit *Heart of Gold*, the record remains one of the definitive artistic and commercial landmarks of early 1970s rock music.

## you gotta know

- Yielded *Neil Young*'s sole number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100 chart, the introspective acoustic track *Heart of Gold*.
- Conceived and recorded largely on Young's ranch in northern California and in Nashville, Tennessee, backed by session musicians known as *The Stray Gators*.
- Guest vocalists featured across key tracks include prominent peers such as *David Crosby*, *Stephen Stills*, *Graham Nash*, *James Taylor*, and *Linda Ronstadt*.
- Includes the controversial anti-racism and civil rights protest anthem *Alabama*, which directly critiqued racial strife in the American South.
- Features lush, somber symphonic backing arranged by *Jack Nitzsche* with the *London Symphony Orchestra* on *A Man Needs a Maid* and *There's a World*.
- Concludes with *The Needle and the Damage Done*, an acoustic lament chronicling the tragic heroin addiction of fellow bandmate *Danny Whitten*.

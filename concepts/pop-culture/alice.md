## summary

**Alice** refers to *American McGee's Alice*, a 2000 psychological horror action-adventure video game designed by American McGee, developed by Rogue Entertainment, and published by Electronic Arts, as well as its 2011 sequel *Alice: Madness Returns*. The franchise presents a dark, macabre reimagining of *Lewis Carroll*'s classic Victorian novels *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* and *Through the Looking-Glass*. Renowned for its gothic art direction and disturbing psychological themes, the series explores trauma, grief, and institutionalization through a corrupted fantasy landscape.

## you gotta know

- Features an orphaned, traumatized protagonist named *Alice Liddell*, who is institutionalized at *Rutledge Asylum* following a catastrophic house fire that killed her family.
- Reinterprets Wonderland as a twisted, grotesque projection of her shattered psyche, ruled despotically by the *Queen of Hearts*.
- Arms the protagonist with macabre toys and implements, most famously the iconic, razor-sharp *Vorpal Blade*, alongside cards, dice, and explosive jacks.
- Directed by prominent game designer *American McGee*, formerly known for his level design work on pioneering titles at id Software.
- Features an acclaimed, eerie atmospheric soundtrack composed by former Nine Inch Nails collaborator *Chris Vrenna*, using antique toys, clocks, and ambient instrumentation.
- Spawned the 2011 sequel *Alice: Madness Returns*, developed by Spicy Horse in Shanghai, which expanded the lore into Victorian London street life.
- Followed by extensive crowdfunded pre-production for a proposed third entry titled *Alice: Asylum*, which was officially shelved in 2023.

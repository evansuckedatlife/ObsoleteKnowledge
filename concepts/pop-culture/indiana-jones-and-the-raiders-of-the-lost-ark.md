## summary

**Raiders of the Lost Ark**, later marketed as **Indiana Jones and the Raiders of the Lost Ark**, is a 1981 American adventure film directed by *Steven Spielberg* and produced by *George Lucas*. Starring *Harrison Ford* as the globetrotting archaeologist *Dr. Henry "Indiana" Jones Jr.*, the film depicts a race against Nazi German occult forces in 1936 to recover the biblical Ark of the Covenant. Celebrated for revitalizing the classic action serial, it won five Academy Awards and established one of the most enduring hero archetypes and franchises in cinema history.

## you gotta know

- Conceived by George Lucas while vacationing in Hawaii with Steven Spielberg following the release of *Star Wars*, envisioning an updated homage to the cliffhanger serials produced by Republic Pictures in the 1930s and 1940s.
- Features Harrison Ford in his signature role, sporting a fedora, leather jacket, and bullwhip, a part cast only after original choice *Tom Selleck* was forced to drop out due to contractual commitments on the television series *Magnum, P.I.*.
- Contains the famous opening set piece inside a Peruvian temple, where Indy navigates pressure plates, dart traps, and a collapsing cavern before outrunning a massive rolling boulder, followed by betrayal by guide *Satipo*, played by *Alfred Molina*.
- Introduces Indy's French archaeological rival *René Belloq*, played by *Paul Freeman*, who aligns himself with the German military under *Colonel Dietrich* and the sinister Gestapo agent *Arnold Ernst Toht*.
- Includes the legendary improvised scene in Cairo where an exhausted Indy casually shoots a flashy scimitar-wielding swordsman with a revolver, devised on set because Ford was suffering from severe dysentery and could not film a protracted sword fight.
- Highlights Indiana's paralyzing phobia of snakes ("Why did it have to be snakes?"), tested when he and feisty companion *Marion Ravenwood* (played by *Karen Allen*) are trapped inside the venomous Well of Souls at Tanis.
- Concludes with the cataclysmic ritual opening of the Ark on an Aegean island, where divine spirits transform into terrifying apparitions, melting the faces of the Nazi officers while Indy and Marion survive by closing their eyes, ending with the artifact crated away inside a colossal, anonymous government warehouse.

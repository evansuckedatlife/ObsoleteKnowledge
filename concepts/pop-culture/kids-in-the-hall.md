## summary

**The Kids in the Hall** is a seminal Canadian sketch comedy troupe formed in Toronto in 1984, consisting of comedians *Dave Foley*, *Bruce McCulloch*, *Kevin McDonald*, *Mark McKinney*, and *Scott Thompson*. Their eponymous television series, which originally aired from 1989 to 1995, achieved widespread cult acclaim in both Canada and the *[[united-states]]* for its surreal, boundary-pushing, and character-driven satire. Frequently performing in drag and tackling taboo topics with avant-garde absurdity, the group fundamentally shaped modern alternative comedy and Canadian entertainment.

## you gotta know

- Composed of five core members: *Dave Foley*, *Bruce McCulloch*, *Kevin McDonald*, *Mark McKinney*, and *Scott Thompson*, without any outside regular cast additions.
- Discovered and championed by *Saturday Night Live* creator *Lorne Michaels*, who helped finance and executive produce their television debut on *CBC* and *HBO*.
- Renowned for surreal recurring characters, including the spiteful "Head Crusher" (who "crushes" distant people between his fingers), the cynical monologuist *Buddy Cole*, and the "Chicken Lady".
- Frequently played female roles across sketches without broad parody, portraying women with dimensional realism, pathos, and domestic normalcy unusual for male sketch performers of that era.
- Produced the 1996 feature film *Brain Candy*, followed by later reunion tours, the 2010 miniseries *Death Comes to Town*, and an acclaimed 2022 revival season on Amazon Prime.
- Heavily utilized *[[physical-comedy]]* and *[[slapstick-comedy]]* blended with deadpan social commentary, leaving an indelible imprint on sketch troupes like *The State* and *Mr. Show*.

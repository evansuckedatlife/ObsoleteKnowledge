## summary

**Charizard** is a draconic Fire- and Flying-type creature introduced in the foundational Generation I *Pokémon* video games developed by Game Freak and published by Nintendo in 1996. Created by designer *Atsuko Nishida*, it represents the final evolutionary stage of the starter creature *Charmander* following its intermediate form *Charmeleon*. Revered as one of the most iconic mascots in global gaming culture, it stands alongside *Pikachu* as a symbol of Japanese media exports and trading-card collecting.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the box mascot for the foundational 1996 video game *Pokémon Red* and its 2004 Game Boy Advance remake *Pokémon FireRed*.
- Evolves from *Charmeleon* starting at level 36, completing the evolutionary line initiated by the fire-type starter *Charmander*.
- Despite its dragon appearance and ability to breathe fire, it was classified strictly as Fire and Flying type rather than Dragon type in original game mechanics.
- Plays a prominent role in the animated adaptation under trainer *Ash Ketchum*, famously exhibiting stubborn disobedience before becoming one of his most dependable battlers.
- Received two distinct Mega Evolutions in *Pokémon X and Y*, designated as *Mega Charizard X* (which gains the secondary Dragon type and black coloration) and *Mega Charizard Y*.
- Featured as a standalone playable fighter in Nintendo's competitive crossover brawler *Super Smash Bros.*, both independently and alongside *Squirtle* and *Ivysaur*.
- Its original 1999 English base-set holographic trading card, illustrated by *Mitsuhiro Arita*, is among the most sought-after and high-value collectibles in modern auction history.

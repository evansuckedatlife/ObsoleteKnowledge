## summary

**Amanda Gorman** is an American poet and activist whose spoken-word and print poetry addresses themes of racial justice, democracy, feminism, and historical memory. In January 2021, she gained worldwide renown as the youngest inaugural poet in United States history after reciting her poem *The Hill We Climb* at the inauguration of Joe Biden. Her widespread prominence bridged contemporary social activism with mainstream pop culture and modern [[american-literature]].

## you gotta know

- Delivered *The Hill We Climb* at the 59th presidential inauguration in 2021, instantly becoming an international cultural phenomenon and the youngest inaugural poet in [[united-states]] history.
- Named the first-ever National Youth Poet Laureate of the [[united-states]] in 2017 after previously serving as the youth poet laureate of Los Angeles.
- Overcame an auditory processing disorder and speech impediment in childhood, which inspired her deep devotion to [[linguistics]], performance cadence, and spoken-word poetry.
- Authored celebrated poetry collections including *The One for Whom Food Is Not Enough* and *Call Us What We Carry*, as well as children's picture books such as *Change Sings: A Children's Anthem*.
- Recited an original poem titled *Chorus of the Captains* before the coin toss at Super Bowl LV in 2021, marking the first time a poet performed during the sports championship broadcast.
- Honored as co-chair of the 2021 Met Gala in [[new-york-city]] alongside Timothée Chalamet, Billie Eilish, and Naomi Osaka, showcasing her unique standing at the crossroads of literary art and global celebrity.
- Frequently collaborates with international public figures and humanitarians, including co-authoring and amplifying advocacy campaigns with activist [[angelina-jolie]] focusing on women's rights and youth civic engagement.

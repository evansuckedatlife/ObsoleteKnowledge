## summary

**Seymour Skinner**, born **Armin Tamzarian**, is the stern, neurotic administrator of *Springfield Elementary School* in the animated television comedy *The Simpsons*. Voiced by actor *Harry Shearer*, the character is defined by his stifling domestic life with his overbearing mother *Agnes Skinner*, unresolved trauma from military service during the *Vietnam War*, and an endless institutional struggle against budgetary decay and student pranks. He stands as one of the most durable caricatures of petty bureaucratic authority and repressed desperation in American popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Serves as the long-suffering head administrator of *Springfield Elementary School*, perpetually trapped between the destructive antics of *Bart Simpson* and the administrative wrath of Superintendent *Gary Chalmers*.
- Spawned the viral internet phenomenon *Steamed Hams* from the 1996 episode *22 Short Films About Springfield*, in which he constructs an escalating web of transparent lies to disguise a ruined roast dinner as regional Albany cuisine.
- Revealed to be an impostor named **Armin Tamzarian** in the controversial 1997 installment *The Principal and the Pauper*, an episode widely scrutinized for shattering long-established continuity by disclosing that he assumed his commanding officer's identity.
- Resides under the stifling domestic tyranny of his elderly mother *Agnes Skinner*, who subjects him to infantile household rules, constant surveillance, and humiliating psychological control.
- Suffered extensive wartime trauma as a United States Army sergeant during the *Vietnam War*, having spent years in a prisoner-of-war camp where he survived on fish-head stew.
- Pursued a tumultuous, clandestine workplace romance with second-grade teacher *Edna Krabappel*, which ultimately collapsed at the altar in the episode *My Big Fat Geek Wedding*.
- Carries the honorific **Principal Skinner** throughout the series, presiding over a perpetually underfunded educational facility characterized by asbestos leaks, low test scores, and substandard cafeteria meat.

## summary

**Leigh Bardugo** is a contemporary American fantasy author best known for creating the sprawling *Grishaverse*, a high-fantasy universe inspired by imperial Russian aesthetics and early modern European politics. Born in Jerusalem and raised in southern California, she achieved international prominence with her bestselling young adult series *Shadow and Bone* and the acclaimed heist duology *Six of Crows*. Her writing combines intricate worldbuilding, heist mechanics, and dark academic intrigue, establishing her as one of the defining voices in modern fantasy and young adult literature.

## you gotta know

- Created the bestselling *Grishaverse*, which begins with the *Shadow and Bone* trilogy (*Shadow and Bone*, *Siege and Storm*, and *Ruin and Rising*) chronicling orphan mapmaker Alina Starkov, who discovers she is a Sun Summoner capable of summoning pure light to combat the Shadow Fold, a dark expanse splitting the kingdom of Ravka.
- Penned the widely acclaimed *Six of Crows* duology (*Six of Crows* and *Crooked Kingdom*), centering on criminal mastermind Kaz Brekker and his crew of Dregs—including Inej Ghafa, Jesper Fahey, Wylan Van Eck, Nina Zenik, and Matthias Helvar—who attempt an impossible heist at the Fjerdan Ice Court to secure the creator of the magic-enhancing drug *jurda parem*.
- Ventured into adult dark academia fantasy with the *Alex Stern* series, beginning with *Ninth House* and its sequel *Hell Bent*, which follows Galaxy "Alex" Stern, an unconventional freshman at Yale University who possesses the rare ability to see ghosts called Grays and is recruited by the mysterious Lethe House to monitor Yale's occult secret societies.
- Expanded the *Grishaverse* through the *King of Scars* duology, which explores the political struggles of King Nikolai Lantsov of Ravka as he battles an inner demonic affliction resulting from the civil war against the Shadow Summoner known as the Darkling.
- Published *The Familiar*, a standalone historical fantasy novel set in Madrid during the Spanish Golden Age, following Luzia Cotado, a crypto-Jewish scullery maid who uses domestic magic to survive social peril during the reign of the Inquisition.
- Developed an intricate magic system known as the Small Science practiced by the Grisha, who manipulate matter at its fundamental level and are divided into Corporalki, Etherealki, and Materialki, contrasting with dangerous true creation magic termed *merzost*.
- Attended Yale University, where her real-life membership in the senior society Wolf's Head directly inspired the institutional secrecy, elite privileges, and esoteric lore found in *Ninth House*.

## connections

- [[lemony-snicket]] — fellow popular American author who writes dark, mystery-laden young adult fiction centered on resilient youths defying corrupt authority figures.
- [[american-literature]] — the literary tradition within which her bestselling fantasy epics and dark academia novels are produced and analyzed.
- [[united-states]] — home country where she studied at Yale University and launched her professional literary and television career.
- [[tragedy]] — narrative framework shaping the grim downfalls of powerful antagonists like the Darkling and the tragic backstories of her juvenile protagonists.
- [[realism]] — stylistic influence grounding her fantastical worldbuilding through detailed historical parallels with tsarist Russia and seventeenth-century Amsterdam.
- [[spain]] — historical setting for her Golden Age crypto-Jewish fantasy novel *The Familiar*.

## summary

**The Cruel Prince** is a bestselling young adult fantasy novel published in 2018 by American author *Holly Black*, launching *The Folk of the Air* trilogy. Set in the treacherous, enchanted realm of Elfhame, the narrative charts the precarious survival of a mortal human girl navigating deadly court politics, bloodthirsty aristocratic rivalries, and intoxicating deceit among immortal faerie royalty. Renowned for its intricate political scheming and morally grey character dynamics, the work revitalized contemporary dark fantasy and became a defining touchstone of modern genre literature.

## you gotta know

- Written by author *Holly Black* and released in January 2018 by Little, Brown Books for Young Readers as the opening volume of *The Folk of the Air*, followed by *The Wicked King* and *The Queen of Nothing*.
- Follows the mortal protagonist *Jude Duarte*, who was violently abducted as a child alongside her twin sister *Taryn* and older half-fey sister *Vivienne* by the warlord *Madoc* after he butchered their human parents.
- Explores the intense psychological conflict between Jude and *Prince Cardan Greenbriar*, the youngest prince of Elfhame, whose aristocratic cruelty and mutual animosity mask complex attraction and deep personal vulnerabilities.
- Centers on Jude's clandestine employment as an intelligence operative for *Prince Dain*, joining a covert spy network known as the Court of Shadows where she trains under operatives named *the Ghost*, *the Roach*, and *the Bomb*.
- Builds toward a catastrophic succession crisis during the royal coronation revel, where the eldest son *Prince Balekin* orchestrates the wholesale slaughter of the High King and his heirs to seize the bloodline crown.
- Features Jude executing a masterclass in political maneuvering by stealing the royal crown, exploiting Cardan's hidden true name, and tricking him into swearing an absolute oath of fealty to her for a year and a day before crowning him High King.
- Achieved widespread international commercial acclaim and explosive viral popularity on online literary communities such as BookTok, praised for eschewing simplistic romance in favor of ruthless ambition and genuine psychological stakes.

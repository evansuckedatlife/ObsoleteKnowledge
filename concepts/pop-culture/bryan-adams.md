## summary

**Bryan Adams**, born **Bryan Guy Adams** in 1959, is an acclaimed Canadian rock singer, songwriter, guitarist, and documentary photographer who became one of the most commercially successful recording artists of the late twentieth century. Rising from Ontario and Vancouver, he achieved monumental international stardom through his raspy vocal delivery, energetic guitar-driven anthems, and sweeping cinematic power ballads. His relentless chart success and long-standing songwriting partnership with *Jim Vallance* firmly established him as a central figure in global mainstream pop-rock.

## you gotta know

- Scored international anthem status with the nostalgic rock classic *"Summer of '69"* and the soaring billboard-topping ballad *"Heaven"*, both anchoring his multi-platinum 1984 breakthrough album *Reckless*.
- Broke modern music chart records with the 1991 cinematic ballad *"(Everything I Do) I Do It for You"*, composed for *Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves*, which spent an astonishing sixteen consecutive weeks at number one on the UK Singles Chart.
- Co-wrote the vast majority of his catalog with Canadian songwriter *Jim Vallance*, crafting classic rock hits such as *"Cuts Like a Knife"*, *"Run to You"*, and *"Somebody"* that defined 1980s commercial rock radio.
- Joined forces with fellow vocalists *Rod Stewart* and *Sting* to perform the global number-one movie theme single *"All for Love"* in 1993 for the feature adaptation of *The Three Musketeers*.
- Repeatedly clarified in press interviews that the lyrics of *"Summer of '69"* refer to the sexual position and timeless romantic nostalgia rather than the historical year 1969, noting he was only nine years old during that summer.
- Earned an Academy Award nomination for co-writing and performing *"Have You Ever Really Loved a Woman?"*, featuring virtuoso flamenco guitarist *Paco de Lucía* for the 1995 soundtrack to *Don Juan DeMarco*.
- Built an internationally recognized parallel career as an editorial fashion and portrait photographer, publishing work with *Vogue* and *Vanity Fair* and capturing the official Golden Jubilee portrait of *Queen Elizabeth II*.
- Commenced his professional recording career in the late 1970s by replacing *Nick Gilder* as the teenage frontman of Canadian glam outfit *Sweeney Todd*, recording the album *If Wishes Were Horses* before going solo.

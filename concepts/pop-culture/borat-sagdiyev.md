## summary

**Borat Sagdiyev** is a satirical fictional television journalist created and portrayed by English comedian *Sacha Baron Cohen*. First rising to prominence on *Da Ali G Show*, the character became a worldwide cultural sensation through the 2006 mockumentary film *Borat: Cultural Learnings of America for Make Benefit Glorious Nation of Kazakhstan*. The character is renowned in popular culture and comedic history for using absurd prejudices and faux-naive interview methods to trick unsuspecting subjects into exposing their own bigotry, ignorance, and social hypocrisies.

## you gotta know

- Portrayed by *Sacha Baron Cohen*, who adopted the persona of an uncouth reporter from *Kazakhstan* to perform unscripted interactions with real, unsuspecting people across the [[united-states]].
- Anchored the 2006 smash-hit mockumentary *Borat: Cultural Learnings of America for Make Benefit Glorious Nation of Kazakhstan*, directed by *Larry Charles*, which earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Adapted Screenplay.
- Popularized ubiquitous catchphrases throughout the 2000s, including "Great success!", "Very nice!", "My wife!", and "Not!", alongside wearing an iconic lime-green swimming garment known as the "mankini".
- Traveled across America in an ice-cream truck accompanied by his obese producer *Azamat Bagatov*, played by comedic actor *Ken Davitian*, ostensibly on a mission to court and marry celebrity *Pamela Anderson*.
- Originated in shorter television sketches during the late 1990s on British television, evolving through *Da Ali G Show* in [[england]] alongside satirical sibling characters *Ali G* and Austrian fashion reporter *Brüno Gehard*.
- Incited numerous lawsuits from unsuspecting participants—including college fraternity members, etiquette coaches, and a rodeo coordinator—who alleged they were deceived into signing release waivers under false pretenses.
- Provoked official protests from the government of *Kazakhstan*, which initially banned the movie and launched major public relations campaigns, before ultimately adopting the catchphrase "Very nice!" in national tourism promotions.
- Returned during the 2020 presidential election in the sequel *Borat Subsequent Moviefilm*, which garnered critical acclaim for co-star *Maria Bakalova* as daughter *Tutar Sagdiyev* and featured an internationally controversial encounter with politician *Rudy Giuliani*.

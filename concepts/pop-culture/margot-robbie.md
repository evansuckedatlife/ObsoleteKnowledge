## summary

**Margot Robbie** is an Australian actress and film producer who became one of the most commercially formidable and critically acclaimed cinematic figures in the contemporary [[united-states]] entertainment industry. Emerging from the television soap opera *Neighbours*, she secured worldwide stardom portraying Naomi Lapaglia in *Martin Scorsese's* 2013 biographical crime tour-de-force *The Wolf of Wall Street*. Through her production banner *LuckyChap Entertainment*, she has evolved into an auteur-championing producer, steering milestone projects including *I, Tonya*, *Promising Young Woman*, and *Greta Gerwig's* 2023 record-breaking cultural juggernaut *Barbie*.

## you gotta know

- Produced and starred in the title role of *Greta Gerwig's* satirical fantasy comedy *Barbie* (2023), which grossed over 1.44 billion dollars globally to become the highest-earning cinematic release in the storied history of *Warner Bros.*
- Broke into major international cinema through her scene-stealing performance opposite *Leonardo DiCaprio* in *The Wolf of Wall Street* (2013), directed by *Martin Scorsese*.
- Founded the production company *LuckyChap Entertainment* in 2014 alongside *Tom Ackerley*, *Josey McNamara*, and *Sophia Kerr*, establishing a firm mandate to champion female filmmakers, unconventional perspectives, and women-led narratives.
- Secured her inaugural Academy Award nomination for Best Actress for embodying embattled Olympic figure skater *Tonya Harding* in the darkly comic mockumentary biopic *I, Tonya* (2017).
- Originated the definitive live-action cinematic representation of DC Comics antiheroine *Harley Quinn*, portraying the character across *David Ayer's* *Suicide Squad* (2016), *Cathy Yan's* *Birds of Prey* (2020), and *James Gunn's* *The Suicide Squad* (2021).
- Earned further Academy Award recognition with a Best Supporting Actress nomination for *Jay Roach's* *Bombshell* (2019), alongside portraying actress *Sharon Tate* in *Quentin Tarantino's* period drama *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood* (2019).
- Commenced her early acting career in Melbourne television, appearing as regular character Donna Freedman on *Neighbours* from 2008 to 2011 before transitioning overseas to American television on the period drama *Pan Am*.

## summary

**Corner Gas** is a beloved Canadian television sitcom created by stand-up comedian *Brent Butt*, which aired on CTV for six seasons between 2004 and 2009. Set in the fictional, flat-prairie community of Dog River, Saskatchewan, the series chronicles the mundane squabbles and eccentricities of a dryly sarcastic gas station operator, his neighboring diner owner, and the town's bored locals. Celebrated for its low-key deadpan delivery and surreal cutaway sight gags, the series was a massive domestic ratings juggernaut and a defining triumph of modern Canadian comedy.

## you gotta know

- The central premise focuses on *Brent Leroy* (played by *Brent Butt*), who runs the only service station for sixty kilometers alongside *The Ruby*, an attached restaurant managed by Toronto transplant *Lacey Burrows* (*Gabrielle Miller*).
- Known for its signature acoustic theme song *Not My Fault* by the Canadian rock group *The Odds*, with its iconic opening lyric proclaiming there is not a lot going on.
- The ensemble includes Brent's belligerent, stingy father *Oscar Leroy* (*Eric Peterson*), whose catchphrase insults brand everyone a jackass, and his sensible mother *Emma* (*Janet Wright*).
- Brent's indolent best friend *Hank Yarbo* (*Fred Ewanuick*), hyper-competent retail clerk *Wanda Dollard* (*Nancy Robertson*), and under-worked small-town police officers *Davis Quinton* (*Lorne Cardinal*) and *Karen Pelly* (*Tara Spencer-Nairn*) complete the cast.
- Filmed in the small agricultural town of Rouleau, Saskatchewan, where the purpose-built gas station and diner sets became major tourist destinations before their eventual dismantling in 2016.
- Established a distinct comedic tempo through rapid cutaway gags and absurd hypothetical vignettes that subverted traditional multi-camera sitcom rhythms.
- Achieved exceptional domestic television ratings in Canada, regularly drawing well over one million viewers per episode and outdrawing major prime-time sitcoms from the [[united-states]].
- Expanded following its 107-episode television run into the 2014 feature-length *Corner Gas: The Movie* and a subsequent primetime animated continuation series from 2018 to 2021.

## summary

**Billie Eilish** is an American singer and songwriter who emerged in the late 2010s as one of the defining pop artists of Generation Z. Raised in Los Angeles, California, she crafts idiosyncratic, darkly atmospheric electropop in close creative collaboration with her older brother and producer *Finneas O'Connell*. Recognized for her intimate whisper-vocal delivery, unconventional minimalist production, and introspective lyricism, she has claimed multiple Grammy Awards, Academy Awards, and record-breaking streaming milestones.

## you gotta know

- First gained viral fame at age thirteen with the ethereal 2015 SoundCloud track *"Ocean Eyes"*, written and produced by her brother *Finneas*.
- Released her critically acclaimed debut studio album, *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?* (2019), which debuted atop the *Billboard* 200 and featured the chart-topping single *"Bad Guy"*.
- Swept all four general field categories at the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards in 2020 (Album of the Year, Record of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best New Artist), becoming the youngest solo artist ever to achieve the feat.
- Composed and performed the James Bond title theme *"No Time to Die"* (2020), which earned her the Academy Award for Best Original Song.
- Won her second Academy Award for Best Original Song with the introspective piano ballad *"What Was I Made For?"*, written for the soundtrack of *Greta Gerwig*'s blockbuster film *Barbie* (2023).
- Expanded her musical palette with subsequent critically lauded full-length studio albums, including *Happier Than Ever* (2021) and *Hit Me Hard and Soft* (2024).
- Characterized visually by an evolving public aesthetic that shifted from oversized streetwear and neon-green hair to classic Hollywood glamour and vintage silhouettes.

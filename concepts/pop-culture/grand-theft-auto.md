## summary

**Grand Theft Auto** is an open-world action-adventure video game franchise created by *David Jones* and *Mike Dailly*, and primarily developed by *Rockstar North* under publisher *Rockstar Games*. Originating as a top-down urban driving game in 1997, the series transitioned to fully three-dimensional sandbox environments with 2001's *Grand Theft Auto III*. Critically revered and commercially unmatched, the franchise delivers sharp satires of contemporary American society, media, and organized crime.

## you gotta know

- Transitioned from a 2D top-down perspective to a groundbreaking 3D open-world simulation with *Grand Theft Auto III* in 2001, defining modern sandbox game design.
- Developed primarily in the United Kingdom by Scottish studio *Rockstar North* (formerly *DMA Design*) under the creative direction of brothers *Sam Houser* and *Dan Houser*.
- Set in fictionalized, satirical representations of iconic American metropolitan cities, most notably *Liberty City* (*New York City*), *Vice City* (*Miami*), and *Los Santos* (*Los Angeles*).
- Released *Grand Theft Auto V* in 2013, which grossed over one billion dollars in its first three days of sales and became one of the most lucrative entertainment products in human history.
- Features prominent licensed radio stations across multiple musical genres, hosted by celebrity disc jockeys and parody commercial advertisements satirizing consumerism.
- Attracted pervasive political controversies and legal battles over depictions of gang violence, criminal conduct, sexual themes, and the infamous 2004 "Hot Coffee" minigame in *San Andreas*.
- Sustained immense continuous revenue through *Grand Theft Auto Online*, a persistent multiplayer mode that redefined live-service video game ecosystems.
- Employs cinematic narrative influences derived from classic crime and mafia cinema, paying homage to films like *The Godfather*, *Scarface*, and *Heat*.

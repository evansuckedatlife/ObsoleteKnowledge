## summary

**Univision** (frequently stylized as **Univisión**) is the flagship Spanish-language free-to-air television network in the *United States*, tracing its lineage to the pioneering San Antonio station *KWEX-TV* in 1962. Catering to the nation's rapidly expanding Hispanic population, the network commands immense cultural authority through its prime-time telenovelas, major sporting coverage, and influential national journalism. It stands as a cornerstone of American minority broadcasting, fundamentally shaping electoral politics and community identity across North America.

## you gotta know

- Traces its structural genesis to the *Spanish International Network* (SIN) created by Mexican broadcasting pioneer *Emilio Azcárraga Vidaurreta*, establishing the first non-English television network in the *United States*.
- Anchored for over three decades by award-winning journalist *Jorge Ramos*, who co-helmed the network's flagship evening broadcast *Noticiero Univision* alongside longtime co-anchor *María Elena Salinas*.
- Broadcast the record-setting Saturday night variety institution *Sábado Gigante* hosted by Chilean television giant *Don Francisco* (*Mario Kreutzberger*), which aired for over five decades before concluding in 2015.
- Partnered historically with Mexican media conglomerate *Televisa* to broadcast globally beloved comedy sitcoms and dramas, notably *El Chavo del Ocho* created by *Roberto Gómez Bolaños*.
- Premiered the long-running morning news and lifestyle program *Despierta América* in 1997, combining domestic news, celebrity gossip, and cultural advocacy in a breezy morning format.
- Merged its parent operating company with *Televisa* in 2022 to form *TelevisaUnivision*, creating a massive Spanish-language multimedia library and launching the streaming platform *ViX*.
- Regularly outpaced major English-language broadcast competitors like ABC, CBS, and NBC in key prime-time demographic ratings across major urban television markets during the 2000s and 2010s.

- [[twitter]] — primary online social channel where network anchors, political debate clips, and sporting matches generate high-volume live commentary.
- [[new-york-city]] — major broadcasting hub that houses corporate operations, advertising upfront presentations, and regional network production facilities.
- [[i-love-lucy]] — groundbreaking television comedy whose inclusion of Cuban bandleader *Desi Arnaz* laid early groundwork for mainstream Hispanic presence on American airwaves.
- [[realism]] — journalistic style guiding the network's hard-hitting field reporting on immigration policy, agricultural labor, and border communities.

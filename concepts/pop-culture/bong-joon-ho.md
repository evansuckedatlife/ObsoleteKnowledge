## summary

**Bong Joon-ho** is an acclaimed South Korean filmmaker whose work combines black comedy, genre deconstruction, and sharp social commentary on class struggle. He gained worldwide critical prominence through masterpieces like *Memories of Murder* and *The Host* before achieving unprecedented global recognition with the 2019 film *Parasite*. As the first Korean director to win the *Palme d'Or* and the *Academy Award for Best Picture*, he stands as a monumental figure in contemporary world cinema.

## you gotta know

- Directed the 2019 black comedy thriller *Parasite*, which made history as the first non-English-language film to win the *Academy Award for Best Picture*.
- Swept the 92nd Academy Awards, winning four Oscars including *Best Director*, *Best Original Screenplay*, and *Best International Feature Film*.
- Won the prestigious *Palme d'Or* by unanimous vote of the jury at the 2019 Cannes Film Festival for *Parasite*.
- Broke into international acclaim with *Memories of Murder* (2003), a rural procedural thriller based on the real-life Hwaseong serial murders.
- Directed *The Host* (2006), a landmark monster film blending ecological catastrophe, family melodrama, and satire of military intervention.
- Made English-language crossover films including the post-apocalyptic train allegory *Snowpiercer* (2013) and the corporate satire *Okja* (2017).
- Famous for meticulous pre-visualization, drawing his own complete storyboards for every scene to eliminate superfluous shooting and minimize coverage.
- Delivered a viral acceptance speech quip advising audiences that "once you overcome the one-inch-tall barrier of subtitles, you will be introduced to so many more amazing films."

## connections

- [[realism]] — artistic tradition of verisimilitude grounding the director's incisive portrayals of poverty, inequality, and systemic neglect.
- [[mise-en-scene]] — visual arrangement of spaces, vertical architecture, and physical levels prominently weaponized in his framing of class divisions.
- [[camera-movement]] — deliberate tracking shots and precise lateral pans used to establish claustrophobic social spaces and sudden narrative tonal shifts.
- [[film-editing]] — rhythmic cutting style executed strictly according to his comprehensive drawn storyboards without standard master coverage.
- [[visual-metaphor]] — recurring symbolic devices throughout his films, such as the scholar's stone, torrential rain, and train compartmentalization.
- [[france]] — host country of the Cannes Film Festival where he achieved his unanimous *Palme d'Or* victory.

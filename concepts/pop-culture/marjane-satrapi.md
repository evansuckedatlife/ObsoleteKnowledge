## summary

**Marjane Satrapi** is an Iranian-born French graphic novelist, illustrator, and film director who achieved international renown for her groundbreaking autobiographical graphic memoir *Persepolis*. Chronicling her upbringing in Tehran during the 1979 Islamic Revolution and the subsequent Iran-Iraq War, her work combines stark high-contrast woodcut-style illustration with searing sociopolitical commentary. Satrapi transformed the medium of graphic autobiography, demonstrating how visual narrative can humanize complex geopolitical conflicts and convey personal and collective trauma.

## you gotta know

- Created the landmark two-volume graphic memoir *Persepolis*, originally published in French between 2000 and 2003, recounting her rebellious youth, her family's Marxist political sympathies, and her exile to Vienna.
- Co-directed the 2007 animated feature film adaptation of *Persepolis* with French comic artist *Vincent Paronnaud*, winning the Jury Prize at the Cannes Film Festival and an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature.
- Published *Embroideries* in 2003, a sharp graphic narrative capturing candid, humorous discussions about sex, love, and gender expectations among generations of Iranian women drinking tea.
- Wrote the tragic graphic novel *Chicken with Plums* in 2004, centering on her great-uncle *Nasser Ali Khan*, a renowned tar player who decides to die after his beloved instrument is broken; she adapted it into a live-action film in 2011.
- Associated closely with *L'Association*, the avant-garde French comic artist collective that revolutionized independent European bande dessinée in the 1990s and early 2000s.
- Directed the 2019 biographical drama *Radioactive*, starring *Rosamund Pike* as pioneering dual Nobel laureate physicist *Marie Curie*.
- Elected to the prestigious *Académie des Beaux-Arts* in Paris in 2024, cementing her formal recognition within the highest echelons of French cultural institutions.

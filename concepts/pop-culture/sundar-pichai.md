## summary

**Sundar Pichai** is an Indian-born American business executive and materials engineer who serves as the Chief Executive Officer of *Alphabet Inc.* and its flagship subsidiary, *Google*. Rising through the engineering and product ranks after joining the company in 2004, he spearheaded foundational digital products that reshaped global computing, including the *Google Chrome* web browser and the *Android* operating system. His leadership marks a central chapter in contemporary technological [[industrialization]], steering major advances in artificial intelligence, cloud architecture, and corporate governance throughout the [[united-states]] and internationally.

## you gotta know

- Appointed Chief Executive Officer of *Google* in August 2015 during a massive corporate restructuring, before succeeding co-founders *Larry Page* and *Sergey Brin* as CEO of parent holding company *Alphabet Inc.* in December 2019.
- Earned his undergraduate degree in metallurgical engineering from the *Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur* before emigrating to the [[united-states]] to complete graduate studies at *Stanford University* and the *Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania*.
- Championed the creation and launch of the *Google Chrome* web browser in 2008, overcoming internal executive skepticism to build what quickly became the dominant web browser in the world.
- Oversaw product development for ubiquitous consumer software suites including *Google Drive*, *Google Maps*, and *Gmail*, streamlining digital productivity and cloud collaboration.
- Took over supervision of the *Android* mobile operating system in 2013 from its original founder *Andy Rubin*, helping cement its position as the world's most widely deployed smartphone platform.
- Reoriented the company's core strategic mission from a "mobile-first" to an "AI-first" enterprise, accelerating investments in large language models, quantum computing, and the *Gemini* neural network architecture.
- Testified before the United States Congress on multiple occasions regarding consumer privacy, search neutrality, antitrust regulations, and the geopolitical impact of artificial intelligence technologies.

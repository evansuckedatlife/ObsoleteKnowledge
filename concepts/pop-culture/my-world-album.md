## summary

**My World** is the debut extended play by Canadian singer *Justin Bieber*, released in November 2009 by Island Records. Spearheaded by the international smash hit *One Time*, the release established Bieber as a global teen idol and marked one of the earliest mainstream commercial triumphs born from internet video discovery. Serving as the initial installment of a two-part debut project, the record achieved multi-platinum certifications and transformed late-2000s teen pop.

## you gotta know

- Introduced pop superstar *Justin Bieber* to the global music industry under the mentorship of manager *Scooter Braun* and R&B star *Usher*.
- Spawned the breakthrough hit single *One Time*, alongside successful follow-up charting tracks including *One Less Lonely Girl* and *Favorite Girl*.
- Achieved a historic milestone on the Billboard Hot 100 as Bieber became the first artist to have seven songs from a debut album chart simultaneously.
- Pioneered a new era of digital-era music stardom that originated directly from viral acoustic covers uploaded to YouTube.
- Completed by the full-length companion studio album *My World 2.0* in March 2010, which featured his era-defining signature hit *Baby*.
- Reached platinum and multi-platinum sales status across North America, entering the top five on album charts in Canada and the *[[united-states]]*.
- Dominated youth culture and gave rise to the global fan phenomenon colloquially termed *Bieber Fever*.

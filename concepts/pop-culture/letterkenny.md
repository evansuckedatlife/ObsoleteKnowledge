## summary

**Letterkenny** is an acclaimed Canadian television sitcom created by *Jared Keeso* that originally debuted on the streaming platform *Crave* in 2016. Developed alongside director *Jacob Tierney*, the series chronicles the eccentric residents of a fictional rural Ontario farming community defined by distinct social cliques. Celebrated for its lightning-fast cadence, elaborate wordplay, and affectionate satire of small-town life, the show grew from an indie web project into a major international cult comedy phenomenon.

## you gotta know

- Created by *Jared Keeso*, who grew up in Listowel, Ontario, and initially introduced the characters through the viral YouTube web series *Letterkenny Problems* before Crave picked it up for television broadcast.
- Structured around four central social factions in the town: the rural agricultural "Hicks," the goth and drug-dealing "Skids," the vain minor-league "Hockey Players," and the First Nations residents of the nearby reserve known as "The Natives."
- Features protagonist Wayne, played by *Jared Keeso*, as the undisputed toughest guy in town who staunchly defends his title in bare-knuckle fistfights while managing chores on his produce farm alongside his sister Katy and best friends Daryl and Squirrelly Dan.
- Characterized by hyper-articulate, rhythmic, and highly repetitive comedic dialogue steeped in specialized rural slang, celebrated catchphrases like "pitter-patter, let's get at 'er," and elaborate rhetorical cold opens that deconstruct language and etiquette.
- Produced the hit hockey-centric spinoff series *Shoresy* in 2022, also created by and starring Keeso as the titular foul-mouthed, chirp-spewing senior AAA hockey player who previously appeared in *Letterkenny* only from behind or in a goalie mask.
- Integrates recurring subplots about local amateur hockey teams like the Letterkenny Shamrocks and Letterkenny Irish, minor agricultural rivalries, local brawls with out-of-town "degens," and gathering at the local bar Modean's.
- Features distinct running gags including Squirrelly Dan's progressive feminist commentary, Katy's fierce protectiveness of her brother, the Skids' synchronized warehouse rave dances, and the pervasive consumption of Puppers premium lager and Gus N' Bru whiskey.

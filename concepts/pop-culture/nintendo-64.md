## summary

The **Nintendo 64** is a fifth-generation home video game console developed by Nintendo and released in 1996 in *Japan* and the *United States*. Named for its 64-bit central processing architecture co-engineered with Silicon Graphics, the platform pioneered analog control standards and free-roaming 3D game spaces. Despite ceding market leadership to Sony's CD-ROM-based PlayStation due to its adherence to silicon ROM cartridges, it produced several of the most influential and critically revered masterpieces in video game history.

## you gotta know

- Launched in 1996 with a distinctive trident-shaped three-handled controller that popularized the central analog thumbstick for precise 3D spatial navigation.
- Introduced camera control buttons (the yellow C-buttons) and an underside trigger button (the Z-trigger) tailored for three-dimensional perspectives and targeting.
- Shipped alongside *Super Mario 64*, a seminal launch title directed by *Shigeru Miyamoto* that established standard conventions for 3D platformers, camera management, and analog momentum.
- Featured four integrated controller ports on the front panel as a standard feature, establishing the system as a preeminent platform for local split-screen multiplayer games like *Mario Kart 64* and *Super Smash Bros.*
- Revolutionized console multiplayer and precision shooting with *GoldenEye 007*, developed by Rare, which demonstrated that competitive first-person combat could thrive on home consoles.
- Retained silicon ROM cartridges rather than optical compact discs, yielding virtually nonexistent loading times and exceptional physical durability at the cost of severely restricted storage capacity and higher manufacturing costs.
- Introduced the *Rumble Pak* accessory, which plugged into the rear expansion port of the controller to provide force feedback vibration, quickly becoming an industry-wide hardware standard.
- Hosted *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* in 1998, which introduced the revolutionary Z-targeting lock-on combat mechanic and is frequently ranked among the greatest games of all time.

## connections

- [[nintendo-entertainment-system]] — predecessor home console whose classic two-dimensional properties were reimagined in pioneering 3D polygonal forms on this hardware.
- [[nintendo-3ds]] — future portable platform that received high-profile stereoscopic three-dimensional remakes of landmark Nintendo 64 software.
- [[first-person-shooter]] — genre transformed on home consoles by the groundbreaking control schemes and split-screen multiplayer deathmatches of *GoldenEye 007* and *Perfect Dark*.
- [[japan]] — home nation where Nintendo collaborated with hardware partners to engineer the console under the pre-release code name *Project Reality* and *Ultra 64*.
- [[esports]] — competitive gaming sphere influenced by early competitive tournament circuits for multiplayer titles like *Super Smash Bros.*

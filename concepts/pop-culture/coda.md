## summary

**CODA** is an award-winning 2021 coming-of-age comedy-drama film written and directed by *Sian Heder*, based on the 2014 French film *La Famille Bélier*. Taking its name from the acronym for Child of Deaf Adults, the narrative centers on seventeen-year-old *Ruby Rossi*, the sole hearing member of a culturally Deaf family residing in the coastal community of Gloucester, Massachusetts. As Ruby juggles serving as an indispensable daily interpreter for her family's struggling commercial fishing business and pursuing her passion for vocal performance at the Berklee College of Music, the story explores autonomy, familial duty, and identity. The film achieved historic acclaim by winning three Oscars, including the Academy Award for Best Picture, marking a triumphant milestone for authentic Deaf casting and representation.

## you gotta know

- Won three Oscars at the 94th Academy Awards: Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay for *Sian Heder*, and Best Supporting Actor for *Troy Kotsur*, becoming the first film distributed by a streaming service to win Best Picture.
- *Troy Kotsur* made history as the first Deaf male actor to win an Academy Award for acting, delivering a funny, poignant performance as the proud fisherman patriarch *Frank Rossi*.
- Stars British actress *Emilia Jones* as *Ruby Rossi*, who spent nine grueling months learning American Sign Language, rigorous vocal training, and real-world commercial trawler operation to perform her scenes authentically.
- Co-stars *Marlee Matlin*, who made cinema history in 1987 as the first Deaf performer to win an Academy Award for *Children of a Lesser God*, and who firmly insisted that all Deaf characters in the film be portrayed by Deaf actors.
- Reaches its emotional climax during Ruby's Berklee audition, where she sings *Joni Mitchell*'s iconic song "Both Sides, Now" while simultaneously signing the lyrics toward the balcony so her watching parents can understand her voice.
- Employs a striking sound design device during Ruby's high school choir recital, abruptly cutting all audio to silence so the audience experiences the performance entirely from her parents' non-hearing perspective.
- Adapted from the French predecessor *La Famille Bélier*, transposing the original dairy farming setting in rural *France* to a working-class New England fishing harbor confronting bureaucratic catch fees.

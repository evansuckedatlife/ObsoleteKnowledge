## summary

**Samuel Frederick Smith**, known professionally as **Sam Smith**, is a Grammy- and Academy Award-winning English singer and songwriter whose expressive vocal range and soulful ballads established them among the most commercially successful pop artists of the twenty-first century. Emerging from London, [[england]], they gained initial prominence as a featured vocalist on electronic dance anthems before releasing record-shattering solo studio albums exploring heartbreak, identity, and personal liberation. Their vocal craft blends traditional gospel arrangement, classic soul aesthetics, and sleek contemporary production, achieving enduring global popularity and cultural influence.

## you gotta know

- Scored breakout international recognition in 2012 as the soaring featured vocalist on *Disclosure*'s garage-house anthem "Latch" and subsequently on *Naughty Boy*'s chart-topping 2013 single "La La La".
- Swept four major honors at the 57th Annual Grammy Awards following their multiplatinum 2014 debut album *In the Lonely Hour*, winning Best New Artist, Record of the Year, Song of the Year for "Stay with Me", and Best Pop Vocal Album.
- Co-wrote and recorded the dramatic orchestral theme "Writing's on the Wall" for the 2015 James Bond film *Spectre*, winning the Academy Award for Best Original Song and the Golden Globe Award.
- Collaborated with German pop artist *Kim Petras* on the seductive 2022 transatlantic number-one single "Unholy", which secured the Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance and marked an artistic evolution toward club-inspired dance-pop.
- Sustained strong international chart success with introspective studio albums including *The Thrill of It All* (2017), led by the poignant ballad "Too Good at Goodbyes", and *Love Goes* (2020).
- Came out publicly as non-binary and genderqueer in 2019, adopting they/them pronouns and emerging as a visible global advocate for LGBTQ+ representation and artistic authenticity in mainstream music.
- Trained extensively throughout childhood in jazz singing, choral composition, and musical theatre under British jazz vocalist *Joanna Eden*, performing in youth choirs and stage ensembles across the United Kingdom.
- Suffered a severe vocal cord hemorrhage in 2015 during an international tour, undergoing specialized microsurgery in the [[united-states]] followed by complete vocal rehabilitation before resuming live concerts.

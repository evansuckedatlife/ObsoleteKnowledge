## summary

**Appetite for Destruction** is the debut studio album by American hard rock band *Guns N' Roses*, released on July 21, 1987, by *Geffen Records*. Produced by *Mike Clink*, the album captured the unvarnished grit, decadence, and danger of the Los Angeles Sunset Strip music scene, sharply departing from the polished glam metal popular at the time. With over 30 million copies sold worldwide, it stands as the best-selling debut album of all time in the United States and a watershed moment in hard rock history.

## you gotta know

- Propelled by the core classic lineup of singer *Axl Rose*, lead guitarist *Slash*, rhythm guitarist *Izzy Stradlin*, bassist *Duff McKagan*, and drummer *Steven Adler*.
- Features the landmark Billboard Hot 100 number-one single *Sweet Child o' Mine*, renowned for *Slash*'s iconic opening string-skipping guitar riff.
- Opens with the menacing street-level anthem *Welcome to the Jungle*, written about the hostile atmosphere of Los Angeles upon arrival from the Midwest.
- Original album cover featured an artwork by *Robert Williams* depicting a robotic predator, which was quickly banned and relocated to the inside sleeve in favor of *Billy White Jr.*'s Celtic cross tattoo design.
- Features *Paradise City*, known for its heavy synthesizer-backed intro, soaring stadium chorus, and blistering double-time guitar outro.
- Closes with the track *Rocket Queen*, notable for its explicit studio audio recordings captured by *Axl Rose* and *Adriana Smith*.
- Shifted the dominant rock paradigm away from polished pop-metal production toward raw, blues-infused, punk-influenced street rock.

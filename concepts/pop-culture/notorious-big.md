## summary

**The Notorious B.I.G.**, born *Christopher George Latore Wallace* and widely known as *Biggie Smalls*, was an iconic American rapper who served as the central figure of East Coast hip-hop in the 1990s. Celebrated for his effortless flow, vivid storytelling, dark humor, and autobiographical narratives of urban hardship, he revitalized New York rap at a time when West Coast hip-hop dominated mainstream music. His life and career were cut short in March 1997 when he was murdered in a drive-by shooting in Los Angeles, securing his legacy as one of the most influential MCs in music history.

## you gotta know

- Rose to international stardom through his 1994 debut studio album *Ready to Die*, released on *Sean "Puffy" Combs*'s Bad Boy Records.
- Grew up in the Clinton Hill and Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhoods of Brooklyn, [[new-york-city]], initially selling drugs before finding musical success.
- Scored landmark Billboard hits with anthems such as "Juicy", "Big Poppa", and "One More Chance".
- Formed the protégé hip-hop collective *Junior M.A.F.I.A.*, which launched the career of female rap pioneer *Lil' Kim*.
- Became embroiled in the fatal East Coast–West Coast hip-hop rivalry with his former friend, Death Row Records superstar *Tupac Shakur*.
- Murdered in Los Angeles on March 9, 1997, at age 24; his homicide remains officially unsolved.
- Released the sprawling double album *Life After Death* posthumously just sixteen days after his death, which debuted at number one and spawned the chart-topping singles "Hypnotize" and "Mo Money Mo Problems".
- Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2020, solidifying his stature among the defining voices of modern music.

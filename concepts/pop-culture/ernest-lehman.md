## summary

**Ernest Lehman** was a prominent American screenwriter, novelist, and producer celebrated for authoring some of Hollywood's most enduring and critically acclaimed cinematic screenplays. Rising to prominence in mid-twentieth-century Hollywood, he collaborated with legendary directors such as *Alfred Hitchcock*, *Billy Wilder*, and *Mike Nichols*. His mastery of razor-sharp dialogue, narrative pacing, and psychological characterization earned him four competitive Academy Award nominations and the inaugural Lifetime Achievement Academy Honorary Award presented to a screenwriter.

## you gotta know

- Authored the acclaimed original screenplay for *Alfred Hitchcock's* Cold War espionage thriller *North by Northwest*, engineering iconic sequences including the crop duster ambush and the climactic chase across Mount Rushmore, while creating the sophisticated undercover agent role played by [[eva-marie-saint]].
- Adapted the stage musical *The Sound of Music* for the screen, restructuring songs and narrative beats to produce one of the highest-grossing and most culturally pervasive film achievements in [[united-states]] cinematic history.
- Wrote the dark Broadway satire novella and co-scripted its iconic film adaptation *Sweet Smell of Success*, creating the venomous, quote-laden dynamic between corrupt columnist *J.J. Hunsecker* and unscrupulous press agent *Sidney Falco* set against nocturnal [[new-york-city]].
- Penned the screenplay for *Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf?*, faithfully translating *Edward Albee's* incendiary, profanity-laced marital drama into a landmark cinematic work that shattered industry censorship standards under the declining Production Code.
- Collaborated with director [[billy-wilder]] on the romantic comedy *Sabrina*, securing an Academy Award nomination for adapting *Samuel A. Taylor's* Broadway play alongside Wilder and Taylor.
- Adapted the landmark Broadway musical *West Side Story* for the silver screen, translating *Arthur Laurents's* book and *Jerome Robbins's* dynamic choreography into a multi-Oscar-winning cinematic adaptation.
- Made his sole feature directorial debut by adapting *Philip Roth's* controversial and explicit comic novel *Portnoy's Complaint*, while also producing successful projects like the film adaptation of *Hello, Dolly!*.
- Awarded the Academy Honorary Award in 2001, making him the very first screen scribe in cinema history to receive an honorary Oscar specifically commemorating an entire body of screenwriting work.

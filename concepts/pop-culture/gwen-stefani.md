## summary

**Gwen Stefani** is an American singer, songwriter, and fashion designer who rose to international stardom as the lead vocalist of the ska-punk band *No Doubt*. Her distinct vocal delivery and striking visual aesthetics helped propel the group's 1995 album *Tragic Kingdom* to diamond status before she launched a major solo career. Throughout the 2000s and beyond, she dominated pop airwaves with hits like *Hollaback Girl* and became a prominent television personality on *The Voice*.

## you gotta know

- Gained global prominence fronting the Southern California band *No Doubt*, whose smash ballad *Don't Speak* topped global charts for weeks.
- The band's third studio album, *Tragic Kingdom*, became a quintessential 1990s release that bridged ska, punk rock, and new wave pop.
- Launched an acclaimed solo career with the 2004 album *Love. Angel. Music. Baby.*, an 1980s-inspired dance record featuring production from *The Neptunes*.
- Achieved the first digital download to sell one million copies in the United States with the chart-topping cheerleader anthem *Hollaback Girl*.
- Created the fashion label *L.A.M.B.* in 2003, expanding her creative reach into runway design, streetwear, and fragrance lines.
- Served as an enduring coach on the American reality singing competition *The Voice*, later marrying fellow coach and country music star *Blake Shelton*.

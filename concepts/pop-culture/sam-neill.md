## summary

**Sam Neill**, born **Nigel John Dermot Neill**, is a distinguished New Zealand and Northern Irish actor, writer, and director whose prolific screen career spans over five decades of international cinema and television. He achieved definitive global fame as the pragmatic, dinosaur-evading paleontologist *Dr. Alan Grant* in *Steven Spielberg*'s landmark blockbuster *Jurassic Park*, while simultaneously earning critical praise for complex, understated roles in auteur-driven international arthouse films. Renowned for his dry charisma, commanding screen presence, and dramatic versatility, he remains one of the most celebrated and internationally recognized figures in Australasian screen history.

## you gotta know

- Starred as the fedora-wearing paleontologist *Dr. Alan Grant* in the record-breaking sci-fi phenomenon *Jurassic Park* (1993), later reprising the beloved role in *Jurassic Park III* and *Jurassic World Dominion*.
- Delivered an acclaimed, chilling performance as the puritanical landowner *Alisdair Stewart* opposite *Holly Hunter* in *Jane Campion*'s Palme d'Or-winning 19th-century New Zealand drama *The Piano* (1993).
- Headlined the groundbreaking political thriller *Sleeping Dogs* (1977), directed by *Roger Donaldson*, which served as the first major New Zealand feature to secure theatrical release in the [[united-states]].
- Portrayed the unravelling, tormented husband *Mark* in *Andrzej Żuławski*'s psychological horror masterpiece *Possession* (1981) opposite *Isabelle Adjani*.
- Anchored prominent sci-fi and supernatural horror classics, including the haunted spaceship designer *Dr. William Weir* in *Event Horizon* and insurance investigator *John Trent* in *John Carpenter*'s *In the Mouth of Madness*.
- Starred as the ruthless, vindictive Belfast lawman *Chief Inspector Chester Campbell* in the early seasons of the popular BBC period crime television drama *Peaky Blinders*.
- Gained widespread acclaim among modern audiences portraying the cantankerous outdoor survivalist *Uncle Hec* in *Taika Waititi*'s adventure comedy-drama *Hunt for the Wilderpeople* (2016).
- Operates *Two Paddocks*, an esteemed family-owned organic vineyard and winery established in 1993 in Central Otago, New Zealand, which produces internationally awarded Pinot Noir wines.

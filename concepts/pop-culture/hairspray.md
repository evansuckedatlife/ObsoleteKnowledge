## summary

**Hairspray** is an acclaimed American musical comedy work originating from the 1988 cult film written and directed by underground cinema pioneer *John Waters*. Set in Baltimore, Maryland, in 1962, the narrative centers on bubbly, full-figured high schooler *Tracy Turnblad*, who pursues her dream of dancing on a popular teen television show while fighting courageously to end racial segregation on the broadcast. Adapted into an eight-time Tony Award-winning Broadway musical in 2002 by *Marc Shaiman* and *Scott Wittman*, followed by a hit 2007 musical film, the work remains an exuberant celebration of inclusion, teenage rebellion, body positivity, and the vibrant music of early 1960s rock and roll.

## you gotta know

- Follows the irrepressible *Tracy Turnblad*, an outcast teenager who auditions for *The Corny Collins Show*, becomes an overnight local dancing celebrity, and leads a march to racially integrate the broadcast.
- Upholds a legendary casting tradition originated by drag icon *Divine* in *John Waters*'s 1988 movie, in which Tracy's timid, agoraphobic mother *Edna Turnblad* is played by a man in drag, including *Harvey Fierstein* on Broadway and *John Travolta* in the 2007 movie.
- The 2002 Broadway stage musical adaptation was a monumental critical and commercial sensation, garnering eight Tony Awards including Best Musical, Best Original Score, and Best Book of a Musical.
- Features a celebrated rhythm-and-blues and 1960s pop score composed by *Marc Shaiman*, boasting iconic numbers such as the energetic curtain-raiser "Good Morning Baltimore", the anthemic finale "You Can't Stop the Beat", and the powerful civil rights lament "I Know Where I've Been".
- Directly engages the historical *civil-rights-movement*, drawing narrative inspiration from the real-life integration protests surrounding Baltimore's teen dance program *The Buddy Deane Show*.
- Pits Tracy and her rhythm-and-blues allies against the bigoted station producer *Velma Von Tussle* and her privileged daughter *Amber*, contrasting systemic prejudice with the irresistible joy of youth culture.
- *Ricki Lake* originated the screen role of Tracy in 1988, while *Marissa Jaret Winokur* won the Tony Award for creating the role on Broadway, and *Nikki Blonsky* earned acclaim starring in the 2007 film adaptation.

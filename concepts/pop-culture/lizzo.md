## summary

**Lizzo**, born **Melissa Viviane Jefferson** in 1988, is an American singer, rapper, songwriter, and classically trained flutist celebrated for her charismatic blend of contemporary hip hop, funk, soul, and vibrant pop music. Rising from grassroots independent music scenes in Houston, Minneapolis, and Los Angeles, she achieved monumental mainstream breakthroughs in 2019 through infectious anthems celebrating body positivity, unapologetic self-love, and emotional resilience. A multiple Grammy Award and Primetime Emmy Award winner, she stands as one of the defining cultural figures of contemporary popular music, famous for incorporating virtuoso orchestral flute performances into high-energy pop concerts.

## you gotta know

- Mastered the orchestral concert flute from childhood under the direction of classical instructors in Houston, continuing her formal music studies at the University of Houston marching band before crossing over into contemporary pop.
- Nicknamed her beloved personal instrument *Sasha Flute*, named after *Beyoncé*'s alter ego *Sasha Fierce*, giving the instrument its own viral social media identity and performing onstage with historic artifacts.
- Relocated to Minneapolis in 2011, performing with indie hip hop groups *The Chalice* and *GRRRL PRTY*, which caught the attention of music icon *Prince*, who invited her to record on his 2014 album *Plectrumelectrum*.
- Achieved historic sleeper-hit success with the empowerment anthem *Truth Hurts*, which surged to the top of the Billboard Hot 100 in 2019 two years after its initial release.
- Released the critically acclaimed, platinum-selling breakthrough third studio album *Cuz I Love You* in 2019, earning eight nominations at the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards and winning three.
- Earned the Grammy Award for Record of the Year in 2023 for the disco-infused pop single *About Damn Time*, showcasing her vocal versatility and energetic groove.
- Created and starred in the competitive reality series *Lizzo's Watch Out for the Big Grrrls*, winning the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Competition Program.
- Played a historic crystal flute belonging to President *James Madison* at the Library of Congress and during a concert in Washington, D.C., in 2022.

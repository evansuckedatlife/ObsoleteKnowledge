## summary

**Scott Pilgrim** is the charmingly oblivious twenty-two-year-old protagonist of *Bryan Lee O'Malley*'s celebrated graphic novel series and its multimedia adaptations, including *Edgar Wright*'s cult action comedy film *Scott Pilgrim vs. the World*. Residing in Toronto as an unemployed slacker and bass guitarist for the garage indie rock band *Sex Bob-omb*, he falls in love with the enigmatic American delivery girl *Ramona Flowers*. To date her, he must defeat her Seven Evil Exes in martial arts combat, navigating an inventive universe where everyday adolescent romantic insecurities manifest as literal arcade video game boss battles.

## you gotta know

- Created by Canadian cartoonist *Bryan Lee O'Malley* across six graphic novel volumes published between 2004 and 2010, taking his name from a 1998 song by Canadian indie rock band *Plumtree*.
- Plays bass guitar in the garage band *Sex Bob-omb* alongside hot-headed drummer *Kim Pine* and ambitious frontman *Stephen Stills*, practicing in cold basements across snowy Toronto.
- Shares an apartment and a single mattress with his witty, cynical, and far more responsible best friend *Wallace Wells*, who routinely finances his lifestyle and critiques his romantic follies.
- Begins the narrative dating high school student *Knives Chau* before falling for *Ramona Flowers*, a roller-skating courier for Amazon who travels through Subspace highways inside his subconscious.
- Compelled to battle the League of Evil Exes founded by music mogul *Gideon Graves*, defeating opponents who burst into showers of collectable coins upon taking lethal damage.
- Undergoes essential emotional maturation by transitioning from fighting for the "Power of Love" to fighting for the "Power of Self-Respect," acknowledging his own past selfish behavior as a bad boyfriend.
- Portrayed on screen by actor *Michael Cera* in the 2010 cinematic adaptation and voiced again in the 2023 animated anime reimagining *Scott Pilgrim Takes Off*.

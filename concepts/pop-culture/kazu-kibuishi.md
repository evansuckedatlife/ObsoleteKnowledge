## summary

**Kazu Kibuishi** is a Japanese-American graphic novelist, illustrator, and editor best known as the creator of the bestselling fantasy graphic novel series *Amulet*. Born in Tokyo in 1978 and raised in the [[united-states]], he emerged as a crucial figure in the North American comics landscape through both his narrative work and his stewardship of independent comic anthologies. His sweeping fantasy epics and iconic illustrations, including redesigning covers for major literary franchises, have played a major role in elevating graphic literature within school curricula and juvenile publishing worldwide.

## you gotta know

- Created the bestselling nine-volume graphic novel epic *Amulet*, published by Scholastic between 2008 and 2024, following siblings *Emily* and *Navin Hayes* in the mystical realm of Alledia.
- Founded and curated the acclaimed *Flight* anthology series, which brought together dozens of emerging webcomic creators and independent illustrators across eight volumes.
- Commissioned by Scholastic in 2013 to illustrate the landmark 15th-anniversary box set cover art for the North American editions of *J.K. Rowling*'s *Harry Potter* novels.
- Began his professional cartooning career with the whimsical, semi-autobiographical webcomic *Copper*, featuring an inquisitive boy and his talking dog.
- Survived a life-threatening bout of bacterial meningitis in 2012, which temporarily incapacitated him and deeply reshaped the thematic trajectory and urgency of *Amulet*.
- Synthesizes dynamic aesthetics from traditional Japanese manga with Western cinematic framing, visual pacing, and atmospheric coloring.
- Created the young-reader graphic novel *Daisy Kutter: The Last Train*, a steampunk Western nominated for the Harvey Award and recognized by the American Library Association.

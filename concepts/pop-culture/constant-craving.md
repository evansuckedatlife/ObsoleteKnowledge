## summary

**Constant Craving** is the celebrated 1992 torch song and breakout crossover hit by Canadian singer-songwriter *k.d. lang*, co-written with multi-instrumentalist *Ben Mink*. Serving as the final track on her breakthrough solo album *Ingénue*, the recording transitioned lang from an alternative country sensation into a globally recognized pop vocalist. The track secured the Grammy Award for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance in 1993 and served as an essential soundtrack to a defining era of queer cultural awakening and mainstream LGBTQ+ visibility.

## you gotta know

- Reached the top ten of the adult contemporary charts in both the United States and the United Kingdom, propelling *Ingénue* to multi-platinum sales figures.
- Captured the 1993 Grammy Award for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance and received nominations for both Song of the Year and Record of the Year.
- Achieved profound social resonance following *k.d. lang*'s decision to come out publicly as a lesbian in *The Advocate* in June 1992, establishing the song as an enduring anthem of longing, desire, and identity.
- Features a distinctive instrumental arrangement built around *Ben Mink*'s driving acoustic guitar strumming, lush violin countermelodies, and subtle accordion flourishes.
- Led *The Rolling Stones* to grant retroactively co-writing credits to *k.d. lang* and *Ben Mink* on their 1997 single *'Anybody Seen My Baby?'* after *Keith Richards* realized its chorus mirrored lang's vocal refrain.
- Accompanied by a stylish black-and-white music video directed by *Mark Romanek*, which drew visual inspiration from a turn-of-the-century theater performance of *Samuel Beckett*'s existentialist masterpiece *Waiting for Godot*.
- Earned the MTV Video Music Award for Best Female Video in 1993, triumphing over heavy commercial competition during the golden age of music video television.

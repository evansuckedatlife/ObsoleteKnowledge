## summary

**Luke Skywalker** is the primary fictional protagonist of George Lucas's original *Star Wars* film trilogy (*1977*–*1983*), immortalized on screen by American actor *Mark Hamill*. Originating as an unpretentious farm boy on the desert planet Tatooine, he masters the mystical ways of the Force under mentors *Obi-Wan Kenobi* and *Yoda* to dismantle the totalitarian Galactic Empire. As an archetype of the monomyth in the cinema of the [[united-states]], his trials, family reconciliation, and selfless defense of hope establish him as one of the most recognized figures in world popular culture.

## you gotta know

- Structured by *George Lucas* around the monomythic Hero's Journey framework popularized by mythologist *Joseph Campbell* in *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*.
- Destroys the Galactic Empire's primary superweapon in *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope* by deactivating his targeting computer, trusting the Force, and firing proton torpedoes into the Death Star's thermal exhaust port.
- Engages in an iconic lightsaber duel on Cloud City in *The Empire Strikes Back*, where the Sith Lord *Darth Vader* severs his right hand and delivers cinema's most famous plot revelation: "No, I am your father."
- Achieves spiritual victory in *Return of the Jedi* by discarding his lightsaber before the galactic ruler Emperor *Palpatine*, refusing hatred and triggering the redemption of his father *Anakin Skywalker*.
- Introduced alongside the evocative dual-sun horizon of Tatooine in the celebrated "Binary Sunset" sequence, underscored by *John Williams*'s orchestral theme of longing and destiny.
- Re-emerges decades later in *Star Wars: The Last Jedi* as a disillusioned, self-exiled hermit on the planet Ahch-To, grappling with the catastrophic fall of his nephew *Ben Solo*.
- Sacrifices his physical life force during the Battle of Crait by projecting an astral illusion across space to face the First Order, allowing the Resistance survivors to escape.

- [[cold-war]] — the geopolitical environment during which the struggle of his outnumbered democratic rebellion against an empire resonated with global audiences.

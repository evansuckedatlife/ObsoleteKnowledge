## summary

**The Breadwinner** is an internationally acclaimed 2000 children's novel by Canadian author *Deborah Ellis*, famously adapted into an Oscar-nominated 2017 animated feature film directed by *Nora Twomey* and executive-produced by *Angelina Jolie*. Set in Kabul, Afghanistan, during the first period of Taliban rule in 2001, the story centers on eleven-year-old *Parvana*, who disguises herself as a boy following her father's unjust arrest in order to earn money, feed her family, and navigate oppressive restrictions placed upon women. The work is widely lauded for introducing young readers to human rights struggles, female resilience, and the power of storytelling amidst conflict.

## you gotta know

- Authored by *Deborah Ellis*, who gathered material while spending months interviewing Afghan women and girls in refugee camps in Pakistan during the late 1990s.
- The protagonist, *Parvana*, cuts her hair and dons the clothes of her deceased older brother Hossain, taking on the identity of a boy to shop at the market and work as a street letter reader and writer.
- Highlights the traditional Afghan practice of *bacha posh*, a cultural phenomenon where families without sons dress a daughter as a boy to grant her freedom of movement and economic utility.
- Explores Parvana's friendship with *Shauzia*, a former classmate also living as a boy named Shafiq, who dreams of escaping Afghanistan to see the sea in France.
- Adapted in 2017 into an animated film by Irish studio *Cartoon Saloon*, utilizing hand-drawn animation that interweaves Parvana's grim reality with a folkloric cut-paper fantasy tale about the Elephant King.
- The 2017 film adaptation received critical acclaim and earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature.
- Ellis donated all royalties from the book to Women for Afghan Women and UNICEF, raising millions of dollars for girls' education and humanitarian relief.

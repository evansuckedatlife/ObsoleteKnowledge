## summary

**Apologies to the Queen Mary** is the acclaimed debut studio album by the Canadian indie rock band *Wolf Parade*, released in September 2005 on *Sub Pop*. Recorded primarily at the *Hotel2Tango* studio in Montreal, the record was co-produced by *Isaac Brock* of *Modest Mouse*, who championed the band after receiving an early demo. Driven by the contrasting songwriting styles and distinctive dual vocals of *Spencer Krug* and *Dan Boeckner*, the album became a foundational release in the mid-2000s North American indie rock boom.

## you gotta know

- Features the dual lead vocals and opposing songwriting sensibilities of *Spencer Krug*, who favored theatrical art-rock keyboards, and *Dan Boeckner*, who favored gritty guitar-driven anthems.
- Named after an incident where band members were ejected from the ocean liner *RMS Queen Mary*, then operating as a floating hotel in Long Beach, California, during a tour stop.
- Co-produced by *Modest Mouse* frontman *Isaac Brock*, who was instrumental in signing the band to the storied Seattle label *Sub Pop*.
- Contains standout anthems including *I'll Believe in Anything*, featuring *Spencer Krug*'s urgent synthesizers, and *Shine a Light*, written by *Dan Boeckner*.
- Track *Fancy Claps* originated from the band's earlier self-titled EP and became one of their signature live staples.
- Emerged directly from the fertile Montreal music scene of the mid-2000s that also produced *Arcade Fire*, *The Dears*, and *Islands*.
- Showcases lyrical themes centered on urban disorientation, mortality, physical decay, and the anxieties of youth transitioning into adulthood.

## summary

**Mayim Bialik** is an American actress, author, television host, and neuroscientist who first attained prominence as a child star in the early 1990s. She achieved international fame portraying the title character in the NBC sitcom *Blossom* before later earning critical acclaim and four Emmy nominations as neurobiologist *Dr. Amy Farrah Fowler* on CBS's *The Big Bang Theory*. A rare Hollywood figure holding an earned doctorate in the natural sciences, she has also served as a primetime host for the venerable game show *Jeopardy!*.

## you gotta know

- Rose to fame as a child performer, playing the young version of Bette Midler's character in the 1988 comedy-drama film *Beaches*.
- Starred as the quirky, floppy-hat-wearing teenager *Blossom Russo* in the hit sitcom *Blossom*, which aired on NBC from 1990 to 1995.
- Stepped away from full-time Hollywood acting to pursue higher education at UCLA, earning a Bachelor of Science in 2000 and a Ph.D. in neuroscience in 2007 with a dissertation on Prader-Willi syndrome.
- Returned to prominent network television in 2010 to join the ensemble of *The Big Bang Theory*, playing Sheldon Cooper's intellectual equal and romantic partner *Amy Farrah Fowler*.
- Served as a recurring guest host and subsequent co-host of syndicated *Jeopardy!* and the primetime *Jeopardy! National College Championship* between 2021 and 2023 alongside Ken Jennings.
- Authored multiple bestselling non-fiction books focused on adolescent development, parenting, and plant-based living, including *Girling Up* and *Boying Up*.
- Hosts the mental health podcast *Mayim Bialik's Breakdown*, exploring emotional wellness, neurobiology, and psychological science with celebrity guests and clinical specialists.

## summary

**Doctor Strange**, secretly named **Stephen Strange**, is a premier superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by artist *Steve Ditko* and writer *Stan Lee* in 1963. Originally an exceptionally gifted yet egotistical neurosurgeon based in [[new-york-city]], his medical career was abruptly ended when a severe car crash shattered the delicate bones and nerves in his hands. Traveling to the Himalayas in a desperate bid for healing, he encountered the *Ancient One*, mastered the mystic arts, and ultimately inherited the sacred title of **Sorcerer Supreme** to defend Earth from interdimensional threats and cosmic entities.

## you gotta know

- Made his first comic book appearance in *Strange Tales* #110 in July 1963, distinguished by Ditko's psychedelic, kaleidoscopic illustrations of abstract cosmic realms.
- Operates out of the *Sanctum Sanctorum*, a three-story mystical brownstone located at 177A Bleecker Street in the Greenwich Village neighbourhood of [[new-york-city]].
- Employs an array of potent mystical relics, most notably the *Cloak of Levitation*, the all-revealing *Eye of Agamotto*, the *Orb of Agamotto*, and the white-magic grimoire known as the *Book of Vishanti*.
- Constantly assisted by *Wong*, his loyal confidant, valet, and fellow mystic arts practitioner who manages the wards and daily operations of the Sanctum.
- Iconic rogue gallery includes *Dormammu*, tyrannical ruler of the Dark Dimension; *Baron Karl Mordo*, an envious fellow student of the Ancient One; *Nightmare*, ruler of the Dream Dimension; and the chaotic entity *Shuma-Gorath*.
- Famous for dramatic verbal incantations invoking extra-dimensional beings, such as the *Crimson Bands of Cyttorak*, the *Flames of the Faltine*, the *Shield of the Seraphim*, and the *Hoary Hosts of Hoggoth*.
- Co-founded the non-traditional superhero team the *Defenders* alongside the *Hulk*, *Namor the Sub-Mariner*, and the *Silver Surfer*, and later joined the secretive superhero council known as the *Illuminati*.
- Brought to international cinematic prominence within the Marvel Cinematic Universe by English actor *Benedict Cumberbatch*, debuting in the eponymous 2016 feature film.

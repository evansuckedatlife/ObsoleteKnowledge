## summary

**The Dark Side of the Moon** is the eighth studio album by the English progressive rock band *Pink Floyd*, released in March 1973 by Harvest Records. Engineered by *Alan Parsons* at London's Abbey Road Studios, the concept album explores thematic preoccupations with greed, the passage of time, mental illness, conflict, and human mortality. Widely regarded as a pinnacle of sonic experimentation and studio engineering, it remains one of the best-selling and most critically acclaimed records in music history.

## you gotta know

- Recorded in two distinct sessions between May 1972 and January 1973 at Abbey Road Studios in London, [[england]].
- Features an iconic gatefold album cover designed by *Hipgnosis* and *George Hardie*, depicting a triangular prism dispersing white light into a spectrum.
- Explores lyrical themes written primarily by bassist *Roger Waters*, motivated in part by the mental breakdown of former bandmate *Syd Barrett*.
- Integrates complex studio effects, including tape loops of ringing cash registers on the track *"Money"* and synchronized chiming clocks on *"Time"*.
- Highlights a wordless, soaring vocal improvisation by singer *Clare Torry* on the monumental track *"The Great Gig in the Sky"*.
- Built on philosophical spoken-word snippets recorded by interviewing studio staff and road crew members with questions about death, violence, and madness.
- Achieved unprecedented chart longevity on the American Billboard 200, charting for over nine hundred weeks.
- Associated with the famous urban legend *"The Dark Side of the Rainbow"*, which syncs the album's playback to the 1939 film *The Wizard of Oz*.
